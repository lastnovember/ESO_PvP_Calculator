Update 50 Inc.3 Live Patch Notes: All Platforms

Posted 2026-08-10 by ZOS_Kevin · forums.elderscrollsonline.com/en/discussion/697520 · archived 2026-09-12

This is the third and final incremental patch for Update 50 in The Elder Scrolls Online. This incremental addresses general fixes and adjustments for Dynamic Encounters and the new Thieves Guild questline. Additionally, there are general improvements and fixes to Tamriel Tomes challenges, to aid in a better play experience.

The full list of patch notes is available below. Thanks, everyone!

  • Overview
  • Fixes & Improvements
    • Base Game
      • Dynamic Encounters
      • Events
      • Exploration & Itemization
      • Quests & Zones
      • Tamriel Tomes

  • Dynamic Encounters
    • Dynamic Encounters will now start every 15 minutes instead of 30.

  • Whitestrakes Mayhem
    • Corrected an error where Whitestrakes Mayhem quests were giving out trade bars unintentionally.

  • Dynamic Encounters
    • Significantly increased the drop rate for Phynaster's Gem of Agility. This is still uncommon, but is much more attainable overall.


Thieves Guild – Glenumbra
  • Codex and Cartel
    • Fixed a case where you could be stuck on the step "Help Basil Menant."
  • Favor for Holgunn One-Eye
    • Updated the quest assist for a much better location for Hunting Ghosts that is not affected by layering or quest completion.
  • Favor for Lady Arabelle
    • Fixed an issue where the quest would vanish from your journal and make it not completable.

  • General
    • The Seasonal Overview page (“Page 0”) has been updated based on the latest Season One schedule.
    • The Valiant Rogue weapons no longer play an unintentional sound effect while equipped.
  • Challenges
    • The Weekly Challenge "Complete Thieves Guild Finder Quests" has been renamed "Complete Thieves Guild Reacquisition Board Quests".
    • Daggerfall Reacquisition quests now properly count for the Weekly Challenges "Complete Guild Daily Quests" and "Complete Thieves Guild Reacquisition Board Quests".
    • The Weekly Challenge "Complete Thieves Guild Reacquisition Board Quests" now requires one quest per repeat, down from eight.
    • The Weekly Challenge "Complete Guild Daily Quests" will now properly give bonus progress for all Thieves Guild Daily Quests. In addition, Undaunted Pledges now also give progress to this Weekly Challenge.
