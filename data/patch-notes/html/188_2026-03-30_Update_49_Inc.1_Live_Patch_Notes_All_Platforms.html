Update 49 Inc.1 Live Patch Notes: All Platforms

Posted 2026-03-30 by ZOS_Kevin · forums.elderscrollsonline.com/en/discussion/690278 · archived 2026-09-12

This is the first incremental patch for Update 49 in The Elder Scrolls Online. This incremental addresses issues in Base Game content. Specifically, this is the first pass to address issues that emerged post Update 49 Launch.

We resolve several opportunities for crashes, fix skill scrolls being used during transformation, and improve the visibility of Add-on dependencies. There are also a few Xbox specific fixes to help with the overall experience by reducing unintended long load times and unintended UI issues on gamepad.

The full list of patch notes is available below. If you expected a specific bugfix and it is not reflected below, we intend to have more fixes out in the next incremental and in Update 50. Thanks to everyone who reported issues during launch!

  • Overview
  • Combat & Gameplay
    • Combat & Abilities
  • Fixes & Improvements
    • Base Game

  • General
    • Item sets and passive effects that trigger off killing blows no longer activate unintended actions to non-killing blows.

  • Cyrodiil
    • The UI now correctly displays your Campaign Leaderboard updates and Reward Rank.
    • Potential Crashing resolved if you gained AP while in a Cyrodiil delve or Imperial City District.

  • Scribing
    • Fixed an issue that caused scribing scripts to have English text when the language was set to Russian or Simplified Chinese.

  • General
    • Xbox Only: Purchasing a crown pack no longer will cause potential game crashes.

  • Battleground Spotlights
    • During Battleground Spotlights, Group Finder listings now specify the event as “Spotlights” rather than the old term, “Battleground Weekends.”

  • Skill Scrolls
    • Skill Scrolls can no longer be used while transformed into a creature with unique ability bars.

  • General
    • Fixed a potential crash when viewing non-combat pets.
    • Fixed a potential crash when the saved display index was not connected to the GPU when launching the game.

  • General
    • Xbox Only:Fixed an issue on Xbox where trying to view the gamer card of a guild invitee in the guild roster would bring up the guild leaders gamer card.
    • Resolved a potential UI error when opening the Gold Coast Bazaar on Gamepad UI.
    • Xbox Only: You will no longer experience long load times after changing the game’s text language on the character select screen.[/color]
  • Add-ons
    • You will no longer fail to show Add-on dependencies when trying to preview certain Add-ons in the Add-on browser.
  • General
    • Issue: Those running a multi-monitor setup, booting up ESO may have the game screen appear on the wrong monitor, despite your settings.
      • Workaround: As a temporary workaround, you can change your monitor assignments in the Windows Display Settings, but we recognize this isn't ideal.
    • Issue: Reports of getting dismounted on the bridge in Stormhaven leading into Wayrest from Dro-Dara Plantation Wayshring
    • Issue: Searing Claw III and Searing Claw IV are not showcasing differently in the tooltip. Which they should be.
    • Issue: [PS5] Change profile option on main login - nothing happens when interacting with this, and it renders the "Play" button non functional as well, requiring closing the game to fix it.
    • Issue: Scion of the Blood Matron Quest not working as intended.
      • Update: We have identified the general cause of the issue. However, we are investigating why the issue is happening.
        Workaround: Based on player reports, abandoning the quest and starting it back up can resolve the issue.
