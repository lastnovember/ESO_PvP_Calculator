Xbox Patch Notes v1.41.1.0 | Update 47 Console Incremental 1

Posted 2025-09-16 by ZOS_Kevin · forums.elderscrollsonline.com/en/discussion/683168 · archived 2026-09-12

The Elder Scrolls Online v1.41.1.0 (Xbox) fixes issues identified post-Update 47 launch. The first incremental patch for Update 47 address issues in Feast of Shadows, Scions of Ithelia, Elsweyr, Solstice and Base Game content. We have resolved the missing text localization. We also have a fix for the issue with guild history information.

Additionally, there have been some dungeon fixes, performance improvements to Sunport City, and general bug fixes overall.

You can find all patch notes for Update 47, Incremental 1 below. We will continue to monitor issues and update in later incremental patches.

  • Combat & Gameplay
  • Fixes & Improvements

General
  • Fixed an issue where some abilities could fail to target entities that were in their line of sight.
    • This is namely a fix for cases like trial environments where bosses have special set play around them – such as Aetherian Archive’s The Mage, and some abilities could become blocked by line of sight with special areas and effects that should not block line of sight.


Black Gem Foundry
  • General
    • The “Show on Map” option for the pledge quest now correctly shows Black Gem Foundry’s map.
    • The Black Gem Foundry Champion achievement no longer designates that it needs to be accomplished in veteran difficulty, as some of the associated achievements can be done in normal difficulty.
    • The Slippery Soul Gem memento reward now has an icon.
  • High Soulbinder Vykand
    • Dalenor will now always face the center of the room when Vykand is defeated.
    • On normal and veteran difficulty (not Hard Mode), Vykand’s Divergence and Acute Enervation no longer have a chance to coincide.
    • Vykand no longer has a chance to summon Unstable Refracted Souls more often than intended. Gotta give those souls a break.
Naj-Caldeesh
  • General
    • The “Show on Map” option for the pledge quest now correctly shows Naj-Caldeesh’s map.
    • Two bowls in the Ritual Quarters no longer appear to be inhabited by spirits causing them to float in the air.
    • The Naj-Caldeesh Champion achievement no longer designates that it needs to be accomplished in veteran difficulty as some of the associated achievements can be done in normal difficulty.
    • The “Fight Through Pain” achievement can now only be completed in veteran difficulty as the description details.
  • Talen-Lah and Bar-Sakka
    • Bar-Sakka no longer has a chance to endlessly roll around the fight space rather than engaging you.


Rimmen Necropolis Public Dungeon
  • The group event in Rimmen Necropolis is once again completable.


Oathsworn Pit
  • Aradros the Awakened
    • Lava in the encounter area will now only damage you and your companions.
    • The “Enduring Retribution” and “Lighting the Embers” achievements can no longer be completed should you perish to environmental hazards.


Performance
  • Sunport City
    • Performance improvements have been made to the outskirts of Sunport City gates.


Cyrodiil
  • Resolved an issue with Keeps so they no longer appear in an incorrect state.


General
  • The Grimshadow Pillion Moose mount will no longer turn invisible when you view it from some specific angles.
Skill Styles
  • The “Snow Bear” skill style no longer reverses the appearance of the Feral Guardian morphs.


Crafting, Writs
  • Clothing, Leather, and Woodworking Writs will now reward the correct unidentified survey report, rather than an unidentified master writ.
  • Dragging Unknown Crafting Writ Envelopes now appropriately shows the correct icons.
  • You no longer can get Master Crafting Writs that ask for a Style that is not yet Live.


General
  • To resolve a problem with UI colors appearing washed out, colors when using HDR have been changed back to those in use before the launch of Update 47. You may need to recalibrate HDR settings in the game.
  • Fixed a crash that could occur while Multithreaded Rendering was enabled.
  • You no longer will be blocked from occasionally being unable to sit in chairs. Sitting crisis averted!
  • Fixed a crash that would sometimes occur while booting up the game in accessibility mode.


General
  • We have resolved some localized text issues where Russian translations were missing. They should now be present in the live game.
Guilds
  • Fixed an issue where guild history permissions didn't refresh when first loading in, causing people to be able to see hidden guild information.
