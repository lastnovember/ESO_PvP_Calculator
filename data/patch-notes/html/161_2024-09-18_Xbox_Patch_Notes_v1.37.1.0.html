Xbox Patch Notes v1.37.1.0

Posted 2024-09-18 by ZOS_Kevin · forums.elderscrollsonline.com/en/discussion/665917 · archived 2026-09-12

The Elder Scrolls Online v1.37.1.0 fixes several issues from Update 43 launch, including fixes for Gold Road and Base Game content. Included are fixes to Summon Shade and Winter’s Embrace abilities, adjustments to Ithelia’s facial animations when speaking, and fixes to some Infinite Archive functionality. You’ll find those and several other notable game fixes and adjustments listed below. The size of this patch is approximately 5.7GB (XB1)/ 2.37 (XBX).

Nightblade
Shadow
  • Summon Shade
    • Shadow Image: Fixed an issue where this morph could be used in areas where movement abilities are meant to be suppressed, such as Imperial City spawn areas. Now, activating the ability in these areas will always fail to spawn the shade but will still act as activating a Shadow ability and drain resources (so activate them to your Magicka’s peril).
Warden
Winter’s Embrace
  • Frozen Gate: Reduced the placement range on this ability and its morphs to 22 meters ( down from 28 meters) so that they can no longer be augmented by range enhancing effects. Fixed an issue where this ability and morphs’ teleport could ignore its ranged requirements and line of sight, creating cases where it could be used to pull enemies through walls or extreme distances. Fixed an issue where this ability and its morphs could be used in areas where movement abilities are meant to be suppressed.


Class Sets
  • Nobility in Decay: Fixed an issue where the special case of treating yourself as a corpse granted from this set would fail to activate conditions that stated “When you consume a corpse.”


Ithelia
  • Made adjustments to Ithelia's facial animations when speaking.
  • Added light reflections to Ithelia’s eyes.


Silorn Public Dungeon
  • Implemented performance and FPS improvements within certain areas of the Silorn public Dungeon (West Weald).


Ayleid Altercations
  • Fixed an issue where you could not see Elfbetta when starting her quest from an alternate location.
Tracking the Trapper
  • Marcien no longer bumps into things as he walks around the area.
General
  • Hassedaz's Voriplasm no longer heal themselves to full after being defeated.


General
  • Fixed an issue where you could encounter an infinite load screen when loading into a Battleground.
  • Fixed an issue with the Ebonheart armory station in Cyrodiil that was incorrectly stating it was out of range during interaction.


General
  • Fixed an issue where icons were not visible when crafting items in the Hircine Bloodhunter Crafting Style.


Motifs
  • Added image to replace missing one in product tiles for West Weald Legion Bundle and the Lucent Sentinel Style.
  • Added image to replace missing one in product tiles for Blind Path Cultist Style.


Infinite Archive
  • Audio
    • SFX & VO mix updates to Tho'at Replicanum bossfight to improve presentation.
  • General
    • The Mystery Transformation Verse consumable has new information on its tooltip to indicate its use is limited to once per Archive attempt.
    • The movement speed effect from having Tentacular Motivation unlocked now correctly reapplies after returning from a Portal to the Unknown.
  • Verses and Visions
    • You will no longer have the chance to select the Storm Projection vision once you have reached five stacks of that vision.
    • The Storm Projection and Adaptive Conqueror visions now function correctly after returning from a Portal to the Unknown.
    • Several Verses including Undead Avatar, Iron Atronach, Ice Avatar, Frenzied Zeal and Temporal Speed now correctly reapply all of their effects after returning from a Portal to the Unknown.

Witches Festival
  • The Lord of Fear: Fixed an issue where certain fixtures in Gatzug's nightmare might not appear.


General
  • Achievements
    • Quartermasters who can function as Guild Traders in Cyrodiil now correctly progress the achievement "Guild Store Shopper."


General
  • Fixed an issue where the Report action would sometimes appear unbound in gamepad mode when visiting a home listed in Home Tours.
  • Fixed an issue with the furnishing "Colovian Chandelier, Metal" where its light was offset.
  • Home Visitors with “Limited Visitor” permissions can now use Home Owners Mundus Stones.
UI
  • Added the “View Gamercard” option to housing occupants list on console. You can now see visiting player’s Gamercards if they have visited your home.


General
  • Fixed an issue where a number of Thalmor-dressed NPCs erroneously had their equipment changed to Divine Prosecution gear, which had the side effect of making many mage-types pantsless.
Glenumbra
  • Fixed an issue where the Red Rook Camp became associated with Gold Road ownership, causing the completion icon to look weird.
The Rift
  • Fixed an issue where a Shardborn Warpriest snuck out of West Weald and into Pinepeak Cavern.


General
  • Switching tabs in the gamepad inventory should no longer produce a UI error when search results are empty.
  • You should no longer encounter issues navigating to the scribing section of the skills menu on gamepad UI while attempting a skill Respec.
