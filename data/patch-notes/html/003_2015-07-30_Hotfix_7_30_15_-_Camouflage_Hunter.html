Hotfix 7/30/15 - Camouflage Hunter

Posted 2015-07-30 by ZOS_GinaBruno · forums.elderscrollsonline.com/en/discussion/201984 · archived 2026-09-12
Hello everyone,

We have just completed a hotfix on the North American and European console megaservers. The Fighters Guild ability Camouflage Hunter (and its morphs) will no longer stack with itself, and will instead replace itself with a new cast when rapidly using the ability. You will not need to relog or download any patch for this to take effect.
