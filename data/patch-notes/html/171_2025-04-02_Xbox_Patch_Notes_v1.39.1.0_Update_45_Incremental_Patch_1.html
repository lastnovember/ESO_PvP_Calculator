Xbox Patch Notes v1.39.1.0 | Update 45 Incremental Patch 1

Posted 2025-04-02 by ZOS_Kevin · forums.elderscrollsonline.com/en/discussion/675528 · archived 2026-09-12

The Elder Scrolls Online v1.39.1.0 (Xbox) fixes issues identified post Update 45 launch. The first incremental patch for Update 45 address issues in the Fallen Banners DLC, Gold Road, Greymoor, High Isle and Base Game content. Specifically, we are fixing the issue where character faces did not have shaders properly applied when face and skin collectibles are applied. Additionally, we have fixes to Undaunted Mask curation in Exiled Redoubt and Lep Seclusa, UI issues causing unintended visual discrepancies in Tales of Tribute, just to name a few. The fixes and improvements noted for this patch are to help smooth out immediate issues from Update 45 launch for a better gameplay experience. You will find those fixes and more in the patch notes listed below. The sizes of today's update are 46 GB (XB1)/ 1.9 GB (XBX).

Exiled Redoubt
  • General
    • Caecilius will now move more gracefully within the dungeon and he will appear more consistently throughout.

Lep Seclusa
  • General
    • The ‘Fight the Darkness’ achievement can now, correctly, only be earned in Veteran difficulty.
    • After dying to Ricochet and being revived, Shadow Cloak and its morphs will be available for use.
    • The Twice Struck achievement is now properly tracked.
    • Undaunted Masks are now properly curated.

  • Achievement
    • When attempting to buy the following envelopes from Faustina Curio the achievement tooltip text now properly reads, “Follower in the Footsteps".
      • West Weald Journeyman Furnisher's Document
      • West Weald Master Furnisher's Document

  • Antiquities
    • Raised two antiquities dig sites in Betnikh above ground level.

  • Tales of Tribute
    • Resolved a UI error which could cause a blank Patron selection screen in a Tribute game.

  • General
    • Battlegrounds music plays once again and is now working as intended!

  • General
    • Resolved an issue presented during Update 45 launch where some in-game face/body markings and skin collectibles, including makeup, resulted in character faces not displaying correctly. Character faces should now be working as intended along with applied collectibles.
    • The Fractured Glory Skin should better interact with hair styles.
    • The Dancer Personality emote /tada has been switched with /curtsey.

  • Eidetic Memory
    • The following books can now be read in their original locations after completing their associated quest:
      • Kharsthun's Translation, Second Stanza
      • Naliara's Notes, Day 2, 8, 13, and 18
    • Additionally, the Folly of Man now has a second spawn location in Alinor, on a table at the Gilded Fork.

  • General
    • The Dueling Mat furnishing no longer has missing textures.

  • General
    • The rare instance of crashing when a nearby player with a pet dies or logs out has been resolved.
  • Companions
    • Corrected Tanlorin's lockpick break reduction chance so it doesn't fire too often.

  • General
    • Fixed a crash that could occur in the settings menu on the login screen.
    • Swapped the order of the logout and quit options in the PC Gamepad main menu.
    • The custom waypoint option has been removed from the login screen. It is still available after login.
