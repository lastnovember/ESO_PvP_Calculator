Hi all, today we deployed a hotfix for an issue where Cake Slices were not properly bound to their owners. To prevent corruption of your cakes, we recommend logging out and back in if you had already purchased any slices this session. If you hadn't bought any yet or were logged out when you saw this message, you're all good.
