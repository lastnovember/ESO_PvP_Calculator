Xbox One Patch Notes v1.6.2.0

Posted 2016-12-14 by ZOS_GinaBruno · forums.elderscrollsonline.com/en/discussion/308570 · archived 2026-09-12
OVERVIEW
The Elder Scrolls Online v1.6.2.0 is an incremental patch for the Xbox One that fixes the issue where you couldn’t fully log out of the game, and also adjusts the brightness of the game after introducing HDR support. The size of this patch is approximately 475 MB.


FIXES & IMPROVEMENTS, BASE GAME PATCH
Miscellaneous
General
  • Fixed an issue where you could not log out if outside of a major city, despite the logout timer reaching zero.

Graphics
  • Fixed an issue where the game was too dark after introducing HDR support. You may need to further adjust your brightness or viewing mode through your TV settings to make ESO in HDR look optimal.

Quests & Zones
Greenshade
  • Fixed an issue where a killable NPC would instantly respawn after meeting his demise.
