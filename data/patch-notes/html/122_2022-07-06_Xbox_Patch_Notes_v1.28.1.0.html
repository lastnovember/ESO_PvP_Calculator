Xbox Patch Notes v1.28.1.0

Posted 2022-07-06 by ZOS_GinaBruno · forums.elderscrollsonline.com/en/discussion/610386 · archived 2026-09-12

The Elder Scrolls Online v1.28.1.0 fixes a number of issues within High Isle including quests, Dreadsail Reef trial, Tales of Tribute, and even made a few changes for the popular Petrified Oaken Loop Lead. We also fixed a few game crashes, un-fixed a fix we made in Update 34 involving emotes and props, and more. The size of this patch is approximately 1.31 GB.


  • Fixes & Improvements
    • High Isle Chapter
    • Combat & Gameplay
      • Combat & Abilities
    • Base Game



Dreadsail Reef Trial
  • General
    • All Dreadsail pirates will now properly engage in the Fleet Queen's Parlors when appropriate.
    • You will now be relocated to the correct location should you fall off in Tempest Heights.
    • Defeating Tideborn Taleria on Veteran Mode will now guarantee a drop for Gold-quality Jewelry, in addition to other Perfected purples.
  • Lylanar and Turlassil
    • Fallen Companions are no longer removed should the encounter reset.
    • Updated the effect tooltip for the Hindered effect.
    • Calamitous Swords and Incendiary Axes no longer explode outward when their caster is defeated.
    • The Destructive Ember and Piercing Hailstone now remain active for longer after either Lylanar or Turlassil are defeated.
    • Decreased the damage of Scorching Hack and Freezing Slash.
    • After casting Charred Constriction, Lylanar no longer follows up immediately with a Scorching Hack.
    • After casting Frigidarium, Turlassil no longer follows up immediately with a Freezing Slash.
    • Being hit by Charred Constriction or Frigidarium now removes Devitalized, Rattled and Hindered.
    • Reduced the stun duration of Charred Constriction and Frigidarium when applied to players holding either dome.
  • Tideborn Taleria
    • Dreadsail Ascension will once again kill all player characters within the Coral Caldera.


Achievements
  • Fixed an issue where the Firepot Explorer achievement would occasionally not be granted after defeating the boss in the Firepot delve.

Antiquities
  • Doubled the drop rate for many Antiquity Leads found in safeboxes, including the Petrified Oaken Loop.
  • Introduced another source for the Petrified Oaken Loop Lead for you to discover.
  • Fixed a dig site in High Isle that was spawning below the ground.


Monsters
  • Esh'curnu the Behemoth in the Firepot Delve will now respawn appropriately.

NPCs
  • The Knight and Squire no longer cause Captain Jimila or any other NPCs following you to despawn when they depart on their adventures.
  • The NPCs in Castle Navire will no longer provide a bounty if attacked.


General
  • Fixed several issues related to quest rewards including unusable items, incorrect enchantments, and item sets.
  • You can now properly travel to your friends as expected around High Isle.
  • Each Volcanic Vent now has a unique name.
  • The Shrine of Mara and the respec shrines in Gonfalon Bay are now present and functional.
  • You can now share the completion of all quest steps in all World Boss-related dailies.

Quests
  • The Ascending Storm:
    • Irnskar will now appear more reliably in Memorial Hall.
    • Fixed an issue where your quest progress could roll back when re-entering Memorial Hall.
  • Escape from Amenos: You can now find Lady Ayrenn where the quest pin says she’s located on the quest step to talk to her.
  • The Tournament Begins: Fixed an issue where you were blocked from turning in this quest.
  • Tournament of the Heart: Fixed an issue where your quest progress could become blocked while in a group on the quest step to Unlock the Catacombs.
  • Tower Full of Trouble:
    • This quest will now properly reset on the correct quest step if you exit the area or reload after lighting the ritual sconces.
    • You can now correctly use the Spoon of Undo to transform the Enchanted Cows back to their natural forms.
    • You can now advance to the end of the quest even if Ember remains behind to examine her handiwork.
  • The Missing Prowler:
    • Staggered group members can now free the Prowler independent of each other.
    • All group mates can now collect the Prowler key or receive it when others in their group obtain it.


General
  • Fixed an issue where achievements requiring Tales of Tribute matches against NPCs were not progressing after NPC matches.
  • If a Tribute player's ready check is declined, the other player is now automatically reentered in the queue.
  • The Tales of Tribute Master achievement now requires all component Achievements to be completed - including Tales of Travel.
    • Note: If you had already completed this achievement without Tales of Travel, you will not need to redo the achievement.
  • Tales of Tribute clues now note that you need to hang onto the clues until you acquire the upgrade.
  • Clues now require a confirmation to delete them.
  • Increased the quality of clues from superior to epic.
  • Fixed an issue where Tales of Tribute experience would sometimes not apply until you logged out and back in.
  • Fixed an issue where invalid Activity Finder errors could prevent matches from starting.
  • Fixed an issue that prevented you from looting an individual Tribute card upgrade when following a clue.

Gameplay
  • Jeering Shadow now is the same cost as the Prowling Shadow card it upgrades into.

NPCs
  • The Eastmarch Tribute club now has an expert player.
  • Tribute NPCs are now a little smarter about strategies using some Patron abilities.
  • Fixed an issue that caused Murzaga gra-Ghorn to disappear when losing a match to her until you reloaded the zone.
  • Tribute Hidden Masters will no longer present redundant dialogue options.

Rewards
  • Fixed an issue where Tales of Tribute PvP rank placement rewards were not granted until the next rank was achieved.

UI
  • Fixed an issue where rank up rewards would not appear in the match end summary.
  • The Tribute post-match summary now correctly displays rewards granted for increasing club rank and displays the correct value for current seasonal XP progress instead of a full bar.
  • The correct name and icon are now displayed in the end of game summary from Tales of Tribute daily Activity Finder rewards.
  • Slayer Deck Fragment now refers to "World Bosses" instead of "Group Bosses."
  • Updated endgame text to make clear that you can meet or exceed your opponent's prestige, not merely exceed.
  • Fixed an issue where the favored text for Founder Fillia's Patron was not showing up.

Visuals
  • Fixed an issue that caused patron/card glows to disappear upon resolving a card choice selection.



World
  • Werewolf
    • Roar
      • Deafening Roar (morph): Fixed an issue where this morph was not applying Off Balance to enemies.



General
  • Rolled back a previous fix affecting fixture props. You can now once again have your own weapons appear when using some emotes, like /sworddance, if you are clever enough to figure out how…


General
  • Fixed an audio issue affecting the Lady Thorn Weapon Pack.


Homes
  • Reverted an unintentional change to the base of one of the archway pillars in Shalidor’s Shrouded Realm.


General
  • Fixed a crash that was most likely to happen when logging out or exiting the game.
  • Fixed a rare crash that could occur when connecting to the server under certain network conditions.
  • Fixed a crash that could occur when closing the game client while having a text edit box selected.
  • Fixed an issue that would cause the game to become unplayable and require a restart when viewing the Credits during the installation process.


Alik’r Desert
  • Lost City of the Na-Totambu: Fixed an issue where Paldeen would get stuck when you moved to check out the tablet.

Grahtwood
  • Bosmer Insight: Fixed an issue where the Spriggan Spirit could end up with a non-functional talk prompt.

Stonefalls
  • Delaying the Daggers: Fixed an issue where you could spawn a lot of Garyn Indorils.


General
  • Fixed an issue where the tooltip for alliance/class/race in character creation would lose its background when toggling the gear preview.
  • Fixed an issue where leaderboards would sometimes not display.
