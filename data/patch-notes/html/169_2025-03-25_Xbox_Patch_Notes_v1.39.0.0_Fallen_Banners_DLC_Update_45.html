Welcome to The Elder Scrolls Online v1.39.0.0(Xbox) Fallen Banners Dungeon Pack and Update 45 base game patch! This update brings two brand new dungeons, new homes, new furnishings, new gear to earn, and several quality-of-life improvements for the base game. Additionally, there are some incremental combat changes and general bug fixes.

Along with the new dungeons, we have some new base game features and quality-of-life features being added in Update 45. Player Response Options open a new way to add roleplay choice as you interact with quest characters, ultimately impacting their responses and quest outcomes. For quality-of-life systems, you’ll see an overhaul of the in-game map, Green Champion Point Star improvements, mount speed adjustments and starter zone art refresh, just to name a few.

There will be additional content going live later in the Update 45 update cycle, like this year’s prologue quest. The prologue quest, “A Guild In Crisis”, sees the resurgence of a once defeated foe in The Worm Cult wandering Tamriel once again. Look forward to this and the all-new Reaper Strikes, Worm Cult themed encounters popping up across Tamriel.

However until then, for a full detailed list of Fallen Banner and Update 45 content, please check our patch notes below. This sizes of this update are 39.98GB (XB1)/ 11.4 GB (XBX).

Fallen Banners Dungeon DLC
Gather your party for two challenging new PvE dungeon experiences: Lep Seclusa and Exiled Redoubt. Unlock new achievements and earn new rewards including item sets, collectibles, and more as you fight to preserve what little peace remains in the war-torn region of Cyrodiil.*

*Please note that the Fallen Banners Dungeon Pack is currently available for ESO+ Members. There will be additional ways to acquire the Dungeon Pack after Update 45 Console Launch, including in the Crown Store. Please see Matt Firor's post here for the original statement, and stay tuned for more information during the April ESO Direct.

Exiled Redoubt
  • The Albus family--once staunch proponents of peace and finding a diplomatic end to the Three Banners War--suddenly turned bloodthirsty. Whatever caused their change of heart sparked a movement that seeks to punish every soldier, the generals that command them, and the heads of the alliances themselves. The Albus family’s machinations started small, but their goal is total destruction.

Lep Seclusa
  • The Three Banners War impacted lives all across Tamriel and perhaps no one felt that impact more than the soldiers that fought and bled on the front lines. As the war dragged on, a growing number of veterans became disillusioned with the constant fighting and sought a means to end the conflict. One such individual is Orpheon the Tactician, the former battlefield emperor of Cyrodiil. Orpheon broke from his chains of allegiance and recruited despondent deserters into a fearsome army. Orpheon’s goal is to seize an Elder Scroll said to have the power to end the bloodshed. All that stands in his way are a handful of monks and moth priests sworn to protect the Scroll.

Base Game

Map Improvements
The team has been looking for ways to improve ESO’s in-game map, with the intention of making it easier to understand and parse for both new players and veteran players alike. While there are still more changes we wish to implement in future packages, the first batch of quality-of-life improvements includes the following changes:
  • The Tamriel view of the map will now only show one Wayshrine per zone.
  • The Tamriel view of the map will now only show favorited and primary homes. You may still favorite as many homes as you’d like.
  • Dungeons and Trials will no longer be shown by default on the Tamriel view of the map, but can still be toggled on.
  • The Tamriel, Blackreach, and Aurbis views of the map now have a more catered filter list.
  • The ‘Wayshrines’ filter has been split into the following filters:
    • Wayshrines
    • Dungeons
    • Trials
    • Houses
  • If you have a focused quest, the quest’s ultimate location will now show on the Tamriel and Aurbis maps. If the ultimate location is on the ring of the Aurbis, the location will be shown on the appropriate symbolic location, like Clockwork City.
  • Quest indicators, group member indicators, and player indicators will now show on symbolic locations if said indicators are pointing to those locations.
  • The names of zones in the Tamriel view of the map will now appear as you zoom in.
  • When hovering over a Wayshrine, the name of the zone will now appear in the tooltip.

Updates to Initial Mount Speed
The team wanted your initial experience unlocking a mount to be more impactful, so we've made significant improvements to the speed of mounts prior to unlocking Major Gallop. The end result of these changes is that mounts will start out noticeably faster than they did previously, i.e. you are now much less likely to be overtaken on foot when mounted, while still ending up at the same speed (or very slightly faster at the top end). The details of these changes are:
  • Increased the base mounted speed bonus to 42%, up from 15%.
  • Major Gallop is now Gallop, and counts as a Minor buff instead of a Major buff. The multiplicative bonus of Gallop is now 15% instead of 30%.
    • Note: Due to the way that this bonus multiplies with base speed, this ends up being no change for fully trained mounts, or a very slight buff if you’re also using the Gifted Rider champion star. See below for more detail!
Here are the percent changes different folks can expect to see, as a percentage change from their prior speed:
  • Sprinting on a brand new mount: +19%
  • Sprinting on a trained mount: +13%
  • Sprinting on a brand new mount with Gallop: +5%
  • Sprinting on a trained mount with Gallop: No Change
  • Sprinting on a trained mount with Gallop and Gifted Rider: +1%

Starter Zone Refresh
The starter zones of Betnikh, Stros M’kai, Bleakrock, Bal Foyen, and Khenarthi’s Roost have all undergone a makeover, bringing them more in line visually with the detail of ESO’s recent additions such as West Weald or the Telvanni Peninsula.

These starter zones have received improvements to their terrain transitions, materials and lighting, and atmosphere. Signs and banners have been positioned to better highlight services (such as crafting or banking locations), and lighting has been adjusted to better define important paths and other areas of detail. With the addition of more organic clutter and props, various portions of the zones should feel a little less sparse and a little more lived in.

Player Response Options – A New Way to Engage in Conversations with Story NPCs
There are now opportunities in some quests that provide a choice as to how you want to roleplay your character known as Player Response Options. While these options usually provide roleplaying choices on how to respond to characters and alter their responses, they can sometimes alter the course of a quest in unexpected ways.
Player Response Options display a special icon when a dialogue choice is available. Here are the types of dialogue choices you may encounter, in addition to the usual neutral voice response:
  • Kind: A supportive, compassionate, or amusing response to an NPC.
  • Grim: A darker, stern, sarcastic, or harsh response to an NPC.
  • Merciful: A charitable response to an NPC in dire circumstances.
  • Ruthless: A vengeful response to an NPC in dire circumstances.
  • Joker: A quip, ironic response, or comedic reply to an NPC.
  • Flirty: An amorous response toward an NPC.

First Stages of Console Add-On Support
In preparation of console add-on support coming later this year, Update 45 will set the groundwork to ensure add-on developers have the tools needed to support their add-ons for console players. There will now be a gamepad version of the add-on manager.

A new UserSetting.txt entry will allow add-on developers to place the game into console mode. This will prevent loading any keyboard/PC specific files and fonts, load console only files, and expose new console specific APIs. The main purpose of this is to allow add-on developers a way to develop and test console add-ons without needing a console.

With this, developers can get a jump on creating and testing console add-ons so that when the feature proper goes live, they'll be able to upload them right away. Please note that console add-on support will only be for current generation consoles, as they can support the memory limitation needed.
