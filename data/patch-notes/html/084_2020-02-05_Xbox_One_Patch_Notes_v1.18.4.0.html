Xbox One Patch Notes v1.18.4.0

Posted 2020-02-05 by ZOS_GinaBruno · forums.elderscrollsonline.com/en/discussion/511363 · archived 2026-09-12

The Elder Scrolls Online v1.18.4.0 adds additional stability and memory leak fixes, along with a few smaller bug fixes. The size of this patch is 650MB.




Items
  • The Dungeon Explorer costume once again hides shields while they are docked on your character’s back.



General
  • Fixed a rare case where some inventory items could disappear when evolving a Nascent Indrik under very specific circumstances.



General
  • Fixed some memory leaks that could cause crashes and degraded performance, particularly when participating in combat or in high traffic zones for long periods of time.
  • Fixed a crash that could happen when retrieving an item from the guild bank.



General
  • Fixed a handful of typos or translation errors involving Greymoor pre-purchase items.
