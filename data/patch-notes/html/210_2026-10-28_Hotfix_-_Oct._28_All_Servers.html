Hotfix - Oct. 28 (All Servers)

Posted 2026-10-28 by ZOS_JessicaFolsom · forums.elderscrollsonline.com/en/discussion/700640 · archived 2026-10-30
Hello! Today, we completed a hotfix to address three issues; below are the fix notes for each. You do not need to restart your game to see these fixes. Thank you!
  • Evil Hunter: Fixed an issue where this morph's damage bonus could stack with itself.
  • Healing Springs: Fixed an issue where this morph's Magicka Recovery could persist longer than intended.
  • Solo March of Sacrifices: Corrected an issue where players would accumulate effects with every death, and eventually stop being able to cast their skills.
