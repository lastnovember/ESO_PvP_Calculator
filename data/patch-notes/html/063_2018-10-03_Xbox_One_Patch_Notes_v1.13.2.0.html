Xbox One Patch Notes v1.13.2.0

Posted 2018-10-03 by ZOS_GinaBruno · forums.elderscrollsonline.com/en/discussion/439019 · archived 2026-09-12

The Elder Scrolls Online v1.13.2.0 reverts the change made to HDR in Update 19. If you are utilizing the HDR feature, it will now appear as it did prior to Wolfhunter and Update 19 launch. We’ve also fixed the issue where you were not able to fight Keelsplitter, the World Boss found in Summerset. The size of this patch is approximately 706MB.
