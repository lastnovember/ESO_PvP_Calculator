Xbox One Patch Notes v1.13.1.0

Posted 2018-09-17 by ZOS_GinaBruno · forums.elderscrollsonline.com/en/discussion/435812 · archived 2026-09-12

The Elder Scrolls Online v1.13.1.0 fixes several issues with dungeons in Wolfhunter and Dragon Bones, some various game crashes, and includes several performance improvements. Please see the list below for the full list of patch notes for this patch, which is approximately 12.8GB in size.





Dungeons
  • Fang Lair
    • Fixed multiple issues with the Dire Immolation ability cast by the Cadaverous Dire Wolves, and the Withering Spores ability cast by the Volatile Fungus.
    • The menagerie encounter no longer resets after the Cadaverous Senche-Tiger or Cadaverous Guar recover from their stun.
  • Scalecaller Peak
    • Plague Concoctor Mortieu now correctly applies his infection debuffs to all players in the encounter, making the achievement “Doctor's Orders” properly obtainable.





Dungeons
  • March of Sacrifices
    • Balorgh
      • Reduced the health of the Stranglers spawned during the Hunting Party.
      • Balorgh will no longer skip his cast of Hunting Party at the respective health thresholds. 
      • Hunting Party no longer fears or stuns players when cast.





General
  • Fixed an issue where pets were displaying the visual effects from crowd control immunity when they were first summoned or teleported back to your side.

World
  • Werewolf
    • Devour: Fixed an issue where Skill Points previously spent into this ability were not being refunded to you.





General
  • Keep upgrades to walls, doors and guards will now have the intended values after being upgraded or downgraded.

Battlegrounds
  • You will no longer be able to stand in the river below the bridge in Istrius Outpost.


General
  • Fixed some issues where you could receive the warning “Someone has declined the invite” when using the Grouping Tool, even if everyone accepted the invite.


General
  • Fixed some game crashes related to quests and animations.
  • Implemented some general performance improvements.


Coldharbour
  • Village of the Lost: Mezha-dro will no longer fail to do his job sealing the Rift if you run to the first location.

Cyrodiil
  • Death to the Crone: Fixed an issue that was preventing you from advancing the quest after killing Brazatii.


General
  • Fixed various UI errors related to Skills that would frequently appear.
  • Fixed an issue where the help button on the Announcement panel would appear when it didn’t go to a specific help page.

Localization
  • Fixed an issue where the five-piece set bonuses for some new items sets were not displaying in French and German.
  • Fixed an issue where the German “Upgrade to Summerset” screen was still displaying pre-purchase information.
