Hotfix 8/11/15 - Vampire & Werewolf

Posted 2015-08-11 by ZOS_JessicaFolsom · forums.elderscrollsonline.com/en/discussion/206355 · archived 2026-09-12
Hi everyone,

We just completed a hotfix on all ESOTU megaservers. This patch hotfixed an exploitable bug that made it possible for a character to be both a Vampire and Werewolf at the same time.

We have already reached out via email to the players who are currently using this exploit, and asked them to drop one of the two skill lines. If you are one of these players, you will find all details related to this issue and our expectations thereof in the email we sent out. If you have any questions about your account or this matter, please reach out to our Support Team here.

You will not need to relog or download any patch for this to take effect.

Thank you.
