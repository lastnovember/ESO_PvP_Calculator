Hotfix for Empty Bags of Jubilee Yesteryear and Reward Box Adjustments (all megaservers)

Posted 2025-04-07 by ZOS_Kevin · forums.elderscrollsonline.com/en/discussion/675750 · archived 2026-09-12
Hi All, today (April 7, 2025) we completed a hotfix to resolve issues with the Bags of Jubilee Yesteryear. Additionally, we are adjusting the drop rate of some event reward box items that are not dropping at the intended rate.
  • Fixed an issue related to the Bags of Jubilee Yesteryear sometimes being empty if you hadn't collected all the pages of the Earthbone Ayleid outfit style.
  • Resolving an issue of highly valuable items in all event reward boxes dropping at an unintended higher rate. The corrected rate will impact highly valuable items in all event reward boxes as well as Archival Sacks of Provisioning, including Diminished and regular Aetherial Dust, Rubyblossom Extract, Perfect Roe, Cyrodiil Citrus, and Mourning Dew.
If you have any empty Bags of Jubilee Yesteryear, please make sure to submit a ticket with customer service to get your tickets refunded. Thanks for your patience as we resolved this matter.
