Xbox Patch Notes v1.41.0.0 | Update 47

Posted 2025-09-03 by ZOS_Kevin · forums.elderscrollsonline.com/en/discussion/682702 · archived 2026-09-12

Welcome to The Elder Scrolls Online v1.41.0.0 and our latest content release, the Feast of Shadows Dungeon Pack and Update 47. This is the continuation of the Season of the Worm Cult Content Pass.

The new Feast of Shadows Dungeon Pack contains two brand new dungeons to challenge your skills. The first dungeon, Naj-Caldeesh, pits you against a group of Argonian necromancers, seeking to corrupt and control an ancient xanmeer’s keystone. It’s up to you and your allies to stop them before they can complete their nefarious machinations. In the second dungeon, Black Gem Foundry, you and your party must venture into a Coldharbour manufactory and shut down their ability to craft terrifying soul-stealing armaments. Both dungeons present new challenges for you and your allies to face, along with new gear to collect.

In addition to the DLC Pack, there are several base game quality of life features coming in Update 47. The long requested Stackable Survey Reports, allowing you to save some inventory space while keeping those survey reports organized. Quest Dialogue Replay is here to help in case you missed the last bit of dialogue in a quest conversation. Last but certainly not least, mount travel has gotten even better with the addition of Mount Swimming.

There are more changes being added to base game, including Infinite Archive Vision Reroll, Compass Improvements, and an update to dodge roll visualization. These along with combat adjustments are all available now in Update 47. The size of today's patch is 37.1GB (XB1)/ 39.1GB (XBX).

Feast of Shadows
Two New Dungeons! A new PvE dungeon pack, Feast of Shadows introduces two challenging new four-player dungeons for Tamriel’s adventurers to explore and overcome. Both dungeons feature their own complete stories and in-game rewards while tying thematically into the larger Seasons of the Worm Cult saga.

Naj-Caldeesh
The legendary Xanmeer of Naj-Caldeesh was hidden long ago. All but forgotten by the Stone-Nest Argonians of Solstice and left with few defenses, it seemed as though the Xanmeer’s secret would remain buried. However, a faction of Argonian Necromancers uncovered the entrance. Help stop the necromancers before they can corrupt the mysterious power inside and unleash Naj-Caldeesh's Voskrona Guardians on the rest of Solstice.

Black Gem Foundry
In one of the many twisted pathways of Coldharbour lies the freshly forged Black Gem Foundry. The cruel masters of this infernal armory are fusing black soul gems into weapons and armor, resulting in armaments that steal life essence with each attack. Dalenor the Summoner, scholar of Daedra and friend of atronachs, barely escaped with his life after discovering the Foundry's nefarious plot to send these armaments to support Coldharbour's forces along the Writhing Wall. He's located a portal that leads directly to the Black Gem Foundry, but he needs your help to shut it down.

Base Game
Unidentified Survey Reports
When you obtain a survey report, they will start in an unopened envelope called “Unidentified [Tradeskill] Survey Report”, where [Tradeskill] is the name of the tradeskill. These unidentified survey reports will stack. Opening these reveal a survey report.

Infinite Archive Vision Reroll
  • You now have the opportunity to use Archival Fortunes to reroll your possible Vision selections when presented.
  • Cost escalates each time the reroll option is used per Vision choice, resetting when a new Vision selection opportunity is presented.

Compass Improvements
The compass has been improved to include more details about the distance from objects that are displayed.

Mount Swimming
Your trusty steed shall fear water in Tamriel no longer! Mounts have been trained to learn how to navigate water, allowing you to traverse Tamriel in new ways.

Dodge Roll Visualization Update
This change is a new visual indicator to convey the increased stamina cost with dodge rolls in quick succession.

Replay Quest Dialogue
Did you miss that last line of dialogue? Well, fear not! With the replay dialogue feature, you can go back and replay the last conversation from the NPC you are actively speaking to.

Introduction
Greetings! It has officially been an entire update with subclassing in the wild, and we’ve been closely monitoring all the fun and wacky antics unfold in its wake. We know there’s a lot of discussion over its impact in the game and how it could potentially change a lot of things moving forward (namely game balance), but we’re going to continue to watch as data comes in and solidify this brave new world! This patch will be relatively light with balance adjustments on classes, since we’re still discussing a lot of potential ways to move forward and waiting to see how behaviors, sentiment, and engagement change. This period is critical to ensure adjustments in the new subclassing era are done right for a positive play experience.

Being light on class balance still means there is some! We still have a few adjustments, largely aimed at correcting some of the less ideal behavior we’re seeing with passive slot effects. Abilities like Grim Focus and Bound Armor are often being used purely passively, granting more power than we’re happy with for how simple they’re being used. So we’re going to do some gentle reworking of what they grant. In this update, neither of these abilities nor their morphs will grant unique, unnamed effects for slotting. Instead, they’ll be granting Major or Minor effects to ensure they’re still helpful, but not in the same way as before.

Outside of this, you can expect two other main initiatives in this patch; regular bug fixing and a balance pass on some of our item sets. This update we’ll be doing a pretty hefty number pass on our trial sourced sets, where we try and better reward stack-based sets for their more restrictive requirements, while also lessening the delta between those who can utilize them perfectly and those who struggle to get good milage from them. The two main ways we’ll be achieving this is by building some amount of power into a passive effect, The other will be through lowering how many stacks you need to reach for them to be worthwhile! Most of these cases result in buffs for reaching their upper limits, while also making them more consistently viable by allowing you to reach those limits.

Outside of these initiatives, we’ll continue to lurk and watch over the discussions and experiences you get into, while we formulate on how to best deliver on improving the gameplay experience Tamriel has to offer. Happy reading, safe travels, and we hope to see you in Tamriel!


General
  • Updated the visual effects for Dodge Roll to better convey the ramping Stamina cost of rolling multiple times in quick succession.
  • Many Fear sources have been updated to no longer place two effects on you while under their influence (a fear effect and an immobilization to prevent you from moving around). Fear will now handle both occurrences in one, resulting in a case where some interaction with passives and sets will change.
  • Block no longer fails if the required resource was gained while actively Bracing.
  • Block no longer fails when using channel or cast time abilities while bar swapping and reinputting the command.
  • Fixed an issue where some effects in your buff tray and character sheet could appear as if they had multiple stack counts, even when they didn’t. This occurred mainly when an effect with a stack count ended; their stacks would not properly flush and instead appear on the UI of the next effect in line.
  • Fixed an issue where some abilities could set your Health to full when activated in very specific and rare cases. Those who know, know.
  • Fixed an issue where some transformation Ultimates could surprise you upon completion, interrupting your abilities while causing them to go on cooldown.
  • Interacting with certain doors no longer prevents your character from being able to use single target abilities against targets. Some doors are better left un-opened.
  • Fixed an issue where temporary summon abilities could be cast and drain resources, even when their summon would fail to spawn due to being at the pet limit. This affects the following abilities:
    • Summon Shade and morphs
    • Sacrificial Bones and morphs
    • Skeletal Mage and morphs
    • Spirit Mender and morphs
  • The following passives granting unique forms of Ultimate when meeting their conditions now have similar effects. With this change, on cooldown, they cannot be stacked.
    • Affects the following: Prism, Transfer, Mountain's Blessing, Savage Beast, Implacable Outcome, and Corpse Consumption.
    • This cooldown works by taking the first effect to activate and using the cooldown of that effect to prevent any of these other effects (including itself) from activating for the listed duration. If you have Corpse Consumption and Prism and trigger Corpse Consumption first – you will not be able to trigger either for 16 seconds. If you trigger Prism after that cooldown window, the next cooldown would be 6 seconds.

General Itemization
  • We’ve updated many stack-based item sets to grant a portion of their bonuses up front with no conditional requirements or grant more bonus per stack, while reducing their maximum stacks. This is in an effort to ensure they offer more consistent power bonuses. This also means some have seen an overall reduction in power, in efforts to reduce their ability to outshine other sets in gameplay. This is a case-by-case point however, where many sets have also been generally buffed.
