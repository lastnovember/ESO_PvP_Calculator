Xbox Patch Notes v1.32.2.0

Posted 2023-07-25 by ZOS_GinaBruno · forums.elderscrollsonline.com/en/discussion/639439 · archived 2026-09-12

The Elder Scrolls Online v1.32.2.0 includes some adjustments to Crux and fixes for a few Mundus Stone issues, along with fixes for several Necrom quests, the Antiquity Leads for the “Music Box, Glyphic Secrets”, several game crashes, and more. The size of this patch is approximately 1.99GB for Xbox One and 1.18GB for Xbox Series X/S.





Arcanist
  • Slowed down the rotation speed of the Crux icons help reduce any potential motion sickness issues.
  • Crux is no longer permanent while outside of combat and instead has a 30 second duration.
  • Curative Runeforms
    • Chakram Shields: Fixed an issue where the damage shields from different morphs of this ability could stack.


Mundus Stones
  • Fixed an issue where Mundus Stones could fail to load properly on login.
  • Fixed a rare issue where some Mundus Stones could have their effects canceled if you were interrupted at the precise time of applying them.
  • Reduced the Critical Chance rating granted from The Thief Mundus Stone to 1212, down from 1333, so it fits properly within power budgets of other stones.


Quests
  • Azandar al-Cybiades
    • Tempting Fates: Updated the quest guide pins to be clearer on where you need to go in the Riften Mages Guild to progress the quest.
  • Sharp-as-Night
    • Dim and Distant Pasts: Sharp will no longer stay rooted in place if your character dies before talking to him outside of Broken Tusk in Shadowfen.
    • The Fateweaver Key:
      • Fixed an issue where you could see multiple Azandars belonging to other players in the same spot of Ciphers Midden.
      • Fixed an issue where Azandar would not properly animate when casting his tracking spell in Ciphers Midden.

Rapport
  • Sharp now correctly gives rapport when looting a heavy sack.





General
  • Fixed an issue where some of the Antiquity Leads for the “Music Box, Glyphic Secrets” could not be acquired more than once.





General
  • The Bewilderment card no longer blocks Patron use.





Sanity’s Edge Trial
  • Ansuul the Tormentor
    • Players affected by Manic Phobia may no longer be the target of Ansuul's Sunburst attack.
    • After raising the Challenge Banner, players attuned and inside of a Torment stage, as well as those recently resurrected, may no longer be affected by Ansuul's Sunburst attack.
    • Enraged Fragments now leave a consumable corpse upon defeat.


General
  • Fixed an issue where a portal would constantly stay open in Leramil’s Study in Cipher’s Midden.
  • Updated several quest-specific books to be available after the quest to assist with Eidetic Memory.

Main Story
  • An Unhealthy Fate: Revised the post-boss sequence in the Mythos to make it easier to use the Black Book and to progress the quest.
  • Truer Words: Added a map pin to help guide you during the quest step “Search for the Fate Anomaly.”

Quests
  • A Memory of Mystery: Updated several lines of dialogue that weren’t originally configured to play during the quest.
  • Manifesting Hope: Fixed an issue where you would see incorrect tallies if doing this quest while in a group.

Delves & Public Dungeons
  • Staxuira in Gorne will no longer remain invisible if killed while teleporting.





General
  • Fixed multiple issues with the animation and deformation of the Topknot Cascade Hairstyle.


General
  • The Fortified Brass and Innate Axiom Attunable Jewelry Stations will now attune correctly.


General
  • Fixed an occasional freeze that could occur when a nearby player's companion mounted a group mount.
  • Fixed a crash that could occur when a certain skill was used in the vicinity of your character.
  • Fixed a crash caused by Daedric Sacrifices in Dragonstar Arena.
  • Fixed a rare crash related to audio.
  • Fixed a crash that could occur if you are logged out or disconnected while controlling a siege engine.
  • Fixed a crash that could occur when loading into certain areas of Reaper's March.
  • Fixed several crashes that could occur due to memory corruption.
