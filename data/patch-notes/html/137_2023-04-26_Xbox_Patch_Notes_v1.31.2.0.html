Xbox Patch Notes v1.31.2.0

Posted 2023-04-26 by ZOS_GinaBruno · forums.elderscrollsonline.com/en/discussion/632384 · archived 2026-09-12

The Elder Scrolls Online v1.31.2.0/2.44/1.26 is a small incremental patch that mainly addresses a few combat and art related issues. The size of this patch is approximately 1.18GB for Xbox One and 1.07GB for Xbox Series X.





General
  • Fixed an issue where animations would fail to play if immediately activated after breaking free.
    • Note: This fix will reintroduce the issue where attempting to Break Free a knock back ability may fail to play the Break Free animation.

Weapons
  • One Hand and Shield
    • Shield Wall: Fixed an issue where this Ultimate and its morphs could lose their effects after activating sprint.





General
  • Fixed an issue where Hide Helm incorrectly hid the Mercymother’s Coronet adornment.

Visual Effects
  • Fixed an issue where the green visual effects on the Euphotic Gatekeeper Maul were offset from the item.


General
  • Fixed an issue where the Bitter Coast Recall Customized Action was not playing the new recall animation.

Customized Actions
  • Fixed an issue with the “Bitter Coast Recall” Customized Action where escalating wayshrine fees were not correctly being applied.


General
  • Made some updates to the in-game profanity filter.
