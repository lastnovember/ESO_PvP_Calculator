Xbox Patch Notes v1.41.2.0 | Update 47 Console Incremental 2

Posted 2025-09-30 by ZOS_Kevin · forums.elderscrollsonline.com/en/discussion/683635 · archived 2026-09-12

The Elder Scrolls Online v1.41.2.0 (Xbox) fixes issues identified post-Update 47 launch. The second incremental patch for Update 47 address issues in Feast of Shadows and Solstice Content.

This is a small patch focusing on fixing achievements granted in Naj-Caldeesh. Additionally, we have added a fix to the daily max of event repeatable quests.

You can find specifics on the patch notes for Update 47, Incremental 2 below. We will continue to monitor issues and update in later incremental patches and the next update patch.

  • Overview
  • Fixes & Improvements
    • Feast of Shadows
    • Solstice


Naj-Caldeesh
  • General
    • The “Unexplored Not Unhurried,” “Grave Song Silenced,” and “Key to the Stone,” achievements will now be awarded in the correct situations.
  • Talen-Lah
    • You can now always earn the “Fight Through Pain” achievement when the conditions are met.


General
  • You will now get the correct daily max of event repeatable quests.
