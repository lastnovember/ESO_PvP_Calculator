Xbox One Patch Notes v1.19.1.0 - Harrowstorm & Update 25

Posted 2020-03-09 by ZOS_GinaBruno · forums.elderscrollsonline.com/en/discussion/515838 · archived 2026-09-12

Welcome to The Elder Scrolls Online v1.19.1.0 and the start of the Dark Heart of Skyrim with the Harrowstorm DLC game pack! Harrowstorm introduces two new 4-player dungeons – Icereach and Unhallowed Grave – in addition to a number of new item sets and collectibles. Our biggest focus for this update was further improving game performance, with an emphasis on frame rate stability, ultimately resulting in fewer drops in your game’s framerate. The combat changes you’ll see support this goal, which you can read more about in this forum post.

Additionally, please take note for this update, we’re refreshing our patching and file manifest data to remove duplicate files and older data that is no longer needed. This should result in improvements to load times, streaming of assets, reliability of asset loads, and a reduction of space taken up by the client on your hard drive. In order to achieve this, you will be required to re-download the entirety of the game. To this end, the size of this update is 68GB. To read more about this patching overhaul, please visit this website article.

After the full client re-download completes, please note that you will see an additional smaller patch download and install. This patch includes some crash fixes that we released to PC/Mac after the Update 25 launch. You don’t need to do anything on your end; just be mindful there will be an additional download that will automatically begin after the full client re-download completes.

Don’t forget, we’ve added a free Crimson Torchbug Pet to the Crown Store for the next two weeks as a thank you for completing the re-download!

HARROWSTORM DLC GAME PACK

Two New Dungeons
  • Icereach: Hidden among the churning waves of the Sea of Ghosts, this frigid isle serves as the seat of power for the cruel Icereach Coven. Now, lashing storms swirl around it. Could this be a natural phenomenon, or something more sinister?
  • Unhallowed Grave: Long ago, the soldiers of the Bangkorai Garrison buried something away in this accursed cavern—an evil too great to leave unguarded. Now, centuries later, even the most daring Redguard explorers know to give this grave a wide berth.
  • Icereach and Unhallowed Grave are 4-player Dungeons which can be reached via Wrothgar and Bangkorai respectively.
    • Icereach’s entrance is in northern Wrothgar, on the coast. The entrance is a boat used to travel to the Dungeon.
    • Unhallowed Grave’s entrance is in southeastern Bangkorai, southwest of the Fallen Wastes Dolmen.
  • Both Dungeons include a Normal version in addition to a challenging Veteran version.
  • There are unique item sets, including two new Undaunted Monster Mask sets, only available within the Dungeons.
  • Unique Achievement awards are available for completing the Dungeons, including:
    • A unique body marking
    • A unique weapon style
    • Several Titles
    • Unique housing items

New Collectibles & Dyes
Several new Collectibles can be obtained by participating in the new Harrowstorm dungeons and completing various achievements:
  • The Lyris’ Icereach Battle Axe outfit style is earned by completing the "Icereach Conqueror" achievement, accomplished by defeating all the bosses in Icereach on Veteran difficulty.
  • The Bloodrune Body Markings are earned by completing the "Unhallowed Grave Conqueror" achievement, accomplished by defeating all the bosses in Unhallowed Grave on Veteran difficulty.
  • The Bloodrune Face Markings are earned by completing the "Knock Knock" achievement, acquired by discovering Voria's Sanctum in the Unhallowed Grave dungeon and killing the beast found there.
  • The Wickerman Mishap emote is awarded for completing the "Harrowstorm Explorer" achievement, accomplished by entering either the Icereach or Unhallowed Grave dungeons.
  • The Frozen Blood dye can be obtained by completing the "Harrowstorm Delver" achievement for completing both dungeons on Veteran difficulty.

New Achievements & Titles
Harrowstorm introduces over 30 achievements for proving your prowess in the Icereach and Unhallowed Grave dungeons. Additionally, there are four titles to obtain:

BASE GAME

Performance Improvements
Frame rate stability improvements have been achieved by optimizing several systems. This means you will now encounter fewer drops in framerate, maintaining a smoother display of motion on screen. Please see below for a brief explanation of the technical work that was completed to support this.
  • Optimized multi-threading implementation to improve performance in situations with lots of effects (particles, projectile effects, weather, etc.) happening at the same time.
  • Optimized specific processes that could cause spikes in resource usage and cause FPS drops.
  • Parallelized the fixture update processes, allowing them to utilize multiple cores to improve overall performance of the game client.
  • Added additional miscellaneous CPU optimizations including the following:
    • Parallelized terrain loading
    • Optimized the Tutorial GUI
    • Optimized compass pins
    • Miscellaneous function improvements
