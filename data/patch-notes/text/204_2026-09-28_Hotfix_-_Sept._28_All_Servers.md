We are pushing a hotfix on all servers around 5:15 PM Eastern Time to address an
issue with master crafting stations
. This hotfix will not result in any downtime, but it will include rolling restarts for instances (such as houses, dungeons, trials, solo content, the Nowhere Vault, etc.) Players in those instanced spaces will see a restart warning pop up on the screen. This is not for the entire game, only the instanced spaces. If you are in an instanced space when the rolling restart happens, you will simply be kicked out of the instance.
This hotfix will stop the issue with master crafting stations from happening. Players who are currently in a bad state will be fixed with the first incremental patch in October.
Thank you!
