Today we completed a hotfix on all platforms for three issues within the Night Market. These hotfixes do not require relogging to see. Below are the hotfix notes.
Faceted Enemies:
Fixed an issue where Faceted Enemies were appearing much less frequently than intended. Faceted Enemies will now appear more often in the Night Market.
Skirmishes:
Fixed an issue where players were sometimes not getting credited for Skirmish participation. Players that revive at Rooftop Oases will now receive expected credit for Skirmish participation.
Web Eater Bust and Trophy:
The Web Eater bust and trophy were not dropping as expected in the Opulent Ordeal. With today's hotfix, they will now drop as follows:
If you have already cleared the Opulent Ordeal once or more before the hotfix went out, clear it one more time and you will get both the Web Eater bust and trophy.
If you have not yet cleared the Opulent Ordeal by the time the hotfix went out, clear it once and you will get the trophy upon completion, then the bust after your second.
With Update 50, we will be updating the tooltip text for the bust to state that it is "Awarded for defeating the Web Eater twice in the Opulent Ordeal."
