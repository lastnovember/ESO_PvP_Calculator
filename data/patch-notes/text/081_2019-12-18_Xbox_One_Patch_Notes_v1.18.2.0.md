The Elder Scrolls Online v1.18.2.0 includes several adjustments to improve overall game stability, in addition to some fixes for Battleground queuing, a fix for offline friends appearing as though they are online, and more. The size of this patch is approximately 644MB.
General
Your character will no longer get stuck in a Keep piece when knocked in the air by Volcanic Rune.
Battlegrounds
Quitting a Battleground before it's over will now result in a 5 minute penalty instead of 20 minutes.
Fixed an issue where you could not queue for a Battleground after waiting for more players to join your group.
Fixed additional edge cases that could result in unsuccessfully joining a Battleground.
Dungeons
Cradle of Shadows
Enraged Hoarvors now appear at the correct location when first entering Velidreth's Catacombs and will once again relentlessly pursue their food. Any Enraged Hoarvors that find themselves outside of the Catacombs will burrow into the ground.
General
Fixed an issue which limited how often Moongrave Fane Motif pages could drop. It will now drop rarely when killing the final boss on Normal, occasionally on Veteran, and guaranteed on Veteran Hard Mode as originally intended.
General
Placing or moving furniture will now always place the furniture in the correct orientation.
General
Fixed an issue with the memory manager that was causing incorrect, excessive memory allocations to occur, leading to game crashes.
Reduced the texture resolution of mounts for other players to aid in stability; your character’s mount will still appear as normal. Note this does not affect the Xbox One X.
Slightly reduced the view distance to help with overall stability. This change also does not apply to the Xbox One X.
Slightly increased the time it takes for characters to transition from low resolution to high resolution in an effort to improve client stability.
General
Fixed an issue that was causing friends or guild members who logout to still appear as online with missing level information.
Guild Finder
Fixed an issue where the Guild Finder would not display any results when there were more than 20.
