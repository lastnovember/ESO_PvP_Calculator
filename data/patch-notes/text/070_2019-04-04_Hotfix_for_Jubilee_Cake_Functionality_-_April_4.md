Hi everyone,
We just completed a hotfix on all platforms and megaservers involving a potential exploit associated with the Jubilee Cake 2019. You are now required to eat your own cake to reap its benefits, rather than using your friends' cakes. Note that you will still see a prompt that you can "use" a friend's cake, even though it can't actually be used. No download is required for this change.
