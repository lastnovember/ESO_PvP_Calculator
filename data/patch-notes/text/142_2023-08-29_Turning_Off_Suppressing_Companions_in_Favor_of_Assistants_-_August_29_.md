We recently identified an issue with the new feature we introduced in Update 39 that allows players to automatically re-summon Companions when they are dismissed by summoning an Assistant. We are temporarily disabling this functionality while we investigate the issue. We will reenable this feature in an upcoming incremental patch.
Thank you for your patience.
