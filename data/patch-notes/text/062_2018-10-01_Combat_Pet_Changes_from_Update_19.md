In today's PTS v4.2.2 patch notes, we noted that player combat pets will no longer take any damage while in dungeons, group arenas and Trials. This raised questions if pets will still be targeted by boss mechanics in these areas. The answer is they will, but we've been working toward making it so this is no longer the case.
With that said, we discovered there were some changes that went in with Update 19 in regards to player pets in Trials that were never noted publicly. The following Trial boss abilities no longer target player pets:
The Celestial Mage's Chain Lightning ability in Aetherian Archive
The Celestial Serpent's World Shaper ability in Sanctum Ophidia
Saint Llothis' Defiling Blast and Noxious Gas abilities in Asylum Sanctorium
Saint Olms' Storm the Heavens ability in Asylum Sanctorium
