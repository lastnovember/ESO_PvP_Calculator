Hi All, today (April 11, 2025) we completed a hotfix for the the following items:
Claiming the Prologue Quest on the Crown Store while using a gamepad no longer causes a book UI issue to appear.
When engaging with Reaper Strikes, you can now interact and destroy a Worm Cult Reaper Device after all enemies are defeated.
This issues should now be resolved. Thanks to those who flagged the issue and we hope you are enjoying the Prologue Quest!
