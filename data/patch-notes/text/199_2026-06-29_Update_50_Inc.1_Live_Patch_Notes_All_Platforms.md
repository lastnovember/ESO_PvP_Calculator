This is the first incremental patch for Update 50 in The Elder Scrolls Online. This incremental addresses issues introduced during Update 50 launch. If you do not see an issue listed here, we have more scheduled for the second incremental.
In this patch, you will see some general fixes for Werewolf and a combat adjustment to Mythic Item Shattered Path Signet. We are keeping an eye on this item, based on PTS feedback, current feedback, and data we are reviewing.
Additionally, there are some fixes for Vengeance, Player Experience Improvement items, Tamriel Tomes and more. These fixes are more general and address proper function to provide a better experience for you.
The full list of patch notes is available below. Thanks, everyone!

Guild
Psijic Order
Concentrated Barrier: Fixed an issue where this passive was not considered a proc and could trigger other effects.
World
Werewolf
Slot passives will no longer be activated while in werewolf form if the player respecs with the ability slotted on an inactive bar.
The Werewolf’s Claw Fury damage impacts no longer cause silent audio. Sounds should now play as expected.
Fixed an issue with NPC death animations when killed by Werewolf’s Slaughter.
The Werewolf tattoo effects will now smoothly fade in and out of existence when Rampage is ready to cast and once Rampage has completed.
Item Sets
Mythic Items
Shattered Path Signet: This set's damage done with status effects now only applies to monsters. Reduced the maximum damage bonus from 170% to 133%, and reduced the Ultimate required to reach the cap to 133, down from 170. Reduced the cooldown for applying Status Effects when at the maximum Ultimate threshold to 2 seconds at 133 Ultimate, down from 4s at 170.
[Developer Comment]
Spoiler Warning
Spoiler
We're making a handful of targeted adjustments to remove this set's overwhelming presence in PvP by removing the large damage bonus to status effects from PvP areas, while simultaneously gently reigning in the ceiling potential of the set overall. At the same time, we’re bumping up the floor by reducing the cooldown for this set to apply status effects.
