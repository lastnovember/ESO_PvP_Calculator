Welcome to The Elder Scrolls Online v1.37.0.0 (Xbox) and the Update 43 base game patch!  This update introduces Home Tours, a new tool to help you find and share housing creations. We’ve also made some updates to Infinite Archive to add new Arenas, Verses and Visions, plus added 7 new Class Sets and some additional curated item sets. You’ll also find new collectibles, achievements, titles, and enjoy a few additional quality of life changes including the game now remembering your last selected Guild Store or Guild Bank.
In addition to these features, we have a long list of bug fixes and improvements to the entire game, such as a number of adjustments affecting Scribing (including some new Ink sourcing!), quest content, some combat balance adjustments, and more. This size of this patch is approximately 41.3 GB (XB1)/ 9.7 GB (XBX).

Home Tours
Find and share incredible housing creations with Home Tours! View recommended homes, mark favorites for easy revisiting, list your homes for touring, and browse a continuously evolving list of homes decorated by some of the most fashionable minds in Tamriel. Start exploring the creativity of the Housing community by visiting the Home Tours section of the Activity Finder today!
List your homes on a server-wide repository of tourable homes, and welcome visitors to your home!
Set a custom nickname to help your home stand out!
Tag your listings with up to three descriptive tags, ranging from Cozy to Sinister.
No limit to the number of homes you can list – if you are a housing aficionado who decorates every home to the utmost, you can list them all!
Tour a rotating selection of the most recommended homes.
This includes a mix of fresh homes that haven’t been in the spotlight recently, as well as homes that continue to be highly recommended time after time.
You can personally recommend up to 10 homes a week, helping choose which homes will appear in the Recommended section!
Find inspiration, hidden gems, and an endless sea of variety by browsing the list of available homes at your leisure.
Filter by specific homeowners you’re a fan of, up to three descriptive tags like “Festive” and “Games”, specific houses, or overall house category.
Favorite up to 100 homes (listed or not!) and revisit them easily from the new Favorite Homes section.

Greetings everyone! Update 43 is here, and with it a slew of changes focused on following up on this year’s big feature, Scribing! We’ve gone through much of the feedback that has come in between Update 42’s PTS cycle and the feature going live, resulting in some updates to the potency of some Scripts and Grimoire combinations, general buffs to numbers, bug fixing, and some functionality changes in rare cases.
We’ve got plenty more outside of Scribing, however, with a handful of updates and additions to some class ability functionality, primarily focused on improving Necromancer’s access to the Major/Minor system, the highly requested in-combat casting of Sacrificial Bones, and a few morph buffs to spice up some gameplay potential. We also have some moderate adjustments to Wardens that aim to help differentiate power levels if you’re a tank or damage dealer, to reduce some of the power the class can easily access in PvP. We’re also reorganizing some Nightblade abilities to make sure they better align with their sourced skill line’s vision and theme, while ever-so-slightly improving their PvE damage production and peeling back some of the ease of defensive power the assassin playstyle has access to.
Outside of that, we have a handful of other adjustments, such as making Vampire’s Undeath passive less restrictive but also less powerful, cleaning up a bunch of item set interactions, better splitting the morph functionality of Abyssal Impact, and a hefty amount of bug fixes involving stealth interaction. That about wraps up the main points to expect this update, thanks for reading and we hope to see you in Tamriel!

Developer Comment
:
Spoiler Warning
Spoiler
In this pass, we're trying to better align the power dynamic between these morphs. Very early in development, Flail was actually used predominantly as an execute with much more potent scaling, but over time during the class's development, we reduced this function when Fatecarver came into being and took the spotlight. Originally, we kept this functionality to pay homage to that, but since both morphs added damage functionalities, it created a situation where one was always going to be better than the other. Rather than trying to have an arms race between the two, we've done more of a hard split, where Flail remains a generic "buff" morph that allows you to gain additional effects and contextual uses, while Dread more radically shifts how the ability is utilized. In addition to this, the increased drain of Crux that Dread asks of you wasn't as rewarding as we would have liked, so we're bumping up the reward for using it well.

Nightblade
Shifted numerous Nightblade abilities to improve their thematic tie into their skill line, while simultaneously addressing some long-standing balance issues the class has had in PvP environments. These changes are listed below, rather than explaining each change in an isolated bullet point:
Moved Blur and its morphs out of the Assassination skill line and into the Shadow skill line. Blur is now the first ability in the Shadow line, taking the place of Veiled Strike. This means Blur and its morphs will now be treated as Shadow abilities and interact with augments that come from the Shadow skill line, rather than the Assassination skill line.
Moved Veiled Strike and its morphs out of the Shadow skill line and into the Assassination skill line. Veiled Strike is now the first ability in the Assassination line, taking the place of Assassin's Blade. This means Veiled Strike and its morphs will now be treated as Assassination abilities and interact with augments that come from the Assassination skill line, rather than the Shadow skill line.
Moved Assassin's Blade and its morphs to the open slot that Blur originally occupied as the 3rd ability unlocked from the skill line.
Developer Comment
:
Spoiler Warning
Spoiler
We're going through and cleaning up some large inconsistencies with the Nightblade class by reorganizing some abilities that felt out of place, both thematically from their skill line as well as balance-wise. Blur, an ability that focuses on aiding your evasive and tank-like capabilities, is now placed in Shadow as it operates more heavily within that theme and skill line's intent. Veiled Strike, an ability that focuses on dealing consistent and precise damage, has been moved to the skill line that focuses on those elements and no longer adds bonuses to your survivability like triggering the Shadow Barrier passive, but instead offers damage focused bonuses like Hemorrhage and Pressure Points. With these adjustments, we hope to reign in and organize some of the bonuses the class interacts with so it feels more sensible and balanced overall.

Grimoires
All Grimoires have received small numerical increases to some of their Focus and Signature script interactions in order to simulate the progression that normal abilities receive as they rank up.
This is mainly accomplished by increasing damage, healing, shielding, or other stat scaling effects on abilities by 3.3% for Focus scripts (which we treat as a “base ability”), and 6.6% for Signature scripts (which we treat as a “morph decision”). This should help increase Scribed abilities feel more numerically viable when compared to non-Scribed abilities in the high-end experiences, without tipping the scales too much in their favor.
