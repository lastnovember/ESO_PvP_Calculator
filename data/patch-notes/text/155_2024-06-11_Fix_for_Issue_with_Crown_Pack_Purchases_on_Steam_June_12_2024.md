We recently identified an issue that affected some Crown Pack purchases players previously placed on Steam. We are pushing a fix on June 12, 2024. After that time, any players who were affected by this issue will automatically receive any owed crowns the first time they log into the game. These crowns will appear in affected players’ crown wallets, not in-game mail.
Thank you!
