This is the second incremental patch for Update 49 in The Elder Scrolls Online. This incremental addresses issues in Base Game content and Season Zero. Specifically, we are addressing additional issues that emerged during the initial Update 49 Launch.
We resolve several opportunities for crashes, incorrect assets, and unintentional sound effects. For example, the Vanos Dungeon Delve Outfit Style no longer plays sound effects not connected to the outfit style. Additionally, we have resolved an issue with Searing Strike and its morphs, as they no longer appear to be unintentionally locked.
We also have some general user experience improvements related to the Tamriel Tome and Seasonal challenges. These fixes aim to address some of the feedback we saw during the launch of Season Zero and the Tamriel Tome.
The full list of patch notes is available below. If you expected a specific bugfix and it is not reflected below, we intend to have more fixes in Update 50. Thanks, everyone!

Season Zero
Base Game
[PC] ESO displaying on the incorrect screen: This has been an ongoing issue from U49 Launch. We have a fix for the issue; however, additional testing is needed before we can push it live. This will be fixed in Update 50.
[PC] Laptop with a secondary high performance GPUs: We know these laptops have had issues running ESO since the U49 launch. We have a fix for the issue; however, additional testing is needed before we can push it live. This will be fixed in Update 50.
Season Zero Challenges
Weekly Challenges
Completing the "Defeat the Symphony of Blades, the Lava Queen, or Molag Kena” Weekly Challenge now receives the appropriate credit.
Completing the “Capture an Imperial City District” Weekly Challenge now receives the appropriate credit.
Fixed an issue that prevented the "Defeat the Serpent of Sanctum Ophidia” Seasonal Challenge from receiving appropriate credit.
Seasonal Challenges
Fixed an issue where the subzones or child zones of Hew's Bane, Gold Coast, and Wrothgar were not counting towards the "Kill 1000 Foes in Hew's Bane, Gold Coast, Wrothgar" Seasonal Challenge. For example, killing monsters in Maelstrom Arena, public dungeons, and delves were not counting towards killing enemies in the Wrothgar zone.
User Experience
Fixed an issue that allowed you to interact with Tome rewards underneath a list of Tome rewards while trying to view that list, such as a list of Furnishings.
Fixed an issue where a Steam player trying to upgrade their Tome could fail without notification if the Steam overlay is disabled.
Made code improvements to the Steam purchase experience when you upgrade your Tome.
Made code improvements to the purchase experience when you upgrade your Tome from the storefront of your choice.

Vanos Dungeon Delve Outfit Style

General

Gamepad UI
