Hello everyone,
We’ve seen reports that a handful of Tamriel Tome Weekly Challenges cannot be completed, which include:
Defeat the Symphony of Blades, the Lava Queen, or Molag Kena (note that defeating the Lava Queen does work)
Capture an Imperial City District
Defeat the Serpent of Sanctum Ophidia
Tomorrow, April 3, we will be publishing a hotfix on all platforms to remove these 3 Challenges from the rotation until they are properly fixed. This hotfix will not require any downtime, and we’ll let everyone know as soon as it’s complete. If you currently have any of these Challenges active, we encourage you to use a Challenge Reroll after the hotfix is live.  As a reminder, you will gain 5 additional Challenge Rerolls every Monday, with rerolls capping at 12.
This hotfix has been published on all platforms and servers - no patch or download required. Thank you!
