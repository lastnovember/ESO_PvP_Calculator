The Elder Scrolls Online v1.36.1.0 is the first Xbox incremental patch post Update 42 launch. This patch includes a number of fixes for the Gold Road and Blackwood zones, Scribing quests, some art model and effects-related issues, and more. The Jubilee Steed mount will be accessible again to those who earned it during the Anniversary Jubilee event. We’ve also fixed an issue where Luminous Ink was not dropping from boss enemies, giving you more opportunities to obtain Ink for Scribing. The size of this patch is approximately 5.07 GB (XB1)/5.0 GB (XBX).
General
Fixed an issue where Luminous Ink was not dropping from boss enemies.
Mages Guild Scripts will no longer be looted as level 50 items. Players below level 50 can now pick up the Mages Guild Scripts and use them to unlock the Script knowledge on those characters.
This will not affect existing Scripts that you have already looted. You can destroy these Scripts, which will allow you to pick the Mages Guild Script up again and the new item will have no level requirements to use.
Fixed some abnormal behavior with the Eyevea Wayshrine that could disallow you from jumping to it.
Fixed an issue where Sprint could become unresponsive or locked out after activating an ability with a cast time. This most notably affected Trample and Elemental Explosion from Scribing.
Grimoires
Mender's Bond: Fixed an issue where the healing done from the Sage's Remedy Signature paired with this Grimoire derived its healing from your Max Magicka/Stamina 10.5x higher than intended.
Vault: Fixed an issue that resulted in this ability granting Crowd Control Immunity after casting.
Scripts
Anchorite’s Potency: Fixed an issue where this Signature could trigger outside of combat.
Quests
The Wing of the Crow
The Fox no longer gets stuck in a “slow” state if you speak to it too quickly.
Fixed an issue where Wards would sometimes not show up.
Fixed an issue where the Fox would get stuck on the bridge and not move.
Added a hint to the quest to tell you how to reset the path if you get stuck.
The Wing of the Dragon
Fixed an issue where you could lose access to various portals to get back to the quest objective areas.
You will no longer have to compete with other players while looting several items during the task steps.
The Wing of the Netch: Updated the quest tracker text to match in-game achievements for quest activities, which says "Complete Mirrormoor Incursions."
The Wing of the Gryphon
If you leave the Deadlands before stealing Cretia, a new portal will allow you quick access back inside.
You will no longer have to compete with other players while looting several items during the task steps.
UI
Fixed an issue where the Scribing UI in Gamepad Mode would not re-narrate when adding, removing, or clearing Scripts.
Fixed issue where tooltips in the "Change Style" dialog in Gamepad Mode from the Skills screen was not being narrated.
Fixed a UI error that could occur when showing ability tooltips in Gamepad Mode.
Fixed an issue where crafted abilities linked in chat would override information in other screens.
General
Renamed the following Achievements to clarify that they are earned in the Blackwood zone:
“Defender of the Plane” is now “Blackwood Portal Defender”.
“Portal Punisher” is now “Blackwood Portal Punisher”.
“Multiplanar Devastator” is now “Blackwood Portal Devastator”.
Lucent Citadel Trial
General
Adjusted the visual effects of the Darkcaster Slasher’s Darkness effect for performance purposes.
Count Ryelaz and Zilyesset
Fixed an issue that could prevent Annihilation from resolving correctly when either boss was killed shortly after starting to cast Annihilation while its counterpart was resurrecting.
Arcane Knot (Final Encounter)
The visual effects will no longer persist if your character dies while holding the Arcane Knot.
Endeavors related to Trial completion will now correctly complete when Lucent Citadel is defeated.
Interplanar Cohesion Achievement:
The achievement is now granted upon defeating Xoryn rather than returning the Arcane Knot to the Null Arca.
You will now have more time to reunite after defeating Baron Rize.
The achievement will no longer fail to be completed should the knot holder move away from group members to drop the knot.
General
Psijic Portals in West Weald will now properly award overland Item Set gear.
Reduced the drop rate of Remnant of Deception and Remnant of Cruelty from the West Weald Public Dungeons.
Bosses
Bosses Shaman Rezzutum and Bone-Breaker in Silorn will now respawn properly when the fight resets.
Talon and Fang, as well as Stri the Fate-Eater, will now count toward World Boss Endeavors.
Hassedaz will use its Reconstruction ability less frequently.
Delve Bosses Vralkuun and Commander Hanhael will now spawn correctly based on achievement progression.
Oakenclaw will now drop aggro when you leave the fight area.
NPC
Tribune Alea Idolus now plays appropriate animations as a follower.
General
Wayshrines throughout West Weald now function more reliably.
Added Eyevea to the locations in the Map UI.
The intended number of Treasure Chests will now always be present within Gold Road delves.
Mirrormoor Incursions
Mirrormoor Incursions will now properly award experience upon completion.
Public Dungeons
Leftwheal Trading Post
Zenefrit will no longer reappear rapidly after being defeated.
Silorn
Reduced the damage of Viikor Brazen Hoof’s Clobber ability.
Viikor Brazen Hoof will now always grant achievement progress when defeated.
Bloodmane no longer has a chance to hang out on its rock during the encounter and will always join in.
Quests
Hold the Line:
Fixed an issue where you could get stuck in Mirrormoor.
Daedra coming from the Feldagard Keep Barracks door after the siege scene now all go to the same area instead of separating.
Shardborn Hunter, around Feldagard Keep, will now aggro and can be targeted.
Ithelia’s Fury: Fixed an issue with the visuals in the Ithelia fight intro.
King Nantharion’s Gambit: The Miscarcand Well now glows with power.
Seeds of Suspicion: Updated King Nantharion’s appearance to be consistent throughout the quest.
Homes
Fixed an issue with the “Merryvine Estate” house where the music that was playing was from High Isle instead of West Weald.
General
Fixed an issue that caused the Saint Alessia deck to not show which player is favored during longer matches.
General
Fixed an issue that could cause mounts from other players to appear to have identical upgrades to your own.
Fixed an issue where some staves were suddenly using the wrong model after Update 42 launch.
Fixed an issue where you could lose progress toward the Jubilee Confetti Conveyor achievement. This fix will restore usage of the Jubilee Steed Mount.
General
Fixed an issue where you could get stuck in an unending loading screen after leaving a dungeon or trial, then changing difficulty, and then attempting to re-enter the dungeon or trial.
Infinite Archive
You can once again make use of the Mystery Scroll once per cycle.
Whitestrake’s Mayhem
Fixed an issue where Tel Var rewards from the passive bonus were double-dipping in certain containers.
Furnishings
Adjusted the Furnishing Limit Type of the "How to Scribe" collectible furnishing to be "Collectible Furnishing" rather than "Traditional Furnishing", to match other collectible books furnishings.
Homes
Corrected the name of the achievement in the Merryvine Estate's gold requirements.
Fixed an issue where you could not preview Rosewine Retreat or Merryvine Estate unless you owned the Gold Road Chapter.
Craglorn
The Seeker’s Archive: Fixed an issue where approaching Hermaeus Mora's statue while engaged in combat during this quest would prevent you from interacting with it.
General
The icon for the “Invite to Trade” radial menu item is now properly greyed out when you have blocked communications from the Xbox account level.
General
The alert that appears when taking attachments from COD mail now properly includes the sender’s name.
