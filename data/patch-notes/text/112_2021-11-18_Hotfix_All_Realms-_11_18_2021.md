Hi Everyone. In response to some player feedback around photosensitivity issues in the Deadlands, we will be pushing out a hotfix shortly to reduce the frequency and timing of some lightning hazards in Annihilarch's Summit. We will continue to investigate ways to improve this experience and ensure the safety of those who are potentially sensitive to these visual effects. There will be no downtime for this hotfix. Thank you for your continued patience.
