Hello everyone,
We are planning maintenance on all platforms and megaservers this Friday, June 12 starting at 4am EDT/9am BST to address the following:
Fixing an issue where linking housing items can freeze the game on Xbox and PlayStation consoles
Fixing an issue where Challenge Difficulty is inadvertently set to Master difficulty instead of Adventurer upon first logging in. After maintenance, anyone who logs in for the first time since Update 50 launched will be correctly defaulted to Adventurer difficulty; this will not affect any Challenge Difficulty settings manually changed since U50 launch.
As a note, this will also fix the issue where some players were dealing zero damage against enemies.
We are currently estimating maintenance to be finished by 9am EDT/2pm BST. Thanks for your patience!
