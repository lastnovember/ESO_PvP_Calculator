The Elder Scrolls Online v1.36.2.0 focuses on fixes and improvements to Gold Road, Wrathstone and Base Game content. You’ll see several fixes to West Weald zone quests, Scribing, and the Depths of Malatar Dungeon. Additionally, based on your feedback, we have disabled the FPS cap on the in-game map on performance mode. The size of this patch is approximately 2.49 GB (XB1)/ 1.9 GB (XBX)
Grimoires
Fixed an issue that could prevent grimoires from appearing in the UI.
Quests
The Wing of the Gryphon
Portalling from the Deadlands to Ardent Hope is now made easier when completing the Steal Creatia from Daedric Princes step of the questline.
UI
Elemental Explosion
A temporary icon will no longer appear on the buff bar when hitting a target with ability when using the "Druid's Resurgence" Signature Script ability
Lucent Citadel
Perfected Jewelry from Lucent Citadel will now reconstruct at Gold Quality.
Houses
Corrected the name of the achievement in the Merryvine Estate's home gold requirements to say, “Gold Road Grand Adventurer”.
West Weald
Fixed an issue with the Clothier crafting certification quest so that the Raw Jute spawners are not submerged in a lake.
The Wendir ruin now shows up in the Zone Guide.
The “Report on Feldagard Keep” book now contains a higher resolution image.
Cavy will now behave like regular critters in the Overland.
Resolve issue where some wayshrines noted “Target out of range”. West Weald wayshrines now work as intended.
Players will now get credit for defeating Balandrus if a group member finishes the fight in questline “Terror of the Weald”.
When completing the questline “The Many Paths”, players in a group now share progress when using the Lamp of Clarity to reveal the many paths.
Fixed a typo in the compass text for “When the Ground Shook” quest.
In the questline “The Missing Cohort, it is now easier for you to use the Warning Candle.
Depths of Malatar
Dark Orb
It is now easier to resurrect allies should they die near the Dark Orb.
General
The Werewolf Hunter Hat now appears properly and with correct textures applied for other players off in the distance.
General
Fixed a crash that could occur when opening Crown Crates.
Infinite Archive
Players no longer have a chance to incorrectly lose an additional Thread should they die while a Marauder is entering the encounter.
Zeal of Zenithar
Corrected the Zeal of Zenithar help entry which incorrectly stated Gold Road Dragoon style pages could come from any box.
Guild History
Fixed an issue where large guild history cache files caused long load screens.
Mail
Fixed an issue where guild trader mails with attachments would show up as System Mail Alerts.
Map
Disabled the energy sustainability 30 FPS cap on the in-game map when playing on Xbox Series in performance mode.
General
Fix an issue where ESO Plus members could potentially be locked out of DLC portals at the end of the Gates of Adamant questline.
