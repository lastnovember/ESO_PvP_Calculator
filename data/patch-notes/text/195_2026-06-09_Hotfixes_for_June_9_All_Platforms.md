Hello! Today we pushed hotfixes for two issues that emerged after yesterday’s Update 50 launch. As a reminder, you may see all emergent known issues we are tracking
here
and all previously known issues for Update 50
here
. Below are the notes for today’s hotfixes.
Fixed an issue where Thieves Guild NPC Fa’ren-Dar was no longer appearing in Hew’s Bane and has instead moved to Glenumbra. This was preventing the completion of heist daily quests. We have returned Fa’ren-Dar to his Hew’s Bane Thieves Guild hideout location for all your heist-related needs.
Please note that a version of Fa’ren-Dar will remain in the Glenumbra guild hideout and you will not be able to interact with him until the new Thieves Guild content releases with Season One on July 8.
Fixed an issue where claiming all rewards in the “Path to Adventure” Golden Pursuit would not successfully claim all expected completion rewards.
No additional action is needed to see these fixes in-game. Thank you!
