Hello! Today, we completed a hotfix to address three issues; below are the fix notes for each. You do not need to restart your game to see these fixes. Thank you!
Evil Hunter:
Fixed an issue where this morph's damage bonus could stack with itself.
Healing Springs:
Fixed an issue where this morph's Magicka Recovery could persist longer than intended.
Solo March of Sacrifices:
Corrected an issue where players would accumulate effects with every death, and eventually stop being able to cast their skills.
