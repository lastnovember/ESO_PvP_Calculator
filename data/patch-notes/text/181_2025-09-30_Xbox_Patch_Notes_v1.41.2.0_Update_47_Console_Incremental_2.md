The Elder Scrolls Online v1.41.2.0 (Xbox) fixes issues identified post-Update 47 launch. The second incremental patch for Update 47 address issues in Feast of Shadows and Solstice Content.
This is a small patch focusing on fixing achievements granted in Naj-Caldeesh. Additionally, we have added a fix to the daily max of event repeatable quests.
You can find specifics on the patch notes for Update 47, Incremental 2 below. We will continue to monitor issues and update in later incremental patches and the next update patch.
