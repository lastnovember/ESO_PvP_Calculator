The Elder Scrolls Online v1.35.2.0 contains a number of crash fixes – including a memory leak and freeze during character selection – additional fixes for missing Guild History items and the removal of some stray Witches Festival decorations (sorry to those who like to celebrate year-round). The size of this patch is approximately 1.71GB for Xbox One and 1.46GB for Xbox Series X.
General
Some stray Witches Festival decorations are now properly stored away until the actual holiday arrives.
General
Fixed a rare crash that could occur when the Monolith of Storms skill was used.
Fixed an issue where your game could freeze during character selection.
Fixed an issue where your game could crash at the Terms of Service or Privacy Policy acceptance screens.
Fixed a memory leak that was predominantly occurring on PS5.
Guild History
Fixed an issue where Guild History events from previous sessions would sometimes not be displayed in the Guild History UI when they were supposed to.
