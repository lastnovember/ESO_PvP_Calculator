The Elder Scrolls Online v1.29.2.0 includes a few adjustments for the encounter with Zelvraak the Unbreathing in Graven Deep, a fix for Encratis' Behemoth Style Pages so they can now be used, and a few additional smaller fixes. The size of this patch is approximately 1GB.
General
Fixed a few locations where the world would disappear when standing in certain locations in Zenithar’s Abbey.
General
Fixed a part of Blackreach where there was an invisible stream of water.
Dreadsail Reef Trial
Tideborn Taleria
Deluge Eruption will no longer trigger on player characters who are swimming.
UI
Fixed a text issue with the card, Hira's End, where it refreshes 3 cards but the text referenced 4 cards. It now correctly displays that it refreshes 3 cards, as intended.
Fixed the tooltip text of the Patron’s Purse reward.
Updated the keybinding in Gamepad Mode to use the standard “Back” button rather than the Options button.
Dungeons
Graven Deep
Zelvraak the Unbreathing
Reduced the health and damage from the Flesh Abominations and Flesh Atronachs.
Sundered Soul no longer reduces Healing Received, Health Recovery, and Damage Shields in Normal Difficulty.
General
Fixed an issue where Encratis' Behemoth Style Pages could not be used.
General
Fixed an issue where Companions would attempt to enter combat while mounted.
Furnishings
Sealed a small gap in the top of a large fish’s tail in the “Aquarium, Abecean Coral” furnishing.
