Today, we have published a hotfix for the North American and European Console megaservers to solve an issue that was blocking progression for the main quest.
Quests & Zones
Main Quest
Daughter of Giants: Lyris can now always be spoken to after retrieving her axe from the Ancient Clannfear.
This fix will not require you to download a separate patch or update.
