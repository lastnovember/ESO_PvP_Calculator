The Elder Scrolls Online v1.23.2.0 fixes the issue where the friends and guild list history was not displaying properly, several crash fixes, a fix for a delay occurring when entering or leaving the Guild Bank, some quest fixes, and more. The size of this patch is approximately 846 MB.
Champion System
Fixed a calculation error in the Rationer star’s effect.
Updated the Rationer Champion Star  to only provide a duration bonus to stat-altering foods and drinks. This Champion Star was never intended to affect the duration of such foods and drinks.
Developer Comment
:
Spoiler Warning
Spoiler
In practical terms, this means it will no longer extend the duration of EXP-boosting Ambrosia drinks or Alliance Skill-boosting War Torte foods. It will still provide additional duration for foods and drinks that have a stat-focused component, such as Hissmir Fish-Eye Rye. Note that the "Gourmand" and "Connoisseur" Provisioner passive skills will still increase the duration of all foods or drinks, respectively, including Ambrosias and War Tortes.
General
The pre-game music during startup no longer starts playing briefly before the Bethesda animation.
General
Fixed an issue where Ayleid Apex and Radiant Apex mounts could not be previewed in the Crown Store if you were inside your Tamrielic home.
General
Fixed an issue where resource nodes would occasionally not appear with their correct model.
General
Fixed an issue that could occasionally result in the guild store tax not getting deposited into the guild bank.
Fixed a crash that could occur involving art assets with visible effects.
Fixed a crash that could occur when leaving the Champion Point menu.
Reduced the delay when entering or withdrawing from the Guild Bank.
Blackwood Prologue
The Emperor's Secret: Fixed an issue if you attempted to use the cart to Riften, multiple errors would occur unless your character was standing in the correct spot.
Grahtwood
Fixed an issue that would cause some NPCs around Southpoint to respawn too quickly.
The Rift
In His Wake: Corporal Bredrek will no longer spawn in odd spots when granting this quest, and will also no longer pursue you to give you his quest.
General
Information about offline players from the Friends/Guild lists are now correctly shown for players that have been offline for a while.
Fixed an issue where the keybind for quickslotting items would display in the incorrect location.
Fixed an issue that could cause the inventory to not display properly when quickly switching between it and another screen.
Fixed an issue that prevented scrolling the list of offerings in a trade window.
Combat
Fixed an issue where the visual state of Champion stars would not display properly in some cases.
