### Reorx_Holybeard (2017-05-31)

At CP160 gold weapons give you the following Spell/Weapon damage:
2H = 1571
DW = 1335 + 1335*0.177 = 1571
DW + Dual Wield Expert 2 = 1571 + 1335*0.06 = 1651
So without the DW passive you should be getting identical spell/weapon damage from 2H or DW. With the passive DW gives a little more. If you can show an example where that is not the case some screenshots and details would help in determining how/why and for reproducing it.
