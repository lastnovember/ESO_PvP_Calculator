# ESO REIMAGINED — VAMPIRE 2.0
## Restore the Predator, Rework the Curse
I love playing Vampire in ESO, but I feel the Vampire skill line has lost some of the identity that originally made Vampirism special. I am not asking ZOS to simply return to the old system. I would like to see the best parts of the original Vampire combined with the strengths of the modern Vampire.
The goal should be simple:
> **Don't just make Vampire stronger. Make Vampire feel like a Vampire.**
## 🩸 1. BRING BACK SUPERNATURAL RECOVERY
One of the old Vampire passives I would absolutely restore is **Supernatural Recovery**.
The original passive provided increased Magicka and Stamina Recovery at Vampire Stage 2+. This gave Vampires a genuine supernatural sustain identity.
**Proposed:** Restore Supernatural Recovery at Stage 2+, providing 5%/10% increased Magicka and Stamina Recovery.
Werewolf has a meaningful resource-recovery identity associated with having its transformation Ultimate slotted. Vampire should have an equivalent benefit that represents its supernatural nature.
## 🩸 2. VAMPIRIC DRAIN — BRING BACK THE STUN
The old **Drain Essence** damaged, healed and stunned its target. The modern Vampiric Drain retained the life-draining concept but lost that iconic control element.
**Proposed Vampiric Drain:**
Drain an enemy's life force, dealing damage, healing you based on your missing Health and **stunning the target when the channel begins**.
### Morphs
**Drain Vigor**
* Retains the stun.
* Restores Stamina during the channel.
* Improves sustain for hybrid and stamina builds.
**Exhilarating Drain**
* Retains the stun.
* Generates Ultimate during the channel.
* Provides a reason to use the ability as part of a Vampire-focused rotation.
This would make Vampiric Drain feel like a true act of feeding rather than simply a weak channel.
## 🦇 3. BRING BACK BAT SWARM
The old **Bat Swarm** was one of the most recognisable Vampire abilities in ESO. The modern Swarming Scion contains a similar bat concept, but I would like players to have a true bat-based Vampire Ultimate without requiring a transformation.
### Bat Swarm
Summon a swarm of bats around yourself.
The bats:
* Damage nearby enemies.
* Heal you for a percentage of damage dealt.
* Remain around you while you fight.
* Visually make you feel like a Vampire rather than a transformed monster.
### Morph — Devouring Swarm
Increases damage and healing.
### Morph — Bat Shroud
The swarm follows and attacks a chosen enemy while providing defensive benefits to the Vampire.
**Blood Scion should remain** for players who want the Vampire Lord transformation. Bat Swarm would simply provide another Ultimate choice.
## 🌑 4. REWORK THE VAMPIRE STAGES
The Stage system should remain because it is one of the most interesting parts of Vampirism.
However, the rewards should feel worth the increasing drawbacks.
### Stage 1 — Sated
* Minimal penalties.
* Basic Vampire abilities.
* Human appearance remains largely intact.
### Stage 2 — Awakening
* Supernatural Recovery activates.
* Vampire abilities become more rewarding.
### Stage 3 — Predator
* Stronger Vampire abilities.
* Stronger survival tools.
* Greater supernatural identity.
### Stage 4 — Blood Starved
* Maximum Vampire power.
* Strongest Vampire passives.
* Greatest drawbacks.
The philosophy should be:
> **The more you embrace the beast, the more powerful you become.**
Currently, Vampire Stages increase Vampire ability bonuses while also increasing Flame damage taken, Health Recovery penalties and regular ability costs. The risk/reward concept should remain, but the rewards need to feel meaningful.
## 🩸 5. MAKE FEEDING MATTER
Feeding should be more than a method of changing Vampire Stage.
Introduce a **Blood Hunger** mechanic.
Combat could build Hunger through:
* Damaging enemies.
* Using Vampire abilities.
* Killing enemies.
Feeding would satisfy Hunger and provide a temporary benefit.
Possible states:
**Sated → Fed → Hungry → Starving**
The more hungry you become, the stronger certain Vampire abilities could become, while the Vampire becomes increasingly vulnerable or suffers greater drawbacks.
This would create a gameplay loop:
> **Hunt → Wound → Drain → Feed → Recover → Hunt again.**
## 🌫️ 6. KEEP MIST FORM, BUT GIVE IT A CLEAR IDENTITY
The modern Mist abilities are useful and should remain, but they should clearly represent two different Vampire fantasies.
### Elusive Mist
**Escape and mobility**
* Dash forward.
* Avoid incoming projectiles.
* Gain defensive movement benefits.
### Blood Mist
**Attack and sustain**
* Become blood mist.
* Damage nearby enemies.
* Drain their life to heal yourself.
So:
> **Elusive Mist = Escape.**
> **Blood Mist = Hunt.**
## 🧠 7. REWORK MESMERIZE
Mesmerize should feel like supernatural Vampire domination.
### Mesmerize
Stun enemies affected by the Vampire's gaze.
### Morph — Hypnosis
Affects enemies around you rather than requiring the same positioning.
### Morph — Domination
Enemies affected by the stun become vulnerable to increased damage from Vampire abilities for a short duration.
This creates synergy instead of making Mesmerize a standalone PvP tool.
## 🩸 8. IMPROVE BLOOD FRENZY
Blood Frenzy is one of the most dangerous and interesting Vampire mechanics because it turns Health into offensive power. The current ability increases Weapon and Spell Damage while draining Health and restricting outside healing.
I would keep the risk/reward concept but make the two morphs more distinct.
### Simmering Frenzy
**Pure offensive power**
* Greater damage.
* Greater Health cost.
* Designed for aggressive players.
### Sated Fury
**Sustain**
* Builds offensive power.
* When deactivated, restores a percentage of the Health sacrificed.
* Could gain additional healing from Vampire damage while active.
The Vampire should feel dangerous to play, but rewarding when mastered.
## ☠️ 9. KEEP UNDEATH — BUT MAKE IT VAMPIRE-SPECIFIC
Undeath is an iconic Vampire survival passive and should remain.
However, it has historically become extremely powerful in PvP, so any restoration or improvement should be carefully balanced.
The objective should not be to create another mandatory PvP passive.
It should represent:
> **The closer a Vampire gets to death, the harder it is to kill them.**
## 🌑 10. IMPROVE THE OTHER PASSIVES
### Dark Stalker
Keep the supernatural stealth identity.
### Strike from the Shadows
Keep the offensive reward for emerging from stealth, invisibility or Mist Form.
### Blood Ritual
Keep the ability to spread Vampirism to another player.
### Unnatural Movement
Keep supernatural sprinting and invisibility, but make the reward feel worthwhile for reaching Stage 4.
The current Vampire line contains six passives: Feed, Dark Stalker, Strike from the Shadows, Blood Ritual, Undeath and Unnatural Movement.
THE VAMPIRE COMBAT IDENTITY
The entire Vampire skill line should work together:
**Hunt**
↓
**Wound**
↓
**Mesmerize**
↓
**Drain**
↓
**Heal**
↓
**Feed**
↓
**Become stronger**
↓
**Hunt again**
This gives Vampire its own gameplay identity rather than simply being a collection of useful abilities.
VAMPIRE COMPANIONS
If ESO allows Companions to become Vampires, I would take the system even further.
A Vampire Companion should receive:
* Vampire stages.
* Feeding.
* Vampire abilities.
* Vampire passives.
* Vampire dialogue and personality changes.
* A unique Vampire Ultimate.
DEVOURING SWARM — COMPANION ULTIMATE
The Companion does **not** transform into a Vampire Scion.
Instead, a huge swarm of bats surrounds them.
The bats:
* Damage nearby enemies.
* Heal the Companion based on damage dealt.
* Remain visible throughout the Ultimate.
This would bring back the classic Vampire fantasy while allowing the Companion to remain recognisable.
THE GOAL
I don't want Vampire to become overpowered.
I want it to become **worth embracing**.
If I choose to become a Vampire, I should feel that choice every time I enter combat.
I want to feel the hunger.
I want to drain my enemies.
I want to manipulate my Health.
I want supernatural recovery.
I want Mist Form.
I want to emerge from the shadows.
I want bats surrounding me.
I want the risk of becoming blood-starved to be balanced by the power of embracing the curse.
ESO already has the foundation.
Please don't just balance the Vampire. Reignite it.
Make the Vampire feel like a Vampire again.
