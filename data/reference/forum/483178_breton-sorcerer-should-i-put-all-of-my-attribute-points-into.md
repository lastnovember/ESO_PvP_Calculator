### AcadianPaladin (2019-07-04)

Assuming you will play a magsorc in PvE, then yes, I'd put all attribute points into magicka.
Your base health will be around 12.5k but you can boost it up to a comfortable level (17-19k) by doing the following:
- Blue Savory food that boosts your mag and hp.  There are provisioning perks that can boost the duration from one hour to an hour and 20 mins.
- Take Undaunted up to level 9 and grab the Undaunted Mettle passive.   That, combined with wearing 5 light, 1 heavy, 1 medium armor will give you +6% to all three of your attributes.
- You will then want one additional hp 'boost' from any of the following sources:  A passive like a pet sorc gets for having a pet summoned.   A hp boost from an armor set bonus.  Last resort if these first two are not good choices for you is to go ahead and enchant one of your big pieces of armor (like your chest piece) with a health glyph.
