### Sailor_Palutena (2018-10-01)

I wonder what is the ideal Magicka Recovery to avoid being hindered in most of the situations.
I am a Magplar using Julianos x5 Necropotence x4 and a random necklace, all Legendary (except rings which I'm still working on to upgrade).
Also, I'm using food.
As DPS my first bar is Destruction Staff x2 and Mage Guild x4
Second bar is Aedric Spear x2 Resto Light x1 and Mage Guild x3
By following some guides, I always thought Magicka Recovery from Mundus Stones was a must. I even got all my possible Divines Traits with that objective. Found out I get only 386 Magicka Recovery even with most Legendary gear. As expected my Mana is almost infinite (2100 using Daggers) 2000 using Destruction/Restoration Staff. As a Templar spamming Aedric Spear I can use it like 50 times in a row before I run out of Magicka (considering not using any recovery skill).
My Max Magicka was 39k with Daggers and 44k with Staff.
The numbers below aren't precise. I just look at the numbers going up on the screen.
Puncturing Strikes were hitting for 2000 each hit. Crushing Blow hitting for 2000 x3.
I thought I was wasting resources and decided to experiment with Max Magicka.
Now I have Max Magicka 43k with Daggers and 48k with Staff.
Puncturing Strikes now hits for almost 3000 each hit. Crushing Blow hits for 4000 x3.
A considerable increase in damage, but now I have 1600 recovery with Daggers and 1500 with Staff.
I have been healing in most dungeons with ease (I even yawn sometimes). Have healer Hel-Ra and Cloudrest when i had higher Recovery.
I wanted to know what the veteran raiders prefer and the ideal number for Max Magicka and Recovery. For me, it looks like working fine, but I'm looking for tips to improve.
I appreciate any feedback.
