Dual Wield Expert
(NOT Twin blade and blunt):
Increases Weapon Damage by 3% of off-hand weapon's Damage.
Grants a bonus based on type of weapon equipped:
For each Dagger: Heavy attacks charge 5% faster
For each Sword: You can block an additional 3% damage.
For each Axe: Light attacks and Heavy attacks deal 3% more damage.
For each Mace: Dealing Critical Damage with a heavy attack has a 25% chance of
applying minor fracture.
setting the target off-balance
