'''Rubedite Ore''' is a shiny dark red ore. It can be found in any zone by characters with Rank 10 in the [[Online:Metalworking|Metalworking]] passive or {{ESO Champion|150 to 160}}. When mined, a [[Online:Nodes|node]] yields three to four samples of the raw material, [[Online:Rubedite Ore|Rubedite Ore]]. Once ten ore samples are collected, they can be refined into seven to ten [[Online:Rubedite Ingot|Rubedite Ingot]]s. Rubedite ingots are used for [[Online:Blacksmithing|crafting]] pieces of heavy [[Online:Rubedite Armor|Rubedite Armor]] and [[Online:Rubedite Weapons|weapons]] at any [[Online:Crafting Stations|blacksmithing station]].
