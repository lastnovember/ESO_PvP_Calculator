'''Ruby Ash''' logs are veiny and reddish-brown in color. They can be found in any zone by players with [[Online:Woodworking (skill)|Woodworking]] Rank 10 or {{ESO Champion|150 to 160}}. One log can be harvested for three to four samples of the raw material, {{Item Link|Rough Ruby Ash|id=71199}}. Once ten samples of rough wood are collected, they can be refined into seven to ten pieces of the refined material, {{Item Link|Sanded Ruby Ash|id=64502}}. Refined Ruby Ash pieces are used for creating [[Online:Ruby Ash Weapons|Ruby Ash staves and bows]], as well as [[Online:Shields|Ruby Ash shields]], at any [[Online:Crafting Stations|woodworking station]]. In order for you to [[Online:Crafting|craft]] Ruby Ash gear your Woodworking ability has to be rank 10.
