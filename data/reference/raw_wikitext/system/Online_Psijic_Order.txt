The [[ON:Psijic Order|Psijic Order]] is a mysterious and ancient order of powerful mages based on the isle of [[Lore:Artaeum|Artaeum]], geographically part of the [[Lore:Summerset Isles|Summerset Isles]]. As the island disappeared from [[ON:Tamriel|Tamriel]] over 350 years ago, around when former member [[ON:Vanus Galerion|Vanus Galerion]] formed the [[ON:Mages Guild|Mages Guild]], the Order has rarely ever been seen.<noinclude>

The Order formerly occupied the island of [[Online:Dranil Kir|Dranil Kir]], and a scrying device known as an [[Lore:Obscuros|Obscuros]] located on it is sought by [[Online:Lilatha|Lilatha]], the master of the glazing glass. After [[Online:Daedric War Storyline|Nocturnal's plot]] in the [[ON:Clockwork City|Clockwork City]] is thwarted, [[ON:Sotha Sil|Sotha Sil]] tells [[ON:Divayth Fyr|Divayth Fyr]] that he will inform the Psijics so that they can prepare for a future threat.

It is a joinable faction in the [[Online:Summerset (Chapter)|Summerset]] [[ON:Chapters|Chapter]]. To join the Psijic Order, you must first complete the first Summerset zone story quest in [[ON:Shimmerene|Shimmerene]], [[ON:The Queen's Decree|The Queen's Decree]]. You will then gain access to Artaeum. Speak with [[On:Loremaster Celarus|Loremaster Celarus]] in [[ON:Ceporah Tower|Ceporah Tower]] to formally join the Psijic Order. 

If you somehow manage to transport to Artaeum by other means (e.g. teleporting to a player or their [[ON:Grand Psijic Villa|Grand Psijic Villa]]) without having completed The Queen's Decree, then Loremaster Celarus will not be present, even if you can see other players talking to someone you can't see. All you will be able to do is discover the Artaeum wayshrine to provide easier access to Artaeum in the future. You may still acquire the Psijic Order skill line by spending Crowns, if another character on your account has completed it.

For historical information about the Psijic Order, see the [[Lore:Psijic Order|lore article]].
