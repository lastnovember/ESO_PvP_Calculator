'''Ancestor Silk''' is a plant with dark red, fern-like stems, and red spiderweb-like strands between them. It can be found in any zone by characters with Rank 10 in the [[ON:Tailoring|Tailoring]] passive or {{ESO Champion|150 to 160}}. When harvested, a [[Online:Nodes|node]] yields three to four samples of the raw material, {{Item Link|Raw Ancestor Silk|id=71200}}. Once ten raw samples are collected, they can be refined into seven to ten {{Item Link|Ancestor Silk|id=64504}}. Ancestor silk is used for [[Online:Clothing|crafting]] pieces of light [[Online:Ancestor Silk Armor|ancestor silk armor]] at any [[Online:Crafting Stations|clothing station]].
