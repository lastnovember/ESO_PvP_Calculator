'''Battlegrounds''' are small-scale player-versus-player (PvP) gameplay locations that can be accessed through the [[Online:Group & Activity Finder|Group & Activity Finder]]. Initially, each battleground featured a three-sided team setup with four players in each team, but since [[Online:Update 44|Update 44]] this has been revamped to two-sided team setups with four or eight players per team. At launch, there were three battleground maps and three game types: Capture the Flag, Team Deathmatch, and Domination, with more were added in later updates. Battlegrounds feature [[ON:Leaderboards#Battlegrounds|leaderboard]] and have their own unique [[Online:Sets|item set]] and [[Online:Styles#Battlegrounds Styles|style pages]] rewards.

Unlike in [[ON:Cyrodiil|Cyrodiil]], Battlegrounds are not [[ON:Alliances|Alliance]]-based. Instead, players can fight for the Fire Drakes (Orange), Pit Daemons (Green), or Storm Lords (Purple). Your team is assigned randomly on entering each Battleground, and you are able to queue up for Battlegrounds individually or in pre-formed groups.

According to the [https://www.pcgamesn.com/the-elder-scrolls-online/mmr-battlegrounds-interview developers], Battlegrounds have had a Matchmaking Rating (MMR) system since their release. Initially it only accounted for your wins and losses, though it later was changed to account for the difference between your team and the enemy team's MMR difference. If a player leaves the match, the team that loses will get a lesser MMR loss. There are other factors, but the developers have left it vague on purpose to avoid it being exploited.

==Game Modes==
Battlegrounds are divided into three types:
*'''[[ON:Deathmatch|Deathmatch]]''': Find and kill enemy players to earn points for your team.
*'''Flag Games:'''
**'''[[Online:Capture the Relic|Capture the Relic]]''': Capture the enemies' relics and return them to your base.
**'''[[Online:Chaos Ball|Chaos Ball]]''': Pick up and try to hold a ball which gives points but deals increasingly serious damage over time.
*'''Land Grab:'''
**'''[[Online:Crazy King|Crazy King]]''': Capture and try to hold capture points that will regularly move to different locations on the map.
**'''[[Online:Domination|Domination]]''': Capture and hold sites across the map, continually earning points based on locations held.
4v4 maps do not have Flag Games ("Capture the Relic" and "Chaos Ball") and Deathmatch includes extra conditions: each player has 3 lives and the team should win 2 out of 3 rounds.

8v8 maps all have powerups, similar to arenas [[Online:Arenas#Sigils|sigils]], spread around the arena to activate. There are 4 types of power ups and lasting 30 seconds: the red axe ({{Furnishing Link|Battleground Powerup, Damage}}) gives increased damage done, the blue shield ({{Furnishing Link|Battleground Powerup, Defense}}) gives increased movement speed, the green feather ({{Furnishing Link|Battleground Powerup, Speed}}) gives the [[ON:Major Expedition|Major Expedition]] buff and the yellow crown ({{Furnishing Link|Battleground Powerup, Ultimate}}) gives {{ESO Ultimate Link|500}}.

In all game modes, tie breakers are as follows: If both teams have the same score, the team with the highest medal score and also in Competitive Deathmatch the most lives remaining, wins. If both teams are still tied, both teams will be assessed as not winning, but also not losing for reward purposes.

When joining, there is a 5 minute timer for all players to join. When it runs out, the battleground closes after 10 seconds. When all players have joined, there is a 45 second timer before the match starts. In Competitive Deathmatch there is also a 10 second timer after a round then a 10 second + 45 second timer between rounds.

Before the game starts, you get the "{{ESO Icon Abbr|skill|Support-Stalwart Guard|24}} {{sic|Nature Grasp|Nature's Grasp}} Suppression", "{{ESO Icon Abbr|skill|Dark Magic-Blood Magic|24}} Chain Pull Range Suppression" and the "{{ESO Icon Abbr|skill|Dark Magic-Blood Magic|24}} Frozen Gate Suppression" effects which prevent you from getting pulled, from pulling enemies and from using [[ON:Frozen Gate|Frozen Gate]] which could be used to bypass the barrier.
Before and when in the game, you get the "{{ESO Icon Abbr|misc|Move Out|24}} Move Out" effect for two minutes when spawning on you team's ledge. When it runs out you are forcefully moved into the arena. This is put to prevent griefing. If the match is still searching for players and the arena hasn't opened yet when Move Out expires, you'll be teleported to your spawn point with the rest of your team, and Move Out will restart.

==Rewards==
As you participate in matches, you will earn points and medals based on the game mode. Points are earned for completing the game mode's primary objectives: killing enemies, capturing flags, defending locations, and holding the chaos ball. Medals are earned for unique feats related to the game mode, including dealing damage, healing allies, performing kill streaks, capturing or defending flags, healing flag carriers, and taking damage.

Your points and medals contribute to your final rewards when the match ends, being: {{ESO AP|Alliance Points}}, Experience, Gold, a [[ON:Styles#Battlegrounds Styles|battleground style]] page and sometimes a {{Item Link|Battlegrounds Memorabilia|id=153501|quality=4}} if you already have all the battlegrounds styles pages.

==Notes==
* Your armor doesn't wear down while in Battlegrounds.
* A prototype battleground called [[Online:Colovian Crossing|Colovian Crossing]], which featured a single, central capture point, was playable at many gaming conventions in 2014 and 2015.
* Battlegrounds originally launched with the [[Online:Morrowind|Morrowind]] chapter of [[Online:Online|ESO]] and only available to owners of Morrowind. It was implied that owning any future Chapters would grant access to Battlegrounds as well, but they are now available to all players as of [[ON:Update 18|Update 18]].
* The daily repeatable quests are collectively repeatable as a group, not individually. You need to complete an open quest of this type before you can receive another from [[Online:Battlemaster Rivyn|Battlemaster Rivyn]].
* While in the battlegrounds, to enable PvP between the teams, your [[Online:Alliances|Alliance]] may be temporarily overridden. Fire Drakes become [[Online:Aldmeri Dominion|Aldmeri Dominion]], Pit Daemons become [[Online:Ebonheart Pact|Ebonheart Pact]], and Storm Lords become [[Online:Daggerfall Covenant|Daggerfall Covenant]]. This override is usually hidden by the user interface, but can be revealed through use of Alliance-specific skills or items, such as [[Online:Dragonknight Standard|Dragonknight Standard]] or the [[Online:Alliance Rider Outfit|Alliance Rider Outfit]].
* The names of the battleground teams are a direct reference to {{TIL|early designs|goblades}} for ''[[Arena:Arena|TES:Arena]]''. Arena was originally conceived as a fighting game featuring a tournament that took the player to each of Tamriel's cities to challenge different gladiatorial teams. According to a file from that stage of development left behind in the final game, the Firedrakes hailed from Torval, the Stormlords from Narsis and the Pit Daemons from Helstrom. 
* In 2022, all game modes but Deathmatch were removed. Separate queues for the other game modes were added as [[Online:Battlegrounds Weekend Events|weekend events]], offering a +25% bonus to Alliance Points earned. This was reverted in an update.
