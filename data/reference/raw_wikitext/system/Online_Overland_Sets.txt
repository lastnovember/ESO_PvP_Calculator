{{Trail|Sets}}[[Category:Online-Sets| Overland Sets]]'''Overland Sets''' are [[ON:Sets|sets]] that provide players with various bonuses when items of the same set are equipped simultaneously. Items constituting overland sets can only be found on monsters or in treasure chests in their respective zones. All overland sets are bind-on-equip. All sets will drop items for all armor and weapon slots, but normally only in a specific armor weight, as listed below. 

*In the case of zones which lack certain of the features (World Bosses, Delves, Overland Events or Public Dungeons), it may sometimes be the case that the items from a certain set will only drop from Treasure Chests, due to the other possible sources not being available.
