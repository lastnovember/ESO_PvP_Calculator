The [[Online:Fighters Guild|Fighters Guild]] is a mercenary organization which has a presence all throughout [[ON:Tamriel|Tamriel]].<noinclude> 

To join, go to any Fighters Guild hall and speak with [[Online:Aicessar|Aicessar]], [[Online:Basile Fenandre|Basile Fenandre]], or [[Online:Hilan (Dark Elf)|Hilan]] (regardless of your alliance) and tell them you're ready to join. To gain reputation with them, you must destroy [[Online:Dark Anchor|Dark Anchors]] and [[ON:Mirrormoor Mosaics|Mirrormoor Mosaics]] or kill [[Online:Daedra|Daedra]] and undead. Good relations with the guild has many benefits, including new skills and abilities.

===Dark Anchor Dailies===
These quests are available after joining the Fighters Guild, and are offered by [[ON:Cardea Gallus|Cardea Gallus]] located within the Fighters Guild buildings of [[ON:Elden Root|Elden Root]] (Grahtwood), [[ON:Mournhold|Mournhold]] (Deshaan) and [[ON:Wayrest|Wayrest]] (Stormhaven). These quests will send you to destroy one [[ON:Dark Anchor|Dark Anchor]] in a random zone. These quests can be shared with other members of your group, so it is possible to do up to 15 of these quests in a day. These quests will give Fighters Guild reputation, as well as a small box of rewards. These quests are one way of obtaining the [[ON:Draugr Style|Draugr motif]]. Quests are given on a fixed 15 day cycle.

==Reputation==
All guilds use reputation for leveling purposes. Fighters Guild {{ESO Reputation|FG}} Reputation is earned by killing undead and Daedra, as well as by completing [[Online:Dark Anchors|Dark Anchor]] [[Online:Dolmens|Dolmens]] and [[ON:Mirrormoor Mosaics|Mirrormoor Mosaics]]. You receive {{ESO Reputation|FG|1}} for one kill, {{ESO Reputation|FG|5}} for killing undead and Daedra bosses, {{ESO Reputation|FG|20}} for destroying a Dark Anchor, {{ESO Reputation|FG|10}} for completing a Dark Anchor [[#Dark Anchor Dailies|daily quest]], {{ESO Reputation|FG|25}} for each{{vn|}} Dark Anchor [[Online:Stay Out Of Tamriel|contract achievement]] and {{ESO Reputation|FG|23}} (15 + Boss + 3 Valkynazes) for completing a Mirrormoor Moasaic.

The reward coffer for daily quests has a small chance for an Fighters Guild Citation worth {{ESO Reputation|FG|70}}. These citations can be stacked and traded.
