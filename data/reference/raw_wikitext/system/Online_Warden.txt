'''Warden''' is a [[Online:Classes|class]] in ''[[Online:Online|Elder Scrolls Online]]''. It was originally only available to players who had purchased the ''[[Online:Morrowind|Morrowind]]'' [[Online:Chapters|Chapter]] and later available as a separate [[Online:Crown Store|Crown Store]] Upgrade for {{ESO Crowns|1500}}, but it has been integrated into the base game on March 9, 2026. Warden is a nature-themed class, with a focus on frost magic and animal companions.

::'''In-Game Description''': ''Wardens are defenders of the Green, master storytellers whose nature tales become magical reality. They wield frost spells against enemies and summon animals to aid them.'' 
{{NewLeft}}
==Skills==
===Animal Companions===
[[Online:Animal Companions|Animal Companions]] is geared towards summoning animals to aid you in battle.

===Green Balance===
[[Online:Green Balance|Green Balance]] is geared towards healing and buffs.

===Winter's Embrace===
[[Online:Winter's Embrace|Winter's Embrace]] is geared towards dealing [[Online:Frost Damage|Frost Damage]] and tanking.

===Class Mastery===
[[ON:Class Mastery#Warden|Class Mastery]] is available if the player is not [[Online:Subclassing|subclassing]].

The Warden allows for one triple-build.  You can meet the requirements for Warden Initiate, Beast Caller, and Master Herbalist all at once.  Neither of the other two builds can be fully mixed with anything, however.
