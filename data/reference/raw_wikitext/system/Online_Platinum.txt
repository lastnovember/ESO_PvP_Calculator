'''Platinum''' seams can be found near cliffs and mountains at [[ON:Engraver|Engraver]] level 5 or at character level {{ESO Champion|150+}}.  They can be harvested for three to four samples of the raw material, {{Item Link|Platinum Dust|id=135145}}. Once ten samples of dust are collected, they can be refined into seven to ten {{Item Link|Platinum Ounces|id=135146}}. Platinum Jewelry is [[Online:Jewelry Crafting|crafted]] from the refined platinum ounces at any of the [[Online:Crafting Stations|jewelry crafting stations]].
