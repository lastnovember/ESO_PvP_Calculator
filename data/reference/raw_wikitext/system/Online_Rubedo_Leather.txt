'''Rubedo Leather''' is a [[Online:Clothing Materials|crafting]] material obtained from [[Online:Animals|animals]] in any zone by characters with Rank 10 in the [[ON:Tailoring|Tailoring]] passive or {{ESO Champion|150 to 160}}. One animal may yield one to two samples of the raw material, {{Item Link|Rubedo Hide Scraps|id=71239}}. Once ten scrap pieces are collected, they can be refined into seven to ten samples of the processed material, {{Item Link|Rubedo Leather|id=64506}}. Rubedo Leather is used for making [[Online:Rubedo Leather Armor|medium armor]] at any [[Online:Crafting Stations|clothing station]].
