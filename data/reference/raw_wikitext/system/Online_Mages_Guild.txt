The [[Online:Mages Guild|Mages Guild]] is an organization dedicated to magical learning with a presence all throughout [[ON:Tamriel|Tamriel]]. It was founded by [[ON:Vanus Galerion|Vanus Galerion]] following his departure from the [[ON:Psijic Order|Psijic Order]].<noinclude>

To join, go to any Mages Guild hall and speak with [[Online:Curinure|Curinure]], [[Online:Nemarc|Nemarc]], or [[Online:Rudrasa|Rudrasa]] (regardless of your alliance) and tell them you're ready to join. To gain reputation with them, you must bring them lost tomes of [[Online:Shalidor's Library|Tamrielic lore]]. Each lorebook adds five points to the Mages Guild skill line, and an increasing number of points is required to gain a rank. Good relations with the guild can earn you new skills, perks, and abilities. The Mages Guild quest line is focused around the Daedric Prince of Madness, [[Online:Sheogorath|Sheogorath]].

The Mages Guild, along with the [[Online:Fighters Guild|Fighters Guild]], is also at the head of an assault into [[Online:Coldharbour|Coldharbour]] which takes place at the culmination of the [[Online:Story Quests|Alliance Storyline]] and [[Online:Main Quest|Main Quest]]. Note that progression through the Mages Guild quest line does not affect this in any way.

===Public Dungeon Dailies===
These quests are available after joining the Mages Guild, and are offered by [[ON: Alvur Baren|Alvur Baren]] located within the Mages Guild buildings of Elden Root (Grahtwood), Mournhold (Deshaan), and Wayrest (Stormhaven). These quests will send you to a random public dungeon to retrieve an artifact associated with Sheogorath. These quests can be shared with other members of your group, so it is possible to do up to 15 of these quests in a day. These quests will give Mages Guild reputation, as well as a small box of rewards. These quests are one way of obtaining the [[ON: Draugr Style|Draugr motif]].

==Reputation==
All guilds use reputation for leveling purposes. Mages Guild {{ESO Reputation|MG}} Reputation is gained by finding [[Online:Shalidor's Library|Lorebooks]]. Each Lorebook recovered grants {{ESO Reputation|MG|5}}, while completing a collection of lorebooks grants {{ESO Reputation|MG|20}}. You can also earn {{ESO Reputation|MG|10}} for each completed Public Dungeon [[#Public Dungeon Dailies|daily quest]], and {{ESO Reputation|MG|5}} for each{{vn|plain}} [[Online:The Madness Isn't Catching|relic recovery achievement]]. Finally destroying a [[ON:Soul Reaper|Soul Reaper]] awards {{ESO Reputation|MG|20}} (once per day). It takes {{ESO Reputation|MG|1380}} Reputation points to reach Mages Guild Rank 10, and it is possible to earn {{ESO Reputation|MG|1365}} by collecting all Lorebooks within your home alliance territories, Coldharbour and Cyrodiil. The remaining {{ESO Reputation|MG|15}} must come from dailies or the zone collections of the other alliance areas.

The reward coffer for daily quests has a small chance for a Mages Guild Citation worth 15 {{ESO Reputation|MG}}. These citations can be stacked and traded.
