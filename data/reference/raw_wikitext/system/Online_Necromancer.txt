'''Necromancer''' is a [[Online:Classes|class]] in ''[[Online:Online|Elder Scrolls Online]]''. It can be purchased from the [[ON:Crown Store|Crown Store]] for {{ESO Crowns|1500}}. Necromancers are death bringers who use a mix of Illusion, Destruction, and Conjuration spells, and mostly deal with the undead and fear. It is available to players who have purchased the retail version of the ''[[Online:Elsweyr|Elsweyr]]'' [[ON:Chapters|Chapter]], but is '''not''' included with the [[Online:Elsweyr (DLC)|DLC version of Elsweyr]].

Performing certain Necromancer abilities in front of some NPCs (for example, [[Online:Tamriel Guard|guards]]) will incur a [[Online:Justice|bounty]]. These skills are marked as {{ESO Criminal Act}}.

::'''In-Game Description''': ''Masters of death, Necromancers can call upon corpses to serve as undead thralls and weave ghastly spells to both harm and heal.''

==Skills==
===Grave Lord===
[[Online:Grave Lord|Grave Lord]] is elemental magic skill with pure damage dealing abilities.

[[Online:Bone Tyrant|Bone Tyrant]] is a set of abilities focused towards defense and tanking.

===Living Death===
[[Online:Living Death|Living Death]] is a mix of healing and resurrecting abilities.

===Class Mastery===
[[ON:Class Mastery#Necromancer|Class Mastery]] is available if not [[Online:Subclassing|subclassing]].

Necromancer is one of the more difficult classes for mixing builds. There is only one dual-build which does not have any conflicting morphs, between Deathbringer and Flesh Sculptor, though these are incompatible for other reasons, since the former is Stamina-based and the latter is Magicka-based. There are no triple-builds with fewer than 2 conflicting morphs.
