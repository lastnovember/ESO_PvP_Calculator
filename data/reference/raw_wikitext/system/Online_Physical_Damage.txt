'''Physical Damage''' is the most common type of [[ON:Martial Damage|Martial Damage]]. Most enemies in the game will deal Physical Damage, particularly if they fight with melee attacks. It is mainly sourced from weapons skill lines, but most classes have a few physical damage skills. It can cause the [[Online:Sundered|Sundered]] status effect.

==Sources==
===Weapons===
*Light and Heavy Attacks with [[ON:Two Handed|Two Handed]], [[ON:Dual Wield|Dual Wield]], [[ON:Bow|Bow]], and [[ON:One Hand and Shield|One Hand and Shield]].
*All weapons enchanted with a [[ON:Glyph of Absorb Stamina|Glyph of Absorb Stamina]] (Deni)
*Bashing With any weapon (Including Staves)

===Champion Points===
*[[ON:Mighty|Mighty]] in the [[ON:Extended Might|Extended Might]] subconstellation of [[ON:Warfare|Warfare]] grants additional Physical Damage.

==Defense==
There are several ways of protecting yourself against Physical Damage.
*A [[ON:Glyph of Decrease Physical Harm|Glyph of Decrease Physical Harm]] Taderi, can be applied to jewelry.
*[[ON:Hardy|Hardy]] in the [[ON:Staving Death|Staving Death]] subconstellation of [[ON:Warfare|Warfare]] reduces Physical Damage taken.
