'''Two Handed''' is one of the six [[Online:Skills#Weapon|weapon skills]] available in Elder Scrolls Online. You can advance through this skill line by equipping a two-handed weapon. 
{{NewLeft}}
== Learning ==
The Two Handed skill line is granted the first time you kill an enemy with a two-handed weapon equipped.  You can increase your skill by engaging in combat with a two-handed weapon equipped, or by having Two Handed skills slotted.  The more skills you have slotted, the faster your skill will increase.  [[ON:Nord|Nord]]s are naturally gifted with Two Handed weapons, and will learn the ability more quickly than other races.  In addition, you can earn free levels in the skill line by reading [[ON:Skill Books|Skill Books]], which may be found randomly on bookshelves anywhere.  The skill books which increase Two Handed are:
*[[ON:Ceryolminwe's Translation|Ceryolminwe's Translation]]
*[[ON:A Hero's Weapon|A Hero's Weapon]]
*[[ON:How to Win a Fight|How to Win a Fight]]
*[[ON:The Legendary Sancre Tor, 1st Ed.|The Legendary Sancre Tor]]
*[[ON:Wuunding and Tumult|Wuunding and Tumult]]

===Other Sets===
* Wearing five pieces of the [[ON:Sword-Singer|Sword Singer]] set from the [[ON:Alik'r Desert|Alik'r Desert]] increases the {{ESO Weapon Damage Link|||y}} and {{ESO Spell Damage Link}} of your Two Handed abilities.
* Wearing five pieces of the [[ON:Red Eagle's Fury|Red Eagle's Fury]] set from the [[ON:Red Eagle Redoubt|Red Eagle Redoubt]] crafting site increases the {{ESO Weapon Damage Link|||y}} and {{ESO Spell Damage Link}} of all your weapon abilities, Two Handed included, but it also increases their cost.
* Wearing five pieces of the [[ON:Daring Corsair|Daring Corsair]] set from the [[ON:Cat's-Claw Station|Cat's-Claw Station]] crafting site decreases the {{ESO Stamina Link}} and {{ESO Magicka Link}} costs of all your weapon abilities by 10%, and grants you [[ON:Minor Heroism|Minor Heroism]] for 8 seconds after you use a weapon skill.
