'''Destruction Staff''' is one of the six [[Online:Skills#Weapon|weapon skills]] available in Elder Scrolls Online. You can advance through this skill line by equipping a [[Online:Destruction_Staves|Destruction type staff]].
None of its skills actually deal [[ON:Magic Damage|Magic Damage]] per se, instead each staff changes the skills based on the elemental type associated with the staff, those being:
:Inferno Staves will only deal [[ON:Fire Damage|flame damage]];
:Ice Staves will only deal [[ON:Frost Damage|frost damage]];
:Lightning Staves will only deal [[ON:Shock Damage|shock damage]]. 
One example of this is the skill [[ON:Destructive Touch|Destructive Touch]], whose damage type changes with the type of staff, but also applies a different effect based on it. An Inferno Staff causes an enemy to be [[ON:Knockback|knocked back]], an Ice Staff [[ON:immobilize|immobilize]]s an enemy, and a Lightning Staff deals damage in an area.

== Learning ==
The Destruction Staff skill line is granted the first time you kill an enemy with a destruction staff equipped.  You can increase your skill by engaging in combat with a destruction staff equipped, or by having Destruction Staff skills slotted.  The more skills you have slotted, the faster your skill will increase.  [[ON:High Elf|High Elves]] are naturally gifted with Destruction Staves, and will learn the ability more quickly than other races.  In addition, you can earn free levels in the skill line by reading [[ON:Skill Books|Skill Books]], which may be found randomly on bookshelves anywhere.  The skill books which increase Destruction Staff are:

*[[ON:Destruction or Distraction|Destruction or Distraction]]
*[[ON:A Hypothetical Treachery, Part 1|A Hypothetical Treachery, Part 1]]
*[[ON:Oshgura's Destruction Journal|Oshgura's Destruction Journal]]
*[[ON:Prudence in Practice|Prudence in Practice]]
*[[ON:The Vanishing Crux|The Vanishing Crux]]

===Other Sets===
* Wearing five pieces of the [[ON:Spider Cultist Cowl|Spider Cultist Cowl]] set from the [[ON:Fungal Grotto I|Fungal Grotto I]] and [[ON:Fungal Grotto II|II]] group dungeons increases the {{ESO Weapon Damage Link|||y}} and {{ESO Spell Damage Link}} of your Destruction Staff abilities.
* Wearing five pieces of the [[ON:Red Eagle's Fury|Red Eagle's Fury]] set from the [[ON:Red Eagle Redoubt|Red Eagle Redoubt]] crafting site increases the {{ESO Weapon Damage Link|||y}} and {{ESO Spell Damage Link}} damage of all your weapon abilities, Destruction Staff included, but it also increases their cost.
* Wearing five pieces of the [[ON:Daring Corsair|Daring Corsair]] set from the [[ON:Cat's-Claw Station|Cat's-Claw Station]] crafting site decreases the {{ESO Stamina Link}} and {{ESO Magicka Link}} costs of all your weapon abilities by 10%, and grants you [[ON:Minor Heroism|Minor Heroism]] for 8 seconds after you use a weapon skill.
