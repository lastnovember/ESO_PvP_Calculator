}}[[File:ON-map-Imperial City.jpg|thumb|right|Map of the Imperial City [[Online:Imperial Districts|districts]]]] The bridges to the city are all blocked with impenetrable force fields, and attempting to swim across will only result in your quick demise due to the infestation of [[Online:Slaughterfish|slaughterfish]]. Entry is available through the [[Online:Alliance War|Alliance War]] menu to all players.

The Imperial City is divided between the [[Online:Imperial Sewers|Imperial Sewers]] and the six [[Online:Imperial Districts|Imperial Districts]]. Each [[Online:Alliances|Alliance]] has a small base in the sewers which provides quick access to the districts, but getting back to base from the districts requires traversing the sewers. Gameplay within the sewers is similar to a large [[Online:Public Dungeons|Public Dungeon]] with PvP, while the districts function as [[Online:Quest Hubs|Quest Hubs]] and battlegrounds. [[Online:Tel Var Stones|Tel Var Stones]] are earned and used within the City, but can be lost by dying. The Imperial City also contains the entrances to two PvE [[Online:Group Dungeons|Group Dungeons]], although these are separate from the campaign.

==Notes==
*Unlike the rest of Cyrodiil, locations where you have to enter dialogue grant a glowing blue "{{ESO Icon Abbr|skill|Support-Stalwart Guard|24}} Sanctuary" passive effect. Glowing players from different Alliances can see each other, but can't fight.
**Each rally point in the districts also grant "{{ESO Icon Abbr|skill|Support-Stalwart Guard|24}} Sanctuary" when standing on the trap door, as well as "{{ESO Icon Abbr|skill|Dark Magic-Blood Magic|24}} Chain Pull Range Suppression", "{{ESO Icon Abbr|skill|Dark Magic-Blood Magic|24}} Frozen Gate Suppression" and "{{ESO Icon Abbr|skill|Support-Stalwart Guard|24}} {{sic|Nature Grasp|Nature's Grasp}} Suppression" effects to the players on the rally point platform. They prevent you from getting attacked by enemies, from getting pulled by many skills, from getting teleported by [[ON:Frozen Gate|Frozen Gate]] and prevents enemies from using [[ON:Nature's Grasp|Nature Grasp]] on you which could be used to exploit.
*Many quests and NPCs were cut from the final release because play testers had a hard time discerning what they should be doing with all of the different quests around them. The cut quests, NPCs, etc are detailed [[Online:Cut Content/Imperial City|here]].
*At its launch in Update 7, the Imperial City shared its campaign population with the rest of Cyrodiil. It was split off into its own separate [[Online:Campaigns|campaign]] group after Update 22.
*Prior to Update 22, the Imperial City was accessible via three sewer entrances located in [[Online:Cyrodiil|Cyrodiil]], provided your Alliance met the campaign-specific requirements. At launch, all Alliances across all campaigns had open access to the city; alternative access requirements, in which an Alliance must be in control of a certain number of [[Online:keeps|keeps]] in order to enter the city, were later added to some campaigns. When Imperial City was transferred into its own campaign, the sewer entrances in Cyrodiil were destroyed.
*The Imperial City also appears in [[Arena:Imperial City|''Arena'']], [[OB:The Imperial City|''Oblivion'']], [[OBMobile:Places|''Oblivion'' Mobile]], and [[LG:The Siege of the Imperial City|Legends]].
*The city as shown in the [[GEN:Trailers|Cinematic Trailers]] more closely resembles the city as it appears in [[OB:Oblivion|''Oblivion'']] than the city as it appears in ESO, with one main bridge leading out of the western district instead of three bridges unattached to the city, and a [[OB:Market District|northeastern district]] filled with buildings and without a [[Online:Memorial District|central garden]].
*The city as seen in ESO is rotated 30° counter-clockwise from its orientation in ''Oblivion''.  This appears to be a mistake, as it means that the entrance to the [[ON:Imperial City Prison|Imperial City Prison]] does not line up with the actual location of the prison still visible on the Cyrodiil map.
