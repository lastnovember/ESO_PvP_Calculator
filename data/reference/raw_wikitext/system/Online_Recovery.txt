'''Recovery''' increases your {{ESO Magicka Link||Recovery}} for each piece of [[ON:Light Armor|Light Armor]] you are wearing, up to a total of 14%, or 28% if you spend an additional point in this passive.

{{NewLeft}}
==Notes==
* This passive's effects were combined into [[ON:Evocation|Evocation]] in [[ON:Patch/4.2.5|Patch 4.2.5]], and replaced with [[ON:Grace|Grace]], which reduces the effectiveness of snares and reduces the cost of Sprint.

==Patch Notes==
{{ESO Patch Note|1.2.3|* Recovery II: Fixed a typo with this passive ability's tooltip.}}
{{ESO Patch Note|3.0.5|* Swapped the [[ON:Evocation|Evocation]] and Recovery passives in the Skill Line window.
* This is now a 3-point passive, originally 2-points. It now increases your Magicka Recovery by 2/3/4% per piece of Light Armor, instead of 2/4%.}}
{{ESO Patch Note|4.2.5|* Recovery: Renamed this ability to [[ON:Grace|Grace]].
** This ability's old effects have been merged into the Evocation passive.
** It now reduces the effectiveness of snares applied to you by 1/2/4% per piece of Light Armor worn, and reduces the cost of Sprint by 1/2/3% per piece of Light Armor worn.
***''Developer Comment: We want Light and Medium armor to have a mobility advantage over Heavy Armor without using penalties to make Heavy Armor feel slow. Medium Armor already has faster sprinting, while Light Armor wearers now can sprint for longer and also isn't affected by snares as strongly.''}}
