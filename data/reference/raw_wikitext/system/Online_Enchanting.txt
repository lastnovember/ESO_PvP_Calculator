'''Enchanting''' is a [[Online:Crafting|crafting]] profession in ''[[Online:Online|Elder Scrolls Online]]''. You can use it to combine [[Online:Runestones|Runestones]] into [[ON:Glyphs|Glyphs]], which add magical effects to [[ON:Weapons|weapons]], [[ON:Armor|armor]] and [[ON:Jewelry|jewelry]]. Once you are [[ON:Crafting_Certification|Certified]] in this craft, you will have access to repeatable [[ON:Crafting_Writs|Crafting Writ quests]].

In addition to glyphs, the Enchanting skill allows you to craft [[ON:Furnishing Praxes|furnishings]] for your home.

== Learning ==
The Enchanting skill line is granted the first time you activate an enchanting station.

=== Gaining Inspiration ===
Experience gained in a craft is called "inspiration".
*Inspiration can be gained by [[ON:Enchanting#Creating_Glyphs|Creating Glyphs]], [[ON:Enchanting#Deconstructing_Glyphs|Deconstructing Glyphs]], translating [[ON:Enchanting#Online:Runestones|Runestones]], completing [[ON:Enchanter_Writ|Enchanter Writs]] or [[ON:A Masterful Glyph|Master Writs]], or reading [[ON:Skill Books|Skill Books]].
*The skill books which increase Enchanting are:
**[[ON:Ahzidal's Descent|Ahzidal's Descent]]
**[[ON:Enigma of the Runestones|Enigma of the Runestones]]
**[[ON:Glyphs and Enchantment|Glyphs and Enchantment]]
**[[ON:Harvest's End|Harvest's End]]
**[[ON:A Request for Relief|A Request for Relief]]

*The amount of inspiration gained from completing writs is based entirely on the potency level required by the writ and is detailed on the [[ON:Enchanter_Writ|Enchanter Writs]] page.
*The amount of inspiration gained from [[ON:Enchanting#Creating_Glyphs|Creating Glyphs]], and [[ON:Enchanting#Deconstructing_Glyphs|Deconstructing Glyphs]] is based on your current enchanting skill level as well as the level and quality of the glyph.

*Deconstructing a glyph made by other characters (alts included) gives more inspiration than deconstructing a glyph made by you. Such glyphs can be readily found at [[ON:Guild Kiosks|Guild Kiosks]], in treasure chests and [[ON:Safebox|Safeboxes]], and other sources.
*Deconstructing a glyph made with the {{Item Link|id=45851|Jejota}} rune gives about twice as much inspiration as a glyph made with {{Item Link|id=45850|Ta}}. Deconstructing a glyph made with the {{Item Link|id=45852|Denata}} rune gives the same Inspiration as a glyph made with {{Item Link|id=45853|Rekuta}}, about 50% more than a glyph made with {{Item Link|id=45851|Jejota}}. Finally, a glyph made with {{Item Link|id=45854|Kuta}} gives about 15% more inspiration than a glyph made with {{Item Link|id=45852|Denata}} or {{Item Link|id=45853|Rekuta}}. Therefore, if purchasing glyphs for deconstruction, it may be more cost effective to focus only on Superior quality glyphs made with {{Item Link|id=45852|Denata}} runes.
*Deconstructing a glyph made with the {{Item Link|id=68341|Repora}} or {{Item Link|id=68340|Itade}} CP 160 runes, give more inspiration than deconstructing a glyph made with any CP 150 [[ON:Runestones#Potency_Runes|Potency Rune]]. 

For example: deconstructing a {{ESO Quality Color|b|'''Common''' (white)}} glyph, not made by you, with a {{Item Link|id=68341|Repora}} and a {{Item Link|id=45850|Ta}}, with the +20% [[ON:Inspiration Boost|Inspiration Boost]] passive champion unlock, will gain you 4569 inspiration at any level.

== Skill Usage ==
===Finding Runes===
In order to create an enchantment, you need three different types of [[Online:Runestones|runestone]]. These are Potency, Essence, and Aspect. Runestones can be harvested from nodes as you travel through the world, extracted from glyphs, or received in mail if you have the Hireling perks. [[ON:Enchanters|Enchanters]] also sell Potency runes for gold.

A single runestone provides multiple runes of different types. Essence and Aspect runes of all kinds can drop in all zones. Potency runes drop half of the time based on your level and the other half based on your rank in the Potency Improvement skill.

=== Creating Glyphs ===
While standing at an Enchanter's table, you combine one Potency, one Essence, and one Aspect runestone to make a Glyph. Unlike [[Online:Provisioning|Provisioning]], you do not need a recipe. If your character doesn't already know what the runestones do, you will learn their effects while creating the Glyph. 

The title and icon of the Glyph will tell you if it is for weapons, armor, or jewelry.

Note that you must use a Potency runestone of the correct level for the item which you wish to enchant. For example, if you wish to enchant a level 10 weapon with a Glyph of Frost, you must use a Potency rune which covers level 10. There are three possibilities, Jora (level 1-10), Porade (level 5-15), and Jera (level 10-20). Obviously the highest strength Glyph will be created from the highest level Potency rune.

=== Applying Glyphs ===
While you must be at an Enchanter's table in order to create a Glyph, you do not need any special equipment in order to enchant an item with the Glyph in your possession. You simply select the item you wish to enchant in the Inventory screen, and choose "Enchant". The Glyph you have must be of the correct type and an appropriate level for the item to be enchanted. 

Glyphs may be applied to equipment which is of the same or higher level, but higher-level glyphs may not be applied to lower-level equipment. You may, for example, apply a Simple glyph to a Level 50/CP160 Rubedite piece of equipment, but you cannot apply an Average glyph to Level 14 Iron gear. Note that any quality of Glyph may be applied to any quality of item, however. You could put a {{ESO Quality Color|l|'''Legendary'''}} quality Glyph on a {{ESO Quality Color|b|'''Base'''}} quality item if you wanted to.

While glyphs may not be removed once they have been applied, enchantments may be changed. For example, an armor piece with an increased Magicka recovery rate enchantment can be changed to a increased Health enchantment by applying an appropriate glyph. By the same token, a sword with a fire damage enchantment can be given a frost damage enchantment by applying an appropriate glyph.

'''Special note about Armor Glyphs:''' Only Head, Chest, Legs, and Shields receive the full effect of an enchantment. Shoulder, Hands, Belt, and Feet receive only 40% of the stated value on the Glyph.
