'''Movement Speed''' is the speed at which you move when walking, running, sprinting, riding, or swimming.  Activities such as sneaking or blocking will naturally reduce your speed.  There are many effects which will increase your movement speed or decrease the speed of your enemies.  Most notable are effects which provide the [[ON:Major Expedition|Major Expedition]] or [[ON:Minor Expedition|Minor Expedition]].  The effect that increases speed while mounted is called [[ON:Major Gallop|Gallop]]. Note that multiple effects which provide the same buff will ''not'' stack, though you can stack a Major effect with a Minor one, and any speed boosts that are not providing Major or Minor Expedition will stack with both of these.

===Other===
*The [[ON:Steed's Blessing|Steed's Blessing]] perk in the [[ON:Craft|Craft]] tree increases your out of combat movement speed by 0.4% per stage, for up to 20% at 50 points.
*The [[ON:Hasty|Hasty]] perk in the [[ON:Fitness|Fitness]]: [[ON:Wind Chaser|Wind Chaser]] tree increases your movement speed when Sprinting by 2% per stage, for up to 4% at 16 points.
*The [[ON:Celerity|Celerity]] perk in the [[ON:Fitness|Fitness]]: [[ON:Wind Chaser|Wind Chaser]] tree increases your Movement Speed by 2% per stage, for up to 10% at 50 points.
*[[ON:The Steed (Mundus Stone)|The Steed]] Mundus Stone increases your movement speed by 10% (without [[ON:Divines|Divines]] traits), and only while running (''not'' mounted).
*Drinking a potion of [[ON:Speed|Speed]] grants you [[ON:Major Expedition|Major Expedition]], increase your movement speed by 30% for 3.9-15.7 sec., or 5.9-16.2 sec. as a triple effect.
*Using a poison of [[ON:Speed|Speed]] does the same, but with a reduced duration of 1.2-5.5 sec., or 1.7-6.0 sec. as a triple effect.
*The [[ON:Swift|Swift]] jewelry trait increases all movement speed by 3–7%.
*The [[ON:Gifted Rider|Gifted Rider]] perk in the [[ON:Craft|Craft]] tree increases your mount speed by 2% per stage, for up to 10% at 100 points. Gifted Rider will stack multiplicatively with Gallop.

*Using a Poison of [[ON:Hindrance|Hindrance]] reduces targets' Movement by 50% for 2.2-10 sec., or 3.2-10.0 sec. as a triple-effect.  (Drinking a Potion of Reduce Speed will have the same effect on you, with a much longer duration.)
