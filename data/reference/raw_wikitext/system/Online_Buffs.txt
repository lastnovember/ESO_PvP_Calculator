{{Trail|Effects}}
'''Buffs''' and '''Debuffs''' are temporary character [[Online:effects|effects]] applied by some [[Online:abilities|abilities]], [[Online:Sets|sets]], [[Online:potions|potions]], and [[ON:Champion|champion perks]]. Buffs are applied to yourself or your allies, and debuffs are applied to enemies.  Two buffs/debuffs with the same name from different sources will ''not'' stack, though a Major and Minor buff of the same type will. Debuffs can be removed by certain skills or items with [[Online:Cleansing|cleansing]] effects.
