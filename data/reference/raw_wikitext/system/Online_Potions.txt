'''Potions''' of various types can be found in many places, for sale by general [[ON:Merchants|Merchants]], in containers, in Alchemy Bottles loose on tables, etc., and on the corpses of your slain enemies.  Some special potions can be found in [[ON:Thieves Trove|Thieves Trove]]s or as quest rewards, while others can only be purchased in [[ON:Cyrodiil|Cyrodiil]] or from the [[ON:Crown Store|Crown Store]].
{{NewLine}}
==Generic Potions==
These come in three types, {{ESO Health Link}}, {{ESO Magicka Link}}, and {{ESO Stamina Link}}.  Their level is tied to your character level when you find them.  In all cases, these will be weaker than the ones you can craft yourself at [[ON:Alchemy|Alchemy]] stations, but they are easy to come by in large quantities.

==Rogue's Potions==
[[File:ON-icon-potion-Speed 03.png|right]]
The potions are generally designed to be useful for illegal activities.  They are essentially identical in effect to generic {{ESO Stamina Link}} Potions, but with additional effects for the Justice system. They can be obtained from:
*[[ON:Thieves Trove|Thieves Trove]]
*[[ON:Shadowy Supplier|Shadowy Supplier]]
*[[ON:Dark Brotherhood Reward Satchel|Dark Brotherhood Reward Satchel]]s
