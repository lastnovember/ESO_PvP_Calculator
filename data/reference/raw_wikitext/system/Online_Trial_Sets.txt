'''Trial Sets''' are available from the bosses and monsters in various trials. Pieces of those sets are also found in coffers rewarded for completing the [[ON:Repeatable_Quests#Trials|quests]] to clear the specific trial. Coffers are only awarded once a week per character. Trial sets are all bind-on-equip and are tradable among the group for a two-hour window, the same as dungeon sets.
