'''Mundus Stones''' are standing stones dedicated to the Constellations. Activating one will grant you a permanent blessing, although only one blessing can be active at a time. The [[Online:Twice-Born Star|Twice-Born Star]] set allows a character to have two blessings at the same time.

==Notes==
*There are three unmarked, broken Mundus Stones in [[Online:Craglorn|Craglorn]] dedicated to the Lord, Lady and Steed. They are related to the quest [[Online:The Corrupted Stone|The Corrupted Stone]] and can't be used to change blessings. 
*No DLC zones contain any Mundus Stones whatsoever (even [[Online:Western Skyrim|Western Skyrim]], which had [[Skyrim:Standing Stone|Standing Stones]] in ''[[Skyrim:Skyrim|Skyrim]]'').
