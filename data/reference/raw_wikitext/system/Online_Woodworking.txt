'''Woodworking''' is a [[Online:Crafting|crafting]] profession in ''[[Online:Online|Elder Scrolls Online]]''. It allows you to create staves, shields and bows using wood materials at the woodworking stations. The list of ingredients (logs, sanded wood and resins) used in Woodworking can be found [[Online:Woodworking Materials|here]]. Once you are [[ON:Crafting_Certification|Certified]] in this craft, you will have access to repeatable [[ON:Crafting_Writs|Crafting Writ quests]].

In addition to staves, shields and bows, the Woodworking skill allows you to craft [[ON:Furnishing Blueprints|furnishings]] for your home.

== Learning ==
The Woodworking skill line is granted the first time you activate a woodworking station.  You can increase your skill by crafting or deconstructing staves, shields and bows, researching traits, and by completing [[ON:Woodworker Writ|Woodworker Writ]]s or [[ON:A Masterful Shield|Master]] [[ON:A Masterful Weapon (Woodworking)|Writs]].  In addition, you can earn free levels in the skill line by reading [[ON:Skill Books|Skill Books]], which may be found randomly on bookshelves anywhere.  The skill books which increase Woodworking are:
*[[ON:Bowyer and Fletcher|Bowyer and Fletcher]]
*[[ON:One Staff, Many Staves|One Staff, Many Staves]]
*[[ON:Strakes and Futtocks|Strakes and Futtocks]]
*[[ON:The Year 2920, Vol. 7|The Year 2920, Vol. 7]]
*[[ON:Arboreal Architecture|Arboreal Architecture]]
*[[ON:Sealed Orders (opened)|Sealed Orders (opened)]]

== Woodworking Stations ==
Woodworking stations can be found in towns, usually next to other crafting stations. At a woodworking station, you can refine raw materials, craft new items, deconstruct existing items to retrieve material components, improve the quality of an item by using resins, or research a trait by destroying an existing item. At a crafting station, your inventory is virtually merged with your bank, so you can use items from your inventory or your bank for any operation, but the materials from the inventory are used first.
