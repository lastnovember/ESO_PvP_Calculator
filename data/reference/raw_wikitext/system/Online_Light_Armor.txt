'''Light Armor''' is a [[Online:skills|skill]] in [[Online:Online|Elder Scrolls Online]]. It consists largely of robes and cloth armor. As with the other armor skills, there are only passive skills and a single active ability. These skills mainly aid magic using characters.

== Learning ==
The Light Armor skill line is granted the first time you equip 3 pieces of Light Armor.  You can increase your skill by taking or blocking damage while wearing Light Armor, or earning experience with the Annulment skill slotted.  The more pieces you are wearing, the faster your skill will increase.  [[ON:Breton|Breton]]s are naturally gifted with Light Armor, and will learn the ability more quickly than other races.  In addition, you can earn free levels in the skill line by reading [[ON:Skill Books|Skill Books]], which may be found randomly on bookshelves anywhere.
