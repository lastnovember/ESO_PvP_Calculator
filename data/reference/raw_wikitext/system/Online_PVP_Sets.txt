{{Trail|Sets}}[[Category:Online-Sets]]'''PVP Sets''' are [[ON:Sets|sets]] of [[ON:Armor|armor]], [[ON:Weapons|weapons]] and [[ON:Jewelry|jewelry]] that can be obtained from [[ON:Cyrodiil|Cyrodiil]], the [[ON:Imperial City|Imperial City]] or [[ON:Battlegrounds|Battlegrounds]]. Items of these sets can usually be bought using Alliance Points or Tel Var stones.

==Alliance War Sets==
The following sets can be obtained from [[ON:Repeatable Quests#Cyrodiil Settlement Quests|Cyrodiil town quests]], as a reward for completing [[ON:Delves#Cyrodiil|delves]] and [[ON:Dolmens#Cyrodiil|dolmens]] throughout [[ON:Cyrodiil|Cyrodiil]], from completing [[ON:Repeatable Quests#PvP Quests|Board Missions]], from each alliance-aligned town's quartermaster using [[ON:Alliance Points|Alliance Points]], and from the [[ON:Alliance Quartermasters|Alliance Quartermasters]], also using Alliance Points. For information on the individual items in each set, see their respective pages.
