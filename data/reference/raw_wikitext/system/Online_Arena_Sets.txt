{{Trail|Sets}}{{Online Update|update=41}}[[Category:Online-Sets| Dropped Sets]]'''Arena Sets''' are five-piece [[ON:Sets|sets]] of [[ON:Armor|armor]], [[ON:Weapons|weapons]] and [[ON:Jewelry|jewelry]] that provide players with various bonuses when items of the same set are equipped simultaneously. Items constituting arena sets can only be found in reward chests for completing rounds of [[ON:Dragonstar Arena|Dragonstar Arena]], [[ON:Maelstrom Arena (place)|Maelstrom Arena]]{{ESO DLC|Orsinium}} and [[ON:Vateshran_Hollows|Vateshran Hollows]]{{ESO DLC|Markarth}}. Dragonstar Arena sets are bind-on-equip while Maelstrom Arena and Vateshran Hollows sets are bind-on-pickup.
