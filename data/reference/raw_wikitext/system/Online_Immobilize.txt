'''Immobilize''', also known as '''Root''', is a debilitating status effect that prevents a target from moving from their location, though they can still attack and defend themselves as normal.  You can escape the effect by performing a Roll Dodge or Break Free action.  Abilities which have a movement built in such as [[ON:Bolt Escape|Bolt Escape]] or [[ON:Teleport Strike|Teleport Strike]] still work, but you are still immobilized after you teleport.  Many tougher enemies are immune to this effect.

==Notes==
*The [[ON:Eternal Hunt|Eternal Hunt]] set causes you to leave behind a trap when performing a Roll Dodge which will [[ON:Poison Damage|Poison]] and Immobilize enemies who trigger it for 1.5 seconds.
*Poisons with the [[ON:Entrapment|Entrapment]] or [[ON:Unstoppable|Unstoppable]] effect will Immobilize your targets.  Entrapment's effect lasts longer, but Unstoppable also protects you from Crowd Control effects.
