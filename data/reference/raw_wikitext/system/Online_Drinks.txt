'''Drinks''' are items that temporarily increase regeneration of one, two, or of all three [[Online:Attributes|attributes]]. 

This page lists all the drinks that can be purchased from the [[Online:Brewers|brewers]] and [[Online:Chefs & Brewers|chef & brewer]] vendors or found throughout [[Online:Tamriel|Tamriel]]. Please note that these beverages are of Normal Quality, in contrast to the ones [[Online:Provisioning|brewed]] by player, and speed up the recovery of only one of the attributes at a time.

Items on this list increase an attribute regeneration for 35 minutes and have a 5 second cooldown. If different drinks are consumed one after another, the effects do not stack.

Although [[Online:Psijic Ambrosia|Psijic Ambrosia]] is listed as a beverage when crafting, it can be consumed alongside other drinks and therefore isn't listed here.

==Staple Beverages==
These beverages are '''{{ESO Quality Color|n|Normal Quality}}''' versions of craftable '''{{ESO Quality Color|f|Fine}}''' [[ON:Beverage Recipes|Beverage Recipes]].  They are less potent than their crafted versions.  These may be found randomly on tables and in kegs throughout the world, or they may be purchased from [[ON:Innkeepers|Innkeepers]] or [[ON:Brewers|Brewers]].  They may also be received as quest rewards from the [[ON:Fighters Guild|Fighters Guild]], [[ON:Mages Guild|Mages Guild]], and [[ON:Undaunted|Undaunted]] daily quests.
