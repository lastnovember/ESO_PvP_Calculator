'''Dungeon Sets''' are [[ON:Sets|sets]] that provide players with various bonuses when items of the same set are equipped simultaneously. Items constituting dungeon sets can only be found from bosses, and sometimes chests, in their respective [[ON:Group Dungeons|Group Dungeons]]. '''All dungeon sets are bind-on-pickup''', but tradeable to your group for two hours. For dungeons with more than one version, all versions will drop the same sets. Bosses have a chance to drop a [[ON:Unique Items|unique item]] in addition to the curated set piece.
