{{Trail|Sets}}[[Category:Online-Sets| Jewelry Sets]]'''Jewelry Sets''' are [[ON:Sets|sets]] of [[ON:Jewelry|jewelry]] and [[ON:Weapons|weapons]] that provide special bonuses to players when the items of the same set are equipped simultaneously. They do not include [[ON:Armor|armor pieces]] other than shields. The way to obtain them depends on the type of the set.

==Level Up Advisor sets==
These sets can be obtained through the [[ON:Level Up Advisor|Level Up Advisor]].
