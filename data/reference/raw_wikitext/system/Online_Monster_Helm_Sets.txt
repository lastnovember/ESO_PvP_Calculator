'''Monster Helm Sets''' (commonly called "monster heads") are a subset of [[ON:Dungeon Sets|dungeon sets]] of [[ON:Armor|armor]]. As the name suggests, the design of these sets are based on various bosses found in [[ON:Group Dungeons|Group Dungeons]]. They all consist of one head piece and one shoulder piece, and come in every weight and trait (other than [[ON:Nirnhoned|Nirnhoned]]). When both pieces are worn they grant a powerful and unique ability or buff that cannot be obtained elsewhere.

The head pieces are guaranteed drops from the associated last boss of a group dungeon on [[ON:Veteran|Veteran]] difficulty, while shoulder pieces can be bought from [[ON:Pledge Masters|Pledge Masters]] for [[ON:Undaunted Keys|Undaunted Keys]]. The head pieces cannot be obtained from Normal mode dungeons. Items from these sets can also sometimes be purchased from [[ON:The Golden|The Golden]] in [[ON:Cyrodiil|Cyrodiil]] for [[ON:Gold|Gold]] or [[ON:Alliance Points|Alliance Points]].

The [[ON:Styles|style]] of Monster Helm Sets can also be applied to your character using the [[ON:Outfit System|Outfit System]], provided the appropriate [[ON:Undaunted Styles|Undaunted Style page]] has been learned.

==Imperial City Sets==
{{Mod Note|Imperial City}}
Six monster helm sets can be found in the [[ON:Imperial City|Imperial City]]. The head pieces are guaranteed drops from the associated boss and have a chance of being found in their respective district's [[Online:Trophy Vaults|Trophy Vaults]], while the shoulder pieces can be bought with [[ON:Tel Var Stones|Tel Var Stones]] from [[ON:Tel Var Grand Armorers|Tel Var Grand Armorers]]: {{ESO Alliances|Dominion=[[ON:Dusk Singer|Dusk Singer]], |Covenant=[[ON:Karifa al-Tahud|Karifa al-Tahud]], and |Pact=[[ON:Birstagg|Birstagg]]|inline=1}}.

==Night Market Sets==
Three monster helm sets can be found in the [[Online:Night Market|Night Market]] and obtaining them will depend on what faction you align with for that season. The head pieces can be received in reward coffers for participating in the inter-faction contest, and shoulder pieces can be obtained by purchasing the associated coffer from a [[ON:Night Market Merchants|Night Market Merchant]].
