'''Dragonknight''' is a [[Online:Classes|class]] in ''[[Online:Online|Elder Scrolls Online]]''. It is geared primarily towards tanking characters, but also utilizing fire to deal magic damage.

::'''In-Game Description''': ''These skillful masters-at-arms use the ancient Akaviri martial arts tradition of battle-spirit, and wield fearsome magic that pounds, shatters and physically alters the world around them.''
{{NewLeft}}
==Skills==
===Ardent Flame===
[[Online:Ardent Flame|Ardent Flame]] is geared towards [[ON:Fire Damage|fire damage]].

===Draconic Power===
[[Online:Draconic Power|Draconic Power]] is geared mainly towards self-preservation and tanking.

===Earthen Heart===
[[Online:Earthen Heart|Earthen Heart]] is a mix of self-defense and support abilities.

===Class Mastery===
[[ON:Class Mastery#Dragonknight|Class Mastery]] available if not [[Online:Subclassing|subclassing]].
