'''Stun''' is a debilitating effect that prevents a target from attacking, defending, or moving for a short time.  The simplest way to Stun an enemy is to interrupt them while casting a spell, most easily with a [[Online:Interrupt|Bash]] attack.  You can escape from a Stun using a [[Online:Break Free|Break Free]] action.  Certain tougher enemies are immune to Stun effects.
