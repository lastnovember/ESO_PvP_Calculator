'''Templar''' is a [[Online:Classes|class]] in ''[[Online:Online|Elder Scrolls Online]]''. They wield the powers of light and the burning sun to smite the wicked, or to heal and support the wounded.
::'''In-Game Description''': ''These traveling knights call upon the powers of light and the burning sun to deal massive damage to their enemies while restoring health, magicka, and stamina to their allies.'' 
{{NewLeft}}
==Skills==
===Aedric Spear===
[[Online:Aedric Spear|Aedric Spear]] uses the power of light to deal damage at any range and protect the caster with a powerful shield.

===Dawn's Wrath===
[[Online:Dawn's Wrath|Dawn's Wrath]] represents the power of the sun, using light and fire to damage enemies directly, assist allies with their damage, or reflecting enemy damage.

===Restoring Light===
[[Online:Restoring Light|Restoring Light]] is focused on defensive support and strong healing abilities.

===Class Mastery===
[[ON:Class Mastery#Templar|Class Mastery]] available if not [[Online:Subclassing|subclassing]].
