}} __NOTOC__ It can be accessed once a player receives the [[Online:Cyrodiil Awaits!|Cyrodiil Awaits!]] message through the in-game mail system at Level 10. Despite the name, the zone encompasses only the central part of the [[ON:Cyrodiil (province)|actual province]].
Cyrodiil's geography is mostly low-lands and forests, with mountains around its borders. The central [[Lore:Heartlands|Heartlands]] is the area around the [[Online:Imperial City|Imperial City]]; the island is almost wholly used for the city while the land to the east is used for farming. The [[Lore:Nibenay Valley|Nibenay Valley]] to the south is too small to have its own unique geological features; it is a mix of the [[Lore:Great Forest|Great Forest]] to the north and west of the Heartlands, and the [[Lore:Nibenay Basin|Nibenay Basin]]'s plains to the east of the Heartlands. The [[Online:Jerall Mountains|Jerall Mountains]] form the border with [[Lore:Skyrim|Skyrim]] to the north.

Politically, the region is in flux, with armies of the three [[Online:Alliances|Alliances]] continually fighting over strategic positions. Nominally however, northwestern Cyrodiil is considered [[Online:Daggerfall Territory|Daggerfall Territory]], northeastern Cyrodiil is considered [[Online:Ebonheart Territory|Ebonheart Territory]], and southwestern Cyrodiil is considered [[Online:Aldmeri Territory|Aldmeri Territory]]. Remnants of the [[Online:Imperial Legion|Imperial Legion]] still control parts of the Imperial City, and legionaries can be found in small military camps all across the region.

At this time of war between the alliances, [[Online:Molag Bal|Molag Bal]] is also attempting to pull [[Lore:Nirn|Nirn]] into [[Online:Coldharbour|Coldharbour]] with the use of huge [[Online:Dark Anchor|Dark Anchors]] attached to Tamriel, a number of which are found in Cyrodiil. The largest of these is located over the Imperial City.

For more historical information, see the [[Lore:Cyrodiil|lore article]].

==Notes==
* Each alliance controls a number of safe areas within Cyrodiil. These places are indicated on the wiki using different iconography depending on the function of the area. See [[Online:Alliance War#Safe Zones|Safe Zones]] for details.
* Unlike most other zones, Cyrodiil had no set level range before [[ON:One Tamriel|One Tamriel]]. It could be accessed at {{ESO Veteran|normal=10}} and [[Online:Battle Leveling|battle-leveling]] combined with the many [[Online:Campaigns#Bonuses|campaign bonuses]] made it possible for lower level players to participate in PvP. PvE enemies were last set at {{ESO Champion|140}} ({{ESO Veteran|veteran=14}}), although previously they were scaled to {{ESO Veteran|veteran=12}}, {{ESO Veteran|veteran=5}}, and originally {{ESO Veteran|normal=50}}. The Imperial City was scaled to {{ESO Champion|160}} ({{ESO Veteran|veteran=16}}).
* Cyrodiil is the largest zone in Elder Scrolls Online, and to make sure battles can be won, there is limited fast-traveling. It is a good idea to bring a horse as it can take some time to travel on foot between areas. You cannot fast-travel to a keep under attack; you must make your way on foot or horseback.
* Some villages are alliance-neutral &mdash; no NPCs will pose a threat. However, alliance-affiliated NPC guards will patrol towns controlled by alliances, and they will react to enemy alliance players. Other villages have been overrun by enemy factions who will attack anyone regardless of their affiliation.
