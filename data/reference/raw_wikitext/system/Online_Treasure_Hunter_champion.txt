'''Treasure Hunter''' increases the quality of the loot found in [[ON:Treasure Chests|Treasure Chests]]. In practice Treasure Hunter simply bumps up the loot table of the treasure chest. This is easily seen with Simple difficulty Chests which give set gear while Treasure Hunter is on (like Intermediate and higher difficulties) when they don't without it.

==Notes==
* Before [[ON:Patch/10.3.5|Update 45]], this Champion Star had to be slotted in order to be active.
