[[ON:Arcanist|Arcanist]] is a [[Online:Classes|class]] in ''[[Online:Online|Elder Scrolls Online]]''. It is available to players who have purchased the ''[[Online:Necrom (Chapter)|Necrom]]'' [[Online:Chapters|Chapter]]. It is also purchasable in the Crown Store for {{ESO Crowns|1,500}} as of June 3, 2024 on PC/Mac or June 18, 2024 on consoles.
Become empowered by the secrets of Apocrypha and the forbidden knowledge of Hermaeus Mora. The Arcanist is a powerful new Class capable of destructive, restorative, or defensive magic by channeling the arcane, ancient runes, and lost tomes of power.  

'''In-Game Description''': ''Channel the eldritch powers of Apocrypha as an Arcanist. These scholarly spellcasters channel secret runes and abyssal spells to destroy their enemies, heal their allies, and defend against any foe.'' 
{{NewLeft}}
== Skills ==

===Herald of the Tome===
[[Online:Herald of the Tome|Herald of the Tome]] is an apocryphal magic skill line with damage dealing abilities.

===Soldier of Apocrypha===
[[Online:Soldier of Apocrypha|Soldier of Apocrypha]] is a set of abilities focused towards defense, tanking, and debuffing.

===Curative Runeforms===
[[Online:Curative Runeforms|Curative Runeforms]] is a mix of healing, shielding and buffing abilities.

===Class Mastery===
[[ON:Class Mastery#Arcanist|Class Mastery]] available if not [[Online:Subclassing|subclassing]].
