'''Blacksmithing''' is a [[Online:Crafting|crafting]] profession in ''[[Online:Online|Elder Scrolls Online]]''. This profession's specialty is to create and improve [[Online:Heavy Armor|heavy armor]] and metal [[Online:Weapons|weapons]] in various styles of different races. To create a weapon or a piece of armor you need to be at a [[ON:Blacksmithing Station|blacksmithing station]]. Such stations are also used to conduct research on different kinds of heavy armor and metal weapons traits. The list of ingredients (ores, ingots, and tempers) used in Blacksmithing can be found [[Online:Blacksmithing Materials|here]]. Once you are [[ON:Crafting_Certification|Certified]] in this craft, you will have access to repeatable [[ON:Crafting_Writs|Crafting Writ quests]].

In addition to armor and weapons, the Blacksmithing skill allows you to craft [[ON:Furnishing Diagrams|furnishings]] for your home.

== Learning ==
The Blacksmithing skill line is granted the first time you activate a blacksmithing station.  You can increase your skill by crafting or deconstructing armor and weapons, researching traits, and by completing [[ON:Blacksmith Writ|Blacksmith Writ]]s or [[ON:A Masterful Plate|Master]] [[ON:A Masterful Weapon (Blacksmithing)|Writs]].  In addition, you can earn free levels in the skill line by reading [[ON:Skill Books|Skill Books]], which may be found randomly on bookshelves anywhere.  The skill books which increase Blacksmithing are:
*[[ON:Smithing: A Worthy Endeavor|Smithing: A Worthy Endeavor]]
*[[ON:Heavy Armor: A Forge-Wife's Advice|Heavy Armor: A Forge-Wife's Advice]]
*[[ON:The Book of Circles: Forging Maxims|The Book of Circles: Forging Maxims]]
*[[ON:Nord Armorers and Armsmen|Nord Armorers and Armsmen]]
*[[ON:Treatise on Metallurgical Anomalies|Treatise on Metallurgical Anomalies]]

You can learn about this profession by speaking with some of the blacksmiths.
:'''I'd like to learn more about blacksmithing.'''

We scour hills and caves looking for rare ores to work.<br>Raw materials will only take you so far. You need [[Online:Crafting Motifs|style books]] and special ingredients for more advanced work."''
:'''I'm sure there's a lot to learn'''
:''"You bet. A true blacksmith can read the metal to determine its potential. During the forging process we can choose a [[ON:Traits|trait]] that best fits the item—provided we've done our [[ON:Traits#Research|research]]. Additional sharpening, extra weight … these are just examples."''
