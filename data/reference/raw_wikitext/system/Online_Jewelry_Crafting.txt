'''Jewelry Crafting''' is a skill line that was released along with the ''[[Online:Summerset (Chapter)|Summerset]]'' [[Online:Chapters|Chapter]] in June 2018.  It for the first time allows you to craft and improve [[ON:Jewelry|Jewelry]], including the ability to make jewelry items in all [[ON:Craftable Sets|Craftable Sets]].  It works in a similar fashion to the [[ON:Blacksmithing|Blacksmithing]], [[ON:Clothing|Clothing]], and [[ON:Woodworking|Woodworking]] lines, with researchable traits and associated trait items, harvestable leveled materials, and crafting sites added in all regular crafting areas.

In addition to rings and necklaces, the Jewelry Crafting skill allows you to craft [[ON:Furnishing Sketches|furnishings]] for your home.

== Learning ==
The Jewelry Crafting skill line is granted the first time you activate a jewelry station (assuming you have access to the ''Summerset'' Chapter).  You can increase your skill by crafting or deconstructing jewelry, and by completing [[ON:Jewelry Crafting Writ|Jewelry Crafting Writs]], which are available after completing [[Online:Jewelry Crafting Certification|Jewelry Crafting Certification]].  In addition, you can earn free levels in the skill line by reading [[ON:Skill Books|Skill Books]], which may be found randomly on bookshelves anywhere.  The skill books which increase Jewelry Crafting are:
*[[ON:Jewels of Yokuda|Jewels of Yokuda]]
*[[ON:The Pretension of High Elf Jewelry|The Pretension of High Elf Jewelry]]

==Materials==
There are five levels of basic materials needed for jewelry crafting.  These materials can be found at Jewelry Seam nodes everywhere.  These are most commonly found near mountains and large rocks, which can be harvested for Dust.  10 Dust can be refined into 7-10 Ounces, which may then be used to craft Necklaces and Rings.  Additionally, you may find Ochre, which is used to make [[ON:Furnishing Sketches|furnishing items]].

In addition, jewelry crafting writs will now sometimes give you [[ON:Intricate|Intricate]] jewelry, just like the other gear crafting writs. ==Platings==
Jewelry items can now also be upgraded in quality, similar to other equipment.  This is done using Platings. Just like other equipment upgrading, you will need to use multiple Platings to ensure that you do not destroy the item in the process.  The [[ON:Platings Expertise|Platings Expertise]] skill will allow you to safely upgrade items with fewer Platings.

Prior to [[Online:Update 40|Update 40]], Platings were mostly found as Grains, 10 of which needed to be refined in order to produce 1 Plating. To make up for the resulting scarcity of Platings, half as many were required to upgrade an item.
