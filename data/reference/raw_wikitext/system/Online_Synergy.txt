{{Trail|Combat/Skills}}
'''Synergies''' are special abilities that other allied players may activate when you use certain skills or sets.  If a Synergy is available to you, a prompt at the bottom of the screen appears letting you know that you can activate a Synergy, by pressing the key or buttons associated with it.  ("'''X'''" by default on PC/Mac, [[File:UESP-icon-XB1 Y.png|20px|Y]]+[[File:UESP-icon-XB1 B.png|20px|B]] on Xbox, [[File:UESP-icon-PS4 Triangle.png|20px|Triangle]]+[[File:UESP-icon-PS4 Circle.png|20px|Circle]] on PS4).  Synergy activations cost no resources and are independent of skill cooldowns. The [[ON:Undaunted|Undaunted]] skill line is particularly based on Synergies, as all of its active skills have one associated with them. Some Synergies only appear with certain skill morphs, or change depending on the morph chosen. 

Synergies are a great way of restoring resources through [[Online:Undaunted Command|Undaunted Command]], the [[Online:Harmony|Harmony]] jewelry trait, or the synergies' effect

===Cooldown===
Synergies have a 20 second cooldown per player for each individual Synergy. A single player can use several different synergies in quick succession, but must wait 20 seconds before using the same skills synergy again. Likewise if a synergy has multiple uses, a different player activating it has no effect on your cooldown or ability to activate it.

All skill morphs synergies share a cooldown, so casting {{ESO Synergy Link|Combustion|nolink=y}} would place you on cooldown for using {{ESO Synergy Link|Healing Combustion|nolink=y}}

Uniquely [[Online:Spear_Shards|Spear Shards]] and [[Online:Necrotic_Orb|Necrotic Orb]]'s synergies also share the same cooldown. Using one will prevent you from using the other for 20 seconds.

===Mechanics===
All synergies have a set amount of uses per cast of the skill. This is typically either 1, 3 or 12 uses. Recasting a skill will refresh that synergy's availability for the group.

Most synergies can only be activated by group members, but there are some exceptions in the table below marked with {{intnote|special}}

The activation range for each synergy is usually the spell's [[Online:Area of Effect|Area of Effect]], but can also be centered around a target or ally.

From Patch 6.0.0
Synergies now have a hierarchy to determine which synergy appears first when
multiple are present to you. Quest, Dungeon, Arena, and Trial-related synergies
now always appear over player-sourced synergies. Furthermore, for player-sourced
synergies, the following ruleset is applied:
Healing and Shield-related synergies will take utmost priority.
Utility, Buff, and Debuff synergies will appear next.
Strictly Damage-related synergies are last in priority.
In a situation where Purify, Conduit, and Combustion are all available to
activate and are given to you, Purify (Healing) will appear first, then
Combustion (Utility), then Conduit (exclusive Damage)

A few synergies have conditions to activate besides being in range.
*{{Anchor||Hidden Refresh}} {{ESO Synergy Link|Hidden Refresh|nolink=y}} you must be below 70% {{ESO Health Link}}
*{{Anchor||Blood_Altar}} {{ESO Synergy Link|Blood Funnel|nolink=y}} and {{ESO Synergy Link|Blood Feast|Blood Funnel|nolink=y}} you must be below 60% {{ESO Health Link}}

==Synergy-Like Events==
{{incomplete|Missing many quest-related synergies.}}
Sometimes, there are other events which behave like Synergies, giving you the option to perform an action during combat.  They are activated in the same way, but do not require another player to be around, or trigger any abilities which are affected by Synergies.  Some examples are:

*The '''[[Online:Blade of Woe|Blade of Woe]]'''{{ESO DLC|Dark Brotherhood}} ability can be used by sneaking close enough to a humanoid target as long as you have the relevant [[Online:Dark Brotherhood|Dark Brotherhood]] passive skill. It instantly kills weak humanoids and is therefore useful for murdering Justice Neutral NPCs. The range is slightly lower than a vampire's Feed ability.
*{{Anchor||Feed}}The '''[[Online:Feed|Feed]]''' ability shows when you are a [[Online:Vampire|Vampire]] and sneaking close enough to a humanoid target to feed.  Doing so will increase your Vampirism Stage by one, as well as restoring your [[Online:Health|Health]].  Your Vampirism stage will decrease every 6 hours if you do not feed. Some of the Vampire abilities are only available or become more powerful if you are in the higher Stages of Vampirism. Doing so is considered an attack, and will be reported as a crime if done to a non-hostile enemy. The range is slightly higher than the Blade of Woe ability.  The same ability shows when giving another player a bite at a Vampire Shrine with the [[Online:Blood Ritual|Blood Ritual]] skill, though this does not affect your Vampirism stage.
*{{Anchor||Insatiable Hunger}}The '''[[Online:Insatiable Hunger|Insatiable Hunger]]''' ability can be used by [[Online:Werewolf|Werewolves]] provided they have invested in the skill line and are currently in Werewolf form. This allows you to consume the corpses of your slain enemies to restore your [[Online:Health|Health]] and gain [[ON:Ultimate|Ultimate]]. The same ability shows when giving another player a bite at a [[Online:Werewolf Ritual Site|Werewolf Ritual Sites]] with the [[Online:Shadow of the Bloodmoon|Shadow of the Bloodmoon]] skill.
*{{Anchor||Slaughter (Werewolf)}}The '''[[ON:Slaughter (Werewolf)|Slaughter]]''' synergy can be used by [[Online:Werewolf|Werewolves]] while they are [[ON:Prowl|prowling]] on targets they have snuck behind, much like with the Blade of Woe and Vampire Feed abilities.
*{{Anchor||Taunt Her}} During the final battle in [[Online:Arx Corinium|Arx Corinium]] against [[Online:Sellistrix the Lamia Queen|Sellistrix the Lamia Queen]], the Nereid sisters will give you the option to "'''[[Online:Taunt|Taunt]] Her!'''", causing Sellistrix to direct her attacks at the Nereid instead of you.  This is part of the [[ON:Force of Nature|Force of Nature]] quest and thus will only be available on your first run-through of the dungeon.
*During [[Online:Stone Cold|Stone Cold]], you have to '''Use Indanas to Collapse Hole'''.
*During the quest [[Online:God of Schemes|God of Schemes]], there are two instances where you are given a pseudo-Synergy event, first to form floating rocks into a bridge using '''Aedric Command''', and later to escape from Molag Bal's clenched fist using '''Sacrifice of Courage'''.
*If you rescue [[Online:Unsulag|Unsulag]] during the [[Online:Forcing the Faith|Forcing the Faith]]{{ESO DLC|Orsinium}} quest in [[Online:Paragon's Remembrance|Paragon's Remembrance]], she will cast a healing spell which gives you a Synergy called '''Restoring Waves''' which restores your health.
*When [[ON:Scruut|Scruut]] is following you, she will periodically give you the '''Apocrypha's Wrath''' synergy to use, which summons [[ON:Scruut's Tentacle|Scruut's Tentacle]] to assist you. During [[ON:Kynreeve Ryl|Kynreeve Ryl]]'s fight, this synergy also stops his "Ryl's Nightmare" ability.{{ESO NE}}
*During some parts in [[ON:Necrom (Chapter)|Necrom]]'s main quests after recovering [[ON:Meln the Mouthless|Meln the Mouthless]], you can use the {{ESO Icon|ON-icon-synergy-Ghost Sight.png||24}}'''Ghost Sight''' synergy against spirit-like enemies which have dramatic damage taken reduction. Dealing damage to those enemies grants you a stack of "Ghost Sight" for 30 seconds, and at 3 stacks a small area appears on the ground which grants you the '''Ghost Light''' synergy. Activating this synergy greatly increases your damage done for a time and causes the spirit-like enemies to immediately change back into their physical form.
*After defeating [[ON:Vaermina's Nightmare (An Unhealthy Fate)|Vaermina's Nightmare]] in [[ON:The Mythos|The Mythos]], you will use the {{ESO Icon|ON-icon-skill-Herald of the Tome-Fatecarver.png||24}} '''Black Book: The Tormented Eye''' synergy twice to fire a beam at [[ON:Vaermina|Vaermina]] herself.
*During [[ON:Chronicle of Fate|Chronicle of Fate]], you will have to use {{ESO Icon|ON-icon-synergy-Meln's Ghost Sight.png||24}}'''Meln's Ghost Sight''' to reveal a passage.
