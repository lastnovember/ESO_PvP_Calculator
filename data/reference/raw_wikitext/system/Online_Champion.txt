[[File:ON-icon-Champion System.png|right]]The [[Online:Champion|Champion]] System in the Elder Scrolls Online is a system of horizontal character development that complements the more vertical progression of [[Online:Leveling|Leveling]] and [[Online:Skills|Skill]] development. The Champion System is unlocked when one of your characters reaches [[ON:Veteran|Veteran]] rank at Level 50. From that point on, experience points provide progression in Champion Points. Champion Points are required to wear Level 50 gear and use Level 50 consumables, and are used to unlock new abilities within the realms of the three Guardian Constellations.

The current cap is 3600, and you are unable to spend any points that you have earned above this cap. This means you are able to spend 1200 points in each of the three constellations.
[[File:ON-misc-Champion Point Menu Animation.jpg|thumb|right|A player with the Champion Points menu open]]
== Perks System ==

The Champion Points system utilizes a comprehensive '''perk-based structure''' organized across three Guardian Constellations: '''Craft''' (Thief), '''Warfare''' (Mage), and '''Fitness''' (Warrior)<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://eso-hub.com/en/champion-points|title=ESO Champion Points System|date=2025-04-13}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://alcasthq.com/eso-championpoints-beginner-guide/|title=Champion Points Beginner Guide for ESO|date=2025-01-02}}</ref>. Each constellation contains multiple types of perks that players unlock by investing Champion Points, with a maximum of 1200 points available per constellation for a total cap of 3600 Champion Points<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://eso-hub.com/en/champion-points|title=ESO Champion Points System|date=2025-04-13}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://alcasthq.com/eso-championpoints-beginner-guide/|title=Champion Points Beginner Guide for ESO|date=2025-01-02}}</ref>.

=== Perk Categories ===

The system features '''four distinct perk categories''' within each constellation:

'''Base Perks''': Fundamental bonuses that provide core improvements like reducing fall damage, increasing gold gain, or reducing ability costs. These are typically low-cost investments that form the foundation of each tree<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://alcasthq.com/eso-championpoints-beginner-guide/|title=Champion Points Beginner Guide for ESO|date=2025-01-02}}</ref>.

'''Base Active Perks''': Essential scaling bonuses that increase primary attributes. Examples include Max Health/Magicka/Stamina increases and core damage/armor improvements. These perks can be invested in up to 50 stages at 1 point per stage<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://eso-hub.com/en/champion-points|title=ESO Champion Points System|date=2025-04-13}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://alcasthq.com/eso-championpoints-beginner-guide/|title=Champion Points Beginner Guide for ESO|date=2025-01-02}}</ref>.

'''Passive Perks''': Specialized bonuses that activate automatically once purchased. Notable examples include Meticulous Disassembly (improving crafting material extraction), Plentiful Harvest (double resource node yield chance), and Treasure Hunter (improved treasure chest quality)<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://eso-hub.com/en/news/eso-u45-simplifying-green-cp-no-need-to-constantly-swap-between-activities|title=ESO U45 Simplifying Green CP, No Need to Constantly Swap|date=2025-06-10}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://alcasthq.com/eso-update-45-overview/|title=ESO Update 45 Overview - Everything you need to know!|date=2025-03-18}}</ref>. As of Update 45, many formerly slottable Craft tree perks became passive, eliminating the need for constant swapping during different activities<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://eso-hub.com/en/news/eso-u45-simplifying-green-cp-no-need-to-constantly-swap-between-activities|title=ESO U45 Simplifying Green CP, No Need to Constantly Swap|date=2025-06-10}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://alcasthq.com/eso-update-45-overview/|title=ESO Update 45 Overview - Everything you need to know!|date=2025-03-18}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://arzyelbuilds.com/eso-update-45/|title=Everything New in ESO Update 45|date=2025-03-08}}</ref>.

'''Active Perks''': Advanced abilities that must be equipped to the '''Champion Bar''' to function. These include powerful bonuses like Shadowstrike (invisibility after Blade of Woe kills), War Mount (removing mount stamina costs), and various combat enhancements<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://eso-hub.com/en/champion-points|title=ESO Champion Points System|date=2025-04-13}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://alcasthq.com/eso-championpoints-beginner-guide/|title=Champion Points Beginner Guide for ESO|date=2025-01-02}}</ref>.

=== Champion Bar System ===

The '''Champion Bar''' appears at the top of each constellation interface and determines which Active Perks are currently functional<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://eso-hub.com/en/champion-points|title=ESO Champion Points System|date=2025-04-13}}</ref>. Players can slot a limited number of Active Perks simultaneously, creating strategic choices about which bonuses to activate. Perks can be swapped anytime while out of combat, allowing build flexibility without respeccing Champion Points<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://alcasthq.com/eso-championpoints-beginner-guide/|title=Champion Points Beginner Guide for ESO|date=2025-01-02}}</ref>.

=== Subconstellation Structure ===

Each main constellation contains '''three specialized subconstellations'''<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://eso-hub.com/en/champion-points|title=ESO Champion Points System|date=2025-04-13}}</ref>:

* '''Craft''': Focus areas for specific activities
* '''Warfare''': Mastered Curation (healing/support), Staving Death (damage resistance), Extended Might (damage/penetration)  
* '''Fitness''': Survivor's Spite (fighting against odds), Wind Chaser (movement/sprinting), Walking Fortress (blocking specialization)

These subconstellations provide targeted bonuses for specific playstyles and roles<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://eso-hub.com/en/champion-points|title=ESO Champion Points System|date=2025-04-13}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://alcasthq.com/eso-championpoints-beginner-guide/|title=Champion Points Beginner Guide for ESO|date=2025-01-02}}</ref>.

=== Investment Mechanics ===

Perks have varying '''costs and stage limits'''<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://alcasthq.com/eso-championpoints-beginner-guide/|title=Champion Points Beginner Guide for ESO|date=2025-01-02}}</ref>:
* Single-stage perks: Major abilities costing 25-120 points
* Multi-stage perks: Scalable bonuses with 2-50 stages  
* Cost per stage: Ranges from 1 point (base attributes) to 50+ points (powerful effects)

Most advanced perks require prerequisite investments in base constellation perks, though each tree contains '''exception perks''' that can be unlocked regardless of other investments<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://alcasthq.com/eso-championpoints-beginner-guide/|title=Champion Points Beginner Guide for ESO|date=2025-01-02}}</ref>.

=== Recent System Updates ===

'''Update 45 (2025)''' significantly streamlined the Craft constellation by converting nine previously slottable perks into automatic passive bonuses<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://eso-hub.com/en/news/eso-u45-simplifying-green-cp-no-need-to-constantly-swap-between-activities|title=ESO U45 Simplifying Green CP, No Need to Constantly Swap|date=2025-06-10}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://alcasthq.com/eso-update-45-overview/|title=ESO Update 45 Overview - Everything you need to know!|date=2025-03-18}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://arzyelbuilds.com/eso-update-45/|title=Everything New in ESO Update 45|date=2025-03-08}}</ref>. The affected perks include Cutpurse's Art, Meticulous Disassembly, Plentiful Harvest, Treasure Hunter, Homemaker, Liquid Efficiency, Rationer, Professional Upkeep, and Infamous<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://eso-hub.com/en/news/eso-u45-simplifying-green-cp-no-need-to-constantly-swap-between-activities|title=ESO U45 Simplifying Green CP, No Need to Constantly Swap|date=2025-06-10}}</ref>. This change eliminated the tedious need to constantly swap Champion Points when switching between combat, crafting, and exploration activities<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://eso-hub.com/en/news/eso-u45-simplifying-green-cp-no-need-to-constantly-swap-between-activities|title=ESO U45 Simplifying Green CP, No Need to Constantly Swap|date=2025-06-10}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://arzyelbuilds.com/eso-update-45/|title=Everything New in ESO Update 45|date=2025-03-08}}</ref>.

The perk system's design promotes both horizontal progression (unlocking new abilities) and vertical progression (strengthening existing bonuses) while maintaining gameplay balance through the Champion Bar's strategic limitations<ref>{{Cite web|url=https://eso-hub.com/en/champion-points|title=ESO Champion Points System|date=2025-04-13}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web|url=https://alcasthq.com/eso-championpoints-beginner-guide/|title=Champion Points Beginner Guide for ESO|date=2025-01-02}}</ref>.

==Enlightenment==
While you are Enlightened, experience gain is multiplied by four, effectively reducing the required XP for one Champion Point to a quarter of its original value. Once per day, you receive 400,000 XP of Enlightenment; after your Enlightenment is used up, you will return to requiring the standard amount of XP to earn a Champion Point. Enlightenment accumulates in a pool with a cap of 4,800,000 XP after twelve days. Each single experience point earned through normal gameplay expends four of the stored points in the Enlightenment pool; killing an enemy usually worth 76 XP will give you 304 XP, while completing a standard Level 50 quest will reward you with 29940 XP instead of 7485 XP.

Note that only Level 50 characters can be Enlightened, and the Enlightenment pool is shared across all Level 50 characters on your account.

==Notes==
* The Champion Point system was added in [[Online:Update 6|Update 6]] and replaced the [[Online:Veteran Rank|Veteran Rank]] progression system in [[Online:Update 10|Update 10]].
* In theory, to unlock all passive perks and the most expensive four active perks 1075 points for Craft, 542 points for Fitness and 520 points for Warfare are needed. This however is based purely on the numbers in the above tables and neglects reachability (e.g. "Bastion" must be unlocked before "Arcane Alacrity" can be unlocked) of the perks, so the actual points needed ingame might be a bit higher.
* Once you have access to Champion Points on an account, you will always have access to them, even if you delete all of your Level 50 characters.
