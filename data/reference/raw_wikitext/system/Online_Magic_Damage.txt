'''Magic Damage''' is the most common type of [[ON:Magical Damage|Magical Damage]] mainly used by [[ON:Arcanist|Arcanist]]s, [[ON:Nightblade|Nightblade]]s, and [[ON:Templar|Templar]]s but is available from many sources. It is usually the "default" for all magical damage effects. It can cause the [[Online:Overcharged|Overcharged]] status effect.

==Sources==
===Weapons===
*All [[ON:Restoration Staff|Restoration Staves]].
*All weapons enchanted with a [[ON:Glyph of Absorb Health|Glyph of Absorb Health]] (Oko), [[ON:Glyph of Absorb Magicka|Glyph of Absorb Magicka]] (Makko), [[ON:Glyph of Prismatic Onslaught|Glyph of Prismatic Onslaught]] (Hakeijo)

===Champion Points===
*[[ON:War Mage (champion)|War Mage]] in the [[ON:Extended Might|Extended Might]] subconstellation of [[ON:Warfare|Warfare]] grants additional Magic Damage.
*[[ON:Cutting Defense|Cutting Defense]] in [[ON:Warfare|Warfare]] deals a small amount of Magic Damage to attackers whenever they damage you with a Direct Damage attack within 7 meters

==Defense==
There are several ways of protecting yourself against Magic Damage.
*A Glyph of Spell Resistance, created with a Makderi Essence rune and a negative Potency rune, can be applied to jewelry.
*[[ON:Elemental Aegis|Elemental Aegis]] in the [[ON:Staving Death|Staving Death]] subconstellation of [[ON:Warfare|Warfare]] reduces Magic Damage taken.
