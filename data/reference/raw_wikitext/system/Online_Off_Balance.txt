'''Off Balance''' is a debilitating effect in itself but it is mostly used to trigger other negative effects. It can be caused by various attacks, but most commonly by Blocking a melee Heavy Attack.

Applying Off Balance stuns the target. When an enemy is Off Balance, they can be knocked down with a Heavy Attack whose damage and resources restoration are boosted by 70%. Both the knockdown and the stun will not occur if the target is immune to crowd control but it will still be vulnerable to effects against Off Balance targets. You cannot get rid of Off Balance by breaking free of the crowd control or by using a cleansing ability.

Off Balance will last 7 seconds regardless of the source or the target and gives an Off Balance Immunity of 15 seconds when it ends.
