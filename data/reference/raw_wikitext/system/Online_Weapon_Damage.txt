'''Weapon Damage''' represents the damage done by non-staff weapons and abilities with a {{ESO Stamina Link}} cost.  Your Weapon Damage can be improved by many [[:Category:Online-Sets with Weapon/Spell Damage|sets]] (click for complete list), as well the [[ON:Major Brutality|Major Brutality]] and [[ON:Minor Brutality|Minor Brutality]] effects, which increase your damage by 20% and 10% respectively.
