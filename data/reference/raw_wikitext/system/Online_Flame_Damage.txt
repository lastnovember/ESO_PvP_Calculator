'''Flame Damage''' is a type of [[ON:Magical Damage|Magical Damage]] mainly used by [[ON:Dragonknight|Dragonknight]]s, but available to other classes as well. It can cause the [[Online:Burning|Burning]] status effect.

Some enemies may use Flame Damage against you, notably [[ON:Flame Atronach|Flame Atronach]]s, which are some of the first enemies you will encounter. [[Online:Dark Elf|Dunmer]] characters receive a bonus to Flame Resistance through their [[Online:Resist Flame|Resist Flame]] passive skill. 

==Sources==
===Weapons===
*All [[ON:Destruction Staves|Inferno Staves]].
*All weapons enchanted with a [[ON:Glyph of Flame|Glyph of Flame]] (using a Rakeipa runestone). Note that staves may be enchanted with a different elemental damage, so a single staff could do both Flame and another type of damage.

===Champion Points===
*[[ON:War Mage (champion)|War Mage]] in the [[ON:Extended Might|Extended Might]] subconstellation of [[ON:Warfare|Warfare]] grants additional Flame Damage.

==Defense==
There are several ways of protecting yourself against Flame Damage.
*[[ON:Dark Elf|Dark Elf]] characters have access to the [[ON:Resist Flame|Resist Flame]] passive skill, which reduces all Flame Damage received.
*A Glyph of Flame Resist, created with a Rakeipa Essence rune and a negative Potency rune, can be applied to jewelry.
*[[ON:Elemental Aegis|Elemental Aegis]] in the [[ON:Staving Death|Staving Death]] subconstellation of [[ON:Warfare|Warfare]] reduces Flame Damage taken.


==Notes==
*This effect was previously referred to as '''Fire Damage''' in a few cases, but has since been corrected for consistency.
