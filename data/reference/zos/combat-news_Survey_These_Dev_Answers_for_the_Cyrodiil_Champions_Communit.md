Check out our answers to the community’s questions

Access to the Public Test Server for Update 45
is open
to
 all players on PC/Mac, and one of the
many features
receiving attention is the Cyrodiil Champions PvP test, also known as the
 Vengeance campaign. This is a preliminary step to ensure functionality is in
 place, with the full test occurring on live servers at a later date after
 Update 45 arrives.
In
Studio Director
 Matt Firor’s letter
at the end of last year, he touched on how the team
 will be experimenting a bit more in the name of future change, innovation, and
 improvement efforts for
ESO
. One such effort is the Cyrodiil Champions
 test, and your feedback will be critical to helping us understand what you
 like, what you don’t like, and ultimately shape the future of ESO.
Cyrodiil Champions is part of an effort to improve
 performance and stability in
ESO
’s open-world PvP experience. After its
 announcement in January, we reached out to the
ESO
community on both the
 official
ESO
forums and Reddit for your questions, and you can now check
 out our answers via
this post on the
ESO
forums
. For more, check out
 this
recent livestream
with the devs!
We hope you’re excited for the upcoming battles in Cyrodiil,
 including this new test campaign now live on the PTS, and later on live servers.
