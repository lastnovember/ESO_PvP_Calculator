Hello Ainaven! Thank you for taking the time to share your  amazing cosplay with the ESO community. To begin, could you tell us how you got  your start cosplaying?
In 2010, I started doing elven-inspired photo shoots. At  that time, I did not know that it was called original cosplay. In 2014, my  friend who is also a cosplayer convinced me to make my first cosplay by myself,  and I really enjoyed the process.

The detail on the chest plate is incredible. What method
 did you use to create the patterns carved into the armor?
Thank you! I made the armor base out of EVA-foam, and for
 decorations I drew all the patterns in vector, then circled them with a laser
 machine on a thin material (1mm EVA foam). However, it turned out that a thin
 pattern cannot be glued together after being cut. Therefore, I first glued a
 sheet with a pattern and then cut out all the patterns along the outline with a
 scalpel. So, we can say that almost all the patterns are hand-carved.

Is there one specific aspect of this cosplay that you’re
 especially proud of?
I am very happy that I was able to recreate all the patterns
 exactly and not miss anything. It took a long time, and some details were hard
 to see, but I did not want to simplify the costume. I had to look at many
 different screenshots from various angles to ensure that I didn't miss
 anything.

Was there a particularly challenging aspect you
 encountered while creating this costume?
A funny fact is that the most difficult or tricky part of
 this costume was the helmet. In the game, the helmet goes through the textures
 of the shoulder pads, but in real life, it is impossible. Thus, I created a
 helmet pattern with the correct proportions, but it was certainly challenging
 to fit it between the shoulder pads.
Crafting the helmet
What sorts of materials do you typically use in your
 cosplay builds?
Most often I use EVA foam of different thicknesses, leather,
 different types of Worbla, molded plastic, and epoxy. For makeup details, such
 as elf ears, I use latex. For some elements I use 3D and photopolymer printing.
