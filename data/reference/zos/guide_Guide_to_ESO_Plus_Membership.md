# Guide to ESO Plus Membership

Source: https://www.elderscrollsonline.com/en-us/guides/esoplusguide
Tier: guide
Published: unknown

ESO Plus™ membership provides The Elder Scrolls Online players with a host of unique in-game benefits and perks, including full access to all DLC in the Crown Store, 1650 monthly crowns, unlimited storage for crafting materials, and more. Check out this guide for more information on all the benefits you can enjoy with ESO Plus membership!
WHAT IS ESO PLUS?
ESO Plus membership is a special program that gives you access to loads of in-game benefits and perks, including:
Seasonal Benefits
Earn +10% Tome Points from Challenges, as well as 1 Cache of Tome Points and 1 Premium Tome Token per month.
Store Benefits
Gain 1,650 Crowns per month and access to exclusive Crown Store and Gold Coast Bazaar offerings.
DLC & Companions Access
Enjoy full access to all DLC available in the Crown Store, including two free Companions and their stories.
Increased Progression
Earn +10% to Experience, Gold, Alliance Points (currency, PvP skill lines, and Alliance Rank only), Tel Var Stones (monster kills only), Archival Fortunes acquisition, Crafting Inspiration, and Trait Research speed.
Storage Benefits
Make use of doubled furnishing and collectible space in your homes, doubled Bank space for your account, and access to the Furnishing Vault.
Crafting & Cosmetic Benefits
Take advantage of exclusive unlimited storage for crafting materials with the Craft Bag, doubled currency cap for Transmutation Crystals, and the exclusive ability to dye costumes.
Antiquities Benefits
Gain doubled Antiquities timer lengths, up to one year for Ultimate Leads.
You can sign up for either a 1-, 3-, 6-, or 12-month membership, and you can find more information on each unique bonus or perk below.
Seasonal Benefits
Members also receive 10% increased Tome Points earned from completing challenges. Rewards in Tamriel Tomes are redeemed with these points, meaning ESO Plus allows you to unlock what you want faster. Additionally, for each month included in your membership period, you will receive a Cache of Tome Points (including 2,000 Tome Points in each) which you can open at your discretion to apply to a currently active Tome of your choice. This could allow you to immediately claim rewards from a new Tome at the start of a Season or help you save up for something of high value. For example, if you join ESO Plus with a six-month membership, when that period begins, you receive six Caches of Tome Points that you can use during any Season for any Tome you have available.
As an added bonus, every time you begin or renew a membership period, you’ll receive a number of Premium Tome Tokens equal to the number of months in that membership. For example, if you started a new 6-month membership, or an existing membership renewed for another 6 months, you will immediately receive 6 Premium Tokens.  When you have accumulated 12 Premium Tokens you can spend them to immediately unlock a Premium + Bonuses Tamriel Tome at no additional cost through the Upgrade Tome Page in-game.
Note that seasonal ESO Plus benefits begin with the launch of Season Zero on April 2, 2026. New memberships from March 9, 2026 onwards will receive their seasonal currencies immediately, while those with active memberships between January 1, 2026 and March 9, 2026 will have their currencies retroactively awarded on March 30, 2026. In both cases, the currencies will not be spendable until the Season Zero launch.
Store Benefits
As an ESO Plus member, you'll receive 1650 crowns for each month of your membership to spend in the in-game Crown Store. With crowns, you can purchase convenience and cosmetic items such as mounts, costumes, pets, items, and more.
Is there a fancy costume or fierce mount you've got your eye on? Grab it with your ESO Plus bonus crowns.
You'll receive all of your due crowns instantly and upfront. For example, if you purchased a 6-month membership, you will receive 9900 crowns to spend in the Crown Store as soon as you log into the game, rather than receiving 1650 crowns every month for the duration of the membership period.
Additionally, ESO Plus membership grants access to exclusive Crown Store deals, including sales, free items, unique deals and more. There are also unique ESO Plus offers in the Gold Coast Bazaar. The items available can change often (and some might only be available as part of a limited-time offer), so be sure to check the ESO Plus tab within the Crown Store UI and within the Gold Coast Bazaar on occasion to see what is available. ESO Plus-only sales and free or exclusive items will be indicated by gold text and the Chalice icon.
DLC & Companions Access
With an ESO Plus membership, you'll have full access to all DLC  currently available in the in-game Crown Store. This means you can enjoy all additional DLC zones and dungeonsf without having to spend any crowns! You can play every one of over twenty released DLC  right now.
If your ESO Plus membership expires, you will lose access to the DLC that you haven't purchased with crowns, but you will not lose any of the items, achievements, gold, or experience you earned while playing them. That rare armor set that you earned for completing Shipwright’s Regret? Don't worry, it's yours for life.
Note: Non-ESO Plus members can still gain access to the DLC by purchasing them through the in-game Crown Store.
There's even other Crown Store unlocks stories that become available with ESO Plus beyond DLCs. The Companion characters Tanlorin and Zerith-var can be recruited by any player after making a purchase in the Crown Store. However, with an active ESO Plus membership, you can claim these two Companions in the Crown Store at no cost. Once the Crown Store purchase, free or otherwise, has been made, you retain access to the recruitment quests for these unique Companions. Even if your ESO Plus membership lapses.
Note that other Companions in the game c still require you to unlock them in their original zones..
Increased Progression
To help you make the most out of your time in Tamriel, ESO Plus members receive a host of bonuses to their in-game character progression. This includes:
10% increase to Experience gains
10% increase to Gold gains
10% increase to Crafting Inspiration gains
10% increase to Trait Research rates
10% increase to Archival Fortunes Acquisition
10% increase to Tel Var Stones earned from defeating monsters in the Imperial City
10% to Alliance Points gained for the purposes of currency earned, PvP skill rank ups, and Alliance War rank increases
With these bonuses, your character will acquire levels and riches faster than ever before.
Note that while players are an ever-present danger in the Imperial City, you do not increase the Tel Var stones you receive for defeating them. You also cannot improve your leaderboard status in a PvP or earn additional Rewards for the Worthy with the Alliance Point gains. You’ll have to get those with grit and courage!
If you lose access to your ESO Plus membership, don't worry. Any progress made and unspent currency gained using the bonuses during the time of membership will remain.
Storage Benefits
Wish you could add just one more Trophy to your home's mantelpiece in ESO? You're in luck: an ESO Plus membership also grants you double the space for home furnishings, allowing you to decorate your abode to your heart's content!
If your membership is no longer active, the items will remain in your homes, but you will not be able to add new ones until you are back under the furnishings cap.
Of course, we know there's no such thing as “too much free space" when it comes to hoarding items. Luckily, ESO Plus membership also doubles the size of your account's bank space. Now you can store all those weapons, pieces of armor, and – of course – those delicious Sweet Rolls, without hassle.
If you choose to cancel your ESO Plus membership, your bank space will revert to the standard size. You will not lose any items that put you over the bank storage cap, but you will not be able to store new items until space is available.
While we’re on the topic of storage, it’s worth remembering that when you have an ESO Plus membership, you are able to hold twice as many Transmute Crystals as you would otherwise. This means you can save up more crystals between trips to the Transmute Station.
If you lose ESO Plus membership, you are able to retain or spend any crystals you have at the time, but you will not be able to acquire new ones until you are back under your normal limit.
Crafting and Cosmetic Benefits
To help you manage your crafting materials, an ESO Plus membership gives you access to the Craft Bag, a special storage option that allows you to store nearly infinite amounts of crafting materials, freeing up space in your inventory and bank.
When you log into the game with a Craft Bag, your crafting materials are instantly moved to the bag, and they are automatically removed from the bag when crafting. To access your Craft Bag, navigate to the Craft Bag icon while viewing your inventory.
If you choose to cancel your ESO Plus membership, you will still retain your Craft Bag and everything stored inside of it. However, you will not be able to add new materials to the bag while your membership remains inactive.
ESO Plus members also have the exclusive ability to dye their costumes at dye stations, allowing you to truly show off your unique style. (It's a surefire way to get whispers of adoration.)
Non-ESO Plus members are only able to dye regular pieces of armor. If your ESO Plus membership is no longer active, your costumes will remain dyed.
Antiquities Benefits
When chasing leads for the antiquities system, sometimes you can’t track down everything right away. Fortunately, ESO Plus helps you stay on top of what you’ve uncovered with doubled duration for lead timer. You’ll have up to a year to uncover the rarest leads, so even the most elusive treasures can be tracked down at your leisure.
How Do I Sign Up
Easy! To acquire an ESO Plus membership, head over to the
ESO Plus
web page. From there, you can choose from membership options based on your platform. You can also find ESO Plus in the Steam Store (if your ESO account is Steam-enabled), PlayStation Store, and Xbox Store. You can also purchase ESO Plus membership via the ESO Plus menu in the in-game Crown Store (it will take you to the appropriate store).
An ESO Plus membership offers a fantastic selection of in-game benefits and perks. If you have any questions or concerns about ESO Plus membership, please don't hesitate to contact ESO Customer Support for further help.
