# Guide to Master Writs

Source: https://www.elderscrollsonline.com/en-us/guides/masterwrits
Tier: guide
Published: unknown

You're a crafter that's reached the pinnacle of your skills. What's there to do now beyond making items for your guildmates and friends? Master Writs are for you, and you can learn more about these exclusive contracts in this guide!
What are Master Writs?
Master Writs are mysterious, exclusive contracts for high-quality and very specific equipment, or carefully formulated alchemical substances, enchantments, or provisions. They take the form of special inventory items that can be traded to other players or consumed to start a quest. The quests granted by Master Writs award Writ Vouchers, a special currency valued by crafters.
Where do I get Master Writs from?
Master Writs can occasionally be found in the rewards from finishing a maximum tier, regular
crafting Writ
.
Who can do Master Writs?
Not just anyone! Master Writs are meant specifically for masters – they require specific sets, crafting styles, recipes, or research that may be rare or difficult to obtain. Further, Master Writs are only awarded to players completing the highest-rank normal crafting Writs, and they are more common when you have proven your dedication to your craft.
For example, Blacksmithing, Woodworking, Jewelry Crafting, and Clothier Master Writs are more common if you've researched many traits and fully learned many motifs; Provisioning Master Writs are more common when you've learned a lot of high-quality recipes; Enchanting Master Writs are more common when you've translated a lot of runes; and Alchemy Master Writs are more common when you've fully identified a lot of alchemical reagents. The more you've done in that tradeskill, the better your odds on each normal Writ you complete.
How do I complete Master Writs?
Pay close attention to the requested items. You'll need to make the items yourself, on the character that has the Master Writ quest. Requested items cannot be traded between characters to satisfy these, and the items cannot be bound. Note that consuming the Master Writ to begin the quest is not reversible—once the item is used, it cannot be recovered, even by abandoning the quest. Be confident you can craft the requested item described by the Master Writ on the tooltip!
How many can I do per day?
As many as you have the quest starter items for. There is no daily limit or cooldown on Master Writs, although you must make sure to have materials ready and relevant tradeskill knowledge for each one.
Additionally, you can trade Master Writs around to people who are more likely or able to complete them. You could even get them on your alternate characters and trade them back to your 'main crafter' if you have one or host a festival with friends to trade Master Writs all day.
Be sure to stock up!
What are Writ Vouchers? What can I buy with them?
Writ Vouchers are a type of currency that Rolis Hlaalu, the Mastercraft Mediator present in Alliance capitals near writ turn-in areas, pays master crafters in for their work. Rolis is an unusual merchant and sells a very specific and select roster of goods. He accepts Writ Vouchers in exchange for his goods such as the Ebony crafting motif, Target Skeletons for practicing combat skills, epic and legendary furnishing plans, or the recipe for high-quality bonus experience drinks. There are many more items available for Writ Vouchers, most of which are tied to the process of crafting.
Additionally, Rolis Hlaalu is accompanied by his assistant Faustina Curio at all locations where he can be found. Faustina also accepts Master Writs and has a different stock, including items Rolis himself previously carried, discounted now that they no longer fit his cutting-edge needs. Many of her goods, whether past offerings of her mentor or otherwise, require specific achievements to be able to purchase them.
Can Master Writ Vouchers themselves be traded/sold to other players?
No. Writ Vouchers are personal, for you and you alone to spend. However, you can store them in your personal bank to transfer them between characters. Additionally, you may trade the Master Writs themselves before they are opened and consumed to begin a quest. You can swap Master Writs with friends to make sure everyone has the skills they need for their specific requests!
Can the items I get from turning in Master Writs be traded/sold?
Many such items can be sold, although not all of them. If you’ve already purchased everything you desire from Rolis Hlaalu or his assistant, you can use your own vouchers to buy things for others, or to resell on the Guild Trader network for profit. However, you might want to stockpile them for future additions to the game. Additionally, you should check the tooltips of each item to be sure of its tradability. Some examples of untradable items available for Writ Vouchers include collectibles such as Storage Containers for your homes, while tradeable items include crafting materials such as unusual Style or Trait items like Night Pumice and Gilding Wax.
What can I do with the items I get for completing Master Writs?
You can sell them if you want or use them yourself. You could outfit all of your characters in the very stylish Ebony motif. You could fill your home with crafting stations attuned to every craftable item set in Tamriel, just to be able to craft anything at home. You could build an army of Target Skeletons right inside the door to your home. You could make improved experience drinks – Aetherial Ambrosia – for yourself and your friends and guild. Or you could sell that Ambrosia for fun and profit.
Will you be adding to the Master Writ system in future updates?
As we add new motifs, traits, and crafted sets, they eventually make their way to the Master Writ rotation of requested goods. And, as we add new features, tools and décor to the player housing system, you can expect the inventory of items available for Writ Vouchers to grow and change. One such update that has occurred many times already is the addition of new unique furnishing patterns to Rolis Hlaalu’s inventory as his previous offerings move to that of his assistant. Many other items have been added to his or Faustina’s inventory over time and will continue to be added in future updates.
