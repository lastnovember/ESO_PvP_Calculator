# Necrom Chapter & Update 38 Now Live on the Public Test Server!

Source: https://www.elderscrollsonline.com/en-us/news/post/64015
Tier: combat-news
Published: unknown

Help us test ESO’s next big Chapter and update by logging
 into the PC/Mac Public Test Server today!
The Elder Scrolls Online: Necrom
arrives June 5 for
 PC/Mac and June 20 for Xbox and PlayStation consoles, but you can get a first
 taste of the adventure to come AND help us make the game better by logging into
 the PC/Mac Public Test Server (PTS) right now.
To access the PTS environment, go to the Settings menu
 within the PC/Mac ESO launcher by toggling the “Show Public Test Environment”
 option. You can then download and access the test environment. Be sure to leave
 feedback and report any bugs you come across by typing “/feedback” or “/bug” in
 chat!
Once you log into the PTS, you’ll be able to explore almost
 everything the
Necrom
Chapter and Update 38 base-game patch has to
 offer. This includes the opportunity to explore the new Telvanni Peninsula and
 Apocrypha zones, delve deeper into the Shadow Over Morrowind storyline, and
 even playtest the all-new Arcanist class.
The PTS is open to any PC/Mac
ESO
players, regardless
 of whether they have pre-purchased
Necrom
or not, so if you’d like to
 get your first look at the upcoming Chapter and more, be sure to jump in!
 Finally, now that the test server is live, you can check out the PTS Patch
 Notes on the
official
 ESO forums
.
