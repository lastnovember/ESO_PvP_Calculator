# Test the Prince of Madness on the Update 50 PTS!

Source: https://www.elderscrollsonline.com/en-us/news/post/69737
Tier: combat-news
Published: unknown

New content and changes come to Update 50 on the PTS.
A new update has hit the PC/Mac Public Test Server, bringing
 with it new content and features to check out. This week new story content
 involving the Daedric Prince Sheogorath is available, as well as updates to
 Whitestrake’s Mayhem. Additionally, the Path to Adventure Golden Pursuit for
 new and returning early-game players will be active and visible for all who log
 into PTS this week for broader visibility and feedback.
To access the PTS environment, go to the Settings menu
 within the PC/Mac
ESO
launcher by toggling the “Show Public
 Test Environment” option. You can then download and access the test
 environment. Don’t forget to leave feedback and report any bugs you come across
 by typing “/feedback” or “/bug” in chat.
For more about Update 50, please read the
PTS Patch Notes
on the official
ESO
forums.
