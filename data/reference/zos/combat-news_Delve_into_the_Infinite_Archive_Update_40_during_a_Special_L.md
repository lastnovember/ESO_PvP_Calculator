# Delve into the Infinite Archive & Update 40 during a Special Livestream Preview

Source: https://www.elderscrollsonline.com/en-us/news/post/64615
Tier: combat-news
Published: unknown

Get ready to tune in to Twitch.tv/Bethesda as we reveal the  new challenges and base-game additions coming soon to The Elder Scrolls Online.
Looking to learn more about the Infinite Archive and upcoming  base-game updates? The New Feature & Update Preview: Infinite Archive stream  will go live on
Twitch.tv/Bethesda
on  Thursday, September 14 at 3PM EDT.
During the show, Senior Community Manager  Gina Bruno will be joined by Creative Director Rich Lambert and Lead Encounter  Designer Mike Finnigan to dive deep into the new Infinite Archive PvE activity and reveal some of the major changes arriving with the  Update 40 base-game patch. This will be your first opportunity to see Infinite Archive gameplay, so don’t miss out!

to be able to earn  the new Tentacular Tome emote!
