# Fallen Banners & Update 45 Now Live on All Platforms

Source: https://www.elderscrollsonline.com/en-us/news/post/67679
Tier: combat-news
Published: unknown

Defend an Elder Scroll and experience a host of changes  and additions to the game with the Fallen Banners dungeon pack and Update 45  base-game patch.
The
Fallen Banners
dungeon pack and free Update 45 base-game patch are now live on PC/Mac, Xbox One, Xbox Series X|S, PlayStation 4, and PlayStation 5. In this release, you can save Cyrodiil from plunging  further into chaos with two new dungeons and experience a host of changes and  quality-of-life additions.
Fallen Banners  dungeon pack
You can get access to the
Fallen Banners
dungeon pack  at launch via
ESO Plus™ membership
. Note that alternative means of acquiring it will be  revealed as part of the upcoming ESO Direct on April 10.
Fallen Banners
Gather your party for two challenging new PvE dungeon  experiences: Lep Seclusa and Exiled Redoubt. Unlock new achievements and earn  new rewards including item sets, collectibles, and more as you fight to  preserve what little peace remains in the war-torn region of Cyrodiil.
Lep Seclusa
The Moth Priests of Lep Seclusa are besieged, and the  cunning Orpheon the Tactician is set on seizing the power of the Elder Scroll  they safeguard. You must help Sister Chana defend the sanctuary against an army  of deserters, protect its people and secrets, and confront Orpheon before he  can use the power of the scroll to forever change the face of Cyrodiil.

Those who brave Lep Seclusa and the Exiled Redoubt also have

Getting Started
At launch, you can access the
Fallen Banners
dungeon
 pack with an ESO Plus membership. If your ESO Plus membership lapses, you will
 lose access to the DLC until you renew your membership or acquire it by other
 means.
Note that while at launch ESO Plus membership is the only
 way to access the dungeon pack, there will be additional ways to acquire it in
 the future—stay tuned for more information during the April ESO Direct.
Once you have access to
Fallen Banners
, you can dive
 right into the two new dungeons via the following methods:
By teleporting directly into the two dungeons from your map
By using the Dungeon Finder tool
By visiting their specific dungeon entrances within the Hew's
 Bane (Lep Seclusa) and West Weald (Exiled Redoubt) zones
As a base-game patch, Update 45 will be automatically
 downloaded by your game clients when it launches.
Update 45 Base-Game Patch
Arriving in tandem with the dungeon pack is the Update 45
 base-game patch, a free update for all
ESO
players that features
 additions, bugfixes, and improvements to the game.
Starter Zone Refresh
To help keep
ESO
as welcoming to new (and returning)
 players as possible, the game’s starter zones have received a visual refresh,
 bringing them closer to the quality of more recent additions. Bleakrock Isle,
 Bal Foyen, Stros M’kai, Betnikh, and Khenarthi’s Roost have each received
 improvements to their lighting, enhanced ground and water textures, updated
 structures, and the addition of more clutter or props.
Discover updated  starter zones
Cyrodiil Champions PC/Mac Test
ESO
’s Alliance War veterans can help the team test  future changes and iterations on the game’s open-world PvP with a PC/Mac-only  test called Cyrodiil Champions. With the goal of improving stability and  performance in Cyrodiil (and perhaps other environments), look for a special  Alliance War campaign called Vengeance to participate from March 24-31, 2025.
General Changes and Improvements
Along with the above, Update 45 also boosts mount starting speed,  overhauls the Craft constellation to ease micromanagement, and introduces a  general UI cleanup of the in-game map to help improve readability. It also moves  the game’s disguises out of the inventory and into your collections and  improves the visibility of Mundus Stone boons—phew!
