Scribing for All!
The Scribing for All Golden Pursuits campaign is now live
 and
runs until August 7, 2025
. During the campaign period, you can
 unlock a host of in-game rewards via Scribing and other in-game activities.
Don’t have access to Scribing? Never fear! Scribing is now
 free for all
ESO
players.
Visit this blog
to find out how to claim Scribing for free and get started.
During this Golden Pursuits campaign, you can unlock a host
 of special rewards by completing select activities. These rewards include:
·      
 Complete one activity—Affix Curated Script
·      
 Complete three activities—Focus Curated Script
·      
 Complete six activities—Signature Curated Script
·      
 Complete nine activities—20 Luminous Ink
·      
 Complete 10 activities—50,000 Gold
·      
 Complete 11 activities—Painting: 10-Year Anniversary furnishing
·      
 Complete 13 activities—Blade Cloak, Mirrormoor Skill Style
·      
 Complete 16 activities—Scribing Altar furnishing

 in July
You might recognize the 10-Year Anniversary painting that we

 wallpaper

You can complete a total of 26 different activities to reach
 the above milestone rewards. Activities include completing quests (including those
 from the Scribing questline), using abilities with active Skill Styles or
 Scribed Abilities, acquiring Grimoires, and more.
Even better, some specific activities grant additional
 rewards, including Crown Crates, Luminous Ink, and another Skill Style! Log in
 now to see the full list of activities and rewards within the "Golden
 Pursuits" section of the Activity Finder.
