# An Update on the Warden and Class Refresh

Source: https://www.elderscrollsonline.com/en-us/news/post/70311
Tier: combat-news
Published: unknown

The ESO development team shares an update on the ongoing
 Warden and Class refresh work.
Over the past year, we've shared updates on our
 Class Identity and the Refresh initiative, including changes
 to Dragonknight as well as Werewolf abilities. Along the
 way, we've also talked about our plans for the Warden and
 the remaining classes. Today, we want to provide an update on where the
 Warden refresh stands, explain why our plans have shifted, and
 what we're working toward.
Warden Timeline Update
Since we first announced our plans for the Class
 Refresh, we've made several adjustments for how we
 approach these ability updates. As we worked through the
 Dragonknight and Werewolf, it became clear that the level of iteration and
 community feedback we received was extraordinarily important, though the
 iteration loop can add time to our development cycle. We
 still believe in sharing updates early, and that iteration is critical. When
 combined with the July restructuring at ZOS, we hope you understand why we've
 had to shift our timelines and goals.
We know many of you are curious
 about when the Warden abilities will
 be refreshed, and we are still working
 hard to determine when we can get it into your
 hands. Rest assured that planning and work on the
 Warden is still underway, though the
recent studio letter
from Nick Giacomini still reflects our current roadmap.
What We're Working Towards
Our goals
 for the Warden abilities haven't changed in light
 of these constraints.
From the beginning, we set out to strengthen the class
 fantasy and better connect the gameplay to themes that
 make the Warden feel unique. We want the class to feel more
 cohesive, more distinctive, and more rewarding to play.
That includes preserving the pillars
 that people already love about the class:
Nature magic
Animals
Ice-themed abilities
Clear identity as protector of the
 natural world
These themes remain central to the
 class. What we're refining is how those themes connect together and are
 expressed through gameplay. Our goal isn't to
 reinvent the Warden; it's to build upon the
 foundation that's already there and make it
 feel more distinctive.
As part of the Warden refresh, we'll be looking at
 the following problem spaces:
The Warden currently lacks a unified visual identity and
 theme.
The Warden’s skill lines are role-centralized, reducing
 their effectiveness when attempting to utilize their entire
 kit.
Wardens lack a unique mechanic to their kit that all roles can
 engage with.
The Warden was also released alongside the
Morrowind
Chapter
 and was heavily themed to align with it. With this class's
 refresh, we are pivoting away from the Morrowind-specific visual
 theming to better represent the natural world across all regions in
 Tamriel, which is based on feedback we’ve also
 received from the community.
To tackle the issues mentioned above, the team plans
 to:
Ensure that the class's abilities reflect all playstyles and are
 present in every skill line
Provide more interesting gameplay loops
 and strengthen the mechanical feel
Ensure the Warden’s core visual themes are all
 preserved, while also spreading those visual themes across abilities for
 every playstyle
We'll share more information about the exact adjustments and solutions to
 those problem spaces as we continue to work through development.
Exploring a Stronger Seasonal Identity
One of the most exciting directions we're pursuing
 with the Warden refresh is a stronger tie to nature’s
 seasonal rhythm (spring, summer, fall, and winter). Rather than treating
 different class themes as separate
 experiences, we're exploring ways to weave them together through
 gameplay that connects nature’s seasons, animals, and frost into a unified
 whole.
The underlying vision is to create a Warden that
 feels more connected, thematic, and rewarding to master.

 words. And we know you want to see how this refresh is taking shape.

 to offer you a glimpse into the ideas and

What's Happening in the Meantime
The Warden refresh is not the only combat work taking place
 in
ESO,
and we’re still monitoring game
 balance, reviewing player feedback, and making combat
 adjustments when needed and as time allows. For example,
 you may have seen our
recent ability adjustments
during the U51 PTS based on
 your feedback. You can continue to expect balance updates and improvements
 where appropriate. The Warden refresh represents a larger effort
 that requires additional development support and time, but
 that doesn't mean combat support stops while we work toward that
 goal.
Where We Are Today
As we've continued to refine our approach to class
 updates, we've learned that delivering meaningful changes takes time.
 Incorporating feedback, iterating on designs, and ensuring updates meet our
 goals has shaped how we approach projects like the Warden refresh.
Changes to our development plans this
 year required us to revisit portions of our roadmap and adjust
 timelines. We're still working through some of these adjustments,
 and the Warden refresh is one of them. Because of
 that, we're not ready to share a release date today. What we can
 share is that development is continuing, the
 vision remains intact, and we're committed to bringing this
 refresh to you when it's ready.
In addition to the timeline change for Warden, we also want
 to share that our previously released roadmap laid out
 that the Sorcerer refresh would follow the Warden
 class. To get the classes out as quickly and effectively as we’re able
 to, we’ve also made the decision to move Templar ahead of Sorcerer.
 We know this may disappoint some of you and
 understand your frustration for a longer timeline for the Sorcerer
 rework.
The passion the community has for the classes has
 been evident throughout this process. Your feedback,
 discussions, theories, and questions have all helped shape how
 we are thinking about the future of the Warden and
 the rest of
ESO
’s classes. We're excited about
 the direction we’re headed, and we can't wait to
 share more as development progresses.
Thank you for being a part of this journey,
