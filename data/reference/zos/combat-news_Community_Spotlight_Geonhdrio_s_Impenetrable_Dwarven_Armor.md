# Community Spotlight – Geonhdrio’s Impenetrable Dwarven Armor

Source: https://www.elderscrollsonline.com/en-us/news/post/62683
Tier: combat-news
Published: unknown

Talented cosplayer and ESO Fan Geonhdrio returns to share
 his latest mechanical masterpiece—the Dwarven Refabricated Heavy Armor!
Hello, and thank you so much for once again joining us! You’ve
 crafted some incredible
ESO objects in the past
, including the Dwarven spider, a Clockwork skeevaton,
 and more. What is it about the Elder Scrolls series that inspires you to create
 such intricate builds?
What I really appreciate in the
Elder Scrolls
series
 is the amount of incredible, meticulous details contained within the lore and
 the overall aesthetic of the world it takes place in. Even the smallest of
 details have been carefully thought through. As a crafter, that challenges me
 to create these sorts of replicas, with the goal in mind to make them as close
 as possible to the original design, bringing these creations to life.
Geonhdrio in full
 gear
You are clearly passionate for one of the Elder Scrolls’
 most mysterious races, the Dwemer. What is it about them that appeals to you so
 much?
The Dwemer are not only one of the most enigmatic of races
 that once walked Tamriel, but they are also revolutionary inventors and masters
 in technology, engineering, and craftsmanship. As a person with a passion for
 Steampunk aesthetics, I was very intrigued by the Dwemer’s steam-powered mechanisms,
 copper/brass aesthetic, and their peculiar eye for detail that makes their
 creations not only functional but also a greatly artistic.
Why did you choose the Refabricated Heavy Armor as your
 next big cosplay project? Did you happen acquire it from the Halls of
 Fabrication trial?
When I first saw this armor set, I instantly knew I wanted
 to recreate it as a wearable cosplay. To be honest, I actually purchased the armor
 style when it was available in the Crown Store, haha! I was already working on
 this armor by the time I was able to dive into the Trial.
The Refabricated Heavy Armor is immensely ornate and
 detailed—how did you go about planning for such a big project?
There actually wasn’t really much planning that preceded tackling
 this outfit. Honestly, I just started working on one piece after another and decided
 to see where it would go next. For me, that’s the big part of the adventure and
 the challenge of creating and progressing along the way.
The Refabricated
 Heavy Armor up close
How did you go about forging the arms and armor?
First, I took a couple of different screenshots from
 different angles as a reference. Next, I had to consider which materials I
 wanted to use. After all, I would be wearing the full get-up, and so I wanted it
 to be comfortable. I started conceptualizing templates, creating and adjusting
 each piece step by step. The main material I used during this process was EVA foam.
Foam cut-outs for
 the armor
How did you give the cosplay that Dwarven bronze finish?
I find that metal paint usually pops out better on a black
 base, so I started out by coloring the full armor set black. I then wanted to
 go for an antique gold color, so I gave the outfit three layers of this color
 to match the exact look that I wanted. After the gold was finished, I wanted to
 apply a more weathered look, so I dry-brushed all of the pieces a couple of
 times with a dark brown ink paint.
Painting the armor
The entire outfit you’ve created is extremely impressive,
 but, if you had to choose, is there one specific aspect of this build that
 you’re especially proud of?
I think I am most proud of how the shield turned out. I
 constructed the shield so it’s magnetically attachable to the back of the chest
 armor while keeping the magnets concealed. It allows me to keep the shield
 attached on my back while I am moving in the outfit, but still easily
 detachable whenever I want to wield it.
Did you encounter any unexpected challenges that you worked
 to overcome as you constructed this cosplay?
The biggest challenge for me with this build was to assure the
 outfit was comfortable enough to wear while also achieving the aesthetic I
 desired. It was a matter of putting the pieces on, seeing how well it fits or
 doesn’t, taking it off, adjusting, and repeating the whole process until I got
 it just right.
Do you have any advice for your fellow cosplayers who
 wish to craft an outfit of their own?
No matter your skill level, be patient and take your time.
 Do not be overly focused on the final result and make sure to enjoy the
 adventure along the way! When you enjoy creating something, it is already an
 amazing cosplay!
