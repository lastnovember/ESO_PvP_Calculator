# Infinite Archive & Update 40 Now Available on the PC/Mac Public Test Server!

Source: https://www.elderscrollsonline.com/en-us/news/post/64751
Tier: combat-news
Published: unknown

Join us on the Public Test Server to try out the new  Inifnite Archive PvE activity and Update 40 base-game patch!
The Update 40 base-game patch is now live on the Public Test  Server (PTS) and is accessible via the PC/Mac ESO launcher. This base-game  update, free to all players, includes the
Infinite Archive PvE activity
. Want to help us test Update 40 and this new dungeon-like  challenge? Log in to the PTS!
To access the PTS, open the Settings menu located within the  PC/Mac ESO launcher and click on the “Show Public Test Environment.” Once  you’ve downloaded everything, you’ll be able to access the testing server. While  you’re exploring the PTS, don’t forget to leave feedback and report any bugs  you come across by typing “/feedback” or “/bug” through the in-game chat.
If you want to learn more about the new challenges that await  you in within the infinite halls of the Infinite Archive or anything else  arriving with the Update 40 patch, be sure to check out our recent preview

forums.
