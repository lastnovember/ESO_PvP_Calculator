# Earn Greater Rewards & Glory with the New PvP Veterancy System

Source: https://www.elderscrollsonline.com/en-us/news/post/69891
Tier: combat-news
Published: unknown

Make your mark in PvP with the new Veterancy progression
 system coming soon with Update 50!
Every form of PvP combat in
The Elder Scrolls Online
is about to get more rewarding when Update 50 arrives June 8, 2026. Part of
 Season Zero, this patch brings a new feature: the Veterancy system. Releasing
 alongside a permanent Vengeance campaign (but not limited to it), Veterancy is a
 new progression system for you to advance through as you battle in PvP modes like
 the Alliance War, Imperial City, and Battlegrounds.
Become a Veteran
Veterancy advances in ranks from one to 100 with rewards at
 every step and a repeatable reward available after max rank. You can advance
 your Veterancy rank by gaining Alliance Points or Experience Points while in a Cyrodiil
 Alliance War campaign, in a Battlegrounds match, or while adventuring within the
 Imperial City. Note that you’ll progress fastest by earning AP, however, earning
 XP by completing quests and slaying monsters in Cyrodiil or its capital will still
 move you up the ranks!
Battle your fellow
 players to earn Veterancy rank
PvP seasons are distinct from the Seasonal content cadence
 for the game overall, and Veterancy ranks will reset roughly every six months,
 typically allowing you to claim the rewards for six “30 day” Cyrodiil Campaigns
 (depending on patch cycle timing in relation to campaign ending timings). When the
 PvP season resets, your rank returns to 1 and temporary titles (more on these
 below) go away, but you will keep all other rewards you’ve earned and get a
 whole new batch to strive for.
You can view your rank and all earned or earnable Veterancy
 rewards for the current PvP season in a new tab within your menus, accessible
 from the Campaign Selection/Scoring UI.
Spoils of War
There are all sorts of rewards from Veterancy, including currencies,
 items, and collectibles. Veterancy also offers a substantial number of Trade
 Bars each PvP season for use in the Gold Coast Bazaar. Collectibles include new
 weapon and armor styles, colorful new skill styles, and more.
PvP Veterancy
 rewards
Additionally, some rewards are earned specifically from your
 Veterancy progress. If you’re interested in playing in the Vengeance campaign, you
 can unlock new loadout perks via Veterancy ranks, with the full set unlocking
 early to maintain a level playing field.
Finally, you can also gain special temporary titles for the
 duration of a PvP season that display how far you’ve climbed through the ranks.
 These titles display on your nameplate when you’re in a PvP area, letting other
 players know to watch out for your prowess (and letting you assess them in
 return).
