# Update 47 Preview—Base Game Improvements

Source: https://www.elderscrollsonline.com/en-us/news/post/68585
Tier: combat-news
Published: unknown

Check out the improvements coming to ESO’s base game with
 Update 47, from swimming mounts to stackable surveys!
Update 47 is nearly here, arriving August 18 on PC/Mac and
 September 3 for Xbox and PlayStation consoles. The base game update is free for
 all players and includes a variety of improvements and new features.
Lead Your Horse (or any Mount) to Water
Starting in Update 47, your mounts will be able to swim
 anywhere you can. No more getting dismounted in watery zones like Shadowfen or
 Vvardenfell. You can ride them across inconvenient rivers or even into the
 ocean—just be mindful of slaughterfish!
“Mount Swimming is a function that we've wanted to
 incorporate for a long time, so you can continue your journey without being
 dismounted in waist-high water,” says Lead Animator Ed Lynch. “We're excited to
 finally bring this improvement to every mount in
ESO
, past and future.”
This quality-of-life improvement comes with custom
 animations, sounds, and more. Ever wondered how a Dwemer spider swims? Take one
 out on the water—you might be surprised!
Traverse Tamriel’s
 waterways
“When showing people a quick prototype after poking around
 some of the swimming and physics code, I thought it would be as simple as just
 not dismounting when in water. However, when we got to work in earnest that was
 just the tip of the iceberg,” recounts Gameplay Engineer Jose Camacho. “We had
 to account for supporting new styles of animations, transitions, sound, VFX,
 and all the things I didn't even start to think about with just the prototype.”
And when we say all mounts can swim, we mean it—ALL mounts.
“The Hobby horse is always one of those mounts that has to
 get special treatment and always needs unique solutions to make it work,” notes
 Senior Animator Radacal Alexander. “But I don’t mind since that was the mount I
 made!”
Hang onto Every Word
The tremendous amount of high-quality voice acting across
ESO
’s
 content helps bring Tamriel to life. If you’re the sort of player who hates to
 miss a word of it, then you’ll appreciate the most recent update to
ESO’
s
 NPC conversations.
“We've added a button that will replay the most recently
 said voiceover from the NPC you are conversing with. The main inspiration for
 this came from a forum post I saw,” shares Senior UI Engineer Seth Leyens. “The
 tech for this also allowed us to implement screen narration for conversations
 without us having to worry about the narration and voiceover overlapping, which
 is something that had been on my accessibility wish list for a while. We were
 able to kill two birds with one stone!”
Enjoy fascinating
 conversations
Now you don’t need to worry about accidentally skipping past
 the delivery of a biting quip or heartfelt plea due to a sudden lapse in
 attention or wayward cat's paw.
Survey Says Stackable
In Update 46, Master Writs began to arrive in stackable
 envelopes that helped reduce inventory clutter.
We previously
 mentioned
that the tech implemented for this feature was something we hoped
 would have more use in the future. Now, we’ve taken that functionality and
 applied it to Survey Reports, further streamlining inventories and reducing
 pressure on your bags.
“The overall idea is the same as master writs, though it's
 worth mentioning that these stackable survey reports are tradable!” shares
 Senior Systems Designer Matthew Bloise
“In the design we discussed whether or not we should bind
 them to the player and follow the same behavior, or if we should let them be
 sellable/tradable. It was generally agreed that this would be a good
 opportunity to let players create a market for them.”
A Change in Vision
The Infinite Archive has received an update to the Visions
 awarded within, allowing you to re-roll the options you’ve been awarded after
 defeating a boss.
“The new system is really targeted at both newer players and
 those familiar with Infinite Archive and how it works,” shares Lead Encounter
 Designer Mike Finnigan. “For newer players, you can spend those hard-earned
 Fortunes on a vision that’s more attractive to your current run and useful so
 you can potentially get further. For veterans of IA, you can fish for that
 special Vision you always love to see.”
The new system allows for a greater degree of control over
 your runs, if you know what you are looking for. Still, it always helps to be
 flexible—Hermaeus Mora does not yield his secrets without cost.
“Take a look and have a good idea of the visions available
 and which ones work best,” Finnigan says. “The costs start to increase pretty
 dramatically so having a good bank of Fortunes is beneficial. After that, it’s
 weighing the options and not letting ‘perfect’ options get in the way of ‘good’
 options.”
