Tier: combat-news
Published: unknown

ESO’s Scribing system is now part of the base game and
 free for all ESO players. Learn how you can claim it and create custom skills
 now.
Claim Scribing for Free
We are thrilled to announce that the Scribing system is now
 part of the
ESO
base game and free for all players. This includes access
 to the core system and its bespoke questline and rewards.
You can claim the Scribing bundle for free from the in-game
 Crown Store, listed under Upgrades. Once you have claimed the bundle, you will
 get the starter quest, titled “The Second Era of Scribing,” and can begin your
 Scribing journey. Get ready to delve deep into the origins of magic!
Find Adept Irnard
 Rirnil to begin!
Note that you
must be level 30
(or have reached the
 Champion System) to acquire the bundle. If you happen to drop the quest, don’t
 worry, you can always find it again within your Collections UI or under Quest
 Starters in the Crown Store. Upon completion, you will unlock the Scribing system
 questline and can begin to acquire and customize skills!
Customize Your Skills
With Scribing, you can master the precursor to what would
 eventually become spellmaking by acquiring grimoires and combining them with
 unique scripts. While grimoires provide the basic function of a skill, scripts
 allow you to change their various effects to meet your needs.
Note that to begin Scribing, you need a character to either
 have reached level 30 or have access to the Champion System. You can learn more
 about Scribing and exactly how it works in
this guide page
.
