Battle for Supremacy
The Cyrodiil Conquest
Golden  Pursuits campaign
is now live and will run until March 31 at 10AM ET. Battle  it out with your fellow players in the Alliance War to complete various PvP  in-game activities and you can earn up to 300,000 bonus Alliance Points!
Note that while this new Golden Pursuits campaign coincides  with the PC/Mac Cyrodiil Champions test, console players can still complete  these challenges and earn the bonus rewards for themselves, too.
Take a look at each milestone reward available to obtain below  and discover how many in-game activities you must successfully finish to unlock  them:
Complete one activity—60,000 Alliance Points
Complete four activities—30,000 Gold
Complete eight activities—240,000 Alliance Points
There are 12 different PvP-related activities available for  you to complete such as defeating foes, capturing territory in Cyrodiil and  more, so feel free to tackle whichever task you prefer!
