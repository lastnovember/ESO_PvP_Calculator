# Update 47 Preview—Feast on a New Dungeon Pack & More

Source: https://www.elderscrollsonline.com/en-us/news/post/68403
Tier: combat-news
Published: unknown

Two new dungeons, base-game additions, and more await
 Tamriel’s adventurers within Update 47.
The Update 47 patch helps continue your adventures in 2025
 by introducing a new dungeon pack and a series of free base-game additions and
 changes. This new patch and content
arrives August 18 on PC/Mac
and
September
 3 on Xbox and PlayStation consoles
.
Want to discover the dungeons and update for yourself? You
 can help us test the upcoming changes when Update 47 arrives on the PC/Mac
 Public Test Server (PTS) this Monday, July 7. If you missed the recent
preview livestream
or simply
 want to know more, check out what’s coming below.
Feast of Shadows
A new PvE dungeon pack,
Feast of Shadows
introduces
 two challenging new four-player dungeons for Tamriel’s adventurers to explore
 and overcome. Both dungeons feature their own complete stories and in-game
 rewards while tying thematically into the larger
Seasons of the Worm Cult
saga.
In the
Black Gem Foundry
, you and your party must
 venture into a Coldharbor manufactory and shut down their ability to craft
 terrifying soul-stealing armaments.
Venture into the
 Black Gem Foundry
In
Naj-Caldeesh
, a group of Argonian necromancers
 seek to corrupt and control an ancient xanmeer’s keystone, and it is up to you
 and your allies to stop them before they can complete their nefarious
 machinations.
Survive new  dangers in Naj-Caldeesh
Feast of Shadows
is included with the 2025 Content  Pass or 2025 Premium Edition,
now available
.  Alternatively, you can get access to
Feast of Shadows
with an ESO Plus™ membership or by acquiring it with crowns from the in-game Crown Store.
Base-Game Patch
Update 47 also features a host of free additions and changes  for all of
ESO
’s adventurers. This includes the long-awaited and  much-requested
Mount Swimming functionality
, finally dispelling the  extreme hydrophobia Tamriel’s mounts have suffered for years and allowing you  to remain mounted in water.
Take your rides
 for a swim!
In addition, Update 47 also introduces
Stackable Survey
 Reports
. Similar to Update 46’s stackable crafting writs, this change will
 allow you to ensure your survey reports don’t litter your inventories—freeing
 up more space for those harvested resources!
These are just two of Update 47’s highlights, so look out
 for additional details on this patch, including new functionality such as the
 ability to replay quest dialog, compass improvements, and more, in the coming months.
