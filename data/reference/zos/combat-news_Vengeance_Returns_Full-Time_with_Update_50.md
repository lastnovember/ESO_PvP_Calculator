# Vengeance Returns Full-Time with Update 50!

Source: https://www.elderscrollsonline.com/en-us/news/post/69939
Tier: combat-news
Published: unknown

Vengeance is back and here to
 stay as a permanent 30-day Cyrodiil campaign!
The Vengeance campaign has returned, but this time it’s here to stay! Starting upon the launch of Update
 50 on June 8, Vengeance will be available full-time as an Alliance War campaign.
 Each campaign lasts 30 days and features massive battles
 with up to 900 players, as well as modified abilities,
 loadouts, and unique perks. It’s time to
 once again take up arms, compete for supremacy, and advance
your Veterancy
 rank
!
Vengeance Basics
Haven’t taken part in a previous
 Vengeance campaign before? Sign up by following the steps below:
Navigate
 to the Alliance War menu
Either
 by pressing L on your keyboard or navigating directly via the UI
Select
 the Campaigns tab
Select
 Cyrodiil and then the Vengeance campaign
The Vengeance campaign is open to all players level 10-50
 and introduces a special ruleset to the Alliance War, templatizing player
 builds to help improve game stability and performance.
All players have normalized stats regardless of level.
Each class has a modified set of abilities and items.
Experience Points, Alliance Points, Gold, and Champion Points are
 retained upon leaving the campaign.
Items earned during the campaign labeled as “Vengeance” cannot be
 taken outside of the campaign. Items that aren’t labeled this way are retained
 upon leaving the campaign.
Your regular skill lines also gain experience upon leaving the
 campaign.
The Champion System is disabled in this campaign.
Upon entering the campaign, your character is changed to
 match a specific template for their class. As mentioned above, this includes
 predetermined abilities, attributes, consumables, and item sets. It also
 includes a backpack of supplies that cannot be removed from the campaign.
 Additionally, you will be given a choice between four loadouts, each altering
 your stats to suit your role in the campaign.
