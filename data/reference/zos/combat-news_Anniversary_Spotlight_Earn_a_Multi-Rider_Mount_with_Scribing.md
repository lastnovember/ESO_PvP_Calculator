# Anniversary Spotlight—Earn a Multi-Rider Mount with Scribing!

Source: https://www.elderscrollsonline.com/en-us/news/post/66071
Tier: combat-news
Published: unknown

Unlock a free multi-rider mount by delving deep into the
 Gold Road Chapter’s new Scribing system this month of June!
Unravel the Mysteries of Magicka
With the upcoming launch of
The Elder Scrolls Online:
 Gold Road
(arriving June 3 for PC/Mac and June 18 for Xbox and PlayStation
 consoles), you can explore the origins of magic in Tamriel via the all-new
 Scribing system. With Scribing, you can
create your own handmade
 abilities
with a near-limitless number of options available.
To celebrate this incredible new feature and Chapter launch,
 we’re happy to announce the Anniversary Spotlight—Gold Road. Running from
June 3 until July 1 for PC/Mac
and
June 18 until July 16 for console
 players
, this new Anniversary Spotlight grants everyone who delves deep
 into the system
a special reward
.
Unlock a Multi-Rider Mount
What special reward? Complete the Scribing meta-achievement
 “Inheritor of the Scholarium” during the Anniversary Spotlight window and you
 can claim the coveted Wayrest Vanner Pillion Steed mount from the in-game Crown
 Store
for free
.
To unlock this achievement, you need to complete every quest
 in the Scribing questline (six in total) and restore the Scholarium.
Travel Tamriel together
While a striking and handsome beast, the Wayrest Vanner
 Pillion Steed is no normal mount, as it supports two adventurers riding at
 once! What better way to explore West Weald?
Note that you need
access to the Champion System
or
 have a
level 30 character
to begin the Scribing quest line. This mount
 can be claimed only during the promotion window (June 3 until July 1 for PC/Mac
 and June 18 until July 16 for console players)—get ready to prove your worth to
 the Luminaries of Magic!
Endeavor to Craft New Abilities
In addition to the unique achievement reward, after
Gold
 Road
’s launch, you’ll discover a selection of Scribing-themed Endeavors to
 complete as well. These new Endeavors task you with using your custom-made
 abilities in your adventures throughout Tamriel, such as defeating enemies,
 healing your allies, and more.
Note that these Endeavors will reward you with the typical number
 of Seals you might expect from other similar tasks and won’t be active until
 June 18 (when the
Gold Road
Chapter and Update 42 are live on all
 platforms).
Celebrating ESO & Gold Road
With the upcoming launch of the new Chapter, we can’t wait
 to see what kind of unique handcrafted abilities the community comes up with
 during the Anniversary Spotlight—Gold Road. Don’t forget, the only way to get
 your hands on that multi-rider mount is to complete the Scribing questline. Let
 us know if you’re excited about the new system in
Gold Road
and how you
 plan to use it via
X (formerly
 Twitter)
,
Instagram
,
 and
Facebook
—have
 fun!
