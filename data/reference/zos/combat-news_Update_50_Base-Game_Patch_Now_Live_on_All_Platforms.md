# Update 50 Base-Game Patch Now Live on All Platforms

Source: https://www.elderscrollsonline.com/en-us/news/post/69965
Tier: combat-news
Published: unknown

Discover the redesigned Werewolf, Class Mastery, and more
 with Season Zero’s Update 50 patch.
Enjoy a host of new Season Zero features and get ready for
 Season One in July with the new Update 50 base-game patch. This update is
free
 for all
ESO
players
(simply patch the game and log in) and it
 includes the following features:
Werewolf skill line refresh
Class Mastery system
PvP Veterancy system
Challenge Difficulty system
Permanent Alliance War Vengeance campaign
New player experience improvements
Including the Guild Mail tool, the addition of Transmute Stations
 to major crafting hubs, and more
Thieves Guild story questline
Arrives July 8 as part of Season One
Dynamic Encounters system
Arrives July 8 as part of Season One
Favors quest system
Arrives July 8 as part of Season One
Sheogorath story questline
Arrives July 29 as part of Season One
Note that many of the above features will not arrive until
 Season One kicks off on July 8 (and beyond). You can dive a little deeper into
 the above additions, below!
Werewolf Refresh
Tamriel’s monstrous lycanthropes return with a major redesign
 as part of the ongoing class and combat refresh work. The Werewolf skill line
 has enjoyed major changes to the way their transformations and core abilities
 work in addition to broad updates to their look and feel, including base model,
 animations, VFX, and sounds.
Stalk Tamriel anew
You can sink your teeth into many of the changes and
 improvements in
this preview blog
.
Class Mastery System
Rewarding those who wish to remain steadfast to their class
 of choice, the new Class Mastery system introduces a host of new passive skills
 that play into your classes’ core elements. If you’ve longed to play a “pure”
 version of your class, this new system offers powerful new tools for doing so.
 To learn more, check out
this preview blog
.
PvP Veterancy System
With the PvP Veterancy System, you can earn ranks and unlock
 new rewards each PvP “season” (approximately six months long). As you gain
 Alliance Points or Experience Points in the Battlegrounds, Alliance War, or
 Imperial City, you increase your rank up to rank 100 and earn a host of rewards
 including currencies, titles, and style pages.
Rise in the PvP ranks
Dive deeper into the Veterancy System in
this preview blog
.
Challenge Difficulty System
If you’ve long wanted a greater challenge when exploring and
 adventuring in Tamriel’s overland zones, the new Challenge Difficulty system
 allows you to choose between four increasingly dangerous difficulty levels. The
 higher the difficulty, the greater the XP and gold rewards! Furthermore, your
 choice of difficulty does not stop you from playing with anybody else who
 chooses differently.
“Enjoy” a more
 dangerous Tamriel
Find out more about Challenge Difficulty in
this preview blog
.
Vengeance Campaign
Vengeance returns as a permanent option for your Alliance
 War campaign choice. Previously used to test various performance tweaks and
 improvements for the large-scale PvP zone, this unique open-world conflict remains
 available for you to experience at any time. You can find the Vengeance
 Campaign in your Alliance War menu. Read a briefing on this latest version of
 the campaign in
this preview blog
.
Player Experience Improvements
The team continues to work on improvements to
ESO
’s
 core experience with a new wave of additions as part of Update 50. These
 include a bespoke Golden Pursuits campaign for new players, the Guild Mail tool,
 the proliferation of Transmute Stations throughout Tamriel’s main crafting hubs,
 and more.
Send and receive
 Guild Mail!
Discover more about the player experience improvements now
 live in
this
 preview blog
.
Thieves Guild Story Questline
Arriving when
Season One kicks off on July 8
, the
 Thieves Guild needs your help establishing a foothold within Glenumbra. In this
 new questline, you must assist Quen as she shepherds the new guild into
 existence while contending with the dangerous Koldane Cartel. Look for more
 information about this new storyline as we get closer to its launch in July!
Dynamic Encounters
A new kind of in-game world event for overland zones,
 Dynamic Encounters are multi-stage experiences for all nearby participating
 players. With three unique Dynamic Encounters arriving when
Season One
 launches on July 8
, be on the lookout for trouble when exploring Glenumbra,
 Auridon, and Stonefalls—with more kinds of encounters coming in later Seasons,
 too.
Favors System
Also arriving
on July 8 with the launch of Season One
,
 the Favors system is a new way to find adventure in Tamriel. Interact with new
 posting boards set up by the Freerunners within each Alliance to acquire a
 daily quest. Each daily quest is part of a larger story specific to the
 Alliance character who posted it, with 20 unique dailies available for each. Want
 to know more? We will share more info about this new quest system closer to the
 Season One launch!
Sheogorath Story Questline
Kicking off
July 29 as part of Season One
, Noth the
 Unhinged needs your help as her master Sheogorath, the Lord of Madness, embarks
 on a new trip (some might say vacation) throughout Tamriel. However, even the
 Dedric Prince of Madness is not without rivals, and it’s up to you to ensure a
 safe journey. Keep both eyes out for more details on this storyline in July!
Greater Adventures to Come
Season Zero continues to add new ways to experience
 Tamriel’s Second Era. Whether you’re a veteran PvPer eager to rise in the
 ranks, a fearsome lycanthrope ready to begin the hunt, or are simply looking
 for a more challenging (and rewarding) combat experience while questing, Update
 50 has something for you!
