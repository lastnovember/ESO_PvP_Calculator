Tier: combat-news
Published: unknown

Help us test ESO’s first big release of the year by
 jumping into Scions of Ithelia and Update 41 on the PC/Mac Public Test Server!
The Public Test Server (PTS) has been updated to include
 both the upcoming
Scions of Ithelia
dungeon DLC and Update 41 base-game
 patch and can be accessed via the PC/Mac
ESO
launcher. If you’d like to help us
 test
Scions of Ithelia

 changes, fixes, and improvements coming with Update 41, now is your chance!
To access the PTS environment, go to the Settings menu
 within the PC/Mac
ESO
launcher by toggling the “Show Public
 Test Environment” option. You can then download and access the test
 environment. Don’t forget to leave feedback and report any bugs you come across
 by typing “/feedback” or “/bug” in chat.
For everything you need to know about
Scions of
 Ithelia
, Update 41, and this particular test, view the
PTS Patch Notes
on
 the official
ESO
forums.
