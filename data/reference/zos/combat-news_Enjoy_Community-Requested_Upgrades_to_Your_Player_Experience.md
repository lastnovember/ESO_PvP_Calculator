# Enjoy Community-Requested Upgrades to Your Player Experience with Update 49

Source: https://www.elderscrollsonline.com/en-us/news/post/69241
Tier: combat-news
Published: unknown

Your ESO player experience is getting some major
 improvements with the first update of 2026!
This upcoming year is shaping up to be a time of change for
ESO
.
 The new
Seasons
allow content to be delivered on a more flexible schedule and with a variety of
 themes, while
Tamriel
 Tomes
and the
Gold Coast
 Bazaar
offer new paths to earnable rewards.
Amidst this flurry of new and exciting features, a dedicated
 team of developers is hard at work on the player experience. As a result, the
 first set of updates focused on quality-of-life improvements for your time in
 Tamriel is coming with
Update 49
, which
arrives simultaneously for
 all platforms on March 9, 2026
. Read on to discover just some of the
 highlights from these player-focused changes and additions.
New Stablemaster Offerings
From kagouti to senches or even Dwemer constructs, you have
 a lot of options for getting around in Tamriel. However, Tamriel’s
 stablemasters have historically only sold horses. Now, they’ve heard the demand
 to ride in style and are opening the gates of their stables to let out some
 exciting new options.
With Update 49,
new mounts become available at various
 Stablemaster NPCs across Tamriel
, all available for a variable cost in
 gold. Mounts range from bears to nix-oxen and are all priced appropriately to
 their nature—it takes a lot more to feed a hungry durzog than a guar!
Ride out in style
You can find each new gold-buyable mount at stables across
 Tamriel appropriate to the mount’s natural habitat and theme—a camel in
 Hammerfell, an elk in Valenwood, and so on. There’s even an all-new mount
 available exclusively for purchase from a particular Stablemaster in-game—maybe
 you’ll be the first to find it!
When you’re at a stable with an unusual steed available,
 it’ll be viewable near the Stablemaster without needing to purchase it, in case
 you want to check its teeth (not advisable with many of these critters).
The three horse variants originally sold by Stablemaster
 NPCs remain available, and you can still train your collection of steeds to
 improve their speed, stamina, and capacity each day. In fact, when visiting a
 Stablemaster to train your mounts, you can enjoy an improved experience—you
now
 receive three points of improvement in the chosen trait
whenever you train!
 This advantage does not affect Crown Riding Lessons—it must be some neat trick
 the Stablemasters are keeping to themselves.
Change Your Build for Free
Player freedom in character builds is a core feature of
ESO
’s
 philosophy, and with Update 49 we’re making it possible to
change your character’s
 attribute and skill point allocations for free
and in any location, as long
 as you’re not in combat or locked into a PvP template. With this change, it’ll
 be easier to experiment with builds and find the one that is just right for
 you, or to swap between whatever playstyles strike your fancy.
This change does merit some extra attention for those of you
 who’ve been hoarding Respecification Scrolls. These are no longer necessary after
 Update 49 goes live, and they leave the Crown Store today, January 12. To make
 sure you still get value for any scrolls you have at that point, they become
 sellable to NPC vendors for gold once the change occurs, although you should
 take the opportunity to use any remaining scrolls for their primary purpose as
 you need!
Build your way
 with free respecs
Adding to the changes in build freedom, we are improving the
 sourcing of bites to unlock the Vampire and Werewolf skill lines, as well as
 the cures that remove those conditions. In-game availability for these curses
 through infection by an NPC become available at multiple new shrines throughout
 many base game zones .
If you want to find a bite for the first time,
free
quest
 starters in the Crown Store direct you on your way to embrace these conditions.
 If you’ve already completed the initial quest for a particular curse, you can
 return to any of the new shrines (now marked on the map after discovery) to
 return to that state without having to repeat the quest again. Similarly, you
 can cure yourself for free in the Crown Store for increased convenience,
 allowing you to shed your affliction(s) at any time.
Account-Wide Outfit Slot Unlocks
Increased freedom isn’t just for powerful builds—it also
 matters for making sure your outfit is up to date, so you can slay while you
 slay. That’s why with Update 49,
Outfit Slots are unlocked on an
 account-wide basis,
giving the purchase of slots greatly increased value.
All future Outfit Slot purchases after Update 49 immediately
 apply to all characters on your account, while existing purchases are combined
 up to a limit of 9 additional (and 10 total) slots on each character. If you’d
 upgraded three of your characters with two unlocked Outfit Slots each, you will
 have seven Outfit Slot unlocked on each character, counting the free slot you
 start with. This change also benefits all other characters on your account,
 including any new characters you make afterward. Each of these slots saves
 outfits separately by character, meaning ten characters could have a wardrobe
 of up to 100 outfits between them!
Outfit yourself
 for the real endgame
This isn’t the only way fashion is getting more flexible in
 this update.
Mimic stones and outfit tokens are entering the
Gold Coast
 Bazaar
, allowing you to buy these handy tools with in-game resources.
 On top of that, a long-requested change is coming to allow you to hide any
 piece of gear you want, similar to how “Hide Shoulders” has worked in the past.
 Show off body markings freely or just escape the tyranny of pants—it’s up to
 you!
Furnishing Limits Increase
Are you always on the lookout for the perfect place for a
 painting or a great spot for a couch? You’ll find it much easier to make those
 decisions thanks to an increase in the furnishing limits on non-Notable houses.
 With Update 49, every house that was Staple or Classic has its
capacity
 increased by at least 50 additional furnishing slots
—or, if you have ESO
 Plus membership, 100 additional furnishings!
Fill your home
 with even more things!
Home size categorization is also changing so that all homes
 within a size category, whether Staple, Classic, or Notable, are now unified in
 their furnishing capacity. Some homes that were Staple or Classic have been
 bumped up into a larger size category, which can give homes an additional 150
 slots! Even apartments and inn rooms are getting in on this increase with more
 furnishing space for our coziest little homes!
Easier Bag and Bank Upgrades
With Update 49, it’s easier than ever to maximize your room
 in both your bank and character inventory, reaching the greatest amount of
 carrying space possible with a minimum of fuss.
Costs for these upgrades are going down significantly,
 especially on the expensive final upgrades. The total cost of
fully
 unlocking both bank and bag space is going down by nearly half
. If you
 still can’t afford to max out your storage yet, it’s easier to return to the
 process later down the line. Instead of needing to track down an appropriate
 merchant, you’ can increase capacity from the inventory screen itself, just as
 you would at an NPC.

The above additions and changes are just some of the
 highlights of the team’s work improving your player experience. In this new
 base-game patch, your crafting research timers are reduced, you have more
 choice in how you use your Zone Guide, you gain tabardless Guild repping,
 vendors sort unusable items away from the tops of their inventory, you earn XP
 for skills even when they are on your back bar, and more.

 improvements

improved ESO
 Plus™
with new bonuses!
