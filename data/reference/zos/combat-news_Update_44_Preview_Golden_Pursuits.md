# Update 44 Preview—Golden Pursuits

Source: https://www.elderscrollsonline.com/en-us/news/post/66867
Tier: combat-news
Published: unknown

In addition to the
Battlegrounds
 update
,
new
 Companions
, and more, Update 44 also introduces Golden Pursuits, a
 new reward system that allows you to complete activities to earn additional in-game
 items, currencies, and collectibles.
This new system will arrive when Update 44 goes live on
 October 28 for PC/Mac and November 13 for Xbox and PlayStation consoles, but
 note that the first Golden Pursuits campaign won’t be active until later in
 November—keep an eye out for details!
A New Way to Earn Rewards
When designing Golden Pursuits, the team wanted to create new
 reasons to play and new ways for players to earn in-game rewards.
“Golden Pursuits are intended to give players the
 opportunity to earn extra rewards for trying out different systems and
 features,” explains Katie Yu, one of
ESO
’s Systems Designers. “They’re
 meant to highlight specific aspects of the game, or to support an event or
 theme. We want to ensure that players know exactly what they have the
 opportunity to earn and that they feel rewarded for completing the Golden
 Pursuit.”
While Daily Login Rewards are set every month, almost every
 aspect of a Golden Pursuits campaign can vary. This includes its ease or
 difficulty of activities, volume and quality of rewards, and even when they’re
 active and duration.
“Unlike many of the systems in
ESO
, Golden Pursuits
 don't operate on a regular daily or weekly cadence. There may be anywhere from
 days, to weeks, to months in between them, and Golden Pursuits will vary in
 length when they are live. They're meant to be exciting, spontaneous events. But
 don't worry, they're pretty difficult to miss: Golden Pursuits will always
 appear on the Announcement Panel and in the Group and Activity Finder when
 they're active.”
A Variety of Challenges
As mentioned above, each Golden Pursuits campaign will vary
 greatly in size and scope, and this variability will be reflected in the
 activities and rewards each individual campaign may offer.
“Generally, the activities will be very similar to what
 players have been seeing from Endeavors,” says Yu. “The number can vary—some
 Golden Pursuits may offer only a few activities, while others may provide more
 options for players to peruse before deciding how they want to earn their
 rewards. Broadly, Golden Pursuits activities are meant to highlight different
 parts of the game, whether that's encouraging players to engage in some
 friendly PvP, or try out Housing and Home Tours, or even simply explore what
 the wide world of Tamriel has to offer.”
Pursue new
 challenges
When it comes to the individual rewards, it was important to
 the team that Golden Pursuits campaigns varied the kinds of things you can
 earn.
“Players can expect to see mounts, pets, costumes,
 furnishings—the list goes on,” explains Yu. “There’s a wide variety of
 cosmetics, minor utility items such as potions and meals, currencies, and more
 from Golden Pursuits, and we're really excited to give players new
 opportunities to earn them.”
With this broad selection of reward types in mind, the team worked
 to ensure that the ideal balance is struck between the effort required to
 complete a Golden Pursuit and what it awards.
“Each Golden Pursuit is designed to be fully completable
 within the duration that it's active. To give a peek behind the curtain, Golden
 Pursuits are typically put together by a multidisciplinary group before
 launch. We have data gathered from player feedback, and we review the performance
 of similar systems in
ESO
to inform the design and expected level of
 investment. Our goal is to make sure playing with an active Golden Pursuit is both
 exciting and rewarding.”
Golden Opportunities

 more ways for you to earn rewards as you play. Note that at launch, a Golden
 Pursuits campaign won’t be active, but keep an eye out for an official
 announcement for the first of its kind around the middle of November. Are you
 excited to complete your first Golden Pursuit? Let us know via
X (formerly Twitter)
,
Instagram
, or
Facebook
