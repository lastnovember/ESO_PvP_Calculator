 within Eastern Solstice

Seasons of the Worm

Once the wall has fallen, simply venture east to begin
 exploring the second half of the Solstice zone. The first wayshine you will

Update 48
Included with the
Seasons of the Worm Cult Part 2
and
 Eastern Solstice, the Update 48 base-game patch introduces new fixes, balance
 changes, and additions to
ESO
. All current
ESO
players can enjoy the
 following features since they do not require any additional purchase.
Heart’s Week Event
Update 48 brings an all-new in-game event to Tamriel! During
 Hearts Week in February, the Belkarth festival grounds will now be home to the priest
 of Mara, Kuzam-jo. During the event period, Kuzam-jo will offer new quests,
 celebrating and spreading the word about Tamriel’s Heart’s Day holiday.
Share the love in  February!
This new event will feature passive bonuses to Companion XP  and rapport gains, plus feature their own reward boxes that can include pages  of a new style. Most notably, the Heart’s Day reward boxes will have a chance  to grant you gold-quality Companion jewelry. This event won’t go live until  February 2026, so look out for more details then!
New Life Event Update
In addition to the brand-new event, Update 48 also breathes  new life into one of
ESO
’s classic in-game celebrations. This December’s  New Life Festival will feature eight additional daily quests, taking place all  over Tamriel. This is in addition to a new style and player house rewards. Look  out for more details closer to the start of the holiday season!
Leaderboard Updates
With Update 48, to create a more unified and fair  experience, all the game’s leaderboards (except for those in Cyrodiil) are now account-based.  As a result, you will only have one entry per leaderboard (your highest score).  This change also removes class-specific leaderboards.
Combat Animation Memory Reduction
Update 48 also introduces some changes that help reduce the  size of combat animations to reduce the impact on the game’s memory. This  change should not have any noticeable impact on in-game visuals and zero impact  on your ability to light attack weave or animation cancel.
For a complete list of everything included in the Update 48  base-game patch, check out the

ESO
players.
