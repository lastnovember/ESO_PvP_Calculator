# Discover Update 50’s Player Experience Improvements

Source: https://www.elderscrollsonline.com/en-us/news/post/69857
Tier: combat-news
Published: unknown

Your ESO experience receives further refinements with  Season Zero and Update 50, including new player guidance, guild mailers, and  more.
Update for May 13, 2026:
A previous version of this article contained incorrect information about the duration of guild mailers, which has been corrected. Thank you for your understanding.
The
Player Experience Improvement team
has returned with a new series of additions hot  on the heels of their massive
Update 49
release.  With Update 50, arriving June 8 for all platforms, the team continues the work to  smooth out the wrinkles, remove inconveniences, and address pain points for your  in-game experience. Read about some of the changes and additions to come below!
Pursue a Path to Adventure
New and early-game players can expect extra support on their  first steps into Tamriel with the Path to Adventure Golden Pursuit. Similar to  the
Hero’s Return
campaign, the Path to Adventure uses the Golden Pursuits framework  to offer optional guidance and structure when getting started in
ESO
.
As you get your new character going, you’ll be introduced to  the basics of common
ESO
activities and mechanics and granted rewards to  help you on your way. You’ll also learn about everything from Mundus Stones to  crafting, get a chance to explore delves and dungeons, and receive consumables,  basic gear, and other helpful goods.
New Guild Mail Tool
Coordinating guilds is going to become much easier in Update  50. Guild leaders (and any other officers with the appropriate permissions)  will gain access to a new Guild Mail tool to share important messages with  other members.
This tool can help you schedule social hangouts, sort out your Trial roster, allow officers to plan Guild Trader bids, and more. Guild Mail can be sent across the entire guild or be filtered by rank. They have a fourteen-day duration, but can be deleted and re-sent at any time by a guild member of the appropriate rank for permissions to make sure they remain relevant to the guild’s status and plans.
Stay up to date
 with your guilds!
When you receive Guild Mail, it appears in a new section in
 your in-game mailbox. Each guild you join can have up to five messages active
 at once, with older messages removed as they expire—messages near this point
 have a warning in red text to make sure you don’t miss anything! The guild’s
 name is displayed as the sender, and these messages are automatically
 color-coded to match what each guild is assigned in your chat window to make
 mail easy to sort through.
Transmutation Spreads Across Tamriel
Transmutation, first introduced alongside the
Clockwork
 City
DLC all the way back in 2017, has become increasingly important to
 Tamriel’s adventurers over time. However, the spread of Transmute Stations at
 which to perform this arcane science has not matched the growing value of the
 system. Update 50 takes steps to correct this, adding Transmute Stations to
 many new zones across Tamriel.
You can of course still collect a Transmute Station in your
 in-game home for convenience or visit the original station in Sotha Sil’s
 domain while you’re there, but you’ll also find stations in or near a number of
 Mages Guild facilities. You’ll find these stations in the following hubs:
Elden Root
Stormhaven
Mournhold
Wrothgar
Vivec City
Alinor
Rimmen
Leyawiin
Gonfalon Bay
Necrom
Eyevea
Find Transmute Stations
 in more locations
Companion Efforts
The efforts above are just the hardest-hitting changes and
 additions coming in Update 50, but they are not the only improvements you can
 expect in this patch. For example, companion XP gain has been slightly
 increased in general, and further increased when you have higher rapport levels
 with the companion being leveled.
Finally, Exemplary items (such as the Nirnhoned item awarded
 for completing the Craglorn zone story, or the Infused jewelry found in Psijic
 Portals) can now be deconstructed for crafting materials like other gear.
