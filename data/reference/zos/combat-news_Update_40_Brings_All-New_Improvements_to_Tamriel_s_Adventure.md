# Update 40 Brings All-New Improvements to Tamriel’s Adventurers

Source: https://www.elderscrollsonline.com/en-us/news/post/64689
Tier: combat-news
Published: unknown

Learn about some of the key additions and changes
 arriving with the free Update 40 base-game patch.
The Update 40 base-game patch arrives October 30 on PC/Mac
 and November 14 on Xbox and PlayStation consoles. In addition to the
Infinite Archive PvE activity
, this update also introduces a host of new features
 and additions completely free for all existing
ESO
players, no
 additional purchases required.
New Grouping Tool
If you enjoy teaming up with your fellow adventures, the new
 Group Finder allows you to create parties for almost any group activity in
ESO
.
 This includes dungeons, arenas, the upcoming Infinite Archive, trials, PvP, and
 more.
You can choose to make a listing to find players to fill out
 your existing group (even when starting solo), or you can search for a group
 that already exists and wants to do the same content that you do. This new tool
 also allows you to tune the criteria your group members must meet, including
 these examples:
Player CP levels
Whether they use in-game voice chat
Their playstyle
A desired group role composition
The Group Finder
 tool
With the new Group Finder, teaming up with your fellow
 adventurers should be as easy as ever!
Grand Master Crafting Stations
Update 40 also introduces Grand Master Crafting Stations,
 allowing you to consolidate all your home’s attuned crafting stations for a
 crafting skill line into a single furnishing. If your base of operations is
 packed full of attuned crafting stations supporting many of
ESO
’s myriad
 item sets, these new consolidated stations will go a long way to improving the
 amount of housing slots spent without giving up any of the functionality you
 enjoyed previously.
You can acquire Grand Master Crafting Stations using Writ  Vouchers from the Achievement Mediator, Faustina Curio. There will be a  consolidated station for Blacksmithing, Jewelry Crafting, Clothing, and  Woodworking—happy crafting!
Assorted Additional Improvements
Jewelry Crafting Rebalance
Also included in Update 40 is a rebalance of Jewelry  Crafting to help bring its costs more in line with the game’s other crafting  disciplines. This includes
completely removing
Jewelry Crafting grains  and
converting them into platings
! However, to ensure that Jewelry  Crafting’s costs line up with the other crafting types, we’ve also increased  the total boosters needed to upgrade your jewelry, too. But don't worry,  upgrading your rings and amulets in Update 40 will be easier and cheaper when  compared to how it is currently.
Quest XP & Gold Standardization
With Update 40, we conducted a broad audit and  standardization of the Experience Point and Gold rewards for many of the game’s  quests. This includes those for quests and questlines associated with zones,  main storylines, prologues, dailies, guild storylines, and many more. Now, you  should receive more consistent rewards for the game’s various quests based on  their complexity or length. In addition, the daily quest rewards for Mages  Guild, Fighters Guild, and Undaunted have received a general boost.
These are just some of the many changes and additions coming  to
ESO
with the Update 40 base-game patch. You can experience them (and  the Infinite Archive) for yourself when the update arrives on the PC/Mac Public  Test Server in a few days  (Monday, September 18) or when it all arrives on  October 30 for PC/Mac and November 14 for Xbox and PlayStation consoles.
