# Guide to Subclassing

Source: https://www.elderscrollsonline.com/en-us/guides/subclassing
Tier: guide
Published: unknown

Customize your gameplay experience with the Subclassing system!
Starting out With Subclassing
Introduced to the game with Update 46, the Subclassing system allows you to mix and match class skill lines in your character builds.
You can access the system accountwide once you have a single character hit level 50 and have the "Level 50 Hero" achievement. All characters can then become eligible to accept the quest "A Study in Discipline." Completing this quest enables use of the Subclassing system on that character.
Learn Subclassing from Bahtra at-Hunding
This quest can be granted in the following ways:
The quest is offered by the adventurer Bahtra at-Hunding, located near the wayshrines at Riften in The Rift, Evermore in Bangkorai, and Dune in Reaper's March.
You can grant yourself the quest starter via the Crown Store.
You can grant yourself the quest directly from the Subclassing UI within the Skills menu.
The Rules of Subclassing
To equip a new class skill line, you'll need to subclass a skill line from another class or master that skill line by achieving rank 50 on that skill line's primary class. You may choose skill lines to subclass via a shrine, a Respecification Scroll, or by talking to Bahtra at-Hunding. After selecting a skill line to subclass you must continue learning it until you reach 50, if you have not previously done so and mastered it. Note you can freely add and remove that skill line via respecification.
Combine three different class skill lines
After subclassing is unlocked, selecting a skill line to progress or swap in becomes part of the normal respecification menu. You can find every class’s skill lines listed to select for subclassing. Once you have options that are subclassed or mastered, you can select a skill line of your current class to replace, then a new option to equip from that menu. Once you confirm your selection, the swap is made, and you can begin using your new skill line!
Subclassing Restrictions
You must always have
at least one skill line
from your
primary class equipped
. You can equip up to two subclassed or mastered skill lines to your build, but only one subclassed or mastered skill line per class.
For example, a Dragonknight must keep at least one of Ardent Flame, Draconic Power, or Earthen Heart. They could draw upon the power of the Grave Lord or Bone Tyrant skill lines, but not both, as they come from the same additional class, the Necromancer. A Dragonknight could, however, replace both Draconic Power and Earthen Heart with Grave Lord and Storm Calling, as each new skill line comes from a different class (Necromancer and Sorcerer, respectively).
Added Skill Lines
Subclassing allows full use of Active, Passive, and Ultimate skills from other classes. Note that subclassed or mastered skill lines level up at a slower rate than your primary class skill lines. Skills from subclassed or mastered skill lines only become available as certain character levels are reached if below level 50. Each skill from subclassed or mastered class skill lines also costs two skill points to unlock, instead of one.
Using Subclassing
Subclass selections are on a per-character basis, and you may only subclass three skill lines at a time. Your subclass skill line progress towards rank 50 is shared account-wide. Primary class skill lines do not share this progress.
Master new class skill lines
When you reach rank 50 with a subclass skill line, that subclass is considered mastered. You will unlock the rank 50 achievement and upgrade collectible for that skill line. You may then choose to subclass a different skill line on that character and begin rank progression anew.
Equipping Skill Lines
At any time, primary class skill lines, mastered skill lines, and your chosen subclassed skill lines may be equipped or unequipped via a shrine of Stendarr, use of a Respecification Scroll, or by talking to Bahtra at-Hunding in her camp.
When you unequip a skill line you will be refunded any spent skill points. Overall rank progress in a skill line will be retained, however you will have to reallocate skill points when you choose to re-equip that skill line.
