Gold Road Chapter
In

West Weald Zone
Located along Cyrodiil’s southwest border and neighboring
 Valenwood and the Gold Coast, this new Chapter allows you to
explore West
 Weald
, a wealthy Imperial province thrown into chaos. Home to the proud and
 cosmopolitan Colovians, West Weald has found itself besieged by mysterious
 unknown Daedra. Even worse, a large part of the region has seemingly overnight become
 overgrown by the vast jungles of Valenwood, resulting in the migration of local
 Wood Elves.
Within this new zone, you can explore the city of Skingrad
 in addition to both the Gold Road region and emergent Dawnwood, where you’ll
 discover a multitude of new side quests, world bosses, delves, public dungeons,
 and more. Discover more about the zone in
this preview
 blog

Scribing System
An all-new system introduced with
Gold Road
, Scribing
 grants you the ability to craft and
customize your
 own handmade abilities
. With Scribing, you can select a base Grimoire and
 tailor it with scripts to create an ability that perfectly fits your playstyle
 and build.
With this new system, you’ll discover a robust questline
 that has you restore an ancient Scholarium while diving deep into the mysteries
 of magicka in Tamriel. Whether you’re looking for greater power, versatility,
 or roleplaying opportunities, Scribing introduces a powerful new way to tailor
 your builds and play your way.

In the Lucent Citadel Trial, you and your large group (12
 players total) must venture into a long-forgotten Daedric vault within the
 realm of Fargrave, contend with its unique boss encounters, and seek out the mysterious
 Arcane Knot.
Team up to defend
 West Weald
Mirrormoor Incursions are a new type of world event plaguing

 You can discover more about both activities in

Getting Started in Gold Road
Ready to venture into West Weald? You can travel to the new
 zone and begin your adventure in the following ways:
Via Wayshrine
You can teleport directly to the zone by accessing your map,
 selecting Gold Road, and then selecting the automatically unlocked Skingrad
 Vineyards Wayshrine.
Via the Gold Road Main Quest
You can have the game lead you to West Weald by picking up
 the Chapter’s first main story quest, “Paths of Chaos.” You can find it by
 navigating to your Collections and then viewing Stories, selecting Chapter, and
 then Gold Road.
Via the Tutorial
You can also travel to West Weald by creating a new
 character and completing the tutorial. At the end of the tutorial experience,
 you can select the zone as your destination and begin.
Adventure awaits  in Gold Road
Note that there are no level or story requirements for the
Gold  Road
Chapter, allowing you to jump right in with any of your  characters—existing or brand new!
Update 42
Launching in tandem with Gold Road is Update 42, a free  base-game patch for all
ESO
players. Update 42 introduces a series of  balance changes, fixes, and additions with no purchase required (beyond access  to the game itself).
Improvements to In-game Mail
With Update 42, we’ve made a series of improvements to  in-game mail, focusing on its interface and notifications. This includes the  addition of categories (player mail, system mail, and system alerts), improved  sorting, attachment preview, and inventory space display.
This update also adds a “Delete After Claim” option (helping  you keep inboxes under control), a “Take All” function, and notifications for  mail with attachments. In addition, messages received from your Hirelings will  now be automatically recorded in the Lore Library under “Hireling  Correspondence.”
Finally, as part of the team’s efforts to improve server  performance and stability, Update 42’s mail changes also include reduced  expiration times based on the mail types.
Environment Sustainability Features
With this update, we’re joining Team Xbox in reducing our  game’s environmental impact with the following new features:
Reduced GPU use during periods of inactivity for PC/Mac, Xbox  Series X|S, and PlayStation 5.
Reducing resolution and imposing a framerate cap during  inactivity.
Screen dimming during periods of inactivity on PC/Mac.
Note that this already occurs on consoles.
Reduced GPU use in Contextual Menus for Xbox Series X|S and  PlayStation 5.
When viewing most menus on consoles, the framerate is capped.
