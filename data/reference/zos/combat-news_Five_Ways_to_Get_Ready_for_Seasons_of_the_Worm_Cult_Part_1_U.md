# Five Ways to Get Ready for Seasons of the Worm Cult Part 1 & Update 46

Source: https://www.elderscrollsonline.com/en-us/news/post/68049
Tier: combat-news
Published: unknown

A new zone, storyline, and base-game features arrive in
 June via the
Seasons of
 the Worm Cult Part 1
and Update 46. Here are five things you can do in
 game right now to get ready for the adventures to come!
Play ESO’s Main Questline
The Seasons of the Worm Cult storyline is a true sequel to
ESO
’s
 original main story questline, so what better way to prepare for Part 1 than to
 make sure your character has completed it?
Battle the Daedric
 Prince of Domination
Help the Five Companions undo the mistakes of the past and
 stop Molag Bal from dragging Nirn into his realm of Coldharbour. Once complete,
 you’ll know all you need to know to get the most out of Seasons of the Worm
 Cult Part 1. Even better,
May’s Golden
 Pursuits campaign
contains a host of base-game activities, including
 completing quests, slaying foes in early zones, and more. Go earn that free
 house!
Prepare for Subclassing
The upcoming
Update 46
 base-game patch
includes the all-new Subclassing system, allowing you to
 mix and match your Class skill lines to create your ideal build.
Prepare for
 Subclassing
If you want to get the most out of Subclassing, you should ensure
 you have a character that has reached level 50 and have a bunch of spare Skill
 Points ready when the new system goes live in June. Hang on to those XP and
 respec scrolls, too!
Find Stalwart Allies
While adventuring in Solstice, you’ll encounter no shortage
 of allies willing to help you battle the cult. However, you can already meet many
 of them on mainland Tamriel right now.
Prince Azah can be found as part of the Alik’r Desert zone
 story, while others, such as Skordo the Knife and Gabrielle Benele are featured
 in the Rivenspire and Glenumbra zone storylines respectively.
Get to know Skordo
 (and others)
Why not get to know your allies before beginning your next
 adventure?
Battle the Worm Cult
ESO
’s main story isn’t the only way to take on the cult,
 and you can often find them
worming
their way into much of Tamriel as
 you explore. Why not learn a little more about your enemy by battling them
 wherever they might be found?
For example, you could always join the famous Fighters
 Guild, which plays a major part in the ongoing war against Molag Bal and his
 minions with its own unique questline and story.
Help the Fighters
 Guild
Alternatively, you can discover cult activities within the
 town of Nimalten in the Rift or the Bone Orchard in Grahtwood. Or you can
 encounter cultists within the delve of Aba-Loria in Coldharbour. Don’t forget
 to shut down any nearby Dark Anchors, too!
Play the New Prologue
Finally, leading directly into Part 1, the
Seasons of the
 Worm Cult Prologue quest
has you team up with Prinze Azah to track down the
 Fighters Guild Guildmaster. The events of this quest point you directly to
 Solstice and Seasons of the Worm Cult Part 1, and it’s free for all
ESO
players.
Begin your new adventure
To begin the prologue, grab the quest “A Guild in Crisis”
 from the in-game Crown Store.
Prepare for a New Saga
The Seasons of the Worm Cult Part 1 and Update 46 base-game
 patch arrive this June, but you can ready yourself for the fight to come right
 now by checking out the above activities and adventures. How are you preparing for
 the war against the Worm Cult? Let us know via
Bluesky
,
X (formerly Twitter)
,
Instagram
, and
Facebook
.
Seasons of the Worm Cult Part 1 is included within the
 2025 Content Pass and 2025 Premium Edition and arrives on June 2, 2025, for
 PC/Mac and June 18, 2025, for Xbox and PlayStation consoles.
