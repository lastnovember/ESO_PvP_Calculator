# ESO 2024 Global Reveal—Introducing Scribing

Source: https://www.elderscrollsonline.com/en-us/news/post/65361
Tier: combat-news
Published: unknown

Discover new ways to play with Scribing, arriving as part
 of The Elder Scrolls Online: Gold Road.
In
The Elder Scrolls Online: Gold Road
(arriving June
 3 on PC/Mac and June 18 on Xbox and PlayStation consoles), you can unlock
 access to an all-new game system: Scribing. With Scribing, you can manipulate
 the nature of magic itself by collecting and customizing special skills,
 granting you even more ways to tailor specific builds and play your way.
Customize New Skills
When designing this new system, the team focused on improving
 opportunities for roleplay and choice for
ESO
’s adventurers by providing
 greater agency when it comes to selecting and adapting the skills that are
 right for them. What exactly does this mean for you and your characters?
 Scribing introduces Grimoires, which are new types of skills for your existing skill
 lines. You can customize these Grimoires beyond their basic functions, transforming
 them into exactly what you want on your action bar.
For example, one new Grimoire, called Soul Touch, allows you
 to pull soul magic from the core of your being and cast it at a nearby enemy or
 ally. You can customize Soul Touch by choosing its Primary, Secondary, and
 Tertiary Scripts. You could use the Primary to create a Damage Shield for an
 ally, pull an enemy to you, or even change it to Magic, Shock, or Physical
 Damage. In addition, you could use the Soul Infusion Secondary to enable the
 ability to consume a soul gem and gain Ultimate, or you can use a Recovery Secondary
 to regain resources for a targeted ally.
Customize your
 Grimoires with Scripts
The above is just one example, but the Scribing system will
 give you a ton of new ways to change your build and playstyle, since you can
 unlock a host of Grimoires and Scripts at launch.
Explore the Origins of Magic
To master the art of Scribing you'll need the
Gold Road
Chapter,
 which grants access to a brand-new questline. In this unique story, ancient
 magic arising in the world attracts the attention of the Mages Guild, who send
 one of their best to investigate.
Working alongside a brave Knight of the Lamp, you'll discover
 a mysterious new location and altar and discover the secrets of the mage who
 built it. Throughout the questline, you'll learn how to scribe, gain a Grimoire
 or two, and meet new friends along the way.
Ally with the
 Luminaries of magic
Fantastic untold lore awaits those willing to delve into the
 esoteric mysteries of magic, but those who commit to unraveling its secrets
 will find new power and freedom as they do.
Style Your Existing Skills, Too!
With the ability to mix and match a wide variety of classes,
 races, skills, and gear sets,
ESO
has always striven to provide you with
 the freedom and power to customize your characters’ unique playstyles.
In the spirit of this core pillar,
Gold Road
also
 introduces a new type of collectible, called Skill Styles. Skill Styles are
 color variation of popular abilities from various weapon, guild, and world skill
 lines, helping you further stand out on the battlefield.
Customize existing
 abilities with Skill Styles
At the launch of
Gold Road
, you can unlock these
 Skill Styles as in-game rewards, but there’s a lot more planned for them beyond
 the Chapter, too.
