# 2025 ESO Direct—Update 46 Preview

Source: https://www.elderscrollsonline.com/en-us/news/post/67841
Tier: combat-news
Published: unknown

Prepare for new features and improvements to ESO’s base
 game with Update 46, including a whole new form of character customization!
When
ESO
’s next big patch, Update 46, arrives in
 June, you can expect great things. A new system will shake up character builds
 and customization like never before, returning players will find a new welcome
 back experience, and ESO Plus™ members get a new tool to handle their
 furnishing storage burdens—plus additional quality of life changes!
Class Your Character Up with Subclassing
Subclassing is a new way to customize your character in
ESO
.
 With this feature, available completely FREE to all players when Update 46
 launches, you can swap out up to two of the three skill lines inherent to your
 character for options from other classes.
Want your Dragonknight to have some ice to go with their
 fire? Add the Winter’s Embrace skill line from the Warden. Maybe you trade out
 your Earthen Heart skill line to do so—who needs stony protection when you can
 both burn and freeze your enemies before they reach you? You could even add in
 the Sorcerer’s Storm Calling Skill line and leave only Ardent Flame from the
 Dragonknight to become a true master of the elements!
Wield flame,
 frost, and lightning
With subclassing, you can swap skill lines whenever you use
 a Shrine of Stendarr or a Scroll of Respecification. The skill lines you can
 choose, how to unlock skill lines for subclassing, and other details are coming
 closer to June. There’s much, much more to share about the incredible
 possibilities subclassing introduces to your builds, so keep an eye on
the official website
for more to come as Update 46 draws closer.
Hero’s Return Welcomes You Back to Tamriel
Sometimes life gets in the way of adventure, and you need to
 take a break from Tamriel. With Update 46, the new Hero’s Return feature makes it
 easy to hit the ground running with reminders and rewards.
Hero’s Return automatically detects when you haven’t logged
 in to
ESO
for an extended period and offers you handy tools and guidance
 to help get back into the thick of things more swiftly. The first of these is a
 brief, optional adventure you can choose to complete to help refresh your
 combat skills.
Tamriel welcomes
 you back
Once you complete this adventure—or if you choose to skip it—you
 gain access to the Hero’s Return Golden Pursuit and a few days of unique Login Rewards.
 This special Golden Pursuit is multifaceted, with multiple smaller campaigns
 within it that point you towards resources, content, and gameplay you may have
 forgotten about during your time away. Rewards from both the Pursuits and the Login
 track have been selected to be helpful in your return to Tamriel, but don’t
 replace other rewards earned in game, so you’ll still have plenty to enjoy
 after completing your Hero’s Return to Tamriel.
Declutter Your Home with the Furnishing Vault
Update 46 also brings with it a new benefit for ESO Plus
 members: the Furnishing Vault. This new collectible lets you store up to 500
 unique furnishing stacks in a vault accessible from any your homes throughout
 Tamriel. Whether placed in a home or accessed directly via the Housing Editor,
 the Furnishing Vault makes it easy to manage your furnishing collection and
 decorate across multiple homes.
A spacious storage  solution
Do you have a spare house full of Daedric thrones or other  goods purchased from the Luxury Vendor Zanil Theran? Now you can clean it up  and decorate properly!
Additional Quality-of-Life Changes
There are also smaller quality-of-life improvements coming  Update 46. For example, your in-game map has undergone further work after  Update 45’s existing changes. The new map updates include adding better  tooltips, enhanced quest visibility, and more.
We’ve also added improved tutorials for the Wayshrine  system, making these map enhancements even more impactful for new and returning  players who need to learn (or relearn) how to traverse Tamriel.
There's another improvement continuing forward from  groundwork laid in the last game update—console addons! We previously expanded the API to enable add-on creators to begin their  work, and Update 46 will allow creators to upload their add-ons and console  players to download add-ons. Look for more information on how it will  work soon on
elderscrollsonline.com
.
As if those weren't already some great quality-of-life additions, we're also changing things so that Master Writs are now awarded in stackable envelopes. Open them whenever you want to save on inventory space in between use.
