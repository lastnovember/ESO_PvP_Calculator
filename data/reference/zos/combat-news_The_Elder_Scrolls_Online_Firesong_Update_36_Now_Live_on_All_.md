# The Elder Scrolls Online: Firesong & Update 36 Now Live on All Platforms

Source: https://www.elderscrollsonline.com/en-us/news/post/63075
Tier: combat-news
Published: unknown

Protect the Legacy of the Bretons and bring your  year-long adventure to its conclusion in The Elder Scrolls Online: Firesong and  Update 36.
The Elder Scrolls Online: Firesong
and Update 36 base game patch is now live on PC/Mac, Xbox One, Xbox Series X|S, PlayStation 4, and PlayStation 5.
Explore an all-new zone, discover the ancient lore of the  druids of the Systres, and confront the Ascendant Lord in this final part of  the year-long Legacy of the Bretons adventure.
The Elder Scrolls Online: Firesong
The untamed island of Galen, home to the druidic circles of
 the Systres Archipelago, is mired in chaos. The Breton city of Vastyr suffers
 unrelenting raids from Dreadsail pirates, while the Firesong Circle has started
 a war between the druids of the Systres.
In this new story DLC, you will venture into the heart of a primeval
 new zone, assist the noble Society of the Steadfast and House Mornard, and
 uncover the forces and motives behind the chaos that has engulfed the island
 before it encompasses all the Systres.
Face the Ascendant
 Lord
This new DLC also includes bonus quests that you can unlock
 only by completing the
Firesong
AND
High Isle
Chapter main
 questlines. In these additional adventures, you’ll bring a final close to the
 year-long Legacy of the Bretons saga and confront the Ascendant Lord once and
 for all.
Explore Wild Galen
Primeval and wild, Galen has been home to the druids of the
 Systres for an age. In this new zone, you will have an opportunity to explore a
 world apart from those you’ve already experienced in the
High Isle
Chapter and get lost within the mysterious island’s dense jungles and perilous
 lava rivers.
Explore an all-new
 zone
In the new zone, you’ll have an opportunity to dive deep
 into the druids of the Systres and their connection to the Bretons of High Isle
 and Tamriel. From the city of Vastyr, a bastion of “civilization” in an
 otherwise untamed land, you’ll set out into the deep wilds and discover a host
 of new challenges, including new delves, roaming world bosses, volcanic vents, and
 stand-alone quests and storylines. Learn more about the new zone
in this preview
.
Uncover Unique Rewards
As you venture into Galen, you’ll have an opportunity to
 earn unique rewards equal to the zone’s dangers, including new item sets, titles,
 Achievements, and collectibles. This includes the Firesong Obsidian Mask (a
 free adornment earned simply by entering the new zone), the Phoenix Moth pet
 (one of many quest rewards), a host of special styles earned via the
 Antiquities system and Tales of Tribute, and much more.
Receive unique
 rewards
In addition to the above,
Firesong
also introduces a
 brand-new Tales of Tribute patron: the Druid King. Unlocked by completing
 various zone activities, the Druid King patron and deck rewards those who
 carefully set up combos, and it can set up big swings in both the mid and late
 game. Learn more about the new patron and deck
in our preview
.
 Don’t forget, you’ll need both
Firesong
and the
High Isle
Chapter
 to unlock and play this new Tales of Tribute deck.
Getting Started in Firesong
The
Firesong
DLC is free for all ESO
 Plus™ members and available for purchase for 2,000 crowns from the in-game
 Crown Store (listed under DLC). Note that if your ESO Plus membership lapses,
 you will lose access to the DLC until you renew or purchase the DLC with
 crowns, but you won’t lose any items or progress you earned while you had
 access.
If you acquired access to
Firesong
via the
Heroes of High
 Isle Reward Bundle
, the DLC will be waiting for you in your collections and
 you can jump right in.
Firesong
 Collector’s Bundle
Releasing in tandem with the base DLC, the
Firesong
 Collector’s Bundle
is also available in the in-game Crown Store for
 4,000 crowns and includes Cinderstone Kagouti mount, the Companion's Wolf Pup
 pet, and Crown Experience Scrolls. You can find the
Firesong
 Collector’s Bundle
in the Featured section of the Crown Store.
Once you have access to the
Firesong
DLC,
 you can begin your exploration of this volatile part of Oblivion by the
 following methods:
By teleporting directly to the Vastyr Outskirts Wayshrine from
      your map
By using the DLC tab of your Collections UI and accepting
      the “A Sea of Troubles” quest from the Zone DLC section
When you arrive in Galen, you can discover the new zone, begin
 the DLC’s storyline, or simply explore—it’s up to you! The first time you enter
 the zone, you’ll receive the Firesong Obsidian Mask collectible completely free
 as a bonus collectible reward.
Update 36-Base Game Patch
Releasing in tandem with the
Firesong
DLC, the Update 36
 base-game patch is a free update for all
ESO
players that introduces Simplified
 Chinese localization to the game’s text along with a host of balance changes,
 bug fixes, and new features. These new additions include the ability to place
 unique target markers on enemies (allowing you to better coordinate with your
 allies), a text-to-speech accessibility option, the option to hide class pets
 within cities and towns (no longer will your crafting station be blocked by an
 unwelcome bear butt!), and a series of improvements to the Housing system.
In particular, the Housing improvements feature the ability  to create chat links within your homes, provides greater visibility as to whose  house you’re currently visiting and how many people are currently present, grants  the ability to see a full list of furnishings within a home, allows you to  cycle through items with the Tab key, and implements improved placement  alignments for hundreds of different wall-hanging furnishings. We can’t wait to  see how the Housing community makes use of these new features and improvements!
