Unlike previous years, you will no longer need to obtain or
 use the Scroll of Pelinal’s Ferocity to enjoy the 100% buff to Alliance Point, XP,
 and Tel Var Stone gains. These bonuses will now be shared passively to
 adventurers competing throughout Cyrodiil, the Imperial City, and
 Battlegrounds.
Now that the Scroll of Pelinal’s Ferocity isn’t required to
 enjoy these boons, there will be no way to pick up the Scroll anywhere in-game,
 and those who already own the item will notice it no longer serves any function.

Bonus Resources & The Golden
In addition, harvesting nodes in Cryodiil will yield extra
 resources when interacted with during Whitestrake’s Mayhem.
Furthermore, the Cyrodiil-based merchant Adhazabi “The
 Golden” Aba-daro’s shop will offer a fresh stock of CP160 legendary-quality jewelry
 from Cyrodiil item sets. Necklaces will be available during the first weekend of
 the celebration and rings from the same sets during the second.
Keep in mind that the Golden will not sell monster masks during Whitestrake’s Mayhem and all purchases in her shop must be made with
 Alliance Points. Despite her name, she will
not
accept gold.
