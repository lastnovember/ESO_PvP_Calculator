Alchemy
Potions of various types can be found in many places, for sale by general Merchants, in containers, in Alchemy Bottles loose on tables, etc., and on the corpses of your slain enemies. Some special potions can be found in Thieves Troves or as quest rewards, while others can only be purchased in Cyrodiil or from the Crown Store.

## Generic Potions
These come in three types, Health, Magicka, and Stamina. Their level is tied to your character level when you find them. In all cases, these will be weaker than the ones you can craft yourself at Alchemy stations, but they are easy to come by in large quantities.
