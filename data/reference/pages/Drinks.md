Beverage Recipes
Drinks are items that temporarily increase regeneration of one, two, or of all three attributes.

This page lists all the drinks that can be purchased from the brewers and chef & brewer vendors or found throughout Tamriel. Please note that these beverages are of Normal Quality, in contrast to the ones brewed by player, and speed up the recovery of only one of the attributes at a time.

Items on this list increase an attribute regeneration for 35 minutes and have a 5 second cooldown. If different drinks are consumed one after another, the effects do not stack.

Although Psijic Ambrosia is listed as a beverage when crafting, it can be consumed alongside other drinks and therefore isn't listed here.

## Staple Beverages
These beverages are Normal Quality versions of craftable Fine Beverage Recipes. They are less potent than their crafted versions. These may be found randomly on tables and in kegs throughout the world, or they may be purchased from Innkeepers or Brewers. They may also be received as quest rewards from the Fighters Guild, Mages Guild, and Undaunted daily quests.
