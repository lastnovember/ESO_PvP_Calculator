# Online:Two Handed

License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Two Handed is one of the six weapon skills available in Elder Scrolls Online. You can advance through this skill line by equipping a two-handed weapon.

## Learning
The Two Handed skill line is granted the first time you kill an enemy with a two-handed weapon equipped. You can increase your skill by engaging in combat with a two-handed weapon equipped, or by having Two Handed skills slotted. The more skills you have slotted, the faster your skill will increase. Nords are naturally gifted with Two Handed weapons, and will learn the ability more quickly than other races. In addition, you can earn free levels in the skill line by reading Skill Books, which may be found randomly on bookshelves anywhere. The skill books which increase Two Handed are:
- Ceryolminwe's Translation
- A Hero's Weapon
- How to Win a Fight
- The Legendary Sancre Tor
- Wuunding and Tumult

### Other Sets
- Wearing five pieces of the Sword Singer set from the Alik'r Desert increases the Weapon Damage and Spell Damage of your Two Handed abilities.
- Wearing five pieces of the Red Eagle's Fury set from the Red Eagle Redoubt crafting site increases the Weapon Damage and Spell Damage of all your weapon abilities, Two Handed included, but it also increases their cost.
- Wearing five pieces of the Daring Corsair set from the Cat's-Claw Station crafting site decreases the Stamina and Magicka costs of all your weapon abilities by 10%, and grants you Minor Heroism for 8 seconds after you use a weapon skill.
