Necromancer (disambiguation)

Necromancer is a class in Elder Scrolls Online. It can be purchased from the Crown Store for 1500. Necromancers are death bringers who use a mix of Illusion, Destruction, and Conjuration spells, and mostly deal with the undead and fear. It is available to players who have purchased the retail version of the Elsweyr Chapter, but is not included with the DLC version of Elsweyr.

Performing certain Necromancer abilities in front of some NPCs (for example, guards) will incur a bounty. These skills are marked as.

::In-Game Description: Masters of death, Necromancers can call upon corpses to serve as undead thralls and weave ghastly spells to both harm and heal.

## Skills

### Grave Lord
Grave Lord is elemental magic skill with pure damage dealing abilities.

### Bone Tyrant
Bone Tyrant is a set of abilities focused towards defense and tanking.

### Living Death
Living Death is a mix of healing and resurrecting abilities.

### Class Mastery
Class Mastery is available if not subclassing.

Necromancer is one of the more difficult classes for mixing builds. There is only one dual-build which does not have any conflicting morphs, between Deathbringer and Flesh Sculptor, though these are incompatible for other reasons, since the former is Stamina-based and the latter is Magicka-based. There are no triple-builds with fewer than 2 conflicting morphs.
