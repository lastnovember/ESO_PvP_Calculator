# Online:Berserk

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Berserk
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Berserk is an Affix Script used in Scribing. It can be earned by completing Undaunted daily delve quests, DLC daily incursion quests, or daily district quests in the Imperial City. Or it may be purchased from Chronicler Firandil in The Scholarium for 20,000 (4,000) or Filer Ool in the Infinite Archive for 10,000 (2,000). (The prices are lower if another character has already learned the script.)

colspan=2|Grimoire | Effects

Smash | Smash
Grants you Minor Berserk for 20 seconds, increasing damage done by 5%.

Traveling Knife | Traveling Knife
Grants you Minor Berserk for 20 seconds, increasing damage done by 5%.

Banner Bearer | Banner Bearer
Grants Minor Berserk, increasing damage done by 5%.

## Notes
- The only identifiable Daedric text on the script is simply the letter "B".
