# Online:Force

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Force
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Force is an Affix Script used in Scribing. It can be earned by completing Undaunted daily delve quests, DLC daily incursion quests, or daily district quests in the Imperial City. Or it may be purchased from Chronicler Firandil in The Scholarium for 20,000 (4,000) or Filer Ool in the Infinite Archive for 10,000 (2,000). (The prices are lower if another character has already learned the script.)

colspan=2|Grimoire | Effects

Smash | Smash
Grants Minor Force for 20 seconds, increasing Critical Damage by 10%.

Vault | Vault
Grants Minor Force for 20 seconds, increasing Critical Damage by 10%.

Ulfsild's Contingency | Ulfsild's Contingency
Grants Minor Force for 22 seconds, increasing Critical Damage by 10%.

## Notes
- The Daedric text on the script reads "Force".
