# Online:Banner Bearer

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Banner_Bearer
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

:Bring out a banner to inspire yourself and nearby group members.

Banner Bearer is a Scribed skill Grimoire on the Support skill line. It can be purchased after earning the Alliance War Skill Apprentice achievement from Chronicler Firandil in the Scholarium once per character. Note that unlike all the other Grimoires, you must earn the associated achievement individually on each character in order to purchase it, while for the others you only need the achievement on the first character.
It costs 50,000 the first time, and 10,000 for other characters on your account after that.

Banner Bearer causes you to bring out a banner, which will be attached to your back. The skill costs a constant amount of Magicka and Stamina every two seconds (Health and Stamina with Immobilize Focus Script). While active, you and any group members near you will gain certain buffs, depending on your choice of scripts. The banner remains active indefinitely until you put it away by casting the skill again, swapping skill bars (unless you have the skill on both bars), running out of one of the two required resources, or changing zones.

- Cast Time: Instant
- Radius: 8 meters

- You cleanse yourself of snares and immobilizations and are immune to them while the banner is active.

- Increases Magical damage done by 6%.
- Cost: 459 Magicka and 459 Stamina every two seconds

Mitigation | Mitigation | Fortifying Banner

- Reduces damage taken by 6%.
- Cost: 459 Magicka and 459 Stamina every two seconds

Multi-Target | Multi-Target | Shattering Banner

- Increases damage done with area of effect attacks by 6%.
- Cost: 459 Magicka and 459 Stamina every two seconds

Physical Damage | Physical Damage | Sundering Banner

- Increases Martial damage done by 6%.
- Cost: 459 Magicka and 459 Stamina every two seconds

Restore Resources | Restore Resources | Restorative Banner

- Reduce the cost of non-Ultimate abilities by 8%.
- Cost: 459 Magicka and 459 Stamina every two seconds

Shock Damage | Shock Damage | Shocking Banner

- Increases damage done with direct damage by 6%.
- Cost: 459 Magicka and 459 Stamina every two seconds

## Signature Scripts
colspan=2|Script | Effects

Cavalier's Charge | Cavalier's Charge
Increase your Weapon and Spell Damage by 6 for every 1% bonus Movement Speed, up to a max of 450.

Class Flourish | Class Flourish
Depends on class. See Class Flourish page for full details.

Crusader's Defiance | Crusader's Defiance
Reduce the effectiveness of negative snares by 30%.

Druid's Resurgence | Druid's Resurgence
Increases Health, Magicka, and Stamina Recovery of other group members by 139.

Sage's Remedy | Sage's Remedy
Heals for 949 Health every 2 seconds.

Thief's Swiftness | Thief's Swiftness
Reduce the cost of Sprint by 22%.

Warmage's Defense | Warmage's Defense
Grants a damage shield every 3 seconds for 3 seconds that absorbs up to 1293 damage.

Wayfarer's Mastery | Wayfarer's Mastery
You count as being in a PvP area for Battle Resurrection, reducing the time it takes to resurrect another player.
