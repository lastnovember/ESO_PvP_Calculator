# Online:Stun (script)

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Stun_(script)
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Stun is a Focus Script used in Scribing. It can be found on a table in the central area of the Daggerfall Mages Guild.

colspan=2|Grimoire | Effects

Elemental Explosion | Elemental Explosion (Dazing Explosion)
Stuns enemies for 5 seconds.

Smash | Smash (Dazing Smash)
Deals 2055 Physical Damage to enemies and fears them for 3 seconds. Beneficial Signature and Affix scripts only apply to you.

Traveling Knife | Traveling Knife (Dazing Knife)
Deals 2014 Physical Damage to an enemy and stuns them and enemies between you and them for 3 seconds on return.

Wield Soul | Wield Soul (Dazing Soul)
Deals 2803 Magic Damage to an enemy and stuns for 3 seconds. Beneficial Signature and Affix scripts only apply to you.

Torchbearer | Torchbearer (Dazing Torch)
Reduces the Movement Speed of enemies by 40% for 4 seconds on the first sweep, immobilizes for 3 seconds on the second sweep, and stuns for 3 seconds on the third sweep. Beneficial Signature and Affix scripts only apply to you.

Trample | Trample (Dazing Trample)
Deals 3116 Physical Damage to all enemies and stuns them for 3 seconds.

## Notes
- The Daedric text on the script reads "Stun".
