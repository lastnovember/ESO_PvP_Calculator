# Online:Maim (script)

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Maim_(script)
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Maim is an Affix Script used in Scribing. It can be found at the Riften Mages Guild, on a shelf at the south side of the upper level.

colspan=2|Grimoire | Effects

Mender's Bond | Mender's Bond
Afflicts Minor Maim to enemies in the link for 10 seconds, reducing damage done by 5%.

Shield Throw | Shield Throw
Afflicts the enemy with Major Maim for 10 seconds, reducing damage done by 10%.

rowspan=2|Smash | rowspan=2|Smash
Afflicts enemies with Minor Maim for 20 seconds, reducing damage done by 5%.

Incompatible with Warding Smash and Healing Smash
