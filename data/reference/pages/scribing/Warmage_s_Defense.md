# Online:Warmage's Defense

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Warmage's_Defense
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Warmage's Defense is a Signature Script used in Scribing. It can be earned by completing Fighters Guild daily quests, DLC daily world boss quests, or by completing town quests in Cyrodiil. Or it may be purchased from Chronicler Firandil in The Scholarium for 60,000 (12,000) or Filer Ool in the Infinite Archive for 20,000 (4,000). (The prices are lower if another character has already learned the script.)

colspan=2|Grimoire | Effects

Elemental Explosion | Elemental Explosion
Grants you a damage shield that absorbs 3204 damage per enemy hit for 6 seconds.

Mender's Bond | Mender's Bond
Grants a damage shield when other allies leave the link that absorbs 1749 damage for 6 seconds.

Traveling Knife | Traveling Knife
Grants you a damage shield that absorbs 3777 damage for 6 seconds.

Banner Bearer | Banner Bearer
Grants a damage shield every 3 seconds for 3 seconds that absorbs up to 1293 damage.

## Notes
- The Daedric text on the script reads "Barrier".
