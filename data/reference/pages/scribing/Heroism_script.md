# Online:Heroism (script)

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Heroism_(script)
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Heroism is an Affix Script used in Scribing. It can be earned by completing Undaunted daily delve quests, DLC daily incursion quests, or daily district quests in the Imperial City. Or it may be purchased from Chronicler Firandil in The Scholarium for 20,000 (4,000) or Filer Ool in the Infinite Archive for 10,000 (2,000). (The prices are lower if another character has already learned the script.)

colspan=2|Grimoire | Effects

Banner Bearer | Banner Bearer
Grants Minor Heroism, generating 1 Ultimate every 1.5 seconds.

Mender's Bond | Mender's Bond
Grants Minor Heroism to the ally and allies in the link for 10 seconds, generating 1 Ultimate every 1.5 seconds.

Torchbearer | Torchbearer
Grants Minor Heroism for 20 seconds with each sweep, generating 1 Ultimate every 1.5 seconds.

Trample | Trample
Grants you Major Heroism for 10 seconds, generating 3 Ultimate every 1.5 seconds.

## Notes
- The Daedric text on the script reads "Hero".
