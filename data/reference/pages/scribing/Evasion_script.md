# Online:Evasion (script)

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Evasion_(script)
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Evasion is an Affix Script used in Scribing. It can be earned by completing Undaunted daily delve quests, DLC daily incursion quests, or daily district quests in the Imperial City. Or it may be purchased from Chronicler Firandil in The Scholarium for 20,000 (4,000) or Filer Ool in the Infinite Archive for 10,000 (2,000). (The prices are lower if another character has already learned the script.)

colspan=2|Grimoire | Effects

Shield Throw | Shield Throw
Grants you Major Evasion for 10 seconds on return, reducing damage from area attacks by 20%.

Vault | Vault
Grants you Major Evasion for 20 seconds, reducing damage from area attacks by 20%.

Torchbearer | Torchbearer
Grants Minor Evasion for 20 seconds with each sweep, reducing damage from area attacks by 10%.

## Notes
- The Daedric text on the script reads "Evasion".
