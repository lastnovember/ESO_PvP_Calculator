# Online:Pull (script)

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Pull_(script)
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Pull is a Focus Script used in Scribing. It can be earned by completing Mages Guild daily quests, DLC daily delve quests, Rewards for the Worthy chests from earning Alliance Points or very rarely as an unbound drop from slain overland enemies. Or it may be purchased from Chronicler Firandil in The Scholarium for 40,000 (8,000) or Filer Ool in the Infinite Archive for 15,000 (3,000). (The prices are lower if another character has already learned the script.)

colspan=2|Grimoire | Effects

Shield Throw | Shield Throw (Leashing Throw)
Deals 2145 Physical Damage to an enemy, pulls them to you, and taunts them for 15 seconds if they are not already taunted.

Traveling Knife | Traveling Knife (Leashing Knife)
Deals 2014 Physical Damage to an enemy and pulls them and enemies between you and them to you on return.

Wield Soul | Wield Soul (Leashing Soul)
Deals 2803 Magic Damage to an enemy, pulls them to you, and taunts them for 16 seconds if they are not already taunted. Beneficial Signature and Affix scripts only apply to you.

Soul Burst | Soul Burst (Leashing Burst)
After 2 seconds, pulls enemies within 8 meters to you. Beneficial Signature and Affix scripts only apply to you.

## Notes
- The Daedric text on the script reads "Pull".
