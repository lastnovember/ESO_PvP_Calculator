# Online:Elemental Explosion

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Elemental_Explosion
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

:Channel the power in your staff to fling a bolt of volatile magic, causing an elemental explosion at the target location.

Elemental Explosion is a Scribed skill Grimoire, on the Destruction Staff skill line. It can be purchased after earning the Destruction Staff Master achievement from Chronicler Firandil in the Scholarium once per character.
It costs 50,000 the first time, and 10,000 for other characters on your account after that. The name of the scribed skill changes depending upon your choice of Focus Script.

- Cast Time: 2 seconds
- Target: Ground
- Radius: 10 meters
- Cost: 3704 Magicka
