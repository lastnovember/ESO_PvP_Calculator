# Online:Damage Shield (script)

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Damage_Shield_(script)
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Damage Shield is a Focus Script used in Scribing. It can be found at the Davon's Watch Mages Guild, on a table at the bottom of the right-hand stairway. It is generally incompatible with offensive Signature and Affix scripts.

colspan=2|Grimoire | Effects

Mender's Bond | Mender's Bond
Grants the ally and other allies in the link a damage shield that absorbs 942 damage every 1 second.

Smash | Smash (Warding Smash)
Grants you and your allies a damage shield that absorbs 6047 damage for 6 seconds. Beneficial Signature and Affix scripts only apply to you.

Wield Soul | Wield Soul (Warding Soul)
Grants you or an ally a damage shield that absorbs 6522 damage for 6 seconds, scaling off the higher of your Max Health or Magicka and capped at 55% of your Max Health. Beneficial Signature and Affix scripts apply to you or an ally.

Soul Burst | Soul Burst (Warding Burst)
Grants you and your allies a damage shield that absorbs 5568 damage for 6 seconds, scaling off the higher of your Max Health or Magicka, and capped at 55% of your Max Health. Beneficial and Affix scripts apply to you or an ally.

Ulfsild's Contingency | Ulfsild's Contingency (Warding Contingency)
Grants a damage shield to you and your allies that absorbs 5622 damage for 8 seconds, scaling off the higher of your Max Health or Magicka and capped at 55% of your Max Health. Beneficial Signature and Affix scripts apply to you or an ally.

## Notes
- The Daedric text on the script reads "Shield".
