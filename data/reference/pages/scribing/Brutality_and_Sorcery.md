# Online:Brutality and Sorcery

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Brutality_and_Sorcery
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Brutality and Sorcery is an Affix Script used in Scribing. It can be earned by completing Undaunted daily delve quests, DLC daily incursion quests, or daily district quests in the Imperial City. Or it may sometimes be purchased from Chronicler Firandil in The Scholarium for 20,000 (4,000) or Filer Ool in the Infinite Archive for 10,000 (2,000), depending on if the vendor has it available during their weekly rotation. (The prices are lower if another character has already learned the script.)

colspan=2|Grimoire | Effects

Elemental Explosion | Elemental Explosion
If an enemy was hit, grants you Major Brutality and Major Sorcery for 10 seconds, increasing Weapon and Spell Damage by 20%.

Wield Soul | Wield Soul
Grants Major Brutality and Major Sorcery for 10 seconds, increasing Weapon and Spell Damage by 20%.

Ulfsild's Contingency | Ulfsild's Contingency
Grants Major Brutality and Major Sorcery for 10 seconds, increasing Weapon and Spell Damage by 20%.

Banner Bearer | Banner Bearer
Grants Major Brutality and Major Sorcery, increasing Weapon and Spell Damage by 20%.

## Notes
- The Daedric text on the script reads "Brutal Sorcery".
