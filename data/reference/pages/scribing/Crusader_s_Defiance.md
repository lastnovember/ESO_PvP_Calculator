# Online:Crusader's Defiance

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Crusader's_Defiance
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Crusader's Defiance is a Signature Script used in Scribing. It can be earned by completing Fighters Guild daily quests, DLC daily world boss quests, or by completing town quests in Cyrodiil. Or it may be purchased from Chronicler Firandil in The Scholarium for 60,000 (12,000) or Filer Ool in the Infinite Archive for 20,000 (4,000). (The prices are lower if another character has already learned the script.)

colspan=2|Grimoire | Effects

Mender's Bond | Mender's Bond
Grants immunity to disabling effects for 3 seconds to the ally.

Smash | Smash
Removes and grants immunity to snares and immobilizations for 4 seconds.

Vault | Vault
Removes up to 3 snares or immobilization effects.

Soul Burst | Soul Burst
Removes up to 1 negative effect.

Torchbearer | Torchbearer
Removes up to 1 negative effect with each sweep. This can occur once every 2 seconds per target.

Banner Bearer | Banner Bearer
Reduce the effectiveness of negative snares by 30%.

## Notes
- The Daedric text on the script reads "Break Free".
