# Online:Ulfsild's Contingency

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Ulfsild's_Contingency
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

:Imbue yourself with the magical runes of Ulfsild. These runes trigger when you cast an ability with a cost, causing a burst of magic around you.

Ulfsild's Contingency is a Scribed skill Grimoire, on the Mages Guild skill line. It can be purchased after earning the Mages Guild Skill Master achievement from Chronicler Firandil in the Scholarium once per character.

It costs 50,000 the first time, and 10,000 for other characters on your account after that. The name of the scribed skill changes depending upon your choice of Focus Script.

Ulfsild's Contingency basically works by boosting whatever active or ultimate skill you cast afterwards, causing additional effects in an area around you that occurs at the same time. If you don't use another ability within 22 seconds, it dissipates and does nothing. When you have a Contingency primed, you will see glowing runes around your shoulders indicating this. This effect has the same appearance regardless of your choice of scripts, but the actual appearance when it triggers depends on your choice of Focus script. The casting cost is always 2808 Magicka, even if the ability that triggers it is Stamina or Ultimate based.

You can trigger Ulfsild's Contingency with itself, simply by casting it twice. This will both trigger the effect and simultaneously prime another trigger. You can thus use it over and over triggering itself, with each effect simply one-off delayed until the next casting. Whether this is a valid strategy may depend on your choice of scripts. You can also trigger it without any other skill by using a Roll Dodge. (This does cost Stamina, but far less than most skills do.) Skills without any casting cost such as those found here will not work, however.

- Cast Time: Instant
- Target: Self
- Duration: 22 seconds.
- Cost: 2808 Magicka
