# Online:Protection (script)

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Protection_(script)
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Protection is an Affix Script used in Scribing. It can be earned by completing Undaunted daily delve quests, DLC daily incursion quests, or daily district quests in the Imperial City. Or it may be purchased from Chronicler Firandil in The Scholarium for 20,000 (4,000) or Filer Ool in the Infinite Archive for 10,000 (2,000). (The prices are lower if another character has already learned the script.)

colspan=2|Grimoire | Effects

Mender's Bond | Mender's Bond
Grants Minor Protection to the ally and allies in the link for 10 seconds, reducing damage taken by 5%.

Ulfsild's Contingency | Ulfsild's Contingency
Grants Minor Protection for 22 seconds, reducing damage taken by 5%.

Banner Bearer | Banner Bearer
Grants Minor Protection, reducing damage taken by 5%.

## Notes
- The Daedric text on the script reads "Protection".
