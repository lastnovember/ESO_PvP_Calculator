# Online:Enervation (script)

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Enervation_(script)
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Enervation is an Affix Script used in Scribing. It can be earned by completing Undaunted daily delve quests, DLC daily incursion quests, or daily district quests in the Imperial City. Or it may be purchased from Chronicler Firandil in The Scholarium for 20,000 (4,000) or Filer Ool in the Infinite Archive for 10,000 (2,000). (The prices are lower if another character has already learned the script.)

colspan=2|Grimoire | Effects

Elemental Explosion | Elemental Explosion
Afflicts enemies with Minor Enervation for 20 seconds, reducing Critical Damage by 10%.

Shield Throw | Shield Throw
Afflicts the enemy with Minor Enervation for 10 seconds, reducing Critical Damage by 10%.

rowspan=2|Ulfsild's Contingency | rowspan=2|Ulfsild's Contingency
Afflicts Minor Enervation for 22 seconds, reducing Critical Damage by 10%.

Incompatible with Warding Contingency and Healing Contingency †
† All Affix scripts are compatible with Ulfsild's Contingency if using the "Growing Impact" Signature Script.

## Notes
- The Daedric text on the script is illegible.
