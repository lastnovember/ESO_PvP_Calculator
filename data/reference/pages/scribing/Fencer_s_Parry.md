# Online:Fencer's Parry

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Fencer's_Parry
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Fencer's Parry is a Signature Script used in Scribing. It can be earned by completing Fighters Guild daily quests, DLC daily world boss quests, or by completing town quests in Cyrodiil. Or it may be purchased from Chronicler Firandil in The Scholarium for 60,000 (12,000) or Filer Ool in the Infinite Archive for 20,000 (4,000). (The prices are lower if another character has already learned the script.)

colspan=2|Grimoire | Effects

Shield Throw | Shield Throw
Deflects the next direct damage attack made against you within 3 seconds on return. This can occur once every 3 seconds.

Smash | Smash
Deflects the next direct damage attack made against you within 3 seconds. This can occur once every 3 seconds

Traveling Knife | Traveling Knife
Deflects the next direct damage attack used against you within 3 seconds. This can occur once every 3 seconds.

## Notes
- The Daedric text on the script reads "Blade Turn".
