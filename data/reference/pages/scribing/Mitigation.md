# Online:Mitigation

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Mitigation
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Mitigation is a Focus Script used in Scribing. It was originally unique to the Mender's Bond Grimoire, but now has a second use with Banner Bearer. It can be earned by completing Mages Guild daily quests, DLC daily delve quests, or Rewards for the Worthy chests from earning Alliance Points. Or it may be purchased from Chronicler Firandil in The Scholarium for 40,000 (8,000) or Filer Ool in the Infinite Archive for 15,000 (3,000). (The prices are lower if another character has already learned the script.)

colspan=2|Grimoire | Effects

Mender's Bond | Mender's Bond (Fortifying Bond)
Transfers 10% of the damage the allied player or companion and other allied players or companions in the link take to you.

Banner Bearer | Banner Bearer (Fortifying Banner)
Reduces damage taken by 6%. Reduce your Health, Magicka, and Stamina Recovery by 33%.

## Notes
- The Daedric text on the script reads "Transfer".
