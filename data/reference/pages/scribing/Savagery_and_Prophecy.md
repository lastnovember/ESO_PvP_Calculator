# Online:Savagery and Prophecy

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Savagery_and_Prophecy
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Savagery and Prophecy is an Affix Script used in Scribing. It can be earned by completing Undaunted daily delve quests, DLC daily incursion quests, or daily district quests in the Imperial City. Or it may be purchased from Chronicler Firandil in The Scholarium for 20,000 (4,000) or Filer Ool in the Infinite Archive for 10,000 (2,000). (The prices are lower if another character has already learned the script.)

colspan=2|Grimoire | Effects
