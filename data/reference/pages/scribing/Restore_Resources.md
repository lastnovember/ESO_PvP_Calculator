# Online:Restore Resources

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Restore_Resources
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Restore Resources is a Focus Script used in Scribing. It was originally unique to the Mender's Bond Grimoire, but now has a second use with Banner Bearer. It can be earned by completing Mages Guild daily quests, DLC daily delve quests, or Rewards for the Worthy chests from earning Alliance Points. Or it may be purchased from Chronicler Firandil in The Scholarium for 40,000 (8,000) or Filer Ool in the Infinite Archive for 15,000 (3,000). (The prices are lower if another character has already learned the script.)

colspan=2|Grimoire | Effects

Mender's Bond | Mender's Bond (Restorative Bond)
Restores 215 Magicka and Stamina to the ally and other allies in the link every 1 second.

Banner Bearer | Banner Bearer (Restorative Banner)
Reduce the cost of non-Ultimate abilities by 8%. Reduce your Health, Magicka, and Stamina Recovery by 33%.

## Notes
- The Daedric text on the script reads "Restoration".
