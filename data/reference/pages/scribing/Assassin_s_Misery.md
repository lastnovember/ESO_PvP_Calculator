# Online:Assassin's Misery

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Assassin's_Misery
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Assassin's Misery is a Signature Script used in Scribing. It can be earned by completing Fighters Guild daily quests, DLC daily world boss quests, or by completing town quests in Cyrodiil. Or it may be purchased from Chronicler Firandil in The Scholarium for 60,000 (12,000) or Filer Ool in the Infinite Archive for 20,000 (4,000). (The prices are lower if another character has already learned the script.)

colspan=2|Grimoire | Effects

Elemental Explosion | Elemental Explosion
Afflicts enemies with Burning, Chill, and Concussion.

Traveling Knife | Traveling Knife
Increases the chance to apply status effects on enemies by 100% for 10 seconds.

Trample | Trample
Afflicts enemies with the Concussion, Sundered, and Hemorrhaging status effects.

## Notes
- The Daedric text on the script reads "Condition".
