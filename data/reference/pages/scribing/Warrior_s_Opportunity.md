# Online:Warrior's Opportunity

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Warrior's_Opportunity
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Warrior's Opportunity is a Signature Script used in Scribing. It can be found at the Elden Root Mages Guild, just beside the right onramp to the balcony.

colspan=2|Grimoire | Effects

Traveling Knife | Traveling Knife
Enemies hit take 8% more Martial damage for 5 seconds.

Torchbearer | Torchbearer
Increases damage done to enemies with disabling effects by 10% for 6 seconds with each sweep.

Ulfsild's Contingency | Ulfsild's Contingency
Increase damage done of your next direct attack used within 7 seconds by 2000.

Trample | Trample
Each enemy hit increases the damage to other enemies by 5%, stacking up to 20 times.

## Notes
- The Daedric text on the script reads "Chance".
