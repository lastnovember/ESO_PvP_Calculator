# Online:Multi-Target

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Multi-Target
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Multi-Target is a Focus Script used in Scribing. It can be earned by completing Mages Guild daily quests, DLC daily delve quests, or Rewards for the Worthy chests from earning Alliance Points. Or it may be purchased from Chronicler Firandil in The Scholarium for 40,000 (8,000) or Filer Ool in the Infinite Archive for 15,000 (3,000). (The prices are lower if another character has already learned the script.)

colspan=2|Grimoire | Effects

Shield Throw | Shield Throw (Shattering Throw)
Deals 2145 Physical Damage to an enemy and bounces up to 2 times to random nearby enemies, dealing 2860 Physical Damage and 3575 Physical Damage.

Traveling Knife | Traveling Knife (Shattering Knife)
Deals 2014 Physical Damage to an enemy and 3022 to enemies around them.

Banner Bearer | Banner Bearer (Shattering Banner)
Increases damage done with area of effect attacks by 6%. Reduce your Health, Magicka, and Stamina Recovery by 33%.

## Notes
- The Daedric text on the script reads "Multi Hit".
