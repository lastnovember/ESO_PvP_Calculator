# Online:Brittle

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Brittle
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Brittle is an Affix Script used in Scribing. It can be earned by completing Undaunted daily delve quests, DLC daily incursion quests, or daily district quests in the Imperial City. Or it may be purchased from Chronicler Firandil in The Scholarium for 20,000 (4,000) or Filer Ool in the Infinite Archive for 10,000 (2,000). (The prices are lower if another character has already learned the script.)

colspan=2|Grimoire | Effects

Elemental Explosion | Elemental Explosion
Afflicts enemies with Minor Brittle for 20 seconds, increasing their Critical Damage taken by 10%.

Mender's Bond | Mender's Bond
Afflicts Minor Brittle to enemies in the link for 10 seconds, increasing their Critical Damage taken by 10%.

## Notes
- The Daedric text on the script reads "Brittle".
