# Online:Wield Soul

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Wield_Soul
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

:Launch a concentrated blast of soul magic at a target.

Wield Soul is a Scribed skill Grimoire, on the Soul Magic skill line. It is given away for free by completing The Second Era of Scribing, the first quest in the Scribing quest line. The name of the scribed skill changes depending upon your choice of Focus Script.

- Cast Time: Instant

## Focus Scripts
colspan=2|Script | Skill Name | colspan=2|Effects

rowspan=3|Damage Shield | rowspan=3|Damage Shield | rowspan=3|Warding Soul
colspan=2|
- Grants you or an ally a damage shield that absorbs 6522 damage for 6 seconds, scaling off the higher of your Max Health or Magicka and capped at 55% of your Max Health. Beneficial Signature and Affix scripts apply to you or an ally.
- Target: Area
- Radius: 28 meters
- Duration: 6 seconds
- Cost: 5141 Magicka

Compatible Signature Scripts
Sage's Remedy, Anchorite's Potency, Class Flourish, Druid's Resurgence

Compatible Affix Scripts
Resolve, Brutality and Sorcery, Empower, Intellect and Endurance, Vitality

colspan=2|
- Heals you or an ally for 9570 Health. Beneficial Signature and Affix scripts apply to you or an ally.
- Target: Area
- Radius: 28 meters
- Cost: 5141 Magicka

Compatible Signature Scripts
Sage's Remedy, Anchorite's Potency, Class Flourish, Druid's Resurgence

Compatible Affix Scripts
Resolve, Brutality and Sorcery, Empower, Intellect and Endurance, Vitality

## Signature Scripts
colspan=2|Script | Effects

rowspan=2|Anchorite's Cruelty | rowspan=2|Anchorite's Cruelty
Consumes a Soul Gem to deal 1.5% of the enemy's Max Health as Oblivion Damage every 1 second for 5 seconds, up to a maximum of 1500 damage. Deals up to 100% more damage to enemies under 50% Health and can occur once every 5 seconds.

Incompatible with Warding Soul and Healing Soul

Anchorite's Potency | Anchorite's Potency
Consumes a Soul Gem to generate 4 Ultimate, up to once every 5 seconds.

Class Flourish | Class Flourish
Depends on class. See Class Flourish page for full details.

Druid's Resurgence | Druid's Resurgence
Restores 600 Magicka and 600 Stamina.

rowspan=2|Lingering Torment | rowspan=2|Lingering Torment
Deals 4665 Magic Damage over 10 seconds to the enemy.

Incompatible with Warding Soul and Healing Soul

Sage's Remedy | Sage's Remedy
Heals for 3815 Health over 10 seconds.

## Affix Scripts
colspan=2|Script | Effects

rowspan=2|Breach | rowspan=2|Breach
Afflicts the enemy with Major Breach for 10 seconds, reducing Physical and Spell Resistance by 5984.

Incompatible with Warding Soul and Healing Soul

Brutality and Sorcery | Brutality and Sorcery
Grants Major Brutality and Major Sorcery for 10 seconds, increasing Weapon and Spell Damage by 20%.

rowspan=2|Cowardice | rowspan=2|Cowardice
Afflicts the enemy with Major Cowardice for 10 seconds, reducing Weapon and Spell Damage by 430.

Incompatible with Warding Soul and Healing Soul

rowspan=2|Defile | rowspan=2|Defile
Afflicts the enemy with Major Defile, which reduces healing received and damage shield strength by 12% for 10 seconds.

Incompatible with Warding Soul and Healing Soul

Empower | Empower
Grants Empower for 10 seconds, increasing the damage of Heavy Attacks against monsters by 70%.

Intellect and Endurance | Intellect and Endurance
Grants Major Intellect and Major Endurance, increasing Magicka and Stamina Recovery by 30% for 10 seconds.

Resolve | Resolve
Grants Major Resolve for 10 seconds, increasing Physical and Spell Resistance by 5984.

Vitality | Vitality
Grants Major Vitality for 10 seconds, increasing healing received and damage shield strength by 12%.
