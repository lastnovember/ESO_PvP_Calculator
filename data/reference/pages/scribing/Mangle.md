# Online:Mangle

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Mangle
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Mangle is an Affix Script used in Scribing. It can be earned by completing Undaunted daily delve quests, DLC daily incursion quests, or daily district quests in the Imperial City. Or it may be purchased from Chronicler Firandil in The Scholarium for 20,000 (4,000) or Filer Ool in the Infinite Archive for 10,000 (2,000). (The prices are lower if another character has already learned the script.)

colspan=2|Grimoire | Effects

rowspan=2|Smash | rowspan=2|Smash
Afflicts enemies with Minor Mangle for 20 seconds, reducing Max Health by 10%.

Incompatible with Warding Smash and Healing Smash

rowspan=2|Torchbearer | rowspan=2|Torchbearer
Afflicts enemies with Minor Mangle for 20 seconds with each sweep, reducing Max Health by 10%.

Incompatible with Heroic Torch and Healing Torch

Trample | Trample
Afflicts enemies with Minor Mangle for 20 seconds, reducing Max Health by 10%.

## Notes
- The Daedric text on the script reads "Mangle".
