# Online:Vitality (script)

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Vitality_(script)
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Vitality is an Affix Script used in Scribing. It can be found north of the Rawl'kha Mages Guild building, on top of a crate-sized block of masonry at the edge of the patio connecting the Mages Guild building and the Mages Guild Living Quarters.

colspan=2|Grimoire | Effects

Mender's Bond | Mender's Bond
Grants Minor Vitality to the ally and allies in the link for 10 seconds, increasing healing received by 6%.

Shield Throw | Shield Throw
Grants you Major Vitality for 10 seconds on return, increasing healing received and damage shield strength by 12%.

Smash | Smash
Grants Minor Vitality for 20 seconds, increasing healing received by 6%.

Wield Soul | Wield Soul
Grants Major Vitality for 10 seconds, increasing healing received and damage shield strength by 12%.

Torchbearer | Torchbearer
Grants Minor Vitality for 20 seconds with each sweep, increasing healing received by 6%.

## Notes
- The Daedric text on the script reads "Life".
