# Online:Thief's Swiftness

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Thief's_Swiftness
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Thief's Swiftness is a Signature Script used in Scribing. It can be earned by completing Fighters Guild daily quests, DLC daily world boss quests, or by completing town quests in Cyrodiil. Or it may be purchased from Chronicler Firandil in The Scholarium for 60,000 (12,000) or Filer Ool in the Infinite Archive for 20,000 (4,000). (The prices are lower if another character has already learned the script.)

colspan=2|Grimoire | Effects

Shield Throw | Shield Throw
Grants you the ability to pass through enemies for 5 seconds on return.

Vault | Vault
Removes a stack of the Roll Dodge cost penalty and reduces fall damage taken by 15% for 20 seconds.

Banner Bearer | Banner Bearer
Reduce the cost of Sprint by 22%.

Trample | Trample
Leaves behind an area of speed for 10 seconds which increases the Movement Speed of you and your allies within by 15%.

## Notes
- The Daedric text on the script reads "Mobility".
