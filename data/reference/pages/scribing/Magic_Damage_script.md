# Online:Magic Damage (script)

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Magic_Damage_(script)
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Magic Damage is a Focus Script used in Scribing. It is earned for free during The Second Era of Scribing quest.

colspan=2|Grimoire | Effects

Elemental Explosion | Elemental Explosion (Magical Explosion)
Deals 8225 Magic Damage to enemies.

Mender's Bond | Mender's Bond (Magical Bond)
Grant's the ally magical thorns which deal 440 Magic Damage to attackers, scaling off the ally's stats.

Shield Throw | Shield Throw (Magical Throw)
Deals 4290 Magic Damage to an enemy.

Smash | Smash (Magical Smash)
Deals 4133 Magic Damage to enemies. Beneficial Signature and Affix scripts only apply to you.

Traveling Knife | Traveling Knife (Magic Knife)
Deals 2014 Magic Damage to an enemy and 3022 Magic Damage to enemies between you and them on return.

Wield Soul | Wield Soul (Magical Soul)
Deals 5607 Magic Damage to an enemy. Beneficial Signature and Affix scripts only apply to you.

Soul Burst | Soul Burst (Magical Burst)
Deals 3777 Magic Damage to enemies. Beneficial Signature and Affix scripts only apply to you.

Ulfsild's Contingency | Ulfsild's Contingency (Magical Contingency)
Deals 4155 Magic Damage to enemies. Beneficial Signature and Affix scripts only apply to you.

Banner Bearer | Banner Bearer (Magical Banner)
Increases Magical damage done by 6%. Reduce your Health, Magicka, and Stamina Recovery by 33%.

Trample | Trample (Magical Trample)
Deals 6233 Magic Damage to all enemies.

## Notes
- The Daedric text on the script reads "Magicka".
