# Online:Script Vendors Rotating Stock/2024

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Script_Vendors_Rotating_Stock/2024
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Scripts, used in Scribing, can be purchased from Chronicler Firandil in The Scholarium for gold and from Filer Ool in the Infinite Archive for Archival Fortunes. Both of these merchants carry a rotating stock of scripts that changes every Tuesday.

The Sigil of the Luminary Gryphon is required for the purchase of Focus scripts; the Sigil of the Luminary Dragon is required for the purchase of Signature scripts; and the Sigil of the Luminary Netch is required for the purchase of Affix scripts. Only a character who has not yet learnt a particular script is able to purchase it from either of these vendors.

## Prices
The cost of a script is greater for its first copy, while subsequent copies (for other characters) are offered at a significant discount. The chart below details the prices.
