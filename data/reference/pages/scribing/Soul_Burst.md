# Online:Soul Burst

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Soul_Burst
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

:Unleash a powerful burst of soul magic around you.

Soul Burst is a Scribed skill Grimoire, on the Soul Magic skill line. It is given away for free by completing The Wing of the Crow, the final quest in the Scribing quest line. Alternatively, it can be purchased for 10,000 from Chronicler Firandil, provided another character on your account has completed the quest line. The name of the scribed skill changes depending upon your choice of Focus Script.

- Cast Time: Instant
- Target: Area
- Radius: 8 meters
