# Online:Generate Ultimate

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Generate_Ultimate
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Generate Ultimate is a Focus Script used in Scribing. It can be earned by completing Mages Guild daily quests, DLC daily delve quests, or Rewards for the Worthy chests from earning Alliance Points. Or it may be purchased from Chronicler Firandil in The Scholarium for 40,000 (8,000) or Filer Ool in the Infinite Archive for 15,000 (3,000). (The prices are lower if another character has already learned the script.)

colspan=2|Grimoire | Effects

Mender's Bond | Mender's Bond
Transfers 1 Ultimate from you to the ally every 1 second.

Torchbearer | Torchbearer (Heroic Torch)
Generates 1 Ultimate to you and your allies with each sweep. Beneficial Signature and Affix scripts apply to you and your allies.

## Notes
- The Daedric text on the script reads "Ultimate".
