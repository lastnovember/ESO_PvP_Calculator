# Online:Taunt (script)

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Taunt_(script)
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Taunt is a Focus Script used in Scribing. It can be earned by completing Mages Guild daily quests, DLC daily delve quests, or Rewards for the Worthy chests from earning Alliance Points. Or it may be purchased from Chronicler Firandil in The Scholarium for 40,000 (8,000) or Filer Ool in the Infinite Archive for 15,000 (3,000). (The prices are lower if another character has already learned the script.)

colspan=2|Grimoire | Effects

Shield Throw | Shield Throw (Goading Throw)
Deals 2145 Physical Damage to an enemy and taunts them for 15 seconds.

Vault | Vault (Goading Vault)
Deals 1888 Physical Damage to enemies and taunts one of them to attack you for 15 seconds. Beneficial Signature and Affix scripts apply only to you.

## Notes
- The Daedric text on the script reads "Taunt".
