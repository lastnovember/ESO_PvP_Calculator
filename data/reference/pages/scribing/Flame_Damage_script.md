# Online:Flame Damage (script)

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Flame_Damage_(script)
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Flame Damage is a Focus Script used in Scribing. It can be earned by completing Mages Guild daily quests, DLC daily delve quests, or Rewards for the Worthy chests from earning Alliance Points. Or it may be purchased from Chronicler Firandil in The Scholarium for 40,000 (8,000) or Filer Ool in the Infinite Archive for 15,000 (3,000). (The prices are lower if another character has already learned the script.)

colspan=2|Grimoire | Effects

Elemental Explosion | Elemental Explosion (Fiery Explosion)
Deals 8225 Flame Damage to enemies.

Vault | Vault (Fiery Vault)
Deals 3777 Flame Damage to enemies. Beneficial Signature and Affix scripts apply only to you.

Torchbearer | Torchbearer (Fiery Torch)
Deals 3186 Flame Damage to enemies with each sweep. Beneficial Signature and Affix scripts only apply to you.

Ulfsild's Contingency | Ulfsild's Contingency (Fiery Contingency)
Deals 4155 Flame Damage to enemies. Beneficial Signature and Affix scripts only apply to you.

Banner Bearer | Banner Bearer (Fiery Banner)
Increases damage done with damage over time effects by 6%. Reduce your Health, Magicka, and Stamina Recovery by 33%.
