# Online:Cowardice (script)

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Cowardice_(script)
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Cowardice is an Affix Script used in Scribing. It can be earned by completing Undaunted daily delve quests, DLC daily incursion quests, or daily district quests in the Imperial City. Or it may be purchased from Chronicler Firandil in The Scholarium for 20,000 (4,000) or Filer Ool in the Infinite Archive for 10,000 (2,000). (The prices are lower if another character has already learned the script.)

colspan=2|Grimoire | Effects

Elemental Explosion | Elemental Explosion
Afflicts enemies with Minor Cowardice for 20 seconds, reducing Weapon and Spell Damage by 215.

Shield Throw | Shield Throw
Afflicts the enemy with Major Cowardice for 10 seconds, reducing Weapon and Spell Damage by 430.

rowspan=2|Wield Soul | rowspan=2|Wield Soul
Afflicts the enemy with Major Cowardice for 10 seconds, reducing Weapon and Spell Damage by 430.

Incompatible with Warding Soul and Healing Soul

rowspan=2|Torchbearer | rowspan=2|Torchbearer
Afflicts enemies with Minor Cowardice for 20 seconds with each sweep, reducing Weapon and Spell Damage by 215.

Incompatible with Heroic Torch and Healing Torch

Trample | Trample
Afflicts enemies with Minor Cowardice for 20 seconds, reducing Weapon and Spell Damage by 215.

## Notes
- The Daedric text on the script reads "Fear".
