# Online:Defile (script)

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Defile_(script)
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Defile is an Affix Script used in Scribing. It can be earned by completing Undaunted daily delve quests, DLC daily incursion quests, or daily district quests in the Imperial City. Or it may be purchased from Chronicler Firandil in The Scholarium for 20,000 (4,000) or Filer Ool in the Infinite Archive for 10,000 (2,000). (The prices are lower if another character has already learned the script.)

colspan=2|Grimoire | Effects

Elemental Explosion | Elemental Explosion
Afflicts enemies with Minor Defile, which reduces healing received and damage shield strength by 6% for 20 seconds.

rowspan=2|Wield Soul | rowspan=2|Wield Soul
Afflicts the enemy with Major Defile, which reduces healing received and damage shield strength by 12% for 10 seconds.

Incompatible with Warding Soul and Healing Soul

Trample | Trample
Afflicts enemies with Minor Defile, which reduces healing received and damage shield strength by 6% for 20 seconds.
