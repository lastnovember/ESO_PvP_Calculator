# Online:Lifesteal

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Lifesteal
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Lifesteal is an Affix Script used in Scribing. It can be earned by completing Undaunted daily delve quests, DLC daily incursion quests, or daily district quests in the Imperial City. Or it may be purchased from Chronicler Firandil in The Scholarium for 20,000 (4,000) or Filer Ool in the Infinite Archive for 10,000 (2,000). (The prices are lower if another character has already learned the script.)

colspan=2|Grimoire | Effects

Elemental Explosion | Elemental Explosion
Afflicts enemies with Minor Lifesteal for 20 seconds, healing attackers for 600 Health every 1 second.

Traveling Knife | Traveling Knife
Afflicts enemies with Minor Lifesteal for 20 seconds, healing attackers for 600 Health every 1 second.

rowspan=2|Vault | rowspan=2|Vault
Afflicts enemies with Minor Lifesteal for 20 seconds, healing attackers for 600 Health every 1 second.

Incompatible with Healing Vault

## Notes
- The Daedric text on the script reads "Steal Life".
