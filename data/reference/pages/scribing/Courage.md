# Online:Courage

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Courage
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Courage is an Affix Script used in Scribing. It can be earned by completing Undaunted daily delve quests, DLC daily incursion quests, or daily district quests in the Imperial City. Or it may be purchased from Chronicler Firandil in The Scholarium for 20,000 (4,000) or Filer Ool in the Infinite Archive for 10,000 (2,000). (The prices are lower if another character has already learned the script.)

colspan=2|Grimoire | Effects

Mender's Bond | Mender's Bond
Grants Minor Courage to the ally and allies in the link for 10 seconds, increasing Weapon and Spell Damage by 215.

Soul Burst | Soul Burst
Grants Minor Courage for 20 seconds, increasing Weapon and Spell Damage by 215.

Banner Bearer | Banner Bearer
Grants Minor Courage, increasing Weapon and Spell Damage by 215.

## Notes
- The Daedric text on the script reads "Cou", but is cut off before the remainder of the word, presumably "Courage".
