# Online:Immobilize (script)

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Immobilize_(script)
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Immobilize is a Focus Script used in Scribing. It can be earned by completing Mages Guild daily quests, DLC daily delve quests, or Rewards for the Worthy chests from earning Alliance Points. Or it may be purchased from Chronicler Firandil in The Scholarium for 40,000 (8,000) or Filer Ool in the Infinite Archive for 15,000 (3,000). (The prices are lower if another character has already learned the script.)

colspan=2|Grimoire | Effects

Mender's Bond | Mender's Bond (Binding Bond)
Removes and grants immunity to snares and other immobilizations for 1 second to the ally and other allies in the link.

Shield Throw | Shield Throw (Binding Throw)
Deals 2145 Physical Damage to an enemy and immobilizes them for 3 seconds.

Vault | Vault (Binding Vault)
Deals 1888 Physical Damage to enemies and immobilizes them for 3 seconds. Beneficial Signature and Affix scripts apply only to you.

Soul Burst | Soul Burst (Binding Burst)
Immobilizes enemies for 3 seconds. Beneficial Signature and Affix scripts only apply to you.

Ulfsild's Contingency | Ulfsild's Contingency (Binding Contingency)
Deals 2077 Magic Damage to enemies and immobilizes them for 6 seconds. Beneficial Signature and Affix scripts only apply to you.

Banner Bearer | Banner Bearer (Binding Banner)
You cleanse yourself of snares and immobilizations and are immune to them while the banner is active. Cost: 720 Health and 720 Stamina every two seconds

## Notes
- The Daedric text on the script reads "Immobilize". (It is written twice, one of them rotated upside-down.)
