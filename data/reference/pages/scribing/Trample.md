# Online:Trample

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Trample
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

:Pierce the air with a shrill whistle, calling your mount forth to trample enemies in a line. This ability cannot be re-activated while your mount is already attacking.

Trample is a Scribed skill Grimoire on the Assault skill line. It can be purchased after earning the Alliance War Skill Master achievement from Chronicler Firandil in the Scholarium once per character.

It costs 50,000 the first time, and 10,000 for other characters on your account after that. The name of the scribed skill changes depending upon your choice of Focus Script.

Trample will call your active Mount to run forward from your position in a line, damaging all enemies in its path. The other effects will depend upon your choice of scripts. It works with all mounts, even the toy stick mounts, but the effects are the same regardless.

- Cast Time: 1.5 seconds.
- Target: Area
- Radius: 5 meters
- Duration: 2 seconds

## Focus Scripts
colspan=2|Script | Skill Name | Effects | Notes

Disease Damage | Disease Damage | Pestilent Trample

- Deals 6233 Disease Damage to all enemies.
- Cost: 3046 Stamina

Dispel | Dispel | Dispelling Trample

- Deals 3116 Physical Damage to all enemies. Removes up to 1 damage shield effect from enemy players.
- Cost: 3553 Magicka
This script has no effect (beyond the base physical damage) against NPC enemies, so it should only be used in PvP.

Knockback | Knockback | Repelling Trample

- Deals 3116 Physical Damage to all enemies and knocks them back 8 meters.
- Cost: 3553 Magicka
If you line this up properly, enemies may be knocked back further into the path of the trample, causing them to be hit and knocked back again. It's possible to hit the same target up to 4 times in a single cast if cast from close range with sufficient room behind them that they aren't stopped by a wall or other obstacle.

Magic Damage | Magic Damage | Magical Trample

- Deals 6233 Magic Damage to all enemies.
- Cost: 3046 Magicka

Physical Damage | Physical Damage | Sundering Trample

- Deals 6233 Physical Damage to all enemies.
- Cost: 2479 Stamina

Stun | Stun | Dazing Trample

- Deals 3116 Physical Damage to all enemies and stuns them for 3 seconds.
- Cost: 2092 Stamina

Trauma | Trauma | Traumatic Trample

- Afflicts all enemies with 3116 Healing Absorption for 3 seconds.
- Cost: 3553 Magicka
