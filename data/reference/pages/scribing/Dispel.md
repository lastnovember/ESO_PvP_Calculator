# Online:Dispel

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Dispel
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Dispel is a Focus Script used in Scribing. It can be earned by completing Mages Guild daily quests, DLC daily delve quests, or Rewards for the Worthy chests from earning Alliance Points. Or it may be purchased from Chronicler Firandil in The Scholarium for 40,000 (8,000) or Filer Ool in the Infinite Archive for 15,000 (3,000). (The prices are lower if another character has already learned the script.)

colspan=2|Grimoire | Effects

Elemental Explosion | Elemental Explosion (Dispelling Explosion)
Remove up to 3 enemy area effect abilities.

Trample | Trample (Dispelling Trample)
Deals 3116 Physical Damage to all enemies. Removes up to 1 damage shield effect from enemy players. †
† Note that this has no effect on NPC enemies using Damage Shields, only other players.

## Notes
- The Daedric text on the script reads "Dispel".
