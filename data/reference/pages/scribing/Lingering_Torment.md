# Online:Lingering Torment

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Lingering_Torment

Lingering Torment is a Signature Script used in Scribing. It is earned for free during The Second Era of Scribing quest.

## Notes
- In all cases, if a damage type is selected as the Focus Script, the Damage Over Time effect will use that damage type instead of Magic or Physical.
- The Daedric text on the script reads "Time Pain".
