# Online:Shock Damage (script)

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Shock_Damage_(script)
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Shock Damage is a Focus Script used in Scribing. It can be earned by completing Mages Guild daily quests, DLC daily delve quests, or Rewards for the Worthy chests from earning Alliance Points. Or it may be purchased from Chronicler Firandil in The Scholarium for 40,000 (8,000) or Filer Ool in the Infinite Archive for 15,000 (3,000). (The prices are lower if another character has already learned the script.)

colspan=2|Grimoire | Effects

Elemental Explosion | Elemental Explosion (Shocking Explosion)
Deals 8225 Shock Damage to enemies.

Wield Soul | Wield Soul (Shocking Soul)
Deals 5607 Shock Damage to an enemy. Beneficial Signature and Affix scripts only apply to you.

Soul Burst | Soul Burst (Shocking Burst)
Deals 3777 Shock Damage to enemies. Beneficial Signature and Affix scripts only apply to you.

Banner Bearer | Banner Bearer (Shocking Banner)
Increases damage done with direct damage by 6%. Reduce your Health, Magicka, and Stamina Recovery by 33%.

Ulfsild's Contingency | Ulfsild's Contingency (Shocking Contigency)
Deals 4155 Shock damage to enemies. Beneficial Signature and Affix scripts only apply to you.
