# Online:Druid's Resurgence

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Druid's_Resurgence
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Druid's Resurgence is a Signature Script used in Scribing. It can be found on a bookshelf at the top of the left-hand stairs in the Wayrest Mages Guild.

colspan=2|Grimoire | Effects

Elemental Explosion | Elemental Explosion
Restores 441 Magicka to you per enemy hit.

Mender's Bond | Mender's Bond
Restores 74 Magicka and 74 Stamina every second for 6 seconds to the ally and other allies when they leave the link.

Shield Throw | Shield Throw
Increases your Health, Magicka, and Stamina Recovery by 152 for 10 seconds on return.

Smash | Smash
Restores 1104 Stamina over 10 seconds.

Vault | Vault
Restores 1050 Stamina over 10 seconds.

Wield Soul | Wield Soul
Restores 1000 Magicka and 1000 Stamina.

Torchbearer | Torchbearer
Restores 74 Stamina with each sweep.

Banner Bearer | Banner Bearer
Increases Health, Magicka, and Stamina Recovery of other group members by 139.

## Notes
- The Daedric text on the script reads "Restore".
