# Online:Anchorite's Cruelty

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Anchorite's_Cruelty
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Anchorite's Cruelty is a Signature Script used in Scribing. It can be earned by completing Fighters Guild daily quests, DLC daily world boss quests, or by completing town quests in Cyrodiil. Or it may be purchased from Chronicler Firandil in The Scholarium for 60,000 (12,000) or Filer Ool in the Infinite Archive for 20,000 (4,000). (The prices are lower if another character has already learned the script.)

colspan=2|Grimoire | Effects

rowspan=2|Wield Soul | rowspan=2|Wield Soul
Consumes a Soul Gem to deal 1.5% of the enemy's Max Health as Oblivion Damage every 1 second for 5 seconds, up to a maximum of 1500 damage. Deals up to 100% more damage to enemies under 50% Health and can occur once every 5 seconds.

Incompatible with Warding Soul and Healing Soul

rowspan=2|Soul Burst | rowspan=2|Soul Burst
Consumes a Soul Gem to deal 2.8% of the enemy's Max Health as Oblivion Damage every 2 seconds for 10 seconds, up to a maximum of 2800 damage. Deals up to 100% more damage to enemies under 50% Health, and can occur once every 10 seconds.

Incompatible with Warding Burst and Healing Burst

## Notes
- The Daedric text on the script reads "Soul Collapse".
