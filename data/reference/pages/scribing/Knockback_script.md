# Online:Knockback (script)

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Knockback_(script)
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Knockback is a Focus Script used in Scribing. It can be earned by completing Mages Guild daily quests, DLC daily delve quests, or Rewards for the Worthy chests from earning Alliance Points. Or it may be purchased from Chronicler Firandil in The Scholarium for 40,000 (8,000) or Filer Ool in the Infinite Archive for 15,000 (3,000). (The prices are lower if another character has already learned the script.)

colspan=2|Grimoire | Effects

Elemental Explosion | Elemental Explosion (Repelling Explosion)
Knocks Enemies back 12 meters.

Shield Throw | Shield Throw (Repelling Throw)
Deals 2145 Physical Damage to an enemy and knocks them back 8 meters.

Smash | Smash (Repelling Smash)
Deals 2055 Physical Damage to enemies and knocks them back 8 meters and stuns them for 1.8 seconds. Beneficial Signature and Affix scripts only apply to you.

Torchbearer | Torchbearer (Repelling Torch)
Knocks enemies back 5 meters with each sweep.

Ulfsild's Contingency | Ulfsild's Contingency (Repelling Contingency)
Knocks enemies back 8 meters. Beneficial Signature and Affix scripts only apply to you.

Trample | Trample (Repelling Trample)
Deals 3116 Physical Damage to all enemies and knocks them back 8 meters.

## Notes
- The Daedric text on the script reads "Push".
