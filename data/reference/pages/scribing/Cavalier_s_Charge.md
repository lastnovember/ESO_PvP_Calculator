# Online:Cavalier's Charge

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Cavalier's_Charge
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Cavalier's Charge is a Signature Script used in Scribing. It originally was unique to the Trample Grimoire, but now has a second use with Banner Bearer. It can be earned by completing Fighters Guild daily quests, DLC daily world boss quests, or by completing town quests in Cyrodiil. Or it may be purchased from Chronicler Firandil in The Scholarium for 60,000 (12,000) or Filer Ool in the Infinite Archive for 20,000 (4,000). (The prices are lower if another character has already learned the script.)

colspan=2|Grimoire | Effects

Banner Bearer | Banner Bearer
Increase your Weapon and Spell Damage by 6 for every 1% bonus Movement Speed, up to a max of 450.

Trample | Trample
Increases the damage done by this ability as it travels outwards, increasing by 20% every 0.5 seconds.

## Notes
- The Daedric text on the script reads "Charge Damage".
