# Online:Hunter's Snare

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Hunter's_Snare
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Hunter's Snare is a Signature Script used in Scribing. It can be found on a small table outside the Mournhold Mages Guild, at the northeastern end of the covered walkway encircling the courtyard.

Reduces the Movement Speed of enemies by 30% for 4 seconds.
