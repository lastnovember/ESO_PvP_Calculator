# Online:Intellect and Endurance

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Intellect_and_Endurance
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Intellect and Endurance is an Affix Script used in Scribing. It can be earned by completing Undaunted daily delve quests, DLC daily incursion quests, or daily district quests in the Imperial City. Or it may be purchased from Chronicler Firandil in The Scholarium for 20,000 (4,000) or Filer Ool in the Infinite Archive for 10,000 (2,000). (The prices are lower if another character has already learned the script.)

colspan=2|Grimoire | Effects

Mender's Bond | Mender's Bond
Grants Minor Intellect and Minor Endurance to the ally and allies in the link for 10 seconds, increasing Magicka and Stamina Recovery by 15%.

Wield Soul | Wield Soul
Grants Minor Intellect and Minor Endurance for 20 seconds, increasing Magicka and Stamina Recovery by 15%.

Soul Burst | Soul Burst
Grants Major Intellect and Major Endurance, increasing Magicka and Stamina Recovery by 30% for 10 seconds.

Ulfsild's Contingency | Ulfsild's Contingency
Grants Minor Intellect and Minor Endurance for 22 seconds, increasing Magicka and Stamina Recovery by 15%.

Banner Bearer | Banner Bearer
Grants Minor Intellect and Minor Endurance, increasing Magicka and Stamina Recovery by 15%.

## Notes
- The Daedric text on the script appears to read "Intellect". (It is blurry and cut off on the edge. No sign of "Endurance" appears on the script.)
