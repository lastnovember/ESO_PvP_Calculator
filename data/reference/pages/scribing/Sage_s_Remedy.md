# Online:Sage's Remedy

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Sage's_Remedy
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Sage's Remedy is a Signature Script used in Scribing. It is earned for free by completing The Wing of the Indrik quest.

colspan=2|Grimoire | Effects

Mender's Bond | Mender's Bond
Heals the ally and other allies when they leave the link for 12774 Health over 5 seconds.

Shield Throw | Shield Throw
Heals you for 2591 Health on return, scaling off your Max Health.

Smash | Smash
Heals for 3500 Health over 10 seconds.

Vault | Vault
Heals for 3775 Health over 10 seconds.

Wield Soul | Wield Soul
Heals for 3815 Health over 10 seconds.

Soul Burst | Soul Burst
Heals for 3775 Health over 10 seconds.

Ulfsild's Contingency | Ulfsild's Contingency
Heals for 4530 Health over 12 seconds.

Banner Bearer | Banner Bearer
Heals for 949 Health every 2 seconds.

## Notes
