# Online:Poison Damage (script)

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Poison_Damage_(script)
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Poison Damage is a Focus Script used in Scribing. It can be earned by completing Mages Guild daily quests, DLC daily delve quests, or Rewards for the Worthy chests from earning Alliance Points. Or it may be purchased from Chronicler Firandil in The Scholarium for 40,000 (8,000) or Filer Ool in the Infinite Archive for 15,000 (3,000). (The prices are lower if another character has already learned the script.)

colspan=2|Grimoire | Effects

Traveling Knife | Traveling Knife (Venomous Knife)
Deals 2014 Physical Damage to an enemy and 3022 Poison Damage to enemies between you and them on return.

Vault | Vault (Venomous Vault)
Deals 3777 Poison Damage to enemies. Beneficial Signature and Affix scripts apply only to you.

## Notes
- The Daedric text on the script reads "Poison".
