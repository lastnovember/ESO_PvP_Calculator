# Online:Trauma

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Trauma
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Trauma is a Focus Script used in Scribing. It can be earned by completing Mages Guild daily quests, DLC daily delve quests, or Rewards for the Worthy chests from earning Alliance Points. Or it may be purchased from Chronicler Firandil in The Scholarium for 40,000 (8,000) or Filer Ool in the Infinite Archive for 15,000 (3,000). (The prices are lower if another character has already learned the script.)

colspan=2|Grimoire | Effects

Elemental Explosion | Elemental Explosion (Traumatic Explosion)
Afflicts enemies with 4032 Healing Absorption for 3 seconds.

Trample | Trample (Traumatic Trample)
Afflicts all enemies with 3116 Healing Absorption for 3 seconds.

## Notes
- The Daedric text on the script reads "Trauma".
