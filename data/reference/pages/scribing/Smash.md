# Online:Smash

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Smash
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

:Drag your weapon along the ground to smash a cone in front of you.

Smash is a Scribed skill Grimoire, on the Two Handed skill line. It can be purchased after earning the Two-Handed Master achievement from Chronicler Firandil in the Scholarium once per character.
It costs 50,000 the first time, and 10,000 for other characters on your account after that. The name of the scribed skill changes depending upon your choice of Focus Script.

- Cast Time: 0.6 seconds.
- Target: Cone
- Radius: 8 meters
