# Online:Empower (script)

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Empower_(script)
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Empower is an Affix Script used in Scribing. It can be earned by completing Undaunted daily delve quests, DLC daily incursion quests, or daily district quests in the Imperial City. Or it may be purchased from Chronicler Firandil in The Scholarium for 20,000 (4,000) or Filer Ool in the Infinite Archive for 10,000 (2,000). (The prices are lower if another character has already learned the script.)

colspan=2|Grimoire | Effects

Mender's Bond | Mender's Bond
Grants Empower to the ally and allies in the link for 10 seconds, increasing the damage of Heavy Attacks against monsters by 70%.

Wield Soul | Wield Soul
Grants Empower for 10 seconds, increasing the damage of Heavy Attacks against monsters by 70%.
