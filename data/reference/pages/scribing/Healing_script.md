# Online:Healing (script)

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Healing_(script)
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Healing is a Focus Script used in Scribing. It is earned for free by completing The Wing of the Indrik quest. It is generally incompatible with offensive Signature and Affix scripts.

colspan=2|Grimoire | Effects

Mender's Bond | Mender's Bond
Heals the ally and other allies in the link for 754 Health every 1 second.

Smash | Smash (Healing Smash)
Heals you and your allies for 6047 Health. Beneficial Signature and Affix scripts only apply to you.

Vault | Vault (Healing Vault)
Heals you and your allies for 3777 Health. Beneficial Signature and Affix scripts apply to you and your allies.

Wield Soul | Wield Soul (Healing Soul)
Heals you or an ally for 9570 Health. Beneficial Signature and Affix scripts apply to you or an ally.

Soul Burst | Soul Burst (Healing Burst)
Heals you and your allies for 5666 Health. Beneficial and Affix scripts apply to you and your allies.

Torchbearer | Torchbearer (Healing Torch)
Heals you and your allies for 4343 Health with each sweep. Beneficial Signature and Affix scripts apply to you and your allies.

Ulfsild's Contingency | Ulfsild's Contingency (Healing Contingency)
Heals your and your allies for 6233 Health. Beneficial Signature and Affix scripts apply to you and your allies.
