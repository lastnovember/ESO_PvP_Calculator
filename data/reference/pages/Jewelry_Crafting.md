# Online:Jewelry Crafting

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Jewelry_Crafting
Fetched: 2026-09-19
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Jewelry Crafting is a skill line that was released along with the Summerset Chapter in June 2018. It for the first time allows you to craft and improve Jewelry, including the ability to make jewelry items in all Craftable Sets. It works in a similar fashion to the Blacksmithing, Clothing, and Woodworking lines, with researchable traits and associated trait items, harvestable leveled materials, and crafting sites added in all regular crafting areas.

In addition to rings and necklaces, the Jewelry Crafting skill allows you to craft furnishings for your home.

## Learning
The Jewelry Crafting skill line is granted the first time you activate a jewelry station (assuming you have access to the Summerset Chapter). You can increase your skill by crafting or deconstructing jewelry, and by completing Jewelry Crafting Writs, which are available after completing Jewelry Crafting Certification. In addition, you can earn free levels in the skill line by reading Skill Books, which may be found randomly on bookshelves anywhere. The skill books which increase Jewelry Crafting are:
- Jewels of Yokuda
- The Pretension of High Elf Jewelry

## Materials
There are five levels of basic materials needed for jewelry crafting. These materials can be found at Jewelry Seam nodes everywhere. These are most commonly found near mountains and large rocks, which can be harvested for Dust. 10 Dust can be refined into 7-10 Ounces, which may then be used to craft Necklaces and Rings. Additionally, you may find Ochre, which is used to make furnishing items.

## Traits
Just like the other equipment crafting skills, there will be nine traits which can be researched and crafted. Three of these will be those that currently exist, and there will be six new traits. Unlike the other skills, however, the materials for these traits are mostly found in pulverized form, and must be refined to produce usable materials. Just like refinable style materials, 10 pulverized trait materials can be refined into 1 usable item.

Finding jewelry with the newer traits to research is not as simple as with other gear. Just as before, you will normally only find items with the original three traits: Robust, Arcane, and Healthy, as well as non-craftable Ornate. In addition, jewelry crafting writs will now sometimes give you Intricate jewelry, just like the other gear crafting writs. For the others, you will need to do a bit more work, or get other players to craft items for you to research. The chart below shows how to acquire the rarer traits:

Trait | Source

Bloodthirsty | Rarely found as rewards for weekly Trials quests. Following Mythic antiquities have Bloodthirsty: Belharza's Band, Death Dealer's Fete, Malacath's Band of Brutality, Markyn Ring of Majesty, Ring of the Pale Order rings, Shapeshifter's Chain and Velothi Ur-Mage's Amulet necklaces.

Harmony | Rarely acquired in Undaunted chests

Infused | Rarely found in Psijic Portals

Triune | Rarely found from daily board quests in Cyrodiil. A guaranteed Triune ring is obtained as a reward from the Uxark's Treasure quest, and Torc of Tonal Constancy mythic antiquity is a Triune necklace.

Protective | Ring from completing the Summerset main quest, Necklace from Psijic Order quests. Sea-Serpent's Coil mythic antiquity is a Protective necklace.

Swift | Rarely found from Jewelry Crafting Writs. A guaranteed Swift ring is obtained as a reward from the Path of the Hidden Moon quest, or the Ring of the Wild Hunt mythic antiquity.

## Platings
Jewelry items can now also be upgraded in quality, similar to other equipment. This is done using Platings. Just like other equipment upgrading, you will need to use multiple Platings to ensure that you do not destroy the item in the process. The Platings Expertise skill will allow you to safely upgrade items with fewer Platings.

Prior to Update 40, Platings were mostly found as Grains, 10 of which needed to be refined in order to produce 1 Plating. To make up for the resulting scarcity of Platings, half as many were required to upgrade an item.
