Shadowhide is a crafting material obtained from animals in any zone by characters with Rank 9 in the Tailoring passive or CP90 to 140. One animal may yield one to two samples of the raw material, Shadowhide Scraps. Once ten scrap pieces are collected, they can be refined into seven to ten samples of the processed material, Shadowhide. Refined shadowhide is used for making medium shadowhide armor at any clothing station. For you to be able to create shadowhide armor, your Tailoring ability has to be rank 9.
