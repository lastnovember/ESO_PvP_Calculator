## Quick Walkthrough
# Enter the portal and talk to Maeroth.
# (Optional) Talk to Gelthior.
# Find Treethane Iirdel in Cormount.
# Secure a meeting with Lorchon (talking to sympathizers using the right codes/phrases).
# Meet the Treethane.
# Meet Lorchon at the Butcher's Ground.
# Find Gorinir in the Ancient Cave.
# Defend the Dominion camps.
# Meet Forinor at the west bridge.
# Talk to Maeroth
# (Choice) Be kind to Gelthior's sons or harsh

## Detailed Walkthrough

## Notes
- Enathras won't exist in the town before starting the quest or reaching the related objective.
- Iirdel's journal won't exist before starting the quest or reaching the related objective.
- While the cave is accessible before starting the quest, Gorinir won't exist inside until starting the quest and reaching the related objective.
- The enemies summoned at the Dominion camps don't give experience or loot when killed.
- Following completion of the quest, the Blacksap enemies are replaced by bandits using the same classes.

## Bugs
Exiting the portal without speaking with Maeroth during the second visit to the prison places you at Elden Root instead of Cormount.
Sometimes Gorinir disappears when you first enter the room
- log-out/login again should fix this.
