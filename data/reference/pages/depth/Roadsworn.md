# Online:Roadsworn

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Roadsworn
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Roadsworn are a group of highway robbers that are based in Glenumbra and are led by Abrasia. They are known to steal from caravans and raid trading posts. Some of its members are former Red Rooks.
