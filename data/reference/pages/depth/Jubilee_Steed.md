The Jubilee Steed is awarded for completing the Jubilee Confetti Conveyor achievement. Its default name is "High Trot".

The achievement is earned by using 25 Jubilee Confetti Packs from your inventory: once this is done, the mount will appear in your collections window. The achievement Jubilee Confetti Conveyor has a counter which tracks how many have been used. Jubilee Confetti Packs are acquired by opening Glorious Anniversary Jubilee Gift Boxes, of which you can earn one per day as a reward for the first daily quest you complete while the Anniversary Jubilee event is active.

In 2024, the Anniversary Jubilee ran for 20 days from April 4 to April 23, which was not quite long enough to acquire all 25 confetti packs. If you earned tickets every day that the event was active, you would need to buy 5 additional confetti packs from the Impresario for 3 each to supplement your final total to earn the achievement.

## Physical Appearance
The Jubilee Steed is a white horse with blue eyes and a dark, silvery mane done up in braids not unlike the White-Gold Imperial Courser. Confetti constantly whirls around the horse. The saddle is festooned with red diamonds and intricate gold filigree. A red cushion decorated with diamond stitchwork sewn in gold thread sits atop the saddle.

The bridle and reins are made of brown leather with a single gold stripe running through the middle. A red diamond is mounted in the forehead on the bridle. While mounted, the rider holds a gold plate with a slice of cake from the 2016-2018 Jubilee Cake.

## Notes
- A number of players from the PC-NA server were awarded 25 Jubilee Confetti Packs on May 6, 2024, allowing them to instantly unlock the Jubilee Steed. This formed part of the compensation provided to those players in relation to a rollback, which resulted in affected accounts being locked for the entirety of the 2024 Anniversary Jubilee event.
