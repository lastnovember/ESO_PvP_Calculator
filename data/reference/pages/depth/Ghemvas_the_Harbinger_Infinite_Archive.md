Ghemvas the Harbinger is a Ruinach who can be encountered as a boss within the Infinite Archive. His original manifestation can be found at The Shattered Xanmeer.

## Skills and Abilities
Not all of these are necessarily seen in the IA
;Summon Portal to the Deadlands: Ghemvas will summon four Portals to the Deadlands, Daedric gates which in turn summon more Daedra. They will appear on the sides of the platform. Ghemvas is invulnerable as long as these portals exist, and continues to do considerable damage in this state, so you will need to destroy them as quickly as possible.
;Kynreeve Summon: Periodically, he will resummon the Kynreeves (Kynreeve of Embers, Kynreeve of Scorching, and Kynreeve of Wildfire) if they are slain.
;Daedric Asphyxiation: Ghemvas will grab a player with his magic and begin strangling them midair, fire will then cover the victim before dropping them. The initial attack causes heavy damage and there is a flame DoT afterwards.
;Fire Tornadoes: Ghemvas causes a pair of flaming tornadoes to circle around him. These do moderate Fire Damage.
