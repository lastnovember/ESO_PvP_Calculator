## Detailed Walkthrough
In various locations around Sorrow's Friend (such as next to a tree at the skirmish site or near the northwestern oddity)other locations?, you may come across a sketchbook:

After accepting the quest, you will receive the Dirt-Stained Sketchbook, and your map will be marked with three circles: one located at each of the oases, and the third centered on the building to the southeast of the Brazen Murklight. Visit these areas, and once you are at the correct coordinates, you will get the option to use the sketchbook. More precise locations are as follows:
- In front of the stalls facing north where the Brazen Murklight spawns
- Eastern side the southern rooftop oasis, looking down at the Brazen Murklight area
- Northern rooftop oasis, on the western side, overlooking the Baleful Darkess-effected area

Once you have completed the missing sketches, head to the Northern Rooftop Oasis and place the sketchbook in the collection box:
:
:
:

You will receive some gold, some faction favor, and a Curated Tamriel Reward Coffer.
