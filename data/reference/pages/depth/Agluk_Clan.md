# Online:Agluk Clan

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Agluk_Clan
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Agluk are an Orc clan. Mainly found in the Wrothgarian Mountains, a group of them is found, co-habiting and infighting with the Morkul Clan, in Torog's Spite, a cave located near the city of Evermore. There, they exact their revenge against the Bretons for the sacking of Orsinium.
