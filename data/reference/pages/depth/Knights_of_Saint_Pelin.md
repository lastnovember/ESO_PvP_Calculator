# Online:Knights of Saint Pelin

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Knights_of_Saint_Pelin
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Knights of Saint Pelin are the knightly order of Evermore and Queen Arzhela. They take their name and inspiration from Saint Pelin, a beadle at the Temple of Stendarr. Pelin sacrificed himself to buy time for the soldiers of Bangkorai Garrison to regroup and defeat the vampiric horde known as the Gray Host.
