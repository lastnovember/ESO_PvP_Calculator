Guild Members were unnamed members of the Fighters Guild that could be found patrolling, training or relaxing on the premises of the Windhelm guildhall. They had no unique dialogue.

In Update 6 every Guild Member was substituted with a unique NPC. These were Hrolfgald, Cooks-the-Meat, Nirthongoth and Arns Tharam.
