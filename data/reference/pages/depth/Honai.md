Honai is a Redguard armorer who can be found at the Armory Merchants marketplace located at the Northern High Rock Gate in Cyrodiil.

## Dialogue
When approached, she praises her goods:
:"Heavy armor repels heavy blows. What foe delivers the lovetaps that lighter armor can turn away?"
