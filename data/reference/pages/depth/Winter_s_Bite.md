# Online:Winter's Bite

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Winter's_Bite
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Winter's Bite is one of three class skill lines available to Sharp-as-Night. Abilities in this line focus on avoiding incoming damage. It is roughly equivalent to the Winter's Embrace skill line for Warden players.

## Skill Perks
The Winter's Bite skill line is immediately available to Sharp, and is leveled up as he gains levels.
