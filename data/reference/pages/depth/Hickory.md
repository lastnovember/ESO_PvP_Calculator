Hickory logs are a dull brown, earthy color, with a visible bark pattern. They can be found in any zone by players with Woodworking Rank 4 or levels 36 to 44. One log can be harvested for three to four samples of the raw material, Rough Hickory. Once ten samples of rough wood are collected, they can be refined into seven to ten pieces of the processed material, Sanded Hickory. Hickory staves and bows, as well as hickory shields, are crafted from the refined hickory wood at any woodworking station. In order for you to create hickory gear, your Woodworking ability has to be rank 4.
