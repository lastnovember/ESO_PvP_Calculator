Betty Netches are flying tentacled creatures native to Morrowind, though some have apparently been transported to Coldharbour due to the ongoing Planemeld.

## Related Quests
- Mine All Mine
- Pledge: Darkshade Caverns I

## Skills and Abilities
Betty netches are considered elite creatures (despite showing being normal rank), and are thus immune to all forms of crowd control effects.
;Slap: The netch swings a tentacle at the target as basic melee attack, dealing minor physical damage.
;Thrust: The netch lashes out with all four of its tentacles, doing moderate physical damage as well as staggering the target. This can be blocked to negate the stagger.
