Dancing Spiders are fabricants resembling spiders designed by Sotha Sil. They attack primarily by launching themselves at an enemy and pinning them to the floor. Firepot Spiders that have blown their lids will turn into dancing spiders thereafter.

## Related Quests
- In Search of a Sponsor

## Skills and Abilities
;Swipe: A basic melee attack dealing low physical damage.
;Leap: The spider leaps at you from far away, dealing moderate physical damage and knocking you down. Once on the ground, the spider stabs you repeatedly with its legs, dealing low physical damage for every hit. This attack can be blocked to set the spider off-balance.
;Spinning Jump: The spider jumps sideways, spinning its legs around in the air, indicated by a traveling circular AoE, dealing moderate physical damage.

## Bugs
The SFX of them stabbing you with their legs are still heard even if 'Leap' is blocked and they are set-off balance instead of following up with the rest of the attack animation.
