Camandar, also self-titled as Camandar the Amazing, is the Maormer leader of the Sea Vipers found in the ruins of Bewan. He has a chance to drop Sea Greaves of the Veiled Heritance, a unique item in the Armor of the Veiled Heritance item set.

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Strike:A basic melee attack dealing minor physical damage.
;Shock Aura:The thundermaul shocks the ground around itself as indicated by a red circle dealing moderate shock damage. This can be interrupted to knock the thundermaul off balance.
;Lightning Form:The thundermaul casts Lightning Form on itself, gaining Major Resolve (which increases its armor by 5948) while creating small areas under the target which after a small delay deal shock damage. (On some thundermauls only)
;Uppercut:A melee attack that deals moderate physical damage and knocks you back if it connects. This attack can be blocked to knock the thundermaul off balance.

## Bugs
Despite being an elite rank enemy, Camandar is vulnerable to crowd control.
