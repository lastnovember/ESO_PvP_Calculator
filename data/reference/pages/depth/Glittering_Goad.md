# Online:Glittering Goad

Online:Glittering Goad (set)
The Glittering Goad is one of the three factions in the Night Market, which get their name from the wealth they accumulate. They handle most of the auctions and stalls in the Market and are led Tachien, an eminent auctioneer of Fargrave.

The Goad is a meritocracy that believes that does not care if you are mortal, only that you have the skills to contribute.
