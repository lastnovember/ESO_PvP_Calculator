# Online:Go Forth and Conquer!

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Go_Forth_and_Conquer!
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Go Forth and Conquer! is a system mail sent by Battlemaster Rivyn to players who placed in the top 100 leaderboard positions for each Battleground (Deathmatch, Flag Games, and Land Grab). Each one will contain a Transmutation Geode and a random Legendary item from any Battlegrounds set. Up to three can be earned in every scoring period (one week, ending at 2300 UTC every Sunday and starting at 1200 UTC every Monday) for each of the Battlegrounds. Its text is as follows:
Go Forth and Conquer!

Greetings [character name]!

The Circle of Champions has heard the rumors; gladiators shudder when they hear your name, the crowd roars when you enter the ring, the bards sing your praises after each match! I know a true Battleground champion when I see one and am honored to offer you this reward. May it help you to continue on your path towards greatness. Stay strong, Champion!

-Battlemaster Rivyn
