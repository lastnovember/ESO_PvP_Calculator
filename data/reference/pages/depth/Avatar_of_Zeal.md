The Avatar of Zeal is an icebreath indrik and optional mini-boss in Black Drake Villa. It is found in the caves under a trap door opened by standing on four pressure plates at once, in the first area where you fight enemies in the dungeon.

The Avatar of Zeal can also be fought in the Infinite Archive.

## Skills and Abilities
;Head Beam: A basic ranged attack that deals high magical damage.
;Ethereal Stomp: The Avatar stomps the ground and releases two spectral images of itself that stampede out from its location, dealing very high magical damage and knocking down those hit.
;Teleport: The Avatar fades out of existence and teleports to any of the far ends of the arena.
