Dark Guardians can also be seen inside the purified Xith-Izkul Sanctuary, where they are hostile towards you.

## Related Quests
- A Ghost from the Past

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Strike: A basic melee attack dealing minor physical damage.
;Power Bash: A heavy shield bash dealing moderate physical damage, stunning and setting off balance. This can be blocked to knock the Dark Guardian off balance instead.
;Brace:The Dark Guardian may also briefly block with its shield, reducing damage taken greatly. Players who attempt melee heavy attacks on the Dark Guardian while it is blocking will be set off balance.

## Notes
- Dark Guardians also appear in Oblivion and Legends.

## Bugs
Despite being elite rank enemies in Xith-Izkul Sanctuary, they are vulnerable to crowd control.
