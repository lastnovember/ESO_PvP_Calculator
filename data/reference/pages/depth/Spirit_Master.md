# Online:Spirit Master

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Spirit_Master
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Spirit Masters are a class of hostile humanoid NPCs that attack using frost and alteration magic and summoning abilities. They are usually equipped with a staff with pale blue effects on the end.

## Skills and Abilities
;Ice Arrow: Basic ranged attack dealing minor frost damage.
;Burdening Eye: The enemy summons a Burdening Eye that moves toward a player.
;Spell Absorption: The enemy channels a protection spell on an ally that reduces its damage taken by 80% from Magical Attacks (Magic, Flame, Frost and Shock damage). This spell can be interrupted to knock the enemy off balance.
;Run Away!:When too close to them, the spirit master backs up to put some distance between their target and themselves.
