Pact Quickblades were Argonian melee soldiers of the Ebonheart Pact found near Fort Virak, and inside after certain objectives during the related quests. They were all deleted in a very early update to be replaced by several similar NPCs that had a unique name and position, such as Miun-Eidu and Vushtulm.
