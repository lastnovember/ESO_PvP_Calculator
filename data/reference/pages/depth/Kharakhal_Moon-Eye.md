Kharakhal Moon-Eye is a Senche-raht and leader of the Ruckus, one of the factions that currently explore and plunder the Night Market when it appears. He can be found within the Ruckus' enclave and is who you approach for work within the faction.

Moon-Eye's past is unknown, but there are many conflicting stories about it.

## Quest-Related Events

### Story Quests

#### Those Who Would Rule
When you meet the Curator, he will suggest that you join one of the three factions before exploring the Night Market's districts. He then sends you to speak with the leaders of the Ruckus, Thousand Eyes and the Glittering Goad. Kharakhal Moon-Eye can be found on a stone platform overseeing the spar between Mad Mina and Vesvahz.

Speak with Moon-Eye to see what the Ruckus is about and what they have to offer you:
:"So the new blood trickles up to the Ruckus. Welcome. The Curator sent you to seek a right fit, yes? Kharakhal admits the Eyes and the Goad are more established. But my squalls know the oldest towers topple with the right amount of force."
:Is that what you plan to do? Topple the other factions?
:"The Ruckus topples any who stand in our way. Do you think those Daedra are pleased to contend with mortals outside their control? We carved our way in. Took this ground. Claimed this place as our own. We welcome any who want to help us keep it."
:And what's in it for me if I join you?
:"This one can promise you no more than what the others offer. The Ruckus is for those with spine enough to fight for our place here. With grit enough to make it hurt when they try to take it back. It's your choice if that sounds like you, new blood."
:Understood. I'll think about it.
:"If your interests in joining are more worldly, see our merchant, Najirra. Her wares are for members only. Find her near the plaza stairs. Remember, new blood-the only permission you need to join the Ruckus is your own."

Before you leave, you can continue talking to him and ask some questions:
:I want to know more about the Ruckus.
:"Very well. Ask what you will."
:Why is doing business in the Night Market so important to you?
::"Where else can one face such strength and danger? Where else can one explore such a mysterious anomaly? There is no place like the Night Market. And it sickens Kharakhal to think of it monopolized by Daedra and those beholden to them."
:What do you think of the other factions?
::"This one respects them as equals to the Ruckus. For now. When the Night Market closes, we will see who emerges as the better. But Kharakhal has reasons to keep spoils of the Market from either of their clutches."
::Like what?
::"Molto's rodents pursue secret knowledge just to hoard it, never use it. What is lost to them stays squirreled away. Unused. Wasted. Tachien's gluttony for gold and prestige is less objectionable. But her gloating … fajh'na! Unbearable."
:I want to know about you. What's your story?
::"It has been some time since someone asked this one for his story. Every time the Night Market opens, the weakest take up quills and try to further stain Kharakhal's reputation. But most never notice new smears on what is already painted black."
::So what's the truth, then?
::"Heh. You are not entitled to something just because you ask for it nicely. Kharakhal is generous with wealth and opportunity, but keeps his true origins for himself. Read up on the many libels against me and pick a favorite. That can be your truth."
::What are you trying to hide?
::"Isn't it funny how quickly deception flags attention? Even if it turns out to be harmless. You never know what might slip past you when you're looking the other way. That's a lesson on keeping power. Kharakhal won't even charge you for it."

After you have spoken to the leaders, you can return to the Curator. He will tell you to pledge yourself to one of the faction's banners to join the group and then speak with it's leader. If you join the Ruckus, you can meet with Kharakhal Moon-Eye again to complete the quest:
:"Welcome back, new blood. More questions? Or are you a master of your own fate, ready to throw in with the Ruckus? Every new voice adds strength to our uproar. A voice like yours might even rise above the rest."
:Yes, I chose to join the Ruckus.
:"Kharakhal thought he sensed a fellow disruptor. Glad to see this one was right. Your reward for joining includes keys to the Night's Den. The Curator can tell you more about that curious abode. We're going to do great things together, squall."

#### Make Some Noise
After you have pledged yourself to the Ruckus, Moon-Eye will want to give you a trial run to see how you handle yourself in the districts:
:"Kharakhal is very pleased to count you as one of us. You seem eager for work. But this one has lost one too many overzealous squalls to the perils of the districts. It would be wise for you to learn what exactly makes them so dangerous."
:How do you suggest I do that?
:"Find Grasp-Kyn Stryd, a Dremora at the door to Sorrow's Friend. She knows the district well and has work that benefits the Ruckus. Do as she asks and you will learn firsthand what perils await you. No Daedric pact. Simply an exchange of favors."
:And after that?
:"If you make it back alive, she will direct you to another steward to acquaint you with a different district. And so on, until you get a taste of all three this Night Market has to offer. Ready to make some noise, squall?"
:I'm ready. I'll go find Grasp-Kyn Stryd at the door to Sorrow's Friend.

Once you accept the task, you can ask Moon-Eye some questions before you leave:
:"Much like the districts themselves, the stewards you will meet are unaligned with any faction. You can imagine they need all the help they can get. Happily, we both understand that eschewing pacts does not mean refusing the occasional partnership."
:What can you tell me about Grasp-Kyn Stryd?
:"Grasp-Kyn pay little mind to us mortals so long as we follow the rules. Stryd is capricious, to be sure, but holds no hidden motives and treats each faction equally. Fulfill her work request and try not to bother her with too many questions."
:And the other stewards?
:"Marifah minds the Skittering Precinct. Kharakhal asked her to join the Ruckus many times, but she insists on working alone. To her own peril. The Parch contact is a strange Skaafin called Myshka. Treat him as you like. He just wants attention."

With each steward you will be given a task in the district they watch over. Once are done, you can return to Moon-Eye where you find the Curator talking to him:
:The Curator: "How is the new recruit settling in?"
:Kharakhal Moon-Eye: "Time will tell. But this one has a good feeling."

Talk with Moon-Eye to complete the quest:
:"The Curator and I were just speaking of you. It certainly seems like you made the rounds. But Kharakhal knows appearances can deceive. Did you do as this one asked?"
:I met with Stryd, Marifah, and Myshka. I accomplished what they asked in their respective districts.
:"We don't reward working alone in the Ruckus. Neither do we condemn it. But Kharakhal likes to see how you stand on your own before letting you run with the rest. It sounds like you represented us well, squall."
:Does this mean I can take on more work for the Ruckus?

Leaving and return to the conversation at this point:
:"You met with our contacts to get experience in each of the districts. Kharakhal thinks you are more prepared than ever to face the dangers of the Night Market."
:Does this mean I can take on more work for the Ruckus?
:"Kharakhal was hoping you would ask that. Now that you know the perils of each district, you are more than ready to face the monsters and mysteries that await within. But let's see you paid first, squall. Welcome to the Ruckus! Here's your reward."

Once you have completed the quest, you will unlock the Ruckus daily quests.

### Daily Quests
Kharakhal will greet you with one of the following lines when offering a daily quest:
:"Looking to make some noise, squall? Kharakhal has work that needs doing."

:"You looking for work? Kharakhal just had a job come in for someone like you."

:"Don't just stand around, squall. Kharakhal has work for you. You want a job or not?"

Talking to Kharakhal after starting a daily quest:
:"Go show them your strength, squall. And if you happen to die, at least go out with a ruckus."

:"The districts never sleep. The Ruckus can't afford to. Get out there and make some noise."

Greeting Kharakhal after completing the task:
:"Kharakhal was just thinking of you. How did it go?"

:"Ah, the favorite squall of Kharakhal. Returned victorious, I hope?"

:"Ahh, one of my strongest returns. How was the job?"

Talking to him when the daily has been completed for the day:
:"You did enough today, squall. The others could stand to do half as much as you. Come back later. Walk your own path for now."

:"Find other ways to sharpen your claws, squall. Kharakhal has no work left. But come back again soon, yes?"

:"My moon-eye sees future work for you, squall. But for now, our docket is empty. Go do as your heart pleases."

#### Argent Eradication
If you approach Kharakhal when this daily is available, he may say:
:"We need squalls looking for a real brawl. This one has work for you."

Speak with him to ask about the task:
:What's the job?
:"Argent enemies manifest in the districts. Powerful foes presiding over their brazen underlings. And they hold treasure that might fall into the hands of the Goad or the Eyes. Kharakhal would have you claim them for the Ruckus instead."
:Do you have a specific argent in mind?
:"This one is not picky. Any argent will do. If you cannot find one, you must kill two brazen to lure out their argent. Bring back something good, yes? Something to remind the others that the Ruckus plays rough and keeps what it earns."
:All right, I'll go kill an argent and report back.

After you agree to the job, you can ask Moon-Eye some questions:
:Remind me how to find argent enemies.
:"You must lure them out. Kill two brazen enemies, then search for pyres lit between them. Your argent will be there. So be sure you're ready to fight."
:Do I need a group to kill an argent enemy?
:"Kharakhal gives no extra credit for facing such trials alone. It is your choice, squall. And if passers-by decide to join your fight, you will have little choice in the matter. Remember, the only one worth proving anything to is yourself."

Once you have defeated an argent enemy in the districts, you can return to Kharakhal Moon-Eye:
:I killed an argent in one of the districts. The job is done.
:"A show of strength worth celebrating. Or in Kharakhal's case, worth paying for. Your reward, squall. May your pockets be overfull-but not so full they keep you resting on your laurels."

#### Brazen Eradication
If you approach Kharakhal when this daily is available, he may say:
:"Seeking squalls ready to dust up the districts. Come to Kharakhal."

Speak with him to ask about the task:
:What's the job?
:"The Ruckus cuts its teeth killing brazen enemies and claiming their spoils. We've grown scarce of such treasures lately. Kill some brazen, loot whatever they leave behind, and report back, will you?"
:Are there particular brazen you want me to dispose of?
:"Your choice, squall. Dispatching any of them helps in its own way. The work just needs doing. Come back when you're done and Kharakhal will give you something shiny."
:All right, I'll go kill some brazen enemies and report back.

After you agree to the job, you can ask Moon-Eye some questions:
:How do I find brazen enemies?
:"There are four that can be found per district. You should not have to venture long. Brazen are distinct and significant threats. A tier above the common rabble. But not particularly rare."
:Can I defeat them on my own?
:"That choice is yours, squall. Your reward will not change either way. However, defeating one is not quite the show of strength as defeating, say, an argent. Which is why Kharakhal asks that you slay several brazen to keep your claws sharp."

Once you have defeated two brazen enemies in the districts, you can return to Kharakhal Moon-Eye:
:I killed as many brazen enemies as you asked of me.
:"No small feat. Kharakhal wishes he could have seen your work with his own eyes. But to lead a faction is to be resigned to its enclave. A reward for your vicarious exploits. Spend it as you wish."

#### Fleet and Swift
As you approach Moon-Eye while this quest is available, you may hear him say:
:"This one needs our swiftest. Kharakhal has work for you."

Talk to him to learn about the job:
:What's the job?
:"Kharakhal is concerned that members of the Ruckus are not as fleet of foot as they should be. When racing against the other factions for relics, every second counts. I need someone to lead the way in a daring race. Perhaps this interests you?"
:What does this daring race entail?
:"Each one is unique and requires more than jumping crates and climbing a few ladders. Let Kharakhal mark these courses on your map. Go set an example for the rest of the Ruckus and this one will reward you."
:All right, I'll go complete a daring race and report back.

After you agree to the job, you can ask Moon-Eye some questions:
:Anything I should know about these daring races?
:"Death is no less an obstacle when contending with speed rather than battle. Daring races are a test of both swiftness and action. You may face with rifts in need of closing, pests to be exterminated, or hazards that cut you in twain."
:Will I be racing against people in other factions?
:

Once you have completed a race in the districts, you can return to Moon-Eye:
:The job is done. I completed a daring race.
:"Word reached Kharakhal of this feat before you did. You may be fast, but gossip is faster. Still, you inspired our lesser louts. Just as this one desired. Your reward. Spend it as fast or slow as you like."

#### Keen of Mind
As you approach Moon-Eye while this quest is available, you may hear him say:
:"Got work for a squall with a keen mind. What few of you there may be."

Talk to him to learn about the job:
:What's the job?
:"The Goad and Eyes have nearly monopolized oddities in the districts. Shameful. It goes without saying that we attract more brawn than brains. But you're a clever one, aren't you? You could represent the Ruckus in one of these strange puzzles."
:Maybe. Where can I find an oddity?
:"Kharakhal will mark them on your map. You need only help solve one, squall. No need to overspend what we keep in short supply. Go quick, stay sharp, and report back once you unravel the tangles of this mystery. Kharakhal will have a reward for you."
:All right, I'll go investigate and help solve an oddity in the districts.

After you agree to the job, you can ask Moon-Eye some questions:
:What's an oddity, exactly?
:"Curious puzzles we find in the district. They require focus, concentration, and a quick wit. Search for strange arrangements or unusual configurations of things. Objects. Creatures. Stones. Lights. Oddities are locks and your mind is the key."
:Why is it so important to you that someone from the Ruckus figure out this oddity?
:"The Eyes and Goad don't expect us to be bright. And with good reason. Hard heads, harder natures. If our stock can outwit theirs, it could be very demoralizing. It doesn't matter how clever your solution is, squall. Just that you beat them to it."

Once you have solved a puzzle in the districts, you can return to Moon-Eye:
:The oddity was no match for me. It's done.
:"Appearances can be deceiving, but Kharakhal supposes you are as clever as you look. Good work, squall. Keep it up and you'll become a common name around here."

#### Representing the Faction
As you approach Moon-Eye while this quest is available, you may hear him say:
:"Looking for a squall with some initiative. This one has work for you."

Talk to him to learn about the job:
:What's the job?
:"Skirmishes manifest chaos in the districts. We need someone to jump into the fray. Represent our strength. Remind those other factions we're strong enough to make a place for ourselves among the Daedra. You feel like looking for trouble, squall?"
:Where can I find a skirmish?
:"This one will send you with a map showing the location of each skirmish. Go find trouble in your favorite district. Join the fight. And come back with a story when you're done."
:I'll find a skirmish in the districts and participate on behalf of the Ruckus.

After you agree to the job, you can ask Moon-Eye some questions:
:What are skirmishes, anyway?
:"Swiftly emerging threats that need many hands to dispatch. Fighters from all over a given district work together to drive back overwhelming forces. They are loud and chaotic. This one's fondness of skirmishes is what gave our faction its name."
:So we don't compete against the other factions in a skirmish?
:"We're always competing, squall. And if it were a mere show of might, the Ruckus would rule the districts. But, no-a skirmish is no competition. Factions play nice there, as usual. Think of it like a party. But shorter. And far more lethal."

Once you have completed a district skirmish, you can return to Moon-Eye:
:I found a skirmish and showed the might of the Ruckus.
:"Good. You reminded the Eyes and Goad that this market is not simply the domain of the Daedra. We fight for our place here, and we fight just as hard as any in the districts. A reward, then. For your show of strength."

## Correspondence
Once the current Night Market has finished, if you were part of the Ruckus, Kharakhal will send you a letter and rewards relating to the faction competition. Its contents will depend on how your faction fared:

If the Ruckus were in third place:
For Your Deeds in the Night Market

We squalls raised a ruckus, but the Goad and Eyes outpaced us this Night Market.

The reassurance we can take from our rivals' victories is how hard they worked for them. They considered us enough of a threat to make sure they stayed ahead. Trust Kharakhal-this has not always been the case. They had little need to try before.

Perhaps next time we will taste victory. If this one happens to find you among the Ruckus again, then I am all the more certain it will happen.

Until then, accept these spoils for your hard work. Take what you are owed, both from Kharakhal, and from wherever you wander until the Night Market opens again.

From Kharakhal Moon-Eye, dictated to the hand of Gish-lai
You will be rewarded 1,500 Gold and a Ruckus Reward Coffer.

If the Ruckus were in second place:
For Your Success in the Night Marketplain

This one has heard grousing from our squalls, but second-best in a competition of three is still a victory. May you count two blessings: knowing that our hard work does not go unrecognized by our rivals, and knowing exactly how much harder we must fight to emerge victorious next Night Market.

For his part, Kharakhal will count a third blessing: that you pledged yourself as one of us. Take this reward as thanks for all your efforts.

Should you pledge another patron when the market opens next, make sure they know how fortunate they are to have you. And if they don't listen, make them listen.

From Kharakhal Moon-Eye, dictated to the hand of Gish-lai
You will be rewarded Gold and a Exceptional Ruckus Reward Coffer.

If the Ruckus won the faction competition:
For Our Victory in the Night Marketplain

This one never doubted we could outpace the Eyes and the Goad. We are at our strongest when we are underestimated. We are at our loudest when we feel we are not heard. We are victors when our defeat seems a certainty. And a defeat it would have been were it not for you, squall.

Now that the others know what a threat we can be, the competition next Night Market will be all the more grueling. Relish victory, but do not be content to let your claws go dull.

…Unless you plan to pledge to the Eyes or the Goad. If that is your plan, take this reward and then rest all you like.

From Kharakhal Moon-Eye, dictated to the hand of Gish-lai
You will be rewarded Gold and a Coveted Ruckus Reward Coffer.
