Jee-Jee is an Argonian member of the Ciphers of the Eye who can be found with Flothlozel in the northern part of Cipher's Midden.

## Dialogue
During A Hidden Fate, the pair will comment of the arrival of new visitors and that Apocrypha is experiencing changes:
:Flothlozel: "Rather strange. New faces appear but not ones I have known and not known in time. A presence beneath us shifts, a shift anew that reveals untold changes to our plane."
:Jee-Jee: "A sour briny sea, with a smell of pungent earthy fungi. Sits heavy upon my tongue, and brings memories of a bad place."

Speaking to her:
:"You smell strongly of the saline soil of the Peninsula, a land I knew so long ago. Bad memories."

During Conclave of Fate, the duo will feel a sense of dread in the atmosphere:
:Flothlozel: "The creeping nightmare seeps into our plane. It lingers at the back of my mind and floods my thoughts."
:Jee-Jee: "Mm-my spines feel it as well, the lingering dark is ushered in. A smell that's carried on the wind is faint but sinister."

Speaking to her:
:"A scent surrounds you, a taste of the nightmares distilled. You are dealing with such things that those of us here choose not to, or are bound by duty to not interfere."

During A Calamity of Fate:
:Jee-Jee: "I taste the acrid tang of malice and mightmare on the wind. And a wafting stench of pesilent malignance. My spines tremble and my head swims."
:Flothlozel: "It has infiltrated, the waking dream. A stranger now creeps through our tomes and fates. But we will continue as is our duty, or perish as it required."

Speaking to her:
:"The waxing of power amasses as those who seek to pry convene around you."

While you are investigating the sudden Daedra attacks in Cipher's Midden during An Unhealthy Fate, you will hear Jee-Jee and Flothlozel comment on it:
:Jee-Jee: "Hmm- the stench recedes and the fear falters. The taste is no more but lingers slightly on the air."
:Flothlozel: "The eyes still have turned to Apocrypha, not yet turned from our plane. Not yet."

Speaking to her:
:"Though the danger wanes, more lurks yet still. My spines sense the presence."

After Chronicle of Fate is completed:
:Flothlozel: "A lull has come. I am not convinced it will stay, but perhaps it is the beginning of the end that starts anew."
:Jee-Jee: "Yes, it is quiet, but on the air I still taste a final note. Unclear. More is to come."

Speaking with her:
:"The crisis averted, my spines tell me, for now. You are indeed a brave soul."
