# Online:Bonecrafter

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Bonecrafter
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Shamans are a class of hostile humanoid NPCs that attack using both melee and nature magic abilities. They are usually equipped with a dagger, but sometimes wield staves instead. They are basically a combination of Shaman and Necromancer NPC classes abilities, with an additional one.

## Skills and Abilities
;Coiled Lash: A basic ranged attack dealing minor magic damage.
;Quick Strike: A basic melee attack dealing minor physical damage.
;Summon Aura of Protection: The Bonecrafter summons an Aura of Protection totem that reduces the damage taken of nearby allies by 25%. The totem can be destroyed in five hits from any source or one heavy attack. Destroying the totem will stun all enemies nearby and it bypasses crowd control immunities, excepted those of rank.
;Summon the Dead:The Bonecrafter often begins combat by summoning a zombie, a bloodfiend, a bloodfiend reaver, a Bone Reaver or a Bone Flayer. If the summoned creature killed, another one will be summoned to replace it. The summon scales with the difficulty, faction, and location of the summoner. The summoned creature will have the same health as the current health of their summoner but only to a maximum of 80% of the summoner's Max Health, and have extreme damage resistance during the summoning animation. Bonecrafters take 20% more damage while a summon is active. (Either this or "Summon Abomination")
;Summon Abomination:The Bonecrafter summons a flesh atronach, bone colossus or ghost to fight, spawning with the summoner's current Health. The Bonecrafter's Physical Resistance and Spell Resistance are reduced to 0 while a summon is active. The summon will die if the Bonecrafter is killed. (Either this or "Summon the Dead")
;Bone Surge:The Bonecrafter casts a Bone Shield-like ability on themselves which reduces their melee damage taken by 20% until this disappears.
;Run Away!:When too close to them, the Bonecrafter backs up to put some distance between their target and themselves.
;Bone Cage:The Bonecrafter casts a rune on the ground that activates after a while, dealing very low magic damage and rooting players who step in it. This can be interrupted to knock the Bonecrafter off balance. (On some Bonecrafters)
