The Crowborne Horror is a Wraith-of-Crows group boss faced during the quest Plucking the Crow that takes place only during the Witches Festival. It can be found in the Garden of Shadows, a pocket realm of Evergloam that can be accessed via the Effigy Shrine in Olyve's Brewery.

It can summon Ominous Gravestones and Wailing Wraiths. The Ominous Gravestone will continuously spawn Skeleton archers and Zombies until they are destroyed. The amount of summoned adds increases as the Horror's health decreases.

The Crowborne Horror tends a solitary cauldron containing some Witchemother's Brew in the center of the arena. It will repeatedly gain immunity and fly above it during the fight, causing poisons to spew out and increasing the level of danger on the battlefield.

It drops nothing.

## Skills and Abilities

As a boss rank enemy, the Crowborne Horror is immune to crowd control. All summons die when the Crowborne Horror is defeated.
;Chop: A basic melee attack dealing moderate physical damage.
;Seeker Crows: A basic ranged attack dealing moderate magic damage.
;Carrion Swarm: The Crowborne Horror spews a cursed area with crows that slowly follows a player around, dealing continuous high magic damage over time while also launching groups of crows around periodically, dealing moderate magic damage in a small area each.
;Vampiric Harvest: A two time ability. The Crowborne Horror first jumps then flies at a target, knocking down targets in its wake which deals high physical damage. Any target hit will be linked to the Crowborne Horror via a pink/purple beam which heals it over 7 ticks of 17,619 Health, so 123,837 Health in total. This can be blocked to negate the stagger and the healing.
;Witches' Brew:At 90%, 70% and 40% Health, the Crowborne Horror goes to the center of the arena above the cauldron and flies still while being invincible for a moment. It unlocks "Witches' Brew Spider Venom" at 90%, both "Cleave/Ominous Gravestones" and "Witches' Brew Snake Venom" at 70%, and "Summon Wailing Wraith" at 40%. It directly summons two Ominous Gravestones at 70% and one Wailing Wraith at 40%. Each phase permanently increases the spawn rate of the green spiders and snakes.
;Witches' Brew Spider Venom/Witches' Brew Snake Venom: The cauldron will summon ghostly green spiders that move in a straight line away from the cauldron, surrounded by an area applying "Witches' Brew Spider Venom" which deals low poison damage over 4 seconds. The cauldron will later also summon ghostly green snakes with the same behavior as spiders that apply "Witches' Brew Snake Venom" which deals slightly more damage than "Witches' Brew Spider Venom".
;Cleave/Ominous Gravestones:The Crowborne Horror swings its scythe which sends areas (2 between 70% and 50% Health, 4 between 50% and 30% Health, 6 between 30% and 0% Health) in different directions around of it, each dealing moderate physical damage in their wake and creating an Ominous Gravestone when there disappear.
;Summon Wailing Wraith:The Crowborne Horror periodically summons a Wailing Wraith by throwing a light-blue flame ball to the ground. Only one can be up at a time.
