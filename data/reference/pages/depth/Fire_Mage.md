# Online:Fire Mage

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Fire_Mage
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Fire Mages are a class of hostile humanoid NPCs that attack using fire magic abilities. They are easily identified since they carry a staff with flame effects at the end.

## Skills and Abilities
;Flare:A basic ranged attack dealing minor flame damage.
;Heat Wave:The fire mage releases fire along the ground in the direction it is facing, dealing moderate flame damage to all targets hit. This can be interrupted to set the fire mage off balance. This cannot be blocked.
;Fire Rune:The fire mage shoots two fireballs in the air which form two fire runes when they land. The runes deal minor flame damage over time to all targets within the red circles. Players should be prepared to move as soon as the attack casts as one rune will always be cast on top of you.
;Run Away!:When too close to them, the fire mage backs up to put some distance between their target and themselves.
;Flaming Blink:The fire mage summons a flaming portal and enters it, using it to teleport a distance away. Standing near the portal as it opens deals damage. (Some fire mages starting with Northern Elsweyr and onwards)
