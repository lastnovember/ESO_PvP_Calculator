# Online:Garland Ring

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Garland_Ring
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Garland Ring is a secretive organization led by the Gardener. Its main goal is to protect the Aldmeri Dominion from internal threats of those that would misuse their power. The members are hand-picked by the Gardener and go by flower codenames, named after species with ranges that cover the entire Dominion. The Garland Ring have various "Greenhouses" around the Dominion that serve as safehouses. In recent times, they have clashed with the Ceythalmor.
