## Detailed Walkthrough
Once you have proven yourself capable, speak to your faction leader in the Starlit Plaza to collect one of five daily quests. Find Tachien in the Glittering Goad enclave to the southwest, Kharakhal Moon-Eye in The Ruckus enclave to the northeast, or Molto the Whisperer and his speaker Melandir in the Thousand Eyes enclave to the northwest.

Your faction leader will explain the task.

You can ask some questions to get more information before heading to the districts. The districts can be accessed through doors in the central Starlit Plaza. Enter one of the districts (The Parch, Skittering Precinct, or Sorrow's Friend). You can choose any of the three precincts regardless of your faction.

Keep in mind that the districts are designed for groups, so it may be wise to bring others along. You can find group members through the chat, guilds, or group finder tool. Daily faction leader quests can also be shared with group members, though the number of these you can have from a single faction leader at one time is limited.

Once you arrive in a district, the locations of the brazen enemies will be marked on your map. Head to one of these areas. The enemies are different in each of the three districts.

The two Brazen enemies in south Parch are Brazen Taupezu Azzida and Brazen Sellistrix. The two Brazen enemies in north Parch are Brazen Incineration Beetle and Brazen Doylemish Ironheart. See their pages for details and help killing them.

The two Brazen enemies in south Sorrow's Friend are Brazen Murklight and Brazen Scavenging Maw. The two Brazen enemies in north Sorrow's Friend are Brazen Plague of Crows and Brazen Kynmarcher Strix. See their pages for details and help killing them.

The two Brazen enemies in south Skittering Precinct are Brazen Votary of Velidreth and Brazen Dranos Velador. The two Brazen enemies in north Skittering Precinct are Brazen Khephidaen and Brazen Kraohl the Defiler. See their pages for details and help killing them.

When you have finished the task, leave the district and return to your faction leader to complete the quest and receive your reward coffer, faction favor, and gold.
