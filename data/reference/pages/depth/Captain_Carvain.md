Captain Carvain is a Nibenese knight acting under Euraxia Tharn's will. He and his forces have invaded Star Haven Adeptorium alongside Bahlokdaan.

## Related Quests
- Bright Moons, Warm Sands

## Quest-Related Events
You will first hear his voice when you enter the Catacombs.
:Captain Carvain: "Spread out! Search the area and kill anyone who gets in our way."

You will encounter Captain Carvain in the Tomb of the Grand Adepts, accosting Izjadi, and Runji. He scares Runji so badly, he faints. When you engage him in combat, he yells at you.
:Captain Carvain: "Another corpse for our army! How grand."

## Skills and Abilities
As an elite rank enemy, Captain Carvain is immune to crowd control.
;Quick Strike:A basic melee attack that does minor physical damage.
;Lava Whip:The enemy winds up a Lava whip that does moderate flame damage to the target. This attack can be blocked to set the enemy off balance.
;Heat Wave:The enemy releases fire along the ground in the direction it is facing. This attack does moderate flame damage to all targets hit by it. This spell can be interrupted to set the enemy off balance.
