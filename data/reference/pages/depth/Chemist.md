# Online:Chemist

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Chemist
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Chemists (or Alchemists) are a class of hostile humanoid NPCs who attack using a combination of chemical mixtures and their blades. They are garbed in light armor and wield a single sword. The class was introduced with Murkmire.

## Skills and Abilities
;Lop:A basic melee attack dealing minor physical damage.
;Chop:A basic melee attack dealing minor physical damage.
;Puncture:The enemy thrusts their weapon with disciplined precision at a target, dealing minor physical damage and afflicts the target with Major Breach for 15 seconds.
;Fiery Oil/Volatile Pool: The enemy throws a potion that sets the targeted area alight and bounces once forward and creates a second flame area. The pools deal moderate flame damage over time to all targets within the red circle.
;Blazing Tar:The enemy will cover their weapon in flaming oil, causing Lop and Chop to become Blazing Lop and Blazing Chop.
;Blazing Lop:A basic melee attack dealing minor physical damage and additional flame damage over time.
;Blazing Chop:A basic melee attack dealing minor physical damage and additional flame damage over time.
;Healing Fumes:The enemy waves a potion around to emit fumes that heal themselves or a nearby enemy. This ability is usually used when the target is on low health. This can be interrupted to set the enemy off balance. If the chemist heals an enemy, standing in the fumes heals you as well.
