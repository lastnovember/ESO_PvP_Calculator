Darien Gautier is a Breton guard who is initially encountered in Camlorn while retaking the city, and later assists with stopping Angof the Gravesinger and the Bloodthorn Cult in Glenumbra. Darien can also be met in Rivenspire where he acts as a bodyguard for Countess Eselde Tamrith while dealing with House Montclair's rebellion and attacks on the citizens.

He is the son of General Gautier and is a member of the Lion Guard, though later he signs up with the Fighters Guild. Darien is a recurring figure in the Daggerfall Covenant zones, and is revealed as something of an enigma: He has prophetic dreams about Coldharbour and the Dark Anchors, among other things.

Darien is later met on Stirk as part of the Mages and Fighters Guilds coalition, leading forces throughout the invasion of Molag Bal's realm. During the attack on the Planar Vortex, Darien disappears at the climax of the invasion's final battle, and is thought by most to be dead. However, during the Daedric War storyline in Summerset, Darien returns as the Golden Knight, a servant of Meridia, having been tasked with stopping the Triad's plot for the Crystal Tower.

A few years after these events, during the Season of the Worm Cult, Darien is summoned from the Colored Rooms using the Gift of Death, a formerly corrupted Light of Meridia. He is brought back to provide help against the resurgent Worm Cult and their plans for Solstice.

#### A Lingering Hope
Darien can be first found in the Camlorn church, with the other guards and civilians:
:Darien Gautier: "Lower your blades, friends. This one doesn't look like a werewolf to me."

Darien will give you the master key only if you can clear a route for the civilians to hide in the inn as a safe refuge:
:"What brings you to our pleasant little city? The war? The blood? The thrice-damned werewolves?"
:The Lion Guard holds the courtyard. Your father sent me to find you.
:"That's the first good news I've heard today. But if you're here to take me to my father, you can get that thought right out of your head. There are beautiful women in Camlorn that still need to be rescued."
:General Gautier sent me to retrieve your master key.
:"In all seriousness, I'm not doing anything before we find a safe refuge for the civilians hiding in the surrounding buildings. The inn nearby should be defensible enough and it's full of supplies. There's just one catch."
:What's the catch?

:"The inn's locked up tight and the innkeeper was killed trying to reach us. Get the key from the body and enter the inn, the shine a signal light. I'll rally the civilians and get them to safety. We'll talk about my key after that."

## Skills and Abilities
Need info from other quests
As a follower, Darien uses the following abilities:

### Summerset Quests
;Dawnbreaker: During quests where Darien Gautier is your follower as the Golden Knight (and in possession of Dawnbreaker), he has a unique synergy you can use called "Dawnbreaker". Darien will pose with the sword glowing upon his chest and upon synergy use, it releases a burst of purifying magic around him, dealing very high magic damage to nearby enemies as well as stunning them.

### The Gift of Death and Mor Naril
;Quick Strike: Basic melee attack dealing low physical damage.
;Heavy Attack: Charged melee attack dealing moderate physical damage.
;Empowered Bash: Darien does a Power Bash-like attack, knocking the enemy off-balance and dealing moderate damage split in 45% physical damage and 55% magic damage.
;Shield Defense: In "The Gift of Death", when hit by the Phalanx March attack, Darien raises his shield for a moment which creates an area of protection behind him, granting both of you immunity against the opponent's Phalanx March. In Mor Naril, Darien periodically casts this but it instead grants you extreme damage resistance against projectiles.

### Crossing the Wall
;Quick Strike: A basic melee attack that deals trivial physical damage.
;Ritual of Salvation:Darien casts a Circle of Protection-like area granting allies a very minor Damage Shield every second (Does not stack).

### The Worm Turns
;Quick Strike: A basic melee attack that deals 0 physical damage.

## Notes
- He is the author of The Journal of Darien Gautier, A Plea for Help, and Words of the Fallen.
- While introducing King Dynar to the Fighters Guild during The Army of Meridia, speaking with Darien after Dynar's appearance brings a slight change of text to the scene. Instead of addressing Dynar as the "last Ayleid king", he will simply refer to him as "the last Ayleid".
- Jeremy Sera, Lead Content Designer for ESO, revealed on 10th May 2016 that it wasn't until the very end of Coldharbour development that they decided Darien should disappear during The Final Assault. He also revealed that the Manor of Revelry quest What the Heart Wants was originally supposed to involve Darien, instead of Stibbons.
- Jason Barnes revealed on 9th September 2025 in an r/elderscrollsonline "Ask Us Anything" event that he and Bill Slavicsek (Darien's co-creators and writers) decided Darien's birthday is the 17th of First Seed and hinted at a funny behind-the-scenes reason for this choice.
- Darien mysteriously disappears in the quest The Final Assault, with Meridia not mentioning him and Vanus and Gabrielle not knowing what effect the teleportation spell had on him, his fate being unknown until his reappearance as Meridia's Golden Knight in the Summerset chapter. However, several hints were given before then:
- While liberating the town of Camlorn, you'll come across The Journal of Darien Gautier. In his journal he speaks of nightmares where he communes with a "glowing orb with light so intense that I have to shield my eyes". His nightmares also foretell of the Dark Anchors and of being in Coldharbour. Lastly he speaks of a "creature burst into flames as it drank my blood".
- During Mists of Corruption, Darien notices he's not sickened by the mysterious mist surrounding Cath Bedraud like others. He is puzzled by this and is without an explanation.
- During The Liberation of Northpoint when you rescue Darien from bloodfiends inside Northpoint, a bright flash of golden light kills the two bloodfiends attacking him and eliciting his response of "Well, that's never happened before." He also says the bloodfiends bit him, but by the time Countess Eselde arrives he didn't have a single scratch on him, suggesting a healing factor that makes him immune to vampirism.
- The note A Plea for HelpOrsinium-written by Darien-implies that he was transported to Meridia's realm, which can be mentioned to Gabrielle Benele if she is met in AnvilDark Brotherhood.
- The Grand PrognosticatorClockwork City also mentions a "golden knight" in a prophecy about Summerset, referring to Darien.
- Following his sacrifice to restore Dawnbreaker, further hints are once again provided as to his current status:
- After finishing the Summerset main quest, a book can be found upstairs in the Summerset palace called Words of the Fallen, written by Darien, explaining that he is in the Colored Rooms again and "his light is fading, the darkness pressing in".
- J'saad's NoteDragonhold mentions that Meridia is trying to find another knight to carry her light: "One who will not betray her, One who must correct a previous mistake."
- Ylahizu the Magnificent can summon a Lost Spirit that is wearing Darien's armor and wielding the Dawnbreaker.
- He is voiced by Jon Curry (IMDb), who previously voiced Malborn in Skyrim.
- Darien is also featured in Smolder Scrolls Online.
- Quilting circles in Camlorn and Rivenspire dedicated to Darien make comforters and quilts in his honor. These items can be found as Treasures in the world.
- Some of his correspondence to admirers can be found as Treasures in the game world.
- While speaking to him on Artaeum during the quest Lost in Translation, he mentions interacting with Golden Saints and how pleasant they were to look at while he was stuck in the Colored Rooms. However, Golden Saints are servants of Sheogorath, not Meridia.
- Meridia does have golden servants, the Aurorans, but they are not particularly appealing to look at as he mentions, so his dialogue line still wouldn't make much sense.
