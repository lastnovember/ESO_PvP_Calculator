Bloodfiends are feral vampires that can be found all around Tamriel.

Contact with Bloodfiends in the wilderness of Bangkorai, Reaper's March, and the Rift can cause you to contract vampirism. These groups only rarely spawn at night in specific locations within those zones.

## Skills and Abilities
;Rending Slash:The enemy hits you with its hands or claws, dealing minor physical damage.
;Slash:The enemy hits you with its hands or claws two times, dealing minor physical damage.
;Flurry:A series of four quick swipes in a cone in front of the enemy.
;Devastating Leap: The Bloodfiend jumps up in the air and leaps at the target, knocking them down and dealing moderate physical damage. This attack can be blocked to reduce the damage, preventing the knockdown and set the enemy off balance.
;Teleport Strike: The Bloodfiend briefly turns invisible, teleports behind you and attacks, dealing moderate physical damage before reappearing.
