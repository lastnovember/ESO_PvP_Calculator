The Ruckus (set)]]
The Ruckus is one of the three factions in the Night Market that are vying for control. Members are called "squalls" and their numbers consist of a mortal majority, though Daedra are permitted to join on a case-by-case basis. The Ruckus is led by the mysterious Kharakhal Moon-Eye and as a faction, they prize strength. They will offer protection in the Night Market for a cut of the profits.
