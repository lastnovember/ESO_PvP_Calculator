# Online:Lothid Clan

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Lothid_Clan
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Lothid Clan are an ancient clan of vampires based in Reachwind Depths around 2E 582. According to Adusa-daro and Kathad, until recent times, the Lothid Clan wasn't a problematic clan and mostly kept to themselves. But this has changed under the new leadership of Boss Trenlor, who has begun ordering his vampires to torment and reave across The Reach for his own gain and amusement.
