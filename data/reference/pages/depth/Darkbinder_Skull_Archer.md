Darkbinder Skull Archers are archer Darkbinders found throughout the Writhing Fortress.

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Shot:A basic ranged attack that does minor physical damage.
;Arrow Spray:The Darkbinder Skull Archer shoots a spray of arrows in the direction it is facing as indicated by a red cone. This attack does moderate physical damage as well as snares all enemies hit. This attack can be interrupted to set the Darkbinder Skull Archer off balance. Blocking also prevents the snare and reduces the damage. plain
;Volley:The Darkbinder Skull Archer fires continuously at an area as indicated by a red circle. This attack does moderate physical damage over time. This attack can be interrupted to set the Darkbinder Skull Archer off balance. plain
;Bubbling Acid:The Darkbinder Skull Archer throws a bottle on the ground which creates a small area that expulses projectiles creating small areas dealing continuous minor poison damage.
;Summon Monster:The Darkbinder Skull Archer periodically throws a Flamebreath Skull on the ground that animates and fights you.
