# Online:Gleaners of Aurbis

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Gleaners_of_Aurbis
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Gleaners of Aurbis are a group of extraplanar thieves and spies that work out of Fargrave. They specialize in localizing and extracting valuable resources and items and bringing them back to the city. They have a reciprocal arrangement with the Saraathu Tong who provide Gleaners with quick travel in and out of places quietly, though it's not explicit what the Gleaners provide to the Saraathu.
