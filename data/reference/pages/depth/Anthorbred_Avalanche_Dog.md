The Anthorbred Avalanche Dog is a dog pet. It was available in the Crown Store for 1,000 from July 9, 2020 to. Its default name is "Gertrude".

It was also available as an Epic-level reward in Celestial Crates. It returned to the Store from May 1 to May 4, 2023, where it was offered at a discounted 800 to ESO Plus Members.
