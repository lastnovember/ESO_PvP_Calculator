sign=Craft

desc=Increases your crafting inspiration gained by 10% per stage.

Inspiration Boost increases your crafting Inspiration gained by up to 30%. This accumulates with other Inspiration bonuses such as Craftsman and the passive boost during Zeal of Zenithar. Inspiration is a unique type of Experience point only used for Crafting.
