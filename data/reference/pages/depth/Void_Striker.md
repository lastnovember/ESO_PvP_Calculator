# Online:Void Striker

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Void_Striker
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Void Strikers are a class of hostile humanoid NPCs that attack using melee and purple-colored shadow abilities. They are usually equipped with a two-handed sword, battleaxe or mace. The class was introduced in Clockwork City.

## Skills and Abilities
;Shrike Bash: Basic melee attack with the pommel dealing low physical damage.
;Shrike Thrash:Basic melee attack like a whip dealing slightly more damage than "Shrike Bash".
;Shrike Assault:Basic spinning melee attack dealing slightly more damage than "Shrike Thrash"
;Crow Strike:Basic ranged attack dealing low magic damage.
;Gloam Slash:An Uppercut-like melee attack dealing moderate magic damage, knocking back and stunning for 2 seconds. This can be blocked to set the Void Striker off-balance instead.
;Binding Shadows: The void striker holds their weapons close to them and releases pulsing shadows along the ground, indicated by traveling red circles going in random directions, each dealing moderate magic damage and stunning.
;Shade Aura: The void striker plants their weapon into the ground and channel a spell, indicated by a large growing red circle. When reaching max size, the pool of shadows explodes, dealing moderate magic damage and stunning affected targets. Getting stunned by this automatically gives you some kind of crowd control immunity, but without the eponymous buff. Blocking negates the crowd control but doesn't reduce the damage taken. This can be interrupted to set the enemy off-balance.

## Bugs
Getting stunned by "Shade Aura" lacks the stunned animation on the player character (as in, missing the dazed and confused animation).
