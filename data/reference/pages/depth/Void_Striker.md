# Online:Void Striker

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Void_Striker
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Void Strikers are a class of hostile humanoid NPCs that attack using melee and purple-colored shadow abilities. They are usually equipped with a two-handed sword, battleaxe or mace. The class was introduced in Clockwork City.

## Skills and Abilities
;Gloam Slash: A basic melee attack that deals low magical damage.
;Shrike Bash: A basic melee attack that deals slightly more magical damage than "Gloam Slash".
;Binding Shadows: The void striker holds their weapons close to them and releases pulsing shadows along the ground, indicated by traveling red circles, going in random directions, dealing moderate magical damage and stunning each. Break Free can be used.
;Shade Aura: The void striker plants their weapon into the ground and channel a spell, indicated by a large growing red circle. When reaching max size, the pool of shadows explodes, dealing high magical damage and stunning affected targets. This can be interrupted to set the enemy off-balance.
