# Online:Iron Armor

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Iron_Armor
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

This is a listing of all standard Iron Armor in the game. These can be used with the Heavy Armor skill. Iron armor can be crafted via Blacksmithing from Iron Ingots obtained by refining Iron Ore. The listed armor values assume that the character is CP160; lower level characters may see higher values.
