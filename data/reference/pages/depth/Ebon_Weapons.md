# Online:Ebon Weapons

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Ebon_Weapons
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

This is a listing of all standard Ebon Weapons in the game. These can be crafted via Blacksmithing from Ebony Ingots obtained by refining Ebony Ore. It requires rank 5 in Metalworking. The listed damage values assume that the character is Level 50 CP160; lower level characters may see higher values.
