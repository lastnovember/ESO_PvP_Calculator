# Online:Diseased

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Diseased
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Nature's Grasp Diseased is a debilitating effect that can be applied by doing disease damage. It deals a single hit of disease damage to the enemy and every 4 seconds it can also burst in a 6 meter area around the enemy, dealing disease damage to all enemies, including the original target. It also applies Minor Defile to all targets hit, reducing their Healing received and Shields strength by 6% This status effect can be removed with a Cleansing effect.
