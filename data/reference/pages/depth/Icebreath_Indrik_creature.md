Icebreath Indriks were located at the Event Exchanges located outside the main city of each Alliance's starting zone. They were found in pens next to the Impresario alongside Nascent Indriks.

## Notes
- The Rimedusk Indrik pet is a smaller equivalent of the Icebreath Indrik.
