# Online:Nightwood Weapons

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Nightwood_Weapons
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

This is a listing of all standard Nightwood Weapons in the game. They can be crafted via Woodworking from Sanded Nightwood obtained by refining raw wood samples collected from Nightwood logs. It requires rank 9 in Woodworking. The listed damage values assume that the character is Level 50 CP160; lower level characters may see higher values.

Note that Destruction Staves come in three varieties, "Inferno", "Ice", and "Lightning". The values are identical. The only difference is the type of elemental damage they do, Flame Damage, Frost Damage, and Shock Damage respectively, and the secondary effects caused by certain skills. Restoration Staves also have the same values, but do Magic Damage and use a their own skill tree.
