# Online:Forward Camps

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Forward_Camps
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Forward Camps are siege items that can be used in Cyrodiil to allow allies to respawn after death. They can be bought for 20000 from siege vendors by any player who is Alliance Rank Tyro II or above. Only 20 allies can spawn at the camp before it is depleted, and players can only respawn there if they die within its radius. After using a Forward Camp to respawn, there is a cooldown of five minutes before you can use one again, any placed Forward Camp cannot be picked back. Forward Camps can be burned by enemy players and siege weapons, and have a toughness of 50,000.

## List of Forward Camps
- Covenant Forward Camp
- Dominion Forward Camp
- Pact Forward Camp

## Vengeance Campaign
In the limited Vengeance campaign, only the following Forward Camps were available and you had an infinite quantity of them.
- Covenant Forward Camp of Vengeance
- Dominion Forward Camp of Vengeance
- Pact Forward Camp of Vengeance
