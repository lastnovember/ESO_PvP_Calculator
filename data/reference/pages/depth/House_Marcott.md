# Online:House Marcott

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:House_Marcott
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

House Marcott is a Breton noble family based in Rivenspire that has fallen on hard times. During Ranser's War they were loyal to King Ranser, and in the aftermath, High King Emeric stripped them of their power.

After this, many of the surviving family members took to criminal enterprises.
