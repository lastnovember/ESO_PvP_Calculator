# Online:Root-House Tribe

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Root-House_Tribe
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Root-House Tribe, or "Tum-Taleel" in Jel, is an Argonian tribe found within Murkmire, known for relying on other tribes for things they need. In other words, they take what they want, when they want it.

They are known for their brutality and quick tempers. They have a habit of raiding villages, killing their inhabitants, and settling into the newly vacant huts left behind by their victims. They tend to be significantly larger than Argonians of the other Murkmire tribes, with broad shoulders, narrow eyes, and wide, powerful jaws.

They burned down Bright-Throat Village some time back.
