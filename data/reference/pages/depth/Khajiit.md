# Online:Khajiit

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Khajiit
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The population of the proud feline Khajiit (listen LO-audio-word-Khajiit.ogg ) has dwindled in recent years following a devastating outbreak of Knahaten Flu. They owe a great debt to Ayrenn, Queen of the High Elves, for her help in restoring order from the chaos that followed the plague. They have a wry wit and a hedonistic outlook, but they are fearsome on the field of battle. They are the strong arm of the Aldmeri Dominion.

There are many breeds of Khajiit including the Cathay, Alfiq, Cathay-raht, Dagi, Dagi-raht, Pahmar, Pahmar-raht, and Senche-raht.

## In-Game Description
The racial skills of the feline Khajiit reflect their natural versatility and guile, giving them bonuses to pickpocketing, stealth, resource recovery, as well as base health, magicka, and stamina. These bonuses help them thrive in many ventures of life.

## Skill Perks

Khajiit have an associated skill line that complements their lore-based abilities with a focus on quicker healing powers, increased stealth abilities and their hand-to-hand combat abilities. Abilities are listed below.

## Notes
- Before release, it was suggested that more subspecies could be available later on.
- While the original interview stated that the playable characters were Suthay-raht, this was later proved incorrect with the advent of ESO:Elsweyr, where it was stated they were Cathay.
- A diverse group of Khajiit can sometimes be found near the road northeast of The Stitches wayshrine. If spoken to, each will provide a little information about their subspecies.
