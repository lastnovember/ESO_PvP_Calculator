Bolion is a Wood Elf member of the Mages Guild part of Telenger's team found inside the ruins of Ezduiin.

## Related Quests
- The Mallari-Mora

## Quest-Related Events

### The Mallari-Mora
Talk to the trapped Bolion and he'll say:
:"It burns. It burns from inside out!"
:
:
If you decided to save him, he'll give you a little boost with the words:
:Bolion: "A token of my thanks."

After completing the Ezduiin objective, he'll say:
:"Ah, smell that fresh air. I think from now on, I'll stick to the nice safe translation work. Field work is for senior researchers."

## Notes
- If you chose to save Bolion, you will receive a passive boost that will make it impossible for the Soul Thirsters to pull you into their realm for a short amount of time.

## Bugs
If he is killed, his health bar remains full once dead.
