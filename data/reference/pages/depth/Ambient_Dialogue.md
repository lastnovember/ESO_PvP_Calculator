Ambient dialogue is spoken by NPCs as you pass by them, usually at random and without prompt. The line determining what the NPC will say is dependent on the voice actor and often race, rather than the character itself, and may not even be relevant to the NPC's characterization (For example, Vanus Galerion may say "Lady Estre was our true Queen! Damn you for killing her!" because he is a High Elf, despite his own principles being rather different). Ambient dialogue is not subtitled, though the text for it is found in the files. There are various types of dialogue listed on this page.

## Greetings
Greetings are said by NPCs without prerequisites and are directed at you, and can be said when you pass by an NPC or when you prompt them to speak. NPCs who do not have unique conversations will often say generic greetings instead.
MaormerKothringi

→ For a full list of ambient greetings, see Online:Greetings

## Justice
Justice dialogue is associated with actions that warrant a bounty. There's dialogue for assault, trespassing, murder, theft, getting caught pickpocketing, transforming into a werewolf in public and feeding on an NPC's blood in public as a vampire. There is also dialogue for NPCs expressing suspicion towards a player sneaking while detected, trying to speak to a justice neutral character/merchant with high infamy, and NPCs noticing that their pockets have been picked clean. Each race has a variety of dialogue with which they respond to illegal or suspicious actions.

→ For a full list, see Online:Justice Dialogue

## Dragons
Dragon sighting dialogue usually occurs during events such as Dragon Rise and Rise of the Dragonguard, when dragons are set to fly over major base-game cities. When justice neutral NPCs see a Dragon, they will run away screaming. This dialogue is different for every race.

→ For a full list, see Online:Dragon Sighting Dialogue

## Commentary
Commentary (or alternatively, rumors) is said when you come close to an NPC, and they say dialogue directed at you. Often times it is dialogue relating to quests you have completed, with NPCs telling you their thoughts on what they've heard of your actions. The Scholars and Adepts on Eyevea after The Mad God's Bargain also have ambient dialogue based on their race.

Imperial NPCs can say Breton and Redguard lines.

## Overheard Dialogue
Overheard dialogue is quite the opposite of commentary, and is overheard when you are within hearing range of an NPC (usually groups of NPCs), but the NPC in question has not seen you. Usually it is said by generic soldiers of factions who are hostile. Some might be said by beast races, such as goblins or giants, and is often listed incoherent gibberish.

→ For a full list of overheard dialogue, see the Overheard Dialogue hub page.
