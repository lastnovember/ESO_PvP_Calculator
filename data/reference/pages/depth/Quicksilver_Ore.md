Quicksilver Ore is an ore of silver-white color found near mountains or large boulders. It can be found in any zone by characters with Rank 8 in the Metalworking passive or CP70 to 80. When mined, a node yields three to four samples of the raw material, Quicksilver Ore. Once ten ore samples are collected they can be refined into seven to ten Quicksilver Ingots. Quicksilver ingots are used for crafting pieces of heavy quicksilver armor and weapons at any blacksmithing station. In order for you to create quicksilver gear your Metalworking ability have to be rank 8.
