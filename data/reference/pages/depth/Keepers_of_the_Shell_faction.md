# Online:Keepers of the Shell (faction)

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Keepers_of_the_Shell_(faction)
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Keepers of the Shell are Argonian midwives who serve the Hist at the Hatching Pools. They take care of the eggs in the nests beneath the Hist and preside over the Hatching Ritual.
