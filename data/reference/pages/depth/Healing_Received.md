# Online:Healing Received

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Healing_Received
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

There are various effects which either increase the effectiveness of Healing on you or your allies, or decrease the effectiveness of healing on your enemies.

## Increase Healing Received

### Active and Ultimate Skills
Skill | Increase | Duration | Affects | Cost

Coagulating Blood (Draconic Power) | 8% | 20-23 sec. | Self | 4320 Magicka

Restoring Focus (Restoring Light) | 8% | 15-18 sec. | Self | 1890 Magicka

### Passive Skills
Skill | Increase | Triggered By

Burning Heart (Draconic Power) | 6%, 12% | While any Draconic Power active

Rapid Mending (Heavy Armor) | 4%, 8% | With 5+ Heavy Armor equipped

Quick to Mend (Argonian) | 1%, 3%, 5% | Constant

Monarch (Emperor) | 50% | While in Home Campaign

### Sets
- See Sets with Increase Healing Received
- See Sets with Major Vitality
- See Sets with Minor Vitality

### Alchemy
- Potions with the Vitality effect increase your healing received by 30%.
- Poisons with the Vitality effect increase your healing received by 8%.

### Champion
- The Quick Recovery ability on The Lord increases your healing received by 1-16%.

## Decrease Healing Received

### Active and Ultimate Skills
Skill | Decrease | Duration | Target | Cost

Dragonknight Standard (Ardent Flame) | 30% | 16 sec. | All Enemies in 8m Radius | 250 Ultimate

Death Stroke (Assassination) | 30% | 6 sec. | Single Enemy | 50 Ultimate

Dark Flare (Dawn's Wrath) | 30% | 6 sec. | Target and Nearby Enemies | 3240 Magicka

Lethal Arrow (Bow) | 30% | 10 sec. | Target | 3240 Stamina

Reverberating Bash (One Hand and Shield) | 30% | 10 sec. | Target | 3510 Stamina

### Sets
- See Sets with Reduce Enemy Healing
- See Sets with Major Defile
- See Sets with Minor Defile

### Alchemy
- Poisons with the Defile effect will reduce enemy healing by 30%. (Potions with this effect will do the same to you.)
- Poisons with the Vitality effect will reduce enemy healing by 15%.

### Champion
- The Befoul ability on The Shadow increases the effectiveness of healing reduction by 1-33%.
