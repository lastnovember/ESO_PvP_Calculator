Ascendant Darkblades are desolated scholars and members of the Ascendant Order who can be found at several locations on High Isle.

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Strike:A basic melee attack that deals minor physical damage.
;Heavy Strike:A charged melee attack which deals moderate physical damage damage and staggers the target. This can be blocked to set the enemy off-balance.
;Shadow Cleave:The enemy swings their weapon around, indicated by a wide red cone, dealing low magic damage and snaring affected targets.
;Shadow Ward:The enemy channels a spell that casts a small damage shield for them and nearby allies, that goes away after some time if not broken beforehand. This can be interrupted to set the enemy off-balance.
