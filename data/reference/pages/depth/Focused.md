# Online:Focused

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Focused
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Focused is a trait which can be found on Companion weapons, armor and jewelry. The trait cannot be researched or crafted, and exists only on found loot. Items with the Focused trait increase the Critical Strike Rating of a Companion by an amount dependent on the quality of the item.
