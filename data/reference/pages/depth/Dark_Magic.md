# Online:Dark Magic

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Dark_Magic
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Dark Magic is one of three class skill lines available to Sorcerer characters. It can be learned by other classes via subclassing. Abilities in this line focus on stuns and utility spells.

## Skill Perks
The Dark Magic skill line is immediately available to all Sorcerers, and is leveled up by earning experience while Dark Magic skills are slotted. Skills include:
