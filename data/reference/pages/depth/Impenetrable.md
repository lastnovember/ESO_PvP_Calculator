# Online:Impenetrable

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Impenetrable
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Impenetrable is a trait which can only be found on armor. Once you have researched the trait on a given item type, you can craft Impenetrable armor by using Diamond. Items with the Impenetrable trait will increase your Critical Resistance by an amount dependent on the level and quality of the item, and also reduce the durability loss taken by the armor by 50%.
