# Online:Beldama Wyrd

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Beldama_Wyrd
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Beldama Wyrd is a coven of nature-witches that worship Jephre, the Forest God of the Bretons. They are protected by the three Guardians of Earth, Water, and Air.

They are not to be confused with the Glenmoril Wyrd, which lean towards worshipping the Daedra, primarily Hircine.
