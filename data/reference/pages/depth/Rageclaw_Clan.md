# Online:Rageclaw Clan

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Rageclaw_Clan
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Rageclaw Clan is one of three Reachmen clans invading the Rift. They are a matriarchal society and are fiercely protective of their children. They often absorb smaller weaker clans which causes some friction with the other remaining clans. Tamed bears sometimes accompany them on their raids. Stral Blackthroat has allied with the Worm Cult to turn his warriors into powerful necromantic creatures called the Graveborn.
