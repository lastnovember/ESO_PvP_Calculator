It is occupied by the Bonesnap goblin tribe. The Lion Guard has been sent to the ruins to investigate rumors of Daedra summoning occurring within. A few alchemy, blacksmithing, enchanting and jewelry crafting resource nodes are scattered throughout the public dungeon. Fleshflies, rats and spiders are found in numbers within. Hazards include bottomless pits.

Bosses have a chance to drop Malacath's Brutal Ritual Oil antiquity lead for Malacath's Band of Brutality.
