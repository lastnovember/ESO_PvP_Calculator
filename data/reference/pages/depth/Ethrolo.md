Ethrolo is a Wood Elf member of the Fighters Guild who can be found at the Everlasting Fair. He was part of the squad that was investigating the area for Worm Cult activity, only to get caught up in the Lady of Riot's shenanigans.

He can first be encountered in the Tent of Dark Joy, and he is very unhappy with the situation.

## Related Quests
- The Everlasting Fair
- The Final Dark

## Quest-Related Events

### The Everlasting Fair
Sergeant Alydine asks you for help to find and free her missing squad members from the Fair. While investigating the place, you make a deal with a talking goat, who offers to help you locate and restore the squad in return for ousting the Lady of Riot. While finding parts of the broken Rod of Revels, you discover Sashii who was turned into an Orc and Brunnduhl who was turned into a monkey.

It is only when you go to confront the Lady of Riot in the Tent of Dark Joy, that you finally find out what happened to Ethrolo. In the space where the Lady of Riot waits for you, you will see a very small Ethrolo on a stone platform. He is dressed as a jester and is unable to stop dancing:
:Ertholo: "Please help me! I can't stop dancing!"

You will then need to fight and defeat the Lady of Riot, once this is done the Goat will appear and the exhausted Ertholo is finally able to stop.

You can try talking to Ethrolo before speaking with the goat:
:"Please, help me! I'm so tired!"

After you restore the Rod of Revels to it's rightful place, the goat will generously create a portal for you and Ethrolo to leave the area:
