Dungeon Annihilator and Greater Dungeon Annihilator are awarded for defeating enemies while in Delves, Public Dungeons, Group Dungeons, or Trials. You do not need to land the killing blows, or indeed do any damage at all so long as you participate in the battle in some way, e.g. providing healing or tanking.plain
