Dahari is a Baandari Khajiit found at their trading post located in Malabal Tor.

## Related Quests
- A Tangled Net

## Quest-Related Events

Speaking to her:
:"Many years I have traveled, in search of the finest luxuries! Have you ever lain on a rug that once graced the steps of the White-Gold Tower? Or drank from a cup forged in the bowels of Ash Mountain?"

During A Tangled Net, Eraral-dro will ask you to get support from other merchants in the Baandari Trading Post. When you speak to Dahari during the quest, you can ask her:
:Eraral is looking for ….
:"Don't repeat yourself. I know what's at stake. You'll have support. But make sure Eraral remembers us after he's out of this mess."
:I'll be sure to remind him.

At Fazaddu's House during A Tangled Net:
:"Are you the mercenary we've heard so much about? I am skilled in the ways of the harp. Shall I strum a tune to relax your sore muscles?"

She will be a suitable entertainer for Yggnast.
