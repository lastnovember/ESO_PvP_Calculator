# Online:Invisibility

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Invisibility
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Invisibility causes you to be undetectable by enemies for a short time, instantly entering stealth. This effect differs from traditional stealth in that it is not broken by damage over time effects or an enemy's detection radius.
An invisible enemy can still be revealed through the use of stealth suppressing abilities or damage from ground-targeted effects.

For more information on stealth and stealth suppression, see the main article.

### Potions
:Main Article: Invisible (alchemy)
The following alchemy ingredients can be mixed together to produce a potion that will grant invisibility when consumed:
- Blue Entoloma
- Dragon's Bile
- Namira's Rot
- Nirnroot
- Spider Egg

The following invisibility potions can be obtained through less conventional means:
- Roguish Stealth Draught
- Crown Store Potions
