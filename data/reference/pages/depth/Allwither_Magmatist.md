Allwither Magmatists are druids found in the Temple of Y'ffelon.

## Related Quests
- The Dream of Kasorayn

## Skills and Abilities
;Flare:Basic ranged attack dealing minor Flame Damage
;Firebolt:Heavy ranged attack which deals low Flame Damage. This can be interrupted to put Allwither Magmatist off-balance.
;Lava Wave:Allwither Magmatist rides a lava wave in a straight line, dealing Flame Damage, staggering and snaring targets in their wake.
;Magma Familiar:Allwither Magmatist summons a weak Magma Familiar to fight for them.
