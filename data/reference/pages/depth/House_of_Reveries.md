# Online:House of Reveries

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:House_of_Reveries
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The House of Reveries is a renowned troupe of performers and artistes that provide entertainment throughout Summerset. They are easily identified from the anonymous painted masks and stage names that they use at all times.

They are headquartered in the village of Rellenthil, which is where they have an amphitheater that is used for performances and holding auditions for prospective members, though members can be seen performing far and wide throughout the island, and even in other provinces. Applicants auditioning to join the troupe are called Hopefuls.
