# Online:Overcharged

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Overcharged
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Minor Magickasteal Overcharged is a status effect applied by dealing Magic Damage. An Overcharged target takes instant Magic Damage and restores 65 Magicka to the caster. They also suffer from Minor Magickasteal for 4 seconds, which restores 168 magicka per second to players dealing damage to them. This status effect can be removed with a Cleansing effect.
