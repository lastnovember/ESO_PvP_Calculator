# Online:Sealed Urn

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Sealed_Urn

A Sealed Urn is your reward for completing the tutorial quest, Soul Shriven in Coldharbour. It is given to you by the Prophet. The level of these items will match your character level when the urn is opened.

This rusty urn clearly originated in Coldharbour. It has been tightly sealed. Your name is burned into the side.

## Contents
A Sealed Urn will contain a full set of Superior-quality armor from the Armor of the Trainee set (either Light, Medium, or Heavy), as well as five lockpicks and a single soul gem.

At launch, Sealed Urns instead contained a bow and a random piece of chest armor. These unique items are listed below.
