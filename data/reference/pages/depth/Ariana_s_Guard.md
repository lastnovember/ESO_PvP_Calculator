Ariana's Guards were Nord voidstalkers members of the Greycloak Mercenaries found in Haddock's Market. They were periodically summoned by Ariana At-Fara during her fight after Anu and Padomay were defeated, before being removed with Update 12.

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Strike:A basic melee attack that does minor physical damage.
;Veiled Strike:The Ariana's Guard cloaks, becoming untargetable by most attacks, and prepares to slash the target. This attack does moderate magic damage and will stun the target briefly. This attack can be blocked to set the Ariana's Guard off balance.
;Teleport Strike:When standing further away from them, the Ariana's Guard teleports and stabs the target, dealing moderate magic damage and snaring for a brief moment.
;Roll Dodge: The Ariana's Guard can roll dodge to avoid attacks.
;Blood Craze: The Ariana's Guard slashes the target twice, doing moderate physical damage over time. This attack heals the Ariana's Guard over time.
;Agony: The Ariana's Guard stuns the target for a short time. Taking damage from any source will break the stun, but the target may choose to break free from it instead. After the stun ends, this spell also does moderate magic damage over time to the target. This spell can be interrupted to set the Ariana's Guard off balance. Blocking will prevent the stun.
;Whirlwind: The Ariana's Guard spins around, doing moderate physical damage to all targets as indicated by a red circle.
;Assassinate: The Ariana's Guard strikes at the target's back for moderate physical damage. This attack can only be performed if the Ariana's Guard is behind the target, and thus Ariana's Guards will often use "Passing Through" to get behind the target. Ensure your character is facing the Ariana's Guard at all times to prevent this.
;Passing Through:The Ariana's Guard jumps above the target to get behind them.
;Soul Tether: The Ariana's Guard jumps and casts a rune on the ground. Any targets caught in the AoE attack will take moderate magic damage and be stunned for 4 seconds then minor magic damage over time which heals the Ariana's Guard for the same amount.

## Bugs
Due to having only one sword equipped, their animation for "Blood Craze", "Passing Through" and "Whirlwind" were not playing correctly.
