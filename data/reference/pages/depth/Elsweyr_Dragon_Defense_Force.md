Northern Elsweyr Defense Force
The Elsweyr Dragon Defense Force (also known as the Elsweyr Defense Conference or simply the Elsweyr Defense Force) is a group of dragon hunters dedicated to wiping out the Dragon threat in Northern and Southern Elsweyr. They are a small group, not to be confused with the similarly-named Northern Elsweyr Defense Force.

You will have an opportunity to become an initiate of the Defense Force during the related quest, available only during the Season of the Dragon Celebration event.
