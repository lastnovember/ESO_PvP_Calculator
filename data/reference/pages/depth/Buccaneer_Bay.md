The small island is a functional port in itself, with a lighthouse, lookout towers, warehouse, Portreeve's Office, multiple dwellings and a multi-storey tavern built from a wrecked ship in the center of the port.

To reach Buccaneer Bay, you take a small boat found on the beach to the southwest of Saintsport on Stros M'kai. That boat will take you to the ship that sails you to your destination. If you try swimming in the sea, you will be attacked by Slaughterfish if you go too far out.

This home was first available in the Crown Store from April 15 to April 23, 2026.

## Layout

### Buccaneer Bay Docks
When you enter Buccaneer Bay, you will find yourself on the main dock on the northern side of the island. The docks consists of the exterior areas on ground level, there are three docks on the northern side of the island and two on the southern side. From here you can climb the ladders to get to the top of the two Buccaneer Bay Lookouts or enter the Buccaneer Bay Tavern in the center square.

### Buccaneer Bay Tavern
The Buccaneer Bay Tavern is central building of the port and is four storeys tall. Its roof was made from the hulls of multipleplain ships. It is the only area in Buccaneer Bay that has interior maps.

### Buccaneer Bay Lookouts
There are two tall stone towers which flank the main northern dock, you can reach the top of them by climbing the ladders. The western lookout tower has a path that leads to the Buccaneer Bay Lighthouse.

### Buccaneer Bay Warehouse
A warehouse to the westplain of the tavern.

### Buccaneer Bay Lighthouse
The Buccaneer Bay Lighthouse is located in the northwest corner of the island, it is reached by climbing the western lookout tower then climbing another ladder to reach the top of the lighthouse. From the bottom lighthouse tower is a path that leads south and up onto the cliffs on the western side of the island.

### Portreeve's Office
The Portreeve's Office is in the northeastern corner of the port and has two small docks to the north of it. The office consists of a single room without any furnishings (even when the furnished version of the home was purchased).

### Buccaneer Bay Dwellings
There are three dwellings found in Buccaneer Bay on the eastern side of the island and south of the Portreeve's Office. These dwellings are all unfurnished single rooms, which also share a unique locking feature. At each door leading into a dwelling, there is an interactable lock which can be used to put a chain across the door and to prevent access.

### Upper Buccaneer Bay
In the area where the dwellings are found, there is a ladder that leads up to Upper Buccaneer Bay. This location is on the southeastern part of the island and consists of the cliffs and the many platforms and bridges built atop them. Following the path leads you to the overlook which gives you a good view of the sea to the south of the island.
