# Online:Priests of Trinimac

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Priests_of_Trinimac
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Priests of Trinimac are clergy who spread the ways of Trinimac from the Temple of Ire in Orsinium. While mostly consisting of Orcs, they also count Argonians, High Elves and Redguards among their numbers. The priests can easily be recognized by their distinctive six-winged robes.

The Vosh Rakh, a relatively new sect of Trinimac worshippers, are tainting the priesthood's reputation for some of the Orcs in the region due to their violence and extremism.
