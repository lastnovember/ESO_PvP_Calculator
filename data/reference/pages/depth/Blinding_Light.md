# Online:Blinding Light

Update 6
Dawn's Wrath-Radiant Destruction

24px24px
Blinding Light was a skill in the Dawn's Wrath skill line which caused targets to miss attacks for four seconds, with a chance to be set off balance. Its Searing Light morph reduced the chance of missing attacks but dealt damage to affected targets, while the Blinding Flashes morph pulsed periodically, with each pulse causing nearby enemies to have 50% chance to miss and be set off balance. The ability was removed in Update 6 and replaced with Radiant Destruction.
