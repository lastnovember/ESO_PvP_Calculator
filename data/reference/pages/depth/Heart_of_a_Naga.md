Heart of a Naga and its preceding achievements are earned by completing daily world boss jobs for Bolu in Lilmoth in Murkmire. While there are only two world bosses is Murkmire, there are six quests in total, each of which can only be done once per day, though you will only receive one of them on any given day unless you share quests with other players in a group. The relevant quests are:
- Grave Circumstances
- Grave Expectations
- Grave Mementos
- Old Baubles
- Old Enemies
- Old Growth
