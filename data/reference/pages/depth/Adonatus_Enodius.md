Adonatus Enodius is an Imperial Tribute player and member of the Roister's Club who can be found in various chapters of the Roister's Club.

## Related Quests
- Cards Across the Continent

## Dialogue
If his rank is Proficient and your rank is below Regular rank:
:"I'm sorry, but Roister's Club regulations demand you rise in rank before we play. Good luck out there."

If his rank is Expert and your rank is below his rank:
:"I can't accept your challenge, friend. Not at your present rank. I'm sorry."

If his rank is Novice and your rank is Initiate or higher:
:"Tribute is it? Very well, I accept your challenge."
:"If it's Tribute you fancy, then I am happy to oblige. Just know that in this-as in all things-Imperials always triumph eventually"
:"Working your way up the Tribute rankings, I see. It seems we have a shared ambition. Have a seat. Let's play."
:"You wish to play Tribute. Very well. I relish a challenge"
