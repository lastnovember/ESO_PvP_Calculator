# Online:Deep Winter Charity Writs

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Deep_Winter_Charity_Writs
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Deep Winter Charity Writs New Life Festival themed crafting writs that require players to create some furnishings or provisions on behalf of the communities in coldest parts of Tamriel. It can be used or sold to other players. Consuming it begins the quest A Charitable Contribution.

## Obtaining Writs
Deep Winter Charity Writs are obtained by opening New Life Festival Boxes.

## Crafting
When you open a writ, the following message is displayed:
:"We're sending food and emergency supplies to people living in the coldest reaches of Skyrim, and could use your help. And, of course, your discretion. I don't arrange these things for my reputation." - R. H.

## Reward
Upon completion of a writ, you will be rewarded with Inspiration, the allotted number of writ vouchers, and a Skaal Explorer's Bundle that can be opened to reveal a Skaal Explorer Style Page which can be used or traded to others.

When you turn in the quest, the following message is displayed:
:
:I've finished the job.
:" "Nordic tribes living in Skyrim's harshest environments thank you, and we wish you a fruitful New Life Festival." - FC

## Notes
- The Deep Winter Charity Writs were first released during the 2019 New Life Festival.
