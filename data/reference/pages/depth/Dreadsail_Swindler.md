Dreadsail Swindlers are Maormer pirates affiliated with the Dreadsails.

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Strike: A basic melee attack dealing low physical damage.
;Whirlwind: The Dreadsail Swindler spins around, dealing moderate physical damage to all targets as indicated by a red circle.
;Assassinate: The Dreadsail Swindler strikes at the target's back for moderate physical damage. This attack can only be performed if the Dreadsail Swindler is behind their target, and they will often use "Passing Through" to get there. Face toward the Dreadsail Swindler to prevent them from using this ability.
;Passing Through:The Dreadsail Swindler jumps above the target to get behind them.
;Roll Dodge: The Dreadsail Swindler can perform a roll dodge to avoid incoming damage.
;Backstabber:The Dreadsail Swindler has a passive ability that causes them to deal 20% more damage when attacking the target's back.
;Desecrated Ground:On death, the undead rarely desecrate the ground under them as indicated by a red circle. This does continuous minor magic damage and snares all targets as well as healing undead enemies within the red circle for 3% of the original undead's Max Health per tick.

## Bugs
Dreadsail Swindlers are incorrectly flagged as Undead, and thus can cast "Desecrated Ground" upon death.
