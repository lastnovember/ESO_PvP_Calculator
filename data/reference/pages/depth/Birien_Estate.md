The grounds and manor are patrolled by the Birien Militia, and during the related quest you will need to infiltrate the estate to free some thieves. All areas in the estate and manor are restricted and you will gain a bounty if the wrong people see you.

## Layout

### Birien Manor Exterior

To enter the grounds of the estate, you will have to climb through the broken wall on the northern side. Once over the wall, the estate will be patrolled by militia and the front door is locked. The front lawn is dominated by a large fountain, and Treasure Chests can spawn around here. If you are sighted by the milita, they will kick you out of th estate, as the dungeons are already full.confirm this is what happpens

To get into the manor, you will have to reach the Wine Cellar door on the southern side of the building.

### Birien Wine Cellar

The wine cellar is a dusty, square room filled with cobwebs. There are some barrels and shelves, and Gertn will be wandering around here. Through the door to the north are the dungeons.

### Birien Dungeons
The dungeon beneath the manor has four cells, and they are currently all occupied. Your target Gorbert Madach is found here, and you have the option of freeing the other prisoners too. The hallway to the north leads to the stairs leading to the first floor.

### Birien Manor First Floor

Coming up from the dungeons, you will find yourself in the northwest corner of the first floor. Various fighters and servants can be found wandering around the rooms. From your starting position, the Dog Kennels are to the east, and you can go there immediately to free Carso. However, it is recommended you first go upstairs to grab meat to distract the hungry dogs.

Stairs leading to the second floor can be found in the rooms immediately east and south of your starting position. But the storeroom in the southwest corner has stairs that lead directly to the kitchen area. On the first floor, you can also access the entrance foyer, but you will be unable to open the front doors. All the NPCs here can be pickpocketed, and the furniture containers can be looted. A cat can be found in the storeroom.

### Birien Manor Second Floor

On the second floor, the large living room, kitchen and balcony overlooking the foyer will be connected by hallways. There will be curtains you can hide behind, as well as pot plants. The kitchen is at the southern end of the floor and the Pork Ribs you require will be on the kitchen table. Like on the first floor, the NPCs here are pickpocket-able and the containers can be looted.

### Dog Kennels

The dog kennels are found in the northeast corner of the manor's first floor. It is a small room filled with cages, and is connected to the dog yard outside. As soon as you enter, the two Saarthal Shepherddogs will start to bark. A timer will begin, and you will need to placate the dogs (if you have the pork ribs), to prevent the Kennel Master from coming in.
