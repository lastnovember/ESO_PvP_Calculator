Mages are members of the Mages Guild that appear throughout the Hollow City after rescuing Vanus Galerion and unlocking the Mages Guildhall. They can also be encountered in the wilderness of Coldharbour fighting Daedra, and one can be encountered slightly southwest of Telesubi Ruins launching dark spells at a Redguard statue before being killed by Hagravens. They cannot be interacted with and have no unique dialogue. Multiple logged in Lorkrata Ruins

## Quest-Related Events

### The Citadel Must Fall
As you exit the Reaver Citadel Mages Guild hall, you'll see a Mage fleeing in terror from a Daedric Titan, which flies in from the fortress ahead and lands on top of a building northeast of the guild hall and roars at the invasion force to intimidate them.
:Mage: "Tongues of Xarxes! What is that thing?"
:Mage: "Make it go away! Make it go away!"

### War Within
Generic Mages are also encountered in Sanity's Edge as fictional dream simulacrums. They are found throughout the trial in captive states or being tortured through various means.
