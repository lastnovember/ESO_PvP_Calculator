# Online:Red Sun Bandits

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Red_Sun_Bandits
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Red Sun Bandits are a gang of outlaws settled in the Black Vine Ruins and a tower north of Reman's Bluff, where they are attacking the Barkbite Clan stronghold. Gargak formed the gang after he was exiled from the Barkbite stronghold after failing the battle for leadership of the clan against his brother Dushkul.

They used to be allied with the spy Krin Ren-dro until their relation ended suddenly.
