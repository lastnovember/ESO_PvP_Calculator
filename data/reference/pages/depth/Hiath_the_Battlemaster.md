Hiath the Battlemaster is a Dark Elf follower of Boethiah and the current champion of the Dragonstar Arena.

## Skills and Abilities
Hiath the Battlemaster will summon previously fought bosses as reinforcements at set intervals. At 75%, he summons Champion Marcauld and Mavus Talnarith. At 40%, he summons the Dark Mage and Yavni Frost-Skin. Only in Normal mode at 25%, he summons the Earthen Heart Knight and Pishna Longshot. These can all overlap if they are not slain in time for the next health interval summoning. As a boss rank enemy, Hiath the Battlemaster is immune to crowd control.

During combat, he uses all of the Berserker class abilities, as well as several unique abilities:
;Quick Strike: A basic melee attack that does minor physical damage.
;Blood Craze:Hiath slashes at you twice, doing moderate physical damage over time to the player. This attack also heals the enemy over time.
;Agony:Hiath stuns the player for a short time. Taking damage from any source will break the stun, but the player may choose to break free from it instead. After the stun ends, this spell also does moderate magical damage over time to the player. This spell can be interrupted to set the enemy off balance. Blocking will prevent the stun.
;Roll Dodge:Hiath can perform a roll dodge to avoid incoming damage.
;Assassinate:Hiath strikes at the player's back for moderate physical damage. This attack can only be performed if the enemy is behind the player, and Assassins will often leap to get behind the player. Ensure your character is facing the enemy at all times to prevent this.
;Rapid Strikes:Hiath casts Rapid Strikes, hitting a player multiple times and dealing multiple instances of physical damage.
;Throw Dagger:Hiath backs away from the player and throws a dagger at the player. This attack causes snare and bleeding if it connects. This attack can be interrupted to set the enemy off balance. The dagger can be blocked to prevent the snare and bleed and reduce the damage done.
;Steel Tornado:Hiath spins around, doing moderate physical damage to all players as indicated by a red circle. Blocking will reduce the damage taken.
;Siphon Life:Hiath pulls life energy from a target, dealing Magic damage.
;Power Extraction:Hiath casts a Power Extraction-like ability, dealing damage around him and increasing his damage done.
;Shadow Strike:Hiath briefly becomes invisible (like a nightblade) before reappearing sharpening his swords together (like an assassin). From here, the following attack depends on your positioning. If you are close to him, he quickly hits you with one of his swords, dealing high physical damage and knocking you down. This can be blocked. If farther away, he performs a teleport strike that tracks your location instead, dealing high physical damage. This attack cannot be blocked.
;Marked for Death:Hiath casts a ball of fire at you. Upon making contact, it dissolves into flames beneath you that track your movement for a duration, dealing high continuous flame damage that cannot be avoided. When the effect ends, a ground area of flame is spawned under the targets and in Veteran Mode, a Summoned Flames spawn.
;Invisibility:Hiath briefly becomes invisible, before reappearing close to you. This attack occurs more often when standing further away from him.
;Purifying Light:Hiath casts a Purifying Light-like ability to a player, making you store damage taken that reapply after a few seconds. You can Break Free to purge yourself from this effect.
;Searing Light:Hiath casts Searing Light on itself, making nearby players have 50% chance to miss their attacks, which are dealt damage and put Off Balance if they miss. This is cast according to logs but we need confirmation it works as described in-game.
;Solar Disturbance:Hiath channels the spell for a short duration before casting a Solar Disturbance-like skill from the sky at your location, dealing very high magical damage, followed by high continuous magical damage and reducing targets damage done. This can be interrupted.
;Crystal blast:Hiath casts a Crystal Shard-like ability which deals magic damage and stuns you. You can interrupt the casting.
;Ember Explosion:Hiath causes a Flame damage explosion in a area.
;Backstabber:Hiath the Battlemaster has a passive ability that causes him to deal 20% more damage when attacking the target's back.

### Veteran Mode
;Empowered Pull/Even Ground:Hiath pulls in an extremely large area all players to him, stunning them and dealing a fixed percentage to their health (around 90%/95%) in magic damage (Empowered Pull). After a small delay, an other area of effect deals low magic damage (Even Ground).
