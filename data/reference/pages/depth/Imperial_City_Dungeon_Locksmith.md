Imperial City Dungeon Locksmith is awarded for discovering and opening both of the Trophy Vaults within the Imperial City Group Dungeons, White-Gold Tower and Imperial City Prison. Each of these requires 150 Key Fragments, so you will need to find a total of 300 to earn this achievement.

## Vaults
The two vaults you must open are:
- Daedric Embers Trophy Vault
- Daedric Shackle Trophy Vault

## Bugs
Opening a vault with more a multiple of 150 Key Fragments in your inventory greater than just 150 will waste fragments, causing you to lose as many multiples of 150 as you have on hand. For this reason, you should not open a vault if you are carrying more than 299 Key Fragments. Place any extra keys in your bank to be safe.
