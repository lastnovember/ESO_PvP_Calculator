Hunter-Killer Negatrix is a verminous fabricant who, together with Hunter-Killer Positrox, acts as the first boss of the Halls of Fabrication.

If both Fabricants are close to each other, they form a link which strengthens them,plain but also is the only way to dispel the damage shield on the Refabricated Spheres that spawn during the fight. It is advised that one tank keeps one of the Fabricants in the middle of the room, while the other tank kites the second one around, creating the link to destroy any spheres that spawns. They will also cause a debuff which drains more health the longer it is active, and should be dispelled as soon as possible.

Once they are killed, a chest spawns at the front of the room they are in. The Pinnacle Factotum appears from the planar rift shortly afterwards.

## Skills and Abilities
;Concussion: A basic melee attack dealing moderate physical damage.
;Charged Gore: The fabricant rushes at you and impales you with its horn, dealing high physical damage and bleed damage over time.
;Rending Leap: The fabricant leaps at you if standing further away, dealing high physical damage and knocking you on the ground.
;Shock Lash: The fabricant swipes its tail at you, dealing high physical damage. The tail swipe produces a wave of traveling lightning, which leads into the next ability, Static Cascade.
;Residual Static: The fabricant headbutts the ground, causing a field of electricity to appear, indicated by a large circular AoE, dealing continuous high shock damage.
;Static Cascade: Getting hit by the traveling lightning from Shock Lash deals high shock damage each
;Empowering Beam: A beam of blue energy forms when the two fabricants are close together, greatly increasing their damage output.
;Summons Spheres: Throughout the fight, Refabricated Spheres will appear in the arena to aid the bosses. They are buffed with an impenetrable damage shield. The only way to remove the shield is to bring the bosses close together to arm their Empowering Beam, and make the beam touch the spheres, which instantly removes the damage shield.
;Summon Shalks: At 75%/50%/25% health (of the combined heath bar), a large swarm of Shalk Fabricants are summoned into the arena to aid the bosses.
;Snap: If one of the bosses is killed while the other is alive, the remaining fabricant enters an enraged state that massively increases their outgoing damage, to a near lethal-level.

## Notes
- If the entire party is wiped and Negatrix is killed while Positrox isn't (or vice versa), they will both respawn at full health.
- Both Hunter-Killers appear in the Clockwork City card set from the story expansion Return to Clockwork City for Legends, as the card Hunter-Killers.
