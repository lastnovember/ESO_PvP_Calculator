The Dro-m'Athra Senche was previously available from the Crown Store for 4,000 from May 26 to May 31, 2016. It returned to the store for the first four days after Elsweyr was announced, when it was offered to ESO Plus Members at a discounted 3,000plain. It returned later in 2023 from February 2 to February 9. It can also be found in Dwarven Crown Crates as a Legendary-level reward. Its default name is "Soul-Eater".

## Physical Appearance
The Dro-m'Athra Senche is a black senche with glowing blue eyes and streaks on its body. It has unique sound effects. It leaves a trail of glowing, deep blue pawprints and smoke behind as it runs. When summoned, it rises from the ground through a puff of blue smoke, lifting itself into Mundus with its front legs.

## Crown Store Showcase Description
The Senche-shaped dro-m'Athra are known to the Twilight Cantors as the sar-m'Athra, but everyone else just calls these eerie and uncanny mounts the Dro-m'Athra Senche. Now, for a limited time, you too can look like you just rode out of the Maw of Lorkhaj!
