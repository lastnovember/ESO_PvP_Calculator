# Online:House Silvelle

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:House_Silvelle
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

House Silvelle are a minor Breton noble house of Daggerfall. They were considered loyal subjects of High King Emeric twenty-five years ago, and were therefore charged with the temporary wardship of Bastian Hallix of the former House Hallix, after the child's father was executed for treason. However, Bastian remained with the Silvelles and eventually entered into their employment.
