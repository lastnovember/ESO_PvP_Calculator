# Online:Bow

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Bow
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Bow is one of the six weapon skills available in Elder Scrolls Online. You can advance through this skill line by equipping and using a bow.

## Learning
The Bow skill line is granted the first time you kill an enemy with a bow equipped. You can increase your skill by engaging in combat with a bow equipped, or by having Bow skills slotted. The more skills you have slotted, the faster your skill will increase. Wood Elves are naturally gifted with bows, and will learn the ability more quickly than other races. In addition, you can earn free levels in the skill line by reading Skill Books, which may be found randomly on bookshelves anywhere. The skill books which increase Bow are:
- An Archer's Archive
- Bliss
- Father of the Niben, Fragment One
- The Source of the Bone
- Sporting Chance

### Other Sets
- Wearing five pieces of the Hawk's Eye set from Cyrodiil reduces the cost and increases the damage of your bow abilities by 6%.
- Wearing five pieces of the Marksman's Crest set from Cyrodiil reduces the cost of your Stamina abilities by 5% and increases the damage of your Bow abilities against Players by 8%.
- Wearing five pieces of the Red Eagle's Fury set from Red Eagle Redoubt crafting site increases the Weapon Damage and Spell Damage damage of all your weapon abilities, Bow included, but it also increases their cost.
- Wearing five pieces of the Wilderqueen's Arch set from overland Greenshade makes your Bow attacks reduce the Movement Speed of any enemy they hit by 0-15% and increase your Movement Speed by 0-15% for 3 seconds.
- Wearing five pieces of the Daring Corsair set from the Cat's-Claw Station crafting site decreases the Stamina and Magicka costs of all your weapon abilities by 10%, and grants you Minor Heroism for 8 seconds after you use a weapon skill.
