# Online:Vengeance Perks

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Vengeance_Perks
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Vengeance Perks allow adjusting various stats in the Vengeance campaign. There are three categories of perks, with similar perks grouped together. Perks can be freely swapped at the Alliance Gates and only one perk from each category can be active at a time.

With the upcoming Veterancy reward system, perks will be unlocked from the Veterancy ranks as you earn Alliance Points and XP from PvP activities.
