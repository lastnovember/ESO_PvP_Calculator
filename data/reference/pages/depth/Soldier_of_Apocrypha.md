# Online:Soldier of Apocrypha

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Soldier_of_Apocrypha
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Soldier of Apocrypha is one of three class skill lines available to Arcanists. It can be learned by other classes via subclassing. Abilities in this line focus on defense and tanking.

## Skill Perks
The Soldier of Apocrypha skill line is immediately available to all Arcanists, and is leveled up by earning experience while Soldier of Apocrypha skills are slotted. Skills include:
