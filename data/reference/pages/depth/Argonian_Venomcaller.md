Argonian Venomcallers are Argonians fought during Stage 7: Vault of Umbrage in Maelstrom Arena. When they appear, the screen becomes dark green and they summon more of the flowers that spew poison, and make them explode faster.

As long as they are alive and the screen remains dim, a debuff will be applied on you, getting progressively more intense, draining your health over time. If you take too long to find and kill the Venomcaller, it can easily be enough to defeat you.

They appear alongside the Argonian Behemoth in Bedlam Veil on Hard Mode, where they perform the same function, and will persist after the behemoth is slain, now being summoned by Darkshard.
