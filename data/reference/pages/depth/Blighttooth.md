Blighttooth is a Dark Elf zombie who serves as one of three bosses in Pothole Caverns.

## Skills and Abilities
;Swipe: A basic melee attack dealing minor physical damage.
;Vomit:Blighttooth vomits on its target, indicated by a red cone, dealing moderate disease damage as indicated by a cone.
;Pound:Blighttooth lunges at its target, dealing moderate physical damage and staggering the target. This can be blocked to set Blighttooth off-balance instead.
;Desecrated Ground:On death, the undead rarely desecrate the ground under them as indicated by a red circle. This does continuous moderate magic damage and snares all targets as well as healing undead enemies within the red circle.

## Bugs
Despite being a champion rank enemy, Blighttooth is vulnerable to crowd control.
