Aulus Previa was an Imperial legionnaire who could be found in the Nobles District. His brother Dyus was taken to the arena by Thrallbinder Dzosab.

## Related Quests
- Brother's Legacy

## Quest-Related Events
He would have greeted you in one of several ways before the quest was complete:
:"Keep moving, friend. I'm looking for my brother."

:"Don't worry, I'm not with Legion Zero. I'm loyal to the Empire, and right now, that means whoever's here to help the city."

:"Keep your head down out here. Those Xivkyn are grabbing people and dragging them off to the arena."

:"Keep your guard up. This district is thick with Daedra. I wouldn't be here if I didn't have to be."

During the quest:
:"You must be either brave or foolish to hazard these streets, but I salute you nonetheless as a fellow soldier. I am looking for my brother. He's about my height, a little younger … has a terrible haircut. Don't suppose you've seen him?"

After the quest:
:"Though your tidings were not what I hoped, I thank you all-the-same. At least now, I know his fate."

:"I just wish it could have been me instead of him. Doesn't seem right."

:"One thing's good at least. I haven't seen that damn Thrallbinder in a while."
