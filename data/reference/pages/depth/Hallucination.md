Hallucinations were Dark Elf shadows found in the Apothecarium. Precisely, one appeared for a moment if a player was hit by Merdyndril's "Hallucination/Hallucination of Death" ability and it was only visible for that player. They stayed alive after Merdyndril was defeated. They were last recorded December 6, 2014.

## Skills and Abilities
;Rending Slash:The enemy hits you with its hands, dealing minor physical damage.
;Slash:The enemy hits you with its hands two times, dealing minor physical damage twice.
;Flurry:A series of four quick swipes in a cone in front of the enemy, each dealing minor physical damage.
;Devastating Leap:The enemy leaps at the target, dealing low physical damage and staggering them. This can be blocked to stagger the feral instead.
