Attendant Brunna is a Reachman spirit master of the Stonetalon Clan helping the coven of Hagravens found at Northwind Mine. Along with Attendant Konia, she is one of Jadwiga's attendants. She and Konnia must be killed to draw the hagraven out into the open.

## Related Quests
- Breaking the Coven

## Skills and Abilities
;Ice Arrow: A basic ranged attack that does minor frost damage.
;Summon Burdening Eye: The enemy summons a Burdening Eye that moves toward the player.
;Stare: If the Burdening Eye touches the player, it explodes, temporarily rooting the player in place and doing moderate magical damage.
;Spell Absorption: The enemy channels a shield on an ally that greatly reduces magical damage taken. This spell can be interrupted to knock the enemy off balance.

## Bugs
As long as Jadwiga is not looted after being defeated, her and both of her attendants will continue to quickly respawn a few seconds after.
