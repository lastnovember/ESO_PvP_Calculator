Captain Cedus is an Imperial battlemage and high-ranking member of the Iron Hounds leading the group camped in Bal Ur. She is found at a tent near a weapon rack you must destroy during the related quest. She is seen sharpening blades when idle, and will attack with a staff while in combat.

It is not necessary to fight her to advance the quest.

## Related Quests
- The Scarlet Judge

## Skills and Abilities
;Flare:A basic ranged attack dealing minor flame damage.
;Quick Strike:A basic melee attack dealing minor physical damage.
;Ice Cage:Captain Cedus summons a ring of frost around herself dealing continuous direct moderate frost damage to all targets in the red circle. This cannot be blocked.
;Impending Storm:Captain Cedus charges up and releases lightning from herself as indicated by red circles dealing moderate shock damage to all targets hit. This can be interrupted to set Captain Cedus off balance. This cannot be blocked.
;Empower Weapon: Flame:Captain Cedus infuses her weapon with flame which changes her "Quick Strike" attack into a melee "Flare" attack.
;Flare:Captain Cedus hits the target with her weapon while spinning, dealing minor flame damage.

## Quest-Related Events
When she is approached, she will say:

## Bugs
Despite being an elite rank enemy, Captain Cedus is vulnerable to crowd control.
She may randomly despawn right in front of you.
- However, if one waits long enough, she will respawn.
