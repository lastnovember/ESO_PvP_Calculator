# Online:Carvain Rowdies

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Carvain_Rowdies

The Carvain Rowdies are the employees of Carvain Ranch and answer to Baron Carvain. They can be found around Ontus in West Weald, particularly on ranch grounds and guarding Carvain Manor.
