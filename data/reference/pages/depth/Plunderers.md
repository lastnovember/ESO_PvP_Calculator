# Online:Plunderers

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Plunderers
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Plunderers are a thieving gang led by Plunder King Endelrin found in Stormhaven. They were originally pirates but after Endelrin received some inspiration, their goals changed to general mayhem.
