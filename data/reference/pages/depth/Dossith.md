Dossith is an Argonian who appears at the entrance of Bright-Throat Village during a random encounter. He and Hleeran can begin to appear after The Skin Taker is complete.

## Dialogue
They talk about Milzus.
:Dossith: "Have you seen Milzus?"
:Hleeran: "Not since she found poor skinless Danlai. Her spines were stiff as dry reeds."
:Dossith: "She knows the Skin Taker is dead now, surely."
:Hleeran: "You don't forget something like that so easily."

You cannot speak to him further.

## Bugs
This NPC will sometimes have a hard time spawning into the game, and will disappear as soon as Dossith's dialogue can be heard or seen in the chat window.
- This can be remedied by logging out and back in next to where Dossith and Hleeran spawn.
