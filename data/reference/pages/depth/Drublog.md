# Online:Drublog

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Drublog
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Drublog are a clan of Wood Orcs in Malabal Tor. For many years they lived peacefully near the Bosmer, but they have recently become aggressive and started attacking cities and towns for reasons unknown.

They have allied with outside groups such as the Ra Gada raiders and Houndsmen to elevate the threat of their raids. The reason for their aggression is later revealed to be due to their view that they should've been included in the newly formed Aldmeri Dominion.
