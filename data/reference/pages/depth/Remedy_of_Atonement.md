# Online:Remedy of Atonement

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Remedy_of_Atonement
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Remedy of Atonement is one of three class skill lines available to Zerith-var. Abilities in this line focus on healing. It is roughly equivalent to the Living Death skill line for Necromancer players.

## Skill Perks
The Remedy of Atonement skill line is immediately available to Zerith, and is leveled up as he gains levels.
