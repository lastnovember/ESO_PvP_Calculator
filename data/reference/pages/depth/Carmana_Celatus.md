Carmana Celatus is an Imperial Worm Cultist who can be found at the Aldwilne Citadel.

## Quest-Related Events
need dialogue for if carmana is picked
After infiltrating the citadel with Cariel, you meet with Prince Naemon, who gives you the task of capturing a suspected spy and bringing them to him. When you tell Cariel of this, she will have you pick out a cultist of similar build to her, so they can be subdued and dressed in her armor.

Carmana is one of the cultists that match the criteria, so you have the option of approaching her in one of the tents, where she is examining some barrels:
:"Things were better in this camp before Prince Naemon's entourage arrived. They're just more mouths to feed."
:Why do you hate them so much?
:"Just look at them? They've never known a day of work in their lives. And they still look down their noses at those of us who actually put in the effort to see success. If I had my say, I'd tell them all to camp outside the fortress walls."
:Come with me.
:"Why? Do you have a plan to do something with the loyalists?"
:Something like that.
::"Oh I get it. You don't want them to overhear. All right. Let's go somewhere we can really talk."
:Let's just say, I know what it's like to be ordered around.Kind
::"It's different when it's done by someone you respect. Prince Naemon's followers have done nothing to earn my loyalty. Show me what you want. I can already taste their misery."
:Right. Sit here and complain if you're not willing to do anything about it.Grim
::"And you have a plan? One that won't get us publicly executed or quietly dealt with? Now this is something I have to see. Go on. Show me what real bravery looks like."plain

If you get Carmana to follow you, she will become your follower, and you can lead her to the secluded place Cariel told you about. Speaking with Carmana during this:
:"Don't stop now. You're the one who pulled me from my work. Show me whatever this mysterious thing is."

When you arrive at the spot, you will find a body on the ground:
:Carmana Celatus: "Larus? Someone will pay!"
:
:Cariel: "Well, well. Let's take a look at who you picked."

Talking with Cariel, she will approve of your choice and has you plant evidence on Carmana, while she brews as potion to make Carmana temporarily mute. Afterward, you can wait for Carmana to wake up and speak with her:
:"Ugh. What happened?"
:Do you feel all right? Something hit your head really hard.
:"Was it one of the loyalists? There was a corpse. And my clothes? Whose clothes are these? I wasn't wearing this before. My voice. My throat. It doesn't feel right. Something's wrong with me. What's happening?"
:Come with me. I know someone who can help you.

Carmana will become your follower once more, if you try to talk to her while leading her to Prince Naemon's quarters:
:"My voice. What's happening to my voice?"

Reaching the door to Naemon's Quarters:
:Carmana Celatus: "The prince's quarters?."

Going down the stairs:
:Carmana Celatus: "This better work."

You can then speak with Prince Naemon and he will try interrogating her:
:Prince Naemon: "I am of the same royal line as your queen. Tell me your mission here."
:
:Prince Naemon: "Nothing? Silence protects no one. Search her. Glean what you can from her pockets."

Take the planted evidence from Carmana and present it to Naemon. He will be furious and his following reaction depends on if you previously killed the prince or not. If you did, then he will have you kill Carmana as a final test of loyalty. On the other hand, if you had not previously met Naemon till Solstice, he will deal with the spy himself.
