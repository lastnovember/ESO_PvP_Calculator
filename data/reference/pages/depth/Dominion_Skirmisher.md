Dominion Skirmishers are soldiers serving the Aldmeri Dominion. Like all PvP guards in Cyrodiil, this Dominion guard will attack all members of the opposing factions. These guards wield a sword and shield.

## Skills and Abilities
Despite being champion-rank enemies, Skirmishers are vulnerable to crowd control.
;Quick Strike:Basic melee attack dealing minor physical damage.
;Puncture:Dominion Skirmisher casts a Puncture-like ability to a target, dealing very minor physical damage.
;Brace:Dominion Skirmisher blocks with their shield for a moment, greatly reducing their damage taken. If you make a melee heavy-attack against a blocking Guard, you will be staggered then set off-balance.
;War Horn:Dominion Skirmisher blows a war horn, increasing the health of their nearby allies and themselves.
;Cyrodiil Guard See Stealth:Passive which makes the Skirmisher see through stealth near them.

## Notes
- Skirmishers will appear as base-rank NPCs if you are of the same alliance.

## Bugs
Skirmishers never benefit from the Keep's upgrades.
