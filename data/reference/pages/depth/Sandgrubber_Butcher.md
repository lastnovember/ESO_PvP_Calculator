Sandgrubber Butchers were goblin foot soldiers of the Sandgrubber tribe found in Divad's Chagrin Mine before Update 5, which changed and enlarged the Alik'r Desert delves.

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Strike:A basic melee attack dealing minor physical damage.
;Heavy Attack:A heavy melee attack dealing moderate physical damage. This can be blocked to set the Sandgrubber Butcher off balance.
;Throw Dagger/Throw Dagger Bleed:The Sandgrubber Butcher backs away from the target and throws a dagger at the target. This attack snares, deals minor physical damage (Throw Dagger) then very minor bleed damage over time (Throw Dagger Bleed). This attack can be interrupted to set the Sandgrubber Butcher off balance. The dagger can be blocked to prevent the snare and the bleed.
;Throw Oil/Ignite:A combo move that is only performed if Archers, Fire Mages or other fire using enemies (Flame Atronachs, Scamps etc.) are around. The Sandgrubber Butcher throws a glob of tar under the target's feet as indicated by a red circle. While this does no damage, standing in it snares (Throw Oil). The Sandgrubber Butcher will call for the other enemy to light the tar (usually accompanied by a voice clip) and the area will burn, dealing direct minor flame damage every half-second to targets within the red circle (Ignite). Ignite's damage is attributed to the enemy who lit the area on fire.
