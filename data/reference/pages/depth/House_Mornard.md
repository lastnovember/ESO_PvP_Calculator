# Online:House Mornard

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:House_Mornard
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

House Mornard is a major noble house of the Systres. Before Ranser's War, their house ruled the entire archipelago, but they were stripped of their dukedom by High King Emeric for their role in the rebellion. House Dufort now rule the islands, with House Mornard relegated to their holdings on Galen and Amenos.
