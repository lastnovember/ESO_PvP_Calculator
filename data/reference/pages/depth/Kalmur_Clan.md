# Online:Kalmur Clan

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Kalmur_Clan
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Kalmur Clan is a near-extinct Orc clan from Wrothgar. Almost all of its members, except for their soon to be wise woman Khazasha, their totem spirit Atrozu, and two other warriors were slain by a powerful creature from The Deadlands, which Khazasha named The Ravener, under mysterious circumstances.

Following this event, the survivors gave chase to the creature as it fled back to Oblivion, eventually chasing it to the fortress of Ardent Hope. Along the way, the two warriors were slain by Xivilai, leaving Khazasha as the sole remaining Orc clan member. She is now driven to see The Ravener destroyed.
