# Online:Weighted

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Weighted
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Weighted was a trait which could only be found on weapons. Once you had researched the trait on a given item type, you could craft Weighted weapons by using Citrine. Items with the Weighted trait would increase your attack speed by an amount dependent on the quality of the item. 2-handed weapons provided twice the bonus of 1-handed.

## Notes
- This trait was replaced by Decisive, which increases your Ultimate gain.
