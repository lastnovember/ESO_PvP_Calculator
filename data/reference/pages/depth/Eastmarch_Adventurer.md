Eastmarch Adventurer is awarded for completing 43 quests in Eastmarch. This includes both the Eastmarch Main Quest as well as any side-quests. It does not include Crafting Writs, Fighters Guild or Mages Guild quests, or pledges for the Undaunted.plain This achievement will allow you to purchase a Standing Slab for your house. It costs 5,000 and can be purchased from Listens-to-Sea in Fort Amol.
