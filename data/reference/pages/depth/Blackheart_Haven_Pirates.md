# Online:Blackheart Haven Pirates

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Blackheart_Haven_Pirates
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Blackheart Haven Pirates is a group of pirates operating out of Blackheart Haven. It is led by the pirate, Captain Blackheart. It consists of two crews merged together, Blackheart's original crew which shares his curse, and newer pirates that occupy the entrance to the haven, which are further excluded from the captain's dealings.
