# Online:Living Shade

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Living_Shade
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Living Shade is one of three class skill lines available to Mirri Elendis. Abilities in this line focus on avoiding incoming damage from enemies. It is roughly equivalent to the Shadow skill line for Nightblade players.

## Skill Perks
The Living Shade skill line is immediately available to Mirri, and is leveled up as she gains levels.
