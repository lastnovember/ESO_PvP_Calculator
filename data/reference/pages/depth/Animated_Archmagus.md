The Animated Archmagus is an animated suit of armor encountered in the Scholarium Ruins on the way to the Scholarium proper. It serves as a miniboss, blocking your path as you traverse the ruined halls. It can summon Lesser Air Atronachs, Lesser Flame Atronachs, Lesser Frost Atronachs, and Lesser Storm Atronachs during combat.

## Related Quests
- The Second Era of Scribing

## Skills and Abilities
As an elite rank enemy, the Animated Archmagus is immune to crowd control. An Unstable Void hazard is active throughout the fight, it spawns in the back area but can move around if you go too close to it.
;Elemental Bolt:A basic ranged attack dealing three instances of damage: low flame damage (Flame Bolt), low frost damage (Frost Bolt) and low shock damage (Shock Bolt).
;Empower Weapon:The Animated Archmagus periodically infuses its weapon with either fire (Flame Weapon), ice (Frost Weapon) or electricity (Shock Weapon), which changes the element of its basic melee attack.
;Flame Strike:While infused with fire, the Animated Archmagus hits the target with its staff by either striking directly or by spinning as a basic melee attack, dealing low flame damage.
;Frost Strike:While infused with ice, the Animated Archmagus hits the target with its staff by either striking directly or by spinning as a basic melee attack, dealing low frost damage.
;Shock Strike:While infused with electricity, the Animated Archmagus hits the target with its staff by either striking directly or by spinning as a basic melee attack, dealing low shock damage.
;Heat Wave:The Animated Archmagus releases fire along the ground in the direction it is facing. This attack does moderate flame damage to all targets hit by it. This spell can be interrupted to stagger the Animated Archmagus.
;Fire Runes:The Animated Archmagus shoots two fireballs in the air which form two fire runes when they land. The runes do moderate flame damage over time to all targets within the red circles. Players should be prepared to move as soon as the attack casts as one rune will always be cast on top of you.
;Ice Cage:The Animated Archmagus summons a ring of frost around itself dealing continuous direct moderate frost damage to all targets in the red circle.
;Impending Onslaught:The Animated Archmagus charges up and releases lightning from itself as indicated by red circles. This attack does moderate shock damage to all enemies hit. This attack can be interrupted to stagger the Animated Archmagus.
;Lightning Storm:The Animated Archmagus casts a lightning spell which makes lightning strikes happen in a pentagon pattern around you. (Bugged, see Bugs)
;Glaciate:Animated Archmagus channels a beam on a target that continuously apply "Glaciate" stacks which snares more with each stack, up to 6 stacks for ~95% snare potency. This can be interrupted to stagger the Animated Archmagus.
;Summons:The Animated Archmagus summons either a Lesser Air Atronach, a Lesser Flame Atronach, a Lesser Frost Atronach or a Lesser Storm Atronach to fight for it. Only one summon can be up at a time, if it dies, the Animated Archmagus can summon again one of the four.
