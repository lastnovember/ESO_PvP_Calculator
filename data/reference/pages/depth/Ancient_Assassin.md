The Ancient Assassin was a Skeleton boss in the Forgotten Crypts. He was removed in a very early unknown update, being replaced by Timothe the Sharp between the 24 May 2014 and the 3 May 2015. They both share the same appearance, but not all attacks.

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Strike: A basic melee attack that does moderate physical damage.
;Heavy Attack:Charged dual wield melee attack dealing physical damage. This can be blocked to set the Ancient Assassin off-balance.
;Blood Craze: The Ancient Assassin slashes the target twice, doing moderate physical damage twice then very minor bleed damage over time to the target. This attack also heals the Ancient Assassin over time.
;Agony: The Ancient Assassin deals moderate magic damage and stuns the target for a short time. Taking damage from any source will break the stun, but they may choose to break free from it instead. After the stun ends, this spell also does moderate magical damage over time to the target. This spell can be interrupted to stagger the Ancient Assassin.
