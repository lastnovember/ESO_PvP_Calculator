Pull (script)

Pull is a combat effect that moves targets either to the caster's location or to a target location. It is useful for bringing ranged attackers into melee range, or for concentrating multiple targets into a single area so they can be attacked at once.

## Skills

colspan=2|Beckoning Armor (Bone Tyrant) | Enemies that strike you with ranged attacks will be pulled toward you. | 2700-2430 Magicka

colspan=2|Fiery Grip (Earthen Heart) | Pulls an enemy to you. | 3780 Magicka

colspan=2|Ruinous Cyclone (Volendrung) | Pulls all enemies within 15 meters closer to you. | free

colspan=2|Rune of Displacement (Herald of the Tome) | Places a rune under the target that pulls nearby enemies after a delay. | 3510 Magicka

Additionally, some Scribed skills using the Pull script will have this effect.

## Notes
- In some cases, the target will be unable to move. This can happen in various situations:
- The target is knocked down or immobilized by another attack.
- The target has recently been moved by another player also using Fiery Grip.
- The target is charging up a Heavy Attack.
- Most boss level enemies cannot be pulled.
- All Enemies have a 15 second immunity after being hit with it. (hardcoded into the game)
- Some targets are always immobile, e.g. Stranglers.
