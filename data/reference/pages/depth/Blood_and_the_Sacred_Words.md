## Detailed Walkthrough

### Dying Archivist
Approaching Fharun Stronghold, you'll find a dying archivist who claims the Vosh Rakh have attacked the stronghold, killing people in Trinimac's name. The archivist claims the murderers seek a secret sanctuary and the sacred words of Malacath hidden within. You'll need to gather blood from four archivists that were killed by the Vosh Rakh, starting with hers, to gain access to the sanctuary where the sacred words are stored.

The three remaining archivists are to the north end of the stronghold. Akur's body is in the Feast Hall, Mergorz's is at the Clan House, and Boldarkh's is at the Longhouse. The order you find them in is unimportant. There will be numerous enemies to avoid or to dispatch, at your discretion. In whichever building you find the last body, you will be visited by a member of the Agra Crun, who will give you a book that contains riddle-like guidance to get you through the pathway that leads to the sacred words.

### A Protected Pathway

At the shrine, "only a gift of life's most precious liquid" will open the path. Place the archivists' blood in the offering bowls. The door to the path is Malacath's Head on the altar.

In the Prayer Rooms, "those who refuse to follow Malacath's light will forever walk in darkness." There will be a table with a candle next to each door (to the left of the door). Go through the door next to the only lit candle in each room. Repeat this correctly four times and you will proceed to the next area. Otherwise, you'll keep returning to this starting room over and over.

In the Hall of Faith, "trust that Malacath will catch you." Take a moment to appreciate the magnificent tapestry, then jump in the well.

### The Word is Given

You have reached the Sanctuary of Words. Now you face a terrible task: to obtain the scroll containing the sacred words, you must kill the Keeper. After doing so, Azrath will congratulate you, and will direct you to bring the scroll to Kroma, in Orsinium. Exit the sanctuary (after fishing the three Foul Fishing Holes at the feet of the great statue of Malacath, if you wish) and head to the orc capital. Kroma can be found in Bludrath's House, near Scarp Keep. She will accept the scroll and grant you a reward to close the quest.

## Bugs
Exiting the sanctuary of Malacath without retrieving the sacred words will require you to make the entire way back as the exit door is one-way only.
