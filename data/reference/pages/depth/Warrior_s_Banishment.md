# Online:Warrior's Banishment

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Warrior's_Banishment
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Warrior's Banishment is one of three class skill lines available to Zerith-var. Abilities in this line focus on dealing damage. It is roughly equivalent to the Grave Lord skill line for Necromancer players.

## Skill Perks
The Warrior's Banishment skill line is immediately available to Zerith, and is leveled up as he gains levels.
