Dawnwood Indriks were located at the Event Exchanges located outside the main city of each alliance's starting zone. They were found in pens next to the Impresario alongside Nascent Indriks.

They can no longer be found near the Impresario as of the end of the 2019 Jester's Festival.

## Notes
- The Springtide Indrik pet is the smaller equivalent of the Dawnwood Indrik.
