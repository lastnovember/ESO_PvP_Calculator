Bone Grappler is a Strangler World Boss found in Bone Grappler's Nest, along with two Marrow Feeders.

It has a chance to drop the Face of the Wolf Beast antiquity lead for the Ring of the Wild Hunt. It can also drop the Spinner's Fractured Femur, a unique item in the Spinner's Garments set.

## Skills and Abilities
As a boss rank enemy, Bone Grappler is immune to crowd control, except for one situation (See Bugs)
;Strike: A basic melee attack dealing moderate-high physical damage.
;Spit: A basic ranged attack dealing moderate-high poison damage.
;Grapple: When standing away from Bone Grappler, it pulls its target to itself using a vine. Blocking prevents being pulled.
;Strangle: Bone Grappler drains the target's blood, dealing minor physical damage over time. This can be interrupted to set Bone Grappler off-balance. If this fully completes, Bone Grappler becomes empowered and deals 8 more damage for a time.
;Poisoned Ground: The strangler sends out roots at the target's position, indicated by a red circle, which then close onto them, dealing moderate-high poison damage.

## Achievements
There is one achievement associated with this creature:
Bone Grappler's Bane

## Bugs
Despite being a boss rank enemy, Bone Grappler gets stunned and set off balance if its "Strangle" ability is interrupted.
Using a purging skill cancels "Strangle".
