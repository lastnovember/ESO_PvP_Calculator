# Online:Dusksabers

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Dusksabers
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Dusksabers are a vampiric clan of mercenaries with links to House Telvanni that prowl the Telvanni Peninsula. Their ranks consist of vampires and Bloodknights, and they can be found in the company of Death Hounds. They wear Ebony style armor.
