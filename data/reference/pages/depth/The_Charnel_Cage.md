The Charnel Cage is a flaming bone colossus found inside the Molavar group delve in Lower Craglorn. It is summoned out of a pool of lava after dispatching the nearby flame atronachs in the southwest corner of the map, and is accompanied by Imps.

## Skills and Abilities
;Strike: A basic melee attack that does moderate physical damage.
;Bone Saw: The bone colossus stomps its foot, doing moderate physical damage in a cone in front of it and staggering those hit.

## Achievements
There is one achievement associated with this location:
Molavar Explorer
