# Online:Black Snails

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Black_Snails
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Black Snails are a mercenary company part of House Hlaalu. They are working with Savarak Fels as part of his and House Hlaalu's plot to take control of Vos. They are behind the illness of Mistress Dratha. Several of them can be found in the town, terrorizing citizens, as well as guarding Savarak's residence.
