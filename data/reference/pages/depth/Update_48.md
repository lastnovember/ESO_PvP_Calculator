Update 48:

Update 48 is the forty-eighth major update patch for The Elder Scrolls Online, and introduces the second part of the Seasons of the Worm Cult storyline.

It may refer to:
- Patch 11.2.6 for PC/Mac.
- Patch for PS4/ for PlayStation 5.

## Update 48

### New Features / Updates / Big Changes

#### Seasons of the Worm Cult, Part Two

#### Eastern Solstice
As the battle at the Writhing Wall concludes, the eastern reaches of Solstice open to reveal their secrets. Once home to entrenched Wormcult strongholds, the Stirk Fellowship now marches eastward, determined to strike the enemy at its heart. With the aid of new allies, they must cross the fractured lands of Eastern Solstice and uncover the hidden legacy of the Stone-Nest Argonians if they hope to withstand the corruption that festers there.

Uncover what remains of Eastern Solstice, forge new bonds, and confront familiar friends and foes alike caught in the Wormcult’s reverse planemeld. The march toward the final battle that will decide the fate of Solstice-and beyond-begins here.

#### Accessing Eastern Solstice

#### Bring Your Characters to Phase 3 of the Writhing Wall Event
- Travel to Solstice via Wayshrine.
- Join the Phase 3 Writhing Wall event south of Sunport to break through the barrier.
- Once complete, you may freely enter Eastern Solstice and continue the main quest from the west.

#### Base Game

#### Unsheathing Timing Update
We are updated the speed of unsheathing in combat for all weapon types to be faster. We would like your feedback regarding how this increased time feels in combat.

#### Update to Leaderboards
Going forward, leaderboards are now Account based (except Cyrodiil boards for now). This means you will only have 1 entry per leaderboard, and it will be the highest score for the associated activity. Class specific leaderboards have also been removed. We are taking a look at leaderboards to better streamline the experience overall. The goal is to make leaderboards make a little more sense in a post-subclassing world and to have a more unified and fair experience for players.
With this change, the number of people per leaderboard can be adjusted based on feedback. So please make sure to provide any thoughts on this.

#### Combat Animation Memory Reduction
We are implementing some backend changes to reduce the size of combat animation files to lower their impact on the game’s memory. Specifically, you should experience the following:
- The animation visuals after these changes should be virtually indistinguishable from the existing animations within the game.
- There will be no impact to light attack weaving or animation cancelling - they should work as they did before.

### Combat & Gameplay

#### Introduction
Greetings! For the U48 combat cycle, the core focus is to provide bug fixes. There have been a few item set adjustments, so please take a look and let us know what is working and what isn’t. However, the bulk of the combat patch notes this update is meant to help address specific bug issues present.

We’ll continue to lurk and watch over the discussions and experiences you get into to improve the overall gameplay experience Tamriel has to offer in regard to combat. We are also actively going over the class survey results to help us better understand your pain points with class balance and general combat as well. We will take the feedback to help guide longer term combat updates as well. Happy reading, safe travels, and we hope to see you in Tamriel!

#### General
- The Toggle Sprint setting, while using keyboard and mouse, now works as intended. The setting should no longer fail to toggle off when crouching or casting abilities.
- Updated back-end calculations on the following effects to reduce their strain on the server:
- Pain’s Refuge (Champion Fitness slottable)
- Maarselok (Monster Set)
- Mara’s Balm (PvP Set)
- Going for a dip (entering the swimming state) in any body of liquid or similar substance while crouched or invisible will now properly remove all related effects, fixing some issues where parts of crouch, sneak, or invisibility could remain stuck on your character.
- Fixed an issue when using the Armory without access to a subclassed skill line could fail to generate the saved Loadout that contained the subclassed skills.
- Potions, food, and drink effects can now be consumed while casting or channeling abilities. The sheer amount of talent required to do so is not to be questioned.
- Having multiple instances of “Living Corpse” will now recognize each unique effect as a distinct corpse. This effect refers to affixes such as Beautiful Corpse from the Nobility in Decay set, which allow you to cast a corpse consuming effect on yourself.

#### Combat & Abilities

#### Class Abilities
- Arcanist
- Curative Runeforms
- Apocryphal Gate: Fixed an issue where this ability and its morphs would fail to generate Crux if you entered the portal while Sprinting.

- Warden
- Winter's Embrace

- Necromancer
- Updated all Necromantic summon abilities (Sacrificial Bones, Spirit Mender, Skeletal Mage, and all related morphs) to properly interact with the pet limitation rules. These abilities should now actively flush out existing summons and corpses (granted the summons and corpses are sourced by replaceable creatures) rather than outright failing.
- Bone Tyrant
- Bone Armor: This ability and its morphs no longer summon a corpse when they end or their abilities are refreshed after 10 seconds. Instead, these abilities now grant you Living Corpse if cast while in combat, which allows you to cast a (singular) corpse consuming ability on yourself. This effect is applied instantly but has a 10 second cooldown to ensure the same corpse consuming ability cadence is met.
Developer Comment
In efforts to reduce the performance strain these abilities have, as well as a way to work around the pet and corpse limitation cap, we've reworked these abilities to utilize the same functionality as Nobility in Decay (one of the Necromancer's class sets) where you are treated as a corpse for a single corpse consuming ability. This effectively reaches the same outcome, where these abilities help Necromancers produce their own corpses, with some minor gameplay implications. By granting you the corpse immediately, we remove some of the higher demand strategy making with these abilities so you can get straight into reusing and recycling life as you see fit. Additionally, by no longer summoning an actual corpse, we free you from needing to remain nearby the corpse and even allow special interactions with tether abilities - all while also freeing the servers from needing to track a far more expensive entity and effects!

#### World Abilities

- Werewolf Transformation

This will result in a reduction in power with lower base stats, but an increase in power with higher stats (for example, a character with less than 5000 Weapon or Spell Damage and 22000 Stamina or Magicka would see a decrease in power - but an increase at and above those ranges)
