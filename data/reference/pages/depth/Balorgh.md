Balorgh is an Orc werewolf behemoth who can be found in the March of Sacrifices. He is the leader of one of the groups that are competing in Hircine's Great Hunt. Strangely, he does not seem to hold the Daedric Prince in much esteem but he is willing to play along out of necessity. According to a letter, he was originally entrusted with a mission by Vykosa to infiltrate the Hunting Grounds but some of his fellow pack-members worry that Balorgh is now pursuing his own goals.

Balorgh will watch your progress throughout the various parts of the March. After Tarcyr is killed, he will ambush your group, killing Hanu to take the heart for himself. Hircine denies his request for the boon as he didn't kill Tarcyr, and Balorgh negotiates that killing your group will be enough to earn the boon. At the end of the Nightlands, he transforms into a Werewolf Behemoth and serves as the final boss. Burning the Moon Hunter Pack banner will activate hardmode.

## Skills and Abilities
As a boss type enemy, Balorgh is immune to all forms of crowd control effects.
;Swipe: A basic melee attack that deals minor physical damage.
;Bite: The boss bites its target dealing moderate physical damage, followed by minor physical damage over time.
;Slaughtering Strike: The boss slashes at a target dealing very high physical damage. This must be blocked by the tank or dodged if you are not.
;Icy Roar: The boss shouts dealing high frost damage over time and rooting all targets hit in front of itself as indicated by a red cone.
;Fiery Charge: The boss charges forward dealing high flame damage and knocking down all targets as indicated by a red rectangle.
;Fire Trail: The boss leaves behind a trail of fire when charging that deals moderate flame damage over time as indicated by red circles.
;Fiery Eruption: The boss stomps dealing very high flame damage around itself as indicated by a red circle. This must be avoided at all costs.
;Venomous Spores: After Thunder Stomp ends, the boss summons poisonous plants that will cover the three islands with poisonous AOEs dealing moderate poison damage as indicated by red circles. Stay off the islands to avoid this.
;Thunder Stomp: After the plants disappear, the boss will stomp releasing lightning outwards from itself dealing moderate shock damage and staggering all players hit (Thundering Fissure). The water around the islands will also be electrified (Charged Water) dealing moderate shock damage over time. Stay on the islands to avoid this, while also moving to avoid the lightning strikes that occasionally spawn on top of the player as indicated by red circles (Thunder Storm).

### Hunting Party Phase
At 80, 60, 40 and 20% health, the boss will become invulnerable and fog will cover the screen.
;Summon Dire Wolf: The boss summons Dire Wolves to attack the party. They should be killed quickly to simplify the fight.
;Slaughtering Strike: The boss will target a specific player as indicated by a red target indicator under their feet. If the boss (or his clones on HM) reach their target, they will use a stronger version of the heavy attack from earlier that will deal lethal damage. This must be dodged if possible.

Tharas will set a trap for Balorgh that is indicated by a bright blue pillar. The player targeted must rush over to the pillar to lure the boss into the trap. Once the trap is sprung, the boss will be stunned and made vulnerable again, and the fight repeats.

### Hard Mode changes
;Summon Strangler: When Venomous Spore is cast, Stranglers will also appear in the water spitting poison at you. Kill them quickly. If you are grappled by one, break free immediately.
;Fiery Remnant: On hard mode, Fiery Eruption will spawn four fireballs that shoot towards all group members after the stomp is cast dealing moderate flame damage, followed by moderate flame damage over time. Roll dodge to avoid this if possible.
;Summon Clones: When the Hunting Party phase starts, three additional copies of the boss will appear, and they will all have the Fiery Charge, Fiery Eruption and Fiery Remnant abilities. Venomous Spores andThunder Stomp will also be used, so be prepared to stay on or off the islands while luring the boss to the trap. It is advised all players stay together so healers can keep the group alive through the various incoming DOTs during the phase. The party will need to trap the boss a total of four times before the Hunting Party phase will end (one for each of his copies and one for the main boss).
