# Online:Ash Exiles

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Ash_Exiles
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Ash Exiles are a tribe of exiled Ashlanders who can be found in Nchuleft. They have forcefully evicted the Ashlanders from the nearby Zainab Tribe who were looting the ruins for relics to trade.
