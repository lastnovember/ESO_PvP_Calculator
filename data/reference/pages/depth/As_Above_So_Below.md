## Detailed Walkthrough
The Rat has had nightmarish experiences in the Bloodworks below the City. He informs you that some unfortunate citizens have been dragged down there to suffer, and suggests that you save them. Not that he cares one way or another: the people of Imperial City haven't been kind to the Rat.

Head down to the Bloodworks and look around for survivors. You'll find, so someone could still be alive. Rescue them, then return to the Rat in the Arena District to be rewarded for your valiant efforts.
