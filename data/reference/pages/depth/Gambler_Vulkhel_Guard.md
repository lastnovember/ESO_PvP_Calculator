Gamblers are Bosmer found in Vulkhel Guard who appear to owe Malareth money.

## Related Quests
- New in Town

## Quest-Related Events
When approached they will greet you with one of the following:
:"Greetings."

:"What?"

:"Stars brought you here. What do you want?"

:"Fair day, stranger."
:Malareth sent me.
:"Should have known. Look, she's taken everything."
::[Intimidate] I can easily take what you owe.
::[Persuade] It will be easier on us both if you just pay her.
:::"Stars take you both! Fine. Here's the coin. Just go."
::You know what happens when you can't pay up.
::"Yeah? Let's see what you've got. Today's the day someone finally stands up to Malareth!"
:::Hold on there- (Conversation ends)
::If it's that bad, I'll cover you.
::"You'd, you'd do that? Such generosity!"
:::Right. Get out of here. [20]

Alternatively:
:Malareth says you owe her.
:"To Oblivion with Malareth! She already has all the coin!"
::[Intimidate] I think you know what happens if you don't pay up.
::Sure, sure. Pay up.
::"Just another of Malareth's mercenaries. Always with the same message. This time you get nothing!"
:::Hold up- (Ends conversation)
::I'll pay your way this time. Keep out of trouble.
::"Words fail! Malareth will never see this face, ever again."
:::Get out of here. [20]

If the Gambler is threatened, they may stand up to you:
:Gambler: "The threats end now."

Once you have beaten them up, they will hand you the gold and run away:
:Gambler: "You win! You win! Here's your coin."
:Gambler: "Stop! Take it, take it all."

After dealing with his debt in whatever manner, he will then run off.
