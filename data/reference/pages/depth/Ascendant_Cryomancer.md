Ascendant Cyromancers are frost mages and members of the Ascendant Order found in the Coral Aerie.

## Related Quests
- An Order Gone Awry
- Pledge: Coral Aerie

## Skills and Abilities
;Ice Arrow:A basic ranged attack that does minor frost damage.
;Summon Ice Barrier:The enemy summons an Ice Barrier that absorbs projectiles fired at it until destroyed. The player should move so that there is a clear line of sight between themselves and the enemy.
;Winter's Reach:The enemy unleashes an icy whirlwind in the direction it is facing. This whirlwind does moderate frost damage and roots all players that it hits. The root effect passes through block, but it can be escaped by roll dodging.
;Glaciate: The enemy channels an ice beam to the target, dealing frost damage over time and snaring. This can be interrupted.
