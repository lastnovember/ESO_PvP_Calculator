Captain Candidus is an Imperial battlemage in charge of the Imperial Treasure Hunters found in the ruins of Yldzuun.

## Skills and Abilities
;Flare:A basic ranged attack dealing minor flame damage.
;Quick Strike:A basic melee attack dealing minor physical damage.
;Ice Cage:Captain Candidus summons a ring of frost around himself dealing continuous direct moderate frost damage to all targets in the red circle. This cannot be blocked.
;Impending Storm:Captain Candidus charges up and releases lightning from himself as indicated by red circles dealing moderate shock damage to all targets hit. This can be interrupted to set Captain Candidus off balance. This cannot be blocked.
;Empower Weapon: Flame:Captain Candidus infuses his weapon with flame which changes his "Quick Strike" attack into a melee "Flare" attack.
;Flare:Captain Candidus hits the target with his weapon while spinning, dealing minor flame damage.

## Bugs
Despite being an elite rank enemy, Captain Candidus is vulnerable to crowd control.
