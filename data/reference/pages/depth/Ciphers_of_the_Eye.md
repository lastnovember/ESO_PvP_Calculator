# Online:Ciphers of the Eye

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Ciphers_of_the_Eye
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Ciphers of the Eye are a group of mortal scholars who reside in Apocrypha. They were personally chosen by Hermaeus Mora, and act as caretakers and curators for the ever-growing amount of texts which the Great Eye accumulates.
