Condalin is the forgemaster of Mathiisen. He is suspected to have ties to the Veiled Heritance.

## Related Quests
- Putting the Pieces Together
- The Unveiling

## Quest-Related Events

### Putting the Pieces Together
As you exit Fistalle's house, you can hear him talking to the canonreeve:
:Canonreeve Malanie: "No more delays! Ready the cargo, today. I'm just waiting for the order to go though."
:Condalin: "Fine! Fine. Ware, though. My men tell me of outsiders in town. Keep an eye out."

### The Unveiling
Agree to Razum-dar's plan to let yourself be captured. Outside the barracks, you can hear Ondendil and Condalin talking:
:Ondendil: "Let's tell Malanie about that damn cat lurking around here."
:Condalin: "Aye. If Razum-dar is near, then trouble usually follows."

Enter the barracks. If you talk to him before talking to Malanie, he'll say:
:"Sorry, we're a little busy here. Can this wait?"

After you accuse Canonreeve Malanie of treason, she will signal Condalin to knock you out.

After you have been freed by Raz, you can confront Condalin in his house. When he notices you he will shout:
:Condalin: "You cannot stop the people and their true Queen!"

Despite this, you must defeat him before going after Malanie.

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Strike:A basic melee attack dealing minor physical damage.
;Uppercut:An Uppercut-like attack dealing moderate physical damage, knocking back and stunning. This can be blocked to knock Condalin off balance instead.
;Cleave:Condalin swing his weapon around like Cleave does, dealing minor physical damage to all targets in front of him. This attack has no red cone indicator.
;Health Potion:Condalin drinks a potion, restoring 8,396 Health. This can be interrupted to set the Condalin off balance.

## Bugs
Despite being an elite rank enemy, Condalin is vulnerable to crowd control.
