Adavos Dren is a Dark Elf member of the Ebonheart Pact found in the Roots of Silvenar. He has a chance to drop Thunderbug Carapace Quicksilver Axe, an item in the Thunderbug's Carapace item set.

## Skills and Abilities
Adavos Dren uses a mix of Bonelord and Necromancer NPC class abilities.
;Entropic Bolt:A basic ranged attack that does minor magic damage.
;Summon Abomination:Adavos Dren summons a flesh atronach, bone colossus or ghost to fight, spawning with the summoner's current Health. Adavos Dren is vulnerable to damage while a summon is active. The summon will die if Adavos Dren is killed.
;Empower Undead:Adavos Dren channels a buff on a nearby undead (e.g. skeletons, zombies, and even Bone Colossi and vampires) that increases the undead's damage done by 20% and their Physical Resistance and Spell Resistance resistance by 4,550. This spell appears as a blue glow around the hands of the buffed enemy as well as those of Adavos Dren. This spell can be interrupted to knock Adavos Dren off balance.
;Bone Cage:Adavos Dren casts a rune on the ground that activates after a while, dealing low magic damage and rooting targets who step in it. This attack can be interrupted to knock Adavos Dren off balance.
;Run Away!:When too close to him, Adavos Dren backs up to put some distance between his target and himself.
;Bone Surge:Adavos Dren casts a Bone Shield-like ability on himself which reduces his melee damage taken by 20% until this disappears.

## Bugs
Despite being an elite rank enemy, Adavor Dren is vulnerable to crowd control.
