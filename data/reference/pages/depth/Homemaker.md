sign=Craft
type=passive
stagepoints=25
maxpoints=25
desc=You have a 10% chance to find a second furnishing plan whenever you find a furnishing plan in the world.

Homemaker gives you 10% chance to loot a second furnishing plan of the same loot pool in the same quality whenever you find a furnishing plan in the world. It does not apply for furnishing plans found in daily quests reward boxes or bought from NPC Merchants, Guild Traders and with Writ Vouchers.

## Notes
- Before Update 45, this Champion Star had to be slotted in order to be active.
