Eric Wrobel is a test character that was once often used by the developers as a target dummy to test out abilities on. He is named after developer Eric Wrobel, who served as the game's Lead Combat Designer from 2015 to 2019. In appearance, he is a Breton man with the muscular slider set to the highest option, and wears no clothes save for a jester hat. He has 1 million health points, and will brandish his arms when attacked, but has no attacks of his own.

He has been shown in some videos, such as ESO Live - [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tub8R8b2elc Introduction to the Warden (Morrowind)].

Within the files, there also exists other variants of Wrobel including Cleric Wrobel, Count Wrobel, Invisible Eric Wrobel, Mobile Wrobel, and Vampire Wrobel.

In an update following Wrobel's departure from the company, this character was renamed to Wheels.
