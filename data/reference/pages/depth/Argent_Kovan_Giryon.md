Argent Kovan Giryon is a variant of the Dark Elf Telvanni mage Kovan Giryon, who can be found in the northern area of Sorrow's Friend within the Night Market. As an Argent enemy, the nearby Brazen Kynmarcher Strix and Plague of Crows will need to be slain to lure him out. Conversely, killing him will cause the two Brazen bosses to spawn in the instance.

When he is active, you may experience orange temporal copies of Kovan Giryon appearing around the district which will launch themselves at you and use their Fold attack. Once he and Argent Exarch Molonach are killed, they will provide Gilded Keys, which can be used to access the Mournful Catacomb dungeon.

As an Argent enemy, he is a potential target of the related quest.
