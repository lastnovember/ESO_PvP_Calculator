The Broodmother is a giant spider found at the end of Barkbite Cave.

## Related Quests
- Enemies at the Gate

## Skill and Abilities
;Corrosive Bite: Basic melee attack dealing minor poison damage.
;Bite: The Broodmother takes a fierce bite, dealing minor physical damage. This can be blocked to set the Broodmother off-balance.
;Poison Spray: The Broodmother sprays venom at its target, indicated by a conical AoE, dealing continuous direct minor poison damage.
;Poisonous Burst: The Broodmother spits out a glob of poison at its target position, indicated by a growing red circle. Once the circle becomes full, the cluster explodes, dealing low poison damage.
;Devour: If a nearby spider corpse is present, either big or small, the Broodmother rushes over to it and starts eating it to regain Health every second. This can be interrupted to set the Broodmother off-balance.

## Notes
- This enemy has a near instant respawn rate.

## Bugs
Despite being an elite rank enemy, the Broodmother is vulnerable to crowd control.
