Disastrix Vibius is an Imperial and the leader of the Order of the Waking Flame cell who is about to lead an attack on Wretched Spire.

## Related Quests
- Peace's Wretched Price

## Skills and Abilities
;Flare:A basic ranged attack that does low flame damage.
;Heat Wave:The enemy releases fire along the ground in the direction it is facing. This attack does moderate flame damage to all targets hit by it. This spell can be interrupted to set the enemy off balance.
;Fire Runes:The enemy shoots two fireballs in the air which form two fire runes when they land. The runes do moderate flame damage over time to all targets within the red circles. Players should be prepared to move as soon as the attack casts as one rune will always be cast on top of you.
