# Online:Daedric Summoning

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Daedric_Summoning
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Daedric Summoning is one of three class skill lines available to Sorcerer characters. It can be learned by other classes via subclassing. Abilities on this line call upon Daedric pacts and allies for strength.

## Skill Perks
The Daedric Summoning skill line is immediately available to all Sorcerers, and is leveled up by earning experience while Daedric Summoning skills are slotted. Skills include:
