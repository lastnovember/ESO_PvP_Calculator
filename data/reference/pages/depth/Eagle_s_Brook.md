It is under threat from the Bloodthorn Cult, who have been transforming the residents into zombies. Villagers and members of the Lion Guard reside here.

There is a chest and a heavy sack sitting up against the side of the Bloodthorn-Occupied House. There are chests at the edge of the northwestern cliff, on the cliff above it, along the water on the northwestern shore and against the side of the mountain, west of the village, near a large tree. On the docks, there is a Thieves Trove on the front of a cart. In a beached boat near the docks, there is a chest submerged in shallow waters. To the east, near where the docks begin, there is a heavy sack in the sand.

Eagle's Brook also has an Antiquity excavation site behind the southernmost house.

## Wayshrine
The Eagle's Brook Wayshrine is located southeast of the town, on the main road to Camlorn. A chest can spawn by a large pile of rocks south of the wayshrine.

## Notes
- Pharik at-Toura once lived here before his wife was killed by Bloodthorn cultists.
- As part of grouping improvements in Update 4, the pocket realm accessed during the "Cutting Off the Source" quest was changed from Lord Velian's Plane (a separate instance of Eagle's Brook with an orange tint and occupied by Banekin), to Desolation's End, a nightmarish Daedric ruin.
