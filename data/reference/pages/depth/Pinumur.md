Pinumur was a Khajiit who could be found at the then-blocked eastern gate in Craglorn. He was removed from the game after the zone of Upper Craglorn was added in Update 4 and the gate was opened.

## Dialogue
If you go talk to him, he'll be eager to get revenge on the Iron Orcs.
:"Have you come to slay Iron Orcs? If so, give this one a wide berth. What I do will be … messy."
:You don't seem concerned.
:"Pinumur does not tuck his tail for dung-headed savages. They were lucky the first time. If they attack again, Pinumur will make them pay."
:Goodbye.
