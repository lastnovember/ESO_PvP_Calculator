# Online:Arbalest

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Arbalest
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Arbalests are a class of hostile, humanoid NPCs who attack using ranged abilities. They are usually equipped with a bow, and their attacks have an explosive component. The class was introduced with Morrowind.

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Shot:A basic ranged attack dealing minor physical damage.
;Arrow Stab:A basic melee attack dealing minor physical damage where the Arbalest grabs their arrow and stabs it into the target rather than firing it.
;Bombard:The arbalest crouches and fires several arrows into the air, and where they land will develop several small AoE circles dealing continuous direct low flame damage.
;Quakeshot:A wide range attack where the arbalest fires arrows downwards in the area in front of them, both causing the ground to quake in a cone and launch three areas forward, each dealing moderate physical damage and staggering. Used at close range only.
;Chilled Ground:The arbalest fires a frozen arrow at a target which unleashes a cloud of frost under them for a few seconds, snaring all targets inside.
