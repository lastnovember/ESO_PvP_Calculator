The Abyssal Cabal
The Abyssal Cabal is a group of four Sea Sload who have banded together for a common goal: to destroy Summerset. They have an arrangement with the Court of Bedlam.

Each Sea Sload specializes in a different kind of magic: K'Tora is adept in mind magic, K'Garza in necromancy, B'Korgen in alchemy and Z'Maja in shadow magic. The Cabal is opening Abyssal Geysers across the island in an attempt to sink it. In addition, each member has their own project: Z'Maja has taken Cloudrest, covering the city in a blanket of shadow and enslaving the shadows of all its inhabitants, B'Korgen is alchemically empowering the Abyssal Cabal's servants, the Yaghra, in Welenkin Cove, K'Garza is raising an undead army in Karnwasten, and K'Tora is playing a part in a greater plot.
