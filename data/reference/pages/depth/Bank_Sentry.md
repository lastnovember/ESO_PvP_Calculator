Bank Sentries are guards found in the Bank Lower Level of the Bank of Daggerfall and are only encountered during the related quest.

## Related Quests
- Three Thieves in Daggerfall

## Quest-related Events
During the quest, they can be met in the bank in Daggerfall, upstairs and downstairs. They will react to the trespassing player:
:Bank Sentry:"Halt Intruder!"
:Bank Sentry:"That's it, out with you!"
:Bank Sentry:"You don't belong here!"
