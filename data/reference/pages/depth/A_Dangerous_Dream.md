## Detailed Walkthrough
On the road from Crosswych to Koeglin Village, there is a wrecked wagon with dead guards around it. Talk to Recruit Thomas, who pleads to find Captain Jerick, who was taken by the Supernal Dreamers to a nearby camp. It is just up the road towards Koeglin village. Working your way through the camp, you will come upon a ritual site with two Supernal Dreamers' Brazier holding the captain in place. Run up to one of the braziers and douse the unnatural flames to anger the Daedra holding Captain Jerick.

The Dremora Kynval will send two waves of Banekin at you, then spawn himself to deal with the situation.

:Dremora: "You dare meddle with the Supernal Dreamers?"
:Dremora: "Taste the flames of Oblivion."

When he's calling in Banekin and Supernal Dreamer cultists to deal with you:
:Dremora: "Go, my minions! Slash and rend."

Once you dispatch his allies, he will be frustrated and come out to deal with you himself:
:Dremora: "Why won't you die?"
:Dremora: "In Vaermina's name, die."

After defeating him, shake Captain Jerick awake.

He will awaken, shake off his Daedric chains, and run off to Koeglin village where he will meet you. He can be found recovering at the Sleepy Sailor Inn.
:Captain Jerick: "What happened? I have to get to Koeglin Village. Meet me there!"

Talk to him to complete the quest.
