Blue-Bird is an Argonian found in the town of Darkwater Crossing.

## Related Quests
- Our Poor Town

## Quest-Related Events
When you enter the hiding place of the survivors of the troll attack, she is appealing to Stormy-Eyes to help Sister-of-Wind.
:Stormy-Eyes: "Pain delirious. She falls in and out of consciousness. The troll bite is enflamed and it begins to ooze."
:Blue-Bird: "Please, Stormy. There must be something you can do."

If you speak to her, she tells you how Sister-of-Wind was injured.
:"Sister-of-Wind was injured when the trolls attacked. She's still unconscious and I'm worried about her. Stormy thinks he can save her, but he needs supplies that were left behind when we fled."

If return to the cave before turning in Our Poor Town, you'll find her sitting down.
:"Poor Sister! I've never seen such terrible injuries! Stormy said that everything would be all right. Told me to sit and rest. I hope he can really save her!"
