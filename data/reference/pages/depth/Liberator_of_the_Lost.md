desc=Free 25 Slaves and Refugees from the Pirates and Slavers of Southern Elsweyr.

Liberator of the Lost is awarded for freeing slaves and refugees from hostile forces around Southern Elsweyr. This simply involves killing these pirates or slavers, allowing their captives to flee. (It is not necessary to untie them or anything like that.) The best places to find these captives are South Guard Ruins and Zazaradi's Quarry and Mine.Anywhere else? The player will be credited with freeing four refugees for completing A Childhood in Flames.
