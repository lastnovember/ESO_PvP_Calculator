Disguised Prisoners are the disguised versions of Idrothiel, Helmir the Headscratcher and Shifty Eyes. When you free them and give them their caravan thug disguises, they will put them on and run away from the caravan.

## Related Quests
- Hadran's Fall

## Quest-Related Events
During the related quest, you free three of Hadran's prisoners and give them uniforms to disguise them as some of Hadran's thugs. After they disguise themselves, they run off.

## Bugs
Shifty Eyes's character model and fur pattern change completely when she becomes disguised.
