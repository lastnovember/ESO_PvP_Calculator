# Online:Brackenleaf's Briars (faction)

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Brackenleaf's_Briars_(faction)
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Brackenleaf's Briars is an order of Grahtwood hunters protecting Brackenleaf, a tree which they believe was planted by Y'ffre at the creation of the world. Upon completion of the related quest, you are inducted into the group's ranks.
