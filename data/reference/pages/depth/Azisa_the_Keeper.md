Azisa the Keeper is a Redguard vampire who can be found at the Shrines of Lamae Bal in the three different alliance zones.

## Related Quests
- Scion of the Blood Matron

## Quest-Related Events
If you started the related quest from the Crown Store or from Zalig, you will be asked to seek out a shrine dedicated to Lamae Bal to meet with the vampire waiting for you there. Otherwise, you can meet with her directly.

Speak with Azisa and she will explain the curses and benefits of being vampire, before confirming you wish to proceed with being infected:

## Dialogue
She can be found at the shrines, even when not completing the related quest. Her greeting to you will depend on your current nature:
width=33%| Fellow Vampire:
width=33%| Werewolf:
width=50%| Mortal (While doing the Lycanthropy Quest):

:"Ah, greetings, fellow nightkin. May your thirst never be unsatisfied."

:"Your scent is most unwelcome here, mutt. You befoul my shrine with that stink. Now use those filthy paws to take yourself away from here."

:"Do not wander this shrine idly, stranger. I would not linger long here if I were you. Although … I am getting thirsty. Stay if you wish, at your own peril."plain

If you are a former vampire, you can speak with Azisa and regain your status as vampire, no quest needed:
:"Ah, a former nightkin. How strange! I recognize it on you. I do not know what led you here, but I would help you on your journey. Name your desire, ex-kin, and I will assist if I can."
:Could you turn me back into a vampire?
:"Of course, my child. Dearest Mother Lamae always welcomes her wayward children back into the fold. But your journey has taken many twists and turns already. I must ask, are you certain?"
:Yes, make me a vampire again. / Make me a vampire again.
::"As you wish, bloodkin. The night sings you back into its dark embrace. This will hurt just a pinch, but I am sure you recall that much."
:If you're the one who's biting me, then definitely. Flirty
::"Oh, do you crave my mouth on your neck? You are still mortal, you know. It would be all too easy for things to go awry. Even a vampire as experienced as myself can get carried away at times."
:I'm not sure.
::"Then depart from here. I am unsure as to why you left us, but I have no interest in indulging the flippant whims of a mortal. Return to me when you have decided on your path."

If you accept the bite again, you will reactivate the vampire skill line without having to return to Lamae Bal.
