Dwemer Spider Swarms were Swarming Dwarven Spiders found in Lower Bthanual and Mzulft. They were absolutely identical to Swarming Dwarven Spiders. They have kept this name until at least the July 7, 2014, and were since all properly renamed "Swarming Dwarven Spider". This was possibly a previous name used for Swarming Dwarven Spiders during ESO's developement before being changed when ESO released, but those specific places were missed.

## Skills and Abilities
;Shocking Touch: A basic melee attack dealing minor shock damage.
;Detonation:Upon dying, rarely the Dwemer Spider Swarm explodes which deals minor flame damage in an area around it.
