# Online:Blackfeather Court

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Blackfeather_Court
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Blackfeather Court is a feudal court of talking crows that rules over the realm of Crow's Wood. They were hatched by the Crow Mother, and are led by the Duke of Crows. You will be given citizenship of the Blackfeather Court during the quest Decree of the Court, along with a Blackfeather Court Whistle.

The Court appears once more in the Clockwork City, having stolen a Daedric artifact from Sotha Sil. Upon arriving in the Clockwork City and discovering the large bounty of shinies and trinkets to plunder, they revoked their allegiance to Nocturnal in favor of claiming this place, putting them at odds with a fellow group of crows still loyal to the Night Mistress, known as the Exarchs of Dross.

The Court offers daily quests which reward Crow-Touched Clockwork Coffers, which may contain chapters for learning to craft the Ebonshadow style.
