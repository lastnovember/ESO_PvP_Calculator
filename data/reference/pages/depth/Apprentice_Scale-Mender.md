Apprentice Scale-Mender is an Argonian member of the Mages Guild and part of the expedition found inside Selene's Web.

## Related Quests
- Knowledge Gained

## Quest-Related Events
She can be first met at the entrance with the rest of the expedition:
:"Long way from the Mages Guild. Warlock Carindon speaks for us. You should see him if you have an issue."

Once you have agreed to help the expedition, she will have more to say:
:"The price of knowledge is always high, but I don't like the feel of this place. Mayhem awaits us, I think."

After Apprentice Patraud is killed during his attempt to negotiate with the Bosmer tribe, Scale-Mender will arrive with the rest of the expedition:
:"By the Egg, those Bosmer were vicious. I won't forget Patraud's sacrifice. Let's hope there's research worth doing in this forest."

Once you have killed Treethane Kerninn after losing Evoker Ahainar-jo, Scale-Mender will help escort a Bosmer prisoner into the clearing while wielding a sword:
:"This isn't going to bring Patraud and Ahainar-jo back, but if we don't get some information out of this Bosmer, their deaths are meaningless."

After you defeat Queen Aklayah, a portal will open nearby and Apprentice Scale-Mender and the other mages will step through. If spoken with, she will say:
:"Two of my colleagues have returned to the Root so that the rest of us could stand here. I will honor their memories."

## Bugs
Apprentice Scale-Mender's dead position is misaligned, causing her to float above the ground instead of sitting on the ground near the tree.
