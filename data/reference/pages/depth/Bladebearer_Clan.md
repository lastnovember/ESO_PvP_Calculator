# Online:Bladebearer Clan

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Bladebearer_Clan
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Bladebearer Clan (commonly called Bladebearers) is a nomadic clan of independent Dremora, led by Rynkyus, that is active in The Deadlands. They adhere to a strict code of honor and behavior, bound to no Prince.
