Bone Colossi are large undead creatures constructed from many bones. While most bone colossi are elite enemies, smaller and weaker versions can be summoned by bonelords during battle.

## Skills and Abilities
As elite enemies, bone colossi are immune to all crowd control effects.

### Standard
;Strike: A basic melee attack dealing moderate physical damage.
;Bone Saw: The bone colossus stomps its foot into the ground, indicated by a red cone, dealing moderate physical damage and staggering affected targets.
;Voice to Wake the Dead: The bone colossus summons three Risen Dead to assist it in battle.

### Voriak Solkyn SummonsOrsinium
Bone Colossi in Voriak Solkyn's fight also use this extra ability.
;Rain of Fire: The bone colossus screams and looks up at the sky, summoning an infernal rain of fire all over the arena, indicated by multiple large and overlapping AoEs, dealing continuous high flame damage.

### ThurvokunDragon Bones and Lady Minara SummonsMurkmire
Bone Colossi in Thurvokun's and Lady Minara's fight use those abilities instead. They also have a passive 35% speed boost.
;Clash of Bones: dealing very high physical damage and stunning.
;Coldfire Blast: dealing moderate-high frost damage.

### Dragon SummonsDragonhold
Bone Colossi summoned by Dragons have additional abilities.
;Fireblast:
;Trample:The bone colossus charges in a straight line, dealing high physical damage and knocking down enemies in its path.

### Rimmen Necropolis
The Bone Colossi in Rimmen Necropolis use those abilities instead.
;Strike: A basic melee attack dealing moderate physical damage.
;Pound: The Bone Colossus heavily strikes the ground, dealing moderate physical and snaring targets hit around it.
;Exhume: dealing moderate physical damage.

### Bloodrun Cave
Bone Colossi in Bloodrun Cave also use this extra ability.
;Necromantic Stomp: The bone colossus overcharges with energy, and then stomps the ground, indicated by a very large growing red circle, dealing high magic damage and knocking affected targets away and down to the ground.

### Writhing Fortress
The Bone Colossi in the Writhing Fortress also use those abilities.
Before the fight, the Bone Colossus is surrounded by a bubble and some enemies. Once the surrounding enemies are defeated, the Bone Colossus enters combat and other enemies arrive.
;Collision:The bubble around the Bone Colossus before entering combat. Touching it deals low frost damage and knocks you back.
;Necrotic Wave: The bone colossus overcharges with energy, and then stomps the ground, indicated by a very large growing red circle, dealing moderate flame damage and knocking back targets hit.
;Crushing Charge/Necrotic Fire:The bone colossus charges in a straight line, dealing moderate physical damage and knocking back targets hit (Crushing Charge), while leaving blue flames in its path dealing continuous direct low flame damage (Necrotic Fire).

## Notes
