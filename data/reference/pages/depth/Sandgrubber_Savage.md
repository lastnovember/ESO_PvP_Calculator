Sandgrubber Savages were goblin brutes of the Sandgrubber tribe found in Divad's Chagrin Mine before Update 5, which changed and enlarged the Alik'r Desert delves.

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Strike: A basic melee attack dealing minor physical damage.
;Low Slash: The Sandgrubber Savage slashes the target's shins, dealing moderate physical damage and snaring. Blocking will reduce the damage taken, but not the snare.
;Focused Charge: The Sandgrubber Savage charges forward, dealing moderate physical damage, knocking down and stunning for 1 second any targets in its wake as indicated by the red rectangle. This can be blocked to stun the Sandgrubber Savage instead. If the Sandgrubber Savage does not hit anything, it will be stun itself for 2 seconds.
