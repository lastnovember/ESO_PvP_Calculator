It is only available from the Crown Store.

The house was added in Update 13 and has only been available for purchase as a limited-time offer. It was first available from April 27 to May 1, 2017, and returned at a discounted price from April 18 to April 27, 2019 as part of the Anniversary Jubilee 2019. It returned again from January 14 through 28, 2021, and from June 16 to June 30, 2022.

## Notes
- Although the island is accessed from Grahtwood, the Tamriel world map indicates that it's actually located off the eastern coast of the large island in Topal Bay. The Blackwood zone map places the house in the same place, off the coast of the larger island.
- This house had an extremely rare chance to be found in reward boxes during the Ten Million Stories dungeon event.
- This is, to date, the only home which will earn you Grand Master Decorator immediately upon purchase if you choose the Furnished option. Even Unfurnished, it has enough for Journeyman Decorator and Conservatory Decorator.purchase on PTS: only 337 furnishings
