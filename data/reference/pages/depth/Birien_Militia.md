The Birien Militia are employed by Lady Birien to guard her estate found to the south of Aldcroft in Glenumbra.

## Related Quests
- A Guild Grows In Glenumbra

## Quest-Related Events
Edwane the Hawk will ask you to rescue Gorbert Madach and Carso from Birien Manor. Before you attempt to enter the estate, he will cause a distraction for the guards at the front gate.

Near the entrance to the estate, Edwane will appear with some fellow thieves and taunting the estate's guards:
