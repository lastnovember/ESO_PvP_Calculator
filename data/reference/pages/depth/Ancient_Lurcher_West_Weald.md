Ancient Lurcher is a lurcher of a green aura that is randomly encountered at a spriggan ritual in West Weald. It drops nothing on death.

## Skills and Abilities
;Claw: A basic melee attack dealing minor physical damage.
;Pulverize: The lurcher quickly shoves its target, dealing moderate physical damage as well as staggering its target. This can be blocked to prevent the stagger.
