Aurig Mireh is a Golden Saint who can be found at the Shivering Shrine. She must be defeated to clear the location. Clannfear and scamps will come out of portals during the fight.

She has a chance to drop the Aureal Armor Glaze antiquity lead for the Spaulder of Ruin.

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Strike:A basic melee attack that does minor physical damage.
;Fiery Breath:The enemy breathes fire as indicated by a red cone. This attack does moderate flame damage over time and snares all enemies within the cone. This attack can be blocked to reduce damage or simply avoided.
;Dark Talons:The enemy calls forth talons from the ground, dealing minor magic damage and rooting anyone hit for 4 seconds. The player can roll dodge to escape the root.
;Lava Whip:The enemy winds up a flaming whip that does moderate flame damage to the player. This attack can be blocked to set the enemy off balance.
