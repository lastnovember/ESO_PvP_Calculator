# Online:Swimming

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Swimming
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

A person swimming
Swimming is a type of movement done in water. While shallow water can simply be walked through, going through any water deeper than waist level will result in you swimming through it. Swimming in deeper or dangerous waters will cause you to slowly die to Slaughterfish.

There are some different types of liquids that can be swam through such as lava, which will cause you to burn to death, and the black ooze of Apocrypha, which can kill you through Abyssal Assault.

## Speed
While Swimming, your movement speed is reduced to 60% of your regular walking speed. It can be increased to be a maximum of 120%.

### Methods of Increasing
There are multiple ways to increase your speed while swimming, such as:
- Amphibian passive
- Items that increase speed in general, such as the Ring of the Wild Hunt

## Mount Swimming
Originally, mounts could not swim, and entering deep water would therefore force you to dismount. With Update 47, animations were added for each variety of mount to ensure mounted travel is not disrupted when crossing bodies of water. It is still not possible to mount up while already swimming.

## Underwater Swimming

There are a select few locations such as Graven Deep and Seaveil Spire where one can technically swim underwater by travelling through a magic air bubble.
