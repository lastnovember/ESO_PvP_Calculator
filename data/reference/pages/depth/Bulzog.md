Bulzog is an Orc member of the Bitterhand Bandits found holding hostages in a mill south of the town of Fell's Run. He has orders to execute them if he doesn't hear back from the constable soon. He will be accompanied by a Bitterhand Rogue. He has no unique dialogue.

## Related Quests
- Frightened Folk

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Strike:A basic melee attack dealing minor physical damage.
;Fiery Breath:Bulzog breathes fire as indicated by a red cone, dealing moderate flame damage.
;Lava Whip:Bulzog winds up a Lava whip dealing moderate flame damage to the target. This attack can be blocked to set Bulzog off balance.
;Dark Talons:Bulzog calls forth talons from the ground, dealing minor flame damage and rooting anyone hit for 4 seconds.

## Bugs
Despite being an elite rank enemy, Bulzog is vulnerable to crowd control.
