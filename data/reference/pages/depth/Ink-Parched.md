## Detailed Walkthrough
You come across a Sand-Worn Field Journal in a random location in the Parch district of the Night Market (such as on the floor of a southeastern stall containing mushrooms and carpets or in the dunes northwest of the Timeless Wallow dungeon)other locations?). Check it out to begin the quest:

There will be three locations you need to visit, once you are in an appropriate spot you will be given the option to write in the Sand-Worn Field Journal. The locations are not randomized and are found at the following places.
- The northwest side of the western rooftop oasis, overlooking the Brazen Taupezu Azzida spawn point.
- Sand dunes in the center of the Parch, to the south of the Ritual Skirmish.
- South side of the northeast rooftop oasis.

Once you have filled out the book with the missing details, head to the northern rooftop oasis. Then drop the field journal off at the Field Journal Box:
:<A notice inside this collection box states that it is managed by Myshka. He promises safe delivery of parcels from the Parch to the Starlit Plaza.
:
:

You will receive a curated reward coffer, gold, and some faction favor.
