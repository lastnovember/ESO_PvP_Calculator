## Detailed Walkthrough

### Zhanlaj Khenrathi
Once you reach high enough reputation and have done the previous quests, Zerith-var will get a questmarker over his head. Talk to him and Zerith-var will tell you there is something in the wind, a memory summoning him to Zhanlaj Khenarthi. He tells you that it had fallen to ruins and likely still is in that state, and that he thinks you might be able to find something there that could help you defeat Ravith-morna. After giving you the direction, he asks you to come with him. Agree and the quest starts officially. You can now optionally ask a few more questions about the moon claw, Khenarthi's Rise, the allied Cantor's you met in earlier quests, and Ravith-morna before setting off to Weeping Wind Cave in Reaper's March. When you're ready talking, go there. The easiest way is to go by Willowgrove Wayshrine if you have that unlocked.

After entering Weeping Wind Cave, Zerith-var will tell you to "search in the north". Unexpectedly, your target is in the northern most room. Sadly, the quickest route is one-way from the opposite direction, so you will have to go at least around the room in the middle. The delve is quite small, so that should not cause much inconvenience. Once in the correct room, you'll find the entrance to Khenarthi's Rise in a shadowy area opposite of the hole the ray of light comes in from (although the quest marker likely gave it away already). Enter and you find yourself in what looks like what was once a grand Khajiiti estate fallen to ruin. Zerith-var will mention that he senses Cantor Krin'ze is near, so look for her. Outside if the building you need to go in for the quest, there isn't much else around, an armory with some valueless gear and a temple full of jars (possibly urns) that is remarkably well preserved but not intractable, and a bunch of destroyed buildings. One stair next to your entrance leads to a path to leave Khenarthi's Rise without having to go trough the delve. It's one-way, so don't take it if you don't want to walk around to get back.

Find Krin'ze in the building right opposite of you when entering. She won't notice Zerith-var's attempt to converse and he asks you to try instead. Simply trying to talk to her works about as well for you as it does for Zerith-var, so you need to find some other way. You will also find some books and scrolls around, which mainly serve to help you recall the story so far as well as give you a bit of extra lore (for example, the connection between the Moon Claws and The Moonlight Blade, some contemplation about the origins of Moon Beasts, translation of the Song of Binding). Read or skip those, and talk to Krin'ze. Talbira will appear telling you that if you bring Khenarthi's Rise back to order your path will be made clear.

### Restoring Khenarthi's Rise
Go back outside to start restoring. Unsurprisingly, you need to get to the previous two mentioned buildings as well as a room inside the building Krin'ze is in. Starting with the room in the same building, Zerith-var with mention it's a house of scrolls and suggests to look what knowledge can be salvaged. Between all the papers, there are some scrolls about the duality of weapons and those who wield it. Put those scrolls into the scroll stand and the spirit of Cantor Ta'khida will appear to speak a few words. In case you don't remember her, Zerith-var will immediately remind you. Talk with him, after which you have to give him some advice and (you can choose whichever you like) have restored this house of scrolls.

Next go into the armory. Zerith-var will tell you the blessings of the weapons can be restored with moon-sugar water. Go into the previous room with the jars, which Zerith-var will call the sacristy. Having a choice between an empty jar, a cracked one, and a sealed decanter you can likely guess which one you need (the sealed one). Take it to the armory and empty it over the ancient weapons and the spirit of Cantor Zayabi will appear and speak a few more words to Zerith-var. Talk with him again and advise him, then continue.

Your next objective is in the back, where a bunch of bones from the members of the Hidden Moon are scattered on the floor. Gather them and put them in the open grave site. Zerith-var will say a few last words in their honor, giving them a late but certain burial ceremony. Meanwhile, you get to do the hard work and bury them. This time, the spirit of Cantor Izalgo appears. Once more, talk to Zerith-var, give him some advice, and the place is restored.

Return to Krin'ze. She will go back to normal once you come in, so talk to her. She will ask if Zerith-var is ready for the truth, that he must forgive himself and let go of his guilt as it ties him to Ravith-Morna. She tells you that that connection is why they both came back at the same time, and that indeed Azura didn't refuse Zerith-var's soul but that he himself held him back out of guilt. She will then ask you if you've seen Viti, and that she went to get the other Cantors. You can now optionally ask a bit more about the trance she was in and the things she learned from it, as well as where Viti went. After talking to her talk to Zerith-var, give him advice once more and he will explain how you're going to find Ravith-morna. He tasks you to take out the moon-sugar water used before once more and pour it into the ritual basin, so he can invoke an ancient rite to receive Azurah's direct guidance. Do so and watch the scene.

### Stopping Ravith-morna
After Zerith-var has learned the location of Ravith-morna, he will open a portal to the location. As he does so, blue flames start to burn; Zerith-var explains this means hordes of dro-m'athra are approaching, rushing you to go through the portal. Arriving at the other side, you find the place shrouded in darkness and a bunch of dead Inquisitors on the floor. You can ride your mount in this location if you wish. Talk to Krin'ze and she will be rather worried about the dead Inquisitors, as they would not act without Cantors to aid them. Realising the Cantors must be here as well, you're tasking with saving them next to stop Ravith-morna. Go look for the Twilight Cantors first, while Krin'ze hides. A bit further up the road, you run into Viti and two unnamed Cantors. Talk with Viti and continue to rescue the other 3 groups of Cantors, while Krin'ze takes care of Viti.

After all three groups of Cantors are saved, continue to search for Ravith-morna. Continue on the path, and once you reach the bridge further up, the location will quickly become clear. Cross the bridge and talk to Zerith-var, who will explain what you're seeing is a doorway across planes, used to send Ravith-morna’s Moon Beast to the Crossing. Continue, and Krin'ze arrives soon after. Take the portal and the three of you will appear in the Great Darkness.

Continue down the road. While fighting your way through you'll hear the Moon Beast. Once you reach the end, fight Ravith-morna. Every now and then she will shield herself, after which she summons aspects of the Moon Beast to aid her, but she should not pose too much of a challenge after all your preparations.

After her defeat, Krin'ze will sing her song, while Zerith-var saves Ravith's soul. With Ravith-morna no more, only her Moon Beast is left, which will soon jump in to attack. However, it's easily held at bay by Krin'ze and Zerith-var, who leave you the task of banishing it; do so using Zerith-var's Moon Claw. After a small conversation between Zerith-var and Ravith, Zerith-var opens a portal to return to Nirn. Take it and talk to Zerith-var once more to end the quest.

## Notes

- While the sealed decanter has a clear Kahjiiti appearance, the animation for watering the weapons uses a pre-existing animation using regular translucent bottle.
- While burying the remains, the interaction says "dig" but in reality you put some sand on top.

## Bugs

Krin'ze may not react to you any more than she does Zerith-var, only resetting her animation to the begin position upon interaction
- Try talking to Zerith-var and try again, and make sure you stand in front of Krin'ze (and not next or behind).
