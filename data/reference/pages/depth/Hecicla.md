Hecicla is a Stone-Nest Argonian who lives a hermit's life in eastern Soltice. While you are delivering food to her, she can be found in her home in an abandoned village to the south of Calindvale Gardens.

## Related Quests
- The Hermit in the Hills

## Quest-Related Events
When you meet Wuleen-Kur, he will ask for your help delivering a package of food to Hecicla. He is unable to make the journey due to the cultists and daedra in the way. Following the road leading south past the gardens, you eventually find Hecicla's house in an abandoned village.

Entering the house, Hecicla will call out:
:Hecicla: "Who goes there? I warn you, these old scales are not as brittle as they look!"

Talk to her to introduce yourself and let her know why you arrive:
:"I warn you, if you are one of those death cultists or a bandit come to rob me, you will not be the first to fail in such an endeavor. This hermit knows how to take care of herself."
:Wuleen-Kur asked me to deliver this to you.
:"Ah, my stone-soup from Xor-Hist! I do appreciate the meals they send me. Even this old hermit sometimes misses the comforts of the village. They always send more than enough. If you prepare it, I will happily share a meal with you."
:You want me to cook the stone-soup?
:"Yes, that would be nice. I move much slower these days. More suited to stillness now, like the stone. But I would enjoy sharing a meal with a visitor. It doesn't take long to prepare. Put the cook pot in the fire and remove it before it burns."

After completing the quest, you can talk to her some more while she eats:
:"Thank you for stopping by. The soup was delicious! And tell young Wuleen-Kur I hope he comes to visit me one day. I suppose he just needs to grow a few more spines on his tail. Or maybe get one of the death-dancers to accompany him."

:"You saw the stone that surrounds my home? All raw and exposed? It makes the Xan-Neihave easier to hear, especially when you sit in stillness, away from the bustle of village life. Surely you feel the call of the Stone in this serene place?"
