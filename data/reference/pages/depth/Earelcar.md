Earelcar is a High Elf spirit master found in the ruins of Erokii, working for House Montclair. He serves as one of the delve's three bosses, and has no unique dialogue.

## Skills and Abilities
;Ice Arrow: Basic ranged attack dealing minor frost damage.
;Burdening Eye: Earelcar summons a Burdening Eye that moves toward a player.
;Burden: If the Burdening Eye touches the player, it explodes, dealing minor magic damage and snaring in an area.
;Spell Absorption: Earelcar channels a protection spell on an ally that reduces its damage taken by 80% from Magical Attacks (Magic, Flame, Frost and Shock damage). This spell can be interrupted to knock Earelcar off balance.
;Run Away!:When too close to him, Earelcar backs up to put some distance between his target and himself.

## Bugs
Despite being an elite rank enemy, Earelcar is vulnerable to crowd control.
