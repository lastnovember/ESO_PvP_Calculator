# Online:Invisible Web

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Invisible_Web
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Invisible Web is a clan of hostile Spiderkith and Spider Daedra which is active in Fargrave's Shambles district, specifically in the Skein Row neighborhood. They are a new arrival, having fled there following a recent altercation with The Grasp.

Upon arriving in the Shambles, they took out a small gang of mortal bandits that were using the row as their hideout, and quickly established themselves as a powerful threat in the area, alongside the other two neighboring gangs, The Errants and The Vanquished.
