# Online:House Dufort

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:House_Dufort
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

House Dufort is a major noble house of the Systres, holding the most of its influence on High Isle specifically. It originates from Camlorn on the mainland. Until the events of Ranser's War shook up their relationship, House Dufort and House Mornard worked together to turn the archipelago into a thriving region.

While House Mornard fell out of favor for them backing King Ranser, House Dufort came out on top as they gained most of House Mornard's holdings by decree of High King Emeric.
