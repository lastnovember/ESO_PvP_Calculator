Knights of the Gleaming Blade (book)
The Knights of the Gleaming Blade were a team of Ayleid servants of Meridia tasked by High King Cenedelin of Delodiil with razing the rival city of Abagarlas, destroying the Mortuum Vivicus, and hiding the Prismatic Core in the Dwemer ruins of Mzeneldt.

As part of the Fighters Guild questline, you retrace their steps.
