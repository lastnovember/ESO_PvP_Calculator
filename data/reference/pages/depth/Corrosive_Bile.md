Corrosive Bile is a Voriplasm-like structure encountered if you are swallowed by the Calamitous Dunegorger in the Parch.

## Skills and Abilities
;Acidic Enzyme: The slime sprays a corrosive compound at you, dealing moderate poison damage.

## Bugs
Despite being elite rank enemies, Corrosive Biles are vulnerable to Minor Mangle.
