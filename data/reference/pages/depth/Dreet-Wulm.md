Dreet-Wulm is an Argonian who serves as the scribe to Grand Warlord Zimmeron. She is in charge of picking up scouting reports and sending out campaign mails to soldiers of the Ebonheart Pact.

## Dialogue
When spoken to, she reveals what it's like to work for the Grand Warlord.
:"Grand Warlord Zimmeron is demanding, but at least the constant movement keeps me warm."

## Mission Boards
Dreet-Wulm is the author of several mission board messages.

## Alliance War Mails
Being the scribe to the Grand Warlord, Dreet-Wulm will send you mails as you earn Alliance Points and at the end of campaigns.
Rewards for the Worthy!

,
Grand Warlord Zimmeron extends warmest greetings. He is grateful for your part in our efforts in Cyrodiil. He awards you this gift and trusts you will continue to fight for the Pact.
Dreet-Wulm, Scribe to Grand Warlord Zimmeron

Rewards for the Worthy!

,
Grand Warlord Zimmeron extends warmest greetings to you, loyal Champion. He is grateful for your part in our efforts in Cyrodiil. He awards you this gift and trusts you will continue to fight for the Pact.
Dreet-Wulm, Scribe to Grand Warlord Zimmeron

If the Ebonheart Pact emerges victorious in your home campaign:
For the Pact!

,
With your help, the Pact emerged victorious in this campaign! Grand Warlord Zimmeron applauds your part in our triumph. You are hereby rewarded with gold for your efforts.
Dreet-Wulm, Scribe to Grand Warlord Zimmeron

If the Ebonheart Pact came in second in your home campaign:
Our Thanks, Warriorplain

,
The Pact fought well in this campaign, but we did not win. Your efforts on our behalf were noticed by the Grand Warlord, however. He has rewarded you with gold.
Dreet-Wulm, Scribe to Grand Warlord Zimmeron
If the Ebonheart Pact came in last in your home campaign:
The Pact Thanks Youplain

,
An ill wind blew against the Pact in this campaign, but your efforts were noted. The Grand Warlord has rewarded you with gold.
Dreet-Wulm, Scribe to Grand Warlord Zimmeron

These are standard campaign loyalty rewards.
Campaign Loyalty Reward

,
You fought well in this campaign, warrior. Grand Warlord Zimmeron thanks you for your efforts and offers you gold.
Dreet-Wulm, Scribe to Grand Warlord Zimmeron

Campaign Loyalty Reward

,
For your continued efforts on behalf of the Pact, the Grand Warlord has granted you gold. Your loyalty to our cause is greatly appreciated!
Dreet-Wulm, Scribe to Grand Warlord Zimmeron

If you are in the top 3-10% on the leaderboard for your campaign when it ends, you will receive one of these mails.
Congratulations!

,
Your deeds in this campaign deserve recognition-you were one of our most dependable warriors. Grand Warlord Zimmeron thanks you for your efforts and offers you gold.
Dreet-Wulm, Scribe to Grand Warlord Zimmeron

If you are on the top 2% of the leaderboard when the campaign ends, you will receive this mail.
For the Pact!

,
You bask in glory, warrior! As one of the most feared warriors in all of Cyrodiil, please accept this award of gold from Grand Warlord Zimmeron.
Dreet-Wulm, Scribe to Grand Warlord Zimmeron
