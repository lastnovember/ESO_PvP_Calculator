Domina Ssaranth is a lamia found in the Underroot, and the boss of that dungeon.

## Skills and Abilities
;Strike: Basic melee attack with claws that deals minor physical damage.
;Howling Strike: Domina Ssaranth howls, indicated by a red cone, dealing moderate magic damage and staggering the target. Despite having yellow sparks, blocking this ability doesn't set Domina Ssaranth off-balance.
;Harmony: Domina Ssaranth sings, granting 9,100 spell and physical resistance for the caster and all allies within range for the duration of the effect. Affected enemies are highlighted. This can be interrupted to set Domina Ssaranth off-balance. This cannot be cast if there is only one lamia.
;Block:Domina Ssaranth blocks, reducing damage taken. Heavy attacking the blocking Domina Ssaranth with a melee weapon causes the target to be staggered and be put off-balance. Only used when you attempt a melee attack at Domina Ssaranth.

## Bugs
