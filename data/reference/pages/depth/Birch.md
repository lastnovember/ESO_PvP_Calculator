Birch logs are a dusty white color with gray patches. They can be found in any zone by players with Woodworking Rank 6 or CP10 to 30. One log can be harvested for three to four samples of the raw material, Rough Birch. Once ten samples of rough wood are collected, they can be refined into seven to ten pieces of the processed material, Sanded Birch. From the refined birch pieces a woodworker can craft birch staves and bows, as well as birch shields, at any woodworking station. In order for you to create birch gear, your Woodworking ability has to be rank 6.
