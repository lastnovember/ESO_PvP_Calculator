# Online:Roadside Bandits

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Roadside_Bandits
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Roadside Bandits are bandits found roaming the Wrothgar wilderness. Besides preying on travelers, they poach wildlife and are often seen near animal corpses and traps. They wear Orcish equipment, but are not solely Orcs. Roadside Bandits are mostly encountered in Western Wrothgar, with a few found in Central Wrothgar as well.
