# Online:Keepers of the Dead

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Keepers_of_the_Dead
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Keepers of the Dead are a hierarchy of priests and attendants in Morrowind who serve as the caretakers of the large Necropolis complex in Necrom, the City of the Dead. They are responsible for handling the deceased Dunmer who are brought and prepared for burial with the appropriate rituals within the catacombs of Necrom.
