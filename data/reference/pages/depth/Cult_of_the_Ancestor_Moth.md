# Online:Cult of the Ancestor Moth

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Cult_of_the_Ancestor_Moth
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Cult of the Ancestor Moth is a priesthood dedicated to reading the Elder Scrolls. Their higher ranking members, called Moth Priests or Augurs, study the scrolls for knowledge to the point where they lose their eyesight.

The group is headquartered at the Temple of the Ancestor Moths in Cyrodiil, where the Elder Scrolls they hid were stolen by the three Alliances and housed in large Scroll Temples. A martial art known as the Way of the Peaceful Fist is practiced by the remaining residents of Temple.

In the White-Gold Tower, Moth Sister Terran Arminus has been recruited by Empress Regent Clivia Tharn to find an Elder Scroll in the Tower's ruined library. Tharn's missing husband, Emperor Varen Aquilarios, became a member of the priesthood after waking up at the steps of the Abbey of the Moth Priests with no memory of his past life following the Soulburst.
