# Online:Bolstered

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Bolstered
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Bolstered is a trait which can be found on Companion weapons, armor and jewelry. The trait cannot be researched or crafted, and exists only on found loot. Items with the Bolstered trait reduce the Damage a Companion receives by an amount dependent on the quality of the item.
