Arisho is a Dagi bard who can be found above the Senchal Crypts. He is playing the esraj near Saenssar in Senchal. He has no unique dialogue.

## Bugs
If you throw a mudball at him, he'll stand up and stop performing the animation for playing the esraj. However, the music from the instrument will not stop.
- This can be fixed by logging in and out.
