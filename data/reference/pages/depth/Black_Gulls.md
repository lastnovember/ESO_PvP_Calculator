# Online:Black Gulls

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Black_Gulls
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Black Gulls are a gang of slavers and smugglers who occupy the ruins of South Guard. Their rule of terror started during the time of the Knahaten Flu where their leader, Turo, made use of the chaos to get rid of his competitors.
