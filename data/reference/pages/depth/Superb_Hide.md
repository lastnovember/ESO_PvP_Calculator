Superb Hide is a crafting material obtained from animals in any zone by characters with Rank 8 in the Tailoring passive or CP70 to 80. One animal may yield one to two samples of the raw material, Superb Hide Scraps. Once ten scrap pieces are collected, they can be refined into seven to ten pieces of the processed material, Superb Hide. Refined superb hide is used for making medium superb armor at any clothing station. For you to be able to create superb armor, your Tailoring ability has to be rank 8.
