Eldecil is a High Elf member of the Veiled Heritance found inside the Imperial Underground located beneath the city of Woodhearth.

## Related Quests
- Veil of Illusion

## Skills and Abilities
;Entropic Flare:A basic ranged attack dealing minor magic damage.
;Void:Eldecil summons a purple orb from the sky that explodes when it hits the ground, dealing moderate magic damage in an area. This then sends out magic AoEs across the floor called Void Bursts. This can be interrupted to set Eldecil off balance.
;Void Burst:These AoEs are released after Void lands, each dealing minor magic damage.
;Run Away!:When too close to him, Eldecil backs up to put some distance between his target and himself.

## Dialogue
Make yourself known and he'll remark:
:Eldecil: "So the girl failed to eliminate you. No matter. I will remedy that."

Defeat him and he'll taunt:
:Eldecil: "This changes … nothing."

## Bugs
Despite being an elite rank enemy, Eldecil is vulnerable to crowd control.
