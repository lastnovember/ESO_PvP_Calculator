# Online:Black-Moon Clan

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Black-Moon_Clan
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Black-Moon Clan is a clan of hostile Reachfolk found in the Reach. They reside in the mountains northeast of Markarth, and worship the Daedric Prince Namira. Due to their hostility and seclusion, not much is known about them in comparison with most other clans. Clan members wear Minotaur Style armor.
