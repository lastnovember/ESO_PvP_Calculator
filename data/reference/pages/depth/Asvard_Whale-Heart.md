Asvard Whale-Heart is a skeleton caster found at Skyshroud Barrow, tasked with guarding the Whale Runestone which you need to retrieve in order to open the door to the inner barrow.

## Related Quests
- What Waits Beneath

## Skills and Abilities
;Shock:Basic ranged attack dealing minor shock damage.
;Thunder Thrall: Asvard Whale-Heart teleports to you and summons a bolt of lightning that strikes a large area around himself, dealing moderate shock damage to all targets as indicated by the red circle.
;Run Away!:When too close to him, Asvard Whale-Heart backs up to put some distance between his target and himself.

## Notes
- When you have cleared Skyshroud Barrow, all skeleton NPCs, including Asvard will become neutral and will not attack you anymore.

## Bugs
Despite being an elite rank enemy, Asvard Whale-Heart is vulnerable to crowd control.
