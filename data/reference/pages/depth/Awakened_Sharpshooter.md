Awakened Sharpshooters are animated statues of Akaviri archers that have been roused to protect their tomb from trespassers.

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Shot:A basic ranged attack that does minor physical damage.
;Arrow Spray:The enemy shoots a spray of arrows in the direction it is facing as indicated by a red cone. This attack does moderate physical damage as well as snares all enemies hit. This attack can be interrupted to set the enemy off balance or simply avoided. Blocking also prevents the snare and reduces the damage.
;Volley:The enemy fires continuously at an area as indicated by a red circle. This attack does moderate physical damage over time. This attack can be interrupted to set the enemy off balance or simply avoided.

## Dialogue
While in the delve, you may hear a female voice urging the guardians to awake and defend the tomb. Nearby Awakened will glow and start moving.
:"Kill the intruder. Only the strong can carry our message."
:"Wake from your sleep. Preserve our legacy. Destroy the outsider."
