Dusksaber Tormentors are Blood Mages working for the Dusksabers, a vampiric mercenary group from the Telvanni Peninsula. They each carry a staff and can summon a Bound Hemoptera.

## Related Quests
- Eye of Fate
- Spirit of Fate

## Skills and Abilities
;Fright Force: A basic ranged attack that does minor magic damage.
;Aspect of Terror: The enemy casts a spell that does moderate magic damage and fears the player for a short time, in which they cannot move or perform any actions. This effect passes through block, and players can break free from it. This spell can be interrupted to set the enemy off balance, or avoided using roll dodge.
;Shadow Cloak: If the target is in close proximity to the enemy, they will turn themselves invisible and move to a better location and set the player off balance.
;Summon Bound Hemoptera: The enemy summons a Bound Hemoptera, which channels a buff on their summoner that causes them to do twice the damage and take less damage. If the Hemoptera is interrupted, it becomes an Unbound Hemoptera. They will be rebound to their summoner after a few seconds.
;Sacrifice Hemoptera: The enemy can destroy its Hemoptera to heal itself.
;Red Breath:The vampire breathes blood in a cone, dealing moderate magic damage and knocking back targets hit. This can be blocked to be staggered instead.
