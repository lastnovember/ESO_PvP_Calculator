## Skills and Abilities
Aspect of the Lion are considered elite creatures, and are thus immune to all forms of crowd control effects.
;Claw: A basic melee attack that does minor physical damage.
;Rend: A basic melee attack that hits twice for minor physical damage.
;Double Strike: A charged melee attack that does moderate physical damage. This attack can be blocked to set the lion off balance.plain
;Backstabber: Aspect of the Lion has a passive ability that causes it to deal 20% more damage when attacking the target's back.
