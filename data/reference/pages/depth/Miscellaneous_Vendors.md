## The Golden
The Golden, also known as Adhazabi Aba-daro, is a special vendor that only appears in Cyrodiil at the weekend. She sells rare PvE-only set pieces for large amounts of AP or gold.

## Weapons

Weapons vendors sold both weapons and shields.

- Palmur-dra at the Temple of the Crescent Moons on Khenarthi's Roost originally had this vendor type, but is now an Armsman.
- Louarde Barbe in Shinji's Scarp originally had this vendor type, but is now a Weaponsmith.
