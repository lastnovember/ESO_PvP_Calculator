Nascent Indriks were located at the Event Exchanges located outside the main city of each Alliance's starting zone, as well as Chapter zones. They were found in pens next to the Impresario from 2018 to 2020, even when no event was active. On 5 January, 2021, the Nascent Indriks were removed at the conclusion of the 2020 New Life Festival to mark the retiring of Indrik evolutions as event rewards.
