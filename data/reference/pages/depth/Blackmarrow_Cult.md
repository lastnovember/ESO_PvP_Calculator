# Online:Blackmarrow Cult

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Blackmarrow_Cult
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Blackmarrow Cult are a group of necromancers, led by Orryn the Black, trying to reanimate the skeleton of Thurvokun, a long-dead dragon. They are the main enemies encountered in Fang Lair.

The Fang Lair style is based on this group, and Crafting Motif 58: Fang Lair Style is a manual written by Orryn for how his cultists should dress in order to make "an impressive showing".
