Azandar's Companion is awarded for completing all four of Azandar al-Cybiades's personal quests:
- Earn Azandar al-Cybiades's Loyalty: unlock Azandar as a companion by finishing the quest at.
- Complete Paths Unwalked. Requires.
- Complete Adversarial Adventures. Requires.
- Complete Tempting Fates. Requires

Earning this achievement allows purchasing Replica Fateweaver Key for your house. It costs 20,000 and can be purchased from Vasei in Necrom.
