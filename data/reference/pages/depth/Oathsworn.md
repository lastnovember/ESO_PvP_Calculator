# Online:Oathsworn

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Oathsworn
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Oathsworn are an elite group of Orcish warriors that are sworn to Mauloch and are considered one of the most martially-skilled groups in Tamriel. They are based at the Oathsworn Pit, which is located in the northern mountains of the Reach.

In recent times, the Oathsworn Pit has come under the attack of the Recollection, a Wood Elf cult that came to recruit the Oathsworn to their cause.
