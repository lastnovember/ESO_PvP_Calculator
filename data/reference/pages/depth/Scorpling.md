Scorplings were little Scorpions summoned in groups of four alongside a regular Scorpion by Lakorrah the Matron before being removed with Update 12. They were smaller, did less damage and had ~76% the Max Health of the summoned Scorpion (3,084 compared to 4,033).

## Skills and Abilities

;Pincer:The Scorpling attacks with a pincer, dealing minor physical damage
;Sting:The Scorpling attacks with its tail, dealing minor direct physical damage and minor poison damage over 8 seconds.plain
;Blurred Strike:The Scorpling attacks twice with its pincers, dealing minor physical damage with each strike. This can be blocked to set the Scorpling off-balance.plain
;Paralyze:The Scorpling channels an attack with its tail, dealing low physical damage and snaring for 7 seconds. This can be interrupted to set the Scorpling off-balance.plain
;Hardened Carapace:The Scorpling glows slightly, reducing its damage taken.plain
