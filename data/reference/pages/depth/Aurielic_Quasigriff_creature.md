Aurielic Quasigriffs were located at the Event Exchanges located outside the main city of each Alliance's starting zone, as well as Chapter zones. They began appearing in pens near the Impresario since the start of the 2022 Anniversary Jubilee on April 4, 2022, and were present even when no event is active. They stopped appearing when the Zeal of Zenithar event ended.
