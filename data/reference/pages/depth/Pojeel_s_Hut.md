It is located in the northwestern part of Stormhold, opposite of Looks-too-Long's Hut.

The hut is locked with a simple lock. Within, the house is partitioned by several wooden barricades. A safebox with a simple lock can be found on the floor on the left side of the hut. There is also a cooking fire inside the house. Am-Sakka and Veemarz can be found within.

## Residents
- Am-Sakka
- Veemarz

## Notes
- A Mercenary Recruit and Marauder can be seen fighting just outside, and are related to the quest Unwelcome Guests.
