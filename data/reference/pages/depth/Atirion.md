Atirion is a High Elf necromancer found inside Howling Cave only during the related quest.

## Related Quests
- An Evil Presence

## Skills and Abilities
;Necrotic Spear:A basic ranged attack dealing minor frost damage.
;Summon the Dead:Atirion often begins combat by summoning a zombie, a bloodfiend, a bloodfiend reaver, a bone reaver or a bone flayer. If the summoned creature is killed, another one will be summoned to replace it. The summon scales with the difficulty, faction, and location of the summoner. The summoned creature will have around the same health as the current health of Atirion but only to a maximum of 80% of his Max Health, and have extreme damage resistance during the summoning animation. Atirion's Physical Resistance and Spell Resistance are reduced to 0 while a summon is active as seen by a blue glow on him.
;Empower Undead:The necromancer channels a buff on a nearby undead (e.g. skeletons, zombies, and even bone colossi and vampires) that increases the undead's damage done by 20% and their Physical Resistance and Spell Resistance resistance by 4,550. This spell appears as a blue glow around the hands of the buffed enemy as well as those of Atirion. This spell can be interrupted to knock Atirion off balance.
;Run Away!:When too close to him, Atirion backs up to put some distance between his target and himself.

## Bugs
Despite being an elite rank enemy, Atirion is vulnerable to crowd control.
