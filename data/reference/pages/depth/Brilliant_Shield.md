# Online:Brilliant Shield

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Brilliant_Shield
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Brilliant Shield is one of three class skill lines available to Isobel Veloise. Abilities in this line focus on avoiding incoming damage from enemies. It is roughly equivalent to the Aedric Spear skill line for Templar players.

## Skill Perks
The Brilliant Shield skill line is immediately available to Isobel, and is leveled up as she gains levels.
