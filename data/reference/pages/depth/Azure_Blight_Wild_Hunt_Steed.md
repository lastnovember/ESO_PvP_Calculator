The Azure Blight Wild Hunt Steed is a horse mount in the appearance of an azureblighted Wildbriar Horse. It was first available in the Crown Store as a Crown Gem exclusive for 700/9,600 from June 9, 2022 to June 1, 2023. Its default name is "Scourgekeeper".

## Occurrences
Appearances:
- Crown Store - June 9, 2022 - June 1, 2023
- Crown Store - June 5, 2025 - September 22, 2025
