## Skills and Abilities

### Standard
;Entropic Touch: A basic melee attack dealing minor shock damage.
;Dire Wound: The banekin charges up a more potent spell and then flings it at its target, dealing moderate shock damage. This spell can be interrupted to set the banekin off-balance.
;Envelop: The banekin summons a ball of lightning above itself, indicated by a red circle, dealing continuous low shock damage. This spell can be interrupted to set the banekin off-balance.

### Summoned by Daedric Arches
The Banekins summoned by Daedric Arches only use this abitily.
;Explosive Charge:The banekin chases a player and sacrifices itself by exploding in a lightning burst when near them, dealing low magic damage in an area.

- There are several banekin pets, including the Banekin, Grisly Banekin Mummy, and Wickerfright Banekin.

## Bugs
The friendly Banekin in Eyevea doesn't run anymore, it walks instead, meaning the Daedroth easily outpaces it which often makes it snap close to the Daedroth.
