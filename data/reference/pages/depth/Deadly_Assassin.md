# Online:Deadly Assassin

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Deadly_Assassin
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Deadly Assassin is one of three class skill lines available to Mirri Elendis. Abilities in this line focus on dealing damage. It is roughly equivalent to the Assassination skill line for Nightblade players.

## Skill Perks
The Deadly Assassin skill line is immediately available to Mirri, and is leveled up as she gains levels.
