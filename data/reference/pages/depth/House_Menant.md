# Online:House Menant

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:House_Menant
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

House Menant is a Breton noble house currently led by Seline Menant, which has ties to the criminal underworld. When Seline's grandfather gambled away the family's wealth, her father Basil resorted to criminal means to get it back. Nowadays, the Menants are well immersed in thievery and have a focus on swindles and burgling the wealthy.
