Dominion Quartermasters are generic Alliance Quartermasters associated with the Aldmeri Dominion. They act as both a Guild Trader for the Guild in control of the keep or resource in which the quartermaster is located, as well as Siege Merchants. They can be Altmer, Bosmer or Khajiit. They have generic dialogue.

## Skills and Abilities
As alliance guards, the Dominion Quartermasters benefit from Keep and Resource upgrades.
;Quick Strike:A basic melee attack dealing minor physical damage.
;Heavy Attack:A heavy melee attack dealing moderate physical damage. This can be blocked to set the Dominion Quartermaster off balance.
;Throw Dagger/Throw Dagger Bleed:The Dominion Quartermaster backs away from the target and throws a dagger at the target. This attack snares, deals minor physical damage (Throw Dagger) then very minor bleed damage over time (Throw Dagger Bleed). This attack can be interrupted to set the Dominion Quartermaster off balance. The dagger can be blocked to prevent the snare and the bleed.
;Throw Oil/Ignite:A combo move that is only performed if Archers, Fire Mages or other fire using enemies (Flame Atronachs, Scamps etc.) are around. The Dominion Quartermaster throws a glob of tar under the target's feet as indicated by a red circle. While this does no damage, standing in it snares (Throw Oil). The Dominion Quartermaster will call for the other enemy to light the tar (usually accompanied by a voice clip) and the area will burn, dealing direct minor flame damage every half-second to targets within the red circle (Ignite). Ignite's damage is attributed to the enemy who lit the area on fire.
;War Horn:Very rarely, the Dominion Quartermaster blows a war horn, increasing the Max Health of their nearby allies and themselves by 15%
;Caltrops:Very rarely, the Dominion Quartermaster casts a Caltrops-like ability which deals continuous direct minor physical damage and snares in an area.
;Desecrated Ground:On death, the undead rarely desecrate the ground under them as indicated by a red circle. This does continuous minor magic damage and snares all targets as well as healing undead enemies within the red circle for 3% of the original undead's Max Health per tick. (see Bugs)

## Bugs
At least one Dominion Quartermaster in a keep is incorrectly flagged as Undead, and thus can cast "Desecrated Ground" upon death.
