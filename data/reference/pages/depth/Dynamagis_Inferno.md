Dynamagis Infernoes are Nord pyromancers affiliated with Archwizard Twelvane's Dynamagis that have invaded Warlock Vanton's mindscape. They are encountered in the Vanton's Dream portion of Sanity's Edge.

Spectral versions are also encountered in Vanton's Psyche, though they function identically to Ansuul's Infernoes. (see bugs)
