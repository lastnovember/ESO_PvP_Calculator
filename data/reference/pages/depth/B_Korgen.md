B'Korgen is a Sea Sload alchemist encountered in Welenkin Cove as a world boss.

Using his alchemical prowess and a supply of tainted coral, B'Korgen has been seeking to aid the other members of the Abyssal Cabal and cause the Yaghra to evolve. As a result of his activities, he has been marked for death by the Divine Prosecution.

## Skills and Abilities
As a boss rank enemy, B'Korgen is immune to crowd control. At the start of the fight, there are seven Alchemical Globules around B'Korgen.
;Summon Globules: B'Korgen summons Alchemical Globules throughout the fight, one or two at a time.
;Hallucinosis:When enough of Alchemical Globules are up (usually three or more), B'Korgen will continuously refresh a 15% Max Health damage shield (697,568). Also shadow waves will begin to pulse out from under him in all directions, indicated by small traveling circular AoEs, each dealing high magic damage and snaring for 3 seconds. Thus, in order to inflict significant damage on him, you must ensure that you and your companions are focusing on taking down the globules as they appear. After all of them are destroyed, there will be a period of time where he will no longer summon them, until starting to summon them again or until he uses Watery Escape.
;Mind Blast: A beam connects between the tips of B'Korgen's dorsal fin and all targets, dealing high oblivion damage and fearing for 2 seconds. This ability can be blocked to negate the fear, but it might be hard to spot in the mayhem as it is fast, the sparks indicator is at the top of his head and B'Korgen is quite tall.
;Mental Manipulation: While there are alchemical globules, B'Korgen will occasionally reposition them in the arena with force. He will twirl his hands and the globules are then propelled away, each dealing moderate physical damage and stagger in their wake.
;Pearlescence:When B'Korgen has multiple alchemical globules, he will sometimes fire many purple blobs at targets, each dealing moderate-high magic damage and creating a small lingering area doing
;Summon Yaghra:When B'Korgen has multiple alchemical globules, he will periodically sacrifice some of them to spawn Yaghra Larva Poppers and stronger Yaghra Larvae, Yaghra Spewers and yaghra Striders to assist him in the fight.
; Dissonance: After taking enough damage once his damage shield is broken, B'Korgen slithers underwater and reappears to one of the far ends of the arena. Upon re-emerging, he will always summon six Alchemical Globules.
;Engorge The Horde:B'Korgen heals the Yaghra for ~8-9 thousand.
