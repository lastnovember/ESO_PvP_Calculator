Captain Helenus is an Imperial officer of the Seventh Legion found at the Old Tower in the region of Bangkorai. She is the leader of the Seventh Legion's Second Cohort which took the Old Tower and Craglorn Gates from Covenant forces.

## Related Quests
- A Token Trophy

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Strike: A basic melee attack dealing moderate physical damage.
;Blood Craze: Captain Helenus slashes the target twice, dealing moderate physical damage twice then very minor bleed damage every two seconds over 8 seconds to the target. The bleeding also heals Captain Helenus for around a quarter of the damage done. Blocking the first hit negates the bleeding, but blocking the second one does not.
;Agony: Captain Helenus channels a spell dealing moderate magic damage and stunning the target for 2 seconds. Taking damage from any source will break the stun, but the target may choose to break free from it instead. After the stun ends, this spell also deals minor magic damage every second over 6 seconds to the target. This can be interrupted to set Captain Helenus off balance instead. Blocking will not prevent the damage over time.
;Roll Dodge: Captain Helenus can perform a roll dodge to avoid incoming damage.
;Heated Blades: Captain Helenus rakes her weapons together in a show of sparks, buffing her damage by 25% and applying Minor Maim to the target for 4 seconds.
;Backstabber:Captain Helenus has a passive ability that makes her deal 20% more damage when attacking the target's back.

## Notes
- When viewed from an other player's perspective, she appears as an Imperial Captain instead.

## Bugs
Despite being an elite rank enemy, Captain Helenus is vulnerable to crowd control.
