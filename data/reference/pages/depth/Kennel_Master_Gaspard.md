## Quest-Related Events
After freeing Gorbert Madach from the dungeon, he will ask you to free Carso from the kennels. He suggests you get some food from the upstairs kitchens to distract the noisy dogs in the kennels. Once you enter the kennels, two Saarthal Shepherd dogs will start barking and there will be a timer to placate the dogs.
