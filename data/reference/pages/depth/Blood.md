Globs of animated Blood are summoned every time Sluthrug the Bloodied, the first secret boss in Oathsworn Pit, is damaged. Half of them are on Sluthrug's side, the others against him. More blood against him must be active when he is near death, otherwise, he heals and the fight continues.
