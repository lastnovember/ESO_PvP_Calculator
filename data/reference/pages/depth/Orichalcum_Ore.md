Orichalcum Ore is a rough-textured ore of bright green color. It can be found in any zone by characters with Rank 3 in the Metalworking passive or character level 26 to 34. When mined, a node yields three to four samples of the raw material, Orichalcum Ore, which can then be refined into seven to ten Orichalcum Ingots once ten ore samples are collected. Orichalcum Ingots are used for crafting pieces of heavy orichalc armor and weapons at any blacksmithing station. You will be able to create orichalc gear only if you invested into the third rank of Metalworking.
