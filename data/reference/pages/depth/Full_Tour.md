Full Tour is awarded for defeating all three of Taleria's crewmen from the latter stages of battle - Sea Boiler, Tidal Mage and Venom Evoker - within 10 seconds of each other with challenge banner raised in Veteran Dreadsail Reef.

These enemies spawn in random order when Tideborn Taleria's health reaches 50%, 35% and 20% marks, in alcoves in the rock surrounding Taleria's arena. Bridges will rise from slaughterfish-infested waters to grant access to their hideouts, and a part of the group must head there and lower the mages' health, forcing them to move to the main area where they can be finished. more details? are Taleria health marks correct?
