Outlaws are Khajiit found in Vulkhel Guard, who appear to owe Malareth money.

## Related Quests
- New in Town

## Quest-Related Events
When approached they will greet you with one of the following:
:"Greetings."

:"What?"

:"Stars brought you here. What do you want?"

:"Fair day, stranger."
:Malareth sent me.
:"Should have known. Look, she's taken everything."
::[Intimidate] I can easily take what you owe.
::[Persuade] It will be easier on us both if you just pay her.
:::"Stars take you both! Fine. Here's the coin. Just go."
::You know what happens when you can't pay up.
::"Yeah? Let's see what you've got. Today's the day someone finally stands up to Malareth!"
:::Hold on there- (Conversation ends)
::If it's that bad, I'll cover you.
::"You'd, you'd do that? Such generosity!"
:::Right. Get out of here. [20]

Alternatively:
:I'm here about a debt.
:"Malareth. She won't have another week's pay!"
::[Persuade] You know it's better to pay, and just be done with it.
:::"Fine, fine. The blade's not a familiar friend. Sadly, coin doesn't seem to be either. Here."
::Just pay up. I don't have time for this.
::"Malareth's mercs. So high and mighty. All you'll get today is a fight!
:::Hold up - (Ends conversation)
::I can take care of your debt. This time. [20]

If the Outlaw is threatened, they may stand up to you:
:Outlaw: "You'll get nothing from me!"
:Outlaw: "The threats end now!"

Once you have beaten them up, they will hand you the gold and run away:
:Outlaw: "Please. No more. Here, take it."
