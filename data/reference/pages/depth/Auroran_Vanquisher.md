Auroran Vanquishers are Auroran vanquishers found in the Dawn Wing of Broken Light Temple.

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Strike:A basic melee attack that does minor physical damage.
;Heavy Attack:A heavy melee attack that does moderate physical damage. It can be blocked to set the Auroran Vanquisher off balance.
;Block:The vanquisher blocks with its one-handed blade.
;Retaliation: The vanquisher jumps and performs a spinning strike, dealing low physical damage and staggering their target. This can be blocked, setting them off balance. Retaliation is always used after a successful block, which then makes it impossible for the target to block this attack.
;Javelin: The vanquisher takes out a javelin from behind their back and tosses it at their target, dealing low physical damage and staggering them. This can be interrupted to set them off balance. This attack is used more often when standing further out from the vanquisher.
;Roll Dodge Back: The vanquisher roll dodges away from their target, evading attacks in the process.
