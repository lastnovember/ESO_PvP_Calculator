The Blood King is one of the nine leaders of the ancient Nede tribes who was killed and enslaved by Virmaril the Betrayer in Skyreach Catacombs. He is found in the northernmost room with the rest of the kings.

He is the fifth of the Fallen Kings to be fought, and will summon two spectral giant bats to assist him.

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Strike: A basic melee attack that does minor physical damage.
;Blood Craze:The Blood King slashes at you twice, doing moderate physical damage twice then minor physical damage over time to the target. This attack also heals The Blood King over time.
;Agony:The Blood King stuns the target for a short time. Taking damage from any source will break the stun, but the target may choose to break free from it instead. After the stun ends, this spell also does moderate magical damage over time to the target. This spell can be interrupted to stagger The Blood King. Blocking will prevent the stun.
;Roll Dodge:The Blood King can perform a roll dodge to avoid incoming damage.

## Bugs
Unlike most berserkers, the Blood King wields a two-handed weapon rather than dual wielding which makes his "Blood Craze" ability not display its animation correctly.
