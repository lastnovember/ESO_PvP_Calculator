A giant guar named Bittergreen the Wild resides here, among the scattered remains of its former caravan.

A chest can spawn beside an overturned cart and many corpses on the western edge of Caravan Crest. A House Hlaalu Notice is posted on the thick trunk of a giant mushroom near this western cart. A chest can also spawn beneath the roots of a tree located south of the crest. there's a lore book on a wagon further west of the crest, on the shoreline

## Completion
:Kill the giant guar, Bittergreen the Wild, at Caravan Crest.
To clear this location and mark it as complete, you need to kill the following bosses:
:Bittergreen the Wild
