Alande is a High Elf armorer who can be found at the Armory Merchants market located at the Eastern Elsweyr Gate.

## Dialogue
When approached, she asserts the superiority of heavy armor.
:"Light armor is for cowards who plan to run. Invest in heavy armor and you will never regret it."
