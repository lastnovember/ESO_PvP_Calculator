Cynaceae the Verdant is a spriggan and the boss of Kingscrest Cavern.

## Skills and Abilities
;Lacerate: A basic melee attack that deals minor physical damage.
;Nature's Swarm: Cynaceae sends a swarm of insects forward, indicated by a red cone, dealing low magic damage.
;Summon Beast: Cynaceae summons an animal to assist it in combat. The animal type depend on the stats and location of the summoner. The summons can only have a maximum of 80% of Cynaceae's Max Health and have extreme damage resistance during the summoning animation. Cynaceae takes 20% more damage while a summon is active as indicated by a blue glow on them. (Bugged, see Bugs)
;Control Beast: Cynaceae channels a buff on its summoned animal, or any nearby allied creature, increasing the target's damage done by 100% and their Physical Resistance and Spell Resistance resistance by 4,550. This can be interrupted to set Cynaceae off-balance.
;Healing Salve: When hurt, Cynaceae heals itself. The heal ramps up over time, and will heal for a large amount if not interrupted, which then sets Cynaceae off-balance.
;Backstep:When too close to its target, Cynaceae moves away to put some distance between its target and itself.

## Bugs
Despite being an elite rank enemy, Cynaceae the Verdant is vulnerable to crowd control.
The "Summon Beast" cast animation is showing but nothing happens afterwards. It can be argued this is an intentional choice to reduce bandwith strain on the PvP server.
