Blessed Spriggans are spriggans in the Hunter's Grotto within Vateshran Hollows. When you kill them, they grant you the "Blessing of the Grove" effect, which lasts for ten minutes and allows you to deal damage to enemies with a yellow glow on them. Stronger versions of this enemy are encountered during the battle against the Shade of the Grove.
