# Online:Bright-Throat Tribe

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Bright-Throat_Tribe
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Bright-Throat Tribe is an influential Argonian tribe dwelling in Murkmire. The tribe has a reputation for being the most hospitable tribe in the region, openly trading with nearby Lilmoth and the other tribes as well as for the seed-dolls they carve. Its members are generally known for their bright and colorful scales.

The most notable thing about the tribe is their annual bonding ritual. The Bright-Throats believe that inviting neighboring tribes to their bonding ceremony is a show of respect and kinship, and that bonding together strengthens their relationships with one another as they are all egg-brothers and egg-sisters.

Following completion of Empty Nest, you are considered root-kin by the tribe.
