Knights of the Dragon (book)
The Knights of the Dragon are a knightly order which has the official duty of protecting the city and royal family of Daggerfall. King Joile's Knights of the Dragon participated in the siege on Old Orsinium, assisted by Gaiden Shinji and his Order of Diagna.
