# Online:Bow (Companion)

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Bow_(Companion)
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Bow is one of six weapon skill lines available to Companions.

## Skill Perks
The Bow skill line is immediately available to Mirri Elendis, who starts her progress at level 3. Other Companions can unlock the skill line by equipping them with a Bow. The skill line can be leveled up from earning combat XP while your active Companion has a Bow equipped.
