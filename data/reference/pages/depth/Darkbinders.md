# Online:Darkbinders

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Darkbinders
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Darkbinders are a Dremora clan which serves Molag Bal as jailers in the many prisons of Coldharbour. Despite this role, they are considered a low ranking clan by their peers, and thus will treat any mortals or daedra beneath them with severe spite and contempt.

In recent times, they can be found working with the Worm Cult on Solstice.
