Animated Chillrenders are animated pieces of armor found in the Scholarium and its ruins. They wield staves and use frost magic.

## Related Quests
- The Second Era of Scribing
- The Wing of the Crow

## Skills and Abilities
;Ice Arrow:A basic ranged attack that does minor frost damage.
;Ice Barrier: The enemy summons an Ice Barrier that absorbs projectiles fired at it until destroyed. The enemy cannot be pulled if the wall stands between them and the target. The player should move so that there is a clear line of sight between themselves and the enemy.
;Winter's Reach:The enemy unleashes an icy whirlwind in the direction it is facing. This whirlwind does moderate frost damage and roots all players that it hits. The root effect passes through block. This can be interrupted to set the frost mage off balance.
;Run Away!:When too close to them, the frost mage backs up to put some distance between their target and themselves.

## Quest-Related Events
An Animated Chillrender, together with Animated Incendiary and Animated Tempest are protecting the replacement crystal the Crow requested for the Scribing Altar.

Following the Wing of the Gryphon quest they act as guards around the Scholarium. They also appear within the Wing of the Crow as enemies.
