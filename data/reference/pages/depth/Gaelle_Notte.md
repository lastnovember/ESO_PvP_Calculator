Gaelle Notte is a Breton member of the Worm Cult found at the Aldwilne Citadel.

## Related Quests
- Choice and Consequence

## Quest-Related Events
After meeting with Cariel, she informs you of the theft of the Staff of Magnus and resurrection of Prince Naemon. She asks you to investigate the camp and Gaelle Notte can be eavesdropped on having a conversation with Rexus Nerva discussing Prince Naemon and the Staff of Magnus.
:Rexus Nerva: "Taking the Staff of Magnus from those mages wasn't hard."
:Gaelle Notte: "Why did we resurrect Prince Naemon? Dark Adept Vairisse doesn't even like him."
:Rexus Nerva: "Because no one else can control the Staff of Magnus."
:Gaelle Notte: "That staff better be worth dealing with his entitled demands."
:

Once you have investigated the camp and reported to Cariel, she will have you pick a Bonelord or Apprentice disguise to enter the Citadel, so you can ingratiate yourself with Dark Adept Vairisse and begin to drive a wedge between her and Prince Naemon. With your chosen disguise on, you can enter the citadel and Gaelle can be found working nearby. If you try talking to her:
:"Our plans near fruition, but there's still much to do. We can relax and celebrate once the Planemeld is complete and not before."

After you gain access to Dark Adept Vairisse, you can do a task for her to prove your loyalty. Afterward, you will need to approach Prince Naemon and speak with him. To gain a small amount of his trust, he will ask you to uncover a spy in the citadel and bring them to him. When you tell Cariel of this, she will have you pick out a cultist of similar build to her, so they can be subdued and dressed in her armor.

Gaelle is one of the cultists you can approach. Speak with her to convince her to follow you:
:"I saw you talking to Prince Naemon a little while ago. Dark Adept Vairisse disapproves of that sort of thing."
:You need to come with me.
:"That's cryptic."

If you get her to follow you, she will become your follower and you can lead her to the secluded place Cariel told you about. Speaking with Gaelle during this:
:"Well? Lead the way to whatever it is that needs my attention. I don't have all day."

When you arrive at the spot, you will find a body on the ground:
:Gaelle Notte: "Larus? Oh no. What happened?"
:
:Cariel: "Well, well. Let's take a look at who you picked."

You then speak with Cariel, who deems Gaelle close enough in build for the plan to work. She then has you plant evidence linking Dark Adept Vairisse and the Eyes of the Queen working together on Gaelle, while she prepares a potion to temporarily make Gaelle mute.

Once the evidence is planted, Cariel will then dose Gaelle with the potion and change her clothes. You then wait for Gaelle to wake up. You can then speak with Gaelle to convince her to follow you to Prince Naemon:
:"What happened? Who's clothes are these?"
:Do you feel all right? Something hit your head really hard.
:"My mouth tastes so strange. There was a body. And what happened my clothes? I wasn't wearing this before, was I? My voice. My throat. It doesn't feel right. Something's wrong with me."
:Come with me. I know someone who can help.

Gaelle will become your follower once more, if you try to talk to her while leading her to Prince Naemon's quarters:
:"Talking's becoming … difficult."

Reaching the Naemon's Quarters:
:Gaelle Notte: "My throat hurts."

Going down the stairs:
:Gaelle Notte: "Are we there yet?"

You can then speak with Prince Naemon and he will try interrogating her:
:Prince Naemon: "I am of the same royal line as your queen. Tell me your mission here."
:
:Prince Naemon: "Nothing? Silence protects no one. Search her. Glean what you can from her pockets."

Take the planted evidence from Ashayuna and present it to Naemon. He will be furious, and his following reaction depends on if you previously killed the prince or not. If you did, then he will have you kill Ashayuna as a final test of loyalty. On the other hand, if you had not previously met Naemon till Solstice, he will deal with the spy himself.

If you spared Gaelle, she will hand you the Bloodstained Cloth and flees. If you decide to kill the cultist, you can take the cloth from her body. Afterward, you can bring your proof to Prince Naemon.

Prince Naemon takes the Staff of Magnus and turns Gaelle to stone:
:Prince Naemon: "You will serve as a warning for all who seek to undermine me."
