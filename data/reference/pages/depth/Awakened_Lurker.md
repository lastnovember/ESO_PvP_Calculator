Awakened Lurkers are animated statues of Akaviri poison archers that have been roused to protect their tomb from trespassers.

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Shot:A basic ranged attack that does minor physical damage.
;Poisoned Arrow:A basic ranged attack which poisons the target and causes poison damage over time.
;Snipe:An interruptible ranged attack which fires multiple arrows at once the target.
;Roll Dodge:The enemy can roll dodge to avoid attacks.
;Arrow Spray:The enemy shoots a spray of poison arrows in the direction it is facing as indicated by a red cone. This attack does moderate poison damage as well as snares all enemies hit. This attack can be interrupted to set the enemy off balance or simply avoided. Blocking also prevents the snare and reduces the damage.
;Arrow Stab:A basic melee attack that does minor physical damage. Where the Poison Archer grabs their arrow and stabs it into you rather than firing it.

## Dialogue
While in the delve, you may hear a female voice urging the guardians to awake and defend the tomb. Nearby Awakened will glow and start moving.
:"Kill the intruder. Only the strong can carry our message."
:"Wake from your sleep. Preserve our legacy. Destroy the outsider."
