Cartel Ruffians are members of the Koldane Cartel that can be encountered while doing the related quests.

## Related Quests
- The Codex Caper

## Quest-Related Events

After you and Quen enter Yvierry Manor through the larder, she will quickly sight some unwelcome guests:
:Quen: "Excellent, no servants in sight. Time to-wait. Get down!"
:
:Cartel Ruffian (Male): "Bryggid says the major domo has one key to the strongroom. The other's in Yvierry's nightstand."
:Cartel Ruffian (Female): "I'll get it. His room's in the north hall, right? Any valuables I pass along the way, I'm helping myself."
:Cartel Ruffian (Male): "Whatever. Bryggid said what's in the strongroom is all that matters, so let's get that key."
:
:Quen: "Koldane Cartel thugs! What are they doing here? We need to rethink things."

Later on when you and Quen have taken the Codex and found a strange amulet, you will witness Lord Yvierry being murdered by Bryggid. When this happens, you will need to escape the manor before the cartel tracks you down. If you fail to leave in time, Enforcer Urfan will subdue you.

You awake tied up in a storeroom while Urfan speaks to a Cartel Ruffian:
:Enforcer Urfan: "This is perfect. We'll tell the city guards we found Lord Yvierry's killer."
:Cartel Ruffian: "Yes, Urfan. They're tied up tight."
:

The ruffian keeps staring at you before some smoke appears in the corner of the room:
:Cartel Ruffian: "Huh? What was that?"
:
:Basil Menant: "I'll untie you. One moment."
:
:Basil Menant: "Half of Daggerfall's looking for Quen."
:
:Basil Menant: "That should do it. Now head to the Thieves Den."
:Basil Menant: "Quen will want a full report."
:

You can then leave the room and escape the manor, dealing with Enforcer Urfan along the way.
