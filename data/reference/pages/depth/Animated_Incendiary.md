Animated Incendiaries are animated sets of armor found in the Scholarium and its ruins. They wield staves and use fire magic.

## Related Quests
- The Second Era of Scribing
- The Wing of the Crow

## Quest-Related Events
An Animated Incendiary, together with Animated Tempest and Animated Chillrender are protecting the replacement crystal the Crow requested for the Scribing Altar.

Following the Wing of the Gryphon quest they act as guards around the Scholarium. They also appear within the Wing of the Crow as enemies.

## Skills and Abilities
;Flare:A basic ranged attack that does minor flame damage.
;Heat Wave:The enemy releases fire along the ground in the direction it is facing. This attack does moderate flame damage to all targets hit by it. This spell can be interrupted to set the enemy off balance.
;Fire Runes:The enemy shoots two fireballs in the air which form two fire runes when they land. The runes do moderate flame damage over time to all targets within the red circles. Players should be prepared to move as soon as the attack casts as one rune will always be cast on top of you.
;Run Away!:When too close to them, the fire mage backs up to put some distance between their target and themselves.
