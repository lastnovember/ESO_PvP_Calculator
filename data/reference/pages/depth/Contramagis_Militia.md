# Online:Contramagis Militia

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Contramagis_Militia
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Contramagis Militia are a group of antimagic-extremists who propose the death of all mages. They exist purely in Warlock Vanton's mindscape that was twisted by Ansuul the Tormentor. The group is led by Exarchanic Yaseyla.
