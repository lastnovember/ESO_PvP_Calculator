The Ancient Clannfear is a powerful clannfear found atop a pile of bones in the Foundry of Woe. It is guarding Lyris' Axe, which is buried in the bones next to it.

## Related Quests
- Daughter of Giants

## Skills and Abilities
;Headbutt: A basic melee headbutt that deals minor physical damage.
;Tail Spike: The Ancient Clannfear swings its tail around, indicated by a red circle, dealing moderate physical damage to and staggering targets.
;Rending Leap: When standing away from the Ancient Clannfear, it leaps at its target, dealing moderate generic damage, knocking down and stunning targets in the landing area. "Generic" damage is reduced by your armor and damage reduction effects.
;Feeding: While stunned, the Ancient Clannfear chews on the target, dealing continuous moderate physical damage over time.

## Bugs
Despite showing red sparks, you cannot interrupt "Feeding".
