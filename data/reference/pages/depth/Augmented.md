# Online:Augmented

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Augmented
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Augmented is a trait which can be found on Companion weapons, armor and jewelry. The trait cannot be researched or crafted, and exists only on found loot. Items with the Augmented trait increase the duration of Buffs and Debuffs cast by a Companion by an amount dependent on the quality of the item.
