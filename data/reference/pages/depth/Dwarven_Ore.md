Dwarven Ore is a smooth-textured ore of dark green color. It can be found in any zone by characters with Rank 4 in the Metalworking passive or character level 36 to 44. When mined, a node yields three to four samples of the raw material, Dwarven Ore, which can be refined into seven to ten Dwarven Ingots once ten ore samples are collected. Dwarven Ingots are used for crafting pieces of heavy dwarven armor and weapons at any blacksmithing station. You will be able to create dwarven gear only if you've invested into rank 4 of Metalworking.

## Notes
- According to Kireth Vanos Answers Your Questions, Dwarven Ore acquired its name due to the visual resemblance it bears to Dwarven Metal when forged; however, it lacks the metal's durability, and metallurgists have concluded that it has no connection to the metal harvested from actual Dwemer devices.
