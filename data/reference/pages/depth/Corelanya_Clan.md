# Online:Corelanya Clan

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Corelanya_Clan
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Corelanya Clan was an Altmer clan who lived in the Alik'r Desert during the First Era. They opposed the Redguards when they landed in Hammerfell. Modern members of the clan live on the island of Solstice, primarily in the city of Sunport.
