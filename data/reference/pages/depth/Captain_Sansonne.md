## Quest-Related Events

### The Codex Caper
The Thieves Guild member Quen may reach out to you asking for assistance in forming a Thieves Guild in Daggerfall. You can start the quest by using the Quest Starter from the Crown Store, or while wandering around Daggerfall. Either way, when you reach the Daggerfall Wayshrine square behind the Rosy Lion, you will witness Quen being chased by Captain Sansonne:
:Captain Sansonne: "Stop that thief!"
:Quen: "Curse it! I haven't even done anything yet!"
:
:Skeever Nivo: "Psst, you there! Want to earn some coin and confound the law?"

If you try speaking with Captain Sansonne before Nivo, he will be curt:
:"Not now-I'm after a criminal! Where the blazes did she go?"

When you talk to Skeever Nivo, he will ask if you are interested in joining a caper. If you agree, he will ask you to misdirect Captain Sansonne before meeting with Quen. Approach the captain and speak with him:
:"You there! Did you see a dark-haired High Elf woman run by? She's a dangerous fugitive and must be apprehended!"
:Who is she? What did she do?
:"I'm tracking Quenneth, a thief from Abah's Landing. Also goes by the name of Quen. King Casimir issued a royal warrant for her arrest. For "crimes against the kingdom and the Covenant." That's enough for me. Quickly, now. Which way did she go?"
:The city gate. She was heading for the road to Camlorn.
::"Damn! Of course she'd flee the city, now that she realizes Captain Sansonne of the Lion Guard is after her. You have my thanks, citizen. And the thanks of the crown as well!"
:I saw her kick a kitten and push over an old woman on her way to the docks! You must stop her!Grim
::"Truly? She's more heinous than I thought! Likely seeking passage on the next ship to sail from port. Don't worry, she won't escape me again!"
:Quen, you say? Oh, her! She turned into a blue chicken and flew off in that direction!Joker
::"Don't waste my time with nonsense! This is no joking matter. Bah! She must have headed for the city gate. Quen knows I'm after her. She'll flee Daggerfall by the quickest route to avoid my justice. Out of the way!"

Once you have finished your conversation, Captain Sansonne will run off looking for Quen:
:Captain Sansonne: "She can't be far. I'll get her this time!"
:
:Skeever Nivo: "Well done, cutter. Quen's waiting at the Rosy Lion, so get moving."

Afterward you can meet with Quen and help her with her caper involving the Yvierry Estate and Voernet Codex. During the job, you witness how the Koldane Cartel operates and meet with the leaders of the three Daggerfall gangs that Quen wants to work together.

### Three Thieves in Daggerfall
After stealing the Codex Voernet from Yvierry Manor, Quen will have you work with the gang leaders to find out more information about the Codex. When you meet Seline and Basil at the Bank of Daggerfall, you will find Captain Sansonne speaking with them:
:Captain Sansonne: "I fear the criminal Quenneth remains at large, Lady Seline, but you can be sure I will find her! Good day."
:
:Seline Menant: "Captain Sansonne is very determined. Oh, over here, my dear."

### Codex and Cartel
While questioning Marten Cogwine in the Lion Guard Redoubt Dungeon, you can find out that Captain Sansonne has been investigating the activities of the Koldane Cartel. You also learn that Gavin Koldane is planning to attack Basil Menant. You have the option of asking for the Lion Guard's help before heading to the Menant Hideout near Deleyn's Mill.

Either way, when you enter the hideout, you find Gavin Koldane and some cartel members fighting a squad of Lion Guard led by Captain Sansonne. Halfway through the fight, the cartel will reveal themselves as werewolves and Koldane escapes. If you informed the Lion Guard, they got to the hideout in time to stop Basil from being killed. If you didn't, Basil will be dying and in both cases he wants to talk to you. Speaking with Captain Sansonne before Basil:
:"You again? I have questions for you … but I can see your friend has been injured. Seriously, by the looks of it. We'll talk after you speak with him."

Basil tells you he had found another Nowhere key before the cartel stole it, and suggests speaking with Captain Sansonne to get the Lion Guard's help with dealing with the Koldane Cartel. After speaking with Basil, what happens next depend on how injured he was:

### A Guild Grows In Glenumbra
After the attack on Basil, Quen will decide on plan of creating a trap for the Koldane Cartel and comes around to the idea of getting Captain Sansonne's assistance. Once you have obtained the use of Rols Boron's warehouse, Quen will have you meet with Sansonne at his camp and convince him to work with you. His camp is found to the east of Daggerfall and he will be with a Lion Guard Stalwart and Duelist, when you arrive Sansonne calls out to you:
:Captain Sansonne: "I wondered if I would see you again."

Talk to Captain Sansonne:
:"Once again, the friend of thieves calls upon the Lion Guard. To what do I owe the honor this time?"
:I wanted to continue the conversation we started. How we can work together to stop the cartel.
:"I've been considering what such a course might look like. But the Koldane Cartel has wealth, power, and more than one magistrate on their payroll. I would need to catch them with their hand on the High King's coin purse to make an arrest stick."
:What if we could provide you with a time and place to catch them in just such an act?
:"A tempting offer, but I still don't know if I can trust you. You know I carry a warrant for Quenneth's arrest. I could just show up and take you all into custody. It would certainly be the more prudent course of action."
:Trust goes both ways. We want the cartel and their werewolves to pay for what happened to Basil.
:"Yes, Basil … and a criminal organization of murderous werewolves is certainly a much greater concern than a few gangs of pickpockets and street thieves. All right, get me the information and the Lion Guard will be there to apprehend the cartel."
:I'll get word to you as soon as all the details are in place.
:"While I wait to learn the time and place of this trap, I'll assemble as many knights as the Lion Guard can spare. I may require the assistance of your thieves if the cartel proves as dangerous as I believe them to be. Do not make me regret this."
:You said it yourself. Pickpockets and street thieves aren't soldiers. That's why we need you.
:"Even so, if we enter into this partnership, we do so together. We're all going to have to bloody our swords to stop Koldane. If Quenneth has no stomach for that, don't bother to send word to me. But if I hear from you, I expect you're all in."

Once you have spoken with Sansonne, you will need to meet with Edwane near Aldcroft to the north.

### Wolf Trap
Once the trap is ready for the Koldane Cartel at the warehouse in Eagle's Brook, Quen will ask you to meet with Abrasia and Captain Sansonne to tell them it is time to move in. When you arrive at the Roadsworn camp, you will find Gharelin the Fox talking with Sansonne:
:Gharelin the Fox: "Harassing innocent travelers along Glenumbra's roads. Typical Lion Guard behavior!"
:Captain Sansonne: "Innocent? Bah! You Roadsworn belong in shackles."
:
:Captain Sansonne: "You! Their leader said I'd find you here. Let's talk."

Talk with Captain Sansonne and ask him if he is ready to work with a bunch of thieves:
:"The woman, Abrasia, went out of her way to get me to follow her here. Told me I'd find you, but then she slipped away. I assumed this was about that plan we discussed, but I'm beginning to think this is all just a waste of my time."
:The trap is set. Meet us at Rols Boron's warehouse in Eagle's Brook to catch Gavin Koldane.
:"To think, an honored and decorated captain in the Lion Guard, forced to deal with thieves and rogues. But Gavin Koldane is a serious threat to the realm …. If I can catch him in a criminal act, I can arrest him and dismantle his cartel."
:So you're in? The Lion Guard will arrest Koldane when he goes after the items in the warehouse?
:"House Menant, Streethawks, the Roadsworn … criminals, each and every one. But not monsters …. Yes, the Lion Guard will move against Koldane and the cartel at the appropriate moment. Just make sure your thieves hold up their end of the bargain."

Speaking with Sansonne again before leaving:
:"This plan of yours had best be foolproof. If anything goes wrong, more than my career is at stake. The cartel threatens the realm with untold crime, corruption, and violent werewolves. If we fail here, there may not be another chance to stop them."

When you arrive at Rols Boron's Warehouse in Eagle's Brook, you quickly see things have gone horribly wrong. Rennah Brel tells you that the Cartel arrived sooner than expected and ambushed the Lion Guard and everyone scattered. After you find Edwane the Hawk, he explains that Gavin Koldane was there, along with Rols Boron who sold you out. Seline and Captain Sansonne were taken to the Smuggler Caves below.

After getting access to the caves, you soon find Sansonne hanging above you in a net:
:Edwane the Hawk: "Look, hanging above us! Is that Captain Sansonne?"
:Captain Sansonne: "Get me down from here!"
:Edwane the Hawk: "Maybe use that grapple bow you have."

You will need to use the grapple points so you can reach the lever and release Captain Sansonne. Once you use the lever, Captain Sansonne will fall to the platform below:
:Captain Sansonne: "Oof!"
:Edwane the Hawk: "Haha! Like dumping fish at the docks."
:Captain Sansonne: "The humiliation never ends, but if you still want to stop Koldane, let's talk."

Talk with Captain Sansonne and he will get you up to speed:
:"That wasn't the most dignified moment of my career, but the cartel will pay for that and for the many Lion Guard lives they took today. But we don't have much time. We need to catch Gavin Koldane."
:Where is Koldane taking Seline Menant?
:"Deeper into these smuggler caves. The cartel plans to loot the place and take control of the operation. Rols Boron was a fool to think Koldane would let him keep all this. I heard Seline shouting. Something about a Nowhere Key."
:We'll get to Seline. Have you seen any of the others?
:"That Roadsworn thief Abrasia came by. She saw me bound up and simply smiled. Then she kept walking. Typical scoundrel. But we need to move now if we're going to catch Koldane."

When you finish your conversation, Captain Sansonne will lead you to Gavin Koldane:
:Captain Sansonne: "Koldane went deeper into the caves. Follow me."

If you manage to speak to him:
:"You understand that when we find Gavin Koldane, he's mine. The Lion Guard will deal justice. Not some band of street thieves."

He leads you and Edwane into the Warehouse Back Cave. On the other side, you can see a bound Seline and Gavin Koldane who is with Bryggid Silver-Eye and other Cartel members:
:Seline Menant: "Release me!"
:Captain Sansonne: "Let's see what we're dealing with, before they are alerted."

Further down the passage there is a location where you can clearly view what is going on below you. When you reach this "Observation Point", the following scene will play out:

:Captain Sansonne: "Enough! Send your wolves against me instead!"

After killing Bryggid and freeing Seline, you can leave the caves and find Captain Sansonne lambasting Seline while Edwane and Carso watch from the sidelines:
:Captain Sansonne: "Of course she betrayed you. She's a thief! You're all thieves!"
:Seline Menant: "Obviously, darling. But you make that sound like a bad thing."

Talking to Captain Sansonne before Seline, he is understandably upset by what has happened but tells you to check on her:
:"Unbelievable. Not only did your people let Gavin Koldane get away, but you had a traitor among you. I knew I shouldn't have relied on criminals. Lady Menant appears unharmed. She certainly sounds fine. But you may want to check on her."
:At least Bryggid Silver-Eye won't be a problem any more.

:Where does this leave us?

Speaking with Seline, she reveals that two more locations for the Nowhere keys have been located and tells you to report back to Quen before meeting her and Edwane to recover the keys. Talking with Sansonne again before leaving:
:"Your people shouldn't linger around this warehouse much longer. Once I can rally more Lion Guard, I plan to confiscate every piece of contraband in there. I'd rather arrest you for crimes you did commit, not tie you to ones you didn't."
:At least Bryggid Silver-Eye won't be a problem any more. (Same dialogue as above)

### Racing Into Nowhere
After you recover another Nowhere Key, Quen will have found the location of the Nowhere Vault, on Sage's Isle, an island far off the coast of Glenumbra. She will tell you to meet with Skeel-Kasi for transportation, but when you arrive, Captain Sansonne is there as well:
:Skeel-Kasi: "Ah, Quen. Were you expecting the Lion Guard?"
:Captain Sansonne: "Nervous? What in Oblivion could you possibly be nervous about?"
:Quen: "Easy! We're all friends, here. For now."
:Quen: "I'll deal with Skeel-Kasi while you calm down Captain Sansonne."

Talk with Captain Sansonne:
:"I received a missive from Lady Seline that claimed you have a way to flush Koldane and his cartel out into the open. Is this true? I already trusted you once, and we know where that got us."
:We believe we know where to find Gavin Koldane. Come with us and you can apprehend him.
:"Come with you? Where? On these smugglers' vessels of questionable seaworthiness? Hmm. What are you and these criminals planning this time?"
:Look, we know where an item Koldane plans to steal is located. You want to stop him? Come with us.
:"All right. For another chance to clap that dog's neck in irons, we'll play this out. If this doesn't work this time, I'm after you and your gang next."

Speaking with Sansonne again before boarding the boat:
:"Koldane in shackles would go a long way to me forgetting I know anything about you. Divines help me, I can't believe I said that. But dealing with a violent monster requires sacrifices. The Lion Guard will help you. Just don't make me regret it."

When you arrive on the foggy Sage's Isle your allies will note that the Koldane Cartel has already arrived:
:Seline Menant: "Cartel ships on the coast. They beat us here."
:Edwane the Hawk: "Carso found tracks leading inland. We should follow them."
:Captain Sansonne: "We'll stay with the boats. Find Koldane and drive him this way."

Talking with Sansonne before you begin to explore the island:
:"We'll remain with the boats. Keep watch in case Koldane tries to escape. You go find him and lead him back here for us to capture. Just try not to die in the process."

After you leave the shore, you find your way into the Nowhere Vault and beat all the challenges to reach the Vault of the Thieves Moon. While there you defeat and capture Gavin Koldane while breaking the relic you came there to steal. After returning to the beach via a portal, your group hands over Koldane and his ledger to Captain Sansonne:
:Quen: "Captain Sansonne! We come bearing a gift!"
:Captain Sansonne: "About time. We were about to come looking for you."
:Abrasia: "Here's the fearsome Gavin Koldane, trussed up and ready to arrest. And he had his ledger on him, which proves he really is a stupid criminal."
:Captain Sansonne: "That was easier than I expected. You there, let's talk."

Speak with Captain Sansonne:
:"A thief who keeps their word. And here I thought this was going to turn out to be some new and elaborate ruse. Yet, Gavin Koldane is shackled and justice will be served. His crimes will not go unanswered. As for yours, that's a different story."
:We had a deal. Koldane and his cartel for looking the other way regarding our Thieves Guild.
:"Thieves Guild? Is that what you lot are? Hmm. But a deal is a deal, and I honor my word. Bringing this butcher to justice earns you all some leniency for now. But be warned. There's still the matter of Quen's warrant. The law is the law, after all."
:As you say, captain.

After your conversation, he will formally arrest Gavin Koldane:
:Captain Sansonne: "Gavin Koldane, by the authority given me by the High King Emeric and the Daggerfall Covenant, I place you under arrest for your crimes."
:Quen: "Now that that's done, let's return to the den, people. We have one more thing to discuss!"

Speaking with Sansonne before boarding the boat:
:"Remember, keep your thieving to a minimum and try not to cause too much trouble and I won't need to come looking for you. Or better yet, just don't do it in my jurisdiction and we'll get along fine."

### Quenneth Lumena's Warrant

After returning to the Thieves Den and choosing a leader for the Daggerfall Thieves Guild, Quen lets you know she is still looking into the issue with the outstanding warrant. When you leave the Thieves Den, you will find Captain Sansonne waiting for you in the Outlaws Refuge, dressed in common clothing:
:Captain Sansonne: "You there! We have some unfinished business to deal with."

Talk with Captain Sansonne to see what is going on:
:"How do you stand it down here? No wonder criminals always smell so bad. Look, thanks to you I was able to put an end to the Koldane Cartel. I'm here to pay my debt. To you. And to Quen. I've come to warn you. The warrant on her has changed."
:What happened to your Lion Guard uniform?
:"I couldn't very well wear that down here. But let's focus. Quen's warrant changed from crimes against the realm to the murder of a high-ranking official. They want her taken alive and dragged back to Hew's Bane. Whoever died had influence."
:Quen's against violence. Always has been. She didn't murder anyone.
:"I know that now. Maybe it was an accident, but I suspect she's been falsely accused. This world of yours doesn't forgive and it doesn't hesitate. Here. The warrant. Move fast and warn Quen. The Lion Guard will be hunting her by sundown."
:I'll get word to Quen. Thanks.

After starting the quest, you can ask Sansonne more about the warrant:
:"I'll do what I can to slow down the Lion Guard, but there's a limit before I draw suspicion to myself. I owe you all that much for helping put Gavin Koldane and his werewolves in the royal dungeons. Just make sure Quen gets someplace safe."
:Anything else you can tell me about this warrant that might be of help?
:"I've seen warrants like this before. With enough coin, even usually honest magistrates can be corrupted. Most likely means someone powerful wants to eliminate a problem, and that problem is Quen. She must have made the wrong person very angry."
:Any suggestions on what we should do?
:"Take Quen and get her far from here. From Abah's Landing. Out of Covenant territory completely if you can. There's going to be eyes everywhere looking for her. I'll do what I can, but if they catch her my hands will be tied."

After you finish your conversation, you will have the The Warrant of Quenneth Lumena in you possession. Quen will also arrive and greets Sansonne:
:Quen: "Bold of you to come visit, Sansonne. And I like your outfit. Looks good on you."
:Captain Sansonne: "Just settling my debts. You won't see me here again."

Talking with Quen, you can show her the warrant and she refuses to run. Instead she wants to meet you in Abah's Landing so you can investigate who is responsible for the false accusation. If you talk with Sansonne again:
:"Quen's plan is bold. And foolish. Still, you've gotten this far, so you must be doing something right. I'll do my best to slow down the hunt so you can get out of Daggerfall. Just don't come back until Quen's name is cleared, understand?"
