# Online:Hollow Moon

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Hollow_Moon
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Hollow Moon is a charitable organization of thieves operating at the Redfur Trading Post. They steal from wealthy merchants and give what they take to those in need. Be it gold to buy food, silks to trade for other goods or gold to buy food or medicine for the sickly.

The Hollow Moon often clashes with Jode's Chariot, the local law enforcement group. The two organizations generally have mutual respect one another, as they both agree with the premise of giving to those in need. However, the Hollow Moon must still be light on their feet, as Jode's Chariot is contractually obligated to stop thieves by Shan-ra, the leader of the merchants running the trading coster.

Agents of the organization will often give Hollow Moon Tokens to the infirm so their need is not questioned when they petition the group for aid.

You join the organization if you follow the related quest.
