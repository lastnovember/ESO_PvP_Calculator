# Online:Poisoned

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Poisoned
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Poisoned Poisoned is a debilitating effect that can be applied by doing poison damage. It deals Poison Damage over 4 seconds. The damage is considered Execute damage and does up to 100% more damage to enemies under 100% Health. This status effect can be removed with a Cleansing effect.
