Cardia was an Imperial and member of the bandit gang found in the ruins of Fort Sphinxmoth, on the second floor of the first room, in the western platform. She was Darius' lover, as the Note to Cardia and Cardia's Letter to Father say. She was only recorded once on April 27, 2014.
