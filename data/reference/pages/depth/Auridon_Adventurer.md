Auridon Adventurer is awarded for completing 37 quests in Auridon. This includes both the Auridon Main Quest as well as any side-quests. It does not include Crafting Writs, Fighters Guild or Mages Guild quests, or pledges for the Undaunted.plain This achievement will allow you to purchase Mathiisen Manor outside of Mathiisen for 1,025,000}, and you can purchase an Ancient High Elf Statue for your house. It costs 35,000 and can be purchased from Athrahgor in Skywatch.
