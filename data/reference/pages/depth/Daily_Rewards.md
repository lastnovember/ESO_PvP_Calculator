# Online:Daily Rewards

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Daily_Rewards
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

March 1, 2026
Daily Rewards (sometimes Login Rewards or Daily Login Rewards) were a feature added to ESO with Update 18 and removed at the end of Update 48. The first time you log in each day, you receive a reward such as Gold, Alliance Points, Crown Crates, or Collectibles such as Pets or Mounts. The rewards could be accessed from the announcements menu, as well as from the tab next to the Crown Store menu.

As of March 1, 2026, the Daily Reward system was disabled. It was succeeded by the Tamriel Tomes rewards system.

The rewards follow a sequential order, which is different for each month and corresponds to the number of days in it. A reward can be claimed on each calendar day of the month, at 3 AM UTC (European servers) and 10 AM UTC (North American servers). Rewards are claimed in consecutive order: if you log in for the first three days, claiming rewards, and don't log in or claim for a few days, you'll receive the fourth reward for that month when you next claim a reward. Note that this means that if you miss logging in or claiming rewards for too many days, you may miss the higher-end rewards which tend to be near the end of the month. You cannot simply log in on day 18 and 25 to get the 18th and 25th reward, you must log in 18 or 25 days that month in total.

## Notes
- All consumable items from daily rewards are Bound, and thus may not be sold or traded to other players. Some may even be Character Bound, and therefore cannot be used by or stored on characters aside from the one you used to claim the reward. Pay attention to the bind type before claiming these rewards so that the character who can most benefit from them gets the rewards.
