Aralfin is a Wood Elf that is being charmed by three lamias with Erendor as part of a random encounter near water in West Weald. Upon defeating the lamia trio, Erendor and Aralfin are freed from the spell and walk away.

## Dialogue
When being charmed, Aralfin can be heard trying to resist:
:""

When you defeat the lamia trio, Erendor and Aralfin are freed from the spell and walk away.
:Aralfin: "We're... free at last!"
:Erendor: "I thought we'd be under their spell forever! Thank you, friend!"

If you manage to talk to them again, they'll say:
:Aralfin: "I-I don't hear the music anymore!"
:Erendor: "Thank you for setting us free! The Green bless you!"
