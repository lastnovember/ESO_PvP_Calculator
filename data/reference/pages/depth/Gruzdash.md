Gruzdash is an Orc Soul-Shriven who was found in the Wailing Prison during ESO's beta.

## Dialogue
When you speak to him, he will tell you:
:"I don't remember my life before Coldharbour. It's all fading away. But I'm hungry. I'm … so hungry."

At other times, he would say:
:"What are you doing? Get out of here, you're going to bring the guards down on us!"

:"Please, don't hurt me! I have nothing to give!"

:"Coldharbour is a graveyard full of buried hopes."

:"You want to die, but you don't. You can't. You just are. You can get used to anything. You kill. You eat. You just are."

:"There is no hope. The future is a dark corridor, and at the far end, the door is bolted."

:"Get away from me! I don't want to kill you, but I'm so hungry!"
