Clannfear are aggressive, quick, and dangerous bestial Daedra. They have a small frill, plated spikes on their spines, and a deadly tri-tipped tail. They are flock inclined-they will work together to bring down enemies-but they are still deadly individually. They have bird-like movements, but retain an almost brutish quality to their attacks.

They have a chance to drop the Clannfear Leather Strapping antiquity lead for the Spaulder of Ruin. Their monster trophy is the Daedric Dewclaw.

## Skills and Abilities

### Standard
;Claw/Headbutt: A basic melee headbutt dealing minor physical damage.
;Tail Spike: The clannfear swings its tail around, indicated by a red circle, dealing moderate physical damage and staggering targets.
;Rending Leap: When standing away from the clannfear, it leaps at its target, dealing low physical damage, knocking down and stunning targets in the landing area. This can be blocked to set the clannfear off balance. Only one Clannfear will perform this attack if you fight multiple of them (Summoned Clannfears do not have this restriction).
;Devour: While stunned, the clannfear chews on the target, dealing continuous moderate physical damage and healing itself for 5% of its Max Health every half-second. This can be interrupted to set the clannfear off-balance.

### Summoned by Snapjaw
The Clannfears summoned by Snapjaw use those abilities instead.
;Headbutt: A basic melee headbutt dealing low physical damage.
;Ferocious Charge:The Clannfear backs up then charges in a straight line, dealing moderate-high physical damage to targets in its wake.
;Daedric Explosion:When the Clannfear dies it explodes in blue flames, dealing low magic damage in a medium sized area.

## Notes
- Clannfear found in the merchant district of Dune are much larger than most generic clannfear found elsewhere.
- Prior to an unknown update, the Clannfears summoned by Spellblades were named Xivilai's Servant.

## Bugs
Despite being elite rank enemies, the Clannfears summoned by Snapjaw, in the Brimstone Den and at the Unfinished Dolmen are vulnerable to crowd control.
