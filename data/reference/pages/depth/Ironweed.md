Ironweed is a multiple-stem dark green plant with pointy leaves and violet flowers. It can be found in any zone by characters with either Tailoring Rank 7 or CP40-60. When harvested, a plant yields three to four samples of the raw material, Raw Ironweed, sometimes accompanied by crawlers or worms. Once ten samples of raw ironweed are collected, they can be refined into seven to ten pieces of the processed material, Ironthread, which is used for crafting pieces of light ironthread armor at any clothing station. For you to be able to create ironthread armor your Tailoring ability has to be rank 7.
