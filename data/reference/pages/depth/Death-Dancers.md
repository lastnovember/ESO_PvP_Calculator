# Online:Death-Dancers

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Death-Dancers
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Death-Dancers, also known as the Xul-Kat are a select group of Solstice Argonians who act as intermediaries for Sithis. Their duties include performing funeral rites for the dead, giving merciful deaths to the suffering and executions in the name of justice when necessary.

There are never more than six members at a time.
