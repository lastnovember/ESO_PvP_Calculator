Stronger Dread Daedrats can be found at the Unfinished Dolmen.

## Skills and Abilities
;Slam: A basic melee attack that does low physical damage.
;Rend: The daedrat spins around and bites fiercely, dealing moderate physical damage. This attack can be blocked to set the daedrat off-balance.

## Bugs
Despite being elite rank enemies, the Dread Daedrats at the Unfinished Dolmen are vulnerable to crowd control.
