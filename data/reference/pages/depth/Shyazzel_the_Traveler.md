Shyazzel the Traveler is a Harvester who has invaded the Isle of Balfiera.

## Related Quests
- The Gates of Adamant

## Skills and Abilities
Shyazzel the Traveler has two unique abilities: the rest are normal Harvester abilities.
;Chasten: The harvester shoots a blue orb towards you as a basic ranged attack. This attack does minor magical damage and snares you.
;Black Winter: The harvester winds up and releases three rows of hands that travel along the ground. This attack does high magical damage, and should be interrupted or avoided. Since this attack is fired in the direction the harvester is facing, a player can easily avoid this attack by standing behind or beside the harvester.
;The Feast: When hurt, the Harvester will grab your essence and attempt to steal it. When grabbed, you will take moderate magical damage and be briefly stunned. You may break free from this. Four Feast orbs will then appear around the Harvester and move towards it. If an orb touches the Harvester, it will be healed. To prevent this, you must destroy the orbs before they reach the harvester. Each orb will also heal you when destroyed.
;Ethereal Burst: A single-target ranged ability used by Shyazzel that deals low magic damage.
;Daedric Blast: An AoE ability. Shyazzel throws her head back and outstretches her arms before unleashing a blast of blue Daedric energy that deals moderate magic damage.
