Corrupt Merchants are Nord merchants who can be found in Riften, Shor's Stone, and Nimalten during the related quest.Merchant in Riften is Male Nord. After stealing from him other two disappear. Screenshots of other two merchants are required.

## Related Quests
- The Wing of the Gryphon

## Quest-Related Events
One of the tasks of the Gryphon is to steal from the corrupt merchants. There is one merchant in each settlement in the Rift.
