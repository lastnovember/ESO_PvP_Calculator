# Online:Invigorating

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Invigorating
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Invigorating is a trait which can only be found on armor. Once you have researched the trait on a given item type, you can craft Invigorating armor by using Garnet. Items with the Invigorating trait will increase your Health Recovery, Magicka Recovery, and Stamina Recovery by an amount dependent on the quality of the item.
