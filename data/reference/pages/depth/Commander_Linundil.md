Commander Linundil is a Maormer member of the Sea Vipers found at the Seaside Sanctuary, only during the related quest. He is accompanied by two other Sea Vipers that are seen digging with shovels.

## Related Quests
- A Storm Upon the Shore

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Shot: A basic ranged attack dealing minor physical damage.
;Call Ally: Commander Linundil blows a horn and summons an animal companion to fight. This animal is usually appropriate to the zone the pet ranger appears in. Commander Linundil's Physical Resistance and Spell Resistance are reduced to 0 while a summon is active, as seen by the blue glow on him. Commander Linundil can only have one animal companion at a time and it will instantly die if he is killed. Commander Linundil cannot cast this ability if an animal is already in the fight.
;Roll Dodge: Commander Linundil may perform a roll dodge to avoid attacks.
;Trap Beast:Commander Linundil places a trap under the target's feet that trigger on the first target that walks near it, dealing low magic damage and rooting for 4 seconds. This cannot be blocked.
;Arrow Spray:Commander Linundil shoots a spray of arrows in the direction he is facing as indicated by a red cone, dealing moderate physical damage and snaring all targets hit for 4 seconds. This can be interrupted to set Commander Linundil off balance. Blocking doesn't prevent the snare.

## Bugs
Despite being an elite rank enemy, Commander Linundil is vulnerable to crowd control.
