# Online:Hillhunter Clan

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Hillhunter_Clan
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Hillhunter Clan are a nomadic clan of Reachfolk present in the Reach around 2E 582. They roam in the mountainous vales south of Markarth, and are renowned for their tracking and woodcrafting skills. They prefer keeping to themselves and staying out of the quarrels of larger clans.

When the Gray Host arrived in the region, Chieftain Faorin, against the judgment of some of his clan members, led them to Briar Rock Ruins to protect themselves from the incoming menace. They were initially welcomed in by the Thornroot Clan, only to be betrayed and captured soon after, serving as the latest victims of the Thornroots' forced briarheart rituals.
