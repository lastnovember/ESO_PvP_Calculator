# Online:Emerald Eye

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Emerald_Eye
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Emerald Eye is a powerful family in the Alik'r. They are named for the Emerald Eye, a relic which grants the women of the family visions but saps their lifeforce in the process. The family have exploited these visions to amass power and wealth over the years, and they have become a powerful criminal organization as a result. They have been a thorn in King Fahara'jad's side for many years.
