Lyris Titanborn is a Nord warrior, with giant blood in her veins, who you meet as you escape Coldharbour. She is a friend and ally of the Prophet, a strange but wise man who instructed you to find Lyris while you were still imprisoned.

Lyris Titanborn has a storied past. Her mother died after giving birth to her, she ran away from home to fight for Emperor Varen, she was bullied by her fellow soldiers, and her father was murdered. She appears to enjoy traditional Nord pastimes, including drinking with friends at taverns to celebrate a victory. Although Abnur Tharn teases her intellect and once described her as having "all the emotional depth of a Frost Atronach," she proves she has an acerbic wit, as well.

Talking to Lyris can be useful while building rapport with the companion Isobel Veloise. However, if the player has completed all quests involving Lyris then she may appear difficult to find. Upon completion of the Western Skyrim storyline, she can be found gathered with others in front of the throne in the Blue Palace in Solitude. Upon completion of The Reach storyline, she can be found gathered with others in the Announcement Hall in Understone Keep.

## Skills and Abilities
Lyris Titanborn uses the following abilities when she is your follower in The Foundry of Woe during the Daughter of Giants quest, both when unarmed and with her axe. During "Soul Shriven in Coldharbour" too ?
;Quick Strike:Basic melle attack dealing low physical damage.
;Heavy Attack:Slower but stronger melee attack dealing moderate physical damage.

Otherwise, Lyris Titanborn uses the following Ravager abilities when she is your follower:
;Quick Strike:A basic melee attack that does minor physical damage.
;Uppercut:An Uppercut-like attack that deals moderate physical damage and knocks back. This attack can be blocked which knocks Lyris off balance instead.
;Cleave:Lyris swings her weapon around like Cleave does, dealing minor physical damage to all targets in front of her. This attack has no red cone indicator.
;Cleave Stance:Lyris enrages then exclusively performs strong front cleave attacks dealing moderate physical damage in a conical AoE for a limited time.

In any case, when she reaches 19% Health, one of the two following things can happen:
:Lyris gets to 100% Health instantly and continues to fight.
;Recover:Lyris is stunned in a kneeling state and restores 10% Health every 2 seconds until she reaches 50% Health where she resumes fighting.
