## Skills and Abilities
;Rending Slash:Bone Reaver hits you with its hands or claws, dealing minor physical damage.
;Slash:Bone Reaver hits you with its hands or claws two times, dealing minor physical damage.
;Devastating Leap:Bone Reaver leaps at the target, knocking them down. The attack is blockable.
;Desecrated Ground: Upon death, the undead has a chance to release noxious fluids around itself as indicated by a red circle. This does moderate damage over time and snares all targets within the red circle.
