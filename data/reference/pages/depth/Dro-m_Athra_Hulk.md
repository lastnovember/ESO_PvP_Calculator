Dro-m'Athra Hulks are powerful Dro-m'Athra encountered within the High Lunarium in the Maw of Lorkhaj. They will be summoned by Rakkhat during various points of the fight.

## Skills and Abilities
;Brutal Strike:Basic melee attack dealing very high physical damage to the target and applies/refreshes "Bleeding".
;Bleeding:Deals ticks of high physical damage over time.
;Thunderous Smash:The Dro-m'Athra Hulk smashes its target with its maul with an overhead attack, dealing extremely high physical damage, ticks of high physical damage over time and applies "Armor Weakened" to the target which reduces their armor for a short time.
;Thunderous Roar:The Dro-m'Athra Hulk roars which deals moderate physical damage and stuns nearby targets.
