Dreamcarver Rangers are Dremora pet rangers affiliated with the Dreamcarver Clan.

## Related Quests
- Bastion Nymic - Fallen Hues
- Bastion Nymic - Writhing Wastes
- Devourer of Dreams

## Skills and Abilities

### Standard
;Quick Shot: A basic ranged attack that does minor physical damage.
;Call Ally:Dreamcarver Ranger blows a horn and summons an animal companion to fight. This animal is usually appropriate to the zone the pet ranger appears in. The animal will instantly die if the pet ranger is killed. Dreamcarver Ranger can only have one animal companion at a time.
;Roll Dodge:Dreamcarver Ranger may perform a roll dodge to avoid attacks.
;Trap Beast:Dreamcarver Ranger places a trap which deal magic damage and roots you in place if you walk into it. Performing a roll dodge will break the trap.

### Herald's Seeker's fight and Chthon Plaza exclusive
The Dreamcarver Rangers in Herald's Seekers' fights and in Chthon Plaza also use this ability.
;Summon Dark Anchor:The Dreamcarver Ranger summons a Daedric Arch which in turn periodically summons very weak Banekin to fight for them.
