Pewter seams can be found near cliffs and mountains at Engraver level 1 or at character level 1-25. They can be harvested for three to four samples of the raw material, Pewter Dust. Once ten samples of dust are collected, they can be refined into seven to ten Pewter Ounces. Pewter Jewelry is crafted from the refined pewter ounces at any of the jewelry crafting stations.
