Animated Tempests are animated pieces of armor found in the Scholarium. They wield staves and use shock magic.

## Related Quests
- The Second Era of Scribing
- The Wing of the Crow

## Quest-Related Events
An Animated Tempest, together with Animated Incendiary and Animated Chillrender are protecting the replacement crystal the Crow requested for the Scribing Altar.

Following the Wing of the Gryphon quest they act as guards around the Scholarium. They also appear within the Wing of the Crow as enemies.

## Skills and Abilities
;Shock: A basic ranged attack that does minor shock damage.
;Thunder Thrall: The enemy teleports to you and summons a bolt of lightning that strikes a large area around itself. This attack does moderate shock damage to all targets as indicated by the red circle.
;Run Away!:When too close to them, the storm mage backs up to put some distance between their target and themselves.
