# Online:Login

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Login
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The login screen for ESO allows for character selection. On PC/Mac, this screen appears after you have entered your login credentials. Login is automatic in some cases (e.g. on console, Steam, Stadia). In either case, reaching the character select screen requires an Internet connection. This screen has a shorter disconnect timer for inactivity than the main game; you will be disconnected from the server after 5 minutes of idling.

The login screen offers the following functionality:
- Character Selection
- Character Creation
- Character Deletion
- The ability to move existing characters higher or lower on the list
- Your total number of available character slots and deletions
- Settings
- Add-ons
- Play Cinematic
- Amount of available Style Parlor tokens (Appearance Change, Name Change, Race Change, Alliance Change)
- Event information and timer
- Back (log out)

Selecting an individual character will provide information on their name, alliance, race, class, level, Champion Points, location, visual appearance, equipped mount, and equipped pet.

## Backgrounds
Originally, the background displayed when previewing a character depended on that character's alliance: Aldmeri Dominion, Daggerfall Covenant, or Ebonheart Pact. Starting in 2020, these three backgrounds were retired in favor of common background related to a given year's main content release (Chapters and later Seasons).
