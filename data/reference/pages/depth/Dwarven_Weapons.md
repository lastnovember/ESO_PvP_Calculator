# Online:Dwarven Weapons

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Dwarven_Weapons
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

This is a listing of all standard Dwarven Weapons in the game. These can be crafted via Blacksmithing from Dwarven Ingots obtained by refining Dwarven Ore. It requires rank 4 in Metalworking. The listed damage values assume that the character is Level 50 CP160; lower level characters may see higher values.
