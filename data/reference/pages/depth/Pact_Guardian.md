Pact Guardians were Nord melee soldiers of the Ebonheart Pact found near Fort Virak, and inside after certain objectives during the related quests. They were all deleted in a very early update to be replaced by several similar NPCs that had a unique name and position, such as Alfund and Helunda. A Dark Elf variant was found at the Starved Plain after completing the quests there, and was instead renamed to Pact Soldier.
