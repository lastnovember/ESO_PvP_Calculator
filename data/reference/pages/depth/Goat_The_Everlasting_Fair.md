The Goat is a talking goat with glowing red eyes who can be met at the Everlasting Fair. He is unhappy with the decisions of the Fair's new management, and thus is open to helping you find some missing Fighters Guild members in exchange for your assistance.

If asked about himself, he is evasive and insists that he is just a talking goat. After things at the fair have been resolved, you just may find the goat hanging around in Sunport.

## Related Quests
