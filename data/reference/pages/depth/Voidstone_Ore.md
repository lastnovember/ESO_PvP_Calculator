Voidstone Ore is an ore of shiny silver color found near mountains or large boulders. It can be found in any zone by characters with Rank 9 in the Metalworking passive or CP90 to 140. When mined, a node yields three to four samples of the ore. Once ten ore samples are collected, they can be refined into seven to ten Voidstone Ingots. Voidstone ingots are used for crafting pieces of heavy voidsteel armor and weapons at any blacksmithing station. In order for you to create voidsteel gear, your Metalworking ability must be at least rank 9.
