The Bent Inquisitor is a Khajiit member of the Torval Curiata who was transformed into a Dro-m'Athra and is waiting for you in the Abandoned Mining Tunnel beneath Goldfolly.

## Related Quests
- Beyond a Veil of Twilight

## Skills and Abilities
;Slice: A basic melee attack that deals low physical damage.
;Magic Dagger: A ranged attack where the sentinel throws a magical shadow dagger at their target, dealing low magical damage.
;Shadow Slice: The sentinel teleports behind the target and strikes at them, dealing moderate physical damage and snaring. This attack an be interrupted to knock the sentinel off balance. If done so however, a burst of shadow energy goes off, indicated by a growing red circle, dealing moderate magical damage.
;Abyss: The sentinel rakes their dagger across the floor, sending out a wave of shadow energy forward, indicated by a red circle, dealing low magical damage and sending affected targets backward. This attack can be interrupted to set the sentinel off-balance.
;Grasping Void: The shade sentinel summons a giant shadowy hand from the ground beneath their target, grabbing them in trapping them in place briefly, before slamming them down, dealing moderate physical damage. Break Free can be used.

## Quest-Related Events
While searching for Cantor Izalgo on Cantor Krin'ze's behalf, you learn that he was taken to Goldfolly but Izalgo and his Torval Curiata guards were attacked by the Rajaska. When you go down into the Abandoned Mining Tunnel, one of the corrupted spirits will be waiting.

She shouts out to Zerith-var as he attacks:
:Bent Inquisitor: "Ravith-morna seeks you, Zerith-var!"

## Bugs
