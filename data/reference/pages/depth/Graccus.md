Graccus is a defected Imperial commander of the Legion of the West Weald found inside The Vile Manse. He was ordered by Javad Tharn to destabilize the Aldmeri Dominion, but instead used his resources to destroy a Bosmer village and built a large mansion on top of it, so that he could excavate the Ayleid ruin found below.

Within the ruins, Graccus found the Book of the Frozen Legion, which he used to perform necromantic rituals on the many Bosmer civilians he enslaved and summon Daedra. With his Imperial troops and undead slaves, Graccus continued to excavate the ruins to find the Oghma Infinium under guidance from Hermaeus Mora, so that he could use the tome to attack the Dominion. However, Javad withdrew his supplies to Graccus as he believed him to be incompetent, and Hermaeus Mora deemed him unworthy to keep the Infinium.

## Skills and Abilities
As a boss rank enemy, Graccus is immune to crowd control.
;Madness/What Must Not Be Seen:Shortly after the fight starts, Hermaeus Mora intervenes as seen by numerous black tendrils on the floor and your vision being darker and shaking. Only the player that has started the fight and Graccus are stunned for 11 seconds.
;Ice Arrow:A basic ranged attack dealing minor frost damage.
;Ice Barrier: Graccus summons an Ice Barrier that absorbs projectiles fired at it until destroyed.
;Winter's Reach:Graccus unleashes an icy whirlwind in the direction he is facing. This whirlwind deals high frost damage and roots all targets that it hits. The root effect passes through block. This can be interrupted to stagger Graccus.
;Run Away!:When too close to him, Graccus backs up to put some distance between his target and himself. This does not apply if Graccus is near his Ice Barrier.
