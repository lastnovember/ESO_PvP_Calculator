# Online:Dreamcarver Clan

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Dreamcarver_Clan
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Dreamcarver Clan are a Dremora clan that serve Vaermina. In recent times they have been sighted in Apocrypha serving her interests and are known to work together with the mortal Hidden Kindred cult.
