The Elder Scrolls Online v11.0.2 kicks off week 3 of the PTS Cycle. The main focus of this week's PTS update is to address a variety of fixes and improvements to the Seasons of the Worm Cult, Part 1 content. You will find fixes for Ossein Cage, a good chunk of quests, and overall improvements to smooth out gameplay. There are also fixes for new Base Game additions, like Hero's Return and UI Modernization efforts.

For those looking for combat updates, don't worry. We're giving you the heads up that next week will have a large chunk of combat adjustments. Stay tuned for PTS Week 4.

Additionally, Whitestrake's Mayhem is available for testing this week! There are also general fixes for Greymoor, Fallen Banners, Necrom and Base Game content. Those changes are outlined below.

As always, feel free to provide feedback on PTS v11.0.2 through our feedback and bug threads, located here. The update is 1.04GB.

## New Features / Updates / Big Changes

### Event: Whitestrake's Mayhem
Mayhem returns! No shake-ups here, just the expected functions at this time (review the in-game help entry for all details). The new rewards include:
- Truth in Wisdom armor set, with pages available from the Impresario for 5 tickets each or rarely from Pelinal's Boon Boxes.
- Arkay Unending Cycle style pages go into the Imperial City "sweeper" boss drop tables along with Dovah's Du'ul. Sancre Tor Sentry pages go into Bags of Veteran's Glory instead of from sweeper drops. Dovah's Du'ul pages may also come from Bags of Veteran's Glory if you have yet to collect them all.
- The Impresario will start carrying the first two fragments, "Comfortable Spider Chassis" and "Dwarven Control Rod," for the Law of Julianos Dwarven Spider mount, sold for 10 tickets each. The third and final fragment will come with a later event.

## Template Information
- We have removed the Subclassing template from character creation. The Max Level template has everything this template had and more.
- Heroic Unity can now be found in the Dungeon Heavy Item Sets container on template characters.
- Tanlorin and Zerith are not automatically unlocked for template characters.
- Perfected Kazpian's Cruel Signet container now has its Jewelry container.
- The Crate of Rare Fish has returned to template characters.
- The Mundus Stone consumable will no longer drop its Mundus Stone when you rezone.

## Known Issues

### Attack Animations
- Though many have been fixed, some abilities still contain extraneous movement, particularly in the extremities (hands, tails, weapons, feet, etc.) which looks like twitching or shaking.
- If casting while running diagonally, the character can be pointing up to 45 degrees away from the target (most obvious with beam attacks). This is a more exaggerated version of an issue that's always been a problem. The final fix should keep you facing your target.
- Moving and jumping with a bow or two-handed melee weapon out can cause female characters' arms to distort. This has been fixed internally and will be in the next PTS update.
- Some weapon types such as Two-Handed, Bow, and Destruction Staff can pull the off-hand away from the weapon briefly and snap back when the ability is played through uninterrupted.

### Ossein Cage
- Seeking Spark Surge and Seeking Forge Fire do not deal damage. This will be fixed in a future update.
