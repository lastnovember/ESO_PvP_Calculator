Ash logs are a dull green color with noticeable vine-like texture. They can be found in any zone by players with Woodworking Rank 7 or CP40 to 60. One log can be harvested for three to four samples of the raw material, Rough Ash. Once ten samples of rough wood are collected, they can be refined into seven to ten pieces of the processed material, Sanded Ash. From the refined ash pieces a woodworker can craft ash staves and bows, as well as ash shields, at any woodworking station. In order for you to create ash gear your Woodworking ability has to be rank 7.
