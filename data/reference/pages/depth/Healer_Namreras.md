Healer Namreras is a Wood Elf member of the Stirk Fellowship who can be found at the Sunport Rampart Camp in Solstice. Her husband is the mage Tesamoril.

## Related Quests
- A Cold Grave

## Quest-Related Events
Meeting with Tesamoril's spirit in Eastern Solstice he asks you to inform Namreras of his death, but also to say that he died heroically rather than due to drinking the wrong potion. Once you meet with Namreras at the camp, you can decide what to tell her.

She will immediately realize something happened to Tesamoril when you greet her:
:"Oh, no. You have that look. Like you're about to give me some horrible news. I should know, I've had plenty of practice at it around here. Tesamoril, my husband, is dead, isn't he? Don't tell me. It was some sort of accident?"

Once you have completed the quest, you can speak with Healer Namreras further, your conversation will depend on your prior choice:

:"Well, you and I know the truth about Tesamoril. But I'll be damned if anyone else is going to know my husband died that way. A healer's husband, mixing up his unlabeled potions? Mortifying. I'd never live it down."
:You're taking this news a lot better than most people would.
:"Well, my dear, he's not the first of my spouses to die to some easily preventable mishap that I always harped on about. He's not even the second. He's the third, if you can believe it. I loved him dearly, but this heartbreak isn't new to me."
:What happened to the other two?
:"Girrail, my first, had a habit of eating spoiled meat. Didn't like being wasteful. Didn't like listening, either. And Neeti, my second, loved hand-feeding sharks. I warned her the one day she arrived empty-handed, they'd take what they could get."
:Sounds like bad luck that none of them listened to you.
:"I've heard it said that bad luck comes in threes. If I make it to a fourth, maybe that spell will be broken. Either way, life goes on. Thanks again, adventurer. And I'm sure I don't need to nag at you to be careful out there."

:"Tesamoril really died a hero, then? So strange. I was so certain. I knew him so well! Maybe not as well as I thought. Oh, poor Tessy. At least I can take comfort in knowing he finally saw my nagging as love."
:Why is Tesamoril dying heroically so unusual to you?
:"He never labeled his potions or poisons. As a mage, he used them constantly. I was certain that one day he'd drink the wrong one by accident. You're a warrior. When you need aid in the midst of battle, you consume the first thing at hand, right?"
:That might have happened to me before, yes.
::"See? Thank you! Clearly it didn't cause too much damage, since you're still here, but these mistakes are quite common. So you can see why I was so certain that one day he'd … oh, well. It doesn't matter. I need to accept what happened and move on."
:No way. I'd never make that sort of mistake.
::"Oh, it's like Tessy's still alive, how much you sound like him. Well, cheers to you, then. A moment ago, I'd have called you an idiot. But I guess I'm not as certain of things as I thought. Maybe being a healer has made me too morbid."

Both options converge into the same reply:
:What if I told you that you're actually right about how he died?
:"Oh, stop. You don't need to say that just to try to make an old Elf feel better. This news is hitting harder than I expected, but I'll be all right. Take care, adventurer. I'd tell you to be careful, but who's going to start listening to me now?"
