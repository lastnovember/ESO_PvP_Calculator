Dro-m'Athra Shadow Walkers are Khajiit nightblades who have transformed into Dro-m'Athra. They are summoned as part of a test by Mazza-Mirri in the Temple of the Hidden Moon, and later encountered within the Hidden Moon Crypts.

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Strike:A basic melee attack that does minor physical damage.
;Veiled Strike:The enemy cloaks, becoming untargetable by most attacks, and prepares to slash the target. This attack does moderate physical damage and will stun the target briefly. This attack can be blocked to set the enemy off balance.
;Teleport Strike:When standing further away from them, the enemy teleports and stabs the target, dealing moderate physical damage.
;Roll Dodge:The enemy can roll dodge to avoid attacks.

## Bugs
Unlike most nightblade enemies, they wield a staff instead of dual weapons, which causes some of their ability animations to be displayed incorrectly.
