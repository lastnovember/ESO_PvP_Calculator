Brineclaw is a Hadolid that can be found in Breakwater Cave, serving as its delve boss.

## Related Quests
- A Sheep in Need

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Strike:A basic melee attack that deals low physical damage.
;Lacerate:A charged melee attack that deals high physical damage and staggers the affected target. This can be blocked to stagger Brineclaw.
;Cleave:Brineclaw swings its blade, indicated by a red cone, dealing low physical damage and snaring affected targets.
;Whirlpool:Brineclaw plunges its sword into the ground, causing a pool of swirling waters, indicated by a red circle, to shoot out and appear at a target's location, dealing continuous low magical damage and snaring targets standing inside.
;Screech:Brineclaw rears up, as a wide red cone appears, then releases a magically-infused shriek, dealing low magical damage and knocking down affected targets.
;Flurry:Brineclaw attacks with a series of four rapid strikes, indicated by small red cone, that each deal low physical damage and snare affected targets.
;Flip: If standing far away from targets, Brineclaw performs a spinning lunge through the air at one of them, indicated by a red circle around them, dealing moderate physical damage.
;Runt Quake:Brineclaw raises its sword up and performs a spell that sends out three tremors outward, indicated by red circles, which deal low physical damage and stagger affected targets. After a short while, the tremors end and creatures are summoned to assist Brineclaw. These can consist of Coral Crabs, Hadolid Peelers, and Hadolid Tidecallers.
;Empower:Brineclaw empowers its nearby allies, increasing both their damage done and their Max Health by 25%, as well as restoring them to full health.
;Swarm:When Brineclaw dies, a massive swarm of very weak Coral Crabs appears and attacks targets.

## Bugs
The weak crab swarm may burrow back underground instead of dying when their health is depleted.
