Chief Ogzor is the dying chief of Clan Shatul. His skull was smashed in by the giant, Urgkail the Cleaver, and there is nothing you can do to save his life. His death catalyzes the events of the related quest.

He is being tended to by his mother Yazara, as well as his hunt-wife Glagosh. His hearth-wife, Rogzesh, mourns nearby. He is also the father to an exiled daughter that he made sure no one in the clan would mention.
