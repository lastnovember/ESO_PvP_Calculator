Ending the Louse-Whisperers is obtained by slaying ten Louse-Whisperer Champions that board your ship during the daily event quest's ship battle. You will hear either Ulene Teneran or Captain Nocadanth announce them. These champions are stronger than the rest, have unique names and will have the title "Louse-Whisperer Champion". If the sea battle goes on for a while, multiple champions can appear during the daily quest.

The possible champions you can encounter are:
- Blasphemous Kel
- The Bosun
- Klygasso Twinblades
- Sinolos the Broodhost
- confirm last one, either Aernelrith Sharkguts or Daeandosa Scrimshaw
