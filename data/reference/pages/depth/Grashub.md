Grashub is an Orc who could be found at the then-blocked eastern gate in Craglorn. She was removed from the game after the zone of Upper Craglorn was added in Update 4 and the gate was opened.

## Dialogue
When spoken to, he'll say:
:"Those aren't Orcs, they're monsters! Mauloch help us!"
