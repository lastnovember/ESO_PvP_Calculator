## Skills and Abilities
During The Emperor's Secret, Eveli will aid you with resource restoration. She also uses her bow with the resource restoration while in The Deadlands: Testing Grounds.

### Resource Restoration
;Wood Elf Restorative: Eveli throws a potion on the ground which bounces three times, each time leaving a circle of color (Green then Blue then Yellow). Standing in any will give you the Wood Elf Restorative buff but with different effects: the green circle restores your Stamina, the blue circle restores your Magicka and the yellow circle restores your Health.
;Healing Draught:Eveli throws a potion directly at you which heals you lightly.

### Bow
;Quick Shot:A basic ranged attack that does minor physical damage.
;Arrow Spray:Eveli shoots a spray of arrows in the direction it is facing as indicated by a red cone. This attack does moderate physical damage as well as snares all enemies hit.
;Run Away!:When an enemy is too close to her, Eveli rolls backwards to put some distance between the enemy and herself.

## Notes
- Eveli's voice actor is Mary Faber (IMDb).
- Eveli's character was written by Bill Slavicsek and Jason Barnes. Jason has said her last name may have been changed before release. Bill mentioned on ESO Live on June 23rd, 2017, that Jason later named his daughter after the character (slightly different spelling, same sound).
- Eveli is also featured in Smolder Scrolls Online.
- Eveli has two Meet the Characters about her: one released for Orsinium, and another for Flames of Ambition.
- Although the dialogue options at the end of the interaction with her outside Fort Redmane are red-coded, they don't have an impact on the quest as far as having different results occur, and are just differently-worded replies instead. The same occurs later on during the optional dialogue after defeating Vandacia.
