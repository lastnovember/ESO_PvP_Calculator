Baandari Nomads are Khajiit located at Dragonscours in Northern and Southern Elsweyr. They cannot be spoken to.

## Dialogue
If a dragon starts to land in the area they're in, you'll hear any of the following:
:Baandari Nomad: "Khenarthi lift me! This one suddenly has other places to be!"
:Baandari Nomad: "Dark Moons! This one prefers life over profit, and death by Dragon cuts profits short!"
:Baandari Nomad: "A Dragon? Curiosity entices me toward it, but the winds sent me elsewhere!"

## Bugs
Baandari Nomads will take damage if they are caught in the area of an Unrelenting Force attack. However, since they are friendly, they will have no response to it and will not die if they reach 0 health.
