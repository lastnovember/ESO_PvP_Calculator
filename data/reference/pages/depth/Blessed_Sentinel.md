Blessed Sentinels are giant suits of Auroran armor serving Meridia. They are similar in appearance to Mournful Aegises. They can have one or two pairs of arms.

## Related Quests
- Dust Smote
- Fletching Fetching
- Scholarly Observations

## Skills and Abilities
;Singing Slice/Singing Strike: A basic melee attack dealing low physical damage.
;Dissonant Blow:Charged melee attack dealing moderate physical damage and staggering the target. This can be blocked to set the Blessed Sentinel off-balance instead.

## Known Blessed Sentinels
- Iratan the Lightbringer, the group boss of the Shrine of the Reforged Dragonhold
- Sentinel Carisea, found at Broken Light Temple
- Sentinel Tomarion, found at Broken Light Temple
- Symphony of Blades, the final boss in the Depths of Malatar Wrathstone

## Bugs
Despite being elite rank enemies, Blessed Sentinels are vulnerable to crowd control. The "Flying Immunities" buff which grants immunity to snares and roots that could work as a fail-safe in this case is also absent.
The "Dissonant Blow" ability which is derived from Mournful Aegides' same ability still have the blue particles when the attack hits.
