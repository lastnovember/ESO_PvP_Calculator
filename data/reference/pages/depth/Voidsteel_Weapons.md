# Online:Voidsteel Weapons

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Voidsteel_Weapons
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

This is a listing of all standard Voidsteel Weapons in the game. These can be crafted via Blacksmithing from Voidstone Ingots obtained by refining Voidstone Ore. It requires rank 9 in Metalworking. The listed damage values assume that the character is Level 50 CP160; lower level characters may see higher values.
