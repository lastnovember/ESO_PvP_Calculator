Gemcarver Hynax is a Dremora member of the Soulbinders who appears as one of the secret bosses in Black Gem Foundry.

## Skills and Abilities
;Firestorm: Gemcarver Hynax summons a ring of lethal flames around him as he holds his weapon aloft. It covers almost the entire arena, except for a safe zone inside the ring that spans around 10 meters, which Gemcarver Hynax stands in the middle of. The complete outer edge of the arena can also be safe, depending on where Gemcarver Hynax is when casting the ability.
;Immolating Sphere
;Flare:A basic ranged attack dealing minor flame damage.
;Staff Smash: A heavy attack that applies Essence Drain and Essence Leech.
;Summon Storm Atronach: Summons Lesser Storm Atronach.
;Summon Stone Atronachs: Summons Lesser Stone Atronach.
;Summon Flame Atronachs: Summons Lesser Flame Atronach.
