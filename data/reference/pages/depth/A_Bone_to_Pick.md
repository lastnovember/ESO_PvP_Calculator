# Online:A Bone to Pick

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:A_Bone_to_Pick

Online Achievement Summary
icon = A Bone to Pick
type = DLC
type2 = Lost Depths
type3 = Dungeons
points = 10
desc = Witness the final evolution of the Bone Colossus while fighting Varzunon in Graven Deep.

A Bone to Pick is awarded for allowing enough Skeletal Sacrifices how many are needed to reach Varzunon and change his form to the largest possible during the battle in Graven Deep on any difficulty. The achievement is awarded immediately, and the battle doesn't need to be won.
