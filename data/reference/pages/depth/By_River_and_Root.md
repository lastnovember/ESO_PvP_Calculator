## Detailed Walkthrough

The following NPCs will be present when the Root-Whisper Hist is reawakened, if you completed the corresponding quests beforehand: Jekka-Wass Vozei, Kishi, Mewah-Jez, Nisswo Uaxal, Sulahkeesh, Hands-That-Heal, Tree-Minder Pavu, Iskenaaz, Lady Clarisse Laurent and Stibbons (still in egg form),

## Notes
- Xukas, as well as Jaxsik-Orrn and her Dead-Water warriors won't exist in the village before starting the quest.
- The Dead-Water warriors lack nameplates.
- After returning from the Remnant, the survivor and the gathered Argonians won't exist at the Hist tree before reaching the related objective.

## Bugs
The leaves of the Hist tree all reappear instantly the second the quest is completed, rather than gradually over the course of the ritual when energy courses back into it.
