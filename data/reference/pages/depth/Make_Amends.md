# Online:Make Amends

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Make_Amends
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Online Achievement Summary
icon=Making Amends
type=Justice
points=15
desc=Make a one time transaction of 1,000 gold or greater for crimes committed against the citizens of Tamriel.

Make Amends is awarded for paying off a bounty of at least 1,000 to either a Fence or a Guard. Clearly a Fence is the preferable choice, as they will not confiscate any stolen goods you may be carrying. Note that being killed by guards with a high bounty does not countplain.
