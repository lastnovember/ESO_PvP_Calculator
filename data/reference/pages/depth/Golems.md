# Online:Golems

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Golems
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Golems are a type of artificial construct animated by magic. Roughly humanoid in shape, they are generally hostile.

When killed, Golems drop Elemental Essence like Atronachs. They are sometimes treated like Daedra.plain

## Types of Golem
- Azureblight Infestor
- Fetcherfly Hive Golem
- Magma Golem
- Reef Guardian
