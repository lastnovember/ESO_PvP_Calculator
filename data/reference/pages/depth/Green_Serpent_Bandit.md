This Green Serpent Bandit is a member of the Green Serpents who can be encountered during the Heroes of High Isle event. He can be found standing over a body at the easternmost Scenic Viewpoint on Amenos during the quest The Island Tour. Alternatively, you may encounter Sumalatha Thota here instead.

## Related Quests
- The Island Tour

## Quest-Related Events
When approached, he can be heard saying:
:Green Serpent Bandit: "What a mess. Didn't expect gadabouts from the mainland to put up such a fight."
He will then disappear in a cloud of smoke.
