Drowned Assassins are spectral assassins who can be found in Graven Deep where they have been pressed into service by Zelvraak the Unbreathing.

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Strike: A basic melee attack dealing low physical damage.
;Whirlwind: The assassin spins around, dealing moderate physical damage to all targets as indicated by a red circle.
;Assassinate: The assassin strikes at the target's back for moderate physical damage. This attack can only be performed if the assassin is behind their target, and they will often use "Passing Through" to get there. Face toward the assassin to prevent them from using this ability.
;Passing Through:The assassin jumps above the target to get behind them.
;Roll Dodge: The assassin can perform a roll dodge to avoid incoming damage.
