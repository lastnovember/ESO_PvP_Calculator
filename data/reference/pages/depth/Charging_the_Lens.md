## Detailed Walkthrough
During the Battle for the Writhing Wall, Orinde Glenuvon can be found at the Stirk Fellowship camp on Stirk or at the Sunport Rampart Camp in Western Solstice. For players who want to participate in the event but don't own the 2025 Content Pass, the quest is accessible from Stirk.

Talking with Orinde, she will want your help in gathering Vitrified Souls from powerful Daedra and Worm Cultists around Tamriel. These souls will be used to empower the Meridian Lens, with the aim of breaking through the Writhing Wall. Once you have accepted the quest, Orinde will have marked your map with locations with a prevalence of enemies that will drop Vitrified Souls.

You will need to gather 15 Vitrified Souls from various locations around Tamriel and Oblivion. Some of these areas include:
- Anywhere in Coldharbour.
- Sites of Worm Cult or Daedric activity on Solstice, such as the Swencoast Cottage or the burial grounds to the east of Shell-Tide Village.
- Fellowship Siege Camps on Solstice when they are under attack.

There will also be places on mainland Tamriel, known as invasion sites. These Delves or Public Dungeons will have been partially taken over by the Worm Cult and their allies, and the area's boss will have been replaced by a champion from Coldharbour:

:I gathered the vitrified souls.
:"You have my thanks for your contribution, adventurer. You've done well! Here, for your efforts. Now if you'll excuse me, I must see that these vitrified souls get to where they're needed."

You will receive a Glorious Writhing Rewards Coffer if this was your first daily event quest. Otherwise, you will receive a Writhing Rewards Coffer.

## Notes
- Before Update 48, she gave and received her quest in the Dominion Camp of Stirk after and during the quest Justice for the Fallen, earlier than intended. The existence of the Meridian Lens was leaked into the live environment early through this quest.

## Bugs
You may end up with 30 Vitrified Soul in your quest item inventory rather than the intended 15 at the end of the quest.
None of the locations Orinde sends you to have their enemy spawns changed during the quest; therefore, of the locations she provides, only Deetra Grotto and Swencoast Cottage are valid locations to hunt for the shards.
