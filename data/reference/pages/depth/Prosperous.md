# Online:Prosperous

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Prosperous
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Update 15
Prosperous was a trait which could only be found on armor. Once you had researched the trait on a given item type, you could craft Prosperous armor by using Garnet. Items with the Prosperous trait would increase the amount of Gold found by looting slain enemies by an amount dependent on the quality of the item.

## Notes
- This trait replaced Exploration, which increased your experience gained by discovering locations. It was later replaced by Invigorating, which increases your Health Recovery, Magicka Recovery and Stamina Recovery.
