# Online:Order of the Claw

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Order_of_the_Claw
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

:"To teach within the Order of the Claw, one must first pass the Trials of the Ascetic." -Kansur-jo

The Order of the Claw is one of the Khajiiti clerical orders. It was created by the Lunar Clergy to guard the Do'Krin Monastery and its main relic: the Golden Claw. Its members are also trained in Khajiiti martial arts.

Within the Lunar Clergy are other orders, including the Broken Paw, the Brotherhood of the Fang and the Order of the Crescent.
