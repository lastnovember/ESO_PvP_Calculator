# Online:Pit Rats

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Pit_Rats
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Pit Rats are a group of Dunmeri thugs who have taken over the kwama mine in Darkshade Caverns. Tervur Sadri hired the group to kill the miners while he murdered his partner Selos in order to steal the deed to the mine.
