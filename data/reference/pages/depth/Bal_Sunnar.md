It is a remote Telvanni settlement found in the mountains south of Senie and was established several centuries prior to the Second Era by Matriarch Lladi Telvanni. However the area is subject to a temporal anomaly causing multiple eras of Bal Sunnar to be accessible.It is included in the Scribes of Fate DLC alongside Scrivener's Hall.

Ash hoppers and wasps can be found in the present, while centipedes, shroom beetles and wormmouths in the past, fiendroths, fleshflies and rats reside in the future. Hazards include bottomless pits, meteorites, pus pools and pus sacks.

## Layout
Bal Sunnar's layout is unique because you time-travel four times during the dungeon, you will see how Bal Sunnar has been built and how it has evolved.

### The Present

The Present is the first version of Bal Sunnar you encounter and also the last one, it is set during 2E 582. You discover with the Psijic Saresea that it is a secluded Dunmeri settlement composed of Telvanni forces, nix-hounds, Argonian Behemoths and Argonian slaves. It has Dunmeri architecture, with the landscape being much the same as the outside lands of Stonefalls. The recurring optional content is always located to the southwest, which here you can directly access to free the Argonian slaves. A well stands at the center of the settlement. The Tower of Shadow's Blessing stands at the north after crossing a bridge over a river.

### Tower of Shadow's Blessing

The Tower of Shadow's Blessing is a small tower of Dunmeri architecture. It is composed of a small entry room leading to a staircase which leads to a large circular room. This is where you will find the first boss of the dungeon, Kovan Giryon, using some kind of floating orb. After defeating him, the orb will transport you into the past.

### The Past

The Past is entered after defeating Kovan Giryon and with no clues to say when you ended up, apart from the fact that the Telvanni house already exists and the Journal of Nerile Belvayn you can find. You will wake in the Tower of Shadow's Blessing but is it in construction. Getting out of the tower reveals that you have traveled in the past. You will see that all the settlement is being built, the bridge you crossed earlier and the river are no longer there. Your only option is to delve into the mushroom-filled caves beneath the settlement.

You will encounter Blessed Inheritor forces, primitive nix-oxen and Argonian slaves. The optional content is located at the usual area, but to access it you have to traverse the hidden tunnel before the Excavation Chamber. Instead of a puzzle, you will find a Telvanni slave-handler beating a friendly Argonian Behemoth and you will have to defeat the slave-handler. Back in the tunnels, you will arrive in the Excavation Chamber and encounter Matriarch Lladi Telvanni using the orb, now known as the "Temporal Distortion". When she notices you, she flees and lets her Nix-Ox pet, Roksa the Warped, fight you. Saresea arrives just in time to assist with the fight. After defeating Roksa, the "Temporal Distortion" sends you forward in time.

### The Future

The Future is entered after defeating Roksa the Warped, and it is strongly suggested you ended up during the Red Year in 4E 5. You wake up in the excavation chamber but now lava flows on the sides. A bucket tied to a rope can be used to return to the surface, specifically through the well at the center of the settlement. The settlement is very damaged, fires burn the houses, the sky is dark with clouds and meteors fall everywhere. The river flowing through the settlement is now filled with lava.

While scouring the settlement, the beings you face are voriplasmic corpses and vorisplasms. In the northeast area, you will see a daedric shrine has been constructed. When you enter the shrine, the banners confirm what you may have suspected from the Vessels and Glories you faced. The Blessed Inheritors are now worshipers of Peryite the Taskmaster.

Fighting your way through the Inheritors, you will encounter an older Matriarch Lladi tinkering with Temporal Distortion in a squared-shaped open area. While Saresea attempts to contain the distortion, Matriarch Lladi will fight you as the final boss of the dungeon. Once you have beaten Matriarch Lladi, Saresea sacrifices herself to contain the distortion, which transports you back to the present day, along with the dead Matriarch Lladi. The cost to Saresea was that her own existence was split into multiple points in her personal timeline. When you speak with her, she will hand you the item that was causing the temporal distortion, a Glyphic.
