The section that you visit is split into two areas with different environments: the Burn, which filled with lava and ashen grounds; and the Sever, which is constantly windswept with lightning strikes being common. This realm is currently connected by portals to Fargrave, another plane of Oblivion.

This zone serves as the primary location for The Deadlands DLC storyline and has three roaming sweeper bosses known as Havocrel Executioners.
