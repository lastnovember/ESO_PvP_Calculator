Arkasis the Mad Alchemist is a Nord and a brilliant alchemist who has been causing trouble around Skyrim for decades with his experiments. His current abode is the Stone Garden, within Blackreach. Here, he performs research and experimentation on behalf of his new patron.

He serves as the final boss of the dungeon.

## Related Quests
- Method and Madness
- Pledge: Stone Garden

## Skills and Abilities
The fight in his Laboratory has several phases which involve luring Arkasis out of the dome and the use of the Werewolf Behemoth transformation potions between the times he is on the ground.

### First Phase - Breaking the Pressure Valves
When you start, Gwendis will provide Werewolf Behemoth transformation potions for the Bestial Transformation. Use them and begin breaking the Pressure Valves in the tubes arranged around the device Arkasis is in. As you do this, he will have Shock Emitters fire out of the device. If they are left alone, they will continuously charge up a lightning attack which can affect those nearby. While they are charging (shown by the green-yellow AoE circle filling up), you can break them as a Werewolf Behemoth.

Stone Garden Apprentices may also appear and harass you.

This first stage ends when Arkasis pumps the Wolfsbane Formula into the room which reverses the Bestial Transformation. It will look like a pale yellow mist on the floor.

### Second Phase - Arkasis and Combustible Alchemy
When Arkasis enters the field, he will be wielding a dagger and a potion vial. In this phase, he will fight using most of the chemist class abilities.
;Lop:A basic melee attack that does moderate physical damage.
;Chop:A basic melee attack that does moderate physical damage.
;Puncture:Arkasis thrusts his weapon with disciplined precision at a target, dealing moderate physical and afflicts the target with Major Breach for 15 seconds.
;Heavy Attack: Arkasis deliviers a powerful sword strike, dealing very high physical damage and staggering. This can be blocked.
;Volatile Pool:Arkasis throws a potion that sets the targeted area alight and bounces once forward and creates a second flame area. The pools deal moderate flame damage over time to all players within the red circle.
;Blazing Tar:Arkasis covers his weapon in flaming oil, causing Lop and Chop to become Blazing Lop and Blazing Chop.
;Blazing Lop: A basic melee attack that does moderate physical damage and additional fire damage over time.
;Blazing Chop:A basic melee attack that does minor physical damage and additional fire damage over time.
;Crazed Flames: Arkasis points his weapon forward and sends out three fire storms forward, indicated by a red circle, which then snake around the general area where he is standing, dealing continuous high flame damage.
;Noxious Vial: Arkasis throws a poisonous vial far away from him, which then releases poisonous swirls from it back towards him and his targets, indicated by small red circles, dealing high poison damage and stunning each. At the end, the vial detonates, indicated by a growing large red circle, dealing very high poison damage.
;Summon: During this phase, there will also be adds released, up to three at a time. These should be dealt with by the damage dealers quickly. These include; Alchemized Bristlebacks, Chaurus and Durzogs
:Bloodknights are also potential adds, and the frequency they show up depends on how many Vitalizer Fluid tank valves you closed.

Upon reaching 60% health, Arkasis will retreat back up into his control station and activates his Stone Husks. At this point, Gwendis will actively begin sabotaging the machinery.

### Third Phase - Stone Husk vs. Werewolf Behemoth Phase
These Stone Husks will have much higher health than the ones previously encountered. Gwendis will throw down more vials of the Bestial Transformation potion. Drink them and start beating up the Husks. Much like the first phase, Shock Emitters will be released as well as lab assistants. Eventually, Arkasis will release the antidote once more and then comes back to fight.

Arkasis will fight in the same way as the Second Phase. Adds will also continue to be summoned in the same fashion as before. Upon reaching 15% health, he will retreat once more and release more Stone Husks. At this stage of the fight, it is basically a cycle between the third phase of destroying the Stone Husks as Werewolf Behemoths and dealing damage to Arkasis when he comes down to the floor. This happens until you reach the final phase.

### Execution Phase - Arkasis Ascendant
During his last time in the control station, Arkasis heals back to 40% health. Gwendis then completes her sabotage and the power directed to the central chamber will overload it, filling the glass tube with blue lightning which Arkasis is trapped in. When he escapes, he will have been transformed into something resembling Lightning Form, and he will no longer use his poison-based abilities, but otherwise retains all of his other previous abilities, and gains an array of new ones. The same adds as before will continue to appear.

;Volatile Blast: Arkasis raises his hand up, then emits a forceful burst of electricity from it, indicated by a wide red cone, dealing very high shock damage and staggering. This can be interrupted.
;Homing Orb: Arkasis throws an orb of electricity away from him but still in the general area, indicated by a small red circle, at which point it starts traveling in the direction of nearby targets, dealing continuous high shock damage.
;Bombardment: Arkasis chucks several orbs in the air, which come crashing back down, indicated by large red circles, dealing very high shock damage.
;Pin: Arkasis throws an electrified rod at a target, indicated by a small red circle around them, trapping the target to the ground and dealing quick and very high amounts of shock damage to them. Other allies must approach the trapped target and use the "Remove Bolt" synergy to free them.
