Online Champion 2.0 Summary
sign=Craft
type=passive
stagepoints=10
maxpoints=50
desc=Increases your gold gained by 2% per stage.

Gilded Fingers increases the gold you gain by up to 10%. This only include the gold you generate by doing activities in the game such as looting enemies, Excavation bonus loot gold and quest rewards. This doesn't apply to selling items to NPC Merchants, selling in Guild Traders, receiving gold from a player and from Daily Rewards.
