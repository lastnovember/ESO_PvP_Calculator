Dremora Kynlurkers are Dremora nightblades found in multiple locations.

## Related Quests
- Breaking the Shackle
- Dark Anchors in Tamriel
- Maelstrom Arena (quest)
- Pledge: City of Ash II
- Vanus Unleashed

## Skills and Abilities

### Nightblade
;Quick Strike:Basic melee attack dealing minor physical damage.
;Veiled Strike:The Dremora Kynlurker cloaks, becoming untargetable by most attacks, and prepares to slash the target, dealing moderate magic damage and stunning the target for 2 seconds. This can be blocked to set the Dremora Kynlurker off balance instead.
;Teleport Strike:When standing further away from them, the Dremora Kynlurker teleports and stabs the target, dealing moderate magic damage and snaring for 2 seconds. Blocking does not negate the snare.
;Roll Dodge:The Dremora Kynlurker can roll dodge to avoid attacks.

### Imperial CityImperial City
The Dremora Kynlurker nightblades in Imperial City also use those abilities.
;Health Potion:The Dremora Kynlurker drinks a potion, restoring 7,904 Health. This can be interrupted to set the Dremora Kynlurker off balance.
;Summon Dark Anchor:The Dremora Kynlurker summons a Daedric Arch which in turn periodically summons very weak Banekin to fight for them.
;Desecrated Ground:On death, the undead rarely desecrate the ground under them as indicated by a red circle. This does continuous minor magic damage and snares all targets as well as healing undead enemies within the red circle for 3% of the original undead's Max Health per tick. (see Bugs)

### Ashalmawia
The Dremora Kynlurker nightblades in Ashalmawia also use the following abilities:
;Health Potion:The Dremora Kynlurker drinks a potion, restoring 4,148 Health. This can be interrupted to set the Dremora Kynlurker off balance.
;Summon Dark Anchor:The Dremora Kynlurker summons a Daedric Arch which in turn periodically summons very weak Banekin to fight for them.

### VoidstalkerOrsinium
;Quick Strike: A basic melee attack that does minor physical damage.
;Veiled Strike:The Dremora Kynlurker cloaks, becoming untargetable by most attacks, and prepares to slash the target, dealing moderate magic damage and stunning the target for 2 seconds. This can be blocked to set the Dremora Kynlurker off balance instead.
;Teleport Strike:When standing further away from them, the Dremora Kynlurker teleports and stabs the target, dealing moderate magic damage and snaring for 2 seconds. Blocking does not negate the snare.
;Roll Dodge: The Dremora Kynlurker can roll dodge to avoid attacks.
;Blood Craze: The Dremora Kynlurker slashes the target twice, dealing moderate physical damage twice then very minor bleed damage every two seconds over 8 seconds to the target. The bleeding also heals the Dremora Kynlurker for around a quarter of the damage done. Blocking the first hit negates the bleeding, but blocking the second one does not.
;Agony: The Dremora Kynlurker channels a spell dealing moderate magic damage and stunning the target for 2 seconds. Taking damage from any source will break the stun, but the target may choose to break free from it instead. After the stun ends, this spell also deals minor magic damage every second over 6 seconds to the target. This can be interrupted to set the Dremora Kynlurker off balance instead. Blocking will not prevent the damage over time.
;Whirlwind: The Dremora Kynlurker spins around, doing moderate physical damage to all targets as indicated by a red circle.
;Assassinate: The Dremora Kynlurker strikes at the target's back for moderate physical damage. This attack can only be performed if the Dremora Kynlurker is behind the target, and they will often use "Passing Through" to get behind the target. Ensure your character is facing the Dremora Kynlurker at all times to prevent this.
;Passing Through:The Dremora Kynlurker jumps above the target to get behind them.
;Soul Tether: The Dremora Kynlurker jumps and casts a rune on the ground. Any targets caught in the AoE attack will take moderate magic damage and be stunned for 4 seconds then minor magic damage over time which heals the Dremora Kynlurker for the same amount.
