Jeetra is an Argonian Stone-Nest warrior and Death-Dancer who is first encountered when she stumbles upon Darien Gautier and Walks-in-Ash while they were preparing to attack Gristmung Hold.

## Related Quests

- Hunt for the Great Mage
- The Deep Tombs of Xul-Haj
- The Gates of Mor Naril (quest)
- The Final Dark

## Quest-Related Events

### The Worm Turns
With the Writhing Wall taken down, you and the Stirk Fellowship will begin to press into Eastern Solstice to attack the Worm Cult. You quickly learn that the nearby Gristmung Hold was taken over by the Worm Cult and that they have been using Stone-Nest prisoners to test their new necrotic device.

After you have secured the nearby camp's ballista platforms, Prince Azah will have you check on Darien Gautier and Walks-in-Ash to see what is going on with the delayed signal fire. To the northwest of the Hold, you will find Darien and Walks-in-Ash surrounded by Stone-Nest Warriors led by Jeetra:
:Jeetra: "More invaders? I have had enough of these corpse-stealers!"

:Darien Gautier: "Yes, so kindly watch where you're swinging those maces."

Talking to Baar-Jasa, she reveals that she knew Vanus Galerion and had helped pass on messages. Baar-Jasa mentions that she had a written letter for Gabrielle Benele but that she hid it in a nearby village before she was captured.
