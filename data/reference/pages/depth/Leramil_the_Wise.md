Leramil the Wise is a powerful High Elf mage and scholar who has been working with Hermaeus Mora ever since making a bargain with him a century ago. She does not worship Mora, but makes use of knowledge acquired from the Prince for her own studies.

She will first approach you and ask for your assistance on behalf of the Prince, as he has foreseen a calamitous issue that will affect both Nirn and Apocrypha.

Once you have made a contract with Hermaeus Mora, Leramil will act as your main contact and one of your allies on the Telvanni Peninsula and later in West Weald.
