# Online:Black Fin Legion

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Black_Fin_Legion
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Black Fin Legion is an Argonian militia that governs and protects Gideon and its surrounding terriories. It originally was an actual military legion that was part of the Ebonheart Pact, led by Keshu. When she left the Pact, most of her soldiers followed.
