# Online:Creeping Shadows

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Creeping_Shadows
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Creeping Shadows are purple fields of shadowy mist found in the Cogitum Centralis. They are actually composed of many small invisible overlapping areas that deal direct moderate-high Oblivion damage every second. This effect is referred to as "Impending Darkness". You take damage from each area when standing in multiple at once, and will deactivate this hazard as part of the related quest.

## Related Quests
- Where Shadows Lie

## Bugs
Block, armor and damage taken reduction effects all reduce this hazard's damage despite it dealing Oblivion Damage.
