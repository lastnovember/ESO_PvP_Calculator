# Online:Endeavors

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Endeavors
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

March 2, 2026

Endeavors is a system introduced with Update 30 and was activated on June 17 2021. They are account-wide, limited time activities that reward a new currency, Seals of Endeavor. These can be used to purchase any item from a currently active Crown Crate. Endeavors reward you for completing simple tasks, such as killing a number of creatures, crafting a number of items from a specific tradeskill, etc, and can be found in the Group & Activity Finder.

Endeavors come in two groups: Daily and Weekly. You can complete three of the five Daily Endeavors per day, and one of the three Weekly Endeavors per week, after which the other Endeavors are locked out.

Once you've hit your Endeavors limits, you must wait until they reset to complete more, and progress is frozen for uncompleted endeavors. Daily Endeavors reset daily at 6am UTC, and Weekly Endeavors reset with Leaderboards. The remaining times for these are tracked for you in the Endeavors section of the Group & Activity Finder.
Completing Endeavors automatically rewards Seals of Endeavor, as well as potentially other bonuses like experience, inspiration, and other similar rewards.

As of March 2, 2026, the Endeavor system was disabled. With the release of Update 49 on March 9, Tamriel Tomes will provide Weekly and Seasonal Challenges instead. Tamriel Tomes will also become the source of the Seals of Endeavors.

## Seals of Endeavor
:"Made from a precious mix of metals, these seals are awarded to those with an adventurous spirit. Seals can be traded for costumes, mounts, and much, much more." -Daily Rewards description

Seals of Endeavor are an account-wide currency that you can spend in the related section of the Crown Store to purchase items in currently available Crown Crates. When a new Crate is offered on the Crown Store, items available to purchase with Seals also change to match the active Crate.

You cannot trade, gift, or extract items bought from the Seals of Endeavor section of the Crown Store. They do not have a cap, nor do they expire.

In Update 49, they were renamed to Seals.

## Rewards

### Daily Endeavors
- Of the 5 Daily Endeavors offered, a maximum of 3 can be completed per day.
- Each endeavor will give a set amount of Seals and sometimes Experience, Gold, rarely Tel Var Stones, Transmute Crystals, Undaunted Keys or Writ Vouchers. Possible rewards include:
- Main rewards: 10, 15
- Additional rewards:
- Experience: 1,500, 1,750, 2,000
- Gold: 1,000, 1,250, 1,500, 1,750
- Writ Vouchers: 1 (each day during the Crafter's Celebration event)
- Transmute Crystals: 2 (each day during the Zeal of Zenithar event)
- Special rewards:
- 40, 50, 60, 75, 80, 90, 100, 120, 150, 200 (during DLC promotions like Thieves Guild and Dark Brotherhood)
- Additional rewards:
- Undaunted Keys: 1 - 3 (during Undaunted faction spotlight)
- Outdated rewards: 20, 25, 30
- Additional rewards:
- Experience: 2,500, 3,000, 3,500, 4,000, 4,500, 5,000, 6,000
- Gold: 750, 2,000
- Tel Var Stones: 100 (each day during the Whitestrake's Mayhem event)

### Weekly Endeavors
- Of the 3 Weekly Endeavors offered, only 1 can be completed per week.
- Each endeavor will give a set amount of Seals and sometimes Experience, Gold. Possible rewards include:
- Main rewards: 215, 220, 225, 230, 235, 240, 245, 250, 300
- Additional rewards:
- Experience: 15,000, 20,000
- Gold: 10,000, 15,000
- Writ Vouchers: 20 (on a week with the Crafter's Celebration event)
- Tel Var Stones: 5,000 (on a week with the Whitestrake's Mayhem event)
- Transmute Crystals: 20 (on a week with the Zeal of Zenithar event)
- Outdated rewards: 160, 175, 180, 200, 400
