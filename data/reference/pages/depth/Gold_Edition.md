# Online:Gold Edition

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Gold_Edition
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Released on September 9, 2016, the Gold Edition was an edition of The Elder Scrolls Online which included a copy of the base game and the following digital content:
- The Imperial City DLC.
- The Orsinium DLC.
- The Thieves Guild DLC.
- The Dark Brotherhood DLC.
- The Palomino Horse mount.

The Gold Edition was also advertised as including 500 complimentary Crowns, although this was given to every account as of release.

The Guilds and Glory DLC bundle was released alongside the Gold Edition on the Crown Store for 5,500. The bundle is sold as an alternative for those with pre-existing accounts to purchase. However, it does not come with the Palomino Horse mount.

As of the announcement of the Summerset Chapter on March 21, 2018, the Gold Edition became unavailable to purchase from the PC/Mac Store and Steamconsoles?, and was replaced with the Elder Scrolls Online Collection, which includes the content from the Gold Edition as well as the Morrowind and Summerset Chapters.
