Bone Flayers are diminutive skeletons which can be found throughout Tamriel.

Three Bone Flayers wearing Imperial-style armor can be found in a cave northeast of the bridge leading north out of Tanglehaven. They are guarding a keg, a heavy sack and a chest. These Bone Flayers are foot soldiers.

## Skills and Abilities

### Feral
;Rending Slash:The enemy hits you with its hands, dealing minor physical damage.
;Slash:The enemy hits you with its hands two times, dealing minor physical damage twice.
;Flurry:A series of four quick swipes in a cone in front of the enemy, each dealing minor physical damage.
;Devastating Leap:The enemy leaps at the target, dealing low physical damage and staggering them. This can be blocked to stagger the feral instead. (On most Bone Flayers)
;Desecrated Ground:On death, the undead rarely desecrate the ground under them as indicated by a red circle. This does continuous minor magic damage and snares all targets as well as healing undead enemies within the red circle.

### Fire Mage
;Fire Fling: A basic ranged attack that does minor flame damage.
;Forking Flames: The Bone Flayer releases fire along the ground in the direction it is facing. This attack does moderate flame damage to all players hit by it. This spell can be interrupted to set the Bone Flayer off balance.
;Ground Fire: The Bone Flayer shoots two fireballs in the air which form two fire runes when they land. The runes do moderate flame damage over time to all enemies within the red circles. Players should be prepared to move as soon as the attack casts as one rune will always be cast on top of you.
;Desecrated Ground:On death, the Bone Flayer rarely desecrate the ground under them as indicated by a red circle. This does minor magic damage over time and snares all targets as well as healing undead enemies within the red circle.

### Foot Soldier
;Quick Strike:A basic melee attack that does minor physical damage.
;Heavy Attack:A heavy melee attack that does moderate physical damage. It can be blocked to set the enemy off balance.
;Throw Dagger:The foot soldier backs away from the target and throws a dagger at the target. This attack snares, deals minor physical damage then very minor bleed damage over time. This attack can be interrupted to set the foot soldier off balance. The dagger can be blocked to prevent the snare and the bleed.
;Ignite:A combo move that is only performed if Archers, Fire Mages or other fire using enemies (Flame Atronachs, Scamps etc.) are around. The foot soldier throws a glob of tar under the target's feet as indicated by a red circle. While this does no damage, standing in it snares. The foot soldier will call for the other enemy to light the tar (usually accompanied by a voice clip) and the red circle will burn, doing low flame damage over time to targets within the red circle.
;Desecrated Ground:On death, the Bone Flayer rarely desecrate the ground under them as indicated by a red circle. This does minor magic damage over time and snares all targets as well as healing undead enemies within the red circle.

### Ravager
;Quick Strike:A basic melee attack that does minor physical damage.
;Uppercut:An Uppercut-like attack that deals moderate physical damage and knocks back. This attack can be blocked to knock the Bone Flayer off balance instead.
;Cleave:The Bone Flayer swings their weapon around like Cleave does, dealing minor physical damage to all targets in front of them. This attack has no red cone indicator.
;Desecrated Ground:On death, the undead rarely desecrate the ground under them as indicated by a red circle. This does minor magic damage over time and snares all targets as well as healing undead enemies within the red circle.

### Mor Naril
The Bone Flayers in Mor Naril use the Feral abilities, except "Flurry" and "Desecrated Ground". The Bone Flayers blessed by Mannimarco during his fight use the same abilities but with an additional one.
;Necrotic Implosion/Necrotic Hellfire:The Bone Flayer rushes at its target then explodes, dealing moderate physical damage. It then leaves an area of coldfire dealing continuous direct minor flame damage.
