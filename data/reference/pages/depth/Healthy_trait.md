# Online:Healthy (trait)

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Healthy_(trait)
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Healthy is a trait which can only be found on jewelry. As of Update 18, this may be crafted using Antimony. Items with the Healthy trait will increase your Maximum Health by an amount dependent on the level and quality of the item.
