# Online:Fallen Leaves of West Weald

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Fallen_Leaves_of_West_Weald
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Fallen Leaves of West Weald is an in-game event that runs from September 26 to October 8, 2024 for Gold Road Chapter owners. It encourages exploration of the West Weald and zone by providing bonus rewards and new quests. Additionally, the chapter and related items were discounted in the Crown Store during the event.

During this event, you can earn Fallen Leaves Coffers by adventuring in the Chapter's zone. Additionally, all Skingrad daily quests and the weekly Lucent Citadel trial quest have their regular reward boxes doubled, harvest node yields are increased, and World, Public Dungeon, Trial, and Delve bosses drop additional rewards.

You can earn 2 by completing Gandrinar's event daily quest, any zone daily quest, or the Lucent Citadel weekly quest. These can be used to buy bound Tree-Sap Legion style pages, Molag Bal Illusion Imp base fragments, and all three fragments for the Anchorborn Welwa mount.

## Seek Lost Knowledge, Together!

:Delve into the history of the Heartland elves and unlock special rewards for all Gold Road players during a new event!

This event encourages players to complete the special event daily quest Fallen Leaves of West Weald. Each completion by players contributes points to a running total [https://www.elderscrollsonline.com/fallenleaves which is updated every day] of the event.

The rewards for progressing the community meter are:
- 33% - Vineyard Voriplasm pet
- 66% - Aether-Traveled Varla Stone memento
- 100% - Russet Brekka mount, Tree-Sap Legion arms pack, and a nesting rewards box

## Explorer's Celebration
After the main event, the Explorer's Celebration mini-event will start on October 8 and grants additional XP, Gold, and resource harvesting for everybody in Tamriel during the event period.

Relating to the above event meter, players will be able to unlock additional days of the subsequent Explorer's Celebration event by completing any quests within the West Weald zone. Additionally, for every 33% of the bonus meter completed, they will enable three days of the Explorer's Celebration. This bonus mini-event kicks off as soon as the Fallen Leaves of West Weald event ends on October 8 and grants additional XP, Gold, and resource harvesting for everybody in Tamriel during the event period.

## Fallen Leaves Coffers

Along with the first West Weald daily quest you complete during each day of the event, you will receive a Sublime Fallen Leaves Coffer. Additional regular Fallen Leaves Coffer can be earned as well, but the sublime coffers have a higher chance of containing rarer rewards.

Coffers can be obtained by doing any of the following in West Weald:
- Completing a daily quest
- Defeating a Delve or World Boss
- Stopping a Mirrormoor Incursion
- Defeating any monster
- Opening treasure chests, safeboxes, Psijic Portals, etc.
- Harvesting resource nodes
- Pickpocketing

Coffers have a chance to reward the following:
- A tradeable style page of the Tree-Sap Legion armor style which was introduced during this event
- Random crafting materials
- Style materials
- Treasures
- Zone set items or Companion gear (only while either Azandar or Sharp are active)
- Gold Road furnishings or furnishing recipes
- Treasure Maps or Survey Reports
- A motif page (Lucent Guardian, West Weald Legion,plain or Shardborn)
