# Online:Outposts

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Outposts
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

There are three outposts in central Cyrodiil, and three others at the edges of each Alliance's standard territory. Each is located in a strategic position between the territories of the three Alliances. While not valuable in and of themselves, they form a vital link in the Transitus Network, and keeping them well defended is key to keeping a supply line to Keeps captured outside of an Alliance's home territory, as well as completing the circle of the six Keeps around the Imperial City, in order to crown an Emperor.

The Transitus Shrines within the outposts were originally damaged, and because of this it was only possible to transit into an outpost from the Network, requiring you to journey to the nearest friendly Keep to use a functional Transitus Shrine. This was changed in Update 12, when outposts became valid respawn locations and were fully integrated into the Network. The outposts connected to the Outer Keeps were added in Update 20.
