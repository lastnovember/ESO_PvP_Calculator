desc=Reduces the time it takes to harvest by 10% per stage.

Master Gatherer reduces the time taken to harvest nodes by up to 50%. This is a must-have if you often gather resources.

## Notes
- Many Customized Actions do not account for this time reduction, so the animation continues even after you've finished gathering the node.
