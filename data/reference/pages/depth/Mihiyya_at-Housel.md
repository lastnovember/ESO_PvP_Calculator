Mihiyya at-Housel was a Redguard who could be found at the then-blocked eastern gate in Craglorn. She was removed from the game after the zone of Upper Craglorn was added in Update 4 and the gate was opened.

## Dialogue
If you speak to her, she'll say:
:"Keep your head down. It is not safe here."
:What's going on?
:"Iron Orcs. We thought they had left the gate unoccupied. We were very, very wrong."
