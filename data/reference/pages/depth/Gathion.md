Gathion is a Wood Elf who could be found at the then-blocked eastern gate in Craglorn. She was removed from the game after the zone of Upper Craglorn was added in Update 4 and the gate was opened.

## Dialogue
When spoken to, she'll say:
:"Get out of here. No telling when they'll come back."
