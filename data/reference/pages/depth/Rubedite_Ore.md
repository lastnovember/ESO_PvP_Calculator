Rubedite Ore is a shiny dark red ore. It can be found in any zone by characters with Rank 10 in the Metalworking passive or CP150 to 160. When mined, a node yields three to four samples of the raw material, Rubedite Ore. Once ten ore samples are collected, they can be refined into seven to ten Rubedite Ingots. Rubedite ingots are used for crafting pieces of heavy Rubedite Armor and weapons at any blacksmithing station.
