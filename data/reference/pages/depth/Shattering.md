# Online:Shattering

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Shattering
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Shattering is a trait which can be found on Companion weapons, armor and jewelry. The trait cannot be researched or crafted, and exists only on found loot. Items with the Shattering trait increase the Penetration of a Companion by an amount dependent on the quality of the item.
