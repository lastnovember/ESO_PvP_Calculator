The Daggerfall Watch were Breton and Orc guardsmen who patrolled the streets of Daggerfall in 2014. They have since been replaced by Daggerfall Guards. They wore heavy Breton-style cuirasses and leather helms.

## Dialogue
The Daggerfall Watch had their own ambient dialogue.
:"Move along, citizen. Move along."

:"If you have anything suspicious to report, talk to Captain Aresin or Lieutenant Dubois."
