# Online:Jackdaw Pirates

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Jackdaw_Pirates
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Jackdaw Pirates are a group of pirates that has invaded and took over Haven, in the absence of the Jade Dragoons. Their leader, Gjarma Raven-Hair, plans to raise the bodies of those who died during their invasion for the purpose of creating an undead army and then destroying Haven.
