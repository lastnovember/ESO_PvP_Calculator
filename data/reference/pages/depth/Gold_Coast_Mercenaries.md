# Online:Gold Coast Mercenaries

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Gold_Coast_Mercenaries
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Gold Coast Mercenaries are a band of mercenaries-for-hire from Colovia who can be found across Tamriel. They have a tendency of focusing on threatening or extorting their employers rather than performing what they were originally hired to do.
