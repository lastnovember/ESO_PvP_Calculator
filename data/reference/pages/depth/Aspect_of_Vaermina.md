An Aspect of Vaermina appearing as a Dremora in robes is summoned by Master Shelreni to stop you from interrupting the ritual in the Shrine of Vaermina.

## Related Quests
- Conclave of Fate

## Skills and Abilities

As an elite rank enemy, the Aspect of Vaermina is immune to crowd control.
;Fright Force: A basic ranged attack dealing low magic damage.
;Pool of Shadow/Grasping Void:The Aspect of Vaermina slams her staff into the ground, creating a pool of dark energy under her and a smaller one under your feet, indicated by circular areas which linger for a time, dealing minor magic damage over time (Pool of Shadow). Stepping in either area gets you grabbed by a shadow hand, stunning you, which then smashes you on the ground, dealing moderate physical damage (Grasping Void).
;Teleport Strike:When standing further away from her, the Aspect of Vaermina teleports and stabs the target, dealing low magic damage and snaring for 2 seconds. Blocking does not negate the snare.
;Shadeblast:The Aspect of Vaermina casts a spell that shoots out three pools of corrupted magic along the ground, indicated by red circles. This can be interrupted to set the Aspect of Vaermina off-balance. (Bugged, see Bugs)
;Nightmare Gaze:The Aspect of Vaermina channels a slim purple beam in a straight line dealing continuous fast direct low magic damage and snaring. This can be interrupted to stagger the Aspect of Vaermina.
;Grasping Nightmares:After getting under 60 % Health, the Aspect of Vaermina appears in a spirit-like form which dramatically reduces her damage taken. Dealing damage to her grants you a stack of "Ghost Light" for 30 seconds, and at 3 stacks a small area appears on the ground which grants you the Ghost Light synergy. Activating this synergy both greatly increases your damage done and causes the Aspect of Vaermina to change back into a physical form for 15 seconds. After that, she regains her spirit form.
