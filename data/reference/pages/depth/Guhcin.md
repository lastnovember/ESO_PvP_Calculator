Guhcin is a Naga hunter of the Dead-Water Tribe who can be found captured at the Blackguard Caravan during Missing in Murkmire.

He can later be found in Dead-Water Village during The Skin Taker, where he recognizes you when you come to ask him questions about the killer.

After completing The Skin Taker, he makes the decision to plan to visit the Bright-Throat Village and learn their ways as he mends the wounds he suffered while held captive.

## Quest-Related Events

### Missing in Murkmire
Guhcin is among the prisoners held in the pen by the Blackguards during the quest. He cannot be spoken to then.

### The Skin Taker

#### Questioning the Witness
When you go to Dead-Water village, you can find him on the village wall tower close to the one where Nisswo Uaxal is. He can then be asked about his encounter with the Skin-Taker.

:I'm searching for the Skin Taker. Sulosya says you chased them off.

:So the killer's a woman? Did you get a good look at her?

#### Bringing Back News
Once you have dealt with the Skin Taker, you can return to the village to tell Dajaleen the news. He will now be in the center of the village along next to her.

What he'll say depends on the identity of the Skin Taker. If it was the young Julitta, he will at first be elated by the news but then have second thoughts when he learns it was a fellow tribe members actions which motivated the Skin Taker.
:"You return. There has been no news of the Skin Taker. What of your hunt?"
:I found the Skin Taker. She won't hurt anyone again.
:"I had faith that you would. What honorless toad did this? Whose kin shall we seek in retaliation?"
:The Skin Taker had no kin left.
:"Then their muddied line will trouble us no more. End a battle with finality and you need only fight it once."
:These killings were in retaliation for Jaxsik executing her mother.
:"We are not accustomed to considering the concerns of others. Something we must look to the Bright-Throats to learn. We will never be so unguarded, but tempering our strategy with restraint may be a boon. The lesson is appreciated."

However, if the Skin Taker is someone you have met before, he will instead say:
:"You return. There has been no new sightings of the Skin Taker since we last spoke. Though this Bright-Throat woman arrived. She was asking for you."

Regardless of his previous dialogue, afterwards, if he is spoken to again, he will state his next plans:
:"I may spend my recovery among the Bright-Throats. Observe them."
