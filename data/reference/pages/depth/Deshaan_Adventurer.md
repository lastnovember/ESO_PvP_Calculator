Deshaan Adventurer is awarded for completing 55 quests in Deshaan. This includes both the Deshaan Main Quest as well as any side-quests. It does not include Crafting Writs, Fighters Guild or Mages Guild quests, or pledges for the Undaunted.plain This achievement will allow you to purchase Quondam Indorilia outside Mournhold for 1,265,000, and you can purchase a Swamp Anemone for your house. It costs 15,000 and can be purchased from Listens-to-Sea in Mournhold.
