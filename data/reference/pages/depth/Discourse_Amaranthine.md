Discourse Amaranthine is obtained by exploring the Vile Manse in Reaper's March. To do so, you must complete the dungeon's objective quest, The Waking Dreamer.

## Effect
Causes a vision of Hermaeus Mora, causing the player to scream as their surroundings become tinted with yellow for the duration.
