Bagul is an Orc bandit and shaman found with Dugan the Red at his camp. She'll be sleeping peacefully when you first arrive, and will awaken during combat with Dugan when he reaches around 75% health.

In the Infinite Archive, Bagul has much lower health and unlike the original encounter, she joins the fight when Dugan reaches 70% health instead of 75%.

## Skills and Abilities
;Coiled Lash: A basic ranged attacks dealing moderate-high magic damage.
;Quick Strike: A basic melee attack dealing moderate physical damage.
;Summon Aura of Protection: Bagul summons an Aura of Protection totem that reduces the damage taken of nearby allies by 25%. The totem can be destroyed in five hits from any source or one heavy attack. Destroying the totem will stun all enemies nearby and it bypasses crowd control immunities, excepted those of rank. (Not in Infinite Archive !)
;Run Away!:When too close to her, Bagul backs up to put some distance between her target and herself.

## Achievements

Dugan's Demolisher

## Bugs
Despite being a boss rank enemy at Dugan's Knoll, Bagul is vulnerable to crowd control.
