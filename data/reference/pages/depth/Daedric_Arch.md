Daedric Arches are small portals to Coldharbour, summoned during combat mostly with the minions of Molag Bal in the Imperial City and at the Unfinished Dolmen. They will periodically summon very weak banekin, except when summoned by Zandadunoz the Reborn in which case it summons exploding Stonefire Scamps instead, both until the arch is destroyed.

Daedric Arches cannot move or attack and are automatically destroyed when their summoner is defeated. Despite having health bars, all Daedric Arches only take one fifth of their Max Health per any hit, except heavy attacks which destroy them in one hit. They are also immune to all status effects.
