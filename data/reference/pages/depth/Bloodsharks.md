# Online:Bloodsharks

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Bloodsharks
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Bloodsharks are a group of mutineer sailors located in Cave of Broken Sails, who have mutinied against Captain Linwen and the crew of the Albatross. Brauti Bloodshark has plans to turn his former sailor partners into skeletons to add to his forces.
