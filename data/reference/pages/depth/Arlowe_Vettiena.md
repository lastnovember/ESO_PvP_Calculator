Arlowe Vettiena was an Imperial spirit. He would have granted you a quest to defeat Secundinus the Despoiler.

## Related Quests
- Shroud of Lost Souls

## Quest-Related Events
You could have accepted the quest straight from Arlowe.

:"I reward any who end this monster's reign of terror, if even for a little while. I killed him in life but cannot end his undeath. Those who grant his victims respite from their tortures should be rewarded."

Returning to him after defeating Secundinus:
:"You destroyed the wraith. Not many can say that. You have done his victims a great service. They are free, for now. Sadly, he will come back. He never truly dies."
:
:"Secundinus … he's mad, you see? Too mad to understand that he should stay dead. He's fueled by hatred. Hatred for the Daedra, for the Legion, for the rats and spiders-and for me. That hate will bubble up again soon. When it does, he'll be back."
:What is your part in this?
:"I suppose I'm just as mad as he is. I struck him down in life. Alas, I cannot do the same in death. So I wander around like a fool, waiting for heroes to give him the business. Then I reward them. It's only simple coin. I hope that's enough."
:Point the way.

Speaking to him after the quest:
:"Not many have faced him and lived to tell of it. He will return. He always does. But I reward those who grant his victims peace, if only for a time."
