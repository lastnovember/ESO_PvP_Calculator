Devastator Findonel is a Wood Elf voidstalker and member of the Order of the Waking Flame who can be found in the Imperial Sewers Cache Annex.

## Related Quests
- The Emperor's Secret

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Strike:A basic melee attack that does minor physical damage.
;Veiled Strike:The Voidstalker cloaks, becoming untargetable by most attacks, and prepares to slash the target. This attack does moderate magic damage and will stun the target briefly. This attack can be blocked to set the Voidstalker off balance.
;Teleport Strike:When standing further away from them, the Voidstalker teleports and stabs the target, dealing moderate magic damage and snaring for a brief moment.
;Roll Dodge: The voidstalker can roll dodge to avoid attacks.
;Whirlwind: The voidstalker spins around, doing moderate physical damage to all targets as indicated by a red circle.
;Passing Through:The voidstalker jumps above the target to get behind them.
;Soul Tether: The voidstalker jumps and casts a rune on the ground. Any targets caught in the AoE attack will take moderate magic damage and be stunned for 4 seconds then minor magic damage over time which heals the voidstalker for the same amount.
;Backstabber:The voidstalker has a passive ability that causes them to deal 20% more damage when attacking the target's back.

## Quest-Related Events
She will be guarding one of the chambers and will cry out when she sees you:
:Devastator Findonel: "Blood for Lord Dagon!"

When she is dead you can pick up the ritual notes on the desk in the corner.

## Bugs
Despite being elite rank, Devastator Findonel is vulnerable to crowd control.
