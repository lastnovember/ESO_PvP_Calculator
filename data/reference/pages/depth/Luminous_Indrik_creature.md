Luminous Indriks are located at the Event Exchanges located outside the main city of each alliance's starting zone. They are found in pens next to the Impresario alongside Nascent Indriks, in place of the Dawnwood Indriks that were there before.

They can no longer be found near the Impresario as of the end of the 2019 Anniversary Jubilee, having been succeeded by Onyx Indriks.

## Notes
- Caanerin and the Golden Indriks look like Luminous Indriks.
- The Shimmering Indrik pet is a smaller equivalent of the Luminous Indrik.
