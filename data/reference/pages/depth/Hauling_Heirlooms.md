Hauling Heirlooms and its preceding achievements are earned by completing daily delve jobs for Varo Hosidias in Lilmoth in Murkmire. While there are only two Delves is Murkmire, there are six quests in total, each of which can only be done once per day, though you will only receive one of them on any given day unless you share quests with other players in a group. The relevant quests are:
- Antique Armor
- Grave Subject Matter
- Old Scrolls
- Sacred Candles
- Tools of Slaughter
- The Weight of Words
