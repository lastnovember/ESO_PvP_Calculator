# Online:Bloodthorn Cult

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Bloodthorn_Cult
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Bloodthorn Cult is a cult of necromancers and bandits led by Angof the Gravesinger. They sought to conquer Glenumbra and turn its citizens into zombies. To further their goals, they have allied themselves with the Red Rook Bandits and the coven of Hag Fen.
