Baron Rize is a Grievous Twilight encountered at the Mirror of Opposition in Lucent Citadel after the Orphic Shattered Shard has been defeated and the Arcane Knot has been picked up.

## Related Quests
- The Knot Awaits

## Skills and Abilities
As an elite rank enemy, Baron Rize is immune to crowd control.
;Slash:Basic melee attack dealing high physical damage.
;Rend:A double strike melee attack dealing moderate physical damage twice.
;Swipe:Baron Rize swipes its target with both hands at the same time, dealing high physical damage.
;Twinstrike Sunder:Baron Rize throws a blue orb at all targets in from of him, each dealing 1 tick of moderate magic damage over time.
;Shear/Gloomy Impact:Baron Rize charges a heavy attack with its wings which deals extreme physical damage and stuns (Shear). This can be blocked to stagger Baron Rize instead. Then in Veteran Mode only, meteors fall on all players but the furthest one after 2 seconds as indicated by ring indicators in the order they will hit, which deal very high magic damage and stun. All players must block this and not stack with each other as you take damage from every meteor (Gloomy Impact).
