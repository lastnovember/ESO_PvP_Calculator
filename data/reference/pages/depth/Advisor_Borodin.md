Advisor Borodin is the Nord advisor to Thane Fjora. You will find him upstairs at the Riverside Inn in Nimalten during the related quest.

## Related Quests
- Concealed Weapons

## Quest-Related Events
After you spoke with Thane Fjora, you will need to speak with him upstairs at the Riverside Inn to find clues about the Worm Cult activities however he doesn't have a high opinion of the thane:
:"Let me guess: you're looking for Worm Cultists, right?"
:Yes, I am.
:"Well, here we are! We're all Worm Cultists. Hah! Everyone in Nimalten worships worms and dances with Daedra."
:You haven't learned anything, then?
:"I've learned that the thane needs to settle down. Having a troubleshooter come all the way out here? Because Fjora had a fight with her housecarl? Shameful. It makes Nimalten look bad. I'm thinking we need a new thane."
:Thanks.

Speaking to him again (if you spoke with him first):
:"Under the table, just there! Did you see that? It was a Worm Cultist!"

Speaking with him again (if you spoke with him after Golun):
:"Did you hear that? I think the Worm Cult are crawling around on the roof!"

Speaking to him after addressing Aera Earth-Turner when she arrives at the inn (If you spoke with Borodin after Golun):
:"The murderer propped Betar up. They wanted us to see him. I'm thinking this was a warning."

He will appear after you enter Nimalten Barrow and teleports you to the bottom of the barrow, which is infested with Worm Cultists.
:Advisor Borodin: "My brethren move to take Nimalten. They need you out of the way."
:Advisor Borodin: "I doubt I could take you in a fair fight. Luckily, I don't have to."

You will hear him as you traverse the barrow.
:Anchorite Borodin: "While I keep you busy here, the thane gets a final recruitment offer. If she refuses, she dies."

You will encounter him at a chamber near the surface. You can kill him, then leave the barrow.

Anchorite Borodin reveals himself as a member of the Worm Cult working as an undercover agent. You will be forced to kill him after he betrays you during the related quest.

## Skills and Abilities
;Entropic Bolt:A basic ranged attack dealing minor magic damage.
;Summon Abomination:Anchorite Borodin summons a flesh atronach, bone colossus or ghost to fight, spawning with Anchorite Borodin's current Health. Anchorite Borodin's Physical Resistance and Spell Resistance are reduced to 0 while a summon is active. The summon will die if Anchorite Borodin is killed.
;Bone Cage:Anchorite Borodin casts a rune on the ground that activates after a while, dealing low magic damage and rooting targets who step in it. This attack can be interrupted to stagger Anchorite Borodin.
;Run Away!:When too close to him, Anchorite Borodin backs up to put some distance between his target and himself.
;Bone Surge:Anchorite Borodin casts a Bone Shield-like ability on himself which reduces his melee damage taken by 20% until this disappears.

## Bugs
Upon entering the barrow, when Borodin walks up to you, he will be using a staff-walking animation despite not holding a staff in-hand at the time when doing so.
