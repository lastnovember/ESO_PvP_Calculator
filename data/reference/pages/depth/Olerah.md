Olerah was a Redguard held captive by the Bloodthorn Cult in the Hag Fen during the Wicked Trade quest, but unlike Favia and Gulorz she was also removed at the Wyrd Enclave at the end of the quest, removing her completely from the game. The removal happened in early 2015, possibly with Update 6.
