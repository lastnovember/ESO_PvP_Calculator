High Priest Eraamine is a High Elf and founder of the Candescent Order, a subset of the Three Queens faith that solely worships Meridia. She can be found near the Broken Light Temple where she searches for a way to restore the temple to its former glory.

After you have assisted her at the Broken Light Temple, you can meet her in the West Plaza of Sunport.

## Related Quests
- Mystery of the Broken Temple
