Breakfall Reduces your fall damage taken by up to 35%. The only other similar bonus is the Wood Elf passive Acrobat.

This champion passive is extremely useful as the damage is percentage-based "Fall Damage" which works like Oblivion Damage, meaning Damage Shields do not protect you, and a 40,000 Health with 35,000 Physical Resistance/Spell Resistance Tank is as much vulnerable to it as a 20,000 Health with 21,000 Physical Resistance/Spell Resistance damage dealer.

Even sections of the game are built around fall damage such as Lucent Citadel's Sunken Ruins.
