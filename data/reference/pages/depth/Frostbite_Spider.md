## Skill and Abilities

### Standard
;Corrosive Bite: A basic melee attack dealing poison damage
;Spit: A basic ranged attack dealing poison damage.
;Bite: The spider takes a fierce bite, dealing moderate damage. This can be blocked to set the spider off-balance.
;Poison Spray: The spider sprays venom at you, indicated by a conical AoE, dealing poison damage.
;Devour: If a nearby spider corpse is present, either big or small, the spider rushes over to it and starts eating it to regain health. This can be interrupted to set the spider off-balance.

### Elite Spiders
;Swat: The spider swipes with its front legs, dealing moderate physical damage.
;Poison Spray: The spider sprays venom at you, indicated by a conical AoE, dealing continuous high poison damage.
;Devour: If a nearby spider corpse is present, either big or small, the spider rushes over to it and starts eating it to regain health. This can be interrupted.

## Bugs
The elite rank Frostbite Spiders are vulnerable to crowd control.
