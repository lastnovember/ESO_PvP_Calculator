# Online:Chillfiend

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Chillfiend
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Chillfiends are a class of hostile humanoid NPCs that attack using a combination of frost and void magic abilities. They are usually equipped with a staff with glowing purple effects on the end.

## Skills and Abilities
;Ice Arrow:A basic ranged attack dealing minor frost damage.
;Summon Ice Barrier: The Chillfiend summons an Ice Barrier that absorbs projectiles fired at it until destroyed. The Chillfiend cannot be pulled if the wall stands between them and the target. The player should move so that there is a clear line of sight between themselves and the Chillfiend.
;Winter's Reach: The Chillfiend releases an icy whirlwind in the direction it is facing, dealing moderate frost damage and rooting all targets hit. The root effect passes through block. This attack can be interrupted to set the Chillfiend off-balance.
;Void: The Chillfiend summons a purple orb from the sky that explodes when it hits the ground, dealing moderate magic damage. This then sends out three magic AoEs across the floor called Void Bursts. This attack can be interrupted to set the Chillfiend off-balance.
;Void Burst: These AoEs are released after Void lands, the smaller AoEs deal moderate magic damage.
;Magelight:The Chillfiend summons a mote of magelight, revealing stealthed and invisible players around themselves for five seconds. Exposed players cannot return to stealth or invisibility for 3 seconds.
;Annulment/Dampen Magic: The Chillfiend summons a damage shield that absorbs a small amount of damage.
;Run Away!:When too close to them, the chillfiend backs up to put some distance between their target and themselves.
