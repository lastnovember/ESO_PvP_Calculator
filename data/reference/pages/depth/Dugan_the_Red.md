Dugan the Red is the Orc leader of a bandit trio in Malabal Tor that aims to ambush and rob travelers by feigning grave injury. Dugan starts the fight alone but once he reaches 75% health, he will shout at his partners Bagul and Fangoz to wake up and come assist him.

A note near Dugan's Knoll by a ransacked cart warns of his savagery. The bandit trio serves as a Group Boss of Dugan's Knoll. The trio is also found as a cycle boss in the Infinite Archive.

At Dugan's Knoll, he has a chance to drop the Face of the Wolf Beast antiquity lead for the Ring of the Wild Hunt. He can also drop the unique item Belt of the Waylaid Traveler in the Salvation set.

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Strike:A basic melee attack dealing moderate physical damage.
;Power Bash:A spinning shield attack against a target dealing high physical damage, stunning and setting off-balance. This can be blocked to set Dugan the Red off-balance instead.
;Block:Dugan the Red briefly blocks with his shield, reducing damage taken greatly. Targets who attempt melee heavy attacks on Dugan the Red while he is blocking will be set off-balance.
;Dark Talons:Dugan the Red roots all targets within range for 4 seconds with Dark Talons dealing moderate flame damage.
;Dragonknight Standard:Dugan the Red calls down a fiery banner that plants down into the ground, with a large circular AoE dealing continuous direct minor flame damage and applying Major Defile.
