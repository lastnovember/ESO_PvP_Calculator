# Online:Ardent Flame

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Ardent_Flame
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Ardent Flame is one of three class skill lines available to Dragonknights. It can be learned by other classes via subclassing. Abilities in this line focus on dealing fire damage.

## Skill Perks
The Ardent Flame skill line is immediately available to all Dragonknights, and is leveled up by earning experience while Ardent Flame skills are slotted. Skills include:
