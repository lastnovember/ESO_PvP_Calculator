Dread Clannfear were powerful Clannfear enemies that could spawn as the final bosses of Dark Anchors across Tamriel. There were last logged the March 18, 2017, and are supposed to have been removed with Patch 2.7.10.

The Devourer will summon Dread Clannfear in the Imperial City's Arena throughout the fight.

## Related Quests
- The Watcher in the Walls

## Skills and Abilities
;Headbutt: A basic melee headbutt that deals minor physical damage.
;Tail Spike: The Dread Clannfear swings its tail around, indicated by a red circle, dealing moderate-high physical damage to and staggering targets. Blocking prevents the stagger.
;Rending Leap: When standing away from the Dread Clannfear, it leaps at its target, dealing low physical damage, knocking down and stunning targets in the landing area. Blocking prevents the crowd control.
;Devour: While stunned, the Dread Clannfear chews on the target, dealing continuous minor physical damage and healing itself. This can be interrupted to stagger the Dread Clannfear. Break Free can be used to interrupt this ability yourself.
