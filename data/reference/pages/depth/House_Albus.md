# Online:House Albus

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:House_Albus
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

House Albus (or the Albus Family) is a noble Colovian family from the West Weald. They are headquartered at a fortress called Exiled Redoubt.

Following the death of their son, the clan has taken a vengeful turn.
