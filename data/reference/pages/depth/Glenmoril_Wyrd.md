# Online:Glenmoril Wyrd

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Glenmoril_Wyrd
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Glenmoril Wyrd is a coven of nature-witches mainly found in northern Bangkorai. They worship Hircine and have knowledge of how to cure both lycanthropy and vampirism. Their willingness to cure lycanthropy puts them at conflict with the Reachmen who revere all aspects of Hircine and view lycanthropy as a blessing.

They also have the knowledge of polymorphism. Members have been known to polymorph into ravens, while permanent polymorphism into hagravens and lamias is rarer and thus these hybrids are venerated by the Wyrd.

They are not to be confused with the Beldama Wyrd, who commune with Jephre, the Forest God of the Bretons.
