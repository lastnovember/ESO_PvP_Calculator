# Online:Montclair Captain

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Montclair_Captain
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Montclair Captain appeared as a label on the corpses of the following NPCs after killing them during the quest Shornhelm Divided. This no longer occurs-the correct names of the NPCs now appear when looting the bodies.

- Orlozag gra-Yarzol, an Orc member of House Montclair
- Juberic Gane, a Breton member of House Montclair
