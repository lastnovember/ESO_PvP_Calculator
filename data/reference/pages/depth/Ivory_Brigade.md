# Online:Ivory Brigade

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Ivory_Brigade
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Ivory Brigade is a militia based in Leyawiin, which serve as its defenders and keepers of the peace, as well as neighboring settlements. Many of its members are veterans of the Three Banners War.
