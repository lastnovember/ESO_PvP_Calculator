# Online:Feral

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Feral
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Feral class (also called Flayers and Reavers) is used by hostile humanoid NPCs that attack using their hands and generally behave in an animalistic manner. They are usually unarmored and can be of a variety of races.

## Skills and Abilities
;Rending Slash:The enemy hits you with its hands, dealing minor physical damage.
;Slash:The enemy hits you with its hands two times, dealing minor physical damage twice, the second hit dealing 75% of the first hit's damage.
;Flurry:A series of four quick swipes in a cone in front of the enemy, each dealing minor physical damage. (On some Ferals)
;Devastating Leap:The enemy leaps at the target, dealing low physical damage and staggering them. This can be blocked to set the feral off balance instead. (On some Ferals)
;Desecrated Ground:On death, the undead rarely desecrate the ground under them as indicated by a red circle. This does continuous minor magic damage and snares all targets as well as healing undead enemies within the red circle. (Only with undead ferals)
