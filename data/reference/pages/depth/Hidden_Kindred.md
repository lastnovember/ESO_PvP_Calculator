# Online:Hidden Kindred

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Hidden_Kindred
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Hidden Kindred are a joint force consisting of followers of two Daedric Princes, Vaermina and Peryite. The loose association of cultists is composed of various branches of cults dedicated to the princes. The highest-ranking Hidden Kindred receive armor bearing the benedictions of Vaermina and Peryite. They seek a relic, a rite, and a dream in Apocrypha, and have found a way to enter Apocrypha undetected. A sect was formed to provide the workers needed to accomplish the restoration of Alavelis Glass Mine, as somehow rebuilding a Vaermina statue is the key to Torvesard's goal.

They are allied with the Dreamcarver Clan.
