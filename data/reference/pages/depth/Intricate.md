# Online:Intricate

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Intricate
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Intricate is a trait which can be found on weapons and armor, and as of Update 18 jewelry as well. The trait cannot be researched or crafted, and exists only on found loot. Items with the Intricate trait will provide increased Inspiration when deconstructed by an amount dependent on the quality of the item, as well as providing more materials.
