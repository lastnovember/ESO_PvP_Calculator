A High Elf Iron Wheel Soldier can be encountered in Abah's Landing in the This One Sells Glyphs store. He will be searching for Quen who recently has had a warrant issued for the charge of murder.

## Related Quests
- Quenneth Lumena's Warrant

## Quest-Related Events
After learning from Captain Sansonne that the warrant out for Quen was changed to murder, she will want to meet you in Abah's Landing so you help her find out who is behind this. When you enter the store she asked you to meet at, you will be stopped by a soldier standing by the stairs:
:Iron Wheel Soldier: "You there! Hold a moment!"

Speak with the soldier and see what he wants:
:"I'm with the Iron Wheel. Our bailiffs are on alert, hunting a murderer-a High Elf named Quenneth Lumena. Armed. Extremely dangerous. She's tall, slender, and favors red. Now tell me, does that description mean anything to you?"
:Why are you asking me?
:"Because you fit the description of her partner. But Lord Damar ut-Amay doesn't care about you. He wants Quenneth Lumena. I suggest you not give him a reason to take more notice of you. So I'll ask again. Have you seen Quenneth Lumena?"
:There's a lot of people in Abah's Landing. I don't remember.
::"Then we're done here. If you learn anything about this Quen, report it to the docks in the Warehouse District immediately. Lord Damar's overseeing the hunt from there and will pay well for information that leads to his brother's murderer."plain
