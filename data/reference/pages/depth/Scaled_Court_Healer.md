The Scaled Court Healer was a healer found praying to Sepilisk in Fearfangs Cavern before being removed with Update 12.

## Skills and Abilities
;Minor Wound:A basic ranged attack dealing minor magic damage
;Focused Healing:The Scaled Court Healer will heal any allies that are hurt, including herself but for half as much. Enemies often indicate this by calling out for healing (I'm hurt!, I need healing!, Healer! etc.). This effect appears as a yellow beam connecting the Scaled Court Healer and her target which breaks if the line of sight between them is broken. The first tick begins 0.5 seconds after the channel begins, and also heals half as much for the first 3 ticks. This can be interrupted to set the Scaled Court Healer off balance. The Scaled Court Healer never targets summons from herself or her allies, for example Ice Barrier.
;Run Away!:When too close to them, the healer backs up to put some distance between their target and themselves.
