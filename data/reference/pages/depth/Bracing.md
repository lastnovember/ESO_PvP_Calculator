Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Bracing

Update 10
Heavy Armor-Bracing
Bracing was a skill in the Heavy Armor skill line which reduced the Stamina cost of blocking by 10% at Rank I and 20% at Rank II, if you were wearing five or more pieces of Heavy Armor. The passive was removed in Update 10 and replaced with Wrath.

## Patch Notes
ESO Patch Note|2.4.5|* Renamed this passive ability to Wrath.
- Redesigned this passive ability so it now increases your Weapon and Spell Damage by 10/20 for 6 seconds after taking damage at Ranks I/II respectively, stacking up to 10 times.
