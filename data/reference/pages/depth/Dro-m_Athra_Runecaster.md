Dro-m'Athra Runecasters are Khajiit time bomb mages who have transformed into Dro-m'Athra. They are summoned as part of a test by Mazza-Mirri in the Temple of the Hidden Moon, and later encountered within the Hidden Moon Crypts.

They also appear as enemies in the Infinite Archive.

## Related Quests
- Path of the Hidden Moon

## Skills and Abilities
;Entropic Flare:A basic ranged attack that does minor magic damage.
;Fire Runes:The enemy shoots two fireballs in the air which form two fire runes when they land. The runes do moderate flame damage over time to all enemies within the red circles and should be avoided. Players should be prepared to move as soon as the attack casts as one rune will always be cast on top of you.
;Void:The enemy summons a purple orb from the sky that explodes when it hits the ground splitting into three projectiles dealing moderate magic damage. This skill then sends out magic AoEs across the floor called Void Bursts. This attack can be interrupted to set the enemy off balance or simply avoided.
;Void Burst:These AoEs are released after Void lands, the smaller AoEs deal minor magic damage.

## Bugs
Unlike most time bomb mage enemies, those in the Temple of the Hidden Moon wield a two-handed greatsword instead of a staff, which results in them running away to fight at range and flick their sword around as if they were still using a staff.
