Debruz is a massive flesh atronach found inside Shada's Tear in Lower Craglorn. Created by Mahvia, it is composed of four Nedic Residents. It must be defeated to advance the quest.

## Related Quests
- The Reason We Fight

## Skills and Abilities
;Claw: A basic melee attack with its mace arm that deals minor physical damage.
;Stomp: A melee attack by stomping the ground with its foot, which deals moderate physical damage.
;Fire Brand:Debruz swings its torch hand, as indicated by a red cone, dealing moderate flame damage.
;Unyielding Mace:Debruz slams its mace fist on its target, dealing high physical damage and stunning them. It also applies a "Bleeding" effect, dealing moderate bleed damage over time, and a snaring effects for 8 seconds both. This attack can be blocked to prevent the stun (but not the bleeding and snare) and to stagger Debruz.
;Focused Fury: Debruz begins roaring and glowing red, as a slowly growing red circular AoE forms beneath its feet, culminating in a devastating hammer punch sending you flying backwards across the room. This attack is always followed by Inferno Stomp.
;Ground Stomp:Always follows Focused Fury but can also be cast alone, Debruz stomps on the floor and causes four very large fire AoEs to appear close together, filling the arena in flames that linger for some time, dealing very high flame damage while standing inside. At the same time, two Nedic Warriors and Skeletal Archer are summoned to assist Debruz.

## Bugs
Despite being a damage over time ability, the AoEs created by Ground Stomp can be blocked and roll-dodged through, when those methods don't normally apply.
