# Online:Event Tickets

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Event_Tickets
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Update 49]]
Event Tickets were the currency used to purchase special items from the Impresario during special events throughout the year. A maximum of 12 tickets could be held at any one time. During the Jester's Festival 2019 event, tickets became purchasable through the Crown Store, at 250 per a ticket. They were introduced during the 2018 Witches Festival alongside the Impresario. With Update 49, Event Tickets were converted into Trade Bars]] at an exchange rate of 1:100.

## Using Event Tickets
Event Tickets can be used to purchase items from three vendors who only appear during an active event.
- The Impresario sells fragments related to the current year's morphing collectible and the current event.
- The Impresario's Assistant, Philius Dormier, sells a selection of items (including fragments for morphing collectibles) related to previous years' events.
- The Indrik Vendor, Nenulaure, sells a selection Indrik pets and mount fragments from the first two years of the Event Ticket system.

Event Tickets can be used to buy:

- Morphing Collectibles
- Various (usually bound) Style Pages
- Pets
- Skins
- Furnishings
- Impresarios's Group Repair Kits
- Companion guild skill line Commendations
