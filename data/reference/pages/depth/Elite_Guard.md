Coldharbour Elite Guard
The Elite Guard are a group of three Dragonknights serving as Empress Regent Clivia Tharn's Dragonguard, although they are seemingly unrelated to the true Dragonguard who await a Dragonborn to serve.

They initially serve as companions in the White-Gold Tower during the quest Planemeld Obverse, but later turn on you after Clivia Tharn steals the Elder Scroll and orders them to kill you. They then serve as the only mini-boss encounter in the dungeon.
