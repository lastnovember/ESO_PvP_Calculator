Berne Ravagers are Imperial vampire berserkers and members of the Berne Clan who can be found at the Daedric ruin of Dushariran.

## Related Quests
- Relics of Dushariran

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Strike: A basic melee attack that does minor physical damage.
;Blood Craze: The enemy slashes at you twice, doing moderate physical damage over time to the player. This attack also heals the enemy over time.
;Agony: The enemy stuns the player for short time. Taking damage from any source will break the stun, although the player may choose to break free from it instead. After the stun ends, this spell also does moderate magical damage over time to the player. This spell can be interrupted to set the enemy off balance. Players should note that blocking will not prevent the stun.
;Roll Dodge: The enemy can perform a roll dodge to avoid incoming damage.
;Drain Essence: The enemy drains your life force, dealing high magic damage and restoring its health every 1 second for 3 seconds.
