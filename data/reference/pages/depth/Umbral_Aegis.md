# Online:Umbral Aegis

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Umbral_Aegis
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Umbral Aegis is a class used by hostile humanoid NPCs that attack using both a one-handed weapon and shield, as well as magical attacks. They usually wear heavy armor. The class was introduced with High Isle.

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Strike:A basic melee attack dealing minor physical damage.
;Low Slash:The enemy slashes their target's shins, dealing low physical damage and snaring them. Blocking will reduce the damage taken but doesn't negate the snare.
;Shadow Shield Throw:The enemy throws their shield through their target. They then teleport to its location to catch it, creating a pool of radiating energy, indicated by a red rectangle, which deals continuous low magic damage and snares.
;Javelin:When standing further away, the enemy throws a magical javelin at their target, dealing low physical damage and staggering them. This can be interrupted to set the enemy off-balance.
