# Online:Guard Dialogue/Dremora

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Guard_Dialogue/Dremora
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

When they catch a criminal, Dremora guards will have various things to say. Depending on the choice you make, you will also overhear them comment on said choice:

### Female Dremora
If alerted, they'll call for you to stop where you are.
:"Stop! Flight is futile!"

:"What is this foolishness? Stop!"

They'll accost you when they catch up to you.
:"Little nixad, you have supped carelessly. The only way to avoid the clannfear's claws is to pay your fine. Now."

:"Like the daedrat to the Perthan's web, you have run into me. Make atonement or face your doom."plain

:"Maybe parting with your precious coins will teach your time-shrunken brain to think."

If you pay your bounty, they'll be satisfied and let you go. The result is the same if you ask for clemency.
:[Pay] Here's gold and everything I've stolen. Clear my bounty.
:"Your compliance is noted. Now, begone."

:"A pity. I had hoped for some entertainment. Be on your way."

:[Clemency] I have powerful friends. My bounty has already been covered.
:"All is in order. Be on your way."

:"Bah! You … may go. This time."

If you flee, the guard will utter an ambient line of dialogue before giving chase.
:[Flee in sec] I won't pay the bounty.
:"You shall wish for the torments of the pit before you die!"

:"You choose death? So be it."

### Male Dremora
If alerted, they'll call for you to stop where you are.
:"Halt mortal!"

:"Hold there, skrid!"

:"Freeze, scum!"

:"Don't take another step!"

They'll accost you when they catch up to you.
:"Just the scrum I was looking for. Pay your bounty then scramble back to the shadows with the rest of the scamps."

:"You think yourself cleverer than the Daedra? Pay up. Then contemplate the depth of your naivete."

:"That's as far as you go, mortal. Pay up or be flayed alive."

:"You made me run, Nirnling. Now, I'll make you pay."

:"Bold move breaking the law of Oblivion. But every transgression has its cost."

:"There is nowhere you can run that is beyond our sight. Make your atonement offering. Now."

:"Another Daedrat…just like all the others. Pay your bounty, mortal, then get out of my sight."

:"The laws of Mundus are mere paper. Our laws are flame. Now, pay in coin or you'll pay in blood."

:"Going somewhere, mortal? Here in Oblivion, it's recompense or death. Choose wisely."

:"You will find no soft hearts here, skrid. Pay the bounty or die."

If you pay your bounty, they'll be satisfied and let you go. The result is the same if you ask for clemency.
:[Pay] Here's gold and everything I've stolen. Clear my bounty.
:"Don't make me do this again."

:[Clemency] I have powerful friends. My bounty has already been covered.
:"Know there will not be a next time."

:"You will never escape Oblivion!"

:"It's a blood price, then!"

:"A runner? Hahaha…Excellent."

:"Good choice, Nirnborn."

:"You'll pay for this defiance!"
