# Online:Training

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Training
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Training is a trait which can be found on weapons and armor. Once you have researched the trait on a given item type, you can craft Training items by using Carnelian for weapons or Emerald for armor. Items with the Training trait will increase your Experience from kills by an amount dependent on the quality of the item. Two handed weapons provide twice the bonus of one handed, while armors give the most values. Note that while Shields are counted as armor for crafting they use the one handed weapon values.
