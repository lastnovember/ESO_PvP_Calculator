### The Atronach
Under the Mage's influence, the Atronach boosts your Magicka and trains your Skill-at-Arms.

colspan=2 | Champion Abilities | Effect

Master-at-Arms | Increases the damage of your direct damage attacks by [0-25]%.

Physical Weapon Expert | Increases the damage of your Light and Heavy Attacks with all bows, daggers, swords, axes, and maces by [0-35]%.

Shattering Blows | Increases your damage against targets with a damage shield by [0-25]%.

Staff Expert | Increases the damage of your Light and Heavy Attacks with Destruction and Restoration Staves by [0-35]%.

colspan=2 | Passive Unlocks | Rank | Effect

Retaliation | 10 | Increases the damage of your next Light Attack by 30% after blocking a Heavy Attack.

Riposte | 30 | While blocking, grants a 15% chance to return [x] damage to the attacker when you block a melee attack. This can only happen once every 5 seconds.

Butcher | 75 | Light and Heavy attacks deal 5% more damage to enemies below 25% Health.

Tactician | 120 | Sets enemies off balance when you successfully use Roll Dodge to avoid their attack.
