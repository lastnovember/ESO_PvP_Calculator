# Online:Caterwaul Crew

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Caterwaul_Crew
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Caterwaul Crew consists of daedra, vampires and unfortunate soul-shriven who serve aboard the Caterwaul and its captain, Velehk Sain. After the Caterwaul arrived in Nirn while chasing the Bonnie Spriggan, various crewmembers can be found in the Seaside Caves near Caterwaul Cove.
