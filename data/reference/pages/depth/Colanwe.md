Colanwe is an angry High Elf spirit found at the ruins of Tanzelwil. She has been terrorizing Priestess Aranwen, preventing her from performing certain rituals.

## Related Quests
- In the Name of the Queen

## Skills and Abilities
;Ice Arrow:A basic ranged attack that does minor frost damage.
;Ice Barrier: Colanwe summons an Ice Barrier that absorbs projectiles fired at it until destroyed. Colanwe cannot be pulled if the wall stands between them and the target. The player should move so that there is a clear line of sight between themselves and Colanwe.
;Winter's Reach:Colanwe unleashes an icy whirlwind in the direction she is facing. This whirlwind does moderate frost damage and roots all players that it hits. The root effect passes through block. This can be interrupted to set Colanwe off balance.
;Run Away!:When too close to them, Colanwe backs up to put some distance between their target and herself.

## Bugs
Despite being an elite rank enemy, Colanwe is vulnerable to crowd control.
