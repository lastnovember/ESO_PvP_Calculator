Flax is a small plant with blue flowers. It is shorter than jute and can be harder to spot among lush vegetation. It can be found in any zone by characters with Rank 2 in the Tailoring passive or character level 16 to 24. When harvested, a plant yields three to four samples of Raw Flax, sometimes accompanied by crawlers or worms. Ten samples of raw flax can then be refined into seven to ten pieces of the processed material, Flax, which is used for crafting pieces of light linen armor at any clothing station. For you to be able to create linen armor your Tailoring ability has to be rank 2.
