# Online:Blood-Feathers

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Blood-Feathers
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Blood-Feathers are a group of gladiatorial fighters participating in the events at the Dragonstar Arena in Craglorn. Under the boastful and arrogant demeanor of their leader Nahassar, they appear to be more bark than bite, as a much more playful note can be found in their camp. They appear to be in a rivalry with the Diamond's Daughters, another team present at the arena.
