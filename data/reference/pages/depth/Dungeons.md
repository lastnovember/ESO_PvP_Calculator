# Online:Dungeons

Dungeons are locations with a high concentration of enemies and often at least one named boss. Defeating the named boss(es) is usually required for the dungeon to be considered "cleared", or to complete an associated quest.

Killing enemies and healing damage in locations that are classified as "dungeons" contributes to progress for a group of Dungeon Achievements.

The following place types are all considered to be types of dungeons:
- Delves, dungeons where you may meet other players not in your group
- Public Dungeons, more difficult than delves, designed for group play but still public
- Group Delves, dungeons where you will only see other players in your group
- Group Dungeons, more difficult than Group Delves, where you will only see other players in your group
- Trials, elite dungeons designed for large groups
- Arenas, dungeons with round-based combat challenges

The interiors of most Points of Interest and Unmarked Locations, as well as some Strongholds, are also considered dungeons in the technical sense.

A full list of Dungeons can be found in the associated category.
