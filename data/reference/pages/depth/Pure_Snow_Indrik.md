Pure Snow Indriks are an evolved Indrik located near the Impresario at the Event Exchanges in Auridon, Glenumbra and Stonefalls. They have a white-grey coloration, with feathers that are edged with gold. Around their antlers is a pale gold light.

As of the end of the 2019 New Life Festival, they can no longer be found at the event exchange.

## Notes
- The Frost-Light Indrik pet is the smaller equivalent of the Pure Snow Indrik.
