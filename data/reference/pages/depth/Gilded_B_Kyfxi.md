Gilded B'Kyfxi is a powerful Sea Sload who can be found within the Timeless Wallow located in the Parch. This location is only accessible when the Night Market is active, and you must have access to the Windglass relic. B'Kyfxi is one of the mysterious Gilded enemies of the Night Market's districts.

From within the Wallow, she has been performing various experiments involving temporal magic. Her research journal can be found on the path leading down to her.

## Skills and Abilities
General Abilities:
;Mind Blast: B'Kyfxi light attack, It will hit all players in front of the enemy regardless if taunted. (Once Dark Transformation is cast it will also apply Leeching Shadows a damage over time effect)

;Shadow Beads: During the fight, Gilded B'Kyfxi will summon three Malicious Spheres in the arena. They will float toward the players slowly, If they are not killed in time they will rush at the players. If a player collides with a sphere it will detonate dealing damage with sinister beads and stunning players hit.

;Timeless Tendrils - Sweep: Throughout the fight, Tendrils will appear out of the ground near characters that after a short windup sweep a small area around it dealing damage to all players in the AoE

;Repositioning Dive / Raise Wyrm: At 85%, 70% and 55%, B'Kyfxi will make an announcement before moving to one of the three sides of the arena. She then casts Raise Wyrm and will be invulnerable for 7.7 seconds while resurrecting one of the dragon skeletons as a Bone Wyrm, along 2 Skeletal Archers and 5 Melee skeletons (A random mix of Skeletal Berserkers, Ravagers, and Soldiers)

Low Health Abilities:
;Dark Transformation: At 45%, B'Kyfxi transforms into a shadowy version of herself, gaining the new abilities below and taking % reduced damage

;Immutable Strike: B'Kyfxi's Heavy Attack, Fires an un-dodgable high damage projectile at the player with taunt

;Grave Darkness: AOE circles will appear beneath the 2 closest characters without taunt, Magical beams will blast the ground and follow the characters around for a time.

;Sapping Burst / Sapping Mark: Sets all players health to 50%, and applies Sapping Mark.
;Sapping Mark: A 50% healing debuff that after a 2 second delay, begins dealing 2.5% max health damage every 1.5 seconds, increasing by 2.5% every tick Ex: (2.5%,5%,7.5%,10%)
To remove this debuff your character must be healed to full health.
