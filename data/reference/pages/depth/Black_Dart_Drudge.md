Black Dart Drudges are members of the Black Darts found mining boulders in Zainsipilu. They wield pickaxes as their only weapon in combat.

## Skills and Abilities
;Hack:The Black Dart Drudge does an overhead attack with their weapon, dealing minor physical damage.
;Chop:The Black Dart Drudge hits on the side with their weapon, dealing minor physical damage.
;Reverse Slash:The Black Dart Drudge charges an Uppercut-like attack, dealing low physical damage. This can be blocked to set the Black Dart Drudge off balance. The worker deals more damage if they are under 50% Health and less damage if they are over.

## Bugs
The animation for "Chop" and "Reverse Slash" are broken due to them being two-handed skills while the Black Dart Drudges only wield a single one handed weapon.
