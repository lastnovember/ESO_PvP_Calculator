Birakh-do is a vampire Khajiit knight and leader with Ariane of the gang found inside Portdun Watch.

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Strike:A basic melee attack dealing minor physical damage.
;Fiery Breath:Birakh-do breathes fire as indicated by a red cone, dealing moderate flame damage.
;Lava Whip:Birakh-do winds up a Lava whip dealing moderate flame damage to the target. This attack can be blocked to set Birakh-do off balance.
;Dark Talons:Birakh-do calls forth talons from the ground, dealing minor flame damage and rooting anyone hit for 4 seconds.

## Bugs
Despite being an elite rank enemy, Birakh-do is vulnerable to crowd control.
