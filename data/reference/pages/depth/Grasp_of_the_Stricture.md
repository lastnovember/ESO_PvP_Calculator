# Online:Grasp of the Stricture

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Grasp_of_the_Stricture
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Grasp of the Stricture (or simply the Grasp) is a group of Daedra that serves as arbiters for the inhabitants of Fargrave. They enforce the Stricture, an ancient pact that allows Fargrave to serve as a neutral ground for all Daedra. However, since the Stricture only applies to Daedra and not mortals, the Grasp largely ignores the mortal residents of the city.
