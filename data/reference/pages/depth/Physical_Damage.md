Physical Damage (script)
Physical Damage is the most common type of Martial Damage. Most enemies in the game will deal Physical Damage, particularly if they fight with melee attacks. It is mainly sourced from weapons skill lines, but most classes have a few physical damage skills. It can cause the Sundered status effect.

## Sources

### Weapons
- Light and Heavy Attacks with Two Handed, Dual Wield, Bow, and One Hand and Shield.
- All weapons enchanted with a Glyph of Absorb Stamina (Deni)
- Bashing With any weapon (Including Staves)

### Sets
The following sets either cause or synergize with Physical Damage:

Aerie's Cry
Any
Dealing damage with a Light Attack applies Eagle's Mark to your target for 12 seconds. Eagle's Mark causes an eagle to attack your target every 3 seconds after a 3 second delay, dealing 856 Physical damage per attack. Applying Eagle's Mark grants you Aerie's Call for 12 seconds, increasing your damage done with Animal Companion abilities by 10% against enemies with Eagle's Mark. This effect can occur every 3 seconds but cannot be applied to enemies who have Eagle's Mark. You can have one Eagle's Mark active at a time.

Anthelmir's Construct
Any

Touching the axe reduces the cooldown of this set by 5 seconds.

### Champion Points
- Mighty in the Extended Might subconstellation of Warfare grants additional Physical Damage.

## Defense
There are several ways of protecting yourself against Physical Damage.
- A Glyph of Decrease Physical Harm Taderi, can be applied to jewelry.
- Hardy in the Staving Death subconstellation of Warfare reduces Physical Damage taken.
