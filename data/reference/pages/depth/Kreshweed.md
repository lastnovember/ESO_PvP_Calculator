Kreshweed is a bushy green plant with dark pink blooms. It can be found in any zone by characters with Rank 6 in the Tailoring passive or CP10 to 30. When harvested, a plant yields three to four samples of Raw Kreshweed, sometimes accompanied by crawlers or worms. Once ten samples of raw kreshweed are collected, they can be refined into seven to ten pieces of the processed material, Kresh Fiber, which is used for crafting pieces of light kresh armor at any clothing station. For you to be able to create kresh armor your Tailoring ability has to be rank 6.
