# Online:Morkul Clan

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Morkul_Clan
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Morkul are a clan of Orcs who possess a stronghold in central Wrothgar. Historically, the Morkul were renowned for their blacksmithing skills, but this praise has been on a steady decline throughout the years, ever since they lost access to their ancient forges.

A group of them, co-inhabiting and infighting with the Agluk Clan, has recently occupied Torog's Spite, a cave located near the city of Evermore, from where they intend to exact their revenge on the Bretons for the sacking of Orsinium.

During your time in Wrothgar and after helping them with their troubles, you are officially declared blood-kin to the clan.
