Hollow Armor Duelists are soulrazer knights who can be found alongside Scribes of Mora in Scrivener's Hall. They are also encountered in The Parch.

## Skills and Abilities
As an elite enemy, Hollow Armor Duelist is immune to crowd control.
;Quick Strike:A basic melee attack that deals low physical damage.
;Dual Strike:A charged heavy attack that deals moderate physical damage and staggers the affected target. This can be blocked to stagger the Hollow Armor Duelist.
;Cleave:The Hollow Armor Duelist performs a cross swipe with both of its blades and shoots out a wave of energy, indicated by a red cone, dealing moderate magic damage and snaring affected targets.
;Blade Storm: The Hollow Armor Duelist swings its blades around itself, indicated by a red circle around them, dealing continuous low physical damage and snaring affected targets. The Hollow Armor Duelist can move around while performing this ability.
;Blade Fall: Magical copied blades are launched at targets standing far away from the knight during "Blade Storm", indicated by red circles, dealing low magic damage and snaring them.
