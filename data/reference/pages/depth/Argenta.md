Argenta is a Soul-Shriven Redguardplain that is part of Captain One-Eye crew and can be found in Eastern Solstice near Caterwaul Cove. After she joined the crew, Argenta became their cook.

## Related Quests
- Blood on the Water

## Quest-Related Events
Argenta can be found with Captain One-Eye and Khag at their small camp. Talking with her before Captain One-Eye:
:"Too bright! This place is too bright."

Talking with Captain One-Eye, she will explain how they recently escaped Coldharbour but Velehk Sain's ship chased after them. She asks you to enter the nearby Seaside Caves to reach the Caterwaul and place an explosive rune onboard it. She asks you to be stealthy, otherwise the Caterwaul Watchlings will warn Sain and he likely will kill his prisoners.

Speaking to Argenta before leaving:
:"I felt my leg bones snap in the fall. No pain. But no walking yet, either. Captain says she'll fix me. So you have to sneak for her."

Going through the Sea Caves, you can reach the Caterwaul where Velehk Sain can be found talking with his quartermaster and killing one of the prisoners on a whim. Once you place the explosive rune, you will be hailed by Suraj who has some tasks for you. You can complete either or both of them, and then meet the remaining crew at the Bonnie Spriggan, where Argenta can be met once more:
:"You! Again. Good. The captain seeks you."

When you report what you have done to the captain, she will want you to talk to Argenta and Suraj about additional things that can be done to get the Bonnie Spriggan in working order. Speak with Argenta to see what her idea is:
:"You're here about the ones I found, aren't you? Sailors, no. Prisoners, no-both. Ready to break their shackles. We could help! Help them break free and take on more hands for the Spriggan, yes?"
:It sounds like you're talking about some kind of mutiny.
:"Yes! No-not on Captain One-Eye-never, never. Un-shriven, un-Daedra among Captain Sain's crew. So near mutiny, but something held them back. Made myself known to try to help. They chased me back to the shadows. Someone else can help. Maybe you?"
:Maybe. Where did you find these crew members?
:"In the caves there. Old smuggling tunnels, they must have been. Go down, through, and across a wooden bridge. But beware the Watchlings. If they see you, it will be all the harder to get the mutineers to safety."
:Thanks. I'll see what I can do.

After learning about what Argenta wants you to do, you can ask her questions:
width=50%| Have not spoken to Suraj:
width=50%| Already spoken to Suraj:

:"Did you speak to Suraj? Captain wants you to speak to Suraj. He is small and easy to miss. But he is right there."

:"So many interesting things here. I think I can eat that creature over there."
:Why did you say it would be more difficult to save the mutineers if the Watchlings see me?
:"Eyes everywhere, yes? Always watching, always reporting. But there is one eye bigger than all the rest. Lit with the cold, burning gaze of Coldharbour. It grows all the more vigilant if the Watchlings catch a single glimpse of intruders."
:And that would somehow make it worse for the mutineers?
:"The mutineers, yes. And you as well, with your heart set on helping. Stay unseen. Silence the Watchlings. And pluck out that great eye before its blue gaze falls on you."

Back in the Sea Caves, you can collect the weapon parts and attempt to free the mutineers by destroying the Observer. Once you return to the ship, it will be getting ready to sail, but Captain One-Eye will want to speak with you. Talking to Argenta first:
:"The dirt tastes strange and the air bites my skin. But captain says this plane is worth protecting. I believe her."

After telling Captain One-Eye what you accomplished, she will ask you to give the crew a speech before you set sail. Talking to Argenta before giving the speech:
:"Speak now. Argie will listen."

Once you have finished your speech, you will told to go below deck while the Bonnie Spriggan sails to the naval battleground. During the fight, Argenta can be seen attacking the Caterwaul crew. Once Velehk Sain is dead, the surviving crew of the Bonnie Spriggan will gather in a circle. Talking to Argenta before setting fire to the Caterwaul:
:"Velehk Sain is dead! No more pain! The memories slip away of what he did. I am safe."'

After you set fire to the Caterwaul, Argenta and the others will watch it burn. Speaking with her before go back below decks for the trip back to Solstice:
:"My crew, my family … they live to sail on because of you. I will not forget this."

When you return to Solstice, you can speak with Captain One-Eye to complete the quest. Afterward, you can talk to Argenta:
:"Alcohol hurts the throat and eyes. I prefer to watch the others drink it. Happy times."
:What's next for you, Argenta?
:"Back to my cook-fires. Pirates drink, pirates dance, pirates sleep. And then they wake up hungry. My job to feed them. Many more edible things in this plane, Khag tells me. Perhaps I will use the captain's grapple hook to hunt them."

:"Er-Jaseen. He helped Argie escape the Wailing Prison. I meant for him to escape with me, but he stayed behind to help the others. There were so many others. I don't know if he escaped. I should never have let him stay behind."

::"Thank you. He is my dearest friend. In some ways, you … remind me of him. And like just Er-Jaseen, I hope I will see you again."

## Dialogue
After completing the objective at Caterwaul Cove, Argenta will appear with the rest of the crew at the Sunport Docks near the warehouse:
:"Strange looks from the others here. Captain says she'll cut anyone that hurts me. But I don't want to cause trouble. I will try to stay in the shadows. It is good to see you again."
