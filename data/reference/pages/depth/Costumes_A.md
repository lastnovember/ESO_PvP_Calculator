## Abecean Privateer's Apparel

:The Abecean Sea is fraught with dangers, but your mastery of all things nautical and the feel of your scallop-topped leather boots adds a swagger to your step. Dressed like this, even the fiercest pirates will know better than to challenge you at port.

The Abecean Privateer's Apparel was available as a pre-purchase bonus for the High Isle Chapter. It is now available in the Crown Store for 2,100 as part of the High Isle Collector's Pack.

## Ancestral Homage Formal Gown
Ancestral Homage Formal Gown (male)

:"There's nothing wrong with showing some healthy gray skin while paying homage to the Ancestors," says Voldsea Arvel. "The ancestors won't mind, and your current incarnation deserves to be seen!"

The Ancestral Homage Formal Gown is a Dunmer-style outfit that was sold in the Crown Store for 1,000 from August 3, 2017 to February 12, 2018. It returned to the Crown Store from August 9 to August 16, 2021, and was offered to ESO Plus Members at a discounted 800. It is also available in Hollowjack Crates as an Epic-level reward.
;Dyeing:
:Slot 1: Blue patterned gown
:Slot 2: Front of the dress below the belt
:Slot 3: Black trim
(The green jewels and the white studs lining the trim are not dyeable)
