The Covetous Damned is a diseased flesh atronach found in the back chamber Bahraha's Gloom, surrounded by Necrotic Hoarvors. It serves as an optional boss fight. It will summon additional weaker necrotic hoarvors during combat.

## Skills and Abilities
As an elite rank enemy, The Covetous Damned is immune to crowd control.
;Claw: Fast basic melee attack dealing minor physical damage.
;Stomp/Sweep Shockwave:The Covetous Damned stomps on the ground, dealing moderate physical damage and knocking back in front of it (Stomp). This also sends three shockwaves forward close together but going in slightly different directions, each dealing moderate physical damage (Sweep Shockwave).
;Focused Fury:The Covetous Damned enrages and glows glow red which more than double its damage done for a time. This also summons four Necrotic Hoarvors to fight for it.
;Stumble Forward:Only used when enraged. Fast attack where the Covetous Damned deals moderate-high physical damage and staggers in a cone.

## Bugs

Both "Stomp" and "Sweep Shockwave" have no indicators.
"Stumble Forward" has no animation.
The Covetous Damned constantly tries to cast a "Sweep" ability, to no success.
