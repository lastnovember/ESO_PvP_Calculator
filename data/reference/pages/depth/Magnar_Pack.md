# Online:Magnar Pack

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Magnar_Pack
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Magnar Pack are an infamous pack of werewolves led by Magnar Child-Eater, that were active in High Rock and Wrothgar. They were responsible for wiping out the Order of the Silver Dawn at their headquarters at Argent Mine, which they have then taken as their new lair.
