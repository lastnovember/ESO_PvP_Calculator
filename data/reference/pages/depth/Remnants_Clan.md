# Online:Remnants Clan

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Remnants_Clan
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Remnants Clan (also known as the Remnants) are a Dremora clan that serve Hermaeus Mora as his soldiers; they often take artifacts and research that they deem too powerful and dangerous for others. They came into his service some time in the past, and when they arrived in Apocrypha they found their home-the Cenotaph.

The Remnants also have a curious trait where the minds of their members are linked as a hivemind to some degree, the more Dremora linked to the clan, the stronger they are.
