# Online:Clan Idha-hara

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Clan_Idha-hara
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Clan Idha-hara is a clan of Khajiit found in Sunport around 2E 582. It is said to hold considerable influence and is reputed to be the finest trading house in the city.
