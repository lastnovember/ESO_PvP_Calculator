# Online:Immortal Eight

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Immortal_Eight
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Immortal Eight are a group of immortal Imperial Legion loyalists protecting the Imperial City's Nobles District. The source of their mysterious boon is revealed after assisting them protect their garrison from daedra and Legion Zero forces.
