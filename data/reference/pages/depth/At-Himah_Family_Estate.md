loadtext=At-Himah Estate: Servants of Grand Sermonizer Fithia claim that, late at night, they can hear screams coming from beneath her manor. Of course, these are probably just rumors spread by disgruntled devotees.

The building has been taken over by the Order of the Hour as Grand Sermonizer Fithia's headquarters, with Order soldiers patrolling the grounds and outbuildings. They are hostile to any intruders, however it does not count as trespassing to be on the property. A goat pen is located behind the house with Gaea Numida and a stable to the north with a horse and a Gray Yokudan Charger.

Although the grounds can be visited at any time, the house itself is only accessible during the related quest and has four interior zones: an Entrance Hall, an Interrogation Hall, the Grand Sermonizer's Study, and Escape Tunnels that lead to an Ayleid ruin with an exit to the west of the estate.

## Layout

### Courtyard
The exterior of the estate is guarded by the Order of the Hour.

### Entry Hall

The Primate: Rise to Glory, and note Words of the Grand Sermonizer can be found here.

### Observation Balcony
Words of the Grand Sermonizer can be found in the first room on the right at the bottom of the stairs.

### Interrogation Hall

### Grand Sermonizer's Study

The Grand Sermonizer's Journal can be found on the desk. A trap door in the study leads to an Escape Tunnel through the Estate Caves.

### Estate Caves

The caves are filled with Order of the Hour guards. Grand Sermonizer Fithia can be found in the large central chamber, which is clearly part of an old Ayleid ruin. Centipedes, rats and spiders are found throughout the caves. Numerous crates, barrels and sacks can be found around the chamber, containing a variety of provisioning ingredients. An empty soul gem sits on a crate beside the brazier at the back of the room, behind which a set of steps leads to the rear exit out to the Gold Coast countryside.
