# Online:Iron Orcs

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Iron_Orcs
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Iron Orcs are an isolated Orcish civilization native to Craglorn. They are known for their stone worship and their savage brutality, and often make their home in the Nedic ruins that dot the region. They are culturally separate from other Orcs, and possess their own language. They tame trolls and welwas as war beasts, and primarily use iron arms and armor.

For historical information, see the lore article.
