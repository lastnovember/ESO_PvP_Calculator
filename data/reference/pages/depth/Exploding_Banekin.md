Exploding Banekin are banekin that are summoned by conjurers.

## Skills & Abilities
;Entropic Touch: A close range attack which causes low magic damage.
;Exploding Banekin: The banekin explodes, indicated by a red circle killing itself and dealing a burst of minor magic damage.
