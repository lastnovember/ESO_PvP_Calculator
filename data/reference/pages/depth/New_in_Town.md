## Detailed Walkthrough
While wandering around the beach of Vulkhel Guard, you may find a Bosmer named Malareth in one of the small warehouses. She tells you that she's looking for a few people who owe her money. You offer to get them to pay Malareth the debt they owe to her. You need to look for five Con Artists, Freebooters, Gamblers and Outlaws, then convince them to pay off their debt. If you have the proper perks, you can intimidate or persuade them. You can also offer to cover their debts for a mere 20 gold each. Alternatively, each debtor has an option that leads you to fight them. Defeat them and they'll agree to pay up. Once you've gotten each of the debtors to pay up, return to Malareth. She thanks you for your efforts.
