Ithelia is the Daedric Prince of Paths and Mistress of the Untraveled Road. She had been sealed away by the combined efforts of Hermaeus Mora, in alliance with Azura, Boethiah and Mephala, due to her perceived threat on the fabric of reality, and all memory of her was wiped from the Aurbis.

Ithelia left behind her loyal servant, the Dremora Torvesard, as a contingency plan in her absence. Over time, some of her Scions had a recollection that something significant was missing from reality. During the events of Shadow Over Morrowind, Torvesard, in an alliance with Peryite and Vaermina was able to recover his memory of his Prince and began his efforts in finding and freeing her in earnest.

By the time of the quest Prisoner of Fate, Ithelia's prison is found but the cell is empty and its walls broken. Torvesard believed that the act of remembering her allowed her to escape, and that she had gone to places familiar to her. You soon come to learn that one such place is West Weald where she was once worshipped by the Ayleids of the area. When you first encounter her, she will be disorientated and her memories fragmentary.

Eventually, Ithelia's memory was restored, with her then setting out to reclaim all her power. However, with the help of the Vestige and the support of Hermaeus Mora, she saw the destruction her path would result in, even for herself, and accepted being exiled, seemingly with no possibility of return, to a world without magic, thus ending the danger to reality. The connection to the other reality was than severed using Abolisher, before Hermaeus Mora once again erases all memory of Ithelia’s existence from every being, save for the Vestige and himself.
