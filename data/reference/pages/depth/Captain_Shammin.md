Captain Shammin is a Redguard pirate who leads the Ra Gada pirates in Dead Man's Drop. He's in the large southwestern room making notes in his ledger.

He has a chance to drop Dead Man's Ring of Salvation, a unique item in the Salvation set.

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Strike:A basic melee attack dealing minor physical damage.
;Low Slash:A Low Slash attack dealing minor physical damage and snaring the target. This can be blocked to negate the snare.
;Block:Captain Shammin briefly blocks with his shield, reducing damage taken greatly. Targets who attempt melee heavy attacks on Captain Shammin while he is blocking will be set off-balance.
;Dark Talons:Captain Shammin roots all targets within range for 4 seconds with Dark Talons dealing minor flame damage.
;Dragonknight Standard:Captain Shammin calls down a fiery banner that plants down into the ground, with a large circular AoE dealing continuous direct minor flame damage and applying Major Defile.
;Blink Strike:Captain Shammin jumps while glowing yellow and teleports near his target then lands hard on top of them, dealing moderate physical damage, knocking down and stunning. This can be blocked to set Captain Shammin off-balance instead.

## Bugs
Despite being an elite rank enemy, Captain Shammin is vulnerable to crowd control.
