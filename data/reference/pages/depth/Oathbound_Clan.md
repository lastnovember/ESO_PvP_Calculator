# Online:Oathbound Clan

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Oathbound_Clan
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Oathbound are a clan of Orcs. Despite being loyal to King Kurog, they still worship the Daedric Prince Malacath. They are known for being violent and unfriendly to outsiders. They are also open to work with other outside clans to settle their blood oath demands.
