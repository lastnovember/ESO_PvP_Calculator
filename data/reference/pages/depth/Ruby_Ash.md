Ruby Ash logs are veiny and reddish-brown in color. They can be found in any zone by players with Woodworking Rank 10 or CP150 to 160. One log can be harvested for three to four samples of the raw material, Rough Ruby Ash. Once ten samples of rough wood are collected, they can be refined into seven to ten pieces of the refined material, Sanded Ruby Ash. Refined Ruby Ash pieces are used for creating Ruby Ash staves and bows, as well as Ruby Ash shields, at any woodworking station. In order for you to craft Ruby Ash gear your Woodworking ability has to be rank 10.

## Notes

- Sanded Ruby Ash can also be obtained by purchasing them from Imperial City merchants using Tel Var Stones.
