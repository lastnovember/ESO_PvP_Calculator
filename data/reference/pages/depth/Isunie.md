Isunie is a High Elf hired to guard Tuunerol's House in the city of Skywatch.

## Related Quests
- Assisting the Assistant

## Quest-Related Events
During the quest, you had the option of speaking to her. She'd say:
:"Sorry, but Master Tuunerol is currently away. I can't grant you access without his express permission."
:I just need to step inside for a moment.
:"I'm sorry, you'll have to move along."
::[Persuade] I'm not a threat. Master Tuunerol asked me to get something for him.
::"Well, he is very forgetful. All right, go on inside. But be quick about it."
::Master Tuunerol did send me. With this bonus for being so diligent. [20]
::"Ahh. Master Tuunerol is a kind and attentive employer. Go on in. But be quick about it."

Speaking to her again after this, she says:
:"Just go about your business quickly, stranger."

If you speak to her while not on the quest, she simple says:
:"Stranger."
