Grand Dragonknight Slayer and its preceding achievement are awarded for defeating other players of the Dragonknight class in the Alliance War or Battlegrounds. Unlike Emperor Slayer, you do not need to land the killing blow yourself so long as you participate in the battle and deal at least some damage.Does tanking/healing count?
