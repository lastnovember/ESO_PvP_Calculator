Cirwynia is a Maormer pirate that is trying to pay off her debt to her previous dishonest captain. She can be found outside Wankeen's Shop near the Corelanya Manor Wayshrine.

## Related Quests
- A Pirate's Debt

## Quest-Related Events
As you approach her outside the shop, you may hear her complain:
:"Damn that greedy captain! Double damn my empty coin purse!"

Speak with her to see what is going on:
:"You there! Want to make some coin and help a marooned sailor get off this accursed rock?"
:Did your ship sink or something?
:"I wish it was that simple. I'm in debt to a crooked keel of a pirate-Captain Artellas. He's a scoundrel. Violent, ruthless, and the most underhanded Sea Elf I've ever met. I'd rather be in a rowboat with no oars than spend another day in his crew."
:Can't you just quit?
:"Ha! You wouldn't last long at sea. You don't just quit a Sea Elf crew. On top of that, the captain says I owe him. He means to collect. If I find something valuable to sell, Wankeen the trader will pay handsomely. Help me out and you'll get a cut."

After you have made your choice, you can ask Cirwynia questions about her situation:
:How did you wind up in debt to Captain Artellas in the first place?
:"There's not much to tell. I needed a job and embarked with a bad crew and worse captain. It didn't take long to see how vicious Artellas and his lot were, or how greedy his contract was. It was a barnacle-brained move to sign on without reading it."
:You signed a contract?
:"It's not uncommon on Sea Elf ships. But the terms of this contract are especially bad. It indebted me to the captain immediately, and Artellas falsifies his records so the crew always owes more than we make on any voyage. It's brutally efficient."
:And you think this robbery is the way out?
:"I just need to earn more than I owe without the captain's involvement. He can't claim a cut of the profit if it isn't his job. I can't wait to see the look in his eye when I hand over the payment from Wankeen. Then, it's the next ship out of here."
