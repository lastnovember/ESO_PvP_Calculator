## Related Quests

## Skills and Abilities
;Bite: The Dremnaken attacks with its maw, dealing low physical damage.
;Claw: The Dremnaken attacks with its claw, dealing slightly more physical damage than "Bite".
;Back Kick: If standing behind the Dremnaken, it will kick with its hind legs, indicated by a red cone, dealing moderate physical damage and staggering targets.
;Leap/Roll Dodge: The Dremnaken leaps around its target, though it doesn't deal damage it makes it dodge attacks for a brief moment.
;Pinning Strike: The Dremnaken pounces forward at its target, dealing moderate physical damage and knocking them down. This can be blocked to set the Dremnaken off-balance instead.
;Charge: The Dremnaken charges forwards as indicated by a red rectangle, dealing moderate physical and knocking down targets hit. This can be blocked to set the Dremnaken off-balance instead.
