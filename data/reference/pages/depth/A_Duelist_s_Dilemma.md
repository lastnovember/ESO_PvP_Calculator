## Detailed Walkthrough
Lirendel's full name is "Lirendel Rumilion 'len Inecil Culanarin Salolinwe 'ata Piryaden-Itelnoril Hilnore Firlamil 'cal Ternerben-Nivulirel"

## Notes
- Fandilol and the book Guide to Altmeri Culture (On Dueling) won't exist in the Golden Gryphon before starting the quest.
- Despite being named as a rubbing of the family name (with the icon showing a sheet of paper), reading it in the quest items still depicts the note with a stone background and related SFX.

## Bugs
During the objective to acquire a fencing foil, while in the designated search area, a second objective marker appears in the HUD compass pointing to an inaccessible area below ground.
- This quest marker is only corrected if you talk to Nahdahni and turn down her offer, she will then reveal you can steal one from inside the nearby manor, at which point the quest marker becomes aligned with the manor door.
While speaking to Nahdahni about the fencing foil, the dialogue options to either pay for it or turn down her offer appear red-coded, despite the fact that you can ask her again and pay if you refused the first time.
