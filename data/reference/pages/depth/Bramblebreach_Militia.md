Bramblebreach Militia are generic Bosmer warriors who can be found at the Lhurgash camp near Bramblebreach after Chieftain Thragdosh is killed, having wiped out the rest of the Orcs outside with help of the guardians.

## Dialogue
After you completed Frighten the Fearsome, they talk about how the guardians have made quick work of the Wood Orcs.
:"The guardians just ripped the rest of the Wood Orcs to shreds. I swear-it's just incredible to watch them work."

:"Can you feel it? With the Wood Orcs gone, it's like the forest is breathing a huge sigh of relief."

:"You know what stinks? Dead Orcs. We're going to have to haul all the bodies off and burn them. Otherwise, we'll never get their stench out of this part of the forest."

:"The guardians were amazing! I know you helped too, of course."

:"So you killed Chieftain Thragdosh? I sure would have liked to watch that."

## Bugs
The male militia members say "lurcher" in place of "guardian" whenever they're mentioned in dialogue.
One dialogue about the guardians possibly attacking anything on two legs doesn't match the written text when it's spoken.
