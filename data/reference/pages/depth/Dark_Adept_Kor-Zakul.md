Dark Adept Kor-Zakul is a coldharbour Harvester first seen talking to a Worm Cult Apprentice on a ledge in the Gate Courtyard of Gristmung Hold. Dark Adept Kor-Zakul is found in the Shackle Room soon after, where you must defeat it in order to destroy the great shackle.

As an elite rank enemy, Dark Adept Kor-Zakul is immune to crowd control.
;Chasten: Dark Adept Kor-Zakul shoots a blue orb at its target as a basic ranged attack, dealing low frost damage.
;Creeping Winter: Dark Adept Kor-Zakul casts a spell that sends out four waves of three swarms of crawling spectral hands along the ground in three different directions in front of itself, indicated by moving red circles, dealing moderate frost damage and staggering its target. The crawling spectral hands that hit a wall return to Dark Adept Kor-Zakul, again dealing moderate frost damage and staggering its target. Blocking doesn't negate the stagger.
;Cloak:At 75% Health Dark Adept Kor-Zakul disappears to the southern wall to summon each of the following weakened enemies: a Bonelord, a Nightblade, a Dremora Scythe Knight, a Feral Soul Shriven, a Flame Atronach and a Thundermaul. It also unlocks "Ethereal Fire". At 40% Health it goes to the northern wall instead and summons each of the following weakened enemies: a Bonelord, a Darkbinder Nightblade, a Feral Soul shriven, a Flame Atronach, a Frost Atronach and a Soul Shriven Behemoth.
;Ethereal Fire:Dark Adept Kor-Zakul casts a coldflame wall in an arc going outwards from itself, dealing moderate magic damage and staggering.
;The Feast: This ability is unlocked once Dark Adept Kor-Zakul crosses the 27% Health thresholdWeird health threshold. It grabs its target and pulls their essence from them, dealing moderate magic damage and briefly stunning them. Four Feast orbs then appear around Dark Adept Kor-Zakul in different directions a distance away and move towards it. If an orb touches Dark Adept Kor-Zakul, it gets healed for 10% health. The orbs can be destroyed before they reach Dark Adept Kor-Zakul, and heal those that destroy them. Break Free can be used during the stun but it won't prevent the orbs from spawning.
