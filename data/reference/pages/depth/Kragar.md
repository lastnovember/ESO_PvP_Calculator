Kragar is a Nord soldier of the Ebonheart Pact found on the main road west of Vernim Woods. He will only appear if you have not accepted Dangerous Union from Bermund, Dajaheel, or Ajorda. Will he show up if you have the quest To Vernim Woods?

## Related Quests
- Dangerous Union

## Quest-Related Events

### Dangerous Union
When you approach him on the road:
:Kragar: "Halt. Go no further."
When you speak to him:
:"You'll want to steer clear. Danger on the road ahead."
:What's wrong, soldier?
:"We came to Vernim expecting to find Reachmen, those stinking beasts. Instead we found the place ripe with the smell of necromancy. The Worm Cult is here, in Skyrim."
:The cult? Here?
:"It gets worse. The Reachmen are being … changed. The Worms are performing rituals all past the treeline. Look, I need to keep my post here. Speak to Dajaheel at our forward camp if you want to pitch in."
:I'll speak to her.
If you speak to him again:
:"Dajaheel's at the main camp, just outside the treeline. She'll get you set."
