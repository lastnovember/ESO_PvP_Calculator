Master Gatherer gives up to 50% chance to get more loot from harvest nodes. It actually makes you gather twice on the same node rather than just doubling the loot you get. This is a must-have if you often gather resources.

## Notes
- Before Update 45, this Champion Star had to be slotted in order to be active.
