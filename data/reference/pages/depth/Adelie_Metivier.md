Adelie Metivier is a Breton leatherworker who can be found at the Armory Merchants marketplace located at the Northern High Rock Gate in Cyrodiil.

## Dialogue
When approached, she sounds confident in her goods.
:"My armor protects and conceals. Neither heavy nor light armor does both!"
