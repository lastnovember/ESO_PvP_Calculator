Sellswords were Khajiit found throughout Vulkhel Guard. They were supposedly added with Update 1 and supposedly removed with Patch 2.0.5.

## Dialogue
Sellswords had generic dialogue upon talking to them:

:"Fair day, stranger."
:"What?"
:"Greetings."
:"Stars brought you here. What do you want?"
