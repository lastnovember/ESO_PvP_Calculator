The Aberration is a pale, malformed Argonian who is encountered shambling towards you and Bright-Throat Village before abruptly dying, it is similar to the dead one encountered in the village earlier. By tracking the sloughed off scales it left you can trace its' origin to Blight Bog Sump.

## Related Quests
- Empty Nest

## Bugs
When you move around it, its body will turn while it's in the mud so its head can face you, like the needle on a compass always being compelled to point north. Its head will also move to follow you.
The Aberration's health bar remains full after it dies.
When it dies, its body will sometimes remain upright.
