Greenshade Adventurer is awarded for completing 40 quests in Greenshade. This includes both the Greenshade Main Quest as well as any side-quests. It does not include Crafting Writs, Fighters Guild or Mages Guild quests, or pledges for the Undaunted.plain This achievement will allow you to purchase a Wood Elf Path Marker for your house. It costs 7,500 and can be purchased from Athrahgor in Marbruk.
