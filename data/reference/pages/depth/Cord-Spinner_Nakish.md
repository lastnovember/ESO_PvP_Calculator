Cord-Spinner Nakish is a Stone-Nest Argonian who can be found sweeping the tiles in Xor-Hist.

## Dialogue
You can talk with Cord-Spinner Nakish:
:"I did not expect an outsider, let alone a mainlander, would come to our aid. Perhaps I will carve a stone in your likeness and add it to my cord, memorializing your arrival in Xor-Hist."

:"Our cords will soon the weight of this attack. A burden we will gladly carry and, in time, learn to live with. Such is the way of our tradition. Our tribe's history, its moments of joy and tragedy, make us stronger."

:"While every loss will be mourned, I look forward to laying their stones in the ground. Time will tell what we build in their honor, but rest assured that their names will be remembered."

After you have completed Roots in Stone, you can ask him about the practices of the Stone-Nest:
:Will you tell me more about Stone-Nest practices?
:"Anything for our heroic mainlander. We Stone-Nest weave cords whose knots and carved beads document important events in our lives. As cord-spinner, I chronicle our village's history and maintain those spun before me."
:Every member of your tribe has a cord?
:"Indeed. You may notice bracelets around our wrists. Some carry these to represent the year, at the end of which it is added to our personal cords. Some families even maintain a great ancestral cord which personal cords are added to."
:Where are they kept?
:"At one time, they were kept in large temples protected by death-dancers. I am told our great histories hung from the ceilings like glittering stalactites. But, after their destruction, many chose to maintain their cords in private."
:What happened?
:"That is a story whose cord is heavy with grief. Perhaps some other time, mainlander. Though I do hope that one day we will rebuild our collective history. Who knows what stories lay hidden between unconnected cords."

## Notes
- Cord-Spinner Nakish wears recolored Marshlord Formal Bugshell Robes.
- He is the author of the book Carrying the Weight of History.
- Nakish is quoted for the Stone-Nest Counterweight furnishing.
