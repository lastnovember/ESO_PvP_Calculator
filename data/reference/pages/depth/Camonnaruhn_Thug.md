The Camonnaruhn Thugs were members of the Camonna Tong found in Camonnaruhn. They fought with cookware and broken bottles. One was found in the southeast caves section and wielded a two handed pickaxe. They were replaced by Camonna Tong Thugs, most likely in the Patch 9.1.6, but are otherwise identical.

## Skills and Abilities

### One-Handed
;Lop:A basic melee attack that does minor physical damage.
;Chop:A basic melee attack that does minor physical damage.
;Puncture:Camonnaruhn Thug thrusts his pickaxe with disciplined precision at a target, dealing minor physical damage and afflicts the target with Major Breach for 15 seconds.
;Low Slash:Camonnaruhn Thug slashes the target's shins, dealing moderate physical damage and snaring. Blocking will reduce the damage taken, but not the snare.

### Two-Handed Pickaxe
;Hack:The Camonnaruhn Thug does an overhead attack with her shovel, dealing minor physical damage.
;Chop:The Camonnaruhn Thug hits on the side with her shovel, dealing minor physical damage.
;Reverse Slash:The Camonnaruhn Thug charges an Uppercut-like attack, dealing low physical damage that is increased when hitting a low-health target. This can be blocked to set the Camonnaruhn Thug off balance.
