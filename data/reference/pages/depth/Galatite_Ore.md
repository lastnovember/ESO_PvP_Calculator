Galatite Ore is an ore of pink color. It can be found in any zone by characters with Rank 7 in the Metalworking passive or CP40 to 60. When mined, a node yields three to four samples of the raw material, Galatite Ore. Once ten ore samples are collected they can be refined into seven to ten Galatite Ingots. Galatite ingots are used for crafting pieces of heavy galatite armor and weapons at any blacksmithing station. In order for you to create galatite gear your Metalworking ability have to be rank 7.
