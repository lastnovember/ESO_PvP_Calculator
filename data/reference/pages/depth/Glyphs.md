# Online:Glyphs

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Glyphs
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

This is a list of the various Glyphs that can be found or created in Elder Scrolls Online which can be used in Enchanting. Glyphs can be used to enchant weapons, armor and jewelry.

The level of a Glyph is determined by the Potency rune used, the quality of a Glyph is determined by the Aspect rune, and the effect the Glyph has on your gear is determined by the Essence rune.
