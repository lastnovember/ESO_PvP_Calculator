Hirylaure is a High Elf farmer who owns the small horse farm to the south of Silsailen in Auridon. She can be encountered during the Flowervine Farm Dynamic Encounter, where you will help fight off bandits and rescue her Horses and property.

## Dynamic Encounter Events
When the encounter starts, Hirylaure will be trapped in the barn with some Frightened Horses by burning debris in front of the exit:
:Hirylaure: "Help! Help! I'm trapped!"
:Hirylaure: "Get me out of here!"

During the first phase of the encounter, you will need to fend off the bandit reinforcements that keep coming at you. After you fight off the bandits, you will need to use buckets of water to clear the way out of the barn. Once this is done, a chest will spawn in the barn, Hirylaure will run out and yells for you to rescue the horses:
:Hirylaure: "Thank the Eight! Quick, help me get the horses out!"

With the second phase, you will need to find and rescue the Scared Horses from the surrounding area and led them to the nearby corral, as well as put out fires. Find a horse and led it to the corral. While there are still horses to be rescued:
:Hirylaure: "The horses are too scared to make it to the corral!"
:Hirylaure: "Please rescue my horses. They must be so scared!"

For the fires, grab the buckets that spawn near fires and throw the water on the fires. It may take several buckets to completely remove fire from an area, and if it isn't completely gone the fire will grow once more. As the fires continue to burn Hirylaure will plead that they be put out:
:Hirylaure: "Put out the fires. I beg you!"
:Hirylaure: "This farm is all I have. Don't let it burn!"

After all the horses are rescued, but there is still blazing fires that need to be put out:
:Hirylaure: "They're safe now. See what you can do about those fires!"

Once you have driven off the bandits and put out all the fires, a chest will spawn and the encounter ends:
:Hirylaure: "Those bandits did some damage, but I can rebuild. Thanks to you."

However, if you fail to put out all the fires in time, the stables will burn down and the encounter is failed:
:Hirylaure: "It's no use. There's nothing to rebuild."plain
