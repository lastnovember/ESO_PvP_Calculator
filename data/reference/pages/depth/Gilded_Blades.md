# Online:Gilded Blades

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Gilded_Blades
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Gilded Blades are a group of Maormer pirates. They are led by Captain Siravaen and are loyal to King Orgnum. They were disbanded by Sealord Nalos and are seeking revenge on him and the Dreadsail Pirates. Their ship is called The Mad Maiden.
