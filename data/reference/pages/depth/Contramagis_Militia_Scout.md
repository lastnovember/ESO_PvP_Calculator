Contramagis Militia Scouts are Redguard members of the Contramagis Militia found in Sanity's Edge.

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Strike:A basic melee attack dealing minor physical damage.
;Low Slash:The Contramagis Militia Scout slashes the target's shins, dealing moderate physical damage and snaring them. Blocking will reduce the damage, but doesn't prevent the snare.
;Basilisk Powder:The Contramagis Militia Scout throws poison in a conical AoE in front of itself. Targets hit by the powder will take minor poison damage, get stunned and then take minor poison damage over time.
;Pin: When standing further away, the Contramagis Militia Scout shoots at you with a glowing crossbow. If the bolt hits, you are dealt moderate physical damage and briefly immobilized.
;Twirling Lunge: When standing further away, the Contramagis Militia Scout holds their dual weapons in a rear-handed stance, with a yellow glow visible around their weapons, then dash forward and strike their target, dealing moderate physical damage and knocking the target back. This is almost always used after Pin lands successfully.
;Roll Dodge:The Contramagis Militia Scout can perform a roll dodge to avoid incoming damage.
;Backstabber:The Contramagis Militia Scout has a passive ability that causes them to deal 20% more damage when attacking the target's back.
;Desecrated Ground:On death, the undead rarely desecrate the ground under them as indicated by a red circle. This does continuous minor magic damage and snares all targets as well as healing undead enemies within the red circle for 3% of the original undead's Max Health per tick. (Bugged, see Bugs)

## Bugs
Contramagis Militia Scouts are incorrectly flagged as undead, and thus can cast "Desecrated Ground" upon death.
