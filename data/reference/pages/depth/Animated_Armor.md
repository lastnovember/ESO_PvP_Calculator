Animated Armor is a piece of armor animated by Norianwe. He is located in Isle of Balfiera. You must defeat him in order to progress through the tutorial. This fight is intended to teach new players how combat works. He wields a greatsword.

## Related Quests
- The Gates of Adamant

## Notes
- The race underneath the armor is an Imperial who has been retextured to look pitch black, and made invisible through a filter in-game.
