# Online:Swashbuckler

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Swashbuckler
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Swashbucklers are a class of hostile humanoid NPCs that attack using agile leaps and their blades. They are equipped in light armor and wield a single sword. The class was introduced with Dragonhold.

## Skills and Abilities
;Stab:Basic melee attack dealing minor physical damage.
;Off-Hand Strike:A physical attack dealing minor physical damage. If it hits in the back, it also staggers and applies a bleeding effect. They always use this attack when they reappear behind from using their salt abilities.
;Throw Dagger:The enemy backs away from the player and throws a dagger at the player. This attack causes snare and bleed if it connects. This attack can be interrupted to set the enemy off balance. The dagger can be blocked to prevent the snare and bleed and reduce the damage done.
;Lunge:When far, the swashbuckler charges the target, dealing moderate physical damage and staggering it.
;Volatile Pool:Also known as Fiery Oil. Ranged AOE attack. Throws potion which causes the targeted area to be set on fire, indicated by a circular AoE, dealing continuous minor flame damage.
;Fire Salt Explosion/Burning Fire Salts:The enemy throws down fire salts, causing an explosion and starting a fire, indicated by a circular AoE, that deals continuous minor flame damage over time when you stand in it. The initial blast does low flame damage. The enemy disappears and cannot be targeted while the ability is active..
;Void Salt Explosion/Burning Void Salts:The enemy throws down void salts, causing an explosion and setting down a gout of lightning, indicated by a circular AoE, that deals continuous damage when you stand in it. The initial blast does low shock damage. The enemy disappears and cannot be targeted while the ability is active.
;Roll-Dodge:When performing a melee heavy attack on the swashbuckler, they can roll to evade incoming damage for a brief period.
;Acrobatics/Somersault:The enemy somersaults backwards to put distance between you and them.
;Twirling Lunge: When standing further away, the enemy holds their weapon in a rear-handed stance, with a yellow glow visible around their weapons, then dash forward and strike their target, dealing moderate physical damage and knocking the target back.

## Bugs
While the swashbuckler is sprinting to perform "Twirling Lunge", blocking spark indicators appear around you, instead of around them, and blocking the attack doesn't set them off-balance.
