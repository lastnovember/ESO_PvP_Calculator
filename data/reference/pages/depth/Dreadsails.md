# Online:Dreadsails

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Dreadsails
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Dreadsails (or Dreadsail pirates) are a group of Maormer pirates. They are led by Fleet Queen Taleria and have begun to plague the waters of High Isle and Amenos under her command. They are based out of Dreadsail Reef, a previously-untouched islet. They have also established footholds in Galen, where they are led by Sealord Nalos.

The Dreadsails have invaded Shipwright's Regret in search of lodestones. They are also located on the northern coast of Amenos and at Shipwreck Shoals.

Most Dreadsails wear either Dreadsails style or Syrabanic Marine style.
