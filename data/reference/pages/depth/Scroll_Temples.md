# Online:Scroll Temples

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Scroll_Temples

Scroll Temples are where each Alliance keeps their treasured Elder Scrolls. Each is guarded by an Artifact Gate.

- Scroll Temple of Mnem

### Daggerfall Covenant
