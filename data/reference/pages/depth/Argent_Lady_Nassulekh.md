Argent Lady Nassulekh is a powerful Spider Daedra who can be found in the southwest corner of the Skittering Precinct in the Night Market, to the west of the southern Rooftop Oasis. As an Argent enemy, the nearby Brazen Dranos Velador and Votary of Velidreth will need to be slain to lure him out. Conversely, killing her will cause the two Brazen bosses to spawn in the instance.

Once she and the Argent Flesh Abomination are killed, they will provide Gilded Keys which can be used to access the Gossamer Crypt dungeon. As an Argent enemy, she is a potential target of the related quest.
