Kishkio is a Khajjit and an enigmatic figure found at the Lakehouse within the Nowhere Vault. She has taken on the responsibility of caretaker of the Wandering Souls that find their way the house. If speak with her, she is willing to offer advice about the vaults.

:"Look at that. An experiment experimenting on Voernet's experiments. How wonderfully ironic. You realize there is more to this than just what you see? The hidden secrets in each room? You are smart. Smarter than these poor souls trapped here with me."plain
:Who are you?
::"Kishkio is the mistress of this house. She finds those that Voernet would experiment on. Those who have wandered from their homes. And she cares for them. Just because they are creations does not mean no one should care for them."
::Creations?
::"Oh come, Kishkio knows you jest. These lost souls. Can you even call them that? Voernet needed something to test his experiments on. These are the unfortunate. Destined to be test subjects forever more. This one thinks that is … very sad."
::And so you take care of them?
::"As much as one can. It is safe here in the Lake House. Perhaps there will come a time when too many fill these rooms, but Kishkio will adapt. She always does. It is not like this one did not share a similar fate such a long time ago."
:What is this place?
::"Kishkio calls it the Lake House. It is beautiful, is it not? A safe haven. At least no experiments of the sage have shown up to endanger us. You are not one of the experiments sent to do that, she hopes? No, of course not."
::So there are no challenges here?
::"Some say Kishkio can be challenging herself, hah! But no, you will find no whirling blades or attempts to end your life here. It is safe. It is peaceful. It is as it should be everywhere in this world."
:Anything you can tell that might help me? (Depending on what secrets have been discovered in the Lunar Path Wing, Kishkio will reply with one of the following.)
::"The room with the bridges made of stars. An island waits that you cannot normally get to. But there is a way. Toward the end. That is all this one can say. You must figure out the rest yourself."

::"Within the room with many sounds, a small friend hides away. Perhaps if you are cautious, it will greet you? If so, this one believes it will lead you to where you need to go."

::"The room of darkness and light contains so many bells. You need something to ring them. Something special that would make them sound and show that which is hidden. Ah, Kishkio misses the sound of the bells. The quiet outside wears thin."
