# Online:Dynamagis

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Dynamagis
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Dynamagis are a group of arcane-supremacists who are found purely in Warlock Vanton's mindscape that was twisted by Ansuul the Tormentor. They are led by Archwizard Twelvane, a recreation of the real Twelvane.
