The Dro-m'Athra Burster is a Dro-m'Athra found in the northern temple structure in Moonmont, where it guards the chest containing an ancient ring.

## Related Quests
- Desecrated Ground

## Skills and Abilities
;Entropic Flare:A basic ranged attack dealing minor magic damage.
;Void:The Dro-m'Athra Burster summons a purple orb from the sky that explodes when it hits the ground, dealing moderate magic damage in an area. This then sends out magic AoEs across the floor called Void Bursts. This can be interrupted to set the Dro-m'Athra Burster off balance.
;Void Burst:These AoEs are released after Void lands, each dealing minor magic damage.
;Run Away!:When too close to him, the Dro-m'Athra Burster backs up to put some distance between his target and himself.

## Bugs
Despite beign an elite rank enemy, the Dro-m'Athra Burster is vulnerable to crowd control.
