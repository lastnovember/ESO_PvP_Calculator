Dremora Kynvals are Dremora found in multiple locations. They are clad in heavy armor and primarily wield a one-handed mace and shield, though they often wield different weaponry and use different classes depending on the location.

## Skills and Abilities

### Knight
;Quick Strike:A basic melee attack dealing minor physical damage.
;Fiery Breath:The enemy breathes fire as indicated by a red cone, dealing moderate flame damage.
;Lava Whip:The enemy winds up a Lava whip dealing moderate flame damage to the target. This attack can be blocked to set the enemy off balance.
;Dark Talons:The enemy calls forth talons from the ground, dealing minor flame damage and rooting anyone hit for 4 seconds.

### Isles of Torment and Moonhenge
The Dremora Kynval knights at the Isles of Torment and Moonhenge also use this ability.
;Summon Dark Anchor:The Dremora Kynval summons a Daedric Arch which in turn periodically summons very weak Banekin to fight for them.

### Ravager
;Quick Strike:A basic melee attack dealing minor physical damage.
;Uppercut:An Uppercut-like attack dealing moderate physical damage, knocking back and stunning. This can be blocked to knock the Dremora Kynval off balance instead.
;Cleave:The Dremora Kynval swing their weapon around like Cleave does, dealing minor physical damage to all targets in front of them. This attack has no red cone indicator.

### Elden Hollow II
The Dremora Kynval ravagers in Elden Hollow II also use this ability.
;Health Potion:The Dremora Kynval drinks a potion, restoring a bit of its Health (18,212 in Veteran). This can be interrupted to set the Dremora Kynval off balance.

### Brute
;Quick Strike: A basic melee attack dealing minor physical damage.
;Low Slash: The Dremora Kynval slashes the target's shins, dealing moderate physical damage and snaring. Blocking will reduce the damage taken, but not the snare.
;Focused Charge: The Dremora Kynval charges forward, dealing moderate physical damage, knocking down and stunning for 1 second any targets in its wake as indicated by the red rectangle. This can be blocked to stun the Dremora Kynval instead. If the Dremora Kynval does not hit anything, it will be stun itself for 2 seconds.

### Thundermaul
;Quick Strike:A basic melee attack dealing minor physical damage.
;Thunder Hammer:The enemy swings and shocks the ground in front of it as indicated by a red cone. This attack does moderate shock damage and can be avoided by moving out of the red cone. Blocking will also reduce damage done.
;Shock Aura:The enemy shocks the ground around itself as indicated by a red circle. This attack does moderate shock damage and can be interrupted to knock the enemy off balance or simply avoided.
;Lightning Form:The Dremora Kynval casts Lightning Form on itself, reducing its damage taken while creating small areas under the target which after a small delay deal shock damage.

### Fire Mage
;Flare:A basic ranged attack that does minor flame damage.
;Heat Wave:The enemy releases fire along the ground in the direction it is facing. This attack does moderate flame damage to all targets hit by it. This spell can be interrupted to set the enemy off balance.
;Fire Runes:The enemy shoots two fireballs in the air which form two fire runes when they land. The runes do moderate flame damage over time to all targets within the red circles. Players should be prepared to move as soon as the attack casts as one rune will always be cast on top of you.

### Imperial CityImperial City
The Dremora Kynvals in the Imperial City also use those abilities.
;Health Potion:The Dremora Kynval drinks a potion, restoring 7,904 Health. This can be interrupted to set the Dremora Kynval off balance.
;Summon Dark Anchor:The Dremora Kynval summons a Daedric Arch which in turn periodically summons very weak Banekin to fight for them.
;Desecrated Ground:On death, the undead rarely desecrate the ground under them as indicated by a red circle. This does continuous minor magic damage and snares all targets as well as healing undead enemies within the red circle for 3% of the original undead's Max Health per tick. (see Bugs)

## Quest-Related Events

### Against All Hope
When you reach the battlements of Ardent Hope, you will see a Kynval and his allies cornering the Bladebearers, urging them to surrender or die:
:Arox the Mutilator: "Let me down, master. I can run from here."
:Lyranth: "As I feared. The Bladebearers falter."
:Dremora Kynval: "These are the mighty Bladebearers?"
:Dremora Kynval: "Kneel, Rynkyus, or Valkynaz Nokvroz will erase your clan from existence."

However, once Arox unlocks his true form, the kynval and his allies will be quickly crushed.

### A Dangerous Dream
When you are trying to free Captain Jerick from a daedric ritual, a kynval will attempt to stop you by calling in his allies to deal with you. The subtitles simply call him a "Dremora":
:Dremora: "You dare meddle with the Supernal Dreamers?"
:Dremora: "Taste the flames of Oblivion."

When he's calling in Banekin and Supernal Dreamer cultists to deal with you:
:Dremora: "Go, my minions! Slash and rend."

Once you dispatch his allies, he will be frustrated and come out to deal with you himself:
:Dremora: "Why won't you die?"
:Dremora: "In Vaermina's name, die."

## Notes
- Dremora Kynvals in the Arena District of the Imperial City actually have a fifth ability called "Cheer" which only activates when they are out of combat and it plays the cheering animation.

## Bugs
The Dremora Kynvals in Imperial City are incorrectly flagged as Undead, and thus can cast "Desecrated Ground" upon death.
