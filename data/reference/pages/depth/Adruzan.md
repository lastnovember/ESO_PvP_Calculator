Adruzan is a Redguard archer found at Darkwater Crossing. He and Durdira attack you by the order of Asmalah the Caller.

## Related Quests
- Our Poor Town

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Shot:A basic ranged attack that does minor physical damage.
;Arrow Spray:The enemy shoots a spray of arrows in the direction it is facing as indicated by a red cone. This attack does moderate physical damage as well as snares all enemies hit. This attack can be interrupted to set the enemy off balance. Blocking also prevents the snare and reduces the damage.
;Volley:The enemy fires continuously at an area as indicated by a red circle. This attack does moderate physical damage over time. This attack can be interrupted to set the enemy off balance or simply avoided.

## Bugs
Adruzan will respawn at his original location even after the quest has been completed.
