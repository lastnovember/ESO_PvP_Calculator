Druid Branagh is a Breton Firesong Druid who can be found in the Draoife Dell where he will act as Archdruid Orlaith's champion.

## Quest-Related Events
When you attend the Draoife, it will quickly become a massacre, as the Firesong and Dreadsails ambush the other circles' druids and kill most of them. Druid Laurel and Sir Stefan Mornard will give chase after Archdruid Orlaith. However, when you catch up, you find Sir Stefan has been captured and Archdruid Orlaith sets Branagh and his Primal Ursauk upon you.

He will shout at you as you engage:
:Druid Branagh: "Prepare to meet death, outsider!"

Druid Branagh may say the following during combat:
:Druid Branagh: "Rend and slay, my pet!"

Once Druid Branagh is slain:
:Druid Branagh: "I fall, but the Firesong burn on …."
:Archdruid Orlaith: "You defeated my champion? No matter."
:Archdruid Orlaith: "Tell Count Mornard to stay out of this or his son's life is forfeit!"
