Barkbite Guards were Orc guards protecting the Barkbite Stronghold.They are now replaced by Dominion Guards.

## Notes
- After Update 6, several of these guards were renamed to specific names around the stronghold. Those guards became: Warrior Ogog‎, Warrior Gruzbura‎, Warrior Thaznog‎, and Warrior Grubathag.
