Nuvondo is a High Elf patron of the Walker's Stay, the inn located in the city of Dune. He was a Perquisitor in the Mages Guild who defected to the Stonefire Cult, and used to play a part in the related quest. You would have had to defeat him and Travath after destroying the totem at the guildhall, during the task to close the portal and before talking to Dagila.

## Related Quests
- The Fires of Dune

## Deprecated Content

### Quest-Related Events
During the quest stage directing you to "Close the Portal" outside, with a now-removed hint listed as "Defeat Nuvondo" existed in the files at least up until Update 10. The files for Update 1 contain far more information on his involvement, all of which was removed sometime before Update 5.

Upon defeating Bayya and destroying the totem, you would go to the courtyard outside the guildhall and close the portal found there. Nuvondo and a storm atronach, Travath, would appear together in a spark of lightning to defend the portal. You would have to defeat them before closing the portal with the Sacred Khajiit Fang.
