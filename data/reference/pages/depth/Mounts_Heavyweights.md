# Online:Mounts/Heavyweights

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Mounts/Heavyweights
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Heavyweight is a deprecated mount category. It included senche-rahts, quasigriffs, dremnakens, ursauks, or welwas. They can roar. Despite being discontinued as a category, the mounts all still share the same animation rig, and often contain vestigial MonstrousSenche headings in their mesh names.

It was later split up into the following mount categories in Update 46:
- Senche-Raht
- Quasigriffs
- Welwas
- Ursauks
- Tharrikers

Two mounts, the Sanguine Gargoyle and Shackled Titan, were moved into the Special category.
