# Online:Restoring Light

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Restoring_Light
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Restoring Light is one of three class skill lines available to Templar characters. It can be learned by other classes via subclassing. Abilities in this line focus on magical defense, support, and healing.

## Skill Perks
The Restoring Light skill line is immediately available to all Templars, and is leveled up by earning experience while Restoring Light skills are slotted. Skills include:

These abilities must be activated manually, for a cost of Ultimate.
