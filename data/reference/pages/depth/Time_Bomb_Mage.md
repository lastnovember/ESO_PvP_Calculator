# Online:Time Bomb Mage

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Time_Bomb_Mage

Time Bomb Mages are a class of hostile humanoid NPCs that attack using ranged abilities. They are usually equipped with a staff with glowing purple effects on the end.

## Skills and Abilities
;Entropic Flare:A basic ranged attack dealing minor magic damage.
;Fire Rune:The Time Bomb mage shoots two fireballs in the air which form two fire runes when they land. The runes deal minor flame damage over time to all targets within the red circles. Players should be prepared to move as soon as the attack casts as one rune will always be cast on top of you. (On most Time Bomb Mages)
;Void:The enemy summons a purple orb from the sky that explodes when it hits the ground, dealing moderate magic damage in an area. This then sends out magic AoEs across the floor called Void Bursts. This can be interrupted to set the enemy off balance.
;Void Burst:These AoEs are released after Void lands, each dealing minor magic damage.
;Run Away!:When too close to them, the time bomb mage backs up to put some distance between their target and themselves.
