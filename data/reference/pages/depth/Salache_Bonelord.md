Salache Bonelords were High Elf bonelords who can be found in Aba-Loria before the delve was changed in Update 3.

## Skills and Abilities
;Entropic Bolt:A basic ranged attack dealing minor magic damage.
;Summon Abomination:The Salache Bonelord summons a flesh atronach, bone colossus or ghost to fight, spawning with the summoner's current Health.
;Bone Cage:The Salache Bonelord casts a rune on the ground that activates after a while, dealing low magic damage and rooting targets who step in it. This can be interrupted to knock the Salache Bonelord off balance. This cannot be blocked.
;Run Away!:When too close to them, the Salache Bonelord backs up to put some distance between their target and themselves.
