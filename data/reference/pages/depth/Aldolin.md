Aldolin is a High Elf cauterizer and former member of the Aldmeri Dominion. He defected to the Ebonheart Pact, resulting in the Dominion putting a bounty on his head. He can be found near the Falinesti Spring Site with Silver-Shade and Thorwyn Ravenwing.

## Related Quests
- Swift Justice

## Skills and Abilities
;Minor Wound:A basic ranged attack dealing low magic damage.
;Focused Healing:Aldolin will heal any allies that are hurt, including himself. Enemies often indicate this by calling out for healing (I'm hurt!, I need healing!, Healer! etc.) This effect appears as a yellow beam connecting Aldolin and his target. This can be interrupted to set Aldolin off balance.
;Heat Wave:Aldolin releases fire along the ground in the direction he is facing, dealing moderate flame damage to all targets hit by it. This can be interrupted to set Aldolin off balance.
;Fire Runes:Aldolin shoots two fireballs in the air which form two fire runes when they land. The runes deal continuous direct minor flame damage to all targets within the red circles. Players should be prepared to move as soon as the attack casts as one rune will always be cast on top of you.
;Rite of Passage:Aldolin channels a spell healing himself and nearby allies for 10% of his Max Health every half second for 4 seconds. It takes half a second to cast, and another half a second to begin healing its target once the effect is applied. This ability is usually used when Aldolin is on low health. This can be interrupted to set Aldolin off balance.

## Bugs
Despite being an elite rank enemy, Aldolin is vulnerable to crowd control.
Sometimes the "Rite of Passage" fails to heal anyone.
