# Online:Koldane Cartel

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Koldane_Cartel
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Koldane Cartel is a mercantile guild in Glenumbra led by Koldane. Many suspect it is a front for large-scale criminal activity, and even with their known legitimate operations, there are many stories of shady activity such as price-fixing. The group rose to prominence during the time of Faolchu's attacks on the surrounding realm.
