Dead Stalkers are skeleton nightblades involved in the fight against the Sable Knight at Y'ffre's Cauldron. One is initially present, and many more will be summoned during the encounter.

## Related Quests
- The Sable Knight (quest)

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Strike:A basic melee attack that does minor physical damage.
;Veiled Strike:Dead Stalker cloaks and prepares to slash the player. This attack does moderate physical damage and will stun the player briefly. This attack can be blocked to set the enemy off balance.
;Teleport Strike:Dead Stalker teleports and stabs the player. This attack does moderate physical damage.
;Roll Dodge:Dead Stalker can roll dodge to avoid attacks.

## Bugs
Despite being champion-rank, they are vulnerable to crowd control.
