Dremora Lurkers are Dremora poison archers found at Jynd's Foundry and in Lucent Citadel.

## Skills and Abilities
The Dremora Lurkers in Lucent Citadel do not use "Arrow Spray", 'Roll Dodge" and "Arrow Stab".
;Poisoned Arrow:A basic ranged attack dealing low physical damage and applying a poison damage effect over 10 seconds to the target.
;Arrow Spray:The Dremora Lurker shoots a spray of poison arrows in the direction it is facing as indicated by a red cone, dealing moderate poison damage as well as snaring all targets hit. This can be interrupted to set the Dremora Lurker off balance. Blocking prevents the snare.
;Roll Dodge:The Dremora Lurker can roll dodge to avoid attacks.
;Arrow Stab:A basic melee attack dealing minor physical damage where the Dremora Lurker grabs their arrow and stabs it into the target rather than firing it.
;Shadow Cloak:If their target is in close proximity to them, the Dremora Lurker will turn themselves invisible and move to a better location.
;Snipe:When at a distance from them, the Poison Archer casts a ranged attack which fires multiple arrows at once at the target, dealing moderate physical damage.
;Desecrated Ground:On death, the undead rarely desecrate the ground under them as indicated by a red circle. This does continuous minor magic damage and snares all targets as well as healing undead enemies within the red circle for 3% of the original undead's Max Health per tick.
;Health Potion:The Dremora Lurker drinks a potion, restoring a bit of its Health (6,880 in Veteran). This can be interrupted to set the Dremora Lurker off balance.

## Bugs
The Dremora Lurkers in Lucent Citadel are incorrectly flagged as Undead, and thus can cast "Desecrated Ground".
