Founder Found and Founder's Foe achievements are awarded for challenging and defeating the Tales of Tribute founders at their own game. The former is awarded for defeating any one of the founders, the latter - for defeating all of them.

The founders and their challenge requirements are as follows:
- The Author - Master rank and all other founders defeated
- Fillia - Veteran rank
- Linyia - Veteran rankwas able to fight her at Adept rank
- Murzaga gra-Ghom - Master rank
- Nhorhim - Veteran rank
In addition to rank requirements, you must have The Tribute Champion achievement to challenge any founder.
