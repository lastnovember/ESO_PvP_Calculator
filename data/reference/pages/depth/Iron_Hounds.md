# Online:Iron Hounds

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Iron_Hounds
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Iron Hounds, sometimes referred to as the "Iron Hound Company", are a ruthless group of mercenaries active in Vvardenfell. They have been hired by Marshal Hlaren to help guard the plantations in the Ascadian Isles region, as well as protect her personal interests in Suran.

Another one of their contracts involves guarding the Vassir-Didanat Mine near Balmora on behalf of Malur Rethan and his brother Benar.
