# Online:Aspect of Alkosh

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Aspect_of_Alkosh
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Aspect of Alkosh is a giant golden dragon seen from afar in the Spilled Sand illusion created by the Mask of Alkosh during The Dragonguard. It is incredibly long and spans most of the realm. Its eyes are always closed, slumbering in the sands.

It is not possible to reach it without bypassing the teleportation effect that occurs after getting too far out.

## Related Quests

- The aspect shares its model with Nahviintaas, with every non-gold texture colored pure gold.
