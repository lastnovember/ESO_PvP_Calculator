It is included in the Fallen Banners DLC alongside Lep Seclusa. The entrance is located north of Skingrad.

Beetles, cockroaches, fleshflies, frogs, rats, spiders and torchbugs are found in numbers within.

## Backstory Description
;Update 45 Patch Notes
The Albus family--once staunch proponents of peace and finding a diplomatic end to the Three Banners War--suddenly turned bloodthirsty. Whatever caused their change of heart sparked a movement that seeks to punish every soldier, the generals that command them, and the heads of the alliances themselves. The Albus family’s machinations started small, but their goal is total destruction.

;Fallen Banners & Update 45 Now Live on PC/Mac
Soldiers of the Alliance War, regardless of their faction, are vanishing, and all trails lead to the Imperial fortress known as the Exiled Redoubt. Team up with the Battlemage Lucilla, infiltrate this mysterious stronghold, and put a stop to the schemes of a once noble Imperial house before its chaos can spread beyond its stone walls and throughout Tamriel.

;Fallen Banners Bundle Details
In the wake of a great tragedy, the nobles of Albus house devoted themselves to bloody revenge. In this dungeon, you must journey deep into an underground West Weald stronghold with Lucilla Caprenia to put an end to the Albuses' crusade.

## Dungeon Buffs
Three group wide buffs can be obtained in Exiled Redoubt by finding and defeating the three hidden side bosses. The buffs are the following:
colspan=2|Buff | Description | Obtained By

Bodyguard's Protection | Guard Captain Paratius saves you from death once every 10 minutes. | Defeating Guard Captain Paratius

Possessed Objects | Blessed by Docent 's teachings, your bash now creates a haunted object. Enemies hit by this object will be damaged, stunned, and knocked down. | Defeating Docent Domitius

Mother's Rage | Eliana Albus's rage lives on in you. Every minute, your damage is increased by 25% for 10 seconds. | Defeating Eliana Albus
