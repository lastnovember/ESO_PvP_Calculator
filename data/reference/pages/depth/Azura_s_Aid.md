# Return to Abbot Durak in Wayrest.

## Detailed Walkthrough
Abbot Durak tells you that you have to travel to the Weeping Giant, gather some glow dust from the wisps in the southern shore and some rare mineral called azurite from the northern hills, find the Shrine to Azura, cleanse the shrine which the cultists defiled and pray for Azura's guidance. A Winged Twilight appears. Talk to her and she will agree to help against Vaermina but saving the king will be more difficult than the others. To complete the quest you have to return to Abbot Durak in Wayrest.

## Notes
- The Azurite deposits won't exist in the hills prior to starting the quest.

## Bugs

The Abbot's dialogue mentions the eastern shore but the quest objective is in the South.
