Welcome to The Elder Scrolls Online PTS v11.0.1! This week, we welcome Zeal of Zenithar back to the PTS rotation. Check out the new rewards and list any feedback you have for this event. Additionally, we have several fixes and adjustments for Seasons of the Worm Cult, Part 1 content, including fixes for various quest lines, Solstice Overland and the trial, Ossein Cage. There are also content fixes for Fallen Banners, Gold Road, and Base Game. We have also made a few combat changes based on feedback from week one. However, we are still reviewing your feedback and will have more adjustments during this PTS cycle.

Housing folks, please take special note of the known issues this week. We have outlined known housing and furnishings impacted by the solid black texture issue. We are working on resolving this.

You can find a full list of patch notes below. Continue to provide your feedback and bugs in the designated feedback threads, listed here.A full list of fixes is available below. As always, feel free to provide feedback on PTS v11.0.1 through our feedback and bug threads, located here.

## Event: Zeal of Zenithar
The Zeal is real! Zeal of Zenithar returns with new seasonal rewards, but no other major structural changes at this time (review the in-game help entry for all details). The new rewards include:
- Zenithar Battlesmith armor set, with pages available from the Impresario for 5 tickets each or from Zenithar's Sublime Parcel reward boxes.
- Last year's Gold Road Dragoon style goes into the "old rewards" pool available in the purple boxes available through other expected sources.
- The third and final fragment, "Torc of Wisdom," for the Robes of Truth and Law costume, is available from the Impresario for 10 tickets.

## Template Information
- Added a few missing Collectibles associated with Event Morphs to the PTS Template containers.
- Perfected Harmony in Chaos container for template characters will now grant the Jerkin in Divines rather than Reinforced.
- The Collectible Furnishing, "Kynareth's Icon of Storm Control" is now represented in the "All Previous Collectible Furnishing Crate."
