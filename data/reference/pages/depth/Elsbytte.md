Elsbytte is a Nord you can find in a random encounter in the wilds of Western Skyrim. She will be looking for her pet mammoth, Muffins. She was also available as a houseguest from the Crown Store.

## Dialogue
As you near her location you may hear her cry out one of the following:
:Elsbytte: "Muffins! Where are you?"
:Elsbytte: "Muffins! Come Back!"
:Elsbytte: "Muffins! I have some delicious greens for you!"
:Elsbytte: "Where could she have wandered off to?"
:Elsbytte: "Muffins! Want a nice sweetroll?"'

When speaking to her, she says:
:"Oh, excuse me! Have you seen a mammoth with big adorable eyes stomping around anywhere? Her name is Muffins, and I'm worried sick about her!"
:You have a pet mammoth?
:"Is that so strange? Yes, I do! I raised her from just a wee little thing. I can't imagine a life without her! Just one look at her sweet face and you'd understand. Oh, why did I think a fence would stop her?"
:I wish you luck.

Sometimes the mammoth will run up to her after you finish talking:
:Elsbytte: "There you are, Muffins!"
:
:Elsbytte: "Oh, my baby! I'm so relieved! Don't ever scare me like that again!"

If you stay in the area, she will keep talking to Muffins from a pool of lines:
:Elsbytte: "Did you find some yummy plants, darling?"
:Elsbytte: "You look a little bloated, Muffins. Are you feeling all right?"
:Elsbytte: "Who's a good little mammoth? You are!"
:Elsbytte: "You had me worried half to death! Don't ever run off like that again, all right?"
:Elsbytte: "We'll have some sweetrolls when we get home."

In the case Muffins doesn't appear, you can talk to Elsbytte once more:
:"Maybe I just need to be patient. My Muffins will come back. She has to!"
:[Persuade] Maybe she's too far away to hear you.
:"Oh, that's a good point! Muffins would never ignore my calls. She must have wandered farther than I thought. Thank you, my friend! I'm going to go look for her!"
:Good luck.

She then runs off.
:Elsbytte: "Don't worry, Muffins! I'm coming to get you!"

:"Years ago, I bought a fake mammoth snout from a vendor in Stormhold. That silly gag gift really came in handy when I acquired Muffins! I'd use it to pet her, or circle it around her as she slept. And yes, more than once I used it to bathe her."

:"Never imagined that caring for Muffins would get me into alchemy, but I'm very allergic to mammoth dander. I make a special tonic every day so I don't break out in hives when I bury my face in Muffins' fur. Tastes foul but I'm working on that."

:"My parents always ask why I can't be more like my sister, Elfbetta. Making a name for herself by coddling some hot-headed adventuring type. Not that I don't do my fair share of coddling. But at least Muffins likes me back!"

:"I often get questions about the name Muffins. It's what she smells like if you really bury your nose in her fur and take a deep whiff. Admittedly, there may be something wrong me. Or with every muffin I've ever had. But the name stuck."

:"Muffins sheds. A lot. But don't worry. I'll pick up every scrap of fur she drops. I've got a collection, actually. Someday I'm going to spin all that woolly fur into yarn and knit it into cloak. Or maybe blanket. I haven't decided yet."

:"My parents give me no end of grief about Muffins. They complain that I love her like a child. As if this is such a bad thing! I always tell them, don't be ridiculous! I could never love a child as much as I love my Muffins!"

:"I've considered adopting another mammoth. They're herd animals, aren't they? I worry Muffins gets lonely in our herd of two. But I'm even more worried about jealousy. What if she likes this new mammoth more than me? I don't think I could stand it."

:"Muffins gets on all right with children. She plays a little rough with my nephews, but they're strong Nord boys. They can take it. They're about her size, which just makes me wonder-does she think they're just blond, tuskless mammoths like her?"

:"Muffins and I spotted a herd of mammoths one day while traveling through the Rift. My little sweetheart was simply transfixed. She seemed so … wistful. But then she trumpeted to them, and they answered her call! Oh, I'd never seen her so happy."

:"Muffins was a different color when she was small, if you can believe it. More grayish, greenish, hmm … it's hard to describe. Have you ever seen a baby pocket mammoth? Exactly. Few have. They blend in with their surroundings incredibly well."

:"Someone once asked if Muffins was a timber mammoth, on account of her size. Timber mammoth! Can you believe it? Some people really don't know their mammoths."

:"I rescued Muffins while sneaking around a Riekr camp during my travels in the far north. She was so tiny! And she made the most feeble little trumpet sound. It was love at first sight. I saved her. But sometimes it feels like she saved me."

:"My favorite thing about Muffins? Hmm, let's see …. It's so hard to choose! But if I had to, I'd say her pointy little ears. They're so soft! Lucky for me that she doesn't mind when I pet them, and she loves it when I scratch behind them."

:"I had the most handsome Nord smitten with me. Strong, a good fighter, hair like fire, son of the Skald-King. One day, he said to me, "Elsbytte, enough! It's either me or the mammoth!" As if there was ever any choice! Anyway, I wish him well."

:"Muffins got into the flour again. I'm not sure what she's thinking! She doesn't seem to eat any of it. Just rolls around and get it all over her-and everything else! I never get too angry with her, though. She's just so adorable!"

:"I don't know if Muffins can swim, but she can fish! Rather, I've taught her to suck up schools of minnows with her snout and snort them into a bucket. I sell the minnows to anglers as bait and use the coins to buy her sweetrolls."

:"My brother makes the best mammoth snout pie. But ever since I rescued Muffins, I haven't had the stomach for it. I was worried I'd offend him, but his whole family stopped eating elk after his boys saved an abandoned calf. We all have our limits."

:"Maybe someday, when Muffins and I have seen enough of the world, I'll open a pocket mammoth sanctuary. They make the most wonderful pets! I wish more people knew that."
