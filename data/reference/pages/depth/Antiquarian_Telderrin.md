Antiquarian Telderrin is a High Elf who can be encountered in his home in Western Solstice during the related quest.

## Related Quests
- A Pirate's Debt

## Quest-Related Events
Cirwynia will ask for your help in stealing one of two objects to clear her debt and contract with Captain Artellas. One of the targets is the Tide-Born Idol Antiquarian Telderrin recently found and is keeping in his home.

While Cirwynia planned the job for when he was not home, when you arrive at the Antiquarian's Hut you find him wandering around his home. You can steal the idol from the table, but if Telderrin sees you he will shout:
:Antiquarian Telderrin: "Who are you? What are you doing in my hut? Help! Thieves!"
