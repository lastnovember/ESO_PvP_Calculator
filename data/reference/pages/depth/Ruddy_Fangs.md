# Online:Ruddy Fangs

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Ruddy_Fangs
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Ruddy Fangs are a Khajiit bandit pride operating in Northern and Southern Elsweyr, led by Zayreba Sharpclaw. They have the biggest presence near the Scar, but are a scourge upon all of Elsweyr.

Their main source of income is smuggling things to and from Elsweyr, with one of their biggest products being Skooma. They also partake in the slave trade. Those within the ranks of the Ruddy Fangs are required to throw out any morals, and the only way to get out of the business is death.

Recently, the Ruddy Fangs began preying on refugees fleeing the rage of dragons unleashed from the Halls of Colossus, prowling the roads for any opportunities to obtain more goods. While the Ruddy Fangs in Northern Elsweyr are all Khajiit, those found in Southern Elsweyr include members of other races as well.
