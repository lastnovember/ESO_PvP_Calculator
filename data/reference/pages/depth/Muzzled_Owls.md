# Online:Muzzled Owls

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Muzzled_Owls
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Muzzled Owls are a group of Nord wild mages tending to Kynesgrove. While normally friendly and offering help and refuge to travelers through the grove, they have recently become hostile for unknown reasons, going as far as enthralling visiting Ebonheart Pact soldiers. The reason for their hostility is revealed during the related quest. Their leader forsake Kyne after she believed she lost her son in the Alliance War.
