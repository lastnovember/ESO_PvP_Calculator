# Online:Berserker

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Berserker
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Berserkers are a class of hostile humanoid NPCs who attack using melee abilities. They are usually equipped with dual axes.

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Strike: A basic melee attack dealing minor physical damage.
;Blood Craze: The berserker slashes the target twice, dealing moderate physical damage twice then very minor bleed damage every two seconds over 8 seconds to the target. The bleeding also heals the berserker for around a quarter of the damage done. Blocking the first hit negates the bleeding, but blocking the second one does not.
;Agony: The berserker channels a spell dealing moderate magic damage and stunning the target for 2 seconds. Taking damage from any source will break the stun, but the target may choose to break free from it instead. After the stun ends, this spell also deals minor magic damage every second over 6 seconds to the target. This can be interrupted to set the berserker off balance instead. Blocking will not prevent the damage over time.
;Roll Dodge:When performing a melee heavy attack on the berserker, they can perform a roll dodge to avoid incoming damage.
;Heated Blades: The berserker rakes their weapons together in a show of sparks, buffing their damage by 25% and applying Minor Maim to the target for 4 seconds. (On some berserkers only)
;Backstabber:The berserker has a passive ability that makes them deal 20% more damage when attacking the target's back. (On some berserkers only)
;Assassinate: The Berserker strikes at the target's back for moderate physical damage. This attack can only be performed if the Berserker is behind their target. Face toward the Berserker to prevent them from using this ability. (Very rare)
