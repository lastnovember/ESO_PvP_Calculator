Mossheart Indriks are located at the Event Exchanges located outside the main city of each Alliance's starting zone, as well as the hub cities of each Chapter zone. They were found in pens next to the Impresario alongside Nascent Indriks until the end of the Lost Treasures of Skyrim event.

## Unique
- Jodoro, a hostile indrik found in Earthen Root Enclave Lost Depths

## Notes
- The Sapling Indrik pet is the smaller equivalent of the Mossheart Indrik.
