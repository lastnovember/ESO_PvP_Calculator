# Online:Order of Diagna (faction)

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Order_of_Diagna_(faction)
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Order of Diagna was an ancient order of Redguard warriors hailing from Hammerfell. They are most well-known for their involvement in the Siege of Orsinium, where they and their renowned leader, Gaiden Shinji, assisted King Joile of Daggerfall's invasion of the Orcish kingdom.
