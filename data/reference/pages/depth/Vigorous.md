# Online:Vigorous

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Vigorous
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Vigorous is a trait which can be found on Companion weapons, armor and jewelry. The trait cannot be researched or crafted, and exists only on found loot. Items with the Vigorous trait increase the Maximum Health of a Companion by an amount dependent on the quality of the item.
