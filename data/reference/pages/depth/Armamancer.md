# Online:Armamancer

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Armamancer
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Armamancer are a class of hostile humanoid NPCs that attack using bound weapons that they summon. Among the weapons used are, a sword, bow and a staff. This class was introduced in Blackwood.

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Strike:Basic melee attack with a bound sword dealing low physical damage.
;Heavy Attack:A charged melee attack dealing moderate physical damage. This can be blocked to set the armamancer off-balance.
;Shattering Staff:The armamancer conjures a bound staff and slams it into the ground, indicated by a red circle, dealing moderate physical damage and knocking down affected targets.
;Stunning Arrow:When standing further away, the armamancer summons a bound bow and aims it at their target before shooting, dealing moderate physical damage and stunning them.

## Bugs
The stun from "Stunning Arrow" lacks the associated animation on affected targets (with them looking dazed and wobbly).
Despite not being equipped with a weapon initially, by the nature of their class, armamancers still perform the unsheathe weapon animation when approaching them.
