Journal=A local dispute over a saltrice farm has now gotten to the point where Sulahkeesh, a former Shadowscale, must arbitrate for a resolution to be found. I volunteered to ask the former assassin for her help.

## Quick Walkthrough
# Speak with Sulahkeesh.
# Speak to both Nurhei and Paheiza and investigate their claims.
# Head to the Saltrice Farm and look for evidence.
# Report to Sulahkeesh.
# Get the Bog Willow oil and Perfumed Snapper oil.
# Give the oils to Nurhei and Paheiza.
# Watch the negotiation.
# Speak with Sulahkeesh to be rewarded.

## Detailed Walkthrough

## Notes
- Sulahkeesh, Nurhei and Paheiza won't exist in Lilmoth before starting the quest.
- The "Letter from Paheiza" won't exist in Nurhei's warehouse before starting the quest or reaching the related objective.
- Keema-Ru's grave-stake won't exist in the city's graveyard before starting the quest or reaching the related quest.

## Bugs
After talking to Sulahkeesh in the inn after having gathered the evidence, Chuxu's ambient dialogue line starts playing in the background while you are still in dialogue with Sulahkeesh, resulting in audio overlap that makes it hard to hear either.
