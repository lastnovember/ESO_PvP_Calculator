# Online:Domination

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Domination
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

ON:Domination (skill)
Domination is a battlegrounds game mode falling under Land Grab. Its objective is to secure capture points to earn 500 points for one's team. These Capture points, which are evenly spread around the map, are captured by players who stand close to them. The game has a 15 minute time limit. If there is a tie, the team with the highest medal score wins.

The game begins with all capture points spread around the map, which can be captured by participants standing close to them. 4v4 maps have two or three capture points, 4v4v4 maps have four capture points and 8v8 maps have five capture points. The number of participants affects the speed of capture. If participants from two or more teams are at a point, the point's affiliation will be eroded only if the number of participants not of the point's affiliation is higher than that of the participants belonging to the team. While capture points are under the control of a team, they will earn 4 points for that team for every 'tick' that elapses.

The medal can be earned multiple times per match.
The Tutorials section of the Help menu doesn't show the "Blessed Assistance" medal, and you can only get one of the two per healing hit, with "Helping Hand" having priority.
You do not need to stand on the said point yourself.
Note: The required numbers to get medals can be a little off, as for example you can still get "Blessed Assistance" for a healing hit of +10,000 instead of "Helping Hand" (tested for up to 10,089).
