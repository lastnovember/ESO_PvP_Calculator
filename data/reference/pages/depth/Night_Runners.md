# Online:Night Runners

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Night_Runners
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Night Runners are group of smugglers and criminals scattered across Summerset, with their main base of operations being in the cove at Karnwasten. Their headquarters were recently disturbed and a large part of their forces killed when the powerful Sea Sload, K'Garza, moved into Karnwasten.

## Notes
- Many of their numbers can be found in-game in Karnwasten, having been either converted to help or forcefully raised from the dead by K'Garza. These include:
- Madja, the only one who is willingly helping the Sea Sload while still alive.
- Holdala Reni, Maelrisse, Nolore Stieve, Sakjhera, Yargonk and all of the Risen enemies encountered within the dungeon.
