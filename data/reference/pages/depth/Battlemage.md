# Online:Battlemage

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Battlemage
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Battlemages are a class of hostile humanoid NPCs that attack using both melee and ranged abilities. They are equipped with a staff that they infuse with various elements.

## Skills and Abilities
;Flare:A basic ranged attack dealing minor flame damage.
;Quick Strike:A basic melee attack dealing minor physical damage.
;Ice Cage:The battlemage summons a ring of frost around itself dealing continuous direct moderate frost damage and heavily snaring all targets in the red ring. This cannot be blocked.
;Impending Storm:The battlemage charges up and releases lightning from itself as indicated by red circles dealing moderate shock damage to all targets hit. This can be interrupted to set the battlemage off balance. This cannot be blocked.
;Empower Weapon: Flame:The battlemage infuse their weapon with flame which change their "Quick Strike" attack into a melee "Flare" attack.
;Flare:The battlemage hits the target with their weapon while spinning, dealing minor flame damage.

## Notes
- During the beta, the Battlemage was planned to be the sixth player class. Along with the Warden, it was scrapped at launch in favor of the four standard classes, though the Warden class later returned to the game in Update 14. Some remnants of the class still exist within the files, including icons and a few errant strings. Destruction, Two-Handed, Alteration, and Mysticism were skill lines associated with the class, back when the familiar schools of magic were still used in the game. Parts of this concept were later adapted into the Necromancer and Arcanist classes, both types of Battlemages with specialized fields (Necromancy and Eldritch Knowledge respectively).
