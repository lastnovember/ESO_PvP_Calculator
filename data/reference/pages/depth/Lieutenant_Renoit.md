Lieutenant Renoit is a Breton member of the Lion Guard who can be found at Lion Guard Redoubt.

## Related Quests
- Codex and Cartel

## Quest-Related Events
One of your tasks during the quest is to get into the dungeon at the Lion Guard Redoubt to question Marten Cogwine about what he knows about Koldane Cartel's recent plans for the Nowhere Vault and its keys. During your conversation, he may let slip that there is a planned attack on Basil Menant, as Gavin Koldane believes he is responsible for the theft of the Voernet Codex from the Yvierry Estate before the cartel could take it.

When you inform Seline Menant of this, you have to decide whether to speak with the Lion Guard about the attack to get their help or not. If you decide to approach the Lion Guard, you can find Lieutenant Renoit in the middle of the camp. Your conversation will depend on if you previously assisted the Lion Guard with Faolchu and Camlorn or not:
