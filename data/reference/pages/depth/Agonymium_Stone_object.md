Agonymium Stones are blood-red Daedric stones found in The Dread Cellar. They contain the combined energy of those that were tortured in the prisons. Scorion Collectors are never found far from these, and they will attempt to gorge themselves on the energy. The Scorion Broodlord will also create several during its boss fight.

The stones involved in the Magma Incarnate fight are tougher. Agonymium Stones are also involved with the Descender Skirmish in the Night Market's Sorrow's Friend district.

## Skills and Abilities

### Descender Skirmish
During Phase 2 of the Descender Skirmish fight, three Agonymium Stones will appear surrounding the Spiral Descender it will be unattackable, as you need to remove the Stones first. The Agonymium Stones will at first be invulnerable. You will need to remove their hidden protection, by using the Restless Dead skeletons that spawn. Next to each Agonymium Stone, there will be a floating dark red cloud, this is a spectral Horror that is protecting the stone. You will need to kill the Restless Dead near the hidden horror, a pale circle outline will appear around the skeleton when it dies. If the Horror is within this circle, it will become vulnerable for a limited amount of time. Once the Horror is slain, the Agonymium Stone can then be damaged and destroyed.

During Phase 3 of the fight, the Spiral Descender will periodically summon an Agonymium Stone, which makes it invulnerable and must be destroyed as soon as possible. The Agonymium Stone will also attack nearby attackers with the following:
;Splinters of Suffering:The Agonymium Stone continuously launches red spectral skulls at players, each dealing increasing damage.
