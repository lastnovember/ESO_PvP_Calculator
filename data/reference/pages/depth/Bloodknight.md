Bloodknights are vampiric monstrosities created by allowing a vampire to absorb the power of other vampires. They are introduced in the Greymoor chapter and are likely to be first encountered in Greymoor Keep's courtyard

Bloodknights can also be found within the Stone Garden. When killed, their organs can be used at the alchemy tables found around the laboratory.

## Related Quests
- Destruction Incarnate
- The Lady of Blood
- Terror of the Weald

## Skills and Abilities
As elite rank enemies, Bloodknights are immune to crowd control.
;Talons:Basic fast claw attack dealing minor physical damage.
;Cross Swipe:The bloodknight swipes forward with both hands, dealing moderate physical damage and staggering their target. This can be blocked to stagger the bloodknight instead.
;Blood Tether:The bloodknight spits a growing pool of blood at its target, indicated by a hollow red circle. While the circle fills up, standing in the pool deals continuous low magic damage. Once the circle fills up completely after only about 2 seconds, it explodes, dealing moderate magic damage to targets inside, as well as both pulling them to the bloodknight and stunning the targets. Blocking doesn't negate the crowd control.

## Dialogue
In Stone Garden, the Bloodknights may utter single, emotionless, words as they engage you to fight:
:"Intruder."
:"Wither!"
:"Bleed!"
:"Wane!"

## Unique Bloodknights
- Balandrus the Terror, a bloodknight found in Sutch's mine
- Bloodknight Hlaro and Bloodknight Mondrar, two bloodknights found in the Shrine of the Golden Eye
- The Elder, a pure-blooded vampire imprisoned in Ashbound Hall
- Exarch Savfyr, a bloodknight part of the group boss trio of the Vampire Feeding Grounds
- Morgar the Unquenchable, a bloodknight found in a cave underneath Steadfast Manor
- Pentarch Khorb, a Nord vampire who transforms into a bloodknight during combat in Kagnthamz during the quest Dark Clouds Over Solitude
- Talfyg, a Nord vampire who transforms into a bloodknight in Castle Thorn, serving as its fourth boss Stonethorn

## Variants
- Arcane Hulks, shadowy Bloodknights summoned by Orpheon the Tactician on Lep Seclusa Fallen Banners
- Bitter Knights, Blood Knights, and Crimson Knights, bloodknights found at Kyne's Aegis
- Blood Guardians, shadowy bloodknights summoned by Lady Thorn in Castle Thorn Stonethorn (Veteran Hard Mode only)
- Bloodknight Specters, spectral bloodknights summoned by a resurrected vampire in Grayhome
- Dusksaber Bloodknights, bloodknights found in the Emerald Glyphic Vault
- Essence Manifestations, bloodknights summoned by Ansuul the Tormentor in Sanity's Edge
- Gray Host Bloodknights, bloodknights allied with the Gray Host
- Osteon Bloodknights, bloodknights found in the Ossein Cage
