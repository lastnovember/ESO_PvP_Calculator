# Online:Shadow Mage

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Shadow_Mage
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Shadow Mages are a class of hostile humanoid NPCs that attack using shadow magic abilities. They usually wear light armor and wield a staff. The class was introduced with Dark Brotherhood.

## Skills and Abilities
;Fright Force: A basic ranged attack dealing minor magic damage.
;Pool of Shadow: The Shadow Mage slams their staff into the ground, creating a pool of dark energy all around them, indicated by a circular AoE, which lingers for a time and deals continuous low magic damage when inside.
;Shadeway: The Shadow Mage casts a spell that shoots out pools of corrupted magic along the ground, indicated by red circles, each dealing moderate magic damage. This can be interrupted to set the Shadow Mage off-balance.
;Shadowy Duplicate: The Shadow Mage teleports a short distance away, leaving a Shadow in their place. The shadow will cast Fright Force dealing minor magic damage until it is killed. When killed, it explodes which deals moderate magic damage in an area and leaves a Pool of Shadow in its place.
