Captain Velya is a High Elf officer of the Aldmeri Dominion found in the ruins of Silyanorn, where Vicecanon Hrondar is being held captive. She has no unique dialogue.

## Related Quests
- Getting to the Truth

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Strike:A basic melee attack dealing moderate physical damage.
;Thunder Hammer:Captain Velya swings and shocks the ground in front of her as indicated by a red cone dealing moderate shock damage.
;Shock Aura:Captain Velya shocks the ground around herself as indicated by a red circle dealing moderate shock damage. This can be interrupted to knock Captain Velya off balance. This cannot be blocked.

## Bugs
Despite being an elite rank enemy, Captain Velya is vulnerable to crowd control.
