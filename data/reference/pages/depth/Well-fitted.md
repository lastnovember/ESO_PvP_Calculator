# Online:Well-fitted

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Well-fitted
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Well-fitted is a trait which can only be found on armor. Once you have researched the trait on a given item type, you can craft Well-fitted armor by using Almandine. Items with the Well-fitted trait will reduce the Stamina cost of Sprinting and Roll Dodging by an amount dependent on the quality of the item.
