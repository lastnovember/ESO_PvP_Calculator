Alameric Daillon is a skeleton mage found inside Tribulation Crypt.

## Skills and Abilities
;Shock:Basic ranged attack dealing minor shock damage.
;Thunder Thrall: Alameric Daillon teleports to you and summons a bolt of lightning that strikes a large area around itself, dealing moderate shock damage to all targets as indicated by the red circle.
;Run Away!:When too close to him, Alameric Daillon backs up to put some distance between his target and himself.

## Bugs
Despite being an elite rank enemy, Alameric Daillon is vulnerable to crowd control.
