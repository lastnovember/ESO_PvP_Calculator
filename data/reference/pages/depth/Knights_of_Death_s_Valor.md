# Online:Knights of Death's Valor

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Knights_of_Death's_Valor
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Knights of Death's Valor was a Breton Knightly Order active in the Systres Archipelago centuries before the events of the Elder Scrolls Online. They maintained a keep in the northwest High Isle.

During their golden years, the knights of Death's Valor roamed the countryside and upheld the peace in a torn and bloody time. There are no records as to why the order faded from prominence, though there were many accounts of how the order's home lay in haunted desolation during the Second Era.
