Captain Aurelia Blasio is an Imperial leader of the Gold Coast Mercenaries hired by the Baandari. She'll initially be found at her camp west of the Baandari Trading Post.

## Related Quests
- Enemy of My Enemy (quest)
- A Tangled Net

## Quest-Related Events

### Enemy of My Enemy
As you approach her tent (in disguise), she'll call out:
:Captain Aurelia Blasio: "What's this?"

Speaking to her, she'll say:
:"You're not one of my men. What are you doing here?"
:Just delivering orders, ma'am.
:"New courier? Good to see some fresh blood in the ranks. Now, let's see that letter. Hm. Not what I was expecting. Curious …."
:Your orders, ma'am?
:"You're relieved, soldier. Unless you want to go werewolf hunting. Well, I'm not paid to question orders. Time to rouse these louts out of their cups."

She'll then holler to her men:
:Captain Aurelia Blasio: "Look alive, soldiers! We've got wolves to skin!"

### A Tangled Net
After taking Eraral-dro captive in her tent, she'll remark:
:"So. You're the one who's working with this beast? Funny. You look familiar somehow."
:Why have you attacked this Khajiit?
:"We've been under their employ for a short while. I had questions about recent events affecting me and my men. He wouldn't cooperate, so I was forced to take him prisoner. I won't release him until I get answers."
:Can I speak with him?
:"As you please. But if you make any effort to free him, I'll take action. Swift, bloody action."
Speaking to her again before speaking to Eraral:
:"Don't take too long with him."
Speaking to her as soon as you've stopped talking to Eraral, she'll ask:
:"What was all the muttering about? Go on, clear out. I've got work to do."

After flipping some of her men against her, she loses support but grows hostile enough to attack you on sight, and is waiting behind her tent with two of her guards.

During battle, she'll cry:
:Captain Aurelia Blasio: "I'll put you down myself, dog!"

And at the moment of defeat:
:Captain Aurelia Blasio: "What …?"

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Strike:A basic melee attack dealing minor physical damage.
;Shock Aura:Captain Aurelia Blasio shocks the ground around herself as indicated by a red circle dealing low shock damage. This can be interrupted to knock Captain Aurelia Blasio off balance. This cannot be blocked.
;Lightning Form:Captain Aurelia Blasio casts Lightning Form on herself, gaining Major Resolve (which increases her armor by 5948) while creating small areas under the target which after a small delay deal low shock damage.
;Uppercut:An Uppercut-like attack dealing moderate physical damage, knocking back and stunning. This can be blocked to knock Captain Aurelia Blasio off balance instead.

## Bugs
Despite being an elite rank enemy, Captain Aurelia Blasio is vulnerable to crowd control.
