Dorand is a Breton adventurer that tried to lead a party into Rkindaleft. He thought that the party would have time to enter, retrieve riches, and escape before the dungeon warmed up enough and the dwarven machine awoke. He was wrong...

## Related Quests
- A Heart of Brass

## Quest-Related Events
When you find him he'll say:
:Dorand: "He watches us … he controls them all! Stop him … shut off the valves, before …."
Afterwards, he ceases to respond. But can you read Dorand's Final Notes in hopes of an answer.

## Bugs
Dorand's health bar remains full after he dies.
