It is one of two dungeons available with the Waking Flame DLC, the other being Red Petal Bastion.

The dungeon once served as a prison run by the Imperial Battlemages, but the facility was abandoned after the collapse of the Empire. It has recently been taken over by the Order of the Waking Flame and their Daedric allies, the Crimson Oath. Centipedes, dragonflies, fiendroth, rats and spiders are found in number within. Carts and Wheelbarrows yielding Bone, Crawlers, Gnawed Bone, Guts and Worms are scattered throughout the dungeon. Hazards include lava pools and dread surges.

## Backstory Description
;Update 31 Patch Notes and Waking Flame Bundle Details
The Empire kept its darkest secrets hidden in the depths of Black Marsh, far from prying eyes-the Dread Cellar, a torture chamber meant to hold those the Empire wanted to vanish. Explore this forgotten abattoir and learn its secrets, old and new.

;[https://www.elderscrollsonline.com/en-us/news/post/60565 Waking Flame Preview-The Dread Cellar]
Located in the hills of northern Blackwood, the Dread Cellar once served as one of the Empire's most notorious prisons. Home to dissidents, rebels, and political prisoners, this secret location seldom released those sent to it, and rumors abound of the guard's brutal treatment of their captives. Believed to be shuttered years ago, the Dread Cellar just recently began emanating mysterious magical energies and is now occupied by the Dagonic cult, the Waking Flame. Warned of the prison's reawakening by none other than Lyranth Foolkiller, a Dremora frequently involved in this year's Gates of Oblivion year-long adventure, the battlemages of the Imperial Legion, including the trainee battlemage Lucilla Caprenia and her master, Martus Tullius, have been dispatched to investigate this troubling news.
