Monastic Earrel was a High Elf priest who used to be found at Greenwater Cove. Monastic Falen is his sister.

## Related Quests
- Faith in the Family

## Quest-Related Events
When you approach him, he says:
:Monastic Earrel: "Stars above, give me the to do what must be done."

Speak with him to begin the quest.
:"Eyes of Auri-El! You're a sight. Have reinforcements arrived?"
:No, I'm sorry. Who are you?
:"A monastic to the units that tried to take the docks. The fight did not go well. I was just working myself up to …. Auri-El help me, I just can't do it. I'm no warrior. Please, will you help my sister?"
:Your sister?
:"Falen. We joined the faith together, joined the military at the same time. Will you help her? I have these bandages, this invisibility potion. You could use the supplies on some of the walking wounded. They might be willing to help."
:I'll find your sister. Where can I meet you?
:"I'll set up a triage for Falen and the soldiers under the bridge west of here. The Eight guide your hand, my new friend."
:What happened on the docks?
:"My squad slashed through the front lines and made for water, hoping to cut off their supplies and reinforcements. The vipers caught on, and hemmed us in. Surrounded us, and cut us down. I'm only alive because I ran."

You can patch up some wounded Dominion Soldiers while you search for his sister. Once you rescue her, you need to meet the pair on the beach by the western bridge. They'll be tending to some wounded soldiers. Speak with Earrel to end the quest.
:"Mara's heart! I never could have done the job."
:Happy to have helped, Monastic.
:"Auri-El guide your steps till the end of your days. My sister will live! Thanks to you, and the will of the Eight."

If you speak to him after the quest, he says:
:"May the hand of Auri-El be always on your shoulder. Guide us through life and protect us from harm."
