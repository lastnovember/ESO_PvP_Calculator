# Online:Marsh Warrior

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Marsh_Warrior
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Marsh Warrior are a class of hostile humanoid NPCs that attack using a two-handed weapon and nature magic. This class was introduced in Blackwood.

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Strike: A basic melee attack dealing low physical damage.
;Heavy Attack: A charged melee attack that deals moderate physical damage and staggers. This can be blocked to set the warrior off-balance instead.
;Choking Vine: The warrior stands firmly upon the ground and channels an ability that sends out a cluster of vines at their target, indicated by a red rectangle. If the target is caught, they are trapped by the vines and dealt continuous low physical damage until the ability runs out or they dodge roll to break the vines.
;Vine Whip:When standing outside of melee range, the warrior uses a Nature's Grasp-like ability which pulls them to their target and deals low magic damage.
;Earth Balm/Healing Roots: When wounded, the warrior reaches down to the ground as vines begin to wrap around them, healing them. This can be interrupted to set the warrior off-balance.
