# Online:Black Horse Couriers

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Black_Horse_Couriers
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Black Horse Couriers are a cut faction that would have been involved in the scrapped horse racing minigame. The Black Horse Couriers were called upon by royalty and provincial leadership when they needed messages sent across raging battlefields. The couriers would go so far as to travel to other planes of existence at the behest of high-profile patrons and demigods. Completing every timed horse racing challenge would have granted you membership.
