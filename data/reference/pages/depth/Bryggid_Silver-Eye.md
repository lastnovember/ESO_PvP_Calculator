Bryggid Silver-Eye is a Nord and a high-ranking member of the Koldane Cartel. She is first encountered at the Yvierry Manor during Quen's Codex caper.

## Related Quests
- The Codex Caper
- Wolf Trap

## Quest-Related Events

### Wolf Trap
Working with the Daggerfall gang leaders, you set up a trap for the Koldane Cartel at a warehouse in Eagle's Brook. When they arrive, the Lion Guard is to be there to apprehend them. Your part in the plan is to meet with Bryggid and sell her the information to bait the trap. She runs the Koldane Cartel Den near Crosswych, and Quen will ask you to infiltrate your way in to impress her.

The cartel den can be found beneath an abandoned cottage, and you will need to make your way to Bryggid Silver-Eye's Office. When you enter the room, Bryggid will be working at a desk and she greets you:
:Bryggid Silver-Eye: "I don't recognize you. Nor do I have any appointments. Tell me why I shouldn't kill you where you stand?"

Talking with Bryggid before you leave:
:"If we find the sapphire and our ledger at this warehouse, we'll reward you handsomely. For now, though, you're living on borrowed time. Go on. The ladder leads outside."

After reporting back to Quen, you let the Roadsworn and Lion Guard know that it is time to get into position. However when you arrive at Rols Boron's Warehouse you quickly see things have gone wrong. You learn that Rols Boron sold you out, and that Gavin Koldane himself arrived with his forces quicker than expected. Seline Menant and Captain Sansonne were taken to the Smuggler Caves below the warehouse.

Once you rescue Captain Sansonne, he leads you to where Gavin Koldane and Seline are and you will see that Bryggid is there as well. To rescue Seline, you will need to kill Brygiid first. As you attack her, she mockingly shouts:
:Brygiid Silver-Eye: "Your deception was so obvious, little thief. Pathetic."

When she loses approximately a third of her health, she will transform into a werewolf:
:Brygiid Silver-Eye: "Insolent runt! Die by my claws!"

As she gets close to death, she will disbelievingly shout:
:Brygiid Silver-Eye: "I can't fall! Not to this petty thief!"
