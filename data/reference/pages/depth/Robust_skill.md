# Online:Robust (skill)

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Robust_(skill)
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Update 11
Orc-Robust
Robust was a skill in the Nord and Orc racial passive skill lines which increased health recovery by 10% at Rank I, 20% at Rank II, and 30% at Rank III. The ability was removed in Update 11 and replaced with Stalwart in the Nord line and Unflinching in the Orc line.
