desc = Destroy 5 or more soul gem clusters with a single cast of Quarrymaster Saldezaar's Leveling Charge, Wrathful Rupture, or Galvanizing Blow in Veteran Black Gem Foundry.

Controlled Demolition is awarded for making Quarrymaster Saldezaar destroy 5 or more soul gem clusters in a single ability cast. His abilities capable of breaking those rocks are the following:are the names correct?
- Leveling Charge - Saldezaar charges up and dashes at one of players
- Wrathful Rupture - Saldezaar dives into the ground and shortly after emerges in a lightning explosion
- Galvanizing Blow - Saldezaar aims and then sends a conical wave of lightning towards the player that has aggro
Galvanizing Blow is likely the easiest to get it done with.
