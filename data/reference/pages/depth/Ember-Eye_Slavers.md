# Online:Ember-Eye Slavers

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Ember-Eye_Slavers
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Ember-Eye Slavers are a group of slavers from Morrowind who are raiding the western coast of Stormhaven. Despite the name, they are not solely Dunmer.
