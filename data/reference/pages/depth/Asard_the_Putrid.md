Asard the Putrid is a Breton zombie who can be found at North Shore Point. He is accompanied by two regular zombies.

## Skills and Abilities
As a boss rank enemy, Asard the Putrid is immune to crowd control.
;Swipe: A basic melee attack dealing moderate-high physical damage.
;Vomit:Asard the Putrid vomits on his target, indicated by a red cone, dealing very high disease damage.
;Pound:Asard the Putrid lunges at his target, dealing moderate-high physical damage and staggering the target. This can be blocked to stagger Asard the Putrid instead.

## Achievements
There is one achievement associated with this creature:
Asard's Assassin

## Bugs
Asard the Putrid takes an unusually long time between his attacks, making it very easy to defeat.
