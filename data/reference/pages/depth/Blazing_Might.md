# Online:Blazing Might

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Blazing_Might
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Blazing Might is one of three class skill lines available to Isobel Veloise. Abilities in this line focus on dealing damage. It is roughly equivalent to the Dawn's Wrath skill line for Templar players.

## Skill Perks
The Blazing Might skill line is immediately available to Isobel, and is leveled up as she gains levels.
