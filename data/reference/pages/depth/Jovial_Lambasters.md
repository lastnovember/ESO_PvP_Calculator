# Online:Jovial Lambasters

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Jovial_Lambasters

The Jovial Lambasters are an exclusive group dedicated to the worship of Clavicus Vile in Rimmen. The cult allows the local outlaw refuge to function in return for dues paid by all who work there.
