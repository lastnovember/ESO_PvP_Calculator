Deserter Storm Mages are Breton storm magesplain who joined Orpheon's army after leaving their original alliance. They are part of the siege on Lep Seclusa, encountered primarily in the Secluded Cave and Underground Monastery.

They are also called upon by Garvin the Tracker during battle.

## Related Quests
- Upstart Emperor
- Pledge: Lep Seclusa

## Skills and Abilities
;Shock:Basic ranged attack dealing minor shock damage.
;Shock Strike: A basic melee attack that does high shock damage. If taunted they will be forced to use this attack on the tank only.
;Charged Water: The storm mage imbues the surrounding waters with lightning that electrocutes any who walk into it (Only in Secluded Cave and Inlet Grotto)
;Agonizing Bolts: The enemy raises their arm up as electricity flings out from it, and lands into the ground nearby. Clusters of electricity then travels outward from the pool, indicated by circular AoEs, dealing low shock damage and snaring.
;Expansive Shock
