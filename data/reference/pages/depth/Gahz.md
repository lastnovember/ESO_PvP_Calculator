Gahz is an Orc ghost hunter who can be found outside the Corelanya Manor. Her rival and sister, Oorbesh also came to the Manor to exorcize spirits and the pair will both try and recruit you to aid them in the haunted mansion.

Gahz originally came from as small stronghold in Orsinium and decided to walk her own path, only for Oorbesh to follow. A few years ago, she and Oorbesh became stranded on Solstice after a shipwreck.

## Quest-Related Events
While near the Corelanya Manor, you can meet Oorbesh who is looking for assistance to exorcize the manor, and also complains about her rival Gahz trying to steal her work. Speaking with her about Gahz some more, she claims that during her first haunting case at the Chill House in Glenumbra, she did the hard work while her rival got all the credit.

Once you agree to help, you can then follow the path to the front door of the manor, where there will be an orc in blue and brown clothing looking around:
