## Detailed Walkthrough

## Notes
- Despite being a physical wooden wand, the player character performs a spell with their hand instead of actually using the wand when applied on the chickens.
- The wand can still be used on chickens after obtaining enough chicken dinners to progress to the next objective, however they will not drop more chicken dinners when used in that way.
- Pariah's Hand and Subtle Knife revelers appear in the Fiver Finger Dance after completing the quest and going back through the portal.

## Bugs
Chickens are visibly thrown into the air before disappearing mid-fall when using the wand to transform them into cooked chicken.
