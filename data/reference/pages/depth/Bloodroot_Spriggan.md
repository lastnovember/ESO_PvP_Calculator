The Bloodroot Spriggan is a polymorph resembling a nirnblooded Spriggan from the Bloodroot Forge. It was available in the Crown Store as a Crown Gem exclusive for 500 or 7,200.

## Notes
- It shares a theme with and was released alongside the Bloodroot Wild Hunt Senche.
- Other spriggan polymorphs include:
- Sylvan Spriggan
- Winterborn Spriggan
- Azure Blight Spriggan
