# Online:Aedric Spear

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Aedric_Spear
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Aedric Spear is one of three class skill lines available to Templar characters. It can be learned by other classes via subclassing. Abilities in this line focus on melee weapon attacks.

## Skill Perks
The Aedric Spear skill line is immediately available to all Templars, and is leveled up by earning experience while Aedric Spear skills are slotted. Skills include:
