Blood Crawlers are feral humanoid enemies with a blood-red spectral tint encountered in Castle Thorn. They are summoned by Lady Thorn while a swarm of bats blankets the arena. They will run after group members and attack them, but are rather weak.

## Related Quests
- Blood of the Past

## Skills and Abilities
;Rending Slash:The enemy hits you with its hands or claws, dealing minor physical damage.
;Slash:The enemy hits you with its hands or claws two times, dealing minor physical damage.
;Flurry:A series of four quick swipes in a cone in front of the enemy.
;Devastating Leap:The enemy leaps at the target, staggering them. The attack is blockable.
