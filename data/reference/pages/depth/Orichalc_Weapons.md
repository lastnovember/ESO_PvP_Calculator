# Online:Orichalc Weapons

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Orichalc_Weapons
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

This is a listing of all standard Orichalc Weapons in the game. These can be crafted via Blacksmithing from Orichalcum Ingots obtained by refining Orichalcum Ore. It requires rank 3 in Metalworking. The listed damage values assume that the character is Level 50 CP160; lower level characters may see higher values.
