# Online:Dawn's Wrath

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Dawn's_Wrath
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Dawn's Wrath is one of three class skill lines available to Templar characters. It can be learned by other classes via subclassing. Abilities in this line focus on magical attacks.

## Skill Perks
The Dawn's Wrath skill line is immediately available to all Templars, and is leveled up by earning experience while Dawn's Wrath skills are slotted. Skills include:
