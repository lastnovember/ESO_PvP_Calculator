Dwarven Architon Arbalests are architons found at the Library of Arkthzand.

## Related Quests
- The Study of Souls

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Shot:A basic ranged attack that does minor physical damage.
;Headbutt: A basic melee attack dealing minor physical damage.
;Deep Bolt: The arbalest readies up their crossbow for a more precise shot, as their arms start to glow bright red, as well as their target (like the archers' "Taking Aim" ability, but nowhere near as deadly), and fires a bolt at their target, dealing moderate physical damage and stunning them.
;Explosive Retreat: The arbalest throws down a small automaton resembling a Dwarven Sentry, which explodes right after, indicated by a growing red circle, dealing moderate shock damage. After throwing the automaton, the arbalest leaps backward to create space with its target.

## Notes
- Despite their name, they don't behave at all like actual arbalest class users.

## Bugs
Getting stunned by "Deep Bolt" doesn't display the actual stunned animation on your player character (as in they don't look dazed and wobbly).
Although they use a small arm-mounted crossbow on one of their arms as their weapon instead of an actual bow, they still use the two-handed bow and quiver (which they don't have) animations for their abilities, which doesn't match up visually.
