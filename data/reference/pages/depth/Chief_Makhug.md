Chief Makhug is an Orc leader of the Drublog, a group of Wood Orcs. During the related quest he can be found in the Velyn Harbor town hall. He can be heard making a deal with a Ra Gada Envoy as soon as you enter the building.

## Related Quests
- One Fell Swoop

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Strike: A basic melee attack dealing minor physical damage.
;Blood Craze: Chief Makhug slashes the target twice, dealing moderate physical damage twice then very minor bleed damage every two seconds over 8 seconds to the target. The bleeding also heals Chief Makhug for around a quarter of the damage done. Blocking the first hit negates the bleeding, but blocking the second one does not.
;Assassinate: Chief Makhug strikes at the target's back for moderate physical damage. This attack can only be performed if Chief Makhug is behind his target. Face toward Chief Makhug to prevent him from using this ability.
;Agony: Chief Makhug channels a spell dealing moderate magic damage and stunning the target for 2 seconds. Taking damage from any source will break the stun, but the target may choose to break free from it instead. After the stun ends, this spell also deals minor magic damage every second over 6 seconds to the target. This can be interrupted to set Chief Makhug off balance instead. Blocking will not prevent the damage over time.
;Roll Dodge: Chief Makhug can perform a roll dodge to avoid incoming damage.
;Heated Blades:Chief Makhug rakes his weapons together in a show of sparks, buffing his damage by 25% and applying Minor Maim to the target for 4 seconds.
;Backstabber:Chief Makhug has a passive ability that makes him deal 20% more damage when attacking the target's back.
