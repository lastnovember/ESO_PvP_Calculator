## Detailed Walkthrough
Both at the Vale of Tiers and at the wrecked caravan site, Kamid can be intimidated into assisting you.

## Notes
- In the Vale of Tiers, if you find and read Kamid's mother Maliah's journal before talking to him, you will already have access to the "Use the Passphrase" optional objective.
- While objective markers point you towards Damaged Factotums in order to obtain a working Animo Core, none will have one, and only the hostile factotums will drop it.
- If you loot a Factotum Beacon in the Vale of Tiers, a nearby Factotum Analyst emits a warning, approaches you and self-combusts, dealing moderate fire damage, indicated by a growing circular AoE.
- Gear Grinder won't exist at the caravan wreck before starting the quest or reaching the related objective.
- It's possible to dig up the treasure without alerting and having to fight Gear Grinder.
- After completing the quest, the lorebook Thoughts of the Honored Assistant appears in Slag Town Outlaws Refuge, on top of a crate. It gives the code necessary to get the Fortune and Fate achievement by speaking with the Grand Prognosticator again.
