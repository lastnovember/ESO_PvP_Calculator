### The Apprentice
Under the Mage's influence, the Apprentice increases your Magicka and Empowers your Spells.

colspan=2 | Champion Abilities | Effect

Blessed | Increases the effectiveness of healing done by [0-15]%.

Elemental Expert | Increases your Flame, Frost, Shock, and Magic Damage by [0-15]%.

Elfborn | Increases damage and healing dealt by Spell Criticals by [0-25]%.

Spell Erosion | Increases Spell Penetration by [0-5280].

colspan=2 | Passive Unlocks | Rank | Effect

Vengeance | 10 | Guarantees your next spell will be a critical hit after you block three spells within 10 seconds of each other.

Spell Precision | 30 | Increases your Spell Critical rating by 3%.

Foresight | 75 | Reduces the Magicka cost of your next spell by 80% after you drink a potion.

Arcane Well | 120 | Gives you a 20% chance when you kill an enemy of opening an Arcane Well, which restores [x] Magicka up to three allies within 2.5 meters of the enemy.
