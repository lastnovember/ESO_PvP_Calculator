# Online:Ruinblood Clan

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Ruinblood_Clan
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Ruinblood Clan is a Dremora clan that serves Mehrunes Dagon. They have entered into a strained alliance with the Order of the Waking Flame, to work on the cataclysts and the creation of Incarnates in Mehrunes Dagon's name.

In On Dremora Clans, author Divayth Fyr describes them as the most powerful of the clans serving beneath the Prince of Destruction. They are among his highest ranking commanders, and serve as officers in his Daedric legion. Their leader, Valkynaz Nokvoz, currently rules the citadel of Ardent Hope.
