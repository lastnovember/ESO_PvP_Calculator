Duke Calvus Vanin is first duke of the Duchy of Giovesse, who now exists as an undying wraith. It appears in Castle Giovesse's Mausoleum after the Sword of Giovesse is obtained.

## Related Quests
- The Ghostwriter of Giovesse

## Skills and Abilities
;Ice Bolt: A basic ranged attack that does minor frost damage.
;Winter's Reach: The wraith releases a frost storm in the direction it is facing, dealing moderate frost damage and rooting its target. This spell can be interrupted to set the wraith off balance.

## Bugs
Despite being elite-rank, it is vulnerable to crowd control.
