Onyx Indriks are an evolved Indrik located near the Impresario at the Event Exchanges in Auridon, Glenumbra and Stonefalls. They have a metallic black and grey coloration and a white mist around their antlers and hooves.

The Indrik remained there until the Dragon Rise event ended.

## Notes
- The Ebon-Glow Indrik pet is the smaller equivalent of the Onyx Indrik.
