Ezhkel is a strange goat that will appear when you drink the Swirling Libation found at Sanguine's Shrine in West Weald. They can also be encountered in Solstice in a rather different form.

While on Solstice, they will request your help in their ongoing quest of "enlightenment through experience" by experiencing everything Oblivion has to offer, via changing forms and serving a variety of Daedric Princes.

## Notes
- Ezhkel shares the same form as the Sanguine's Black Goat pet.
- The cut book Drunken Poetry mentions Ezhkel by name and calls them a "goat daedra".
- After you have completed the related quest, a Letter from Ezhkel that is addressed to you can be found in the southern tunnel of the Sunport Outlaws Refuge next to a dead monkey.
- Ezhkel authored the Curator Dossier in which they mention taking form of Vermai, which according to them had "fallen out of fashion".
