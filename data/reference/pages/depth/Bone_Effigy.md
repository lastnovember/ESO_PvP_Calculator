Bone Effigies are skeletal totems encountered in Naj-Caldeesh. They first appear in the starting area of the dungeon and will summon Haunting Specters.

In Death's Walk, Poxito will summon bone effigies that have an additional effect of being tied to her Soul Storm ability, where a beam of blue plasma will connect her sword to the effigies. If there is a bone effigy left standing when Poxito finishes casting the ability, the entire group will be killed instantly, so destroying the bone effigies at the opposite sides of room is essential for surviving the fight.
