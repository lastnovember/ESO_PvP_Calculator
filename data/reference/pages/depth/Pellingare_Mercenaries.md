# Online:Pellingare Mercenaries

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Pellingare_Mercenaries
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Pellingare Mercenaries are a group of mercenaries encountered in the Wayrest Sewers, led by the Pellingare twins - Allene Pellingare and Varaine Pellingare.
