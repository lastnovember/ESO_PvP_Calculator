Carvain Maulers are Redguard berserkers and members of the Carvain Rowdies faction that can be found in Ontus and Zeggar's Blind.

## Related Quests
- Another Death in Ontus

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Strike: A basic melee attack that does minor physical damage.
;Blood Craze: The enemy slashes the target twice, doing moderate physical damage twice then very minor bleed damage over time to the target. This attack also heals the enemy over time.
;Agony: The enemy deals moderate magic damage and stuns the target for a short time. Taking damage from any source will break the stun, but they may choose to break free from it instead. After the stun ends, this spell also does moderate magical damage over time to the target. This spell can be interrupted to set the enemy off balance. Blocking will prevent the stun.
;Roll Dodge: The enemy can perform a roll dodge to avoid incoming damage.
