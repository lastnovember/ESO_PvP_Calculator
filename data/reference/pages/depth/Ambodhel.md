Ambodhel was a High Elf and former curator of the Arcane University who would have asked you to help put out fires in the Elven Gardens District.

## Related Quests
- The Cold Wind Blows

## Quest-Related Events
:"I cannot imagine an Imperial City without its Elven Gardens."

:"The Elven Gardens have always been a symbol of the Summerset Isles' support for the Empire. I worry about the implications if they are destroyed."

:"The Elven Gardens have always been protected by magic, but those protections are no match for the dark power of Coldharbour."
:I have come to help you. What do you need?

:"You mean … you would be willing to help? Y'ffre's bones! Then yes! This enchanted crystal can emit a freezing gale, strong enough to put out fires. Take it, please. If you kill the Xivkyn and put out the fires, there may yet be hope."

:"You mean … you would be willing to help? Y'ffre's bones! Then yes! This enchanted crystal can emit a freezing gale, strong enough to put out fires. Take it, please. If you kill the Xivkyn and put out some of the fires, there may yet be hope."

You could ask him about himself after accepting the quest.
:Where did you get this enchanted crystal?
:"Well, if you must know, I was curator at Arcane University many years ago. I have no skills in magic, however, and eventually they replaced me with someone who did. I thought I deserved to keep a few souvenirs. This crystal was among them."

When you return to him after extinguishing enough flames, he'll say:
:"Ah, the smell of steam and ash. A strange combination, to be sure, but in this context, I might say it was the scent of victory. Were you able to extinguish any of the fires?"
:I put out as many as I could.
:"Then you have preserved an invaluable piece of history. You should be proud. History will see you repaid as well. When I write the account of these dark days, you will be described as nothing less than a saint."

After the quest:
:"I fear the Xivkyn will eventually return to resume their campaign of destruction. If it happens, I hope you too will return to stop them."

:"The district is far from safe, but at least it's not on the verge of being reduced to cinders. Not right now, at least."

:"If you see any Xivkyn out there, feel free to kill them. As far as I know they have not yet restarted their mission to burn down the district, but it may simply be a matter of time."

When he has another quest avaiable for you, he'll speak aloud:
:"The gardens are burning again."

Speaking to him when he has another quest for you:
:"You have excellent timing, my friend. It seems the Xivkyn have returned and begun anew their campaign to burn everything in sight."

:"There you are. History is once again threatened in the Elven Gardens, my friend. The Xivkyn have returned with a fiery vengeance."

:"Do you smell it? The smoke, the fires? The Xivkyn have returned and the district is again in danger of being completely destroyed. I hope you will consider coming to our aid once again."
:
:"I do, and it's recharged. If you would be willing to go out and extinguish more of the fires, I would be happy to loan it to you once again."
:Fine. I'll go put out more fires.
