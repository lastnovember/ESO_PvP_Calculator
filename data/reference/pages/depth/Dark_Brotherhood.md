Dark Brotherhood (DLC)
The Dark Brotherhood is a guild of assassins with holdings all over Tamriel. A DLC pack focused on the faction's activities in the Gold Coast region and across the rest of Tamriel was released in the second quarter of 2016. Purchase of the DLC is required to participate in any Dark Brotherhood Questlines.

For historical information about the Dark Brotherhood, see the lore page.

## Litany of Blood
After completing the first quests for the Dark Brotherhood, you can speak to Nevusa in a small enclave on the left side of the Sanctuary. She introduces you to the Litany of Blood. It holds a special assignment given by the Listener to those deemed worthy - to assassinate a number of persons throughout all faction zones. After killing each target, their ghostly image will appear on a pedestal. Executing all targets completes the Litany of Blood achievement, giving you the Executioner title and Cadaverous Assassin Polymorph.

For more information on the Litany, see the quest page.

## Reputation
All guilds use reputation for leveling purposes. Dark Brotherhood DB Reputation is earned by completing various quests and contracts for the guild. Most story quests award 10, and some award 20. The regular Marked for Death contracts award 5 and the killing spree contracts award 10. While the daily Black Sacrament contracts normally award 10, they can award up to 20 if all the optional objectives are completed.
