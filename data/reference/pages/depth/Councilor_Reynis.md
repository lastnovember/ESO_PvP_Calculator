Councilor Reynis is one of the spectral Dunmer encountered at Salothan's Council, alongside General Tanasa, Orator Salothan, and Regent Beleth.

She uses a mix of Cauterizer, Frost Mage and Nullifier and an additional ability during combat.

## Related Quests
- Salothan's Curse

## Skills and Abilities
As a boss rank enemy, Councilor Reynis is immune to crowd control.
;Ice Arrow:A basic ranged attack dealing moderate frost damage.
;Ice Barrier: Councilor Reynis summons an Ice Barrier that absorbs projectiles fired at it until destroyed. Councilor Reynis cannot be pulled if the wall stands between her and the target. The player should move so that there is a clear line of sight between themselves and Councilor Reynis.
;Winter's Reach:Councilor Reynis unleashes an icy whirlwind in the direction she is facing. This whirlwind does high frost damage and roots all players that it hits. The root effect passes through block. This can be interrupted to stagger Councilor Reynis.
;Focused Healing:Councilor Reynis will heal any allies that are hurt, including herself. Enemies often indicate this by calling out for healing (I'm hurt!, I need healing!, Healer! etc.) This effect appears as a yellow beam connecting Councilor Reynis and her target. This can be interrupted to stagger Councilor Reynis.
;Run Away!:When too close to her, Councilor Reynis backs up to put some distance between her target and herself.
;Negate Magic:Councilor Reynis creates a globe of magic suppression for 10 seconds, removing and preventing all player area of effect abilities from occurring in the area. Players within the globe will be silenced and pets/companions are stunned. Silence prevents players from using any ability costing Health, Magicka, Ultimate or have no cost at all as long as they are in the affected area.
;Rite of Passage:Councilor Reynis channels a spell healing herself and all her allies in the area every second for 10% of her Max Health (58,601) for 4 seconds. It takes half a second to cast, and another half a second to begin healing once the effect is applied. This ability is usually used when Councilor Reynis is on low health. This can be interrupted to stagger Councilor Reynis. (Bugged, see bugs)
;Vengance for Salothan:If Orator Salothan dies before Councilor Reynis, she transforms into a red skeleton and deals 20% more damage. She reverts to her previous form once dead.

## Bugs
"Rite of Passage" only heals Councilor Reynis when it should also heal all her allies in the area.
