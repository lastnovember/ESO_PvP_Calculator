Forgotten Assassins were Redguard mummy assassins found in the Crypt of the Exiles. They were removed in a very early update.

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Strike: A basic melee attack dealing low physical damage.
;Whirlwind: The Forgotten Assassin spins around, dealing moderate physical damage to all targets as indicated by a red circle.
;Assassinate: The Forgotten Assassin strikes at the target's back for moderate physical damage. This attack can only be performed if the Forgotten Assassin is behind their target, and they will often use "Passing Through" to get there. Face toward the Forgotten Assassin to prevent them from using this ability.
;Passing Through:The Forgotten Assassin jumps above the target to get behind them.
;Roll Dodge: The Forgotten Assassin can perform a roll dodge to avoid incoming damage.
