## Quick Walkthrough
# Head to Teeth of Sithis, Tomb of Many Spears and Tsofeer Cavern find suitable pieces of rubble.
# Return to Nisswo Uaxal and hand over the pieces.
# Go to the village's Storage Shed and find a hammer.
# Go back to Nisswo Uaxal and destroy his art piece.
# Speak with Nisswo Uaxal for reward.

## Notes
- The ruin pieces won't exist in the locations before starting the quest.

## Bugs
Speaking to Nisswo Uaxal after he has built his monument results in the dialogue camera clipping into the monument, obscuring him completely.
There is no animation or any SFX when destroying the monument to Sithis. It disappears instantly with no rubble after hitting it with the hammer.
Sometimes after getting the part to destroy the monument, it won't appear.
- log out/log in again should fix this.
