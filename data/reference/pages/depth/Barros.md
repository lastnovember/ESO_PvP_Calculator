Barros is a Khajiit found at Greenleaf Farm. He has aspirations of joining the military against the wishes of his mother, Tsazii. He cannot be spoken to but will argue with his mother often. After the battle for Riverhold is won, he and his family can be found in the Banished Regrets Inn.

Later he'll be at the camp at the Moon Gate of Anequina, and unfortunately, will be the battle's first victim.

## Related Quests
- The Battle for Riverhold
