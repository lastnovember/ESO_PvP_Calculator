Lenonion is a Wood Elf beggar found in the pass south of Dragonstar. He has no unique dialogue.

Update 4]]

## Deprecated Content
Prior to Update 4, Lenonion could be found at the then-blocked western gate near the town of Dragonstar, laying against a cart. He had ambient dialogue with Gulgula.
:Gulgula: "All right-break's over, squirt. Get back to work."
:Lenonion: "Break? I'm not on a break! I'm on lookout duty."
:Gulgula: "Lookout? What in Oblivion are you looking out for?"
:Lenonion: "Raiders! Slavers! Rabid miners! It's dangerous out here. Gotta keep these keen eyes on the horizon!"
:Gulgula: "Good point. There's just no telling how many brigands want to steal our hoard of totally worthless stone."
:Lenonion: "Well … there could be gems in those stones."
:Gulgula: "There could be gems in your head. Should I grab a pick and find out?"
:Lenonion: "Fine, fine. I'm on my way. Gotta, y'know, find my pick and such. Might take a minute … or ten."

## Notes
- At the western gate, Lenonion did not have a nameplate when looking at him.
