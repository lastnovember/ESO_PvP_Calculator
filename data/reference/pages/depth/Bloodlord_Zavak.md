Bloodlord Zavak is a Dremora leading Valkynaz Nokvroz's blood adepts, who can be found at Ardent Hope.

## Related Quests
- Against All Hope

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Strike: A basic melee ability that deals low physical damage.
;Biting Winds: A dual-hitting basic melee attack that deal low physical damage each.
;Dagger Throw: A basic ranged attack dealing low physical damage.
;Feet of Fire: Bloodlord Zavak leaps backwards, putting distance between him and his target. The ground he travels over is engulfed in flames, indicated by a red rectangle, dealing continuous low flame damage.
;Summon Bound Hemoptera: Bloodlord Zavak summons a Bound Hemoptera, which channels a buff on their summoner that causes them to do twice the damage and take less damage. If the Hemoptera is interrupted, it becomes an Unbound Hemoptera. They will be rebound to their summoner after a few seconds.
;Sacrifice Hemoptera: Bloodlord Zavak can destroy his Hemoptera to heal himself.
;Tainted Blood: Bloodlord Zavak throws thick blood at his target which splatters all over the ground, indicated by a large red circle, dealing continuous moderate magical damage.
;Blood Flow: Bloodlord Zavak raises his hand and conjures a spell that causes six blood geysers to erupt at his target's location one after the other, indicated by large red circles, each dealing moderate magical damage. This can be interrupted.
;Summon: Bloodlord Zavak summons a Dremora Blood Adept and a Dremora Bloodmaster to assist him.
