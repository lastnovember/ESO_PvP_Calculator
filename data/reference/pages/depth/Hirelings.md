# Online:Hirelings

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Hirelings
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

These hirelings send you various crafting materials through the in-game mail if you have the appropriate passive skill:
:* Abnab - a hireling for Enchanting, occasionally assisted by his brother Ezdab
:* Aksel Rockbiter - a miner hireling for Blacksmithing
:* Eron Salt - an Enchanting hireling who works for the Velothril Sisters
:* Farrokh - a Blacksmithing hireling who works for Ludovic Laumont
:* Gaereleth Surefoot - an Enchanting hireling who works for the Velothril Sisters
:* Gavin Gavonne - a hireling for Provisioning
:* Granny Gravius - an outfitter hireling for Clothing
:* Karamel Little-Leaf - a miner hireling for Blacksmithing (assistant to Valinka)
:* Low-Neck - an outfitter hireling for Clothing (assistant to Madam Firi)
:* Ludovic Laumont - a Blacksmithing hireling
:* Madam Firilanya - an outfitter hireling for Clothing
:* Margog the Hammer - a lumberjack hireling for Woodworking
:* Melina Cassel - a vampiric hireling for Enchanting
:* Nonette Gavonne - a Provisioning hireling who takes over messaging you from her nephew, Gavin Gavonne
:* Orbinor - a lumberjack hireling for Woodworking
:* Pacrooti - a lumberjack hireling for Woodworking
:* Sixul the Thorough - an Enchanting hireling who works for the Velothril Sisters
:* Uraviin Dres - an outfitter hireling for Clothing (rival to Veesk)
:* Urbek - a miner Blacksmithing hireling which occasionally takes over for Ludovic Laumont
:* Valgus the Barrow-breaker - an Enchanting hireling who works for the Velothril Sisters
:* Valinka Stoneheaver - a miner hireling for Blacksmithing
:* Valyne Hlan - an Enchanting hireling who works for the Velothril Sisters
:* Veesk-Olan - an outfitter hireling for Clothing
:* Velothril Sisters - Enchanting hireling, with several employees working for them
:* Zukki-dar - a Provisioning hireling
