Bak-Deelith Xankapa is a Tide-Born Argonian warrior who can be found with her friend Bak-Deelith Dosu-Jasuda in Eastern Solstice.

## Dialogue
There are multiple conversations available with Bak-Deeliths Dosu-Jasuda and Xankapa, the one you get is random.

### Encounter One
When you approach them, you may hear them discussing methods of killing daedra:
:Bak-Deelith Xankapa: "Watchers? I go for the tentacles first. Naturally."
:Bak-Deelith Dosu-Jasuda: "And I for the eyes. I would think an eyeless Watcher more helpless than a limbless one."
:Bak-Deelith Xankapa: "Perhaps this mainlander knows something of slaying Daedra."

Talk with her to ask some questions:
:"Have you slain many Daedra, mainlander? My friend and I were discussing the best ways of dispatching them. He thinks he knows better because his Stone-Nest have been fighting them behind the wall this whole time. But I disagree."
:So you think the Tide-Born are better at slaying Daedra?
::"Naturally. We fought them off and helped the Stirk Alliance break through the Writhing Wall. That said, I have seen Dosu-Jasuda fight Daedra, and it was like watching an ornaug cut through the waves. Perhaps there are methods I can learn from him."
:Who are you?
::"I am Xankapa of the Tide-Born. Dosu-Jasuda and I titled ourselves Bak-deelith. A mainlander translated this term as War-sophists. We normally wander the island as a pair. The Writhing Wall made that very difficult when it came between us."
::Did you try to break through?
::"I first tried to swim under, and then to sail around. Strange things happened where the Wall met deep water. I knew Dosu would speak no end of my foolishness if I died trying to get to him. So I aided the Stirk Alliance to breach the wall instead."

After you finish talking to her, she will ask Dosu-Jasuda another question:
:Bak-Deelith Xankapa: "I meant to ask. Have you found the way to best kill a Daedroth?"
:Bak-Deelith Dosu-Jasuda: "We Stone-Nest learned the waddle controls their fire breath. Slit it with claws or dagger, and the fight is much easier."

### Encounter Two
They can also be encountering arguing on the side of the road, regarding the nature of Sithis:
:Bak-Deelith Dosu-Jasuda: "We do not agree. On this, my will is as set as stone."
:Bak-Deelith Xankapa: "Only a fool thinks stone mightier than the will of the sea. Itz-Teyeesh shall prove me right."

Talk with Bak-Deelith Xankapa to see what is going on:
:"Dosu-Jasuda is as thick-shelled as ever. Perhaps he will listen to the words of an outsider. Will you settle a friendly debate on the nature of Itz-Teyeesh? Sithis, as I think you mainlanders say."
:All right. What are you debating? / And what are you debating with Dosu-Jasuda?
::"Dosu-Jasuda thinks the will of Itz-Teyeesh is immovable. That one would sooner swim through stone than defy such a nature. I say it is more like the sea. Chaotic and ever-changing. Everything comes from, and eventually returns to, the sea."
::And you want to know which perspective I agree with?
::"I ask that you hear from Dosu-Jasuda before you decide. My words may not do justice to his views. It is only fair."
:Who are you?
::"I am Xankapa of the Tide-Born. Dosu-Jasuda and I titled ourselves Bak-deelith. A mainlander translated this term as War-sophists. We normally wander the island as a pair. The Writhing Wall made that very difficult when it came between us."
::Did you try to break through?
::"I first tried to swim under, and then to sail around. Strange things happened where the Wall met deep water. I knew Dosu would speak no end of my foolishness if I died trying to get to him. So I aided the Stirk Alliance to breach the wall instead."

You can then speak with Dosu-Jasuda to get his thoughts, and then give your opinion. They will then react to your opinion:

### Encounter Three
:Bak-Deelith Xankapa: "I meant to pay this custom of yours a compliment. A votive of stonework shows dedication. Devotion."
:Bak-Deelith Dosu-Jasuda: "There is so much more to be said for the sand murals of your Tide-Born. A bond that says you will weather the changing tides together. Or do I not understand it as you do?"

Talking with Bak-Deelith Xankapa about their disagreement. You can also speak with Dosu-Jasuda for more information:
:"The one time I express interest in this stone-chewer's customs, he decides it's the one he's least interested in. Why do I even bother?"
:What appeals to you about the Stone-Nest custom more than the Tide-Born?
:"I see it as entrusting a piece of yourself to another. The absence of that piece makes you more, not less. And the bond it creates is further imbued with meaning. It is a concrete reflection of the significance we ascribe to these connections."
:What does it matter if Dosu-Jasuda agrees with you or not?
:"Dosu and I disagree on everything. Always have. But recently, we found something we agree on. Our feelings for one another. But in how to consecrate this bond, we clearly disagree. Which courting custom would you find more meaningful, mainlander?"
:If I had to choose, I'd pick the sand murals of the Tide-Born.
::"Then I am outnumbered. Perhaps I will find new meaning in this ritual of my people through the eyes and heart of Dosu-Jasuda. Thank you, mainlander. May all your choices be as easy as this one you've made for me."plain
:If I had to choose, I'd pick the carved totems of the Stone-Nest.
::"I am glad we agree. If you and I shared what I have with Dosu-Jasuda, this would be easy. But this stone-chewer is set in his ways. Not likely to listen to an outside opinion. But that is exactly why I feel for him as I do."
