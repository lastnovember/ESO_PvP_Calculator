High Seas of Tamriel is an in-game event based in the Gold Coast.
2

## Event Quests
The inaugural run of the event in 2026 has two quests:
- Raising the Riotous Redress
- Bounty of the Abecean Sea

You will be able to start "Raising the Riotous Redress" from the Crown Store's High Seas of Tamriel quest starter, or speaking with the Baron-Captain ghost found on an island to the west of Anvil. Once you have completed this story quest, the daily quest "Bounty of the Abecean Sea" will become available and can be started from the Riotous Redress Logbook.

## Deep Plunder Boxes

:A[n] [legendary/epic] parcel containing Style Pages, crafting materials, antiquity leads, and other assorted awards. A box of sunken treasure courtesy of Captain Guy Mard.
During the event, players will be able to earn Deep Plunder Boxes by completing the related quests. The first box you earn daily will be a legendary-tier Deep Plunder Box. Opening this box will give you 300 Trade Bars, along with a host of other rewards. Any subsequent boxes rewarded during that day will instead be an epic-tier Deep Plunder Box.

These boxes can hold many items, including:
- Abecean Sea Privateer Weapon Style Pagesonly weapons or also armor?
- Galen Seawater fragment for the Suncleft Grotto Ornaug mount
- Event-specific furnishings:
- Golden King of the Abecean Sea
- Book, Ship's Log
-
- Antiquity Leads
- Style Materials
- Crafting Materials
- Repair Kits (Equipment Repair Kit and Impressario's Group Repair Kit)
- Unopened Treasure Maps

Winning the ship-to-ship battle during the daily quest, will reward you Abecean Spoils of War chests that can be brought aboard your ship and have a different loot pool. See their related page for more information.

## High Seas of Tamriel 2026
September 30 - October 14, 2026

### The Impresario
The Impresario will sell bound pages for the Abecean Sea Privateer weapons style, along with the Floating Desires Petal fragment needed for the Grotto of Desires house.

### Golden Pursuits: High Seas of Tamriel
A related Golden Pursuit will run alongside the 2026 event between September 30, 2026 to October 14, 2026.

### Crown Store Exclusives
During the event, the Crown Store will sell various items matching the event's theme in September and October:
- The Fair Winds player house
- Statue, Bendu Olo
- Furnishing Pack: Maormer Boarding Party
- Music Box: Bleak Beacon Shanty
- Sea Witch Weather Totem
- Gonfalon Sea Cur
- Eltheric Revenant Arms Pack
