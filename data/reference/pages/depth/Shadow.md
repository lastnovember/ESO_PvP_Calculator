# Online:Shadow

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Shadow
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Online:Shadow (condition)

Shadow is one of three class skill lines available to Nightblades. It can be learned by other classes via subclassing. Abilities in this line focus on self-preservation through limiting enemies' attacks and hiding.

## Skill Perks
The Shadow skill line is immediately available to all Nightblades, and is leveled up by earning experience while Shadow skills are slotted. Skills include:
