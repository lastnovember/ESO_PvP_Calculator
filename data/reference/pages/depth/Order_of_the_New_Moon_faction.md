# Online:Order of the New Moon (faction)

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Order_of_the_New_Moon_(faction)
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Order of the New Moon (also called the New Moon Cult) is a Khajiiti Dragon Cult that worships a Dragon named Laatvulon. They are dedicated to helping Laatvulon's master Kaalgrontiid ascend to become a new moon called the Dark Aeon, and shift their worship to Kaalgrontiid after Laatvulon's death.

You infiltrate and "become" a member of the cult during the main questline.

## Shared Dialogue
During Order of the New Moon, several members of the Order will call out to you as you escape the fortress.
:"You won't leave this fortress alive!"
:"Stop them!"
:"By the New Moon's light, I will end you!"
:"Don't let them get away!"
:"Halt!"
:"Intruders! After them!"
:"You'll make a fine sacrifice to the New Moon!"
:"Stop them!"

During The Dark Aeon, several cultists will sacrifice themselves at the aeonstone ritual sites on Dragonhold.
:"Our lives for the Dark Aeon!"
