# Online:Sparks

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Sparks

Sparks (wolf)Update 6
Dual Wield-Sparks
Sparks was a skill in the Dual Wield skill line which blinded targets for four seconds. Its Heated Blades morph added an increase to the Weapon Power of the next attack, while the Ember Explosion morph caused nearby targets to gain a 40% miss chance for 4 seconds and dealt Physical Damage to affected enemies. The ability was removed in Update 6 and replaced with Blade Cloak.

## Patch Notes
ESO Patch Note|1.6.5|* This ability has been removed, and replaced with a new ability: Blade Cloak.
- When activated, Blade Cloak reduces area of effect damage taken by the caster by 20% for 15 seconds. Whenever the caster takes area of effect damage, this ability will deal damage to all nearby enemies. Blade Cloak can only fire once every 3 seconds.
- Quick Cloak: This ability provides the buff Major Expedition for 5 seconds.
- Deadly Cloak: When cast, this ability allows you to throw a dagger that deals high damage. This ability can only be used once, and only within ten seconds of the cast.
