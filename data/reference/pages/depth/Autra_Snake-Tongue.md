Autra Snake-Tongue is a skeleton assassin found at Skyshroud Barrow during the related quest. Autra is an undead ancient Nord charged with guarding the Snake Runestone needed to open the barrow.

## Related Quests
- What Waits Beneath

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Strike: A basic melee attack dealing low physical damage.
;Whirlwind: Autra Snake-Tongue spins around, dealing moderate physical damage to all targets as indicated by a red circle.
;Assassinate: Autra Snake-Tongue strikes at the target's back for moderate physical damage. This attack can only be performed if Autra Snake-Tongue is behind her target, and she will often use "Passing Through" to get there. Face toward Autra Snake-Tongue to prevent her from using this ability.
;Passing Through:Autra Snake-Tongue jumps above the target to get behind them.
;Roll Dodge: Autra Snake-Tongue can perform a roll dodge to avoid incoming damage.
;Backstabber:Autra Snake-Tongue has a passive ability that causes her to deal 20% more damage when attacking the target's back.

## Notes
- When you have cleared Skyshroud Barrow, all skeleton NPCs, including Autra, will become neutral and will not attack you anymore.

## Bugs
Despite being an elite rank enemy, Autra Snake-Tongue is vulnerable to crowd control.
