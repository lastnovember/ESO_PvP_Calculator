Baron-Captain Olsien Guy Mard was a Breton captain of the Riotous Redress, who sailed with the All Flags Navy. Prior to this, his ship sailed under a different name and was rumored to have engaged in piracy. He is also considered one of the historical members of the Guimard family.

During the High Seas of Tamriel event, his spirit is sighted on an island near Anvil on the Gold Coast. He is seeking to free his ship, his crew and himself from a curse.

## Quest-Related Events
You can start the quest either from the High Seas of Tamriel Quest Starter in the Crown Store, or finding Captain Guy Mard yourself on the island west of Anvil.

When you approach, you will find him looking out over the sea:

Talk with the spirit to learn who he is, and why he has appeared:

:"The bell of the ravishing Riotous Redress. My ship, long scuppered at the bottom of the sea. The bell's enchanted toll can coax her to the surface, good as new! It was just here on the beach. But some damnable Hadolid pilfered it and scuttled off!"
:What happened to it?
:"North by northwest. Dipped beneath the waves afore those Ayleid ruins. You seem apt and able. If you retrieve it and break our curse, I'll heap better than treasure upon you. Upon my honor as Olsien Guy Mard, Baron-Captain of the All Flags Navy!"
:Let me see what I can do.
:"Would that I could follow you to retrieve that bell, albatross. You'd sooner get the wind to hold a weapon than this old ghost. Ah, what a pity to be so cursed! Give that crab-man an extra crack on the head from Captain Guy Mard, will you?"

Heading north from the island, you will see Hadolids hanging around on the shores to the east of the Garlas Malatar ruins. A whirlpool beneath an overhanging rock will lead to a Deep Sea Cavern. Clackgrack the Covetous Claw can be found inside. In its pile of goods, you can find the Fractured Ship's Bell. When you return to the captain, he will greet you:
:Baron-Captain Olsien Guy Mard: "My albatross returns! And you have the bell? Let me see."

Show the bell to Guy Mard, he will realise that it needs further repairs:
:"You're back! But the bell is … missing its clapper? Was this from mishandling by that villainous crab-man? Or perhaps the storm ensured no easy journey to the surface. Well, there is no chance it will ring unless you see to its repair."

:"No, no, no. The bell is an antique! And holds a very complex enchantment. It needs the careful touch of one who specializes in repair of such ancient things. Not your average smith or spellcrafter."

:I'm not sure I know anyone like that. / Well, I'm not sure I know anyone like that.

Before you head to Kvatch, you can ask some questions:
:Avoid port towns? Why? / Why do you think I should avoid port towns when finding someone to repair the bell?

:What if people have questions about the bell?
::"Telling the truth won't hurt … but do try to answer so they won't seek to rob you. Just tell them the bell is an ancient artifact you need repaired in order to save the cursed souls of a very noble crew of privateers."
::Privateers?
::"Ahem! Yes! Baron-Admiral Olo was so keen on our joining his cause that he thought we might be compensated for our assistance. There is nothing wrong with that. He was a king. They do that sort of thing all the time."

While in Kvatch, Ulene Teneran at the Eight Blessings will direct you to Tuindoril. Tuindoril has done antique restorations before, and agrees to fix the bell and meet you on the island. When you return to the island there are several more people that before. You will find a few ghostly and skeletal Privateers hanging around, as well as Ulene, Tuindoril, Ukhul the Rower and Brennan the Monster-Tamer. Additionally, a large metal device will now be on the shore.

Talking with Captain Guy Mard before Tuindoril:
:"My albatross returns! I knew you could find someone to suit our needs. I just didn't anticipate someone who asks so many questions! I'm sure this Tuindoril has plenty to tell you. Just don't keep us waiting too long on that bell, if you please."

Speaking with Tuindoril, he will tell you about the Dwemer Diving Chamber and mentions that his research into the captain brought up some discrepancies in the story Guy Mard has told you. Speaking with Guy Mard before ringing the bell, he assures you he will answer any questions afterward:
:"This will work, albatross. Admiral Olo would never steer me wrong. I will answer any questions you might have once I see my Riotous Redress on the waves again."

Ringing the Restored Ship's Bell there will be an explosion of water from the nearby sea and the Riotous Redress will arise:
:Tuindoril: "Gods above and below! It worked!"
:Baron-Captain Olsien Guy Mard: "My blushing bride of the sea! I knew I could put my faith in you, albatross!"

If you leave and return to the conversation after saying "If you're sure. Thank you, Captain Guy Mard" before completing the quest:
:"The ship is yours, albatross. Those left on my crew that choose to stay will heed your orders until they see fit to move on. Sail her well."

:"Use that book as you will. Profit from your finds, if you like. What matters is that someone knows and remembers those of us that sailed together for Tamriel when it mattered most. May you fare well on your voyages … captain."

Completing the quest, you will receive the Ostentatious Pirate Wig and Hat and a Deep Plunder Box. Captain Guy Mard will then address his crew for some final words before they leave this plane:
