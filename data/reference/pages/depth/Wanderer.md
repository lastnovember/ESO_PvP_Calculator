# Online:Wanderer

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Wanderer

Online Champion 2.0 Summary
sign=Craft
type=passive
stagepoints=10
maxpoints=50
desc=Reduces the cost of Wayshrine usage by 10% per stage.

Wanderer reduces the gold cost of wayshrines usage by up to 50%. This is pretty handy as gold is an important resource and wayshrine usage gets quite costly if you teleport often. Remember that teleporting from one wayshrine to another costs no gold, same with teleporting to a player and a house you own.

With it maxed out, your cost should only be 73 gold.
