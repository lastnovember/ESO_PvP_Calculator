## Detailed Walkthrough

### Gristmung Hold: A casual stroll through a Daedric fortress
Previously after the defeat of Mannimarco and the Worm Cult at Mor Naril, you and your allies will have returned to the Vinutilmo Palace in Sunport for some final words and celebration. However, you may have noticed Darien Gautier walking out of the palace without a word after the quest was completed.

You can find Darien waiting outside the palace, and he'll call you over once he sees you:
:Darien Gautier: "Hey, got a moment? There's something important I want to talk to you about."

Speak with him to see what is going on. Darien is glad to be alive after dealing with Mannimarco, but he feels something is wrong. After he willingly gave up his light at the wraithforge, Darien is sure he should have died and been taken back by Meridia. But he is still alive, and at Mor Naril he found fragments of letter which spoke about his stolen light and Gristmung Hold. Darien believes Mannimarco had discovered something unknown about himself, and wants you to go with him to Gristmung Hold to investigate.

Before you leave Sunport, you can talk with Darien some more about what is going on. Darien will mention that ever since he was brought back by Gabrielle Benele, he has experienced memories that don't fit his life and doesn't know where they came from.
:Darien Gautier: "Let's head to Gristmung Hold. And hey. let's keep this just between us for now, all right?"

The closest wayshrine to Gristmung Hold is the Grand Juncture Pass Wayshrine in Eastern Solstice. Darien will be waiting for you in the cavern beneath the fortress, where you previously entered during The Worm Turns:
:Darien Gautier: "Hey, this is just like sneaking into Northpoint again! Except less vampires and more sewer water. That's a stink that's going to linger!"

Entering Gristmung Hold, you will find some remaining Worm Cultists and daedra which can be cleared up as you make your way to the laboratory and library area. The last time you were there, you found an inactive Soul Flayer. When you enter the library, Darien will comment:
:Darien Gautier: "A library! We were too busy last time to pay this area much mind. Let's rectify the situation, shall we?"

In the corner you can find an crumpled and unsent Missive to Mor Naril. The author confirms Mannimarco had been researching Darien's Light of Meridia and mentions they hid the research information away:
:Darien Gautier: "Mannimarco did hide something here! Well, a cultist did. A grin that never fades? Look for a face, friend!"

Looking around, you can find an Argonian skeleton on a table with a glowing skull. When you touch the echo gem within the skull, a sigil will appear on the floor nearby.

It will show a recorded memory of Mannimarco as he talks about Darien's Light of Meridia:

:Darien Gautier: "Hey, what did you touch and why is the center of the room glowing?"
:
:Mannimarco: "This light contains almost unlimited power. How did Meridia accomplish that? Did she use a part of herself to fuel it and hide its true nature? Even the knight was oblivious. But I see it. The light is a cage. And something inside stirs."
:Darien Gautier: "Power inside the light? I thought it was just Meridia's gift. If Mannimarco saw something else, what in Oblivion was inside me?"

At this, you can talk with the shaken Darien about what you just witnessed. He is annoyed that no one can seem to give him a clear answer of who or what he is. Darien speculates that there is something else that exists within his light without him being aware of it, and seems to have been waking up since he returned. As for the aforementioned radiant knights, Darien realizes that Mannimarco was talking about Meridia's daedra, and will want you to go to Broken Light Temple to confront them and get some answers.

### Broken Light Temple: Meeting with the Radiant Knights
Reaching Broken Light Temple, you can either use the Sunport Docks Wayshrine and head northwest across Sunport Bay, or get to the Shor's Stand Wayshrine and take the road northeast. When you arrive at Broken Light Temple, Darien can be located in two different locations. If you have previously completed the locations objective, he will be waiting for you on a ruined pavilion across from the entrance into the temple. On the other hand, if you have not the completed Broken Light Temple objective, Darien will instead be near the bridge leading up to the temple.

Either way, Darien will call out to you as you approach:
:Darien Gautier: "This is definitely a temple to Meridia. Subtlety's never really been her strong point. Ugh! Place feels like it's watching me."

Speak with Darien to go over the plan for once you get inside. If you previously completed Mystery of the Broken Temple, the bridge created by High Priest Eraamine's Translocator Rod will still be there. Otherwise, you will be prompted to complete the location's objective by finding someone who can assist before you can enter the temple. Once you have access to the temple, you and Darien can enter the Great Hall, where Darien will run towards the cynosure's doors:
:Darien Gautier: "Over here. This is it. Be ready for anything. Actually, with my luck, it will probably be worse than that."
:

When you follow Darien in, you will find him walking towards a group of Auroran Guardians led by Lortharil the Radiant:
:Darien Gautier: "I should have guessed, Aurorans. Meridia's way of saying she wants something from me. Doesn't she always?"
:
:Lortharil the Radiant: "Slayer of Mannimarco, savior of Solstice, the star that walks beside you is fractured. The Golden Knight returns, but not whole. The light within him stirs. It wishes to return home."
:Darien Gautier: "Joke's on you. I gave up the light. Gave it to my friend here who sent it straight through Mannimarco. If Meridia wants me back, she can't have me. I'm through being her-argh!"
:
:Lortharil the Radiant: "Stay your hand, champion. This pain is not our doing. It is the Golden Knight's burden, the light remembering what it is supposed to be."
:

You will need to speak with Lortharil about what is happening and what the Aurorans want. Lortharil lays it out for you, Darien's light cannot be removed as it is intertwined with him. He was meant to fulfil his purpose and return to Meridia's realm, so his soul could be preserved and reshaped for a furture purpose. The light within Darien was not meant to linger in the mortal world and he was brought back against Meridia's wishes. Lortharil and the guardians are there to make sure the light-and by proxy, Darien-are returned to Meridia.

In respose to this, you can try and bargain with or threaten Lortharil. But something that was happening behind you abruptly stops the conversation. Darien will have just killed the four Auroran Guardians around him, and his appearance has become akin to the Purified. He will then stalk towards Lortharil:
:Lortharil the Radiant: "What's happening? My fellow Aurorans! What did you do?"
:Darien Gautier: "I don't understand. What's happening to me? Burning skies … a tower falling. Why do I remember this? What did Meridia do to me?"
:
:Lortharil the Radiant: "No! This isn't what Meridia intended! This is-ugh!"

:Darien Gautier: "I'm me. I know I'm me. But these memories-rushing through my mind? They aren't mine! They can't be mine."

The power of the light and the unknown memories have started overwhelming Darien. At this point of the quest is the biggest divergence which depends on your previous experiences with Darien and how this crisis is handled:

- Lack enough shared memories.
- Only met Darien on Solstice.
- Replied with the "That's all I can remember[...]" prematurely.

### Back to Sunport

#### Explanations by Vanus Galerion

Once you are back in the city, you will need to find Vanus Galerion and talk to him about what happened. He can be found in the Palace District in the eastern corner outside the palace:
width=50%| Were able to help Darien:
width=50%| Unable to help Darien:

:Vanus Galerion: "Ah, champion. I expected you to turn up sooner."

:Vanus Galerion: "Skordo sits with Darien and the knight is stable-for now. As long as he keeps his distance from Meridia, the calm should hold. But I fear she won't make that easy."

Vanus's conversation will with you will differ depending on what happened at Broken Light Temple but the same points are covered. The deeper part of Darien's light was unsealed when Gabrielle brought him back to life, and proximity to Meridia's presence through places or people awakens it and the memories associated with it. However, Darien's attachment to his friends helps him control it, and the power will have settled down for now. You can also ask Vanus a variety of questions about what happened and what it means for the future.

If you were unable to help Darien in Broken Light Temple, Vanus will mention that Darien and Skordo are down at the docks and that you should speak with Darien before he leaves. However, if you were able to help Darien, he will be waiting at the city tavern for that drink he previously offered.

#### Sunport Docks: Off to Eyevea
In the case that you were unable to stop Darien at the temple, you can head to the southern-most pier of the Sunport Docks to say your goodbyes to Darien and Skordo.

When you arrive, Skordo will be convincing Darien that he is going with him no matter what:
:Darien Gautier: "Skordo, you don't have to do this. I'll be fine, Besides, I don't want to put you in any danger."
:Skordo the Knife: "Only thing in danger is your arse if you try to leave without me. We lost you too many times. The big bright lady comes for you, I'm stomping her lights out."

Speaking with Darien, he sees himself as a threat and doesn't know what would have happened if Vanus hadn't intervened at the temple. After discussing things with Vanus, Darien will be heading to Eyevea to stay out of Meridia's way and so Vanus can keep an eye on him and perhaps discover what is going on.

You can continue talking with Darien to complete the quest:
:"Time for Skordo and me to go find this magical little island to kick back on for a bit. Vanus didn't give us, uh, instructions. Really wish people would just talk clearly sometimes. Even our own damn mages love their riddles."
:Safe travels, my friend.
:"One can only hope, right? Here-take this. I don't have much to give, and I'm not even sure how I'm paying for this boat ride. But hey, don't worry about that. Really. Just don't forget me. And keep being you."
With the quest completion you receive the Exalted Bread memento and the ring named Darien's Promise.

Once the quest is completed, Darien will pat at his pockets:
:Darien Gautier: "Hey, Skordo. You don't happen to have a few coins to spare, do you? I think left my gold in the Colored Rooms."
:Skordo the Knife: "You still owe me for the last time we were at the tavern! Wait, why do we need money? Isn't Vanus covering this?"
:

#### The Sleepy Sloth: A promised drink
In the case that you successfully helped Darien regain control over his power, he will be waiting for you at the Sleepy Sloth Inn and Pub where you can finally get that drink Darien has promised you multiple times. Entering the Sleepy Sloth, you can find Darien in the room with the fireplace on the ground floor. As you approach, you will hear the tail-end of Skordo's explanation of what happened in Tamriel while Darien was gone:

:Skordo the Knife: "… and that's just the start of the wild adventures you missed, pal. The world's pretty much been in constant chaos. Felt like the longest year of my life."
:Darien Gautier: "I'm sorry I missed it, friend! The trouble we would've all gotten into together. It would have been glorious!"
:
:Skordo the Knife: "And look who it is! I'll leave you two to catch up. Saw a lass in the other room. Looked like she could kick my arse. Think I'm in love."
:

Speak with Darien to get that drink and have a long chat with him. You can ask him for more details on what happened at Broken Light Temple, as well as his time in the Colored Rooms. When you are ready to progress the quest, you can select the "So after all this time, we finally get to have a drink together." response. From here you and Darien can talk about your relationship, if you give the neutral or friendly reply to Darien you can complete the quest at the Sleepy Sloth:
:""
:So what are we drinking to?
:"To the heroes of Solstice! To never-ending adventures! To being free of that Oblivion realm and that crazy Daedric lady. And to finally sharing a drink with you. But most of all, to you, my friend. Huzzah!"

Darien will then give you the Exalted Bread memento and the ring called Darien's Promise.

However, if you reply to Darien with the FlirtyYeah, just you and me. Together. Nothing to worry about except … where do we go from here? response, a third ending will become available. Darien will admit that ever since he was trapped in the Colored Rooms, you were the top person he wanted get back to if he escaped, and that he has not been able to stop thinking about you. In response, you can either say you want to remain just friendsKind or reciprocate his romantic feelingsFlirty.

With the platonic reply, Darien will accept this and it leads to the above ending at the Sleepy Sloth. If you go with the romantic reply, there is a fade to black and you wake up in the bedroom of Laredon's House. Darien can be found nearby, half-naked and eating a loaf of bread.

Talk with him about what recently happened, Darien will launch into his speech that he had written and been practicising while you were asleep. It is about how he is a flawed person, but he loves you and should have told you a long time ago. You can respond by either replying in kindFlirty or disregarding the confession because you are hungry and the bread he has looks goodJoker. Either way, Darien will let you in on his current plans but will want to stay with you for a little while longer:
:"Meridia is going to want whatever is inside of me back. She's going to keep sending more Daedra my way. I need to find some answers. I have to ignore Vanus's warning if I want to figure out what or who I am. I can't keep running away."
:What are you planning?
:"I think it's time to visit dear old dad and ask him a few questions. Like who my mother was and why I can't remember her. But that's later. Right now, let's just enjoy this moment. Just me and you. Who knows when we'll get another chance like this?"

Completing the quest, you will receive the Exalted Bread memento and the ring named Darien's Promise.
