# Online:Shield Master

Online Champion 2.0 Summary
sign=Fitness
type=slotted
stagepoints=10
maxpoints=50
desc=Reduces the cost of your damage shield abilities by 2% per stage.

Shield Master reduces the cost of damage shield abilities by 2% per stage, up to 10%. This only affects abilities whose first stat is giving a damage shield such as Conjured Ward and Defensive Posture (and their morphs), and Hardened Armor.
Skills such as Magma Shell and Cleave (and its morphs) do not benefit from this Champion Node as their primary stat is damage dealing.
