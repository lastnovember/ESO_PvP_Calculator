Copper seams can be found near cliffs and mountains at Engraver level 2 or at character level 26-49. They can be harvested for three to four samples of the raw material, Copper Dust. Once ten samples of dust are collected, they can be refined into seven to ten Copper Ounces. Copper Jewelry is crafted from the refined copper ounces at any of the jewelry crafting stations.
