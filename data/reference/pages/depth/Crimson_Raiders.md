# Online:Crimson Raiders

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Crimson_Raiders
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Crimson Raiders are a group of pirates led by Captain Siro inhabiting the Crimson Cove, and sailing the seas near it.

While at first appearing to be simple pirates, it is revealed that they are in fact soldiers in the Imperial Legion masquerading as pirates in order to position themselves closer to Aldmeri Dominion lands without attracting too much attention. Septima Tharn of the Seventh Legion appointed Siro to lead them, with the task of stopping any Dominion ships, or even civilian ships, that pass near the cove.
