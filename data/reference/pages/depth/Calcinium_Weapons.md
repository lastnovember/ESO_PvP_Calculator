# Online:Calcinium Weapons

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Calcinium_Weapons
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

This is a listing of all standard Calcinium Weapons in the game. These can be crafted via Blacksmithing from Calcinium Ingots obtained by refining Calcinium Ore. It requires rank 6 in Metalworking. The listed damage values assume that the character is Level 50 CP160; lower level characters may see higher values.
