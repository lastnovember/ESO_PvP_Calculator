The 2025 Content Pass was the first and currently only seasonal content pass for ESO. Unlike with future Seasons from Season Zero onwards, purchasing the pass was required to unlock gameplay content, and it can therefore be considered a transitional phase between the previous paid Chapters and DLC model, and the current Seasonal model.

It follows the Seasons of the Worm Cult storyline and includes the following content releases:
- Fallen Banners dungeon pack (released March 2025)
- Western Solstice zone (June 2025)
- Battle for the Writhing Wall in-game event (October 2025)
- Feast of Shadows dungeon pack (August 2025)
- Eastern Solstice and story finale (Q4 2025)

Additionally, the 2025 Premium Edition includes everything listed above as well as access to the base game content and all previous chapters.

## Official Description
- A DIRECT SEQUEL TO ESO'S MAIN STORY - Travel to the mysterious island of Solstice and begin your new adventure right where The Elder Scrolls Online's main story left off. Though Molag Bal and Mannimarco were defeated in the Oblivion plane of Coldharbour, the Worm Cult has returned. Unravel their dire plans and uncover the secrets of the Writhing Wall, a colossal arcane barrier that divides the island. Tamriel hangs in the balance!

- A WHOLE YEAR OF ADVENTURES - Discover four major content updates, featuring a world-changing in-game event where you and your fellow players must stand against the Worm Cult and breach the Writhing Wall! Includes two dungeon packs (four new dungeons total), two-part story content, the in-game event, and a brand-new zone to explore.

- EXPERIENCE NEW CHALLENGES & STORIES - Venture into a new 12-person PvE trial, contend with dangerous new monsters and bosses, immerse yourself in captivating new delves and public dungeons, experience a host of new stand-alone quests and characters, and earn unique in-game rewards.

## Collectibles

Both the 2025 Content Pass and the 2025 Premium Edition unlock the following exclusive digital bonus content:
- Skulltooth Coastal Durzog mount - Prized by pirates and outlaws, this fearsome mount is always poised to strike.
- Golden Eagle pet - A majestic bird of prey, this pet is the perfect companion for hunters and nobles alike.
- Remnant of Meridia's Light memento - Shine forth with this memento, a beautiful remnant of Meridia's power.

The following pre-order collectibles are also included in both the 2025 Content Pass and the 2025 Premium Edition if ordered by June 2nd for PC/Mac or June 18th for Xbox/PS:

- 10-Year Lion Guard Steed mount - Charge into battle and explore Solstice atop this golden, faithful mount.
- 10-Year Anniversary Mudcrab pet - Scuttle across Tamriel with this gilded and gussied up mudcrab pet.
- Shell-Tide Beach emote pack (Beachside Bask, Beachside Drink, and Teeba Trick) - Make time for recreation on the beach with this emote pack.
- Mages Guild Recall customized actionqc - Use this Customized Action to blend teleportation and illusion at will.

Players received the pre-order collectibles immediately after pre-purchasing the 2025 content, while the rest of the bonus items were received with the launch of Seasons of the Worm Cult Part 1 in June.
Mages Guild Recall]] customized action is only available to those who pre-order the 2025 Content Pass or 2025 Premium Edition before May 7th, 2025.
