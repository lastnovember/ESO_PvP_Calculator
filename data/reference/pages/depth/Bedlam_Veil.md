loadtext=Filled with powerful weapons and objects of fame, the Bedlam Veil is the trophy house of Fa-Nuit-Hen. Very few visiting warriors are given access to the veil, though the ones that do describe it as a triumph of martial curation.
It is part of the realm of Maelstrom and is where Fa-Nuit-Hen keeps their trophies. A cult known as the Blind Path have invaded and are searching for something. The entrance is located northeast of Siege Road Wayshrine, along the walls of Old Orsinium.

A Chub Loon, daedrats, fetcherflies, frogs, scorpions, shroom beetles and skein spiders are found in numbers within. Hazards include bottomless pits, frigid waters, lava pools, and volatile poison.

It is included in the Scions of Ithelia DLC alongside Oathsworn Pit.

## Layout

### Foundation Core
Shroom Beetles scuttle throughout the Foundation Core.

#### Upper Foundation Core
This is where you'll start your delve. You can accept the quest from Fa-Nuit-Hen here.

#### Lower Foundation Core
Tutor Riparius's List is leaning against a tree. The portal to the Northern Foundation Core lies at the south end of the courtyard: alternatively, you can jump down the western cliff.

#### Northern Foundation Core
The first pull occurs here, consisting of a Blind Path Hollow Sentinel, two Blind Cult Frost Archers, and two Blind Path Champions. The portal to the next area opens once the enemies are dead. fetcherflies and a Lava Pool are here.

#### Western Foundation Core
This pull consists of two Blind Path Glaziers, three Glass Shards, and Blind Path Champion.plain

#### Southern Foundation Core

### Hall of Barons
Daedrats, frogs and skein spiders wander the grounds here. Two dread scorpions, a flesh atronach, and two Blind Path Geomancers make up the first pull. The first and pull feature an obelisk of light which works exactly the same way they do in Round 6 of Maelstrom Arena: Each Obelisk emits a glow that repels spider swarms, but these obelisks may be covered with webs spun by enemies. To remove them, you have to kill a Venomous Hoarvor near them, causing them to explode with acid. Alternatively, if a hoarvor is not killed near an obelisk, it will leave behind an acid sac. That sac can be thrown (using a synergy) at webbed obelisks to free them. If all the room's obelisks are freed, they will stun all enemies (including bosses) for 10 seconds, before being covered in webs again. Freeing the obelisks is very encouraged during Darkshard's fight, as the "Champion of Audacity" will enrage after a time which increase its damage done and only freeing the five obelisks will un-enrage it.

Heading north from the first room will lead you to a serene overlook where Fa looks over the vast expanse of Maelstrom while floating. A Challenger's Thoughts can be found propped against a tree near the edge of the cliff.

Heading through the south door and staying straight on that path will lead you to another outdoor overlook where you can talk to Fa and their tutor. As well, you'll find In Memoriam of the Gentle Warrior lying on the ground near Riparius. Heading east at the fork instead will bring you to the next add pull, consisting of a daedroth, two Blind Path Champions, and two scamps. Heading south from here will bring you to another dead end where you'll find In Memoriam of the Scorching Storm.

The northern path leads you to the next pull in an icy room: an ogre, ogre elder, and two troll breakers will contend with you here. A chub loon waddles across the slushy floor. Standing in the frigid waters will damage and snare you. Heading through the east corridor will bring you to a dead end where you can read In Memoriam of the Salt Spray.

The northern corridor will bring you to another fork: the western path brings you to Fa-Nuit-Hen's favorite dead end, where you'll find In Memoriam of the Ashy Wind. The eastern path heralds progression: three feral Argonians and a wamasu block the way forward. This room is similar to Round 7 of Maelstrom Arena: poisonous plants will sprout from the ground. Occasionally, they'll pop with a Poisonous AoE. If you're afflicted by their volatile poison, you need to enter the glowing pool of water to cleanse yourself. Allowing the feral Argonians to stand in the water while it's glowing will grant them invulnerability: keep the enemies out of the water.

A heavy sack can spawn in the northwest corner of this room. Frogs hop through the grass in this verdant chamber. The eastern hallway will bring you out into the Skyvault Foyer.

### Skyvault Foyer
An Argonian Behemoth, the Champion of Atrocity, and Maxus the Many hang from the ceiling at the far end of this room. The decor combines elements of the Spiral Shadows and the Vault of Umbrage.

## Dungeon Buffs
Three group wide buffs can be obtained in Bedlam Veil by finding and solving the three puzzles in the Skyvault. The buffs are as follows:
colspan=2|Buff | Description | Obtained By

Zephyrus Obscuris | When backed up against a barrier, you can destroy threats to the barrier and protect the land in front of you from additional attacks for a few seconds. Shares a cooldown with other magical protection charms.
|Solving the first puzzle

Ocular Disperser | Protect yourself from hazardous waves and watch as they part before you. Shares a cooldown with other magical protection charms. | Solving the second puzzle
