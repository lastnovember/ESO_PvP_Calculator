Brazen Pestilent Colossi are Flesh Colossi summoned during Brazen Plague of Crows's fight. One is summoned in the 50% Health phase and two are summoned in the 25% Health phase.

## Skills and Abilities
As elite rank enemies, Brazen Pestilent Colossi are immune to crowd control.
