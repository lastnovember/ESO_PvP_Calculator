# Online:Siphoning

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Siphoning
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Siphoning is one of three class skill lines available to Nightblades. It can be learned by other classes via subclassing. Abilities in this line focus on magicka attacks and debuffs.

## Skill Perks
The Siphoning skill line is immediately available to all Nightblades, and is leveled up by earning experience while Siphoning skills are slotted. Skills include:
