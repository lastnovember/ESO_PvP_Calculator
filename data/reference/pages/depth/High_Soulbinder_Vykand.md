High Soulbinder Vykand is a Soulbinder Dremora in charge of Black Gem Foundry, and serves as the final boss.

She oversees the production of weapons and armor affixed with black soul gems, and intends to supply the Worm Cult with these armaments for their invasion of Solstice. She is also experimenting with the process of refracting souls through black soul gems. While Vykand is the current leader of the clan, there are signs that her underlings want to overthrow her.

## Related Quests
- Deadly Armaments

## Skills and Abilities
;Acute Enervation: Vykand casts a DoT on a player and summons four Anguished Refracted Souls. One soul will appear green to the affected player. The other group members must kill this soul to cleanse the DoT.

### Hard Mode Mechanics
;Ominous Vision: At 60% health and below, Vykand pulls all players to the center of the arena. She will also play four different animations, one for each player. One section of the arena will be safe to stand in, though the specific section differs for each player. All group members must remember their respective animation and safe area.
;Annihilation: Vykand will once again pull all players to the center of the arena. She will only play one of her four animations. The player who saw this animation during Ominous Vision must communicate where their respective safe area is, or Vykand will wipe the group.
