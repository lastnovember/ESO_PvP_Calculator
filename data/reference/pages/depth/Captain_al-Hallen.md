Captain al-Hallen is a Redguard member of the Ra Gada bandits who led the attack on the town of Velyn Harbor. You'll find him in the lighthouse towards the end of the quest.

## Related Quests
- One Fell Swoop

## Skills and Abilities
Captain al-Hallen is accompanied by two Ra Gada enemies picked at random.
;Quick Strike:A basic melee attack dealing low physical damage.
;Thunder Hammer:Captain al-Hallen swings and shocks the ground in front of him as indicated by a red cone dealing moderate shock damage.
;Shock Aura:Captain al-Hallen shocks the ground around himself as indicated by a red circle dealing moderate shock damage. This can be interrupted to knock Captain al-Hallen off balance. This cannot be blocked.
;Lightning Form:Captain al-Hallen casts Lightning Form on himself, gaining Major Resolve (which increases his armor by 5948) while creating small areas under the target which after a small delay deal low shock damage.
;Uppercut:An Uppercut-like attack dealing moderate physical damage, knocking back and stunning. This can be blocked to knock Captain al-Hallen off balance instead.

## Ambient Dialogue
Inside the lighthouse, you can hear Captain al-Hallen trying to convince the keeper of the lighthouse, Bail at-Jabra, to let them climb up.

## Bugs
Despite being an elite rank enemy, Captain al-Hallen is vulnerable to crowd control.
