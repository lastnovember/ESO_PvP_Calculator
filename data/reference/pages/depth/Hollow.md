# Online:Hollow

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Hollow
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Hollow are stone constructs created by the Wilderking as a form of resurrection. Their purpose is to protect Greenheart from those with malicious intent who would seek to enter the Wilderking's domain.

They are unthinking in their duty, and as such they can be encountered as enemies in certain areas. They are considered undead (meaning they are affected by the Fighters Guild skill line) and drop Elemental Essence upon death.

## Notes
- The Hollow all use an Altmer base model and have a stone skin, similar to Fiend Statues. This is because, according to Sumiril's Book, the Hollow were Altmer construction workers that were killed during Ostion and the Valenwood's battle, who later worked together to resurrect them.
- They are all clad in Ancient Elf Style weapons and armor, which is obscured by their stone skin.
