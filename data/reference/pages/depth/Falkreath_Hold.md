## Hard Mode

Hard Mode is only available in the Veteran version of Falkreath Hold. When you get to the final boss fight against Domihaus, you have the option to activate a Warhorn to the right of the door after you enter the Jarl's Hall. Activating the Warhorn will destroy two of the wooden pillars prior to the fight beginning and will make the boss fight more difficult. If your group dies, the horn will need to be sounded again to reenable Hard Mode.

## Boss Mechanics

### Morrigh Bullblood

Highlight Box|
As a boss type enemy, Morrigh Bullblood is immune to all crowd control effects.

;Quick Strike: A basic melee attack dealing minor physical damage.
;Basilisk Powder:The enemy throws poison in a conical AoE in front of herself. Targets hit by the powder will take minor poison damage, get stunned and then take minor poison damage over time.
;Catapult: Morrigh Bullblood directs siege to fire upon the group dealing moderate flame damage as indicated by red circles. When she puts up a shield, hide inside it to avoid all the incoming siege fire.
;Health Potion:Morrigh Bullblood drinks a potion, restoring a bit of her Health (10,700 in Normal mode). This can be interrupted to stagger Morrigh Bullblood.
;Backstabber:Morrigh Bullblood has a passive ability that causes her to deal 20% more damage when attacking the target's back.

### Siege Mammoth

Highlight Box|
As a boss type enemy, the Siege Mammoth is immune to all crowd control effects.
;Swipe: A basic melee attack that deals minor physical damage.
;Charge: The boss charges forwards dealing moderate physical damage and knocking targets down as indicated by a red rectangle. The tank should position the boss facing the wall to prevent this.
;Sweeping Tusks: A charged heavy melee attack that deals high physical damage. This must be blocked or dodged if possible. Do not stand in front of the boss if you are not the tank.
;Stomp: At 50%, the Reachmen will light the boss on fire, adding a new attack to the fight. Occasionally, the boss rears up and stomps, dealing very high flame damage around itself. This must be blocked by everyone when it occurs.

### Necromancers

Highlight Box|
As boss type enemies, all three Necromancers immune to crowd control effects.

;: When defeated, instead of dying, these enemies kneel down and are surrounded with a glowing AoE. To continue, pick these up, and carry them to one of the three glowing graves in the arena. If this is not done fast enough, the affected enemy will heal to % of their total Health, and continue fighting.

Erbogar
;Defiled Ground: Summons an AoE below a player, which deals continuous fast ticks of direct minor magic damage and snares.
;Earthbane Arrow: Charges a shot that if performed sends out several AoE's in front of him, which deal minor damage and stagger if entered.
;Grip of Death: Summons an AoE below a player, which roots them if not avoided.
;Inhabiting Spirit
;: Shoots out an arrow, which upon impact splits into four traveling AoE's, that apply a strong flame damage over time effect.

Mochveda
;Ice-Shard Arrow: Ice projectile which deals minor frost damage.

Tuecille
;Death's Herald: Knocks back a player and deals a high amount of damage.
;Grip of Death: Summons an AoE below a player, which roots them if not avoided.

### Cernunnon

Highlight Box|
As a boss type enemy, Cernunnon is immune to all crowd control effects.
;Coldfire Blast: A basic ranged attack that deals minor frost damage.
;Clash of Bones: A charged heavy melee attack that deals very high physical damage and knocking the target back. This must be blocked by the tank or dodged if you are not the tank.
;Aspect of Winter: The boss summons meteors that will land on top of all players dealing high frost damage around them. When the rune appears around you, move away from your group members and hold block. Avoid stacking on top of each other during this attack as the damage from multiple meteors can be overwhelming.
;Brush with Death: The boss summons wraiths that will roam the arena, touching one will deal moderate frost damage and fear you momentarily. This will also summon some Ravenous Revenants to attack the group.
;Pull of the Underworld: When you enter the arena, you'll see a message declaring that the Void tugs at your soul. You will notice a barrier of swirling soul energy around the area. If you stand outside the designated area, you'll take high magical damage over time. To avoid this, stand in the eye of the soulstorm when this happens.
;Summon Minions: At 50% health, the boss disappears and resurrects the three necromancers from before. Kill them as you did earlier and the boss returns and the fight repeats.

----

### Deathlord Bjarfrud Skjoralmor

Highlight Box|
As a boss type enemy, Deathlord Bjarfrud Skjoralmor is immune to all crowd control effects.
;Quick Strike: A basic melee attack that deals minor physical damage.
;Fiery Breath: The boss breathes fire dealing moderate flame damage in front of itself as indicated by a red cone.
;Lava Whip: A charged heavy melee attack that deals moderate flame damage. This must be blocked if possible.
;Dark Talons: The boss roots all players around itself dealing moderate flame damage. Roll to escape this.
;Summon Draugr: The boss summons smaller draugr to attack the party. They are weak and will die easily, but are important for another mechanic in this fight.
;Deathlord's Fury: The boss channels a spell that will causes magical bolts to shoot out from all uncleansed draugr corpses around the arena. This deals high magical damage to all players that increases with each corpse and is unavoidable; even leaving 4 corpses up can cause lethal damage depending on the health of the group. To prevent this, have one or two people concentrate on purifying the draugr corpses using the buffs obtained from the urns at the corner of the room. If no corpses are present, this attack deals no damage.

----

### Domihaus the Bloody-Horned

Highlight Box|
As a boss type enemy, Domihaus is immune to all crowd control effects.
;Fire Bolt: A basic ranged attack that deals minor flame damage.
;Fiery Blast: A charged heavy ranged attack that deals very high flame damage. This must be blocked or dodged if possible if you are the dps.
;Ring of Fire: The boss summons flames on the ground behind the pillars dealing continuous direct high flame damage. This forces players into the center of the arena.
;Pillar Eruption/Pillars of Nirn: While Ring of Fire is active, the boss pulls all players towards him and snares them. Following that, nirncrux pillars will appear under each player's feet, dealing moderate flame damage (Pillar Eruption) and linger which deal continuous direct moderate flame damage (Pillars of Nirn). Keep moving and do not overlap with other players to avoid the red AOEs.
;Pillar Explosion: After a brief moment, the pillars explode dealing moderate flame damage.

;Pyroclasm: The boss summons flames around him dealing continuous direct high flame damage as indicated by a red circle. This forces players to stay behind the pillars on the edge of the arena.
;Stoke the Embers: The boss pounds the ground causing fireballs to shoot out from itself traveling along the ground dealing moderately high flame damage. If the outer edge is safe, players can hide behind the pillars to avoid the fireballs.
;Master of the Elements/Horned Monolith: At 80%, 60%, 40% and 20% health, the boss becomes invulnerable and summons a flame, frost, stone, and storm atronach to attack the group (Master of the Elements). They should be killed quickly before the boss wakes up, as dealing with the boss and the adds at once can be overwhelming. When Domihaus wakes up, he deals moderate generic damage in a small area around him (Horned Monolith) plain. Do note that as the boss's health depletes, he will become invulnerable for shorter amounts of time giving the group less time to deal with the adds.
;Shout of Desolation: This attack is denoted by an onscreen notification saying Domihaus is preparing to shout! At 70, 50, 30 and 10% Health, the boss shouts dealing very high amounts of oblivion damage. This attack is lethal on veteran mode. To prevent this, all players should hide behind an unbroken pillar in the hall. However, any of the pillars hidden behind will break, so to maximize the amount of hiding spots you have make sure all four group members hide behind the same pillar.
;Scorch/Slam/Snap/Spark: During the fight, each of the four totems in the room (Flame, Frost, Shock and Stone) shoot a projectile of their element at a random player, dealing moderate flame damage (Scorch), frost damage (Snap), shock damage (Spark) or earth damage (Slam).

Execute Phase
;Shield: Once below 20% health, he will alternate between using Shout of Desolation and summoning adds on a timer. Be sure to kill the boss before no pillars remain, in which case the next shout will wipe the whole group. In addition, on veteran hard mode once below 20% health, the boss will activate a damage shield that absorbs incoming damage. The group should save ultimates for the final phase to break his shield and burn him down quickly before the damage becomes overwhelming.

## Notes
- Falkreath appeared in Arena as Falcrenth, and also appears in Skyrim and Legends.
- Locations from Skyrim reappear, such as the Falkreath Watchtower and South Shriekwind Bastion; they can be seen in the distance but cannot be traveled to.
- Hakkvild's High Hall has a view of the city.
- Dialogue with Denskar Earth-Turner reveals that he lived here in his youth.
- Falkreath Hold was seen on a pre-beta map of the game as a planned full-sized zone, extending across the northern edge of Cyrodiil all the way from Craglorn to The Rift, much as it was seen in Skyrim.
