A House Diel Soldier is found next to both Benjamin Diel and Leonce Diel. Depending on your quest choice, two House Diel Soldiers are found loading the harpy eggs on a boat docked at Miltrin's Fishing Cabin with Lord Alain Diel if you agreed to give the eggs to him, otherwise if you choose to expose him, two House Diel Soldiers will accompany him.

## Related Quests
- The Dagger's Edge

## Quest-related Events
While you are distracting Lord Diel, Gelvin Feldrin will take down the two House Diel Soldiers with him quietly before releasing the Harpies.

## Notes
- Prior to Update 6, this NPC was named Diel House Guard.
