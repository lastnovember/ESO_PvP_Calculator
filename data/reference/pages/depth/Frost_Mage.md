# Online:Frost Mage

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Frost_Mage
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Frost Mages are a class of hostile humanoid NPCs that attack using frost magic abilities. They are easily identified as they carry a staff with frost effects at the end.

## Skills and Abilities
;Ice Arrow:A basic ranged attack dealing minor frost damage.
;Ice Barrier: The enemy summons an Ice Barrier that absorbs projectiles fired at it until destroyed. The enemy cannot be pulled if the wall stands between them and the target. The player should move so that there is a clear line of sight between themselves and the enemy.
;Winter's Reach:The enemy unleashes an icy whirlwind in the direction it is facing, dealing moderate frost damage and rooting all targets hit. The root effect passes through block. This can be interrupted to set the frost mage off balance.
;Run Away!:When too close to them, the frost mage backs up to put some distance between their target and themselves. This does not apply if the frost mage is near its Ice Barrier.
