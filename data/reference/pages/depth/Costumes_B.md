## Bardic Tavern-Singer's Dress

## Black Marsh Keeper
Black Marsh Keeper (male)

:This light outfit of tooled leather resembles those worn by the Keepers in Black Marsh, who assist at the Argonian Hatching Pools.

The Black Marsh Keeper is an Argonian-style costume worn by the Keepers of the Shell at Shadowfen's Hatching Pools. Part of the Tribal Shamans Pack, which was sold in the Crown Store for 700 from March 17, 2015 to July 31, 2016. It returned to the Crown Store for 1,200 from April 15 to April 19, 2021, and was offered to ESO Plus Members at a discounted 960. It is also available as a Superior-level reward in Wild Hunt Crown Crates.
;Dyeing:
:Slot 1: Robe, gloves, hat
:Slot 2: Trims
:Slot 3: Feathers
(The ornaments on the hat, chest, gloves, and belt are not dyeable)

## Blacksmith
Blacksmith (male)

:This heavy leather smith's apron and gauntlets denotes a master of the forge.

The Blacksmith costume was sold in the Crown Store for 400 from March 17 to May 26, 2015. This costume was on sale for 200 from May 21 to May 26, 2015 before being removed from the Crown Store. The Blacksmith costume also dropped from Undaunted Reward Boxes during 2018's Undaunted Celebration, and is now available as a Superior-level reward in Gloomspore Crates.
