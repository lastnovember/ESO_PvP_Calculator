Captain Vrol is a Sea Giant warrior and the second boss of Kyne's Aegis.

## Related Quests
- The Fight for Kyne's Aegis

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Strike: A basic melee attack dealing high physical damage.
;Thunderous Bash: A heavy shield bash that deals very high physical damage and shock damage and stuns if it hits the target. This attack can be blocked.
;Frigid Fog: Captain Vrol points his hammer at a target, putting a tracking frost field around that that deals continuous high frost damage to them and nearby allies that stand in the tracking circle.
;Shocking Harpoon: Captain Vrol hurls a Sharpened Conduit into the arena floor, which then tethers and pulls multiple targets towards it, while inflicting them with continuous very high shock damage.
;Summon: Captain Vrol summons a Storm Twin, a copy of himself that uses the same abilities.
;Vrolsworn: During the fight, Vrolsworn Conjurers will appear one at a time on the boat near the arena and summon portals that spawn reinforcements, in the form of Half-Giant Apothecaries, Half-Giant Bulwarks, and Half-Giant Harpooners. The most vital summons are the Bulwarks as they can make Captain Vrol invulnerable when standing near him.
:There are two options to dispatch the conjurers. A player can take a portal to the ship to battle them directly, or you can use the ballista. Take Ballista Parts (found in barrels at the edges of the cove furthest from the water) to repair the Broken Ballista and then fire ballista bolts at Vrol's ship to kill them. Two shots are required to kill them.
:When Captain Vrol reaches 50%, instead of one conjurer, two Vrolsworn Fire Shamans will appear, and behave in the same way as those in the Yandir the Butcher fight. Three shots are needed to kill them instead of two.
