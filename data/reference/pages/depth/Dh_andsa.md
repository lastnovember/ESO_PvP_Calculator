Dh'andsa is a Redguard member of the Ra Gada pirates found in the town of Velyn Harbor. You'll find her on board The Scourge's Mark.

## Related Quests
- The Unkindest Cut

## Skills and Abilities
Dh'andsa is accompanied by two Ra Gada Bravos.
;Flare:A basic ranged attack dealing minor flame damage.
;Quick Strike:A basic melee attack dealing minor physical damage.
;Ice Cage:Dh'andsa summons a ring of frost around herself dealing continuous direct moderate frost damage to all targets in the red circle. This cannot be blocked.
;Impending Storm:Dh'andsa charges up and releases lightning from herself as indicated by red circles dealing moderate shock damage to all targets hit. This can be interrupted to set Dh'andsa off balance. This cannot be blocked.
;Empower Weapon: Flame:Dh'andsa infuses her weapon with flame which changes her "Quick Strike" attack into a melee "Flare" attack.
;Flare:Dh'andsa hits the target with her weapon while spinning, dealing minor flame damage.
;War Horn:Very rarely, Dh'andsa blows a war horn, increasing the Max Health of her nearby allies and herself by 15%.

## Bugs
Despite being an elite rank enemy, Dh'andsa is vulnerable to crowd control.
