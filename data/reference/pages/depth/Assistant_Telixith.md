Assistant Telixith is an Argonian keeper who is responsible for tending to the eggs found at the Hatching Pools. Alongside Assistant Ahaht-Ei, she is an assistant to Keeper Uxith-Ei.

## Related Quests
- Keepers of the Shell

## Quest-Related Events
Assistant Telixith is first encountered by the nests underneath the west side of the great Hist tree, clearly upset.
:"Have you come to stop the Dominion soldier who devours my kin?"
:What? No, Keeper Uxith-Ei asked me to find you.
::"Keeper Uxith-Ei can go count his scales. A Dominion dryskin cooked and ate one of our unhatched eggs! Though I'm sworn to the Shell, I will not allow him to harm another."
::Your duty is to Keeper Uxith-Ei.
:::"What good is the hatching ritual if these despicable fools eat our young? Tell me, what platitudes would Keeper Uxith-Ei say to that? This dryskin must be stopped for good!"
:::[Persuade] Calm yourself. If you strike him, you put every keeper at risk.
::::"I wasn't thinking of the others, I …. Your words humble me. I'll return to Keeper Uxith-Ei shortly. He'll know how to stop this madness, I'm sure of it."
:::Tell me what I can do.
::::"Make the egg he consumed his final meal! Spill his blood upon the ground, where it may feed the roots of future generations. Show we fight to protect our young."
::::I'll stop him from eating any more eggs.
::::"He must pay for his crimes!"
::What do you have in mind?
::"Make the egg he consumed his final meal! Spill his blood upon the ground, where it may feed the roots of future generations. Show we fight to protect our young."

Returning after killing Fimrimion:
:"Thank you. Those people are savages. I hear they eat their own dead. I'll return to Keeper Uxith-Ei shortly."

If you speak to her at the base of the Hist after lighting the braziers atop both xanmeers:
:"Since the first egg cracked, the memories of the Hist have shown us our sacred role as keepers. Keeper Uxith-Ei is eldest amongst us, and his wisdom will lead us through the Dominion menace. It must."

If you speak to her again, you can consider helping her with killing the Dominion soldier who ate the eggs:
:"Have you come to stop the Dominion soldier who devours my kin?"
:What? No, Keeper Uxith-Ei asked me to find you. (Leads to quest dialogue on how to deal with the soldier.)

After she's convinced to join in the hatching ceremony, she can be found next to Assistant Ahaht-Ei. They will begin the hatching ritual.
:Keeper Uxith Ei: "The time of hatching has come. We gather by the boughs of the Hist...."
:Keeper Uxith Ei: "What have they... no, this cannot be!"
:Keeper Uxith Ei: "Bark and birth! What have they done?"
Assistant Ahaht-Ei dies on the spot.
:Assistant Telixith: "Ahaht-Ei! No!"
:Keeper Uxith Ei: "The Dominion.... They'll doom us all!"

Speaking to her after escaping from the pools with as many unhatched eggs as you can carry, she will realize the Dominion's control of the Mnemic Egg has sever the Argonians' link to the Hist:
:"He's gone... he died for the eggs.... Did it work? Are they safe?"
:I saved some of the eggs.
:"Some, you say. They murdered our hatchlings, our children. Why? But you saved some. That is … it's a good thing. It must be a good thing."
:What will you do now?
:"We must restore our link to the Hist. Without it, the Hatching Pools will never see our young rise from its cool waters. The Dominion could only cause such a thing with a powerful artifact."
:The Dominion possesses something called the Mnemic Egg.
:"Then the danger is graver than I thought. The Mnemic Egg bears the memories of the Hist. Severing our link is the least they could do with it. Come, we must leave this place!"

She will follow you out of the Hatching Pools. When you reach the top of the stairs, she breathes a sigh of relief seeing the Pact reinforcements:
:Assistant Telixith: "Vicecanon Servyna! Thank the Hist!"

Speaking with her after the quest:
:"The keepers will remember you for what you've done. The Hist will remember you."
