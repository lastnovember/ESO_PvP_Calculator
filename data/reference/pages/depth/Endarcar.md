Endarcar is a High Elf warrior and member of the Stirk Fellowship who can be found on Stirk or at the command tent at the Sunport Rampart Camp on Solstice.

## Dialogue
At the Sunport Rampart Camp, he has unique ambient dialogue:
:"The cultists are everywhere! We need more forces."

:"Don't let them see you tremble. They thrive on fear."plain

:"Prince Azah told us to hold the line, and that's exactly what I mean to do."
