# Online:Deathbringer Clan

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Deathbringer_Clan
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Deathbringer Clan is a clan of Dremora that serves Molag Bal, led by Valkynaz Seris. The clan rose to prominence through its betrayal of the Foolkillers Clan. They command the Reaver Citadel and guard the way to the Lord of Schemes' inner sanctum.
