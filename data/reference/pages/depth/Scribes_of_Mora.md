# Online:Scribes of Mora

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Scribes_of_Mora
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Scribes of Mora are a cult dedicated to the Daedric Prince Hermaeus Mora that is based in the Scrivener's Hall in the Rift. The group acts as librarians and scribes, and aim to document everything in existence. The group leader's title is known as the "Magnastylus" and only they have the authority to wield the Itinerant Staff.

In recent times, they will be going through a civil conflict and planned allegiance change that is fought within their stronghold.
