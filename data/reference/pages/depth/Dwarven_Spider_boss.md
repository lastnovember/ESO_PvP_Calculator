This Dwarven Spider is a powerful Dwarven Spider which can be found in the Scraps, where it serves as the boss delve. Outside of having higher health, and appearing with "Static Field" already active (but bugged), it uses the same abilities as standard spiders.

It has a chance to drop a lead for the Dwemer Body Marking collectible.

## Related Quests
- Dwemer Disassembly

## Skills and Abilities
;Shocking Touch: A basic melee attack that does low shock damage.
;Double Strike: A charged melee attack that does high physical damage twice. This attack can be blocked to set the spider off balance and makes it only hit once.
;Overcharge: The spider explodes upon death, dealing moderate shock damage to all targets around it as indicated by a red circle.
;Summon: The spider walks over a buried automaton and digs it out so that it can aid in the fight. These can be other Dwarven Spiders, Dwarven Arquebuses or Dwarven Spheres.

## Notes
- It has a much faster respawn rate than standard for delve bosses.

## Bugs
The boss's Static Field doesn't affect the automatons the boss digs up, resulting in them not becoming overcharged.
The boss's Static Field has a chance of disappearing instantly at the start of the fight.
