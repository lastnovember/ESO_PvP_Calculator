# Online:Backstreet Gang

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Backstreet_Gang
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Backstreet Gang is a gang of petty thugs active in Wayrest. Following the Midnight Union's fall in power and notoriety, the group started getting bolder, going as far as intimidating and robbing people in broad daylight within the city's streets.
