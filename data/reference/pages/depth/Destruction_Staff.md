# Online:Destruction Staff

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Destruction_Staff
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

colspan=2|

Destruction Staff is one of the six weapon skills available in Elder Scrolls Online. You can advance through this skill line by equipping a Destruction type staff.
None of its skills actually deal Magic Damage per se, instead each staff changes the skills based on the elemental type associated with the staff, those being:
:Inferno Staves will only deal flame damage;
:Ice Staves will only deal frost damage;
:Lightning Staves will only deal shock damage.
One example of this is the skill Destructive Touch, whose damage type changes with the type of staff, but also applies a different effect based on it. An Inferno Staff causes an enemy to be knocked back, an Ice Staff immobilizes an enemy, and a Lightning Staff deals damage in an area.

## Learning
The Destruction Staff skill line is granted the first time you kill an enemy with a destruction staff equipped. You can increase your skill by engaging in combat with a destruction staff equipped, or by having Destruction Staff skills slotted. The more skills you have slotted, the faster your skill will increase. High Elves are naturally gifted with Destruction Staves, and will learn the ability more quickly than other races. In addition, you can earn free levels in the skill line by reading Skill Books, which may be found randomly on bookshelves anywhere. The skill books which increase Destruction Staff are:

- Destruction or Distraction
- A Hypothetical Treachery, Part 1
- Oshgura's Destruction Journal
- Prudence in Practice
- The Vanishing Crux

## Skill Perks
Destruction Staves have an associated skill line that is revealed through use of a Destruction Staff. Known abilities are listed below.

- While the descriptions for Elemental Storm, Wall of Elements, Destructive Touch, and Impulse say they deal Magic Damage, when slotted, this will change to the type of elemental damage (Flame, Frost, Shock) that matches your staff

### Other Sets
- Wearing five pieces of the Spider Cultist Cowl set from the Fungal Grotto I and II group dungeons increases the Weapon Damage and Spell Damage of your Destruction Staff abilities.
- Wearing five pieces of the Red Eagle's Fury set from the Red Eagle Redoubt crafting site increases the Weapon Damage and Spell Damage damage of all your weapon abilities, Destruction Staff included, but it also increases their cost.
- Wearing five pieces of the Daring Corsair set from the Cat's-Claw Station crafting site decreases the Stamina and Magicka costs of all your weapon abilities by 10%, and grants you Minor Heroism for 8 seconds after you use a weapon skill.
