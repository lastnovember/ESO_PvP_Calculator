Husk Stalkers are Dark Elf Plague Husks who can be found north of Old Ida's Cottage. Norgred's Pants can be found on a fallen husk. There is also a husk inside of the cottage, where it is standing overtop the Axe of Halmer Stormhand.

## Related Quests
- The Naked Nord

## Skills and Abilities
As they are considered undead, Husk Stalker are especially vulnerable to Fighters Guild skills.

;Strike: A basic melee attack that does minor physical damage.
;Vomit: The husk vomits, doing moderate disease damage to all enemies as indicated by a red cone.
;Devastate: A charged melee attack that does moderate physical damage and stagger. This attack can be blocked to set the enemy off balance.plain
;Desecrated Ground:On death, the undead rarely desecrate the ground under them as indicated by a red circle. This does moderate damage over time and snares all targets as well as healing undead enemies within the red circle.
