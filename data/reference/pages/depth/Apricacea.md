Apricacea is a spriggan boss found at Mist Mirror Refuge, the lake near Thalara's Winery. She emerges from the waterfall on the eastern side.

## Skills and Abilities
;Lacerate: A basic melee attack that deals minor physical damage.
;Nature's Swarm: The spriggan sends a swarm of insects forward, indicated by a red cone, dealing moderate magic damage.
;Control Beast: The spriggan channels a buff on its summoned animal, or any nearby allied creature, increasing the target's damage done by 100% and their Physical Resistance and Spell Resistance resistance by 4,550. This can be interrupted to set the spriggan off-balance.
;Healing Salve: When hurt, the spriggan heals itself. The heal ramps up over time, and will heal for a large amount if not interrupted, which then sets the spriggan off-balance.
;Backstep:When too close to its target, the Spriggan moves away to put some distance between its target and itself.
;Summon Beast: The spriggan summons an animal to assist it in combat. The animal type depend on the stats and location of the summoner†. The summons spawn with the Spriggan's current Health, for a maximum of 80% of the Spriggan's Max Health and have extreme damage resistance during the summoning animation. The Spriggan's Physical Resistance and Spell Resistance are reduced by 9,100/18,200 (Group Dungeons and Trials) while a summon is active as seen by a blue glow on them. If multiple Spriggans are in combat, only one can have an animal, however if multiple decide to summon an animal at the same time, each one will summon one. (Bugged, see #Bugs)
