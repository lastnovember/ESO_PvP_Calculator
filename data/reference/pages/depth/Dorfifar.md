Dorfifar is a Khajiit vampire who can be found at the Starlit Plaza. He will send you out on a daily quest to kill beings in the Night Market districts.

## Related Quests
- Blood on the Sands

## Quest-Related Events
As you approach you may hear him mutter:
:"Dorfifar wants them dead!"

When you speak with him before starting his quest, he will say one of the following:
:"Want gold? Or favor with your faction? Kill for this one, and he can tell your patron how skillful you are."

:"This one needs killers. Warriors. Fighters. Whatever name you prefer. Dorfifar has bloody work to give out. He could make sure that your patron knows about your successes."

:"If Dorfifar asked you to kill something-many somethings in fact-would you do it? He would owe you a favor. Maybe spread some words to the right ears about how skilled you are."

Speak with him to learn what he wants you to do:
:Kill what, exactly?
:"Anything in the districts. As many creatures as possible. What Dorfifar seeks is more sustaining than blood. He seeks revenge! Dorfifar will see every enemy punished for what they did! The more you kill, the more he will reward you."
:What did they do?
:"Do you see his eye? One of those cowardly creatures blinded Dorfifar when he was feeding. He cannot risk losing his other eye. Dorfifar barely escaped with his life. Now he pays you to end theirs. What do you say? Will you kill for Dorfifar?"
:All right, I'll kill enemies in the districts for you.

After you start the quest, he will say one of the following:
:"You won't forget about Dorfifar in your bloody rampage, yes? Our agreement holds if you make his enemies suffer in his name. Kill many. Kill all. Kill for Dorfifar."

:"You know what to do. Hunt. Fight. Kill. Survive. Return drenched in the blood of Dorfifar's enemies. The more you kill, the more this one will reward your efforts."

:"It's unfortunate that no one is allowed to harm another in the plaza. The districts are much more enjoyable in that respect, but Dorfifar cannot take advantage of the chaos. So he leaves it to you. Kill his enemies. All of them."

Before you leave, you can ask Dorfifar why he is sending you rather than getting revenge himself:
:Why don't you get your own revenge?
:"Go back in there? Dorfifar is not so foolish. He does not care to risk his life, not when he can make you-and those like you-the instrument of his revenge. More warriors. More corpses. Less danger for the half-blind vampire, Dorfifar."
:Are you scared?
:"Are you not? Perhaps this is because you are factioned. Dorfifar does not form alliances. His knees shake with fear and rage, but they do not bend. This work will not cause issues with your patron. They graciously agreed to help repay your efforts."
:How did you convince them to do that?
:"It was not difficult. Kharakhal took one look at this one's missing eye and understood. Molto heard Dorfifar's plan to incentivize killing our enemies before seeing the value. And Tachien couldn't resist after the other two agreed."
:You played them off each other.
:"You could see it that way. Or you could say they lent their support because winning battles make their factions look better. So the more you kill, the better they seem. No one loses. Except Dorfifar's fetid enemies. Which is his goal."

While in the Night Market districts, you will at first be asked to kill 25 enemies. You can either return to Dorififar once this done, or earn a better reward by killing more enemies. The amounts for the tiers are as follows, 25, 50 and 75 The maximum for the quest is 150 enemies in total, and once you decide are done, you can return to Dorfifar.

He will say one of the following when you greet him:
:"You arrived and the stench of battle wafted here. It's delicious. But was it enough? Tell Dorfifar. Did you wreak havoc on his enemies?"

:"Finally. This one thought you suffered the same fate he did in the districts. Is it done?"

:"You return. Did you kill for this one? You smell like you did."

Talking to Dorfifar after completing the daily quest:
:"The annoyance of his station no longer plagues Dorfifar. He is satisfied. Vengeance is a consistent friend to the wronged. It will return. If you do as well, maybe you can help Dorfifar with his battles."

:"This one smelled his fill of violence for the day. Come back when his appetite returns."

:"Are you still suffering from bloodlust? This one's needs are met. He has no further use for the metallic scent of death. Return once your good work has worn off."
