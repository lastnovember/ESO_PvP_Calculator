Ajorda is a Nord soldier located south of Vernim Woods and east of Mammoth Ridge. She will only appear if you have not accepted Dangerous Union from Bermund, Dajaheel, or Kragar. Will she show up if you have the quest To Vernim Woods?

## Related Quests
- Dangerous Union

## Quest-Related Events
When you approach her, she will call out:
:Ajorda: "Stop, traveler."
When you speak to her:
:"Fair day, traveler. Best steer clear of here. Vernim is not safe."
Regardless of whether or not you've actually fought any Reachmen before, you will respond:
:I know. I've fought Reachmen before.
:"Well, it's not just the Reachmen. The Worm Cult haunts these woods. They perform foul rituals amid the old stones."
:The cult? Here?
:"I know. It gets worse. They're doing … something to the Reachmen in those rituals. I have to keep my post. Report in to Dajaheel. Tell her I sent you."
:I'll speak to her.
Speaking to her again:
:"Dajaheel's at the main camp, just outside the treeline. She'll set you aright. Good luck.
