### Abecean Ratter Cat

:The Abecean Ratter is famed from Hegathe to Woodhearth as a prime rodent-catcher.
The Abecean Ratter Cat was available in the Crown Store for 400 from July 23, 2015 to December 11, 2017. It is now available as a reward for reaching level 24. Its default name is "Caramel".

### Abecean Ship Rat

:Superstitious sailors along Abecean trade routes like to keep one caged light-furred rat aboard, claiming it knows when pirates draw near.
The Abecean Ship Rat is an exotic rat pet that is available as a superior-tier reward in Warrior Wave Crates. Its default name is "Lucky."

### Abyssal Bunny

:"I wished for ears that could listen to dreams. He gave them to me. How lonely was I that I needed to be part of your most private wonders? Now I wander a forest of waking horrors. Please, take care of him. It was my fault."-Note found in Jasper's basket
The Abyssal Bunny was available in the Crown Store for 2,000 alongside the Shor's Fox mount as part of the Hare and Fox Bundle. The bundle was available from March 7 to March 14, 2024. Its default name is "Jasper".

### Abyssal Tomeshell

:"Love books, do you? What if they had tentacles and an eye? The gift of flight? Like them as much then? Yes? Oh, delightful! Here, befriend my beloved Philosophicus Aeternum-Philo for short. Expand its knowledge of Nirn."-The Archivist of the Lost
The Abyssal Tomeshell is a tomeshell pet that is available as an Epic-level reward in the Unfeathered Crown Crates. Its default name is "Philo".

### Akaviri Potentate Bear Cub

:"The cubs trained into steeds by the Potentate in 2E 23 proved so successful that this practice continues today. Seeing one melted my heart-that tail tassel! In their training barding, they walk through Leyawiin's streets each week."-Mirannah of Gwylim
The Akaviri Potentate Bear Cub was available as a guaranteed reward in Akaviri Potentate Preview Crates. It was also available in the Crown Store for 1,000 from March 1 to March 8, 2021. Its default name is "Tailbobber".

### Akaviri Potentate Guar Calf

:"The Potentate first mounted up on guar steeds in 2E 27 under the Red Mountain's shadow, but they trained guar calves to the saddle just outside Mournhold. One barded calf demanded nose-pats from me when I visited-I couldn't resist."-Mirannah of Gwylim
The Akaviri Potentate Guar Calf was available as a guaranteed reward in Akaviri Potentate Preview Crates. It was also available in the Crown Store for 1,000 from February 22 to March 1, 2021. Its default name is "Scritchnose".

### Akaviri Potentate Pony

:"In 2E 5, companies using the new enchanted barding designed by Versidue-Shaie's battlemages received their orders: Hunt and kill Reman loyalists. Though initially only horses, ponies and more earned the special barding and training."-Mirannah of Gwylim
The Akaviri Potentate Pony was available as a guaranteed reward in Akaviri Potentate Preview Crates. It was also available in the Crown Store for 1,000 from March 15 to March 18, 2021. Its default name is "Daintyhoof".

### Akaviri Potentate Sabre Cat Cub

:"Before heading to Skyrim in 2E 7, the Potentate had to train sabre cat cubs to the saddle. Watching these cubs pounce while wearing their training barding all these many years later almost made me lose my composure and squeal."-Mirannah of Gwylim
The Akaviri Potentate Sabre Cat Cub was available as a guaranteed reward in Akaviri Potentate Preview Crates. It was also available in the Crown Store for 1,000 from March 8 to March 15, 2021. Its default name is "Threadchaser".

### Akaviri Potentate Wolf Cub

:"Talking with the trainers of the wolf riders that Versidue-Shaie established in 2E 4 allowed me to see the armored pups at play. I adored how they bonelessly stretched on the ground. Hard to believe they grow to be so fierce!"-Mirannah of Gwylim
The Akaviri Potentate Wolf Cub was available as a guaranteed reward in Akaviri Potentate Preview Crates. It was also available in the Crown Store for 1,000 from February 15 to February 22, 2021. Its default name is "Puddlejumper".

### Alabaster Jerboa

:Need some happiness in your day? The Alabaster Jerboa is your answer. Its hopping and air-dancing acrobatics can bring a smile to even the grumpiest Dark Elf.
The Alabaster Jerboa was available in the Crown Store for 700 from August 15 to August 19, 2019. It returned from May 14 to May 18, 2020. It is also available as a Superior-level reward in Nightfall Crates. Its default name is "Oochee".

### Albinoscript Fangtome

:Fangtomes know not the importance or content of the books they inhabit. In the event that your rare first editions or vital academic texts have gained a tenant, simply befriend the tome via ample spine massages and a gentle ruffling of its pages.
The Albinoscript Fangtome is a tomeshell non-combat pet. It was first available in the Crown Store for 1,000 from September 14 to September 21, 2023. Its default name is "Grim Moira".

### Alcaire Blue Cockeral

:The cockerels of Alcaire Castle uphold their noble duty and make as much noise during daybreak as possible. Their screeching continues well into the morning, waking any lucky enough to miss out on their earliest morning calls.
The Alcaire Blue is available as a Superior-level reward in Sunken Trove Crates. Its default name is "Stunning Peter".

### Alik'r Dune-Hound

:The Dune-Hound is the loyal dog of the Redguard hunters of the Alik'r.
The Alik'r Dune-Hound was available from the Crown Store for 400 from March 17, 2015 to June 3, 2017. It is now available as a reward for reaching level 24. Its default name is "Sandy".

### Alinor Ringtail

:"Cute little thing, isn't it? 'Course, what I care about is that a Ringtail, properly trained, can get into places no one else can, and carry out small items in their cheek pouches. Lost goods, as it were, or misplaced valuables. Right?" -
The Alinor Ringtail was available from the Crown Store for 700 from August 16 to August, 2018. It returned to the store from October 10 to October 14, 2019, where it was offered at a discounted 560 to ESO Plus members. It is also available as a Superior-level reward in Baandari Pedlar Crates. Its default name is "Desperado".

### Alliance War Dog

:War dogs can be found in the armies of all three Alliances, mainly in the capacity of aides to the military police. They are as loyal to their masters as they are savage to their enemies.
The Alliance War Dog can be acquired as part of the Any Race, Any Alliance Bundle. Previously available from the Crown Store on their own for 1,000, it is now part of the Any Race, Any Alliance Bundle for 2,000. The appearance of the dog is specific to your character's alliance. When placed in a home, the dog's appearance depends on the allegiance of any player looking at it, so the same dog can simultaneously appear different to multiple players. Its default name is "Bruiser".

### Amaranthine Pocket Watcher

:"My notes indicate these vibrantly hued watchers once inhabited the ruins of Zaintiraris until an unfortunate explorer set them free. Given their color and origin, I suspect Sheogorath holds some sway over them. Also unfortunate."-Warlock Aldaale
The Amaranthine Pocket Watcher was available from the Crown Store for 200 from September 10 through September 12, 2020. Its default name is "Everstare".

### Amber Ash Hopper

:Native to the upper Ashlands and the slopes of Red Mountain, the perky Amber Ash Hopper is a favorite pet of Dunmer expatriates, as it's relatively low maintenance (it digs its own hole in the ground), and it reminds the Dark Elves of home.
The Amber Ash Hopper was available in the Crown Store for 700. It is also available as a Superior-level reward in Xanmeer Crates. Its default name is "Ald'Skippy".

### Amberdust Wamasu Hatchling

:"I found the wamasu that … resisted my care. It bit my hand. It's blue! Well, more of a tealish color. Did the druid's magic affect the creature? Or was it some other factor? I think I'll release more of them!"-Valerine Cadiou, Master Beast Tamer
The Amberdust Wamasu Hatchling is available as an Epic-level reward from Dark Chivalry Crown Crates. Its default name is "Aquamarine".

### Ambersheen Vale Fawn

:Its amber-gold glimmer creates a warmth that one stablemaster states, "Can get you through chilly nights under the Skyrim stars." All it takes to keep one of these vale fawns at your side is a lick of honey now and again.
The Ambersheen Vale Fawn is available for free from the Crown Store throughout the two week long duration of the Heroes Reforged event. It was available on PC/Mac for 14 days starting March 8, 2021, and was be available for two weeks on PS4 and Xbox One starting March 16, 2021. Its default name is "Honeywine".

### Ancestor Moth Swarm

:"The Ancestor Moths that accompany some mystically-attuned mortals are more than mere insects-they are animated by the loving spirits of the dearly departed, and may grant wisdom and awareness to those they visit." -Sister Terran Arminus
The Ancestor Moth Swarm is available in the Wild Hunt Crown Crates as an Epic-level reward. Its default name is "Fjyron", which loosely translates to "will to peace".

### Ancestral Haj Mota

:In Telvanni lands, haj mota sometimes lay their eggs in broken catacombs or abandoned cemeteries. The hatchlings acquire a coating of green grave-mold on their shells and become sensitive to the presence of ghosts.
The Ancestral Haj Mota is available as an Epic-level reward in Buoyant Armiger Crown Crates. Its default name is "Gravedigger".

### Anchorborn Pony

:"Now, I says to my friend, look at all this dark anchor debris. No one's using it. No one owns it. And I look at his pony, this sickly little thing, and I say, why not make ourselves something nice?"-"Innovator" Delan
The Anchorborn Pony was available as a daily reward on day 21 of December 2023. Its default name is "Quarter Horse".

### Ancient Dragon Hunter Wolf

:This wolf senses the impending arrival of Dragons, making it an excellent pet for veteran Dragon Hunters.
The Ancient Dragon Hunter Wolf was available in the Crown Store for 1,000 or as part of the Scalebreaker Collector's Bundle. It first became available on August 12, 2019 for PC, and on August 27, 2019 for consoles. Its default name is "Intuition".

### Anequina Ginger Cat

:This adorable ginger cat has more purrs than you can count. He longs to be out and about, whether it's on an adventure or watching you craft potential kitty toys.
The Anequina Ginger Cat is a non-combat pet available as a Superior-level reward from Hidden Kindred Crown Crates. It has a bobbed tail and heterochromatic eyes. Its default name is "Rumblefur".

### Anthorbred Avalanche Dog

:With this loyal dog at your side, you have a companion who can help you make it through even the most treacherous of avalanches in Pale Pass and other mountainous areas … even if it has to pull you out from under a pile of fallen snow and detritus.
The Anthorbred Avalanche Dog is available in the Crown Store for 1,000, starting July 9, 2020. Its default name is "Gertrude".

### Apocryphal Bookwyrm

:Some claim these curious Daedric wyrms from Apocrypha devour books to vouchsafe the knowledge for their master. Others believe cultists of Hermaeus Mora use these creatures to hunt tomes long thought lost. Perhaps you know the truth?
The Apocryphal Bookwyrm was obtainable by pre-ordering the Necrom Deluxe Edition. Its default name is "Knows-Everything".

### Appleback Salamander

:Brigands in Wayrest tried to use these creatures to help them harass nobles of that town, but after that plan backfired on them, they sold their stock. Lucky for you, these fiery salamanders receive loyalty training before being sold now.

### Arctic Fennec Fox

:The Arctic Fennec is native to the snowy shores of Skyrim's Sea of Ghosts, and the local Nords, when they spot one romping in the drifts, respond with the traditional oath of "Shor's Ears!"
The Arctic Fennec Fox is available as an Epic-level reward in Flame Atronach Crates. Its default name is "Powder".

### Arenthian Skewbald Pony

:A reliable companion on the roads of Reaper's March. Especially popular amongst skooma smugglers that require work out of the caves along the River Strid.
The Arenthian Skewbald Pony is a domestic non-combat pet. It is available as a Superior-level reward in Carnaval Crates. Its default name is "Skewbald Smuggler".

### Ascadian Cliff Strider

:This bluish breed of Vvardenfell aerial reptile, known as the Azure or Ascadian Cliff Strider, is prized by flying-lizard fanciers as the prettiest and most ornamental of its species. Bonus: its screech terrifies crying babies into silence.
The Ascadian Cliff Strider was available in the Crown Store for 1,000 from May 31 to June 4, 2018. It returned to the store in 2022 from June 10 to June 17, where it was offered to ESO Plus Members for a discounted 800. Its default name is "Strides-So-High".

### Ascadian Umber Sheep

:Legends claim the Red Mountain's first eruption not only gave brown sheep their current hue, but also imbued them with the fortitude to thrive despite hardship. Knowing their sheep remain placid while in danger, many Dark Elves believe these tales.
The Ascadian Umber Sheep is available as a Superior-level reward in the Grim Harlequin Crates. Its default name is "Sundeath".

### Ashbone Echalette

:Even at this small size, this echalette is rather vicious. It's not afraid to use its tiny tusks to ram perceived threats, other echalettes, or really anything that moves. It's not a very effective tactic, but it is strangely cute.
The Ashbone Echalette is available in the Crown Store individually for 1,000, or as part of the Scions of Ithelia Collector's Bundle alongside the Ashbone Sabre Cat for 4,000. It entered the Crown Store on March 11, 2024 for PC, and March 26, 2024 for consoles. Its default name is "Tusker".

### Ashen Cliff Strider

The Ashen Cliff Strider is available as a Superior-level reward in Buoyant Armiger Crown Crates. Its default name is "Graywing".

### Ashen Scar Fellrunner

### Ashen Scar Jerboa

### Atmoran Bantam Guar

### Azure Fledgling Gryphon

:The Gryphons that nest on the highest pinnacles of Eton Nir, the tallest mountain on Summerset, have a distinctive bluish tint to their plumage. Some sapiarchs call this sky-blue camouflage, but the Priests of Auri-El see in it a touch of the Divine.
The Azure Fledgling Gryphon pet can be obtained on PC/Mac only via the use of a promotional code included with the official gryphon plush from Gaming Heads. Its default name is "Cerulean".

### Azurian Moth

The Azurian Moth is a moth pet available as an epic-tier reward in Warrior Wave Crates. Its default name is "Waterwing".
