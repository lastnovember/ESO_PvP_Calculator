Daedroth are a large reptilian Daedra. The common blue variety are associated with the Daedric Prince Molag Bal, while the green variety are associated with Mehrunes Dagon and can be found in the Deadlands and Apocrypha. They may sometimes be equipped with armor.

Daedroth guard gateways within The Brimstone Den in Vateshran Hollows. Certain Daedroth in The Deadlands can be seen biting the head off of a banekin when approaching them.

## Skills and Abilities
As they are considered elite creatures, daedroth are immune to all forms of crowd control effects.

### Standard
;Jagged Claw: A basic melee attack dealing minor physical damage.
;Fiery Jaws: A basic attack dealing low flame damage.
;Fiery Breath: The daedroth breathes fire, indicated by a red cone, dealing continuous direct minor flame damage.
;Ground Tremor: The daedroth winds up its foot before stomping down on the ground, sending four shockwaves forward close together but going in slightly different directions, indicated by moving red circles, dealing moderate physical damage each, staggering and snaring for 4 seconds. This can be interrupted to stagger the daedroth. Blocking doesn't negate the snaring but does negate the stagger.
;Burst of Embers: When standing far away from the daedroth, it spits a fireball at its target, dealing moderate flame damage, knocking them back and creating a pool of lingering flames afterward at the target's position for additional continuous minor flame damage. Blocking prevents the knockback but not the pool of flames from forming. Avoiding the projectile with dodge roll negates all.

### Writhing Fortress
The Daedroth in the Writhing Fortress use those abilities instead.
;Jagged Claw: A basic melee attack that deals minor physical damage.
;Fiery Jaws: A basic attack that deals low flame damage.
;Fiery Breath: The daedroth breathes fire as indicated by a red cone, dealing continuous direct minor flame damage.
;Ground Tremor: The daedroth winds up its foot before stomping down on the ground, sending four shockwaves forward close together but going in slightly different directions, indicated by moving red circles, dealing minor physical damage each, staggering and snaring for 4 seconds. This can be interrupted to stagger the daedroth. Blocking doesn't negate the snaring but does negate the stagger.
;Daedroth's Heat: The daedroth punches the ground, and gets enveloped in blue flames that deal continuous direct minor flame damage to anyone standing near it. Afterwards, a large blue flame aura is applied around it, as shown by a red circular AoE, which lasts for a long time and deals continuous direct minor flame damage to targets in the area. The daedroth also enrages, which increases its damage output and gives itself a 25% Max Health damage shield.
;Ignite Flame: The daedroth breathes blue flame at your location rapidly, forming a daedric rune beneath your feet, indicated by a circular AoE, dealing continuous direct moderate flame damage. The damage application of the AoE kicks in almost before the AoE is visible. Dodging the attack negates the AoE application and ensuing damage.
The champion rank daedroths also use this ability. They are immune to crowd control.
;Flaming Charge:The Daedroth charges in a straight line, dealing moderate-high physical damage and knocking back targets hit in its wake.

## Quest-Related Events

### The Sublime Brazier
A daedroth is a part of General Malivus' siege on the Dragonfire Cathedral. He unleashes the beast upon the Cathedral when his soldiers prove ineffective in tearing down the barriers set up by the Immortal Eight. You and Captain Caudex must destroy it in defense of the Sublime Brazier.
