# Online:Broken Paw

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Broken_Paw
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Broken Paw is one of the Khajiiti clerical orders. They function as caretakers and mentors to the Lunar Champions. Within the Lunar Clergy are other orders, including the Brotherhood of the Fang, the Order of the Crescent and the Order of the Claw.
