desc=Increases the chance to get higher-quality loot when pickpocketing.

Cutpurse's Art sometimes increases the quality of the loot you pickpocket off NPCs.

No precise numbers are known about this passive as ZOS never revealed them and no player has conducted a large-scale test. The general consensus is that indeed, you get more Blue and Purple quality loot and less White quality loot with Curpurse's Art than without.

## Notes
- Before Update 45, this Champion Star had to be slotted in order to be active.
