# Online:Draconic Power

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Draconic_Power
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Draconic Power is one of three class skill lines available to Dragonknights. It can be learned by other classes via subclassing. Abilities in this line focus on avoiding damage.

## Skill Perks
The Draconic Power skill line is immediately available to all Dragonknights, and is leveled up by earning experience while Draconic Power skills are slotted. Skills include:
