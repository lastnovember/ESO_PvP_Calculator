# Online:Malacath Fanatics

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Malacath_Fanatics
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Malacath Fanatics are a group of Orcs that invaded the Temple of Ire in Orsinium and took the priests of Trinimac hostage. They are secretly members of the Vosh Rakh, extremist Trinimac worshippers who hope to give Wrothgar's Malacath traditionalists a bad name.

A separate sect can be found on Vvardenfell, at the daedric ruins of Ashurnibibi. They became the target of a Morag Tong writ after they interfered in House Telvanni affairs.
