Dusk Scholars can be encountered in the Library of Dusk. They have magically disguised themselves as the ensorcelled scholars who have been trapped in the ruins. If you approach one of the while they are transformed, the Dusk Scholar will mock you for being fooled and then try to kill you.

## Related Quests
- The Library of Dusk

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Strike:A basic melee attack that does minor physical damage.
;Thunder Hammer:The enemy swings and shocks the ground in front of it as indicated by a red cone. This attack does moderate shock damage and can be avoided by moving out of the red cone. Blocking will also reduce damage done.
;Shock Aura:The enemy shocks the ground around itself as indicated by a red circle. This attack does moderate shock damage and can be interrupted to knock the enemy off balance.

## Bugs
They revert to the appearance and name of the scholar they were impersonating upon death.
