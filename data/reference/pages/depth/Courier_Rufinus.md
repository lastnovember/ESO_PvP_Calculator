After he is killed, his body is brought soon after to Rejmina's House by Eraral-dro's people, where his uniform can be acquired to be used as a disguise.

## Skills and Abilities
;Ice Arrow:A basic ranged attack dealing minor frost damage.
;Summon Ice Barrier: Courier Rufinus summons an Ice Barrier that absorbs projectiles fired at it until destroyed. Courier Rufinus cannot be pulled if the wall stands between him and the target. The player should move so that there is a clear line of sight between themselves and Courier Rufinus.
;Winter's Reach: Courier Rufinus releases an icy whirlwind in the direction he is facing, dealing moderate frost damage and rooting all targets hit. The root effect passes through block. This attack can be interrupted to set Courier Rufinus off-balance.
;Void: Courier Rufinus summons a purple orb from the sky that explodes when it hits the ground, dealing moderate magic damage. This then sends out three magic AoEs across the floor called Void Bursts. This attack can be interrupted to set Courier Rufinus off-balance.
;Void Burst: These AoEs are released after Void lands, the smaller AoEs deal moderate magic damage.
;Magelight:Courier Rufinus summons a mote of magelight, revealing stealthed and invisible players around himself for five seconds. Exposed players cannot return to stealth or invisibility for 3 seconds.
;Annulment/Dampen Magic: Courier Rufinus summons a damage shield that absorbs a small amount of damage.
;Run Away!:When too close to him, Courier Rufinus backs up to put some distance between his target and himself.

## Notes
- Rufinus' appearance is randomly generated.

## Bugs
Despite being an elite rank enemy, Courier Rufinus is vulnerable to crowd control.
Despite wielding a fire staff, his basic attacks and some of his spells are ice-based.
The uniform on his corpse is not the same as what he was wearing at the camp or what is in your inventory to equip.
