Brunnduhl is a Nord member of the Fighters Guild who can be found at the Everlasting Fair. He was part of the squad that was investigating the area for Worm Cult activity, only to get caught up in the Lady of Riot's shenanigans.

When you finally catch up with him, he will not be his usual self.

## Related Quests
- The Everlasting Fair
- The Final Dark

## Quest-Related Events

### The Everlasting Fair
After speaking with Sergeant Alydine, you learn that several members of her squad became trapped in the Everlasting Fair and are unable to leave. She suggests you search for their Alfiq scout Sashii at the fair. Mingling with the attendees leads you to meeting a talking goat, who says the Lady of Riot has taken over the fair and has been changing things to satiate her ego.

The goat agrees to help you with the squad members in return for finding parts of the broken Rod of Revels. While searching Misfortune Teller's Garden, you find a note from Brunnduhl, which claims that he was turned into a monkey and that he took the piece of the rod with him from the prize chest.

You can find the Goat on a nearby dock and he works out where the Lady of Riot has hidden the other piece of the rod. After claiming it from Blood 'n' Baubles, you can bring the parts to the Goat who will restore the Rod of Revels. You can then use the Rod to find the Tent of Dark Joy where the Lady of Riot and the last missing squad member, Ethrolo are. While you go to confront the Lady of Riot, Sashii stay behind to look after Brunnduhl.

After you defeat the Lady of Riot, you can restore the Rod of Revels to its proper place and the Goat will generously open a portal for you and Ethrolo to leave. Going through the portal, you will find yourself in the main fairgrounds where Sashii, Brunnduhl and Ethrolo are waiting.

:"Bah, my head hurts. I could really use something to soothe me. Like a nice, sweet banana. Er, no. That's not right. I meant a drink. I need a drink."

Talking to Brunnduhl before completing the quest:
:"I hate to ask, but … do you have any bananas? Sorry, no that's not what I meant to say! Thanks for your help in there. Without it, I might still be a monkey. I'm sure the cravings will stop soon. Nothing to worry about, certainly."

After you have completed the quest, the Goat will have some final words for the group. Speaking to Brunnduhl before leaving the fair:
:" It's fine! Just a hiccup. It'll pass. Hopefully."
