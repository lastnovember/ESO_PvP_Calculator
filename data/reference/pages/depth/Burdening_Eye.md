Burdening Eyes are flying blue orbs cast by certain enemies, such as dreadweavers and spirit masters. They will follow a target until they either vanish after a certain period, they are destroyed or they touch a player. Despite having health bars, all Burdening Eyes take one fifth of their Max Health per any hit, except heavy attacks which destroy them in one hit.

## Skills and Abilities
;Burden:When the Burdening Eye touches a player, it explodes which deals minor magic damage and snares in a area.

## Notes
- It uses the same model as The Feast and the Mortuum Vivicus.
- Your attacks actually directly damage them, but they are all reduced to 1 damage.

## Bugs
Despite being floating enemies, Burdening Eyes do not have "Flying Immunities" which would protect them from snares and roots.
