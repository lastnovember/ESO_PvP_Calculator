# Online:House Ravenwatch

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:House_Ravenwatch
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

House Ravenwatch, or often referred to as the Ravenwatch, is a noble family of High Rock based out of Ravenwatch Castle in Rivenspire. House Ravenwatch was founded by Count Verandis Ravenwatch after he left the Gray Host.

The members are mostly vampires, with the House serving as a refuge for them in the region. Following the disappearance of Verandis at the end of the zone's questline, Adusa-daro and Fennorian assume charge of the House, with Gwendis as second-in-command.

You can opt to join House Ravenwatch at the conclusion of the Dark Heart of Skyrim storyline by earning the achievement Taking up the Mantle, which grants you the title "of House Ravenwatch".
