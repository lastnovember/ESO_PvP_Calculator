Armelle Madach is a Breton Undaunted neophyte who joined up with Captain One-Eye's crew in Coldharbour after she was rescued alongside Adrolir. However, when the Bonnie Spriggan was chased by the Caterwaul through a portal back to Nirn, Armelle was one of the crew captured by Captain Velehk Sain.

## Related Quests
- Blood on the Water

## Quest-Related Events

You first see Armelle as one of the prisoners gathered on the Caterwaul's deck as Velehk Sain speaks with Quartermaster Xynark. Once you place the rune and escape the ship, Suraj will approach you with some a task from Captain One-Eye. While she would like you to collect some of the Lost Fleet Bones and bring them back to her, Suraj also has a request. He gives you the Bonnie Spriggan Ship Bell and suggests you use it to lure out Quartermaster Xynark and kill her. You can do either or both of these tasks.

If you decide to slay Quartermaster Xynark, you can find the Spacious Cavern in the Seaside Caves and ring the bell there. Quartermaster Xynark drops from the ceiling holding Armelle:

:"I'm here! Thank the Divines you saved me. Thought for sure I was going to go the same way they took Adrolir. Oh, Adrolir. I hope the others don't end up like him."

Speaking with Captain One-Eye, she will ask you to talk with Surah and Argenta for more tasks to get the Bonnie Spriggan ready to sail. You can talk with Armelle afterward and ask how she's doing:

Suraj will want you to collect parts of the Coldharbour's Teeth siege weapon he hid in the caves, while Argenta will you to help some mutineers and rescue them. Talking with Armelle before you go:
:"We lost so many. I thought once we made it back, it'd be different. But we just keep losing people. But at least we have you. That counts for something."

After collecting the weapon parts and attempting to rescue the prisoners, you can return to the Bonnie Spriggan to talk to Captain One-Eye about what you have done. Speaking with Armelle first, she will be speaking the Undaunted oath to herself:
:"No fear of beast or blade. No fear of Daedric planes. No fear of death or age …. I can do this. I'm ready."

Once you have told Captain One-Eye what you accomplished, she will ask you to give the crew a speech before you set sail. Talking to Armelle before giving the speech:
:"Go speak! We're listening!"

The ship will sail north from Solstice until it is met by the Caterwaul. This is the location of the battle at sea, and during this Armelle can be seen fighting the Caterwaul crew. Once Captain Velehk Sain is killed, the surviving crew of the Bonnie Spriggan will gather in a circle. Talking to Armelle before setting fire to the Caterwaul:
:"The scourge of Coldharbour is no more! I've never seen the crew look this happy before. Even Murph is smiling. Or-well, he's not anymore. But trust me, he was."

After you set fire to the Caterwaul, Armelle and the others will watch it burn. Speaking with her before go back below decks for the trip back to Solstice:
:"Draigh, the way you fight! It's inspiring! Adrolir would have written a song for you, I'm sure. But since he's no longer with us, maybe I'll take up writing songs in his stead."

When you return to Solstice, you can speak with Captain One-Eye to complete the quest. Speaking with Armelle after completing the quest:

## Dialogue
After completing the objective at Caterwaul Cove, Armelle will appear with the rest of the crew at the Sunport Docks near the warehouse:
:"They really should make an Undaunted enclave out here. If I ever decide to leave the Bonnie Spriggan, maybe I'll see about setting one up myself."

## Notes
- Her use of the druid word "Draigh" implies she is from the Systres.
