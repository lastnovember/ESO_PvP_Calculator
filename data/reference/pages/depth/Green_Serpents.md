# Online:Green Serpents

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Green_Serpents
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Green Serpents are a group of prisoners that banded together for protection, after being sent out into the jungles of Amenos. In recent times, the Serpents are considered to control vast swathes of the island.
