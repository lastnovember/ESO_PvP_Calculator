# Online:Bitterfish Tribe

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Bitterfish_Tribe
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Bitterfish Tribe is a tribe of Goblins located in West Weald. Some of their territory is in close proximity to the Sharp Tooth Tribe. They are initially hostile towards you but they become neutral once the related quest is completed.
