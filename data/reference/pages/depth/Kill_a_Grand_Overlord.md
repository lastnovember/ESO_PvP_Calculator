Kill a Grand Overlord is awarded for killing another player in the Alliance War who has achieved the maximum Alliance Rank of Grand Overlord (either Grade 1 or 2). Killing an Emperor is almost guaranteed to work, as Emperors were by definition the highest rank in their Alliance at the time they took the throne. (This may not be the case early on in non-veteran campaigns.) You must land the killing blow yourself, however.plain
