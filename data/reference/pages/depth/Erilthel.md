Erilthel is a Wood Elf member of the Thieves Guild, apprenticed to Seeks-the-Dark. She can be found inside Eton Nir Grotto on Summerset, Blackrose Prison in Murkmire, and Borderwatch in Blackwood.

She wishes to establish herself as an infamous thief under the alias "Lady Twilight." She was previously available as a Houseguest in the Crown Store for 1,500.

:"I've struck a deal with Drakeeh, the leader of the Blackguards. If I can defeat their gauntlet challenge, he'll release Seeks. Unfortunately, I'm more of a sneak than a fighter. But you! I bet you've got combat skills aplenty. Mind helping me out?"

:"What? No! Well, maybe just the little snacks coming by on the trays, but that's what you do at parties, right? Seeks and I just wanted to check in on you. He says maintaining good contacts is important in the business. Plus, you know, free food."

:"Seeks was afraid we'd get fleeced on the payment, but the old guy was totally fine with it being busted! He was relieved even! Said it's one less dangerous object in the world. Turns out he's got a vault full of cursed stuff! Weird thing to collect."
