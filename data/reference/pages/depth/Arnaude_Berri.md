Arnaude Berri is a Breton cultist who can be found at Amenos Station.

## Dialogue
When spoken to, she says:
:"What! I'm not doing anything illegal! Can't a person just chant nonsense in private if they want to? It's a free island. Some of it. Sort of."

:"Oh. Hi. Um, what am I doing? Oh, nothing Daedric I assure you. I'm just … decorating! Yes, that's it. Just sprucing up this little section of Amenos Station."

## Bugs
