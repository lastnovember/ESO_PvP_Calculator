Dimitri is a Breton and the leader of the Midnight Union forces found inside Farangel's Delve. He is accompanied by a Midnight Union Butcher.

## Skills and Abilities
Dimitri uses skills from both Archer and Pet Ranger classes, plus a unique cosmetic one:
;Evil Casting:When Dimitri is out of combat, a red glowing effect is seen on his hands.
;Quick Shot:A basic ranged attack that does minor physical damage.
;Arrow Spray:Dimitri shoots a spray of arrows in the direction he is facing as indicated by a red cone, dealing moderate-high physical damage and snaring all targets hit. This can be interrupted to set Dimitri off balance.
;Volley:Dimitri fires continuously at an area as indicated by a red circle, dealing continuous direct low physical damage. This can be interrupted to set Dimitri off balance.
;Call Ally: Dimitri blows a horn and summons an animal companion to fight. This animal is usually appropriate to the zone the pet ranger appears in. Dimitri's Physical Resistance and Spell Resistance are reduced to 0 while a summon is active, as seen by the blue glow on him. Dimitri can only have one animal companion at a time and it will instantly die if Dimitri is killed. Dimitri cannot cast this ability if an animal is already in the fight.
;Roll Dodge: Dimitri may perform a roll dodge to avoid attacks.
;Trap Beast:Dimitri places a trap under the target's feet that trigger on the first target that walks near it, dealing moderate magic damage and rooting for 4 seconds. This cannot be blocked.

## Bugs
Despite being an elite rank enemy, Dimitri is vulnerable to crowd control.
