# Online:Blacksmithing Materials

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Blacksmithing_Materials
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

There are four types of materials involved in Blacksmithing: Ores, Ingots, Tempers, and Gems.

Ores are the raw materials that can be found in the world in mineral deposits. By themselves they cannot be used, but they may be refined into Ingots at any armor crafting station. Unlocking the "Plentiful Harvest" and "Master Gatherer" perks in the Craft Champion tree will also provide bonuses when harvesting. There is a small chance of producing tempers when refining raw materials. The ores you find are determined by your character's level and your Metalworking level-if you are level 40 with a Metalworking level of 2, you will find both High Iron Ore and Dwarven Ore out in the world.

Ingots are used to make Heavy Armor as well as all bladed or blunt melee weapons.

Tempers can be used to improve existing armor or weapons. Tempers can be obtained by deconstructing existing armor sets. Ores and tempers can both be acquired through Miner Hireling mails. Ingots and tempers can be obtained as rewards for completing Blacksmith Writs.

Gems are used to craft weapons and armor with traits, provided that trait has been researched on that piece. Gems can be found by looting jewelry boxes throughout the world, opening Wet Gunny Sack obtained while fishing, and deconstructing armor pieces that have traits.

## Tempers

colspan=2|Temper | Effect

Honing Stone
Improve quality from Normal to Fine

Dwarven Oil
Improve quality from Fine to Superior

Grain Solvent
Improve quality from Superior to Epic

Tempering Alloy
Improve quality from Epic to Legendary

In addition to blacksmithing items, tempers are also used in Diagrams for furnishings. Specifically:
- Normal quality diagrams do not require any tempers
- Fine quality diagrams require 9 Honing Stone
- Superior quality diagrams require 6 Dwarven Oil
- Epic quality diagrams require 3 Grain Solvent
- Legendary quality diagrams require 1 Tempering Alloy

Dwarven Oil is also used in one Provisioning recipe:
- Spring-Loaded Infusion
