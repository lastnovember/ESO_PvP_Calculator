Akata is a powerful Dremora responsible for the corruption of the wildlife found near the town of Shattered Grove.

## Related Quests
- A Village Awakened

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Strike:A basic melee attack dealing minor physical damage.
;Fiery Breath:Akata breathes fire as indicated by a red cone, dealing moderate flame damage.
;Lava Whip:Akata winds up a Lava whip dealing moderate flame damage to the target. This attack can be blocked to set Akata off balance.
;Dark Talons:Akata calls forth talons from the ground, dealing minor flame damage and rooting anyone hit for 4 seconds.

## Notes
- Prior to Update 6, Akata was named, and was, a Bear. When the game first released, this bear was named Enormous Bear.

## Bugs
Despite being an elite rank enemy, Akata is vulnerable to crowd control.
