Ahmat the Forgotten is a skeletal Redguard warrior of the Anka-Ra found inside the Ruins of Kardala. He must be summoned using a Spirit Stone at the "Tomb of the Forgotten", which can be acquired by slaying Yokudan Spirits in the ruins.

## Skills and Abilities
;Ice Arrow: A basic ranged attack that does minor frost damage.
;Aspect of Terror:Ahmat the Forgotten casts a spell which lobs a dark orb of magic at you. It does moderate magic damage and fears the player for a short time, in which they cannot move or perform any actions. This effect passes through block, and players can break free from it. This spell can be interrupted to stagger Ahmat the Forgotten.
;Shadow Cloak: If the target is in close proximity to Ahmat the Forgotten, he will turn himself invisible and move to a better location and set the player off balance.
;Summon Burdening Eye:Ahmat the Forgotten summons Burdening Eye that moves toward the player.
;Stare: If the Burdening Eye touches the player, it explodes, temporarily rooting the player in place and doing moderate magic damage.
;Summon Dark Shade:Ahmat the Forgotten summons a Dark Shade of himself to attack a player. The shade's attacks inflict Minor Maim, reducing the player's damage done by 5% for 4 seconds. The summon will die if Ahmat the Forgotten is killed.

## Notes
- Ahmat the Forgotten doesn't award experience or loot when killed, though he still gives Fighter's Guild reputation.

## Bugs
Although he uses abilities tied to a staff-wielding class, Ahmat the Forgotten wields a sword and shield. This causes the animation of "Ice Arrow" to not play correctly.
