Baldan is a Nord and former Skaal found on the shore of Lake Honrich in the Rift during the New Life Festival. He is accompanied by a bear named Garth, who Baldan believes will destroy the world unless he can keep him happy.

## Related Quests
- The Judgment of Garth

## Quest-Related Events
When you approach him and haven't started the quest yet:
:"Strong arm, mind juggling knives suit you?"
:"My juggling used to soothe you."

When you greet him, he'll be juggling knives and will urge you not to disturb his performance for long.
:"Speak quickly, traveler. Garth's not in the best of moods, as you can plainly see. If I halt my performance for too long, he may decide I'd be better as his next meal than his entertainer. And then he'll turn his appetite on all of Tamriel!"
:Garth wants to eat Tamriel?
:"Eat, destroy, some difference. The Skaals in the village don't believe it, but Garth has a piece of the All-Maker in him. And it's his jolly mood during New Life that determines Tamriel's fate for the coming year! Right now, it's not looking good."
:Why is Garth in a bad mood?
:"Ask the fool in his belly the next time you meet him. At least he was calm while I juggled and … wait. Honeycomb! That will brighten Garth's mood! Bring him some while I keep juggling and we may just save Tamriel and New Life yet!"
:All right. Where can I find some honeycomb?

You can ask him about Garth and the All-Maker:
:"The forest around Hunter's Glade has some of the best honeycomb in the Rift. Go gather some and bring it back here. If we don't lighten Garth's mood, I shudder to think what will happen. A bear with a bit of the All-Maker in him is no joking matter!"
:How did you and Garth meet?
::"Oh, I've known Garth forever! Before he became a bear, he was my brother. We were Skaals, but we lost our parents in a fierce blizzard. Cold took Garth, too. I barely survived …. Wouldn't have, either, if Garth hadn't returned to save me."
::Your dead brother saved you?
::"No, he came back as a live bear. Dragged me into a cave, kept me warm. The rest of the village calls me mad, but I know that's my brother. And that he's got a bit of the All-Maker in him. Which is why we need to lighten his mood and save New Life!"
:Tell me about the All-Maker.
::"The All-Maker is revered by the Skaals as the wellspring of all creation. Through him, we seek harmony with the world. The All-Maker gave us the six aspects of nature, and he dwells in the world of the spirit. That's how I know Garth is my brother."
::What do you mean?
::"Death is just the beginning of a new existence. When the spirits of the dead return to the All-Maker, he shapes them into new life and returns them to our world. Why else would Garth not devour me like a wild beast? Because he's my brother!"

When you return with the honeycomb, the situation will have worsened.
:Baldan: "All-Maker preserve us! Garth's mood grows dire. Quick, place the honeycomb on his feeding stone!"
He will admonish you if you try to talk to him:
:"Tamriel's fate hangs in the balance and you're talking to me? Hurry and give Garth the honeycomb!"

Do so, and Garth will begin to eat, and then pull away.
:Baldan: "That should brighten Garth's mood! Wait …."
:Baldan: "I forgot! Garth likes music when he eats! Quick, play him a tune!"

Speaking to him before you play music:
:"Garth likes the honeycomb, but I always play him a tune at meal time. And now this juggling has me exhausted! You need to do it, friend. Play Garth a tune and put him in a good mood before Tamriel and New Life are doomed!"

Once you soothe Garth with music:
:Baldan: "Look at that happy bear! You saved New Life, friend!"

He will thank you for relaxing Garth and will give you your reward:
:"Nice work! Just look at how content you made Garth. His mood has improved a thousandfold and New Life-indeed all of Tamriel-is safe for another season. I think."
:You think?
:"As long as a piece of the All-Maker resides in Garth, his mood will always hang like a sharp blade over New Life. I'll continue to do my best to keep him entertained, but should you return I may call upon you again for assistance. Happy New Life!"

Speaking to him after the quest:
:"The other Skaals are as blind as a moth priest. The All-Maker speaks through Garth and his mood determines the fate of Tamriel!"
