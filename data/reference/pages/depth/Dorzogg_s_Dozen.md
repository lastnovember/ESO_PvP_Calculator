# Online:Dorzogg's Dozen

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Dorzogg's_Dozen
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Dorzogg's Dozen are a team of gladiatorial fighters led by their self-appointed king Dorzogg. They can be found participating in the events at Dragonstar Arena in Craglorn. While Dorzogg is only found with two of his fellow warriors at their camp, he claims his other "knights" are around to help, however they're just too sneaky to be seen.
