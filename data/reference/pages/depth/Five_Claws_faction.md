# Online:Five Claws (faction)

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Five_Claws_(faction)
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Five Claws are a group of five Khajiit gladiators participating in the events at Dragonstar Arena in Craglorn. Before becoming pit fighters, they were all Baandari, but decided to form a group together and shift careers after they discovered they were better at killing than thieving.

Despite this, they are not ruling out the option of simply stealing and running off with the prize rather than winning it fair and square if the option presents itself.
