Commander Ilyria is a Maormer member of the Sea Vipers found at the Seaside Sanctuary, only during the related quest. She is accompanied by two other Sea Vipers.

## Related Quests
- A Storm Upon the Shore

## Skills and Abilities
;Flare:A basic ranged attack dealing minor flame damage.
;Quick Strike:A basic melee attack dealing minor physical damage.
;Ice Cage:Commander Ilyria summons a ring of frost around herself dealing continuous direct moderate frost damage to all targets in the red circle. This cannot be blocked.
;Impending Storm:Commander Ilyria charges up and releases lightning from herself as indicated by red circles dealing moderate shock damage to all targets hit. This can be interrupted to set Commander Ilyria off balance. This cannot be blocked.
;Empower Weapon: Flame:Commander Ilyria infuses her weapon with flame which changes her "Quick Strike" attack into a melee "Flare" attack.
;Flare:Commander Ilyria hits the target with her weapon while spinning, dealing minor flame damage.

## Bugs
Despite being an elite rank enemy, Commander Ilyria is vulnerable to crowd control.
