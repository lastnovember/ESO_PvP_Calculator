icon = Court Collector

points = 10
neededfor = *Card Connoisseur
desc = Collect the upgraded cards from the Duke of Crows deck in Tales of Tribute.

Court Collector is awarded for obtaining all upgraded cards for Duke of Crows deck in Tales of Tribute.

The cards required are:
- Blackfeather Knave - obtained from Blackfeather Knave Clue
- Murder of Crows - obtained from Murder of Crows Clue
- Plunder - awarded for Dictum of the Duke achievement: use Duke of Crows's patron power 10 times in match-made games
- Toll of Silver - awarded for reaching Novice rank in Roister's Club is the rank correct?
