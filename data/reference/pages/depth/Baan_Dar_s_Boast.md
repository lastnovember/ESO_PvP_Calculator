## Detailed Walkthrough

### Queen Ayrenn's Army of Kittens

Bring the poisoned strangler stems to Yanabir-ja and help him roast them.
:"Hello to you, Aniel's friend. This one wishes we had brought more moon-sugar with us. Roasts always taste better lathered in sweet."
:I have these specially treated Strangler parts. I heard they're quite incredible when grilled.
:"From Stranglers? Never would have thought of that. You know what? These will go right into the fire. Time we had a celebration! Thank you, traveler."

:Yanabir-ja: "Everyone! Gather around the fire. Our visitor has introduced me to a new delicacy. This one thinks it will be a most fragrant experience. Baan Dar's tail, what is this? This one has never seen such things!"

The roasted strangler parts produce a thick green smoke. Everyone starts laughing, coughing or vomiting. A hilarious hallucination of Queen Ayrenn and her army of kittens appears, with a kitten of Razum-dar following her.

The Queen makes a speech to her troops, using some of these random phrases: "I am disappointed, Centurion Longtail. Look alive! Smarten up, Captain Fuzzypaws! Raz, the troops' tails are not poofy enough. Centurion Softpaws, front and center! Scouty McWhiskers! Straighten that uniform! Raz, remind me to give you extra treatums when we get back to the palace. Troops, we will never face the puppy commandos in this shape!".

## Notes
- The general quest description refers to Aniel as a Bosmer rather than an Altmer.
- After being introduced to Dolirdor, while Ariel asks for privacy with you to talk about what comes next, the objective to speak with her unlocks when walking only a few feet away and she can be then spoken with among all the revelers.
- The poison is picked up from a pre-existing backpack in the Bosmer camp that until the related objective contains regular loot which is counted as stolen if picked up.
- The empty Rotmeth barrel won't exist in the Bosmer camp before starting the quest or reaching the related objective.
- Unlike most quest followers, who normally stop following when exiting a location's radius, the water area with the giant snakes and stranglers is still within Thormar's location radius but Aniel won't follow you down there.
- Stranglers not found within the designated circled area can also be used for the related objective.
- While the objective after collecting the parts needed for the pranks states to play the pranks on both groups, the objective description states that you must choose which group you wish to prank out of the two.
- The objective description is wrong. After completing one of the pranks, the objective changes to perform the prank that is left to do.
- Despite it being Baan Dar's Boast (party) we never actually get to meet Baan Dar.
