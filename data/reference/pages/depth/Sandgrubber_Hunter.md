Sandgrubber Hunters were goblin pet rangers of the Sandgrubber Tribe who can be found in Divad's Chagrin Mine before Update 5, which changed and enlarged the Alik'r Desert delves.

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Shot: A basic ranged attack dealing minor physical damage.
;Call Ally: The Sandgrubber Hunter blows a horn and summons an animal companion to fight. This animal is usually appropriate to the zone the pet ranger appears in, and for Goblin Pet Rangers the animal spawns with the summoner's current Health. The Sandgrubber Hunter can only have one animal companion at a time. The Sandgrubber Hunter cannot cast this ability if an animal is already in the fight.
;Roll Dodge: The Sandgrubber Hunter may perform a roll dodge to avoid attacks.
;Trap Beast:The Sandgrubber Hunter places a trap under the target's feet that trigger on the first target that walks near it, dealing low physical damage and rooting for 4 seconds. This cannot be blocked.
