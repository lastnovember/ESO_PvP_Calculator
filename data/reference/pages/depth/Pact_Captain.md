Pact Captains were members of the Ebonheart Pact found in the camp at the base of Trolhetta when Securing the Pass was unlocked, and at Trolhetta in front of a line of four Pact Soldiers after destroying the Necrosis Orb in the Trolhetta Sanctum during that same quest. They were removed with Update 6.
