The Avatar of Vigor is a frozen nereid and optional mini-boss in Black Drake Villa. It is found in the Glowing Cavern under a trap door beneath the large chamber in the Servant Walkway, opened by activating all four Guardian Orbs surrounding it. Two of the orbs are already activated when you arrive, so to activate the rest two groups of two must go up the stairs on the opposite sides of the room, pull the levers simultaneously to open the portcullis which goes up and stand on two pressure plates at once, which will reveal the orbs. Activate the Guardian Orbs and carry their essence down to the main chamber to open the grate in the middle that leads down to the Glowing Cavern, where you can walk to the seal and activate it to fight the Avatar of Vigor.

## Skills and Abilities
;Frost Bolt: A basic range attack dealing high frost damage.
;Chilly Backhand: The Avatar strikes you with her hand, dealing high physical and frost damage and snaring you.
;Crushing Ice: The Avatar forms a spiky ball of ice and drops it on you, dealing massive frost damage. This attack can be blocked.
;Poison Spouts: The Avatar kneels down into the water, causing several poisoned water spouts to erupt, dealing high poison damage.
