# Online:Two Handed (Companion)

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Two_Handed_(Companion)
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Two Handed is one of six weapon skill lines available to Companions.

## Skill Perks
The Two Handed skill line is immediately available to Zerith-var, who starts his progress at level 3. Other Companions can unlock the skill line by equipping them with any Two Handed weapon. The skill line can be leveled up from earning combat XP while your active Companion has a Two Handed weapon equipped.
