Amuur is a High Elf resident of the town of Phaer. He is Hendil's assistant, and helps him tend to the sick in Aninwe's barn.

## Related Quests
- Plague of Phaer
- The First Patient

## Dialogue
If you speak to him prior to starting the quest, he'll say:
:"Please, we're very busy here. Give us some room."

## Quest-Related Events

### Plague of Phaer
When you first speak to him, he and Hendil are treating an ill patient:
:"Need to see this woman. No time to talk."

He and Hendil help Curorne:
:Hendil: "We're losing her, Amuur! Bring me that salve!"
:Hendil: "Quickly!"
:Amuur: "This is the last of the salve, sir. I hope it's enough."
:Hendil: "We can get more later. Hurry!"
:Hendil: "Yes, just like that. Apply the salve."
:Curorne: "Please. Don't let me die."
:Hendil: "She's alive. That's all that matters. Take her to quarantine."

After you bring Hendil the ingredients, Amuur will make some salve:
:Amuur: "I'll make the new batch right away, sir."
:Hendil: "Make this batch extra potent, Amuur."
:Amuur: "It's ready, sir. I put an extra dose in. Here you go."

After the quest, he will say:
:"Thanks. Sorry, still a lot to do here."

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Strike:A basic melee attack dealing minor physical damage.
;Heavy Attack:A heavy melee attack dealing moderate physical damage. This can be blocked to set Amuur off balance.
;Throw Dagger/Throw Dagger Bleed:Amuur backs away from the target and throws a dagger at the target. This attack snares, deals minor physical damage (Throw Dagger) then very minor bleed damage over time (Throw Dagger Bleed). This attack can be interrupted to set Amuur off balance. The dagger can be blocked to prevent the snare and the bleed.
;Throw Oil/Ignite:A combo move that is only performed if Archers, Fire Mages or other fire using enemies (Flame Atronachs, Scamps etc.) are around. Amuur throws a glob of tar under the target's feet as indicated by a red circle. While this does no damage, standing in it snares (Throw Oil). Amuur will call for the other enemy to light the tar (usually accompanied by a voice clip) and the area will burn, dealing direct minor flame damage every half-second to targets within the red circle (Ignite). Ignite's damage is attributed to the enemy who lit the area on fire.

## Bugs
Amuur's face, hair, and facial hair change when he appears in the mine.
Despite being an elite rank enemy, Amuur is vulnerable to crowd control.
