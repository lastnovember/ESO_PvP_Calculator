# Online:Iron Wheel

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Iron_Wheel
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Iron Wheel are a militaristic and zealous order of bailiffs based out of Taneth. They have been hired by Magnifica Falorah to hunt down the Thieves Guild of Abah's Landing following their failed dowry heist at her family tomb.
