Kvatch Murder Spree is awarded for murdering 20 innocent victims in the city of Kvatch. Below is a list of potential murder victims in the city. Note that killing the same victim more than once after they respawn still counts.plain

- Agrius Albutian
- Auiphion Messenia
- Hyponea Cipia
- Pamunara
- Pilper Varo
- Septius Trasius
- Vilia Ceno
- Vistilia Falto
