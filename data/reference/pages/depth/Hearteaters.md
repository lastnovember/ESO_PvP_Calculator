# Online:Hearteaters

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Hearteaters
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Hearteaters are a force of Reachman invaders led by Nelmir the Hearteater that have recently razed and pillaged the Highlander Camp in Cyrodiil. They took some villagers captive and performed a number of sacrifices for reasons unknown.
