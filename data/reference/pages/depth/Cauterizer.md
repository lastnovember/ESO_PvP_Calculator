# Online:Cauterizer

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Cauterizer
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Cauterizers are a class of hostile humanoid NPCs that use a combination of restoration and fire magic abilities. They are usually equipped with a staff with glowing white effects at the end.

## Skills and Abilities
;Minor Wound:A basic ranged attack dealing minor magic damage. (Either this or Flare)
;Flare:A basic ranged attack dealing minor flame damage. (Either this or Minor Wound)
;Focused Healing:The Cauterizer will heal any allies that are hurt, including itself but for half as much. Enemies often indicate this by calling out for healing (I'm hurt!, I need healing!, Healer! etc.). This effect appears as a yellow beam connecting the Cauterizer and its target which breaks if the line of sight between them is broken. The first tick begins 0.5 seconds after the channel begins, and also heals half as much for the first 3 ticks. This can be interrupted to set the Cauterizer off balance. The Cauterizer never targets summons from themselves or their allies, for example Ice Barrier.
;Heat Wave:The Cauterizer releases fire along the ground in the direction it is facing, dealing low flame damage to all targets hit by it. This can be interrupted to set the Cauterizer off balance. This cannot be blocked
;Fire Runes:The Cauterizer shoots two fireballs in the air which form two fire runes when they land. The runes deal continuous direct minor flame damage to all targets within the red circles. Players should be prepared to move as soon as the attack casts as one rune will always be cast on top of you.
;Rite of Passage:The Cauterizer channels a spell healing itself and nearby allies for 10% of the caster's Max Health every half second for 4 seconds. It takes half a second to cast, and another half a second to begin healing its target once the effect is applied. This ability is usually used when the enemy is on low health. This can be interrupted to set the Cauterizer off balance.
;Run Away!:When too close to them, the Cauterizer backs up to put some distance between their target and themselves.
;Grand Healing:The Cauterizer summons restoring spirits with its staff, healing itself and nearby enemies in the target area for 8 seconds. Try to pull or move the enemies out of the healing circle to stop them from healing. (Some Cauterizers only)
