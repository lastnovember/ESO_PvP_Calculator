## Quest-Related Events

### The Codex Caper
After helping with Quen's caper in stealing the Codex Voernet and escaping Yvierry Manor, you can meet up with her at the Thieves Den in Daggerfall Outlaws Refuge. When you arrive, Edwane will be part of the group discussing the stolen Codex:
:Seline Menant: "The Codex Voernet? I knew Father's inquiries would pay off!"
:Edwane the Hawk: "All I see are pages covered in gibberish. What good is a book we can't read?"
:Abrasia: "Edwane's using his head for once. Old stories don't fill my coin purse-we need to take down the cartel."
:Basil Menant: "I'm telling you, this changes everything-ah. Quen's accomplice is back. Might I have a word?"

Talking to Edwane before Basil:
:"I'm beginning to suspect old Basil there is not as retired as he claims to be. I'd talk to him if I were you, stranger. We'll get around to introductions soon enough."

After you talk with Basil, Quen will want to talk to you to complete the quest. While this happens, Edwane and the other gang leaders will move to various places around the Den. Talking to Edwane before Quen:
:"Sounds like you've been busy, stranger. Finish your business with Quen, and then we'll see where things stand."

Speaking with him after talking to Quen but before starting the next quest:
:"It appears you did good work at the estate. And I understand you got that old book that Basil has been going on about. I'm not sure what good it will do us, but apparently Quen has a plan. And about Quen, I think she wants to talk to you."
