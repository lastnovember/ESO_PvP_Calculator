desc=When you kill an enemy with Blade of Woe, you become invisible for 5 seconds after a short delay while distracting nearby enemies or potential witnesses. While under this effect you can cast Blade of Woe.

Shadowstrike enhances the Blade of Woe passive skill, obtainable via the Dark Brotherhood DLC, by making you able to kill a target with nearby witnesses without getting caught and giving you invisibility after killing the target. This actually doesn't conflict with Spectral Assassin (skill) passive because Spectral Assassin still gives you a 15% chance for you to not be caught even by farther witnesses.
