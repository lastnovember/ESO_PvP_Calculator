# Online:Disloyal Nobles

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Disloyal_Nobles
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Disloyal Nobles is a witch coven occupying Rubble Butte, led by Lady Edwyge. Edwyge is seeking to experiment with knowledge surrounding the Dragon Break that occurred previously in order to recreate it, with the goal of taking Queen Arzhela's place as the ruler of the kingdom of Evermore.
