# Online:Radiating Heart

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Radiating_Heart
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Radiating Heart is one of three class skill lines available to Bastian Hallix and Tanlorin. Abilities in this line focus on healing and buffing the player. It is roughly equivalent to the Earthen Heart skill line for Dragonknight players.

## Skill Perks
The Radiating Heart skill line is immediately available to Bastian and Tanlorin, and is leveled up as they gain levels.
