# Online:Moon Hunter Pack

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Moon_Hunter_Pack
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Moon Hunter Pack is a massive pack of werewolves that follows Vykosa the Ascendant, which can be mainly found at Moon Hunter Keep, a former Silver Dawn fortress that they ransacked. A contingent of the pack is also found in the Nightlands of the March of Sacrifices, assisting Balorgh in the Great Hunt.
