# Online:Anniversary Jubilee

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Anniversary_Jubilee
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Anniversary Jubilee is a holiday event that typically takes place in the month of April or May. It is a yearly celebration of the release of ESO. During this event players can obtain a 100% XP buff and the opportunity to acquire a variety of items. The festival also has a special quest associated with it, named Ache For Cake, which repeats every year. In 2024, this quest was replaced by the event quest For Cake's Sake. A notable feature of the event is the inclusion of Anniversary Jubilee Gift Boxes - special reward containers that grant players various event-themed items.

## Anniversary Jubilee Gift Box

:This box contains a variety of crafting-related materials, intended for celebrants of the Anniversary Jubilee.
An Anniversary Jubilee Gift Box can be obtained by completing daily or weekly quests during the event. Multiple can be earned each day. As of 2024, they can also be obtained by killing and looting final bosses, completing an Incursion event, receiving Rewards for the Worthy, and possibly receiving Tales of Tribute reward mails. Each Gift Box contains a selection of items, some of which are related to the Anniversary Jubilee. This includes crafting motifs, recipes, furnishing plans and crafting materials (style materials, ores, raw materials, wood, dust, potency runestones, provisioning ingredients, solvents). All of these items can be traded with other players. Rewards vary slightly each year as detailed below.

As of 2024, the first gift box obtained each day will be a Glorious Anniversary Jubilee Gift Box. These boxes contain similar items to the regular gift boxes, but whereas the regular boxes only have a chance at obtaining the rarer event rewards, the Glorious Anniversary Jubilee Gift Boxes have guaranteed event-specific rewards.

### Glorious Anniversary Jubilee Gift Boxes
- Style Page for event's new outfit style (will change each year).
- Crafting Materials
- Random Crafting Motifs chapter, furnishing recipe, or a style item.
- Random number of Transmute Crystals (1-10)
- Desecrated Grave Soil
- Jubilee Steed fragment.

### Regular Anniversary Jubilee Gift Boxes
- Crafting materials.
- Chance for a motif chapter, furnishing recipe, or style item.
- Small chance for random number of Transmute Crystals (1-10)
- Small chance for Desecrated Grave Soil, the Worm Cult motif style item
- A chance for Anniversary Jubilee-themed furnishings
- A small chance for a tradeable style page from any of the previous years' Jubilee outfit styles:
- Bonemold Style
- Earthbone Ayleid Style
- Worm Cult Style
- Worm Cult Hunter Style

## Jubilee Cake

A unique feature of the Anniversary Jubilee is the Jubilee Cake, baked by Chef Donolon or his Apprentice Mogh. Previously, once the Ache For Cake quest had been completed, the cake was granted as a Memento that could be called into the world. Once placed, anybody within your group could take a slice and receive a two-hour 100% XP buff that stacked with any other active XP bonuses.

As of 2024, Chef Donolon's quest was replaced by a new event quest: For Cake's Sake, available from the young Khajiit chef Apprentice Mogh. The 100% experience bonus previously obtained by eating a slice of cake will now be passively applied to all participants; however, eating a slice of cake is still required to obtain Event Tickets. The cake's appearance changes every year, becoming taller and more elaborate or themed to the yearly chapter. Once the event is over, the cake can still be summoned and interacted with but does not grant tickets or an XP buff.

## Anniversary Jubilee 2019

The fifth year anniversary event ran from April 4 to May 9, 2019. This event was similar to the previous year (including the XP buff and a new 2019 Jubilee Cake Memento) but ran for five weeks - one week to represent each year since launch.

The activities players partook in to earn a Anniversary Jubilee Gift Box were more varied, with a different one for each week of the event. The first week players earned Gift Boxes by completing crafting writs. The second week was by completing Delve and World Boss Dailies. The third week was by completing Alliance War or Battlegrounds daily quests. The fourth week was by completing daily Pledges for Dungeons and Trials. Finally, the fifth week players could complete any of the previous weeks activities to earn Gift Boxes. In addition, the contents of Gift Boxes were altered slightly each week, giving a chance to drop a different Companion Style page for four of the Five Companions.
