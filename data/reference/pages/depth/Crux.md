# Online:Crux

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Crux
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Crux is a resource unique to the Arcanist class. Various skills generate or consume Crux, and some have different effects based on how much Crux you currently have. Some skills may either generate or consume based on your choice of morph. Unlike most resources, Crux is visible to all players, appearing as glowing green triangles that circle around your body. Crux may only be generated while in combat. Casting skills which generate Crux outside of combat has no effect, and any Crux you have active will expire after 30 seconds when no longer in combat.

## Skills Which Generate Crux
These skills will usually generate one Crux per cast. You can have up to 3 Crux at a time. Any skill that generates more Crux at that point will have no effect and passives which trigger when you generate Crux will not work.
- Cephaliarch's Flail (morph of Abyssal Impact)
- Apocryphal Gate - generates Crux each time you teleport, which can be done more than once per casting.
- Chakram of Destiny (morph of Chakram Shields)
- Cruxweaver Armor (morph of Fatewoven Armor)
- Runeblades - also increases damage based on number of Crux.
- Runemend - also reduces cost based on number of Crux.
- Runic Jolt - also reduces damage taken based on number of Crux, just by being slotted.
- Tome-Bearer's Inspiration - only generates Crux if you currently have none.
- Scribed skills using the Class Flourish Signature Script.

## Skills Which Consume Crux
Skills which consume Crux will always consume all Crux you have, with increasing effects based on how many are consumed. Note that these skills can still be cast without any Crux, but they will be less effective.
- Tentacular Dread (morph of Abyssal Impact) - Increases damage.
- Tidal Chakram (morph of Chakram Shields) - Heals targets based on the strength of the Damage Shield.
- Fatecarver - Reduces the Magicka cost, also increases the duration with the Exhausting Fatecarver morph.
- Unbreakable Fate (morph of Fatewoven Armor) - Gains additional Block damage mitigation.
- Remedy Cascade - Restore Magicka and Stamina to your allies.
- Runespite Ward - Increases the Healing. Also refunds Magicka cost with the Spiteward of the Lucid Mind morph.

## Skills Which Trigger on Crux
- Fated Fortune - Increases Critical Damage and Critical Healing for 7 seconds when you generate or consume Crux.
- Healing Tides - Increases Healing done for each active Crux.
- Hideous Clarity - Restores Magicka or Stamina when you generate Crux.
- Implacable Outcome - Grants Ultimate when you consume Crux.

## Other
- The Reawakened Hierophant set from Infinite Archive has a variety of effects which trigger when casting Curative Runeforms skills based on how much Crux you have.
- The Spattering Disjunction set from Infinite Archive has effects when dealing damage with arcanist abilities, the Herald of the Tome skill line having extra effects.
