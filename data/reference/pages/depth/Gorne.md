The boat serving as the entrance is located directly to the west of Necrom. All creatures here have a chance to drop Lost Graht-Oak Acorns. Butterflies, centipedes, netch calves, phoenix moths, rats, shroom beetles and torchbugs are found in numbers within. Hazards include bottomless pits.

## Layout

### Smuggler's Hideout
This location is accessible during The Double Edge. There's a cooking fire at the campsite.

The Reformer Sun Mage Captain and the following goons are fought here: Reformer Sword Dancer, Reformer Sun Mage, Reformer Knight, and Reformer Magma Melder.
