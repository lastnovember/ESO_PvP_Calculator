# Online:Verdant Growth

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Verdant_Growth
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Verdant Growth is one of three class skill lines available to Sharp-as-Night. Abilities in this line focus on healing. It is roughly equivalent to the Green Balance skill line for Warden players.

## Skill Perks
The Verdant Growth skill line is immediately available to Sharp, and is leveled up as he gains levels.
