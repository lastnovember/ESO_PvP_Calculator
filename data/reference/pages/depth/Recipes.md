# Online:Recipes

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Recipes
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

There are 570 recipes to be collected in total: 288 food recipes and 282 beverage recipes, including 258 Standard recipes, 230 Difficult recipes, 68 Complex recipes, and 14 Legendary recipes. Learning a recipe will remove it from your inventory, but in exchange, you will learn how to craft its meal indefinitely. Chefs and Brewers sell food and drink recipes respectively for large amounts of gold. Alternatively, recipes can be found in trunks, wardrobes, desks, nightstands, and the likes. The recipe received when you loot a container is based on your level and your Recipe Improvement rank. To cook food or brew a beverage, you must have the required provisioning skills. If you do, simply visit a cooking fire found in most towns to begin combining ingredients. Eating food and drinking beverages temporarily gives your attributes a bonus when consumed. However, food and beverage bonuses do not stack, so you have to pick only one.

There are two skills required to craft food and beverages.
- Recipe Improvement ranges from 1 to 6 and lets you make higher Level-Requirement meals.
- Recipe Quality ranges from 1 to 4 and allows you to craft Standard, Difficult, Complex, and Legendary versions of recipes.

For the full list of recipes see the Food Recipes, Beverage Recipes, and Special Recipes pages.

## Food Recipes
Food Recipes

### Standard Recipes
- Meat Dishes (increase Max Health ): meat + [ medium ingredient ]
- Fruit Dishes (increase Max Magicka ): fruit + [ medium ingredient ]
- Vegetable Dishes (increase Max Stamina ): vegetable + [ medium ingredient ]
Dishes of Fine Quality can be cooked for all levels.

### Difficult Recipes
- Savouries (increase Max Health and Max Magicka ): meat + fruit + [ medium ingredient ]
- Ragout (increase Max Health and Max Stamina ): meat + vegetable + [ medium ingredient ]
- Entremet (increase Max Magicka and Max Stamina ): fruit + vegetable + [ medium ingredient ]
Dishes of Superior Quality can be cooked for levels 10 and up.

### Complex Recipes
- Gourmet (increase Max Health, Max Magicka and Max Stamina ): meat + fruit + vegetable + improvement ingredient
Dishes of Epic Quality can be cooked for levels 20 and up.

### Delicacies
- Delicacies have unique combinations of bonuses that cannot be found elsewhere, and often require ingredients not used in other Provisioning recipes. These cover the full range of qualities, with 5 Standard recipes, 9 Difficult recipes, 2 Complex recipes, and 7 Legendary recipes.
Delicacies have no level requirement and can be consumed at any level.

## Beverage Recipes
Beverage Recipes

### Standard Recipes
- Alcoholic Drinks (increase Health Recovery): alcoholic base + [ medium ingredient ]
- Tea (increase Magicka Recovery): tea base + [ medium ingredient ]
- Tonics (increase Stamina Recovery): tonic base + [ medium ingredient ]
Beverages of Fine Quality can be brewed for all levels.

### Difficult Recipes
- Liqueurs (increase Health Recovery and Magicka Recovery): alcoholic base + tea base + [ medium ingredient ]
- Tinctures (increase Health Recovery and Stamina Recovery): alcoholic base + tonic base + [ medium ingredient ]
- Cordial Teas (increase Magicka Recovery and Stamina Recovery): tea base + tonic base + [ medium ingredient ]
Beverages of Superior Quality can be brewed for levels 10 and up.

### Complex Recipes
- Distillates (increase Health Recovery, Magicka Recovery and Stamina Recovery): alcoholic base + tea base + tonic base + improvement ingredient
Beverages of Epic Quality can be brewed for levels 20 and up.

### Delicacies
- Delicacies have unique combinations of bonuses that cannot be found elsewhere, and often require ingredients not used in other Provisioning recipes. These cover the full range of qualities, with 1 Standard recipe, 5 Difficult recipes, 4 Complex recipes, and 7 Legendary recipes. Delicacies have no level requirement and can be consumed at any level.
