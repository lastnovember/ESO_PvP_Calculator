Diabolist Vethisa is a Dark Elf berserker and a member of the Shadowed Path. She serves as one of three bosses in Pothole Caverns. There is a letter addressed to her within the cavern.

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Strike: A basic melee attack that does minor physical damage.
;Blood Craze: Diabolist Vethisa slashes the target twice, doing moderate physical damage twice then very minor bleed damage over time to the target. This attack also heals Diabolist Vethisa over time.
;Agony: Diabolist Vethisa channels a spell dealing moderate magic damage and stunning the target for a short time. Taking damage from any source will break the stun, but the target may choose to break free from it instead. After the stun ends, this spell also deals minor magic damage over time to the target. This can be interrupted to set Diabolist Vethisa off balance instead. Blocking will not prevent the damage over time.
;Roll Dodge: Diabolist Vethisa can perform a roll dodge to avoid incoming damage.
;Heated Blades: Diabolist Vethisa rakes her weapons together in a show of sparks, buffing her damage by 25% and applying Minor Maim to the target for 4 seconds.
;Backstabber:Diabolist Vethisa has a passive ability that causes her to deal 20% more damage when attacking the target's back.

## Bugs
Despite being a champion rank enemy, Diabolist Vethisa is vulnerable to crowd control.
