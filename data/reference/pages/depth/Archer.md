# Online:Archer

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Archer
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Archers are a class of hostile humanoid NPCs that attack using ranged abilities. They are usually equipped with a bow.

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Shot:A basic ranged attack dealing minor physical damage.
;Arrow Spray:The archer shoots a spray of arrows in the direction it is facing as indicated by a red cone, dealing moderate physical damage and snaring all targets hit for 4 seconds. This can be interrupted to set the archer off balance. Blocking doesn't prevent the snare.
;Volley:The archer fires continuously at an area as indicated by a red circle, dealing continuous direct low physical damage. This can be interrupted to set the archer off balance. (Only on some archers)
;Taking Aim:A powerful sniping bow attack that takes time to charge up which deals high physical damage. This can be interrupted to set the archer off-balance. (Primarily in Craglorn)
;Arrow Stab:A basic melee attack dealing minor physical damage where the Archer grabs their arrow and stabs it into you rather than firing it. (Only on some)
;Run Away!:When too close to them, the archer backs up to put some distance between their target and themselves.
