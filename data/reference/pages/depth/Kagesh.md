# Online:Kagesh

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Kagesh
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Kagesh are an Ashlander tribe living in the hills near the Iliath Temple. Despite having a truce with the monks of the temple, their ashkhan, Ziddak Dunhaki, led a force of tribesmen in an attempt to raze it to the ground.
