# Online:Prolific

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Prolific
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Prolific is a trait which can be found on Companion weapons, armor and jewelry. The trait cannot be researched or crafted, and exists only on found loot. Items with the Prolific trait increase the Ultimate Generation speed of a Companion by an amount dependent on the quality of the item.
