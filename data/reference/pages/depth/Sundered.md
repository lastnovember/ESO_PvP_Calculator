# Online:Sundered

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Sundered
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Minor Fracture Sundered is a status effect applied by dealing Physical Damage. A Sundered target takes instant Physical Damage and suffers from the Minor Breach debuff for 4 seconds. Applying Sundered also increases your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage by 100 for 4 seconds. This status effect can be removed with a Cleansing effect.
