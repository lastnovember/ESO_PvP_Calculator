Gavin Koldane is a Breton businessman, werewolf and leader of the Koldane Cartel, a mercantile group with rumored criminal activities. He is first met in person at the Menant Hideout to the south of the Deleyn's Mill. Your theft of the Voernet Codex from the Yvierry Estate and pursuit of the Nowhere Vault has put you and the remaining criminal gangs outside his control in his sights.

## Quest-Related Events

### Codex and Cartel
After the job at the Yvierry Estate, you assist Quen and the gang leaders in following leads about the Nowhere Vault and the Koldane Cartel's plans. When you question Marten Cogwine at the Lion Guard Redoubt, he may let slip that Gavin plans to attack Basil Menant, as he believes that he is responsible for the codex's theft before the cartel could take it.

When you have decided whether to inform the Lion Guard or not, you can meet Seline Menant and Edwane the Hawk outside the hideout. Entering the hideout, you find Gavin Koldane and some cartel members fighting a squad of Lion Guard led by Captain Sansonne.

During the fight, you will need to deal with Koldane and his underlings. He will use lightning magic to attack, and shouts the following when casting Lightning Grasp:
:Gavin Koldane: "Dance with the lightning, fool!"
:Gavin Koldane: "You're not going anywhere!"
:Gavin Koldane: "Ha! The lightning found you, didn't it?"

At a certain point during the fight, Koldane will give the following command to his surviving underlings:
:Gavin Koldane: "Enough! Rend them with fang and claw, my brothers!"

Several werewolves will appear and attack the group, and Gavin Koldane escapes in the confusion. Afterward, Basil will want to talk to you, his survival depending on whether you informed the Lion Guard before arriving or not.

### Racing Into Nowhere
After recovering another Nowhere Key, Quen will have discovered the location of Nowhere Vault, Sage's Isle, far northwest of Glenumbra. When you arrive you find signs the Koldane Cartel are alread there, but their forces have steadily been killed by creatures and various traps. As you explore the ruins with your partner, you overhear Gavin Koldane in the ritual chamber below you:
:Gavin Koldane: "I beat your damn puzzle, you dusty old mage. The vault is mine!"
:Partner: "Hear that? Just ahead. Let's take a look."

Approaching the nearby hole in the wall, you see Koldane with several Cartel thugs (some alive, some dead) and Abrasia. The vault then opens:
:Gavin Koldane: "We solved the pattern of the lights! All of you, through the door!"
:
:Partner: "Koldane opened the vault. And was that Abrasia with them?"

Once you enter the Nowhere Vault, you and your allies will be scattered, and you'll need to find and help them through their challenges. After you reunite in the Nexus, you enter the portal to the Vault of the Thieves Moon. In the Thieves Moon Vault Antechamber, you find Abrasia fighting Gavin Koldane:
:Abrasia: "Don't hurt my Roadsworn, Koldane. Just let them go."plain
:Gavin Koldane: "The key, Abrasia. Give me yours. Now."
:Abrasia: "No! Don't lay a claw on them!"
:Seline Menant: "Koldane has Abrasia! Help her!"

When you approach, Abrasia will shout instructions to you:
:Gavin Koldane: "Ah, the rest of the gutter thieves. Tear them apart!"
:
:Abrasia: "Your Nowhere Keys! Place them with the others!"
:Edwane the Hawk: "Do it, friend! Trust her!"

Abrasia will keep Koldane busy while you deal with the ambush and place your Nowhere Keys on the altar. Once the final key is placed, everyone will be teleported, and you will arrive in the Vault of the Thieves Moon, but you will be separated from the others.

You and Abrasia can race to the main chamber of the Vault of the Thieves Moon, where you find Gavin Koldane standing at the opposite end, in front of the full moon:
:Gavin Koldane: "You're too late. The Thieves' Moon is mine!"

After he finishes his transformation into Werewolf Behemoth he jumps to the floor. At the same time Edwane and Seline will fighting off werewolves. When you attack Koldane, Abrasia will join in:
:Gavin Koldane: "Come, gutter thieves! Face my claws!"
:Abrasia: "Focus on Koldane, I'll deal with his thugs!"

You will have deal with both Koldane and his minions. At a certain point in the fight, Koldane will start howling and everyone will be paralyzed. Quen gets to her feet and grabs the relic and calls you over so she can throw it to you. Once this happens, a cutscene will play out, time slows and you will see Koldane leaping at you from behind. You activate the Thieves Moon and use it on Koldane which reverses time for him, reverting him to human form and knocking him out:
:Edwane the Hawk: "What happened? Did we win?"
:Seline Menant: "I think so. The Thieves' Moon did something to … time? Turned Koldane back into a man again."
:Abrasia: "Quen. The Thieves' Moon … it broke."
:Quen: "We stole it, used it, and it did its job. Nice catch, by the way, partner."

After talking to Quen, Sage Voernet's Memorium appears and politely yet firmly tells you to leave. While this was happening, Edwane, Seline and Abrasia will have surrounded Koldane and Abrasia kicks him as he wakes up and staggers to his knees. Going through the provided portal, you arrive back on the beach of Sage's Isle and can watch while a restrained Koldane is handed over to Captain Sansonne, along with his ledgers.

## Skills and Abilities

### Menant Hideout
When Gavin Koldane reaches 25% Health, he stops fighting and escapes via a locked door.
;Quick Strike:A basic melee attack dealing minor physical damage.
;Thunder Hammer:Gavin Koldane swings and shocks the ground in front of him as indicated by a red cone dealing low shock damage.
;Shock Aura:Gavin Koldane shocks the ground around himself as indicated by a red circle dealing low shock damage. This can be interrupted to knock Gavin Koldane off balance. This cannot be blocked.
;Lightning Form:Gavin Koldane casts Lightning Form on himself, gaining Major Resolve (which increases his armor by 5948) while creating many small areas nearby which after a small delay deal low shock damage.
;Uppercut:An Uppercut-like attack dealing moderate physical damage, knocking back and stunning. This can be blocked to knock Gavin Koldane off balance instead.
;Lightning Grasp:Gavin Koldane channels lightning at you, stunning you for 8 seconds and dealing continuous direct moderate shock damage as long as you remain stunned. This can be blocked to both negate the damage and the crowd control, as well as locking Gavin Koldane in the channel animation for the full duration. This can be interrupted to set Gavin Koldane off balance instead.
