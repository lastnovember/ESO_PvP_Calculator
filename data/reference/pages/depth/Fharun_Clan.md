# Online:Fharun Clan

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Fharun_Clan
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Fharun Clan is one of the oldest and strongest of the Orc clans in Wrothgar. It is led by Chief Bazrag, from their clan seat at Fharun Stronghold. They are clan that holds traditions and isolation dear, hence their opposition to King Kurog and his policies that are more open to the other races implicating themselves in Wrothgar's affairs.

Unbeknownst to most in Wrothgar, Fharun Clan also runs a lucrative prison built underneath the stronghold, which is used to keep some of the region's most dangerous criminals, as well as Orcs from other clans whose leaders don't wish them to be simply killed or exiled as is usually custom.
