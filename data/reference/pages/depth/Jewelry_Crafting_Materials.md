# Online:Jewelry Crafting Materials

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Jewelry_Crafting_Materials
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

There are four types of materials involved in Jewelry Crafting: Dust and Ounces, Platings, and Trait Materials.

Dust is the raw material that can be found in the world in jewelry crafting seams. By themselves they cannot be used, but they may be refined into Ounces at any jewelry crafting station. Unlocking the "Plentiful Harvest" and "Master Gatherer" perks in the Craft Champion tree will also provide bonuses when harvesting. There is a small chance of producing platings when refining raw dust. The dust you find is determined by your character's level and your Engraving level-if you are level 40 with an Engraving level of 1, you will find both Pewter and Copper Dust out in the world.

Ounces are used to make Jewelry, including Necklaces and Rings.

Platings can be used to improve existing jewelry. They can be obtained by deconstructing existing jewelry pieces. Ounces and platings can be obtained as rewards for completing Jewelry Crafting Writs.

Trait Materials (once refined from their Pulverized form) are used to craft jewelry with traits, provided that trait has been researched on that piece. Every trait material is acquired through different means, though all of them have a chance to drop when deconstructing jewelry pieces that have traits.

Prior to Update 40, Dust nodes also dropped materials called Grains, which could be refined to Plating the same way Dust is refined to Ounces. In an order to rebalance the Jewelry Crafting system, the game developers completely removed Grains from the game. All Grains in players' inventories were replaced with Platings, and they were given 10 Platings for every original Plating. Platings now have a small chance to be acquired from jewelry seams the same way Grains could.

## Platings

colspan=2|Platings | Effect

Terne Plating
Improve quality from Normal to Fine

Iridium Plating
Improve quality from Fine to Superior

Zircon Plating
Improve quality from Superior to Epic

Chromium Plating
Improve quality from Epic to Legendary

In addition to jewelry items, platings are also used in Sketches for furnishings. Specifically:
- Normal quality sketches (if they existed) would not require any platings
- Fine quality sketches require 9 Terne Plating
- Superior quality sketches require 6 Iridium Plating
- Epic quality sketches require 3 Zircon Plating
- Legendary quality sketches require 1 Chromium Plating
