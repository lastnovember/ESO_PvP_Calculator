The Big Rabbu Nightmare is a specter of Big Rabbu that appears in Spindleclutch II during the battle with Praxin Douare, when he begins hallucinating enemies from the initial journey through the ruins. Once it and all the other nightmares are killed, Praxin will transform into a wraith.

## Related Quests
- Blood Relations
- Pledge: Spindleclutch II

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Strike: A basic melee attack that does minor physical damage.
;Power Bash: A heavy shield bash that does moderate physical damage and stuns if it hits the target. This attack can be blocked to knock the enemy off balance instead.
;Block:The enemy may also briefly block with its shield, reducing damage taken greatly. Players who attempt melee heavy attacks on the enemy while it is blocking will be set off balance.

## Bugs
Despite being an elite rank enemy, Big Rabbu Nightmare is vulnerable to crowd control.
