Dispersive Defeat is awarded for defeating High Soulbinder Vykand in Black Gem Foundry on Veteran difficulty level, after raising the challenge banner, which enables Hardmode and makes the fight more difficult by the following:
- Enemies become generally stronger
- Getting hit by Vykand's ground slam AoE creates Refracted Soul Essence
- Anguished Refracted Souls ("kill the green ghost" mechanic) have very high damage resistance for the affected player, so they need help from the group in killing the Soul. Failing this mechanic becomes deadly. Also, the AoE circle on the first affected player deals massive damage to anyone else caught in it, when it goes off.
- Below 60% Vykand's health colored runes mechanic is replaced with a different one:
- First round: Vykand will highlight one of the floor tiles of the arena and float in the middle, assuming some pose - holding her staff up, down, to the right, or doing a swirl with it - with both tile and pose different for each player. To survive, move to your highlighted tile. Players also need to communicate, which tile matches which pose, for the next round
- Second round: she will float and strike one of the four poses, and all players have to go to the tile matching this pose from the first round
- After one rotation of this mechanic, she will start summoning Refracted Souls in pairs ("bring the ghost to matching crystal")
- Big adds spawn more often as Vykand's health goes down.unsure about specifics, may be also tied to change of mechanics rotation?
-

Raising the challenge banner for this boss requires defeating both Quarrymaster Saldezaar and Black Gem Monstrosity on Hardmode.
