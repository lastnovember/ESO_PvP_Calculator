Void Bloom is a medium height plant with blue stems and blue flowers. It can be found in any zone by characters with Rank 9 in the Tailoring passive or CP90-140. When harvested, a plant yields three to four samples of the raw material, Raw Void Bloom, sometimes accompanied by crawlers or worms. Once ten samples of raw void bloom are collected, they can be refined into seven to ten pieces of the processed material, Void Cloth, which is used for crafting pieces of light shadowspun armor at any clothing station. Crafting shadowspun armor also requires Tailoring rank 9.
