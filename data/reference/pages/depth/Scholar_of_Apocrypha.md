# Online:Scholar of Apocrypha

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Scholar_of_Apocrypha
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Scholar of Apocrypha is one of three class skill lines available to Azandar al-Cybiades. Abilities in this line focus on dealing damage. It is roughly equivalent to the Herald of the Tome skill line for Arcanist players.

## Skill Perks
The Scholar of Apocrypha skill line is immediately available to Azandar, and is leveled up as he gains levels.
