Malareth was a Wood Elf who could be found near the docks of Vulkhel Guard. She had some debtors who needed to be reminded of their debt.

She was disabled by Patch 1.0.1, and although the patch notes implied she would be re-added, later, this seems fairly unlikely.

## Related Quests
- New in Town

## Dialogue
When you approach her, she'll mutter:
:Malareth: "Hmph. Hard to make an honest living around here."

Speaking to her, she'll say:
:"New in town, eh? If you're looking to get your feet wet, I have some work needs doing."
:What do you have in mind?
:"Just a simple misunderstanding. You see, there are a few people around town that owe me coin. They've um … forgotten to pay me. I just want you to go remind them."
:I just need to mention your name, then?
:"Now, see? The Green was telling me you were smart. Yeah, just mention me. Remind them of their obligations. And make sure they come running straight to me with that coin."
:Right.

If you speak to her again, she'll say:
:"Remember. No is not an acceptable answer."

When you return:
:"So. How'd it go?"
:The merchants should trouble you no more. / Your financial problems should be over now, Malareth.
:"Hah! That's the way to make a good first impression in town.Can't wait to see the shine on their coins. It's so very, very lovely. Well done. Very well done indeed."

If you speak to her again after completing the quest, she'll say:
:"I could use someone like you in my organization. Maybe we'll run into each other again."
