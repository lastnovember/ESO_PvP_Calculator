General Zasimba is a Khajiit member of the Stirk Fellowship who can be found at the Sunport Rampart Camp, here she will be overseeing the siege camps' defenses.

She first appeared during the Battle for the Writhing Wall. Following the conclusion of the event, she is the only daily questgiver still found at the camp.

If you speak with her again after accepting the quest, she will greet you with one of the following:

:"The five siege camps are spread out along the Writhing Wall from north to south. We tried to cover as much of the wall as we could, as you can see on your map. But the attacks are endless. Even the strongest Senche-raht must rest sometimes, no?"

:"Your strength is a welcome addition to the camps. There are five of them, running north to south along the Writhing Wall. You should leave soon. We're tracking the Worm Cult attacks, as you can see on your map. Hit them hard, for Zasimba."

Before you leave, you can ask her some questions:
:What can you tell me about the attacks on the siege camps?
::"It is a constant barrage, usually a mix of Worm Cultists and their Daedric allies. They hit the siege camps hard and fast, placing soul reapers to fuel their twisted plans. Just as we repel one attack, another camp is hit. It never ends."
::Soul reapers?
:::"Necromantic devices that absorb the souls of any who die nearby. They are horrible things! What the Worm Cult does with the stolen souls, Zasimba does not know. But make sure to destroy every soul reaper you come across."
:Tell me about yourself.
::"Ah, you wonder why you should follow General Zasimba's orders? She respects that. This one is a proud Fighters Guild member. After what the Worm Cult did to Guildmaster Merric and the Earth Forge, she had to join Prince Azah and come to Solstice."
::How did you become a general?
:::"That is a long story involving the last Planemeld and many dead Worm Cultists. Zasimba does not wish to brag, but she is good at what she does, no? The guild rewards skill when it sees it. But now it's all she can do to protect the siege camps."
:Why do we need to defend the siege camps?
::"The camps are crucial forward positions. Surely you can see that, walker? We are staging our forces near the Writhing Wall to bring the fight to the Worm Cult once the wall comes down. Until then, we must defend our positions."
::What's the Writhing Wall really made of, anyway?
:::"Dark moons! Zasimba wishes she knew. Something about frozen soul energy keeping us out of eastern Solstice. It's beyond this one. Zasimba prefers to focus on keeping her claws sharp and her soldiers ready to strike. Bold action is best, no?"
:Why is the Worm Cult attacking the siege camps?
::"Now that we've breached the Writhing Wall, the camps are key staging areas for our forces moving into eastern Solstice. That makes them vital to supporting our campaign... and a threat to the Worm Cult. Thus, the attacks continue."
::What was the writhing Wall?
::"You ask a question General Zasimba cannot easily answer. As she understands things, it was a massive wall of necromantic energy fueled by captured souls. The worms cowered behind it until we broke through. Now, we can take the fight to them."
