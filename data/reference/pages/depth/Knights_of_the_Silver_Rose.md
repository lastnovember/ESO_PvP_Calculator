# Online:Knights of the Silver Rose

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Knights_of_the_Silver_Rose
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Knights of the Silver Rose are a Bretonic knightly order based in northeastern Glenumbra. Avid crusaders against evil Daedra, they amassed a vast collection of Daedric relics in an attempt to keep them safely locked away from those who would seek to use their powers for ill. The same knights who swore to seal away said Daedric corruption have fallen under its sway, and are now wielding the relics they've stored in their vault instead.
