# Online:Charged

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Charged
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Charged is a trait which can only be found on weapons. Once you have researched the trait on a given item type, you can craft Charged weapons by using Amethyst. Items with the Charged trait will increase your chances of applying status effects such as Burning, Chilled or Concussion by an amount dependent on the quality of the item. 2-handed weapons provide twice the bonus of 1-handed.
