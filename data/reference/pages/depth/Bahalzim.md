Bahalzim is a Redguard who can be found maintaining his shield by the docks in the Dragonguard Sanctum. He is sitting near Mogdal.

## Dialogue
He talks about his experiences with the Dragonguard.
:"Mogdal and I have faced countless battles together. Sometimes I think we know each other better than we know ourselves. I'd ask her to marry me, but she's already committed to her blade"

:"The Dragonguard who came before us certainly left some nice pieces of armor. Flexible enough to move quickly, sturdy enough to withstand a Dragon's attack. Mogdal keeps saying they're too pompous, but that's just her way."

:"You've certainly turned this Dragonguard into something spectacular. From a ragtag crew of smugglers to a Dragon hunting order of great notoriety. That's why Mogdal and myself joined up. We wanted to be part of something great."
