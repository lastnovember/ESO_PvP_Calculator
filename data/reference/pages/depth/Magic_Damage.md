Magic Damage (script)
Magic Damage is the most common type of Magical Damage mainly used by Arcanists, Nightblades, and Templars but is available from many sources. It is usually the "default" for all magical damage effects. It can cause the Overcharged status effect.

## Sources

### Weapons
- All Restoration Staves.
- All weapons enchanted with a Glyph of Absorb Health (Oko), Glyph of Absorb Magicka (Makko), Glyph of Prismatic Onslaught (Hakeijo)

### Champion Points
- War Mage in the Extended Might subconstellation of Warfare grants additional Magic Damage.
- Cutting Defense in Warfare deals a small amount of Magic Damage to attackers whenever they damage you with a Direct Damage attack within 7 meters

## Defense
There are several ways of protecting yourself against Magic Damage.
- A Glyph of Spell Resistance, created with a Makderi Essence rune and a negative Potency rune, can be applied to jewelry.
- Elemental Aegis in the Staving Death subconstellation of Warfare reduces Magic Damage taken.
