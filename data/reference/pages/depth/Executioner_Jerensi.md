Executioner Jerensi is an Imperial executioner of House Albus serving as the first boss of Exiled Redoubt.

## Related Quests
- Undying Requiem
- Pledge: Exiled Redoubt

## Skills and Abilities
At 80%, 50%, and 30% health, she will summon Albus Jailers and Albus Torturers, who must be killed for her to lose her invulnerability.
