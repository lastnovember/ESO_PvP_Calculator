## Detailed Walkthrough

Speak to Glirion the Redbeard and he will provide the details of his quest.

Accepting the pledge from him:
:"Imperial Prison. The Daedra have overrun it, and a creature called Lord-Warden Dusk leads them, for the glory of Molag Bal. Killing the Lord-Warden, breaking the Daedra's hold over that prison … it's a task worthy of an Undaunted."
:
:"If you're looking for an extra challenge, reach the Lord-Warden and defeat him without being seen by the Vigilant Watchers. The Watchers lurk the halls, ensuring prisoners don't escape."

:"Up for another challenge? You maybe could handle it. There's another book, called the Warden's Tome-read that, and then defeat the Lord Warden. If you can."
:I pledge to explore the Imperial City Prison and return Undaunted.

Travel to the Imperial City Prison and clear it of enemies. More details can be found on the page itself.

Once complete, return to Glirion to claim your reward. Some of his lines changed between Updates 5 and 6.
