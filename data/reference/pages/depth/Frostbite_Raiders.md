# Online:Frostbite Raiders

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Frostbite_Raiders
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Frostbite Raiders are a mostly Nord raiding band active in the Sea of Ghosts, with a hideout on the Frozen Coast in Western Skyrim. The group was recently attacked by the Sea Giant, Chief Gruthar, leading his Half-Giant kin to invade the island, killing many raiders-including their captain.
