# Online:Sun Mage

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Sun_Mage
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Sun Mages (or Suncasters) are a class of hostile humanoid NPCs that attack using melee and magical abilities. They are usually equipped with a two-handed sword, battleaxe, or mace. The class was introduced in Murkmire, but it became more common in Dragonhold.

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Strike:A basic melee attack dealing minor physical damage.
;Sunshot:Basic ranged attack dealing low flame damage.
;Cleave:The Sun Mage imbue their weapon with flames before striking their target three to five times back to back, dealing low flame damage each.
;Sundogs:The Sun Mage plant their weapon into the ground, summoning two orbs of solar energy near them, which then move towards their target and launch themselves at them, dealing moderate flame damage followed by low flame damage over time. This attack can be interrupted to set the Sun Mage off-balance.
;Sunburst/Afterimage:The Sun Mage spins around then plant their weapon into the ground which makes a meteor fall at their target's feet, dealing low flame damage and knocking down in an area. Shortly after another meteor falls nearby, dealing low flame damage and knocking down in an area (Sunburst) from which emerge flame projectiles that travel in a straight line in six directions around it, each dealing low flame damage (Afterimage).
