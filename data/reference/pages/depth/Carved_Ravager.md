Carved Ravagers are stone Bretons that are awakened by Menhir Stoneskin.

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Strike:A basic melee attack dealing moderate physical damage.
;Uppercut:An Uppercut-like attack dealing high physical damage, knocking back and stunning. This can be blocked to knock the Carved Ravager off balance instead.
;Cleave:The Carved Ravager swing their weapon around like Cleave does, dealing moderate-high physical damage to all targets in front of them. This attack has no red cone indicator.

## Bugs
Despite being champion rank enemies, the Carved Ravagers are vulnerable to crowd control.
