Blackmaw is a giant armored troll that serves as one of the champions of Labyrinthian. Upon approach, it is seen dispatching a group of skeletons.

## Skills and Abilities
;Slap: A basic melee attack dealing high physical damage.
;Boulder Toss: Blackmaw digs up a boulder and throws it a target, indicated by a red circle, dealing very high physical damage and knocking them down.
;Tremor: Blackmaw pounds the ground in front of itself five times (instead of three times like standard troll), indicated by a red circle, dealing moderate physical damage with each hit.
;Groundbreak: Blackmaw pounds the ground, in a similar fashion to Tremor, but this time sends out shockwaves forward at targets, dealing high physical damage each. These don't have AoE indicators.
;Annihilate: Blackmaw stands firmly upon the ground, as a wind storm begins to build around it, before slamming the ground and causing the storm to burst. This is indicated by a very large and quickly growing red circle and deals massive physical damage, bordering on being a one-shot.

## Achievements
