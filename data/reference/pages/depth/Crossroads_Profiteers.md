# Online:Crossroads Profiteers

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Crossroads_Profiteers
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Crossroads Profiteers are a ragtag group of scavengers who follow battles and loot the corpses of fallen soldiers in Cyrodiil to resell at a profit. They recruit Alliance War deserters and prisoners to aid them.

The Profiteers also hire Level 8 woodworkers, blacksmiths, enchanters and clothiers and Level 4 alchemists to make goods for them to sell alongside their scavenged loot. They have a camp in Upper Craglorn on the road west of Dragonstar, near the Iron Orcs' territory.
