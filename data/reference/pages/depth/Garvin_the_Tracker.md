Garvin the Tracker is a Breton soldier who tracked down the island of Lep Seclusa and led Orpheon's army to it. He serves as the first boss of the dungeon. He uses many poison-based attacks.

## Related Quests
- Upstart Emperor
- Pledge: Lep Seclusa

## Skills and Abilities

During the fight, he will summon Monstrous Dunerippers, Deserter Flame Archers, Deserter Infusers, and Deserter Storm Mages.
;Slice:
;Whirlwind: Heavy attack, applies Healing Absorb
;Noxious Boulder: Garvin channels a green spell that shoots a large boulder at the target, which stuns those who do not block and will create a toxic AoE when it hits the ground. Can be aimed at Monstrous Dunerippers to kill them
;Noxious Aura:
;Vanishing Powder: Garvin teleports away to set traps. Sister Chana's tracking spell will reveal his location by outlining him.
;Hunter's Trap: Traps that are cast after he disappears, setting them up in corners of the room. Will cause bleeding damage.
;Piercing Dervish: Casts a damage over time effect in an hourglass shaped radius
;Wild Spores: Garvin puts a highly damaging damage over time effect on everyone for 8 seconds, giving everyone a green aura. Should be outhealed to avoid damage.

;Venom Eruption: Garvin holds his dagger up and lets out bursts of venom in a large area around him. Using rocks as cover can block the damage.
;Venomous Cloud:
;Ricochet (Veteran only): Garvin puts Ricochet on two players; if they come into contact with each other, they will take massive damage
;Duneripper Spawn: Around 70% and 40% health, will summon dunerippers.
;Summon Storm Mage: Around 80% health, will summon a storm mage that electrifies the waters.

## Quest-Related Events
When you approach the entrance to the Underground Monastery in the Secluded Cave, Garvin the Tracker will be seen at the top of the staircase, where he will hurl a boulder at Hushiah and Mubanat before vanishing:
:Garvin the Tracker: "Caught you!"
:Hushiah and Mubanat:

As you make your way through the monastery rooms, you will be attacked by Garvin when exiting Lewin Frey's chamber, where he will hurl the Noxious Boulders as a trap before vanishing once again:
:Garvin the Tracker: "Taste dirt!"

When you reach him in his main chamber of the Inlet Grotto, Sister Chana and Khafdeed will arrive beside you:
:Khafdeed: "He's the dog who sniffed our island out. The tracker who led Orpheon's army here."
:Sister Chana Nirine: "There's something familiar about his magic. I can help."
:Khafdeed: "Help from up here. I can protect you from attacks or uneven terrain. Not both."

During the fight, Garvin the Tracker will periodically disappear from sight and appear somewhere else in the room. When this happens, Sister Chana will use her abilities to make Garvin visible to you through obstacles and inform you each time she does.
