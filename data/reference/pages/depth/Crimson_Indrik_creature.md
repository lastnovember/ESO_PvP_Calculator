Crimson Indriks are located at the Event Exchanges located outside the main city of each Alliance's starting zone. They are found in pens next to the Impresario.

They can no longer be found near the Impresario as of the end of the 2020 New Life Festival.

## Notes
- The Rosethorn Indrik pet is the smaller equivalent of the Crimson Indrik.
