Dead Priests are skeleton healers involved in the fight against the Sable Knight at Y'ffre's Cauldron. One is initially present, and many more will be summoned during the encounter.

## Related Quests
- The Sable Knight (quest)

## Skills and Abilities
;Minor Wound:A basic ranged attack that does minor magical damage.
;Focused Healing:The enemy will heal any allies that are hurt, including itself. Enemies often indicate this by calling out for healing (I'm hurt!, I need healing!, Healer! etc.) This effect appears as a yellow beam connecting the healer and its target. This spell can be interrupted to set the enemy off balance.

## Bugs
Despite being champion-rank, they are vulnerable to crowd control.
