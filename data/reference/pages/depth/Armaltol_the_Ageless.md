Armaltol the Ageless is a necromancer in High Elf form who will ask for your assistance in entering a new host when he is encountered as a random encounter on Western Solstice.

## Skills and Abilities

### Bonelord
;Quick Strike:A basic melee attack that does minor physical damage.
;Entropic Bolt:A basic ranged attack that does minor magic damage.
;Summon Abomination:Armaltol the Ageless summons a Zombie to fight. The summon will die if Armaltol the Ageless is killed.
;Bone Cage/Closed Fist:Armaltol the Ageless casts a rune on the ground that activates after a while, dealing low magic damage and rooting targets who step in it. This attack can be interrupted to knock Armaltol the Ageless off balance.

## Dialogue
When you approach him, he will be standing beside four different bodies, a Nightblade, Berserker, Beastcaller, and Swashbuckler.
:Armaltol the Ageless: "Mortal, come aid a weary soul."
:Armaltol the Ageless: "The body I currently possess grows frail. I need a new host."
:Armaltol the Ageless: "Before I can enter a new host, this body must be defeated in battle. Fight me, mortal!"

After you fight and defeat him, he will jump into another body. This will happen three times, and on the fourth jump Armaltol will be happy with the body he picked:
;Berserker:
:Berserker: "A brawler would be different!"

;Nightblade (Khajiit):
:Nightblade: "Perhaps a nightblade this time."

;Beastcaller (Argonian):
:Beastcaller: "An Argonian? Interesting."

;Swashbuckler (Maormer):
:Swashbuckler: "Swashbuckling could be fun!"

After you defeat three of the four bodies, he will say the following line regardless of his current form:
:Swashbuckler/Nightblade/Beastcaller/Berserker: "That's enough! A new host awaits me. Blessings upon you, mortal."
