The Ancient Spriggan is an optional spriggan mini-boss in Elden Hollow I. She can be found along with other spriggan enemies in a small side chamber, to the south of the room where Akash gra-Mal is found.

## Skills and Abilities
As a boss type enemy, the Ancient Spriggan is immune to all crowd control effects.
;Lacerate: A basic melee attack that does minor physical damage.
;Nature's Swarm: The spriggan summons a swarm of insects, dealing moderate physical damage to all enemies in front of it as indicated by a red cone.
;Summon Animal: The spriggan will summon an animal to assist it in combat. The animal will remain even if the spriggan is killed. These animals are usually appropriate to the zone the spriggan appears in, and include bears, durzogs, spiders, senche-tigers, and wolves.
;Empower Allyplain: The spriggan will channel a buff on the animal, causing it to do double damage. This spell can be interrupted to set the spriggan off balance.
;Healing Salve: When hurt, the spriggan will heal itself. This spell can be interrupted to set the spriggan off balance.
