The Antecedent Scholar's Guar is a guar mount. It is be available in the Crown Store for 3,000 and was added on March 13, 2023 on PC/Mac and March 28, 2023 on Xbox/PlayStation. It is also available as part of the Scribes of Fate Collector's Bundle for 4,000 alongside the Blightlord's Skeever. Its default name is "Grimgaw".
