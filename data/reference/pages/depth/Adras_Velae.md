Adras Velae is a mendicant Chimer mage who is imprisoned in Heimlyn Keep. In life, he and Rendrasa Malena, devotees of the Daedric Prince Namira, created the Spellplague as a weapon against the Dwemer, but after it proved to be uncontrollable they were bound to the vault with their wicked creation. He is fought at his spell cage, and later opens a portal so you may fight Mendicant Sulinor and dispel the curse.

## Skills and Abilities
;Flare:A basic ranged attack dealing minor flame damage.
;Heat Wave:Adras Velae releases fire along the ground in the direction he is facing, dealing moderate flame damage to all targets hit. This can be interrupted to set Adras Velae off balance. This cannot be blocked.
;Fire Runes:Adras Velae shoots two fireballs in the air which form two fire runes when they land. The runes deal minor flame damage over time to all targets within the red circles. Players should be prepared to move as soon as the attack casts as one rune will always be cast on top of you.
;Run Away!:When too close to him, Adras Velae backs up to put some distance between his target and himself.

## Quest-Related Events
You can speak to him after binding him.
:"Begone! Your living breath pollutes our bone-dry air."

He will be present in the ritual chamber during your fight with Mendicant Sulinor.
