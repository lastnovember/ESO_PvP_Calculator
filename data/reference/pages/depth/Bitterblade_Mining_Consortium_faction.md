# Online:Bitterblade Mining Consortium (faction)

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Bitterblade_Mining_Consortium_(faction)
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Bitterblade Mining Consortium is a Nord-ran mining company active in Skyrim. They were the first to venture down into Blackreach to open a mine, which they built Dusktown around.

As expected, it has proven difficult to manage. As a consequence of the whole secrecy of the operation, miners working for the Consortium must be blindfolded before being brought to Blackreach, and must remain at the mine for the entirety of their contract.
