icon = Chiseling out Corruption
type = DLC
type2 = Lost Depths

desc = Defeat Corruption of Stone after placing the challenge banner in Veteran Earthen Root Enclave.

Chiseling out Corruption is awarded for defeating Corruption of Stone in Veteran Earthen Root Enclave after placing the challenge banner, which will enable Hardmode and make the fight more difficult:
- The enemies will be generally stronger.
- Shockwaves after Earthquake Stomp will have increased area.is it size, though? that ability feels different, can't quite certainly put my finger on why yet
- All four Stone Atronachs will come to life after Unstable Explosion phases (instead of three in non-Hardmode).
