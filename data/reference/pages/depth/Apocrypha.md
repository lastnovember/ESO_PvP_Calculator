It is the larger of the two zones in the Necrom Chapter, the other being the Telvanni Peninsula.

It is inhabited by Hushed, Lurkers, Seekers and Tomeshells.

## Notes
- The Daedric text written around the zone on the Aurbis map translates to THE REALM OF APOCRYPHA ETERNAL LIBRARY OF HERMAEUS.
- Apocrypha was first mentioned in The Doors of Oblivion and first appeared in Dragonborn.
- The cut quest "Devotees of Knowledge" for Apocrypha's Gate was intended to involve a gate to Apocrypha, where you would throw a Diadem of Memories filled with knowledge taken from Dagon's Dremora into Apocrypha for Mora to analyze.
- The word "Apocryphal" means: A story or statement of doubtful authenticity, although widely circulated as being true.
