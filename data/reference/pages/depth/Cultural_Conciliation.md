## Notes
- Rigurt can be in two different locations at once during this quest. If the ingredients for the feast are gathered first after having acquired both tasks, Rigurt will be in Chillwind Depths and the fishing stream at the same time. This also can occur if both tasks have been completed and are set to return to the respective thanes.
- The description of the Dungcap Mushrooms incorrectly state that they grow in Shadowgreen, while they are acquired in Chillwind Depths.

## Bugs
After arriving at Thane Hraldi's fishing spot and after he and Rigurt finish their dialogue, it is possible that the prompt to speak with Hraldi fails to appear, blocking the quest.
- Going away and returning to the location fixes this.
