Anchorite Garmar is a Nord Worm Cultist found on the Riften docks during the related quest. He is raising corpses from Lake Honrich as drowned zombies, and you will have to kill him and his minions.

## Related Quests
- Pulled Under

## Skills and Abilities
;Flare:A basic ranged attack that does minor flame damage.
;Heat Wave:The enemy releases fire along the ground in the direction it is facing. This attack does moderate flame damage to all players hit by it. This spell can be interrupted to set the enemy off balance or simply avoided.
;Fire Runes:The enemy shoots two fireballs in the air which form two fire runes when they land. The runes do moderate flame damage over time to all enemies within the red circles and should be avoided. Players should be prepared to move as soon as the attack casts as one rune will always be cast on top of you.
