Iogreth is a Nord poet who can be heard reciting her work outside the Whale Splash Inn in Shor's Stand.

## Dialogue
When you approach her, she will recite a line from her work-in-progress, which is one of the following:
:Igoreth: "Visions of islands drowned in hopes. Look to tankards and then to ship's ropes."
:Igoreth: "The waves our boat stole. They climbed aboard barrels and over waves did roll."
:Igoreth: "With bow aglow Njor shot the night. Silence bloomed with dawn's first light."
:Igoreth: "All over and done, our village founded with ease. They sat back and ate the cheese."

You can also speak to her:
:"Like what you heard? It's an original poem. I'm working on chronicling our great village's stories in verse. It's an enormous amount of work, but the Hall of Feathers pays me well and appreciates my efforts."

During The Stubbornness of Nords, she will have different dialogue:
:"I don't take requests. I only read stories or poetry I like."Paraphrased and definitely not her exact dialogue

After completing The Stubbornness of Nords, she will instead say the following:
:"So, we're going to help the Stirk Fellowship rid this island of the Stirk Fellowship? How wonderful. That should give me plenty of new material to write poems about."
