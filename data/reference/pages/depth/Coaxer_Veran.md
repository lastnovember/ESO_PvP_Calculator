Coaxer Veran is a Dark Elf member of the Sixth House found in the Forgotten Wastes. Together with Castigator Athin and Confessor Dradas, she forms a group that is one of the bosses of that dungeon.

She uses a mix of Cauterizer and spirit master class abilities during combat.

## Skills and Abilities
As a champion rank enemy, Coaxer Veran is immune to crowd control.
;Minor Wound:A basic ranged attack dealing minor magic damage.
;Focused Healing:Coaxer Veran will heal any allies that are hurt, including himself but for half as much. Enemies often indicate this by calling out for healing (I'm hurt!, I need healing!, Healer! etc.). This effect appears as a yellow beam connecting Coaxer Veran and his target which breaks if the line of sight between them is broken. The first tick begins 0.5 seconds after the channel begins, and also heals half as much for the first 3 ticks. This can be interrupted to stagger Coaxer Veran. Coaxer Veran never targets summons from himself or his allies, for example Ice Barrier.
;Burdening Eye: Coaxer Veran summons a Burdening Eye that moves toward a player.
;Spell Absorption: Coaxer Veran channels a protection spell on an ally that reduces its damage taken by 80% from Magical Attacks (Magic, Flame, Frost and Shock damage). This spell can be interrupted to stagger Coaxer Veran instead.
;Rite of Passage:Coaxer Veran channels a spell healing himself and nearby allies for 10% of his Max Health every half second for 4 seconds. It takes half a second to cast, and another half a second to begin healing its target once the effect is applied. This ability is usually used when Coaxer Veran is on low health. This can be interrupted to stagger Coaxer Veran instead. (Bugged, see #Bugs)
