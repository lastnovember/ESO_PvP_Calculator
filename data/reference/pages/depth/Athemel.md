Athemel is a Wood Elf chef working at the Silver Moons Inn at the Baandari Trading Post. You can find her behind her counter to the left perpetually eating a loaf of bread.

## Related Quests
- A Tangled Net

## Quest-Related Events

At the inn, she's busy eating.
:"It's rude to interrupt someone while they're eating! Hmm, or is it the other way around?"

At Fazaddu's House during A Tangled Net:
:"This dress is ridiculous! How can I cook a decent meal in this? Well, if you're interested in Bosmer cuisine, let me know. I'll change into something more practical."
She will make Longstig happy.
