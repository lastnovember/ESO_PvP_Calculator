Dremora Ravagers are Dremora warriors who can be found in the Rimmen Necropolis, in various Deadlands-related locations in Blackwood content, in The Brandfire Reformatory, and in the Infinite Archive.

## Skills and Abilities

### Standard
;Quick Strike:A basic melee attack dealing minor physical damage.
;Uppercut:An Uppercut-like attack dealing moderate physical damage, knocking back and stunning. This can be blocked to knock the ravager off balance instead.
;Cleave:The ravager swing their weapon around like Cleave does, dealing minor physical damage to all targets in front of them. This attack has no red cone indicator.

### Rimmen Necropolis
The Dremora Ravagers in Rimmen Necropolis also have this ability, see Bugs.
;Desecrated Ground:On death, the Dremora Ravager rarely desecrates the ground under them as indicated by a red circle. This does continuous minor magic damage and snares all targets as well as healing undead enemies within the red circle for 3% of the original Dremora Ravager's Max Health per tick.

### Infinite Archive
The Dremora Ravagers in Infinite Archive also have this ability.
;Cleave Stance:Dremora Ravager enrages then exclusively performs strong front cleave attacks, dealing moderate physical damage, in a conical AoE for a limited time.
