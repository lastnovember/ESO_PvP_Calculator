## Detailed Walkthrough

At Wretched Spire, the player will have the option to speak to additional NPCs if their respective quests were completed in Blackwood, namely Captain Rian Liore, Commander Axius, Keshu the Black Fin, Otumi-Ra, Elossi, Meer-Ta and Elam Drals.

## Bugs
While the Anchorite examines the relic and performs the holding animation, there is nothing actually in her hand to represent the relic.
The objective area for the "Reclaim the Siege Weapons" objective is missing from the local map.
Mehrunes Dagon's first line of dialogue when he is hit by the siege weapons plays double over itself.
