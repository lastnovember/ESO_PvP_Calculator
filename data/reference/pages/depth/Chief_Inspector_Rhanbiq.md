Chief Inspector Rhanbiq is a Redguard and one of the Chief Inspectors of the Iron Wheel. He is in charge of the investigation hunting down the culprits who stole the dowry of Magnifica Falorah and has been shaking down the local Thieves Guild of Hew's Bane.

He is known to be a stoic and humorless individual, on the other hand he is also considered unbribable and focused on duty.

While tracking down Nicolas, you will be given the lead about someone known as the "Desert Falcon", who is also looking for him. Once you encounter the Falcon in one of the inns across Tamriel, you will discover that he is none other than Chief Inspector Rhanbiq. Except, he was stripped of that title and had to resign from the Iron Wheel in the aftermath and Rhanbiq is not pleased to see you.

Speak with him and ask what he knows about Nicolas:
:"You are the last person I expected to see. Did you come to gloat? To tell me my search for Nicolas will end in failure? And how did you learn I was the Desert Falcon?"
:Chief Inspector Rhanbiq? You're the Desert Falcon?
:"I'm no longer chief inspector of anything. After your guild escaped No Shira Citadel, I had no choice but to resign from the Iron Wheel. The journal was a nice touch. I'd hoped it would seal the fate of Nicolas at his trial. But a storm did that. Allegedly."
:Why are you looking for Nicolas?

If you did not surrender the Skull in Fulstrom, he will be reluctant to help you:
:"You don't get that from me. You couldn't follow simple instructions to put down a sparkling trinket. How I can I possibly trust you?"
:[Persuade] The Iron Wheel killed many good people. Is their blood on your hands, or is it on Nicolas?
::"We thought Abah's Landing was full of bandits! The merchant lords pointed us in your guild's direction. When I realized they used us to settle old debts, I withdrew the Iron Wheel to No Shira Citadel. You think me heartless? That I don't regret our methods?"
