# Online:House Indoril

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:House_Indoril
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

House Indoril is one of the Great Houses of Morrowind. Their house seat, Mournhold, is also the capital city of the Ebonheart Pact. House Indoril is a rather militaristic and religious house, hence their heavy contributions and involvement with the Pact, as well as having a significant presence within the Ordinators.

The House is ruled by Grandmaster Tanval Indoril, who resides in the Indoril city of Davon's Watch. The Vanguard are a subfaction of this Great House.
