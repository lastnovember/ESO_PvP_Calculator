Bog Dog Bonecrushers are bog dogs found in the hills southeast of Leyawiin, near The Silent Halls, and in the Dawnwood.

## Skills and Abilities
;Bite:The bog dog attacks with its maw, dealing low physical damage.
;Claw:The bog dog attacks with its claw, dealing slightly more physical damage than "Bite".
;Back Kick:If standing behind the bog dog, it will kick with its hind legs, indicated by a red cone, dealing moderate physical damage and staggering targets.
;Leap: The bog dog leaps around its target, though it doesn't deal damage.
;Pinning Strike:The bod dog pounces forward at its target, dealing moderate physical damage and knocking them down. This can be blocked to set the bog dog off-balance.
