# Online:Iron Weapons

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Iron_Weapons
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

This is a listing of all standard Iron Weapons in the game. These can be crafted via Blacksmithing from Iron Ingots obtained by refining Iron Ore. The listed damage values assume that the character is CP160; lower level characters may see higher values.
