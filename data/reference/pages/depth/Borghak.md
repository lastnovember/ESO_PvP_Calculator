Borghak was an Orc who could be encountered leaning against a wall in Port Hunding. She was removed in an unknown patch and can no longer be found in-game.

## Dialogue
She gave you a piece of friendly advice.
:"Don't cross the headman here, stranger. You'll end up in the grave."
