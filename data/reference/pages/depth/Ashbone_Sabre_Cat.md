The Ashbone Sabre Cat was available in the Crown Store individually for 3,000, or as part of the Scions of Ithelia Collector's Bundle alongside the Ashbone Echalette for 4,000. It entered the Crown Store on March 11, 2024 for PC, and March 26, 2024 for consoles. Its default name is "Sybille".

The bundle was available for free to ESO Plus members for free during a two-week window starting on each specific platform's launch: from March 11 to March 25 for PC/Mac, and March 26 to April 9 for Xbox and PlayStation consoles.

As of Update 49, this mount was added to stablemasters in Morthal, Solitude, Bangkorai Garrison, Hallin's Stand, and the Pelin's Watch Stables in Evermore for 100,000.
