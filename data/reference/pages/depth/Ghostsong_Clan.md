# Online:Ghostsong Clan

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Ghostsong_Clan
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Ghostsong Clan are a clan of Reachfolk found in the Reach around 2E 582. They worship Namira, whom they refer to as the "Spirit Queen". As such, many among their ranks have performed rituals to commune with the Void, one of which can be seen at Four Skull Lookout.

Arana, the leader of the Wayward Guardians, was the previous matron of the clan before she departed following the rift between her and her sister Nathari, concerning the direction the clan should be taken into.
