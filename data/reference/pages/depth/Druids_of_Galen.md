# Online:Druids of Galen

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Druids_of_Galen
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Druids of Galen were the first druids, and they were followers of Y'ffre. The name is used as a retroactive nomenclature for the Nedic ancestors of the Bretons, though they had already transitioned into Manmer and referred to themselves as Druids of Galen by the time they were led on an exodus by the last Druid King to settle on the Systres Archipelago. There, they disbanded and reorganized themselves into the three modern druidic circles. They can be encountered in flashbacks or as undead.
