# Online:Broken Coast Bandits

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Broken_Coast_Bandits
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Broken Coast Bandits, also referred to by Captain Khammo as the Broken Coast Bastards, are a crew of Bosmer treasure hunters and smugglers operating on the eponymous coast in Malabal Tor.

They sometimes offer ferrying services to fellow pirates, such as Captain Khammo, to places such as nearby Anvil, as long as the offer is good enough for them.
