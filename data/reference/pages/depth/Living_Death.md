# Online:Living Death

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Living_Death
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Living Death is one of three class skill lines available to Necromancers. It can be learned by other classes via subclassing. Abilities in this line focus on support and healing. Known abilities in this skill are listed below.

## Skill Perks
The Living Death skill line is immediately available to all Necromancers, and is leveled up by earning experience while Living Death skills are slotted. Skills include:
