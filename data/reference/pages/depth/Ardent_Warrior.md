# Online:Ardent Warrior

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Ardent_Warrior
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Ardent Warrior is one of three class skill lines available to Bastian Hallix. Abilities in this line focus on dealing damage. It is roughly equivalent to the Ardent Flame skill line for Dragonknight players.

## Skill Perks
The Ardent Warrior skill line is immediately available to Bastian, and is leveled up as he gains levels.
