Boltwyrms are shock-based elemental wraiths found in The Dread Cellar. They are also summoned by Cyronin Artellian during combat.

## Skills and Abilities
;Arresting Bolt: The wyrm tethers itself on a target, dealing increasing amounts of shock damage and snaring them.
