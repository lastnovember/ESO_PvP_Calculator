Endris is a Dark Elf who is imprisoned in the Cauldron. He is Drathas' father and Avron's brother. By the conclusion of the dungeon, he is freed and reunited with his son.

## Related Quests
- Into the Deep

Once Baron Zaudrus has been defeated in the Exalted Summit, Drathas will meet you there, along with his father. After you speak with Lyranth and turn in the quest, you'll hear Endris' words to his son, which Lyranth will interrupt, so Drathas can open the lockbox and fulfill his part of the deal:
:Endris: "I am proud of you Drathas. I thought for sure my death was near. Never did I imagine you capable of such strength."
:Drathas: "Thank you, father. My friends helped me more than I can say."
:Lyranth: "A touching reunion indeed. But you must fulfill the bargain, Drathas."
:Drathas: "Yes, of course. One moment."
