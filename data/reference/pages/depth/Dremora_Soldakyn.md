Dremora Soldakyn are Dremora spirit masters that can be found in the Imperial City's Arboretum and Nobles District.

## Skills and Abilities

### Arboretum District
The Dremora Soldakyns in the Arboretum District are Spirit Masters.
;Ice Arrow: Basic ranged attack dealing minor frost damage.
;Burdening Eye: The Dremora Soldakyn summons a Burdening Eye that moves toward a player.
;Burden: If the Burdening Eye touches the player, it explodes, dealing minor magic damage and snaring in an area.
;Spell Absorption: The Dremora Soldakyn channels a protection spell on an enemy that reduces its damage taken by 80% from Magical Attacks (Magic, Flame, Frost and Shock damage). This spell can be interrupted to knock the Dremora Soldakyn off balance.
;Run Away!:When too close to them, the Dremora Soldakyn backs up to put some distance between their target and themselves.
;Summon Dark Anchor:The Dremora Soldakyn summons a Daedric Arch which in turn periodically summons very weak Banekin to fight for them.
;Health Potion:The Dremora Soldakyn drinks a potion, restoring 7,524 Health. This can be interrupted to set the Dremora Soldakyn off balance.
;War Horn:Very rarely, the Dremora Soldakyn blows a war horn, increasing the Max Health of their nearby allies and themselves by 15%. (see Bugs)

### Nobles District
The Dremora Soldakyns in the Nobles District are Frost Mages.
;Ice Arrow:A basic ranged attack dealing minor frost damage.
;Ice Barrier: The Dremora Soldakyn summons an Ice Barrier that absorbs projectiles fired at it until destroyed. The Dremora Soldakyn cannot be pulled if the wall stands between them and the target. The player should move so that there is a clear line of sight between themselves and the Dremora Soldakyn.
;Winter's Reach:The Dremora Soldakyn unleashes an icy whirlwind in the direction it is facing, dealing moderate frost damage and rooting all targets hit. The root effect passes through block. This can be interrupted to set the Dremora Soldakyn off balance.
;Run Away!:When too close to them, the Dremora Soldakyn backs up to put some distance between their target and themselves. This does not apply if the Dremora Soldakyn is near its Ice Barrier.
;Summon Dark Anchor:The Dremora Soldakyn summons a Daedric Arch which in turn periodically summons very weak Banekin to fight for them.
;Health Potion:The Dremora Soldakyn drinks a potion, restoring 7,524 Health. This can be interrupted to set the Dremora Soldakyn off balance.
;War Horn:Very rarely, the Dremora Soldakyn blows a war horn, increasing the Max Health of their nearby allies and themselves by 15%. (see Bugs)

## Bugs
The Dremora Soldakyns can use the "War Horn" ability despite it being reserved for Alliance NPCs
