## Detailed Walkthrough

The Worm Cult plans on raiding a Stone-Nest necropolis: they may also be holding Vanus captive there. The burial complex, known as Xul-Haj, lies along the far northeastern coast of Solstice, close to Jeetra's home village. Speak with Jeetra before heading over to her village.

You'll arrive to find the village abandoned, a Soul Flayer set smack-dab in the middle of town. There's a dead cultist and a note nearby: read it, then head into the building behind you to find Jeetra.

The Worm Cult got to the village before you could. Everyone is dead. Jeetra agrees to meet you at Xul-Haj after she's performed her duties as death-dancer. Xul-Haj lies in the hills east of the village: head on over. Inside, you'll find the Worm Cult has erected a ward over the door to the deep tombs. Speak with Jeetra: she explains that the lower halls can be flooded and cleared with a complex irrigation system. She's going to take you through an alternate route, but you're going to have to swim.

Before you can do anything, Darien hears Gabrielle's voice near the ward and falls through a trapdoor. Completely ignoring Jeetra's warnings about deceptive spirits, he chases after the voice. Jeetra stomps in frustration and beckons you to the southeastern door, which leads to the lower halls. Not terribly far into the ruin, you'll encounter an Argonian spirit that points you towards Darien. Speak with it before moving on.
:"Something has disturbed our slumber, awakened spirits, and roused warriors to defend the xanmeer. Was it you or the egg-kin beside you? Perhaps the disillusioned knight who chases shadows? No, you are not looters. Yet you stir the dreaming Stone."
:Necromancers called the Worm Cult seek to pillage your tomb. We're here to stop them.
:"Yes, I see. It happens again. The corpse-stealers use iron roots and cursed magic to bind this realm to Coldharbour. We stopped such an attempt long, long ago. Let me help you end this threat in the here and now."
:Can you tell us how to get to the cultists or how to save our friend the knight?
:"This empty cistern once carried waters that filled these halls and protected the interment sites. But dry-skin hands have drained the pools dry, for they seek the bones of our kin. Stir the waters, raise the pools, and the path will reveal itself."
:How do I raise the pools?
:"The nearby door leads to the sacred wellsprings. Restore their flow to swim to your goal. But know that malign spirits lurk here. They will tempt you. Do not let their shadows overtake you. And the corpse-stealers, they have begun their ritual. Go!"

The Worm Cult has drained the cistern to access the bones of those interred in Xul-Haj: the spirit explains that raising the water again will reveal the path forward. Head through the nearby door and traverse the ruins until you reach the Wellspring Chamber. The water is displaced because the Worms moved the sacred wellspring stones out of position: you'll need to put them back to flood the ruins. The stones need to be pushed back onto the square tiles on the east and west sides of the chamber's carved stone centerpiece. You must attack the blocks to move them, and can activate the floating, glowing green bismuth chunks to reset the stones to their original positions. Once the stones are back in place and the water flows, exit the wellspring room through the southern hallway and continue your excursion.

Now that the water has risen, you can swim across the cistern to a door on the opposite side of the room, but it needs to rise a little higher if you want to reach Darien. Swim over to the other side of the flooded cistern and pass through the door: inside, you'll find another sliding stone puzzle.

For the stone closest to the entrance: slide it north, then east. At the intersection, push it south to the tile. This opens the gate keeping the next cube from being pushed out of its home. Push the next cube east past the gate, then return to the first cube and start pushing it all the way to the north end of the track. Push it east at the earliest opportunity, then set the second cube on the tile at the south end of the next fork. This opens the second gate for the other cube. Now go back and push the second cube east through the gate and set it on the tile at the end of the track, at the base of the pillar of carved heads. Once the water has resumed flowing, head through the nearby hallway and return to the cistern chamber to find Darien.

Now that the water level is high enough, you can swim over to the central platform and cross the bridge leading to the western door. You'll arrive just in time to see the apparition posing as Gabrielle Benele doing something to Darien. Defeat the nearby Worm Cultists, and the "spirit" reveals itself to be a Darkbinder Harvester Enchanter. Defeat it and talk to a dazed Darien.
:"I wanted to tell Gabrielle that I'm sorry. She gave everything to return me to life, and …. Damn it, I thought I could go back to being … normal. But when I heard her voice, I was overwhelmed by despair."
:It was a trap, Darien. That was the Worm Cult, not Gabrielle.

With Darien safe and sound, it's time to find Vanus before the Worm Cult expends his power on their ritual. The southern door in the next room over will bring you to the central burial chamber, where you'll find the great mage entrapped by Mannimarco.

Defeat the waves of Worm Cultists and undead until Jeetra frees Vanus. A Soul Shriven Behemoth arrives on the scene to contend with you while Vanus duels with Mannimarco. Once the behemoth is down, use the Light of Meridia to destroy the shackle. Exit the necropolis through the portal Vanus opens for you, then talk to the spirit Ukka-Raza outside.
:"Thank you, warrior. You stopped the desecration of our remains and saved us from enslavement to the corpse-stealers. The Stone will not forget this service."
:Spirit, you never told me who you are.
:"Ah, it has been so long since I had the opportunity to use the courtesies of life. I am Ukka-Raza, the architect of Xul-Haj. We built this xanmeer as a necropolis for our people. But I must deliver one last warning before I return to rest."
:What warning?
:"Mor Naril, the Final Dark, has a new master. One who seeks to pull the wraithforge from Coldharbour and restore it in the Daedric temple. You cannot defeat your foe while he commands the wraithforge."
:How do you know all this?
:"You are not the first to face the forces of death and Oblivion on this isolated island. If this new master gains full control of the wraithforge, you will never defeat him. Now, go. Your allies have a new camp near Mor Naril. They await you there."

Speak with Vanus once you're done with Ukka-Raza.
:"The spirit's warning coincides with what I pieced together through the things I overheard during my captivity. And what the helpful Worm Cult defector shared with me. Pray tell, did Baar-Jasa deliver my notes to Gabrielle?"
:We got your notes, but Gabrielle is dead, Vanus. And so is Baar-Jasa.
:"What? Gabrielle? What happened? I assume it has something to do with the return of Meridia's knight. And Baar-Jasa, too? I was taken by surprise when I learned Mannimarco had returned, but now I owe him for these deaths. And so many others …."
: sacrificed herself to stop Wormblood from using the Gift of Death. She brought back Darien.
:"That explains why Mannimarco's full resurrection failed and he had to possess his servant's body. A shame about Gabrielle. She had such promise. And Baar-Jasa, too. She was looking forward to reconciling with her egg-sister."
:So what do you know about this wraithforge?
:"It's some sort of Daedric crucible created by Molag Bal to collect and utilize soul energy. It was originally part of Mor Naril here in Solstice, but Bal withdrew with it back to Coldharbour when the Argonians defeated him long ago."
:And?
:"Mannimarco is using the soul reapers and flayers to fill it. And the Planemeld is the mechanism employed to draw it back from Coldharbour. I assume the allies the spirit mentioned include Prince Azah? I hope he brought an army. Let's go to the camp."

Head straight east from your position to find your allies and their new camp, and report to Prince Azah with what you've learned.
