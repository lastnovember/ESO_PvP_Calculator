# Online:Bronzefists

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Bronzefists
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Bronzefists are a hostile group of Altmer mercenaries found within Nchuleftingth. They were hired by Artisan Lenarmen to keep Neramo out of the ruins.
