# Online:Bone Tyrant

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Bone_Tyrant
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Bone Tyrant is one of three class skill lines available to Necromancers. It can be learned by other classes via subclassing. Abilities in this line focus on defense and tanking. Known abilities in this skill are listed below.

## Skill Perks
The Bone Tyrant skill line is immediately available to all Necromancers, and is leveled up by earning experience while Bone Tyrant skills are slotted. Skills include:
