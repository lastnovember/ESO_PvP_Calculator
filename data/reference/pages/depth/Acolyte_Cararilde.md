Acolyte Cararilde is a High Elf acolyte who used to be found in the Temple of Auri-El in Vulkhel Guard. As the Temple is no longer accessible except when it is instanced during the quest A Hostile Situation, she cannot be encountered in-game.

## Dialogue
When spoken to, she would say one of two things:
:"Auri-El, soul of Anu and of Everything, graced the world with coherency of time and being. Through Auri-El's grace, was the tyrant Lorkhan defeated, and through his benevolence, did we learn to reach Aetherius. Praise be to him."
:"Observations and praises of Anui-El are carried out in this chamber, one of many sites dedicated to his glory. And so, we gather to preserve and prepare this location, and exalt him, so that all may hear our song."
