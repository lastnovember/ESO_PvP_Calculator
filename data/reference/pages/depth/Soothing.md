# Online:Soothing

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Soothing
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Soothing is a trait which can be found on Companion weapons, armor and jewelry. The trait cannot be researched or crafted, and exists only on found loot. Items with the Soothing trait increase the Healing done by a Companion by an amount dependent on the quality of the item.
