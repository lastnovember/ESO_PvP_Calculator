It was located near the Narsis marketplace, next to Varlas House.

The house had an intermediate lock. Several bookcases used to be found inside the house, as well as many owned containers. The owner of the home, Rilos Lenith, used to be found on the ground floor, which consists of the entrance hall and a small side room. A safebox with an advanced lock used to be found behind the desk in this sideroom. Caretaker Beldros was formerly found sweeping the top floor, consisting of the landing and the master bedroom. A second safebox with an advanced lock is located next to the bed.

The interior had two sealed doors and no basement.

With the release of player housing, the Lenith House was converted into the player home Velothi Reverie.
