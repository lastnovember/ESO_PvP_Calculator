# Online:Precise

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Precise
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Precise is a trait which can only be found on weapons. Once you have researched the trait on a given item type, you can craft Precise weapons by using Ruby. Items with the Precise trait will increase your Weapon Critical and Spell Critical by an amount dependent on the quality of the item. 2-handed weapons provide twice the bonus of 1-handed.
