Alliance Guards are foot soldiers sent to Solstice via a portal to guard the Alliance representatives who are meeting with the Stirk Fellowship. Despite representatives from all three alliances supposedly arriving, only guards from the Daggerfall Covenant and Ebonheart Pact can be seen appearing out of the portal. They have no unique dialogue.
