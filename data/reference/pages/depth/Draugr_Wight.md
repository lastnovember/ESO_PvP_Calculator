Draugr Wights are draugr frost mages found in various Nordic burial sites.

## Skills and Abilities

### Standard
;Ice Arrow:A basic ranged attack dealing minor frost damage.
;Ice Barrier: The enemy summons an Ice Barrier that absorbs projectiles fired at it until destroyed. The enemy cannot be pulled if the wall stands between them and the target. The player should move so that there is a clear line of sight between themselves and the enemy.
;Winter's Reach:The enemy unleashes an icy whirlwind in the direction it is facing, dealing moderate frost damage and rooting all targets hit. The root effect passes through block. This can be interrupted to set the frost mage off balance.
;Run Away!:When too close to them, the frost mage backs up to put some distance between their target and themselves. This does not apply if the frost mage is near its Ice Barrier.

### Angarvunde Mound
The Draugr Wights at Angarvunde Mound use an other set of abilities.
;Minor Wound:A basic ranged attack dealing minor magic damage.
;Focused Healing:The Cauterizer will heal any allies that are hurt, including itself but for half as much. Enemies often indicate this by calling out for healing (I'm hurt!, I need healing!, Healer! etc.). This effect appears as a yellow beam connecting the Cauterizer and its target which breaks if the line of sight between them is broken. The first tick begins 0.5 seconds after the channel begins, and also heals half as much for the first 3 ticks. This can be interrupted to set the Cauterizer off balance. The Cauterizer never targets summons from themselves or their allies, for example Ice Barrier.
;Flame Blossom:The Cauterizer releases fire along the ground in the direction it is facing, dealing low flame damage to all targets hit by it. This can be interrupted to set the Cauterizer off balance.
;Run Away!:When too close to them, the Cauterizer backs up to put some distance between their target and themselves.
