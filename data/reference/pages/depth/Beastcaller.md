# Online:Beastcaller

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Beastcaller
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Beastcallers are a class of hostile, humanoid NPCs who attack using melee and magical abilities. They are usually equipped with a sword and shield. The class was introduced in Morrowind as the NPC version of the Warden player class.

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Strike:Basic melee attack dealing low physical damage.
;Spectal Claw:Basic melee attack dealing low magic damage.
;Swarm:A basic ranged attack where a swarm of spectral fetcherflies-like swarm is thrown at the target, dealing low magic damage.
;Summon Feral Guardian/Raise the Earth:The beastcaller summons a Feral Guardian to fight for them. If the summoned creature is killed, another one will be summoned to replace it. The summon scales with the difficulty, faction, and location of the summoner. The summoned creature will have around the same health as the current health of their summoner but only to a maximum of 80% of the summoner's Max Health, and have extreme damage resistance during the summoning animation. The Beastcaller's Physical Resistance and Spell Resistance are reduced to 0 while a summon is active as seen by a blue glow on them.
;Dive:The beastcaller sends a cliff racer/Falcon diving towards the target, dealing moderate magic in an area.
;Subterranean Assault/Gore:The beastcaller summons a Haj Mota that digs in a straight line, dealing moderate magic damage, knocking back and stunning in its wake.
