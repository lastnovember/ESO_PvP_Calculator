This is a list of the various effects found in the game.

## Damage
Damage in ESO can be one of the following types
- Bleed Damage
- Disease Damage
- Flame Damage
- Frost Damage
- Magic Damage
- Oblivion Damage
- Physical Damage
- Poison Damage
- Shock Damage
- "Fall Damage", which is the damage taken when you land from a high enough fall. It deals a percentage of your Max Health that scales with the height of your fall and bypasses damage shields. Fall Resistance doesn't exist, though some bonuses can reduce your Fall Damage taken. (Fall Damage isn't a damage type per-se, it is in its own category (see Note))
- Non-player sourced damage types: Those damage types aren't like the usual previous damage types. The game never mentions them, they have unique properties and some of them are found only in very precise scenarios.
- "Generic Damage", which is actually found in many places with Traps and NPCs sometimes dealing it. It is theorized to be a placeholder damage type until it is changed into a regular damage type in the development, but devs sometimes forget to change it. Generic Damage is reduced by damage taken reduction effects and by your Generic Resistance which is the highest between your Physical Resistance and Spell Resistance.
- "Drown Damage", which is found in Slaughterfish, Abyssal Assault and the "Exsanguinating Ichor" area in Runemaster's Acropolis. This is not reduced by your damage taken reduction effects, and it bypasses your damage shields. Players have 0 Drown Resistance. Coincidently, those three hazards negate all your healing taken with Heal Absorption.
- "Earth Damage", which is found in Sihadid's "Sands of Awakening" attack, Domihaus the Bloody-Horned Stone Totem's "Slam" attack and in the Corruption of Stone's "Unstable Explosion". It is also found in the Boulder Bash effect gained by completing a Boulder Dash oddity in the Night Market. It is theorized to be a very early damage type that was deprecated, but its recent (and unexpected) re-appearance in the Night Market gently pushes against this notion. Earth Damage is reduced by damage taken reduction effects and by your Earth Resistance which is the highest between your Physical Resistance and Spell Resistance.
- "None Damage", which is found in the Imperial Law hazard when approaching a Dremora Channeler on a Cyrodiil bridge to the Imperial City. Due to the erratic damage numbers of this hazard it has not been determined if it is or isn't reduced by your armor and damage taken reduction effects, and if it bypasses damage shields.
- "Daedric Weapon Damage", which is the damage taken when Volendrung's bar is fully depleted. It deals 100% of the wielder's Max Health + 1 damage. There is no protection against it and it bypasses your damage shields. (This isn't a damage type per-se, it has its own category in the files (see Note))

### Note
- Damage is written in the code as "DAMAGE,(type of Damage)", such as for example Magic Damage appears as "DAMAGE,MAGIC". Flame Damage still retains its old name and appears as "DAMAGE,FIRE", as well as Frost Damage appearing as "DAMAGE,COLD". Fall Damage instead appears as "FALLDAMAGE,GENERIC", and Daedric Weapon Damage appears as "KILLED_BY_DAEDRIC_WEAPON,GENERIC"
