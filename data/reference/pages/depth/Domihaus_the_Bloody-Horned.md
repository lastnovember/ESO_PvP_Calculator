Domihaus the Bloody-Horned is a minotaur of the Dreadhorn Clan, and acts as the final boss of Falkreath Hold. He will arrive by bursting through the Jarl's Hall and landing on Jarl Skjoralmor, fatally crushing him. During the fight, he will summon four types of atronachs (Flame, Frost, Stone, Storm).

He has a chance to drop a Minotaur Bezoar.

## Skills and Abilities
As a boss type enemy, Domihaus is immune to all crowd control effects.
;Fire Bolt: A basic ranged attack that deals minor flame damage.
;Fiery Blast: A charged heavy ranged attack that deals very high flame damage. This must be blocked or dodged if possible if you are the dps.
;Ring of Fire: The boss summons flames on the ground behind the pillars dealing continuous direct high flame damage. This forces players into the center of the arena.
;Pillar Eruption/Pillars of Nirn: While Ring of Fire is active, the boss pulls all players towards him and snares them. Following that, nirncrux pillars will appear under each player's feet, dealing moderate flame damage (Pillar Eruption) and linger which deal continuous direct moderate flame damage (Pillars of Nirn). Keep moving and do not overlap with other players to avoid the red AOEs.
;Pillar Explosion: After a brief moment, the pillars explode dealing moderate flame damage.

;Pyroclasm: The boss summons flames around him dealing continuous direct high flame damage as indicated by a red circle. This forces players to stay behind the pillars on the edge of the arena.
;Stoke the Embers: The boss pounds the ground causing fireballs to shoot out from itself traveling along the ground dealing moderately high flame damage. If the outer edge is safe, players can hide behind the pillars to avoid the fireballs.
;Master of the Elements/Horned Monolith: At 80%, 60%, 40% and 20% health, the boss becomes invulnerable and summons a flame, frost, stone, and storm atronach to attack the group (Master of the Elements). They should be killed quickly before the boss wakes up, as dealing with the boss and the adds at once can be overwhelming. When Domihaus wakes up, he deals moderate generic damage in a small area around him (Horned Monolith) plain. Do note that as the boss's health depletes, he will become invulnerable for shorter amounts of time giving the group less time to deal with the adds.
;Shout of Desolation: This attack is denoted by an onscreen notification saying Domihaus is preparing to shout! At 70, 50, 30 and 10% Health, the boss shouts dealing very high amounts of oblivion damage. This attack is lethal on veteran mode. To prevent this, all players should hide behind an unbroken pillar in the hall. However, any of the pillars hidden behind will break, so to maximize the amount of hiding spots you have make sure all four group members hide behind the same pillar.
;Scorch/Slam/Snap/Spark: During the fight, each of the four totems in the room (Flame, Frost, Shock and Stone) shoot a projectile of their element at a random player, dealing moderate flame damage (Scorch), frost damage (Snap), shock damage (Spark) or earth damage (Slam).

### Execute Phase
;Shield: Once below 20% health, he will alternate between using Shout of Desolation and summoning adds on a timer. Be sure to kill the boss before no pillars remain, in which case the next shout will wipe the whole group. In addition, on veteran hard mode once below 20% health, the boss will activate a damage shield that absorbs incoming damage. The group should save ultimates for the final phase to break his shield and burn him down quickly before the damage becomes overwhelming.
