# Online:Bonelord

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Bonelord
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Bonelords are a class of hostile humanoid NPCs that attack using melee, necromantic magic and summoning abilities. They are usually equipped with a one-handed weapon.

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Strike:A basic melee attack dealing minor physical damage. (Some Bonelords only)
;Entropic Bolt:A basic ranged attack dealing minor magic damage.
;Summon Abomination:The enemy summons a flesh atronach, bone colossus or ghost to fight, spawning with the summoner's current Health. The Bonelord's Physical Resistance and Spell Resistance are reduced to 0 while a summon is active as seen by a blue glow on them. The summon automatically dies if the Bonelord is killed.
;Bone Cage:The Bonelord casts a rune on the ground that activates after a while, dealing low magic damage and rooting targets who step in it. This can be interrupted to knock the Bonelord off balance. This cannot be blocked.
;Run Away!:When too close to them, the bonelord backs up to put some distance between their target and themselves.
;Bone Surge:The Bonelord casts a Bone Shield-like ability on themselves which reduces their melee damage taken by 20% until this disappears. (On a few Bonelords)
;Empower Undead:The bonelord channels a buff on a nearby undead (e.g. skeletons, zombies, and even Bone Colossi and vampires) that increases the undead's damage done by 20% and their Physical Resistance and Spell Resistance resistance by 4,550. This spell appears as a blue glow around the hands of the buffed enemy as well as those of the bonelord. This can be interrupted to knock the bonelord off balance. (very few Bonelords)
