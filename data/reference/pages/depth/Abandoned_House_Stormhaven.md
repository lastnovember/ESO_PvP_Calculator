A copy of A Child's Tamriel Bestiary can be found inside. The room is in disarray; a desk is shoved sideways into an empty fireplace, there's a skeletal corpse slumped against one of many stacks of books. There's a coffin with a knife stuck in it to the left of the front door. Snakes and game are hung erratically from the ceiling.

A Thieves Trove can be found inside the house. A pair of scrolls, a black soul gem and two skulls with melted candles attached to the cranium are sitting in the grass on the cliff in front of the house.

## Notes
- Before update, this house was called "Sheogorath's Shrine"
- During the Tamriel Tales: Sheogorath storyline, the interior of the house will become the Shivering Shack. Once you complete "The Last Laugh", the interior will return to the original.
