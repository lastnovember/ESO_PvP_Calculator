# Online:Key Fragments

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Key_Fragments
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Update 44]]
Key Fragments were special items that could be obtained from the Imperial City. If you had enough fragments they would be converted to a Vault Key if you tried entering a Trophy Vault. It would take 150 Fragments to enter a Dungeon Trophy Vault and 60 to enter an Imperial City vault.

Previously, there were different types of trophies associated with each of the different vaults, and you needed 60 of the given type of associated trophy for each vault, or 150 for the vaults in the two dungeons. The dungeon trophies only dropped from bosses in the Veteran versions of those dungeons. If a player is carrying any combination of Key Fragments or old trophies to one of the eight trophy vault doors totaling a multiple of 60 or 150, they will be consumed as if they were all Key Fragments. These were later all turned into Key Fragments, using the icon of the Daedric Embers. With Update 44, the Key Fragments were converted to an account-wide currency called Imperial Fragments.
