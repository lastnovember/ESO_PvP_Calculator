Bloodscent Assassins are Nord assassins found between the Whispwood and Nightlands of the March of Sacrifices.

## Related Quests
- The Great Hunt

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Strike: A basic melee attack dealing minor physical damage.
;Blood Craze: The Bloodscent Assassin slashes the target twice, dealing moderate physical damage twice then very minor bleed damage every two seconds over 8 seconds to the target. The bleeding also heals the Bloodscent Assassin for around a quarter of the damage done. Blocking the first hit negates the bleeding, but blocking the second one does not.
;Assassinate: The Bloodscent Assassin strikes at the target's back for moderate physical damage. This attack can only be performed if the Bloodscent Assassin is behind their target, and they will often use "Passing Through" to get there. Face toward the Bloodscent Assassin to prevent them from using this ability.
;Agony: The Bloodscent Assassin channels a spell dealing moderate shock damage and stunning the target for 2 seconds. Taking damage from any source will break the stun, but the target may choose to break free from it instead. After the stun ends, this spell also deals minor shock damage every second over 6 seconds to the target. This can be interrupted to set the Bloodscent Assassin off balance instead. Blocking will not prevent the damage over time.
;Roll Dodge: The Bloodscent Assassin can perform a roll dodge to avoid incoming damage.
;Heated Blades: The Bloodscent Assassin rakes their weapons together in a show of sparks, buffing their damage by 25% and applying Minor Maim to the target for 4 seconds.
;Backstabber:The Bloodscent Assassin has a passive ability that makes them deal 20% more damage when attacking the target's back.
