# Online:Hordrek's Stalkers

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Hordrek's_Stalkers
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Hordrek's Stalkers are a band of Nord werewolves terrorizing Hjaalmarch Hold in Western Skyrim. They can be currently be found at a camp near Morthal, about to make a meal of the camp dwellers.
