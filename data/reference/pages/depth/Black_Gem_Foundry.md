## Layout

### Abandoned Cave
This small cave serves as the first area accessed by the entrance. It consists of a small descending pathway, leading to a portal to the Foundry proper at the end. Dalenor the Summoner stands over the bodies of two dead Daedra here. There are also white torchbugs floating around the portal.

### Black Gem Foundry
The Black Gem Foundry is an open-air black soul gem quarry in desolate Coldharbour. Feral Shriven linger in the open while Stone Atronachs work under the lash of a variety of Soulbinder Tyrants. Potent Black Soul Gem nodes can be mined to receive Potent Black Gems can be mined here. These can be filled with the essence of the Soulbinder Tyrants to receive Filled Potent Black Gemsplain.

The entrance to the Prospector's Field Forge is located in a cave off the northeast side of the road in the middle of the quarry. After the final group of trash mobs, players will encounter the door to the Pulverizing Chamber, where the first boss resides.

### Prospector's Field Forge
Prospector Lyrakta can be fought here. Once she is defeated, you can craft the Lustrous Cabochon for a buff to stamina and magicka regeneration.

### Pulverizing Chamber
Quarrymaster Saldezaar works here.

## Dungeon Buffs
Mine Potent Black Gems from the quarry, and fill them to get Filled Potent Black Gems. Then, at each of the secret bosses' locations, you will be able to craft a special item that grants you a buff for the remainder of the dungeon, with a stronger version of the buff being available if your Jewelry Crafting rank is at least 49. This process is revealed in Notes on Our Secret Project, and crafting all three items awards you the achievement Cut, Color, and Clarity.
colspan=2|Ability | Description

Lustrous Cabochon | Magicka/Stamina regeneration

Lustrous Pendeloque | Faster ally resurrection, resurrecting grants a damage shield

Lustrous Trilliant | Light/Heavy attacks add Weapon and Spell damage for a short duration, with cooldown

You need to visit all the first workstation to craft the item before you can enhance it at the other two. The version of the item you craft (based on Jewelry Crafting rank) determines which version of each buff you'll get. For example, you can't craft the regular version of the item and then give it the enhanced buffs at the other two stations, or vice versa.
