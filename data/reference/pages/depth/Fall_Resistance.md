# Online:Fall Resistance

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Fall_Resistance
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Fall Resistance is an attribute which protects you from Fall Damage. Its sources are:

-The Champion Star Breakfall which reduces fall damage taken by 7% per stage, for up to 35%

-The Wood Elf race passive Acrobat which reduces fall damage taken by 10%.
