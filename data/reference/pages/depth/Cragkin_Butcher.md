## Skills and Abilities

### Foot Soldier
;Quick Strike:A basic melee attack dealing minor physical damage.
;Heavy Attack:A heavy melee attack dealing moderate physical damage. This can be blocked to set the enemy off balance.
;Throw Dagger:The foot soldier backs away from the target and throws a dagger at the target. This attack snares, deals minor physical damage then very minor bleed damage over time. This attack can be interrupted to set the foot soldier off balance. The dagger can be blocked to prevent the snare and the bleed.
;Ignite:A combo move that is only performed if Archers, Fire Mages or other fire using enemies (Flame Atronachs, Scamps etc.) are around. The foot soldier throws a glob of tar under the target's feet as indicated by a red circle. While this does no damage, standing in it snares. The foot soldier will call for the other enemy to light the tar (usually accompanied by a voice clip) and the red circle will burn, doing low flame damage over time to targets within the red circle.

### Assassin
;Quick Strike: A basic melee attack dealing low physical damage.
;Whirlwind: The assassin spins around, dealing moderate physical damage to all targets as indicated by a red circle.
;Assassinate: The assassin strikes at the target's back for moderate physical damage. This attack can only be performed if the assassin is behind their target, and the assassin will often use "Passing Through" to get there. Face toward the assassin to prevent them from using this ability.
;Passing Through:The assassin jumps above the target to get behind them.
;Roll Dodge: The assassin can perform a roll dodge to avoid incoming damage.
;Backstabber:The assassin has a passive ability that causes them to deal 20% more damage when attacking the target's back.

## Bugs
Despite being elite rank emenies at Big Ozur's Valley, Cragkin Butchers there are vulnerable to crowd control.
