Aud Soft-Hands is a Nord monk and cheesemaker who can be found at his stall in Windhelm, to the north of the Windswept Stalls. He and his fellow monks produce Fest Eidar, a special cheese made in the Valorhorn Crypt found within the Hall of the Dead.

## Related Quests
- Sheogorath Takes a Holiday

## Quest-Related Events
After agreeing to act as a guardian for Sheogorath during his holiday trip as a mortal in Tamriel, you can meet him at the first stop on his itinerary. He has come to Windhelm to sample the Fest Eidar, but when you meet him there, you find Aud apologizing to Sheogorath about the lack of product at his stall:
:Sheogorath: "Skeevers? Is Vaermina punishing me for spoiling one of her nightmares?"
:Aud Soft-Hands: "Apologies. If someone could clear them out for us, the Fest Eidar is yours to sample."

Speaking to Aud before asking Sheogorath what is going on:
:"Greetings. Is that your friend there? I've never seen someone so anxious to celebrate the memory of the dead with my Fest Eidar cheese. Such a noble soul. Perhaps the two of you can think of a way to drive the skeevers from the vault."

Talking with the annoyed Sheogorath, you learn that there is a sudden infestation of skeevers in the Valorhorn Crypt, and if you deal with the vermin, you get the cheese. Sheogorath expects you to clear the way, and says to meet him in the Hall of the Dead. Before he runs off he suggests that you ask Aud some questions.

Speak with Aud, he can tell you about the crypt and the Fest Eidar:
:"The Servants of Valor are indebted to you, my friend. Our dead will drink and dine in your name in Shor's Hall! We will taste the Fest Eidar with them. Take care inside Valorhorn Crypt. Something terrible lurks inside."
:Tell me about Valorhorn Crypt.
:"The monks of my order age our blessed cheese there to honor the fallen warriors who now feast with Shor. We craft our Fest Eidar in memory of those interred in the crypt, and then share it with their families and any who wish to remember them."
:Have you always had trouble with skeevers and other vermin?
:"Not so much, no. I cannot remember the last time so much as a draugr wandered into the vault where we age our cheese. Your friend is a good man for wanting to help us."

When you enter the Valorhorn Crypt, you find the skeevers fighting with Frenzied Daedrats and draugr are wandering around. In the main cheese storage area, you and Sheogorath find a giant Daedrat, which Sheogorath has you deal with.

Speaking with Aud after clearing out the vermin:
:"Ate all the Fest Eidar? Well, better you than the rats and skeevers! The Servants of Valor are indebted to you. And your heroic friend."plain
