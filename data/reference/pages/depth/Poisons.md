## Crown Store Poisons
These poisons can be purchased from the Crown Store, received from Daily Rewards, or obtained in Crown Crates.

colspan=2|Poison | Effects | Price

Crown Lethal Poison
Deals 1266 Poison Damage per second for 3.5 seconds, and 822 Poison Damage per second for 6.4 seconds. (10 second cooldown)
200 for 80 25 for 100

colspan=4|

Gold Coast Debilitating Poison
Applies Creeping Ravage Health and Hindrance to your target, dealing 822 Poison damage per second and reducing Movement Speed by 40% for 6.4 seconds. (10 second cooldown)

Gold Coast Draining Poison
Drains Stamina and Magicka from your target, increasing the cost of their Stamina and Magicka abilities by 10%, and restores 151 Stamina and Magicka to you per second for 3.5 seconds. (10 second cooldown)

Gold Coast Enfeebling Poison
Applies Minor Maim and Minor Defile, reducing your victim's Damage by 5% and reducing healing your victim takes and their Health Recovery by 8% for 3.5 seconds. (10 second cooldown)

Gold Coast Trapping Poison
Applies Hindrance and Immobilize to your target, immobilizing them for 3.5 seconds and reducing Movement Speed by 40% for 6.4 seconds. (10 second cooldown)
