# Online:Crystaljack Bandits

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Crystaljack_Bandits
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Crystaljack Bandits are a group of bandits found in the West Weald. They are led by the former vintner Gilroy Vassinus and have tamed the strange crystalline insects that have started nesting in Legion's Rest, which give the group its name.
