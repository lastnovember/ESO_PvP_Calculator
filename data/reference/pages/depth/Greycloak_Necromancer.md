Graycloak Necromancers were male necromancers of the Greycloak Mercenaries found in Haddock's Market. They were removed with Update 12 and never replaced.

## Skills and Abilities
;Necrotic Spear:A basic ranged attack that does minor frost damage.
;Summon the Dead:The necromancer often begins combat by summoning a zombie, a bloodfiend, a bloodfiend reaver, a Bone Reaver or a Bone Flayer. If the summoned creature killed, another one will be summoned to replace it. The summon scales with the difficulty, faction, and location of the summoner. The summoned creature will have around the same health as the current health of their summoner.
;Empower Undead:The necromancer channels a buff on a nearby undead (e.g. skeletons, zombies, and even Bone Colossi and vampires) that reduces the damage taken by the target. This spell appears as a blue glow around the hands of the buffed enemy as well as those of the necromancer. This spell can be interrupted to knock the necromancer off balance.
;Run Away!:When too close to them, the necromancer backs up to put some distance between their target and themselves.

## Notes
- They wielded Inferno Staves.
