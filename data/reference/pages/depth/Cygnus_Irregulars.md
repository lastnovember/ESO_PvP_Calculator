# Online:Cygnus Irregulars

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Cygnus_Irregulars
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Cygnus Irregulars are a sect of the Imperial Legion led by Prefect Calo. They are primarily military engineers, scouts, and trainers. They initially were part of Euraxia Tharn's forces that ventured into Northern Elsweyr, but they split off and started opposing her once she revealed her true intentions by conquering Rimmen and imposing herself as its new queen.
