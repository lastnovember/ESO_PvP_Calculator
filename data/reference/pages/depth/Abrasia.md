Abrasia is the leader of Roadsworn, one of the gangs in Daggerfall that focuses on highway robbery.

## Quest-Related Events

### The Codex Caper
After helping with Quen's caper in stealing the Codex Voernet and escaping Yvierry Manor, you can meet up with her at the Thieves Den in Daggerfall Outlaws Refuge. When you arrive, Abrasia will be part of the group discussing the stolen Codex:
:Seline Menant: "The Codex Voernet? I knew Father's inquiries would pay off!"
:Edwane the Hawk: "All I see are pages covered in gibberish. What good is a book we can't read?"
:Abrasia: "Edwane's using his head for once. Old stories don't fill my coin purse-we need to take down the cartel."
:Basil Menant: "I'm telling you, this changes everything-ah. Quen's accomplice is back. Might I have a word?"

Talking to Abrasia before Basil:
:"Every time I turn around, there's someone new down here. No idea who you are or what business you've got with Basil, but go ahead and humor the old burglar. I want to see what this is all about."

After you talk with Basil, Quen will want to talk to you to complete the quest. While this happens, Abrasia and the other gang leaders will move to various places around the Den. Talking to Abrasia before Quen:
:"So you're Quen's mysterious new accomplice? When she said she found someone to help at the estate job, I figured she meant a disposable distraction. I'm still not sure I trust her, so I'm certainly not sure about you."

Speaking with her after talking to Quen but before starting the next quest:
:"I'm getting bored. Apparently you and Quen got to go out and do some thieving, but she asked the rest of us to wait until she talked to you. So what are you standing here for? Go talk to her!"

### Three Thieves in Daggerfall
After the job at Yvierry Manor, there will be several leads to follow about what you found. Quen will have you work with the gang leaders, to learn about them and to show your worth. She will ask you to speak with about looking into the Koldane Cartel. Abrasia can be found in the upper area of the Thieves Den:

:We got the codex, but the cartel killed Lord Yvierry. We need to know their role in all this.
:"Obviously they somehow got word we were after the Nowhere Vault and decided to swoop in. But Quen's right. We need more information-and I know where to begin. The Red Rook bandits at Ilessan Tower. We can dig up some answers there."

### Codex and Cartel
Entering the Map Roon and talking to Abrasia before starting the quest with Quen:
:"Quen wants to speak with you outside. Which means she wants to talk about the three of us, of course. Well, go on. I'll try not to take it personally or stab any of the others before you return."

Talking with Quen, she will tell you that the gang leaders are engaged with claiming the prize of the Nowhere Vault and dealing with the Koldane Cartel. However, she wants to see what the gang leaders suggest doing and if they can work together.
