## Layout

### Castle Navire Chapel

The chapel is a simple building to southeast of the main castle. Entering through the front door after receiving the key during Isobel's companion quest, puts you in a small room on the main floor. On the left, there is a staircase down to a lower floor where you can find the main door to the Castle Navire Catacombs.

### Castle Navire Courtyard

### Castle Navire Knight's Wing
The Knight's Wing can be accessed through a door to the left of the Courtyard. Entering the door, there is a short hallway the ends in an intersection with a longer hallway. Taking the left turn will lead to a door to the Office of the Knight Commander. In the far corner of this room is another door that leads to the Knight Commander's Private Chamber. Alternately, turning right at the intersection will take you down a staircase. At the bottom of the stairs, the hallway makes a left turn. Ahead is another staircase that leads down to the Knights' Barracks, and to the right is Castle Library. Off the back of the library, there is a staircase that leads to a door to the Castle Navire Tourney Grounds.

## Wayshrine

The Castle Navire Wayshrine is located on the northern road out of the town.
