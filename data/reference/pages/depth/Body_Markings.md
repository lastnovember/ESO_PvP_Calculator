Online:Body Markings/Crown Crate Rewards
Body Markings are cosmetic collectibles worn on a character's body. These collectibles can be scars, body paint, tattoos, or any number of markings applied to a character's body. Many of these collectibles have a matching head marking.

## Default Options
The following body marking options are available when creating a character for the first time. They are also available in the Cosmetic Pack: Body Markings which is sold in the Crown Store for 500.

### Natural Markings (for Men/Mer)

#### Freckles and Moles

:One of the standard body markings available at every Tamrielic salon.
Part of Cosmetic Pack: Body Markings which is sold in the Crown Store for 500. Usable by all races except Khajiit and Argonians.

#### No Body Marking

:One of the standard body markings available at every Tamrielic salon.
Part of Cosmetic Pack: Body Markings which is sold in the Crown Store for 500. Usable by all races.
