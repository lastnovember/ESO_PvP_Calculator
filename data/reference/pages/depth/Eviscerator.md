# Online:Eviscerator

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Eviscerator
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Eviscerators are a class of hostile humanoid NPCs that attack using melee abilities. They are usually equipped with a shield and a one-handed weapon.

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Strike:A basic melee attack dealing minor physical damage.
;Low Slash:A Low Slash attack dealing minor physical damage and snaring the target. Blocking does not negate the snare. (On most Eviscerators)
;Shield Bash/Power Bash:A spinning shield attack against a target dealing moderate physical damage, stunning and setting off-balance. This can be blocked to set the enemy off-balance instead. (Some Eviscerators only)
;Block:When performing a heavy attack near or on the eviscerator, they can briefly block with their shield, reducing damage taken greatly. Targets who attempt melee heavy attacks on the enemy while they are blocking will be set off-balance.
;Dark Talons:The enemy roots all targets within range for 4 seconds with Dark Talons dealing minor flame damage. (On most Eviscerators)
;Dragonknight Standard:The enemy calls down a fiery banner that plants down into the ground, with a large circular AoE dealing continuous direct minor flame damage and applying Major Defile.
;Blink Strike:The enemy jump while glowing yellow and teleport near their target then land hard on top of them, dealing moderate physical damage, knocking down and stunning. This can be blocked to set the enemy off-balance instead. Formerly Brute NPC class' "Focused Charge" was used instead.(On some Eviscerators only)
