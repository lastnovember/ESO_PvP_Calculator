Deckhand Bazler is a Breton sailor of the trapped ship The Silver Cormorant. He can be found just south of Folgunthur Barrow or southwest of the Frozen Coast boat, where he is freezing and desperately looking for someone to help his crew.

After you have helped out the Silver Cormorant, he can later be found at the Whispering Axe Tavern in Morthal.

## Related Quests
- Of Ice and Death

## Quest-Related Events
When you find him, he'll be shivering from the cold. Bazler will call out when you approach:
:Deckhand Bazler: "You there! Please! I need help!"

Talk with him to see what has happened.
:"I didn't think I'd find anyone out here in this frozen wasteland. You, my friend, are a glorious sight."
:What are you doing out here by yourself?
:"I'm part of the crew from the merchant ship Silver Cormorant. The snows … overtook us. Trapped us in the ice. Now, our shipmates are missing, and we're out of food. I went looking for both. Please. I can't go on. The frost will take my toes soon."
:How can I help?
:"If we just had some meat, we could last a few more days, I'm sure. I tried to kill a horker earlier but my hands were frozen and I dropped my hunting knife. Please, my crew mates will starve to death if help doesn't come soon."
:I'll find some meat for your crew.

After you agree to help, Bazler will say he'll return to the ship. But you can ask him some questions before you go.
:"I'll head back to the ship. I'm no good to anyone if I lose my toes. Glad you came along when you did. Hopefully you can help get us out of this mess."
:Where will I find your ship?
::"Not far. She's still afloat but she's trapped in the ice. It was a nightmare. Clear one moment, then the sky grew dark. The snow howled like a beast. And the ice … black ice … I didn't know water could freeze up that fast."
:Why did they send you out here? / Why did your crew send you out here?
::"It was a last resort. It was either send someone out into the cold, or just sit there and accept our fate. Someone had to try. Not that I've done anything out here other than freeze my arse off."

Once you find The Silver Cormorant, Bazler will be in one of the upper deck cabins:
:"Just … need to warm up a moment. It's cold. It's so horribly cold."

## Dialogue
After helping his crew, he can be found at the Whispering Axe Tavern where he is doing much better.
:"It's nice to be warm and have a full belly. It'll be sometime before I'm ready to leave the comfort of a fire."
