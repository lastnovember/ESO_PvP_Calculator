Sahalu was a Khajiit who could be found at the then-blocked western gate near the town of Dragonstar. He was removed from the game after the zone of Upper Craglorn was added in Update 4 and the gate was opened.

## Dialogue
Speaking to him, he'll say one of four things, mostly lamenting his position in life:
:"Look at this pile of debris! Sahalu will die of old age before it is cleared. Poor Sahalu."

:"This one is wasted as a laborer. Sahalu is management material."

:"After this, no more mining and shoveling for Sahalu. This one will get a new job as a chef … or maybe an acrobat. There's a lot of gold in the flippy tumbling business, yes?"

:"Sahalu is full of big ideas! Instead of digging through the rock, we should dig underneath! No one listens to Sahalu, though. "We'll get crushed," they say. Feh. Ziss on them. I dig my hole anyway."
