Corrupted Lurkers are corrupted weaker Lurkers found in The Infinite Panopticon during the A Hidden Fate and A Calamity of Fate quests.

## Skills and Abilities
As elite rank enemies, Corrupted Lurkers are immune to crowd control.
;Quick Strike:Basic melee attack with the claw dealing minor physical damage.
;Heavy Strike:The Corrupted Lurker prepares a double strike attack with its claws. The first strike deals moderate physical damage and the second strike deals minor damage and staggers. Blocking this staggers the Corrupted Lurker instead.
;Quake:The Corrupted Lurker punches and stomps the ground four times, dealing low physical damage and snaring each time in a area around them. The last stomp knocks back and stuns as well. Blocking the last stomp staggers instead.
;Bile Sweep:The Corrupted Lurker spits in a arc in front of them, creating four areas that travel forward which deal continuous low disease damage as well as snaring.
;Foul Spit:The Corrupted Lurker spits one large area of bile under a target dealing moderate disease damage and snaring.
