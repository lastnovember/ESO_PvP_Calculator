# Online:Battle for the Writhing Wall

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Battle_for_the_Writhing_Wall
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Battle for the Writhing Wall (or Writhing Wall Event) is a unique event included as part of the 2025 Content Pass that unites Tamriel on a mission to combat the Worm Cult and destroy the Writhing Wall which is dividing the two halves of Solstice. This event progresses through three phases, with more activities becoming available as the event continues and the final battle approaches.

It began on October 13, 2025, at 10AM EDT and includes elements in both the base game and Solstice zones.

## Event Phases

### Phase 1: Supply and Defense

:Complete the Meridian Lens and improve camp defenses to complete this phase.
With Phase One, players will be gathering resources for the campaign to come. You can provide supplies and resources via daily quests on Stirk or at the Sunport Rampart Camp on Solstice by speaking to Quartermaster Nurwalne or Armorer Reistaff. The siege camps will also start being attacked, and you can talk to General Zasimba regarding missions to defend the camps.

There will also be increased planar incursions on mainland Tamriel. During this phase, the Worm Cult will take over various delves and Public Dungeons and make them invasion sites. The added monsters in these areas will be more difficult than the other creatures in the area, and are related to Orinde Glenuvon's daily quests in gathering Vitrified Souls.

All ESO players can help progress this stage, but only 2025 Content Pass owners can acquire quests on Solstice.

### Phase 2: The Assault Begins

:Power the Meridian Lens and defend siege camps to complete this phase.
When Phase Two starts, the siege camps on Solstice will be attacked more frequently as two camps will be under attack at the same time, and the Maldrith Ghishzor will appear on the island as a World Boss. During this phase, Armorer Reistaff will offer additional daily quests, while Quartermaster Nurwalne will have changed her tasks.

Additionally, you will be asked to investigate sabotage by the Worm Cult on the Fellowship's supply shipments.

All ESO players can help progress this stage, but only 2025 Content Pass owners can acquire quests on Solstice.

### Phase 3: Fall of the Writhing Wall

:Invade the Writhing Fortress and destroy the Soul Tower to complete this phase.
When Phase Three starts, the Writhing Wall will be breached and it will be time to break through to the other side by clearing the Writhing Fortress public dungeon. Unlike the other phases, which last a variable length of time depending on player successes and mobilization, the third phase lasts one week from when the Writhing Fortress is first completed on a given server, bringing the event to a close after that period.

During this phase, only 2025 Content Pass owners can attempt to complete this phase.

## Quests

Daily Quests during the event can be picked up from the Stirk Fellowship Camp on Stirk or the Sunport Rampart Camp on Solstice. Each completion of these quests will contribute to the Writhing Wall Progress meter.

You can start the introductory quest, Support the Fellowship, from the Crown Store, from the Writhing Wall event page in the Activity Finder, or from Skordo the Knife at either of the camps.

The Daily Quest Providers are as follows:

## Siege Camps

Stationed around the Writhing Wall are five Fellowship Siege Camps which are manned by Stirk Fellowship Soldiers, Battlemages and commoners during an attack. During the event they will constantly come under attack by Worm Cultists and their allies and you can defend or recapture them. "Central Siege Camp" is special as it has twice the number of enemies and gets attacked less often.

The Siege Camps are as follows:
- North Siege Camp
- Sunport Siege Camp
- Central Siege Camp
- Warm-Stone Siege Camp
- South Siege Camp

When the signal fire in the middle of a camp turns blue, that is a sign of a pending attack. Then three Soul Reapers (six at the Central Siege Camp) will appear around the camp with guards. Before you can destroy a soul reaper you will need to defeat the waves of enemies that come to defend them, which culminates in a Worm Cult champion appearing. They will be randomized, can appear more than once per camp and will be one of the following:

## Rewards

During this event, rewards can be obtained by opening Reward Coffers received from completing the above daily quests. The first daily event quest you complete from each event quest-giver will award you a will give you a Glorious Writhing Rewards Coffer which has increased chances for the rarer items in reward pool. Subsequent daily quests will provide quest-related reward coffers. During this event, there will be no Event Tickets rewarded.

In tandem with the Battle for the Writhing Wall event, will be the related Golden Pursuits campaign which will give items such as the Coldharbour Dremnaken Runt pet and the Wormwrithe Bear-Lizard mount.

During the event, Antiquity Leads normally dropped from specific bosses in Delves and Public Dungeons that become invasion sites will drop from event bosses in that Delve or Public Dungeon for event's duration. Additionally, players who participate in the event will earn the title "Wall Breaker" by completing the Writhing Fortress in Phase 3.
