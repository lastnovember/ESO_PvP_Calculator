# Online:Keel Cutters

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Keel_Cutters
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Keel Cutters are a group of ruthless pirates and slavers led by Captain Virindi Slave-Taker. They occupy Thrall Cove on the northwestern coast of Hew's Bane.
