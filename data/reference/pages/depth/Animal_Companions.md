# Online:Animal Companions

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Animal_Companions
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Animal Companion is one of three class skill lines available to Wardens. It can be learned by other classes via subclassing. Abilities in this line focus on dealing damage.

## Skill Perks
The Animal Companions skill line is immediately available to all Wardens, and is leveled up by earning experience while Animal Companions skills are slotted. Skills include:

## Buffs to Animal Companions

### Armor Sets
The following armor sets help amplify your animal companions:
- Aerie's Cry - Increases your damage done with Animal Companion abilities by 10% against enemies with Eagle's Mark.
