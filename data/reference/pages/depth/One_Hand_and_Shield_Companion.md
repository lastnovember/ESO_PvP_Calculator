One Hand and Shield is one of six weapon skill lines available to Companions.

## Skill Perks
The One Hand and Shield skill line is immediately available to Isobel Veloise, who starts her progress at level 3. Other Companions can unlock the skill line by equipping them with any One Handed weapon and a Shield. The skill line can be leveled up from earning combat XP while your active Companion has a One Handed weapon and a Shield equipped.
