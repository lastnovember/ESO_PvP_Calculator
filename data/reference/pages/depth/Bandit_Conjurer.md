Bandit Conjurers are bandits that can rarely ambush you during a Random Encounter during the Zeal of Zenithar event.

## Skills and Abilities
Bandit Conjurers use all the Conjurer NPC class abilities, plus a unique one.
;Flare: Basic ranged attack dealing low flame damage.
;Daedroth's Breath: The conjurer summons an orange-tinted daedroth that breathes fire, indicated by a cone, dealing continuous direct low flame damage, before disappearing. The area from which the daedroth emerges from is also enflamed, indicated by a red circle, and standing in it deals continuous direct low flame damage as well.
;Summon Exploding Banekin: The conjurer creates a shock portal that summons two Exploding Banekin, which rush at the target and detonate, indicated by red circles, dealing minor magic damage.
;Summon Scamp: The conjurer creates a flaming portal that summons a Scamp to assist.
;:The Bandit Conjurer channels a spell which causes a Flame Atronach to crash on the ground, dealing moderate in the landing area. The Flame Atronach then attacks you. It spawns with the Bandit Conjurer's current Health.
;Run Away!:When too close to them, the conjurer backs up to put some distance between their target and themselves.

## Dialogue
As you dig up the Dirt Mound, rarely the Bandit Conjurer appears and says this to you.
:Bandit Conjurer: "Whatever's in there doesn't belong to you!"
