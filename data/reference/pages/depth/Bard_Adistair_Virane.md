## Quest-Related Events

### People of Import
He will play a part in a distraction that Jakarn is setting up, so you can retrieve secret documentation from Duchess Dufort on the missing delegation. Speaking to him:
:"Something you need, friend?"
:I want to request the song Stagger and Sway.
:"Er, I don't really take requests. Duchess Elea provided a list of approved songs she wanted performed. That one definitely isn't one of them. Not that she would notice. I don't think anyone is really listening to the music."

:Would this buy me a song?
::"It's good to see that someone appreciates the arts. All right, then. You'll have your song!"
::Thank you.
:Anything I can do to convince you to play the song?
::"Well, there is a lady I'm interested in. Layla. Highborn, you know-out of my class. Normally I'd never even be in the same room as her. Could you give her this letter? Even if she hands it back, at least I'll know I told her how I feel."
::All right. I'll deliver the letter to Layla. Which one is she?
:::"The most beautiful lady at the banquet! Hair like golden wheat, eyes as blue as the sea, pale skin like the beach at sunrise … and freckles! She even has freckles. Ahem. Sorry. She's the one in the red dress with a flower in her hair."

When spoken to after giving the letter to Layla:
:"You delivered the letter? What did she say? Did she seem … pleased?"
:I think she was pleased.
:"Really? By Mara's grace, I hope she agrees to let me court her properly! I'll convince her father I'm worthy of her hand. Anyway, I believe I owe you a song. What was it again?"
:Stagger and Sway, please.
:"Nothing more suitable when love is in the air. All right. I'll get started right away!"
:Thank you.
