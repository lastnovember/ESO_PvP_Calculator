Dremora Morikyn are Dremora storm mages who can be found in multiple locations, primarily in the Imperial City.

## Related Quests
- Breaking the Shackle

## Skills and Abilities

### Standard
;Shock:Basic ranged attack dealing minor shock damage.
;Thunder Thrall: The Dremora Morikyn teleports to you and summons a bolt of lightning that strikes a large area around itself, dealing moderate shock damage to all targets as indicated by the red circle.
;Run Away!:When too close to them, the Dremora Morikyn backs up to put some distance between their target and themselves.

### Imperial CityImperial City
The Dremora Morikyns in Imperial City also use those two abilities:
;Summon Dark Anchor:The Dremora Morikyn summons a Daedric Arch which in turn periodically summons very weak Banekin to fight for them.
;Health Potion:The Dremora Morikyn drinks a potion, restoring 7,524 Health. This can be interrupted to set the Dremora Morikyn off balance.
;Desecrated Ground:On death, the undead rarely desecrate the ground under them as indicated by a red circle. This does continuous minor magic damage and snares all targets as well as healing undead enemies within the red circle for 3% of the original undead's Max Health per tick. (Bugged, see Bugs)

## Notes
- Dremora Morikyns in the Arena District of the Imperial City actually have a fourth ability called "Cheer" which only activates when they are out of combat and it plays the cheering animation.

## Bugs
Dremora Morikyn very rarely create a Desecrated Ground upon death in Imperial City (Logged in Antediluvian Vaults). This ability is normally only used by undead.
