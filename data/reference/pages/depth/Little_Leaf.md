Little Leaf is a Wood Elf member of the Scaled Court and the seneschal of Regent Cassipia, who is also her love interest.

## Related Quests
- A Leaf in the Wind
- The Serpent's Fang
- Holding Court
- Dawn of the Exalted Viper
- Old Life Observance

## Quest-Related Events

If you met and helped Regent Cassipia first, she will send you to meet Little Leaf.

When you kill Little Leaf, the Exalted Viper will emerge from the pool of nirncrux and resurrect her as a spirit to fight you. During this fight, Little Leaf focuses on healing the Exalted Viper over attacking you. Killing the Viper will also banish Little Leaf's spirit if you have not yet killed it.

## Skills and Abilities

### Alive
;Ice Arrow:A basic ranged attack that does minor frost damage.
;Ice Barrier:Little Leaf summons an Ice Barrier that absorbs projectiles fired at it until destroyed. The caster cannot be pulled if the wall stands between them and the enemy. The player should move so that there is a clear line of sight between themselves and Little Leaf.
;Winter's Reach:Little Leaf unleashes an icy whirlwind in the direction it is facing. This whirlwind does moderate frost damage and roots all players that it hits. The root effect passes through block, but it can be escaped by roll dodging.
;Rite of Passage:Little Leaf channels a spell healing herself and nearby allies for 4 seconds. It takes half a second to cast, and another half a second to begin healing once the effect is applied. This ability is usually used when Little Leaf is on low health. This spell can be interrupted to stagger Little Leaf.

### Spirit
;Little Leaf's Devotion:Little Leaf heals The Exalted viper for 8,030 health. This can be interrupted.

### Old Life Observance

After her death, the spirit of Little Leaf may return from Aetherius and appear to you during the New Life Festival event quest Old Life Observance when you perform a remembrance ceremony at one of the Old Life shrines.

## Notes
- While Little Leaf is following you around, other players will see her as Scaled Court Defector.
- Little Leaf's health differs on quest progression: 39959 for when she's a quest giver and followerplain during The Serpent's Fang, 80,309 when you encounter her in Skyreach Temple, and dba when the Exalted Viper revives her during the final fight of Dawn of the Exalted Viper.
- Little Leaf doesn't give experience or loot when slain.
