Dremora Harstryl are Dremora archers who can be found in multiple locations. Some Harstryl specialize as pet rangers or arbalests.

## Related Quests

- The Emperor's Secret
- Pledge: City of Ash II

- Will of the Council

## Skills and Abilities
Instead of animals, they can summon Banekins, clannfear, flame atronachs, scamps, and winged twilights.

### Standard
;Quick Shot: A basic ranged attack dealing minor physical damage.
;Call Ally: The Dremora Harstryl blows a horn and summons an animal companion to fight. This animal is usually appropriate to the zone the pet ranger appears in, and for Daedric Pet Rangers the animal spawns with the summoner's current Health. The Dremora Harstryl's Physical Resistance and Spell Resistance are reduced to 0 while a summon is active, as seen by the blue glow on them. The Dremora Harstryl can only have one animal companion at a time and it will instantly die if the Dremora Harstryl is killed. The Dremora Harstryl cannot cast this ability if an animal is already in the fight.
;Roll Dodge: The Dremora Harstryl may perform a roll dodge to avoid attacks.
;Trap Beast:The Dremora Harstryl places a trap under the target's feet that trigger on the first target that walks near it, dealing low magic damage and rooting for 4 seconds. This cannot be blocked.

### ArcherImperial City
;Quick Shot:A basic ranged attack dealing minor physical damage.
;Arrow Spray:The Dremora Harstryl shoots a spray of arrows in the direction it is facing as indicated by a red cone, dealing moderate physical damage and snaring all targets hit for 4 seconds. This can be interrupted to set the Dremora Harstryl off balance. Blocking doesn't prevent the snare.
;Volley:The Dremora Harstryl fires continuously at an area as indicated by a red circle, dealing continuous direct low physical damage. This can be interrupted to set the Dremora Harstryl off balance.
;Run Away!:When too close to them, the Dremora Harstryl backs up to put some distance between their target and themselves.
;Taking Aim:A powerful sniping bow attack that takes time to charge up which deals high physical damage. This can be interrupted to set the Dremora Harstryl off-balance.

#### Imperial SewersImperial City,Isles of Torment and Moonhenge
Both types of Dremora Harstryl in the Imperial Sewers and the Dremora Harstryl Pet Rangers at the Isles of Torment and Moonhenge also use this ability.
;Summon Dark Anchor:The Dremora Harstryl summons a Daedric Arch which in turn periodically summons very weak Banekin to fight for them.

### Imperial CityImperial City
Both types of Dremora Harstryl in the Imperial City also use this ability.
;Desecrated Ground:On death, the undead rarely desecrate the ground under them as indicated by a red circle. This does continuous minor magic damage and snares all targets as well as healing undead enemies within the red circle for 3% of the original undead's Max Health per tick. (see Bugs)

### Arbalest
;Quick Shot:A basic ranged attack dealing minor physical damage.
;Arrow Stab:A basic melee attack dealing minor physical damage where the Arbalest grabs their arrow and stabs it into the target rather than firing it.
;Bombard:The arbalest crouches and fires several arrows into the air, and where they land will develop several small AoE circles dealing continuous direct low flame damage.
;Quakeshot:A wide range attack where the arbalest fires arrows downwards in the area in front of them, causing the ground to quake in a cone and launch three areas forward, each dealing moderate physical damage. Used at close range only.
;Chilled Ground:The arbalest fires a frozen arrow at a target which unleashes a cloud of frost under them for a few seconds, snaring all targets inside.
