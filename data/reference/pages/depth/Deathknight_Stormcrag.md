Deathknight Stormcrag is a draugr found inside his tomb which is located in Eastmarch.

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Strike:A basic melee attack dealing minor physical damage.
;Power Bash:A spinning shield attack against a target dealing moderate physical damage, stunning and setting off-balance. This can be blocked to set the Deathknight Stormcrag off-balance instead.
;Block:When performing a melee heavy attack on the Deathknight Stormcrag, they can briefly block with their shield, reducing damage taken greatly. Targets who attempt melee heavy attacks on the Deathknight Stormcrag while they are blocking will be set off-balance.
;Dark Talons:The Deathknight Stormcrag roots all targets within range for 4 seconds with Dark Talons dealing minor flame damage.
;Dragonknight Standard:The Deathknight Stormcrag calls down a fiery banner that plants down into the ground, with a large circular AoE dealing continuous direct minor flame damage and applying Major Defile.

## Bugs
Despite being an elite rank enemy, the Deathknight Stormcrag is vulnerable to crowd control.
