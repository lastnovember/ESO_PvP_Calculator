Centurion Uragor is an Orc officer of the Daggerfall Covenant found at their camp just outside Fort Arand. He is accompanied by a Covenant Storm Mage.

## Related Quests
- Suspicious Silence

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Strike:A basic melee attack dealing minor physical damage.
;Thunder Hammer:Centurion Uragor swings and shocks the ground in front of him as indicated by a red cone dealing low shock damage.
;Shock Aura:Centurion Uragor shocks the ground around himself as indicated by a red circle dealing low shock damage. This can be interrupted to knock Centurion Uragor off balance.

## Bugs
Despite being an elite rank enemy, Centurion Uragor is vulnerable to crowd control.
