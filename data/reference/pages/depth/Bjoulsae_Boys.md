# Online:Bjoulsae Boys

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Bjoulsae_Boys
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Bjoulsae Boys are a group of bandits settled in the ruins of Viridian Watch. There are rumors saying that the loot they steal from nobles goes to the less fortunate, but once inside their lair, it is clear that they are false.
