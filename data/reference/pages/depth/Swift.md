Swift Warrior
Swift is a trait added in Update 18 which can only be found on jewelry. Jewelry with this trait does not drop normally, but may be obtained by completing daily Jewelry Crafting Writs, or by acquiring them from another player. Once learned, items with this trait may be crafted using Gilding Wax, which may be purchased from Rolis Hlaalu for 5. Items with the Swift trait will increase your Movement Speed by an amount dependent on the quality of the item.
