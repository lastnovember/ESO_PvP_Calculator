Avrusa Duleri is the Dunmer leader of the Camonna Tong found inside Khartag Point. She and her cronies can be found in the center of the map on a ledge only accessible from the north, guarding the way to Khartag. She cannot be spoken to.

She uses a mix of Thundermaul abilities and a modified sea mage class ability during combat.

## Related Quests
- A Smuggler's Last Stand
- Unsettled Syndicate

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Strike:A basic melee attack dealing moderate physical damage.
;Shock:Basic ranged attack dealing moderate Shock Damage.
;Thunder Hammer:Avrusa Duleri swings and shocks the ground in front of her as indicated by a red cone dealing moderate shock damage.
;Shock Aura:Avrusa Duleri almost instantly shocks the ground around herself as indicated by a red circle dealing moderate shock damage, knocking back and stunning for 2 seconds. This can be interrupted to set Avrusa Duleri to off balance instead.
;Agonizing Strike/Agonizing Fury: Avrusa Duleri raises her arm up as electricity flings out from it and lands into the ground nearby, dealing moderate shock damage in the area (Agonizing Strike). Areas of electricity appear from the lingering lightning, one after the other in a counter clockwise order and travel outward as indicated by circular areas dealing low shock damage and snaring (Agonizing Fury).
