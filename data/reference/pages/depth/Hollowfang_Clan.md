# Online:Hollowfang Clan

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Hollowfang_Clan
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Hollowfang Clan is a Khajiit vampire clan operating out of Moongrave Fane in Northern Elsweyr. They plan to drain the dragon Sahrotnax's blood to gain more power, and have employed their champion, the fallen Dragonguard Grundwulf, for this purpose.
