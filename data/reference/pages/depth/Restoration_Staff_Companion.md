Restoration Staff is one of six weapon skill lines available to Companions.

## Skill Perks
The Restoration Staff skill line is immediately available to Azandar al-Cybiades, who starts his progress at level 3. Other Companions can unlock the skill line by equipping them with a Restoration Staff. The skill line can be leveled up from earning combat XP while your active Companion has a Restoration Staff equipped.
