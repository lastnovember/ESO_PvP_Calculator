The Arkthzand Insight Vertex Shroud is a hat available via the Antiquities system. The Lead is looted from an Inactive Construct across the broken bridge south of the Library of Arkthzand in Blackreach: Arkthzand Cavern 141460. A Void Anomaly must be used to cross the bridge.
