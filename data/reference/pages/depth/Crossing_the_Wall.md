## Detailed Walkthrough

### Researching Writhing Wall Weakpoints
After the events at the Broken Light Temple and the Colored Rooms, Mannimarco has taken over Wormblood's body and there are efforts underway to bring down the Writhing Wall, with the new reinforcements sent by the Alliances. The quest can be started by speaking with Prince Azah at the Vinutilmo Palace or the Sunport Rampart Camp, or being informed by Messenger Korilde that Azah wants to talk to you.

When this quest first became available, it was concurrent with the Battle for the Writhing Wall event and thus materials and support needed to be provided by the servers at large to fuel the Meridian Lens destroying the Wall. Once the event concluded much of the hard work, like destroying the Wall's power source in the Writhing Fortress has been achieved. However the Wall is still standing and Prince Azah will want you to talk with Midconjuror Mirmal, the creator of the Meridian Lens to see what else needs to be done.

Mirmal can be found at the Sunport Rampart Camp on the stone plinth where the Lens is set up. Speaking with him, Mirmal will theorize that the Worm Cult is actively strengthening the Wall against the Meridian Lens's attack. He then asks that you look into the late Gabrielle Benele's research into the Writhing Wall's potential weak points. Her research has been kept in the Regent's Study of Vinutilmo Palace, and Darien Gautier may need help going through it all.

It has not been long since Darien was brought back to life by Gabrielle's sacrifice and the Gift of Death. He has been going through her writings to see if he figure out why she did what she did. When you mention the Writhing Wall's anchor points, Darien points out her research notes and journal he found and placed on the nearby desks. Before you read the books, you can speak with Darien some more about what happened recently and the past. When you read Gabrielle's Journal, it covers previous events and mentions her research for the Wall's anchor points and possible weak points. Her research notes speculate that the anchor points could be detected by fluctuations and that the mountains passes near Li-Xal Pass and Shell-Tide Village would be the first places to check:
:Darien Gautier: "Fluctuations? Anchor points? Are you as confused as I am?"
:Darien Gautier: "Gabrielle's notes mention a couple of specific locations she wanted to look at."

Afterward, you can go over what was in the books with Darien. While Gabrielle did not have time to create a means of detecting the distortions in the Writhing Wall, Darien believes he will be able to find them due to being a walking Light of Meridia and having a sensitivity to death-magic. Once you leave the palace, Darien will become your follower and will travel with you across Solstice to the marked locations in the north and southern ends of the Wall. You are able to visit the two anchor point sites in either order.

As mentioned you visit the marked locations near the Li-Xal Pass and Shell-Tide Village to search for the theorized anchor points and destroy them. See below for what happens in each location.

#### Li-Xal Pass
After Darien uses his powers on the distortion at the northern end of the Wall, you can follow the spectral chain into the portal. You will find it takes you to the beach outside the Carapace Cavern found in the northwestern corner of the island:
:Darien Gautier: "Ugh! Didn't expect to get my boots wet. Looks like the ghostly chains lead to that cave."

Enter the delve and follow the chain through the cavern, to the southeast area where the chain goes through an ancient door. On the other side, you will find yourself in the Carapace Cavern Shrine and it is filled with Worm Cultists and Daedra. You can find the anchor shrine at the far end of the ruin, and it slightly resembles a Soul Reaper, smash it to weaken the wall further:
:Darien Gautier: "There! That obviously evil shrine is what we're looking for. Destroy it!"

After destroying the shrine, you can leave through the side door to exit the area.

#### Shell-Tide Village
When you check for distortions in Wall's southern end, a portal will open nearby which drops you near the Tarnur Mine:
:Darien Gautier: "Looks like the binding energy flows from somewhere inside that cave. We should investigate."

Entering the old Bismuth mine, you can follow the ghostly chain to an entrance to the Anchor Point Cave in the northeast corner of the mine. Once inside, you can go through the passage and search until you reach the Anchor Shrine:
:Darien Gautier: "I'd know the pungent smell of necromancy anywhere. We're getting close."

The shrine will be guarded by Necromancer Hezsatari and her fellow cultists defending the shrine:
:Necromancer Hezsatari: "To arms! Protect the shrine!"
:Darien Gautier: "I'd say we found it! Destroy that shrine, friend."

After dealing with Hezsatari, you can read the nearby Hezsatari's Instructions to get some background information on the anchor points and the wall. Once the shrine is destroyed, you can leave the mine through a side passage.

### Reporting Back to Camp
Once you have visited both of the anchor point sites and destroyed the shrines, you can return to the Sunport Rampart Camp and report to Prince Azah. He will be in the middle of speaking with Midconjuror Mirmal who agrees that progress is being made. When you report to Prince Azah about the anchor points you destroyed, he is pleased to say that the Writhing Wall is starting to show signs of damage. Furthermore, a recent landslide has revealed a fissure which may grant passage to Eastern Solstice. He will ask you and Darien to travel to the nearby ruin and investigate if a way through to the other side of the island is possible.

Afterward, you will have the option of speaking with Mirmal at the Meridian Lens for a status update before you leave.

### Investigating the Fissure Ruins
The aforementioned Fissure Ruins are to the southeast of the camp, and Darien will catch up with you when you arrive:
:Darien Gautier: "Hold on there, friend. If you're walking into a dark cave, you might want a Light of Meridia with you."

When you and Darien enter the ruins, you will see that the architecture are a mix of Coldharbour and the Worm Cult. Further inside, you will find a balcony that overlooks a large open space and an unwelcome figure can be seen empowering a shrine similar to the ones found at Tarnur Mine and the Carapace Cavern.

Mannimarco (in Wormblood's body) can be seen addressing a Maldrith Rebuker and some scamps:
:Mannimarco: "The Writhing Wall is weakening. We cannot let it fall."
:Mannimarco: "Begin the ritual. I will supply the necessary souls from Mor Naril."
:
:Darien Gautier: "They're trying to patch the Writhing Wall. Come on, we have to stop the ritual."

From your starting point, you will need to head through the passage to the north and make your way through the cavernous passage until you reach the rest of the Daedric ruins. From there, you head southwards till you reach the doors to the Ritual Plaza.

Entering the Plaza, you will need to deal with the Maldrith Rebuker before you can approach Mannimarco:
:Darien Gautier: "Take out that guardian! Then we can deal with Mannimarco's ritual."

During this fight, the Maldrith will summon Daedra to its side when it hits 80% and 51% Health. While these summons are still active the Maldrith will keep throwing blue fireballs at you. For Darien's part, he will periodically announce and cast the Ritual of Salvation which produces a circular AoE that provides a minor Damage Shield.

Once the Maldrith Rebuker is defeated, Darien will turn his attention to Mannimarco and the shielded altar:
:Darien Gautier: "Mannimarco's shielding that altar. Hang on, I can take care of that!"

A golden light surrounds Darien and he then swings his sword down on the shielded altar and breaks the shield. However, Mannimarco has a response to that:
:Mannimarco: "Meridia's fool. You were all that silly mage bought with her life?"
:Mannimarco: "You are a disappointing vessel for Meridia's light. I have a better use for it!"

Mannimarco strikes Darien with a beam of magic. Black smoke surrounds Darien, causing him to stagger and fall to his knees.>
:Darien Gautier: "Don't worry about me! Destroy the altar!"

When you destroy the altar, Mannimarco finishes siphoning the Light of Meridia from Darien which coalesces in his staff:
:Mannimarco: "You have my thanks. You delivered a Light of Meridia into my hands, just when I needed one the most!"
:Mannimarco: "The wall served its purpose. I am done here."

Mannimarco turns away and walks through the Writhing Wall. Darien stands up and staggers backwards, falling against the wall:
:Darien Gautier: "What did he do to me? I feel … different."

However, due to destroying the altar, Darien believes the Writhing Wall is ready to come down. He tells you to go on ahead and report back to Prince Azah at the Sunport Rampart Camp.

### "Vestige, tear down this Wall!"
After leaving the Fissure Ruins, you can return to the Fellowship camp where Prince Azah will be waiting for you near the Meridian Lens. With him will be Midconjuror Mirmal, Skordo the Knife, Razum-dar and Walks-in-Ash†, and Darien will catch up after you reach the group. Speak with Prince Azah about what happened at the Fissure Ruins, prior to Phase 3 of the Writhing Wall event, you would be asked to continue supporting the Fellowship's efforts while the Lens was powered up.

From Phase 3 and onwards, the Writhing Wall will have been sufficently weakened that it will take one more blast from the Meridian Lens to bring it down. Prince Azah gives you the honor of activating the Lens for the final time. Using the Lens, a powerful beam will hit the wall and your screen with go white. When your vision clears, some remaining golden lights will spark away from where the Writhing Wall was.
:Prince Azah: "Yes! You did it! The Writhing Wall has fallen!"
:Skordo the Knife: "Ha! Those tusking cultists got no place to hide now. Time for a fight!"
:Prince Azah: "Well said, Skordo! I have some thoughts on that, too. A word, comrade?"

With the Writhing Wall down, all the previously blocked mountain passes through to Eastern Solstice are now accessible. Speak with Prince Azah to complete the quest:
:"We did it, comrade! The Writhing Wall no longer stands in our way. The Stirk Fellowship can finally strike into eastern Solstice and end the threat of the Worm Cult. If we save our world from a new Planemeld in the process, even better!"
:So what comes next?
:"Our assembled forces begin the march east. We'll scour this island clean of the Worm Cult and all their evil works. We could not have gotten this far without you. Here. A reward for your efforts. On behalf of the Stirk Fellowship, I salute you!"

After completing the quest, the Light of Meridia from the Lens will float down to hover atop the crystal plinth. You can speak with Azah to get your orders for the initial push into eastern Solstice.

## Bugs
If you go in wrong direction when you approach the Li-Xal part of the Wall, Darien Gautier may disappear and you will not see his interactions with the Wall.
- Wait a bit, eventually the portal will appear and you can enter it to progress the quest.
In the Tanur Mine Shrine, you are unable to read the glowing note on the ground.
During the optional step to meet with Midconjuror Mirmal at the Meridian Lens, he will not appear there and stays in the camp.
