icon = Opulent Eradicator

type2 = Night Market
type = Exploration
points = 15
desc = As an individual in separate encounters, defeat the Opulent Web Eater, Opulent Arid Varlet, and Opulent Knightshade while having the Cobweb, Drylands, and Eclipse Affinities in the Night Market's Opulent Ordeal.

Affinity for Everything is awarded for completing the Opulent Ordeal while having the Cobweb, Drylands, and Eclipse Affinities during separate runs of the trial.
