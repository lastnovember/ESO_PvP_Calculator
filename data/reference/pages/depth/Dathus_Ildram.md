Dathus Ildram is a Dark Elf who can be found at the castle in Kvatch.

He has made some powerful enemies and is a target for the Morag Tong. According to Naryu Virian "he swindled some rather important clients and had the audacity to steal from House Hlaalu itself. The last straw was when he closed two orphanages to open a cornerclub."

## Quest-Related Events
After you've opened the secret passage, Naryu will get to work:
:Naryu: "Wake, wake, my darling. You can sleep when you're dead-which will be sooner than you think."
:Dathus: "What? Who are you? What are you doing in my room?"
:Naryu: "A better question would be, "Are you here to kill me?""
:Dathus: "Guards! Guards!"
:Naryu: "The answer, of course, is most definitely, 'Yes.'"

Naryu then kills Dathus.

## Notes
- The story of Naryu's contract on Dathus is discussed in Naryu's Journal, which was part of Morrowinds physical Collector's Edition. She lied about the true nature of her contract, and the Dathus Ildram you helped her find is not the real Dathus, but rather a body double.
