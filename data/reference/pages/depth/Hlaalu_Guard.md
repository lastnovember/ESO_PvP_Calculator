Hlaalu Guards were Dark Elf soldiers of House Hlaalu found in two parts of Deshaan. They were renamed with Update 6, as it introduced the Justice system, and all NPCs with "Guard" in their name got renamed.

Two were accompanying Captain Diiril inside The Apothecarium and near its entrance in the Obsidian Gorge. They had a set appearance and were renamed Servos Nirith and Urene Gimalvel.

Many were found outside Mzithumz fighting with House Dres warriors and accompanying Bedyni the Artificer inside Mzithumz. They were renamed House Hlaalu Retainer.

## Related Quests
- Mechanical Murder
- Plague Bringer

## Quest-Related Events
Inside the Apothecarium, the two Hlaalu Guards will be scavenging through the lair looking for clues on what created the Llodos plague.

Once you discovered the recipe that created the Llodos Plague, Merdyndril will turn The Hlaalu Guards into Plague Stalkers causing them to turn on you and the captain:
:Captain Diiril: "What'd you find? What is that?"
:Captain Diiril: "That's the recipe for the Llodos Plague. We've done it!"
:Captain Diiril: "Wait … what's happening?"

The two Hlaalu Guards that were accompanying Captain Diiril were turned into plague stalkers by Merdyndril, and you and the Captain need to kill them.

## Skills and Abilities
;Slash:The Hlaalu Guard hits you with their hands two times, dealing minor physical damage twice.
;Flurry:A series of four quick swipes in a cone in front of the Hlaalu Guard dealing minor physical damage each hit.
;Frenzy:When low on Health, The Hlaalu Guard enrages which increases their damage by a little.
;Desecrated Ground:On death, the undead rarely desecrate the ground under them as indicated by a red circle. This does continuous moderate magic damage and snares all targets as well as healing undead enemies within the red circle.
