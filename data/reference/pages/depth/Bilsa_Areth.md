Bilsa Areth is a Dark Elf who can be encountered with her donkey Braylis in Stonefalls during the Bilsa's Delivery Dynamic Encounter. She will ask for help with reaching Granny Tarani Areth's home.

## Dynamic Encounter Events
When the Dynamic Encounter starts, Bilsa will appear with Braylis on the shore near Hightide Hollow, and calls out for help:
:Bilsa Areth: "Please! My granny's sick and I lost the supplies she needs. It's too dangerous to find more on my own."
:Bilsa Areth: "I need to fill my donkey's packs with viper eel and fetid herbs. Can you help me?"

You will have approximately ten minutes to collect enough Viper Eels and Fetid Herbs from fishing and foraging in the nearby hills respectively, and bringing them to Bilsa. She will say the following during this phase:

Talking about the Fetid Herbs:
:Bilsa Areth: "That looks like the drawing Granny showed me."
:Bilsa Areth: "Granny told me these smelly plants keep the monsters away."
:Bilsa Areth: "Just a few more herbs and we'll have enough to make it to Granny's."

Talking about the Viper Eels:
:Bilsa Areth: "I can't believe you caught that eel already. It takes me so long!"
:Bilsa Areth: "You caught viper eel? I can almost taste Granny's famous eel pie."
:Bilsa Areth: "Just a few more eels like that and we'll be on our way."

If you fail to reach 100% with the progress bar before the timer ends, you will fail the encounter:
:Bilsa Areth: ""

If you got enogh items, the next stage will begin, escorting Bilsa and her donkey to her grandmother's home:
:Bilsa Areth: "That's everything! Please, come with me to Granny's. The path is dangerous, but I can't disappoint her."

Bilsa and Braylis will start running ahead and you can keep up with them. Throughout the journey they'll be attacked by swarms of Shalk, Nix-Hounds and Nix-Hound Prowlers. During this, you will need to protect Bilsa and she will throw out bunches of herbs to help:
:Bilsa Areth: "Lead them to the herbs!"
:Bilsa Areth: "They really don't like those herbs."
:Bilsa Areth: "The herbs will help you. I promise!"

When you reach Granny Tarani Areth's tent west of the Sulfur Pools Wayshrine, the final phase will start:
:Bilsa Areth: "Granny Tarani. I'm here!"
:
:Bilsa Areth: "A werewolf! It better not have hurt my granny!"

Defend Bilsa from the giant werewolf, once you have defeated it, it will transform back into Granny Tarani:
:Granny Tarani Areth: "I haven't lost myself like that in years. I'm sorry for scaring you, my brave granddaughter."
:Bilsa Areth: "Granny, you're alive? I thought you'd been eaten!"
:Granny Tarani Areth: "Thank you for returning me to my senses. I hope this makes up for your trouble."
:

If you enter the tent, you will find Bilsa sitting down with a cup of tea, while her granny can be found stirring a pot in front of the oven.

If you take too much time to defeat Granny Tarani Areth:
:Bilsa Areth: "I'll visit Granny when it's less dangerous. I don't want to die!"
