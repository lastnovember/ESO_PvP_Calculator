# Online:Houndsmen

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Houndsmen
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Houndsmen are a group of Hircine worshippers working for the Hound. They have allied with the local Drublog Clan to enlist their aid in attacking the Silvenar.
