Online Champion 2.0 Summary
sign=Fitness
type=slotted
stagepoints=10
maxpoints=50
desc=Increases the effectiveness of your damage shields and damage against shielded enemies by 3% per stage.

Bastion increases the size of your damage shields and your damage against damage shields by 3%, up to 15%.

The Damage Shield bonus works for all your damage shields excepted skills which primarily deal damage, such as Cleave and its morphs. For example, Magma Shell and Hardened Armor will still get boosted by Bastion even though they also deal (low) damage.

Bastion is the only Fitness Champion Node to give a direct damage bonus.
