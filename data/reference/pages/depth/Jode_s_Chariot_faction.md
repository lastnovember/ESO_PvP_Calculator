# Online:Jode's Chariot (faction)

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Jode's_Chariot_(faction)
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Jode's Chariot is a law enforcement agency employed by Shan-ra, the leader of the merchants in charge of the Redfur Trading Post.

They have a minor rivalry with the Hollow Moon, a local group of thieves that steals from the rich and gives to the needy. Though the two groups generally have mutual respect for one another due to the nature of the Hollow Moon's cause, the Chariot must still arrest Hollow Moon members should they be caught.
