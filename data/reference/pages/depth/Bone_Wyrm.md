The Bone Wyrm is a small bone dragon that can be summoned by Zumog Phoom in the Sepulcher of Mischance to help guard the body of Cadwell the Betrayer. Initially it is seen as an inanimate cadaver until Phoom resurrects it later on during the fight while, prior to his bone colossus transformation. It cannot be fought, though will be defeated if Phoom is killed.

## Related Quests
- Cadwell the Betrayer (quest)

## Notes
- The Frozen Colossus necromancer skill was originally intended to summon a bone wyrm before it was scrapped, though Zumog Phoom still uses the original skill (called Bone Wyrm Resurrection). The effects (still found in the database) were:
- Bone Dragon: Unleash an undead frost wyrm to freeze an enemy to the bone, causing them to take 2429 Frost Damage over 6 seconds and applying Major Vulnerability to them, increasing their damage taken by 30%.
- Plague Dragon: Unleash an undead green wyrm to spread pestilence to all enemies in front of it, causing them to take 2513 Disease Damage over 6 seconds and applying Major Vulnerability to them, increasing their damage taken by 30%.
- Frost Dragon: Unleash an undead frost wyrm to freeze an enemy to the bone, causing them to take 3590 Frost Damage over 9 seconds and applying Major Vulnerability to them, increasing their damage taken by 30%.
