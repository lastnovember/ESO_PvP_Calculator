Abal-jo is a Khajiit mercenary employed by House Montclair who can be found in the ruins of Erokii. He serves as one of the delve's three bosses, and has no unique dialogue.

## Skills and Abilities
;Minor Wound:A basic ranged attack dealing minor magic damage
;Focused Healing:Abal-jo will heal any allies that are hurt, including himself but for half as much. Enemies often indicate this by calling out for healing (I'm hurt!, I need healing!, Healer! etc.). This effect appears as a yellow beam connecting Abal-jo and his target which breaks if the line of sight between them is broken. The first tick begins 0.5 seconds after the channel begins, and also heals half as much for the first 3 ticks. This can be interrupted to set Abal-jo off balance. Abal-jo never targets summons from himself or his allies, for example Ice Barrier.
;Run Away!:When too close to him, Abal-jo backs up to put some distance between his target and himself.

## Bugs
Despite being an elite rank enemy, Abal-jo is vulnerable to crowd control.
