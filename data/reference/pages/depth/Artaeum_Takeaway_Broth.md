The Artaeum Takeaway Broth is a rare food learned from the Recipe: Artaeum Takeaway Broth. Its recipe can only be found inside a Waterlogged Psijic Satchel which can be obtained by fishing on Artaeum only.

Unlike regular recipes, the Artaeum Takeaway Broth recipe is account-bound on pickup, meaning it cannot be traded or sold. If you wish to buy it, you will need to take a chance and buy Waterlogged Psijic Satchels instead.

## Notes
- This recipe was originally bugged and was considered to be a beverage, but was changed to a food item in Patch. Despite being originally flagged as a drink, it did not trigger drink-related set bonuses or work with either of the food and drink duration passives.
