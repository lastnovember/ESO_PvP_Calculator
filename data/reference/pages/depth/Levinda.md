Levinda is a and the sister of Zephyra who can be found at the Ritual Skirmish in the Parch when it is active. They will be in the middle of performing a massive weather ritual for an unknown reason.

## Related Quests
- Representing the Faction

## Skills and Abilities
Zephyra and Levinda will deign to fight you during Phase 3 of the Ritual Skirmish after you have attuned and empowered the Antiweather Pylons surrounding the ritual site. During the fight, she will use the following abilities:
;Thunderbolt:
;Storm Call:
;Fulmination:
;Summon Dremora Windcaller: She summons Dremora Windcallers
