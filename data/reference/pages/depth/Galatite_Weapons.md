# Online:Galatite Weapons

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Galatite_Weapons
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

This is a listing of all standard Galatite Weapons in the game. These can be crafted via Blacksmithing from Galatite Ingots obtained by refining Galatite Ore. It requires rank 7 in Metalworking. The listed damage values assume that the character is Level 50 CP160; lower level characters may see higher values.
