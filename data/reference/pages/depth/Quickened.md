# Online:Quickened

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Quickened
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Quickened is a trait which can be found on Companion weapons, armor and jewelry. The trait cannot be researched or crafted, and exists only on found loot. Items with the Quickened trait reduce the Cooldown Time of a Companion's Abilities by an amount dependent on the quality of the item.
