# Online:Gaspard's Stalkers

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Gaspard's_Stalkers
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Gaspard's Stalkers is a now-disbanded gang of mercenaries. When it was active, it was led by Captain Gaspard Esmery and operated in Hammerfell and Elsweyr. King Kurog and Chief Bazrag were members when they were younger, known as Kurog gro-Bagrakh and Bazrag the Butcher at the time.
