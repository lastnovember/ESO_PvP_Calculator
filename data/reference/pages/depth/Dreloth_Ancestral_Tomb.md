## Notes
- The tomb also appeared in Morrowind.
- Despite the fact that Ancestral Adversity begins here, it is actually part of the Veloth Ancestral Tomb Objective.
- True to the loading screen text, a Dunmer named Riinsi Dreloth can indeed be found in Kragenmoor.

## Bugs
Although the map indicates the entrance is up on the side of the hill (which is inaccessible), the actual entrance is at the "coffin" marker slightly to the north.
