# Online:Assassination

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Assassination
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Assassination is one of three class skill lines available to Nightblades. It can be learned by other classes via subclassing. Abilities in this line focus on melee weapon attacks.

## Skill Perks
The Assassination skill line is immediately available to all Nightblades, and is leveled up by earning experience while Assassination skills are slotted. Skills include:
