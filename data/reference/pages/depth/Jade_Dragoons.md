# Online:Jade Dragoons

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Jade_Dragoons
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Jade Dragoons are an elite band of soldiers within the Aldmeri Dominion, led by General Endare. The Dragoons were responsible for the massacre at Cormount. Until recently, they were charged with protecting the city of Haven, until the order was given from Endare to leave the city on some mysterious mission. This led to the city being quickly taken over by the Jackdaw Pirates.
