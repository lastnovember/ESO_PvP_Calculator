Desecrated Ghosts are ghosts that appear in the second wave of the Hergor the Fallen Group Event fight in Rulanyil's Fall.

## Skills and Abilities
;Double Strike: A basic melee attack that hits twice for minor physical damage.
;Chill Touch: The Desecrated Ghost casts a short-ranged spell that deals moderate frost damage and snares their target. The spell can be interrupted to set the Desecrated Ghost off-balance. Blocking doesn't prevent the snare.
;Haunting Spectre: The Desecrated Ghost winds up and releases an apparition in the direction it is facing. Getting touched by the apparition deals moderate frost damage and fears their target, though Break Free can be used. This spell can be interrupted to set the Desecrated Ghost off-balance. You cannot block this attack.

## Achievements
There is one achievement associated with this creature:
Rulanyil's Fall Group Event

## Bugs
Despite being flying enemies, Desecrated Ghosts do not have "Flying Immunities" which should make them immune to snares and roots.
