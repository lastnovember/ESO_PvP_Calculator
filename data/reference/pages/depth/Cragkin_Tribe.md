# Online:Cragkin Tribe

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Cragkin_Tribe
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Cragkin are a tribe of goblins found in Bangkorai and Reaper's March. The goblins in the Qharroa Ruins appear to be worshiping the gargoyles roaming the area, as many are found kneeling in reverence before them. The same can be seen with the goblins at Big Ozur's Valley, where they are found worshiping and defending Big Ozur.
