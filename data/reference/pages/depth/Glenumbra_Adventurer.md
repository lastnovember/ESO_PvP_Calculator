Glenumbra Adventurer is awarded for completing 53 quests in Glenumbra. This includes both the Glenumbra Main Quest as well as any side-quests. It does not include Crafting Writs, Fighters Guild or Mages Guild quests, or pledges for the Undaunted.plain This achievement will allow you to purchase Hunding's Palatial Hall outside Port Hunding for 1,295,000, and you can purchase a Hagraven Totem for your house. It costs 5,000 and can be purchased from Lozotusk in Daggerfall.
