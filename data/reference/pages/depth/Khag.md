Khag is an Orc pirate and part of the crew of Captain One-Eye who can be found in Eastern Solstice, near Caterwaul Cove.

## Related Quests
- Blood on the Water

## Quest-Related Events
Khag can be found with Captain One-Eye and Argenta at their small camp. Talking with him before Captain One-Eye:
:"Is this really Nirn? Malacath, it's been so long. I forgot that dirt and the ocean smelled like this."

Talking with Captain One-Eye, she will explain how they recently escaped Coldharbour but Captain Velehk Sain's ship chased after them. She asks you to enter the nearby Sea Caves to reach the Caterwaul and place an explosive rune onboard it. She asks you to be stealthy, otherwise the Caterwaul Watchlings will warn Sain and he will likely kill his prisoners.

Speaking with Khag afterward:
:"Hope you're better at sneaking than me. Argenta's pretty good at it, but she's in no shape right now. Glad you're here. Whoever you are."

As you leave for the Sea Caves, Captain One-Eye and Khag will talk:
:Khag: "Don't know if we can sail with just us few, captain."
:Captain One-Eye: "If we even have a ship fit to sail. We'll think of something."

Going through the Sea Caves, you can reach the Caterwaul where Velehk Sain can be found talking with his quartermaster and killing one of the prisoners on a whim. Once you place the explosive rune, you will be hailed by Suraj who has some tasks for you. You can complete either or both of them, and then meet the remaining crew at the Bonnie Spriggan, where Khag can be met once more:
:"Captain ordered me to stay here and protect the surviving crew. I would rather be out there doing what you're doing, but orders are orders."

You can inform Captain One-Eye of what you have done, and she will ask you to speak with Argenta and Suraj for more tasks to get the Bonnie Spriggan sailing again. Like before, you will be able to choose what tasks you want to complete. Once you have finished speaking with her, Captain One-Eye will decide that is time to detonate the previously placed explosive rune:
:Captain One-Eye: "Since you're well clear of that ship, it's time to set off my rune. Shu naxan, tzilnech!"
:
:Khag: "Mara's rosy backside, captain! Where'd you learn to make something like that?"
:Captain One-Eye: "That's just how we do things south of Morrowind."

Talking to Khag before Suraj and Argenta:
:"Got work to do, pirate. Captain says Suraj and Argenta have more for you to do, though. Heard it might involve sneaking. Sure don't envy that."

Suraj will want you to collect parts of the Coldharbour's Teeth (a weapon) he hid in the caves, while Argenta will you to help some mutineers and rescue them. Speaking with Khag before you leave, you can ask him some questions:
:"Trying to keep an eye on Argie. She likes to stab things, especially things that scuttle. Great against scamps back in Coldharbour. But I'd like to keep from fending off a swarm of mudcrabs. They don't deserve that."
:What's your role on the Bonnie Spriggan?
:"My role? Depends on the day, I suppose. I'd expect Captain One-Eye is the only soul aboard the Spriggan with a single job. The rest of us wear many hats. Carpenter is the one I wear the most. Though I heard I was next up for bosun after Salvus."
:Would that make you second in command?
:"Don't think so. That was Din, our quartermaster. But he's gone now. Suppose Suraj might take his place. But it's up to the captain, and I expect she's more focused on other things at the moment."

Back in the Sea Caves, you can collect the weapon parts and attempt to free the mutineers by destroying the Observer. Once you return to the ship, it will be getting ready to sail, but Captain One-Eye will want to speak with you. Talking to Khag first:
:"Seems like we're as patched up as we're going to be. Might even be able to survive this fight. So long as another portal doesn't come along and pull us back into Coldharbour."

After telling Captain One-Eye what you accomplished, she will ask you to give the crew a speech before you set sail. Talking to Khag before giving the speech:
:"Captain said you're going to speak before we set sail. Go on! We're ready."

During you speech to the crew, if you say, "Your crew may be fractured, but you grew back stronger. I've faced far worse with far less." Khag will respond:
:Khag: "Careful. Some here might take that as a challenge."

After the speech, you can talk to Khag before going below deck:
:"Seems like we're as patched up as we're going to be. Might even be able to survive this fight. So long as another portal doesn't come along and pull us back into Coldharbour."

The ship will sail north from Solstice until it is met by the Caterwaul. This is the location of the battle at sea, during this Khag will man the reassembled Coldharbour's Teeth ballista and periodically fires bolts at enemies. Once Velehk Sain is dead, the surviving crew of the Bonnie Spriggan will gather in a circle. Talking to Khag before setting fire to the Caterwaul:
:"Salvus, Din, Sails, and the others … I hope wherever they are now, they can rest easy knowing we banished Velehk Sain back to Oblivion. I won't forget them."

After you set fire to the Caterwaul, Khag and the others will watch it burn. Speaking with him before go back below decks for the trip back to Solstice:
:"We managed to scrape through that battle without losing anyone else. Might be a first for the Spriggan. All due to that speech of yours, I'd bet. First time we've heard something so stirring. Hope it's not the last."

When you return to Solstice, you can speak with Captain One-Eye to complete the quest. Afterward, Captain One-Eye will address the crew:
:Captain One-Eye: "No complaining, now. This is a time to celebrate."
:Khag: "Ordering us to have fun, are you, captain?"

## Skills and Abilities
;Cold Fire Ballista Bolt/Burning:During the fight against Captain Velehk Sain, Khag will periodically fire a coldfire ballista bolt at enemies as indicated by a large green circle, dealing 2,024 flame damage on hit in an area (Cold Fire Ballista Bolt) then 1,286 flame damage over time to enemies hit (Burning).

## Dialogue
After completing the objective at Caterwaul Cove, Khag will appear with the rest of the crew at the Sunport Docks near the warehouse:
:"This city's full of life. And even better, full of animals. Hard not to pet every dog I see, especially when most of them don't take kindly to some strange Orc coming at them with his hand out."

:"The Spriggan's going to be ferrying some well-paying passengers off Solstice. I recognize them from back home, but they haven't recognized me yet. Strange seeing Gahz and Oorbesh be so civil. And I'll be keeping an eye on their … friend." (Said if Ghosthunters is completed)
