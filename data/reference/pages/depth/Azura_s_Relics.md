## Quick Walkthrough
# Find the three Stolen Relics.
# Find the captured Spirit Warden.
# Get the Conjuration Box Key.
# Lock the two Conjuration Boxes.
# Talk to the Spirit.
# Go to the bottom of the waterfall and use the scroll of sight.
# Kill the Daedra.
# Return to Brother Perry to complete the quest.

## Detailed Walkthrough

## Notes
- The summoned Scamp will remain with you full time while you are in the area, until the location is cleared. Leaving Weeping Giant despawns it, and returning to the area after offers you the prompt to summon it again.
- Brother Perry will not exist at the Weeping Giant until reaching the specific related objective.
- The conjuration boxes won't be visible until accepting the quest but also will start appearing prematurely, before they are mentioned by Brother Perry, but cannot be interacted with then.
- The summoned Daedra boss will not give experience or loot when killed.

## Bugs
Canceling the animation of the Scamp summoning causes you to lose the option to summon it at all moving forward.
- Completing your current objective will make the prompt appear again.
The prompt to summon the Scamp will reappear after completing each new objective, even while you currently have one scamp active. Using the prompt will perform the animation again but nothing will occur and your summoned Scamp will remain.
