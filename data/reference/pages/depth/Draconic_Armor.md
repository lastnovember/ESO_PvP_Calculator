# Online:Draconic Armor

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Draconic_Armor
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Draconic Armor is one of three class skill lines available to Bastian Hallix and Tanlorin. Abilities in this line focus on avoiding incoming damage from enemies. It is roughly equivalent to the Draconic Power skill line for Dragonknight players.

## Skill Perks
The Draconic Armor skill line is immediately available to Bastian and Tanlorin, and is leveled up as they gain levels.
