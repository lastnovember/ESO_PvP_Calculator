Captain Tremouille is a Breton sea captain who can be found in Vulkhel Guard aboard The Interim Suitor, near the docks. If you have begun Cadwell's Silver or Gold for the Dominion, he will have brought you to the port on the behest of Razum-dar.

## Related Quests
- Storm on the Horizon
