# Online:Herald of the Tome

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Herald_of_the_Tome
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Herald of the Tome is one of three class skill lines available to Arcanists. It can be learned by other classes via subclassing. Abilities in this line focus on dealing damage.

## Skill Perks
The Herald of the Tome skill line is immediately available to all Arcanists, and is leveled up by earning experience while Herald of the Tome skills are slotted. Skills include:
