Dixulti the Moldering is an Argonian necromancer attempting to access the tomb of the dragon priest Morokei inside Labyrinthian. He aims to absorb Morokei's power.

## Related Quests
- The Fading Fire

## Skills and Abilities
At the beginning of the fight, Dixulti stops his ritual and places a necromantic ward over himself, making him invulnerable. You must defeat the enemies he summons to drop the ward. There are two waves of enemies you must defeat to accomplish this. The first group consists of a Draugr Archer and a Draugr Savage, which he summons when his shield first goes up. The second group contains a Draugr Charger and a Draugr Icecaster he summons when the first two draugr are dead. Dixulti will use his other abilities while warded.

;Abyss: Deals major magic damage when you get too close to Morokei.
;Crypt Slash: A basic melee attack dealing moderate physical damage.
;Heinous Flurry: A basic melee attack dealing moderate physical damage.
;Necrotic Barrage: Dixulti throws a handful of necromantic orbs all around the arena, shown as small growing red circles, which then detonate, dealing moderate magical damage each.
;Deadly Rake: Dixulti swipes the ground with his daggers, causing necromantic shockwaves to shoot forward, dealing moderate magical damage each. These don't have AoE indicators.
;Closed Fist: Dixulti casts a rune on the ground that activates if stepped into, rooting affected targets. The root can be escaped by roll dodging.
;Grasping Void: A ghastly hand reaches out from the ground, grabs its target and throws them down, dealing moderate physical damage and causing a knockdown effect.
;Skeletal Cyclone: Dixulti rakes his daggers together for a time, as a very large red circle forms, then spins, dealing very high physical damage. The spin also places several bone traps all around the arena, indicated by red circles that disappear shortly after.
;Skeletal Cyclone Burst: Stepping into the area marked by the previously displayed circles detonates the trap, dealing high magical damage. If not detonated before then, when Dixulti next uses Grasping Void, all traps detonate together at once.
