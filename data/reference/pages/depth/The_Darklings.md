# Online:The Darklings

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:The_Darklings
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Darklings are a group of hostile Nocturnal worshippers who have settled in the ruined fort of Faldar's Tooth. They are led by a winged twilight, Rozelun, and are in possession of Winter's Bite, an artifact that belongs to the Companions.
