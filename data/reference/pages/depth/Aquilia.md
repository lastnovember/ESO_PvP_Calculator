Aquilia was an Imperial Legion soldier who could be found in the Imperial Sewers. She's been separated from her squadmates, Martina, Subonia, and Eulintus.

## Related Quests
- Blood in the Water

## Quest-Related Events
When you initially approach her, she'll mutter to herself:
:Aquilia: "Where are the others? We agreed to meet here. Or was it somewhere else?"

When you speak to her, she'll say:
:""
:How long have you been hiding here?
:"Ever since our squad got jumped. Martina and Subonia got away, I think, but the Daedra caught Eulintus. I saw them drag him off. I couldn't stop them. I hope he's still alive."
:Where did they take him?
:"I didn't stick around to watch, but I'd guess they dragged him into the Arena. That's where they take all their toys, to make them die for their amusement. If…if you're going up there, would you look for Eulintus?"
:Sure, I'll look for him.
