# Online:Tank

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Tank
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Tanks are a class of hostile humanoid NPCs that attack using melee abilities. They are usually equipped with heavy armor and carry a one-handed sword and a shield. They also have more health than the average NPC.

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Strike: A basic melee attack dealing minor physical damage.
;Power Bash: A heavy shield bash dealing moderate physical damage, stunning and setting off balance. This can be blocked to knock the enemy off balance instead.
;Brace:When performing a heavy attack near or on the tank, they may briefly block with its shield, reducing damage taken greatly. Players who attempt melee heavy attacks on the enemy while it is blocking will be set off balance.
