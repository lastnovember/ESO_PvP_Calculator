Abnajah is a Redguard who could be found at the then-blocked western gate near the town of Dragonstar. He was removed from the game after the zone of Upper Craglorn was added in Update 4 and the gate was opened.

## Dialogue
When speaking to him, he'd say:
:"Here to join up? We're offering decent pay for a hard day's work. And with this much rock to move … well … there's plenty of job security."
:What happened here?
:"Earthquake. Worst one I've ever seen. Damn near shook the teeth right out of my head."
:Was anyone inside when the gap collapsed?
:"Can't be sure. I just arrived a few days ago. But if there was anyone on the road when it happened, I wouldn't hold out much hope."
:This is a real mess. How long will it take to clear?
:"Oh, just a few days I reckon."
:You're kidding.
:"Of course I'm kidding. Look at this place. If I get these diggers working double shifts we might be able to clear the gap in a century or two. I wouldn't hold out much hope though."
:So why are you here?
:"Keeping up appearances I guess. King Fahara'jad, blessings be upon him, sent me out here as a disaster relief. As long as I'm here pointing at people, the King can say that he's doing his part. Politics."
:Well, good luck.
:"Thanks. We need it."
