# Online:Ornate

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Ornate
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Ornate is a trait which can be found on weapons, armor and jewelry. The trait cannot be researched or crafted, and exists only on found loot. Items with the Ornate trait are worth more Gold when sold to Vendors by an amount dependent on the quality of the item, as well as providing more materials.
