Commander Norevalion is a Maormer member of the Sea Vipers found at the Seaside Sanctuary, only during the related quest. He is accompanied by two other Sea Vipers, one is standing besides him and one is kneeling before him.

## Related Quests
- A Storm Upon the Shore

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Strike:A basic melee attack dealing minor physical damage.
;Power Bash:A spinning shield attack against a target dealing moderate physical damage, stunning and setting off-balance. This can be blocked to set Commander Norevalion off-balance instead.
;Block:Commander Norevalion briefly blocks with his shield, reducing damage taken greatly. Targets who attempt melee heavy attacks on Commander Norevalion while he is blocking will be set off-balance.
;Dark Talons:Commander Norevalion roots all targets within range for 4 seconds with Dark Talons dealing minor flame damage.
;Dragonknight Standard:Commander Norevalion calls down a fiery banner that plants down into the ground, with a large circular AoE dealing continuous direct minor flame damage and applying Major Defile.

## Bugs
Despite being an elite rank enemy, Commander Norevalion is vulnerable to crowd control.
