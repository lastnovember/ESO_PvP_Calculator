A portal to the Infinite Archive can be found north of Runemaster's Acropolis, inside a cavern that can be accessed on the shoreline. Alternatively, it can be accessed by clicking on the icon on the Apocrypha map and teleporting directly to the Archive. You can also get there by talking with Filer Pix, who can be found outside of Molag Mar in Vvardenfell. It is an endless library which has become infected by Tho'at Replicanum, a Daedric being of living ink.

The Infinite Archive is part of the base game and is accessible for all players, regardless of ESO Plus subscription or ownership of the Necrom Chapter.

## Functionality
The Infinite Archive is an "unending" dungeon/duo-arena added in Update 40. The Infinite Archive does not have a normal or veteran mode; it gets progressively more difficult with each 'round' one completes. Players may enter solo, with an NPC Companion, or with one friend (in a duo). There is a separate Leaderboard for solo and duo completions of the archive.

What makes The Infinite Archive different from other PvE content in ESO is that the majority of its encounters are randomly generated. Each 'round' consists of randomly selected enemies, which can conjure up unusual combinations of various enemies from throughout the game, such as bone colossi fighting alongside dwarven automata. At the start of a round, a blob of roiling ink will spawn, and walking under it will trigger the enemies to appear. There are three such waves in each round. The second and third waves will spawn immediately after finishing the previous one. You must complete all three waves without dying to complete the round. If you are killed during any wave (and have any Threads remaining), you will need to start the round over.

A round's final encounter spawns a Boss, again randomly selected from dungeon, world, and trial content throughout ESO, including Expansions. The bosses' HP and damage are reduced, making them more suitable for a 1 or 2 player battle. However, they do retain most of their original mechanics, so having knowledge and experience of a wide range of bosses in ESO is advantageous.

### Threads
When you first enter the Archive, you will have three Threads available, or four if you or your (player) partner have acquired Destozuno's Safety Net. This is increased to five if both players have Destozuno's Safety Net. You may additionally receive a "Supplemental Thread" as a Vision at the end of a boss stage, but this can only happen once during a run of the Archive, meaning that you have a potential maximum of six threads. If you are killed by any means in the archive, you will lose one of these Threads. When you have lost your last thread, you cannot continue and must start over. If this happens during the first Arc (see Progression, below), you will not be able to complete the daily quest without starting over. The "Rebirth" Verse can save you from death and prevent you from losing a Thread, but this can only occur once in a Stage and, like all Verses, the protection only lasts for one Stage. Dying during one of the side puzzles does not cost you a Thread (though it will fail the puzzle). You can buy Tentacular Motivation from Filer Tezurs to increase your movement speed in the Infinite Archive which helps both in combat and in the side puzzles.

If you are playing with another player, you will share the same Thread count, but you will only lose a Thread if both of you are killed. Resurrecting each other does not consume a Thread. Note that this applies whether or not you are still in combat. So if one player dies and the other completes the stage alone, the dead player should still wait for their partner to resurrect them rather than reviving themselves, which would waste a Thread.

### Solo, Duo, Companions
While the Infinite Archive is neither normal nor veteran, the ramping difficulty is static regardless of whether you play solo or as a duo. A player may certainly last longer and complete more rounds playing with another player. Companions are available to select within the Archive, however, they are unable to make choices based on content and therefore are not the best at staying alive as difficulty increases.

- Best method: Duo with a friend.
- Nominal: With a Companion (and a quick rez).
- Challenging: Solo. One must have a solid 'solo build' as well as in-depth knowledge of game mechanics.

### Progression
The Infinite Archive is broken down into smaller portions to make it more structured and manageable:

- Stages - Each time you go through a Portal in the Infinite Archive, you are taken to a new Stage. There are two Stages of enemies, each one containing three waves of randomly generated factions. The next stage will contain a boss, randomly selected from a long list of potential bosses found throughout ESO. If you die (or both you and your partner die if you are with another player) at any point during a Stage, you will have to start the Stage over (assuming you have any remaining Threads), even if you had already completed 1 or 2 waves. N.B. For the purposes of endeavors and achievements, the boss round does not count as a "stage".
- Cycles - After beating the boss at the end of the final Stage, you will begin a new Cycle. Each Cycle has the same structure as that described above, two Stages and a boss fight on the final one.
- Arc - On the fifth Cycle, there are two Stages as usual, but for the boss Stage, you will face Tho'at Replicanum, who is the final boss of the Arc. She is tough and can easily end your run. After completing the boss, you will move on to another Arc, beginning the process anew (Arc 2, Cycle 1, Stage 1). Each Arc is more difficult than the last - enemies and bosses will hit harder and you may occasionally find surprise bosses (Marauders) mixed in with the standard enemies.

Non-boss enemies' health increase by 2% per Cycle in an Arc, meaning by Cycle 5 they will have 8% more health than in the Cycle 1 of the same Arc.

All enemies also have their health increased every Arc. The increase formula(s) is/are unknown, though non-boss enemies' health are multiplied by ~1.685 from the end of Arc 1 to the start of Arc 2 and by ~2.22 from the end of Arc 2 to the start of Arc 3.

### Visions and Verses
Verses are temporary buffs that last until the end of the next Stage. At the end of each Stage, you will be given a choice between 2 random Verses (or 3 if you have purchased the Sweeter Deal upgrade from Filer Tezurs). You can also receive Verses from completing the side wing challenges. In addition, you may purchase or acquire random mystery Verses:
- Mystery Defensive Verse
- Mystery Offensive Verse
- Mystery Utility Verse
- Mystery Transformation Verse
You may only use one of these per Cycle, and it lasts for only one Stage, just as the normal ones do. Thus it is possible to have up to three Verses simultaneously, but normally you will have just one. Purchasing the Ghostly Gratitude upgrade from Filer Tezurs will improve the strength of your Verses.

Visions are enduring buffs that will be on your character until you finish the Infinite Archive. At the end of each Cycle (including the 5th Cycle where you face Tho'at), you will be given a choice between 2 random Visions (or 3 if you have purchased Filer Kor's Truesight from Filer Tezurs). Most Visions can stack, so you can benefit from having multiples of the same Vision. A few are one-time only, and you won't see them again if you already have them. Purchasing the Aramril's Training upgrade from Filer Tezurs will improve the quality of your Visions.

### Rewards
After completing a Cycle, Arc or side puzzle, a Muniment Chest will be awarded, containing a variety of rewards including gold, equipment, soul gems and a special new currency called Archival Fortunes.

Archival Fortunes can be spent at the merchants in the entrance area of the Infinite Archive in return for special rewards, such as Monster Mask style motifs, Legendary quality treasure maps, non combat pets, Companion gear and more.

The Infinite Archive item sets are Class-specific. A player will typically only find pieces of the set for the class currently being played. As with all curated Sets, once the 'Stickerbook' is completed, other set pieces for other classes will begin to drop.

### Bosses
There are many different bosses one can potentially find in the The Infinite Archive. These bosses are reflections of dungeon, world and trial bosses throughout ESO, and typically retain most of their mechanics and attacks, however their health and damage is scaled down to make them more balanced for 1 or 2 players.

As one progresses through the Infinite Archive, bosses will get tougher, with more health and damage. For instance, a boss in Arc 2 will have 40% more health and deal more damage than the same boss encountered in Arc 1. Does this increase at the same rate for Arc 3+?

### "Portal to the Unknown" zones
At the end of a non-boss round, a "Portal to the Unknown" may sometimes appear to the right of the main portal leading to the next round. Entering this portal will take you to one the following zones, selected randomly, and is the only way to access them. These zones act as mini-games, which, if completed successfully, reward the players with a Muniment Chest and an extra Verse at the end. Several achievements are tied to these zones, and the majority of your active Verses and Visions are temporarily deactivated in those zones, for example Apocryphal Emissary still summoning an ally at your side. In all cases, you can take a portal back to the Infinite Archive Index Chamber before starting the mini-game, and a portal to the next round whether or not you succeed at the mini-game.

#### Treacherous Crossing

One of the locations accessible through Portal to the Unknown. This area is a long corridor you have to cross but the way is filled by hazardous patches of tentacles. Pillars stand before you that can deactivate various patches for a limited time. You have to find the correct order to have enough time to cross the corridor.

#### Haefal's Butchery

One of the locations accessible through Portal to the Unknown. This area is a small arena. When you move, Butcher Haefal will throw bottles that transform players into goats. To get out of this area you have to eat 3 Sweetrolls in order to cast your ultimate into Butcher Haefal, which kills him. With two players, Butcher Sja also appears and needs to be killed.

#### Filer's Wing

One of the locations accessible through Portal to the Unknown. This is an area filled with book walls, Tentacles, Incunabular Storms and hostile Hushed. Filer Kor transforms you into a watchling and asks you to hunt the Tomeshells within.

#### Echoing Den

One of the locations accessible through Portal to the Unknown. This area is in a cave. A spirit stands in the center and disappears when you approach. You have to collect lights and bring them to the center to restore the spirit.

#### Destozuno's Library

One of the locations accessible through Portal to the Unknown. This area is a library made by Destozuno. It is only accessible when you either don't have any of her quests or have a current one completed and have to talk to her.

#### Theater of War

One of the locations accessible through Portal to the Unknown. This area is a large square room. Aramril stands in the center waiting to fight you. You have to fight her to get out of the room.

## Leaderboard
The Infinite Archive has several leaderboards where players can compete against each other. These scores are NOT time dependent, as each player or team will work through the dungeon arcs at different paces. Score is improved by completing more stages, cycles and arcs. Solo and Duo Leaderboards are separate. Having a Companion active upon entering the Archive will place the player in the Duo Leaderboard if they score well.
