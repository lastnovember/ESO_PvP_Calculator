Online:Woodworking (skill)

Woodworking is a crafting profession in Elder Scrolls Online. It allows you to create staves, shields and bows using wood materials at the woodworking stations. The list of ingredients (logs, sanded wood and resins) used in Woodworking can be found here. Once you are Certified in this craft, you will have access to repeatable Crafting Writ quests.

In addition to staves, shields and bows, the Woodworking skill allows you to craft furnishings for your home.

## Learning
The Woodworking skill line is granted the first time you activate a woodworking station. You can increase your skill by crafting or deconstructing staves, shields and bows, researching traits, and by completing Woodworker Writs or Master Writs. In addition, you can earn free levels in the skill line by reading Skill Books, which may be found randomly on bookshelves anywhere. The skill books which increase Woodworking are:
- Bowyer and Fletcher
- One Staff, Many Staves
- Strakes and Futtocks
- The Year 2920, Vol. 7
- Arboreal Architecture
- Sealed Orders (opened)

## Carpenter Dialogue
You can learn about this profession by asking some of the carpenters.
:Can you tell me about woodworking?
:"Certainly! Woodworkers shape wood into all manner of useful tools, but bows, staves, and shields are our specialty. This work requires fine lumber and a sturdy work table. We also seek out style books and rare ingredients for more advanced work."
:Can you tell me about the process?
:"Sure. While shaping the wood, we can choose a special trait to focus on. It takes research to discover these techniques, but it's well worth the effort. Increasing the weight or tuning the angle can draw out different advantages in the final product."
:Is there more to it?
:"Well, the piece can be improved even more through the use of resins-special elements that enhance the value and quality of your work. The improvement process is delicate, so be sure you've got enough tempers and a steady hand. Good luck!"

## Woodworking Stations
Woodworking stations can be found in towns, usually next to other crafting stations. At a woodworking station, you can refine raw materials, craft new items, deconstruct existing items to retrieve material components, improve the quality of an item by using resins, or research a trait by destroying an existing item. At a crafting station, your inventory is virtually merged with your bank, so you can use items from your inventory or your bank for any operation, but the materials from the inventory are used first.
