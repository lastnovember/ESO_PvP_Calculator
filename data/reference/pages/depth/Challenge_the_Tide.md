## Detailed Walkthrough
With the storage huts ablaze, Svanhildr requests one last thing from you. You must burn the Covenant banners to demoralize the Oathbound, then kill their leader, Gruznak the Impatient.

Burning the banners is a simple enough task. Climb the towers, kill the guard at the top of each tower, and burn the banner. After the banners are ruined, you need to find the war horn by the massive door and blow it to challenge and slay Gruznak. Gruznak is unhappy that you've come to challenge him. He jumps down from his position and engages you in combat. Once he's dead, escape the stronghold and find Svanhildr in the path to the north to receive your reward.
