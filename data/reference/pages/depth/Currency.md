# Online:Currency

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Currency
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Elder Scrolls Online has many types of Currency.

## Unrestricted Currency
The following currencies can be freely withdrawn or deposited into your account-wide bank. They do not take up inventory space.
- Gold - The main currency, used to pay for most services and trade with players
- Alliance Points - A currency earned for participation in the Alliance War and Battlegrounds
- Tel Var Stones - A currency earned and spent in the Imperial City
- Writ Vouchers - A currency accepted by Mastercraft Mediators, earned for completing Master Writs

## Restricted Currency
The following currencies are locked and account-wide. They do not take up inventory space. They cannot be deposited or withdrawn from the bank. Most items purchased with Crowns are able to be gifted to other players, but Crowns cannot be gifted directly.
- Archival Fortunes - A currency used in the Infinite Archive
- Crowns - A currency used in the Crown Store
- Crown Gems - A currency tied to Crown Crates
- Outfit Change Tokens - A currency used at Outfit Stations, usually purchased from the Gold Coast Bazaar
- Seals - A currency tied to the Crown Crate items and earned through the Tamriel Tome challenges, renamed from Seals of Endeavor
- Tome Points - A currency used with Tamriel Tomes to unlock pages for more rewards, earned by completing challenges.
- Cache of Tome Points - earned from bonuses of purchasing the premium version of a Tamriel Tome. A cache will contain 2,000
- Premium Tome Tokens - earned from the ESO Plus subscription, can be used to unlock the premium+bonus rewards for any Tamriel Tome.
- Transmute Crystals - A currency used to transmute the traits of equipment, earned from various activities (e.g. Undaunted pledges)
- Undaunted Keys - A currency used to purchase items from the Undaunted Pledge Masters
- Imperial Fragments - A currency used to purchase items from the Trophy Vaults vendor in the Imperial City
- Trade Bars - A currency used to purchase items from the Gold Coast Bazaar, replaced the Event Tickets upon release and cannot be purchased from Crown Store

### Service Tokens
Service Tokens are only visible on the character select screen and can only be used from there. They are usually purchased from the Crown Store
- Alliance Change Tokens - A token used to change the alliance your character is aligned to
- Appearance Change Tokens - A token used to change your character's appearance
- Name Change Tokens - A token used to change your character's name
- Race Change Tokens - A token used to change your character's race

## Currency Items
The following currencies exist as inventory items and can be deposited into the bank.
- Arena Gladiator's Proof - A currency used to buy special collectables from the War Researchers. One a day can be earned from Conquest daily quest in Cyrodiil.

- Battlemaster Token - A currency used to buy special collectables from the Battleground Supplies Merchants. One a day can be earned from Battlemaster Rivyn's quests.

- Siege of Cyrodiil Merit - A currency used to buy special collectables from the Tel Var General Merchants. One a day can be earned from Imperial City daily quests.

## Notes
- As of Update 44, the Key Fragment item currency was converted into the Imperial Fragments restricted currency.
- As of Update 23, the Undaunted Keys item currency was converted into the restricted currency. The conversions occurred after logging into characters which held them.
- As of Update 49:
- Event Tickets were converted into Trade Bars, 1 ticket = 100 bars.
- Seals of Endeavor were renamed to Seals, while using the same icon. Previously on March 2, 2026 the Endeavors system was disabled in preparation for the update.
