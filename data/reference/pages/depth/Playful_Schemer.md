# Online:Playful Schemer

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Playful_Schemer
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Playful Schemer is one of three class skill lines available to Ember. Abilities in this line focus on healing. It is roughly equivalent to the Daedric Summoning skill line for Sorcerer players.

## Skill Perks
The Playful Schemer skill line is immediately available to Ember, and is leveled up as she gains levels.
