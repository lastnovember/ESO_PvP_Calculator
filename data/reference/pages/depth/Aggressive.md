# Online:Aggressive

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Aggressive
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Aggressive is a trait which can be found on Companion weapons, armor and jewelry. The trait cannot be researched or crafted, and exists only on found loot. Items with the Aggressive trait increase the Damage done by a Companion by an amount dependent on the quality of the item.
