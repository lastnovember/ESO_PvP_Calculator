# Online:Huntmasters

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Huntmasters
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Huntmasters are a group primarily consisting of Altmer who venerate Hircine. Their leader, Lady Ghisiliane, hosts hunts to amuse the bored nobles of Senchal. They use not only exotic animals for the hunt, but mortals as well, and kidnap people for this purpose.
