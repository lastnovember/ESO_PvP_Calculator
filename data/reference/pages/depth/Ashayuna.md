Ashayuna is a Redguard Worm Cultist found at the Aldwilne Citadel.

## Related Quests
- Choice and Consequence

## Quest-Related Events
After infiltrating the citadel with Cariel, you meet with Prince Naemon, who gives you the task of capturing a suspected spy and bringing them to him. When you tell Cariel of this, she will have you pick out a cultist of similar build to her, so they can be subdued and dressed in her armor.

Ashayuna is one of the cultists that match the criteria, so you have the option of approaching her in one of the tents:
:"Dark Adept Vairisse only gives me as much work as I can handle. It's not my fault I can handle more than most."
:You need to come with me.
:"Why?"

If you get Ashayuna to follow you, she will become your follower, and you can lead her to the secluded place Cariel told you about. Speaking with Ashayuna during this:
:"Don't stop for me. You told me to follow you. I can't do that from the front."

When you arrive at the spot, you will find a body on the ground:
:Ashayuna: "A body? Who would do this?"
:
:Cariel: "Well, well. Let's take a look at who you picked."

Talking with Cariel, she will approve of your choice and has you plant evidence on Ashayuna, while she brews as potion to make Ashayuna temporarily mute. Afterward, you can wait for Cariel to dose and dress Ashayuna before she wakes up.

Speak with her once she is conscious:
:"I haven't felt this bad since we broke into the celebratory spirits. What happened?"
:Do you feel all right? Something hit your head really hard.
:"These clothes feel weird. They look weird. They're not my clothes. This really is like the last time I drank with the deathsingers. Wait. My voice. My throat. It doesn't feel right. Something's wrong with me. What's happening?"
:Come with me. I know someone who can help you.

Ashayuna will become your follower once more, if you try to talk to her while leading her to Prince Naemon's quarters:
:"The words. They won't come out."

Reaching the door to Naemon's Quarters:
:Ashayuna: "Please. Hurry."

Going down the stairs:
:Ashayuna: "Is it far?"

You can then speak with Prince Naemon and he will try interrogating her:
:Prince Naemon: "I am of the same royal line as your queen. Tell me your mission here."
:
:Prince Naemon: "Nothing? Silence protects no one. Search her. Glean what you can from her pockets."

Take the planted evidence from Ashayuna and present it to Naemon. He will be furious and his following reaction depends on if you previously killed the prince or not. If you did, then he will have you kill Ashayuna as a final test of loyalty. On the other hand, if you had not previously met Naemon till Solstice, he will deal with the spy himself.

Prince Naemon takes the Staff of Magnus and turns Ashayuna to stone:
:Prince Naemon: "You will serve as a warning for all who seek to undermine me."
