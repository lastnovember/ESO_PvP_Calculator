Devastator Varro is an Imperial eviscerator and member of the Order of the Waking Flame who can be found in the Imperial Sewers Cache Annex.

## Related Quests
- The Emperor's Secret

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Strike:A basic melee attack that does minor physical damage.
;Low Slash:A minor physical damage attack that snares the target. This can be blocked to negate the snare.
;Block:The enemy briefly blocks with their shield, reducing damage taken greatly. Targets who attempt melee heavy attacks on the enemy while they are blocking will be set off-balance.
;Dark Talons:The enemy roots all targets within range with claws that deal minor flame damage. (On most Eviscerators)
;Dragonknight Standard:The enemy calls down a fiery banner that plants down into the ground, with a large circular AoE, and causes persistent minor flame damage and applies Major Defile.
;Blink Strike:The enemy teleports towards their target and land hard on top of them, causing moderate physical damage and knocking them down. It can be blocked to set the enemy off-balance and negate the knockdown.

## Quest-Related Events
Devastator Varro can be found in the second chamber of the annex, where he will be trying to break the wards on a cache. When Varro sees you he will yell:
:Devastator Varro: "Another sacrifice for Lord Dagon!"

When he is dead you are free to break the ward on the chest and take the book, The Journal of Emperor Leovic.

## Bugs
Despite being elite rank, Devastator Varro is vulnerable to crowd control.
