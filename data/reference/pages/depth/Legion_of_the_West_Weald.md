# Online:Legion of the West Weald

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Legion_of_the_West_Weald
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Legion of the West Weald, also known as the West Weald Legion, are an Imperial Legion based in Colovia. Their primary mandate is to defend Skingrad and its surrounding territories.

In recent years however, a sizeable force led by General Lavinia Axius, splintered off and invaded the bordering Aldmeri Dominion lands. They annexed Arenthia Vale in Reaper's March and occupied the city of Arenthia.

In the process of liberating Arenthia, it is revealed that the legion is working on behalf of and under threat by the Stonefire Cult, which have grander plans for the region.
