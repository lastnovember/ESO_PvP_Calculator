Ebonthread is a medium height one-stem plant with a feathery black flower. It can be found in any zone by characters with Rank 5 in the Tailoring passive or character level 46 to 50. When harvested, a plant yields three to four samples of Raw Ebonthread, sometimes accompanied by crawlers or worms. Once ten samples of raw ebonthread are collected, they can be refined into seven to ten pieces of the processed material, Ebonthread, which is used for crafting pieces of light ebonthread armor at any clothing station. For you to be able to create ebonthread armor your Tailoring ability has to be rank 5.
