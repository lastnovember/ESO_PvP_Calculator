The Endling is a powerful chimera that can be a boss in the Infinite Archive. His original manifestation can be found in the Grove of the Chimera on High Isle.

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Strike:The Endling's Wamasu or Gryphon head will either bite or peck the target, dealing moderate Physical Damage.
;Heavy Attack:The Endling charges a heavy claw attack which deals moderate physical damage and staggers the target.
;Slam:The Endling gets on its back legs then slams the ground, dealing massive physical damage, knocking back, stunning and snaring targets in the area. Blocking this attack reduces the damage and only staggers.
;Lightning Bolt:The Endling casts a lightning bolt on a target, dealing high shock damage to it and nearby targets.
;Fire Breath:The Endling's lion head breaths fire in front of them in a cone that sweep the area, dealing continuous moderate Flame Damage and snaring targets inside.
;Hail Stones:The Endling's gryphon head screams and 3 icy whirlwind areas appear which roots first then deals continuous moderate Frost Damage and snares targets inside.
;Pounce:The Endling pounces and when it lands, it deals massive physical damage and knockbacks targets in the area.
;Summon Aspects: The Endling will summon Aspect of the Wamasu, Aspect of the Lion and Aspect of the Gryphon along with Firesong Magmatists.
