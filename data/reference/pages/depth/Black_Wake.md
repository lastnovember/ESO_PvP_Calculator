# Online:Black Wake

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Black_Wake
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Black Wake are a group of outlaws and exiles operating out of the Shimmerene Waterworks. They are led by Mirulon, who is working on behalf of the Court of Bedlam to assassinate Aicantar, the Sapiarch of Shimmerene.

They appear to be working in the smuggling and drug trades, as material related to both are found in ample numbers in their hideout.
