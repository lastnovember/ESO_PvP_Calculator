# Online:Spellblade

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Spellblade
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Spellblade is a class used by hostile humanoid NPCs in heavy armor that attack using both a two-handed weapon and magical attacks. While the most common user of this class are Xivilai, other NPCs also use it as well.

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Strike: A basic melee attack dealing low physical damage.
;Heavy Attack: The spellblade performs an overhead swing dealing moderate physical damage. This can be blocked to set the Spellblade off-balance.
;Hand of Flame: The spellblade sends three fire lines towards their target, indicated by moving red circles, dealing moderate flame damage. The fires split off a second time shortly afterward. This can be interrupted to set the spellblade off-balance.
;Lightning Grasp: The spellblade raise their hand and shoot a bolt of lightning which strikes five times in their order of apparition, as indicated by small red circles, dealing low shock damage each.
;Ice Cage:The spellblade summons a ring of frost around themselves, indicated by a donut-shaped red circle, dealing continuous low frost damage and snaring targets. This cannot be blocked.
;Summon Daedra: The spellblade summons a Daedra to fight for them. It can either be a Clannfear, Flame Atronach, Flame Ogrim, Spider Daedra, or a Winged Twilight. The daedra scales with the difficulty and location of the summoner. The daedra spawns with the summoner's current Health, for a maximum of 80% of their Max Health and have extreme damage resistance during the summoning animation. The summoner's Physical Resistance and Spell Resistance are reduced to 0 while a summon is active, as indicated by a blue glow on them. The daedra automatically dies if its summoner is defeated. (Not all spellblades)
;Empower Weapon: Frost:The Spellblade infuse their weapon with frost which makes "Quick Strike" and "Heavy Attack" apply a snaring effect (Frost Edge). (Maybe only on some spellblades)
