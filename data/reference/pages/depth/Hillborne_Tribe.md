# Online:Hillborne Tribe

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Hillborne_Tribe
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Hillborne Tribe is a tribe of goblins found in Breagha-Fin. Most of them were originally Nords from an exiled clan in Skyrim that followed their leader Malofar, until he betrayed them by transforming and controlling them with the power of the Emerald Chalice to turn into his own personal obedient army.
