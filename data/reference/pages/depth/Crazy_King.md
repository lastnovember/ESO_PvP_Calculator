# Online:Crazy King

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Crazy_King
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Crazy King is a Battlegrounds game mode falling under Land Grab. Its objective is to secure capture points to earn 500 points for one's team. These Capture points, which spawn and respawn in different locations periodically, are captured by players who stand close to them. The time limit is 15 minutes. If no team reaches 500 points, the team with the most points win.

The first capture point will spawn when the game starts, and will despawn and respawn in different locations several times before the next spawns. As with the first, the second capture point will despawn and respawn several times before the third capture point spawns. Late in the game on 4v4v4 maps, the fourth capture point will spawn too. While capture points are under the control of a team, it will earn 4 points for that team for every 'tick' that elapses. As each capture point approaches the end of its tenure in one location, flashing pillars will surround it, first yellow, but red in the final seconds of the point's existence. When the point respawns at a nearby location, its team affiliation will be removed, and it will be ready to be captured again. Flags move every minute.

On the 4v4 maps, there are 3 capture points. The first spawns when the game starts, the second spawns when there are 10 minutes lefts and the third where there are 5 minutes left.

The Tutorials section of the Help menu doesn't show the "Blessed Assistance" medal, and you can only get one of the two per healing hit, with "Helping Hand" having priority.
You do not need to stand on the said point yourself.
Note: The required numbers to get medals can be a little off, as for example you can still get "Blessed Assistance" for a healing hit of +10,000 instead of "Helping Hand" (tested for up to 10,089).

## Notes
- As a land grab Battleground, Crazy King games have the potential to net Fire Drake Style style pages.
- A popular stratagem among players is to move from one capture point to another as soon as one is captured, as the transient nature of the capture points often means that expending resources and time fighting over capture points can waste time and cause your team to lose the opportunity to take other capture points.
