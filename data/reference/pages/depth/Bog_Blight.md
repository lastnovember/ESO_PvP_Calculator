Bog Blights are Argonian zombies that can be found in swamps and bodies of water which contain grave-stakes throughout Norg-Tzel and Murkmire.

In appearance, they are near-nude and wear ancient Argonian masks. One of the functions of the grave-stake is to pin down an Argonian's corpse so it does not rise to the surface, however, when the stake is removed, a bog blight may wake up. The taking of grave-stakes as curiosities has led to many an unwary treasure hunter inadvertently waking them up, though strong smells such as rotting meat can mask someone's scent from a bog blight.

In Norg-Tzel, they are accompanied by Swarming Zombies and at first resemble any other zombie. However, when they are alerted, they will attempt to rush you and explode.

## Skills and Abilities
;Swipe: A basic melee attack dealing low physical damage.
;Exuding Flames: When the bog blight takes damage, it throws two globs of an inflammable substance, one to its left and one to its right, each creating a small circle area dealing continuous direct low flame damage. The Bog Blight can create new areas only after the previous ones disappear.
;Combustion: Rarely, the bog blight begins to glow and grow larger in size, before exploding, dealing high flame damage and leaving a large pool of lingering fire on the ground dealing continuous moderate flame damage. Both of these are indicated by a large circular AoE. Bog blights that appear half-buried in the ground will use this ability right away when starting combat with them.

### Ruins of Tuniria specific
The Bog Blights at the Ruins of Tuniria behave differently by having only one ability, being taunted to a random player and by being immune to taunts.
;Bog Blight's Burst: When getting close to the taunted player, the Bog Blight bursts, dealing very high magic damage in a small area and launching blood projectile to three other targets, dealing very high magic damage.
