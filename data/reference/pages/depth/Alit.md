Alits are ferocious animals, closely related to kagouti, that are native to Morrowind. They can also be found in Shadowfen, Grahtwood, Khenarthi's Roost and Northern Elsweyr. Alits are perhaps best described as "mouths on legs" by Bolga gra-Bur in her book.

## Skills and Abilities
;Bite: A basic melee attack that deals minor physical damage.
;Lacerate/Laceration: The alit jumps up and slashes their target with its feet, dealing moderate physical damage, staggering and applying the "Laceration" effect dealing minor bleed damage over time. Blocking sets the alit off-balance and prevents the stagger and the "Laceration" effect from being applied.
;Surround:The alit positions itself on a different side from the target.
;Block:The alit blocks with its head, reducing damage taken. Perform a melee heavy attack while it is blocking staggers and puts its target off-balance.
;Backstabber:The alit has a passive ability that causes it to deal 20% more damage when attacking the target's back.

## Notes
- The alits found in Elden Hollow I are considerably larger than those usually found elsewhere.
- Alits on Vvardenfell and in Northern Elsweyr have a different coloration, appearing predominantly brown with some green. Another brown alit appears in the Telvanni Peninsula during a random encounter.
