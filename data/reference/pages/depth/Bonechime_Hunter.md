Bonechime Hunter are Khajiit fighters and members of the Bonechime Gang, poachers who have recently moved into the Prowl and have been generally disruptive.

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Strike:A basic melee attack that causes minor physical damage.
;Low Slash:The enemy slashes the player's shins, dealing moderate physical damage and snaring the player Blocking will reduce the damage, but doesn't prevent the snare.
;Basilisk Powder:The enemy throws poison in a conical AoE in front of itself. Players hit by the powder will take minor poison damage and get stunned.
;Pin: When standing further away, the enemy shoots at you with a glowing crossbow. If the bolt hits, you are briefly immobilized.
;Twirling Lunge: When standing further away, the enemy holds their dual weapons in a rear-handed stance, with a yellow glow visible around their weapons, then dash forward and strike their target, dealing moderate physical damage and knocking the target back. This is almost always used after Pin lands successfully.
;Roll Dodge:The enemy can perform a roll dodge to avoid incoming damage.

## Bugs
While Bounty Hunter enemies normally have dual weapons, Bonechime Hunters only have one sword, which causes the animation of the poison throw attack to fail to play.
