The Corrupt Sentinel is a red Lurcher found in Sunscale Ruins. It walks back and forth through the green hallway, which you must use Nature's Balm to pass through safely.

## Related Quests
- The Fangs of Sithis

## Skills and Abilities
;Claw: A basic melee attack dealing minor physical damage.
;Pulverize: The Corrupt Sentinel quickly shoves its target, dealing moderate physical damage as well as staggering its target.
;Crushing Limbs: The Corrupt Sentinel slams its fist on the ground, dealing moderate physical damage as well as stunning its target for 1 second. This can be blocked to set the Corrupt Sentinel off-balance instead.
;Choking Pollen: The Corrupt Sentinel releases a cloud of poisonous spores around itself, indicated by a red circle, dealing continuous direct low poison damage. This can be interrupted to set the Corrupt Sentinel off-balance.

## Bugs
Despite being an elite rank enemy, the Corrupt Sentinel is vulnerable to crowd control.
