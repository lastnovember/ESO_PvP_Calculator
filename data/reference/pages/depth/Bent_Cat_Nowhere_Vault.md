Bent Cats are Khajiit dro-m'Athra that can be encountered in the Twilight Maze and Eclipsed Hall challenge rooms of the Nowhere Vault.

In the Twilight Maze, they will be one of the hostile beings you will encounter in the maze. Fighting with them can disrupt the Twilight Cantors song due to noise. Meanwhile in the Eclipsed Hall, they will relentlessly attack any Lanterns trying to reach the gate. They will need to be dealt with to complete the hall's challenge.
