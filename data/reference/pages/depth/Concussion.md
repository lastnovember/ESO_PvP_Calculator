# Online:Concussion

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Concussion
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Shock Ranged Concussion is a debilitating effect applied by Shock Damage. It deals a single Shock Damage tick and makes targets more vulnerable to certain subsequent effects, such as Wall of Storms setting Concussed enemies Off Balance. Applying Concussion to an enemy you have recently applied Concussion to adds 15% extra damage. Concussion also applies Minor Vulnerability for 4 seconds. The status effect can be removed with a Cleansing effect.
