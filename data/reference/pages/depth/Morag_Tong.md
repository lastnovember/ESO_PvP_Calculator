The Morag Tong

The Morag Tong are a Morrowind-based group of assassins who have recently been outlawed. They have an old rivalry with the Dark Brotherhood, originally a splinter organization of the Tong.

The Morag Tong features prominently in the Deshaan zone questline and the Balmora questline in the Morrowind chapter. You can complete quests for them, but the faction itself cannot be joined.
