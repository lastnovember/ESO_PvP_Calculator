Bangkorai Adventurer is awarded for completing 36 quests in Bangkorai. This includes both the Bangkorai Main Quest as well as any side-quests. It does not include Crafting Writs, Fighters Guild or Mages Guild quests, or pledges for the Undaunted.plain This achievement will allow you to purchase the Forsaken Stronghold near Lakewatch Tower for 1,285,000, and you can purchase a Glenmoril Wyrd Stone for your house. It costs 4,000 and can be purchased from Lozotusk in Evermore.

## Quests
Note that despite taking place in Bangkorai, Claim to Fame does not count towards this achievement.
