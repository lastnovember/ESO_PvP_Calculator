Bloodroot Guardians are spriggans who fight to defend the strangler Bloodroot in Toothmaul Gully. They must all be defeated to awaken Bloodroot.

## Skills and Abilities
;Bloodroot Link:All Bloodroot Guardians are constantly channeling a spell to Bloodroot, making them unable to attack unless you interrupt them which sets them off balance. Once interrupted, a Bloodroot Guardian will not cast "Bloodroot Link" again.
;Lacerate: A basic melee attack that deals minor physical damage.
;Nature's Swarm: The Bloodroot Guardian sends a swarm of insects forward, indicated by a red cone, dealing moderate magic damage.
;Summon Beast: The Bloodroot Guardian summons an animal to assist it in combat. The animal type depend on the stats and location of the summoner. They can summon ambush beetles, dire wolves, durzogs, great bears, senche-tigers, Spiders thunderbug lords, venomspit spiders, websnare spiders or wolves. The summons can only have a maximum of 80% of the Spriggan's Max Health and have extreme damage resistance during the summoning animation. Spriggans take 20% more damage while a summon is active as indicated by a blue glow on them. (Bugged, see bugs)
