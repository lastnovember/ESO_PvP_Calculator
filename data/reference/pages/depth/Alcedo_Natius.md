## Quest-Related Events
After the plan to trap the Koldane Cartel went horribly wrong, you and Edwane the Hawk need to enter the Smuggler Caves so you can rescue Seline Menant and Captain Sansonne. To get into the caves, you are told to find Alcedo and take Rols Boron's Key to the Lower Store Rooms from him.

When Alcedo sees you he shouts:
:Alcedo Natius: "You little pests think you'll get past me? Ha!"
