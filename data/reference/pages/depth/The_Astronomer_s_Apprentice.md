:And cultists are stealing these memories?

### Restore the Three Primary Stars
There are three wings in the building, each wing representing a constellation: the Thief (east wing), Mage (north wing), and Warrior (west wing). Each wing also has 3 floors: Lower, Upper (which is actually the middle), and finally Hall. For example:
- Lower Thief's Wing
- Upper Thief's Wing
- Hall of the Thief

From the central area on the ground floor head to either the Thief or Warrior wing (the Mage wing doesn't have access to the middle floor). Go to the lifts near the outer edge of the building and take it to the Upper Wing. From this middle floor now move to the inner lift and take it to the Hall floor. You can access the elevator to the Mage's Hall from the corridor connecting the Thief and Warrior wings on the upper level.

In the central place of the top floor you will see three telescopes, each on different platforms. Amili will be present, after a while, and will explain that you should look through the telescope to see where the gaps in the constellation are. You will also notice a pattern on the floor matching the constellation, and two floating stars. This is a puzzle wherein you guide the errant floating stars to their location on the floor.

To solve the puzzle, go to the location on the floor where there was a gap when you looked through the telescope, and select the prompt to guide the star there. Wait for it to move into place before attempting to guide the second star; if you make a mistake, leaving and re-entering the Hall will reset the puzzle. Looking through the telescope first gives a clear image of the star pattern, but afterwards going to the far side of the constellation pattern on the floor from the telescope and then looking toward the telescope and up you can see the same image in the sky unmagnified. Thus making it easier to review what stars are missing.

Once both stars on a platform have been guided to the correct spot on the floor you'll see a memory fragment. The first is of Sotha Sil speaking with the Astronomer, informing him that he must be willing to take on this task. The second is again of Sotha Sil speaking with the Astronomer, informing him that he will teach him the spell for his successor. The third is of the Astronomer saying to Sotha Sil that he is willing to sacrifice everything.

In the main hall, you, Amili and the Astronomer will watch the complete memory and discover that the man who became the current Astronomer is willing to sacrifice everything for succession, even his own memories. Amili is horrified about what this means for her and runs off. The Astronomer will ask you to talk to her. After speaking with her near the entrance, you can convince her to follow through her duties and sacrifice her memories, or refuse and face whatever consequences are in store for her.

### The Choice

If you convince Amili to give up her memories and become the Astronomer, she will return to the Planisphere for the succession ritual:
:Amili Lloryn: "I've decided. I'll become the next Astronomer. I give myself willingly, to serve the Chrononymic Will as it has seen fit."
:The Astronomer: "So be it. I'll start the process now. Take care of the Planisphere... and yourself as well, Amili."
:Amili Lloryn: "You remembered my... augh!"
:The Astronomer: "I can finally rest."

The Astronomer will turn to dust, and Amili Lloryn's nameplate changes to The Astronomer:
:The Astronomer: "What was I... oh, a visitor."

After witnessing the succession, you can talk to her to complete the quest:
:"The Mnemonic Planisphere welcomes you, traveler. Due to the recent attack, we are not holding memory viewings. You'll have to return at another time. How strange, I feel a sense of gratitude towards you, though I'm not sure why."
:I helped put missing stars back into the Planisphere's sky.
:"Ah, so you have? You have the gratitude of the Clockwork Apostles."
:"Now, if you'll excuse me, I must inspect the memories to see if they were damaged by the attack. You would be surprised how fragile they can be."
She will award you with The Astronomer's Orbit and some gold,

If you convince Amili to keep her memories, she will return to the Planisphere to tell The Astronomer:
:Amili Lloryn: "I'm sorry, Astronomer. I just can't do it. I just can't give up who I am."
:Amili Lloryn: "I can better serve Lord Seht as myself. And I'll find someone to take my place, I swear it. Someone willing."
:The Astronomer: "So be it. You have made your choice, apostle. And now you will bear the consequence."
:The Astronomer: "You will be shunned from our order. Cast out, until the time which you can undo the wrong you have begot upon your people. Until you have brought me my successor."
:Amili Lloryn: "I... I'll do it. I'll accept this fate."
:The Astronomer: "May Sotha Sil watch over your path, for I can no longer guide you."

Amili will then leave the Planisphere and you can talk to The Astronomer to complete the quest:
:"What is it that you want?"'
:Will you be alright?
:"I have maintained this position for over a millennium. It is my sole purpose, the path given to me by the Divine Metronome. I have no regrets."
:"I thank you for your efforts. A reward is common for these situations, yes? Here you are."
He will award you with The Astronomer's Orbit and some gold.

## Notes
- The Astronomer won't exist inside the complex before starting the quest.
- The quest item icon for the Star-Scooper depicts a crystal rather than something that actually looks somewhat like the device you use when retrieving the star.
- The stars apply the same effect as lantern-bearing patrollers in trespassing areas throughout the game when near them, preventing you from sneaking.
- This doesn't apply to those who are fixed in place.
- Stars reveal hidden Whispering Cultists when they pass over them.
- During the objective to give the Star-Scooper back, a "Talk" prompt appears when interacting with the Astronomer. However, no dialogue occurs when using this prompt and it leads to short scene instead, and the dialogue occurs after this, once the prompt is used again.
- During the objective to speak with the Astronomer after giving the stars, the objective description in the Journal states that you must speak with Amili when you must instead speak to the Astronomer.
- Afadi, Chath-Jaa and Zaimira won't exist in the upper level of the complex before starting the quest or reaching the related objective.
- If you convinced Amili to keep her memories she will take up residence in the Slag Town Outlaws Refuge after the quest, where she will have additional, optional, dialogue.
