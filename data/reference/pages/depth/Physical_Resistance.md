# Online:Physical Resistance

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Physical_Resistance
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Physical Resistance is an attribute which protects you from all forms of Martial Damage, including Bleed Damage, Disease Damage, Physical Damage, and Poison Damage. It caps at 33,000 for players and 25,000 for NPCs which reduces all forms of martial damage taken by 50%
