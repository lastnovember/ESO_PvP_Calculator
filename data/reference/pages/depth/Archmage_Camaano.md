Archmage Camaano is an undead High Elf and leader of the Veiled Heritance forces found in the ruins of Naril Nagaia. Camaano has a chance to drop Archmage 's Mace of the Beekeeper, a unique item in the Beekeeper's Gear item set.

He is accompanied by two random heritance members from the delve in battle. He used the knowledge within Mercano's Journal to transform himself into an undead monstrosity.

During the related Undaunted daily quest, you will be tasked with harvesting a Heritance Liver from the archmage's corpse.

## Related Quests
- Culinary Justice in Greenshade

## Skills and Abilities
;Coiled Lash: A basic ranged attack dealing minor magic damage.
;Quick Strike: A basic melee attack dealing minor physical damage.
;Summon Aura of Protection: Archmage Camaano summons an Aura of Protection totem that reduces the damage taken of nearby allies by 25%. The totem can be destroyed in five hits from any source or one heavy attack. Destroying the totem will stun all enemies nearby and it bypasses crowd control immunities, excepted those of rank.
;Summon Abomination:Archmage Camaano summons a flesh atronach, bone colossus or ghost to fight, spawning with Archmage Camaano's current Health. Archmage Camaano's Physical Resistance and Spell Resistance are reduced to 0 while a summon is active. The summon will die if Archmage Camaano is killed.
;Bone Surge:Archmage Camaano casts a Bone Shield-like ability on himself which reduces his melee damage taken by 20% until this disappears.
;Run Away!:When too close to him, Archmage Camaano backs up to put some distance between their target and themselves.
;Bone Cage:Archmage Camaano casts a rune on the ground that activates after a while, dealing very low magic damage and rooting players who step in it. This can be interrupted to knock Archmage Camaano off balance.

## Bugs
Despite being an elite rank enemy, Archmage Camaano is vulnerable to crowd control.
