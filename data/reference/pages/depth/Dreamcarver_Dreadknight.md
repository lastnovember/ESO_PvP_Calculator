Dreamcarver Dreadknights are Dremora members of the Dreamcarver Clan who can be found in Bastion Nymic.

## Related Quests
- Bastion Nymic - Fallen Hues
- Bastion Nymic - Glasscrag
- Bastion Nymic - Writhing Wastes

## Skills and Abilities
As champion rank enemies, Dreamcarver Dreadknights are immune to crowd control.
;Quick Strike:Basic melee attack dealing moderate physical damage.
;Heavy Attack: Dreamcarver Dreadknight performs an overhead swing dealing moderate-high physical damage. This can be blocked to set the Dreamcarver Dreadknight off-balance.
;Hand of Flame: The Dreamcarver Dreadknight sends three fire lines towards their target, indicated by moving red circles, dealing high flame damage. The fires split off a second time shortly afterward. This can be interrupted to set the Dreamcarver Dreadknight off-balance.
;Lightning Grasp: The Dreamcarver Dreadknight raise their hand and shoot a bolt of lightning which strikes five times in their order of apparition, as indicated by small red circles, dealing moderate shock damage each.
;Teleport Strike:When far from them, the Dreamcarver Dreadknight teleports and stabs the target, dealing moderate magic damage and snaring the target briefly.
;Shadeblast:The Dreamcarver Dreadknight slowly sweeps its right hand in an arc in front of it, launching five areas one after the other, each area hitting you deals moderate magic damage, snares, and creates a ground area dealing moderate magic damage over time.

## Bugs
The Dreamcarver Dreadknights do not have the Dremora voice modifier, and so have human voices.
