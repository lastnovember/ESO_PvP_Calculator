Dresuu Var is a Celestial daedroth summoned inside Skyreach Pinnacle. It appears at the end of the first wave of enemies, consisting of Celestial Scamps and Crocodiles. After it appears, Celestial Scamps periodically spawn to fight for it.

## Skills and Abilities
Dresuu Var uses some of the abilities of standard daedroth, as well as one additional unique ability:
;Jagged Claw: A basic melee attack that deals moderate physical damage.
;Fiery Jaws: A basic attack that deals moderate flame damage.
;Fiery Breath:Dresuu Var breathes fire, indicated by a red cone, dealing continuous minor flame damage.
;Ignite Flame: Dresuu Var breathes blue flame at your location rapidly, forming a daedric rune beneath your feet, indicated by a circular AoE, dealing continuous high flame damage. The damage application of the AoE kicks in almost before the AoE is visible. Dodging the attack negates the AoE application and ensuing damage. Blocking reduces the damage taken.

## Bugs
Despite being a grounded area of effect ability, roll-dodging and blocking inside "Ignite Flame" negates and reduces the damage taken when it shouldn't.
