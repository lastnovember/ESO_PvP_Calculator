# Online:Items

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Items
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Elder Scrolls Online is full of items both magical and mundane. The articles listed here provide information on the items in the game. For the game systems which manage items, see Inventory and Collections.

## Finding Items
Depending upon whether you are looking for a specific item or just browsing for general information, there are several different ways to navigate through the many item-related pages.

### Search
If you know the exact name of the specific item you want to find, type its name into the search box at the left-hand side of the page and then click on the magnifying glass image or press the Enter key on your keyboard.

### Categories
Within the category Online-Items are many subcategories listing nearly every item in the game by function. If you don't know the exact spelling of a specific item, browsing through one of the categories may help you to find it.

### General Articles on Items
Most of these articles provide general information on various types of items, and also include lists of the mundane (non-enchanted and widely available) varieties of the items.
- Alchemy Ingredients: A complete list of alchemical solvents and reagents
- Armor: Base unenchanted armor

- Blacksmithing Materials: A complete list of ores, ingots, and tempers
- Books: All books in the game
- Clothing Materials: A complete list of cloths, leathers, and tannins
- Contraband: Stolen items which exist only to sell to a Fence
- Contraband Clothing: Stolen clothes, which can be worn or sold (only to Fences)
- Crafting Motifs: A list of all crafting motifs and their associated styles
- Currency: The types of currencies in the game
- Disguises: Cosmetic outfits which cause certain enemies to become passive
- Drinks: Brewed beverages that provide temporary benefits
- Food: Cooked meals that can provide beneficial effects
- Furnishings: All the Furnishings available in Homestead
- Furnishing Materials: A complete list of primary furnishing materials
- Glyphs: A list of all glyphs that can be used for enchanting
- Jewelry: Base unenchanted clothing and jewelry
- Jewelry Crafting Materials: A complete list of dust, ounces, grains, and platings
- Potions: All potions and poisons
- Quest Items: All quest items found in-game that cannot, for at least a time, be removed from your inventory
- Provisioning Ingredients: All ingredients needed to cook food and brew drinks
- Recipes: All recipes for cooking food and brewing
- Runestones: All runestones for crafting glyphs
- Sets: All item sets and where to find them
- Shields: Base unenchanted shields
- Soul Gems: All soul gems in the game and how to use them
- Style Materials: A list of materials that determine the style of crafted equipment
- Tools: Includes gems, minor items, etc.
- Trophies: Special rewards from certain quests and activities
- Treasure Maps: Clues to hidden treasure stashes
- Survey Reports: Maps to high-yield crafting nodes
- Weapons: Base unenchanted weapons
- Woodworking Materials: A complete list of logs, sanded wood, and resins

- Collections: Account-wide cosmetic and convenience items, stored in a separate interface which do not take up inventory space
- Assistants: Summonable NPCs that offer specific Services
- Costumes: Cosmetic outfits with no effect on gameplay
- Mementos: Quest reward items, which can be activated to produce a short-lived cosmetic effect
- Mounts: Ridable creatures for quickly traveling across Tamriel
- Pets: All vanity creatures that can follow the player

### Creating Custom Items
- Alchemy: Create your own custom potions.
- Blacksmithing: Create and improve metal weapons and heavy armor.
- Clothing: Create and improve light and medium armor.
- Enchanting: Create glyphs to enchant weapons, armor, and jewelry.
- Jewelry Crafting: Create and improve rings and necklaces.
- Provisioning: Cook food and brew drink at a cooking fire.
- Woodworking: Create and improve staves, shields, and bows.

- Armor Dyes: Unlock new dye colors for your armor based on your achievements.
