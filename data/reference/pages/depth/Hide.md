Hide is a crafting material obtained from animals in any zone by characters with Rank 2 in the Tailoring passive or levels 16 to 24. One animal may yield one piece of the raw material, Hide Scraps. Once ten scrap samples are collected, they can be refined into seven to ten pieces of the processed material, Hide. Refined hide is used for making medium hide armor at any of the clothing stations. To create hide armor you need to have rank 2 in Tailoring.
