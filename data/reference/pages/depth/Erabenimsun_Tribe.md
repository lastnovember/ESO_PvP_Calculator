# Online:Erabenimsun Tribe

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Erabenimsun_Tribe
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Erabenimsun Tribe is a tribe of Ashlanders living in Vvardenfell. Ashlander Tribes and Customs describes them as a war-loving tribe, heavily armed, and valuing strength above the lore and history that the other tribes value. The Erabenimsun notably was the first to tame wild nix-oxen.
