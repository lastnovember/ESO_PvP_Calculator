Several DLC packs are currently available, while a number of other content updates have been teased. ESO Plus members have free access to all DLC packs for the duration of their membership, and so do not need to pay for them with Crowns. However, if their ESO Plus membership expires, access to DLC is lost (aside from any items obtained) unless the packs are purchased separately or ESO Plus is renewed. "Collector's Edition" DLC bundles last for one update cycle, and are retired following the release of the next major update or DLC pack.
