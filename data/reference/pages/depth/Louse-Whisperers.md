# Online:Louse-Whisperers

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Louse-Whisperers
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Louse-Whisperers are a Maormer pirate clan that sail in the waters of the Abecean Sea. They have a feared reputation and are said to be barely tolerated even by other Maormer fleets. Their name comes from the parasitical sea lice they breed, and clan members go as far to willingly self-mutilate by having the parasites attach to their tongues.

They are encountered during the High Seas of Tamriel event, while sailing aboard the Riotous Redress.
