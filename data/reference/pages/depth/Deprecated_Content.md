# Online:Deprecated Content

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Deprecated_Content
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Deprecated Content is content that has been removed from Elder Scrolls Online. Removals usually happen during large updates and are sometimes mentioned in patch notes. A few examples include:

- Battle Leveling
- Daily Rewards
- Dye Stations (replaced by outfit stations)
- Endeavors
- Hireling Chests
- Jewelry before Update 18
- Mercenary Contracts
- Subzones
- Undaunted Chests
- The old requirements for Clothier, Woodworker and Blacksmithing Writs
