## Notes
- The Direnni Sage Fledgling Gryphon matches this mount.
- Urli confirms it is a female quasigriff.
- Its default name, Finullie, is likely a pun on the word "finally". Similarly, the name of Voernet's most dedicated assistant, Urli, is likely a pun on the word "early".
