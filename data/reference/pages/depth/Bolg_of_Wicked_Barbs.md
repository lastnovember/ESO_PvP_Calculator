Bolg of Wicked Barbs is an Orc spirit and an ancient member of the Oathsworn. He is the second of the three secret bosses in Oathsworn Pit.

## Related Quests
- Victory Through Strife
- Pledge: Oathsworn Pit

## Skills and Abilities
;Shot: A basic ranged attack dealing moderate physical damage.
;Arrow Stab: A basic melee attack dealing moderate physical damage.
;Barbed Rain: Bolg fires a volley of flaming arrows, which falls back down while tracking a target, indicated by a red circle, dealing continuous high flame damage.
;Summon Spirits: Bolg stomps the ground, summoning a Spirit of Conquest. It slowly travels to a marked brazier. If allowed to reach it, the brazier is claimed by Bolg. You can claim braziers of your own by using a synergy. If Bolg has more claimed braziers than you when he is near death, he heals up and the fight continues (Fiery Resilience).
;Summon Archers: Bolg teleports across the arena. When he does, two Spectral Archers are summoned, one on each side of the arena.
