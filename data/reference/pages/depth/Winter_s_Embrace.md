# Online:Winter's Embrace

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Winter's_Embrace
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Winter's Embrace is one of three class skill lines available to Wardens. It can be learned by other classes via subclassing. Abilities in this line focus on dealing frost damage, and shielding you and your group from damage.

## Skill Perks
The Winter's Embrace skill line is immediately available to all Wardens, and is leveled up by earning experience while Winter's Embrace skills are slotted. Skills include:
