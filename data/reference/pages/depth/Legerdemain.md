# Online:Legerdemain

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Legerdemain
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Legerdemain is a skill line available to all players. It becomes available once you successfully pickpocket an NPC, steal an item, pick a lock, or Fence/Launder stolen goods at an Outlaws Refuge. Legerdemain abilities focus on improving your ability to steal and fence items as part of the Justice System. The max level is 20.

## Notes
- A good place to level up your skill is the Necrom Underways. You can steal with impunity (no guards or NPCs), and there is an Outlaw Refuge there as well. The urns also refill quickly.

## Advancement
Advancement through the Legerdemain skill is earned by the following actions:
- Fencing/Laundering items: 1 point per item
- Lockpicking: Simple locks: 1, Intermediate locks: 2, Advanced locks: 4, Master locks: 8
- Pickpocketing: Easy targets: 1, Medium targets: 2, Hard targets: 4
