Aeonstone Conduits are large Aeonstone formations that were erected on the floating island of Dragonhold by Kaalgrontiid as focus points for his amassing power.

They must be destroyed during the related quest. They cannot be harmed directly. The Mask of Alkosh must be powered up by slaying Dark Aeon enemies that are summoned to defend the conduits, to gain access and use the Draconic Power synergy, which calls in Nahfahlaar which burns down and destroys the conduits, and the Dark Aeon Sorcerers guarding them.

## Bugs
