# Online:Voidstalker

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Voidstalker
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Voidstalkers are a class of hostile humanoid NPCs that attack using melee abilities. They are usually equipped with dual axes.

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Strike:Basic melee attack dealing minor physical damage.
;Veiled Strike:The Voidstalker cloaks, becoming untargetable by most attacks, and prepares to slash the target, dealing moderate magic damage and stunning the target for 2 seconds. This can be blocked to set the Voidstalker off balance instead.
;Teleport Strike:When standing further away from them, the Voidstalker teleports and stabs the target, dealing moderate magic damage and snaring for 2 seconds. Blocking does not negate the snare.
;Roll Dodge:When performing a melee heavy attack on the voidstalker, they can roll dodge to avoid attacks.
;Blood Craze: The voidstalker slashes the target twice, dealing moderate physical damage twice then very minor bleed damage every two seconds over 8 seconds to the target. The bleeding also heals the voidstalker for around a quarter of the damage done. Blocking the first hit negates the bleeding, but blocking the second one does not.
;Agony: The voidstalker channels a spell dealing moderate magic damage and stunning the target for 2 seconds. Taking damage from any source will break the stun, but the target may choose to break free from it instead. After the stun ends, this spell also deals minor magic damage every second over 6 seconds to the target. This can be interrupted to set the voidstalker off balance instead. Blocking will not prevent the damage over time.
;Whirlwind: The voidstalker spins around, doing moderate physical damage to all targets as indicated by a red circle.
;Assassinate: The voidstalker strikes at the target's back for moderate physical damage. This attack can only be performed if the voidstalker is behind the target, and thus voidstalkers will often use "Passing Through" to get behind the target. Ensure your character is facing the voidstalker at all times to prevent this.
;Passing Through:The voidstalker jumps above the target to get behind them.
;Soul Tether: The voidstalker jumps and casts a rune on the ground. Any targets caught in the AoE attack will take moderate magic damage and be stunned for 4 seconds then minor magic damage over time which heals the voidstalker for the same amount.
;Backstabber:The voidstalker has a passive ability that causes them to deal 20% more damage when attacking the target's back. (On some voidstalkers only)
