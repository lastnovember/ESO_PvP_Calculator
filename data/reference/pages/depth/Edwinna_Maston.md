Edwinna Maston is a Breton magus of the Mages Guild who can be found at the guild branch in Gonfalon Bay. She keeps her store on the first floor, on the far side from the main entrance.

## Dialogue
Speaking to her will have her make esoteric remarks:
:"Fiery blood we can't contain. Black pressure, deep and angry. Old chains working loose. The mountain stirs-I can feel it. Apologies. I was lost in reverie. How may I help you?"

:"The words twist inside out and gnaw at my mind. What do they mean? What do they-ah. Sorry, sorry. I am vexed by a puzzle. Now, what may I help you with?"

## Bugs
A faint red glow appears around her hands, although she isn't casting any spell that would create this effect, both in and outside of dialogue.
