A Ceythalmor Operative can be found dead in Hyacinth's Greenhouse in Greenshade. They were part of the Ceythalmor force sent to capture Hyacinth, but were killed by a ballista bolt and pinned to a bookcase. On their body, you find the Ceythalmor Mission Orders.
