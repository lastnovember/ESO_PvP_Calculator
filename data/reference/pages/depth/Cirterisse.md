Cirterisse is a High Elf necromancer found at the end of Aba-Loria. She must be defeated to clear the delve. She has a chance to drop Cirterisse's Vampiric Staff. A weak Bone Colossus accompanies her.

## Skills and Abilities
Cirterisse has a Bone Colossus with her at the start of combat, you may want to kill it first to make the fight easier as it has very little health.
;Coiled Lash: A basic ranged attack that does minor magic damage.
;Quick Strike: A basic melee attack that does minor physical damage.
;Summon Aura of Protection: Cirterisse summons an Aura of Protection totem that gives nearby allies increased resistance to damage. The totem can be destroyed in five hits from any source or one heavy attack. Destroying the totem will stun all enemies nearby and it bypasses crowd control immunities, excepted those of rank. (See Bugs)
;Summon the Dead:Cirterisse often begins combat by summoning a zombie, a bloodfiend, a bloodfiend reaver, a Bone Reaver or a Bone Flayer. If the summoned creature killed, another one will be summoned to replace it. The summon scales with the difficulty, faction, and location of the summoner. The summoned creature will have around the same health as the current health of their summoner but only to a maximum of 80% of the summoner's Health, and have extreme damage resistance during the summoning animation. Cirterisse takes 20% more damage while a summon is active.
;Bone Surge:Cirterisse casts a Bone Shield-like ability on herself which reduces her melee damage taken by 20% until this disappears.
;Run Away!:When too close to her, Cirterisse backs up to put some distance between her target and herself.

## Bugs
Despite being an elite rank enemy, Cirterisse is vulnerable to crowd control.
Her "Aura of Protection" is bugged and does not reduce the damage taken of enemies while in its area, nor destroying it stuns or puts enemies off-balance.
