Oak logs are brown logs with a small amount moss and white flowers growing on them. They can be found in any zone by players with Woodworking Rank 2 or levels 16 to 24. A log can be harvested for three to four samples of the raw material, Rough Oak. Once ten samples of the rough wood are collected, they can be refined into seven to ten pieces of Sanded Oak. Oak staves and bows, as well as oak shields, are crafted from the refined oak wood at any of the woodworking stations. In order for you to create oak gear your Woodworking ability has to be rank 2.
