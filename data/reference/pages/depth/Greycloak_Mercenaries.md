# Online:Greycloak Mercenaries

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Greycloak_Mercenaries
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Greycloak Mercenaries are a group of bandits dealing in illicit activities all over Craglorn. They have their headquarters in Haddock's Market and Hircine's Haunt but are also found at their camps all over the region. The bandits inhabiting the haunt are at varying stages of Lycanthropy, and have been responsible for attacking numerous caravans traveling through Craglorn.
