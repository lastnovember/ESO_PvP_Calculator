Filer Jun is a watchling that can be found in the Infinite Archive Index Chamber.

## Related Quests
- Replication Elimination
- The Margins of Ire

## Quest-Related Events

### The Margins of Ire
When you speak to them to use their merchant services:
:"Have you noticed that some wings of the Archive look similar? The Endless Archive used to be one continuous landmass, but that's obviously not the case anymore. This doesn't matter to me because I can fly. You, however, must use portals."

### Index Chamber
You can find them floating around in the entrance room and speak with them:
:"The problems here aren't small. If you think you can solve them, be my guest."

:"I hope you're exploring the Index Chamber on your own. I don't want to have to point out everything of note."

:"Don't try to comprehend the scope of our responsibilities. Last time Master Malkhest tried, he had to lie still with his eyes closed. Endless is not a misnomer."

### Infinite Archive
You can find them during the completion of the Archive. They serve as a merchant there:
As they appear, they may say one of the following:
:Filer Jun: "Verses and Visions aren't just gifts. They make your fights more interesting. Don't waste them."

:Filer Jun: "Make your choice while Master Malkhest prepares the portal for you."

:Filer Jun: "Filers don't get rewards. Our work is always satisfactory, and therefore its own reward."

:Filer Jun: "This is the perfect spot to create the next portal. It has the best temporal elasticity."

You can talk to them:
:"Don't underestimate anything in the archive. Just because you defeated an enemy before doesn't mean you will again. I read somewhere that overconfidence has killed more mortals than daedrats. Truly!"

:"The Verses and Visions offered throughout the archive are gifts from Hermaeus Mora. Choose the ones you accept strategically. They could determine your chances of survival here."

:"Have you noticed that some wings of the archive look similar? The Endless Archive used to be one continuous landmass, but that's obviously not the case anymore. This doesn't matter to me as I can fly. You, on the other hand, must rely on portals."

### Replication Elimination
In subsequent runs of the Infinite Archive she may be one of the Filers to collect the nymic fragment from the boss:
:Filer Jun: "We're doing very important research back at the Index. I volunteered to come retrieve this."

:Filer Jun: "Master Malkhest instructed me to retrieve this fragment for him."

:Filer Jun: "Master Malkhest calls these things nymic fragments, but it's more likely that they're paleonymic fragments. Just informing you." (listen ON-audio-Filer Jun.ogg )

:Filer Jun: "Master Malkhest must have this nymic fragment. It's a matter of some urgency, he says."
