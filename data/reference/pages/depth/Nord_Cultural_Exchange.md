# Online:Nord Cultural Exchange

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Nord_Cultural_Exchange
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Nord Cultural Exchange is an Eastern Skyrim-based group which aims to strengthen ties with other nations, both within the Pact and without, as well as spreading Nord culture along the way. Their current lead diplomat is Rigurt the Brash, and there are mixed opinions about whether he is an oaf or a diplomatic genius. He was also the leader of the project of the Nord Cultural Exchange, the Glorious Expedition for Nord Cultural Exchange
