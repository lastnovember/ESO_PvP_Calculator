Kharg of the Stone is an Orc warrior member of the Iron Orcs found in the Valley of Scars. He is located northeast of the Valley of Scars Wayshrine, near the road leading towards The Howling Sepulchers and Iron Orc Nirncrux Mine. Kharg is accompanied by welwas and Iron Orcs.
