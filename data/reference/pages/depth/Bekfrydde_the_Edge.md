Bekfrydde the Edge is a Dark Elf found in the city of Riften. She can be found in the warehouse at the docks with Giruss, sharpening her blades. She cannot be spoken to.

If you fail to intimidate Giruss, one of his mercenaries will attack you. Bekfrydde may be the one to attack.

## Related Quests
- Into the Outside

;Quick Strike:A basic melee attack dealing minor physical damage.
