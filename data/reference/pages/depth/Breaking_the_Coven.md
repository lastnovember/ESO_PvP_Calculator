## Detailed Walkthrough
Tovisa asks you to avenge her lost eyes by killing the hagravens of Northwind Mine and destroying their coven. There are three hagravens in the mine: Dyta, Jadwiga, and their matriarch, Kasmira.

:"Thank you for finding me. They were going to kill me too."

:I need to get you out of here.

:"Forget it. Kill those Hagravens, make them pay for what they've done!"

:Where are they?

:"There are three sisters, all nearby. Kill the twins first. Lure Dyta out with Reachman eyes. Kill Jadwiga's attendants and she'll attack. Then use the twins' heads to bring out the matriarch."

:I'll see to it.
To kill the matriarch, you'll need to take out the other two hagravens, who are twins. Mounting their heads on spikes should be enough to anger the matriarch. However, you'll need to lure the twins out of hiding before you take their heads. You can lure Dyta out of her lair with fresh Reachman eyeballs. Jadwiga can be provoked by killing her two attendants. You can complete these tasks in whichever order you please. If you took Gnarled-Root's Hagraven Ward, you can use it to stun the hagravens for a few seconds in battle.

To lure Dyta out, kill nearby Reachmen or loot them from dead bodies. Each action will give you two Eyeballs. Once this is finished, go to the northern section of the cave and place the eyeballs in the Offering Bowl. Dyta will show herself. Kill her and take Dyta's Head.

To lure Jadwiga out, go to the southern sector and kill her two attendants, Brunna and Konia. Jadwiga will appear, and you can then kill her. Take Jadwiga's Head once you've killed her.

When you have both heads, take them to the western chamber to lure Matriarch Kasmira out of hiding. Place the sisters' heads on the spikes and Matriarch Kasmira will appear. Once she's dead, Tovisa and Porath will appear.

:"That's the end of the coven, but we still need to get out of here."
:Agreed. Let's go.
:"Go slowly. I'll try to follow, but I can't see or fight."
:I'll get you out of here.

Break the barricade covering the door in the back of the matriarch's chamber, then lead them out of the mine. Complete the quest by talking to Tovisa outside. Porath will lead her back to Shor's Stone. Tovisa would appreciate it if you stopped by to visit some time.
:"I need healing. Thank you for everything. I'll see you in Shor's Stone."
:Are you going to be all right?
:"I'll never regain my eyesight, but I'll live. Porath will see me back to Shor's Stone. Come see me, if you ever return."

## Bugs
As long as Dyta is not looted for her head, the option to put eyes into the offering bowl will remain available, making it possible to respawn and kill her multiple times back to back.
