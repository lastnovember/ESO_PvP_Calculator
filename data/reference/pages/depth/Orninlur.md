Orninlur is a High Elf bard who was found in Shimmerene, playing the lute for Teldwennisse and Meeh-Chuna. He has no unique dialogue.

He was present in [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3LT-mkj_ezo&t=11m18s footage] from coverage of the ESO: Summerset preview, but was replaced by Orninlusea in the official release.
