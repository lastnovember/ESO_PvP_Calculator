Azilkh is an Orc brewer of the Barkbite Clan found in Reman's Bluff. She doesn't have a store, but she stands just east of the forge where she'll sell her wares between swigs from her mug.

## Related Quests
- Enemies at the Gate
- The Enemy Within
- A Chief Concern

## Quest-Related Events

### Enemies at the Gate
Before you found the antidote, she will be inside the longhouse, just watching over her tribesmen.

### The Enemy Within
After you completed the quest, she will be trying to stand up and recovering.

### A Chief Concern
If you ended the conflict amicably, she says the following after the quests are complete:
:"Let me get this straight. Gargak's built another stronghold just across the way, with its own mine? Wonder if he needs a good brewer …. I've always liked him. Thought he seems strong. Speaking of brewing, if you thirst, I can quench it."

## Bugs
The second half of her post-quest dialogue, after "brewer...", doesn't appear in the text box match her spoken dialogue.
