# Online:Quicksilver Weapons

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Quicksilver_Weapons
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

This is a listing of all standard Quicksilver Weapons in the game. These can be crafted via Blacksmithing from Quicksilver Ingots obtained by refining Quicksilver Ore. It requires rank 8 in Metalworking. The listed damage values assume that the character is Level 50 CP160; lower level characters may see higher values.
