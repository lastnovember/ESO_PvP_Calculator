Ash Titan is a boss in Infinite Archive that primarily deals flame damage.

## Skills and Abilities
;Lava Bolt: A basic ranged attack that deals minor flame damage. This attack will hit anyone standing in front of the boss (not just the tank) so players are advised to keep behind the boss if possible.
;Ethereal Flame: Getting hit by Lava Bolt will cause a flame AOE to appear under you dealing moderate flame damage over time.
;Monstrous Cleave: The boss swings its arm dealing high flame damage and knockback to all enemies in front of it as indicated by a red cone.
;Molten Rain: The boss shoots flame in the air causing fireballs to rain down on players and the general area dealing moderate flame damage as indicated by red circles. Some of the fireballs are unavoidable, and so players should be ready to shield up or heal up once the attack occurs.
;Fire Swarm: The boss flaps its wings knocking back nearby players. Following that the boss releases waves of flame that deal high flame damage and stunning any players hit. Stay far away from the boss during this to avoid being hit. If you are hit, break free immediately so you can avoid any other incoming waves.
;Wing Gust: The Ash Titan gets down on the ground and raises its wings, then unleashes a powerful gust, dealing high physical damage and launching you away.
