Key Collector is awarded for opening Trophy Vaults in the Imperial City. These require 60 Key Fragments each, or 150 for the two in the White-Gold Tower and Imperial City Prison. So you will need a minimum of 1,500 fragments or as many as 3,750 if you use the ones in the dungeons.

## Vaults
The vaults you may open are:
- Bone Shard Trophy Vault
- Clawed Trophy Vault
- Daedric Embers Trophy Vault
- Daedric Shackle Trophy Vault
- Ethereal Trophy Vault
- Legionary Trophy Vault
- Monstrous Tooth Trophy Vault
- Planar Armor Trophy Vault
Since there are only 8 total vaults, you will need to open some of the multiple times.

## Bugs
Opening a vault with more a multiple of 60 Key Fragments (or 150 for the dungeons) in your inventory greater than the amount needed for that vault will waste fragments, causing you to lose as many multiples as you have on hand. For this reason, you should not open a vault if you are carrying more than twice the needed number of Key Fragments. Place any extra keys in your bank to be safe.
