Disastrix Maglagor is an Imperial thundermaul and a member of the Order of the Waking Flame. He can be found guarding the entrance to the central chamber of the Ne Salas Cache Annex, where Pedagogue Eralin works at breaking the ward on the cache.

## Related Quests
- A Mortal's Touch

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Strike:A basic melee attack that does minor physical damage.
;Shock Aura:Disastrix Maglagor shocks the ground around itself as indicated by a red circle dealing moderate shock damage. This can be interrupted to set Disastrix Maglagor off balance.
;Lightning Form:Disastrix Maglagor casts Lightning Form on himself, gaining Major Resolve (which increases his armor by 5948) while creating small areas under the target which after a small delay deal shock damage.
;Uppercut:A melee attack that deals moderate physical damage and knocks you back if it connects. This attack can be blocked to set Disastrix Maglagor off balance.

## Quest-Related Events
As you approach the main chamber, Pedagogue Eralin will call out to Maglagor to deal with the intruders. When he sees you, the Waking Flame cultist will cry out:
:Disastrix Maglagor: "Let disaster rain upon you, fool!"

Defeat him to progress.

## Bugs
Despite being elite rank, Disastrix Maglagor is vulnerable to crowd control.
