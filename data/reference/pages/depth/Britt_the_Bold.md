Britt the Bold is a Nord diplomat and member of the Nord Cultural Exchange. She recently joined the organization to follow in her brother Rigurt's footsteps and her first mission is to convince the warriors of Shor's Stand to ally with the Stirk Fellowship on Solstice.

### The Stubbornness of Nords

When you arrive in the town square, you will witness Britt being lambasted in front of a crowd of onlookers:

#### The Feast of Friendship
When you go to the Hall of Feathers, you can speak with Speaker Frogirde who accuses Britt and you of not bothering to learn about Shor's Stand history or culture. She will then direct you to some books, and tells you to prove that you are willing to learn about and partake in their culture.

Britt will be waiting for you outside the Hall of Feathers, and you can tell her about Speaker Frogirde's task:
:"You met with Speaker Frogirde? I don't see any spit on you. What did she say?"
:Speaker Frogirde thinks that the fellowship won't respect their culture.
:"Won't respect … but this is Nord Cultural Exchange! Respect is what we do! Well, we just have to convince her otherwise. My brother would start gifting now, but we have no gifts. I could give beautiful and moving ram horn recital?"
:I found some books in the Hall of Feathers. Maybe one of them could be useful.
:"List of forenord names is too complicated. And brawling wouldn't be most convincing. Hmm. This recipe looks good. Nothing says Nord culture like feast! But words are stranger than usual."
:What's wrong with the words?
:"Look at ingredients. Words make no sense. Wait. It's puzzle. I love puzzles! You find half and I'll do same."
:Which ones should I find?
:"Hmm. Well, I saw farm outside village. It could have spuds. And try lake in west for swimmers' scales. But buzzer's treasure … Oh! Visit local mead maker for honey! Find those and meet behind Bear Hall. Ooo, Rigurt says I am most excellent cook!"

Talking to Britt before searching for ingredients:
:"I find other ingredients, and must invite village speakers to our glorious friendship feast. You know where to look for your ingredients, yes? Farm, lake, and mead maker. Go, assistant, and be cultural!"

You will need to speak with Kjer behind the Wolf Hall for the Jar of Honey; Barogar down at the docks to eventually get the Fish and Farmer Hrolf at the farm to the northwest for the Dried Potatoes. Once you have all the ingredients you can meet with Britt behind the Bear Hall.

Britt will have changed into cooking clothes, and calls you over to the outdoor kitchen area:
:Britt the Bold: "Look here! Not bad for cook spot, no?"

Talk to Britt to show her the ingredients you found:
:"That was adventure and a half. I went all over Shor's Stand. Even peeked into Wolf Hall. Learned not to talk to Speaker Sogrard yet. He's very shouty. How did you fare? Find all your ingredients?"
:I have the swimmer's scales, spuds from the deep, and buzzers' treasure.
:"Perfect, I'll set everything out and then we can get started. Chefs say it's important to be organized when cooking. Otherwise you might confuse radishes with eggs."
:You can't tell the difference between radishes and eggs? Maybe I should cook.
:"Oh, I am excellent cook. Hardly burn anything. Brother Rigurt loves my rabbit meatballs, too! Don't worry so much. You tell what order to add ingredients. End of recipe is confusing. Think old Nords had trick up their boots with this one."

Britt will then walk over to the stew pot over the cooking fire:
:Britt the Bold: "Let's get this feast started!"

The selection of ingredients will laid out on tables, and you will need to use the Together Stew Recipe to figure out the order of the ingredients. If you select the wrong item, Britt will recognize that it wouldn't work with the recipe you are cooking and gets you to start again.

Talking to Britt during the cooking of the Together Stew:
:"Fresh ingredients make freshest food. I never heard of together stew, but I sampled eatstuffs from across Tamriel. Well, some. This is actually first mission. And first time away from home. Tell me how recipe goes and we'll cook this up right."

If you get something wrong, she may say one of the following:
:Britt the Bold: "That will burn before cooking's done. Throw it out."
:Britt the Bold: "Not make stew before, hmm? Try again."
:Britt the Bold: "I know good Nord cooking smells. This not it."
:Britt the Bold: "What's that smell? Our feast food? Can't serve that."

When you place the right ingredient Britt will inform you of the progress:
First Ingredient:
:Britt the Bold: "That smell will make mouths drool."
Second Ingredient:
:Britt the Bold: "Yes! Look how stew thickens!"
Third Ingredient:
:Britt the Bold: "That smells scrumptuful!"
Adding the fourth and last ingredient:
:Britt the Bold: "We did it! I need to make ready. Take stew and serve speakers in village square."

After taking the Together Stew you can go the tables outside the Whale Splash Inn where the three Speakers will be seated and waiting for you to serve the stew. Britt will arrive after you serve the first bowl of soup:
:Britt the Bold: "I'm not too late! Let the Feast of Friendship commence!"

Talking to Britt while serving the soup:
:"Our stew smells better than lard fried carrots! Serve to speakers. I will watch for feasting expressions. And burps. Burps usually mean food was liked!"

Once you have served the Speakers, Speaker Frogirde will call you over to give you her judgment regarding her test. Talking to Britt first:
:"No one spat it out or vomited! Our feast is success! I hope. What does Speaker Frogirde say? Does she hate it? Oh gods, I can't look. You do talking. I'm so nervous I could bite a snowbank!"

Talking with Speaker Frogirde, she explains that the Together Stew is meant for harvest festivals, but she can see the earnestness and willingness to learn in its creation. The Hall of Feathers now agrees to support the Fellowship, and she will hand you the book The Founding of Shor's Stand. When asked, Frogirde explains that she has an idea what Speaker Sogrard will ask and says the book's contents will help.

Afterwards, Speakers Hialf and Frogirde will talk to each other:
:Speaker Hialf: "See Frogirde? I told you this ambassador was different."
:Speaker Frogirde: "It's the wrong season for a harvest feast. But, their effort is commendable."
:Britt the Bold: "It worked? Our feast worked?"

Talking to Britt before Speaker Sogrard:
:"Bear Hall for safety. Hall of Feathers for feasting. What does Wolf Hall do? Speaker Sogrard holds many opinions and much power. Find out assistant. He must be convinced."

#### Britt the Bold gives a Speech
Talking with Speaker Sogrard, he will explain his position and his observations that the Fellowship's leaders will not consider Shor's Stand's needs. He then gives his challenge, Britt must give speech that convinces him and she needs to incorporate the village's founding story into it. And you are not allowed to speak on her behalf.

You can then pass on Speaker Sogrard's challenge to Britt, the book you received from Speaker Frogirde will be useful:
:"Speaker Sogrard talked to you, without being mean and unsettling. I knew you were lucky. What did Speaker Sogrard want? Did he change his mind about endorsing Fellowship?"
:He thinks the village won't be able to make its own decisions if they join the Stirk Fellowship.
:"That is ridiculous. When have political allies ever overruled each other? We'll have to prove him wrong. What does he want?"
:He wants you to give a speech about the village's founding story and why they should join.
:"Oh no. A speech? Not some grand display of helpfulness? Speaking's not my family's strongest-most skill. And the founding story? How will we get that?"
:Speaker Frogride gave it to me. She knew what Sogrard would want.
:"All right. Here's plan. I give speech like Speaker Sogrard wants. But you need to help when my memory fails. If you gesture to the things I forget in the speech, I can add them and no one will know. Is good plan, yes? You ready?"
:I'm ready, let's start!
::[Progresses Quest]
