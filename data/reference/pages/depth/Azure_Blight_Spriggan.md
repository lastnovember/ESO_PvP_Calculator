The Azure Blight Spriggan is a polymorph in the appearance of an azureblighted Spriggan. It is available in the Crown Store as a Crown Gem exclusive for 500/7,200 from August, 2022 to September, 2023.

## Occurrences
Appearances: 2
- Crown Store - August - September, 2023
- Crown Store - June 5 - September 22, 2025
