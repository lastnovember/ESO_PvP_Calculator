Armorer Reistaff is a Nord from Eastmarch who can be found at the Sunport Rampart Camp or the Stirk Fellowship Camp during the Battle for the Writhing Wall. He will reach out to adventurers for their crafting skills to help with supplying weapons and armor.

## Dialogue
As you approach Armorer Reistaff, you may hear him say:
:"Where am I going to get more shields?"

:"Help equip soldiers with suitable armor for tropical heat!"

:"Our archers need bows to pierce those Worms where they stand!"

:"Forge weapons to cut down the Worm Cult!"

:"Craft helms for our headstrong allies!"

:"There's nothing heroic about fighting in worn out gear!"

:"Aid our healers! Craft for their needs!"

:"Supply hearty meals to fuel the Fellowship!"

:"I could use someone with a talent for enchanting!"

:"Use your skills in alchemy to heal our injured!"

:"Our warriors need armor for the fight at the Writhing Wall!"

Talking to Armorer Reistaff start a quest, he will greet you with one of the following:
:"An ally of the Stirk Fellowship! Good with your hands? We need to stock up on our arms and provisions. The Worm Cult thinks we can't keep an army on Solstice properly equipped. We'll show them how we take care of our own."

:"The Stirk Fellowship needs a steady supply of armaments and provisions to win this war. If you're someone with a knack for crafting, lend a hand, will you? We'll make sure the Worm Cult stays buried this time."

:"Heard you offered to help us crush the Worm Cult. Thank you for pitching in. If you can contribute by crafting arms and provisions for the Stirk Fellowship, you're speaking to the right Nord."

:
:"The Worm Cult's meddled with the dead and the living long enough. Time to put them in the ground! And you can help the Stirk Fellowship fashion the right equipment to do just that."

:"Let those cultists ruin some gear or spoil a few rations. They'll never stop the Stirk Fellowship's efforts to take care of our own. Lend me your aid, and we'll ready our allies for every worm they face!"

:"Good swords aren't all we craft here. This is war, and I need all hands to make sure our forces have what they need for a fight, whatever that may be. Those Worm Cultists won't escape the Stirk Fellowship this time."

Once you have accepted a task, he will greet you with the following if you keep talking to him:
:"Keep your wits about you while you're crafting. Had a Daedra the size of an ogre charge me while I was busy looking for a nearby ore deposit."

:"The stakes are high, so make every moment count. We'll show those worms they should've stayed buried underground."

:"Best to put your nose to the grindstone quickly. Every day, our people put their lives on the line to bring down the Writhing Wall. Our responsibility is just as great."

If you leave and return to your conversation when completing a task, Reistaff will instead greet you with the following:
:"If you've got the goods, bring them here. Each and every thing we craft helps the Stirk Fellowship gain the upper hand!"

:"Ready to deliver? Everything we craft helps to bring down the Writhing Wall and crush the Worm Cult! Oh, I can see them squirming already."

:"Got something for me? I admit, part of me wishes I could see Fellowship soldiers putting our handiwork to use on the front lines. I know my place is here for now … but maybe I'll see you out there before it's all over."

After you have completed six daily quests, Reistaff will have no more work for you:
:"You've done all you can for now. Mead is what I usually go for after a day's work, but I'm saving that crisp taste for when we've finally stomped out those worms."

:"That's all the help I need for now. I'm not looking for an apprentice yet, but your work's sharp. And that's exactly what we need to cut down Worm Cultists!"

:"Once I've passed along your fine work, I'll speak to a few of our allies. News travels fast from the front lines about what's in need. We'll have another task for you tomorrow!"

With each task you start, you will be able to ask him questions. While some relate to the job (as noted below), the other questions remain the same and are as follows:
:Tell me about the Stirk Fellowship.
::"The Fellowship's an alliance from the mainland with one goal-to crush the Worm Cult, once and for all. The cult put up that nasty Writhing Wall so they can hide in eastern Solstice. We win by bringing it down."
::Who's in the Stirk Fellowship?
:::"Folks from the different guilds, the Three Alliances, and even some heroes who fought the cult before. We have our differences, but we've been able to put what's best for Tamriel ahead: wiping out those worms. Wish I could fight with them."
:::Why can't you join the fight?
::::"I have a family waiting for me in Eastmarch. I'm all they have. For their sake, I need to be smart about where and how I choose to fight. I'm no coward-I'll fight if I must. But I'm more useful here."
:What sorts of tasks do you offer?
::"We'll be forging weaponry, making armor, managing provisions, and even creating enchantments! It's our job to ready the Fellowship's armory for any surprises this war springs. And I'd be grateful for your aid."

## Quest-Related Events

### A Call for Arm Cops
:What can I do to help?
:"Some Fellowship soldiers are complaining about running around Solstice in heavy armor. It's the island's sweltering climate. The last thing we want is an army of sluggish soldiers. It's time to provide some lighter alternatives."
:What do you need?
:"Arm cops made from whatever cloth or hide you can work with would be a good start. We'll begin with two sets, and see how they do. Nothing too fancy. But if you turn in good work, you'll be paid well when you're finished."
:All right. I'll make two arm cops.

After you agree to help, you can ask some questions:
:How does crafting with cloth and hide help the Fellowship?
:"I'm an armorer, but I admit heavy armor isn't right for every battle. Solstice is a warm climate for an iron suit. And our scouts and mages prefer mobility over protection. Providing them choices for lighter armor lets us stay a step ahead."

You can craft two arm cops in any style or material and bring them to Armorer Reistaff. When you talk to him, you will specify what material you used:
:I crafted two [MaterialName] Arm Cops. / Here are the arm cops you requested.
:"These should make our soldiers more comfortable. If we don't like fighting in tropical heat, I bet the Worm Cult doesn't like it either. The sooner we crush them on Solstice, the sooner we can all go home. Well, one battle at a time, right?"

### A Call for Jacks
:What can I do to help?

:How many jacks do you need?
:"Two should do for now. We've got plenty of brave soldiers, but charging into fights means lots of armor to replace later. Craft the jacks from any suitable material you know how to work, and I'll make sure you get paid."
:All right. I'll make two jacks.

After you agree to help, you can ask some questions:
:How does crafting with cloth and hide help the Fellowship?
:"I'm an armorer, but I admit heavy armor isn't right for every battle. Solstice is a warm climate for an iron suit. And our scouts and mages prefer mobility over protection. Providing them choices for lighter armor lets us stay a step ahead."

Craft two jacks in the material specified by your Clothing skill level. Then return to Armorer Reistaff. When you talk to him, you will specify what material you used:
:I crafted two [MaterialName] Jacks./Here are the jacks you requested.
:"Well done! With each passing day, our battle only gets fiercer. The more care we take to protect our own, the more soldiers we'll have standing when the Writhing Wall comes down. I hope we're both here to see that day. My thanks!"

### An Enchanting Request
Speak with him to see what he needs:
:What can I do to help?
:"It's time we show those underhanded Worm Cultists some tricks of our own! If they plan to keep spewing curses at us, then we'll counter with enchantments. Never really used one myself, but a Fellowship mage told me what we need."
:What kind of enchantments are you looking for?
:"Whatever two glyphs you can create with a ta aspect rune would help strengthen our arsenal. We're fighting enemies who have some dreadful magic at their command. The best answer is some magic of our own. You'll be paid well for your time."
:All right. I'll enchant two glyphs.

After you agree to help, you can ask some questions:
:What more do you know about enchantment glyphs?
:"Never could wrap my head around magic. But do you see my headband? A kind Fellowship mage engraved a glyph on it. Gives me a boost of stamina for those long hours at my forge. Huh. I guess that's magic wrapped around my head!"

The potency and essence runestones used during the task will depend on your Enchanting skill level. Once you have the glyphs, you can return to Reistaff:
:I enchanted two [Potency Level] Glyphs of [Glyph Effect]. / Here are the enchantment glyphs you requested.
:"I wish I could see the looks on the cultists' faces when they catch sight of these glyphs! But we all have our roles to play in this war. In my case, that means passing along rewards for your efforts."

### Axes for the Assault
:What can I do to help?
:"The Worm Cult's defenses are slowing us down. I'd like nothing better than seeing those worms squirm, but there are a lot of them. If our soldiers are going to push ahead, they need the best weapons we can provide."
:What kind of weapons do you need?
:"Axes to cleave through whoever or whatever gets in our way! I need three of them. You can make the axes from any material you're comfortable working with. Give me blades of iron or steel, and I'll give you a pouch of gold. That's fair."
:All right. I'll craft three axes.

After you agree to help, you can ask some questions:
:Don't Fellowship soldiers already have weapons and armor?
:"Battle's hard on gear. Blades get notched or bent, armor gets cloven or dented. We fix what we can, but sometimes it's better to just replace something damaged. Fortunately, we've got our own blacksmithing station nearby."

You can craft three axes in any style or material and bring them to Armorer Reistaff. When you talk to him, you will specify what material you used:
:I forged three [MaterialName] Axes. / Here are the three axes you requested.
:"Fine work with a polished touch, comrade. Makes me wish I could charge into battle and try out one of these axes myself! But a smith's hammer fits my hand better these days. Still, cut down a cultist for me, eh?"

### Bows for the Fellowship
:What can I do to help?
:"A lot of those devious Worm Cultists prefer to hang back from a fight and cast their curses at a distance. We need to switch up our tactics and equip more of our soldiers with bows. Now we just need some bows."

:"Three bows should even the playing field for now. Keep them simple and efficient. Work with whatever kind of wood you're comfortable with. Never met a necromancer who could focus on magic with an arrow in the eye!"

:"Of course! We set up crafting stations in the Fellowship camps on both Stirk and Solstice. But any good woodworking station will do. Carving wood may seem simple, but it's a crucial skill. Our soldiers are counting on you!"

You can craft three bows in any style or material and bring them to Armorer Reistaff. When you talk to him, you will specify what material you used:
:I crafted three [MaterialName] Bows. / Here are the three bows you requested.
:"Fine work. These should help our soldiers deal with cultists trying to stay out of sword's reach! Maybe I'll shoot a few arrows myself when my work here is finished. I'll get these to the armory for the next archers who need them."

### Famished Fellowship
:What can I do to help?
:"Those cowardly Worm Cultists like to target our provisions. They're constantly attacking our storehouses and supply lines. Our losses aren't too bad, but we must send our allies more provisions. Hungry soldiers don't fight well."
:What kind of provisions do you need me to make?
:"Whatever keeps them in the fight! Many favor soups, chowders, drinks to wash both down, and cakes for when a sweet tooth aches. Our chefs and brewers have recipes if you need them. Cook any of these, and the Fellowship will reward you."
:All right. I'll craft the provisions.

After you accept the task, you can ask some questions:
:Why do you need help with provisions?
:"The Fellowship's cooks can cater to any palate. But with the Worm Cult targeting our supply larders, it's been challenging. War also breeds … enormous appetite. Find yourself a cooking pot and fill a plate, will you?"

The type of food or drink required will depend on your Provisioning skill. Once you have made three provisioning items, you can then bring them to Reistaff:
:I crafted one [ProvisioningRecipe]./Here are all the provisions you requested.
:"Just the smell of this should put a little fire in our soldiers' bellies! Believe it or not, you've done lifesaving work here. Such is the value of a meal in this war. Now, fill yourself up with this."

### First Aid Provider
:What can I do to help?
:"One of our squads fought off a Worm Cult attack, but it got a bit messy. We need someone with an alchemist's touch to craft healing potions. That should be enough to get our wounded soldiers back here for further care."
:How many potions do you need?
:"Three health potions for the injured in the Fellowship force should do. Make them however strong you see fit. Anything that would help them make it back to our healers' tent. And while you work, I'll set aside your coin."
:All right. I'll concoct three health potions.

After you agree to help, you can ask questions:
:Why don't you have enough healing potions?
:"The fighting's heavier than we expected. Soldiers in fortified positions like our siege camps should be well protected, but the Worm Cult constantly raids them. Our alchemists can't keep up with the demand for healing."

Once you have made three health potions, the type will depend on your alchemy skill, you can then bring them to Reistaff:
:I concocted three [Type of Health Potion]. / Here are the health potions you requested.
:"This will get our injured back up on their feet! They should be able to make their way to our healers for more care. Hopefully, we'll only need bottles of mead once this is all over. Here. Buy yourself one on me with this."

### Shields for the Fellowship

:How many shields do you need?

After you agree to help, you can ask some questions:

:I crafted two [MaterialName] Shields./Here are the two shields you requested.
:"Thank you, friend. I hope you know how much your efforts mean to us in this war. Every shield can save a life. That's a victory in itself! May Tsun the Bear watch over every brave soldier who carries these into battle!"
