# Online:Order of the Albatross

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Order_of_the_Albatross

The Order of the Albatross is a Breton knightly order based in the Systres Archipelago. The order is considered one of the largest in the Systres and is focused on daring adventures and exploration.
