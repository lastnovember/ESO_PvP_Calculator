Gagni is a Redguard farmer found on the road north of the Laughing Moons Plantation and south of Mistral. His route takes him between Tirusa and Sibud-ja's flocks. When he reaches the fork in the road halfway between destinations, he stops and looks down the path leading towards Laughing Moons Plantation.

There is a sheep following him up and down the road. If he is killed, the sheep disappears. He has no unique dialogue.
