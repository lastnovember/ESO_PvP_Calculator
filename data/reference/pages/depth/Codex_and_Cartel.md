## Detailed Walkthrough
Hidden Library Puzzles:
- Potion: Hint plaque guides you to mix red, green, blue, in that order. Then drink to enhance your vision
- Statue: Abrasia guides you to clap
- Chest: Hint plaque guides you to not get stuff unfairly, be honest...so fail the lockpicking on purpose

## Bugs
Sergeant Tothbert doesn't appear outside tower during the quest, but only the quest marker does.
- You will have to go the undisguised route, lockpick the door to enter the dungeon. You automatically lose any disguise you were wearing. Do not worry if you are caught inside, the quest acccounts for this and will continue without any issue
