Grahtwood Adventurer is awarded for completing 38 quests in Grahtwood. This includes both the Grahtwood Main Quest as well as any side-quests. It does not include Crafting Writs, Fighters Guild or Mages Guild quests, or pledges for the Undaunted.plain This achievement will allow you to purchase The Gorinir Estate outside Cormount for 780,000, and you can purchase a Valenwood Brazier for your house. It costs 4,000 and can be purchased from Athrahgor in Redfur Trading Post.
