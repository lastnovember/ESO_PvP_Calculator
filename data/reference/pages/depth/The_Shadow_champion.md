### The Shadow
Under the Thief's influence, the Shadow grants you Stamina and helps you Elude your enemies.

colspan=2 | Champion Abilities | Effect

Befoul | Increases the effectiveness of healing reduction abilities by [0-55]%.

Shade | Reduces the cost of Stealth by [0-35]%.

Shadow Ward | Reduces the cost of Block by [0-25]%.

Tumbling | Reduces the Stamina cost of Roll Dodge by [0-25]%.

colspan=2 | Passive Unlocks | Rank | Effect

Fortune Seeker | 10 | Increases the amount of gold you find in treasure chests and safeboxes by 50%.

Merchant Favored | 30 | Reduces the cost of armor repairs by 10%.

Treasure Hunter | 75 | Increases the quality of items you find in treasure chests.

Shadowstrike | 120 | After killing a target with a Heavy Attack enter stealth for 2.5 seconds. This can only happen once every 5 seconds.
