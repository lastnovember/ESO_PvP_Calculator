Ariana At-Fara is a Redguard member of the Greycloak Mercenaries found in Haddock's Market, in the northeast corner, at the large tents near the skyshard. She is accompanied by senche-tigers Anu and Padomay, and a Greycloak member nearby.

## Related Quests
- Heartsick Follies

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Strike:A basic melee attack that does minor physical damage.
;Fiery Breath:Ariana At-Fara breathes fire as indicated by a red cone. This attack does moderate flame damage over time and snares all enemies within the cone.
;Lava Whip:Ariana At-Fara winds up a flaming whip that does moderate flame damage to the player. This attack can be blocked to stagger Ariana At-Fara.
;Dark Talons:Ariana At-Fara calls forth talons from the ground, dealing minor flame damage and rooting anyone hit for 4 seconds. The player can roll dodge to escape the root.
;Health Potion:Ariana At-Fara drinks a potion, restoring Health. This can be interrupted to stagger Ariana At-Fara.

## Notes
- Her pet senche-tigers and the Greycloak member die instantly if she is killed first.
- Before Update 12, Ariana At-Fara was Boss Rank. She also used to periodically summon Ariana's Guards to help her prior this update.
