Gurag gro-Malak is an Orc who can be found in Wind Keep.

## Dialogue
:"I must return to Pariah Abbey and rejoin my brothers. Our sacred duty is protecting Menevia. May the Divines protect you."

## Notes
- His presence appears to be by mistake or bug. He only appears after Unanswered Questions but before. He has no equipment or clothing. He mentions belonging to Pariah Abbey, but his location in Wind Keep has nothing to do with the Spirit Wardens quest line.
