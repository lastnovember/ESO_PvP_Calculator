# Online:Ashlanders

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Ashlanders
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

ON:Ashlander (NPC)
Ashlanders are a faction of nomadic Dunmer. It comprises numerous different tribes, mainly found in Stonefalls, Deshaan and on the island of Vvardenfell. On Vvardenfell, there are four main tribes; the Ahemmusa, Erabenimsun, Urshilaku, and Zainab, plus some bandit groups consisting of outcasts from the main tribes. On the mainland, there are two major tribes; the Kagesh and Mabrigash, and two smaller hostile tribes; the Ulath and Vereansu. For more information, see the lore page.

This faction has two unique equipment styles, one of which can be learned by the player. The chapters for this style are occasionally given as rewards for completing the Ashlander daily quests given by Huntmaster Sorim-Nakar and Numani-Rasi. Crafting Ashlander items requires Ash Canvas. Shadow of the Red Mountain is a medium armor set in the other Ashlander style that can be found in Stonefalls.

## Tribes
- Ahemmusa Tribe - a tribe whose main camp is in the Bitter Coast region of Vvardenfell.
- Ash Exiles - a minor hostile tribe found primarily in Nchuleft in Vvardenfell.
- Erabenimsun Tribe - a tribe whose main camp is in the Molag Amur region of Vvardenfell.
- Kagesh Tribe - a tribe living near Iliath Temple in Stonefalls.
- Mabrigash Tribe - the tribe settled in the Vale of the Ghost Snake in Deshaan.
- Red Exiles - a hostile tribe found all across Vvardenfell.
- Ulath Tribe - a minor hostile tribe found near the Cave of Memories in Stonefalls
- Urshilaku Tribe - a tribe whose main camp is in the West Gash region of Vvardenfell.
- Vereansu - a minor hostile tribe found near the Muth Gnaar monastery in Deshaan.
- Zainab Tribe - a tribe whose main camp is in the Grazelands region of Vvardenfell.
