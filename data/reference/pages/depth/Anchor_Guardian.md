The Anchor Guardian is a Harvester who can be found in The Wailing Prison. She is summoned by Molag Bal in an attempt to halt the Prophet's escape from Coldharbour.

## Related Quests
- Soul Shriven in Coldharbour

## Notes
- The Anchor Guardian was removed from the game and replaced by the Child of Bones when the related quest was altered with Update 6.
