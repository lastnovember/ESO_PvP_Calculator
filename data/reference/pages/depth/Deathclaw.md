Deathclaw is a giant bat found in its lair on Bleakrock Isle. By request of a local hunter, Hoknir, you need to track Deathclaw down and kill it.

During the quest, you will need to take a Deathclaw Talon from its corpse to claim Hoknir's bounty.

## Skills and Abilities
Deathclaw is considered as flying creature, and is thus immune to snares and roots.
;Scrape: A basic claw attack dealing low physical damage.
;Screech: Deathclaw screams at its target, indicated by a red cone in front of them dealing moderate physical damage and fearing them.
;Draining Bite: Deathclaw winds up before lunging forward to bite their target thrice as minor physical damage over time. If not interrupted, Deathclaw regains around half the damage inflicted as Health. This can be interrupted to set Deathclaw off-balance.

## Bugs
Despite being an elite rank enemy, Deathclaw is vulnerable to crowd control.
