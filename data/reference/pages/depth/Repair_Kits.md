** This page is outdated! The repair kit system has been altered with Update 41. **

There are two different types of Repair Kits, the standard ones used to repair armor, which can be found anywhere, and those used in the Alliance War. All repair kits are single-use.

## Armor Repair Kits
These can be used to repair damage received in combat. Each Repair Kit has a level, which means that any items up to a certain level will be 100% repaired with one Kit. Items above that level will receive a smaller amount of repair, requiring multiple kits to fully repair. Standard kits can be purchased from Merchants. Crown Repair Kits can only be purchased from the Crown Store, but will fully repair all of your equipped armor, regardless of level.

## Alliance War Repair Kits
These items are used to repair Siege Weapons and structures in the Alliance War, and can be bought at Siege Merchants at all factions gates in Cyrodiil. With update 41 the 4 different repair kits got replaced with the Cyrodiil Repair Kit, which can be used to repair every type of structures. These can also be found in Rewards for the Worthy.
