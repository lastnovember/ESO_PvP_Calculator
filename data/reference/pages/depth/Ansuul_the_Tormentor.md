Ansuul the Tormentor is a Gloam Knight who appears throughout Sanity's Edge as the main antagonist. She is a creature of nightmare who is attempting to break free of her prison within an artifact known as the Dreamstone. To achieve this, she has taken control of Warlock Vanton's mind and is attempting to break through into Tamriel. You must enter Vanton's dreamworld to defeat Ansuul and save him.

Ansuul appears throughout the trial as a projection, either to speak directly to your group or as part of one of Vanton's fragmented memories of his dealings with the creature. She can also be observed as a giant red specter in the sky above the surreal landscapes within Sanity's Edge, observing your group from above.

You will face her within The Twisted Memory, the final section of the trial, where she serves as the final boss.

There are also three different-colored versions of Ansuul the Tormentor that are Gloam Knight copies of Ansuul, each being either red, blue, or green in color. They first appear atop the balconies during the fights with Warlock Vanton, where they watch over your battle and attempt to manipulate Vanton's mind. Towards the end of the fight, all three will appear at once to fight the group.

### Death Recap Tips
- Neural Takeover: Ansuul is tormenting Vanton through his desires. You must reach Vanton before his mind is corrupted.
- Neural Takeover: Send a team of four onto the mind stage to defeat Vanton's delusional state and prevent him from becoming totally corrupted.
- Defeat Fragments before your party is overwhelmed.
- Stepping into Anxiety's Grip will slow your movement, causing you to be more vulnerable to other attacks.
- After being banished by Manic Phobia you must overcome your personal demon before approaching Ansuul.

## Dialogue
You'll see visions of Warlock Vanton speaking with Ansuul the Tormentor and exaggerations of his greatest fears projected as you traverse his nightmare. The first vision occurs as you enter the dream.
:Warlock Vanton: "Why is this happening?"
:Ansuul the Tormentor: "Because you are too scared to save the Mages Guild."

Once Exarchanic Yaseyla is defeated, a vision of Warlock Vanton and Ansuul will play out. She speaks down at him from the throne.
:Ansuul the Tormentor: "You couldn't save Twelvane. You aren't powerful enough to save anyone. You're weak!"
:Warlock Vanton: "You can make this stop... can't you?"
:Ansuul the Tormentor: "Together, we can make it so no mage is ever hunted again."

After the first group of trash mobs are defeated post-Exarchanic Yaseyla, a conversation between Ansuul and Warlock Vanton will play.
:Ansuul the Tormentor: "With Twelvane in charge, there will be no weaklings left. You'll rise above the rabble."
:Warlock Vanton: "I can't stand up to Twelvane. Not when she's like this."

On the second to last group of trash mobs before the Chimera's Den, another conversation between Ansuul and Warlock Vanton will play.
:Ansuul the Tormentor: "You're strong, Vanton. Don't you feel it?"

:Ansuul the Tormentor: "Join Twelvane. Take control. It's what you want."

Once Ansuul has been defeated, Wizard Twelvane appears.
