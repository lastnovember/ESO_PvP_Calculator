Anogon is a Wood Elf light elite gear vendor who can be found at the Armory Merchants market located at the Eastern Elsweyr Gate.

## Dialogue
When approached, she will say one of the following:.
:"It's not about the size of the blade, it's about the quality of the edge. And I have the best!"
:"Hey! It's dangerous out there, friend. Take a look at what I've got. You'll need it."
