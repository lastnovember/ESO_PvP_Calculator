# Online:Knight

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Knight
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Knights are a class of hostile humanoid Dragonknight NPCs that attack using melee abilities. They are usually equipped with a one-handed weapon and a shield.

;Quick Strike:A basic melee attack dealing minor physical damage.

;Lava Whip:The enemy winds up a Lava whip dealing moderate flame damage to the target. This attack can be blocked to set the enemy off balance.
