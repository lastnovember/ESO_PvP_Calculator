It is separated from the other half of the island by the Solstice Range, along with the more recent Writhing Wall. Due to the "Reverse Planemeld" caused by the Worm Cult's actions, swathes of the landscape has been melded with that of Coldharbour.

Following the completion of the Battle for the Writhing Wall event in November 2025, it became accessible through a gap in the wall east of the Sunport Rampart Camp.
