Euraxian Soldiers are Nibenese Imperial bodyguards who are part of Euraxia Tharn's forces found in the Halls of Colossus.

## Related Quests
- Bright Moons, Warm Sands
- The Battle for Riverhold
- Two Queens

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Strike: A basic melee attack that does minor physical damage.
;Power Bash: A heavy shield bash that does moderate damage and stuns if it hits the player. This attack can be blocked to set the enemy off balance.
;Shield Charge:The Bodyguard charges at you from a distance with their shield raised, dealing moderate physical damage and staggering in an area.
;Block: The enemy may also briefly block with its shield, reducing damage taken greatly. Players that attempt heavy attacks on the enemy when it is blocking will be set off balance.
;Shard Shield:The Bodyguard will cast Shard Shield at an ally, which grants the ally a massive damage shield. This spell can be interrupted to set the Bodyguard off balance.

Euraxian Soldiers in Northern Elsweyr are friendly and can be found outside the workhouse in Rimmen, and later take part of the assault on Riverhold, where they fight against Defense Force Warriors.

Some are also present at the gates to the siege weapon platforms during the quest Two Queens.

## Quest-Related Events

### The Battle for Riverhold
After you have set up Cadwell's traps, some squads of Euraxian Soldiers on their way to Riverhold will trip them.
:Euraxian Soldiers: "For Queen Euraxia!"
:
:Cadwell: "Riverhold, with cats so bold! I'll see you there, if truth be told!"

### Two Queens
While you work with Captain Nala-do to destroy the siege weapons holding Rimmen hostage, she will distract the Euraxian Soldiers acting as guards.

;Southern Weapons Platform:
Nala-do approaches the guard:
:Captain Nala-do: "Gharesh-ri has been spotted in the market. Hurry!"
:Euraxian Soldier: "Finally, some action!"
The soldier runs off and leaves her post.

;Northern Weapons Platform:
:Captain Nala-do: "The south platform is under attack!"
:Euraxian Soldier: "Stupid cats!"
:Euraxian Soldier: "Wait, I better warn my lieutenant first."
:
:Captain Nala-do: "Go. Destroy the siege weapons. Nala-do will clean up this mess."

Euraxian Soldiers were Foot Soldiers that were attacking the Star Haven Adeptorium.

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Strike:A basic melee attack that does minor physical damage.
;Heavy Attack:A heavy melee attack that does moderate physical damage. It can be blocked to set the enemy off balance.
;Throw Dagger:The foot soldier backs away from the target and throws a dagger at the target. This attack snares, deals minor physical damage then very minor bleed damage over time. This attack can be interrupted to set the foot soldier off balance. The dagger can be blocked to prevent the snare and the bleed.
;Ignite:A combo move that is only performed if Archers, Fire Mages or other fire using enemies (Flame Atronachs, Scamps etc.) are around. The foot soldier throws a glob of tar under the target's feet as indicated by a red circle. While this does no damage, standing in it snares. The foot soldier will call for the other enemy to light the tar (usually accompanied by a voice clip) and the red circle will burn, doing low flame damage over time to targets within the red circle.
