Coldharbour Adventurer is awarded for completing 29 quests in Coldharbour. This includes both the Coldharbour Main Quest as well as any side-quests. It does not include Crafting Writs or pledges for the Undaunted.plain This achievement will allow you to purchase a Molag Bal Banner for your house. It costs 20,000 each and can be purchased from Lozotusk in The Hollow City.
