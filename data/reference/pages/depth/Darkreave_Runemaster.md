Darkreave Runemasters are arcanists of the Darkreave Curators faction.

## Related Quests
- Bastion Nymic - Fungal Downs
- Bastion Nymic - Writhing Wastes
- The Many Paths

## Skills and Abilities

### Standard
;Seeking Runescrawl:The Darkreave Runemaster casts a Runeblades-like ability which hits you thrice, dealing minor magic damage each time.
;Fatecarver:The Darkreave Runemaster casts a Fatecarver-like ability which deals continuous magic damage and snares when inside the large green beam. This can be interrupted to set the Darkreave Runemaster off-balance.
;Rune of Displacement:The Darkreave Runemaster casts a Rune of Displacement-like ability which pulls nearby targets to its center after a small delay, dealing low magic damage then it deals low magic damage over time to targets hit.
;Health Potion:The Darkreave Runemaster drinks a potion, restoring a bit of its Health (7,928 in Bastion Nymic). This can be interrupted to set the Darkreave Runemaster off balance.

### Fire Mage
;Flare:A basic ranged attack that does minor flame damage.
;Heat Wave:The Darkreave Runemaster releases fire along the ground in the direction it is facing, dealing moderate flame damage to all targets hit. This can be interrupted to set the Darkreave Runemaster off balance. This cannot be blocked
;Fire Runes:The Darkreave Runemaster shoots two fireballs in the air which form two fire runes when they land. The runes deal minor flame damage over time to all targets within the red circles. Players should be prepared to move as soon as the attack casts as one rune will always be cast on top of you.

:Darkreave Runemaster: "I tire of your incompetence! You are as inept as a rock!"

:Darkreave Runemaster: "You're as worthless as you look!"

:Darkreave Runemaster: "Clumsy oaf! How dare you drop priceless works in my presence!"

Once the Ogrim is defeated, the Runemaster will leave in a portal:
:Darkreave Runemaster: "That oaf got a far cleaner and merciful death than it deserved."
