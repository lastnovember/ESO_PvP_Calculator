Cectyda is a Maormer storm mage and member of the Sea Vipers. She can be found at the ritual site north of Cat's Eye Quay, where she is overloading the Storm-Slave in an attempt to destroy the town of Mistral. She will not talk to you.

## Related Quests
- The Tempest Unleashed

## Skills and Abilities
;Shock:Basic ranged attack dealing minor shock damage.
;Thunder Thrall: Cectyda teleports to you and summons a bolt of lightning that strikes a large area around herself, dealing moderate shock damage to all targets as indicated by the red circle.
;Run Away!:When too close to her, Cectyda backs up to put some distance between her target and herself.

## Bugs
Despite being an elite rank enemy, Cectyda is vulnerable to crowd control.
