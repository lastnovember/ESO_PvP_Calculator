# Online:Driladan Clan

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Driladan_Clan
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Driladan Clan are a clan of Bosmer that originally lived in the Driladan Pass. Their homes and families were devastated when the Veiled Heritance raised the dead all around them, with only a few managing to flee with their lives. They found refuge in the Shadows Crawl, a ruin located near the settlement of Bramblebreach. There they were attacked once again, this time by the Hollow, stone constructs built by the Wilderking.
