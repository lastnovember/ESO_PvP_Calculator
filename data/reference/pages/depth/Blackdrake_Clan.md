# Online:Blackdrake Clan

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Blackdrake_Clan
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Blackdrake Clan is one of the more well-known clans of Reachfolk, as they are the ones who founded the Longhouse line of Emperors. Shortly before 2E 582, Ard Caddach executed all living members of the clan after they returned from Cyrodiil, solidifying his rule of the Reach, and rendering the clan all but extinct in the process.
