The Argent Ozezan the Inferno is an Infernium which can be found in the northeast settlement in the Night Market's Parch. As an Argent enemy, the nearby Brazen Incineration Beetle and Brazen Doylemish will need to be slain to lure it out. Conversely, killing it will cause the two Brazen bosses to spawn in the instance.

While it is active, you may find several Broodlings and Evolved Broodlings appearing around the northern settlement.

Once it and the Argent Ash Titan are killed, they will provide Gilded Keys, which can be used to access the Timeless Wallow dungeon. As an Argent enemy, it is a potential target of the related quest.
