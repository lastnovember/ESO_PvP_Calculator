Salache Cryomages were High Elf frost mages who could be found in Aba-Loria before the delve was changed in Update 3.

## Skills and Abilities
;Ice Arrow:A basic ranged attack dealing minor frost damage.
;Ice Barrier: The Salache Cryomage summons an Ice Barrier that absorbs projectiles fired at it until destroyed. The Salache Cryomage cannot be pulled if the wall stands between them and the target. The player should move so that there is a clear line of sight between themselves and the Salache Cryomage.
;Winter's Reach:The Salache Cryomage unleashes an icy whirlwind in the direction it is facing, dealing moderate frost damage and rooting all targets hit. The root effect passes through block. This can be interrupted to set the Salache Cryomage off balance.
;Run Away!:When too close to them, the Salache Cryomage backs up to put some distance between their target and themselves. This does not apply if the Salache Cryomage is near its Ice Barrier.
