# Online:Blind Path

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Blind_Path
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Blind Path are a newly emerged cult that is dedicated to a lost Prince. After undergoing the trials for membership, all members will don blindfolds and never remove them. They are encountered in Bedlam Veil, where they intend to steal a particular item from Fa-Nuit-Hen.
