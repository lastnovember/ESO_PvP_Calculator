Topgrain Hide is a crafting material obtained from animals in any zone by characters with Rank 6 in the Tailoring passive or CP10 to 30. One animal may yield one to two pieces of the raw material, Topgrain Hide Scraps. Once ten scraps are collected, they can be refined into seven to ten pieces of the processed material, Topgrain Hide. Refined topgrain hide is used for making medium brigandine armor at any of the clothing stations. For you to be able to create brigandine armor your Tailoring ability needs to be rank 6.
