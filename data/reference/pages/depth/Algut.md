Algut is an Orc traveling merchant who can be found walking along the roads between Sunport and Shor's Stand, and will take a break at the Corelanya Manor Wayshrine.

She is accompanied by a pet sloth called Sleepy who trails behind.

## Dialogue
:"We're a long way from Sunport, traveler. Even the hardiest warriors need adequate supplies. Take a look and see if anything appeals to you."

:"It's good to see someone else out here who doesn't appear to be a dark cultist or a bandit. You aren't a dark cultist or bandit, are you? No, of course not. How can I assist you, friend?"

:"Even a merchant needs to be careful on the road these days, what with all the dark cultists and other dangers suddenly loose upon our shores. If you assure me you mean me no harm, feel free to browse my stock."

## Notes
- Algut wears a recolored Vengeance Day Dress.
