# Online:Moon-Sentinels

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Moon-Sentinels
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Moon-Sentinels are ancient and mysterious mummified Khajiiti guardians found both inside the Shadow Dance Temple and the Plane of Jode. In life, they were the protectors of the Moon-Priests, and continue to perform their duty in the afterlife, fighting off against all trespassers.
