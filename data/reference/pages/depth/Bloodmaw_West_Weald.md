Bloodmaw is a large bear who can be found at Lake Olo. It wanders around above the waterfalls until being called into battle by Oakenclaw.

## Skills and Abilities
As a champion rank enemy, Bloodmaw is immune to crowd control.
;Crackdown:When Bloodmaw jumps in the arena, it deals extreme physical damage and knocks down targets in the landing area. During combat, Bloodmaw gets on its hind legs before crashing down with its front legs, dealing extreme physical damage in a cone area.
;Bite: A basic melee attack that does moderate-high physical damage.
;Lunge: A basic melee attack that hits twice for moderate-high physical damage each.
;Powerful Lunge: A basic melee attack that hits twice for high physical damage each.
;Pounce:Bloodmaw jumps far at a target, dealing very high physical damage and knocking back targets in the landing area.
;Terrifying Roar:Bloodmaw roars which fears nearby targets.
