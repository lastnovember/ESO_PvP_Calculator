# Online:Army of Orpheon

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Army_of_Orpheon
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Army of Orpheon (or Orpheon's army) are a group of Alliance War deserters who follow Orpheon the Tactician, a former battlefield Emperor. They have a history in Cyrodiil, notably for besieging the city of Bruma.

They have tracked down Lep Seclusa and laid siege to it in an effort to obtain the Elder Scroll kept within.
