# Online:Bloodmist Clan

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Bloodmist_Clan
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Bloodmist Clan is an Orc vampire clan located within the Bloodworks of the Imperial Sewers. They are led by their patriarch, Otholug gro-Goldfolly. The Bloodmist Clan has entered an alliance with Molag Bal's invasion force. The clan members have their own ambient dialogue.
