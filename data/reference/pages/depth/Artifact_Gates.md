# Online:Artifact Gates

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Artifact_Gates
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Artifact Gates are the last line of defense protecting each Alliance's Scroll Temples, preventing the opposing Alliances from seizing the Elder Scrolls stored there. Each gate is protected by two keeps, which both have to be captured by the other alliances to open the gate.

## List of Artifact Gates

### Aldmeri Dominion
- Gate of Altadoon
- Gate of Mnem

### Daggerfall Covenant
- Gate of Alma Ruma
- Gate of Ni-Mohk

### Ebonheart Pact
- Gate of Chim
- Gate of Ghartok
