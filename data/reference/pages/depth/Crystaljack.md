Crystaljacks are glass dragonfly-like creatures from Mirrormoor, and can be found in places in West Weald that have been affected by the recent incursions. Their cocoons are crystalline and purple in color, and they emit their own light.

## Related Quests
- Hold the Line
- Ithelia's Fury
- Mirrormoor Incursion (quest)
- The Many Paths
- The Untraveled Road
- Venom Hunt

## Skills and Abilities

### Stardard
;Quick Strike:Basic melee attack dealing minor physical damage. Also rarely applies "Phantasmal Swarm" for 6 seconds which makes you attacked by weak phantasmal crystaljacks that disappear when this effect ends.

### Summoned by Swarmkeeper Xvarcon
Crystaljacks summoned by Swarmkeeper Xvarcon die after some time and also have this one unique ability.
;Prism Cloud:Upon death, the Crystaljack explodes which deals moderate-high magic damage.

## Notes
- Crystaljacks summoned by Crystaljack Cocoons take damage from their burst attack.
- A crystaljack can be obtained as a pet.

## Bugs
Despite being a flying creature, Crystaljacks do not benefit from Flying Immunities excepted in Leftwheal Trading Post.
Crystaljacks summoned by Crystaljack Cocoons never attack.
