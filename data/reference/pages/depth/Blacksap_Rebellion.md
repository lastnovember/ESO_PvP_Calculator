# Online:Blacksap Rebellion

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Blacksap_Rebellion
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Blacksap Rebellion are a group of Bosmer who believe that Valenwood is being corrupted by the Aldmeri Dominion.

The original rebellion was formed and led by Gelthior, King Camoran Aeradan's cousin, due to a schism over the Green Pact. After he was taken into custody at an unknown location, his sons took up the mantle at their last remaining cell in Cormount.
