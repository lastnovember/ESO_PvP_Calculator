Ayleid Shade Sentinels are undead Ayleids found in Niryastare and Nonungalo.

## Skills and Abilities
;Slice: A basic melee attack that deals low physical damage.
;Magic Dagger: A ranged attack where the sentinel throws a magical shadow dagger at their target, dealing low magic damage.
;Shadow Slice: The sentinel teleports behind the target and strikes at them, dealing moderate magic damage and snaring. This attack an be interrupted to knock the sentinel off balance but also creates a "Shadow's Gift".
;Shadow's Gift: A burst of shadow energy goes off from the sentinel, indicated by a growing red circle, dealing moderate magical damage.
;Abyss: The sentinel rakes their dagger across the floor, sending out a wave of shadow energy forward, indicated by a red circle, dealing low magical damage and sending affected targets backwards. This attack can be interrupted to set the sentinel off-balance.
;Grasping Void: The shade sentinel summons a giant shadowy hand from the ground beneath their target, grabbing them in trapping them in place briefly, before slamming them down, dealing moderate physical damage. Break Free can be used.
;Desecrated Ground:On death, the undead rarely desecrate the ground under them as indicated by a red circle. This does moderate damage over time and snares all targets as well as healing undead enemies within the red circle.

## Bugs
Ayleid Shade Sentinels lack a lot of their animations due to them only having one weapon equipped.
