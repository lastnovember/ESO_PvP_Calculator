Beech logs are smooth logs of earthy color. They can be found in any zone by players with Woodworking Rank 3 or levels 26 to 34. One log can be harvested for three to four samples of the raw material, Rough Beech. Once ten samples of rough wood are collected, they can be refined into seven to ten pieces of Sanded Beech. Beech staves and bows, as well as beech shields, are crafted from the refined beech wood at any of the woodworking stations. In order for you to create beech gear your Woodworking ability has to be rank 3.
