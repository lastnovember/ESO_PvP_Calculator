Heralda Garscroft is a Breton holiday achievement furnisher who can be found in each of the alliances' capital cities, as well as Vivec City. She sells furnishing items associated with the following holiday events:

- Jester's Festival
- Midyear Mayhem
- Witches Festival
- New Life Festival
These furnishing items can only be purchased once the requisite achievements have also been completed.

## Dialogue
If you don't own a house:
:"You don't have a home? Where do you hang your pictures?"

:"Homeless in Hammerfell? There's no reason for that. Plenty of places available, if you're not too picky about where you want to live."

:"No house? However do you get by? I can't imagine sleeping in the altogether without four sturdy walls around me!"

If you do own a house, her dialogue will vary depending on how many achievements you've completedevent achievements? zone?:

Stage 1
:"You should be honored. I only sell quite interesting things to quite interesting people."

:"Names are like noses. Everyone has one. What you want is a reputation. I love to help turn a house into a home when a good reputation is involved."

:"Now, I usually only deal with heroes of a higher stature, but I like you. So this time I'll make an exception."

:"Starting to make a name for yourself? I like that. Let me show you something that will enhance your reputation and decorate your home at the same time."

Stage 2
:"Now you I know and respect! Let's get right down to business, shall we?"

:"You have the air of a very important person. Someone I can really snuggle up to. Let me show you my special stock."

:"Ah, hero, you grace me with your exalted presence! Would you like me to heap praises upon you first or would you like to get right down to business?"

:"Your reputation proceeds you, hero! Let's share a drink and see if anything in my private stock speaks to you today."

Stage 3
:"You're here? You're really here! You honor me, champion. Now let me honor you. I only show these furnishings to a select few."

:"When you reach the pinnacle of fame and fortune, commonplace items just won't do. Have no fear. My furnishings are far from commonplace."

:"I've been saving a few items for that special someone, and there isn't anyone more special than you. Want to take a look?"

:"I so look forward to your visits, champion. It makes me want to slash my prices to make certain you'll take something home with you."
