Altenrel is a High Elf who can be found in Sunport.

## Dialogue
:"Sunport and its people blossom under the wise rule of Clan Corelanya."

:"Clan Corelanya rules Sunport well, despite the current circumstances, I assure you."

:"These difficult times try our fair city, but Clan Corelanya always rises to meet any challenge. The regent will see us through."
:Tell me about Clan Corelanya.
:"Oh, it's a sad tale with a happy ending. Once we were powerful necromancers with ambitions that almost destroyed us. So our ancestors were exiled here and we turned away from the dark arts. Now we live in peace and follow the Three Queens."

## Notes
- Altenrel wears the Sapiarch heavy armor without the shoulders.

## Bugs
Altenrel is unable to be pickpocketed and doesn't have pickpocket information.
