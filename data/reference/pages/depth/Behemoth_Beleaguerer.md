Online Achievement Summary
icon = Behemoth Beleaguerer
type = DLC
type2 = Blackwood
type3 = Trials
points = 15
desc = Defeat Oaxiltso after raising the challenge banner in Veteran Rockgrove.

Behemoth Beleaguerer is awarded for defeating Oaxiltso in Veteran Rockgrove after raising the challenge banner, making the battle more difficult by the following:
- The enemies get generally stronger.
- Oaxiltso's Savage Blitz (charge attack), when he's under 50% health, will be targeting two players, one after another.
- Each Havocrel Annihilator at some point casts a skill Hot Spring?, which turns one of cleansing pools into unusable pool of lava (it will also start erupting and randomly targeting players with small Magma Sludge flame AoEs). Therefore, all four pools will be disabled by the end of the battle. This mechanic targets farthest clean (as in, not currently poisoned) pool, if possible, otherwise just farthest - consider having the group cleanse only in specific pools to avoid turning wrong ones to lava early on and having to adjust.
