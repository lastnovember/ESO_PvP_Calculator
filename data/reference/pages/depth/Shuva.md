Shuva is a Khajiit furnisher who would have helped run Pride of Place Furnishings at the merchant square in Senchal.

## Dialogue
If you don't own a house:
:"Shuva can, of course, show you her fine goods, but has to wonder why someone with no house would be interested in purchasing furnishings."
:"You have no house, not even a humble but cozy room or cottage, yet you wish to see my furnishings? Eh, very well."

If you have few Dragonhold Achievements:
:"This one has a few items now. Perhaps more later. Who knows? It's in the hands of Jone and Jode."
:"One day, perhaps, you'll accomplish many more great deeds. Then this one may have many more great deals and interesting wares for you!"

If you have some Dragonhold Achievements:
:"Ah, this one has heard a few things about you. You may do many more great things, and when that happens, Shuva can have many different wares for you."
:"This one has some fine things you can purchase. Shuva reserves greater goods for those who have had many great accomplishments. This one thinks you'll achieve those soon enough, though!"

If you have a lot of Dragonhold Achievements:
:"Shuva has some very fine things for one whose deeds shine so brightly. Take a look."
:"Your reputation precedes you. In the best way, of course. This one has items that reflect well on your status, my friend."
