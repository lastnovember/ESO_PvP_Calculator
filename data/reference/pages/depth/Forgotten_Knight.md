Forgotten Knights were Redguard mummy knights found in the Crypt of the Exiles. They were removed in a very early update.

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Strike:A basic melee attack dealing minor physical damage.
;Fiery Breath:The Forgotten Knight breathes fire as indicated by a red cone, dealing moderate flame damage.
;Lava Whip:The Forgotten Knight winds up a Lava whip dealing moderate flame damage to the target. This attack can be blocked to set the Forgotten Knight off balance.
;Dark Talons:The Forgotten Knight calls forth talons from the ground, dealing minor flame damage and rooting anyone hit for 4 seconds.
