## Detailed Walkthrough
Outside Bloodrun Cave you'll find Mim-Jasa, a priest of Sithis. He needs some help with some bodily remains, to destroy them with dignity. Enter the cave and you'll immediately find the first.

The second set of remains is below the delve's skyshard (which is perched on a ledge above the northernmost of the central chambers). If you intend to claim the skyshard, you should take the eastern path after destroying the first set of remains since this will allow you to reach the skyshard without having to backtrack.

The third set of remains is in the next large chamber in the center of the map. You'll hear the priest mention it is missing its skull. After destroying the remains, talk to the priest and he will ask for your assistance destroying Ajum-Shei's skull, which is down in the largest chamber of the map. It is guarded by the delve's boss, Tumma-Maxath. After defeating him and taking care of the skull, continue south to exit through an alternate route. Speak with the priest to complete the quest.

## Bugs
Despite wielding a staff and entering a ready stance during combat, Mim-Jasa doesn't attack enemies.
