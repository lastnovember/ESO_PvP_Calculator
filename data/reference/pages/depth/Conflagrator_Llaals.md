Conflagrator Llaals is a Dark Elf member of the Sixth House found in the Forgotten Wastes. He has summoned the Iron Atronach Mynar Igna, and the two of them form a boss of this dungeon.

He will summon a bound flame atronach in combat; mechanically he is identical to a Sixth House Fire-Binder.

## Skills and Abilities
;Necrotic Spear: A basic ranged attack dealing moderate frost damage.
;Heat Wave: Conflagrator Llaals releases fire along the ground in the direction he is facing, dealing moderate-high flame damage to all targets hit. This can be interrupted to set Conflagrator Llaals off balance. This cannot be blocked.
;Volcanic Debris: Conflagrator Llaals crosses his arms together before releasing them wide open, summoning a flame atronach to crash down from above, indicated by a circular AoE, launching you backwards and dealing 0 flame damage.
;Empower Atronach:Conflagrator Llaals channels a buff on a nearby Flame Atronach or Iron Atronach that increases the atronach's damage done by 20%, its Physical Resistance and Spell Resistance resistance by 4,550 and healing it for 1,000 Health every half-second. This spell appears as a blue glow around the hands of the buffed atronach as well as those of Conflagrator Llaals. This can be interrupted to set Conflagrator Llaals off balance.
