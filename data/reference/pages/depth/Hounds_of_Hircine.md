Hounds of Hircine (quest)
The Hounds of Hircine are a group of legendary hunters in Valenwood. They honor Hircine, the Daedric Prince of the Hunt, and take their name from the Hounds that accompany him on his hunts. One of their number, Haras, is said to be unmatched in his skill at tracking.
