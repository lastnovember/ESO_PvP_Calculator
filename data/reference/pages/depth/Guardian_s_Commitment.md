# Online:Guardian's Commitment

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Guardian's_Commitment
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Guardian's Commitment is one of three class skill lines available to Zerith-var. Abilities in this line focus on tanking. It is roughly equivalent to the Bone Tyrant skill line for Necromancer players.

## Skill Perks
The Guardian's Commitment skill line is immediately available to Zerith, and is leveled up as he gains levels.
