The Aspect of Sorrow takes the form of a spectral, blue Gloam Knight and is part of Gilded Fateline's Knell who is found in the Mournful Catacomb.

## Skills and Abilities
As a boss rank enemy, the Aspect of Sorrow is immune to crowd control. Partway through the fight, it will appear and a player will have to defeat it on their own by entering the rift. If the Wraith-of-Crows is not interrupted, it enters the Aspect of Sorrow's arena which also triggers the following changes: The Aspect of Sorrow's Max Health is reduced to 750,055, and an Echo of Grief is periodically summoned. A Shrike Executioner is always periodically summoned. abilities, skills etc
