# Online:Dark Champion

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Dark_Champion
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Desolated Scholars are a class of hostile humanoid NPCs that wield two-handed weapons and use shadow magic. The class was introduced with High Isle.

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Strike:A basic melee attack dealing minor physical damage.
;Heavy Strike:A charged melee attack dealing moderate physical damage damage and staggering the target. This can be blocked to set the Dark Champion off-balance.
;Shadow Cleave:The Dark Champion swings their weapon around, indicated by a wide red cone, dealing low magic damage and snaring affected targets.
;Shadow Ward:The Dark Champion channels a spell that casts a damage shield of 5% of the caster's Max Health for them and nearby allies, that goes away after 4 seconds if not broken beforehand. This can be interrupted to set the Dark Champion off-balance.
