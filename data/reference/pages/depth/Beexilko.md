Beexilko is an Argonian Behemoth enslaved by the Blessed Inheritors who is restrained by Urvel Drath as punishment. If you choose to fight Urvel, Beexilko will tackle him to help you, and you can kill the mage to free him. He and Hleewos will later rejoice.

## Related Quests
- Unstuck From Time

## Quest-Related Events
Upon arriving through the Arena Tunnels, you will see Urvel holding Beexilko down with a spell:
:Urvel Drath: "Disobedient creature. You will never escape Bal Sunnar!"
:Beexilko: "You … not win!"

When you engage Urvel in combat:
:Urvel Drath: "This beast posed no trouble for me. What makes you different?"

Beexilko will take the opportunity to shake free, saying one of the following:
:Beexilko: "Not today!"

:Beexilko: "Ragh!"

:Beexilko: "I show you…power!"

Urvel Drath will respond with one of two lines:
:Urvel Drath: "Down, beast!"

:Urvel Drath: "Wait for your turn, beast."

:Urvel Drath: "Know your place!"plain

:Urvel Drath: "Insolent cur!"

Once killed, Urvel manages to spit out a few final words before Beexilko approaches to look down on the corpse.
:Urvel Drath: "Lackwits. Can't … defeat me. Not your place!"
:Beexilko: "Know … your place. Beast."
