## Quick Walkthrough
# Talk to Manacar outside the Oleander Coast Winery.
# Talk to Lilawen.
# Search for Tableau.
# Read the note.
# Talk to Ghrum at the Alinor Outlaws Refuge.
# Enter King's Haven Pass.
# Find is the locket and a broken cart.
# Talk to Manacar.
# Follow Cur to find the Blackbirds.
# Choose Tableau's future.

## Detailed Walkthrough

## Notes
- Despite being described as a fence in the quest journal objective description, Ghrum doesn't actually provide this service.

## Bugs
Ghrum's lip-syncing appears broken when speaking to him after Manacar departs, due to his mouth animating him still eating while he is also talking.
Cur will not stop during this quest to let you keep up.
- The location where Tableau is held is designated with a circled area on the map the moment you get the objective so you don't have to rely on Cur to find her.
