# Online:Woodworking Materials

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Woodworking_Materials
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

There are four types of materials involved in Woodworking: Wood, Sanded Wood, Resins, and Gems.

Wood is the raw material that can be found in the world by harvesting fallen logs. By themselves they cannot be used, but they may be refined into Sanded Wood at any woodworking station. Unlocking the "Plentiful Harvest" and "Master Gatherer" perks in the Craft Champion tree will also provide bonuses when harvesting. There is a small chance of producing resins when refining raw materials. The logs you find are determined by your character's level and your Woodworking level-if you are level 40 with a Woodworking level of 2, you will find both Rough Oak and Rough Hickory out in the world.

Sanded Wood is used to make shields as well as all bows and staves.

Resins can be used to improve existing shields or weapons. Resins can be obtained by deconstructing existing armor sets. Rough wood and resins can both be acquired through Lumberjack Hireling mails. Sanded wood and resins can be obtained as rewards for completing Woodworker Writs.

Gems are used to craft weapons or armor with traits, provided that trait has been researched on that piece. Gems can be found by looting jewelry boxes throughout the world, opening Wet Gunny Sack obtained while fishing, and deconstructing armor pieces that have traits.

## Resins

colspan=2|Resin | Effect

Pitch
Improve quality from Normal to Fine

Turpen
Improve quality from Fine to Superior

Mastic
Improve quality from Superior to Epic

Rosin
Improve quality from Epic to Legendary

In addition to woodworking items, resins are also used in Blueprints for furnishings. Specifically:
- Normal quality blueprints do not require any resins
- Fine quality blueprints require 9 Pitch
- Superior quality blueprints require 6 Turpen
- Epic quality blueprints require 3 Mastic
- Legendary quality blueprints require 1 Rosin
