The Collector's Galleries can be accessed from the door on the right side of the courtyard. The Collector's Prized Exhibits can be found inside the Collector's Galleries. The Collector's Playroom can be entered through the door afterward. It connects to The Collector's Private Gallery, where from one can return to the courtyard again.

Deadlands Scorpions and Sandroaches can be seen in the galleries. Various hostile plants can be encountered in on the path leading to the arboretum, and various creatures from throughout Tamriel are held in the menagerie.
