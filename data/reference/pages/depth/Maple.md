Maple logs are light brown in color, with patches of mossy green. They can be found in any zone by players with Woodworking Rank 1 or levels 1 to 14. One log can be harvested for three to four samples of the raw material, Rough Maple. Once ten samples of rough wood are collected, they can be refined into seven to ten pieces of Sanded Maple. Maple staves and bows, as well as maple shields, are crafted from the refined maple wood at any of the woodworking stations.
