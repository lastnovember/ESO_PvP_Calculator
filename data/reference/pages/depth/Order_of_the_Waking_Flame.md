# Online:Order of the Waking Flame

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Order_of_the_Waking_Flame
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Order of the Waking Flame (also simply known as the Waking Flame or Dagonists) are a cult dedicated to the Daedric Prince Mehrunes Dagon. They are determined to acquire their lord's Four Ambitions, and will kill anyone who get in the way of their goal.

In the Deadlands, the cult has entered into a tenuous alliance with the Ruinblood Dremora clan, as they both work on studying and making use of the cataclysts to create Incarnates for Mehrunes Dagon.
