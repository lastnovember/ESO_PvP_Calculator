Coral Mudcrabs are coral crabs summoned by the Queen of the Reef during combat, after she resurfaces following her bombardment attack where she flings crabs all over the arena. They are also summoned by the massive coral crab found in Sareloth Grotto.

## Related Quests
- The Sickening Sea
- Tidewash Stranded

## Skills and Abilities

### The Queen's Hatchery
;Claw Swipe: The crab snips its target with its claw, dealing low physical damage.
;Monstrous Claw: The crab backhands its target with its big claw, dealing moderate physical damage and staggering. This can be blocked to set the coral mudcrab off-balance.
;Flying Crab:The crab jumps at a target from a distance, dealing moderate physical damage.

### Tidewash Strand specific
The Coral Mudcrabs in this location have different abilities.
;Claw Swipe: The crab snips its target with its claw, dealing moderate physical damage.
;Claw Pound: The crab slams its target with its claw, dealing moderate physical damage.
;Monstrous Claw: The crab backhands its target with its big claw, dealing moderate physical damage and staggering. This can be blocked to set the coral mudcrab off-balance.
;Water Jet: The crab leans back and spits a high-velocity jet of water at a single target dealing moderate physical damage.
;Flying Crab:The crab jumps at a target from a distance, dealing moderate physical damage.
;Perforating Shard/Poisoned Shard:Melee attack applying two Damage over Time Effects at the same time. "Perforating Shard" deals continuous direct minor physical damage while ""Poisoned Shard" deals continuous direct minor poison damage.
