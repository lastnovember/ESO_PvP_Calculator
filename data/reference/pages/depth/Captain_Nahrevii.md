Captain Nahrevii is a Khajiit member of the Renrijra Maor leading the assault on Sunscale Strand. She is the captain of The Clenched Jaws.

## Related Quests
- Pull the Last Fang

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Strike:A basic melee attack dealing minor physical damage. (Some Bonelords only)
;Entropic Bolt:A basic ranged attack dealing minor magic damage.
;Summon Abomination:Captain Nahrevii summons a flesh atronach, bone colossus or ghost to fight, spawning with her current Health. Captain Nahrevii's Physical Resistance and Spell Resistance are reduced to 0 while a summon is active as seen by a blue glow on her. The summon automatically dies if Captain Nahrevii is killed.
;Bone Cage:Captain Nahrevii casts a rune on the ground that activates after a while, dealing low magic damage and rooting targets who step in it. This can be interrupted to knock Captain Nahrevii off balance. This cannot be blocked.
;Run Away!:When too close to her, the bonelord backs up to put some distance between her target and herself.
;Bone Surge:Captain Nahrevii casts a Bone Shield-like ability on herself which reduces her melee damage taken by 20% until this disappears.

## Quest-Related Events
As you enter her ship's hold, you'll see her approaching her first mate menacingly before killing him:
:First Mate: "... is madness! You need this ship! You need us!"
:Captain Nahrevii: "Need? This one needs nothing but the Fang!"

When engaged in combat, she'll call out:
:Captain Nahrevii: "My sister! Where is she? No matter, Sithis will bring us together in time."

## Bugs
Despite being an elite rank enemy, Captain Nahrevii is vulnerable to crowd control.
