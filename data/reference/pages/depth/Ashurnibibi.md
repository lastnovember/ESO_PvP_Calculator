The ruins are occupied by Malacath worshippers. After they interfered with House Telvanni affairs, the House hired the Morag Tong to eradicate the cult. Wounded and dead Malacath Champions can be found on the path leading into the sanctum.

It is not marked on the map, but is one of the locations you need to visit for the Daedric Explorer achievement.
