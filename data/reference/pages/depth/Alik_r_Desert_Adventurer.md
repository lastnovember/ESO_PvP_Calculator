Alik'r Desert Adventurer is awarded for completing 42 quests in Alik'r Desert. This includes both the Alik'r Desert Main Quest as well as any side-quests. It does not include Crafting Writs, Fighters Guild or Mages Guild quests, or pledges for the Undaunted.plain This achievement will allow you to purchase a Standing Ansei Statue for your house. It costs 15,000 and can be purchased from Lozotusk in Kozanset.
