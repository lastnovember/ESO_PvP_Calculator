# Online:Reach Mage

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Reach_Mage
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Reach Mages are a class of hostile humanoid NPCs that attack using ranged magic. They are usually equipped in light armor and usually wield a single sword or dagger, though some can be seen using a destruction staff. The class was introduced with Orsinium.

## Skills and Abilities
;Flare:A basic ranged attack dealing minor flame damage.
;Quick Strike:A basic melee attack dealing minor physical damage.
;Flames:The enemy channels magic and releases a gout of flame from their hand which they sweep slowly in front of them, launching flaming areas forward, each dealing low flame damage to targets hit. This can be interrupted to set the enemy off balance.
;Grasping Vines:The enemy places down seven flaming vines that travel forward then sideways, indicated by a circular AoE. The initial placement is accompanied by an explosion that deals low physical damage.
;Grasping Vineburst:Getting hit by Grasping Vines roots you in place and inflicts minor flame damage over time.
