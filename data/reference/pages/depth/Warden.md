# Online:Warden

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Warden
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Warden (disambiguation)

Warden is a class in Elder Scrolls Online. It was originally only available to players who had purchased the Morrowind Chapter and later available as a separate Crown Store Upgrade for 1500, but it has been integrated into the base game on March 9, 2026. Warden is a nature-themed class, with a focus on frost magic and animal companions.

::In-Game Description: Wardens are defenders of the Green, master storytellers whose nature tales become magical reality. They wield frost spells against enemies and summon animals to aid them.

## Skills

### Animal Companions
Animal Companions is geared towards summoning animals to aid you in battle.

### Green Balance
Green Balance is geared towards healing and buffs.

### Winter's Embrace
Winter's Embrace is geared towards dealing Frost Damage and tanking.

### Class Mastery
Class Mastery is available if the player is not subclassing.
