Dreadsail Deadeyes are Maormer pirates affiliated with the Dreadsails. They can be found at Dreadsail Reef.

## Skills and Abilities
;Blazing Arrow: As you progress through the Dreadsail Beach until you reach the Bloodsport Arena's door, the Dreadsail Deadeye will constantly bombard the area with flaming charges from the sky as seen by two growing indicator circles, one always spawning under a player, each dealing very high flame damage in an area.
