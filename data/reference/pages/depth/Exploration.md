# Online:Exploration

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Exploration
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Exploration was a trait which could only be found on armor. Once you had researched the trait on a given item type, you could craft Exploration armor by using Garnet. Items with the Exploration trait would increase your experience earned from discovering locations by an amount dependent on the quality of the item.

## Notes
- This trait was replaced by Prosperous, which increases your Gold looted from enemies. This in turn was replaced with Invigorating, which increases your resource recovery rates.
