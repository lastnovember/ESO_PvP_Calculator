A Pact Sergeant used to be found inside Davon's Watch, next to a shack near the northwest door with Holgunn One-Eye and numerous Pact Soldiers, during the city's attack and disappeared once the Delaying the Daggers quest was completed. Others were at Fort Arand. They were removed in an unknown patch.
