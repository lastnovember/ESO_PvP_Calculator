Adara'hai is a Khajiit and the current Chief Crier of the Twilight Cantors. She can be found at the entrance of the Maw of Lorkhaj. She asks for your help defeating Kulan-dro‎, who has been affected by the Bent Dance.

She has a twin sister named Ka'ishka, who lives in Black Heights with Adara'hai's brother-in-law, Vijari.

## Related Quests
- Into the Maw
- Chiaroscuro Crossroads
- The Pride of Elsweyr
