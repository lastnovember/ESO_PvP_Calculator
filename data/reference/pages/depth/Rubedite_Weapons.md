# Online:Rubedite Weapons

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Rubedite_Weapons
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

This is a listing of all standard Rubedite Weapons in the game. These can be crafted via Blacksmithing from Rubedite Ingots obtained by refining Rubedite Ore. It requires rank 10 in Metalworking. The listed damage values assume that the character is Level 50 CP160; lower level characters may see higher values.
