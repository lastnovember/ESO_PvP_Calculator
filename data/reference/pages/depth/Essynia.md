Essynia is a Wood Elf writer found in Belkarth during the Hearts Week festival. She is part of the theatrical troupe that will be performing an play based on the Legend of the Lovers for the event. She can first be found at the Crossroads Tavern, near the city's waterfall and at the Belkarth Festival Grounds.

## Related Quests
- Heartsick Follies

## Quest-Related Events
Kuzam-jo will ask you to speak with the troupe's director Dreams-in-Scenes, as he has heard that there are troubles with the production of the play meant for the festival. If you speak with Essynia before the director:
:"You had a good run, dear. A few well-received plays. A couple of popular bard songs. A dream short-lived is better than no dream at all."

After you have spoken with the director at the inn, you will need to speak with some of the troupe's members and help them. Essynia can be found on the inn's upper floor, writing while seated on a bench:
:""My sweet flower, your petals shine in rebuke of the night's hungering dark." That's not right. He wouldn't describe his lover as something so delicate. It needs bravado, weight. Perhaps a moose? Drinking her fill of moonlight? Ugh, that's stupid."
:Your director sent me to help you.
:"Oh dear. Did she sound disappointed? I just can't seem to wrap my head around this play. I thought I would write to Ghadaz's strengths, really help him shine, but it's not working. He's as emotional as the scenery and it's all my fault."
:Can you tell me what the play is about?
:"It's about a warrior rising to meet a great evil that has stolen away his love. How he will stop at nothing to be by their side again, even if it kills him. The main character is brave, dashing, kindhearted. All the qualities I see in Ghadaz."
:But he's still struggling with the words?
:"Yes, but it's all my fault. My writing has lost all its rhythm. I find myself tripping on the words, and I wrote them! Was it wrong of me to try to write to Ghadaz's strengths? I know he has this character in him, but I fear I'm just getting in his way."
:You should write to your strengths. Let Ghadaz focus on his performance.
::"Of course. All this time I've been trying to make his performance choices for him. Make him fit this mold I carved in his likeness. He's a good actor. I know he is. I'll trust him to find his own way there."
:Take inspiration from Ghadaz's best performances.
::"Years ago, I saw him in a performance of Tillius and Constantina. He was radiant. Commanded the stage like he owned the space. In that moment, I knew I wanted nothing more than to write for him. I need to return to our beginning. Thank you."
:It sounds like you have feelings for Ghadaz. Let those feelings guide you.
::"You aren't entirely wrong about that. I've been so focused on making Ghadaz look good that I've forgotten why I was writing the words in the first place. The play is my admission of love. Something I hope at least Mara will appreciate."

If you speak to her again after giving advice, her dialogue will depend on if you have already helped the other two or not:
width=50%| Helped all three troupe members:
width=50%| Still need to help people:

:"Well, here we go. No stress. It's not like this play will end my career and any hope at a love life, right? Right."

:"Apologies, I really appreciate your help, but I've got to get these words down. All at once, I can feel new scenes welling in my head. They must pour out! I can hardly keep up! I may even start another play once I finish these rewrites."

You will also need to assist the lead actor Ghadaz and the choreographer Tilelme the Nimble. Once all three have been helped, you can head to the Belkarth Festival Grounds to watch the latest performance. Essynia will be standing next to the stage, watching the show. Dreams-in-Scenes will be happy with the improvements and tells everyone to take a break before the next performance.

You can then approach Essynia and speak with her:
:"That was remarkable! Ghadaz finally embodied the hero! I knew he had it in him. Thank you, dear stranger, for all the help."
:How do you think it went?
:"We should probably cut the musical number in the vampire lord's court, but otherwise, I think we have something really special here. And Ghadaz was remarkable. Inspiring even. Maybe it's time I have a little chat with him."
:Glad to be of help.

Afterward, you can watch Ghadaz approach Essynia and talk to her.is there difference in dialogue if you pick different choices?
:Essynia: "Ghadaz. I was transfixed by the way you shaped my words in your mouth. You were stunning."
:Ghadaz gro-Shakar: "No Essynia, it was your words that allowed me to bare my soul. I am clay molded by your writerly hands."

Talking with Essynia before meeting with Dreams:
:"I hope you know that Dreams-in-Scenes will take all the credit. It's her right as director, of course, but know that we are all very grateful for your assistance."

Dreams-in-Scenes will be happy with your assistance and tells you to give Kuzam-jo her thanks. You can talk to Essynia before you leave:
:"I've never been so bold as to attempt to court a leading actor, but wouldn't you know it, he was the one to start blushing and blubbering. What a sweetheart!"
