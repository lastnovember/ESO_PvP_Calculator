Basil Menant is a Breton and former leader of House Menant, a criminal organization in Daggerfall with links to burglary and swindle schemes. He is the father of Seline Menant, the current leader of their house. He has an interest in the ancient mage Sage Voernet and the relics related to him.

## Related Quests
- The Codex Caper
- Three Thieves in Daggerfall
- Codex and Cartel
- Racing Into Nowhere

## Quest-Related Events

### The Codex Caper

After speaking with Skeever Nivo about the caper job Quen wants help with, you can meet with her in the Rosy Lion to learn more. As you approach her in the tavern, you will find Quen talking to Basil:
:Quen: "If your information's correct, we have to move quickly."
:Basil Menant: "I agree. The leaders of the three gangs need to see some results or this guild idea of yours will be finished before it starts."

If you talk with Basil before Quen, he will not want to chat:
:"I'm sure I'm mistaken, but for a moment there I thought you were listening to our conversation. If you have no business with myself or my companion, I'll thank you to move along."

Talking with Quen, you learn about the job she wants help with. To steal the Codex Voernet from the nearby Yvierry Manor which is being kept in a strongroom. This is part of her larger plan of uniting the gangs of Daggerfall into a new Thieves Guild. Before you go to the estate, Quen will ask you to get the Yvierry Estate Map from upstairs. It was placed there by a bribed servant and shows where to enter the manor.

### Three Thieves in Daggerfall
After the job at Yvierry Manor, there will be several leads to follow about what you found. Quen will have you work with the gang leaders, to learn about them and to show your worth. She will have you speak with Seline about getting at the items Lord Yvierry stored the Bank of Daggerfall.

When you approach Seline and Basil in the den, they will be talking:
:Basil Menant: "Quen's onto something, daughter. The Nowhere Vault is exactly the job this Thieves Guild needs."
:Seline Menant: "We're hardly a Thieves Guild, father. At least not yet. But I agree. The Nowhere Vault is too tempting a target to ignore."

If you talk to Basil here, he will defer to his daughter and mentions the arrival of an expert scholar of the Prowler's Talisman:
:"I am merely an interested spectator, friend. I retired from this life ages ago. My daughter handles all House Menant's business now. You should talk to her. Oh, and seek out Mylen Dechery here in the den to learn more about the Prowler's Talisman."

Talk with Seline, she will agree to assist and offer you some Somnal Powder to practice with before heading to the bank. If you speak to Basil after talking to all three gang leaders but before deciding whom to help first, he will encourage you:
:"Which is it to be, my friend? The bank, the Mages Guild, or Ilessan Tower? Whatever your decision, I am sure it will be the right one."

If you decide to help Seline first, Basil will follow her out of the Den. If you picked someone else, you can talk to him before you leave the den:
:"Never an idle moment. See to your business with the other gang leaders, then come find Seline at the bank when you're ready."

When you arrive at the bank, they will be standing near the fireplace and talking to Captain Sansonne. If you talk to Basil before going over the plan with Seline:
:"Captain Sansonne is one very persistent Lion Guard. Quen needs to watch her step. Speak with Seline, my friend. She can explain the plan to you. It's all her idea, anyway-I merely play a minor role in the action."

When you speak with Seline, she will have you steal debt-bonds from the upstairs office before sneaking into the vault below and taking the Nowhere Key. Once you are ready, Seline will provide the distraction with Basil playing a small but key role:
:Seline Menant: "Oh, Eliza! First that horrible tragedy at Yvierry Estate and now a criminal at large?"
:
:Eliza Guerette: "It's terrible! I'm glad Captain Sansonne is looking into it personally."
:Seline Menant: "Indeed! I have the utmost confidence in the Lion Guard."
:Basil Menant: "The captain is … I … I ugh!"
:
:Seline Menant: "Oh, no! Father!"

You can then begin your work, and once you have taken the Nowhere Key from the vault, you can escape through the side passage and meet with Seline and Basil outside:
:Seline Menant: "Did I forget to mention that secret exit? Never quite found a need to use it until now."
:Basil Menant: "Keeping secrets from your father? Good for you! I wondered why you told me to meet you here."

If you talk to Basil before Seline:
:"I'm obviously not as frail as I led the banker to believe. Although I am surprised to learn that Seline knew about a secret door in Daggerfall I was not familiar with. Finish up with her before we return to the thieves den."

When you talk with Seline you can give her the stolen debt-bonds and key and ask her some questions. Afterward, you can also talk to Basil some more before you leave:
:"Thank you for retrieving the key and those debt-bonds before the Koldane Cartel managed to collect them. I have a feeling the key is important, and keeping those contracts away from the cartel just seems prudent."

Once you have been helped out by the three gang leaders, you can meet them in the map room of the Thieves Den. When you arrive, they will be arguing and Basil will be standing with Seline. Talking to him before Quen:
:"Pay the gang leaders no mind. They carry on like this all the time. Now go tell Quen how things went at the Bank of Daggerfall. I'm sure she would appreciate your perspective on what happened there."

### Codex and Cartel
Speaking with Basil in the Map Room before starting the quest with Quen:
:"I believe Quen wants to speak with you outside the Den. No doubt she plans to enlist your help in using the legend of the Nowhere Vault to continue her Thieves Guild project."

Talking with Quen, she will tell you that the gang leaders are engaged with claiming the prize of the Nowhere Vault and dealing with the Koldane Cartel. However, she wants to see what the gang leaders suggest doing and if they can work together. When you enter the map room, the gang leaders and Basil will be arguing about what to do, as Quen walks up to the table:
:Seline Menant: "Someone knows more about the Nowhere Key. We can make inquiries."
:Edwane the Hawk: "And announce to the world that we have it?"
:Abrasia: "So? Use the key to draw out Koldane. He's the real problem."
:Basil Menant: "Listen, it's the librarian that's important! Oh, curse it all. Friend, a word?"

### Racing Into Nowhere
If Basil is alive, he will appear at the main table in the Thieves Den after returning from the Nowhere Vault:
:"Seline gave me the overview of what happened at the Nowhere Vault, but I'd love to hear all the details from you when you have a moment. But now, I think we all want to hear what Quen has to say to you."

Speaking with Quen, she will want you to speak with the three gang leaders and decide who you think should lead the Daggerfall Thieves Guild:
:"I am glad I survived to see this moment, when the gangs of Daggerfall come together to become a Thieves Guild. It's been my dream. And I won't try to sway you, even if Seline is my daughter. I trust you. Select the thief you think will do the best."

After making your choice for Daggerfall Guildmaster:
:"I agree wholeheartedly with your choice, my friend. Our new Thieves Guild is in excellent hands!"

## Dialogue
After the events of Codex and Cartel, Basil will stay behind at the Thieves Den if he survived.
:"Thank you again, friend. If not for you, Seline would be an orphan now. I'm alive and on the mend, but I suspect my days of accompanying her on jobs are over. Ah, well. More time to read, I suppose. But we'll talk more later. I need to rest."

Talking to him while planning the sting operation for the Koldane Cartel:
:"Quen has been working with me to study the Codex Voernet. Fascinating if somewhat infuriating book … the magic both delights and terrifies me. Oh, congratulations on getting the gang leaders to form a plan. And using the Lion Guard? Brilliant!"

Talking to him after the events of Wolf Trap:
:"Those cartel bastards! And how dare Rols betray my daughter like that! I'm just glad it didn't turn out worse. Thank you for saving Seline. And the others. We'll find a way to deal with Koldane yet. But Abrasia, I thought she was one of us …."

Talking to him after the events of The Nowhere Keys:
:"I'm pleased to see Seline and Edwane working together. With a Nowhere Key in our possession and Quen's success at deciphering the codex, you have a real chance to get into the Nowhere Vault and steal the Thieves' Moon. I wish I could join you!"
