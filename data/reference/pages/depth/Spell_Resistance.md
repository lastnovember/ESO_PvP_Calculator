# Online:Spell Resistance

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Spell_Resistance
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Spell Resistance is an attribute which protects you from all forms of Magical Damage, including Magic Damage, Flame Damage, Frost Damage, and Shock Damage. It caps at 33,000 for players and 25,000 for NPCs which reduces all forms of magical damage taken by 50%.
