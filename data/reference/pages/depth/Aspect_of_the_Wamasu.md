The Aspect of the Wamasu is a ghostly apparition that appears at the Grove of the Chimera.

## Related Quests
- Recovered Relics
- Shrines on Shaky Ground
- Three-Pronged Approach

## Skills and Abilities
Aspect of the Wamasu are considered elite creatures, and are thus immune to all forms of crowd control effects.
;Bite: A basic melee attack that does moderate physical damage.
;Sweep: The Aspect of the Wamasu swings its tail, doing moderate physical damage as well as knocking down all enemies in front of it as indicated by a red cone.
;Charge: The Aspect of the Wamasu charges forward, doing high physical damage as well as knocking down all enemies standing in the red rectangle.plain
;Repulsion Shock: The Aspect of the Wamasu breathes out lightning, doing high shock damage over time to all enemies in front of it as indicated by a red cone.plain
