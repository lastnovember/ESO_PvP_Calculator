Leather is a crafting material obtained from animals in any zone by characters with Rank 3 in the Tailoring passive or levels 26 to 34. One animal may yield one piece of the raw material, Leather Scraps. Once ten scrap samples are collected, they can be refined into seven to ten pieces of the processed material, Leather. Refined leather is used for making medium leather armor at any of the clothing stations. For you to be able to create leather armor your Tailoring ability needs to be rank 3.
