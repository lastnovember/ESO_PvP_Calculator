# Online:Mercenary Contracts

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Mercenary_Contracts
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Hotfix 6/3/14
Mercenary Contracts could be purchased from mercenary merchants in Cyrodiil. When used, they summoned a temporary mercenary NPC to guard a siege point at a keep. The merchants were removed by a hotfix due to several bugs and exploits, but the contracts themselves can still be traded and used.
