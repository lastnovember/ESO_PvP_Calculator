Corrupted Hushed Vanguards are corrupted Hushed Dark Champions found in The Infinite Panopticon during the A Hidden Fate quest.

## Skills and Abilities

;Quick Strike:A basic melee attack dealing minor physical damage.
;Heavy Strike:A charged melee attack dealing moderate physical damage damage and staggering the target. This can be blocked to set the Corrupted Hushed Vanguard off-balance.
;Shadow Cleave:The Corrupted Hushed Vanguard swings their weapon around, indicated by a wide red cone, dealing low magic damage and snaring affected targets.
;Shadow Ward:The Corrupted Hushed Vanguard channels a spell that casts a damage shield of 5% of the caster's Max Health for them and nearby allies, that goes away after 4 seconds if not broken beforehand. This can be interrupted to set the Corrupted Hushed Vanguard off-balance.

## Bugs
Some Corrupted Hushed Vanguards are equipped with a one-handed weapon and shield, which breaks the animations of "Heavy Strike" and "Shadow Cleave".
