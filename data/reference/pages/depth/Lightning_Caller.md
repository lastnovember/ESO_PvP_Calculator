# Online:Lightning Caller

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Lightning_Caller
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Lightning Caller is one of three class skill lines available to Ember. Abilities in this line focus on dealing damage. It is roughly equivalent to the Storm Calling skill line for Sorcerer players.

## Skill Perks
The Lightning Caller skill line is immediately available to Ember, and is leveled up as she gains levels.
