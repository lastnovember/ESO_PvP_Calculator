# Online:Buoyant Armigers

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Buoyant_Armigers
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Buoyant Armigers are a military order of the Tribunal Temple. They seek to emulate Vivec's deeds through their actions and words. They often serve as protectors for temple priests while they undergo religious missions.

The Buoyant Armiger's have adopted the Deep-Moss Ash Hopper as their mascot, as they admire the creature's green carapace.
