Cursed War-Priests are former Khajiit monks of the Temple of Seven Riddles, but now are cursed by the Bent Dance and transformed into Dro-m'Athra warpriests.

## Related Quests
- Into the Maw

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Strike:A basic melee attack dealing minor physical damage.
;Fiery Breath:The Cursed War-Priest breathes fire as indicated by a red cone, dealing moderate flame damage.
;Lava Whip:The Cursed War-Priest winds up a Lava whip dealing moderate flame damage to the target. This attack can be blocked to set the Cursed War-Priest off balance.
;Dark Talons:The Cursed War-Priest calls forth talons from the ground, dealing minor flame damage and rooting anyone hit for 4 seconds.
;Desecrated Ground:On death, the undead rarely desecrate the ground under them as indicated by a red circle. This does continuous minor magic damage and snares all targets as well as healing undead enemies within the red circle for 3% of the original undead's Max Health per tick.

## Bugs
The Cursed War-Priests are incorrectly flagged as Undead, and thus can cast "Desecrated Ground" upon death.
