Commoners are members of the Stirk Fellowship found in camps beside the Writhing Wall. They have no unique dialogue. They can be seen around the camps, either carrying out construction work or supervising it.

During attacks on the siege camps around the Wall, Bretonplain commoners will appear and will call out active Soul Reapers.

## Dialogue
During Worm Cult sieges, if you've arrived to defend the camp from the invaders, the Commoner will shout:
;Male Breton Commoners:
:Commoner: "Defend the camp!"
:Commoner: "Quickly, to your stations!"
:Commoner: "We're under attack!"

As the enemies are defeated, they'll say:
:Commoner: "Don't forget the soul reapers!"
:Commoner: "They have soul reapers! Destroy them!"
:Commoner: "Destroy the soul reapers!"

After a soul reaper has been destroyed:
:Commoner: "One less reaper to deal with!"
:Commoner: "Good work! Don't leave any behind."
:Commoner: "Nice work! That reaper's finished."

The commoners will also have ambient dialogue when camp is safe:
;Female Commoners:
:"I'll defend this camp to my dying breath!"
:"Every attack is just another chance to kill those worms!"plain
:"We're here for a reason. For the Fellowship!"plain

;Male Commoners:
:"The other camps aren't faring any better, I hear."
:"The waiting is almost worse than the attacks! Almost."
:"They'll be back. They always come back."plain
:"The pile of bodies is high enough. Burn them all!"plain
