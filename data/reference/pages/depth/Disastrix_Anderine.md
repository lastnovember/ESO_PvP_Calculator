Disastrix Anderine is a High Elf arbalest and member of the Order of the Waking Flame who can be found in the Imperial Sewers Cache Annex.

## Related Quests
- The Emperor's Secret

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Shot:A basic ranged attack that does minor physical damage.
;Arrow Stab:A basic melee attack that does minor physical damage where the Arbalest grabs their arrow and stabs it into you rather than firing it.
;Arrow Spray:The enemy shoots a spray of arrows in the direction it is facing as indicated by a red cone. This attack does moderate physical damage as well as snares all enemies hit. This attack can be interrupted to set the enemy off balance. Blocking also prevents the snare and reduces the damage.
;Bombard:The arbalest crouches and fires several arrows into the air, and where they land will develop several small AoE circles dealing low flame damage.
;Quakeshot:A wide range attack where the arbalest fires arrows downwards in the area in front of them, causing the ground to quake and deal moderate physical damage. Used at close range.

## Quest-Related Events
Disastrix Anderine can be found in the first large chamber of the annex, where she will alternate between trying to break the wards on a cache and walking over to her notes on the other side of the room. When she sees you she will yell:
:Disastrix Anderine: "These treasures belong to the Order of the Waking Flame!"

When she is dead you are free to break the ward on the chest and take the scroll, A Deal is Struck.
