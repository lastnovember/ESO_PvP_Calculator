Crocodiles are large, hostile reptiles that can be found near water throughout most of Tamriel.

## Related Quests
- Crocodile Bounty
- Deception in the Dark
- The Heart of the Beast
- Old Adventurers
- Pledge: Wayrest Sewers I
- Plowshares to Swords
- A Predator's Heart
- School Daze
- Uncaged

## Skills and Abilities

;Bite: A series of two melee attacks, the first one dealing minor physical damage and the second one a third of this damage.
;Crushing Chomp: A basic melee attack dealing low physical damage.
;Sweep: The Crocodile swings its tail in front of itself, as indicated by a red cone dealing moderate physical damage.
;Ancient Skin: The Crocodile's skin becomes redder and becomes slightly more resistant to damage.

## Unique Crocodiles
- Gathongor the Mauler, the group boss of Gathongor's Mire. (Replaced by an Argonian Behemoth of the same name.)
- Iron Head, a crocodile found in Hircine's Haunt
- Old Snapper, a crocodile encountered in Shad Astula during the quest School Daze
- Old Tooth, a crocodile found near Bolga's Hunting Camp
- Ripplestrike, a crocodile found at Dro-Dara Plantation during the quest A Predator's Heart
- Rootbiter, one of the champions in Root Sunder Ruins
- Slimecraw, the first mini-boss in the Wayrest Sewers I
- Trapjaw, the group boss of Trapjaw's Cove (Replaced by a Wamasu of the same name.)
- Vengeful Crocodile, the transformed shape of Murk-Watcher, found at Dro-Dara Plantation during the quest A Predator's Heart

## Variants
- Celestial Crocodiles, crocodiles summoned in Skyreach Pinnacle
- Crocodile Brutes (no longer present in Old S'ren-ja Docks)
- Crocodile Runts (no longer present in Old S'ren-ja Docks)
- Hot-Headed Crocodiles, crocodiles summoned by Wo-Xeeth
- Quickwater Caimans, crocodiles found in Quickwater Cave
- Spirit Mote Crocodile, crocodiles found in Y'ffre's Path Firesong

## Notes
- Unlike the rest of the other similar wildlife and denizens of Evergloam, crocodiles found there don't have the "Gloam" prefix in their name.
- Prior to an early update, the smaller crocodiles at Old S'ren-ja Docks were named Crocodile Runt while the regular ones were named Crocodile Brute.

## Bugs
The Crocodiles summoned by Argonian Behemoths are vulnerable to crowd control despite being elite rank.
