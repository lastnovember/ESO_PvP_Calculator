# Online:Dark Witnesses

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Dark_Witnesses
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Dark Witnesses are a clan of Reachmen in Mournoth. They are intent on reclaiming the area for the Reach. Their leader, the hagraven Uela, has gained Hircine's favor, and the Witnesses treat her like a god.
