Jute is a tall plant with yellow flowers. It can be found in any zone by characters with Rank 1 in the Tailoring passive or character level 1 to 14. When harvested, a plant yields three to four samples of Raw Jute, sometimes accompanied by crawlers or worms. Ten samples of raw jute can be refined into seven to ten pieces of the processed material, Jute, which then can be used for crafting pieces of light homespun armor at any clothing station.
