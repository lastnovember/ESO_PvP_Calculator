Boss Kezo is a Nord chemist and the leader of the Errants. He is found in the center of the Shambles, above and northwest of the entrance to the Outlaws Refugee 152090.

## Skills and Abilities
;Lop:A basic melee attack that does minor physical damage.
;Chop:A basic melee attack that does minor physical damage.
;Puncture:The enemy thrusts their weapon with disciplined precision at a target, dealing minor physical damage and afflicts the target with Major Breach for 15 seconds.
;Fiery Oil/Volatile Pool: The enemy throws a potion that sets the targeted area alight and bounces once forward and creates a second flame area. The pools deal moderate flame damage over time to all players within the red circle.
;Blazing Tar:The enemy will cover their weapon in flaming oil, causing Lop and Chop to become Blazing Lop and Blazing Chop.
;Blazing Lop:A basic melee attack that does minor physical damage and additional fire damage over time.
;Blazing Chop:A basic melee attack that does minor physical damage and additional fire damage over time.
;Healing Fumes:The enemy waves a potion around to emit fumes that heal themselves or nearby allies. This ability is usually used when the enemy is on low health. This can be interrupted to set the enemy off balance. If you stand in the healing fumes, your health can be restored.
