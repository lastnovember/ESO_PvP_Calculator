:"When you're out exploring, keep your ears open for crackling flames-it may save you!"
Flame Atronachs are common yet deadly Daedric creatures of an almost completely malevolent nature. Their graceful motion hides a desire to annihilate all mortals they encounter. They float across the ground, spinning and twirling at leisure, while wreathed in magical fire.

When Molag Bal banished flame atronachs from his realm, one of his denizens took it upon himself to find a replacement. After searching over 37,000 planes and realms, he found a suitable replacement on the Fourth Sinus of Takubar, the apparent inversion of the flame atronach known as the cold-flame atronach.

## Skills and Abilities
As flying enemies, Flame Atronachs are immune to snares and roots.

### Standard
;Flare: A basic ranged attack that deals minor flame damage.
;Radiance: The atronach surrounds itself in an aura of flames, dealing continuous low flame damage to all nearby targets.
;Lava Geyser: The atronach summons a pillar of flame from the ground at its target's location, indicated by a red circle, dealing moderate flame damage.
;Combustion: When defeated, the atronach explodes, indicated by a growing red circle, dealing moderate flame damage to targets caught in the blast.

### Summoned by Councilor Vandacia and SombrenThe Deadlands
Flame Atronachs summoned by Councilor Vandacia and Sombren only have this ability.
;Detonate: Flame atronachs summoned by Councilor Vandacia and Sombren's fire runes rush at targets and explode, indicated by large growing red circles, dealing moderate flame damage and knocking them away.

### Summoned by Kynreeve Kev'ni and The Supernal Trespasser
The Flame Atronachs summoned by Kynreeve Kev'ni and The Supernal Trespasser only have this ability.
;Self Destruct: Flame atronachs summoned by the fire runes rush at targets and explode, indicated by large growing red circles, dealing minor flame damage, knocking back and stunning. This can be blocked to be staggered instead.
