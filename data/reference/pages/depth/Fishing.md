# Online:Fishing

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Fishing
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

A Guide to Fishing Tamriel]]|thumb|left|350px]]

Fishing is one of several activities available in Elder Scrolls Online. It requires bait and a fishing hole. Bait can be gathered from various creatures around the world, such as beetles, rats, and butterflies as well as obtained via fishing or gathering plants. Fishing holes are numerous and close to shore. They are characterized by sparkling water and leaping fish. Different fish can be caught in different locales and respond to appropriate types of bait.

Once at a fishing hole, the next step is to choose the type of bait. Using the correct type of bait for that water type will yield more success and prevent the appearance of Used Bait. If you get one, it means you are using the incorrect bait type for that fishing hole. While fishing, you also have a chance to reel in a Wet Gunny Sack instead of a fish, which contains miscellaneous crafting materials such as trait materials, tempers, style materials, or enchanting runes. When a fish is on the line, the float will sink into the water and your character will pull on the fishing rod. Activating it in time will bring in a fish.

Every fishing hole has 15 catches, except ones in the Imperial City, which have 30. It is possible to come across fishing holes with fewer catches, because players can abandon a fishing hole before it is emptied, leaving the rest of the catches for later players. On rare occasions, a fishing hole can respawn shortly after having been emptied, or even sometimes while you are currently fishing out of it.

When you catch a common fish, you are able to fillet it if you use it from your inventory or bring it to a Cooking Fire. Doing so gives you a guaranteed Fish provisioning ingredient, and also has a very small chance (1%) of yielding a Perfect Roe, one of the provisioning ingredients used to brew Psijic Ambrosia (as well as Orzorga's Smoked Bear Haunch).

## Types of Water

There are four main types of water sources that you can fish from. As detailed in A Guide to Fishing Tamriel, certain types of bait work better in certain types of water, and different common fish are caught depending on the water type. A fifth type of water, "Spawning", is quest-related and isn't usually encountered. Oily, Mystic, and Foul Abyssal are zone-specific water types not found elsewhere.

### Foul

Foul water fishing holes can be found in fetid, murky pools of water such as sewers, swamps, and near decomposing corpses.

### River

River fishing holes can be found in moving bodies of fresh flowing water that drain into a lake or ocean.

### Lake

Lake fishing holes can be found in still bodies of water with deep holes or overgrowth.

### Saltwater

Saltwater fishing holes are found almost anywhere on the coast. Coastal cities, such as Ebonheart and Skywatch, have docks with fishing holes.

### Mystic

Mystic fishing holes are only found on Artaeum, and they function identically to Saltwater fishing holes (although any bait type can be used for the same result).

### Oily

Oily fishing holes are only found in the Clockwork City, and they function identically to Foul fishing holes.

### Foul Abyssal

Foul Abyssal fishing holes are only found in Apocrypha. They function identically to Foul fishing holes, although with different types of fish. You might also rarely fish up a hostile Lurker.

### Spawning

Spawning fishing holes are related to specific quests, as detailed below.

### Abecean Saltwater

Abecean Saltwater fishing holes will only appear in the waters near the Anchorage of the Riotous Redress on the Gold Coast, during the High Seas of Tamriel event. They functionally identically to Saltwater fishing holes, but will offer unique trophy fish.

### Trueflame

During early access, it was possible to very rarely obtain Trueflame while fishing in Stonefalls. This was a Veteran Rank sword that was part of the Arms of Infernace item set. It was quickly removed before the game's official release with Patch 1.0.1, but existing copies of the sword can still be found in-game and can be traded to other players.

As part of ESO's tenth anniversary celebrations, a Trueflame Sword Replica was made available as a weapon style. During the Anniversary Jubilee 2024 event, all fishing nodes had a rare chance to drop a Bound Style Page: Trueflame Sword Replica in reference to the original bug from 2014.

## Fishing Buffs

### Champion Point Perks
There are two Champion perks associated with fishing. Both are in the Craft tree, and both are active (i.e. must be slotted to take effect). These are Reel Technique and Angler's Instincts. The former reduces the time needed for a fish to bite by 25%; the latter increases the chance to snag a rare fish and stacks with other bonuses such as the one from Lure Allure detailed below.

### Fish Eye

:Increases Magicka and Stamina Recovery by x and grants keen eyes toward fish.
Fish Eye is a status effect gained by drinking Hissmir Fish-Eye Rye. In addition to a level-scaled buff to Magicka Recovery and Stamina Recovery, the drink grants "keen eyes" similar to the Keen Eye perks available for crafting material nodes. With this status effect, fishing holes will appear to glow, and the water type will be displayed when you approach (e.g. "River Fish", "Spawning Fish", etc). While the glow effect may be useful in highlighting fishing holes, the secondary effect is largely obsolete since water types are now automatically displayed even without this drink active.

### Lure Allure
:Increases Max Health and Max Magicka. Additionally, fish may be drawn to you.
Lure Allure is a buff gained by eating Artaeum Pickled Fish Bowl. In addition to a level-scaled increase to your maximum Health and Magicka, the food increases your chance of fishing rare fish.

### 

:Fish bite at an increased rate and have a higher chance of being trophy fish.
Sharp's Patience is the Companion Perk granted by Sharp-as-Night, a Companion added by the Necrom Chapter. When he is with you, his perk causes fish to bite at an increased rate, and also increases your chances of catching a trophy fish. Trophy fish obtained due to this perk will have the Companion icon displayed next to them on the UI when caught. Note that the perk does not reduce your chance to hook special items, nor does it affect the rate at which you catch non-trophy fish that can be fileted for Perfect Roe; it only reduces your chance to catch bait.

Summoning Sharp at max rapport after he reaches level 20 will grant you Sharp's Handmade Fishing Lure, a Keepsake which permanently unlocks the Sharp's Patience perk without the need to have him as your active Companion.

## Fishing Achievements

There are many achievements associated with fishing. Every zone except the starter islands has 12 rare fish you can catch. Catching all of the rare fish in a zone will give you that zone's achievement. If you get all of the major zone achievements in one alliance, you'll receive another achievement for that alliance. Get all three of those, along with the starter island achievements and the separate ones for Cyrodiil and Coldharbour, and you'll receive the rare Master Fisher achievement. Note that the fish on the starter islands are not necessary for the alliance fishing achievements, but they are necessary for the Master Fisher achievement. Completing the Master Fisher achievement grants the title "Master Angler".

Craglorn Angler and the DLC achievements are not needed to earn the Master Fisher achievement. Fish Boon Angler is a quest-related holiday event achievement.

## Bugs
Occasionally you will receive the message "You cannot fish in Werewolf form" while attempting to fish.
- This can be fixed by relogging.
Sometimes while fishing at a lake in Auridon, you will catch both a Barbel and a Zebra Oto, the latter of which is a rare fish from the Rift, and will get credit for both.
Some very few fishing holes will only give Used Bait.
Sometimes while fishing in the fishing hole under the bridge to the closed Green Emperor Way District in the Arboretum District, you will catch a Sewer Eel, a Cyrodiil foul water fish.

## Notes
- Trophy fish were curated during the 2025 Pan-Tamriel Celebration, meaning that players were guaranteed to receive a previously uncollected fish. This was particularly useful for completing fishing achievements with a large drop pool, e.g., zones with only Foul water fishing holes.
- Guts are a quest item in The Witchmother's Bargain.
