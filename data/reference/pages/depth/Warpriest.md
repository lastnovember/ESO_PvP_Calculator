# Online:Warpriest

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Warpriest
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Warpriests are a class of hostile, humanoid NPCs who attack using melee and luminous magical abilities. They are usually equipped with heavy armor and wield a sword with a shield. They were introduced in Orsinium with Update 8.

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Strike: A basic melee attack dealing low physical damage.
;Divine Leap: The warpriest leaps high up in the air and crashes down with great force, staggering and dealing moderate physical damage to anyone within the displayed AoE.
;Enrage: When you kill one of their allies, the warpriest yells and gains a red glow, increasing their damage done by 20%. This is the same effect as "Rally".
;Rally:The warpriest waves and points their weapon forward, granting themselves and their nearby allies a red glow, increasing their damage done by 20%. This is the same effect as "Enrage".
