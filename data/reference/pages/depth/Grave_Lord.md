# Online:Grave Lord

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Grave_Lord
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Grave Lord is one of three class skill lines available to Necromancers. It can be learned by other classes via subclassing. Abilities in this line focus on dealing damage. Known abilities in this skill are listed below.
