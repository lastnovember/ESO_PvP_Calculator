# Online:Ropefish

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Ropefish
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Ropefish are a group of Orcs who smuggle escaped slaves out of Vvardenfell.

They initially had a large presence at Khartag Point before they were accosted by the Camonna Tong. After the Ropefish crew refused to let the syndicate force themselves on their operation, they were almost entirely wiped out.

They have another crew in operation in the area surrounding Sadrith Mora, finding steady work in the slave capital on the island.
