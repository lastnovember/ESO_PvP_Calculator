Bavura the Blizzard is a senche-tiger that can be found at Nindaeril's Perch in Grahtwood alongside Nindaeril the Monsoon. The two of them together form a Group Boss duo. They have a chance to drop Nindaeril's Tooth of the Green Pact, a unique item in the Green Pact set.

## Skills and Abilities
As a boss rank enemy, Nindaeril the Monsoon is immune to crowd control.
;Bite: A basic melee attack dealing high physical damage.
;Rend: A basic melee attack that hits twice for moderate physical damage.
;Double Strike: Bavura readies up before swiping its target, dealing very high physical damage. This can be blocked to stagger Bavura instead.
;Hunter's Pounce:Bavura jumps at a distant target which deals very high physical damage, knocks back and stuns the target.
;Regal Fury:If Nindaeril the Monsoon dies first, Bavura enrages as seen by her red glow and a sound cue, which increases her damage done permanently. (Bugged, see bugs)
;Surround:Bavura positions on an different direction from you.
;Backstabber:Bavura has a passive ability that causes it to deal 20% more damage when attacking the target's back.

Monsoon Marauder

## Bugs
For "Regal Fury", if Nindaeril dies first, she's still the one glowing red and not Bavura, though Bavura correctly deals increased damage.
