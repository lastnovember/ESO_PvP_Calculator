## Detailed Walkthrough
After meeting with Lyranth at Madam Whim's, talk to Arox to bring him along with you. Head to Wretched Spire and talk with Rynkyus, who will point you to investigate two sites.

The first is Jynd's Foundry, a short distance north of Wretched Spire. You can chat with Rynkyus when you arrive; otherwise, head into the foundry. It is an open-air site, with mechanical devices all about the area. To investigate the site, locate three curiosities, about each of which Arox will have some commentary: a Daedric Deathstone (located to the east), a set of Jynd's schematics (located to the west), and some orders from Sister Celdina (located to the north). After finding these, go meet Rynkyus further north, at a ritual site near one of the spawn points for the Atoll of Immolation, to conclude this portion of your investigation.

Zynoahz's Gaol (pronounced like "jail") is up next. It is far to the southwest of Wretched Spire. Again, you can chat with Rynkyus, if you wish, before heading in. The gaol is open to the air and filled with dangerous chasms, so don't fall in! Again, you'll need to find three curiosities: a Flawed Runic Crux (located to the east), a mortally wounded Disastrix Daelon (located to the north and who swears that he did not reveal the mysterious "final gift" of "the blind seer") and a sketch by Zynoahz (located to the northwest). Exit the gaol and head north. There, you will witness a conversation between Rynkyus, Lyranth, the Anchorite, and Arox. After revealing Elegian as the inventor of the Cataclyst, Arox will point out that he knows where the inventor is and that neither Rynkyus nor Lyranth will be able to enter the place without triggering alarms.

Head west to reach The Blood Pit. At the south end of it you'll find the Warden's Overlook, reachable by a portal below it. Head up and examine the items there. (See bug below.) By this point, Arox is livid. Head to the Durance Vile, to the west. Inside, speak with the Anchorite, and then figure out how to open Elegian's cell. To open the cell, you'll need to collect some lucents and bring them up to the balcony, place them in the labrums, and activate the Rune of Unsealing. Once this is done, enter his cell. Here, you'll need to find an elixir to calm the mind of the mad man. There are four elixirs in the cell, but speaking with the Anchorite gives you the clue as to which one will work: the Green Grass Elixir. Use it to fill the two braziers near him and he'll regain sanity for a short time. He speaks of his regret about creating the Cataclyst, and says you'll need to head to Annihilarch's Summit to find and destroy the Cataclyst. He will also provide insight as to the mystery behind the identity of the Anchorite: the enchantment he created for her mask resembles that of the Gray Cowl of Nocturnal; it wipes all memory of the previous person's existence -- not just for her, but for everyone who ever knew her! No one who ever knew her before wearing the mask can now remember that she ever existed.

Head back to Wretched Spire to talk with Lyranth and conclude the quest.

## Notes
- Although the note and quest objective description mentions that the blind seer is guarded by someone named Fivax the Oppressor, no such NPC is encountered during the quest, both on the way to the cell, and while leaving it.

## Bugs
After Arox enters your pack at the start of the quest, it will appear again scurrying to Lyranth's side when leaving for the stairs nearby.
Although there are three items on the Warden's Overlook, you won't be able to read the "Warden's Orders for the Day" if you read the Prisoner Report first; it will disappear.
- Workaround: Read the orders (on the left) first.
- The book will reappear on the table after you finish the quest.
