The Argent Ash Titan is a Daedric Titan that can be found the northern part of the southwest settlement of the Night Market's Parch. As an Argent enemy, the nearby Brazen Sellistrix and Taupezu Azzida will need to be slain to lure it out. Conversely, killing it will cause the two Brazen bosses to spawn in the instance.

Once it and Argent Ozezan the Inferno are killed, they will provide Gilded Keys, which can be used to access the Timeless Wallow dungeon. As an Argent enemy, it is a potential target of the related quest.
