# Online:House Hexos

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:House_Hexos
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

House Hexos is a group of Imperial nobles that resides in Fargrave. They are the most influential mortal faction active in the city. Long ago, they gave up their claims in Tamriel for a lucrative position in the realm. They mostly deal in the acquiring and auctioning of rare artifacts, though they also work with other mortal groups as well to foster cooperation. House Hexos
