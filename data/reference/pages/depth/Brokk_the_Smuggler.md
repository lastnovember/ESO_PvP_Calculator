Brokk the Smuggler is an Orc swashbuckler who can be encountered by the river northeast of Black Heights. He owns a sack, which contains an Alfiq named Janessa and starts the related quest when opened.

## Related Quests
- Take Your Lumps

## Skills and Abilities
;Stab:Basic physical attack.
;Off-Hand Strike:A physical attack dealing minor damage. If it hits in the back, it also staggers and applies a bleeding effect. They always use this attack when they reappear behind from using their salt abilities.
;Throw Dagger:The enemy backs away from the player and throws a dagger at the player. This attack causes snare and bleed if it connects. This attack can be interrupted to set the enemy off balance. The dagger can be blocked to prevent the snare and bleed and reduce the damage done.
;Lunge:When far, the swashbuckler charges the target, dealing damage and staggering it.
;Volatile Pool:Also known as Fiery Oil. Ranged AOE attack. Throws potion which causes the targeted area to be set on fire, indicated by a circular AoE. Causes fire damage.
;Fire Salt Explosion:The enemy throws down fire salts, causing an explosion and starting a fire, indicated by a circular AoE, that deals damage over time when you stand in it. The initial blast does fire damage. The enemy disappears and cannot be targeted while the ability is active.
;Burning Fire Salts:The fire started by Fire Salt Explosion. Deals flame damage over time.
;Void Salt Explosion:The enemy throws down void salts, causing an explosion and setting down a gout of lightning, indicated by a circular AoE, that deals damage over time when you stand in it. The initial blast does lightning damage. The enemy disappears and cannot be targeted while the ability is active.
;Burning Void Salts:The pool of lightning caused by Void Salt Explosion. Deals lightning damage over time.
;Roll-Dodge: The swashbuckler rolls to evade incoming damage for a brief period.
;Somersault:The enemy somersaults backwards to put distance between you and them.
;Twirling Lunge: When standing further away, the enemy holds their weapon in a rear-handed stance, with a yellow glow visible around their weapons, then dash forward and strike their target, dealing moderate physical damage and knocking the target back.

## Bugs
The related quest can still be started using the bag on the cliff nearby, while still having the bag Brokk drops in your inventory. Once the quest is started, the inventory bag remains but nothing occurs when interacting with it, and it will disappear once the quest is completed.
