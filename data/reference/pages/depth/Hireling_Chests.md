Hirelings originally sent loot boxes via mail, which could then be opened to obtain the crafting materials. They were removed in Patch 1.1.2 to ensure that the raw materials received were appropriate for the character's level. However, unopened chests were not removed from inventories and can therefore still be seen in-game.
