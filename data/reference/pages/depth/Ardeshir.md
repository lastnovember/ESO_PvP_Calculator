Ardeshir is a Redguard farmer who can be found in Satakalaam. Before the Imperials are driven off, he can be found on the roof of a warehouse in the eastern portion of the city. After completing Imperial Incursion, he's sweeping the steps of the house just east of Nahirah's House.

## Dialogue
He's happy the town has been retaken.
:"I'm joyful this has all ended, though I may never rest easily again. Beware of shifting sands, wayfarer."

## Bugs
Using the talk prompt before speaking with Thoda and completing the location quest causes the dialogue to not trigger correctly.
