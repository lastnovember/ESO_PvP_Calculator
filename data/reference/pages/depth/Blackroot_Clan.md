# Online:Blackroot Clan

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Blackroot_Clan
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Blackroot Clan is a clan of Bosmer warriors located in Greenshade. In life, led by General Malgoth, they conducted a vicious campaign of conquest against surrounding clans and villages, following the Meat Mandate after every battle by consuming the flesh of their enemies.

While seemingly unbeatable, they finally met their end during their raid on Vullain. After eating the fallen, the soldiers realized the villagers had poisoned themselves to defeat the Blackroot Clan in death, and they all perished soon after.

After many years had gone by, the Veiled Heritance arrived in the area, where they raised the Blackroot Clan soldiers as undead warriors to put at their command.
