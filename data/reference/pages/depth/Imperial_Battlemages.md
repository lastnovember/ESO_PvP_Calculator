# Online:Imperial Battlemages

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Imperial_Battlemages
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Imperial Battlemages are a group of war mages serving in the Imperial Legion, trained by the Imperial Battle College at the Battlespire.

They are headquartered in the Battlespire, where they are led by the Spire Council, and can be encountered on Tamriel as part of various missions.
