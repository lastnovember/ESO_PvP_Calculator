Dremora Kyngalds are Dremora fire mages that can be encountered throughout Tamriel.

A Dremora Kyngald appears for the combat tutorial in the Wailing Prison. You must interrupt her as she channels her spell. Four Kyngalds can be encountered within the Brimstone Den in Vateshran Hollows.

The Dremora Kyngalds encountered during Stage 9: Theater of Despair in Maelstrom Arena wear medium armor, wield bows and use fire bombs.

## Skills and Abilities

### Standard
;Flare:A basic ranged attack that does minor flame damage.
;Heat Wave:The enemy releases fire along the ground in the direction it is facing. This attack does moderate flame damage to all targets hit by it. This spell can be interrupted to set the enemy off balance.
;Fire Runes:The enemy shoots two fireballs in the air which form two fire runes when they land. The runes do moderate flame damage over time to all targets within the red circles. Players should be prepared to move as soon as the attack casts as one rune will always be cast on top of you.
;Run Away!:When too close to them, the fire mage backs up to put some distance between their target and themselves.

### Firsthold, The Grotto of Depravity, the Isles of Torment, Knife Ear Grotto, Laeloria Ruins, Moonhenge, Norvulk Ruins, The Refuge of Dread and Wormroot Depths
The Dremora Kyngalds at Firsthold, The Grotto of Depravity, Isles of Torment, Knife Ear Grotto, Laeloria Ruins, Moonhenge, Norvulk Ruins, The Refuge of Dread and Wormroot Depths also use this ability.
;Summon Dark Anchor:The Dremora Kyngald summons a Daedric Arch which in turn periodically summons very weak Banekin to fight for them.

### Imperial CityImperial City
The Dremora Kyngalds in the Imperial City also use those abilities.
;Health Potion:The Dremora Kyngald drinks a potion, restoring 7,524 Health. This can be interrupted to set the Dremora Kyngald off balance.
;Summon Dark Anchor:The Dremora Kyngald summons a Daedric Arch which in turn periodically summons very weak Banekin to fight for them.
