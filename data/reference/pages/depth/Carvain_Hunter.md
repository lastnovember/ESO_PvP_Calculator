Carvain Hunters are Imperial poison archers and members of the Carvain Rowdies faction that can be found in Ontus and Zeggar's Blind.

## Related Quests
- Another Death in Ontus

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Shot:A basic ranged attack that deals minor physical damage.
;Venomous Arrow:A ranged attack that deals moderate poison damage and applies a poison damage over time effect to the target.
;Arrow Spray/Poison Spray:The enemy shoots a spray of poison arrows in the direction it is facing as indicated by a red cone. This attack does moderate poison damage as well as snares all enemies hit. This attack can be interrupted to set the enemy off balance or simply avoided. Blocking also prevents the snare and reduces the damage.
;Roll Dodge:The enemy can roll dodge to avoid attacks.
;Arrow Stab:A basic melee attack that does minor physical damage where the poison archer grabs their arrow and stabs it into the target rather than firing it.
;Noxious Cloud:The enemy shoots an arrow that detonates on contact with the ground at a target's location, leaving a cloud of noxious fumes, which deals continuous low poison damage.
