# Online:Imperial University

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Imperial_University
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Imperial University is an institution of knowledge located in the Imperial City. Due to the siege of the city during the Interregnum, they are not as implicated in worldly matters as they would normally be. They can only be seen as part of a small group that sometimes occupies the Smuggler's Den.
