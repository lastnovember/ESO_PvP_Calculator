Ascendant Militias are Breton and Pahmar-raht umbral aegises and members of the Ascendant Order who can be found at Sareloth Grotto and Sword's Rest Isle, as well as several locations on High Isle.

## Related Quests
- Ascending Doubt‎
- A King's Retreat‎

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Strike:A basic melee attack that does minor physical damage.
;Low Slash:The enemy slashes their target's shins, dealing low physical damage and snaring them. Blocking will reduce the damage taken but doesn't negate the snare.
;Shadow Shield Throw:The enemy throws their shield at their target, dealing low physical damage. They then to its location to catch it, creating a pool of radiating energy, indicated by a red rectangle, which deals continuous low magic damage.
;Javelin:When standing further away, the enemy throws a magical javelin at their target, dealing low physical damage and staggering them. This can be interrupted to set the enemy off-balance.
