## Detailed Walkthrough
You can come across an Argonian called Wuleen-Kur on the shore next to the bridge south of the Bismuth Grotto in eastern Solstice. He is a courier for the Stone-Nest, and was tasked with making a food delivery to Hecicla, a hermit who lives in an abandoned village to the south. However, the many Worm Cultists and Daedra have made the journey highly dangerous and he'll ask for your help with bringing the package to her.

After you agree to help, Wuleen hands you the Hermit's Shipment and tells you Hecicla's home is to the south past the Calindvale Gardens. From Wuleen's location, you can follow the road west, until you find the dirt trail that peels off south from the road. Follow that trail past the Gardens until you reach the small abandoned village, alternatively, you can teleport to the Gristmung Hold Camp Wayshrine and head southwards from there.

Find Hecicla's small home in the village and enter the building, Hecicla will be startled but prepared to defend herself:
:Hecicla: "Who goes there? I warn you, these old scales are not as brittle as they look!"

Talk to Hecicla and let her know you have a package for her, she will reveal it is some Stone-Soup from Xor-Hist and asks you to prepare it for her. You have the option of agreeing to make the soup, or refusing and asking for your payment immediately. If you agreed to make the soup, the package will be opened and you will receive the Uncooked Soup which you can cook at the nearby fireplace place in your home. The result will depend on your provisioning skills, if you cook it well, you will make Cooked Soup. However, if you lack the skills, you will instead make Overcooked Soup.

You can then speak with Hecicla to give her a serving of soup, or if you you refused to cook it, ask for payment. Either way, talking with Hecicla will complete the quest from here:
