Jubilee Cake 2019 is a memento. It was obtained by completing Ache For Cake during the 2019 Anniversary Jubilee event.

## Effect
Causes the player to cheer and place the cake on a stand. It can then be eaten. The cake is decorated with fruits and blue flowers, with an ouroboros on top. During the event, the cake glowed golden, and eating a slice would result in a 100% bonus to experience earned for the next hour. This effect was removed at the end of the event, and the cake lost its glow.

## Physical Appearance
The cake is three layers high. Each layer has two sponges of sweetroll cake with berries baked in, and white icing in between and on top. Each layer is topped with flowers and white balls,What are these and a golden ouroboros on the very top. It is placed on a wooden stand platter and rimmed with blue flowers, almond slices, apples, oranges, and grapes, with vines hanging off. A slice of cake is available as Replica Jubilee Cake Slice 2019.
