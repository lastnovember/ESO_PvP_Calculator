# Online:Murtag Clan

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Murtag_Clan
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Murtag Clan are a clan of Orcs led by General Godrun, a close friend of High King Emeric. They work for the Daggerfall Covenant military. They are currently facing the Ironhand ogres, an old enemy of the Orcs back in Wrothgar.
