Forgotten Mystics were Redguard mummy spirit masters found in the Crypt of the Exiles. They were removed in a very early update.

## Skills and Abilities
;Ice Arrow: Basic ranged attack dealing minor frost damage.
;Burdening Eye: The Forgotten Mystic summons a Burdening Eye that moves toward a player.
;Burden: If the Burdening Eye touches the player, it explodes, dealing minor magic damage and snaring in an area.
;Spell Absorption: The Forgotten Mystic channels a protection spell on an ally that reduces its damage taken by 80% from Magical Attacks (Magic, Flame, Frost and Shock damage). This spell can be interrupted to knock the Forgotten Mystic off balance.
;Run Away!:When too close to them, the Forgotten Mystic backs up to put some distance between their target and themselves.
