Anka-Ra Sentinels are Anka-Ra knights found in various places in Craglorn, mostly in the Belkarth Region.

## Skills and Abilities

### Knights

;Quick Strike: A basic melee attack that does minor physical damage.
;Fiery Breath:Anka-Ra Sentinel breathes fire as indicated by a red cone. This attack does moderate flame damage and snares all enemies within the cone.
;Lava Whip:Anka-Ra Sentinel winds up a flaming whip that does moderate flame damage to the player. This attack can be blocked to set Anka-Ra Sentinel off balance.
;Dark Talons:Anka-Ra Sentinel calls forth talons from the ground, dealing minor flame damage and rooting anyone hit for 4 seconds. The player can roll dodge to escape the root.

### Brutes
;Quick Strike: A basic melee attack that does minor physical damage.
;Low Slash:Anka-Ra Sentinel slashes the player's shins, dealing moderate physical damage and snaring the player.
;Charge:Anka-Ra Sentinel charges forward, doing moderate physical damage and knocking down any player within the red rectangle. Blocking will reduce the damage taken and prevent the knock down. If Anka-Ra Sentinel does not hit anything, it will be stunned for a few seconds.
