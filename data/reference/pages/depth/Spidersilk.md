Spidersilk is a small green plant with green stems covered in spider webs. It can be found in any zone by characters with Rank 4 in the Tailoring passive or character level 36 to 44. When harvested, a plant yields three to four samples of Raw Spidersilk, sometimes accompanied by crawlers or worms. Once ten samples of raw spidersilk collected, they can be refined into seven to ten pieces of the processed material, Spidersilk, which is used for crafting pieces of light spidersilk armor at any clothing station. For you to be able to create spidersilk armor your Tailoring ability has to be rank 4.
