Dwarven Scarabs are small passive Dwarven automatons. They can be found inside Dwemer ruins in the Reach and Blackreach: Arkthzand Cavern, as well as throughout the cavern itself.

## Related Quests
- Thorns and Blossoms

## Unique Dwarven Scarabs
- Portal Curio, a dwarven scarab summoned in Sunhold during the quest Thorns and Blossoms

## Notes
- A Dwarven Scarab pet is also available to obtain as a pet.
- A Dwarven Scarab was previously seen in Redguard, and schematics for them were later seen in Morrowind.

## Bugs
No looting SFX (similar to when looting other types of automatons) play when looting destroyed Dwarven Scarabs.
