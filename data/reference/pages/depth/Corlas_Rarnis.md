Corlas Rarnis is a Breton who can be encountered in Cyrodiil at a mine between Fort Aleswell and Bleaker's Outpost. He is being attacked by three wraiths, and you have the option to intervene and save his life.

## Dialogue
He will cry out for help when you approach.
:Corlas Rarnis: "Help! They're going to kill me!"
If you defeat the wraiths, he thanks you.
:Corlas Rarnis: "Thanks, friend. I'm getting out of here. You should too, before more of them show up!"

## Bugs
His thanking line plays each time one of the wraiths are defeated, instead of when all three are defeated.
