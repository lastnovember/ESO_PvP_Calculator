The Dirge of Thorns is a strangler and one of the bosses of the Deathsong Cleft site in Reaper's March, the other being Ravenous Loam.

## Skills and Abilities
As a boss rank enemy, the Dirge of Thorns is immune to crowd control, except for one case (see Bugs).
;Strike: A basic melee attack dealing minor physical damage.
;Spit: A basic ranged attack dealing minor poison damage.
;Grapple: When standing away from the Dirge of Thorns, it pulls its target to itself using a vine. Blocking prevents being pulled.
;Strangle: The Dirge of Thorns drains the target's blood, dealing moderate physical damage over time. This can be interrupted to stun the Dirge of Thorns. If this fully completes, the Dirge of Thorns becomes empowered and deals 8 more damage for a time.
;Poisoned Ground: The Dirge of Thorns sends out roots at the target's position, indicated by a red circle, which then close onto them, dealing moderate poison damage.

## Notes
- In the very early days of ESO, the Dirge of Thorns was simply named "Strangler"

## Bugs
Interrupting "Strangle" stuns the Dirge of Thorns when it should only stagger it as a crowd control immune enemy.
