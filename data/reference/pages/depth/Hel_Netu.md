# Online:Hel Netu

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Hel_Netu
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Hel Netu are a group of militant monks headquartered on the island refuge of Lep Seclusa. They have a partnership with the Cult of the Ancestor Moth.

Their island has recently been attacked by Orpheon the Tactician.
