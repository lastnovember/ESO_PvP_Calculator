Battlescribe Shadow Bruisers are members of Scribes of Mora found in the Scrivener's Hall.

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Strike:A basic melee attack that does minor physical damage.
;Heavy Strike:Desolated Scholar prepares a heavy attack which deals damage and staggers the target. You can block this attack to put Desolated Scholar off-balance.
;Shadow Ward:Desolated Scholar casts a damage shield for them and nearby allies. You can interrupt this attack to put Desolated Scholar off-balance.
