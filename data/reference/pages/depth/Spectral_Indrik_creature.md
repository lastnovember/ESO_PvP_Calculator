Spectral Indriks are located at the Event Exchanges located outside the main city of each alliance's starting zone. They were found in pens next to the Impresario alongside Nascent Indriks until the end of the 2020 Jester's Festival.

## Notes
- The Haunting Indrik pet is the smaller equivalent of the Spectral Indrik.
