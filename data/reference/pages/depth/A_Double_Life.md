## Detailed Walkthrough
You may find a letter addressed to you from Quen on one of the tables in the Thieves Den.
:"[PlayerName] There's a place near the Den where I go to think. Can you come by when you have a free moment? I need your help with a personal matter. -Quen PS: The mustard stain doesn't represent anything."
:I should meet Quen.

Meet her outside at her Rooftop Retreat to the southeast. She'll tell you that she's looking for her missing father, which is why she tried to steal the Giovessen Skull-to trade it for information. She has come to suspect that her father, a merchant, was actually working with the Thieves Guild. She needs your help to continue her search:
:All right. What do you need?
:"Walks-Softly remembers the Thieves Guild sold many relics to Lady Sulima of Abah's Landing. My father must have been the one to broker the sales. I want to break into her mansion and pick up on his trail. What do you say … partner?"
:Count me in.
:"Meet me by Lady Sulima's mansion when you're ready. I'm not religious, but I said a prayer to Auri-El this morning. If we find nothing, I have no idea what to do next. This is the only trail leading anywhere near my father."
:What do you know about Lady Sulima?
:"The last time I asked around, the Iron Wheel set a trap for us in Fulstrom Homestead. This time, I listened. Lady Sulima rarely leave her estate, and has a connection to one of the merchant lords. Must be why the city's guards patrol her estate. That's all I know."

Make your way to Sulima Mansion, the northernmost estate in the city. Quen will meet you outside the wall surrounding the estate. After a brief exchange, she'll unbar the gate and let you know you can enter. You'll need to sneak past several guards and servants in the courtyard in order to reach the mansion, and you may need to pick a lock to get inside.

Once inside, search upstairs for clues as to Quen's father's whereabouts. Be careful to avoid the guards and servants as you traverse the rooms and hallways. Quen will comment as you discover the clues. Finding the children's book The Elf, the Egg, and the Almost-Dragon:
:Quen: "My father gave me that book when I was a child! At his last visit, he took it to have it re-bound. Why is it in this woman's home?"

Finding Saroldo's Portrait cameo:
:Quen: "I gave that to my father after a holiday in Skywatch. He kept it with him for luck. What is it doing here?"

She will also find a key:
:Quen: "I found something too-a necklace with an old key. My father wore this wherever he went! And if there's a key, there must be a lock …."

Searching around one of the rooms will point you towards a suspicious wardrobe, which reveals a secret room and the Lady Sulima herself. Quen will immediately start questioning her.
:Quen: "You! Why do you have my father's things?"
:Lady Sulima: "Tu'whacca's breath! Saroldo's daughter stands before me. Why are you in Abah's Landing instead of your academy?"
:Quen: "I'm looking for my father! Why do you know who I am?"
:Lady Sulima: "My dear child … he's gone. When the Iron Wheel came for him, he fled to the rooftops. They gave chase and he fell. I'm so sorry."
:Quen: "You're lying!"
:Lady Sulima: "No, child. I grieve for him every day. He was the only person I've ever loved."

Speak to Lady Sulima to ask why she didn't inform Quen sooner. She will explain that Saroldo gave her very little information about his daughter (not even her name). In addition, the Iron Wheel knows Lady Sulima was connected to Quen's father, and she is afraid she will be killed if she leaves the protection of the mercenaries guarding her house. She does mention that she recently found a ledger belonging to Saroldo, and since she can't make sense of its symbols, she gives it to you to pass on to Quen. She then points out a discreet backdoor through which you can exit.

Quen, feeling overwhelmed, has left the building. Meet her outside and give her Saroldo's Ledger:
:"What's wrong with me? I'm a High Elf, I'm not supposed to cry."
:Lady Sulima wanted you to have this ledger.
:"It must be important if the Iron Wheel wants it. Father did love his puzzles. I should see if it means anything. Thank you for this. It's not the answer I wanted, but I'm glad you were by my side."

This completes the quest. Quen will return to the Thieves Den once she has had time to process.

## Bugs
While sneaking within the mansion, there is a chance that Quen may disappear as your follower when going through the mini-loading screens between the rooms.
