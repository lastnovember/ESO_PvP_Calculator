Flame-Herald Bahsei is a Naga warrior-mage and member of the Sul-Xan tribe who can be found atop the Xanmeer Pinnacle in Rockgrove. She is the second boss of the trial. She is the leader of the forces that attacked Rockgrove.

Bahsei is a worshipper of Mehrunes Dagon and has opened an Oblivion Gate in an effort to summon Xalvakka to Tamriel. She has used her magic to harvest the souls of all who have died in the attack on the settlement, to be sacrificed to Xalvakka. When you confront her, she will transform into a Bone Goliath before the fight starts.

## Skills and Abilities
As a boss rank enemy, Flame-Herald Bahsei is immune to crowd control.
;Carve: A double-hitting basic melee attack, dealing moderate physical damage twice.
;Slice: A double-hitting basic melee attack, dealing slightly more damage than "Carve".
;Rend Flesh: Flame-Herald Bahsei strikes a pose with her dual blades, before delivering a powerful overhead blow with her left blade, dealing massive physical damage. This can be blocked to stagger Flame-Herald Bahsei instead.
;Skull Salvo: Flame-Herald Bahsei raises her sword up and very rapidly shoots blue skulls at all players in order, each dealing high frost damage. This can be interrupted to stagger Flame-Herald Bahsei.
;Cursed Ground/Unholy Spike: Flame-Herald Bahsei targets the two farthest players and summons lich crystals at their feet, creating an area on the ground dealing moderate frost damage, snaring and applying "Death Touch" anyone caught in the area around those crystals (Cursed Ground) before exploding, dealing extreme frost damage in an area (Unholy Spike).
;Death Touch/Kiss of Death: More commonly called the "Curse", deals continuous high frost damage over 10 seconds (Death Touch), then sends two magical swirls away from the affected player on a counter-clockwise curved trajectory. These swirls, if touched, will apply the same curse, and also explode, dealing high frost damage to other players nearby (Kiss of Death).
;Sickle Strike: Flame-Herald Bahsei swings with her magical scythe at the taunted target, sending three tornadoes of corrupted magic their way dealing high physical damage, staggering and applying "Death Touch".
;Meteor Swarm: When Flame-Herald Bahsei reaches 50% Health, she triggers this ability where she calls on a rain of meteors onto the arena, indicated by several large red circles, each dealing very high flame damage. When she reaches 30% Health, this becomes an execute-phase as more meteors are landing and occasionally one of them turns into Prime Meteor after landing, and if it isn't destroyed within ten seconds, it explodes, one-shotting with flame damage nearby targets.
;Summoning Runes: Before the battle, Flame-Herald Bahsei will place six runes around the arena. During the fight, Forgotten Archers are occasionally summoned from the runes. Also, at Health thresholds of 90% / 85% / 80% / 75% / 70% / 65% / 60%, Bahsei will summon a Flesh Abomination at one of the runes. Following the 55% Health threshold, these summons cease, in favor of the three following summons.
;Summon Behemoth: At health thresholds of 50% / 40% / 25% / 20% / 10%, a Fire Behemoth will rush out of the Oblivion portal.
;Summon Specters: From 55% Health threshold and onward, occasionally, Burning Specters will be summoned from the lava surrounding the arena.
;Dagon's Wrath: From 55% Health threshold and onward, moving walls of flames will occasionally run through the arena, starting from the lava surrounding the arena, each dealing very high flame damage and knocking back. These don't have AoE indicators.

### Hard Mode
In hard mode, Flame-Herald Bahsei deals more damage and has more health as expected, but also brings in a whole new threat.
;Creeping Eye/Bitter Marrow/Soul Crusher/Grave Thorn/Embrace of Death/Malignant Marrow:Periodically a Coldharbour Sentinel-like eye appears in the center of the arena, three players must go near it which stuns them for 3 seconds then transports them into a spirit world without enemies while being dealt continuous moderate-high frost damage (Bitter Marrow). They must kill still ghostly passive wraiths while three areas that one-shot with frost damage slowly follow them until all wraiths are dead (Soul Crusher). Killing a wraith deals 50% of the player's Max Health as frost damage (Grave Thorn), though the damage can be dodged by using certains skills such as Mist Form. Then three pillars of blue light in the spirit world and one in the original world appear from which four players must activate "Creeping Eye" at the same time to blast the eye with a beam. When you succeed, the three players in the spirit world exit it and get applied "Malignant Marrow" for 2 minutes which kills them if they try to reuse "Creeping Eye" in that timeframe. During all this in the original world, the eye's gaze one-shots as frost damage in a wide blue cone taking a quarter of the arena which slowly travels either clockwise or anti-clockwise and has no indicators (Embrace of Death).
