# Online:Gold Coast Trading Company

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Gold_Coast_Trading_Company
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Gold Coast Trading Company is a trading company who, among other business interests, hire out their kiosks to guilds. They are listed as the sellers of tabards in Guild Stores. They also manufacture and distribute Crown Crates. Their logo can be seen on the cards, as well as the crates themselves. Some items found within crates also contain their name, such as the elixirs, poisons, and experience scrolls.

The company has an office in Anvil, and have operations within the Ayleid ruins of Garlas Agea on the Gold Coast. A related quest reveals the Gold Coast Trading Company import dangerous creatures from Craglorn.
