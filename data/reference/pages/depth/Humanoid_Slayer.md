Humanoid Slayer is awarded for killing humanoid enemies.

The achievement is divided into 3 criteria:
Criteria | Num. Req. | Notes

Kill Humanoids | 1000 | Includes members of all the playable Races, but not other players in PvP plain.

Kill Goblin-kin | 500 | Includes Goblins, Riekr, etc.

Kill Giant-kin | 100 | Includes Giants, Ogres (but not Ogrim), etc.
