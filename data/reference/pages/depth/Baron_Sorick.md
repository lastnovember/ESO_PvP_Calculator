Baron Sorick is a Breton noble in charge of the settlement of Dreughside and can be found on the top floor of Arbogasque's home. He has little love for the peasants that live on his land.

## Related Quests
- The Sower Reaps

## Quest-Related Events
After finding the suspicious sack in the barracks, you will find Baron Sorick in Arbogasque's home:
:"What's this? Another guttersnipe here to whine about the dreugh?"
:I have your note ordering your men to plant the eggs in Dreughside. It proves you're to blame.
:"Pfui. I'll just say you wrote it yourself. It's my word against yours, and I'm a Breton nobleman. Don't waste my time."
:There's supporting evidence: Arcady Charnis saw your men placing the eggs. Worried yet?

You will have to choose whether you'll accept a bribe, expose the Baron or threaten him to put the eggs back where they belong:
:"I admit that might be enough to be … inconvenient. Both Dreughside and the hatching pools are in my fief. Both are infested with pests. I just came up with a brilliant way to have them exterminate each other. What of it, lowborn?"
:Maybe nothing. For the right price, I'll keep this just between us. [Pay Gold]
::"I knew you'd understand. Soon, the dreugh will clear these lowlifes out. Then I'll have my land back. Here, this ought to cover your trouble. As for Charnis, I'll deal with him."
:I think I'll expose your plan, and we'll see how brilliant it is.
::The conversation ends and Baron Sorick will run away shouting:
::Baron Sorick: "You'll never catch me, fool!"
:[Intimidate] Get those eggs out of Dreughside, or I'll kill you where you stand.
::"You, kill me? A Lord of Wayrest? Perhaps you would. You have that look about you. Very well. I'll have my men remove the eggs. Satisfied?"
::What about the Dreughsiders? You owe them something.
::"What do you want me to say? I'll give them their land back and compensate them for this … minor inconvenience. After that, I'm leaving! I refuse to live in the mud. Emeric can find a swineherd to rule his pen of pigs."

If interacted with again before picking a dialogue option:
:"I see no point in debate. Take my money, and be on your way."

If you chose to take his bribe but exited the conversation before turning in the quest, he will alternatively say:
:"I knew you'd see things my way. These people are scum."
:I'd like my money now. (Leads to quest completion dialogue)

If you chose to threaten him into returning the eggs and compensating the Dreughsiders before turning in the quest, he will alternatively say:
:"You have my word. I'll have my men remove the eggs. For Mara's sake, stop looking at me like that!"
:You will make amends for what you did. (Leads to quest completion dialogue)

If Sorick attempted to escape, he can be found just outside with an injured leg.
:"My leg! One of these ruffians broke my leg!"
:These people may do worse, now that they know what you did.
:"Please, don't leave me here! Have mercy!"

If you talk to him again before deciding on his fate:
:"Please. Show mercy. You can't leave me here with these savages."

If you allow Constable Charlic to take him into custody and before you turn in the quest:
:"You showed mercy. Thank you!"

If you let the angry Dreughsiders dish out their own justice before you turn in the quest:
:"You're just like them. You're trash!"

After the quest depending on what has happened to Baron Sorick.

If you persuaded him to make things right, he'll add:
:"I'll compensate the Dreughsiders. I'll leave, stay in Wayrest. Please, just let me go free!"

If you told Constable Charlic to apprehend him before the mob lynches him:
:"Thank you for not leaving me with this mob. I wouldn't have lasted long."

If you let him pay you off, he'll remark:
:"Must be off. More dreugh could arrive at any time."
