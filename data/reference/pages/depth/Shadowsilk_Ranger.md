Shadowsilk Rangers were goblins pet rangers of the Shadowsilk Tribe who could be found in Mephala's Nest. They were last recorded the 4th April 2017 and were very likely removed with Patch/2.7.13 which added The Missing Prophecy quest which involves Mephala's Nest for Ebonheart Pact players.

## Skills and Abilities
;Quick Shot: A basic ranged attack dealing minor physical damage.
;Call Ally: The pet ranger blows a horn and summons an animal companion to fight. This animal is usually appropriate to the zone the pet ranger appears in, and for Goblin Pet Rangers the animal spawns with the summoner's current Health. The Pet Ranger's Physical Resistance and Spell Resistance are reduced to 0 while a summon is active, as seen by the blue glow on them. The pet ranger can only have one animal companion at a time and it will instantly die if the pet ranger is killed. The Pet Ranger cannot cast this ability if an animal is already in the fight.
;Roll Dodge: The pet ranger may perform a roll dodge to avoid attacks.
;Trap Beast:The pet ranger places a trap which deals low magic damage and roots you in place if you walk into it.
