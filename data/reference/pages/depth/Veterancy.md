# Online:Veterancy

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Veterancy
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Veterancy is a reward system for the PvP modes in Elder Scrolls Online that was introduced towards the end of Season Zero with Update 50. It has unique cosmetics, titles, perks for the Vengeance Campaign in Cyrodiil. Veterancy has 100 Ranks, at Rank 50 and beyond, you can earn permanent titles. Once you reach rank 100, you will start earning progress for a repeatable reward.

While in Veterancy zones, the HUD will display your temporary seasonal title, where the CP level is normally displayed. Each Veterancy Season will last for approximately 6 months. Progress through the Veterancy ranks can be achieved by earning XP and Alliance Points from participating in Cyrodiil Campaigns, Imperial City and Battlegrounds. This include completing daily quests in these areas.
