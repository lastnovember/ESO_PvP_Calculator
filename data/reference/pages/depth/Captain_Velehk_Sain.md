Captain Velehk Sain is a Dremora pirate and captain of the Caterwaul. After his previous defeat at the Dark Anchors, he lost favor with Molag Bal, and so he was charged with hunting down Captain One-Eye and reclaiming the Crown of Bones from her. The chase across the azure seas took at turn when Captain One-Eye sailed the Bonnie Spriggan through a whirlpool, and the Caterwaul followed. This led to both ships crashing down in Solstice, at the now named Caterwaul Cove.

Captain One-Eye will want to deal with him before he sets sail onto Nirn's waters.

## Skills and Abilities
As an elite rank enemy, Captain Velehk Sain is immune to crowd control. Almost all his attacks are identical to Captain Numirril's abilities. At the start of the fight are periodically summoned: Banekin, Scamps, Soul Shriven Raveners, Soul Shriven Reavers, and Soul Shriven Spellfiends.

During the fight, Khag will periodically fire a coldfire ballista bolt at enemies as indicated by a large green circle, dealing 2,024 flame damage on hit in an area (Cold Fire Ballista Bolt) then 1,286 flame damage over time to enemies hit (Burning).
;Strike/Battered: A basic melee attack dealing minor physical damage and applying a stack of "Battered" for 16 seconds. "Battered" deals continuous direct minor physical damage.
;Swell: Captain Velehk Sain rises up on a water spout, then comes swinging down on you with his sword, dealing low frost damage and staggering.
;Jet: Captain Velehk Sain suddenly dashes across the screen while riding a water spout, indicated by a circular AoE beneath him, dealing low physical damage, knocking back and stunning. This can be blocked to be staggered instead. This often leads into "Spout".
;Spout: Captain Velehk Sain summons numerous large water spouts back to back close to each other, tracking close behind your movement, indicated by circular AoEs dealing moderate frost damage each. (See Bugs)
;Drown:A column of water will lift you unless blocked or dodged. You are dealt low frost damage upon being picked up, minor frost damage over time as you drown then low frost damage as the attack ends. You are stunned for the whole duration and it cannot be broken free from.
;:Captain Velehk Sain periodically blows a horn to summon 2 Lightningfang Vipers.
;Liftoff: Upon reaching 84% and 41% Health, Captain Velehk Sain dashes outside of the arena and rests on a tall water spout overlooking the arena, becoming invulnerable to incoming damage. At 84% Health, among many other adds, he will summon a Voidmother that you must kill to end the phase. From now on Caterwaul Bushwackers, Caterwaul Darkbows, Caterwaul Freebooters, Caterwaul Geomancers, Caterwaul Paindancers, Caterwaul Serpent-Tongues, Caterwaul Swashbucklers and Winged Twilights start to be periodically summoned while Banekin, Scamps and Soul Shriven stop being summoned. At 41% Health, among many other adds, he summons a Maldrith that you must kill to end the phase. From now on Shackled Titans will periodically be summoned.
;Splash:During "Liftoff", Captain Velehk Sain summons large roiling water waves across the arena as indicated by massive circular AoEs, dealing low frost damage and knocking you back if hit.
