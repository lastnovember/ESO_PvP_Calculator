Carved Mages are stone Bretons cauterizer accompanying Menhir Stoneskin.

## Skills and Abilities
;Minor Wound:A basic ranged attack dealing moderate magic damage.
;Focused Healing:The Carved Mage will heal any allies that are hurt, including itself but for half as much. Enemies often indicate this by calling out for healing (I'm hurt!, I need healing!, Healer! etc.). This effect appears as a yellow beam connecting the Carved Mage and its target which breaks if the line of sight between them is broken. The first tick begins 0.5 seconds after the channel begins, and also heals half as much for the first 3 ticks. This can be interrupted to set the Carved Mage off balance. The Carved Mage never targets summons from themselves or their allies, for example Ice Barrier.
;Heat Wave:The Carved Mage releases fire along the ground in the direction it is facing, dealing moderate-high flame damage to all targets hit by it. This can be interrupted to set the Carved Mage off balance.
;Fire Runes:The Carved Mage shoots two fireballs in the air which form two fire runes when they land. The runes deal continuous direct moderate flame damage to all targets within the red circles. Players should be prepared to move as soon as the attack casts as one rune will always be cast on top of you.
;Rite of Passage:The Carved Mage channels a spell healing itself and nearby allies for 10% of the Carved Mage's Max Health every half second for 4 seconds. It takes half a second to cast, and another half a second to begin healing its target once the effect is applied. This ability is usually used when the Carved Mage is on low health. This can be interrupted to set the Carved Mage off balance.
;Run Away!:When too close to them, the Carved Mage backs up to put some distance between their target and themselves.
;Grand Healing:The Carved Mage summons restoring spirits with its staff, healing itself and nearby allies in the target area for 8 seconds. Try to pull or move the enemies out of the healing circle to stop them from healing.plain

## Bugs
Despite being champion rank enemies, the Carved Mages are vulnerable to crowd control.
