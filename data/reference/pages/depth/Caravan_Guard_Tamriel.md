Caravan Guards were Tamriel Guards protecting the Baandari Caravan, the Martyr's Crossing's Baandari camp and the traveling merchants Beshnus, Io-Io, Jambi, Jomer the Sharp, Murak-Lei and Wemeerit. Several Caravan Guards were also at a crossroad south of the Cave of Memories with two Pact Guards. The two accompanying Io-Io and Wemeerit were replaced by Caravaneer Iggistill. The one at Martyr's Crossing was replaced by the Caravan Watchman. They were replaced by Covenant Guards at the Baandari Caravan. They had no unique dialogue. They were removed or replaced with Update 6 which added the Justice system, and all NPCs with "Guard" in their name got renamed.

All Caravan Guards were removed or changed with Update 6, except one unique Caravan Guard that was under the big mushroom at the Caravan Crest, next to a cowering Villager. They would help you take down Bittergreen the Wild if the boss was close enough to them. They had no unique dialogue. They were last seen in mid September 2016.
