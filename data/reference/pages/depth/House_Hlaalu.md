# Online:House Hlaalu

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:House_Hlaalu
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

House Hlaalu is one of the Great Houses of Morrowind. Their house seat, Narsis, on the banks of Lake Hlaalu, is an important trade city. Their main priorities are business and trade, making compromise a way of life. As a result of that, they had no real objection to the Ebonheart Pact, rather they saw it as an opportunity to open more trading routes.

Following a golden age under Grandmaster Delmene centuries ago, the Hlaalu are long past their prime and currently at their nadir, clinging to what little settlements they control including Narsis and Seyda Neen. House Redoran currently control Balmora and Suran, which are only built in the Hlaalu style as part of a construction contract.
