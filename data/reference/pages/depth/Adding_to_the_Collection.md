## Detailed Walkthrough
Travelling through the Parch, you may come across the scroll Add to the Collector's Villais it always same spot or is it random?. The Watcher Gazmod the Collector has put out a request for several strange curios and has added approximate locations for them:
- Kaleidoscopic Spyglass - "last seen in the northeastern portion of the Parch in the rock-strewn shadow of a building" (Northeastern Parch, northwest of the Timeless Wallow dungeon)
- Hindsight Basin - "last seen in the northernmost portion of the Parch, near where a beetle's claws snap and bite" (Northern Parch, northwest of the Brazen Incineration Beetle boss)
- Unlit Wick - "last seen in the western portion of the Parch, where wares are polished by the sands" (Western Parch, north of the Brazen Sellistrix boss)

Once you have collected the three items, head to the rooftop oasis in the northeast corner of the map and use the Collector's Item Collection Box:

You will receive some gold, a Curated Tamriel Reward Coffer, and 40 Faction Favor.
