Azeez-Eix was an Argonian member of the Mages Guild found in the hall in the city of Ebonheart. She spent her time firing her frost staff off at the nearby training dummy.

## Notes
- According to a note, Azeez-Eix may have been romantically involved with a Dunmer named Lalisii Dres.
- There is another note addressed to one such Pash-Riha of Lukiul Uxith, suggesting that Lalisii Dres is either seeing both of them, or the note in question replaces the one addressed to Azeez-Eix after her removal from the game.
