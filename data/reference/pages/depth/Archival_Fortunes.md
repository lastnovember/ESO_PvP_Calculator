# Online:Archival Fortunes

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Archival_Fortunes
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Archival Fortunes are the currency earned when battling through the Infinite Archive and can be used to purchase a number of items from the merchants within. ESO Plus members earn 10% more Archival Fortunes from battling in the Infinite Archive.

Archival Fortunes can be used to purchase a variety of Furnishings, containers of materials used in Crafting, including those for Furnishing Crafting, Curated Armor Boxes, Visions, Verses, Antiquity Leads, Deck Fragments, Clues and Threads of Fate.

## Sources
You earn Archival Fortunes from the following:
- Looting a Muniment Chest. The higher the Arc the more Archival Fortunes you will get.
- Looting a Marauder. The higher the Arc the more Archival Fortunes you will get.
- Archival Fortunes Container from Esalinwe's, Destozuno's and Master Malkhest's quests: Base 500
- Digging up Archival Fortune Pouch (200), Archival Fortune Pouch, Grand (1,000), Archival Fortune Pouch, Lucky (2,000) in Apocrypha, from antiquity leads obtained in the Infinite Archive.
