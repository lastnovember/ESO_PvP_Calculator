## Detailed Walkthrough
This quest culminates in a pivotal battle against the Ascendant Order and their leader, the Ascendant Magus.

Begin the quest by talking to Lady Arabelle Davaux at Mandrake Manor in Gonfalon Bay. She informs you of the Ascendant Order's impending attack on All Flags Islet and requests your assistance. Use the provided ship to sail to All Flags Islet. Upon arrival, you'll find the island under siege by Ascendant Order. Join forces with King Emeric, Queen Ayrenn, and Prince Irnskar to repel the attackers. Engage in battles across the castle grounds, ensuring the safety of the alliance leaders. Accompany Prince Irnskar to the battlements. Together, fend off multiple waves of Ascendant Order soldiers.

Proceed to the castle courtyard, where the Ascendant Magus awaits. Engage in a challenging battle, utilizing crowd control and healing abilities as needed. After defeating the Magus, exit the castle.

Outside the castle, find Lady Arabelle and speak with her to conclude the quest. She commends your efforts and discusses the implications of the recent events.

## Bugs
Exiting through the same door you enter might block progress.
