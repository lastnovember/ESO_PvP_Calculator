Agganor is an Orc shaman of the Iron Orcs who as directly allied with the Scaled Court. He can be found at Exarch's Stronghold. He summons Scaled Court Coldbloods and Venomblades during combat.

## Related Quests
- Iron and Scales

## Skills and Abilities
Agganor uses a mix of shaman and time bomb mage class abilities, as well as one additional unique ability:
;Coiled Lash: A basic ranged attack that does minor magic damage.
;Entropic Flare:A basic ranged attack that does minor magic damage. (Only used in melee range and most probably bugged)
;Summon Aura of Protection: The enemy summons an Aura of Protection totem that gives nearby allies increased resistance to damage. The totem can be destroyed in five hits from any source or one heavy attack. Destroying the totem will stun all enemies nearby.
;Void:The enemy summons a purple orb from the sky that explodes when it hits the ground splitting into three projectiles dealing moderate magic damage. This skill then sends out magic AoEs across the floor called Void Bursts. This attack can be interrupted to set the enemy off balance or simply avoided.
;Void Burst:These AoEs are released after Void lands, the smaller AoEs deal minor magic damage.
;Summon Allies:At the start of the fight and multiple health thresholds, Agganor summons a Scaled Court Coldbloods or a Venomblades to fight for him.
;Stone Armor: Upon reaching 25% health, Agganor stomps his staff into the ground and encases himself in stone, becoming invulnerable to all damage for a few seconds. At the same time, one of each of his summoned allies appear by his side.

:Agganor: "Stones and steel protect me!"

When casting Stone Armor:

:Agganor: "It cannot defeat Agganor! The Stones will not allow it!"

## Notes
- He used to have a higher difficulty, and he used to have much more HP than he currently possesses. However, at some point, his difficulty was reduced along with his health, most likely coinciding with the update that made Craglorn zone questline soloable, as his location at Exarch's Stronghold is a necessary part of that zone quest.

## Bugs
Agganor's basic melee attack is bugged as the animation shows him doing a light attack with his staff that launches no projectile and deals damage after a ~1 second delay.
