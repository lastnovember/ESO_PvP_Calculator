# Online:Crystaljack Cocoon

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Crystaljack_Cocoon
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Crystaljack Cocoons are cocoons housing Crystaljacks found in Leftwheal Trading Post, Legion's Rest and the West Weald. Upon approach, it bursts open, dealing low magic damage in an area around it (Prism Cocoon Burst) and spawns a Crystaljack to fight you. Note that enemies, including the spawned Crystaljack, take damage from this attack.

## Bugs
The summoned Crystaljacks are bugged and do not attack.
