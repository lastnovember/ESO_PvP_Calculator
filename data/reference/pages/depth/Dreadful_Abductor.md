Dreadful Abductors are watchers who target one member of the group and kidnap them, whisking them away to a location near one of the secret bosses. The kidnapped player cannot survive alone, and must be rescued.

## Dialogue
When a Dreadful Abductor kidnaps someone, they'll speak to the victim.

When kidnapping a player near Gedna Relvel's haunt:
:Dreadful Abductor: "You're rather thin, but the Red Witch will be pleased nonetheless."
:Dreadful Abductor: "You have much life in you. My Lady will gladly take it."
:Dreadful Abductor: "I caught one!"
:Dreadful Abductor: "You get special treatment. Be very afraid."
:Dreadful Abductor: "You have the Red Witch's attention. That's not a good thing."

When caught near the torturers:
:Dreadful Abductor: "The torturers took special interest in you."
:Dreadful Abductor: "I'm getting better at this!"
:Dreadful Abductor: "There's special levels of pain in store for you."
:Dreadful Abductor: "You won't enjoy this, but that's rather the point."
:Dreadful Abductor: "You'll be dead before your friends can save you. Enjoy!"

When caught near the vampire lord:
:Dreadful Abductor: "Plump and blood filled, just what I was told to retrieve."
:Dreadful Abductor: "You seem alive. My Lady will enjoy that."

:Dreadful Abductor: "Come with me, mortal. You've been summoned."
