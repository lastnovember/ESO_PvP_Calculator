Beenumpa is an Argonian who can be found in the Infinite Archive's Echoing Den. He requires your help.

## Dialogue
As you approach him, Beenumpa will say one of the following:
:Beenumpa: "I thought the frog would stop this from happening!"

:Beenumpa: "Did the frog do this?"

As the timer approaches the end:
:Beenumpa: "I feel funnier than I did before. It makes my fins shake."

If you manage to save him:
:Beenumpa: "I erect the spine of gratitude. Now, if you'll excuse me, I have a frog to find."
