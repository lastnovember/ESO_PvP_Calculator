Bog Dogs are a species of stout pack hunters native to Blackwood. They disguise themselves in the marshes of the region with vegetation that appears to grow on their backs. They are not related to actual dogs.

## Skills and Abilities
;Bite:The bog dog attacks with its maw, dealing low physical damage.
;Claw:The bog dog attacks with its claw, dealing slightly more physical damage than "Bite".
;Back Kick:If standing behind the bog dog, it will kick with its hind legs, indicated by a red cone, dealing moderate physical damage and staggering targets.
;Leap: The bog dog leaps around its target, though it doesn't deal damage.

## Unique Bog Dogs
- Bethany, a friendly bog dog found in the Atoll of Immolation at the conclusion of the Lost in the Wilds achievement
- Gorra, a bog dog found in Shipwright's Regret Ascending Tide

## Variants
- Bog Dog Bonecrushers, bog dogs found in the hills southeast of Leyawiin and in the Dawnwood
- Moss Hounds, bog dogs found in Shipwright's Regret Ascending Tide
- Wildburn Bog Dogs and Wildburn Bog Dog Bonecrushers, bog dogs found in the Wildburn
