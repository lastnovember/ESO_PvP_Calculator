Rubedo Leather is a crafting material obtained from animals in any zone by characters with Rank 10 in the Tailoring passive or CP150 to 160. One animal may yield one to two samples of the raw material, Rubedo Hide Scraps. Once ten scrap pieces are collected, they can be refined into seven to ten samples of the processed material, Rubedo Leather. Rubedo Leather is used for making medium armor at any clothing station.
