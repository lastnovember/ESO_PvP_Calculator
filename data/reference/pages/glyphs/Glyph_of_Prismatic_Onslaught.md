# Online:Glyph of Prismatic Onslaught

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Glyph_of_Prismatic_Onslaught
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Glyphs of Prismatic Onslaught are created by using a Hakeijo rune and a Subtractive Potency rune. They can be applied to any weapon of equal or greater level, and deals damage and restore a small amount of Health, Magicka and Stamina.
