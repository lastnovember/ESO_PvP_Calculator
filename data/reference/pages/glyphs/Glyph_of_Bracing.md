# Online:Glyph of Bracing

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Glyph_of_Bracing
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Glyphs of Bracing are created by using a Kaderi rune and a Subtractive Potency rune. They can be applied to any jewelry of equal or greater level, and will reduce the Stamina cost of Blocking.
