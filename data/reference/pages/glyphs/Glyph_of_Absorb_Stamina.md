# Online:Glyph of Absorb Stamina

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Glyph_of_Absorb_Stamina
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Glyphs of Absorb Stamina are created by using a Deni rune and a Subtractive Potency rune. They can be applied to any weapon of equal or greater level, and will deal Physical Damage and restore your Stamina you when you attack.
