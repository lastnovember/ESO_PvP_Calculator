# Online:Glyph of Increase Physical Harm

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Glyph_of_Increase_Physical_Harm
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Glyphs of Increase Physical Harm are created by using a Taderi rune and an Additive Potency rune. They can be applied to any jewelry of equal or greater level, and increase your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage. They also provide 10 Stamina Recovery at all levels and qualities.
