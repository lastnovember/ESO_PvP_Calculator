# Online:Glyph of Poison

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Glyph_of_Poison

Glyphs of Poison are created by using a Kuoko rune and an Additive Potency rune. They can be applied to any weapon of equal or greater level, and will add Poison Damage to your attacks.
