# Online:Glyph of Reduce Skill Cost

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Glyph_of_Reduce_Skill_Cost
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Glyphs of Reduce Skill Cost are created by using a Indeko rune and an Subtractive Potency rune. They can be applied to any jewelry of equal or greater level, and will decrease the Magicka Cost, Health Cost and Stamina Cost of abilities while worn.
