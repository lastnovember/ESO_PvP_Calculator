# Online:Glyph of Reduce Feat Cost

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Glyph_of_Reduce_Feat_Cost
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Glyphs of Reduce Feat Cost are created by using a Denima rune and a Subtractive Potency rune. They can be applied to any jewelry of equal or greater level, and will reduce the Stamina cost of abilities when worn.
