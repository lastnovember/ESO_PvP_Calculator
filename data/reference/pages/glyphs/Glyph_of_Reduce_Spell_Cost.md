# Online:Glyph of Reduce Spell Cost

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Glyph_of_Reduce_Spell_Cost
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Glyphs of Reduce Spell Cost are created by using a Makkoma rune and a Subtractive Potency rune. They can be applied to any jewelry of equal or greater level, and will reduce the Magicka cost of abilities when worn.
