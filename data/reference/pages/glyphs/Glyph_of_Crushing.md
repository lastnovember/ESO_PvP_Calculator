# Online:Glyph of Crushing

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Glyph_of_Crushing
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Glyphs of Crushing are created by using a Deteri rune and a Subtractive Potency rune. They can be applied to any weapon of equal or greater level, and will reduce your targets' Spell Resist and Physical Resist for 5 seconds when attacking. The debuff icon is Glyph of Crushing
