# Online:Glyph of Frost

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Glyph_of_Frost

Glyphs of Frost are created by using a Dekeipa rune and an Additive Potency rune. They can be applied to any weapon of equal or greater level, and will add Frost Damage to your attacks.
