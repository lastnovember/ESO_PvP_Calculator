# Online:Glyph of Weapon Damage

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Glyph_of_Weapon_Damage
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Glyphs of Weapon Damage are created by using an Okori rune and an Additive Potency rune. They can be applied to any weapon of equal or greater level, and will increase your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage for 5 seconds after attacking.
