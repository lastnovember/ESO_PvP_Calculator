# Online:Glyph of Potion Boost

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Glyph_of_Potion_Boost
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Glyphs of Potion Boost are created by using an Oru rune and an Additive Potency rune. They can be applied to any jewelry of equal or greater level, and increases the duration of all consumed Potions.
