# Online:Glyph of Hardening

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Glyph_of_Hardening
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Glyphs of Hardening are created by using a Deteri rune and an Additive Potency rune. They can be applied to any weapon of equal or greater level, and will grant you a Damage Shield for 5 seconds after attacking.
