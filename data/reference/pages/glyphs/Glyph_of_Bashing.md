# Online:Glyph of Bashing

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Glyph_of_Bashing
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Glyphs of Bashing are created by using a Kaderi rune and an Additive Potency rune. They can be applied to any jewelry of equal or greater level, and add Weapon and Spell Damage to your Bash Attacks.
