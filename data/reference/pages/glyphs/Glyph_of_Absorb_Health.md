# Online:Glyph of Absorb Health

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Glyph_of_Absorb_Health
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Glyphs of Absorb Health are created by using an Oko rune and a Subtractive Potency rune. They can be applied to any weapon of equal or greater level, and will deal Magic Damage and Heal you when you attack.
