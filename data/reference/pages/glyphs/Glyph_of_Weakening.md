# Online:Glyph of Weakening

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Glyph_of_Weakening
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Glyphs of Weakening are created by using an Okori rune and a Subractive Potency rune. They can be applied to any weapon of equal or greater level, and will reduce your targets' Weapon and Spell Damage for 5 seconds after attacking.
