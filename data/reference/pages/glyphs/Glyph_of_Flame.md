# Online:Glyph of Flame

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Glyph_of_Flame

Glyphs of Flame are created by using a Rakeipa rune and an Additive Potency rune. They can be applied to any weapon of equal or greater level, and will add Flame Damage to your attacks.
