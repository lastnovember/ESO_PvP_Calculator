Flame Damage (script)
Flame Damage is a type of Magical Damage mainly used by Dragonknights, but available to other classes as well. It can cause the Burning status effect.

Some enemies may use Flame Damage against you, notably Flame Atronachs, which are some of the first enemies you will encounter. Dunmer characters receive a bonus to Flame Resistance through their Resist Flame passive skill.

## Sources

### Weapons
- All Inferno Staves.
- All weapons enchanted with a Glyph of Flame (using a Rakeipa runestone). Note that staves may be enchanted with a different elemental damage, so a single staff could do both Flame and another type of damage.

### Champion Points
- War Mage in the Extended Might subconstellation of Warfare grants additional Flame Damage.

## Defense
There are several ways of protecting yourself against Flame Damage.
- Dark Elf characters have access to the Resist Flame passive skill, which reduces all Flame Damage received.
- A Glyph of Flame Resist, created with a Rakeipa Essence rune and a negative Potency rune, can be applied to jewelry.
- Elemental Aegis in the Staving Death subconstellation of Warfare reduces Flame Damage taken.

## Notes
- This effect was previously referred to as Fire Damage in a few cases, but has since been corrected for consistency.
