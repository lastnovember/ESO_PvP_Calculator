Bleed Damage (script)
Bleed Damage is a type of Martial Damage. It is reduced by physical resistance.

### Sets
The following sets either cause or synergize with Bleed Damage:

Set | Weight | 5 piece bonus

Aegis Caller
Medium
When you deal critical damage with a Martial melee attack, summon a Lesser Aegis for 11 seconds. After 2.5 seconds, the Lesser Aegis spins its blades, dealing 621 Bleed Damage every 1 second. This effect can occur once every 12 seconds and scales off the higher of your Weapon or Spell Damage.

Blooddrinker
Medium
Increases your Bleed damaging attacks by 20%.

Crimson Twilight
Heavy
After taking damage you surround yourself in an 8 meter mist of blood. After 2 seconds, the mist is violently siphoned into your body, dealing 1946 Bleed Damage to nearby enemies and healing you for 100% of the damage done. This effect can occur once every 8 seconds and scales off your Max Health.

Deadlands Assassin
Medium
Dealing damage to an enemy within 10 meters of you with a Heavy Attack causes you to throw a cone of knives, dealing 1061 Physical Damage to enemies hit. If an enemy hit has 50% Health or less, they also take an additional 2121 Bleed Damage over 13 seconds. This effect can occur once every 15 seconds and scales off the higher of your Weapon or Spell Damage.

Dragon's Appetite
Any
Increase your damage done to Bleeding enemies by 5-225. Dealing non-Bleed damage to Bleeding enemies generates a persistent stack of Dragon's Appetite, up to once per half second. After 10 stacks you consume Dragon's Appetite and heal for 4635 Health. The heal effect scales off the higher of your Max Magicka or Stamina.

Dro'Zakar's Claws
Medium
For every bleed effect you have on an enemy, increase your Weapon Damage by 2-103 against them.

Pestilent Host
Medium
Whenever you kill an enemy afflicted with a poison effect, their corpse releases a writhing mass of Fetcherflies, which seek out the nearest enemy within 12 meters, causing them to bleed for 7605 Bleed Damage over 10 seconds. This effect scales off the higher of your Weapon or Spell Damage.

Pillar of Nirn
Medium
When you deal damage, you create a fissure underneath the enemy after 1 second, dealing 888 Bleed Damage to all enemies within 2.5 meters and causing them to bleed for an additional 4440 Bleed Damage over 10 seconds. This effect can occur once every 10 seconds and scales off the higher of your Weapon or Spell Damage.

Savage Werewolf
Medium
Your Light and Heavy Attacks rend flesh, causing your enemy to bleed for 302 Bleed Damage every 1 second for 4 seconds. This effect scales off the higher of your Weapon or Spell Damage.

Stinging Slashes
Weapons
Twin Slashes deals 38-1635 more damage for each hit of the initial attack and bleed.

Strength of the Automaton
Medium
Adds 9-400 Weapon Damage to your Physical and Bleed Damage abilities.

Twin Sisters
Medium
When you block an attack, you cause all enemies within 5 meters of you to bleed for 2514 Bleed damage over 10 seconds. This effect can occur once every 10 seconds and scales off the higher of your Weapon or Spell Damage.

Unleashed Terror
Medium
When you deal direct damage with a Blink, Charge, Leap, Teleport, or Pull ability, you cut your enemy, causing them to bleed for 10 seconds, dealing 2592 Bleed Damage over the duration and applying the Hemorrhaging status effect every tick. This ability can occur once every 10 seconds per target and scales off the higher of your Weapon or Spell Damage.

Macabre Vintage
Medium
When you kill a monster they burst with blood magic, dealing 50% of their Max Health to enemies within 6 meters as Bleed Damage, up to 24096 Bleed Damage. This damage cannot critically strike. This effect can occur once every 0.5 seconds.

### Champion Points
- Mighty in the Extended Might subconstellation of Warfare grants additional Bleed Damage.

## Defense
- Hardy in the Staving Death subconstellation of Warfare reduces Bleed Damage taken.
