Dual Wield is one of the six weapon skills available in Elder Scrolls Online. You can advance through this skill line by equipping two One-Handed weapons.

## Learning
The Dual Wield skill line is granted the first time you kill an enemy with two weapons equipped. You can increase your skill by engaging in combat with two weapons equipped, or by having Dual Wield skills slotted. The more skills you have slotted, the faster your skill will increase. Dark Elves are naturally gifted at Dual Wielding, and will learn the ability more quickly than other races. In addition, you can earn free levels in the skill line by reading Skill Books, which may be found randomly on bookshelves anywhere. The skill books which increase Dual Wield are:

- Crush, Slash, Bash, and Stab
- Exiled from Exile, Volume 7
- Glinting Talons
- Mazubar-do's Advice
- Quotes from the Greats

### Other Sets
- Wearing five pieces of the Sword Dancer set from the Blessed Crucible group dungeon increases the Weapon Damage and Spell Damage of your Dual Wield abilities.
- Wearing five pieces of the Red Eagle's Fury set from the Red Eagle Redoubt crafting site increases the Weapon Damage and Spell Damage damage of all your weapon abilities, Dual Wield included, but it also increases their cost.
- Wearing five pieces of the Daring Corsair set from the Cat's-Claw Station crafting site decreases the Stamina and Magicka costs of all your weapon abilities by 10%, and grants you Minor Heroism for 8 seconds after you use a weapon skill.
