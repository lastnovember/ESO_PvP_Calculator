Disease Damage (script)
Disease Damage is a type of Martial Damage. It is somewhat rare, mostly only being seen on some of the Nightblade and Necromancer skills' morphs, or when attacked by Zombies and their ilk. It can cause the Diseased status effect, which inflicts instant direct disease damage that splashes in a 6 meter radius to enemies, up to once every 4 seconds. The enemy also suffers from Minor Defile debuff for 4 seconds, decreasing their healing received and damage shield strength by 6%.

## Sources

### Weapons
- All weapons enchanted with a Glyph of Foulness (using a Haoko runestone).

### Champion Points
- Mighty in the Extended Might subconstellation of Warfare grants additional Disease Damage.

## Defense
There are several ways of protecting yourself against Disease Damage.
- Argonian characters have access to the Argonian Resistance passive skill, which reduces all Disease Damage received.
- Wood Elf characters have access to the Resist Affliction passive skill, which reduces all Disease Damage received.
- A Glyph of Disease Resist, created with a Haoko Essence rune and a negative Potency rune, can be applied to jewelry.
- Hardy in the Staving Death subconstellation of Warfare reduces Disease Damage taken.
