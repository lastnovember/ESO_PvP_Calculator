# Online:Weapon Sets

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Weapon_Sets
Fetched: 2026-09-12
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Weapon Sets are sets of weapons and shields that provide special bonuses to players when the items of the same set are equipped simultaneously. They do not include armor pieces other than shields. Note that most of these sets consist only of a single two-handed weapon, so only one item is needed to receive the full bonus. The way to obtain them depends on the type of the set.

## Asylum Weapons

Asylum Weapons drop in Asylum SanctoriumClockwork City, a trial in the Clockwork City. Perfected versions of the weapons drop on Veteran mode. All weapons bear the Asylum Style.

## Blackrose Weapons

Blackrose Weapons drop in Blackrose PrisonMurkmire, an arena in Murkmire. Perfected versions of the weapons drop on Veteran mode. All weapons bear the Honor Guard Style.

## Maelstrom Weapons

Maelstrom Weapons drop in Maelstrom ArenaOrsinium. Perfected versions of the weapons drop on Veteran mode. All weapons bear the Maelstrom Style.

## Master's Weapons

Master's Weapons drop in Dragonstar Arena. Perfected versions of the weapons drop on Veteran mode. All weapons bear a different Tier 5 Racial Style.

## Vateshran Weapons

Vateshran Weapons drop in Vateshran HollowsMarkarth, a solo arena in The Reach. Perfected versions of the weapons drop on Veteran mode. All weapons bear a resemblance to the Ancestral Reach weapons, but corrupted by void essence.
