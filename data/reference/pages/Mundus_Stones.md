Mundus Stones are standing stones dedicated to the Constellations. Activating one will grant you a permanent blessing, although only one blessing can be active at a time. The Twice-Born Star set allows a character to have two blessings at the same time.

## Notes
- There are three unmarked, broken Mundus Stones in Craglorn dedicated to the Lord, Lady and Steed. They are related to the quest The Corrupted Stone and can't be used to change blessings.
- No DLC zones contain any Mundus Stones whatsoever (even Western Skyrim, which had Standing Stones in Skyrim).
