# Online:Elemental Succession

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Elemental_Succession
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Elemental Succession is a set which drops in the Maelstrom Arena. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Malacath Style in Light Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
3 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
4 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
5 items: Whenever you deal Flame, Shock, or Frost Damage, you gain 11-492 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage for Flame, Shock, or Frost Damage for 4 seconds. Each effect is picked randomly and an effect that is already active cannot refresh.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
