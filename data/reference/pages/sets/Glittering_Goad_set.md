Glittering Goad
Glittering Goad is a monster mask set found in the Night Market. It cannot be crafted. The set is available in all armor weights, and it has its own unique outfit style.

### Bonuses

1 item: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
2 items: Dealing damage with a Bash or Heavy Attack deals 812 Frost damage over 8 seconds to the enemy. This effect can occur once every 8 seconds. If the effect completes, the enemy applies Minor Brittle to up to 6 enemies within 12 meters of itself for 10 seconds. If the enemy dies, the enemy releases glittering energy, restoring 847 Magicka and Stamina to you and group members within 12 meters of the defeated enemy.

### Pieces
The set consists of two pieces, shoulder and head armor.

### Drop Locations
- Head pieces are found in reward boxes for participating in the Night Market faction competition if you sided with the Glittering Goad.
- Curated Glittering Goad Coffers purchased from Nymisasha will contain a shoulder piece with a random trait (other than Nirnhorned).
- Possible loot from the Opulent Ordeal trial.plain
