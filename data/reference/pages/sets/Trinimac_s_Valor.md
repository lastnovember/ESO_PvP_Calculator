# Online:Trinimac's Valor

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Trinimac's_Valor
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Trinimac's Valor is an overland set that drops in Wrothgar. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Trinimac Style in Light Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Magicka Recovery
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
4 items: Adds 3-129 Magicka Recovery
5 items: When you cast a damage shield, you call down a fragment of Trinimac that heals you and group members for 1396 Health and damages enemies for 1396 Magic damage in a 5 meter radius after a 2 second delay. This effect can occur once every 5 seconds and scales with the higher of your Max Magicka or Stamina.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Diagnan Hauberk, the quest reward for The Ashes of Our Fathers
- Morkul's Mighty Mallet, the quest reward for The Hand of Morkul
- Riekr Wrecker, the quest reward for Riekr Revenge
