# Online:Hunt Leader

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Hunt_Leader
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Hunt Leader is a set which drops in the Maelstrom Arena. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Malacath Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: When your pets attack an enemy you have also attacked in the last 10 seconds, you heal for 56-2433 Health and restore 28-1216 Stamina. This effect can occur once every 5 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
