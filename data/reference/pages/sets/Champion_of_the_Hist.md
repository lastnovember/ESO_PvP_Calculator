# Online:Champion of the Hist

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Champion_of_the_Hist
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Champion of the Hist is an overland set that drops in Murkmire. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Elder Argonian Style in Heavy Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
4 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
5 items: Adds 37-1600 Maximum Health
5 items: Gain Minor Heroism at all times while you are in combat, generating 1 Ultimate every 1.5 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Rootmender's Staff, the quest reward for By River and Root
