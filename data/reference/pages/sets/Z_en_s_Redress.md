# Online:Z'en's Redress

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Z'en's_Redress
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Z'en's Redress is a dungeon set that drops in the Lair of Maarselok. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Stags of Z'en Style in Light Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Magicka Recovery
3 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
4 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
5 items: Your Light Attacks apply the Touch of Z'en on enemies for 20 seconds. Enemies with the Touch of Z'en take an additional 1% more damage for each damage over time effect you've placed on them, up to 5%. Enemies can only be affected by one Touch of Z'en at a time.

### Pieces
The set consists light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
