# Online:Knight-Errant's Mail

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Knight-Errant's_Mail
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Knight-Errant's Mail is a dungeon set that drops in Blackheart Haven. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Mercenary Style in Heavy Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: Adds 10-450 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage to your One Hand and Shield abilities. When you use a One Hand and Shield ability, you heal for 35-1537 Health.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as one-handed weapons of all types and shields (other weapons may still exist but cannot be obtained new anymore). Unique pieces include:
- Pillager's Ring, unique loot from Iron-Heel.
- Shield of the Black Death, unique loot from Captain Blackheart.
