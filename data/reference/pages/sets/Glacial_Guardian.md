# Online:Glacial Guardian

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Glacial_Guardian
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Glacial Guardian is a dungeon set that drops in Coral Aerie. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Ascendant Order Style in Heavy Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 34-1487 Armor
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
4 items: Adds 3-129 Health Recovery
5 items: When you Block, tether to the enemy if they are within 8 meters of you for 8 seconds. While tethered, you deal 256 Frost damage every second to enemies touching the tether and cause them to have a 200% increased Status Effect Chance. The tether breaks if the target moves more than 12 meters away. This effect can occur once every 12 seconds and the damage scales off your Max Health.

### Notes
- Tethers are considered Area of Effect damage and are reduced by Major/Minor Evasion.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
