# Online:Death-Dancer

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Death-Dancer
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Death-Dancers]]
Death-Dancer is an overland set that drops in Western Solstice. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Tide-Born Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
3 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
4 items: Adds 3-129 Stamina Recovery
5 items: Adds 35-1528 Critical Chance while you have 50% or more Stamina. Reduce the cost of your Core Combat abilities by 0-23% while you have less than 50% of your Stamina.

### Pieces
The set consists of Medium Armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
