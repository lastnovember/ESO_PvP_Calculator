# Online:Mother's Sorrow

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Mother's_Sorrow
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Mother's Sorrow is an overland set that drops in Deshaan. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Argonian Style in Light Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
3 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
4 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
5 items: Adds 35-1528 Critical Chance

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Argonian Muck Minder of Mother's Sorrow, the quest reward for The Saving of Silent Mire.
- Ashlander's Honor of Mother's Sorrow, the quest reward for Honor Bound in the Dark Elf Style.
- Bittergreen's Scale of Mother's Sorrow, a unique drop from the world boss Bittergreen the Wild.
- Drulshasa's Dark Band of Mother's Sorrow, a unique drop from Drulshasa, the final boss of Knife Ear Grotto.
- Forgotten Truncheon
- Kagouti Slayer Medallion of Mother's Sorrow, a unique drop from the world boss Short-Tusk.
- Mabrigash Spectral Blade of Mother's Sorrow, a unique drop from the world boss Mabrigash Shade in the Dark Elf Style.
- Plague Mask, the quest reward for Bad Medicine in the Dark Elf Style.
- Selforan Temple Sash, the quest reward for Rescue and Revenge in the Dark Elf Style.
- Shad Astula Academy Robes, the quest reward for Vision Quest in the Dark Elf Style.
