# Online:Soldier of Anguish

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Soldier_of_Anguish
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Soldier of Anguish is an Elite Gear Set that was formerly available from "Rewards for the Worthy" mails, earned by participating in PvP, prior to Update 39. It can now be obtained in boxes purchased using Alliance Points from the Bruma Quartermaster, as quest rewards in Bruma, as a reward for completing delves and dolmens throughout Cyrodiil, from completing Board Missions, and from Elite Gear Vendors. It cannot be crafted and does not drop from monsters. Pieces are Aldmeri Dominion, Daggerfall Covenant, or Ebonheart Pact Style in Medium Armor, depending on the player's alliance.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
3 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: Your melee Light Attacks place a ring on the ground that explodes after 1.3 seconds, applying 112-4830 Heal Absorption to all enemies hit for 4 seconds, negating the next 112-4830 points of healing done. This effect can occur once every 6 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as melee weapons and shields, with the exception of bows and staves.
