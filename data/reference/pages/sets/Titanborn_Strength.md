# Online:Titanborn Strength

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Titanborn_Strength
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Titanborn Strength is a dungeon set that drops in the Icereach. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Icereach Coven Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 34-1487 Offensive Penetration
3 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
4 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
5 items: Adds 2-110 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage and 28-1240 Offensive Penetration. While in combat, this bonus doubles when you are under 75% Health and quadruples when you are under 50% Health.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
