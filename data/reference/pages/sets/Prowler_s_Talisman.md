# Online:Prowler's Talisman

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Prowler's_Talisman
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

:A disc-shaped amulet on a chain, an engraved boot balanced on the tip of its toes and the words "Prowler's Talisman" decorate the metallic surface.
The Prowler's Talisman is a Mythic necklace that comes with the Swift trait. It can be obtained through The Codex Caper quest and can be further upgraded through collecting gems through various means. If the mythic item is lost, it can be reacquired by speaking to Mylen Dechery.

This item was released with Season One during Update 50. It is the first mythic to be obtained outside the Antiquities system.

### Bonuses
Note: The following stats are for the initial version of the Prowler's Talisman, before it is upgraded.

1 item: While Battle Spirit is inactive, bracing while crouching turns you invisible for 10 seconds. This can occur once every 45 seconds.

Increase your chances of successfully Pickpocketing by 5%. On dealing Critical Damage, increase your Max Magicka and Max Stamina for 10 seconds, up to 1900 at 10 stacks.

On dealing non-Critical Damage, increase your Health, Magicka, and Stamina Recovery for 10 seconds, up to 160 at 10 stacks. Either effect can occur up to once every 1 second.

Talisman upgrades: 0

### Upgrades
As mentioned this artifact can be further upgraded. You will need to recover Phynaster's Gems from various sources and bring them to Mylen Dechery in the Thieves Den of the Daggerfall Outlaws Refuge so she can add the gems to the empty sockets in the amulet. The upgrades are linked to the amount, not the particular gems you recover.

## Notes
- Upgrades to one talisman apply to all talismans in your account.
- Mylen Dechery will replace it for free. No questions asked.

## Bugs
Not all players haven been receiving the Prowler's Talisman as a reward for completing the Codex Caper.[https://help.bethesda.net/#en/answer/75180 Acknowledgement of issue]
- Speaking with Mylen Dechery may give you the item. She offers a dialogue option to restore the Talisman if it has been lost.
- When another player links their talisman, you will see your upgrade count instead of theirs.
