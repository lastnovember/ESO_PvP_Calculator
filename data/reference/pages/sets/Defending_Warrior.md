# Online:Defending Warrior

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Defending_Warrior
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Defending Warrior is a trial set which drops in the Aetherian Archive. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Celestial Style in Heavy Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 34-1487 Armor
3 items: Adds 34-1487 Armor
4 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
5 items: When you Block, you deal 1196 Magic damage to all enemies within 10 meters of you and heal for 100% of the damage done. This effect can occur once every 5 seconds and scales off your Max Health.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
