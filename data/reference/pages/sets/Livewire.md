# Online:Livewire

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Livewire
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Livewire is an overland set that drops in the Clockwork City. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Apostle Style in Heavy Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 4% Healing Taken
3 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
4 items: Adds 4% Healing Taken
5 items: When you take damage, you overload your circuits, applying Concussion to enemies within 8 meters of you and healing for 76-1000 Health for each enemy hit. This effect can occur once every 6 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Explorer's Boots, a quest reward for Family Feud.
- Proctor's Bronze Fists, a quest reward for Where Shadows Lie.
- Spare Serpentine Belt, a quest reward for Most Complicated Machine
- Traitor's Cuirass, a quest reward for Deepening Shadows
- Welder's Greaves, a quest reward for The Halls of Regulation.
