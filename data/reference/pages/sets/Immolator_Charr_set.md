Immolator Charr

Immolator Charr is a monster helm set found in the Imperial City. It consists of two armor pieces: head and shoulder, both of which are available in all three armor weights and standard traits. This set cannot be crafted.

### Bonuses

1 item: Adds 34-1487 Armor
2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
2 items: When you take damage while below 50% Health, gain immunity to immobilization, snares, and crowd control effects for 10 seconds. This effect can occur once every 40 seconds.

### Drop Locations
- The head drops from Immolator Charr in the Imperial City Temple District or from the chest in the Monstrous Tooth Trophy Vault.
- The shoulders can be obtained from the Tel Var grand armorer in your alliance sewer headquarters for 20,000.
