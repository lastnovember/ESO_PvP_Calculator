# Online:Knightmare

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Knightmare
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Knightmare is a dungeon set that drops in Spindleclutch I and II. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Wood Elf style in Heavy Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 34-1487 Armor
3 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
4 items: Adds 34-1487 Armor
5 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
5 items: When you deal Martial melee damage, you apply Minor Maim to all enemies within 8 meters of you for 5 seconds, reducing their damage done by 5%. This effect can occur once every 3 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Guardsman's Mark, unique loot from Big Rabbu.
- Gem of Madness, unique loot from Mad Mortine.
- Signet of the Stalker, unique loot from Urvan Veleth.
- Spindlecleave, unique loot from The Whisperer.
- Spinner's Pierce, unique loot from the Swarm Mother.
