# Online:Tava's Favor

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Tava's_Favor
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Tava's Favor is one of the craftable sets providing special bonuses to armor and weapons. It is available for crafting to players pursuing blacksmithing, clothing, woodworking, and/or jewelry crafting professions. The special crafting site with stations dedicated to creation of this set are located in the Hew's BaneThieves Guild.

To make an item for this set, a crafter must first complete research on five traits for this particular item at a corresponding crafting station.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
3 items: Adds 3-129 Stamina Recovery
4 items: Adds 3-129 Magicka Recovery
5 items: When you dodge an attack, you receive the blessing of Tava, generating 12 Ultimate over 3 seconds. This effect can occur once every 3 seconds.

### Crafting Site
Forebear's Junction
