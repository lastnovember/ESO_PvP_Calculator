# Online:Nunatak (set)

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Nunatak_(set)
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

equipped setNunatak

Nunatak is a monster helm set found in the Imperial City. It consists of two armor pieces: head and shoulder, both of which are available in all three armor weights and standard traits. The set cannot be crafted.

### Bonuses

1 item: Adds 34-1487 Armor
2 items: On dealing Frost Damage, create a 6 meter area under the target for 6 seconds, dealing 191 Frost damage to enemies every 1 second and applying a 40% snare for 4 seconds. Enemies damaged four times become immobilized and afflicted with Major Brittle for 4 seconds, causing the target to take 20% increased Critical Damage. This effect can occur once every 15 seconds and scales off the higher of your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage.

### Drop Locations
- The head drops from Nunatak in the Imperial City Memorial District or from the chest in the Bone Shard Trophy Vault.
- The shoulders can be obtained from the Tel Var grand armorer in your alliance sewer headquarters for 20,000.
