# Online:Claw of the Forest Wraith

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Claw_of_the_Forest_Wraith
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Claw of the Forest Wraith is one of the craftable sets providing special bonuses to armor and weapons. It is available for crafting to players pursuing blacksmithing, clothing, woodworking, and/or jewelry crafting professions. The special crafting site with stations dedicated to the creation of this set is located at the Oaken Forge on Galen.

To make an item for this set, a crafter must first complete research on five traits for that particular item at a corresponding crafting station.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
3 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
4 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
5 items: Adds 47-2037 Critical Chance to your Class abilities.

### Crafting Site
Oaken Forge
