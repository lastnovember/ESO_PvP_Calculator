# Online:Pangrit Denmother

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Pangrit_Denmother
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Pangrit Denmother is a dungeon set that drops in Graven Deep. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Drowned Mariner Style in Heavy Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 4% Healing Taken
3 items: Adds 34-1487 Armor
4 items: Adds 3-129 Health Recovery
5 items: Adds 43-1890 Maximum Stamina
5 items: When you Block, you and up to 3 group members within 12 meters gain Minor Courage for 10 seconds, increasing your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage by 215. This effect can occur once every 5 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
