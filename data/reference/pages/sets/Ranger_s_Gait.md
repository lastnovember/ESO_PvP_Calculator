# Online:Ranger's Gait

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Ranger's_Gait
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Ranger's Gait is an overland set that drops in Grahtwood. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Wood Elf Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Stamina Recovery
3 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
4 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
5 items: Adds 3-129 Stamina Recovery
5 items: Reduce the effectiveness of snares applied to you by 50%.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Cheliceral Hook, the quest reward for A Silken Garb
