# Online:Xanmeer Spellweaver

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Xanmeer_Spellweaver
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Xanmeer Spellweaver is a dungeon set that drops in Naj-Caldeesh. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Voskrona Guardian Style in Light Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
3 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: Increase your damage done with Flame, Frost, and Shock Damage by 5%. Increase your damage done with Burning, Chilled, and Concussion by an additional 15%.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
