# Online:Oblivion's Foe

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Oblivion's_Foe
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Oblivion's Foe is one of the craftable sets providing special bonuses to armor and weapons. It is available for crafting to players pursuing blacksmithing, clothing, woodworking, and/or jewelry crafting professions. The special crafting site dedicated to creation of this set is available in Coldharbour. Completion of the main quest line is not required, as both crafting sites in Coldharbour can be accessed as soon as you gain access to the zone.

To craft an item for this set, a crafter must first complete research on eight traits for this particular item at a corresponding crafting station.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
3 items: Adds 34-1487 Offensive Penetration
4 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
5 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: When you deal damage with Soul Magic, you mark the target's soul dealing 1875 Magic Damage over 10 seconds, as long as they are not already marked from you. This effect scales off the higher of your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage.

### Crafting Sites
Font of Schemes

### Pieces
In addition to crafted pieces, the quest rewards for God of Schemes are part of this set:
- The Emperor's Blessing, the reward when Varen Aquilarios is sacrificed
- Great Axe of the Titanborn, the reward when Lyris Titanborn is sacrificed
- Sword Saint's Blade, the reward when Sai Sahan is sacrificed
