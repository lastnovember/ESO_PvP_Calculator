# Online:Blind Path Induction

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Blind_Path_Induction
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Blind Path Induction is a dungeon set found in Bedlam Veil. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Blind Path Cultist Style in Light Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
3 items: Adds 4% Healing Done
4 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
5 items: Increase the strength of your Damage Shields by 13% to you and targets within 15 meters of you. Increase your Healing Done by 13% to targets more than 15 meters away from you.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
