# Online:New Moon Acolyte

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:New_Moon_Acolyte
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

New Moon Acolyte is one of the craftable sets providing special bonuses to armor and weapons. It is available for crafting to players pursuing blacksmithing, clothing, woodworking, and/or jewelry crafting professions. The special crafting site with stations dedicated to creation of this set are located in Southern ElsweyrDragonhold.

To make an item for this set, a crafter must first complete research on nine traits for this particular item at a corresponding crafting station.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
3 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
4 items: Adds 34-1487 Offensive Penetration
5 items: Adds 9-401 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage. Increases the cost of your active abilities by 5%.

### Crafting Site
Fur-Forge Cove
