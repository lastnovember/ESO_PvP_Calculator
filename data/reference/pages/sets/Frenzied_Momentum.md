# Online:Frenzied Momentum

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Frenzied_Momentum
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Frenzied Momentum is a Vateshran Weapons item set which drops in normal Vateshran Hollows. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are based on the Ancestral Reach Style. The perfected version of this set can be looted on veteran difficulty.

### Bonuses

2 items: While Momentum is active, casting Stamina abilities while in combat generates a stack of Frenzied Momentum for 20 seconds, increasing your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage by 0-30 up to 10 times. After 5 or more stacks, your next Heavy Attack consumes all stacks and releases a violent explosion of energy around you, dealing 1084 Physical Damage to all enemies within 8 meters, scaling off the higher of your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage.

### Pieces
The set consists of Two Handed melee weapons of all types.

### Notes
- While the description states that 2 items are required, this is because 2-handed weapons count as 2 items by themselves, so only 1 item is actually needed.
