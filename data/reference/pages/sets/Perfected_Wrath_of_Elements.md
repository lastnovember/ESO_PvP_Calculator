# Online:Perfected Wrath of Elements

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Perfected_Wrath_of_Elements
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Perfected Wrath of Elements is a Perfected Vateshran Weapons item set which drops in veteran Vateshran Hollows. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are based on the Ancestral Reach Style. The non-perfected version of this set can be looted on normal difficulty.

### Bonuses

2 perfected items: Adds 27-1190 Offensive Penetration
2 items: Casting Weakness to Elements on a target within 15 meters of you causes a surge of elemental energy to tether between you for 10 seconds. While the tether persists, enemies touching the tether take 331 Flame Damage, 331 Shock Damage, or 331 Frost Damage every 1 second, which increases by 1% each time it deals damage, up to 20%. This effect can occur once every 10 seconds, and scales off the higher of your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage.

### Pieces
The set consists of destruction staves of all three types.

### Notes
- While the description states that 2 items are required, this is because staves are 2-handed weapons, and thus count as 2 items by themselves, so only 1 item is actually needed.
- Tethers are considered Area of Effect damage and are reduced by Major/Minor Evasion.
