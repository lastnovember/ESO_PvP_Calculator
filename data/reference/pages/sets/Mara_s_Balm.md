# Online:Mara's Balm

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Mara's_Balm
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Mara's Balm is an Elite Gear Set that was formerly available from "Rewards for the Worthy" mails, earned by participating in PvP, prior to Update 39. It can now be obtained in boxes purchased using Alliance Points from the Vlastarus Quartermaster, as quest rewards in Vlastarus, as a reward for completing delves and dolmens throughout Cyrodiil, from completing Board Missions, and from Elite Gear Vendors. It cannot be crafted and does not drop from monsters. Pieces are Aldmeri Dominion, Daggerfall Covenant, or Ebonheart Pact Style in Heavy Armor, depending on the player's alliance.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 34-1487 Armor
3 items: Adds 9-424 Critical Resistance
4 items: Adds 4% Healing Taken
5 items: When a negative effect is removed from you, restore 25-1106 Health. This effect can occur once every 1 second. When you take damage and have 6 or more negative effects, restore 25-1106 Health per negative effect and then cleanse all. This effect can occur once every 30 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
