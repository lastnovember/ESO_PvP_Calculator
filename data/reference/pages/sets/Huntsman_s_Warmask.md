Huntsman's Warmask (achievement)
:"What Aspect will he show you? The flank of the unicorn? The skull of the wolf? Bloody claws or bloody hooves? Aye, or even the paw print of the fox? Whatever face you see, so Uraccanach says, you are blessed in the seeing." - Uraccanach the Witchman

The Huntsman's Warmask is a Mythic helmet that comes with the Divines trait. It can be obtained through the Antiquities system. It was released as part of the Seasons of the Worm Cult: Part 2 update.

### Bonuses

1 item: Dealing damage with a Bash or fully-charged Heavy Attack places the Mark of Hircine on your target for 1 minute. While active, you gain Minor Berserk, increasing damage done by 5%, and deal 15% more damage to the target and 50% less damage to all other enemies. If the enemy dies while marked, you gain 2418 Stamina or Magicka, based off your highest maximum resource. This effect can trigger up to every 10 seconds.

### Fragments
- Five fragments are required to create the item, all of which require a master-quality Lead in order to scry, and are classed as master difficulty to excavate.
- You are required to be at least level 7 in Scrying, and have the Antiquarian Insight IV passive in order to scry Master difficulty leads.
- Fragment Leads are largely found in the same zone as where the fragment is excavated, save for a few cases where it may be found in different zone.

Teeth? No, fangs. The scrimshaw is definitely Reachfolk work. I believe the markings represent a chant to Hircine in his aspect as Alrabeg, the Hunter: The five are all, the five are one. Five are true and death-in-the-woods. - Verita Numida
Five is a number that comes up with Hircine. These fangs represent the Spear of Bitter Mercy, also called Spear with Five Points. They would have been fixed in a war-mask belonging to a great chief of the Ket Keptu. - Ugron gro-Thumog
Ah, of course! The Ket Keptu were hunters and warrior-smiths, a people devoted to the Lord of the Hunt. To carry the Spear of Bitter Mercy was a high honor for their great champions. Whoever wore this war-mask was indeed favored by Hircine! - Reginus Buca

Is this a scarf? No, it's a headwrap made from some sort of felt. I believe it is part of a ceremonial Reachfolk mask. A shaman or chief would wear this beneath the mask itself. Curious. The cloth part of a mask is usually the first part to rot away. - Verita Numida
It is not felt made from wool, but the fur of a fox. A Reachfolk mask incorporating fox-fur certainly suggests Hircine worship. A fox is one of the Huntsman's aspects. - Muzah-Tei
Reachfolk know that aspect of Hircine as Gulibeg, the Quick Fox. He seeks to confound mortal hunters and run them in circles until they are so confused they follow him over a cliff or into a mire. - Reginus Buca

Now this is interesting. A carved wooden face-mask in the shape of a snarling beast's snout. I saw similar relics when I visited the Shrine of the Hunt-Father in Markarth. The shaman there explained that it represented an aspect of Hircine. - Verita Numida
That's Hrokkibeg, the Mighty Bear. Reachfolk believe he comes to our world seeking solitude and a renewal of his spirit, but beware. If you disturb his rest, he will tear you asunder! - Amalien
Ancient Nedic heroes of the Ket Keptu often wore bear emblems or totems. I think the war-mask this snout was a part of must have belonged to an important war-chief or hunt-leader. A champion of Hircine. - Ugron gro-Thumog

That's because this is no ordinary leather. It's made of hide blessed for shape-changing. I imagine it belonged to a powerful Reachfolk shaman, a follower of Hircine. He is the Father of Manbeasts, after all. - Amalien
Uraccanach the Witchman described the Manbeast as one of the five aspects of Hircine that Reachfolk worship. They call this aspect Storihbeg. He comes to the wild places of Nirn to hunt with his children, the Skinshifters. - Reginus Buca
