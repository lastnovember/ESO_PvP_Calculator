# Online:Arena Sets

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Arena_Sets
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Arena Sets are five-piece sets of armor, weapons and jewelry that provide players with various bonuses when items of the same set are equipped simultaneously. Items constituting arena sets can only be found in reward chests for completing rounds of Dragonstar Arena, Maelstrom ArenaOrsinium and Vateshran HollowsMarkarth. Dragonstar Arena sets are bind-on-equip while Maelstrom Arena and Vateshran Hollows sets are bind-on-pickup.
