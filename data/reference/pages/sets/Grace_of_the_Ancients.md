# Online:Grace of the Ancients

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Grace_of_the_Ancients
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Grace of the Ancients is an item set found in boxes purchased using Alliance Points from the Cropsford Quartermaster, as quest rewards in Cropsford, as a reward for completing delves and dolmens throughout Cyrodiil, from completing Board Missions, and from Elite Gear Vendors. It cannot be crafted and does not drop from monsters. Pieces are Aldmeri Dominion, Daggerfall Covenant, or Ebonheart Pact Style in Light Armor, depending on the player's alliance.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
3 items: Adds 55-2406 Maximum Magicka

### Pieces
The set consists of jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
