# Online:Marksman's Crest

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Marksman's_Crest
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Marksman's Crest is an Elite Gear Set that was formerly available from "Rewards for the Worthy" mails, earned by participating in PvP, prior to Update 39. It can now be obtained in boxes purchased using Alliance Points from the Bruma Quartermaster, as quest rewards in Bruma, as a reward for completing delves and dolmens throughout Cyrodiil, from completing Board Missions, and from Elite Gear Vendors. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Aldmeri Dominion, Daggerfall Covenant, or Ebonheart Pact Style in Medium Armor, depending on the player's alliance.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Stamina Recovery
3 items: Adds 3-129 Stamina Recovery
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: Reduces the cost of your Stamina abilities by 5% and increases the damage of your Bow abilities against Players by 8%.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots and a bow (no melee weapons, shields, or staves though they were droppable at some point due to an oversight).

### Notes
- Before Update 39, this set was in Abah's Watch Style.
- For a short period of time in 2024, you could unbox non-bow weapons from this set such as Shields, Maces or Daggers. This has been quickly fixed and the unboxed items haven't been deleted.
