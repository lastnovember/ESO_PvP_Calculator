# Online:Mechanical Acuity

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Mechanical_Acuity
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Mechanical Acuity is one of the craftable sets providing special bonuses to armor and weapons. It is available for crafting to players pursuing blacksmithing, clothing, woodworking, and/or jewelry crafting professions. The special crafting site with stations dedicated to creation of this set are located in the Clockwork CityClockwork City.

To make an item for this set, a crafter must first complete research on six traits for this particular item at a corresponding crafting station.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: When you deal non Critical Damage, you gain a stack of Mechanical Acuity for 4 seconds, granting you 20% Critical Strike chance per stack, up to once every 1 second and stacking up to 5 times. Upon reaching max stacks or after the effect ends, this effect cannot occur again for 25 seconds.

### Crafting Site
Pavilion of Artifice
