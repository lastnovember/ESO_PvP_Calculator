# Online:Oblivion's Edge

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Oblivion's_Edge
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Oblivion's Edge is a dungeon set that drops in Vaults of Madness. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Breton Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
3 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
4 items: Adds 3-129 Stamina Recovery
5 items: Adds 6-258 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: When you kill an enemy, you fill an empty Soul Gem and heal for 5906 Health. The heal can occur once every 10 seconds and scales off the higher of your Max Magicka or Stamina.

### Notes
This set's ability to trap souls affects Zerith-var's (2024 Companion) rapport negatively.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique items include:
- Death's Amulet, unique loot from Death's Head.
- Grothdarr's Fist, unique loot from the Grothdarr.
- Fleshsear, unique loot from the Grothdarr.
- Band of the Daedroth, unique loot from Achæraizur.
- Ill Omen, unique loot from Iskra the Omen.
- Architect's Shield, unique loot from The Mad Architect.
