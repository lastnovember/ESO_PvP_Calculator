Bloodspawn

Bloodspawn is a monster helm set. It consists of two armor pieces: head and shoulder, both of which are available in all three armor weights and standard traits. The set cannot be crafted. There is an outfit style based on this armor set. There is also an opal outfit style based on this armor set.

### Bonuses

1 item: Adds 3-129 Stamina Recovery
2 items: When you take damage, you have a 6% chance to generate 0-13 Ultimate and increase your Physical Resistance and Spell Resistance by 86-3731 for 5 seconds. This effect can occur once every 5 seconds.

### Drop Locations
- The head drops from Vorenor Winterbourne in Veteran Spindleclutch II.
- The shoulders can be obtained from Maj al-Ragath's reward chest at Undaunted Enclaves.
