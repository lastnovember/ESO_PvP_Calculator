# Online:Noble Duelist's Silks

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Noble_Duelist's_Silks
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Noble Duelist's Silks is a dungeon set that drops in the Blessed Crucible. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Breton Style in Light Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Stamina Recovery
3 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: When you deal damage with a Light or Heavy Attack in melee range, you increase the damage of your Light and Heavy Attacks against monsters by 49-2110 for 15 seconds. This effect can occur once every 12 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Beastmaster's Crop, unique loot from the Troll King.
- Brimstone Cowl of the Noble Duelist, a quest reward for Fires of Battle with Unique Style.
- Brimstone Staff, unique loot from The Lava Queen.
- Claymore of the Crucible, unique loot from The Lava Queen.
- Thoran's Bond-Ring, unique loot from Captain Thoran.
