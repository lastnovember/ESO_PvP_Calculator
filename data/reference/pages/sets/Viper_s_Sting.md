# Online:Viper's Sting

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Viper's_Sting
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Viper's Sting is a dungeon set that drops in Fungal Grotto I and II. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Argonian Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
4 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
5 items: When you deal damage with a Martial melee attack, you deal an additional 928 Poison damage over 4 seconds. This effect can occur once every 4 seconds and scales off the higher of your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique items include:
- Debaser, unique loot from the Spawn of Mephala.
- Goblinsbane, unique loot from Kra'gh the Dreugh King.
- Huntmaster's Leash, unique loot from Tazkad the Packmaster.
- Knife of Shadows, unique loot from Gamyne Bandu.
- Spidersilk Garland, unique loot from Mephala's Fang.
- Tazkad's Band, unique loot from Tazkad the Packmaster.
