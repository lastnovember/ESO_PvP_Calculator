# Online:Vykosa

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Vykosa
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Vykosa the Ascendant

Vykosa is a monster helm set. It consists of two armor pieces: head and shoulder, both of which are available in all three armor weights and standard traits. The set cannot be crafted. There is an outfit style based on this armor set.

### Bonuses

1 item: Adds 4% Healing Taken
2 items: When you Bash an enemy you've taunted, you frighten them with a deafening howl, applying Major Cowardice to them, lowering their Weapon Damage and Spell Damage by 430 for 8 seconds. This effect can occur once every 15 seconds.

### Drop Locations
- The head drops from Vykosa the Ascendant in Veteran Moon Hunter Keep.
- The shoulders can be obtained from Urgarlag Chief-bane's reward chest at Undaunted Enclaves.
