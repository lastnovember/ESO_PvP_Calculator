# Online:Defiler

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Defiler
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Defiler is an overland set that drops on Vvardenfell. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Hlaalu Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
3 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
4 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
5 items: When you deal Critical Damage, you summon a Hunger that spews poison to all enemies in front of it, dealing 990 Poison Damage and stunning any enemy hit for 5 seconds. This effect can occur once every 5 seconds and scales off the higher of your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Andule's Cuirass of the Defiler, a quest reward for The Lost Library
- Great Axe of the Defiler, a quest reward for Divine Intervention
- Nirith's Bulwark of the Defiler, a quest reward for Echoes of a Fallen House
- Seal of Hleran, a quest reward for Ancestral Ties
- Tribal Helm of the Defiler, a quest reward for An Armiger's Duty
- Veya's Axe of the Defiler, a quest reward for Family Reunion
