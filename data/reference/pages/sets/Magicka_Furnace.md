# Online:Magicka Furnace

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Magicka_Furnace
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Magicka Furnace is a dungeon set that drops in Direfrost Keep. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Dark Elf Style in Light Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 34-1487 Armor
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: When you take damage while you are under 50% Magicka, you restore 176-7590 Magicka. This effect can occur once every 30 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, in addition to weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Apprentice's Chain, unique loot from Drodda's Apprentice.
- Snowy Signet, unique loot from Drodda's Apprentice.
- Witchdrinker, unique loot from the Keeper of the Flame.
- Witch-Hunter's Habit of the Magicka Furnace, a quest reward for Nobles' Rest.
