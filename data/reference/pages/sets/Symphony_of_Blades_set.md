Symphony of Blades

Symphony of Blades is a monster helm set. It consists of two armor pieces: head and shoulder, both of which are available in all three armor weights and standard traits. The set cannot be crafted. There is an outfit style based on this armor set. Unlike many other styles, the helmet has a customized look to cater for the uniquely shaped heads and faces of Argonian and Khajiit characters.

The Symphony of Blades set improves the healing you can do with one item. The bonus effect from two items improves stamina and magicka generation, but as it can only trigger every 18 seconds per target, it is less helpful if multiple people in the same group are using this set simultaneously.

## Bonuses

1 item: Adds 4% Healing Done
2 items: When you heal a group member who is under 50% of their primary resource, grant them Meridia's Favor, which restores 13-570 Magicka or Stamina every 1 second for 6 seconds. This effect can occur every 18 seconds per target. This effect cannot target yourself, and the resource returned is based off the target's highest maximum resource.

## Drop Locations
- The head drops from Symphony of Blades in Veteran Depths of Malatar.
- The shoulders can be obtained from Urgarlag Chief-bane's chest at Undaunted Enclaves.
