# Online:Bloodthorn's Touch

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Bloodthorn's_Touch
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Bloodthorn's Touch is an overland set that drops in Glenumbra. Pieces are Primal Style in Light Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
4 items: Adds 3-129 Magicka Recovery
5 items: When you deal direct damage, you restore 18-800 Magicka and Stamina. This effect can occur once every 5 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Bloodthorn Bloodcaller Clogs, the drop from Grivier Bloodcaller.
- Bloodthorn Brooch, the drop from Asard the Putrid.
- Bloodthorn Crocodile Hide Gloves, the drop from Trapjaw.
- Bloodthorn Stibbons's Dapper Chapeau, the quest reward for The Jeweled Crown of Anton.
- Bloodthorn Lilou's Curative Coif
- Bloodthorn Guardian's Hammer
