# Online:Witchman Armor

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Witchman_Armor
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Witchman Armor is an overland set that drops in The Rift. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Barbaric Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Stamina Recovery
3 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
4 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
5 items: When you use an Ultimate ability, you heal for 1-51 Health and restore 1-51 Stamina and Magicka per Ultimate spent.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
