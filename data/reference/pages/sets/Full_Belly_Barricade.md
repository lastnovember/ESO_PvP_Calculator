# Online:Full Belly Barricade

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Full_Belly_Barricade
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Full Belly Barricade is an overland set that drops in Western Solstice. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Tide-Born Style in Heavy Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
4 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
5 items: While you have a food buff active, your Block Mitigation is increased by 0-8% and block cost is reduced by 0-8%.

### Pieces
The set consists of Heavy Armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
