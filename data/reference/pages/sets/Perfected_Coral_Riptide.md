# Online:Perfected Coral Riptide

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Perfected_Coral_Riptide
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Perfected Coral Riptide is a trial set found in veteran Dreadsail Reef. It cannot be crafted. Set pieces are Syrabanic Marine style in Medium Armor. The non-perfected version of this set can be looted on normal difficulty.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
3 items: Gain Minor Slayer at all times, increasing your damage done to Dungeon, Trial, and Arena Monsters by 5%.
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 perfected items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
5 items: Increases your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage by up to 600, based on your missing Stamina, reaching the maximum at 50% Stamina. Current bonus: 0 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
