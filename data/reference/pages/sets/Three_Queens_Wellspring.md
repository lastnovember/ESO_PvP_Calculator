# Online:Three Queens Wellspring

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Three_Queens_Wellspring
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Three Queens Wellspring is an overland set that drops in Western Solstice. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Tide-Born Style in Light Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
4 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
5 items: Gain 1 Magicka Recovery for every 100 Max Magicka you have. Current Increase: 120 Magicka Recovery

### Pieces
The set consists of Light Armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
