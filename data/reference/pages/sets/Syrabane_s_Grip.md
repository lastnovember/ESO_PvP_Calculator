# Online:Syrabane's Grip

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Syrabane's_Grip
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Syrabane's Grip is an overland set that drops in Grahtwood. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are High Elf Style in Light Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Magicka Recovery
3 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
4 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
5 items: When you Block Flame, Frost, Shock, or Magic Damage, you restore 18-805 Magicka. This effect can occur once every 1 second.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Charging Chestpiece of Syrabane, a unique drop from Sgolag
- Stunning Wormstaff of Syrabane, a unique drop from Raynia
- Unfortunate Wanderer's Boots of Syrabane, a unique drop from Lady Solace
- Thugrub's Bane of Syrabane, a unique drop from Thugrub the Reformed
- Sheogorath's Choice, a reward for completing The Grip of Madness in Grahtwood
- Scented Leathers, a reward for completing Until Death
