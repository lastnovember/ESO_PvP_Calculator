# Online:Jerensi's Bladestorm

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Jerensi's_Bladestorm
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Jerensi's Bladestorm is a dungeon set that drops in Exiled Redoubt. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Exile's Revenge Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
3 items: Adds 34-1487 Offensive Penetration
4 items: Adds 34-1487 Offensive Penetration
5 items: While in combat, casting an ability that leaves an effect on the ground creates a 6 meter Spike Vortex that deals 3855 Physical damage over 5 seconds to enemies inside it. After 5 seconds, enemies within the Vortex are dealt 1925 Physical damage over 5 seconds. This effect can occur once every 15 seconds and scales off the higher of your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
