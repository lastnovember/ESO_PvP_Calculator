# Online:Jailbreaker

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Jailbreaker
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Jailbreaker is a dungeon set that drops in the Banished Cells I or II. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Khajiit Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
3 items: Adds 3-129 Stamina Recovery
4 items: Adds 3-129 Stamina Recovery
5 items: Adds 3-142 Stamina Recovery
5 items: Gain Minor Expedition at all times, increasing your Movement Speed by 15%.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Corruptor's Maul, unique loot from High Kinlord Rilis in Banished Cells II.
- Firetooth, unique loot from Keeper Imiril in Banished Cells II
- Guilehammer, unique loot from High Kinlord Rilis in Banished Cells I.
- Reanimating Pendant, unique loot from the Cell Haunter.
- Render's Axe, unique loot from Shadowrend.
