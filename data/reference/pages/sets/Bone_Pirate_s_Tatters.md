# Online:Bone Pirate's Tatters

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Bone_Pirate's_Tatters
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Bone Pirate's Tatters is a dungeon set that drops in Blackheart Haven. It cannot be crafted. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Draugr Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
3 items: Adds 3-129 Stamina Recovery
4 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
5 items: While you have a drink buff active, your Max Stamina is increased by 46-2000 and Stamina Recovery by 3-133.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Bone Pirate's Pillaging Garb, a quest reward for Jumping Ship.
- First Mate's Mark, unique loot from First Mate Wavecutter.
- Skullmaker, unique loot from Captain Blackheart.
