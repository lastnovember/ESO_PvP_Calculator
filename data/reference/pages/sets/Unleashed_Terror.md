# Online:Unleashed Terror

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Unleashed_Terror
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Unleashed Terror is a dungeon set that drops in Castle Thorn. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Thorn Legion Style style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
4 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
5 items: When you deal direct damage with a Blink, Charge, Leap, Teleport, or Pull ability, you cut your enemy, causing them to bleed for 10 seconds, dealing 1870 Bleed damage over the duration and applying the Hemorrhaging status effect every tick. This ability can occur once every 10 seconds per target and scales off the higher of your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
