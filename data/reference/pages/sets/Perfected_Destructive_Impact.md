# Online:Perfected Destructive Impact

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Perfected_Destructive_Impact
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Perfected Destructive Impact is a Perfected Master's Weapons item set found in veteran Dragonstar Arena. It cannot be crafted. The frost staff is in the Nord Style, the inferno staff is in the Dark Elf Style, and the lightning staff is in the High Elf Style, all of which are in the unobtainable tier 5 versions. The non-perfected version of this set can be looted on normal difficulty.

### Bonuses

2 perfected items: Adds 2-103 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
2 items: Reduces the cost of Destructive Touch by 10% and increases your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage by 13-600 for 4 seconds after activating it.

### Pieces
The set consists of destruction staves of all three types.

### Notes
- While the description states that 2 items are required, this is because staves are 2-handed weapons, and thus count as 2 items by themselves, so only 1 item is actually needed.
