# Online:Armor of the Trainee

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Armor_of_the_Trainee
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Armor of the Trainee is an overland set that drops in Bal Foyen, Betnikh, Bleakrock Isle, Khenarthi's Roost, and Stros M'Kai. It cannot be crafted. Dropped pieces are found in the racial style appropriate to the region you are in. Reconstructed pieces are in the style of the character's race in all weights. Non-unique pieces of this set are always looted with the Training trait.

### Bonuses

1 item: Adds 33-1454 Maximum Health
2 items: Adds 33-1454 Maximum Magicka
3 items: Adds 33-1454 Maximum Stamina

### Notes
- The drop rate for set items from normal enemies in these zones is near zero; completing quests, defeating named enemies, and looting chests are the far better options for farming this gear.
