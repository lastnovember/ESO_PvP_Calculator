# Online:Nocturnal's Favor

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Nocturnal's_Favor
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Nocturnal's Favor is one of the craftable sets providing special bonuses to armor and weapons. It is available for crafting to players pursuing blacksmithing, clothing, woodworking, and/or jewelry crafting professions. The special crafting site with stations dedicated to creation of this set are located in Summerset Isle.

To make an item for this set, a crafter must first complete research on nine traits for this particular item at a corresponding crafting station.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Stamina Recovery
3 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
4 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
5 items: Whenever you successfully Dodge, you heal yourself for 2820 Health over 5 seconds. This effect can occur every 5 seconds and scales off the higher of your Max Magicka or Stamina.

### Crafting Site
Augury Basin
