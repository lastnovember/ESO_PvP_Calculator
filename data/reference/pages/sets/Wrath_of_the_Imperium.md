# Online:Wrath of the Imperium

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Wrath_of_the_Imperium
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Wrath of the Imperium is an item set found in boxes purchased using Alliance Points from the Cropsford Quartermaster, as quest rewards in Cropsford, as a reward for completing delves and dolmens throughout Cyrodiil, from completing Board Missions, and from Elite Gear Vendors. It cannot be crafted and does not drop from monsters. Pieces are Aldmeri Dominion, Daggerfall Covenant, or Ebonheart Pact Style in Light Armor, depending on the player's alliance.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
3 items: Adds 7-325 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage to your ranged direct damage abilities.

### Pieces
The set consists of jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
