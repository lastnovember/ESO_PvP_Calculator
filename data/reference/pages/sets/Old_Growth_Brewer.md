# Online:Old Growth Brewer

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Old_Growth_Brewer
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Old Growth Brewer is one of the craftable sets providing special bonuses to armor and weapons. It is available for crafting to players pursuing blacksmithing, clothing, woodworking, and/or jewelry crafting professions. The special crafting site with stations dedicated to the creation of this set is located at the Old Port Mornard on Galen.

To make an item for this set, a crafter must first complete research on three traits for that particular item at a corresponding crafting station.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Stamina Recovery
3 items: Adds 3-129 Magicka Recovery
4 items: Adds 3-129 Health Recovery
5 items: When you drink a potion while in combat, you gain 5-245 Health, Magicka, and Stamina Recovery for 45 seconds.

### Crafting Site
Old Port Mornard
