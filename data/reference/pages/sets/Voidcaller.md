# Online:Voidcaller

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Voidcaller
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Voidcaller is an overland set found in The Reach. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Nighthollow Style in Light Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: When you take damage, your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage is increased by 0-24 for 5 seconds, stacking up to 20 times. This effect can occur once every half second. Upon reaching 20 stacks, the duration is doubled but can no longer be refreshed.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
