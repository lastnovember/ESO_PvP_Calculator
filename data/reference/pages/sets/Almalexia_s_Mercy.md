# Online:Almalexia's Mercy

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Almalexia's_Mercy
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Almalexia's Mercy is an item set found in boxes purchased using Alliance Points from the Cropsford Quartermaster, as quest rewards in Cropsford, as a reward for completing delves and dolmens throughout Cyrodiil, from completing Board Missions, and from Elite Gear Vendors. Pieces are Aldmeri Dominion, Daggerfall Covenant, or Ebonheart Pact Style (depending on the player's alliance) in Light Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
3 items: Adds 34-1487 Armor
4 items: Adds 34-1487 Armor
5 items: When you take damage, you heal you and your group members within 7 meters of you for 1412 Health over 3 seconds. This effect can occur once every 3 seconds and scales off the higher of your Max Magicka or Stamina.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
