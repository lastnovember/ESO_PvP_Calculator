# Online:The Worm's Raiment

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:The_Worm's_Raiment
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Worm's Raiment is a dungeon set that drops in the Vaults of Madness. It cannot be crafted. Armor pieces are in the old Worm Cult Style in Light Armor, and shield and weapon pieces are Soul-Shriven Style.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Magicka Recovery
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
4 items: Adds 3-129 Magicka Recovery
5 items: Grants 3-145 Magicka Recovery to you and up to 11 other group members within 28 meters of you. This bonus persists through death.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique items include:
- Maleficence, unique loot from Ulguna Soul-Reaver.
- Soul-Reave, unique loot from Ulguna Soul-Reaver.
- Watcher's Eye, unique loot from The Ancient One.
- Ring of the Ancient One, unique loot from The Ancient One.
- Ruin, unique loot from Iskra the Omen.
- The Mad Cleaver, unique loot from the Mad Architect.
- The Mad Maul, unique loot from the Mad Architect.
