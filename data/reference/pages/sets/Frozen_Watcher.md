# Online:Frozen Watcher

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Frozen_Watcher
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Frozen Watcher is a dungeon set that drops in the Depths of Malatar. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Meridian Style in Heavy Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
3 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
4 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
5 items: Summon a blizzard around you while you are Bracing, inflicting 496 Frost damage every 1 second to enemies within 6 meters. This effect scales off your Max Health and has a 15% chance of inflicting the Chilled status effect.

### Pieces
The set consists of Heavy Armor armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
