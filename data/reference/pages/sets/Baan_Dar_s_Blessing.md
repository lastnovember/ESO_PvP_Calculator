# Online:Baan Dar's Blessing

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Baan_Dar's_Blessing
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Baan Dar's Blessing is an Elite Gear Set that was formerly available from "Rewards for the Worthy" mails, earned by participating in PvP, prior to Update 39. It can now be obtained in boxes purchased using Alliance Points from the Vlastarus Quartermaster, as quest rewards in Vlastarus, as a reward for completing delves and dolmens throughout Cyrodiil, from completing Board Missions, and from Elite Gear Vendors. It cannot be crafted and does not drop from monsters. Pieces are Aldmeri Dominion, Daggerfall Covenant, or Ebonheart Pact Style in Heavy Armor, depending on the player's alliance.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 34-1487 Armor
3 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
4 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
5 items: Reduce the duration of Breakable Crowd Control, Immobilizes, and Snares on you by 75%. Reduce the duration of your Breakable Crowd Control, Immobilizes, and Snares by 38%.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
