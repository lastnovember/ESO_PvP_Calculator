# Online:Arms of Infernace

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Arms_of_Infernace
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Arms of Infernace is an item set. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Daedric Style. These items no longer drop in-game, but those who received them when they were available still have them.

### Bonuses

3 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage

### Pieces
The set consists of a necklace and swords.
- Amulet of Conflagration
- Burning Amulet
- Burning Brand
- Flamedancer
- Hopesfire
- Trueflame

### Drop Locations
- Veteran Banished Cells, Veteran Spindleclutch, Veteran Fungal Grotto, Veteran Wayrest Sewers, Veteran Elden Hollow, and Veteran Darkshade Caverns.
