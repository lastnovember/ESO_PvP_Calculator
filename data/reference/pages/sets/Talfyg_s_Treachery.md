# Online:Talfyg's Treachery

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Talfyg's_Treachery
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Talfyg's Treachery is a dungeon set that drops in the Castle Thorn. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Thorn Legion Style in Light Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: Increases your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage by 8-372. Increases your damage taken from Flame and Guild abilities by 5%.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
