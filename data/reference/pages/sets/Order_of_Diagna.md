# Online:Order of Diagna

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Order_of_Diagna
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Order of Diagna is an overland set that drops in the Alik'r Desert. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Redguard Style in Heavy Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
3 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
4 items: Adds 4% Healing Taken
5 items: Adds 5% Healing Taken
5 items: Gain Minor Vitality at all times, increasing your healing received and damage shield strength by 6%.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Gauntlets of the Ra Gada, the quest reward for Risen From the Depths.
- Zariya's Sorrow, the quest reward for Honoring the Dishonored.
- Helm of the Ash'abah, the quest reward for The Initiation.
- Justice Longbow, the quest reward for Gone Missing.
- Greaves of Firsthold, the quest reward for Thwarting the Aldmeri Dominion in the High Elf Style.
- Master's Sharpsword, the quest reward for Master of Leki's Blade.
- Caravan Chain, the quest reward for Trapped in the Bluffs in the Khajiit Style.
- Liberator's Spaulders, the quest reward for Imperial Incursion in the Nord Style.
- Charged Rod of the Order of Diagna, a unique drop from Captain Candidus, the final boss of Yldzuun in the High Elf Style.
- Bladethirst of the Order of Diagna, a unique drop from Feremuzh, the final boss of Coldrock Diggings in the Breton Style.
- Embossed Chausses of the Order of Diagna, a unique drop from Nokhailaedhaz, the final boss of Divad's Chagrin Mine in the Orc Style.
- Infused Buckler of the Order of Diagna, a unique drop from the world boss Staada at Lesser Circle.
