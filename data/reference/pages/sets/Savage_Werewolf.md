# Online:Savage Werewolf

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Savage_Werewolf
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Savage Werewolf is a dungeon set that drops in the Moon Hunter Keep. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Silver Dawn Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
3 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: Your Light Attacks made within melee range rend flesh, causing your enemy to bleed for 290 Bleed damage instantly and additionally every 2 seconds for 4 seconds. This effect scales off the higher of your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage. If you are in Werewolf Transformation, you also gain 6% damage done.

### Pieces
The set consists medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
