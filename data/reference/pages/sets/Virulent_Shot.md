# Online:Virulent Shot

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Virulent_Shot
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Virulent Shot is a Blackrose Weapons item set awarded from normal Blackrose Prison. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Honor Guard Style. The perfected version of this set can be looted on veteran difficulty.

### Bonuses

2 items: Scatter Shot applies a damage over time effect that deals 65% of your initial attack as Poison Damage every 2 seconds for 4 seconds. The duration increases if you are farther away from your target to a maximum of 12 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists of a Bow.

### Notes
While the description states that 2 items are required, this is because bows are 2-handed weapons, and thus count as 2 items by themselves, so only 1 item is actually needed.
