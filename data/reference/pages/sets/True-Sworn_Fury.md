# Online:True-Sworn Fury

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:True-Sworn_Fury
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Kinras's Wrath is a dungeon set that drops in Black Drake Villa. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are True-Sworn Style in Light Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
3 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
4 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
5 items: Adds 16-708 Critical Chance and increases your Critical Damage by 0-4%. While in combat, this bonus doubles when you are under 75% Health and quadruples when you are under 50% Health.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
