# Online:Fortified Brass

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Fortified_Brass
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Fortified Brass is one of the craftable sets providing special bonuses to armor and weapons. It is available for crafting to players pursuing blacksmithing, clothing, woodworking, and/or jewelry crafting professions. The special crafting site with stations dedicated to creation of this set are located in the Clockwork CityClockwork City.

To make an item for this set, a crafter must first complete research on four traits for this particular item at a corresponding crafting station.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
3 items: Adds 34-1487 Armor
4 items: Adds 34-1487 Armor
5 items: Adds 80-3460 Armor

### Crafting Site
Restricted Brassworks
