# Online:Unleashed Ritualist

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Unleashed_Ritualist
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Unleashed Ritualist is a dungeon set found in The Cauldron. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Waking Flame style in Light Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
3 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: Increases your damage done with summoned pets by 0-20%.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
