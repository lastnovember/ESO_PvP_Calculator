# Online:Phoenix Moth Theurge

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Phoenix_Moth_Theurge
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Phoenix Moth Theurge is an overland set that drops in Galen. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Firesong Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Stamina Recovery
3 items: Adds 3-129 Stamina Recovery
4 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
5 items: Healing yourself or an ally grants the target Minor Courage and Minor Force for 10 seconds, increasing their Weapon Damage and Spell Damage by 215 and their Critical Damage done by 10%. This effect can occur once every 12 seconds per target.

### Pieces
The set consists of Medium Armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
