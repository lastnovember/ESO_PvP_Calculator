# Online:Deadlands Assassin

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Deadlands_Assassin
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Deadlands Assassin is an overland set that drops in Blackwood. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Daedric Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
3 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
4 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
5 items: Adds 4-200 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: Dealing damage to an enemy within 10 meters of you with a Heavy Attack causes you to throw a cone of knives, dealing 816 Physical damage to enemies hit. If an enemy hit has 50% Health or less, they also take an additional 1631 Bleed damage over 14 seconds. This effect can occur once every 15 seconds and scales off the higher of your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
