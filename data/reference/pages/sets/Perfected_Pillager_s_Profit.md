# Online:Perfected Pillager's Profit

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Perfected_Pillager's_Profit
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Perfected Pillager's Profit is a trial set found in veteran Dreadsail Reef. It cannot be crafted. Set pieces are Syrabanic Marine style in Light Armor. The non-perfected version of this set can be looted on normal difficulty.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 4% Healing Done
3 items: Gain Minor Aegis at all times, reducing your damage taken from Dungeon, Trial, and Arena Monsters by 5%.
4 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
5 perfected items: Adds 3-129 Magicka Recovery
5 items: Casting an Ultimate ability while in combat grants 2% of Ultimate spent as Ultimate to up to 11 other group members within 12 meters every 2 seconds over 10 seconds. Group members can only be affected by this set once every 45 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
