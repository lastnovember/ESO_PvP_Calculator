# Online:Sul-Xan's Torment

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Sul-Xan's_Torment
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Sul-Xan's Torment is a trial set which drops in normal Rockgrove. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Sul-Xan Style in Medium Armor. The perfected version of this set can be looted on veteran difficulty.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
3 items: Gain Minor Slayer at all times, increasing your damage done to Dungeon, Trial, and Arena Monsters by 5%.
4 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
5 items: When an enemy you recently damaged dies, they leave behind a vengeful soul for 6 seconds. You can only create one vengeful soul at a time. Touching the soul increases your Critical Chance by 50-2160 and your Critical Damage by 12% for 30 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
