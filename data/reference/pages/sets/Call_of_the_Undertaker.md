# Online:Call of the Undertaker

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Call_of_the_Undertaker
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Call of the Undertaker is an overland set found in Northern Elsweyr. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Anequina Style in Heavy Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 4% Healing Taken
3 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
4 items: Adds 4% Healing Done
5 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
5 items: When you deal damage, you apply Minor Lifesteal to your enemy for 8 seconds, healing you and your allies for 600 Health every 1 second when damaging them.

### Pieces
The set consists of Heavy Armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Anequina Crypt Cudgel, the quest reward for A Rage of Dragons
- Greatsword of the Cygnus Irregulars, the quest reward for The Final Order
- Investigator's Burden, the quest reward for The Riverhold Abduction
- Poacher's Grips, the quest reward for Preserving the Prowl|
- Ruddy Fang Helm, the quest reward for Moonstruck in Manacles
- Sabatons of the Lich, the quest reward for The Witch of Azurah
- Sheggorath's Greaves, the quest reward for The Lunacy of Two Moons
- Staff of Corrupted Springs, the quest reward for Scars of the Past
- Sugar-Baron's Defender, the quest reward for Home Sweet Home
- Tenarr Zalviit Pauldrons, the quest reward for Blood and Tears
