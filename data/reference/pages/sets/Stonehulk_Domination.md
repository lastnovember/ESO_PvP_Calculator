# Online:Stonehulk Domination

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Stonehulk_Domination
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Stonehulk Domination is a dungeon set that drops in Naj-Caldeesh. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Voskrona Guardian Style in Heavy Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 4% Healing Taken
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
4 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
5 items: When you taunt an enemy, apply Major Vulnerability to them, increasing their damage taken by 10% for 12 seconds. This effect can occur once every 15 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
