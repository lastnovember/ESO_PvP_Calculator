# Online:Harmony in Chaos

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Harmony_in_Chaos
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Harmony in Chaos is a trial set found in normal Ossein Cage. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are in the Coldharbour Dominator Style in Light Armor. The perfected version of this set can be looted on veteran difficulty.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
3 items: Gain Minor Slayer at all times, increasing your damage done to Dungeon, Trial, and Arena Monsters by 5%.
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: Adds 4-200 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: While in combat, activating a synergy increases your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage by 88 for 15 seconds, stacking up to 5 times.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
