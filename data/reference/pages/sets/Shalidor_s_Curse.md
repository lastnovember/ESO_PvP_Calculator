# Online:Shalidor's Curse

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Shalidor's_Curse
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Shalidor's Curse is one of the craftable sets providing special bonuses to armor and weapons. It is available for crafting to players pursuing blacksmithing, clothing, woodworking and/or Jewelry Crafting professions. The special crafting site dedicated to creation of this set is available in Eyevea on completion of the Mages Guild quest line.

To craft an item for this set, a crafter must first complete research on eight traits for this particular item at a corresponding crafting station.

The Spellbreaker's Staff received on completion of the Mages Guild quest line is a part of the Shalidor's Curse set.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
3 items: Adds 4% Healing Taken
4 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
5 items: While you are under 50% Health, dealing damage with a Light or Heavy Attack heals you for 941 Health. This effect can occur once every 1 second and scales off the higher of your Max Magicka or Stamina.
