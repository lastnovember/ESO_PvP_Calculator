# Online:Macabre Vintage

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Macabre_Vintage
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Macabre Vintage is an overland set that drops in West Weald. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Shardborn Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
3 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: Adds 3-150 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: When you kill a monster they burst with blood magic, dealing 50% of their Max Health to enemies within 6 meters as Bleed Damage, up to 24096 Bleed Damage. This damage cannot critically strike. This effect can occur once every 0.5 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists of Medium Armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
