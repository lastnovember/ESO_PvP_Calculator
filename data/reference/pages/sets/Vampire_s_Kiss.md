# Online:Vampire's Kiss

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Vampire's_Kiss
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Vampire's Kiss is one of the craftable sets providing special bonuses to armor and weapons. It is available for crafting to players pursuing blacksmithing, clothing, woodworking, and/or jewelry crafting professions. One special crafting site with stations dedicated to creation of this set is located in the fourth zone of each of the three Alliances.

To make an item for this set, a crafter must first complete research on five traits for this particular item at a corresponding crafting station.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
4 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
4 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
5 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: When you kill an enemy, you heal for 10512 Health over 10 seconds. This effect scales off the higher of your Max Magicka or Stamina.
