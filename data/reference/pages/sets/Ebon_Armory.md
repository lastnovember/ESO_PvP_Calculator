# Online:Ebon Armory

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Ebon_Armory
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Ebon Armory is a dungeon set that drops in the Crypt of Hearts I and II. It cannot be crafted. Armor pieces are Ebon Style in Heavy Armor, and shield and weapon pieces are Soul-Shriven Style.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
3 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
4 items: Adds 4% Healing Taken
5 items: Increases Max Health by 23-1000 for you and up to 11 other group members within 28 meters of you. This bonus persists through death.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Bonehand's Amulet, a unique drop from Uulkar Bonehand.
- Dremora Collar, a unique drop from Dogas the Berserker.
- Heartbreaker's Ebon Greaves, a quest reward for Lover's Torment.
- Ibelgast's Promise, a unique drop from Ibelgast.
- Mallet of the Devourer, a unique drop from the Ilambris Amalgam.

### Notes
- Standard items in this set bear the name "Ebon [piece]", which is identical to the names of non-set items crafted from Ebony Ore. Don't be fooled by impostors.
- While the armor pieces look like they are of Ebon Style, deconstructing them doesn't yield any Night Pumice.
- The Ebon Armory buff displays the following icon on your buff bar: 32
