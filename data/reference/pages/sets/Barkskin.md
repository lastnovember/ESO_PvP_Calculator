# Online:Barkskin

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Barkskin
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Barkskin is a dungeon set that drops in Elden Hollow I and II. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Primal Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
3 items: Adds 3-129 Stamina Recovery
4 items: Adds 34-1487 Armor
5 items: Reduces the duration of immobilizations and snares applied to you by 50%. When an immobilization or snare is applied to you, heal for 39-1715 Health and restore 19-858 Stamina. This effect can occur once every 5 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Bogdan's Claw, unique loot from Bogdan the Nightflame.
- Canonreeve's Scepter, unique loot from Canonreeve Oraneth.
- Murkhammer, unique loot from Murklight.
- Tribal Hair-Knot, unique loot from Nenesh gro-Mal.
