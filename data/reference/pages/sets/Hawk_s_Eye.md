Hawk Eye
Hawk's Eye is an item set found in boxes purchased using Alliance Points from the Bruma Quartermaster, as quest rewards from Bruma, as a reward for completing delves and dolmens throughout Cyrodiil, from completing Board Missions, and from Elite Gear Vendors. It cannot be crafted and does not drop from monsters. The helmet and arm cops are Hawk's Eye Style. Other pieces are Aldmeri Dominion, Daggerfall Covenant, or Ebonheart Pact Style in Medium Armor, depending on the player's alliance.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
3 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: Reduces the cost and increases the damage of your Bow abilities by 6%.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as bows (other weapons and shields may still exist but cannot be obtained new anymore).
