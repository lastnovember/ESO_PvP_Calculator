# Online:Explosive Rebuke

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Explosive_Rebuke
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Explosive Rebuke is a set which drops in the Vateshran Hollows. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Ancestral Reach Style in Heavy Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
3 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: When you Block, you place a bomb on the enemy for 10 seconds. When they are hit with a fully-charged Heavy Attack, the bomb detonates, dealing 0 Flame damage to all enemies within 8 meters and applying the Burning status effect to them. This effect can occur once every 8 seconds and scales off the higher of your Physical Resistance or Spell Resistance.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
