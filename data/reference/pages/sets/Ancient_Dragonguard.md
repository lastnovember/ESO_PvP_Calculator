# Online:Ancient Dragonguard

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Ancient_Dragonguard
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Ancient Dragonguard is one of the craftable sets providing special bonuses to armor and weapons. It is available for crafting to players pursuing blacksmithing, clothing, woodworking, and/or jewelry crafting professions. The special crafting site with stations dedicated to creation of this set are located in Southern ElsweyrDragonhold, which is available after completing Restoring the Order.

To make an item for this set, a crafter must first complete research on three traits for shoulder pieces at a corresponding crafting station, and six traits for all other pieces.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
3 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
4 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
5 items: Adds 6-300 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage while your Health is above 50%. Adds 80-3460 Physical Resistance and Spell Resistance while your Health is 50% or less.

### Crafting Site
Dragonguard Armory

### Pieces
The set consists of every type of armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Ancient Dragonguard Sword, a quest reward for The Dragon's Lair.
