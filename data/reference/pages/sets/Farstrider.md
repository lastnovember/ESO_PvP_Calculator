# Online:Farstrider

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Farstrider
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Farstrider is a Battlegrounds Set rewarded by Battlegrounds. It cannot be crafted and does not drop from monsters. Pieces are Militant Ordinator Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 34-1487 Armor
3 items: Adds 9-421 Critical Resistance
4 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
5 items: When you deal direct damage with a Blink, Charge, Leap, Teleport, or Pull ability, decrease the Critical Resistance of your enemy by 40% and increase your Critical Resistance by an equal amount for 10 seconds. This effect can occur once every 7 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
