# Online:Balorgh (set)

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Balorgh_(set)
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Balorgh

Balorgh is a monster helm set. It consists of two armor pieces: head and shoulder, both of which are available in all three armor weights and standard traits. The set cannot be crafted. There is an outfit style based on this armor set.

### Bonuses

1 item: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
2 items: When you use an Ultimate ability, you gain Weapon Damage and Spell Damage equal to the amount of total Ultimate consumed, and Physical Penetration and Spell Penetration equal to 23 times the amount for 12 seconds.

### Drop Locations
- The head drops from Balorgh in Veteran March of Sacrifices.
- The shoulders can be obtained from Urgarlag Chief-bane's reward chest at Undaunted Enclaves.
