# Online:Eternal Vigor

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Eternal_Vigor
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Eternal Vigor is an overland set found in Western Skyrim. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are in the Greymoor Style in Heavy Armor, except for the Restoration Staff of Eternal Vigor which is in the Anequina Style.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Magicka Recovery
3 items: Adds 3-129 Stamina Recovery
4 items: Adds 3-129 Health Recovery
5 items: Adds 7-337 Stamina and Magicka Recovery while your Health is above 50%. Adds 23-1011 Health Recovery while your Health is 50% or less.

### Pieces
The set consists of Heavy Armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Dragonhome Helm, the quest reward for Spellbound
- Kelbarn's Mantlet, the quest reward for Kelbarn's Mining Samples
- Drendek's Gloves, the quest reward for The Pale Man
- Morthal Barrow Breastplate, the quest reward for Danger in the Holds
- Girdle of Inguya, the quest reward for Inguya's Mining Samples
- Aggrieved Greaves, the quest reward for The Maelmoth Mysterium
- Karthwatch Walking Boots, the quest reward for The Blood of Old Karth
- Hircine's Moonring, the quest reward for Hounds of Hircine
- Dragon Bridge Bastion, the quest reward for A Crisis at Dragon Bridge
- Frost-Cursed Staff, the quest reward for Of Ice and Death
- Nchuthnkarst Halberd, the quest reward for The Tones of the Deep
- Viggol's Mallet, the quest reward for One Last Adventure
- Sword of Solitude, the quest reward for Daughter of the Wolf
