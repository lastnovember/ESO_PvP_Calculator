# Online:Medusa

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Medusa
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Medusa is a dungeon set that drops in Arx Corinium. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Argonian Style in Heavy Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
3 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
4 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
5 items: Adds 20-892 Critical Chance
5 items: Gain Minor Force at all times, increasing your Critical Damage by 10%.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Swampbinder, the Ancient Lurcher's unique loot.
- Sellistrix's Siphon, Sellistrix's unique loot.
- Fang of the Lamia Queen, Sellistrix's unique loot.
