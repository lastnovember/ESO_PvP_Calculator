# Online:Black Foundry Steel

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Black_Foundry_Steel
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Black Foundry Steel is a dungeon set that drops in Black Gem Foundry. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Black Soul Gem Style in Heavy Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
4 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
5 items: Automatically block all attacks at no cost for 2 seconds when you begin a Heavy Attack. This effect can occur once every 12 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
