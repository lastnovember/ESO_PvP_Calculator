# Online:Siegemaster's Focus

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Siegemaster's_Focus
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Siegemaster's Focus is a Rewards for the Worthy Set. Set pieces are awarded via "Rewards for the Worthy" mails by participating in PvP. It cannot be crafted and does not drop from monsters. Pieces are Aldmeri Dominion, Daggerfall Covenant, or Ebonheart Pact Style in Heavy Armor, depending on the player's alliance.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 9-424 Critical Resistance
3 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
4 items: Adds 34-1487 Armor
5 items: While you are using a non-Ram Siege Weapon you are immune to knockback and disabling effects, you reduce your damage taken by 33%, and you can see enemies through walls.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
