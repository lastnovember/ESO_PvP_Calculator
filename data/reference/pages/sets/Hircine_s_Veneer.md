# Online:Hircine's Veneer

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Hircine's_Veneer
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Hircine's Veneer is a dungeon set that drops in Selene's Web. It cannot be crafted. Armor pieces are Savior's Hide Style in Medium Armor, and shield and weapon pieces are Soul-Shriven Style.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Stamina Recovery
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
4 items: Adds 3-129 Stamina Recovery
5 items: Grants 3-145 Stamina Recovery to you and up to 11 other group members within 28 meters of you. This bonus persists through death.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Adventurer's Lost Medallion, unique loot from Queen Aklayah.
- Longclaw's Bow, unique loot from Longclaw.
