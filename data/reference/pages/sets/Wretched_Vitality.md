# Online:Wretched Vitality

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Wretched_Vitality
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Wretched Vitality is one of the craftable sets providing special bonuses to armor and weapons. It is available for crafting to players pursuing blacksmithing, clothing, woodworking, and/or jewelry crafting professions. The special crafting site with stations dedicated to creation of this set are located at Stormwright's Cleft in the DeadlandsThe Deadlands.

To make an item for this set, a crafter must first complete research on three traits for this particular item at a corresponding crafting station.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Magicka Recovery
3 items: Adds 3-129 Stamina Recovery
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: While in combat, applying a Major Buff or Debuff with a duration to a target grants you 260 Magicka and Stamina Recovery for 15 seconds. While in combat, applying a Minor Buff or Debuff with a duration to a target grants you 130 Magicka and Stamina Recovery for 15 seconds.

### Crafting Site
Stormwright's Cleft

### Notes
- The 5 piece bonuses of this set only trigger if you are in combat.
- Minor Buffs and Debuffs applied via status effects do not trigger this set's 5 piece bonuses.
- This set uses the following icons for each of its bonuses: 130 recovery -> Champion and 260 recovery -> Grand Champion
