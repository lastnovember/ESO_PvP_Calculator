# Online:Dead-Water's Guile

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Dead-Water's_Guile
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Dead-Water's Guile is an overland set that drops in Murkmire. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Dead-Water Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
3 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
4 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
5 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: Whenever you kill an enemy, you gain 0-15 Ultimate.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Maul of the Fallen Comrade, the quest reward for The Swamp and the Serpent
