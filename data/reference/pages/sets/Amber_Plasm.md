# Online:Amber Plasm

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Amber_Plasm
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Amber Plasm is a dungeon set that drops in the Ruins of Mazzatun. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Mazzatun Style in Light Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
3 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: Adds 5-245 Magicka Recovery
5 items: Adds 5-245 Stamina Recovery
5 items: Adds 5-245 Health Recovery

### Pieces
The set consists light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
