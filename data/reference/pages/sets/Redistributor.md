# Online:Redistributor

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Redistributor
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Redistributor is one of the craftable sets providing special bonuses to armor and weapons. It is available for crafting to players pursuing blacksmithing, clothing, woodworking, and/or jewelry crafting professions. The special crafting site with stations dedicated to creation of this set are located in the Imperial CityImperial City.

To make an item for this set, a crafter must first complete research on seven traits for this particular item at a corresponding crafting station.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
4 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
5 items: When you overheal yourself, you heal a group member within 15 meters of you for 663 Health. This effect can occur once every 1 second and scales off the higher of your Max Magicka or Stamina.

### Crafting Site
Arboretum Armory
