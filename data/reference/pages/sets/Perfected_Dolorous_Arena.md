# Online:Perfected Dolorous Arena

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Perfected_Dolorous_Arena
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Perfected Dolorous Arena is a trial set found in veteran Ossein Cage. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are in the Coldharbour Dominator Style in Heavy Armor. The non-perfected version of this set can be looted on veteran difficulty.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
3 items: Gain Minor Aegis at all times, reducing your damage taken from Dungeon, Trial, and Arena Monsters by 5%.
4 items: Adds 34-1487 Armor
5 perfected items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
5 items: While bracing you create a distortion barrier around yourself after 2 seconds. After the delay, every 2 seconds you gain 14-614 Armor and enemies within the distortion barrier have their Armor reduced by 42-1843 for 5 seconds. These effects stack up to 3 times.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
