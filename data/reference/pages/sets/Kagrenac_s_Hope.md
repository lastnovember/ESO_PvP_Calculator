# Online:Kagrenac's Hope

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Kagrenac's_Hope
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Kagrenac's Hope is one of the craftable sets providing special bonuses to armor and weapons. It is available for crafting to players pursuing blacksmithing, clothing, woodworking, and/or jewelry crafting professions. The special crafting site dedicated to creation of this set is available in the Earth Forge on completion of the Fighters Guild quest line.

To craft an item for this set, a crafter must first complete research on eight traits for this particular item at a corresponding crafting station.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
3 items: Adds 3-129 Magicka Recovery
4 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
5 items: Adds 5-222 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: Decreases the time it takes to resurrect an ally by 25%. When you successfully resurrect an ally, you restore 40-1720 Magicka.
