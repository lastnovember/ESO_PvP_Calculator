# Online:Undaunted Infiltrator

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Undaunted_Infiltrator
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Undaunted Infiltrator is a dungeon set that drops in Arx Corinium. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Mercenary Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
4 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
5 items: When you use an ability that costs Magicka while in combat, you increase the damage of your Light and Heavy Attacks against monsters by 38-1645 for 10 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Chain of the Snake-Charmer, the Fanged Menace's unique loot.
- Sweetsong, Sliklenia the Songstress' unique loot.
