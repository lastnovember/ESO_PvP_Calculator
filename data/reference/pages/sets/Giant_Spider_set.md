# Online:Giant Spider (set)

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Giant_Spider_(set)
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Giant Spider is a dropped item set. It cannot be crafted. Set pieces are available in all kinds of armor and standard traits.

### Bonuses

1 item: Adds 4% Healing Taken
2 items: When you Block an enemy that is between 8 and 22 meters from you, you spin strands of spider silk to pull the enemy to you and taunt them for 15 seconds if they are not already taunted. This effect can occur once every 1 second.

### Pieces
The set originally consisted of two pieces: head and shoulder. Only the headpiece is acquirable.

### Drop Locations
- The headpiece of this set was acquirable during the weekend of January 5-8, 2018. It was intended to be a discarded set replaced by the Swarm Mother set.
