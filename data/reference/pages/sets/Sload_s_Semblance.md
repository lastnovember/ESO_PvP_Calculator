# Online:Sload's Semblance

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Sload's_Semblance
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Sload's Semblance is one of the craftable sets providing special bonuses to armor and weapons. It is available for crafting to players pursuing blacksmithing, clothing, woodworking, and/or jewelry crafting professions. The special crafting site with stations dedicated to creation of this set are located in Summerset Isle.

To make an item for this set, a crafter must first complete research on six traits for this particular item at a corresponding crafting station.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: Damaging an enemy has a 10% chance to fire a Shadow Pearl at them, dealing 3% of the target's Max Health as Oblivion Damage every 1 second for 6 seconds. The Shadow Pearl cannot be reflected. Effect can occur every 6 seconds. This can deal a maximum of 69-3000 Oblivion Damage per tick.

### Crafting Site
Artaeum Craftworks

### Notes
- This set uses the following icon for its death recap and debuff: Oblivion
