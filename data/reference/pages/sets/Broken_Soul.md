# Online:Broken Soul

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Broken_Soul
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Broken Soul is a jewelry item set. It can only be acquired from the Level Up Advisor at Level 5, and consists of 2 Rings. It cannot be crafted.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health

### Pieces
- Left Ring of the Broken Soul
- Right Ring of the Broken Soul

### Notes
- Since this only drops at one specific level, the range of the bonus is not variable.
