# Online:Serpent's Disdain

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Serpent's_Disdain
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Serpent's Disdain is one of the craftable sets providing special bonuses to armor and weapons. It is available for crafting to players pursuing blacksmithing, clothing, woodworking, and/or jewelry crafting professions. The special crafting site with stations dedicated to creation of this set are located at Stonelore Forge and Craft on High Isle.

To make an item for this set, a crafter must first complete research on five traits for this particular item at a corresponding crafting station.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
3 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
4 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
5 items: Increase the duration of Status Effects you apply by 16 seconds.

### Crafting Site
Stonelore Forge and Craft
