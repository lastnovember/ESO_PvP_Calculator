# Online:Spell Strategist

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Spell_Strategist
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Spell Strategist is an Elite Gear Set that was formerly available from "Rewards for the Worthy" mails, earned by participating in PvP, prior to Update 39. It can now be obtained in boxes purchased using Alliance Points from the Cropsford Quartermaster, as quest rewards in Cropsford, as a reward for completing delves and dolmens throughout Cyrodiil, from completing Board Missions, and from Elite Gear Vendors. It cannot be crafted and does not drop from monsters. Pieces are Aldmeri Dominion, Daggerfall Covenant, or Ebonheart Pact Style in Light Armor, depending on the player's alliance.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
3 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: When you deal damage with a Light Attack, you place a mark over your target for 5 seconds, granting you 10-460 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage against your marked target. This effect can occur every 4 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.

### Notes
- In addition to placing a visible mark over the head of your opponent, Spell Strategist also has a visible effect around you. This is important in PvP because it means that your target can tell which opponent has hit them with this effect. Conversely, if you see the mark over your head, look around for the enemy player with the purple swirls around them in order to know who is more dangerous to you right now.
