# Online:Perfected Peace and Serenity

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Perfected_Peace_and_Serenity
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Perfected Peace and Serenity is a trial set found in veteran Sanity's Edge. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are in Clan Dreamcarver style in Light Armor. The non-perfected version of this set can be looted on normal difficulty.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
3 items: Gain Minor Slayer at all times, increasing your damage done to Dungeon, Trial, and Arena Monsters by 5%.
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 perfected items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: While you are standing still, you gain 10-465 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage. While you are moving, you gain 4-203 Health, Magicka, and Stamina Recovery.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
