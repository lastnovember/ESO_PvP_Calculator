# Online:Daring Corsair

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Daring_Corsair
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Daring Corsair is one of the craftable sets providing special bonuses to armor and weapons. It is available for crafting to players pursuing blacksmithing, clothing, woodworking, and/or jewelry crafting professions. The special crafting site with stations dedicated to creation of this set are located in Southern ElsweyrDragonhold.

To make an item for this set, a crafter must first complete research on three traits for this particular item at a corresponding crafting station.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Stamina Recovery
3 items: Adds 3-129 Magicka Recovery
4 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
5 items: Reduce the cost of your Weapon abilities by 10% Magicka or Stamina. After casting a Weapon ability, you gain Minor Heroism for 8 seconds, generating 1 Ultimate every 1.5 seconds while in combat. This effect can occur every 8 seconds.

### Crafting Site
Cat's-Claw Station
