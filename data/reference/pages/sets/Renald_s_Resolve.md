# Online:Renald's Resolve

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Renald's_Resolve
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Hollowfang Thirst is a dungeon set that drops in the Moongrave Fane. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Moongrave Fane Style in Heavy Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
3 items: Adds 4% Healing Taken
4 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
5 items: Whenever you deal direct damage, gain a stack of Resolve for 5 seconds. Each stack increases your healing received by 1% and your Physical Resistance and Spell Resistance by 9-420. This effect can occur once every half second and can stack up to 9 times.

### Pieces
The set consists heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
