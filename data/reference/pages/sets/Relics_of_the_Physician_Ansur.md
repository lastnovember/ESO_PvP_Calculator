# Online:Relics of the Physician, Ansur

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Relics_of_the_Physician,_Ansur
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Relics of the Physician, Ansur is an item set. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Daedric Style. These items no longer drop in-game, but those who received them when they were available still have them.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
3 items: Increases your healing done by 5%.

### Pieces
The set consists of rings and restoration staves.
- Healing Staff of Thorn
- Restoration Ring of Thorn
