# Online:Magnus' Gift

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Magnus'_Gift
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Magnus' Gift is one of the craftable sets providing special bonuses to armor and weapons. It is available for crafting to players pursuing blacksmithing, clothing, woodworking, and/or jewelry crafting professions. One special crafting site with stations dedicated to creation of this set is located in the third zone of each of the three Alliances.

To make an item for this set, a crafter must first complete research on four traits for this particular item at a corresponding crafting station.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
3 items: Adds 3-129 Magicka Recovery
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: When you cast a Magicka ability, you have a 15% chance to negate that ability's cost.
