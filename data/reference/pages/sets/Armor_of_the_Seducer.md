# Online:Armor of the Seducer

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Armor_of_the_Seducer
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Armor of the Seducer is one of the craftable sets providing special bonuses to armor and weapons. It is available for crafting to players pursuing blacksmithing, clothing, woodworking, and/or jewelry crafting professions. One special crafting site with stations dedicated to creation of this set is located in the second zone of each of the three Alliances.

To make an item for this set, a crafter must first complete research on three traits for this particular item at a corresponding crafting station.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Magicka Recovery
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
4 items: Adds 3-129 Magicka Recovery
5 items: Reduces the cost of your Magicka abilities by 10%.
