Grundwulf

Grundwulf is a monster helm set. It consists of two armor pieces: head and shoulder, both of which are available in all three armor weights and standard traits. The set cannot be crafted. There is an outfit style based on this armor set.

### Bonuses

1 item: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
2 items: Whenever you deal critical damage, restore 16-700 Magicka or Stamina, whichever maximum is higher. You also gain 8-350 of the other resource. This effect can occur once every 5 seconds.

### Drop Locations
- The head drops from Grundwulf in Veteran Moongrave Fane.
- The shoulders can be obtained from Urgarlag Chief-bane's reward chest at Undaunted Enclaves.
