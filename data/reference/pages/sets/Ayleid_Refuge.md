# Online:Ayleid Refuge

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Ayleid_Refuge
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Ayleid Refuge is an overland set that drops in West Weald. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Shardborn Style in Heavy Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
3 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
4 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
5 items: Blocking an attack reduces your damage taken by 11% for 3 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists of Heavy Armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Ring of Faithfulness, reward from the delve quest for Fyrelight Cave in West Weald, A Matter of Propriety
