# Online:Scorion's Feast

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Scorion's_Feast
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Scorion's Feast is a dungeon set that drops in The Dread Cellar. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Crimson Oath Style in Light Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
3 items: Adds 3-129 Magicka Recovery
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: When you deal damage with a fully-charged Heavy Attack, you gain an Imbued Aura for 10 seconds, granting you and up to 3 other group members 7-307 Magicka and Stamina Recovery. This effect can occur once every 20 seconds. If you deal damage with a fully-charged Heavy Attack with an Imbued Aura active, consume it and gain an Overflow Aura for 10 seconds, granting you and up to 3 other group members 7-307 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
