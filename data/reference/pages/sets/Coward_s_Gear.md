# Online:Coward's Gear

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Coward's_Gear
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Coward's Gear is a Battlegrounds Set rewarded by Battlegrounds. It can be obtained in boxes purchased using Alliance Points from the Bruma Quartermaster. It cannot be crafted and does not drop from monsters. Pieces are Militant Ordinator Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
3 items: Adds 3-129 Stamina Recovery
4 items: Adds 3-129 Stamina Recovery
5 items: Adds 5-250 Stamina Recovery
5 items: While Sprinting you gain Major Expedition and Major Protection, increasing your Movement Speed by 30% and reducing your damage taken by 10%.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
