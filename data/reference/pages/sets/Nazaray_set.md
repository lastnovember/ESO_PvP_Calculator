# Online:Nazaray (set)

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Nazaray_(set)
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

equipped setNazaray
Nazaray is a monster helm set. It consists of two armor pieces: head and shoulder, both of which are available in all three armor weights and standard traits. The set cannot be crafted. There is an outfit style based on this armor set.

### Bonuses

1 item: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
2 items: When you use an Ultimate ability, the closest 6 enemies within 12 meters have all Major and Minor debuffs applied to them extended by 1 second per 20 Ultimate spent. This effect can occur once every 30 seconds per target.

### Drop Locations
- The head drops from the Captain Numirril in Veteran Shipwright's Regret.
- The shoulders can be obtained from Urgarlag Chief-bane's reward chest at Undaunted Enclaves.
