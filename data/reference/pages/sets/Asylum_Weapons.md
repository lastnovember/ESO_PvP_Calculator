# Online:Asylum Weapons

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Asylum_Weapons
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Asylum Weapons are a special weapon set that drops in the Asylum Sanctorium trial. All weapons bear the Asylum Style.

## Types
Asylum weapons are available in two types - normal and perfected. Perfected versions of weapons are slightly more powerful, by virtue of bearing extra stats, longer damage effects or similar bonuses.

- Normal weapons can drop from the last boss of the Trial on normal difficulty.
- The Perfected versions of these weapons only drop in Veteran Hard Mode or can be awarded to players who successfully rank on the weekly Trials leaderboard.
