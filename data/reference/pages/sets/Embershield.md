# Online:Embershield

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Embershield
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Embershield is a dungeon set that drops in the City of Ash I and II. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Daedric Style in Heavy Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
3 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
4 items: Adds 34-1487 Armor
5 items: When you complete a fully-charged Heavy Attack, you increase your Physical Resistance and Spell Resistance by 76-3300 and deal 0 Flame damage to all enemies within 5 meters of you every 1 second for 6 seconds. The damage scales off the higher of your Physical Resistance or Spell Resistance.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Aegis of Ash, unique loot from the Warden of the Shrine.
- Betrayer's Axe, unique loot from Razor Master Erthas.
- Charged Loop, unique loot from the Xivilai Boltaic and Fulminator.
- Rothariel's Necklace, unique loot from Rothariel Flameheart.
