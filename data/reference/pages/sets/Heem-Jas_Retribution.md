# Online:Heem-Jas' Retribution

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Heem-Jas'_Retribution
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Heem-Jas' Retribution is a dungeon set that drops in the Ruins of Mazzatun. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Mazzatun Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Stamina Recovery
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
4 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
5 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: When you are within 28 meters of an enemy you are in combat with, and the enemy dies, you gain Major Berserk for 10 seconds, increasing your damage done by 10%.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
