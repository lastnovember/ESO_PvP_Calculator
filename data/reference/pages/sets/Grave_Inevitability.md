# Online:Grave Inevitability

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Grave_Inevitability
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Grave Inevitability is a dungeon set that drops in Graven Deep. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Drowned Mariner Style in Light Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
3 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
4 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
5 items: Dealing damage with a Light or Heavy Attack grants you a stack of Remorseless for 5 seconds, up to 5 stacks, up to once every 0.5 seconds. While you have 5 stacks, you generate an aura of focus, granting you Major Force, increasing your Critical Damage by 20%, while group members within 12 meters of you gain Minor Force, increasing their Critical Damage by 10%.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
