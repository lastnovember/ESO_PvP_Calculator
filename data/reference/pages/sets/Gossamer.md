# Online:Gossamer

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Gossamer
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Gossamer is a dungeon set that drops in the Cradle of Shadows. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Silken Ring Style in Light Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Magicka Recovery
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
4 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
5 items: When you heal yourself or an ally, you grant them Major Evasion for 4 seconds, reducing damage from area attacks by 20%.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
