# Online:Witch-Knight's Defiance

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Witch-Knight's_Defiance
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Witch-Knight's Defiance is an overland set found in The Reach. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Wayward Guardian Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
3 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: Increases your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage by 8-369 against enemies inflicted with a Poison Damage effect.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
