# Online:Telvanni Enforcer

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Telvanni_Enforcer
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Telvanni Enforcer is a dungeon set that drops in Bal Sunnar. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Blessed Inheritor Style in Heavy Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
3 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
4 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
5 items: While Bracing, increase your Magicka Recovery by 369. While you are not Bracing, increase your Stamina Recovery by 369.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
