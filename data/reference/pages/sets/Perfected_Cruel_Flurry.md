# Online:Perfected Cruel Flurry

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Perfected_Cruel_Flurry
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Perfected Cruel Flurry is a Perfected Maelstrom Weapons item set found in veteran Maelstrom Arena. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Maelstrom style. The non-perfected version of this set can be looted on normal difficulty.

### Bonuses

2 perfected items: Adds 2-103 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
2 items: When you deal damage with Flurry, your single target damage over time abilities used within 5 seconds gain 39-1690 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage.

### Pieces
The set consists of one-handed weapons of all types. There is no shield.

## Notes
- You will always receive two weapons when this set drops, and they must be wielded together. If you use one with a shield or another weapon, it will not provide any bonus. You may, however, mix and match other items of this set found on subsequent runs of the trial.
