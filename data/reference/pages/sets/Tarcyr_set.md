Tarcyr
Tarcyr is a monster helm set. It consists of two armor pieces: head and shoulder, both of which are available in all three armor weights and standard traits. The set cannot be crafted.

### Bonuses

1 item: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
2 items: Taking direct damage while Battle Spirit is inactive causes your attacker to cower in Fear for 2 seconds. Enemies who are not Feared by this effect are instead afflicted with Major Cowardice for 3 seconds, reducing Weapon Damage and Spell Damage by 430.

Either effect can occur once every 0.5 seconds.

### Drop Locations
- Parts can drop in the March of Sacrifices Solo Dungeon. The chance depends on the difficulty of a run.
- Also can be obtained from a Curated Tarcyr Coffer purchased from Veya Thenim at the Undaunted Enclaves for 300.
