# Online:Radial Uppercut

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Radial_Uppercut
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Radial Uppercut is a Blackrose Weapons item set awarded from normal Blackrose Prison. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Honor Guard Style. The perfected version of this set can be looted on veteran difficulty.

### Bonuses

2 items: Uppercut deals 45% of its damage to all enemies near your primary target.

### Pieces
The set consists of Two Handed melee weapons of all types.

### Notes
- While the description states that 2 items are required, this is because 2-handed weapons count as 2 items by themselves, so only 1 item is actually needed.
