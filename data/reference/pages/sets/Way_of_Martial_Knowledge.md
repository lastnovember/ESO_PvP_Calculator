# Online:Way of Martial Knowledge

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Way_of_Martial_Knowledge
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Way of Martial Knowledge is an overland set that drops in Craglorn. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Yokudan Style in Light Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
3 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: While your Stamina is below 50%, your Light Attacks cause the enemy to take 8% additional damage for 5 seconds. This effect can occur once every 8 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Lost One's Cowl of Martial Knowledge given by Star-Gazer Trilion in Belkarth
- Riven Hourglass, Sword of Martial Knowledge, the quest reward of The Time-Lost Warrior in the Imperial Style
