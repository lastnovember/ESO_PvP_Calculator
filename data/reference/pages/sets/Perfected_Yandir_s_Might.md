# Online:Perfected Yandir's Might

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Perfected_Yandir's_Might
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Perfected Yandir's Might is a trial set which drops in veteran Kyne's Aegis. Pieces are Sea Giant Style in Medium Armor. The non-perfected version of this set can be looted on normal difficulty.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
3 items: Gain Minor Slayer at all times, increasing your damage done to Dungeon, Trial, and Arena Monsters by 5%.
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 perfected items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: Dealing Critical Damage increases your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage by 2-86, stacking up to 5 times. This effect can occur once every 0.5 seconds. Completing a fully-charged Heavy Attack removes all stacks and increases your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage by 3-136 per stack removed. You cannot have both effects active at the same time.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
