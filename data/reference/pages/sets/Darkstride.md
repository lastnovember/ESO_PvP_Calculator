# Online:Darkstride

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Darkstride
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Darkstride is an overland set that drops in Rivenspire. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Thieves Guild Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
4 items: Adds 3-129 Stamina Recovery
5 items: Reduces the cost of Sprint and Sneak by 50%.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
