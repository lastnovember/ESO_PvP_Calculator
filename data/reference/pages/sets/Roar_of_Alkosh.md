# Online:Roar of Alkosh

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Roar_of_Alkosh
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Roar of Alkosh is a trial set which drops in the Maw of Lorkhaj. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Dro-m'Athra Style in Medium Armor. When equipped, weapons and the shield emit a unique blueish purple magical smoke effect.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
3 items: Gain Minor Slayer at all times, increasing your damage done to Dungeon, Trial, and Arena Monsters by 5%.
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: When you activate a synergy, you send a shockwave from your position that deals 318 Physical Damage and an additional 1590 Physical Damage over 10 seconds. Reduces the Physical Resistance and Spell Resistance of any enemy hit by the amount of your Weapon Damage for the duration, up to a maximum of 6000. The damage scales off the higher of your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage.

### Effect
- The 5 piece effect is triggered when you activate a synergy. This sends a frontal cone shockwave from your position that deals physical damage and a unique penetration debuff which stacks alongside other penetration debuffs, including Breach and Fracture. The effect lasts for 10 seconds, but given that synergy effects have a 20 second cooldown, at least two different types of synergies are required to achieve a high uptime on the five piece effect.

### Pieces
- The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
