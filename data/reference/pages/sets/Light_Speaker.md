# Online:Light Speaker

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Light_Speaker
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Light Speaker is a dungeon set that drops in Elden Hollow I and II. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Wood Elf Style in Light Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: Adds 13-600 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage to your Restoration Staff abilities.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as restoration staves (other weapons and shields may still exist but cannot be obtained new anymore). Unique pieces include:
- Band of the Shadow Guard, unique loot from The Shadow Guard.
- Chokethorn's Healing Root, unique loot from Chokethorn.
- Dark Root of Restoration, unique loot from Dark Root.
- Grandmother's Lace, unique loot from the Ancient Spriggan.
- Jewel of Azara, unique loot from Azara the Frightener.
- Leafring, unique loot from Leafseether.
- Light Speaker's Last Gift, a quest reward for Ancient Remains.
