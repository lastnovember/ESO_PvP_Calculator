# Online:Glorious Defender

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Glorious_Defender
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Glorious Defender is a set which drops in the Maelstrom Arena. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Trinimac Style in Heavy Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
3 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: When you Bash an enemy, you gain Glorious Defense for 10 seconds, allowing you to dodge the next attack made against you and all others afterwards for 1 second. This effect can occur once every 13 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
