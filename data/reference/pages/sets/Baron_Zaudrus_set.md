Baron Zaudrus

Baron Zaudrus is a monster helm set. It consists of two armor pieces: head and shoulder, both of which are available in all three armor weights and standard traits. The set cannot be crafted. There is an outfit style based on this armor set.

### Bonuses

1 item: Adds 12-548 Maximum Stamina
1 item: Adds 12-548 Maximum Magicka
1 item: Adds 14-603 Maximum Health
2 items: Applying a status effect to an enemy grants you a stack of Ambition for 10 seconds, up to 3 stacks max. When you gain 3 stacks, the stacks are removed and you gain 4 Ultimate. On gaining Ultimate, you cannot gain additional stacks of Ambition for 1 second.

### Drop Locations
- The head drops from Baron Zaudrus in Veteran The Cauldron.
- The shoulders can be obtained from Urgarlag Chief-bane's reward chest at Undaunted Enclaves.

### Notes
- This set uses the following icon with a number on the bottom right to track Ambition stacks: 32
