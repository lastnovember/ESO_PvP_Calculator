# Online:Willpower

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Willpower
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Willpower is a jewelry set found in Imperial City Treasure Vaults and some random group dungeons. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Xivkyn Style.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 40-1752 Maximum Magicka
3 items: Adds 4-206 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage

### Pieces
The set consists of jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.

### Drop Locations
- The final boss of any group dungeon, provided all prior bosses and minibosses have been defeated. The item scales to the player's level. No longer true?
- Opening the reward chests within the Bone Shard or Ethereal trophy vaults within the Imperial City. The item will scale to your level.
- As a possible Daily Dungeon reward, using the Group Finder tool. The item scales to your level at the time of earning, not when opened.

## Notes
- This set was first introduced with Update 7.
