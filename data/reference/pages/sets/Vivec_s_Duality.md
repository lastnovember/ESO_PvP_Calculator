# Online:Vivec's Duality

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Vivec's_Duality
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Vivec's Duality is an overland set that drops in Apocrypha and the Telvanni Peninsula. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Dead Keeper Style in Light armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
4 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
5 items: Your weapon charges over 15 seconds while not Weapon Swapping. Once fully charged, restore 6636 Magicka and Stamina after Weapon Swapping.

### Pieces
The set consists of Light Armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
