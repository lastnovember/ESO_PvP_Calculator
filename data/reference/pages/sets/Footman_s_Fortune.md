# Online:Footman's Fortune

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Footman's_Fortune
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Footman's Fortune is a set which drops in the Dragonstar Arena. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Orcish Style in Heavy Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
3 items: Adds 34-1487 Armor
4 items: Adds 34-1487 Armor
5 items: Increases the amount of damage you block by 14%.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
