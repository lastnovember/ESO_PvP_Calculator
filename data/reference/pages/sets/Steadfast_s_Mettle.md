# Online:Steadfast's Mettle

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Steadfast's_Mettle
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Steadfast's Mettle is an overland set that drops in High Isle. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Steadfast Society Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Stamina Recovery
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
4 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
5 items: While you have a food buff active, reduce the cost of your Core Combat abilities by 25%.

### Pieces
The set consists of Medium Armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
