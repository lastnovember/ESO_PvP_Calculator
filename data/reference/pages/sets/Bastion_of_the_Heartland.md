# Online:Bastion of the Heartland

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Bastion_of_the_Heartland
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Bastion of the Heartland is an item set found in boxes purchased using Alliance Points from the Vlastarus Quartermaster, as quest rewards in Vlastarus, as a reward for completing delves and dolmens throughout Cyrodiil, from completing Board Missions, and from Elite Gear Vendors. It cannot be crafted and does not drop from monsters. Pieces are Aldmeri Dominion, Daggerfall Covenant, or Ebonheart Pact Style in Heavy Armor, depending on the player's alliance.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
3 items: Adds 4% Healing Taken
4 items: Reduces your damage taken from Players by 3%.
5 items: Reduces your damage taken from Siege Weapons and Player area of effect abilities by 10%.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
