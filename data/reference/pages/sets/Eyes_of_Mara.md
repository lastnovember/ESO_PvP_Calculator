# Online:Eyes of Mara

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Eyes_of_Mara
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Eyes of Mara is one of the craftable sets providing special bonuses to armor and weapons. It is available for crafting to players pursuing blacksmithing, clothing, woodworking, and/or jewelry crafting professions. The special crafting site dedicated to creation of this set is available in Eyevea on completion of the Mages Guild quest line.

To craft an item for this set, a crafter must first complete research on eight traits for a particular item at a corresponding crafting station.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Magicka Recovery
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: Reduces the cost of your Restoration Staff abilities by 15%.
