# Online:Azureblight Reaper

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Azureblight_Reaper
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Azureblight Reaper is a dungeon set that drops in the Lair of Maarselok (Requires Scalebreaker DLC). It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Stags of Z'en Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
3 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: Dealing damage over time applies a stack of Blight Seed to the target for 5 seconds. At 20 stacks, Blight Seed explodes, dealing 667 Disease damage to the target and nearby enemies, increasing by 150% per additional monster hit, up to 600%. The damage scales off your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage and can occur once every 1 second. Enemies can only have one Blight Seed at a time and require 20 more stacks per group member wearing this set.

### Pieces
The set consists medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
