# Online:Merciless Charge

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Merciless_Charge
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Merciless Charge is a Maelstrom Weapons item set found in normal Maelstrom Arena. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Maelstrom style. The perfected version of this set can be looted on veteran difficulty.

### Bonuses

2 items: Activating Critical Charge stirs up a barbarous lust for battle within you for 18 seconds, increasing your damage done with direct damage attacks by 1%, up to a maximum of 12%. This effect scales off the higher of your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage.

### Pieces
The set consists of Two Handed melee weapons of all types.

### Notes
- While the description states that 2 items are required, this is because 2-handed weapons count as 2 items by themselves, so only 1 item is actually needed.
