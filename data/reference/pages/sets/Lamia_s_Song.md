# Online:Lamia's Song

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Lamia's_Song
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Lamia's Song is a dungeon set that drops in Arx Corinium. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are High Elf Style in Light Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
3 items: Adds 3-129 Magicka Recovery
4 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
5 items: When you use an Ultimate ability, you and your group members within 20 meters of you heal for 0-25 Health and restore 0-25 Magicka and Stamina per Ultimate spent.

### Pieces
The set consists of armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Matron's Mark, Matron Ixniaa's unique loot.
- Ixniaa's Jewels, Matron Ixniaa's unique loot.
- Belisaro's Bolt, Ganakton the Tempest's unique loot.
- Lamia's Boon of the Three Sisters, a quest reward for Force of Nature.
