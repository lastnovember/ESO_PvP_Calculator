# Online:Robes of Transmutation

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Robes_of_Transmutation
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Robes of Transmutation is an Elite Gear Set that was formerly available from "Rewards for the Worthy" mails, earned by participating in PvP, prior to Update 39. It can now be obtained in boxes purchased using Alliance Points from the Cropsford Quartermaster, as quest rewards in Cropsford, as a reward for completing delves and dolmens throughout Cyrodiil, from completing Board Missions, and from Elite Gear Vendors. It cannot be crafted and does not drop from monsters. Pieces are Aldmeri Dominion, Daggerfall Covenant, or Ebonheart Pact Style in Light Armor, depending on the player's alliance.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 9-424 Critical Resistance
3 items: Adds 4% Healing Done
4 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
5 items: When you heal yourself or an ally with a healing over time ability, grant them 31-1363 Critical Resistance for 4 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
