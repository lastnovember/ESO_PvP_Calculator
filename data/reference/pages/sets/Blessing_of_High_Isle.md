# Online:Blessing of High Isle

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Blessing_of_High_Isle
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Blessing of High Isle is an overland set that drops in High Isle and Amenos. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Ancestral Breton Style in Light Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
3 items: Adds 3-129 Magicka Recovery
4 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
5 items: When you are healed while in combat, increase your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage by 8-369 for 5 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists of Light Armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.

### Notes
- There is no cooldown for the 5-piece bonus, making it particularly useful in situations where you have a dedicated healer (such as Group Dungeons).
