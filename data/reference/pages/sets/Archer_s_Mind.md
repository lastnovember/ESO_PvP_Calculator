# Online:Archer's Mind

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Archer's_Mind
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Archer's Mind is a set which drops in the Dragonstar Arena. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Wood Elf Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
3 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
4 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
5 items: Increases your Critical Damage and Healing by 8%. Increases your Critical Damage and Healing by an additional 16% when you are Sneaking or Invisible.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
