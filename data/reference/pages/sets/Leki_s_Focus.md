# Online:Leki's Focus

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Leki's_Focus
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Leki's Focus is an Elite Gear Set that was formerly available from "Rewards for the Worthy" mails, earned by participating in PvP before Update 39. It can now be obtained in boxes purchased using Alliance Points from the Bruma Quartermaster, as quest rewards in Bruma, as a reward for completing delves and dolmens throughout Cyrodiil, from completing Board Missions, and from Elite Gear Vendors. It cannot be crafted and does not drop from monsters. Pieces are Aldmeri Dominion, Daggerfall Covenant, or Ebonheart Pact Style in Medium Armor, depending on the player's alliance.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
3 items: Adds 9-424 Critical Resistance
4 items: Adds 9-424 Critical Resistance
5 items: Reduces your damage done with area of effect attacks by 5%, but grants Major Evasion at all times; reducing your damage taken from area of effect attacks by 20%.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.

### Notes
- Before Update 39, this set was in Abah's Watch Style.
