# Online:Spider Cultist Cowl

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Spider_Cultist_Cowl
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Spider Cultist Cowl is a dungeon set that drops in Fungal Grotto I and II. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Daedric Style in Light Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: Adds 13-600 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage to your Destruction Staff abilities.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as destruction staves of all types (other weapons and shields may still exist but cannot be obtained new anymore). Unique items include:
- Band of the Breaker, unique loot from Reggr Dark-Dawn.
- Broodmother's Knot, unique loot from Broodbirther.
- Shroud of the Resolute, a quest reward for Lighting the Shadows.
- Vila's Recompense, a quest reward for Kings of the Grotto.
- War Chief's Firebrand, unique loot from War Chief Ozozai.
- Widow's Voidstaff, unique loot from Vila Theran.
