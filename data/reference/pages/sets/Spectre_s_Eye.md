# Online:Spectre's Eye

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Spectre's_Eye
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Spectre's Eye is one of the craftable sets providing special bonuses to armor and weapons. It is available for crafting to players pursuing blacksmithing, clothing, woodworking, and/or jewelry crafting professions. The special crafting site dedicated to creation of this set is available in Coldharbour. Completion of the main quest line is not required, as both crafting sites in Coldharbour can be accessed as soon as you gain access to the zone.

To craft an item for this set, a crafter must first complete research on eight traits for this particular item at a corresponding crafting station.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 34-1487 Armor
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
4 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
5 items: When you cast a Magicka ability while in combat you gain Major Evasion for 30 seconds, reducing damage from area attacks by 20%. This effect can occur once every 30 seconds.
