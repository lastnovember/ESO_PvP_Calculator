# Online:Shattered Paths Signet

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Shattered_Paths_Signet
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

:Occluded are the poems which sing of shattered paths. Fractured rhymes. Marred cantos. Ruined verse. Perhaps, in obstruction, a more perfect reflection is captured. -Hivisk the Blind
The Shattered Paths Signet is a Mythic ring that comes with the Triune trait. It can be obtained through the Antiquities system. It was released as part of Season Zero: Dawn and Dusk, with the leads dropping in Night Market areas.

### Bonuses

1 item: Increase your damage done to monsters with status effects by 1% for every 1 Ultimate you have, up to 133%. The cap is halved if Feral Guardian is active. While you have 133 or more Ultimate, your Light and Heavy Attacks apply a random status effect, up to once every 2 seconds. While equipped, applies Minor Timidity to you, consuming 1 Ultimate every 1.5 seconds in combat.

### Fragments
- Five fragments are required to create the item, all of which require a master-quality Lead in order to scry, and are classed as master difficulty to excavate.
- You are required to be at least level 7 in Scrying, and have the Antiquarian Insight IV passive in order to scry Master difficulty leads.
- Fragment Leads are largely found in the same zone as where the fragment is excavated, save for a few cases where it may be found in different zone.
