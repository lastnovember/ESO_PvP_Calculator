# Online:Prayer Shawl

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Prayer_Shawl
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Prayer Shawl is a dungeon set that drops in Spindleclutch I and II. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Dark Elf Style in Light Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
3 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
4 items: Adds 3-129 Magicka Recovery
5 items: When you overheal yourself or an ally, you grant them a damage shield that absorbs 116-4997 damage for 6 seconds. This effect can occur once every 2 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Chain of the Coven, unique loot from Urvan Veleth.
- Fleshlinks, unique loot from the Flesh Atronach Trio.
- Silky Threads, unique loot from Spindlekin.
- Swarm Mother's Sprig, unique loot from the Swarm Mother.
- Vampiric Scepter, unique loot from Vorenor Winterbourne.
- Widow's Band, unique loot from Cerise the Widow-Maker.
