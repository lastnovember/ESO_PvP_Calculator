# Online:Beckoning Steel

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Beckoning_Steel
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Beckoning Steel is an item set found in boxes purchased using Alliance Points from the Vlastarus Quartermaster, as quest rewards in Vlastarus, as a reward for completing delves and dolmens throughout Cyrodiil, from completing Board Missions, and from Elite Gear Vendors. It cannot be crafted and does not drop from monsters. Pieces are Aldmeri Dominion, Daggerfall Covenant, or Ebonheart Pact Style in Heavy Armor, depending on the player's alliance.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 34-1487 Armor
3 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
4 items: Adds 34-1487 Armor
5 items: Generate an aura that causes you and 11 group members within the aura to take 10% less damage from projectiles. This bonus persists through death. You can only be affected by one instance of Beckoning Steel.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
