# Online:Monolith of Storms

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Monolith_of_Storms
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Monolith of Storms is a Class set found in the Infinite Archive. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Monolith of Storms Style in all weights. It buffs the Storm Calling skill line for Sorcerers.

### Bonuses

Class: Sorcerer
2 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
3 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: Dealing damage with a Storm Calling ability's initial hit or every 5th tick creates a Monolith near the enemy for 10 seconds, up to one every 1 second, up to 3 total. Your Monoliths within 28 meters of one another link together, dealing 742 Shock damage, scaling off of the higher of your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage, every 2 seconds to enemies near the links, up to once every 2 seconds per enemy. Each Monolith active grants you 100 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage.

### Pieces
The set consists of armor of all weights and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
