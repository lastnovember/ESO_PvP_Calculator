# Online:Oakfather's Retribution

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Oakfather's_Retribution
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Oakfather's Retribution is an Elite Gear Set that was formerly available from "Rewards for the Worthy" mails, earned by participating in PvP, prior to Update 39. It can now be obtained in boxes purchased using Alliance Points from the Cropsford Quartermaster, as quest rewards in Cropsford, as a reward for completing delves and dolmens throughout Cyrodiil, from completing Board Missions, and from Elite Gear Vendors. It cannot be crafted and does not drop from monsters. Pieces are Aldmeri Dominion, Daggerfall Covenant, or Ebonheart Pact Style in Light Armor, depending on the player's alliance.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
3 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: Gain 449 Offensive Penetration against enemies for each Major Buff they have active. Gain 20 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage against enemies for each Minor Buff they have active.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
