# Online:Mark of the Pariah

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Mark_of_the_Pariah
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Mark of the Pariah is an overland set that drops in Wrothgar. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Malacath Style in Heavy Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
3 items: Adds 34-1487 Armor
4 items: Adds 34-1487 Armor
5 items: Increases your Physical Resistance and Spell Resistance by up to 237-10206 based on your missing Health. Current value: 55-2373

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Barbute of Clan Shatul, the quest reward for A Question of Succession
- Blade of the Bloody Tusk, the quest reward for Flames of Forge and Fallen
- King's Snug Girdle, the quest reward for The King's Gambit
- Malacath's Final Word, the quest reward for Blood and the Sacred Words
- Pauldrons of Clan Tumnosh, the quest reward for Quarry Conundrum
- Trinimac's Blessed Plackart, the quest reward for Forcing the Faith
- Vambrace of Sorrow, the quest reward for Sorrow's Kiss
