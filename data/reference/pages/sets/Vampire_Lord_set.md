# Online:Vampire Lord (set)

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Vampire_Lord_(set)
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Vampire Lord is an overland set that drops in Bangkorai. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are High Elf Style in Light Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
3 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
4 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
5 items: Increases the bonuses and penalties of your Vampire Stage, depending on how far you've progressed. Stage 1/2/3/4 1%/2%/4%/6% additional Flame Damage Taken. 1%/2%/4%/6% increased Regular Ability cost. 5%/10%/15%/20% reduced Vampire Ability cost.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Crozier of St. Pelin, the quest reward for The Waking Darkness in the Breton Style.
- Robe of Nature's Fury, the quest reward for Heart of Evil in the Nord Style.
- Saint Pelin Leggings, the quest reward for Raiders at the Crossing in the Nord Style.
- Scorched Shoulderpads, the quest reward for The Returned in the Redguard Style.
- Skystaff of Secrets, the quest reward of both Sunken Knowledge and The Water Stone.
- Staff of Secundus, the quest reward of The Shifting Sands of Fate in the Khajiit Style.
- Vampire Lord Edwyge's Coven Gown, a unique drop from Lady Edwyge, final boss of Rubble Butte in the Redguard Style.
- Vampire Lord Rune Charm Choker, a unique drop from Lorogdu gra-Gulash, final boss of Torog's Spite.
- Vampire Lord's Cold-Bite, a unique drop from the world boss Nomeg Hyril at Nilata Falls in the Nord Style.
- Vampire Lord's Gnawed Staff, a unique drop from Pale Squnque, final boss of Troll's Toothpick in the Breton Style.
