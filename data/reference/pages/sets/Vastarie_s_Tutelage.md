# Online:Vastarie's Tutelage

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Vastarie's_Tutelage
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Vastarie's Tutelage is one of the craftable sets providing special bonuses to armor and weapons. It is available for crafting to players pursuing blacksmithing, clothing, woodworking, and/or jewelry crafting professions. The special crafting site with stations dedicated to creation of this set are located in Northern Elsweyr.

To make an item for this set, a crafter must first complete research on three traits for this particular item at a corresponding crafting station.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
3 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
4 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
5 items: Adds 3-129 Magicka Recovery
5 items: Adds 3-129 Stamina Recovery
5 items: When you resurrect an ally, you and your ally gain 6-258 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage and 10% cost reduction to non-Ultimate abilities for 10 seconds.

### Crafting Site
Rimmen Masterworks
