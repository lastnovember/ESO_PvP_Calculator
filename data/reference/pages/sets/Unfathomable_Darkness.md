# Online:Unfathomable Darkness

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Unfathomable_Darkness
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Unfathomable Darkness is an overland set that drops in the Clockwork City. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Ebonshadow Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
3 items: Adds 34-1487 Offensive Penetration
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: When you deal damage, you call a murder of crows around you for 12 seconds. Every 3 seconds a crow will be sent to peck the closest enemy within 12 meters of you, dealing 871 Physical Damage. This effect can occur once every 15 seconds and scales off the higher of your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Cleft Striders a quest reward for The Shadow Cleft
- Evergloam Leggings, a quest reward for Unto the Dark.
- Grips of the Impostor, a quest reward for The Strangeness of Seht.
- Living Shadow Skin, a quest reward for To The Clockwork City.
- Tenebrous Girdle, a quest reward for Lost in the Gloam.
