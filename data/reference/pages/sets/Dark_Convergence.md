# Online:Dark Convergence

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Dark_Convergence
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Dark Convergence is an Elite Gear Set that was formerly available from "Rewards for the Worthy" mails, earned by participating in PvP, prior to Update 39. It can now be obtained in boxes purchased using Alliance Points from the Cropsford Quartermaster, as quest rewards in Cropsford, as a reward for completing delves and dolmens throughout Cyrodiil, from completing Board Missions, and from Elite Gear Vendors. It cannot be crafted and does not drop from monsters. Pieces are Aldmeri Dominion, Daggerfall Covenant, or Ebonheart Pact Style in Light Armor, depending on the player's alliance.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
3 items: Adds 34-1487 Offensive Penetration
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: While in combat, casting abilities that leave an effect on the ground will create an area that applies a 60% snare and pulls enemies after a 1 second delay, stunning them for 1 second. After 4 seconds, the area deals 821 Magic damage to all enemies in the 10 meter area, and 1642 Magic damage to enemies within 3 meters of the center, increasing by 50% for each target. This effect can occur once every 25 seconds and scales off the higher of your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
