# Online:Jorvuld's Guidance

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Jorvuld's_Guidance
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Jorvuld's Guidance is a dungeon set that drops in Scalecaller Peak. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Scalecaller Style in Light Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Magicka Recovery
3 items: Adds 3-129 Magicka Recovery
4 items: Adds 4% Healing Done
5 items: While in combat, increases the duration of all Major buffs, Minor buffs, and damage shields you apply to yourself and allies by 40%.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
