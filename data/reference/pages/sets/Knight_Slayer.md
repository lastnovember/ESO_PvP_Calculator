# Online:Knight Slayer

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Knight_Slayer
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Knight Slayer is an Battlegrounds Set rewarded by Battlegrounds. It can be obtained in boxes purchased using Alliance Points from the Cropsford Quartermaster. It cannot be crafted and does not drop from monsters. Pieces are Militant Ordinator Style in Light Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 9-424 Critical Resistance
3 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
4 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
5 items: Your fully-charged Heavy Attacks deal an additional 8% of an enemy's Max Health as Oblivion Damage. This can deal a maximum of 186-8000 Oblivion Damage.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.

### Notes
- This set uses the following icon for death recap: Oblivion
