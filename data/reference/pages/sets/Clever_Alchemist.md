# Online:Clever Alchemist

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Clever_Alchemist
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Clever Alchemist is one of the craftable sets providing special bonuses to armor and weapons. It is available for crafting to players pursuing blacksmithing, clothing, woodworking, and/or jewelry crafting professions. The special crafting site with stations dedicated to creation of this set are located in the Hew's BaneThieves Guild.

To make an item for this set, a crafter must first complete research on seven traits for this particular item at a corresponding crafting station.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
3 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: When you drink a potion during combat you feel a rush of energy, increasing your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage by 23-675 for 20 seconds.

### Crafting Site
No Shira Workshop
