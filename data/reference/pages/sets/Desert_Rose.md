Desert Rose (cat)
Desert Rose is an item set found in boxes purchased using Alliance Points from the Cropsford Quartermaster, as quest rewards in Cropsford, as a reward for completing delves and dolmens throughout Cyrodiil, from completing Board Missions, and from Elite Gear Vendors. It cannot be crafted and does not drop from monsters. Pieces are Aldmeri Dominion, Daggerfall Covenant, or Ebonheart Pact Style in Light Armor, depending on the player's alliance.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 34-1487 Armor
3 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
4 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
5 items: When you take damage, you have a 25% chance to restore 46-2012 Magicka. This effect can occur once every 4 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
