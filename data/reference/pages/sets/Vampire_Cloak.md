# Online:Vampire Cloak

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Vampire_Cloak
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Vampire Cloak is an overland set that drops in Rivenspire. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Breton Style in Heavy Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
3 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
4 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
5 items: Adds 3-171 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: Gain Minor Protection at all times, reducing your damage taken by 5%.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Dathieu's Traitorous Axe of the Vampire's Cloak, the quest reward for A Traitor's Tale.
- Shornhelm Guard Sollerets of the Vampire's Cloak.
- Fell's Run Hero Necklace of the Vampire's Cloak, the quest reward for Fell's Justice.
- House Dorell Cuirass of the Vampire's Cloak, the quest reward for The Liberation of Northpoint.
- Ravenwatch Staff of the Vampire's Cloak, the quest reward for The Lightless Remnant.
- Fingaenion's Toe Gloves of the Vampire's Cloak, a unique drop from Fingaenion Forestsmasher.
- Miruin's Devastation of the Vampire's Cloak, a unique drop from Miruin Woodwalker.
- Darkblood Staff of the Vampire's Cloak, a unique drop from Louna Darkblood.
- Magdelena's Ancient Ring of the Vampire's Cloak, a unique drop from Magdelena.
