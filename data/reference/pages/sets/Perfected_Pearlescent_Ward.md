# Online:Perfected Pearlescent Ward

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Perfected_Pearlescent_Ward
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Perfected Pearlescent Ward is a trial set found in veteran Dreadsail Reef. It cannot be crafted. Set pieces are Syrabanic Marine style in Heavy Armor. The non-perfected version of this set can be looted on normal difficulty.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
3 items: Gain Minor Aegis at all times, reducing your damage taken from Dungeon, Trial, and Arena Monsters by 5%.
4 items: Adds 4% Healing Taken
5 perfected items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
5 items: Grants you and up to 11 other group members Pearlescent Ward. This bonus persists through death. Pearlescent Ward increases Weapon Damage and Spell Damage by up to 4-180 based on the number of group members that are alive. Current 4-180 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage. Pearlescent Ward increases damage reduction from non-player enemies out of 66% based on the number of group members that are dead. Current 0% damage reduction.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
