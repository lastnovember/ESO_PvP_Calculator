# Online:Widowmaker

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Widowmaker
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Widowmaker is a dungeon set that drops in the Cradle of Shadows. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Silken Ring Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
3 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
4 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
5 items: When your alchemical poison fires, you drop a poisonous spore in front of you that bursts after 1 second, dealing 2856 Poison damage over 10 seconds to all enemies within 5 meters. This effect scales off the higher of your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
