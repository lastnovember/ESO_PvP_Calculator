# Online:Hatchling's Shell

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Hatchling's_Shell
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Hatchling's Shell is an overland set that drops in Shadowfen. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Argonian Style in Heavy Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 34-1487 Armor
3 items: Adds 34-1487 Armor
4 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
5 items: Whenever you take damage you gain a damage shield that absorbs up to 3200 damage for 6 seconds. This effect can occur once every 6 seconds and scales off your Max Health.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Chieftain's Greaves, a quest reward for Strength of the Father.
- Egg-Tender's Guard, a quest reward for Keeper's of the Shell.
- Stormhold Cuirass, a quest reward for Getting to the Truth.
- Warden's Gauntlets, a quest reward for The Keystone.
- Essentia of the Hatchling's Shell, a unique drop from Peers-Through-Glass.
- Slimy Acidic Staff of the Hatchling's Shell, a unique drop from Vlaga and Argablog.
- Dessicated Belt of the Hatchling's Shell, a unique drop from Talkynd.
- Discarded Bonds of the Hatchling's Shell, a unique drop from Rendrasa.
