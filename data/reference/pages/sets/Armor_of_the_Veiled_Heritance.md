# Online:Armor of the Veiled Heritance

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Armor_of_the_Veiled_Heritance
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Armor of the Veiled Heritance is an overland set that drops in Auridon. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are High Elf Style in Heavy Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
3 items: Adds 34-1487 Armor
4 items: Adds 34-1487 Armor
5 items: When you interrupt an enemy, you gain 12-516 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage for 15 seconds. Your Bash attacks deal 12-516 more damage.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Despot's Shoulderplates, the quest reward for Rightful Inheritance.
- Patriot's Mace, the quest reward for The Veiled Choice.
- Queensguard Chestplate, the quest reward for The Veil Falls.
- Queensguard Greaves, the quest reward for A Hostile Situation.
- Scampcrusher, the quest reward for Through the Ashes.
- Thalmor Helm, the quest reward for Harsh Lesson.
- Sea Greaves of the Veiled Heritance, a unique drop from Camandar, the final boss of Bewan.
- Snapjaw's Tooth of the Veiled Heritance, a unique drop from the world boss Snapjaw in the Wood Elf Style.
- The Captain's Helm of the Veiled Heritance, a unique drop from the world boss Captain Blanchete.
- Loyalist's Longbow of the Veiled Heritance, a unique drop from the world boss Anarume.
