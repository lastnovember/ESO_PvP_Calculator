# Online:Stygian

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Stygian
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Stygian is an overland set that drops in Coldharbour. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Draugr Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
3 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
4 items: Adds 34-1487 Offensive Penetration
5 items: When you leave Sneak or invisibility while in combat, your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage is increased by 8-369 for 15 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Ayleid's Wrath, the quest reward of The Final Assault in the High Elf Style.
- Black Forge Blade, the quest reward of Vanus Unleashed in the Daedric Style.
- Blood-Red Brogans, the quest reward of The Harvest Heart in the Nord Style.
- Bow of Penumbra, the quest reward of An Unusual Circumstance and The Shadow's Embrace in the Wood Elf Style.
- Dungeon-Delving Mantle, the quest reward of The Library of Dusk in the Dark Elf Style.
- Everfull Girdle, the quest reward of Special Blend in the Nord Style.
- Fighters Guild Visor, the quest reward of Into the Woods in the Khajiit Style.
- Hist Bark Hide, the quest reward of Wisdom of the Ages in the Argonian Style.
- Morrowind Vambraces, the quest reward of The Anguish Gem in the Dark Elf Style.
- Stygian Cirterisse's Vampiric Staff, a unique drop from Cirterisse, the final boss of Aba-Loria in the High Elf Style.
- Stygian Forgefire's Brutal Blade, a unique drop from Haman Forgefire, the final boss of The Vault of Haman Forgefire in the Nord Style.
- Stygian Marwoleath's Torment, a unique drop from Marwoleath, the final boss of The Wailing Maw.
- Stygian Nerazakan's Fascination, a unique drop from Nerazakan, the final boss of The Grotto of Depravity in the Daedric Style.
