# Online:Robes of Destruction Mastery

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Robes_of_Destruction_Mastery
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Robes of Destruction Mastery is a set which drops in the Dragonstar Arena. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are High Elf Style in Light Armor and contains only Destruction Staves as weapons.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
3 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: While you have a Destruction Staff equipped, your Max Magicka is increased by 66-2840.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as Destruction Staves of all three types.
