# Online:Draugr's Heritage

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Draugr's_Heritage
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Draugr's Heritage is an overland set that drops in The Rift. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Draugr Style in Heavy Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 4% Healing Taken
3 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
4 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
5 items: When you take damage while you are under 35% Health, you knockdown all enemies within 4 meters of you for 2 seconds and heal for 2506 Health. This effect can occur once every 7 seconds and the heal scales off the higher of your Max Magicka or Stamina.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
