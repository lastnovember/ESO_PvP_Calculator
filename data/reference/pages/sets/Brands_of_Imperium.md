# Online:Brands of Imperium

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Brands_of_Imperium
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Brands of Imperium is a dungeon set that drops in the White-Gold Tower, as well as being found in the Daedric Embers Trophy Vault. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Xivkyn Style in Heavy Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
3 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
4 items: Adds 4% Healing Taken
5 items: When you take damage, you grant you and your group members within 8 meters a damage shield that absorbs 261-11260 damage for 6 seconds. This effect can occur once every 12 seconds.
