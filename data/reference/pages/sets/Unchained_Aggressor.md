# Online:Unchained Aggressor

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Unchained_Aggressor
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Unchained Aggressor is a craftable item set providing special bonuses to armor and weapons. It is available for crafting to players pursuing blacksmithing, clothing, woodworking, and/or jewelry crafting professions. The special crafting site with stations dedicated to creation of this set is located Bruma Armory in Bruma. To be able to enter the armory, your alliance must hold Bruma.

To craft an item for this set, a crafter must have researched three traits for the particular item.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
3 items: Adds 3-129 Stamina Recovery
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: After Breaking Free, gain Major Berserk for 12 seconds, increasing your damage done by 10%. This effect can occur once every 14 seconds.

### Crafting Sites
