# Online:Twice-Fanged Serpent

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Twice-Fanged_Serpent
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Twice-Fanged Serpent is a trial set which drops in the Sanctum Ophidia. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Celestial Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
3 items: Adds 34-1487 Offensive Penetration
4 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
5 items: Adds 53-2305 Offensive Penetration
5 items: When you deal damage, your Offensive Penetration is increased by 15-660 for 5 seconds, stacking up to 5 times. This effect can occur once every half second.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
