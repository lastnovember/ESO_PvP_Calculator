# Online:Senche-raht's Grit

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Senche-raht's_Grit
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Senche-raht's Grit is one of the craftable sets providing special bonuses to armor and weapons. It is available for crafting to players pursuing blacksmithing, clothing, woodworking, and/or jewelry crafting professions. The special crafting site with stations dedicated to creation of this set are located in Northern Elsweyr.

To make an item for this set, a crafter must first complete research on five traits for this particular item at a corresponding crafting station.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 4% Healing Done
3 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
4 items: Adds 4% Healing Taken
5 items: After being damaged by a damage over time ability, increase your Healing Received by 0-6% and your Physical Resistance and Spell Resistance by 60-2585 for 3 seconds.

### Crafting Site
Starlight Adeptorium
