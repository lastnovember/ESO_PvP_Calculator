# Online:Alessia's Bulwark

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Alessia's_Bulwark
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Alessia's Bulwark is one of the craftable sets providing special bonuses to armor and weapons. It is available for crafting to players pursuing blacksmithing, clothing, woodworking, and/or jewelry crafting professions. One special crafting site with stations dedicated to creation of this set is located in the fourth zone of each of the three Alliances.

To make an item for this set, a crafter must first complete research on five traits for this particular item at a corresponding crafting station.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 34-1487 Armor
3 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
4 items: Adds 34-1487 Armor
5 items: When you take damage from a Martial melee attack, you reduce the attacker's Weapon Damage and Spell Damage by 6-300 for 5 seconds.
