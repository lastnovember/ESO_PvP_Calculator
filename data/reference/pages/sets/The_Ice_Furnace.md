# Online:The Ice Furnace

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:The_Ice_Furnace
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Ice Furnace is a dungeon set that drops in Direfrost Keep. It cannot be crafted. Dropped pieces are Nord Style but reconstructed pieces are in the Redguard Style, both in Heavy Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
3 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: When you deal Frost Damage, you deal an additional 238 Flame damage to all enemies within 8 meters around the initial target. This effect can occur once every 1 second and scales off the higher of your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Drodda's Boreal Staff, unique loot from Drodda of Icereach.
- Shard of Iceheart, unique loot from Iceheart.
- Troll-Tooth Necklace, unique loot from Teethnasher the Frostbound.
