# Online:Maelstrom Weapons

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Maelstrom_Weapons
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Maelstrom Weapons are a special weapon set that drops in the Maelstrom Arena. All weapons bear the Maelstrom Style.

Maelstrom weapons are available by defeating the last boss of the Maelstrom Arena. Perfected Maelstrom weapons drop on Veteran difficulty while non-perfected Maelstrom weapons drop on Normal difficulty. Weapons can also be awarded to players who successfully rank on the weekly Maelstrom Arena leaderboard.
