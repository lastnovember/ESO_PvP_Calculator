# Online:Night Mother's Embrace

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Night_Mother's_Embrace
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Night Mother's Embrace is an overland set that drops in Deshaan. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Thieves Guild Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
3 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
4 items: Adds 3-129 Stamina Recovery
5 items: Adds 3-171 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: Reduces the radius you can be detected while Sneaking by 2 meters. Reduces the cost of Sneak by 25%.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Abomination's Bulwark of Mother's Embrace, a unique drop from the world boss The Abomination at the Grove of the Abomination in the Dark Elf Style.
- Bonetooth's Charm of Mother's Embrace, a unique drop from Bonetooth, the final boss of Triple Circle Mine.
- Dungeon-Delving Mittens of Mother's Embrace, a unique drop from Egg-Eater, the final boss of Taleon's Crag in the Dark Elf Style.
- Kwama Mining Gloves, the quest reward for Search and Rescue in the Primal Style.
- Naryu's Sniper's Bow, the quest reward for A Favor Returned in the Dark Elf Style.
- Naryu's Spare Blade, the quest reward for Hiding in Plain Sight in the Dark Elf Style.
- Ordinator Breastplate, the quest reward for The Judgment of Veloth in the Dark Elf Style.
- Ordinator Chausses, the quest reward for Plague Bringer in the Dark Elf Style.
- Ordinator Helm, the quest reward for The Seal of Three in the Dark Elf Style.
- Sharpfang's Jagged Blade of Mother's Embrace, a unique drop from the world boss Sharpfang at Dire Bramblepatch in the Dark Elf Style.
- Snakeskin Belt, the quest reward for The Trial of the Ghost Snake in the Dark Elf Style.

### Notes
- Not to be confused with the craftable set Night Mother's Gaze. Items of both sets used to have the same naming convention, " of the Night Mother", but pieces of the Night Mother's Embrace set have since been renamed to " of Mother's Embrace" to prevent confusion.
