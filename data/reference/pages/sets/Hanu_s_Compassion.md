# Online:Hanu's Compassion

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Hanu's_Compassion
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Hanu's Compassion is a dungeon set that drops in the March of Sacrifices. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Huntsman Style in Light Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Magicka Recovery
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
4 items: Adds 3-129 Magicka Recovery
5 items: Adds 45-1963 Maximum Magicka
5 items: When you resurrect an ally while in combat, both you and your ally gain Major Heroism for 15 seconds, granting 3 Ultimate every 1.5 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
