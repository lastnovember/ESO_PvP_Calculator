# Online:Dro'zakar's Claws

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Dro'zakar's_Claws
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Dro'zakar's Claws is a dungeon set that drops in the Moongrave Fane. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Moongrave Fane Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 34-1487 Offensive Penetration
3 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
4 items: Adds 3-129 Stamina Recovery
5 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: For every bleed effect you have on an enemy, increase your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage by 2-103 against them.

### Pieces
The set consists medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
