# Online:Pillar of Nirn

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Pillar_of_Nirn
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Pillar of Nirn is a dungeon set that drops in Falkreath Hold. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Dreadhorn Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
3 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: When you deal damage, you create a fissure underneath the enemy after 1 second, dealing 803 Bleed damage to all enemies within 2.5 meters and causing them to bleed for an additional 2405 Bleed damage over 10 seconds. This effect can occur once every 10 seconds and scales off the higher of your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
