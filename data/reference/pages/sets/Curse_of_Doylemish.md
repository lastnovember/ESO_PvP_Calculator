# Online:Curse of Doylemish

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Curse_of_Doylemish
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Curse of Doylemish is a dungeon set that drops in Scalecaller Peak. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Scalecaller Style in Heavy Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
3 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: When using a fully-charged melee Heavy Attack on a taunted monster or any enemy who is stunned, feared, or immobilized, you will deal 1811 Physical damage. This effect can occur once every 4 seconds and scales off the higher of your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
