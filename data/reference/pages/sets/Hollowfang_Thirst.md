# Online:Hollowfang Thirst

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Hollowfang_Thirst
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Hollowfang Thirst is a dungeon set that drops in the Moongrave Fane. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Moongrave Fane Style in Light Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
4 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
5 items: When your healing critically strikes or when you critically damage a target, spawn a ball of Hemoglobin at their location. After 2 seconds the ball explodes, restoring 40-1750 Magicka and applying Minor Vitality to you and your group members within 6 meters of the ball for 9 seconds, increasing their healing received and damage shield strength by 6%. This effect can occur once every 9 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
