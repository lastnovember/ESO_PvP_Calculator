Glorgoloch the Destroyer

Glorgloloch the Destroyer is a monster helm set. It consists of two armor pieces: head and shoulder, both of which are available in all three armor weights and standard traits. This set cannot be crafted.

Visually, the set bonus appears on your character as a set of flesh-like armor across the shoulders when it is active.

### Bonuses

1 item: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
2 items: While in combat, each second you stand still grants you a stack of Flesh Fortress, up to 10 stacks. Each stack increases your Armor by 8-380 and your Critical Resistance by 0-38. Each second you move removes a stack of Flesh Fortress. Exiting combat removes all stacks of Flesh Fortress. Using charge and teleport abilities do not remove stacks of Flesh Fortress.

### Drop Locations
- The head drops from Glorgoloch the Destroyer in the Imperial City Arena District or from the chest in the Ethereal Trophy Vault.
- The shoulders can be obtained from the Tel Var grand armorer in your alliance sewer headquarters for 20,000 or from the chest in the Ethereal Trophy Vault.
