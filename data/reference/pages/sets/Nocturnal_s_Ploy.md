# Online:Nocturnal's Ploy

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Nocturnal's_Ploy
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Nocturnal's Plot is an Elite Gear Set that was formerly available from "Rewards for the Worthy" mails, earned by participating in PvP, prior to Update 39. It can now be obtained in boxes purchased using Alliance Points from the Bruma Quartermaster, as quest rewards in Bruma, as a reward for completing delves and dolmens throughout Cyrodiil, from completing Board Missions, and from Elite Gear Vendors. It cannot be crafted and does not drop from monsters. Pieces are Aldmeri Dominion, Daggerfall Covenant, or Ebonheart Pact Style in Medium Armor, depending on the player's alliance.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 34-1487 Offensive Penetration
3 items: Adds 9-424 Critical Resistance
4 items: Adds 3-129 Stamina Recovery
5 items: When you directly apply a Major Debuff to an enemy, remove a Major Buff with a duration from the target. When you directly apply a Minor Debuff, remove a Minor Buff from the target. These effects can both occur once every 15 seconds and a target can be affected once every 15 seconds. This does not apply to abilities with delays.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
