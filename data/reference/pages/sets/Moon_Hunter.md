# Online:Moon Hunter

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Moon_Hunter
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Moon Hunter is a dungeon set that drops in the Moon Hunter Keep. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Silver Dawn Style in Light Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
3 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
4 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
5 items: When your alchemical poison fires, increase your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage by 12-547 for 8 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
