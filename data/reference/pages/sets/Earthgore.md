# Online:Earthgore

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Earthgore
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Earthgore Amalgam

Earthgore is a monster helm set. It consists of two armor pieces: head and shoulder, both of which are available in all three armor weights and standard traits. The set cannot be crafted. There is an outfit style based on this armor set.

### Bonuses

1 item: Adds 4% Healing Done
2 items: When you heal yourself or a group member that is under 50% Health, you conjure a pool of blood underneath them, immediately removing all harmful area effects, and healing the lowest Health group member in the area after 2 seconds for 1702 Health every 2 seconds over 8 seconds. This effect can occur once every 20 seconds and scales off the higher of your Max Magicka or Stamina.

### Drop Locations
- The head drops from the Earthgore Amalgam in Veteran Bloodroot Forge.
- The shoulders can be obtained from Urgarlag Chief-bane's reward chest at Undaunted Enclaves.
