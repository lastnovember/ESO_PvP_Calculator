# Online:Shadow Dancer's Raiment

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Shadow_Dancer's_Raiment
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Shadow Dancer's Raiment is an overland set that drops in Greenshade. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Thieves Guild Style in Light Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: After leaving Sneak or invisibility, you dodge all incoming attacks within the next 1 second. This effect can occur once every 8 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Ashenblade, a quest reward for Throne of the Wilderking.
- Basri's Blade of the Shadow Dancer, a unique drop from Overseer Basri.
- Circlet of the Wooded Eye, a quest reward for The Flooded Grove.
- Croc Hunter's Axe of the Shadow Dancer, a unique drop from Gathongor the Mauler.
- Earth-Shaker Staff, a quest reward for The Witch of Silatar.
- Pariel's Torn Hood, a quest reward for Beasts of Falinesti.
- Retribution's Cowl of the Shadow Dancer, a unique drop from Retribution.
- Rootwater Robes of the Shadow Dancer, a unique drop from the Heart of Rootwater.
- Staff of the Serpent, a quest reward for The Serpent Lord.
- Staff of Memory, a quest reward for A Tangled Knot.
- Robes of the Wilder, a quest reward for Audience with the Wilderking.
- Vale's Legacy Leggings, a quest reward for The Fading Tree.
