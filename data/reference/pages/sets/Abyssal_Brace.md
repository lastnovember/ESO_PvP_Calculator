# Online:Abyssal Brace

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Abyssal_Brace
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Abyssal Brace is a dungeon set that drops in Scrivener's Hall. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Scribes of Mora Style in Heavy Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
4 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
5 items: Adds 39-1710 Maximum Health
5 items: While you are Bracing, you and group members within 12 meters of you gain Minor Evasion, reducing damage from area attacks by 10%.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
