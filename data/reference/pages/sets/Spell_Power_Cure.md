# Online:Spell Power Cure

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Spell_Power_Cure
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Spell Power Cure is a dungeon set that drops in the White-Gold Tower, as well as being found in the Daedric Embers Trophy Vault. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Xivkyn Style in Light Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: When you overheal yourself or an ally, you give the target Major Courage for 5 seconds which increases their Weapon Damage and Spell Damage by 430.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Mara's Blessing of Spell Power Cure, the quest reward for Planemeld Obverse.
- The Unbroken, drops from the Planar Inhibitor
- Molag Kena's Touch, drops from Molag Kena
