# Online:Soulshine

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Soulshine
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Soulshine is an overland set that drops in Reaper's March. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Khajiit Style in Heavy Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
3 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: Activating an ability with a cast or channel time grants you 8-369 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage for 5 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Ex-Legionairy's Breastplate of Soulshine, dropped from Ravo Peltrasius in Fardir's Folly
- Nimriian's Shortblade of Soulshine, dropped from Nimriian in Weeping Wind Cave
- Pircalmo's Rimestaff, a quest reward for Questionable Contract
- Blade of the Eye, a quest reward for Gates of Fire
- Gauntlets of the Claw, a quest reward for The Golden Claw
- Gavel of the Hallowed, a quest reward for Motes in the Moonlight
- Big Ozur's Ogre Grips of Soulshine, dropped from Big Ozur at Big Ozur's Valley
- The Overlord's Sun Shield of Soulshine, dropped from Overlord Nur-dro at Ushmal's Rest
- Fire of Shazah's Wisdom, a quest reward for The Den of Lorkhaj
- Mara's Breath, a quest reward for An Affront to Mara
