# Online:Healer's Habit

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Healer's_Habit
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Healer's Habit is a set which drops in the Dragonstar Arena. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Ancient Elf Style in Light Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
3 items: Adds 3-129 Magicka Recovery
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: Adds 5% Healing Done
5 items: Gain Minor Mending at all times, increasing your healing done by 8%.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
