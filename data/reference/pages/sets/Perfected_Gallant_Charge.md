# Online:Perfected Gallant Charge

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Perfected_Gallant_Charge
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Perfect Gallant Charge is a Perfected Blackrose Weapons item set awarded from veteran Blackrose Prison. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Honor Guard Style. The non-perfected version of this set can be looted on normal difficulty.

### Bonuses

2 perfected items: Adds 27-1190 Armor
2 items: Reduces the cost of Shield Charge by 25% and reduces the cost of your next non-Shield Charge One Handed and Shield ability cast within 3 seconds by 100%.

### Pieces
The set consists of a one handed weapon and shield of all types.

## Notes
- You will always receive two weapons when this set drops, and they must be wielded together. If you use one with a shield or another weapon, it will not provide any bonus. You may, however, mix and match other items of this set found on subsequent runs of the trial.
- The cost reduction of your next non-Shield Charge One Handed and Shield ability doesn't apply to its Ultimate Shield Wall and its morphs.
