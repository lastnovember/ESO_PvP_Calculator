# Online:Tide-Born Wildstalker

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Tide-Born_Wildstalker
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Tide-Born Wildstalker is one of the craftable sets providing special bonuses to armor and weapons. It is available for crafting to players pursuing blacksmithing, clothing, woodworking, and/or jewelry crafting professions. The special crafting site with stations dedicated to the creation of this set is located at the Tide-Born Foundry in Western Solstice.

To make an item for this set, a crafter must first complete research on five traits for that particular item at a corresponding crafting station.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
3 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
4 items: Adds 34-1487 Offensive Penetration
5 items: Increase your direct damage done to monsters by 15%.

### Crafting Site
Tide-Born Foundry
