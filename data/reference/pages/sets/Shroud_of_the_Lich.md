# Online:Shroud of the Lich

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Shroud_of_the_Lich
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Shroud of the Lich is a dungeon set that drops in the Crypt of Hearts I and II. It cannot be crafted. Armor pieces are Lich Style in Light Armor, and shield and weapon pieces are Daedric Style.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Magicka Recovery
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
4 items: Adds 3-129 Magicka Recovery
5 items: When you cast an ability that costs resources while under 33% Magicka while in combat, your Magicka Recovery is increased by 25-1106 for 20 seconds. This effect can occur once every 1 minute.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Archmaster's Ritestaff, unique loot from Archmaster Siniel.
- Mage-Master's Signet, unique loot from the Mage Master.
- Mezeluth's Ring, unique loot from Mezeluth.
- Necromancer's Lich-Heart, a quest reward for Edge of Darkness.
- Nerien'eth's Forsaken Staff, unique loot from Nerien'eth.
- Zaven's Doomstaff, unique loot from Ilambris-Athor and Ilambris-Zaven during the first version of the dungeon.
