# Online:Aetherial Ascension

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Aetherial_Ascension
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Aetherial Ascension is one of the craftable sets providing special bonuses to armor and weapons. It is available for crafting to players pursuing blacksmithing, clothing, woodworking, and/or jewelry crafting professions. The special crafting site with stations dedicated to creation of this set are located in The ReachMarkarth.

To make an item for this set, a crafter must first complete research on nine traits for this particular item at a corresponding crafting station.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 34-1487 Armor
3 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
4 items: Adds 34-1487 Armor
5 items: Adds 171-7377 Armor. Increases the cost of Block, Sprint, Dodge, and Break Free by 20%.

### Crafting Site
Philosopher's Cradle
