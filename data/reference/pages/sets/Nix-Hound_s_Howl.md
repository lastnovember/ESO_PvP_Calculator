# Online:Nix-Hound's Howl

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Nix-Hound's_Howl
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Nix-Hound's Howl is a dungeon set that drops in Bal Sunnar. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Blessed Inheritor Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
3 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: Completing a fully-charged Heavy Attack applies Major Cowardice to your target for 1 second per 1000 Weapon Damage you have, lowering their Weapon Damage and Spell Damage by 430. You then gain Major Courage for the same duration, increasing your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage by 430. This effect can occur once every 12 seconds. Current Durations Major Cowardice: 1 second Major Courage: 1 second

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
