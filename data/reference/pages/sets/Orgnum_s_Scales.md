# Online:Orgnum's Scales

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Orgnum's_Scales
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Orgnum's Scales is one of the craftable sets providing special bonuses to armor and weapons. It is available for crafting to players pursuing blacksmithing, clothing, woodworking, and/or jewelry crafting professions. The special crafting site dedicated to creation of this set is available in the Earth Forge on completion of the Fighters Guild quest line.

To craft an item for this set, a crafter must first complete research on eight traits for this particular item at a corresponding crafting station.

The Prismatic Weapon chosen by the player during the Fighters Guild quest Will of the Council is part of the Orgnum's Scales set.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Health Recovery
3 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
4 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
5 items: While you are under 60% Health, your Health Recovery is increased by 18-800 and your Physical Resistance and Spell Resistance is increased by 148-6400.
