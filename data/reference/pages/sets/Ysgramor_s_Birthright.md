# Online:Ysgramor's Birthright

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Ysgramor's_Birthright
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Ysgramor's Birthright is an overland set that drops in The Rift. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Nord Style in Light Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: Adds 9-400 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage to your Frost Damage abilities.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Bardic Surcoat, the quest reward for Save Your Voice
- Bonebane, the quest reward for The Thunder Breaks
- Cloudkeeper's Cap, the quest reward for A Walk Above the Clouds
- Staff of Rime, the quest reward for Pulled Under
- Svari's Staff, the quest reward for The Rise of Sage Svari
- Tovisa's Dagger, the quest reward for Breaking the Coven
- Ysgramor's Frost Gem Necklace, a unique drop from Aereus
- Ysgramor's Frostmaiden's Tear, a unique drop from Frostmaiden Apa
- Ysgramor's Ghormagham's Shawl, a unique drop from Ghoragham
- Ysgramor's Jakolar's Staff, a unique drop from Jakalor
