# Online:Toothrow

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Toothrow
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Toothrow is a dungeon set that drops in the Wayrest Sewers. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Primal Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
3 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
4 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
5 items: Adds 3-171 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: Gain Major Savagery and Prophecy at all times, increasing your Weapon Critical and Spell Critical rating by 2629.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Allene's Deathly Bow, a unique drop from Allene Pellingare in Wayrest Sewers II.
- Malubeth's Stinger, a unique drop from Malubeth the Scourger.
- Ratskewer Bow, a unique drop from Allene Pellingare in Wayrest Sewers I.
- Rattail Necklace, a unique drop from the Rat Whisperer.
- Uulgarg's Charm, a unique drop from Uulgarg the Hungry.
