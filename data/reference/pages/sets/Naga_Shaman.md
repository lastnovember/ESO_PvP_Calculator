# Online:Naga Shaman

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Naga_Shaman
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Naga Shaman is one of the craftable sets providing special bonuses to armor and weapons. It is available for crafting to players pursuing blacksmithing, clothing, woodworking, and/or jewelry crafting professions. The special crafting site with stations dedicated to creation of this set are located in MurkmireMurkmire.

To make an item for this set, a crafter must first complete research on two traits for this particular item at a corresponding crafting station.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 4% Healing Taken
3 items: Adds 4% Healing Done
4 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
5 items: When you apply a damage shield to yourself or an ally, you gain Minor Mending and Minor Vitality for 6 seconds, increasing your healing done by 8% and healing received and damage shield strength by 6%. This effect can occur once every 6 seconds.

### Crafting Site
Deep Swamp Forge
