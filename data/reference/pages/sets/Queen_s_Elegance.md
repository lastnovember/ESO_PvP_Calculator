# Online:Queen's Elegance

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Queen's_Elegance
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Queen's Elegance is an overland set that drops in Auridon. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are High Elf Style in Light Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
4 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
5 items: When you use a Light Attack you reduce the Health, Magicka, or Stamina cost of your next active ability cast within 2 seconds by 10%. When you use a fully-charged Heavy Attack you gain Empower for 3 seconds, increasing the damage of your Heavy Attacks against monsters by 150%. Each of these effects can occur once every second.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Artificer's Bolt; the quest reward for The Mallari-Mora.
- Binder's Robe, the quest reward for A Village Awakened.
- Elegant Bakhig's Purestaff, a unique drop from Bakhig, final boss of Entila's Folly in the Khajiit Style.
- Elegant Deathcaller's Cowl, a unique drop from the world bosses Eraman, Norendo and Quendia at Soulfire Plateau.
- Elegant Serpent's Flame, a unique drop from the world boss The Nestmother at Nestmother's Den in the Wood Elf Style.
- Elegant Wormtainted Kilt, a unique drop from Nolonir, final boss of Wansalen.
- Estre's Dreadstaff, the quest reward for Sever All Ties.
- Kilt of the Fractured Mind, the quest reward for Depths of Madness.
- Plaguetender's Staff, the quest reward for The First Patient.
- Traitor's Flame, the quest reward for Teldur's End.
- War-Mage's Cowl, the quest reward for The Wayward Dagger in the Breton Style.
