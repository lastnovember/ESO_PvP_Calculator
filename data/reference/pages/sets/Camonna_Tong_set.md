Camonna Tong
Camonna Tong is an overland set that drops in Apocrypha and the Telvanni Peninsula. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Dead Keeper Style in Medium armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
3 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: When you kill a monster and gain Experience Points, gain 1 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage for every 50 Experience Points the monster is worth for 30 seconds. This bonus can stack up to a maximum of 540 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage. This item set is not affected by Experience Point boosting effects.

### Pieces
The set consists of Medium Armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Cruxstone Bow
