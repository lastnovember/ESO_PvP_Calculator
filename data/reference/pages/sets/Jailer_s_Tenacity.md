# Online:Jailer's Tenacity

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Jailer's_Tenacity
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Jailer's Tenacity is a dungeon set that drops in the Moon Hunter Keep. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Silver Dawn Style in Heavy Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 4% Healing Taken
3 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
4 items: Adds 4% Healing Taken
5 items: When you lose 7000 or more Health from a single attack, you gain Major Vitality for 8 seconds, increasing your healing received and damage shield strength by 12%.

### Pieces
The set consists heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
