# Online:Hist Bark

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Hist_Bark
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Hist Bark is one of the craftable sets providing special bonuses to armor and weapons. It is available for crafting to players pursuing blacksmithing, clothing, woodworking, and/or jewelry crafting professions. One special crafting site with stations dedicated to creation of this set is located in the third zone of each of the three Alliances.

To make an item for this set, a crafter must first complete research on four traits for this particular item at a corresponding crafting station.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 34-1487 Armor
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
4 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
5 items: While you are Bracing, you gain Major Evasion, reducing damage from area attacks by 20%.
