# Online:Perfected Kazpian's Cruel Signet

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Perfected_Kazpian's_Cruel_Signet
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Perfected Kazpian's Cruel Signet is a trial set found in veteran Ossein Cage. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are in the Coldharbour Dominator Style in Medium Armor. The non-perfected version of this set can be looted on normal difficulty.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
3 items: Gain Minor Slayer at all times, increasing your damage done to Dungeon, Trial, and Arena Monsters by 5%.
4 items: Adds 34-1487 Offensive Penetration
5 perfected items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: On dealing area direct damage, place a Sword above an enemy, up to once every 1 second. You cannot place a Sword on an enemy who already has one from you. After 6 seconds, the Sword falls, dealing 772 Physical Damage. This damage scales off the higher of your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage. Applying a Sword increases your damage done by 2% for 6 seconds, stacking up to 6 times.

### Pieces
The set consists of Medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
