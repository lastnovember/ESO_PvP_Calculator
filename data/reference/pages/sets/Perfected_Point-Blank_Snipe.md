# Online:Perfected Point-Blank Snipe

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Perfected_Point-Blank_Snipe
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Perfected Point-Blank Snipe is a Perfected Vateshran Weapons item set which drops in veteran Vateshran Hollows. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are based on the Ancestral Reach Style. The non-perfected version of this set can be looted on normal difficulty.

### Bonuses

2 perfected items: Adds 2-103 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
2 items: Increases your damage done with Snipe based on how close you are to your target. Max Damage: 33% at 3 meters. Minimum Damage: 5% at 18.5 meters.

### Pieces
The set consists of a Bow.

### Notes
- While the description states that 2 items are required, this is because bows are 2-handed weapons, and thus count as 2 items by themselves, so only 1 item is actually needed.
