Slimecraw

Slimecraw is a monster helm set. It consists of two armor pieces: head and shoulder, both of which are available in all three armor weights and standard traits. The set cannot be crafted. There is an outfit style based on this armor set.

### Bonuses

1 item: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
2 items: Adds 2-113 Critical Chance
2 items: Gain Minor Berserk at all times, increasing your damage done by 5%.

### Drop Locations
- The head drops from Allene Pellingare in Veteran Wayrest Sewers I.
- The shoulders can be obtained from Maj al-Ragath's chest at Undaunted Enclaves.
