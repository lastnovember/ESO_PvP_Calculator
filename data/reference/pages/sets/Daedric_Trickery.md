# Online:Daedric Trickery

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Daedric_Trickery
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Daedric Trickery is one of the craftable sets providing special bonuses to armor and weapons. It is available for crafting to players pursuing blacksmithing, clothing, woodworking, and/or jewelry crafting professions. The special crafting site with stations dedicated to creation of this set are located in the Vvardenfell.

To make an item for this set, a crafter must first complete research on eight traits for this particular item at a corresponding crafting station.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
4 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
5 items: When you deal damage you gain one of 5 random Major buffs for 21 seconds every 9 seconds. Eligible buffs are Expedition, Protection, Mending, Heroism, or Vitality.

### Crafting Site
Randas Ancestral Tomb

### Notes
- The major buffs this set bestows are:
- Major Expedition - Increase Movement Speed by 30%.
- Major Protection - Reduce damage taken by 10%.
- Major Mending - Increase Healing done by 16%.
- Major Heroism - Generates 3 Ultimate every 1.5 seconds.
- Major Vitality - Increase Healing received and Shield strength by 12%.
- If this set triggers while mounted, one will be dismounted 21 seconds after an enemy is engaged.
