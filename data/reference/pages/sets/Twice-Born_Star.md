# Online:Twice-Born Star

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Twice-Born_Star
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Twice-Born Star is one of the craftable sets providing special bonuses to armor and weapons. It is available for crafting to players pursuing blacksmithing, clothing, woodworking, and/or jewelry crafting professions. The special crafting site dedicated to creation of this set is available in Craglorn.

To make an item for this set, a crafter must first complete research on nine traits for this particular item at a corresponding crafting station.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
4 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
5 items: You can have two Mundus Stone boons at the same time.

### Crafting Site
Atelier of the Twice-Born Star
