# Online:Twilight's Embrace

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Twilight's_Embrace
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Twilight's Embrace is one of the craftable sets providing special bonuses to armor and weapons. It is available for crafting to players pursuing blacksmithing, clothing, woodworking, and/or jewelry crafting professions. One special crafting site with stations dedicated to creation of this set is located in the second zone of each of the three Alliances.

To make an item for this set, a crafter must first complete research on three traits for this particular item at a corresponding crafting station.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
3 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: Increases your healing received by 10%.
