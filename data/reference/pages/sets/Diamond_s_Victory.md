# Online:Diamond's Victory

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Diamond's_Victory
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Diamond's Victory is one of the craftable sets providing special bonuses to armor and weapons. It is available for crafting to players pursuing blacksmithing, clothing, woodworking, and/or jewelry crafting professions. The special crafting site with stations dedicated to creation of this set are located in Pentric Run set Station Blackwood.

To make an item for this set, a crafter must first complete research on five traits for this particular item at a corresponding crafting station.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
3 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: While in combat, using an ability with a range of 7 meters or less grants you Range Supremacy for 5 seconds, adding 10-437 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage to your damage over time and ranged attacks. While in combat, using an ability with a range of over 7 meters grants you Melee Supremacy for 5 seconds, adding 10-437 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage to your melee attacks.

### Crafting Site
Pentric Run
