# Online:Essence Thief

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Essence_Thief
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Essence Thief is a dungeon set that drops in the White-Gold Tower, as well as being found in the Daedric Embers Trophy Vault. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Xivkyn Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: Dealing damage with a Light or Heavy Attack draws essence from the enemy that pools near them for 5 seconds. Drawing from the pool heals you for 101-4356 Health, restores 101-4356 Stamina, and increases your damage done by 10% for 10 seconds. A pool can only be created once every 10 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Harvest Dirk, drops from The Adjudicator
