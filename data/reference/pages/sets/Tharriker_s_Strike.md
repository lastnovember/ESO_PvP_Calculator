# Online:Tharriker's Strike

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Tharriker's_Strike
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Tharriker's Strike is one of the craftable sets providing special bonuses to armor and weapons. It is available for crafting to players pursuing blacksmithing, clothing, woodworking, and/or jewelry crafting professions. The special crafting site with stations dedicated to the creation of this set is located at Singer's Outpost in West Weald.

To make an item for this set, a crafter must first complete research on three traits for that particular item at a corresponding crafting station.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
3 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: Dealing damage with a fully-charged Heavy Attack grants you Major Berserk for 4 seconds, increasing your damage done by 10%. This effect can occur once every 1 second.

### Crafting Site
Singer's Outpost
