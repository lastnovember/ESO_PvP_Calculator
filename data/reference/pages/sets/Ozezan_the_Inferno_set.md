Ozezan the Inferno

Ozezan the Inferno is a monster helm set. It consists of two armor pieces: head and shoulder, both of which are available in all three armor weights and standard traits. The set cannot be crafted.

### Bonuses

1 item: Adds 3-129 Magicka Recovery
2 items: Overhealing yourself or an ally grants them 4272 Armor for 1.1 seconds. Healing yourself or an ally grants them Minor Vitality for 1.1 seconds, increasing their healing received and damage shield strength by 6%.

### Drop Locations
- The head drops from Ozezan the Inferno in Veteran Scrivener's Hall.
- The shoulders can be obtained from Urgarlag Chief-bane's reward chest at Undaunted Enclaves.
