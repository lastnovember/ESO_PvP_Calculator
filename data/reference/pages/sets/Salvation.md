# Online:Salvation

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Salvation
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Salvation is an overland set that drops in Malabal Tor. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are High Elf Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
3 items: Adds 3-129 Stamina Recovery
4 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
5 items: Reduces the cost of your Werewolf Transformation ability by 33%. While in Werewolf form, your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage is increased by 5-150.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Dead Man's Ring of Salvation, a unique drop from Blueclaw Matron.
- Waterlogged Chestpiece of Salvation, a unique drop from Thjormar the Drowned.
- Belt of the Waylaid Traveler of Salvation, a unique drop from Dugan the Red.
- Commander's Signet of Salvation, a unique drop from Commander Faldethil
