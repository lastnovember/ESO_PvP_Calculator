# Online:Rattlecage

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Rattlecage
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Rattlecage is a dungeon set that drops in the Vaults of Madness. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Daedric Style in Heavy Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
3 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: Adds 3-171 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: Gain Major Brutality and Sorcery at all times, increasing your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage by 20%.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Forbidden Jewels, unique loot from The Cursed One.
- Gem of Curses, unique loot from The Cursed One.
- Soul-Siphon, unique loot from Ulguna Soul-Reaver.
- Bonebound Ring, unique loot from Death's Head.
- Despair, unique loot from the Grothdarr.
- Achæraizur's Chain, unique loot from Achæraizur.
- Omen's Edge, unique loot from Iskra the Omen.
- Vengeance, unique loot from the Mad Architect.
