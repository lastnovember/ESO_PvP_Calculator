Roksa the Warped

Roksa the Warped is a monster helm set. It consists of two armor pieces: head and shoulder, both of which are available in all three armor weights and standard traits. The set cannot be crafted.

### Bonuses

1 item: Adds 1-70 Stamina Recovery
1 item: Adds 1-70 Magicka Recovery
1 item: Adds 1-70 Health Recovery
2 items: Each second you are in combat, gain a stack of Darklight, up to 30 stacks max. Each stack of Darklight increases your Stamina Recovery, Magicka Recovery, and Health Recovery by 8. Each second you are out of combat, lose a stack of Darklight.

### Drop Locations
- The head drops from Roksa the Warped in Veteran Bal Sunnar.
- The shoulders can be obtained from Urgarlag Chief-bane's reward chest at Undaunted Enclaves.
