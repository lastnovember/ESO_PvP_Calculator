# Online:Robes of Alteration Mastery

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Robes_of_Alteration_Mastery
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Robes of Alteration Mastery is an item set found in boxes purchased using Alliance Points from the Cropsford Quartermaster, as quest rewards in Cropsford, as a reward for completing delves and dolmens throughout Cyrodiil, from completing Board Missions, and from Elite Gear Vendors. It cannot be crafted and does not drop from monsters. Pieces are Aldmeri Dominion, Daggerfall Covenant, or Ebonheart Pact Style in Light Armor, depending on the player's alliance.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Magicka Recovery
3 items: Adds 3-129 Magicka Recovery
4 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
5 items: Reduces the cost of all of your abilities by 6%.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
