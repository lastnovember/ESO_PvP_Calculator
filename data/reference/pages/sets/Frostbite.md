# Online:Frostbite

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Frostbite
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Frostbite is an overland set that drops in Blackwood. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Ivory Brigade Style in Light Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
3 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: Increases your damage done with Frost abilities by 8%. Increases your damage done against Chilled enemies by 4%. Increases your damage done against enemies afflicted with Minor Brittle by 2%.

### Pieces
The set consists of Light Armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Stibbons's Slime-Covered Spaulders, An Abundance of Stibbons quest reward
- Aloysius's Empty Gold Belt, Dog Days quest reward
- House Tirethi Signet Band, Giving Up the Ghost quest reward
- Band of Zenithar, The Golden Anvil quest reward
- Faric's Finest Staff of Frigidity, Haunted Legacy quest reward
- Black Fin Fire Hat, Love Among the Fire quest reward
- Captain Voldar's Hammer, The Pirate's Gift quest reward
- Heem-Jas's Heroic Breeches, Shadows in the Swamp quest reward
- Nisswo Prayer Boots, Tome of Shadow quest reward
- Light of the Twin Ambitions, A Hidden Vault quest reward
