# Online:Noxious Boulder

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Noxious_Boulder
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Noxious Boulder is a dungeon set that drops in Lep Seclusa. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Militant Monk Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
3 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
4 items: Adds 34-1487 Offensive Penetration
5 items: Dealing damage with a fully-charged Heavy Attack creates a 3 meter poison boulder. After 1.2 seconds the boulder rolls forward until it hits an enemy, dealing 1049 Poison damage in a 6 meter area and creating a 6 meter pool of poison for 5 seconds, dealing 730 Poison damage and applying the Poisoned status effect each second. This effect can occur once every 20 seconds and scales off the higher of your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
