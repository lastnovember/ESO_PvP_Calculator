# Online:Sergeant's Mail

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Sergeant's_Mail
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Sergeant's Mail is a dungeon set that drops in the Wayrest Sewers. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Dark Elf Style in Heavy Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
3 items: Adds 3-129 Health Recovery
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: When you deal damage with a Heavy Attack, you gain a stack of Sergeant's Focus for 5 seconds, increasing the damage of your Heavy Attacks by 15-645 per stack. This effect can occur once every second and stacks up to 4 times.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique items include:
- Mightmaker
- Smuggler's Luck Beads
- Garron's Cold Betrayal
- Ring of the Risen
- Varaine's Death Knell
- Reaper's Choker
- Investigator's Uniform of the Sergeant
- Pellingare War Plate of the Sergeant

### Notes
- You actually have to deal damage with the heavy attack in order to get a stack or refresh its timer, so doing heavy attacks against invincible enemies will not work.
- You do not have to fully charge the heavy attack in order to get a stack or refresh its timer, barely charging a "medium" attack counts as well.
- This set uses the following icon for its buff: Sergent's Mail
