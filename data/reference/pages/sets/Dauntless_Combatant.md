# Online:Dauntless Combatant

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Dauntless_Combatant
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Dauntless Combatant is a craftable item set providing special bonuses to armor and weapons. It is available for crafting to players pursuing blacksmithing, clothing, woodworking, and/or jewelry crafting professions. The special crafting site with stations dedicated to creation of this set is located Cropsford Armory in Cyrodiil. To be able to enter the armory, your alliance must hold Cropsford.

To craft an item for this set, a crafter must have researched three traits for the particular item.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
3 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
4 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
5 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
5 items: When you are affected by a disabling effect, you automatically Break Free for no cost. After using this effect, you become Winded and cannot trigger this effect or others like it for 21 seconds.

### Crafting Sites
Cropsford Armory
