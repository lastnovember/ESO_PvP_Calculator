# Online:War Maiden

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:War_Maiden
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

War Maiden is an overland set that drops on Vvardenfell. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Telvanni Style in Light Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: Adds 13-600 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage to your Magic Damage abilities.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Artificer's Coat of the War Maiden, a quest reward for The Heart's Desire.
- Blackstone Hammer of the War Maiden, a quest reward for Like Blood from a Stone.
- Dark Staff of the War Maiden, a quest reward for At Any Cost.
- Dratha's Epaulettes of the War Maiden, a quest reward for Reclaiming Vos.
- Governor's Ring of the War Maiden, a quest reward for Breaking Through the Fog.
- Revus's Spare Staff of the War Maiden, a quest reward for Hatching a Plan.
- Tear-Stained Staff of the War Maiden, a quest reward for The Heart of a Telvanni.
