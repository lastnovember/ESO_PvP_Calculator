# Online:Perfected Mender's Ward

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Perfected_Mender's_Ward
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Perfected Mender's Ward is a Perfected Blackrose Weapons item set awarded from veteran Blackrose Prison. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Honor Guard Style. The non-perfected version of this set can be looted on normal difficulty.

### Bonuses

2 perfected items: Adds 2-103 Magicka Recovery
2 items: Steadfast Ward applies Major Vitality to your target for 4 seconds, increasing their healing received and damage shield strength by 12%.

### Pieces
The set consists of a restoration staff.

### Notes
- While the description states that 2 items are required, this is because staves are 2-handed weapons, and thus count as 2 items by themselves, so only 1 item is actually needed.
