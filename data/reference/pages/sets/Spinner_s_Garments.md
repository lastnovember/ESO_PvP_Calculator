# Online:Spinner's Garments

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Spinner's_Garments
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Spinner's Garments is an overland set that drops in Malabal Tor. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Wood Elf Style in Light Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: Adds 80-3460 Offensive Penetration

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Spinner's Sapstung Helmet, a unique drop from Blackvine Strangler.
- Spinner's Sea Tamer's Torc, a unique drop from Tallatta the Lustrous.
- Spinner's Fractured Femur, a unique drop from Bone Grappler.
- Spinner's Skullbreaker, a unique drop from Skullbreaker.
- Acrobat's Boots, a quest reward for Raise the Curtain.
- Sapmagic Ring, a quest reward for The Prisoner of Jathsogur.
