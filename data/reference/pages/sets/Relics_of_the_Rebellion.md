# Online:Relics of the Rebellion

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Relics_of_the_Rebellion
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Relics of the Rebellion is an item set. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Daedric Style. These items no longer drop in-game, but those who received them when they were available still have them.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
3 items: When you deal damage, you have a 5% chance to gain a damage shield that absorbs 70-3010 damage for 5 seconds

### Pieces
The set consists of necklaces, maces, and shields.
- Auriel's Shield
- Crusader's Mace
- Crusader's Necklace
- Elf-Doom Maul
- Necklace of Sardavar
- Rebellion Mace
- Shield of the Man-Bull

### Drop Locations
- The Elf-Doom Maul drops in Veteran Crypt of Hearts. plain
