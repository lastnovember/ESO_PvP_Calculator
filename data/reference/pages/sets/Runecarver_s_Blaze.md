# Online:Runecarver's Blaze

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Runecarver's_Blaze
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Runecarver's Blaze is a dungeon set that drops in Scrivener's Hall. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Scribes of Mora Style in Light Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
3 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
4 items: Adds 34-1487 Offensive Penetration
5 items: Your damage over time effects deal an additional 209 Flame damage every third damage tick. This damage scales off your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
