# Online:Seeker Synthesis

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Seeker_Synthesis
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Seeker Synthesis is one of the craftable sets providing special bonuses to armor and weapons. It is available for crafting to players pursuing blacksmithing, clothing, woodworking, and/or jewelry crafting professions. The special crafting site with stations dedicated to the creation of this set is located at Versicolor Carrels in Apocrypha.

To make an item for this set, a crafter must first complete research on seven traits for that particular item at a corresponding crafting station.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
4 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
5 items: When you cast an ability that costs resources while in combat, you reduce your potion cooldown by 1 second. This effect can occur once every 2 seconds.

### Crafting Site
Versicolor Carrels
