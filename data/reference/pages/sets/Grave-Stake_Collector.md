# Online:Grave-Stake Collector

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Grave-Stake_Collector
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Grave-Stake Collector is one of the craftable sets providing special bonuses to armor and weapons. It is available for crafting to players pursuing blacksmithing, clothing, woodworking, and/or jewelry crafting professions. The special crafting site with stations dedicated to creation of this set are located in MurkmireMurkmire.

To make an item for this set, a crafter must first complete research on seven traits for this particular item at a corresponding crafting station.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
4 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
5 items: When an enemy you recently damaged dies, they drop a grave-stake for 6 seconds. When you touch the grave-stake, you restore 106-4564 Stamina and Magicka. Only one grave-stake can appear at a time.

### Crafting Site
Sweet Breeze Overlook
