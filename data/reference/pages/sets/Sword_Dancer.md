# Online:Sword Dancer

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Sword_Dancer
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Sword Dancer is a dungeon set that drops in the Blessed Crucible. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Redguard Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: Adds 13-600 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage to your Dual Wield abilities.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as one-handed weapons of all types (other weapons and shields may still exist but cannot be obtained new anymore). Unique pieces include:
- Captain's Medallion, unique loot from Captain Thoran.
- Mark of the Morag Tong, unique loot from Teranya the Faceless.
