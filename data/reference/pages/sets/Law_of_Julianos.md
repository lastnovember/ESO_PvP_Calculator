# Online:Law of Julianos

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Law_of_Julianos
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Law of Julianos is one of the craftable sets providing special bonuses to armor and weapons. It is available for crafting to players pursuing blacksmithing, clothing, woodworking, and/or jewelry crafting professions. The special crafting site with stations dedicated to creation of this set are located in WrothgarOrsinium.

To make an item for this set, a crafter must first complete research on six traits for this particular item at a corresponding crafting station.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
4 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
5 items: Adds 6-300 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage

### Crafting Site
Boreal Forge
