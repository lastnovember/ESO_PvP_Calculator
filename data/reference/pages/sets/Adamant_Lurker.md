# Online:Adamant Lurker

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Adamant_Lurker
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Adamant Lurker is an overland set that drops in Apocrypha and the Telvanni Peninsula. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Dead Keeper Style in Heavy armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Health Recovery
3 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
4 items: Adds 3-129 Health Recovery
5 items: Gain 35-1505 Health Recovery while you are standing still.

### Pieces
The set consists of Heavy Armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
