# Online:Morkuldin

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Morkuldin
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Morkuldin is one of the craftable sets providing special bonuses to armor and weapons. It is available for crafting to players pursuing blacksmithing, clothing, woodworking, and/or jewelry crafting professions. The special crafting site with stations dedicated to creation of this set are located in WrothgarOrsinium.

To make an item for this set, a crafter must first complete research on nine traits for this particular item at a corresponding crafting station and finish the quest line associated with access to the area.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
3 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: When you deal damage with a Light or Heavy Attack, you summon an animated weapon to attack your enemies for 15 seconds. The animated weapon's basic attacks deal 422 Physical Damage. This effect can occur once every 15 seconds and scales off the higher of your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage.

### Crafting Site
Morkuldin Forge
