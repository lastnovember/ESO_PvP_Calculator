# Online:Gryphon's Ferocity

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Gryphon's_Ferocity
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Gryphon's Ferocity is an overland set that drops in Summerset. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Ancient Elf Style in medium armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
3 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: After dealing direct damage, you gain Minor Force and Minor Expedition for 10 seconds, increasing your Critical Damage done by 10% and increasing your Movement Speed by 15%.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Ghost's Skullcap
- Tower Sentinel Brigandine
- Boots of the Courier
- Menagerie Keeper's Gloves
- Corgrad Heirloom Belt
- Sunnawel's Shining Espaulders
