# Online:Eye of the Grasp

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Eye_of_the_Grasp
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Eye of the Grasp is an overland set that drops in the Deadlands and Fargrave. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Fargrave Guardian Style in Light Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
3 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
4 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
5 items: When you deal Critical Damage, generate 7 Ultimate. This effect can occur once every 5 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
