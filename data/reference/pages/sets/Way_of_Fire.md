# Online:Way of Fire

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Way_of_Fire
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Way of Fire is an overland set that drops in Craglorn. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Yokudan Style in Heavy Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
3 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
4 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
5 items: When you deal damage with a Light or Heavy Attack, you deal an additional 571 Flame damage. This effect can occur once every 2 seconds and scales off the higher of your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Unstable Element Staff of the Fire from Erold in Belkarth
- Sandstone Guard of the Fire also from Erold in Belkarth
