# Online:Overland Sets/DLC Drops

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Overland_Sets/DLC_Drops
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Zones drop pieces from their sets:
- Delve bosses drop a waist, foot, or unique item piece.
- Overland bosses drop a head, chest, leg, weapon, or unique item piece. In Hew's Bane and Gold Coast, they also drop jewelry pieces.
- Public dungeon bosses drop a shoulder, hand, weapon, or unique item piece. In Solstice, they also drop jewelry pieces.
- Chests in Overworld Events drop a jewelry set piece.
- Chests in Trials and Arenas drop a jewelry set piece.
- Treasure chests have a chance to grant any set piece that can drop in that zone; the chance is increased by having the Treasure Hunter champion perk.
- Zone story quest rewards are often part of overland sets.

Dropped items range from Fine quality to Epic quality, while Legendary quality items must be obtained via improvement or purchase from Adhazabi Aba-daro.
