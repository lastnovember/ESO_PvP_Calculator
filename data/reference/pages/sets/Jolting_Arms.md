# Online:Jolting Arms

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Jolting_Arms
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Jolting Arms is a dungeon set that drops in Tempest Island. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Ancient Elf style in Heavy Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
3 items: Adds 34-1487 Armor
4 items: Adds 34-1487 Armor
5 items: When you Block, you charge your arms, increasing your Physical Resistance and Spell Resistance by 107-4620 for 6 seconds and causing your next Bash attack to deal an additional 81-3513 damage. This effect can occur once every 6 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique items include:
- Matriarch's Ring, unique loot from Sonolia the Matriarch.
- Stormcaller's Blade, unique loot from Valaran Stormcaller.
- Tempestshield, unique loot from Stormfist.
