# Online:Bog Raider

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Bog_Raider
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Bog Raider is an overland set that drops in Blackwood. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Black Fin Style in Heavy Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
3 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
4 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
5 items: When an enemy you have recently damaged dies, gain 10 Ultimate and increase your Health Recovery by 26-1156 for 10 seconds. This effect can occur once every 10 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
