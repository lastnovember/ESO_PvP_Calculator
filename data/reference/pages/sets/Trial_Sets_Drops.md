# Online:Trial Sets/Drops

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Trial_Sets/Drops
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Every trial drops pieces associated with its sets:
- Non-final bosses will drop one of: Hands, Waist, Feet, Chest, Shoulder, Head, Legs.
- Final bosses will drop one of: Weapon or Shield.
- Quest reward containers (1 per week) associated with the Trial will drop one piece of Jewelry, a Weapon or Shield, but these are bound on pick-up.
- Chests have a slight chance to drop armor, increased with lock difficulty and by the Treasure Hunter passive.

Items dropped in Veteran Trials will always be of Epic quality, except for Jewelry from Perfected sets, which are Legendary quality. Items dropped in Normal Trials will always be of Superior quality.
