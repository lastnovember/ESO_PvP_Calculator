# Online:Blunted Blades

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Blunted_Blades
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Blunted Blades is an Elite Gear Set that was formerly available from "Rewards for the Worthy" mails, earned by participating in PvP, prior to Update 39. It can now be obtained in boxes purchased using Alliance Points from the Bruma Quartermaster, as quest rewards in Bruma, as a reward for completing delves and dolmens throughout Cyrodiil, from completing Board Missions, and from Elite Gear Vendors. It cannot be crafted and does not drop from monsters. Pieces are Aldmeri Dominion, Daggerfall Covenant, or Ebonheart Pact Style in Medium Armor, depending on the player's alliance.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 34-1487 Armor
3 items: Adds 9-421 Critical Resistance
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: When you take direct damage, Minor Enervation is applied to your enemy for 4 seconds, reducing their Critical Damage done by 10%. This can occur once every 5 seconds. Increase your damage done to enemies with Minor Enervation by 6%.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
