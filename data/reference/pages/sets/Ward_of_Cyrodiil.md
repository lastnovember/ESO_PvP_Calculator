# Online:Ward of Cyrodiil

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Ward_of_Cyrodiil
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Ward of Cyrodiil is an item set found in boxes purchased using Alliance Points from the Bruma Quartermaster, as quest rewards in Bruma, as a reward for completing delves and dolmens throughout Cyrodiil, from completing Board Missions, and from Elite Gear Vendors. It cannot be crafted and does not drop from monsters. Pieces are Aldmeri Dominion, Daggerfall Covenant, or Ebonheart Pact Style in Medium Armor, depending on the player's alliance.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
3 items: Adds 3-129 Stamina Recovery
4 items: Adds 3-129 Stamina Recovery
5 items: When you deal Martial melee damage, you apply Major Defile to the enemy for 4 seconds, reducing their healing received and damage shield strength by 12%. This effect can occur once every 5 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as melee weapons and shields (no bows or staves). Unique pieces include:
- Bruuke's Hide of Cyrodiil's Ward, a unique drop from Bruuke
- Traug's Arrow Flinger of Cyrodiil's Ward, a unique drop from Traug Wolfbreath
