# Online:Gorethief

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Gorethief
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Gorethief is a PVP set that was added to the game with Update 49. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Breton Style in all armor weights and weapons.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
3 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: Dealing direct damage generates a stack of Gorethief for 30 seconds, up to 10 stacks. While you have Gorethief equipped, Roll Dodge consumes 1 stack and heals you for 2021 Health, scaling off your Max Health. At max stacks, while you have Gorethief equipped, bash an enemy to consume all stacks and deal 2144 Bleed damage, scaling off the higher of your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage, to enemies within 8 meters of you.

### Pieces
The set consists of armor of all weights and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.

### Drop Locations
- Pieces of the set can be looted from Rewards for the Worthy boxes.
