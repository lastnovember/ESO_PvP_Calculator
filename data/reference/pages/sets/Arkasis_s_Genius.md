# Online:Arkasis's Genius

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Arkasis's_Genius
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Genius is a dungeon set that drops in the Stone Garden. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Hazardous Alchemy Style in Heavy Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
3 items: Adds 34-1487 Armor
4 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
5 items: Whenever you drink a potion while in combat, you and 3 group members gain 1-44 Ultimate. This effect can occur once every 30 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
