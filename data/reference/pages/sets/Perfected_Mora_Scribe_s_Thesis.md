# Online:Perfected Mora Scribe's Thesis

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Perfected_Mora_Scribe's_Thesis
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Mora Scribe's Thesis is a trial set found in veteran Lucent Citadel. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are in Lucent Sentinel Style in Light Armor. The non-perfected version of this set can be looted on normal difficulty.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
3 items: Gain Minor Slayer at all times, increasing your damage done to Dungeon, Trial, and Arena Monsters by 5%.
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 perfected items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
5 items: Increase your Critical Chance by 128 for every Major Buff active on you, up to 1536 Critical Chance. Increase your Critical Damage done by 1% for every Minor Buff active on you, up to 12% Critical Damage done. Current Bonus: 0 Critical Chance and 0% Critical Damage

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
