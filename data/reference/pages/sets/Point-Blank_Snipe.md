# Online:Point-Blank Snipe

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Point-Blank_Snipe
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Point-Blank Snipe is a Vateshran Weapons item set which drops in normal Vateshran Hollows. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are based on the Ancestral Reach Style. The perfected version of this set can be looted on veteran difficulty.

### Bonuses

2 items: Increases your damage done with Snipe based on how close you are to your target. Max Damage: 33% at 3 meters. Minimum Damage: 5% at 18.5 meters.

### Pieces
The set consists of a Bow.

### Notes
- While the description states that 2 items are required, this is because bows are 2-handed weapons, and thus count as 2 items by themselves, so only 1 item is actually needed.
