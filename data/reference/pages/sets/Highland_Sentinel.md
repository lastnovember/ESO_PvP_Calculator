# Online:Highland Sentinel

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Highland_Sentinel
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Highland Sentinel is one of the craftable sets providing special bonuses to armor and weapons. It is available for crafting to players pursuing blacksmithing, clothing, woodworking, and/or jewelry crafting professions. The special crafting site with stations dedicated to the creation of this set is located at Leftwheal Granary in West Weald.

To make an item for this set, a crafter must first complete research on five traits for that particular item at a corresponding crafting station.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
3 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
4 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
5 items: Adds 22-986 Critical Chance
5 items: While in combat, each second you stand still grants you a stack of Sentinel's Eye, increasing your Critical Chance by 10-443 per stack, stacking up to 3 times. Each second you move, one stack is removed. Using charge and teleport abilities does not remove stacks, and exiting combat will remove all stacks of Sentinel's Eye.

### Crafting Site
Leftwheal Granary
