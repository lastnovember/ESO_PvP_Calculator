# Online:Stinging Slashes

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Stinging_Slashes
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Stinging Slashes is a Master's Weapons item set found in normal Dragonstar Arena. It cannot be crafted. The sword is in the Nord Style, the axe and dagger is in the Khajiit Style, and the mace is in the Argonian Style, all of which are in the unobtainable tier 5 versions. The perfected version of this set can be looted on veteran difficulty.

### Bonuses

2 items: Twin Slashes deals 209 more damage for each hit of the initial attack and bleed. This effect scales off the higher of your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage.

### Pieces
The set consists of one-handed weapons of all types. There is no shield.

## Notes
- You will always receive two weapons when this set drops.
