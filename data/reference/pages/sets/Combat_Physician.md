# Online:Combat Physician

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Combat_Physician
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Combat Physician is a dungeon set that drops in Wayrest Sewers I and II. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Redguard Style in Light Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
3 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
4 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
5 items: When your healing critically strikes, you grant your target a damage shield that absorbs 100-4308 damage for 6 seconds. This effect can occur once every 6 seconds per target.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
