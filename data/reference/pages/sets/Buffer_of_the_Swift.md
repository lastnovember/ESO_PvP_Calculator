# Online:Buffer of the Swift

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Buffer_of_the_Swift
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Buffer of the Swift is an item set found in boxes purchased using Alliance Points from the Cropsford Quartermaster, as quest rewards in Cropsford, as a reward for completing delves and dolmens throughout Cyrodiil, from completing Board Missions, and from Elite Gear Vendors. It cannot be crafted and does not drop from monsters. Pieces are Aldmeri Dominion, Daggerfall Covenant, or Ebonheart Pact Style in Light Armor, depending on the player's alliance.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
3 items: Adds 34-1487 Armor
4 items: Adds 34-1487 Armor
5 items: Reduce damage taken from Players by 10%.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
