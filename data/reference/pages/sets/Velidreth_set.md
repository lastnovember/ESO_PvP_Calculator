# Online:Velidreth (set)

Velidreth

Velidreth is a monster helm set. It consists of two armor pieces: head and shoulder, both of which are available in all three armor weights and standard traits. The set cannot be crafted. There is an outfit style based on this armor set. There is also an opal outfit style based on this armor set.

### Bonuses

1 item: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
2 items: When you deal damage, you have a 20% chance to spawn 3 disease spores in front of you after 1 second that deal 1557 Disease damage to any enemy they hit. This effect can occur once every 8 seconds and scales off the higher of your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage.

### Drop Locations
- The head drops from Velidreth in Veteran Cradle of Shadows.
- The shoulders can be obtained from Urgarlag Chief-bane's chest in the Undaunted Enclaves.
