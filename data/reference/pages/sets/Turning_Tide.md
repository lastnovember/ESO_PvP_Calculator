# Online:Turning Tide

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Turning_Tide
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Turning Tide is a dungeon set that drops in Shipwright's Regret. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Dreadsails Style in Heavy Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
4 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
5 items: When you Block, you gain Flowing Water for 10 seconds, causing your next Bash attack to deal 2223 Magic damage to up to 6 enemies in a 5 by 10 meter line, and apply Major Vulnerability for 10 seconds, increasing their damage taken by 10%. This effect can occur once every 15 seconds and scales off your Max Health.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
