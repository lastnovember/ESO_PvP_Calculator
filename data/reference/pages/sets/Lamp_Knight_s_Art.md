# Online:Lamp Knight's Art

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Lamp_Knight's_Art
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Lamp Knight's Art is a Rewards for the Worthy Set. Set pieces are awarded via "Rewards for the Worthy" mails by participating in PvP. It cannot be crafted and does not drop from monsters. Pieces are Aldmeri Dominion, Daggerfall Covenant, or Ebonheart Pact Style in Medium Armor, depending on the player's alliance.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: Gain 100000 Offensive Penetration. Reduce your damage done by 46%.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
