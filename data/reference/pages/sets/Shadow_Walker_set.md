# Online:Shadow Walker (set)

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Shadow_Walker_(set)
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Shadow Walker is an item set found in boxes purchased using Alliance Points from the Bruma Quartermaster, as quest rewards in Bruma, as a reward for completing delves and dolmens throughout Cyrodiil, from completing Board Missions, and from Elite Gear Vendors. It cannot be crafted and does not drop from monsters. Pieces are Aldmeri Dominion, Daggerfall Covenant, or Ebonheart Pact Style in Medium Armor, depending on the player's alliance.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
4 items: Adds 3-129 Stamina Recovery
5 items: While you are Sneaking or invisible and are not moving, heal for 50-2150 Health and restore 50-2150 Stamina every 1 second.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
