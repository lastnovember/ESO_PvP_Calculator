# Online:Wilderqueen's Arch

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Wilderqueen's_Arch
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Wilderqueen's Arch is an overland set that drops in Greenshade. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Wood Elf Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
3 items: Adds 3-129 Stamina Recovery
4 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
5 items: Your Bow attacks reduce the Movement Speed of any enemy they hit by 0-15% and increase your Movement Speed by 0-15% for 3 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as bows only. Unique pieces include:
- Lamiascale Bow of the Wilderqueen, a unique drop from Domina Ssaranth
