# Online:Leeching Plate

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Leeching_Plate
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Leeching Plate is a dungeon set that drops in the Imperial City Prison, as well as being found in the Daedric Shackle Trophy Vault. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Xivkyn style in Heavy Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
3 items: Adds 4% Healing Taken
4 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
5 items: When you take damage, you summon a cloud of leeching poison under the assailant. The cloud deals 427 Poison damage every 1 second for 5 seconds and heals you for 100% of the damage caused. This effect can occur once every 5 seconds and scales off your Max Health.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Grievous Leeching Ward, quest reward for completing Summary Execution. Unlike the generic version, this shield is Daedric style. Drawing this weapon may cause the Soul of Dusk to speak to you.
- Warden's Courage, unique loot from Lord Warden Dusk.
