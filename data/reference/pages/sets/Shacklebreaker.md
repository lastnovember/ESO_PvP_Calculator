# Online:Shacklebreaker

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Shacklebreaker
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Shacklebreaker is one of the craftable sets providing special bonuses to armor and weapons. It is available for crafting to players pursuing blacksmithing, clothing, woodworking, and/or jewelry crafting professions. The special crafting site with stations dedicated to creation of this set are located in the Vvardenfell.

To make an item for this set, a crafter must first complete research on six traits for this particular item at a corresponding crafting station.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Stamina Recovery
3 items: Adds 3-129 Magicka Recovery
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: Adds 48-2065 Maximum Stamina
5 items: Adds 48-2065 Maximum Magicka
