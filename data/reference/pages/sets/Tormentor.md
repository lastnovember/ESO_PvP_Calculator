# Online:Tormentor

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Tormentor
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Tormentor is a dungeon set that drops in the Banished Cells I or II. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Daedric Style in Heavy Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
3 items: Adds 4% Healing Taken
4 items: Adds 4% Healing Taken
5 items: When you deal direct damage with a Blink, Charge, Leap, Teleport, or Pull ability, you gain 84-3642 Physical Resistance and Spell Resistance, taunt the first enemy hit to attack you for 15 seconds, and heal for 45% of the damage done.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Blessing of the Keepers of the Tormentor, a quest reward for Banishing the Banished.
- Cirion's Last Stand of the Tormentor, a quest reward for The Plan.
- Darktarge, unique loot from Shadowrend.
- Keeper's Insignia, unique loot from Keeper Voranil in Banished Cells II.
- Keeper's Medallion, unique loot from Keeper Voranil in Banished Cells II.
- Vertebral Ring, unique loot from the Skeletal Destroyer.
