# Online:Chimera's Rebuke

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Chimera's_Rebuke
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Chimera's Rebuke is one of the craftable sets providing special bonuses to armor and weapons. It is available for crafting to players pursuing blacksmithing, clothing, woodworking, and/or jewelry crafting professions. The special crafting site with stations dedicated to the creation of this set is located at the Fort Avrippe on Galen.

To make an item for this set, a crafter must first complete research on seven traits for that particular item at a corresponding crafting station.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
3 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
4 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
5 items: Dealing damage with a fully-charged Heavy Attack also restores 54-2363 Magicka or Stamina to the three allies nearest your target. The resource restore is based on the type of weapon you are wielding.

### Crafting Site
Fort Avrippe
