Anthelmir's Construct
Anthelmir's Construct is a monster helm set. It consists of two armor pieces: head and shoulder, both of which are available in all three armor weights and standard traits. The set cannot be crafted. There is currently no outfit style based on this armor set.

### Bonuses

1 item: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
2 items: Dealing damage with a fully-charged Heavy Attack throws an axe at the enemy, dealing 1572 Physical damage and reducing their Armor by 400 for 5 seconds. This effect can occur once every 10 seconds and scales off the higher of your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage. The axe drops to the ground after traveling to your target for 5 seconds. Touching the axe reduces the cooldown of this set by 5 seconds.

### Drop Locations
- The head drops from Aradros the Awakened in Veteran Oathsworn Pit.
- The shoulders can be obtained from Urgarlag Chief-bane's reward chest at Undaunted Enclaves.
