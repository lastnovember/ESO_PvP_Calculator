# Online:Green Pact

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Green_Pact
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Green Pact is an overland set that drops in Grahtwood. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Wood Elf Style in Heavy Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
3 items: Adds 3-129 Health Recovery
4 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
5 items: While you have a food buff active, your Max Health is increased by 58-2500 and Health Recovery by 8-356.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Time Buckler of the Green Pact, a unique drop from Lieutenant Khari.
- Spiderstaff of the Green Pact, a unique drop from Spider Queen.
- Nindaeril's Tooth of the Green Pact, a unique drop from Nindaeril the Monsoon.
- Valanir's Lost Hope of the Green Pact, a unique drop from Valanir the Restless.
- Ukaezai's Scalpel, a quest reward for Heart of the Matter.
