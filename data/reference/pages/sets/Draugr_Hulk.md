# Online:Draugr Hulk

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Draugr_Hulk
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Draugr Hulk is a dungeon set that drops in Direfrost Keep. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Draugr Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
4 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
5 items: Adds 59-2550 Maximum Stamina

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Dreadlord's Mark, unique loot from Drodda's Dreadlord.
- Drodda's Withering Edge, unique loot from Drodda of Icereach.
