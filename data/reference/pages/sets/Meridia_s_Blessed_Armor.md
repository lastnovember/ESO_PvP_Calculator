# Online:Meridia's Blessed Armor

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Meridia's_Blessed_Armor
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Meridia's Blessed Armor is an overland set that drops in Coldharbour. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Ancient Elf Style in Heavy Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 34-1487 Armor
3 items: Adds 3-129 Stamina Recovery
4 items: Adds 34-1487 Armor
5 items: When you Block, you gain Meridia's Blessed Armor for 5 seconds, causing you to dodge all incoming attacks. This effect can occur once every 25 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Arbiter's Vambraces, the quest reward of Hall of Judgment in the Daedric Style.
- Ayleid's Hope, the quest reward of The Army of Meridia.
- Ayleids' Coat of Memories, the quest reward of Light from the Darkness in the High Elf Style.
- Bulwark of the Citadel, the quest reward of The Citadel Must Fall in the Daedric Style.
- Brolsgerbwd's Bone Staff of Meridia, a unique drop from the world boss Brolsgerbwd at Risen Court in the Daedric Style.
- Mal Sorra's Curse of Meridia, a unique drop from Mal Sorra, the final boss of Mal Sorra's Tomb.
- Meridia's Charm of the Dawn, the quest reward of Cadwell's Silver.
- Meridia's Emblem of Searing Light, the quest reward of Cadwell's Gold.
- Molag Grunda's Girdle, the quest reward of Crossing the Chasm in the Daedric Style.
- Nolagha's Scale Leggings of Meridia, a unique drop from the world bosses Keggahiha, Nolagha and Rsolignah at Daedroth Larder in the Daedric Style.
- Skordo's Stalwart, the quest reward of Truth, Lies, and Prisoners in the Orc Style.
- Hide Shield of Meridia, a unique drop from the world boss Sthorha the Crazed at Aba-Darre in the Daedric Style.
- The Observer's Battle Helm, the quest reward of A Bargain With Shadows, A Thorn in Your Side and The Will of the Worm in the Daedric Style.
- The Observer's Battle Boots, the quest reward of The Endless War in the Daedric Style.
