# Online:Undaunted Bastion

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Undaunted_Bastion
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Undaunted Bastion is a dungeon set that drops in Elden Hollow I and II. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Mercenary Style in Heavy Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
3 items: Adds 34-1487 Armor
4 items: Adds 34-1487 Armor
5 items: When you use a taunt ability while you are under 75% Health, gain a damage shield that absorbs 328-14127 damage for 6 seconds. This effect can occur once every 10 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Akash's First Love, unique loot from Akash gra-Mal.
- Bogdan's Flame, unique loot from Bogdan the Nightflame.
- Daedroth Tooth Necklace, unique loot from Dubroze the Infester.
- Oraneth's Lost Jewels, unique loot from The Shadow Guard.
- Pendant of Vines, unique loot from Leafseether.
- The Thalmor Gavel, unique loot from Canonreeve Oraneth.
