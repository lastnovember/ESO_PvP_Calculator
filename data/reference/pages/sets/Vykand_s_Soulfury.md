# Online:Vykand's Soulfury

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Vykand's_Soulfury
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Vykand's Soulfury is a dungeon set that drops in Black Gem Foundry. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Black Soul Gem Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
3 items: Adds 34-1487 Offensive Penetration
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: While in combat, completing a fully-charged Heavy Attack grants you either Major Force, Major Berserk, or Major Courage for 12 seconds, increasing your Critical Damage done by 20%, your damage done by 10%, or your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage by 430. This effect can occur once every 9 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
