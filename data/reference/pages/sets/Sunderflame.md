# Online:Sunderflame

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Sunderflame
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Sunderflame is a dungeon set that drops in the City of Ash I and II. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Daedric Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
3 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
4 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
5 items: When you deal damage with a fully-charged Heavy Attack, you deal an additional 1925 Flame damage over 10 seconds and apply Minor Breach to the enemy, reducing their Physical Resistance and Spell Resistance by 2974. This effect scales off the higher of your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry pieces in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Bow of the Deadlands, unique loot from Razor Master Erthas.
- Ember Eye, unique loot from the Dark Ember.
- Erthas' Sunderflame Bow, a quest reward for Return to Ash.
- Grove-Watcher's Sunderflame Brigandine, a quest reward for Razor's Edge.
- Skoria's Charred Claymore, unique loot from Valkyn Skoria.
- Splitting Axe of the Titan, unique loot from the Ash Titan.
- Urata's Mask of Mockery, unique loot from Urata the Legion.
- Wildmaul, unique loot from the Infernal Guardian.
