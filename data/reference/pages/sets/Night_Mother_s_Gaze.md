# Online:Night Mother's Gaze

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Night_Mother's_Gaze
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Night Mother's Gaze is one of the craftable sets providing special bonuses to armor and weapons. It is available for crafting to players pursuing blacksmithing, clothing, woodworking, and/or jewelry crafting professions. One special crafting site with stations dedicated to creation of this set is located in the last zone of each of the three Alliances.

To make an item for this set, a crafter must first complete research on six traits for this particular item at a corresponding crafting station.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
3 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
4 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
5 items: When you deal Critical Damage you apply Major Breach to the enemy, reducing their Physical Resistance and Spell Resistance by 5948 for 4 seconds.

### Notes
- Not to be confused with the overland set Night Mother's Embrace. Items of both sets used to have the same naming convention, " of the Night Mother", but pieces of the Night Mother's Embrace set have since been renamed to " of Mother's Embrace" to prevent confusion.
