# Online:Destructive Mage

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Destructive_Mage
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Destructive Mage is a trial set which drops in the Hel Ra Citadel. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Celestial Style in Light Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
3 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: When you deal damage with a fully-charged Heavy Attack, you mark the enemy with unstable energies for 10 seconds. If your mark was already active before applying, it instead detonates, dealing 1071 Magic damage all enemies within 8 meters. This effect can occur once every 3 seconds and scales off the higher of your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
