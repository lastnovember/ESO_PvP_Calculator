# Online:Flanking Strategist

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Flanking_Strategist
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Flanking Strategist is an overland set that drops in the Gold Coast. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Order of the Hour Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
3 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: Adds 34-400 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage to your damaging abilities when you attack an enemy from behind or their sides.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
