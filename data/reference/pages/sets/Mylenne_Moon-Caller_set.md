Mylenne Moon-Caller
Mylenne Moon-Caller is a monster helm set. It consists of two armor pieces: head and shoulder, both of which are available in all three armor weights and standard traits. The set cannot be crafted.

### Bonuses

1 item: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
2 items: Dealing direct damage with a Class ability or Werewolf attack deals 911 Bleed damage to enemies within 8 meters of the target. You restore 10% of the damage done as Stamina. This effect can occur once every 10 seconds, scales off the higher of your Weapon Damage or Spell Damage, and deals up to 100% more damage to monsters based on how full your Stamina is.

While in Werewolf form you also gain 15 Fury and 1 Blood Hunger.

### Drop Locations
- Parts can drop in the Moon Hunter Keep Solo Dungeon. The chance depends on the difficulty of a run.
- Also can be obtained from a Curated Mylenne Moon-Caller Coffer purchased from Veya Thenim at the Undaunted Enclaves for 300.
