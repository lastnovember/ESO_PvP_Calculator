# Online:Sheer Venom

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Sheer_Venom
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Sheer Venom is a dungeon set that drops in the Imperial City Prison, as well as being found in the Daedric Shackle Trophy Vault. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Xivkyn Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
3 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: When you deal damage with an Execute ability you infect the enemy, dealing 948 Poison damage over 6 seconds and dealing up to 100% more damage to enemies under 100% Health. This effect can occur once every 6 seconds per target and scales off the higher of your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Toxic Cleaver, unique loot from Ibomez the Flesh Sculptor.
- The Vigilant One, unique loot from the Gravelight Sentry.
