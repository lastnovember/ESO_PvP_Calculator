This page lists item sets that were removed from the Elder Scrolls Online.

## Removed Sets
Treasures of the Earthforge

## Altered Sets
The following sets used to only drop in specific pieces, but that is no longer the case. Instead of being named for the character's alliance, they now use a common name and come in the style of the character's alliance. The deprecated drops are listed below:

### Blessing of the Potentates
Aldmeri Dominion | Daggerfall Covenant | Ebonheart Pact

|
- Dominion's Choker
- Dominion's Shield
- Dominion's Sword
|
- Covenant's Choker
- Covenant's Shield
- Covenant's Sword
|
- Pact's Choker
- Pact's Shield
- Pact's Sword
