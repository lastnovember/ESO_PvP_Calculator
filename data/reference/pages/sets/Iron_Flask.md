# Online:Iron Flask

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Iron_Flask
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Iron Flask is one of the craftable sets providing special bonuses to armor and weapons. It is available for crafting to players pursuing blacksmithing, clothing, woodworking, and/or jewelry crafting professions. The special crafting site with stations dedicated to creation of this set are located at Forgotten Feretory in FargraveThe Deadlands.

To make an item for this set, a crafter must first complete research on five traits for this particular item at a corresponding crafting station.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
3 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
4 items: Adds 34-1487 Armor
5 items: When you drink a potion while in combat, gain 7779 Armor for 20 seconds.

### Crafting Site
Forgotten Feretory
