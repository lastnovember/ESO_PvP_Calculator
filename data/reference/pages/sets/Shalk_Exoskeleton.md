# Online:Shalk Exoskeleton

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Shalk_Exoskeleton
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Shalk Exoskeleton is an overland set that drops in Stonefalls. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Barbaric Style in Heavy Armor. It is named after the shalk, a fiery insectoid native to the region.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
3 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
4 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
5 items: Adds 3-171 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: Gain Minor Heroism at all times while you are in combat, generating 1 Ultimate every 1.5 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Alexandra's Froststaff, a quest reward for The Coral Heart
- Ancient Warden's Greaves, a quest reward for Rending Flames
- Ashlander's Girdle, a quest for Savages of Stonefalls
- Dagon Morag, a quest reward for The Death of Balreth
- Gauntlets of the Akaviri War, a quest reward for Cleansing the Past
- Guarblood Ring, a quest for Recovering the Guar
- House Cousin's Helm, a quest for Saving the Son
- Kwama Cuttle Shield, a quest reward for Percussive Ranching
- Lionbanner Chestplate, a quest reward for The General's Demise
- Pauldrons of the Holdfast, a quest for Kinsman's Revenge
- Rebellion Mace, a quest for A Story Told in Footprints
- Scampstamper Sabatons, a quest reward for Close the Scamp Caves
- Strifeswarm Legplates of the Shalk, a unique drop from the Strifeswarm Champion found at the Strifeswarm Hive
- Guardian's Plate-Helm of the Shalk, a unique drop from Zozuzetharus found at the Inner Sea Armature
