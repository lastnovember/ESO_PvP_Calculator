# Online:Spriggan's Thorns

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Spriggan's_Thorns
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Spriggan's Thorns is an overland set that drops in Bangkorai. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Wood Elf Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: Adds 80-3460 Offensive Penetration

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Anexiel's Thorax - reward for completing A Marriage in Ruins in the Redguard Style.
- Chausses of the Cured - reward for completing Freedom's Chains in the Breton Style.
- Foe-Smasher - reward for completing The Charge of Evermore in the Breton Style.
- Hircine's Boon - reward for completing The Heart of the Beast.
- Sentinel's Longbow - reward for completing The Will of the Woods.
- Spriggan's Bark-Skinned Gages - drops from Woodblight.
- Spriggan's Centurion Breaker - drops from Klathzgar's Centurion in the Redguard Style.
- Spriggan's Thief-Lord's Slippers - drops from Ulbazar Thief-Lord in the Redguard Style.
- Spriggan's Wolf-Jaw - drops from Garach Wolf-Father in the Nord Style.
- Stoneskin Gloves - reward for completing Tongues of Stone in the Redguard Style.
- The Scarab's Sting - reward for completing Scavenging for a Scarab in the Redguard Style.
