# Online:Dragonguard Elite

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Dragonguard_Elite
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Dragonguard Elite is an overland set found in Southern Elsweyr. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Dragonguard Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
3 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
4 items: Adds 3-129 Stamina Recovery
5 items: You gain a stack of Dragonguard Tactics for 5 seconds when you deal direct damage to an enemy from the front or their sides, up to 10 stacks. Each stack of Dragonguard Tactics increases your Critical Chance by 6-275. You can only gain one stack every 0.5 seconds.
