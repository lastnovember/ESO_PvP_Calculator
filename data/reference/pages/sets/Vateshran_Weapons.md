# Online:Vateshran Weapons

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Vateshran_Weapons
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Vateshran Weapons are special weapon sets that drop in the Vateshran Hollows solo arena.

## Types
Vateshran weapons are available in two types - normal and perfected. Perfected versions of weapons are slightly more powerful, by virtue of bearing extra stats.

- Normal weapons drop from the last boss of the arena on normal difficulty.
- The Perfected versions of these weapons only drop in veteran or can be awarded to players who successfully rank on the weekly leaderboard.
