Archdruid Devyric

Archdruid Devyric is a monster helm set. It consists of two armor pieces: head and shoulder, both of which are available in all three armor weights and standard traits. The set cannot be crafted.

### Bonuses

1 item: Adds 34-1487 Offensive Penetration
2 items: When you deal damage with a Heavy Attack, after 1.2 seconds deal 650 Shock damage to up to 6 enemies within a 7 by 5 meter line on your target, applying Major Vulnerability to them for 7 seconds, increasing the damage enemies take by 10%. This effect can occur once every 15 seconds and scales off the higher of your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage.

### Drop Locations
- The head drops from Archdruid Devyric in Veteran Earthen Root Enclave.
- The shoulders can be obtained from Urgarlag Chief-bane's reward chest at Undaunted Enclaves.
