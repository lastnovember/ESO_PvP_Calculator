# Online:Dagon's Dominion

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Dagon's_Dominion
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Dagon's Dominion is a dungeon set found in The Cauldron. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Waking Flame style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: Adds 11-492 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage to your area of effect abilities.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
