# Online:Perfected Vrol's Command

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Perfected_Vrol's_Command
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Perfected Vrol's Command is a trial set which drops in veteran Kyne's Aegis. Pieces are Sea Giant Style in Heavy Armor. The non-perfected version of this set can be looted on normal difficulty.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
3 items: Gain Minor Aegis at all times, reducing your damage taken from Dungeon, Trial, and Arena Monsters by 5%.
4 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
5 perfected items: Adds 4% Healing Taken
5 items: After completing a fully-charged Heavy Attack, apply Major Aegis to you and up to 11 nearby group members for 12 seconds, reducing damage taken from Dungeon, Trial, and Arena enemies by 10%. This effect can occur once every 21 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
