# Online:Stuhn's Favor

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Stuhn's_Favor
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Stuhn's Favor is one of the craftable sets providing special bonuses to armor and weapons. It is available for crafting to players pursuing blacksmithing, clothing, woodworking, and/or jewelry crafting professions. The special crafting site with stations dedicated to creation of this set are located in Western Skyrim.

To make an item for this set, a crafter must first complete research on five traits for this particular item at a corresponding crafting station.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
3 items: Adds 34-1487 Offensive Penetration
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: When you deal damage to an enemy who is Off Balance, your Offensive Penetration is increased by 123-5312 for 10 seconds.

### Crafting Site
Hunter's House
