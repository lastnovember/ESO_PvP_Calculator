# Online:Sentry (set)

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Sentry_(set)
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Sentry is an item set found in boxes purchased using Alliance Points from the Bruma Quartermaster, as quest rewards in Bruma, as a reward for completing delves and dolmens throughout Cyrodiil, from completing Board Missions, and from Elite Gear Vendors. It cannot be crafted and does not drop from monsters. Pieces are Aldmeri Dominion, Daggerfall Covenant, or Ebonheart Pact Style in Medium Armor, depending on the player's alliance.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 9-424 Critical Resistance
3 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
4 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
5 items: Adds 6-266 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: When you begin to crouch, gain Stealth Detection for 10 seconds. This effect can only be activated every 30 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
