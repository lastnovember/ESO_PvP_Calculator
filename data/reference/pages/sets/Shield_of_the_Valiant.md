# Online:Shield of the Valiant

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Shield_of_the_Valiant
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Shield of the Valiant is an item set found in boxes purchased using Alliance Points from the Bruma Quartermaster, as quest rewards in Bruma, as a reward for completing delves and dolmens throughout Cyrodiil, from completing Board Missions, and from Elite Gear Vendors. It cannot be crafted and does not drop from monsters. Pieces are Aldmeri Dominion, Daggerfall Covenant, or Ebonheart Pact Style in Medium Armor, depending on the player's alliance.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
3 items: Adds 4% Healing Taken
4 items: Reduces your damage taken from Players by 3%.
5 items: Reduces the cost of Break Free by 40%.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
