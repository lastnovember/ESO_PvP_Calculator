# Online:Eagle Eye

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Eagle_Eye
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Eagle Eye is an item set found in boxes purchased using Alliance Points from the Bruma Quartermaster, as quest rewards in Bruma, as a reward for completing delves and dolmens throughout Cyrodiil, from completing Board Missions, and from Elite Gear Vendors. It cannot be crafted and does not drop from monsters. Pieces are Aldmeri Dominion, Daggerfall Covenant, or Ebonheart Pact Style, depending on the player's alliance.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
3 items: Adds 38-1652 Critical Chance to your ranged direct damage abilities.

### Pieces
The set consists of a bow, staves, and jewelry in all slots.
