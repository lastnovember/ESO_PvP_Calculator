# Online:Chokethorn (set)

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Chokethorn_(set)
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Chokethorn

Chokethorn is a monster helm set. It consists of two armor pieces: head and shoulder, both of which are available in all three armor weights and standard traits. The set cannot be crafted. There is an outfit style based on this armor set. There is also an opal outfit style based on this armor set.

### Bonuses

1 item: Adds 3-129 Magicka Recovery
2 items: When you use a heal ability with a cost, you have a 33% chance to summon a strangler sapling that heals you or an ally for 6986 Health over 6 seconds. This effect can occur once every 10 seconds and scales off the higher of your Max Magicka or Stamina.

### Drop Locations
- The head drops from Canonreeve Oraneth in Veteran Elden Hollow I.
- The shoulders can be obtained from Maj al-Ragath's reward chest at Undaunted Enclaves.

## Notes
- The set calls them "Strangler Sapling" but they actually use the "Chokethorn" name.
