# Online:Stendarr's Embrace

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Stendarr's_Embrace
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Stendarr's Embrace is an overland set that drops in Eastmarch. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Wood Elf Style in Light Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Magicka Recovery
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
4 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
5 items: When you heal yourself or an ally, you remove all negative effects from them. This effect can occur once every 30 seconds per target.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Aleeto's Alchemy Gloves, the quest reward for Alchemical Analysis in the Argonian Style.
- Bathrobe of Vigor, the quest reward for Bath Time in the Nord Style.
- Burial Shawl of the Brief Queen, the quest reward for Snow and Flame in the Nord Style.
- Chill Hollow Pendant of Stendarr, a unique loot from Nomeg Chal.
- Dreamwalker Staff, the quest reward for Eternal Slumber in the Nord Style.
- Icehammer's Frigid Maul of Stendarr, a unique loot from Thane Icehammer in the Nord Style.
- Rageclaw's Collar of Stendarr, a unique loot from Rageclaw.
- Snow Bear's Fang of Stendarr, a unique loot from Beralagr.
- Sonya's Bridal Veil, the quest reward for Torn Asunder in the Nord Style.
- Staff of Nature's Accord, the quest reward for Nature's Accord in the Nord Style.
- Steady-Hand's Shot Brace, the quest reward for Best of the Best in the Argonian Style.
- Thane Watch, the quest reward for One Victor, One King in the Nord Style.
- Voljar's Meadery Seal, the quest reward for A Friend in Mead.
- Voljar Meadery Trousers, the quest reward for A Brother's Revenge in the Nord Style.
- Wolf-Sister Sandals, the quest reward for Lifeline in the Nord Style.
