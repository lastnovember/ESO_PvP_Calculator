# Online:Storm Master

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Storm_Master
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Storm Master is a dungeon set that drops in Tempest Island. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Ancient Elf Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
3 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: When you deal Critical Damage with a fully-charged Heavy Attack, your Light and Heavy Attacks deal an additional 35-1542 damage to monsters for 8 seconds. This effect can occur once every 5 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique items include:
- Windwhisper, unique loot from Stormreeve Neidir.
- Raining Hammer, unique loot from Stormfist.
- Storm Master's Leggings of the Lamia-Slayer, a quest reward for Eye of the Storm.

### Notes
- You actually get the buff when the game knows your heavy-attack is critical and not when you deal critical damage with it, therefore it buffs this heavy attack before it strikes. This is easily seen if you strike an enemy when you are hidden as it will always critically strike.
- This set uses the following icon for its buff: Shock Melee
