# Online:Tracker's Lash

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Tracker's_Lash
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Tracker's Lash is a Rewards for the Worthy Set. Set pieces are awarded via "Rewards for the Worthy" mails by participating in PvP. It cannot be crafted and does not drop from monsters. Pieces are Aldmeri Dominion, Daggerfall Covenant, or Ebonheart Pact Style in Light Armor, depending on the player's alliance.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
3 items: Adds 34-1487 Offensive Penetration
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: When your attack is dodged, increase your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage by 95 for 7 seconds, stacking up to 5 times. This effect can occur once every 0.5 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
