# Online:Radiant Bastion

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Radiant_Bastion
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Radiant Bastion is an overland set found in The Reach. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Arkthzand Armory Style in Heavy Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
3 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
4 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
5 items: Deal 10% of all direct damage done to you back to the attacker, up to 4250 damage.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
