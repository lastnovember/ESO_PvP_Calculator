# Online:Wizard's Riposte

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Wizard's_Riposte
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Wizard's Riposte is an Battlegrounds Set rewarded by Battlegrounds. It can be obtained in boxes purchased using Alliance Points from the Cropsford Quartermaster. It cannot be crafted and does not drop from monsters. Pieces are Militant Ordinator Style in Light Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
3 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
4 items: Adds 9-424 Critical Resistance
5 items: Adds 15-660 Critical Resistance
5 items: When you take direct Critical Damage, you apply Minor Enervation to the enemy for 5 seconds, reducing their Critical Damage done by 10%.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
