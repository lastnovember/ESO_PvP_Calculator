# Online:Swamp Raider

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Swamp_Raider
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Swamp Raider is an overland set that drops in Shadowfen. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Argonian Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: Adds 13-600 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage to your Poison and Disease Damage abilities.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Swamp Raider's Bile-Soaked Boots, a unique drop from Stormscale
- Swamp Raider's Boneaxe, a unique drop from Captain Bones
- Swamp Raider's Crocodile Skin, a unique drop from Urrai
- Swamp Raider's Fang of Haynekhtnamet, a unique drop from Haynekhtnamet
- Stormgrinder's Dagger, the quest reward for Schism
- Wispcutter, the quest reward for Broken Apart
- Sailcutter Dagger, the quest reward for A Pirate Parley
- Histwood Bow, the quest reward for Scales of Retribution
- Cursed Fang of Sithis, the quest reward for Pull the Last Fang
