# Online:Perfected Caustic Arrow

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Perfected_Caustic_Arrow
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Perfected Caustic Arrow is a Perfected Master's Weapons item set found in veteran Dragonstar Arena. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are in the unobtainable tier 5 Wood ElfStyle. The non-perfected version of this set can be looted on normal difficulty.

### Bonuses

2 perfected items: Adds 2-103 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
2 items: Increases your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage by 7-330 against targets affected by your Poison Arrow.

### Pieces
The set consists of a Bow.

### Notes
- While the description states that 2 items are required, this is because bows are 2-handed weapons, and thus count as 2 items by themselves, so only 1 item is actually needed.
