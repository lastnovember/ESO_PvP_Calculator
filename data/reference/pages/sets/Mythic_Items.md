# Online:Mythic Items

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Mythic_Items
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Mythic Items are special one-piece sets that can only be obtained through the Antiquities system and if you have access to the related DLC. Only one mythic item can be worn at a time, but they each grant powerful and unique bonuses.

All mythic items are broken up into five fragments which each need to be found to create the final piece. All fragments require a legendary-quality Lead to scry, and are classed as Master difficulty to excavate. You are required to be at least level 7 in Scrying, and have the Antiquarian Insight IV passive to scry Master difficulty leads.

Additional DLCs or Chapters may be required (including the DLC or Chapter the Mythic item was released alongside) to obtain each fragment. For example, the Ring of the Pale Order was released with the Markarth DLC, but it also requires access to the Orsinium DLC to find all fragment Leads. In addition, the Greymoor Chapter is also required to Scry these Leads via the Scrying skill line. The table below shows the DLCs required to complete a particular Mythic item.
