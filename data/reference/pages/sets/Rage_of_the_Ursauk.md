# Online:Rage of the Ursauk

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Rage_of_the_Ursauk
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Rage of the Ursauk is a dungeon set that drops in Earthen Root Enclave. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Y'ffre's Will Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 34-1487 Offensive Penetration
3 items: Adds 34-1487 Offensive Penetration
4 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
5 items: When you deal damage with a Heavy Attack, create a 12 meter area for 12 seconds that grants Empower to group members inside, increasing the damage of their Heavy Attacks against monsters by 150%. This effect can occur once every 8 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
