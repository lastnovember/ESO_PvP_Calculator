Mighty Chudan

Mighty Chudan is a monster helm set. It consists of two armor pieces: head and shoulder, both of which are available in all three armor weights and standard traits. The set cannot be crafted. There is an outfit style based on this armor set.

### Bonuses

1 item: Adds 34-1487 Armor
2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
2 items: Gain Major Resolve at all times, increasing your Physical Resistance and Spell Resistance by 5948.

### Drop Locations
- The head drops from Tree-Minder Na-Kesh in Veteran Ruins of Mazzatun.
- The shoulders can be obtained from Urgarlag Chief-bane's chest in the undaunted enclave.
