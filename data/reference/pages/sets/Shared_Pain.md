# Online:Shared Pain

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Shared_Pain
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Shared Pain is a Rewards for the Worthy Set. Set pieces are awarded via "Rewards for the Worthy" mails by participating in PvP. It cannot be crafted and does not drop from monsters. Pieces are Aldmeri Dominion, Daggerfall Covenant, or Ebonheart Pact Style in Medium Armor, depending on the player's alliance.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 9-424 Critical Resistance
3 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: Whenever you are dealt damage from a damage over time ability, you deal 139 Magic damage to the attacker. This effect scales off the higher of your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage. Reduce your damage taken from over time abilities by 8%.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
