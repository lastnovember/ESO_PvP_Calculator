# Online:Bulwark Ruination

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Bulwark_Ruination
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Bulwark Ruination is a Battlegrounds Set rewarded by Battlegrounds. It cannot be crafted and does not drop from monsters. Pieces are Militant Ordinator Style in Light Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
3 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: When an enemy blocks one of your attacks, reduce the amount of damage they can block by 8% for 5 seconds. This effect can stack up to 5 times and can occur once every 0.3 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
