# Online:Nikulas' Heavy Armor

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Nikulas'_Heavy_Armor
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Nikulas' Heavy Armor is a dungeon set that drops in the Blessed Crucible. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Nord Style in Heavy Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 34-1487 Armor
3 items: Adds 34-1487 Armor
4 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
5 items: When you Block, you gain a stack of Nikulas' Resolve for 5 seconds, up to once every half second. After 8 stacks you consume Nikulas' Resolve and deflect the next direct damaging attack made against you within 5 seconds, avoiding it completely.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Necklace of Giant Toes, unique loot from Grunt the Clever.
- Wolfpack Staff, unique loot from Dynus Aralas, Kayd at-Sal, Nusana, and Snagg gro-Mashul.
