# Online:Warrior-Poet

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Warrior-Poet
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Warrior-Poet is an overland set that drops on Vvardenfell. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Redoran Style in Heavy Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
3 items: Adds 34-1487 Armor
4 items: Adds 34-1487 Armor
5 items: Adds 37-1600 Maximum Health
5 items: Gain Minor Toughness at all times, increasing your Max Health by 10%.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Blade of the Warrior-Poet, a quest reward for Divine Restoration
- Breastplate of the Warrior-Poet, a quest reward for Divine Blessings
- Greaves of the Warrior-Poet, a quest reward for Divine Delusions
- Pauldrons of the Warrior-Poet, a quest reward for Ancestral Adversity
- Star-Shell Dagger, a quest reward for Bound by Love
