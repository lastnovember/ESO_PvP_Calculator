# Online:Silks of the Sun

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Silks_of_the_Sun
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Silks of the Sun is an overland set that drops in Stonefalls. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Breton Style in Light Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: Adds 9-400 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage to your Flame Damage abilities.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Ancestor's Kilt of the Sun, a unique drop from the Ulath Ancestor
- Apprentice's Leggings, the quest reward for The Wizard's Tome
- Ashwalker's Boots, the quest reward for Desperate Souls
- Boltstaff of Koal Canton, the quest reward for This One's a Classic
- Indoril Pyrostaff, the quest reward for City Under Siege
- Lionbanner Buckler of the Sun, a unique drop from Dugrul
- Mota Fang, the quest reward for A Storm Broken
- Murkreed Mantle, the quest reward for Enlightenment Needs Salt
- Oodegu's Flamestick of the Sun, a unique drop from Oodegu
- Redoran Archmage's Robes, the quest reward for Restoring Order
- Sedura Morag, the quest reward for Sadal's Final Defeat
- Shadowsilk Headwrap of the Sun, a unique drop from Gozzak
- Sigil of House Dres, the quest reward for Vengeance for House Dres
- Sommelier's Gloves, the quest reward for Waylaid Wine Merchant
- Spiritmender's Staff, the quest reward for Enslaved in Death
- Telvanni Spellhood, the quest reward for What Was Done Must Be Undone
- Telvanni Warsash, the quest reward for The Brothers Will Rise
