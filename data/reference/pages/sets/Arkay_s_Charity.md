# Online:Arkay's Charity

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Arkay's_Charity
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Arkay's Charity is a Rewards for the Worthy Set. Set pieces are awarded via "Rewards for the Worthy" mails by participating in PvP. It cannot be crafted and does not drop from monsters. Pieces are Aldmeri Dominion, Daggerfall Covenant, or Ebonheart Pact Style in Light Armor, depending on the player's alliance.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Magicka Recovery
3 items: Adds 3-129 Magicka Recovery
4 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
5 items: When you cleanse a negative effect from yourself or an ally, restore 13 Ultimate to the target. This effect can occur once every 9 seconds per target.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
