# Online:Plague Slinger

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Plague_Slinger
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Plague Slinger is a dungeon set that drops in Scalecaller Peak. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Scalecaller Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
3 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: When you take damage, summon a Skeever corpse which will launch five poison balls over 5 seconds that each deal 737 Poison damage to the nearest enemy within 10 meters. This effect can occur once every 8 seconds and scales off the higher of your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
