# Online:Vestments of the Warlock

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Vestments_of_the_Warlock
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Vestments of the Warlock is a dungeon set that drops in Selene's Web. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Breton style in Light Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Magicka Recovery
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
4 items: Adds 3-129 Magicka Recovery
5 items: When you cast an ability that costs resources while under 25% Magicka, you restore 263-11350 Magicka. This effect can occur once every 45 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Foulhide's Fang, unique loot from Foulhide.
- Selene's Scepter, unique loot from Selene.
- Treethane's Sigil, unique loot from Treethane Kerninn.
