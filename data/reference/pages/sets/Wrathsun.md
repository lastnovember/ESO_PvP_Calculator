# Online:Wrathsun

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Wrathsun
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Wrathsun is a Class set found in the Infinite Archive. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Wrathsun Style in all weights. It buffs the Dawn's Wrath abilities for Templars.

### Bonuses

Class: Templar
2 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
3 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: When you deal damage with a Dawn's Wrath ability, you gain a stack of Sunlight for 15 seconds, once per attack and up to 30 times. Each stack grants 21 Magicka Recovery. When at max stacks, stacks no longer refresh, your Templar skill line abilities deal 25% bonus damage but is reduced to 10% against players, and a Wrathful Nova is cast on the enemy. When Sunlight expires at max stacks, you lose 50% of your current Magicka.

### Pieces
The set consists of armor of all weights and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
