# Online:Para Bellum

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Para_Bellum
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Para Bellum is a set which drops in the Maelstrom Arena. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Trinimac Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
3 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
4 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
5 items: When you deal damage you gain a damage shield that absorbs up to 195-8412 damage for 6 seconds. This effect can occur once every 6 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
