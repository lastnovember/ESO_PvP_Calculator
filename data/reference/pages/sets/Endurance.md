# Online:Endurance

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Endurance
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Endurance is a jewelry set found in Imperial City Treasure Vaults and some random group dungeons. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Xivkyn Style.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 44-1928 Maximum Health
3 items: Adds 14-618 Health Recovery

### Pieces
The set consists of jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Grievous Ward, originally a quest reward for completing Summary Execution but subsequently replaced by a Leeching Plate shield in later patches. Unlike the generic version, this shield is Daedric style. Drawing this weapon may cause the Soul of Dusk to speak to you.

### Drop Locations
- The final boss of any group dungeon, provided all prior bosses and minibosses have been defeated. The item scales to the player's level. No longer true?
- Opening the reward chests within the Legionary or Planar Armor trophy vaults within the Imperial City. The item will scale to your level.
- As a possible Daily Dungeon reward, using the Group Finder tool. (Scales to player's level at the time of earning, not when opened)
