Thousand Eyes is a monster mask set found in the Night Market. It cannot be crafted. The set is available in all armor weights, and it has its own unique outfit style.

### Bonuses

1 item: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
2 items: Dealing direct critical damage applies Shived to the enemy, dealing 1079 Bleed damage and increasing their damage taken from you by 2% for 15 seconds. This damage scales off the higher of your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage. When the enemy dies, Shived is reapplied to the lowest health enemy within 12 meters of the corpse and increases the damage taken by 2%, up to 8%. You may only have 1 enemy with Shived.

### Pieces
The set consists of two pieces, shoulder and head armor.

### Drop Locations
- Head and shoulder pieces are found in reward boxes for participating in the Night Market faction competition if you sided with the Thousand Eyes.
- Curated Thousand Eyes Coffer purchased from Fennel will contain a shoulder piece with a random trait (other than Nirnhorned).
- Possible loot from the Opulent Ordeal trial.plain
