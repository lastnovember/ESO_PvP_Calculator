# Online:Domihaus

Domihaus the Bloody-Horned

Domihaus is a monster helm set. It consists of two armor pieces: head and shoulder, both of which are available in all three armor weights and standard traits. The set cannot be crafted. There is an outfit style based on this armor set.

### Bonuses

1 item: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
1 item: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
2 items: When you deal damage, you create a 10 meter ring of molten earth around you for 10 seconds, which deals 90 Flame damage and 90 Physical damage every 1 second. Standing within the ring grants you 6-300 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage. This effect can occur once every 15 seconds and the damage scales off the higher of your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage.

### Drop Locations
- The head drops from the Domihaus the Bloody-Horned in Veteran Falkreath Hold.
- The shoulders can be obtained from Urgarlag Chief-bane's reward chest at Undaunted Enclaves.

## Notes
- This set deals damage to enemies only along the outer 3 meters of the ring.
