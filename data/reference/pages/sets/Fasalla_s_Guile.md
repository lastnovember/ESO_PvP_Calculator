# Online:Fasalla's Guile

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Fasalla's_Guile
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Fasalla's Guile is an Elite Gear Set that was formerly available from "Rewards for the Worthy" mails, earned by participating in PvP, prior to Update 39. It can now be obtained in boxes purchased using Alliance Points from the Vlastarus Quartermaster, as quest rewards in Vlastarus, as a reward for completing delves and dolmens throughout Cyrodiil, from completing Board Missions, and from Elite Gear Vendors. It cannot be crafted and does not drop from monsters. Pieces are Aldmeri Dominion, Daggerfall Covenant, or Ebonheart Pact Style in Heavy Armor. depending on the player's alliance.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
3 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
4 items: Adds 4% Healing Taken
5 items: Adds 34-1487 Armor
5 items: While in combat, every 1 second you apply Minor Defile to enemies within 5 meters of you for 2 seconds, reducing their healing received and damage shield strength by 6%.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.

### Notes
- Before Update 39, this set was in Abah's Watch Style.
