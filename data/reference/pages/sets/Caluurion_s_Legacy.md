# Online:Caluurion's Legacy

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Caluurion's_Legacy
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Caluurion's Legacy is a dungeon set that drops in Fang Lair. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Fang Lair Style in Light Armor. When the effect activates with 5 pieces equipped, Caluurion's laughter can be faintly heard.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
3 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
4 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
5 items: When you deal Critical Damage with a Light or Heavy Attack, you launch a projectile at your target that deals 894 Flame, 894 Frost, 894 Shock, or 894 Disease damage and applies the respective status effect. This effect can occur once every 5 seconds and scales off the higher of your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
