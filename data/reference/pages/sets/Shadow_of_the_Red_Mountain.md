# Online:Shadow of the Red Mountain

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Shadow_of_the_Red_Mountain
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Shadow of the Red Mountain is an overland set that drops in Stonefalls. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are in the original Ashlander Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
4 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
5 items: When you deal damage with a weapon, you spawn a volcano that erupts after 1 second, launching liquid hot lava at the closest enemy dealing 1860 Flame damage. This effect can occur once every 8 seconds and scales off the higher of your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage.
