# Online:Immortal Warrior

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Immortal_Warrior
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Immortal Warrior is a trial set which drops in the Sanctum Ophidia. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Celestial Style in Heavy Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 4% Healing Taken
3 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
4 items: Adds 4% Healing Taken
5 items: When you take damage while below 35% Health you gain Major Protection for 20 seconds, reducing your damage taken by 10%. This effect can occur once every 20 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
