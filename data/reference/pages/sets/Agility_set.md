# Online:Agility (set)

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Agility_(set)
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Agility is a jewelry set found in Imperial City Treasure Vaults and some random group dungeons. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Xivkyn Style.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 40-1752 Maximum Stamina
3 items: Adds 4-206 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage

### Pieces
The set consists of jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.

### Drop Locations
- The final boss of any group dungeon, provided all prior bosses and minibosses have been defeated. The item scales to the player's level. No longer true?
- Opening the reward chests within the Clawed or Monstrous Tooth trophy vaults within the Imperial City. The item will scale to your level.
- As a possible Daily Dungeon reward, using the Group Finder tool. (Scales to player's level at the time of earning, not when opened)
