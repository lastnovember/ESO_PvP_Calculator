# Online:Spriggan's Vigor

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Spriggan's_Vigor
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Spriggan's Vigor is a dungeon set that drops in Shipwright's Regret. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Dreadsails Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: When you deal Critical Damage, restore 2-100 Stamina and gain a stack of Wild Growth for 5 seconds, increasing your Max Stamina by 5-222. This effect can stack up to 10 times and can occur once every 0.5 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
