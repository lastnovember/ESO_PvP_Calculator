# Online:Wild Impulse

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Wild_Impulse
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Wild Impulse is a Blackrose Weapons item set awarded from normal Blackrose Prison. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Honor Guard Style. The perfected version of this set can be looted on veteran difficulty.

### Bonuses

2 items: Reduce the cost of Impulse by 10%. Impulse places lingering elemental damage on your targets, dealing 605 Flame, 605 Shock, and 605 Frost Damage over 8 seconds. Each element ticks once over the duration and its damage scales off the higher of your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage.

### Pieces
The set consists of destruction staves of all three types.

### Notes
- While the description states that 2 items are required, this is because staves are 2-handed weapons, and thus count as 2 items by themselves, so only 1 item is actually needed.
