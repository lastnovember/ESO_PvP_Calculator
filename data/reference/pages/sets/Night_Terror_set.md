# Online:Night Terror (set)

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Night_Terror_(set)
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Night Terror is an overland set that is found in Stormhaven. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Daedric Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
3 items: Reduces the radius you can be detected while Sneaking by 2 meters. Reduces the cost of Sneak by 0-10%.
4 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
5 items: When you take melee damage, you deal 285 Poison damage to the attacker. This effect can occur once every 1 second and scales off the higher of your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.

Named pieces that are looted:
- Bone-Handled Dirk of Night Terror, from Uncle Bones
- Dimitr's Leathers of Night Terror, from Dimitri
- Flame-Twist Blade of Night Terror, from Nariam
- Gar Xuu Gar's Axe of Night Terror, from Gar Xuu Gar
Named pieces gained as Quest rewards:
- Carved Bone Medallion, from Curse of Skulls
- Ebony-Inlaid Longbow, from Revenge Against Rama
- Goblin-Slayer's Coif, from Blood Revenge
- King Aphren's Seal, from King Aphren's Sword
- Murk-Watcher's Greaves, from A Predator's Heart
- Scimitar of Suffering, from Divert and Deliver
- Sir Edain's War Belt, from Legacy of the Three
