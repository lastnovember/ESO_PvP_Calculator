# Online:Bahsei's Mania

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Bahsei's_Mania
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Bahsei's Mania is a trial set which drops in Normal Rockgrove. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Sul-Xan Style in Light Armor. The perfected version of this set can be looted on Veteran difficulty.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
3 items: Gain Minor Slayer at all times, increasing your damage done to Dungeon, Trial, and Arena Monsters by 5%.
4 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
5 items: Increases your damage done against monsters by up to 15% based on your missing Magicka, reaching the maximum at 50% Magicka. Current value: 0%

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
