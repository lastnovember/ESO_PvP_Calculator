Bar-Sakka
Bar-Sakka is a monster helm set. It consists of two armor pieces: head and shoulder, both of which are available in all three armor weights and standard traits. The set cannot be crafted.

### Bonuses

1 item: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
2 items: While in combat, performing a Roll Dodge creates a 6 meter pool of blood. After a 1 second delay, enemies in the pool take 788 Magic Damage. Gain a Damage Shield for 6 seconds based on total damage done by the pool to enemies, up to 15000 damage. This effect can occur once every 10 seconds and the amount of damage done scales off your Max Health and cannot critically hit.

### Drop Locations
- The head drops from the Talen-Lah and Bar-Sakka fight in Veteran Naj-Caldeesh.
- The shoulders can be obtained from Urgarlag Chief-bane's reward chest at Undaunted Enclaves.
