# Online:Kvatch Gladiator

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Kvatch_Gladiator
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Kvatch Gladiator is one of the craftable sets providing special bonuses to armor and weapons. It is available for crafting to players pursuing blacksmithing, clothing, woodworking, and/or jewelry crafting professions. The special crafting site with stations dedicated to creation of this set are located in the Gold CoastDark Brotherhood.

To make an item for this set, a crafter must first complete research on five traits for this particular item at a corresponding crafting station.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
3 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: You gain 128-1475 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage against targets who are at or below 25% Health.

### Crafting Site
Marja's Mill
