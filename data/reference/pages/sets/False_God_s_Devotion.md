# Online:False God's Devotion

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:False_God's_Devotion
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

False God's Devotion is a trial set which drops in Normal and Veteran Sunspire. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Sunspire style in Light Armor. The Perfected version of this set can be looted on Veteran difficulty.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
3 items: Gain Minor Slayer at all times, increasing your damage done to Dungeon, Trial, and Arena Monsters by 5%.
4 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
5 items: Reduces the cost of your Magicka abilities by 8%. When an enemy you recently damaged dies, you restore 57-2454 Magicka and gain Major Expedition for 8 seconds, increasing your Movement Speed by 30%. These effects can occur once every second.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
