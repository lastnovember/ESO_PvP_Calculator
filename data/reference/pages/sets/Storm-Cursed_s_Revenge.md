# Online:Storm-Cursed's Revenge

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Storm-Cursed's_Revenge
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Storm-Cursed's Revenge is a dungeon set that drops in Shipwright's Regret. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Dreadsails Style in Light Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
3 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
4 items: Adds 34-1487 Offensive Penetration
5 items: When you deal Shock Damage, you have a 15% chance to deal 433 Shock damage to the enemy and up to two other enemies within 5 meters. This effect scales off the higher of your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
