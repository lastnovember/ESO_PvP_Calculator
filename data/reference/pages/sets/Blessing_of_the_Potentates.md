# Online:Blessing of the Potentates

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Blessing_of_the_Potentates
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Blessing of the Potentates is an item set found in boxes purchased using Alliance Points from the Vlastarus Quartermaster, as quest rewards in Vlastarus, as a reward for completing delves and dolmens throughout Cyrodiil, from completing Board Missions, and from Elite Gear Vendors. It cannot be crafted and does not drop from monsters. Pieces are Aldmeri Dominion, Daggerfall Covenant, or Ebonheart Pact Style in Heavy Armor, depending on the player's alliance.

### Bonuses

2 items: Reduces your damage taken from Players by 3%.
3 items: Reduces the cost of your Ultimate abilities by 15%.

### Pieces
The set consists of jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
