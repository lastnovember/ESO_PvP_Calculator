# Online:Duneripper's Scales

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Duneripper's_Scales
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Duneripper's Scales is a dungeon set that drops in Volenfell. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Primal Style in Heavy Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
3 items: Adds 4% Healing Taken
4 items: Adds 4% Healing Taken
5 items: While you are Bracing, your Physical Resistance and Spell Resistance are increased by 123-5310.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Tremorscale's Tooth, a unique drop from Tremorscale.
- Ancient Imperial Hammer, a unique drop from the Guardian Council.
- Duneripper Ancient Crown, the quest reward for Blood and Sand.
