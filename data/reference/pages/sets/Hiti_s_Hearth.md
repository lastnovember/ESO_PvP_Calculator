# Online:Hiti's Hearth

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Hiti's_Hearth
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Hiti's Hearth is a dungeon set that drops in Icereach. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Icereach Coven Style in Light Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Magicka Recovery
3 items: Adds 3-129 Magicka Recovery
4 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
5 items: When you heal yourself or an ally with an ability, gain a Warming Aura for 10 seconds. While in the Warming Aura, you and group members restore 565 Health every 2 seconds and reduce the cost of Sprint, Block, and Roll Dodge by 5%. This effect can occur once every 12 seconds and the heal scales off the higher of your Max Magicka or Stamina.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
