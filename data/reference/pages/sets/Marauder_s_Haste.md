# Online:Marauder's Haste

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Marauder's_Haste
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Marauder's Haste is an overland set found in Southern Elsweyr. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Pellitine Style in Light Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Magicka Recovery
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
4 items: Adds 3-129 Magicka Recovery
5 items: When you apply a damage shield to yourself or an ally, increase your Movement Speed by 0-20% for 9 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as shields and weapons of all types.
- Paint-Stained Ring, received for completing Chiaroscuro Crossroads.
- Moonlit Cove Sash, received for completing Skooma Sequela.
- Talon of Nahfahlaar, received for completing The Dragonguard
- Aeonstone Weave, received for completing The Dark Aeon.
