# Online:Beekeeper's Gear

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Beekeeper's_Gear
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Beekeeper's Gear is an overland set that drops in Greenshade. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Khajiit Style in Heavy Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
3 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
4 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
5 items: Adds 20-900 Health Recovery

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Archmage Macano's Mace of the Beekeeper, a unique drop from Archmage Camaano.
- Beasthunter's Pauldrons of the Beekeeper, a unique drop from Razorclaw.
- Jahla's Shield of the Beekeeper, a unique drop from Jahlasri.
- Merethic Signet
- Storm Staff of the Beekeeper, a unique drop from Zymel Etitan.
