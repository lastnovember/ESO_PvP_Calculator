# Online:Vandorallen's Resonance

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Vandorallen's_Resonance
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Vandorallen's Resonance is a dungeon set that drops in Exiled Redoubt. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Exile's Revenge Style in Light Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 34-1487 Offensive Penetration
3 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: Dealing damage with a fully-charged Heavy Attack places an electric charge on yourself and below your enemy. After 1 second, both charges explode in a 4 meter blast, each dealing 914 Shock damage to enemies hit. This effect can occur once every 6 seconds and scales off the higher of your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
