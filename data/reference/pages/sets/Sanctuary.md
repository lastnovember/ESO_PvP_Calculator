# Online:Sanctuary

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Sanctuary
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Sanctuary is a dungeon set that drops in the Banished Cells I or II. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Orcish Style in Light Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
3 items: Adds 4% Healing Taken
4 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
5 items: Increases your healing received by 10% for you and up to 11 group members within 10 meters of you. This bonus persists through death.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Angata's Signet, unique loot from Angata the Clannfear Handler
- Areldur's Ring, unique loot from Keeper Areldur in Banished Cells II
- High Kinlord's Revenge, unique loot from High Kinlord Rilis in Banished Cells I
- Staff of Conflagration, unique loot from the Maw of the Infernal
- Staff of the Banished, unique loot from High Kinlord Rilis in Banished Cells II
- Talisman of the Sisters, unique loot from the Sisters Sihna and Vera
