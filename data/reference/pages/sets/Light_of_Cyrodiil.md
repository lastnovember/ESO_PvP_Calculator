# Online:Light of Cyrodiil

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Light_of_Cyrodiil
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Light of Cyrodiil is an item set found in boxes purchased using Alliance Points from the Cropsford Quartermaster, as quest rewards in Cropsford, as a reward for completing delves and dolmens throughout Cyrodiil, from completing Board Missions, and from Elite Gear Vendors. It cannot be crafted and does not drop from monsters. Pieces are Aldmeri Dominion, Daggerfall Covenant, or Ebonheart Pact Style in Light Armor, depending on the player's alliance.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
3 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: While you are casting or channeling an ability, your damage taken is reduced by 15%. When you are interrupted, your next Health, Magicka, or Stamina ability cast within 30 seconds will cost no resources.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.Unique pieces include:
- Sargos' Vestments of Cyrodiil's Light, a unique drop from Sargos Grent
