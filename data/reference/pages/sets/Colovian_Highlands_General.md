# Online:Colovian Highlands General

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Colovian_Highlands_General
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Colovian Highlands General is an Elite Gear Set. Set pieces can be obtained from the Elite Gear Vendors for 50,000. It cannot be crafted and does not drop from monsters. Pieces are Cumberland Cavalier Style in all armor weights.

The set's motif is named Cumberland Cavalier, named after House Cumberland, which High King Emeric, the leader of the Daggerfall Covenant belongs to.

### Bonuses

1 item: Adds 34-1487 Offensive Penetration
2 items: When you kill a Player, gain a stack of Blood Debt for 0.5 seconds. When Blood Debt expires, you and up to 5 group members within 28 meters of you gain 15 Ultimate per stack of Blood Debt.

### Pieces
The set consists of head pieces and shoulder pieces of all weights.
