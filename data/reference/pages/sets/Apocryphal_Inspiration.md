# Online:Apocryphal Inspiration

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Apocryphal_Inspiration
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Apocryphal Inspiration is a dungeon set that drops in Scrivener's Hall. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Scribes of Mora Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
3 items: Adds 3-129 Stamina Recovery
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: You and group members within 28 meters of you gain Major Fortitude, Major Intellect, and Major Endurance, increasing your Health Recovery, Magicka Recovery, and Stamina Recovery by 30%.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
