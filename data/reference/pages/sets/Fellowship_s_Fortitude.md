# Online:Fellowship's Fortitude

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Fellowship's_Fortitude
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Fellowship's Fortitude is one of the craftable sets providing special bonuses to armor and weapons. It is available for crafting to players pursuing blacksmithing, clothing, woodworking, and/or jewelry crafting professions. The special crafting site with stations dedicated to the creation of this set is located at the Fellowship Forge in Eastern Solstice.

To make an item for this set, a crafter must first complete research on five traits for that particular item at a corresponding crafting station.

### Bonuses

5 items: Adds 172-7425 Armor
10 items: Adds 140-6020 Maximum Health
12 items: Gain Major Protection, reducing your damage taken by 10%.

### Crafting Site
Fellowship Forge
