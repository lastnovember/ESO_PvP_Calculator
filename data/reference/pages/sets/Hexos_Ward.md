# Online:Hexos' Ward

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Hexos'_Ward
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Hexos' Ward is an overland set that drops in the Deadlands and Fargrave. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are House Hexos Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
3 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
4 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
5 items: Dealing direct Critical Damage grants you a damage shield that absorbs up to 286-12305 damage for 6 seconds. This effect can occur once every 7 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
