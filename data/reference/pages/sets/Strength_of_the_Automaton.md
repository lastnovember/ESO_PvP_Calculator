# Online:Strength of the Automaton

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Strength_of_the_Automaton
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Strength of the Automaton is a dungeon set that drops in Darkshade Caverns. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Dwemer Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: Adds 9-400 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage to your Physical and Bleed Damage abilities.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Automaton's Helm of House Selos, the quest reward for Mine All Mine.
- Ancient Great Axe, a unique drop from the Engine Guardian.
- Imperial Warhammer, a unique drop from the Sentinel of Rkugamz.
- Lambent Netch Tentacles, a unique drop from the Cavern Patriarch.
- Pincer of the Guardian, a unique drop from the Engine Guardian.
