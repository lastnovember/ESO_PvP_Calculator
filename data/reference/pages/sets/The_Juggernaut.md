Juggernaut
The Juggernaut is an item set found in boxes purchased using Alliance Points from the Vlastarus Quartermaster, as quest rewards in Vlastarus, as a reward for completing delves and dolmens throughout Cyrodiil, from completing Board Missions, and from Elite Gear Vendors. It cannot be crafted and does not drop from monsters. Pieces are Aldmeri Dominion, Daggerfall Covenant, or Ebonheart Pact Style in Heavy Armor, depending on the player's alliance.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
3 items: Adds 34-1487 Armor
4 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
5 items: When you take damage while under 30% Health, heal for 4428 Health immediately and gain 465-20000 Armor for 15 seconds. This effect can occur once every 1 minute and the heal scales off your Max Health.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
