# Online:Plague Doctor

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Plague_Doctor
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Plague Doctor is an overland set that drops in Deshaan. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Breton Style in Heavy Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
3 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
4 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
5 items: Adds 65-2804 Maximum Health

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Dres Dirk, the quest reward for Mechanical Murder in the Nord Style.
- Redoran General's Gauntlets, the quest reward for What Lies Beneath in the Dark Elf Style.
- Gruznak's Chestplate, the quest reward for Challenge the Tide in the Orc Style.
- Dungeon-Delving Slippers, the quest reward for The Light Fantastic in the Dark Elf Style.
- Plague Doctor Celdien's Pauldrons, a unique drop from General Celdien, the final boss of the Corpse Garden.
- Plague Doctor Llarel's Sheltering Shield, a unique drop from Lady Llarel, the final boss of Lady Llarel's Shelter.
- Plague Doctor's Talisman of Bthanual, a unique drop from Bthanual Centurion, the final boss of Lower Bthanual.
- Plague Doctor's Dremora's Dread, a unique drop from the world boss Druitularg at Druitularg's Ritual Altar.
