# Online:Jerall Mountains Warchief

visual effect on enemy
Jerall Mountains Warchief is an Elite Gear Set. Set pieces can be obtained from the Elite Gear Vendors for 50,000. It cannot be crafted and does not drop from monsters. Pieces are Highborn Gallant Style in all armor weights.

### Bonuses

1 item: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
2 items: Dealing damage applies a stack of Malady to your enemy, reducing healing received by 1% for 5 seconds, up to 35 stacks. You can apply a stack once every 0.5 seconds. Applying Malady gives you a stack of Contagion, reducing healing received by 1% for 5 seconds, up to 15 stacks. You can only gain a stack once every 1 second.

### Pieces
The set consists of head pieces and shoulder pieces of all weights.
