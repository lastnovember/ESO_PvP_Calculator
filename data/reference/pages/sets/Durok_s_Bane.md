# Online:Durok's Bane

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Durok's_Bane
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Durok's Bane is a dungeon set that drops in Selene's Web. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Redguard style in Heavy Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
3 items: Adds 3-129 Health Recovery
4 items: Adds 4% Healing Taken
5 items: When you take damage, you apply Major Defile to the enemy for 5 seconds, reducing their healing received and damage shield strength by 12%. This effect can occur once every 7 seconds per enemy.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Arachnoid Ring, unique loot from Mennir Many-Legs.
- Bearslayer, unique loot from Foulhide.
- Selene's Battle Greaves of Durok's Bane, a quest reward for Knowledge Gained.Correct quality?
- Webcutter, unique loot from Selene.
