# Online:Akaviri Dragonguard

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Akaviri_Dragonguard
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Akaviri Dragonguard is an overland set that drops in Eastmarch. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Nord Style in Heavy Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Magicka Recovery
3 items: Adds 4% Healing Taken
4 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
5 items: Adds 3-158 Health Recovery
5 items: Reduces the cost of your Ultimate abilities by 15%.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Bonelord's Annihilator of the Dragon, a unique drop from the world bosses Vivien Armene and Draugr Stormlord at Dragon Mound in the Breton Style.
- Deathknight Aegis of the Dragon, a unique drop from Deathknight Stormcrag, the final boss of Stormcrag Crypt.
- Dungeon-Delving Harness, the quest reward for Kireth's Amazing Plan in the Dark Elf Style
- Hefty Bonestrewn Gauntlets, the quest reward for Dark Deeds.
- Kyne's Honor, the quest reward for For Kyne's Honor.
- Mammoth Band of the Dragon, a unique drop from the world bosses Mannskadi and Ulgorus at Dragon's Hallow.
- Shell Slicer, the quest reward of Security Details in the Argonian Style.
- Skald-King's Greaves, the quest reward for Gods Save the King.
- Skald-King's Favor, the quest reward for The War Council.
- Skald-King's Pauldrons, the quest reward for Blindsided.
- Skald-King's War Plackart, the quest reward for A Council of Thanes.
- Skald-King's Sabatons, the quest reward for Songs of Sovngarde.
- Stormfist's Holy Hammer of the Dragon, a unique drop from Braxek, Eorim the Hammer and Gadof, the final bosses of Old Sord's Cave.
