# Online:Way of Air

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Way_of_Air
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Way of Air is an overland set that drops in Craglorn. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Yokudan Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Stamina Recovery
3 items: Adds 3-129 Stamina Recovery
4 items: Increases the radius you can detect Sneaking enemies by 2 meters.
5 items: Adds 3-129 Stamina Recovery
5 items: When you use Roll Dodge, your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage is increased by 9-391 for 6 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Edge of the Black Sands of the Air, a quest reward from The Warrior's Call
- Pendant of the Sundered of the Air, a quest reward from The Missing Guardian
