# Online:Way of the Arena

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Way_of_the_Arena
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Way of the Arena is one of the craftable sets providing special bonuses to armor and weapons. It is available for crafting to players pursuing blacksmithing, clothing, woodworking, and/or jewelry crafting professions. The special crafting site dedicated to creation of this set is available in Craglorn.

To make an item for this set, a crafter must first complete research on eight traits for this particular item at a corresponding crafting station.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
4 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
5 items: Adds 3-165 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: Reduces the cost of Break Free by 40%.

### Crafting Site
Lanista's Waystation

## Notes
- In earlier versions of the game, this set was simply called Arena.
