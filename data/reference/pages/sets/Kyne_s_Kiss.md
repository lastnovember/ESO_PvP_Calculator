# Online:Kyne's Kiss

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Kyne's_Kiss
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Kyne's Kiss is an item set found in boxes purchased using Alliance Points from the Bruma Quartermaster, as quest rewards in Bruma, as a reward for completing delves and dolmens throughout Cyrodiil, from completing Board Missions, and from Elite Gear Vendors. It cannot be crafted and does not drop from monsters. Pieces are Aldmeri Dominion, Daggerfall Covenant, or Ebonheart Pact Style in Medium Armor, depending on the player's alliance.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
3 items: Adds 3-129 Stamina Recovery
4 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
5 items: When you deal direct damage while 12 meters or further from your target, you heal for 521 Health and restore 30-1314 Stamina. This effect can occur once every 5 seconds and the heal scales off the higher of your Max Magicka or Stamina.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as bows and staves of all types (no melee weapons or shields).
