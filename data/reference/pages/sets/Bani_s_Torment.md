# Online:Bani's Torment

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Bani's_Torment
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Bani's Torment is a dungeon set that drops in Icereach. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Icereach Coven Style in Heavy Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 4% Healing Taken
3 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
4 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
5 items: When you taunt a nearby enemy, you tether to them for 15 seconds, as long as you remain within 8 meters of them. While tethered, you apply Major Maim to your enemy, reducing their damage done by 10%, and you gain Major Vitality, increasing your healing received and damage shield strength by 12%. This effect can occur once every 15 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
