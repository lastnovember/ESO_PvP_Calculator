# Online:Bright-Throat's Boast

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Bright-Throat's_Boast
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Bright-Throat's Boast is an overland set that drops in Murkmire. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Elder Argonian Style in Light Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
4 items: Adds 3-129 Magicka Recovery
5 items: While you have a drink buff active, your Max Magicka is increased by 46-2000 and Magicka Recovery by 3-133.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Helm of the Schemer, the quest reward for Missing in Murkmire
