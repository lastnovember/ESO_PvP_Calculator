# Online:Ansuul's Torment

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Ansuul's_Torment
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Ansuul's Torment is a trial set found in normal Sanity's Edge. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are in Clan Dreamcarver style in Medium Armor. The perfected version of this set can be looted on veteran difficulty.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
3 items: Gain Minor Slayer at all times, increasing your damage done to Dungeon, Trial, and Arena Monsters by 5%.
4 items: Adds 34-1487 Offensive Penetration
5 items: Increases your damage done against monsters by 0-7%. When you interrupt an enemy, you increase your damage done against monsters by an additional 0-14% for 30 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
