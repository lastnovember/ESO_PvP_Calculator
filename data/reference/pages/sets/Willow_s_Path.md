# Online:Willow's Path

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Willow's_Path
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Willow's Path is one of the craftable sets providing special bonuses to armor and weapons. It is available for crafting to players pursuing blacksmithing, clothing, woodworking, and/or jewelry crafting professions. One special crafting site with stations dedicated to creation of this set is located in the last zone of each of the three Alliances.

To make an item for this set, a crafter must first complete research on six traits for this particular item at a corresponding crafting station.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Magicka Recovery
3 items: Adds 3-129 Stamina Recovery
4 items: Adds 3-129 Health Recovery
5 items: Increases your Health, Magicka, and Stamina Recovery by 18%.
