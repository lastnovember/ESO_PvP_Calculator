# Online:Shattered Fate

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Shattered_Fate
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Shattered Fate is one of the craftable sets providing special bonuses to armor and weapons. It is available for crafting to players pursuing blacksmithing, clothing, woodworking, and/or jewelry crafting professions. The special crafting site with stations dedicated to the creation of this set is located at Artisan's Hermitage in Apocrypha.

To make an item for this set, a crafter must first complete research on five traits for that particular item at a corresponding crafting station.

### Bonuses

5 items: Adds 184-7918 Offensive Penetration
10 items: Adds 15-687 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
12 items: Adds 35-1528 Critical Chance

### Crafting Site
Artisan's Hermitage
