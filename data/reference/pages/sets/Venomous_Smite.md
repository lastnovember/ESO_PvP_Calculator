# Online:Venomous Smite

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Venomous_Smite
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Venomous Smite is an overland set found in Western Skyrim. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Greymoor Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
3 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: Dealing Critical Damage inflicts Hunter's Venom on your enemy for 10 seconds, dealing 714 Poison damage to your target and enemies within 6 meters of them every 2 seconds. This effect can occur once every 15 seconds and scales off the higher of your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage.

### Pieces
The set consists of Medium Armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Angrar's Brace, Potent Poison quest reward
- Bitterblade Mining Helmet, Digging Up Trouble quest reward
- Chillwind Chitinous Gauntlets, Poison's Sting quest reward
- Deathstone Spiral, The Lady of Blood quest reward
- Draugr's Demise, Prisoner of the Past quest reward
- Ghamborz's Rough Leggings, Ghamborz's Mining Samples quest reward
- Icewalker's Boots, The Fate of the Frozen quest reward
- Mor Khazgur Crusher, A Clan Divided quest reward
- Reeh-La's Scaled Vest, Reeh-La's Mining Samples quest reward
- Spriggan's Lament, The Mountain Bellows quest reward
- Zabusihr's Soft Shoulders, Adanzda's Mining Samples
- Longbow of Solitude, The Gray Host quest reward
