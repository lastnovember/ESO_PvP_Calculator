# Online:Armor of Truth

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Armor_of_Truth
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Armor of Truth is a dungeon set that drops in Darkshade Caverns. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Dark Elf Style in Heavy Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: When you deal damage to an enemy who is Off Balance, your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage are increased by 10-460 for 10 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Fist of the Hive Lord, unique loot from the Transmuted Hive Lord.
- Foreman's Ring, unique loot from The Fallen Foreman.
- Imperial Arm-Cleaver, unique loot from the Sentinel of Rkugamz.
- Kwama Shepherd's Charm, unique loot from Head Shepherd Neloren.
- Transmuted Kwama Shell, unique loot from the Transmuted Hive Lord.
