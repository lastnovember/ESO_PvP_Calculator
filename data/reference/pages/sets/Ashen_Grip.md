# Online:Ashen Grip

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Ashen_Grip
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Ashen Grip is one of the craftable sets providing special bonuses to armor and weapons. It is available for crafting to players pursuing blacksmithing, clothing, woodworking, and/or jewelry crafting professions. One special crafting site with stations dedicated to creation of this set is located in the first zone of each of the three Alliances.

To make an item for this set, a crafter must first complete research on two traits for this particular item at a corresponding crafting station.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
3 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
4 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
5 items: When you deal Martial melee damage, you breathe fire to all enemies in front of you for 774 Flame Damage. This effect can occur once every 4 seconds and scales off the higher of your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage.
