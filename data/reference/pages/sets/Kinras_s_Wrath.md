# Online:Kinras's Wrath

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Kinras's_Wrath
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Kinras's Wrath is a dungeon set that drops in Black Drake Villa. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are True-Sworn Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
3 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: Dealing damage with a Light or Heavy Attack grants you a stack of Burning Heart for 5 seconds, up to 5 stacks. While you have 5 stacks, you generate an aura of wrath, granting you Major Berserk, increasing your damage done by 10%. While you have an aura of wrath, group members within 12 meters of you gain Minor Berserk, increasing their damage done by 5%.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
