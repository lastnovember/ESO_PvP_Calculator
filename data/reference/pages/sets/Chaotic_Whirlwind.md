# Online:Chaotic Whirlwind

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Chaotic_Whirlwind
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Chaotic Whirlwind is an Asylum Weapons item set which drops in normal Asylum Sanctorium after defeating Saint Olms the Just. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Asylum Style. The perfected version of this set can be looted on veteran difficulty.

### Bonuses

2 items: When you cast Whirlwind while in combat, you gain a stack of Chaotic Whirlwind for 5 seconds, granting you 5% Movement Speed per stack, up to a maximum of 5 times. While Chaotic Whirlwind is active, you gain 6 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage for every 1% bonus Movement Speed you have, up to a maximum of 450. At 5 stacks, the duration doubles but cannot be refreshed.

### Pieces
The set consists of one-handed weapons of all types. There is no shield.

## Notes
- You will always receive two weapons when this set drops, and they must be wielded together. If you use one with a shield or another weapon, it will not provide any bonus. You may, however, mix and match other items of this set found on subsequent runs of the trial.
