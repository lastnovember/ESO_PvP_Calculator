# Online:Basalt-Blooded Warrior

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Basalt-Blooded_Warrior
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Basalt-Blooded Warrior is a Class set found in the Infinite Archive. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Basalt-Blood Warrior Style in all weights. It buffs the Earthen Heart skill line for Dragonknights.

### Bonuses

Class: Dragonknight
2 items: Adds 3-129 Magicka Recovery
3 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: Casting an Earthen Heart ability grants you a Rock Stance for 10 seconds. While on your Primary Weapon you gain Molten Stance, granting you Major Heroism, generating 3 Ultimate every 1.5 seconds. While you are on your Secondary Weapon you gain Obsidian Stance, increasing your Healing Done and damage shields by 14%. Bar Swapping will swap your Stance automatically.

### Pieces
The set consists of armor of all weights and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
