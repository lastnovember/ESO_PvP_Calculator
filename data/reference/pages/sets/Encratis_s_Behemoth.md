# Online:Encratis's Behemoth

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Encratis's_Behemoth
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Pyroturge Encratis

Encratis's Behemoth is a monster helm set. It consists of two armor pieces: head and shoulder, both of which are available in all three armor weights and standard traits. The set cannot be crafted. There is an outfit style based on this armor set.

### Bonuses

1 item: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
2 items: Dealing Flame Damage to an enemy grants you Behemoth's Aura for 12 seconds that reaches up to 12 meters. You and up to 11 group members in the aura reduce Flame Damage taken by 5%. Enemies in the aura increase their Flame Damage taken by 5%. This effect can occur once every 15 seconds.

### Drop Locations
- The head drops from the Pyroturge Encratis in Veteran Black Drake Villa.
- The shoulders can be obtained from Urgarlag Chief-bane's reward chest at Undaunted Enclaves.
