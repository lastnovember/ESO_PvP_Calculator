# Online:Telvanni Efficiency

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Telvanni_Efficiency
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Telvanni Efficiency is one of the craftable sets providing special bonuses to armor and weapons. It is available for crafting to players pursuing blacksmithing, clothing, woodworking, and/or jewelry crafting professions. The special crafting site with stations dedicated to the creation of this set is located at Tel Hlurag Ven on the Telvanni Peninsula.

To make an item for this set, a crafter must first complete research on three traits for that particular item at a corresponding crafting station.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
3 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
4 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
5 items: While you have a living Companion, reduce the cooldown of their abilities by 50%. While you do not have a living Companion, reduce the cost of your Magicka, Stamina, Health, and Ultimate abilities by 8%.

### Crafting Site
Tel Hlurag Ven
