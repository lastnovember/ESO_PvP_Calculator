Spawn of Mephala

Spawn of Mephala is a monster helm set. It consists of two armor pieces: head and shoulder, both of which are available in all three armor weights and standard traits. The set cannot be crafted. There is an outfit style based on this armor set.

### Bonuses

1 item: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
2 items: When you deal damage with a fully-charged Heavy Attack, you create a web under the target for 10 seconds that deals 218 Poison damage every 1 second to all enemies within 4 meters. This effect can occur once every 10 seconds and scales off the higher of your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage.

### Drop Locations
- The head drops from Vila Theran in Veteran Fungal Grotto II.
- The shoulders can be obtained from Maj al-Ragath's chest in the Undaunted Enclaves.
