# Online:Wise Mage

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Wise_Mage
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Wise Mage is a trial set which drops in the Sanctum Ophidia. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Celestial Style in Light Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
3 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: Enemies you damage with fully-charged Heavy Attacks are afflicted with Minor Vulnerability for 10 seconds, increasing their damage taken by 5%.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.

### Similar sets
