# Online:Crest of Cyrodiil

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Crest_of_Cyrodiil
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Crest of Cyrodiil is an item set found in boxes purchased using Alliance Points from the Vlastarus Quartermaster, as quest rewards in Vlastarus, as a reward for completing delves and dolmens throughout Cyrodiil, from completing Board Missions, and from Elite Gear Vendors. It cannot be crafted and does not drop from monsters. Pieces are Aldmeri Dominion, Daggerfall Covenant, or Ebonheart Pact Style in Heavy Armor, depending on the player's alliance.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 9-424 Critical Resistance
3 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
4 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
5 items: When you Block, heal for 2871 Health. This effect can occur every 4 seconds and scales off your Max Health.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Kerk's Pauldrons of Cyrodiil's Crest, a unique drop from Kerks Half-Ear found in Muck Valley Cavern
- Ogre Biter's Shell of Cyrodiil's Crest, a unique drop from Ogre Biter found in Muck Valley Cavern
- The Black Dagger of Cyrodiil's Crest, a unique drop from Hegris the Black Dagger found in Breakneck Cave
- Meb's Chopper of Cyrodiil's Crest, a unique drop from Mebs Runnyeye found in Cracked Wood Cave
- Ashham's Breastplate of Cyrodiil's Crest, a unique drop from Gaston Ashham found in Lipsand Tarn
- Marbita's Helm of Cyrodiil's Crest, a unique drop from Marbita found in Lipsand Tarn
- Barasatii's Chain of Cyrodiil's Crest, a unique drop from Barasatii found in Nisin Cave
- The Harrower of Cyrodiil's Crest, a unique drop from Volgo the Harrower found in Nisin Cave
- Blighttooth's Embrace of Cyrodiil's Crest, a unique drop from Blighttooth found in Pothole Caverns
- Virane's Boots of Cyrodiil's Crest, a unique drop from General Virane found in Toadstool Hollow
- Dreadfang's Bite of Cyrodiil's Crest, a unique drop from Dreadfang found in Toadstool Hollow
