# Online:Prisoner's Rags

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Prisoner's_Rags
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Prisoner's Rags Style
Prisoner's Rags is an overland set that drops in Coldharbour. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Soul-Shriven Style in Light Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
3 items: Adds 3-129 Stamina Recovery
4 items: Adds 3-129 Stamina Recovery
5 items: Reduces the cost of Sprint by 50%. While Sprinting, restore 24-1035 Magicka every 1 second.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Ahirunz's Beloved Sash, the quest reward for A Misplaced Pendant in the Khajiit Style.
- Battle Bracers, the quest reward for News of Fallen Kin in the Nord Style.
- Battle Minstrel's Mantle, the quest reward for The Lost Lute in the Dark Elf Style.
- Dungeon-Delving Staff, the quest reward for Through the Daedric Lens in the Daedric Style.
- Galerion's Spare Staff, the quest reward for Breaking the Shackle in the High Elf Style.
- Old Bone's Shivering Timber, the quest reward for Old Bones in the Primal Style.
- Prisoner Undulating Band, a unique drop from the world boss Cynhamoth at Cynhamoth's Grove.
- Prisoner Frigid Amulet, a unique drop from the world boss Duriatundur at Duriatundur's Killing Field.
- Prisoner Gnilokelo's Dread, a unique drop from Gnilokelo, the final boss of The Cave of Trophies in the Daedric Style.
- Prisoner Zemarek's Visor, a unique drop from the world boss Zemarek-thul at Zemarek's Hollow in the High Elf Style.
- Privateer Clogs, the quest reward for Between Blood and Bonein the Argonian Style.
- Seal of Sacrifice, the quest reward for Soul Survivors.
- Soul-Meld Staff, the quest reward for The Soul-Meld Mage in the Khajiit Style.
- Stibbon's Dress Shirt of the Prisoner, the quest reward for What the Heart Wants in the Breton Style.
- Stibbon's Dress Trousers of the Prisoner, the quest reward for Saving Stibbons in the Breton Style.
