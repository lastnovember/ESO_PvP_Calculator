# Online:Automated Defense

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Automated_Defense
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Automated Defense is a trial set which drops in the Halls of Fabrication. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Refabricated Style in Heavy Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
3 items: Gain Minor Aegis, reducing damage taken from monsters by 5%.
4 items: Adds 4% Healing Taken
5 items: When you use an Ultimate ability while in combat, you and the closest 5 group members within 28 meters of you gain Major Aegis for 1 second per 10 Ultimate spent, reducing damage taken from monsters by 10%.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
