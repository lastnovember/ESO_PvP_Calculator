# Online:Thunderbug's Carapace

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Thunderbug's_Carapace
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Thunderbug's Carapace is an overland set that drops in Malabal Tor. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Primal Style in Heavy Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 34-1487 Armor
3 items: Adds 34-1487 Armor
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: When you take Physical Damage, you deal 714 Shock damage in a 5 meter radius around you. This effect can occur once every 3 seconds and scales off the higher of your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
