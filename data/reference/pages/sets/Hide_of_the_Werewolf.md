# Online:Hide of the Werewolf

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Hide_of_the_Werewolf
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Hide of the Werewolf is an overland set that drops in Glenumbra. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Primal Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: When you take damage, you generate 6 Ultimate. This effect can occur once every 5 seconds and the Ultimate triples while in Werewolf Transformation.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Werewolf Hide Red Rook Ailettes, the quest reward for Crosswych Reclaimed.
- Werewolf Hide Werewolf Slayer, the quest reward for Lineage of Tooth and Claw.
- Werewolf Hide King Deleyn's Longbow, the quest reward for Servants of Ancient Kings.
- Werewolf Hide Daggerfall Guard Tabard, the quest reward for Disorganized Crime.
- Werewolf Hide Kip's Roguish Leathers, the quest reward for Vines and Villains.
- Werewolf Hide Dagger's Combat Stompers, the quest reward for The Dagger's Edge.
- Werewolf Hide Everblooded Dagger, dropped from Valenwe.
- Werewolf's Hide Razor, dropped from Sincano.
- Crosswych Mining Maul, the quest reward for The Miners' Lament.
- Daggerfall Guard Waist Band, the quest reward for Red Rook Resources.
- Graufang's Werewolf Hide Belt, dropped from Graufang.
- Werewolf Hide Wolf's-Head Ring, dropped sometimes from Salazar the Wolf.
