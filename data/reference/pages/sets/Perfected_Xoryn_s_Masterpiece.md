# Online:Perfected Xoryn's Masterpiece

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Perfected_Xoryn's_Masterpiece
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Perfected Xoryn's Masterpiece is a trial set found in veteran Lucent Citadel. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are in Lucent Sentinel Style in Light Armor. The non-perfected version of this set can be looted on normal difficulty.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
3 items: Gain Minor Aegis at all times, reducing your damage taken from Dungeon, Trial, and Arena Monsters by 5%.
4 items: Adds 3-129 Magicka Recovery
5 perfected items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
5 items: Increases Max Magicka and Max Stamina by 1667 for you and up to 11 other group members within 28 meters of you. This bonus persists through death.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
