# Online:Drake's Rush

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Drake's_Rush
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Drake's Rush is a dungeon set that drops in Black Drake Villa. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are True-Sworn Style in Heavy Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
4 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
5 items: When you Bash an enemy, you and up to 3 group members within 15 meters of you gain Major Heroism for 12 seconds, granting you 3 Ultimate every 1.5 seconds. This effect can occur once every 18 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
