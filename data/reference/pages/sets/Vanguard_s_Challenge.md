# Online:Vanguard's Challenge

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Vanguard's_Challenge
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Vanguard's Challenge is an Battlegrounds Set rewarded by Battlegrounds. It can be obtained in boxes purchased using Alliance Points from the Vlastarus Quartermaster. It cannot be crafted and does not drop from monsters. Pieces are Militant Ordinator Style in Heavy Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
3 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
4 items: Adds 4% Healing Taken
5 items: When you taunt an enemy player, they deal 35% less damage to all other players but 35% more damage to you for 15 seconds. This effect is lost if you enter Sneak, invisibility, or move further than 28 meters away from them.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
