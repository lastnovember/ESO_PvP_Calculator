# Online:Dreamer's Mantle

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Dreamer's_Mantle
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Dreamer's Mantle is an overland set that drops in Stormhaven. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are High Elf Style in Light Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
3 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
4 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
5 items: When you take damage while you are under 35% Health, you knockdown all enemies within 4 meters of you for 2 seconds and restore 24-1058 Magicka. This effect can occur once every 7 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
