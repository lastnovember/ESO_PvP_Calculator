# Online:The Arch-Mage

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:The_Arch-Mage
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

The Arch-Mage is an item set found in boxes purchased using Alliance Points from the Cropsford Quartermaster, as quest rewards in Cropsford, as a reward for completing delves and dolmens throughout Cyrodiil, from completing Board Missions, and from Elite Gear Vendors. Pieces are Aldmeri Dominion, Daggerfall Covenant, or Ebonheart Pact Style in Light Armor, depending on the player's alliance.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Magicka Recovery
3 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
4 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
5 items: When you complete a fully-charged Heavy Attack, you gain an additional 11-506 Magicka Recovery for 10 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
