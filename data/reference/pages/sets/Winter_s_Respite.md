# Online:Winter's Respite

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Winter's_Respite
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Winter's Respite is an overland set found in Western Skyrim. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Blackreach Vanguard Style in Light Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Magicka Recovery
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
4 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
5 items: While in combat, casting abilities that leave an effect on the ground will create a circle of healing frost for 10 seconds. You and your group members restore 1125 Health every 2 seconds while inside the circle. This effect can occur once every 10 seconds and scales off the higher of your Max Magicka or Stamina.

### Pieces
The set consists of Light Armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Fire Staff of the Gray Host, The Vampire Scholar quest reward
- Spiderprod Staff, Scraps Matter quest reward
- Meridia's Healing Rod, Meridia's Brilliance quest reward
- Ambritt's Iceflame, The Gathering Storm quest reward
- Greymoor Skullcap, Greymoor Rising quest reward
- Antiquarian's Gloves, The Aspiring Scholar quest reward
- Surcoat of Solitude, Dark Clouds Over Solitude quest reward
- Rigurt's Fancy Shirt, Cultural Conciliation quest reward
- Seeker's Cincture, The Fading Fire quest reward
- Dancer's Treble Leggings, Orchestrations quest reward
- Dancer's Treble Shoes, A Salskap to Remember quest reward
- Frost-Cursed Lament, Long Journey Home quest reward
