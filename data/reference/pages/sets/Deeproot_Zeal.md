# Online:Deeproot Zeal

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Deeproot_Zeal
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Deeproot Zeal is a dungeon set that drops in Earthen Root Enclave. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Y'ffre's Will Style in Heavy Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
4 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
5 items: When you deal direct damage to a taunted target, restore 12-525 Stamina and Magicka. This effect can occur once every 2 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
