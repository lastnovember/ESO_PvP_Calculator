# Online:Wisdom of Vanus

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Wisdom_of_Vanus
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Wisdom of Vanus is an overland set that drops on Summerset. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Psijic Style in light armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
3 items: Adds 3-129 Magicka Recovery
4 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
5 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: After completing a fully-charged Heavy Attack, you gain Major Mending for 5 seconds, increasing your healing done by 16%. This duration is increased with each rank of the Restoration Staff Passive of Essence Drain when using a Restoration Staff.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
