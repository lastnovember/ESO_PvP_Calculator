ON:Briarheart (Condition)
Briarheart is an overland set that drops in Wrothgar. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Reach Winter Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
4 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
5 items: When you deal Critical Damage, you increase your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage by 15-450 for 10 seconds. While this effect is active, dealing Critical Damage heals you for 95 Health. This effect can occur once every 15 seconds and the heal scales off the higher of your Max Magicka or Stamina.

### Pieces
The set consists of Medium armor and Jewelry in all slots, as well as Weapons and Shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Barrow Brigandine, the quest reward for Draugr Dilemma
- Dwarven Brass Sallet, the quest reward for A Heart of Brass
- Materre's Bodkin, the quest reward for Where Loyalty Lies
- Winterborn Gardbrace (arm cops), the quest reward for The Hidden Harvest
