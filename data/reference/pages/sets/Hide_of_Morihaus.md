# Online:Hide of Morihaus

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Hide_of_Morihaus
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Hide of Morihaus is an overland set that drops in the Gold Coast. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Minotaur Style in Heavy Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
3 items: Adds 34-1487 Armor
4 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
5 items: Adds 3-129 Stamina Recovery
5 items: When you Roll Dodge through an enemy, you deal 0 Physical Damage and knock them down for 3 seconds. This effect scales off the higher of your Physical Resistance or Spell Resistance.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
