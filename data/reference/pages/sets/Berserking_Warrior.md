# Online:Berserking Warrior

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Berserking_Warrior
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Berserking Warrior is a trial set which drops in the Hel Ra Citadel. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Celestial Style in Heavy Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
3 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
4 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
5 items: Adds 22-986 Critical Chance
5 items: When you deal Martial melee damage, your Critical Chance is increased by 6-298 for 5 seconds, stacking up to 5 times. This effect can occur once every half second.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types; the bow, however, is no longer available.
