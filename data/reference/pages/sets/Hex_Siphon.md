# Online:Hex Siphon

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Hex_Siphon
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Hex Siphon is a set which drops in the Vateshran Hollows. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Ancestral Reach Style in Light Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
3 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
4 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
5 items: Adds 3-171 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: Whenever you Bash an enemy, you release a cone of hexing energy in front of you. Each enemy hit increases your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage by 2-88 for 8 seconds, up to a maximum of 6 enemies. This effect can occur once every 8 seconds. Whenever you successfully interrupt a target, you restore 23-1000 Magicka.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
