# Online:Swarm Mother (set)

the enemy

Swarm Mother is a monster helm set. It consists of two armor pieces: head and shoulder, both of which are available in all three armor weights and standard traits. The set cannot be crafted. There is an outfit style based on this armor set. There is also an opal outfit style based on this armor set.

### Bonuses

1 item: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
1 item: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
2 items: When you Block an enemy that is between 8 and 22 meters from you, you spin strands of spider silk to pull the enemy to you and taunt them for 15 seconds if they are not already taunted. This effect can occur once every 1 second.

### Drop Locations
- The head drops from The Whisperer in Veteran Spindleclutch I.
- The shoulders can be obtained from Maj al-Ragath's chest in the Undaunted Enclaves.
