Molag Kena

Molag Kena is a monster helm set. It consists of two armor pieces: head and shoulder, both of which are available in all three armor weights and standard traits. The set cannot be crafted. There is an outfit style based on this armor set.

### Bonuses

1 item: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
2 items: When you consecutively Light Attack 2 times, you trigger Overkill for 6 seconds, which increases your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage by 13-560 but also increases the cost of your abilities by 8%. This effect can occur once every 6 seconds.

### Drop Locations
- The head drops from Molag Kena in Veteran White-Gold Tower.
- The shoulders can be obtained from Urgarlag Chief-bane's chest in the undaunted enclave.
