# Online:Fiord's Legacy

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Fiord's_Legacy
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Fiord's Legacy is an overland set that drops in Eastmarch. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Nord Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
3 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
4 items: Adds 3-129 Stamina Recovery
5 items: Reduces the cost and increases the Movement Speed bonus of Sprint by 15%.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Blood Mask, the quest reward for The Better of Two Evils.
- Burial Jewel of the Brief Queen, the quest reward for Sleep for the Dead.
- Darkwater Hand Warmers, the quest reward for Our Poor Town in the Argonian Style.
- Dungeon-Delving Brigandine, the quest reward for In Search of Kireth Vanos in the Dark Elf Style.
- Draugr Bane, the quest reward for On a Dare
- Fiord's Agnenor's Brutal Blade, a unique drop from Agnenor the Blade, the final boss of The Bastard's Tomb.
- Fiord's Ammabani's Pride, a unique drop from the world boss Ammabani at Ammabani's Pride.
- Fiord's Frigid Troll Bones, a unique drop from Frostbite Mangler, the final boss of The Frigid Grotto.
- Fiord's Ratmaster's Bloodbow, a unique drop from the world boss Olveidi the Ratmaster at Ratmaster's Prowl.
- Forester Leggings, the quest reward for Land Dispute.
- Frostwind Axe, the quest reward for Giant Problems.
- Giant-Friend, the quest reward for The Siege of Cradlecrush.
- Lost Knife Mining Boots, the quest reward for Mystery Metal.
- Lost Knife Mining Brace, the quest reward for Do Kill the Messenger.
- Riekling Skull-Cleaver, the quest reward for Merriment and Mystery in the Orc Style.
- Rigurt's Robust Ring, the quest reward for Party Planning.
- Wolf Pelt Mantle, the quest reward for Collector of Pelts.
