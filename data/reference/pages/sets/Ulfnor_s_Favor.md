# Online:Ulfnor's Favor

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Ulfnor's_Favor
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Ulfnor's Favor is a dungeon set that drops in Fang Lair. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Fang Lair Style in Heavy Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
3 items: Adds 3-129 Magicka Recovery
3 items: Adds 3-129 Stamina Recovery
4 items: Adds 4% Healing Taken
5 items: Increases the Magicka or Stamina your Heavy Attacks restore by 30%.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
