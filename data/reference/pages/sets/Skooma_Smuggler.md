# Online:Skooma Smuggler

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Skooma_Smuggler
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Skooma Smuggler is an overland set that drops in Reaper's March. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Khajiit Style in Light Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
3 items: Adds 3-129 Magicka Recovery
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: When you drink a potion while in combat, you gain Major Expedition for 30 seconds, increasing your Movement Speed by 30%.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Khaj Rawlith Crescent of the Smuggler
- Artificer's Vestments
- Emancipator's Gloves
- Pathwalker's Shield
- Smuggler's Vestments of Jode's Light
- Smuggler's Excruciatus Staff
- Smuggler's Gloves of Three Mercies
- Smuggler's Devouring Earth Greatstaff
