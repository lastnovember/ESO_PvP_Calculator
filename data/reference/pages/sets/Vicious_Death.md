# Online:Vicious Death

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Vicious_Death
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Vicious Death is an Elite Gear Set that was formerly available from "Rewards for the Worthy" mails, earned by participating in PvP, prior to Update 39. It can now be obtained in boxes purchased using Alliance Points from the Cropsford Quartermaster, as quest rewards in Cropsford, as a reward for completing delves and dolmens throughout Cyrodiil, from completing Board Missions, and from Elite Gear Vendors. It cannot be crafted and does not drop from monsters. Pieces are Aldmeri Dominion, Daggerfall Covenant, or Ebonheart Pact Style in Light Armor, depending on the player's alliance.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
3 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
4 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
5 items: Adds 34-1487 Offensive Penetration
5 items: When you kill a Player, they violently explode for 3322 Flame Damage to all other enemies in a 5 meter radius. This effect scales off the higher of your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.

### Notes
- Before Update 39, this set was in Abah's Watch Style.
- This set uses this icon for the death recap: Two Handed-Arcane Fighter
