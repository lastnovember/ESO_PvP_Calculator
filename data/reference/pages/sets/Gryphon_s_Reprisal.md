# Online:Gryphon's Reprisal

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Gryphon's_Reprisal
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Gryphon's Reprisal is a dungeon set that drops in Coral Aerie. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Ascendant Order Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
3 items: Adds 34-1487 Offensive Penetration
4 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
5 items: When you deal Critical Damage, you or an ally can activate the Gryphon's Reprisal on the enemy within the next 8 seconds, causing a small gryphon to fly by and deal 4758 Bleed damage over 10 seconds to enemies within 5 meters. This effect can occur once every 20 seconds and scales off the higher of the synergy activator's Weapon Damage and Spell Damage.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
