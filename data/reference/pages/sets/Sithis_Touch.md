# Online:Sithis' Touch

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Sithis'_Touch
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Sithis's Touch is an overland set that drops in the Gold Coast. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Dark Brotherhood Style in all armor weights.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: When you kill an enemy you gain 5% movement speed for 30 seconds and become invisible for 3 seconds. The movement speed stacks up to 20 times.

### Pieces
The set consists of armor of all weights and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.

### Drop Locations

### Notes
- Once the Dark Brotherhood skill Shadowy Supplier has been unlocked, Remains-Silent (located in Outlaw's Refuges across Tamriel) will occasionally give a random set piece when dialogue option "Have any equipment today?" is selected.

## Old versions
