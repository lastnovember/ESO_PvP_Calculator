# Online:Leviathan

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Leviathan
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Leviathan is a dungeon set that drops in the Crypt of Hearts I and II. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Barbaric Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
3 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
4 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
5 items: Adds 35-1528 Critical Chance

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Athor's Sundering Voice, unique loot from Ilambris Athor and Zaven.
- Band of Bone Cracking, unique loot from the Chamber Guardian.
- Bonehammer, unique loot from Death's Leviathan.
- Pulsing Dremora Ring, unique loot from Dogas the Berserker.
- Ruzozuzalpamaz's Charged Barb, unique loot from Ruzozuzalpamaz.
