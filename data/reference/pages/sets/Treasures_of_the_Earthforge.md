# Online:Treasures of the Earthforge

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Treasures_of_the_Earthforge
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Treasures of the Earthforge is an item set. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Daedric Style. These items no longer drop in-game, but those who received them when they were available still have them.

### Bonuses

3 items: Adds 34-1487 Armor

### Pieces
The set consists of 2 pieces, a maul and a pair of rings. Since both weapons are two-handed, only 3 pieces can be used together.
- Forge Master's Ring
- The Ebon Sledge

At one point, a second ring and maul were datamined, but these items are no longer in the database and it is unknown if they ever appeared in-game:
- Malacath's Ring
- Volendrung
