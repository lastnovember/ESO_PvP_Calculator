# Online:Vesture of Darloc Brae

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Vesture_of_Darloc_Brae
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Vesture of Darloc Brae is an overland set that drops in Northern Elsweyr. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Anequina Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Magicka Recovery
3 items: Adds 3-129 Stamina Recovery
4 items: Reduces the radius you can be detected while Sneaking by 2 meters. Reduces the cost of Sneak by 0-10%.
5 items: While you are crouching and not Bracing, you restore 15-670 Magicka and Stamina and heal for 15-670 Health every second.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Riverhold Dragonfeller, from The Battle for Riverhold
- Usurper Slayer, from Two Queens
- Blade of the Betrayer, from Cadwell the Betrayer
- Blade of the Rawlith Khaj, from The Usurper Queen
- Moon Sentinel's Arc, from Jode's Core
- Serpent Strand, from Strange Messengers
- Uncremated Necropolis Buckle, from Relatively Speaking
- Hidden Moon Band, from Path of the Hidden Moon
- Athlete's Treads, from Hunting the Hunters
- Vault-Cracker's Vizard, from Thick as Thieves
- Daegonite Jerkin, from Iron in the Blood
- Orcrest Quarantine Spaulders, from Unhealthy Preoccupation
- Skein-Woven Grips, from An Animal's Grim Abode
