# Online:Varen's Legacy

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Varen's_Legacy
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Varen's Legacy is one of the craftable sets providing special bonuses to armor and weapons. It is available for crafting to players pursuing blacksmithing, clothing, woodworking, and/or jewelry crafting professions. The special crafting site with stations dedicated to creation of this set is located in Gold CoastDark Brotherhood.

To make an item for this set, a crafter must first complete research on seven traits for this particular item at a corresponding crafting station.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
4 items: Adds 4% Healing Taken
5 items: When you Block, your next direct damage area of effect attack used within 5 seconds deals an additional 76-3290 damage. This bonus does not work with channeled effects. This effect can occur once every 2 seconds.

### Crafting Site
Strid River Artisans Camp
