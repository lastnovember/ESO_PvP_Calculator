# Online:Scathing Mage

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Scathing_Mage
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Scathing Mage is a dungeon set that drops in the Imperial City Prison, as well as being found in the Daedric Shackle Trophy Vault. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Xivkyn Style in Light Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
3 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
4 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
5 items: When you deal direct damage, you have a 20% chance to increase your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage by 12-516 for 5 seconds. This effect can occur once every 5 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Abomination's Clout, unique loot from the Flesh Abomination.
- Subjugation Fist, unique loot from the Overfiend.
- Warden's Trust, unique loot from Lord-Warden's Council.
