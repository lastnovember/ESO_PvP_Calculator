# Online:Prophet's

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Prophet's
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Prophet's is a jewelry item set. It can be acquired from the Level Up Advisor at levels 9, 15, 25, and 41, you have a choice of set weapons in fine, superior, epic, and superior quality, respectively. At level 33, you have a choice of superior set chest armor, and at level 47, you have a choice of superior set rings. Note that while the full bonus of this set is received from only one item, it is still technically a set, and this means that you cannot receive this bonus more than once, even if you equip more than one of these items. It cannot be crafted.

### Bonuses

1 item: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
