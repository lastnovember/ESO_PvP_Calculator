# Online:Armor Master

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Armor_Master
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Armor Master is one of the craftable sets providing special bonuses to armor and weapons. It is available for crafting to players pursuing blacksmithing, clothing, woodworking, and/or jewelry crafting professions. The special crafting site with stations dedicated to creation of this set are located in the Imperial CityImperial City.

To make an item for this set, a crafter must first complete research on nine traits for this particular item at a corresponding crafting station.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
3 items: Adds 34-1487 Armor
4 items: Adds 34-1487 Armor
5 items: While you have an Armor ability slotted, your Max Health is increased by 5%. When you use an Armor ability while in combat, your Physical Resistance and Spell Resistance is increased by 138-5940 for 10 seconds.

Memorial Armory
