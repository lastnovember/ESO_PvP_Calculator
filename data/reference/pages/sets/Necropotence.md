# Online:Necropotence

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Necropotence
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Necropotence is an overland set that drops in Rivenspire. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Argonian Style in Light Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
4 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
5 items: While you have a pet active, your Max Magicka is increased by 72-3132.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Barrow Hammer of Necropotence, the quest reward for Crimes of the Past
- Corpse-Caller's Fury of Necropotence, a unique drop from Leidmir Corpse-Caller
- Edrald's Wedding Veil of Necropotence, the quest reward for A Change of Heart
- Garth's Gages, the quest reward for The Spider's Cocoon
- Heloise's Staff of Necropotence, the quest reward for Under Siege
- Magister's Staff, the quest reward for Ancient Power
- Miremonwe's Magicka Pendant of Necropotence, a unique drop from Miremonwe Spellslinger
- Reezal-Jul's Necrotic Robes of Necropotence, the quest reward for The Blood-Cursed Town
- Siren's Call of Necropotence, a unique drop from Desuuga the Siren
- Stibbons's Fancy Epaulettes of Necropotence, the quest reward for The Emerald Chalice
- Stroda's Orcish Coif of Necropotence, a unique drop from Stroda gra-Drom
