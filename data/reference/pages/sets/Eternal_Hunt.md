# Online:Eternal Hunt

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Eternal_Hunt
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Eternal Hunt is one of the craftable sets providing special bonuses to armor and weapons. It is available for crafting to players pursuing blacksmithing, clothing, woodworking, and/or jewelry crafting professions. The special crafting site with stations dedicated to creation of this set are located in the Hew's BaneThieves Guild.

To make an item for this set, a crafter must first complete research on nine traits for this particular item at a corresponding crafting station.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
3 items: Adds 3-129 Stamina Recovery
4 items: Adds 3-129 Stamina Recovery
5 items: When you use Roll Dodge, you leave behind a rune that detonates when an enemy comes close, dealing 992 Poison Damage and immobilizing them for 1.5 seconds. This effect scales off the higher of your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage.

### Crafting Site
The Lost Pavilion
