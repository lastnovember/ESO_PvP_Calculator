# Online:Zoal the Ever-Wakeful (set)

image of proc effect (tentacles)Zoal the Ever-Wakeful

Zoal the Ever-Wakeful is a monster helm set. It consists of two armor pieces: head and shoulder, both of which are available in all three armor weights and standard traits. The set cannot be crafted.

### Bonuses

1 item: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
2 items: When you Break Free, you release a wave of Watcher energy, causing enemies within 8 meters of you to become Feared for 3 seconds. You also gain 0-34 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage for each enemy hit, up to 6 enemies, for 7 seconds.

### Drop Locations
- The head drops from Zoal the Ever-Wakeful in the Imperial City Elven Gardens District.
- The shoulders can be obtained from the Tel Var grand armorer in your alliance sewer headquarters for 20,000 or from the vendor in the Planar Armor Trophy Vault.
