# Online:Rallying Cry

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Rallying_Cry
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Rallying Cry is an Elite Gear Set that was formerly available from "Rewards for the Worthy" mails, earned by participating in PvP, prior to Update 39. It can now be obtained in boxes purchased using Alliance Points from the Cropsford Quartermaster, as quest rewards in Cropsford, as a reward for completing delves and dolmens throughout Cyrodiil, from completing Board Missions, and from Elite Gear Vendors. It cannot be crafted and does not drop from monsters. Pieces are Aldmeri Dominion, Daggerfall Covenant, or Ebonheart Pact Style in Light Armor, depending on the player's alliance.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
3 items: Adds 9-424 Critical Resistance
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: When your healing critically strikes while Battle Spirit is active, you and group members within 12 meters of you gain 22-986 Critical Resistance and 6-300 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage for 20 seconds. Each group member affected reduces the Weapon Damage and Spell Damage by 0-15 and Critical Resistance by 1-50. This effect can occur once every 15 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.

## Notes
- Rallying Cry effects neither stack nor are they additive. If an allied "Rallying Cry" effect is applied to you while you're already under the effects of one, it only refreshes the duration, and you will always get the bonuses of the strongest one.
- Triggering the "Rallying Cry" effect will stop any emote you are performing.
