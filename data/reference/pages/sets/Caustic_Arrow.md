# Online:Caustic Arrow

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Caustic_Arrow
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Caustic Arrow is a Master's Weapons item set found in normal Dragonstar Arena. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are in the unobtainable tier 5 Wood Elf Style. The perfected version of this set can be looted on veteran difficulty.

### Bonuses

2 items: Increases your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage by 7-330 against targets affected by your Poison Arrow.

### Pieces
The set consists of a Bow.

### Notes
- While the description states that 2 items are required, this is because bows are 2-handed weapons, and thus count as 2 items by themselves, so only 1 item is actually needed.
