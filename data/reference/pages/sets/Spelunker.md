# Online:Spelunker

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Spelunker
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Spelunker is a dungeon set that drops in Spindleclutch I and II. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Mercenary Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
3 items: Adds 3-129 Stamina Recovery
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: Reduces the cost of your Undaunted abilities by 10%. When an ally uses one of your Undaunted ability synergies you restore 16-730 Magicka and Stamina. This effect can occur once every 5 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique items include:
- Aegis of Meridia, a quest reward for Blood Relations.
- Axe of Sacrifice, unique loot from Vorenor Winterbourne.
- Bloodfury, unique loot from the Blood Spawn.
- Gilded Choker, unique loot from Cerise the Widow-Maker.
- Nightmare, unique loot from Praxin Douare.
- Spelunker's Chausses of the Spider-Slayer, a quest reward for Deadly Whispers.
- Whisperwoe, unique loot from The Whisperer.
