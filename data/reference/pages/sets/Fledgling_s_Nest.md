# Online:Fledgling's Nest

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Fledgling's_Nest
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Fledgling's Nest is a dungeon set that drops in Lep Seclusa. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Militant Monk Style in Light Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Magicka Recovery
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
4 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
5 items: While in combat, casting an ability that leaves an effect on the ground creates an 8 meter Gryphon Nest for 10 seconds. You and group members inside the Nest gain 168 Magicka and Stamina Recovery. The first time you or a group member leaves the Nest, they gain Minor Courage for 10 seconds, increasing their Weapon Damage and Spell Damage by 215. This effect can occur once every 10 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
