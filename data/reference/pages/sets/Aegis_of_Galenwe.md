# Online:Aegis of Galenwe

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Aegis_of_Galenwe
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Aegis of Galenwe is a trial set found in normal Cloudrest. It cannot be crafted. Set pieces are Welkynar style in Heavy Armor. The perfected version of this set can be looted on veteran difficulty.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
3 items: Gain Minor Aegis at all times, reducing your damage taken from Dungeon, Trial, and Arena Monsters by 5%.
4 items: Adds 4% Healing Taken
5 items: When you Block, you grant Empower to 11 other group members within 15 meters for 3 seconds, increasing the damage of their Heavy Attacks against monsters by 150%. This effect can occur once every 2 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
