# Online:Druid's Braid

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Druid's_Braid
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Druid's Braid is one of the craftable sets providing special bonuses to armor and weapons. It is available for crafting to players pursuing blacksmithing, clothing, woodworking, and/or jewelry crafting professions. The special crafting site with stations dedicated to the creation of this set is located at the Hidden Foundry on Amenos.

To make an item for this set, a crafter must first complete research on seven traits for that particular item at a corresponding crafting station.

### Bonuses

1 item: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
4 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
5 items: Adds 36-1565 Maximum Magicka
6 items: Adds 36-1565 Maximum Stamina
7 items: Adds 40-1722 Maximum Health
8 items: Adds 36-1565 Maximum Magicka
9 items: Adds 36-1565 Maximum Stamina
10 items: Adds 40-1722 Maximum Health
11 items: Adds 36-1565 Maximum Magicka
12 items: Adds 36-1565 Maximum Stamina

### Crafting Site
Hidden Foundry
