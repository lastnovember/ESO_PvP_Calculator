# Online:Thunder Caller

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Thunder_Caller
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Thunder Caller is a dungeon set that drops in Red Petal Bastion. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Silver Rose Style in Light Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 34-1487 Offensive Penetration
3 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: Dealing damage with a fully-charged Heavy Attack calls a bolt of lightning at your target, dealing 467 Shock Damage and leaving a 4 meter lightning crater at their location for 7 seconds, dealing 467 Shock Damage per second to enemies inside. This effect can occur once every 12 seconds and scales off the higher of your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
