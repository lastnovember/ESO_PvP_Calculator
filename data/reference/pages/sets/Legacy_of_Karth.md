# Online:Legacy of Karth

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Legacy_of_Karth
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Legacy of Karth is one of the craftable sets providing special bonuses to armor and weapons. It is available for crafting to players pursuing blacksmithing, clothing, woodworking, and/or jewelry crafting professions. The special crafting site with stations dedicated to creation of this set are located in The ReachMarkarth.

To make an item for this set, a crafter must first complete research on six traits for this particular item at a corresponding crafting station.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
3 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
4 items: Adds 34-1487 Offensive Penetration
5 items: Enemies affected by your alchemic poisons take 285 Disease damage every 1 second. This effect scales off the higher of your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage.

### Crafting Site
Druadach Redoubt
