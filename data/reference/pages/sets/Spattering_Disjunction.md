# Online:Spattering Disjunction

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Spattering_Disjunction
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Spattering Disjunction is a Class set found in the Infinite Archive. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Reawakened Hierophant Style in all weights. It buffs Arcanist's ability, most importantly the Herald of the Tome skill line for Arcanists.

### Bonuses

Class: Arcanist
2 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
3 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: Dealing damage with an Arcanist ability applies two random status effects to your target and generates a Crux. This effect can occur once every 7 seconds. Dealing damage with a Herald of the Tome ability reduces this cooldown by 0.5 seconds, up to once every 1 second. Increases your damage done with status effects by 40%.

### Pieces
The set consists of armor of all weights and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
