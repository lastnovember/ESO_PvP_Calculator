# Online:Burning Spellweave

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Burning_Spellweave
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Burning Spellweave is a dungeon set that drops in the City of Ash I and II. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Daedric Style in Light Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
3 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
4 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
5 items: When you deal damage with a Flame Damage ability, you apply the Burning status effect to the enemy and increase your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage by 11-490 for 8 seconds. These effects can occur once every 12 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Fire Maw's Fang, unique loot from Horvantud the Fire Maw.
- Scamplord's Signet, unique loot from Golor the Banekin Handler.
- Staff of the Grove, unique loot from the Warden of the Shrine.
- Rukhan's Rugged Kilt, unique loot from Rukhan.
