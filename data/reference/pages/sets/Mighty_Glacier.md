# Online:Mighty Glacier

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Mighty_Glacier
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Mighty Glacier is a dungeon set that drops in Frostvault. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Coldsnap Style in Heavy Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
4 items: Adds 3-129 Stamina Recovery
4 items: Adds 3-129 Magicka Recovery
5 items: When you activate a snare or immobilize ability or hit a target with a snare or immobilize ability, you and up to 11 group members gain Major Resolve for 15 seconds, increasing your Physical Resistance and Spell Resistances by 5948. This effect can occur once every 10 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists of Heavy Armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
