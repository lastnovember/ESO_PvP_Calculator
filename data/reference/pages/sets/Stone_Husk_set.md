Stone Husk

Stone Husk is a monster helm set. It consists of two armor pieces: head and shoulder, both of which are available in all three armor weights and standard traits. The set cannot be crafted.

### Bonuses

1 item: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
2 items: Your Heavy Attacks tether you to enemies for 5 seconds, as long as you remain within 10 meters of them. Enemies touching the tether take 212 Physical damage every 1 second and you gain a stack of Husk Drain, up to 15 stacks. This effect can occur once every 10 seconds and scales off the higher of your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage. When the tether ends, you consume the stacks and gain 0-30 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage per stack for 5 seconds.

### Drop Locations
- The head drops from Arkasis the Mad Alchemist in Veteran Stone Garden.
- The shoulders can be obtained from Urgarlag Chief-bane's reward chest at Undaunted Enclaves.

### Notes
- Tethers are considered Area of Effect damage and are reduced by Major/Minor Evasion.
