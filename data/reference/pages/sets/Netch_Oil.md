# Online:Netch Oil

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Netch_Oil
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Netch Oil is a Battlegrounds Set rewarded by Battlegrounds. It cannot be crafted and does not drop from monsters. Pieces are Militant Ordinator Style in Heavy Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
3 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
4 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
5 items: While you have Crowd Control Immunity, you are immune to Snares and Immobilizations.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
