# Online:Deadlands Demolisher

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Deadlands_Demolisher
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Deadlands Demolisher is one of the craftable sets providing special bonuses to armor and weapons. It is available for crafting to players pursuing blacksmithing, clothing, woodworking, and/or jewelry crafting professions. The special crafting site with stations dedicated to creation of this set are located at The Razorworks in The DeadlandsThe Deadlands.

To make an item for this set, a crafter must first complete research on seven traits for this particular item at a corresponding crafting station.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
3 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
4 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
5 items: Your Bash attacks deal 935 more damage. When you Bash, you also deal 0 Physical damage in a cone in front of you, interrupting any enemies hit. This effect can occur once every 2 seconds and counts as Bash damage.

### Crafting Site
The Razorworks

## Notes
- The death recap icon for this set is: Deadlands Demolisher
