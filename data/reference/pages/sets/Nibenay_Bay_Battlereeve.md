# Online:Nibenay Bay Battlereeve

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Nibenay_Bay_Battlereeve
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Nibenay Bay Battlereeve is an Elite Gear Set. Set pieces can be obtained from the Elite Gear Vendors for 50,000. It cannot be crafted and does not drop from monsters. Pieces are Thane of Falkreath Style in all armor weights.

### Bonuses

1 item: Adds 9-424 Critical Resistance
2 items: When you are forcefully moved via a Pull, Knockback or Teleport ability, gain a damage shield that absorbs up to 15112 damage for 6 seconds. This damage shield is not affected by Battle Spirit. This effect can occur once every 14 seconds. While you do not have the damage shield from this set, reduce your damage taken from players by 5%.

### Pieces
The set consists of head pieces and shoulder pieces of all weights.
