The Stonekeeper

Stonekeeper is a monster helm set. It consists of two armor pieces: head and shoulder, both of which are available in all three armor weights and standard traits. The set cannot be crafted. There is an outfit style based on this armor set.

### Bonuses

1 item: Adds 12-548 Maximum Stamina
1 item: Adds 12-548 Maximum Magicka
1 item: Adds 14-603 Maximum Health
2 items: When you Block, you gain an energy Charge stack. When you gain 6 Charges, you release the energy, restoring 57-2480 Stamina and Magicka, and healing for 57-2480. After releasing the Charges, you cannot regain new Charges for 14 seconds. Charges can be accumulated up to once every.25 seconds.

### Drop Locations
- The head drops from The Stonekeeper in Veteran Frostvault.
- The shoulders can be obtained from Urgarlag Chief-bane's chest at Undaunted Enclaves.
