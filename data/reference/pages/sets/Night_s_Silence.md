# Online:Night's Silence

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Night's_Silence
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Night's Silence is one of the craftable sets providing special bonuses to armor and weapons. It is available for crafting to players pursuing blacksmithing, clothing, woodworking, and/or jewelry crafting professions. One special crafting site with stations dedicated to creation of this set is located in the first zone of each of the three Alliances.

To make an item for this set, a crafter must first complete research on two traits for this particular item at a corresponding crafting station.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
3 items: Adds 3-129 Stamina Recovery
4 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
5 items: Ignore the Movement Speed penalty of Sneak.
