# Online:Might of the Lost Legion

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Might_of_the_Lost_Legion
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Might of the Lost Legion is one of the craftable sets providing special bonuses to armor and weapons. It is available for crafting to players pursuing blacksmithing, clothing, woodworking, and/or jewelry crafting professions. The special crafting site with stations dedicated to creation of this set are located in MurkmireMurkmire.

To make an item for this set, a crafter must first complete research on four traits for this particular item at a corresponding crafting station.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
5 items: When you Block, you gain Empower, increasing the damage of your Heavy Attacks against monsters by 150% for 3 seconds.

### Crafting Site
Ruined Village
