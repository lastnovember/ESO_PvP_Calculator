# Online:Lady Malygda (set)

set effectLady Malygda

Lady Malygda is a monster helm set found in the Imperial City. It consists of two armor pieces: head and shoulder, both of which are available in all three armor weights and standard traits. The set cannot be crafted.

### Bonuses

1 item: Adds 34-1487 Offensive Penetration
2 items: When you deal direct damage to a target within 12 meters, create a 5 meter area that after 1 second, quickly moves forward and then returns, dealing 811 Shock damage to enemies and applying Minor Enervation for 5 seconds, causing the target to deal 10% less Critical Damage. This effect can occur once every 15 seconds and scales off the higher of your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage.

### Drop Locations
- The head drops from Lady Malygda in the Imperial City Arboretum District or from the chest in the Clawed Trophy Vault.
- The shoulders can be obtained from the Tel Var grand armorer in your alliance sewer headquarters for 20,000.
