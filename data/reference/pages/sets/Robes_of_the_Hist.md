# Online:Robes of the Hist

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Robes_of_the_Hist
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Robes of the Hist is an overland set that drops in Shadowfen. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Argonian Style in Light Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
3 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
4 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
5 items: After being hit by a disabling effect, immobilization, or snare, you heal for 46-2000 Health every 1 second for 5 seconds. The effect can apply once every second, but cannot stack.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Bogstaff, a quest reward.
- Chain of Hist's Power, a quest reward for The Dream of the Hist.
- Deepswamp Staff of Hist Sap, a unique drop from Fenlord.
- Wrap of My Enemy's Skin, a quest reward for The Skin-Stealer's Lair.
- Reveler's Robes
- Staff of Deadfire
