# Online:Assassin's Guile

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Assassin's_Guile
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Assassin's Guile is one of the craftable sets providing special bonuses to armor and weapons. It is available for crafting to players pursuing blacksmithing, clothing, woodworking, and/or jewelry crafting professions. The special crafting site with stations dedicated to creation of this set are located in the Vvardenfell.

To make an item for this set, a crafter must first complete research on three traits for this particular item at a corresponding crafting station.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
3 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
4 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
5 items: Increases the duration of your alchemical poisons by 4 seconds.

### Crafting Site
Marandus
