# Online:Netch's Touch

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Netch's_Touch
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Netch's Touch is a dungeon set that drops in Darkshade Caverns. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Barbaric Style in Light Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: Adds 9-400 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage to your Shock Damage abilities.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Alit Tongue Charm, a unique drop from the Transmuted Alits.
- Ancient Netch-Robes, a quest reward for What Was Lost.
- Deadly Pincer, a unique drop from the Hive Lord.
- Grobull's Charge, a unique drop from Grobull the Transmuted.
- Hive Lord's Shell, a unique drop from the Hive Lord.
- Llothan's Burning Crutch, a unique drop from Foreman Llothan.
- Lost Imperial Band, a unique drop from the Cutting Sphere.
