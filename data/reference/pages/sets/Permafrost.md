Permafrost (morph)
Permafrost is a set which drops in the Maelstrom Arena. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Reach Winter Style in Heavy Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
4 items: Adds 3-129 Magicka Recovery
5 items: While you have a damage shield on you, your Health Recovery is increased by 25-1106.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
