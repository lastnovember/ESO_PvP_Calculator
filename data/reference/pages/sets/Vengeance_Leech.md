# Online:Vengeance Leech

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Vengeance_Leech
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Vengeance Leech is an item set found in boxes purchased using Alliance Points from the Bruma Quartermaster, as quest rewards in Bruma, as a reward for completing delves and dolmens throughout Cyrodiil, from completing Board Missions, and from Elite Gear Vendors. It cannot be crafted and does not drop from monsters. Pieces are Aldmeri Dominion, Daggerfall Covenant, or Ebonheart Pact Style, depending on the player's alliance.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
3 items: When you kill an enemy, you heal for 64-2772 Health and restore 64-2772 Magicka and Stamina.

### Pieces
The set consists of jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
