# Online:Whitestrake's Retribution

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Whitestrake's_Retribution
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Whitestrake's Retribution is one of the craftable sets providing special bonuses to armor and weapons. It is available for crafting to players pursuing blacksmithing, clothing, woodworking, and/or jewelry crafting professions. One special crafting site with stations dedicated to creation of this set is located in the third zone of each of the three Alliances.

To make an item for this set, a crafter must first complete research on four traits for this particular item at a corresponding crafting station.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
3 items: Adds 34-1487 Armor
4 items: Adds 3-129 Health Recovery
5 items: When you take damage while you are under 30% Health, you gain a damage shield that absorbs 303-13035 damage for 6 seconds. This effect can occur once every 15 seconds.
