# Online:Kynmarcher's Cruelty

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Kynmarcher's_Cruelty
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Kynmarcher's Cruelty is an overland set that drops in the Deadlands and Fargrave. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Annihilarch's Chosen Style in Heavy Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
3 items: Adds 3-129 Stamina Recovery
4 items: Adds 34-1487 Armor
5 items: When you deal direct damage, you apply one of 5 random Major debuffs to enemies within 8 meters of you for 18 seconds. This effect can occur once every 8 seconds and only if an enemy is in range. Eligible effects are Major Breach, Major Cowardice, Major Defile, Major Maim, and Major Vulnerability.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
