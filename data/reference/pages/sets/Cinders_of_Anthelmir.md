# Online:Cinders of Anthelmir

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Cinders_of_Anthelmir
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Cinders of Anthelmir is a dungeon set found in Oathsworn Pit. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are The Recollection Style in Light Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 34-1487 Offensive Penetration
3 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: Dealing damage calls forth a Cindermoth to chase your target for 8 seconds. If your target dies before being reached, the Cindermoth seeks a new target within 8 meters. When the Cindermoth nears any enemy within 3 meters, it bursts after 1 second, dealing 1283 Flame damage and Burning enemies around it. This effect reveals you, can occur once every 8 seconds, scales off your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage, and is considered pet damage.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
