# Online:Cinders of Anthelmir

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Cinders_of_Anthelmir
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Cinders of Anthelmir is a dungeon set found in Oathsworn Pit. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are The Recollection Style in Light Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 34-1487 Offensive Penetration
3 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: On dealing damage, summon a Cindermoth that chases your target without mercy for 8 seconds. If your target dies before the moth reaches them, it seeks a new target within 8 meters. The first enemy that nears the Cindermoth causes it to stop and burst after 1 second, dealing 1283 Flame damage and Burning enemies within 8 meters. This can occur once every 8 seconds and scales off your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
