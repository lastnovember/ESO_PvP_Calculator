# Online:Blackrose Weapons

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Blackrose_Weapons
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Blackrose Weapons are a special weapon set that drops in the Blackrose Prison arena. All weapons bear the Honor Guard Style.

## Types
Blackrose weapons are available in two types - normal and perfected. Perfected versions of weapons are slightly more powerful, by virtue of bearing extra stats, longer damage effects or similar bonuses.

- Normal weapons can drop from the last boss of the Arena on normal difficulty.
- The Perfected versions of these weapons only drop in Veteran Mode or can be awarded to players who successfully rank on the weekly Arena leaderboard.
