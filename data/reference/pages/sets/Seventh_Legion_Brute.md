# Online:Seventh Legion Brute

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Seventh_Legion_Brute
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Seventh Legion Brute is an overland set that drops in Bangkorai. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Orcish Style in Heavy Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
3 items: Adds 3-129 Health Recovery
4 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
5 items: When you cast an ability that grants Major or Minor Resolve while in combat, you gain 7-341 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage and 7-341 Health Recovery for 15 seconds. This effect can occur every 15 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- A Cheap Trinket, the quest reward for Present in Memory.
- Cuirass of the Defender, the quest reward for Beyond the Call in the Breton Style.
- Greatshield of Bangkorai, the quest reward for Storming the Garrison in the Breton Style.
- Greaves of Evermore, the quest reward for Imperial Infiltration in the Breton Style.
- Seventh Legion Gauntlets, the quest reward for Rising Against Onsi's Breath.
- Shore's Edge, the quest reward for To Walk on Far Shores in the Redguard Style.
- Seventh Legion's Ayleid Breastplate, a unique drop from Qumehdi and Zaman at Telesubi Ruins in the Altmer Style.
- Seventh Legion's Conjured Oath-Band, a unique drop from the world bosses Caecilia Attius and Watcher Tyrant at Summoner's Camp.
- Seventh Legion's Lava-Charred Greaves, a unique drop from the world boss Dayarrus at Arlimahera's Sanctum in the Altmer Style.
- Seventh Legion's The Wolfguard, a unique drop from Curnard the Generous, the final boss of Viridian Watch in the Breton Style.
- The Negotiator, the quest reward for The Parley in the Breton Style.
