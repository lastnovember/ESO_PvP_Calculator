# Online:Perfected Arms of Relequen

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Perfected_Arms_of_Relequen
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Perfected Arms of Relequen is a trial set which drops in veteran Cloudrest. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Welkynar Style in Medium Armor. The non-perfected version of this set can be looted on normal difficulty.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
3 items: Gain Minor Slayer at all times, increasing your damage done to Dungeon, Trial, and Arena Monsters by 5%.
4 items: Adds 34-1487 Offensive Penetration
5 perfected items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: Your Light and Heavy attacks apply a stack of harmful winds to your target for 5 seconds, reduced to 1 second against players. Harmful winds deal 51 Physical damage per stack every 1 second. This effect can stack once every 0.5 seconds, up to 10 times, and scales off the higher of your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
