# Online:Lustrous Soulwell

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Lustrous_Soulwell
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Lustrous Soulwell is a dungeon set that drops in Black Gem Foundry. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Black Soul Gem Style in Light Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Health Recovery
3 items: Adds 3-129 Magicka Recovery
4 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
5 items: When you use an Ultimate ability while in combat, you and the closest 3 group members within 28 meters of you gain 465 Health Recovery, Magicka Recovery, and Stamina Recovery that lasts for 2 seconds for each 20 Ultimate spent.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
