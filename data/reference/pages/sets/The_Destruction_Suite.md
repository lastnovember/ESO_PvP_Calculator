# Online:The Destruction Suite

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:The_Destruction_Suite
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Destruction Suite is an item set. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Daedric Style. These items no longer drop in-game, but those who received them when they were available still have them.

### Bonuses

3 items: Adds 34-1487 Armor

### Pieces
The set consists of rings and destruction staves.
- Arsonist's Staff
- Levin Master's Ring
- Ring of Blue Ice
- Ring of Embers
- Staff of Everfrost
- Staff of Fulmination

### Drop Locations
- Veteran Banished Cells, Veteran Spindleclutch, Veteran Fungal Grotto, Veteran Wayrest Sewers, Veteran Elden Hollow, and Veteran Darkshade Caverns.
