# Online:Pestilent Host

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Pestilent_Host
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Pestilent Host is a set which drops in the Vateshran Hollows. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Ancestral Reach Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 34-1487 Offensive Penetration
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: Adds 34-1487 Offensive Penetration
5 items: Whenever you kill an enemy afflicted with a poison effect, their corpse releases a writhing mass of Fetcherflies, which seek out the nearest enemy within 12 meters, causing them to bleed for 5850 Bleed damage over 10 seconds. This effect scales off the higher of your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
