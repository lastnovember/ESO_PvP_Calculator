# Online:Phylactery's Grasp

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Phylactery's_Grasp
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Phylactery's Grasp is a dungeon set that drops in Graven Deep. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Drowned Mariner Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
3 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
4 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
5 items: On Light Attack Critical Damage, you apply Withered Soul, dealing 1500 Disease damage over 10 seconds. On Light Attack Critical Damage on an enemy with Withered Soul, restore 13-575 Stamina. Withered Soul can occur once every 10 seconds and the damage scales off the higher of your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
