# Online:Storm Knight's Plate

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Storm_Knight's_Plate
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Storm Knight's Plate is an overland set that drops in Stormhaven. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Breton Style in Heavy Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 34-1487 Armor
3 items: Adds 34-1487 Armor
4 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
5 items: When you take non-Physical Damage, you deal 357 Shock damage to all enemies within 5 meters of you every 2 seconds for 6 seconds. This effect can occur once every 6 seconds and scales off the higher of your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:

- Carapace-Shaped Heater of the Storm Knight
- The High King's War Axe
- Azura's Vigilance
