# Online:Heroic Unity

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Heroic_Unity
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Heroic Unity is a dungeon set that drops in Lep Seclusa. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Militant Monk Style in Heavy Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
3 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
4 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
5 items: Activating a Synergy gives you and up to 3 group members within 12 meters Major Heroism for 6 seconds, granting 3 Ultimate every 1.5 seconds. This effect can occur once every 1 second.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
