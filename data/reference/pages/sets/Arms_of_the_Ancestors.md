# Online:Arms of the Ancestors

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Arms_of_the_Ancestors
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Arms of the Ancestors is an item set. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Daedric Style. These items no longer drop in-game, but those who received them when they were available still have them.

### Bonuses

3 items: Adds 34-1487 Offensive Penetration

### Pieces
The set consists of rings and a bow.
- Auriel's Bow
- Auriel's Ring
- Tenth Ring of Trinimac
- White Bow of Alinor
