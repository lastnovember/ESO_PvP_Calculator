# Online:Sword-Singer

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Sword-Singer
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Sword-Singer is an overland set that drops in Alik'r Desert. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Redguard Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
3 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
4 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
5 items: Adds 13-600 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage to your Two Handed abilities.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as two-handed melee weapons of all types (other weapons and shields may still exist but cannot be obtained new anymore). Unique pieces include:
- Suturah's Hubris, the quest reward for Amputating the Hand.
- Wildur's Oath-Ring, the quest reward for Whose Wedding?
- Throne Keeper Coif, the quest reward for A Reckoning with Uwafa.
- Arachnid Gauntlets, the quest reward for Crawling Chaos.
- Mantle of Honor, the quest reward for Trials of the Hero.
- Curse-Breaker's Boots, the quest reward for Past in Ruins.
- Mauloch's Cleaver, the quest reward for The Oldest Orc.
- Sword-Singer's Sandgrubber Boots, a unique drop from Tarrent Herano, the final boss of Santaki.
- Sword-Singer's Backscratcher, a unique drop from the world boss Lonely Papa.
