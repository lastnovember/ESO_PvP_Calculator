# Online:Tzogvin's Warband

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Tzogvin's_Warband
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Tzogvin's Warband is a dungeon set that drops in Frostvault. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Coldsnap Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
3 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
4 items: Adds 34-1487 Offensive Penetration
5 items: When you deal Critical Damage, you gain a stack of Precision, increasing your Critical Chance by 4-177 for 5 seconds, up to 10 stacks max. At max stacks, you also gain Minor Force, increasing your Critical Damage done by 10%. This effect can occur once every half second.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
