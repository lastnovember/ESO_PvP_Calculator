# Online:Senche's Bite

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Senche's_Bite
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Senche's Bite is an overland set that drops in Reaper's March. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Khajiit Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Stamina Recovery
3 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
4 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
5 items: Whenever you successfully Dodge, increase your Critical Damage and Critical Healing by 0-15% for 10 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Limb-Render of the Senche, a unique drop from Limbrender.
- Fishbreath's Leathers of the Senche, a unique drop from Fishbreath.
- Niramo's Gravegloves of the Senche, a unique drop from Gravecaller Niramo.
- Crocmother Fang of the Senche, a unique drop from Ysolmarr the Roving Pyre.
- Great Blade of Thizzrini, the quest reward for The Champion Division.
