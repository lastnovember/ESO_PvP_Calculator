# Online:Grace of Gloom

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Grace_of_Gloom
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Grace of Gloom is an overland set that drops on Summerset. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Ebonshadow Style in heavy armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
3 items: Adds 4% Healing Taken
4 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
5 items: When you take damage, you become a living shadow for 10 seconds, causing you to heal for 927 every 2 seconds and gaining Major Evasion, reducing damage from area attacks by 20%. This effect can occur every 20 seconds and scales off your Max Health.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Ritemaster's Memorial Battlesword
- Targe of Foul Story
- Sea Keep Siege Cuirass
- Battlereeve's Abyssal Gauntlets
- Greaves of the Insatiable
- Ritemaster's Gifted Gardbraces
