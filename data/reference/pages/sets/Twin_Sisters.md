# Online:Twin Sisters

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Twin_Sisters
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Twin Sisters is an overland set that drops in Auridon. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are High Elf Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Stamina Recovery
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
4 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
5 items: When you Block, you cause all enemies within 5 meters of you to bleed for 1932 Bleed Damage over 10 seconds. This effect can occur once every 10 seconds and scales off the higher of your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Divine-Touched Aegis, the quest reward for Blessings of the Eight.
- Eaglebanner Longbow, the quest reward for Rites of the Queen.
- First Mate's Leathers, the quest reward for The Serpent's Beacon.
- Queen's Eye Leathers, the quest reward for The Unveiling.
- Sea-Raider's Hood of the Twin Sisters, a unique drop from the world boss Quenyas at Seaside Scarp Camp.
- Sea Serpent's Tooth of the Twin Sisters, a unique drop from Aluvus, the final boss of Ondil.
- Serpent-Rider Spaulders of the Twin Sisters, the quest reward for Eye of the Ancients in the Dark Elf Style.
- Spitescarred Leggings of the Twin Sisters, a unique drop from Mati, the final boss of Mehrunes' Spite in the Wood Elf Style.
- Veiled Executioner of the Twin Sisters, a unique drop from Polinus, the final boss of Del's Claim.
