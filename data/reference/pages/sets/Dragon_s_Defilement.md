# Online:Dragon's Defilement

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Dragon's_Defilement
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Dragon's Defilement is a dungeon set that drops in the Lair of Maarselok. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Stags of Z'en Style in Heavy Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
4 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
5 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
5 items: When you take damage from a melee attack, gain a corrupting aura for 5 seconds that applies Minor Breach to all enemies within 8 meters of you, reducing their Physical Resistance and Spell Resistance by 2974. This effect can occur every 5 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
