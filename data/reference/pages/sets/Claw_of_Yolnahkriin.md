# Online:Claw of Yolnahkriin

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Claw_of_Yolnahkriin
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Claw of Yolnahkriin is a trial set found in normal Sunspire. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are in Sunspire style in Heavy Armor. The perfected version of this set can be looted on veteran difficulty.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
3 items: Gain Minor Aegis at all times, reducing your damage taken from Dungeon, Trial, and Arena Monsters by 5%.
4 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
5 items: When you taunt an enemy, you give yourself and 11 group members Minor Courage for 15 seconds, increasing your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage by 215. This effect can occur once every 8 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.

### Notes
- This set and Perfected Claw of Yolnahkriin are the only item sets that offer Minor Courage to players. The Werewolf skill line Ultimate's Pack Leader morph also offers Minor Courage.
- This set has a hidden maximum range of 28 meters for applying Minor Courage to allies.
