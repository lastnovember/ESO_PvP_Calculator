# Online:Ravager

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Ravager
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Ravager is an item set found in boxes purchased using Alliance Points from the Vlastarus Quartermaster, as quest rewards in Vlastarus, as a reward for completing delves and dolmens throughout Cyrodiil, from completing Board Missions, and from Elite Gear Vendors. It cannot be crafted and does not drop from monsters. Pieces are Aldmeri Dominion, Daggerfall Covenant, or Ebonheart Pact Style in Heavy Armor, depending on the player's alliance.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
3 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: Each time you attempt to reduce the target's Physical Resistance or Spell Resistance, you gain a stack of Ravager for 5 seconds, increasing your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage by 3-146. You can gain a stack every 1 second. At 4 stacks, the duration doubles but cannot be refreshed.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
