The Engine Guardian

Engine Guardian is a monster helm set. It consists of two armor pieces: head and shoulder, both of which are available in all three armor weights and standard traits. The set cannot be crafted. There is an outfit style based on this armor set. There is also an opal outfit style based on this armor set.

### Bonuses

1 item: Adds 3-129 Health Recovery
2 items: When you use an ability that costs resources, you have a 25% chance to summon a Dwemer automaton to restore 12-550 Stamina or Magicka or 45-1955 Health to you every 0.5 seconds for 6 seconds. This effect can occur once every 10 seconds.

### Drop Locations
- The head drops from the Engine Guardian in Veteran Darkshade Caverns II.
- The shoulders can be obtained from Maj al-Ragath's reward chest at Undaunted Enclaves.
