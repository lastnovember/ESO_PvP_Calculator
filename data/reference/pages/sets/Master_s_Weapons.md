# Online:Master's Weapons

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Master's_Weapons
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Master's Weapons are a special weapon set that drops in the Dragonstar Arena. All weapons bear a different Tier 5 Racial Style. Master's weapons are available by defeating the last boss of the Dragonstar Arena on Normal difficulty. The Perfected versions drop when completing the arena on Veteran difficulty.
