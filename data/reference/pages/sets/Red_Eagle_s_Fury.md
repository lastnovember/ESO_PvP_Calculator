# Online:Red Eagle's Fury

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Red_Eagle's_Fury
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Red Eagle's Fury is one of the craftable sets providing special bonuses to armor and weapons. It is available for crafting to players pursuing blacksmithing, clothing, woodworking, and/or jewelry crafting professions. The special crafting site with stations dedicated to creation of this set are located in The ReachMarkarth.

To make an item for this set, a crafter must first complete research on three traits for this particular item at a corresponding crafting station.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
3 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: Adds 10-469 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage to your Weapon Skill abilities. Increases the cost of your Weapon Skill abilities by 5%.

### Crafting Site
Red Eagle Redoubt
