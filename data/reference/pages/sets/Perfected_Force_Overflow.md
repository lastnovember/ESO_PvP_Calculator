# Online:Perfected Force Overflow

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Perfected_Force_Overflow
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Perfected Force Overflow is a Perfected Vateshran Weapons item set which drops in veteran Vateshran Hollows. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are based on the Ancestral Reach Style. The non-perfected version of this set can be looted on normal difficulty.

### Bonuses

2 perfected items: Adds 20-877 Maximum Magicka
2 items: Casting Force Siphon on a target within 15 meters of you causes a surge of magical energy to tether between you for 10 seconds. While the tether persists and you remain in range, you and allies in the tether restore 4-175 Magicka and Stamina every 1 second. This effect can occur once every 10 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists of a restoration staff.

### Notes
- While the description states that 2 items are required, this is because staves are 2-handed weapons, and thus count as 2 items by themselves, so only 1 item is actually needed.
- Tethers are considered Area of Effect.
