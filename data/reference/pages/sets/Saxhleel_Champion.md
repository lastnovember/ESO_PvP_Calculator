# Online:Saxhleel Champion

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Saxhleel_Champion
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Saxhleel Champion is a trial set which drops in normal Rockgrove. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Sul-Xan Style in Heavy Armor. The perfected version of this set can be looted on veteran difficulty.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
3 items: Gain Minor Aegis at all times, reducing your damage taken from Dungeon, Trial, and Arena Monsters by 5%.
4 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
5 items: When you use an Ultimate ability while in combat, you and up to 11 group members within 28 meters of you gain Major Force for 1 second per 15 Ultimate spent, increasing your Critical Damage done by 20%.

### Pieces
The set consists of heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
