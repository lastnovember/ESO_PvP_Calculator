# Online:Healing Mage

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Healing_Mage
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Healing Mage is a trial set which drops in the Aetherian Archive. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Celestial Style in Light Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Magicka Recovery
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
4 items: Adds 4% Healing Done
5 items: When you use an area of effect heal ability, you apply Minor Cowardice to enemies within 10 meters of you, reducing their Weapon Damage and Spell Damage by 215 for 5 seconds. This effect can occur once every 3 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
