# Online:Trial by Fire

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Trial_by_Fire
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Trial by Fire is one of the craftable sets providing special bonuses to armor and weapons. It is available for crafting to players pursuing blacksmithing, clothing, woodworking, and/or jewelry crafting professions. The special crafting site with stations dedicated to creation of this set are located in WrothgarOrsinium.

To make an item for this set, a crafter must first complete research on three traits for this particular item at a corresponding crafting station.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
3 items: Adds 34-1487 Armor
4 items: Adds 4% Healing Taken
5 items: While under the effect of an Elemental Status Effect you gain 197-8500 Armor.

### Crafting Site
Malacath Statue
