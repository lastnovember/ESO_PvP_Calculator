# Online:Adept Rider

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Adept_Rider
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Adept Rider is one of the craftable sets providing special bonuses to armor and weapons. It is available for crafting to players pursuing blacksmithing, clothing, woodworking, and/or jewelry crafting professions. The special crafting site with stations dedicated to creation of this set are located in Summerset Isle.

To make an item for this set, a crafter must first complete research on three traits for this particular item at a corresponding crafting station.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 34-1487 Armor
3 items: Adds 34-1487 Armor
4 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
5 items: Gain Gallop and Major Expedition at all times, increasing your Movement Speed by 30% and Mounted Speed by 15%.

### Crafting Site
Shimmerene Dockworks
