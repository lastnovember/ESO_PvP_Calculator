# Online:Haven of Ursus

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Haven_of_Ursus
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Haven of Ursus is a dungeon set that drops in the March of Sacrifices. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Huntsman Style in Heavy Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
3 items: Adds 4% Healing Taken
4 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
5 items: When you take damage while you are under 50% Health, summon Ursus's spirit for 6 seconds at a random group member. You or a group member can activate a synergy on Ursus to grant a shield that protects up to 6 group members within a 28 meter radius from 13967 damage for 6 seconds. This effect can occur once every 10 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists heavy armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
