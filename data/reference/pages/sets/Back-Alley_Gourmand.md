# Online:Back-Alley Gourmand

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Back-Alley_Gourmand
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Back-Alley Gourmand is an overland set that drops in Galen. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Firesong Style in Light Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
3 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
4 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: While you have a food buff active, your Critical Damage and Critical Healing is increased by 13%.

### Pieces
The set consists of Light Armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
