# Online:Perfected Slivers of the Null Arca

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Perfected_Slivers_of_the_Null_Arca
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Slivers of the Null Arca is a trial set found in normal Lucent Citadel. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are in Lucent Sentinel Style in Medium Armor. The non-perfected version of this set can be looted on Normal difficulty.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
3 items: Gain Minor Slayer at all times, increasing your damage done to Dungeon, Trial, and Arena Monsters by 5%.
4 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
5 perfected items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
5 items: Dealing Critical Damage gives you a stack of Sliver for 10 seconds, up to once every 0.5 seconds. Upon gaining your third stack, they merge into a crystal that launches at the last enemy you damaged, dealing 1952 Physical Damage. This effect scales off the higher of your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage, and you cannot gain Sliver for 4 seconds after the crystal is launched.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
