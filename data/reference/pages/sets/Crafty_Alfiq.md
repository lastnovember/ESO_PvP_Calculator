# Online:Crafty Alfiq

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Crafty_Alfiq
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Crafty Alfiq is an overland set found in Northern Elsweyr. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Pellitine Style in Light Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
4 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
5 items: Adds 59-2550 Maximum Magicka

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as shields and weapons of all types.
- Anequina Healer's Rod, received for completing In Sickness and In Health.
- Potentate's Staff of Storms, received for completing Descendant of the Potentate.
- Shard of the Crossing, received for completing The Moonlight Blade.
- Khajiit Scholar's Vestments, received for completing the Rithana-Di-Renada after The Singing Crystal.
- Royal Staff of Anequina, received for completing The Heir of Anequina.
