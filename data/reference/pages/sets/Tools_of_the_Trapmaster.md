# Online:Tools of the Trapmaster

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Tools_of_the_Trapmaster
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Tools of the Trapmaster is a dungeon set that drops in Naj-Caldeesh. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Voskrona Guardian Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
3 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
4 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
5 items: Dealing direct damage with a non-Heavy Attack grants you a stack of Trapmaster for 5 seconds, up to 4 stacks max. You can gain a stack every 1 second. Dealing damage with a fully-charged Heavy Attack consumes the stacks, applies Sundered, and deals 353 Physical damage per stack consumed.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
