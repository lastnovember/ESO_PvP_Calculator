# Online:Systres' Scowl

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Systres'_Scowl
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Systres' Scowl is an overland set that drops in High Isle and Amenos. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Systres Guardian Style in Heavy Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
3 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
4 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
5 items: When you Bash an enemy, place Systres' Scowl on them for 15 seconds. When hit by a Light Attack, a target with Systres' Scowl takes 45-1973 Frost Damage, up to once every second.

### Pieces
The set consists of Heavy Armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
