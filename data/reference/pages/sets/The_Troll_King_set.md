The Troll King

The Troll King is a monster helm set. It consists of two armor pieces: head and shoulder, both of which are available in all three armor weights and standard traits. The set cannot be crafted. There is an outfit style based on this armor set. There is also an opal outfit style based on this armor set.

### Bonuses

1 item: Adds 4% Healing Done
2 items: When you heal yourself or an ally, if they are still below 50% Health, their Health Recovery is increased by 21-925 for 10 seconds.

### Drop Locations
- The head drops from The Lava Queen in Veteran Blessed Crucible.
- The shoulders can be obtained from Glirion the Redbeard's chest in the Undaunted Enclaves.
