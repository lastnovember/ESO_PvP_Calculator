# Online:Spectral Cloak

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Spectral_Cloak
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Spectral Cloak is a Blackrose Weapons item set awarded from normal Blackrose Prison. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Honor Guard Style. The perfected version of this set can be looted on veteran difficulty.

### Bonuses

2 items: Activating Blade Cloak while in combat allows you to gain Spectral Cloak whenever Blade Cloak deals damage for 2 seconds, reducing your damage taken and increasing your damage done by 6%.

### Pieces
The set consists of one-handed weapons of all types. There is no shield.

## Notes
- You will always receive two weapons when this set drops, and they must be wielded together. If you use one with a shield or another weapon after getting Spectral Cloak, it will not provide any bonus.
