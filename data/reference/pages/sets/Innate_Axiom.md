# Online:Innate Axiom

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Innate_Axiom
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Innate Axiom is one of the craftable sets providing special bonuses to armor and weapons. It is available for crafting to players pursuing blacksmithing, clothing, woodworking, and/or jewelry crafting professions. The special crafting site with stations dedicated to creation of this set are located in the Clockwork CityClockwork City.

To make an item for this set, a crafter must first complete research on two traits for this particular item at a corresponding crafting station.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
4 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
5 items: Adds 9-400 Weapon Damage and Spell Damage to your Class abilities.

### Crafting Site
The Refurbishing Yard
