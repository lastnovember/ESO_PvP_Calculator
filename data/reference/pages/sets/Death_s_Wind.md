# Online:Death's Wind

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Death's_Wind
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Death's Wind is one of the craftable sets providing special bonuses to armor and weapons. It is available for crafting to players pursuing blacksmithing, clothing, woodworking, and/or jewelry crafting professions. One special crafting site with stations dedicated to creation of this set is located in the first zone of each of the three Alliances.

To make an item for this set, a crafter must first complete research on two traits for this particular item at a corresponding crafting station.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 34-1487 Armor
3 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
4 items: Adds 28-1206 Maximum Health
5 items: When you take melee damage while you are under 35% Health, you knockback and stun all enemies within 8 meters of you for 4 seconds. This effect can occur once every 15 seconds.
