# Online:Trappings of Invigoration

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Trappings_of_Invigoration
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Trappings of Invigoration is a dungeon set that drops in Fang Lair. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Fang Lair Style in Medium Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 3-129 Stamina Recovery
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Stamina
4 items: Adds 3-129 Stamina Recovery
5 items: When you cast an ability that costs resources while under 25% Stamina, you restore 263-11350 Stamina. This effect can occur once every 45 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists of medium armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types.
