# Online:Undaunted Unweaver

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Undaunted_Unweaver
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Undaunted Unweaver is a dungeon set that drops in Blackheart Haven. It cannot be crafted. Pieces are Mercenary Style in Light Armor.

### Bonuses

2 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
3 items: Adds 25-1096 Maximum Magicka
4 items: Adds 15-657 Critical Chance
5 items: When you use an ability that costs Stamina while in combat, you increase the damage of your Light and Heavy Attacks against monsters by 38-1645 for 10 seconds.

### Pieces
The set consists of light armor and jewelry in all slots, as well as weapons and shields of all types. Unique pieces include:
- Atarus' Masher, unique loot from Atarus.
- Hollow-Heart Pendant, unique loot from the Hollow Heart.
- Jewels of the Iliac Bay, unique loot from Iron-Heel.
- Roost Mother's Branch, unique loot from the Roost Mother.
