# Online:Weapon Damage

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Weapon_Damage
Fetched: 2026-09-17
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Weapon Damage represents the damage done by non-staff weapons and abilities with a Stamina cost. Your Weapon Damage can be improved by many sets (click for complete list), as well the Major Brutality and Minor Brutality effects, which increase your damage by 20% and 10% respectively.

## Skills
;Earthen Heart (Dragonknight)
- Igneous Weapons - grants Major Brutality to you and allies for 30 seconds
;Siphoning (Nightblade)
- Drain Power - Grants Major Brutality for 20 seconds
;Storm Calling (Sorcerer)
- Surge - Grants Major Brutality for 33 seconds
;Aedric Spear (Templar)
- Balanced Warrior - Increases Weapon Damage by 3-6%
;Werewolf
- Hircine's Rage - Grants Major Brutality for 20 seconds
;Fighters Guild
- Slayer - Each slotted Fighters Guild ability increases 3% of weapon and spell damage (as of level 3)
;Two Handed
- Momentum - Provides Major Brutallity and Major Sorcery for yourself for 40 seconds.
;Dual Wield
- Hidden Blade - Provides Major Brutallity and Major Sorcery for yourself for 20 seconds.

## Alchemy
- Potions of Increase Weapon Power grant Major Brutality
- Poisons of Increase Weapon Power grant Minor Brutality
