Platinum seams can be found near cliffs and mountains at Engraver level 5 or at character level CP150+. They can be harvested for three to four samples of the raw material, Platinum Dust. Once ten samples of dust are collected, they can be refined into seven to ten Platinum Ounces. Platinum Jewelry is crafted from the refined platinum ounces at any of the jewelry crafting stations.
