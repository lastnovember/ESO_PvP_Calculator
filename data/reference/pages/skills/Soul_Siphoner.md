Soul Siphoner increases the effectiveness of any healing spells you use for each slotted Siphoning skill, ultimate included, giving up to 6%/18% increased healing done.

## Patch Notes
[Patch 1.6.5] * This passive now increases healing done for each Siphon ability slotted by 2% at Rank I, or 3% at Rank II.
[Patch 11.0.5] * Reduced the rank 1 of this passive to 1% Healing Done per Siphoning ability slotted, down from 2%.
