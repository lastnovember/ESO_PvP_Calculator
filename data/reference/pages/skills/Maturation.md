Maturation increases your targets' Maximum Health whenever you heal them.

## Notes
- The duration of Minor Toughness resets on each tick of a heal over time.

## Patch Notes
ESO Patch Note|4.2.5|* The Minor Toughness granted by this passive now applies even if the target was completely over-healed, and its duration has been increased to 10/20 seconds from 5/10 seconds.
- Developer Comment: The goal of this change is to ensure that all types of Wardens now have the ability to bring Minor Toughness to the group.
