Shadow Barrier increases your physical and spell resistance for a short time whenever you use a Shadow active ability. Wearing Heavy Armor increases this duration by 2 seconds per armor piece, for a up to a maximum of 20 seconds duration, or 26 seconds duration with 2 points invested in the passive.

## Patch Notes
[Patch 1.6.5] * This passive now provides the buffs Major Resolve and Major Spell Resist for 4 seconds after using a Shadow ability, with the duration increased when wearing heavy armor.
ESO Patch Note|3.0.5|* Increased this passive ability's base duration of Major Ward and Major Resolve to 3/6 seconds from 2/4 seconds.
- Developer Comments: Increasing the duration of this passive's bonuses will improve the overall survivability of Nightblades. Tank-focused Nightblades in Heavy Armor, in particular, should have a much easier time maintaining more consistent uptime on these buffs when using strategic combinations of abilities such as Refreshing Path or Summon Shades.
ESO Patch Note|10.1.5|* This passive now grants its Major Resolve for 6/12 seconds, rather than 3/6 seconds. It now increases by 2 seconds per piece of Heavy Armor worn at either rank, rather than extending the duration by 25% per piece worn.
- Developer Comment: Now that Veiled Strike no longer activates this passive, we're making it easier to manage for the class as a whole, while still creating incentives to benefit tanks over damage dealers. Overall, this should now be easier to upkeep at all levels but should still have less uptime for Nightblades that aren't engaging with the more evasive side of their toolkit. These new values should now also better align with the duration effect abilities that are extended with Dark Veil. We don’t expect this to rock the boat too much in either PvE or PvP but should reinforce the need to cast Shadow abilities more regularly, rather than being able to maintain it simultaneously while dealing their bread and butter damaging attack.
