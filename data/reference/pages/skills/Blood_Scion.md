## Notes
- While transforming you are invincible and untargetable but you are locked in place, you cannot perform any action and you do not count for capturing/defending a capture point in PvP.
- Chaos Ball maps also feature the transformation when picking up certain chaos balls. They will remain on as long as the ball is carried, and the only way to restore health by yourself is to attack other players.
- This skill replaced Bat Swarm in Update 26, meaning that the progress you had with that skill was transferred into your progression with Blood Scion (i.e. reaching Bat Swarm III would grant Blood Scion III after the update, reaching Clouding Swarm II would grant Swarming Scion II etc).
- Although Blood Scions resemble Vampire Lords, they have no wings and are in fact a form of mimicry, created by Lamae Beolfag to rival such beings. Early files indicated a Vampire Lord transformation was planned at one point, with some rough modeling already done.
