Scales of Pitiless Justice reduces your Bounty and Heat if you are witnessed committing murder or assault. This synergizes with Swiftly Forgotten, which affects Bounty and Heat for all crimes.

## Notes
- The name of this skill refers to the Scales of Pitiless Justice that appeared as a reward during the Dark Brotherhood questline in The Elder Scrolls IV: Oblivion.
