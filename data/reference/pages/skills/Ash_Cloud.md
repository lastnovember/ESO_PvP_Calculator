Ash Cloud reduces the Movement Speed of affected enemies and heals allies over time. The Cinder Storm increases the amount of healing done to allies while the Eruption morph deals Flame Damage, instead of healing, over time and instantly.

## Notes
- The cost listed is per second. The total cost of Ash Cloud and Cinderstorm is 5265 and Eruption is 4050 assuming they are not recast before they expire.
- Prior to Update 32, these abilities had a large upfront magicka cost rather than cost magicka/s.
