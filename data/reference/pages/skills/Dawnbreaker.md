Dawnbreaker is the Fighters Guild Ultimate ability, unlocked at the final rank of the skill line. Activating it causes instant AoE damage in front of you. Despite the skill's relatively low damage, the small Ultimate cost allows for it to be used frequently. The Dawnbreaker of Smiting morph increases the skill's damage, and also knocks down any enemies that get caught in the blast. Though it does less damage itself, the Flawless Dawnbreaker morph provides a temporary increase to Weapon Damage and Spell Damage after being activated, making other abilities more potent for a little while.

## Outfit Style
- A Dawnbreaker artifact outfit style is available to purchase in the Crown Store every January (Morning Star), as it coincides with the month that Meridia's Summoning Day falls on.
