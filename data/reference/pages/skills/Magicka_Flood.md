Magicka Flood increases your maximum Magicka if you have any Siphoning active ability slotted. Note that you only need one to receive this benefit, and there is no additional benefit to slotting multiple Siphoning abilities.

## Patch Notes
* This ability now correctly increases your maximum magicka.
* This passive now increases your Max Magicka and Stamina by 3/6% when you have a Siphoning ability slotted, rather than increasing your Max Magicka by 4/8% when you have a Siphoning ability slotted.
