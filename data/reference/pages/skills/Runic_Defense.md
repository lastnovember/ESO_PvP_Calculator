## Patch Notes
ESO Patch Note|10.2.5|* Runeguard of Freedom: Reduced the healing granted by this morph by 50%. Increased the Armor granted to 3300 at rank IV, up from 3000. Increased the duration of the Armor and Crowd Control Immunity granted to 7 seconds. Fixed an issue where the Crowd Control Immunity failed to apply until after the Armor effect ended.
- Developer Comment: We're toning down the effectiveness of this morph from being a straight upgrade morph into more of a conversion morph, since the Crowd Control Immunity effect adds a tremendous amount of potential in PvP. As such, we're gently bumping up the duration and Armor density so it has enough power to warrant the tradeoff.
