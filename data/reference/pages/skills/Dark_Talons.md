Dark Talons deals Flame Damage and immobilizes enemies for a period of time. Allies can activate the Ignite Synergy synergy to deal Flame Damage. Burning Talons adds additional Flame Damage over time to all affected enemies, while Choking Talons reduces their damage dealt while affected.

## Notes
- Burning Talons is a recommended morph for the Dragonknight Initiate and Inferno Dragon builds, while Choking Talons is recommended for Stoneskin Dragon.
