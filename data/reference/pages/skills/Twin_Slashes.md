Twin Slashes deals direct Bleed Damage twice then damage over time. The Rending Slashes morph increases the damage of the initial hits which now apply the Hemorrhaging status effect and reduces your target's movement speed, while Blood Craze also restores your Health any time this ability does damage.

## Notes
- Blood Craze is a recommended morph for the Venomous Dragon build, while Rending Slashes is recommended for Arcane Warrior.
- The Stinging Slashes set from Dragonstar Arena will increase the initial hits and the over time damage from this ability by a set ammount.
