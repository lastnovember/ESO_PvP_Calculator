Panacea causes you to restore the Health of you and/or your allies once a second for 5 seconds. The Light's Champion morph applies Major Force on any healed target, increasing their Critical Damage. Or you can use Life Giver, which automatically casts Regeneration, Blessing of Protection, and Steadfast Ward at no cost. Note that you don't need to have unlocked any of these skills for this to occur, but it will cast any Morphs for those abilities if you have them unlocked.

## Notes
- This skill doesn't actually give a 5 second heal over time to you or a nearby ally. It is always cast on you and it decides every second who to heal based on your and your nearby allies' health. This also means the skills cast by the Life Giver morph are always cast on you.
