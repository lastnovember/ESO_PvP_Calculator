Bone Totem places a totem on the ground that reduces the damage taken of you and your allies and causes nearby enemies to be afflicted with Fear, holding them in place and preventing them attacking for a short time. (Note that more powerful targets are immune to these effects.) and reduces enemies' Weapon Damage and Spell Damage. Remote Totem allows you to place the totem further away, while Agony Totem increases the duration and allows your allies to activate the Pure Agony Synergy Synergy, dealing Magic damage over time.

## Notes
- Agony Totem is a recommended morph for the Necromancer Initiate, Undying Warrior, and Flesh Sculptor builds.
