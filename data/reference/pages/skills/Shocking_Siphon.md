Shocking Siphon consumes a corpse to deal Shock Damage to all enemies near the corpse or between you and the corpse. The bond is broken if you move too far from the corpse or are physically blocked by walls or other static obstacles. It also increases your damage done while slotted. With the Detonating Siphon morph, the damage changes to Disease Damage, and the corpse will explode at the end of the siphoning, dealing additional damage. Alternatively, the Shocking Siphon morph restores your Health, Magicka, and Stamina while the bond is active.

## Notes
- Detonating Siphon is a recommended morph for the Deathbringer build, while Mystic Siphon is recommended for Necromancer Initiate and Baleful Summoner.
- The Detonating Siphons corpse explode effect occurs even if you recast the ability early.
- Tethers are considered Area of Effect damage and are reduced by Major/Minor Evasion.
