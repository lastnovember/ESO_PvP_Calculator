Warmth causes all damage over time attacks to deal increased damage and reduce enemy movement speed when dealing damage with an Ardent Flame ability.

## Notes
- Damage over time abilities not from the Ardent Flame skill line also receive the bonuses of Warmth, as long as you activate it with a damaging Ardent Flame ability.

## Patch Notes
ESO Patch Note|4.1.5|* This ability now only snares targets when you deal direct damage with an Ardent Flame ability (was any type of damage).
- Decreased the duration of the snare to 3 seconds for all ranks (previously 2/4 seconds).
* Fixed an issue where this passive’s visual effects could become stuck on targets.
* This passive now causes direct damage from Ardent Flame abilities to increase the damage an enemy takes from your damage over time attacks by 3/6% and reduce their Movement Speed by 15/30%, instead of only reducing their Movement Speed.
* Renamed to Traumatic Burns. This passive now causes enemies you've damaged with a direct Ardent Flame ability to take 5% increased Flame Damage while reducing their movement speed for 5 seconds, rather than increasing your damage over time effects against them by 3/6% and reducing their movement speed for 3 seconds.
