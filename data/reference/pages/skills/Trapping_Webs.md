Trapping Webs creates spider webs, which reduce the movement speed of all enemies in the area, and deal Poison Damage to them at the end of the effect. Your allies may activate the Spawn Broodlings Synergy synergy, which deals more Poison Damage, and summons spiders to attack them. Shadow Silk changes this to Black Widows Synergy Spawn Broodlings, which adds additional damage over time and summons more powerful spiders. Or you can choose Tangling Webs, which changes it to Arachnophobia Synergy Spawn Broodlings, which also fears the enemies.

## Notes
- Despite the description, the creatures spawned appear to be Hoarvors, not Spiders.
