See the Unseen allows you to see and interact with Psijic Portals all around Tamriel. You will receive this skill for free immediately upon acquiring the Psijic Order skill line by completing the first quest, The Psijics' Calling.

## Loot
These portals may contain a variety of valuable loot, including:

- Chapters from crafting motif books associated with the zone the portal was found in (e.g. Sapiarch motifs in Summerset).
- Armor or weapon Overland Set pieces associated with the zone the portal was found in.
- Jewelry with the Infused trait for research as well as the Aurbic Amber material for that trait (in refined or, more commonly, pulverized form)
- Runestones and other assorted crafting materials, including Hakeijo rune with a very low chance.
- Very rarely a Psijic Ambrosia consumable.
- Lead: Sticky Integument Leather antiquity lead for the Thrassian Stranglers (Summerset and Artaeum only).
- Lead: Beacon of Tower Zero antiquity lead for the Beacon of Tower Zero antiquity furnishing (Stormhaven only).
- Lead: Dov-rha Sabaton Toe Cap antiquity lead for the Dov-rha Sabatons (Southern Elsweyr only).

## Notes
- Psijic Portals appear in the same locations where Runestone nodes are typically found, near old ruins and burial sites. Similar to Runestone nodes, they will always contain one Runestone of each type, Aspect, Essence, and Potency, but the Aspect rune will always be Jejota Rune or better.
- This is similar in many respects to the Thieves Guild skill Finders Keepers. It differs in that unlike Thieves Troves, Psijic Portals are not even visible to those without the skill, and also interacting with them is not a crime.
