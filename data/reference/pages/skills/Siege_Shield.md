Siege Shield creates a shield that protects you and your allies from all enemy siege weapons. The Siege Weapon Shield morph also protects your allies' siege weapons, while Propelling Shield increases the range of all long-range attacks, including those from siege weapons, which can allow you to set up a siege attack just outside of your enemies' siege weapon range if they aren't using Propelling Shield as well.

## Patch Notes
[Patch 1.3.3] * This ability now gives you an armor and resistance bonus instead of reducing all damage taken.
ESO Patch Note|1.6.5|* This ability now reduces the damage taken by siege weapons by 35%.
- Propelling Shield: This ability now grants the increased range to any ability with a base range of 20 meters, and reduced the cost of this morph by 10%.
[Patch 2.1.4] * Propelling Shield: Fixed an issue where this ability wasn't increasing the snipe range bonus in Cyrodiil.
[Patch 4.1.5] * This ability and its morphs now reduce the damage you take from siege attacks by 50%, originally 35%.
ESO Patch Note|5.1.5|* This ability and its morphs now affect up to 12 allies, instead of only 6.
- Reduced the base cost to 8100 from 9450.
- Propelling Shield: Reduced the cost to 7830 from 8496.
- Siege Weapon Shield:
- The protection for siege weapons will now apply to all friendly siege weaponry, including your own, rather than only your allies' siege.
- Fixed an issue where the damage mitigation from siege attacks on players was applying to anyone in the area, instead of only you and allied players.
[Patch 8.1.5] * Fixed an issue where this ability and its morphs would end early.
[Patch 10.0.5] * Propelling Shield (morph): This ability now only affects abilities with a range of 28 meters or higher, to ensure it behaves closer to the Reach passive and Battle Spirit, as well as ensuring it does not affect abilities it should not.
