Glacial Obstinance improves Bond with Nature so it also activates while casting Winter's Embrace abilities. It will also increase Weapon Damage and Spell Damage if you are at full Health after the heal.

## Patch Notes
[Patch 12.0.0] *Glacial Obstinance: This passive upgrades Bond with Nature to also trigger off activating Winter's Embrace abilities, and causes the effect to grant you 15% Weapon and Spell Damage for 10 seconds if you are at full Health after the heal.
