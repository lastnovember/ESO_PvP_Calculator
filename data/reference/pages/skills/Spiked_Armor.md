Spiked Armor increases Physical Resistance and Spell Resistance, as well as dealing Flame Damage back to melee attackers. The Hardened Armor morph will give you a Damage Shield, absorbing more hits while the armor is in effect, or you can deal Flame Damage over time to nearby enemies on casting with Volatile Armor.

## Notes
- Volatile Armor is a recommended morph for the Dragonknight Initiate build.
