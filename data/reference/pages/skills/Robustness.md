Robustness increases your Health, Magicka and Stamina Recovery.

## Notes
- This ability has undergone several name changes. Before Update 7, it was called "Robust" and only increased Health Recovery. Until Update 11, it was called "Robust Constitution", and increased Max Health instead. It was changed to "Nimble" along with this change, and then in Update 21 to its current name. This update also added Magicka Recovery and changed the benefits from a percentage based increase to a flat value.

## Patch Notes
* Removed the Robust passive, and replaced it with Robust Constitution. This will increase your Health regeneration by 6/13/20% and Stamina regeneration by 3/6/10%.
* Robust Constitution: This passive ability has been renamed to "Nimble."
* Nimble: Gain 20% Health Recovery and 10% Stamina Recovery → Robustness: Increases your Health Recovery by 100 and your Stamina and Magicka recovery by 85.
* This passive now increases your Health, Magicka, and Stamina Recovery by 90 at their final rank, rather than your Health Recovery by up to 100 and your Magicka and Stamina Recovery by up to 85.
