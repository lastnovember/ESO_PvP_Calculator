Weakness to Elements reduces an enemy's Physical Resistance and Spell Resistance. It has no casting cost, and is instant, so the only thing you're giving up is a slot on your weapon bar, to make all of your attacks more effective. The Elemental Susceptibility morph increases the range and applies Burning, Chilled and Concussion every 7.5 seconds, while Elemental Drain lasts for 1 minute and restores Magicka to anyone who deals damage to the target.

## Notes
- Casting Weakness to Elements or Elemental Drain on a target is not treated as an attack and will not trigger a response from the target. Elemental Susceptibility is treated as an attack as it deals damage.
- The Wrath of Elements set from Vateshran Hollows will create a tether between you and the target for a time when using Weakness to Elements or its morphs on them. The tether deals increasing amounts of Flame, Frost and Shock damage over time to targets touching it.
- With Update 28, the amount of magicka returned by Minor Magickasteal was reduced from 300 to 168.
- Elemental Drain is a recommended morph for the Deathweaver build.
