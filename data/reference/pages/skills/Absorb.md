## Patch Notes
ESO Patch Note|7.1.5|* This passive now procs on any successful block, rather than only against spells, which was already a half truth as it procced on plenty of non-spells as well.
- Increased the restore to 300/600 Magicka on proc, up from 288/540.
- This passive now has a 250ms internal cooldown.
