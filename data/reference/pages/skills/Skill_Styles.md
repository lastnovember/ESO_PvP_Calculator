# Online:Skill Styles

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Skill_Styles
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Skill Styles are a feature introduced by the Gold Road Chapter and expanded with subsequent updates, that allow you to change the appearance of certain skills. The effect is purely cosmetic, and does not change the effects of the skills. They can be used with either the base skill or its morphs, and do not change in appearance based on the choice of morph.

They are generally earned from certain Achievements, though some are unlocked by quests or other activities. Once a skill style is unlocked by one character, it is available for all characters on that account.
