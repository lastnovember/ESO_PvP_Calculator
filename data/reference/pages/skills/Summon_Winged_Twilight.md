Summon Winged Twilight summons a Winged Twilight familiar to aid you in battle. The familiar remains until killed or unsummoned and has a special ability which will heal it as well as an ally when cast again while alive. The Summon Twilight Tormentor morph summons a Twilight Tormentor with a special ability which you can activate by casting the spell again while it is alive, which causes its attacks to deal more damage to enemies above 50% Health. Alternatively, the Twilight Matriarch summoned by Summon Twilight Matriarch heals an additional ally when it's special ability is activated.

## Notes
- Previously, casting the spell with a familiar active would cause them to be unsummoned rather than using a special ability. To do it now, you must either right-click the corresponding active effect on your character page, or remove the skill from your skill bar (at least temporarily). This can be done with a weapon swap if you don't have the skill in the same slot on both weapon tabs. (Conversely, if you don't want the familiar to go away when you switch weapons, then you must have the skill on both tabs.)
- It is possible to use this skill along with Summon Unstable Familiar, allowing you to have two summoned allies at once.
- This familiar attacks with lightning attacks, generally keeping her distance from the enemy. This makes her less likely to be killed in battle than Summon Unstable Familiar and its morphs, but also less likely to draw fire away from you.
- Twilight Tormentor's special ability bears the distinction of being the only attack in the game that does more damage to high Health enemies. This synergizes well with Sorcerer's Amplitude passive which also increases your damage to high health enemies.
- Summon Twilight Matriarch is a recommended morph for the Sorcerer Initiate and Eldritch Mender builds.
