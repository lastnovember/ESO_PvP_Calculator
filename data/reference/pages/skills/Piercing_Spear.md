Piercing Spear increases your damage from critical strikes and your damage against blocking targets.

## Patch Notes
[Patch 1.2.3] * This passive now adds a melee critical strike in addition to a spell critical strike when using spear abilities.
[Patch 1.6.5] * This passive now provides increased critical damage when a spear ability is slotted, instead of increased critical hit.
[Patch 2.3.6] * Fixed an issue where this passive ability was not applying after relogging or zoning into a new area.
[Patch 4.0.5] * This passive ability now only affects Critical Damage, and does not affect Critical Healing.
[Patch 5.3.6] * Fixed an issue where this passive did not work against blocking targets.
[Patch 10.2.5] * Updated this passive to notify that its bonuses only work with players, as NPCs do not “block” as players do.
[Patch 11.0.5] * Buffed this passive's increase to Critical Damage and damage done to blocking players to 6/12%, up from 5/10%
