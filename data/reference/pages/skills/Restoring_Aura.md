Restoring Aura increases the attribute recovery rate of the player for as long as it is slotted in the active ability bar. When cast, applies these recovery buffs to you and your allies. Radiant Aura increases the area of effect and duration. The Repentance morph gives you the same passive ability, but no longer gives it to your nearby allies on cast, instead it "consumes" nearby corpses for instant Health and Stamina restoration to you and nearby allies.

## Notes
- Multiple players can use Repentance on the same corpse. Prior to Update 20 only one player could consume a corpse with this ability.
- Corpses despawn much quicker when they've been looted. If you want to save corpses for Repentance it may be a good idea to not loot them right away.
- As with all other "While slotted on either ability bar" skills, this doesn't work if you have Oakensoul Ring and have this skill in the disabled ability bar. Though here it would be redundant as Oakensoul already provides the slotted effects of Restoring Aura and its morphs.
- Before Update 13, Restoring Aura increased Health and Stamina regeneration upon activation, with Radiant Aura also increasing Magicka regeneration. It provided the same passive bonus.
- Repentance is a recommended morph for the Templar Initiate, Luminous Warrior and Bastion of Light builds.
