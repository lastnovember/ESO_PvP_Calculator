Expunge sacrifices some of your Health to remove 2 negative effects. It also reduces the cost of all other abilities while slotted. Expunge and Modify restores your Magicka and Stamina for each effect removed, while Hexproof more than doubles the number of potential effects removed and reduces the cost.

## Notes
- Expunge and Modify is a recommended morph for Deathbringer and Flesh Sculptor builds, while Hexproof is recommended for Necromancer Initiate and Undying Warrior.
