Blood Ritual lets you infect another player with Vampirism once per week. The two players must be at one of the ritual sites in The Rift]] 8605 in order to perform the ritual. The receiver may complete the quest at any ritual site, regardless of alliance.

## Notes
- While this ritual is activated via Synergy in the same way as feeding on NPC victims, performing the ritual will not change your Vampirism stage.
- Should the synergy not appear, the player you wish to infect needs to die and respawn before the synergy can appear.
