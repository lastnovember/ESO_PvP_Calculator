Master Ritualist reduces the time it takes to resurrect a fallen ally, and increases the Health they have when they come back. You also have a 1 in 2 chance of scoring a free Soul Gem each time you successfully resurrect an ally.

## Patch Notes
ESO Patch Note|1.1.2|* Decreased this ability's resurrection cast speed bonus.
- Those who have spent Skill Points in the Master Ritualist passive will have those points returned to their overall skill point pool. You may place those Skill Points back in this ability if you wish, or invest them elsewhere.
