Death Gleaning restores your Magicka and Stamina every time an enemy dies near you while you are in combat with them.

## Patch Notes
ESO Patch Note|8.3.5|* This passive now restores 333/666 Magicka and Stamina when a nearby enemy dies, up from 100/200.
- Developer Comment: For masters of death, Necromancers can often find themselves out of fuel even when there are corpses littering the battlefield. To try and remedy this without tipping the scales too far in areas where Necromancer is already doing well, we're increasing these bonuses by a decent amount.
