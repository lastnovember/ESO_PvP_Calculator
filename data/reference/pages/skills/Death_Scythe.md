Death Scythe deals Magic Damage to all enemies within a conical range in front of you, healing you for each target hit. The Ruinous Scythe converts the casting cost to Stamina, deals Bleed Damage, applies the Hemorrhaging status effect and sets enemies Off Balance, while Hungry Scythe adds healing over time after each attack.

## Notes
- Hungry Scythe is a recommended morph for Undying Warrior, while Ruinous Scythe is recommended for Deathbringer.
- Death Scythe is depicted as a card in Legends.
