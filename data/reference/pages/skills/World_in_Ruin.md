World in Ruin increases your damage done with area and damage over time attacks.

## Notes
- Prior to Update 10, this skill was called World in Flame and only affected Fire Damage.
- Prior to Update 32, this skill increased the damage of your Flame Area Effect attacks and reduced the Stamina cost of your Poison abilities.
- Prior to Update 49, this skill increased the damage of your Flame and Poison attacks.
