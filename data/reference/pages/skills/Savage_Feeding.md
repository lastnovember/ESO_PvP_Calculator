Savage Feeding causes targets you feed upon to be Off-Balanced and stunned for a short time. It could be triggered by sneaking behind a target and activating the "Feed" synergy that popped up (akin to Blade of Woe), causing the target to begin levitating as they helplessly flailed around in the air, while the performer of the action would drink through a magical bloody stream leading directly to their mouth.

Following Update 26, it was replaced by Feed.
