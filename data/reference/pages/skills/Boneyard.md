Boneyard deals Frost Damage to all enemies within a selected area. If there is a corpse in the area to consume, the damage is increased. Allies may activate the Grave Robber Synergy Synergy to deal additional damage and also restore their Health. The Unnerving Boneyard morph reduces the resistances of all affected enemies as well as making them more vulnerable to further damage, while Avid Boneyard allows you to activate the Synergy yourself.

## Notes
- Unnerving Boneyard is a recommended morph for the Necromancer Initiate and Baleful Summoner builds.
