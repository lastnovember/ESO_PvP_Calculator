Blade Cloak reduces your damage taken from area attacks, and deals Physical Damage to nearby enemies. Quick Cloak increases your movement speed after casting and increases the duration, while Deadly Cloak increases the damage.

## Notes
- Blade Cloak and its morphs will pulse only if a target is nearby.
- Blade Cloak and its morphs can trigger your poisons and enchantments.
- The Spectral Cloak set from Blackrose Prison will increase your damage done and reduce your damage taken for a small time when this ability hits a target.
- Deadly Cloak is a recommended morph for the Umbral Assassin and Luminous Warrior builds.
- Blade Cloak was added in Update 6, replacing Sparks.
