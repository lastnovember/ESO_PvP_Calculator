# Online:Vault

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Vault
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

:Fire a burst at your feet while flipping backwards 15 meters. Casting again within 4 seconds increases the cost by 33%.

Vault is a Scribed skill Grimoire, on the Bow skill line. It can be purchased after earning the Bow Master achievement from Chronicler Firandil in the Scholarium once per character.
It costs 50,000 the first time, and 10,000 for other characters on your account after that. The name of the scribed skill changes depending upon your choice of Focus Script.

In order to use this skill effectively, you need to pay attention to what's behind you in order to avoid falling off a cliff or into another hazard, or alerting more enemies. The safest thing to do may be to maneuver yourself so that your back is to a wall. You can also take advantage of the fact that you generally cannot jump to a higher elevation than where you started. So casting the skill while facing downwards on a slope or stairway will prevent you from going too far back when flipping. Also be aware that the skill has a tendency to double-cast (most likely because you cast it while still in the middle of another attack animation, so you don't jump immediately and press the key twice not realizing that the first cast was just delayed). So in locations where falling off the edge is a real danger (e.g. boss fights in the Infinite Archive), you might want to avoid using this skill at all.

- Cast Time: Instant
- Target: Area
- Radius: 6 meters
