Destructive Touch deals magic damage and additional damage over time.

The Destructive Clench morph removes the damage over time but adds additional effects based on the staff type:
- 18 Flame Clench knocks the target back.
- 18 Frost Clench reduces the damage but increases the range and immobilizes, taunts and applies Major Maim of the target.
- 18 Shock Clench deals splash damage to other nearby enemies.
Alternatively, the Destructive Reach morph allows the ability to be used at a greater distance, changing the name to:
- 18 Flame Reach
- 18 Frost Reach
- 18 Shock Reach

## Notes
- Shock Clench's guaranteed Concussion apply on initial hit works only for the initial target, not the whole AoE.
- The Destructive Impact set from Dragonstar Arena will reduce the Magicka cost of Destructive Touch and its morphs by 10% and will increase your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage after casting for a short time.
- Destructive Clench is a recommended morph for the Deathweaver build.
