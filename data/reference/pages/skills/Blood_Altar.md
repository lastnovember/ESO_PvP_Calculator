Blood Altar creates a field which heals you and your allies when attacking enemies within the field. It also allows allies to activate the Blood Funnel Synergy synergy, which gives them additional healing. The Overflowing Altar morph changes the synergy to Blood Feast Synergy Blood Funnel, which increases the amount of healing they can receive. Alternatively, the Sanguine Altar morph increases the duration and reduces the cost of the spell.

## Notes
- Only one Blood Altar per player may be active at a time. Casting a second one while the first is still active will cause it to disappear.
