Dark Exchange allows you to sacrifice Stamina to restore Health and Magicka, and increase your damage dealt. The Dark Deal morph switches this to sacrificing Magicka in favor of Health and Stamina, while Dark Conversion increases the amount of Health and Magicka restored, and increases Critical Damage dealt.

## Notes
- Dark Conversion is a recommended morph for the Sorcerer Initiate and War Mage builds, while Dark Deal is recommended for Arcane Warrior and Eldritch Mender.
