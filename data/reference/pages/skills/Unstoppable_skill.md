Unstoppable increases your resistances for a longer time and gives you immunity to disabling effects for a shorter time but reduces your movement speed for the duration of the immunity. The Unstoppable Brute morph reduces the cost of the skill and also decreases the cost of Break Free for each piece of Heavy Armor worn for up to 35% for the whole skill duration, while Immovable extends the resistances buff duration and both increases the amount of damage you can block and further reduces your movement speed for each piece of Heavy Armor worn for up to 35% while under the immunity effect.

## Notes
- The movement speed penalty cannot be cleansed.
- As you must wear at least 5 pieces of heavy armor to use this skill, "Unstoppable Brute" always gives at least decreases the cost of Break Free by 25% and "Immovable" always gives at least 25% more block mitigation and 25% more movement speed penalty for a total of at least 90% movement speed penalty.
- If you wear 7 pieces of heavy armor and use the "Immovable" morph, you get a 100% movement speed decrease which is additive with movement speed bonuses, so you can still walk, sprint and swim if you have movement speed bonuses while under the 100% speed decrease.
- Prior to Update 27, the Unstoppable skill was called Immovable, the Unstoppable Brute morph was called Immovable Brute and the Immovable morph was called Unstoppable.
