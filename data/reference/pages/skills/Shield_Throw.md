# Online:Shield Throw

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Shield_Throw
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

:Hurl your shield at an enemy, which then returns to you.

Shield Throw is a Scribed skill Grimoire, on the One Hand and Shield skill line. It can be purchased after earning the One Hand and Shield Master achievement from Chronicler Firandil in the Scholarium once per character.

It costs 50,000 the first time, and 10,000 for other characters on your account after that. The name of the scribed skill changes depending upon your choice of Focus Script.

As the name implies, this skill causes you to throw your shield at an enemy target. It will then bounce back to you, possibly having some effect both when it hits the enemy and when it returns, based on your choice of scripts. The visuals for this skill unfortunately do not reflect your choice of Crafting Motifs for your shield - it just looks like a glowing circle (color dependent on Focus) regardless of your gear.

- Cast Time: Instant
- Target: Enemy

- Deals 4290 Physical Damage to an enemy.
- Range: 28 meters
- Cost: 2538 Stamina

- Range: 22 meters
- Cost: 3553 Stamina

- Deals 2145 Physical Damage to an enemy and taunts them for 15 seconds.
- Range: 28 meters
- Cost: 2538 Stamina
