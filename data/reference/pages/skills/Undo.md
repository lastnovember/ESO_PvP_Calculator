Undo allows you to reverse time - restoring your Health, Magicka, Stamina, and position to their values 4 seconds prior to casting. (Ultimate is not reset for obvious reasons.) This can effectively double your available resources for a short time if cast strategically. The Precognition morph allows it to be used even if you are stunned or otherwise disabled, and grants you immunity to such effects for a short time afterward. It can also save you if, for example, you are knocked off the platform during a boss fight in the Infinite Archive. Alternatively, Temporal Guard reduces your damage taken just for being slotted.

## Soloing Dungeons
One of the more significant gameplay changes caused by this spell is that it can be used to escape from some multiplayer-mechanic crowd-control effects in Dungeons, allowing them to be solo'd.

Since some of these attacks must be escaped multiple times in a single combat, this sometimes needs to be combined with boosts to Ultimate regen, such as the Blessing of the Potentates set, Decisive weapon trait, or the Heroism buff.

Multiplayer-mechanic attacks affected include:
- Blackheart Haven - Captain Blackheart's "Black Plague".
- Crypt of Hearts II - Ruzozuzalpamaz' "Spider Bites".
- Fungal Grotto II - Gamyne Bandu's "Torture Session".
- Fang Lair - Sabina Cedus' "Spectral Chains".

It cannot be used to escape from other, similar multiplayer-mechanic attacks in Dungeons:
- Wayrest Sewers II - Malubeth the Scourger's "Rend Soul".

## Notes
- This spell can have a negative effect if, for example, cast immediately after healing, or if your previous position is now more dangerous than your current one. Situational awareness is important in order to get the most benefit and avoid such pitfalls.
