Reanimate offers an alternative means of resurrecting your allies. Unlike standard resurrection, the cast time is instant and thus less subject to being interrupted, no Soul Gem is required, and you can resurrect up to 3 allies at once. The Renewing Animation morph restores your Magicka and Stamina for each ally resurrected, while Animate Blastbones also consumes up to 3 corpses to summon Blighted Blastbones, which will attack the nearest enemies, dealing Disease Damage and applying the Diseased status effect, and leaving corpses which can be used for other purposes.

## Notes
- Renewing Animation is a recommended morph for the Flesh Sculptor build.
- Reanimate is depicted as a card in Legends.

## Patch Notes
* Fixed an issue where this ability and its morphs would not trigger events that required Resurrections to proc.
