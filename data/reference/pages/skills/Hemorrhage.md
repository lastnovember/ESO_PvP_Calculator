Hemorrhage increases the damage done by Critical Strikes, and also gives you and your allies an increased Weapon Critical chance for each successful Critical Strike for a short time.

## Notes
- The Nocturnal Inspiration Class Mastery ability also generates 2 Ultimate on critical strikes.

## Patch Notes
* This passive now provides the Minor Force buff for 20 seconds to all allies whenever you crit.
