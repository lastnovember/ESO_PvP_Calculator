Mending increases the effectiveness of your healing done. The effect scales based on how low Health the target is, so it is most effective on near-death allies.

## Patch Notes
[Patch 1.6.5] * This passive now grants your Restoring Light heals a bonus based on the amount of missing health on the target of the heal. This can be up to a 5% bonus at Rank I, or up to a 10% bonus at Rank II.
[Patch 2.1.4] * Fixed an issue where this passive was not applying the full percentage bonus.
[Patch 2.2.4] * Slightly adjusted the bonus healing functionality for this passive, which will progressively scale up as your health decreases, up to a 10% healing bonus at low health.
[Patch 3.0.5] * This passive ability now increases the healing done by Restoring Light abilities by 6/12% based on the target's missing health, up from 5/10%.
[Patch 11.0.5] * This passive now increases all of your healing done up to 13% based on your target's missing health, rather than only increasing your Restoring Light ability's healing done up to 12% based on their missing health.
