Shadowy Supplier gives you a contact who supplies you with various items of a legally dubious nature. Unlocking this skill will cause Brotherhood member Remains-Silent to appear in Outlaws Refuges, the Dark Brotherhood Sanctuary canteen, and the Thieves Den in Abah's Landing. Every day, she can provide you with useful items.

When you speak to her, you can pick one of three options, each of which yielding a container of useful items.
