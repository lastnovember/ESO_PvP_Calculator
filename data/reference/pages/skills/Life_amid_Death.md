Life amid Death heals yourself and allies in a target area. You may also consume a corpse nearby to also heal over time. Renewing Undeath will also remove negative effects by consuming a corpse, while Enduring Undeath can consume multiple corpses to further extend the heal over time.

## Notes
- Renewing Undeath is a recommended morph for Flesh Sculptor build.
