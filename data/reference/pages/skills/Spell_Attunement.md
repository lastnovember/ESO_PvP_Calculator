Spell Resistance increases your resistance to spells, moreso if you are affected by certain debilitating effects. It also increases your Magicka Recovery.

## Notes
- Prior to Update 21, this ability was known as "Spell Resistance", and only increased your Spell Resistance, though by a greater degree. The update increased the effect while you have certain debilitating conditions, so it effectively increases your resistance to damage over time, while reducing your resistance to direct damage. The Magicka Recovery was also added in this update.

## Patch Notes
* Gain 3960 Spell Resistance → Gain 2310 Spell Resistance and 100 Magicka Recovery. The Spell Resistance granted by this effect will be doubled if you are afflicted with Burning, Chilled, or Concussed.
* Increased the Magicka Recovery granted from this passive to 130, up from 100.
