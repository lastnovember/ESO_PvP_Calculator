Impaling Shards deals Frost Damage that has increased chance to apply Chilled and reduces the Movement Speed of all nearby enemies. The damage dealt is based off of your Maximum Health. The Gripping Shards morph also Immobilizes affected enemies and reduces the cost, while Winter's Revenge allows you to cast the effect at a distance, and also increases the damage dealt based on your offensive stats instead and deals increased damage if you cast it with a Destruction Staff equipped.

## Notes
- Winter's Revenge is a recommended morph for the Warden Initiate and Beast Caller builds, while Gripping Shards is recommended for Guardian of the Wild.
