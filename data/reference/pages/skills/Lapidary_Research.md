Lapidary Research reduces the time it takes to research traits on Jewelry items. Note that unlike the Blacksmithing, Clothing, and Woodworking research skills, this one does not allow you to research more than one item at once no matter how many points you invest in it. It is recommended to research Ring traits before Amulet traits, since you can wear two rings, so you'll have more potential benefit from being able to craft set jewelry sooner.
