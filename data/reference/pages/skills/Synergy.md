# Online:Synergy

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Synergy
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Synergies are special abilities that other allied players may activate when you use certain skills or sets. If a Synergy is available to you, a prompt at the bottom of the screen appears letting you know that you can activate a Synergy, by pressing the key or buttons associated with it. ("X" by default on PC/Mac, + on Xbox, + on PS4). Synergy activations cost no resources and are independent of skill cooldowns. The Undaunted skill line is particularly based on Synergies, as all of its active skills have one associated with them. Some Synergies only appear with certain skill morphs, or change depending on the morph chosen.

Synergies are a great way of restoring resources through Undaunted Command, the Harmony jewelry trait, or the synergies' effect

### Cooldown
Synergies have a 20 second cooldown per player for each individual Synergy. A single player can use several different synergies in quick succession, but must wait 20 seconds before using the same skills synergy again. Likewise if a synergy has multiple uses, a different player activating it has no effect on your cooldown or ability to activate it.

All skill morphs synergies share a cooldown, so casting Combustion Synergy nolink=y would place you on cooldown for using Healing Combustion Synergy nolink=y

Uniquely Spear Shards and Necrotic Orb's synergies also share the same cooldown. Using one will prevent you from using the other for 20 seconds.

### Mechanics
All synergies have a set amount of uses per cast of the skill. This is typically either 1, 3 or 12 uses. Recasting a skill will refresh that synergy's availability for the group.

Most synergies can only be activated by group members, but there are some exceptions in the table below marked with special

The activation range for each synergy is usually the spell's Area of Effect, but can also be centered around a target or ally.

From Patch 6.0.0
Synergies now have a hierarchy to determine which synergy appears first when
multiple are present to you. Quest, Dungeon, Arena, and Trial-related synergies
now always appear over player-sourced synergies. Furthermore, for player-sourced
synergies, the following ruleset is applied:
Healing and Shield-related synergies will take utmost priority.
Utility, Buff, and Debuff synergies will appear next.
Strictly Damage-related synergies are last in priority.
In a situation where Purify, Conduit, and Combustion are all available to
activate and are given to you, Purify (Healing) will appear first, then
Combustion (Utility), then Conduit (exclusive Damage)

A few synergies have conditions to activate besides being in range.
- Hidden Refresh Synergy nolink=y you must be below 70% Health
- Blood Funnel Synergy nolink=y and Blood Feast Synergy Blood Funnel you must be below 60% Health

### Infinite Archive
- The Undead Avatar verse grants the Call the Dead Synergy nolink=y Synergy, which summons Skeletal Mages, Skeletal Archers and Skeletal Defenders in a chosen area to fight by your side.
- The Eye Catching verse grants the Knower's Eye Synergy nolink=y Synergy, which summons a Knower's Eye in a chosen area that distract enemies.
- The Iron Atronach verse grants the Fire Eruption Synergy nolink=y Synergy, which deals continuous massive Flame Damage to all nearby enemies and summons a friendly Prime Meteor which deals devastating flame damage
- The Phalanx verse grants the Synergy, which summons 5 ghostly defenders that stagger and interrupt nearby enemies, and shields allies behind them.
- The Ice Avatar verse grants the Whiteout Synergy nolink=y Synergy, which freezes enemies solid, resurrects dead allies and summons friendly Frostkin when defeating enemies.
- The Gilded Sleight verse grants the Shakedown Synergy nolink=y Synergy, which charges a golden enemydealing very high Physical Damage, staggers, stuns, sets off-balance and leaves behind a pile of Gold.
- The Shackled Resolve verse grants the Synergy, which grants a damage shield which increases based on enemies tethered.
- The Pustulent Globs verse grants the Synergy, Which lets you throw the pustule causing it to detonate at a new location
- The Guardian of Pestilence verse grants the Synergy, Which detonates your guardians corpse, dealing Disease Damage and plauging the area for 30 seconds.

### Companion Skill Synergies
- Crimson Font grants the Crimson Funnel Synergy nolink=y Synergy to Allies, which heals the activator for 50% of their health.
- Savage Instinct grants the Savage Implosion Synergy nolink=y Synergy to an ally, which causes the enemy to implode after 2 seconds dealing Magic Damage to them and other nearby enemies
- Skeletal Aegis grants the Bone Aegis Synergy nolink=y Synergy to an ally, which gives a damage shield for 50% of their Max Health for 6 seconds.

## Synergy-Like Events
Missing many quest-related synergies.
Sometimes, there are other events which behave like Synergies, giving you the option to perform an action during combat. They are activated in the same way, but do not require another player to be around, or trigger any abilities which are affected by Synergies. Some examples are:

- The Blade of WoeDark Brotherhood ability can be used by sneaking close enough to a humanoid target as long as you have the relevant Dark Brotherhood passive skill. It instantly kills weak humanoids and is therefore useful for murdering Justice Neutral NPCs. The range is slightly lower than a vampire's Feed ability.
- The Feed ability shows when you are a Vampire and sneaking close enough to a humanoid target to feed. Doing so will increase your Vampirism Stage by one, as well as restoring your Health. Your Vampirism stage will decrease every 6 hours if you do not feed. Some of the Vampire abilities are only available or become more powerful if you are in the higher Stages of Vampirism. Doing so is considered an attack, and will be reported as a crime if done to a non-hostile enemy. The range is slightly higher than the Blade of Woe ability. The same ability shows when giving another player a bite at a Vampire Shrine with the Blood Ritual skill, though this does not affect your Vampirism stage.
- The Insatiable Hunger ability can be used by Werewolves provided they have invested in the skill line and are currently in Werewolf form. This allows you to consume the corpses of your slain enemies to restore your Health and gain Ultimate. The same ability shows when giving another player a bite at a Werewolf Ritual Sites with the Shadow of the Bloodmoon skill.
- The Slaughter synergy can be used by Werewolves while they are prowling on targets they have snuck behind, much like with the Blade of Woe and Vampire Feed abilities.
- During the final battle in Arx Corinium against Sellistrix the Lamia Queen, the Nereid sisters will give you the option to "Taunt Her!", causing Sellistrix to direct her attacks at the Nereid instead of you. This is part of the Force of Nature quest and thus will only be available on your first run-through of the dungeon.
- During Stone Cold, you have to Use Indanas to Collapse Hole.
- During the quest God of Schemes, there are two instances where you are given a pseudo-Synergy event, first to form floating rocks into a bridge using Aedric Command, and later to escape from Molag Bal's clenched fist using Sacrifice of Courage.
- If you rescue Unsulag during the Forcing the FaithOrsinium quest in Paragon's Remembrance, she will cast a healing spell which gives you a Synergy called Restoring Waves which restores your health.
- When Scruut is following you, she will periodically give you the Apocrypha's Wrath synergy to use, which summons Scruut's Tentacle to assist you. During Kynreeve Ryl's fight, this synergy also stops his "Ryl's Nightmare" ability.
- During some parts in Necrom's main quests after recovering Meln the Mouthless, you can use the 24Ghost Sight synergy against spirit-like enemies which have dramatic damage taken reduction. Dealing damage to those enemies grants you a stack of "Ghost Sight" for 30 seconds, and at 3 stacks a small area appears on the ground which grants you the Ghost Light synergy. Activating this synergy greatly increases your damage done for a time and causes the spirit-like enemies to immediately change back into their physical form.
- After defeating Vaermina's Nightmare in The Mythos, you will use the 24 Black Book: The Tormented Eye synergy twice to fire a beam at Vaermina herself.
- During Chronicle of Fate, you will have to use 24Meln's Ghost Sight to reveal a passage.
