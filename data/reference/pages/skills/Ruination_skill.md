Ruination increases your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage.

## Notes
- Prior to Update 11, this skill was called "Flame Talent", and only increased Flame Damage. This update changed the name to "Destructive Ancestry" and added Frost and Lightning Damage. Update 21 then changed it to its current name and replaced the elemental damage boosts with a simple increase to all Weapon Damage and Spell Damage.

## Patch Notes
* Ranks II and III of this passive ability now provide the correct amount of spell damage bonus to fire attacks.
* Updated this tooltip to state that it increases all abilities dealing Flame Damage, not just abilities scaling off Spell Damage.
* Flame Talent: This passive ability has been renamed to "Destructive Ancestry." Also, in addition to the current benefit it now increases your Frost and Shock Damage by 0/1/2%.
