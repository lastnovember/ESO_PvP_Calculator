Follow Up increases your damage for one attack after performing a heavy attack with a Two Handed weapon.

## Notes
- This skill was added in Update 5, replacing 20 Arcane Fighter, which increased your chances to apply certain debilitating status effects.

## Patch Notes
[Patch 1.5.2] * Replaced the Arcane Fighter passive with Follow Up. This will increase your damage by 5%/10% for 7 seconds after activating a fully charged heavy attack.
[Patch 2.6.4] * Fixed an issue where you could proc this passive's bonus using unintended means.
[Patch 9.1.5] * This passive now increases your damage done with all two handed attacks by 5/10% for 4 seconds after completing a fully charged Heavy Attack, rather than increasing the damage of your next direct damage by 10% after completing a fully charged Heavy Attack.
