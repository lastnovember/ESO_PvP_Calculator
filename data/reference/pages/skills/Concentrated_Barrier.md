Concentrated Barrier gives you a Damage Shield when you Block, provided you have any Psijic Order ability slotted. This affect can occur every 10 seconds. Note that this effect is similar to the Reinforced perk from Warfare, and will work well in concert with it. They are different, however, in that Concentrated Barrier requires you to stop blocking for 10 seconds in order to replenish the shield, while with Reinforced, you can stop blocking and then block again immediately to replenish it.

## Notes
- Pay attention to a circular orb effect when you have been not blocking for a bit - this is your cue that the barrier is fully recharged and can be reactivated by blocking again.
