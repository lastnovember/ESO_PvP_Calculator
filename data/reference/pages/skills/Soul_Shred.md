Soul Shred deals Magic Damage to enemies and stuns them, while allies can activate the Soul Leech Synergy synergy to steal their Health. The Soul Siphon morph changes it into a group healing spell and removes the damage and stun effect, though your allies can still cause damage using the synergy. Or you can use Soul Tether, which adds damage over time and heals you.

## Notes
- Soul Tether is a recommended morph for the Nightblade Initiate build, while Soul Siphon is recommended for Blood Magus.
