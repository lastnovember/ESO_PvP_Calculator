Silver Bolts deals Physical Damage to a single opponent, or up to six opponents with the Silver Shards morph. Choosing the Silver Leash morph increases the cost and reduces the range and damage but pulls the target to you while reducing their movement speed and attempting to taunt them.

## Notes
- In some cases, when using Silver Leash, the target will be unable to move. This can happen in various situations:
- The target is knocked down or immobilized by another attack.
- The target has recently been moved by another player also using Fiery Grip.
- The target is charging up a Heavy Attack.
- Most boss level enemies cannot be pulled.
- Some targets are always immobile, e.g. Stranglers.
- Using Silver Leash around other players can cause confusion or even annoyance. As a matter of etiquette, it's probably best to avoid using it on a target who is already engaged with another player, unless they appear to be in need of a rescue.
- The skill was known as 'Crossbow Pistol' at an early point in development, with the description "Fire a pistol shot that stuns undead and daedra. Target evaporates into ash."
