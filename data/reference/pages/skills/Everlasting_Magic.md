Everlasting Magic increases the duration of all Mages Guild active abilities. (It does not extend the healing prevention from Equilibrium, but it does extend the helpful effects of both of its morphs.)

## Notes
- Both levels of Everlasting Magic are required to affect Entropy and its morphs because damage ticks every 2 seconds.

## Patch Notes
ESO Patch Note|10.0.5|* This passive now increases the duration of Mages Guild duration abilities by 1/2 seconds, rather than 10/20%.
- Developer Comment: This change was done to fix truncation issues and reduce its potency with some of the abilities in the skill line.
