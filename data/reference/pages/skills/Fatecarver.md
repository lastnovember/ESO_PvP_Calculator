Fatecarver is a bright green beam, channeled area of effect dealing fast ticks of direct magic damage that you aim with your cursor. It costs resources for as long as you hold the beam but you can stop this attack at any moment. Fatecarver also consumes your Crux to increase its damage. The Exhausting Fatecarver morph adds a snare and consuming Crux will also increase the snare's potency and the whole skill length. The Pragmatic Fatecarver morph gives you a large damage shield which also grants crowd control immunity for as long as you channel this skill and it isn't depleted. Consuming Crux will also reduce the skill's cost.

## Notes
- The cost and the damage are listed as per 0.3 seconds while in reality it is per 0.338 seconds.
- Fatecarver does not deal damage for the first 0.5 seconds of the channel.
- It is currently the only skill in the game that casts on button press instead of button release.
