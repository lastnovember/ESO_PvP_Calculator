Hidden Blade deals Physical Damage to a distant enemy and interrupts, sets off-balance and stuns them if they're casting an ability, and also increases your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage. Shrouded Daggers causes the dagger to bounce and hit up to three additional targets, while Flying Blade increases your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage for longer and, if cast on the same target a second time, makes you jump to that target, dealing extra damage for no cost.

## Notes
- The Executioner's Blade set from Vateshran Hollows gives this skill up to 250% more damage against targets under 100% Health when you are standing behind them and restores you 1890 Stamina if the target was below 50% Health.

[Patch 2.6.4] * Shrouded Daggers: Fixed an issue where this morph would not bounce the daggers if the dagger damage was the killing blow.

- Flurry
- Twin Slashes
- Whirlwind
- Blade Cloak
- Hidden Blade

[Patch 4.1.5] * Flying Blade: Reduced the snare duration from this ability and its morphs to 4 seconds.
ESO Patch Note|4.3.5|* Fixed an issue where Major and Minor Evasion were affecting skills they should not have been. The following abilities and sets will no longer be reduced by Major and Minor Evasion's damage reduction:
- Force Pulse
- Overwhelming Surge
- Radial Uppercut
- Selene's

ESO Patch Note|5.1.5|* Reworked this ability from a ranged spammable into a utility skill.
