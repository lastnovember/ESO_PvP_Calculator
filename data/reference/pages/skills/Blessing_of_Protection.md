Blessing of Protection restores the Health and increases the physical and spell resistance of you and your allies in front of you. The Blessing of Restoration morph increases the area size, the duration, and the healing amount, while Combat Prayer increases the damage done by you and your allies.

## Notes
- The Timeless Blessing set from Asylum Sanctorium will reduce the Magicka and Stamina costs of your healing abilities when you cast this spell.
- Combat Prayer is a recommended morph for the Blood Magus, and Eldritch Mender builds.
