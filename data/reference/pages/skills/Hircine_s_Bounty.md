Hircine's Bounty lets you howl to restore your Health and 10% Stamina, the latter scaling up to 100% depending on your current health. The ability also passively increases your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage while slotted.

The Hircine's Rage morph will scale the healing to your offensive stats rather than max health. This morph also increases your damage done and taken for 17-20 seconds by up to 12%, based on how high your current Health percent is. When slotted it also provides Minor Berserk and will generate double Fury upon activation.

The Hircine's Fortitude morph increases the amount of Health and Stamina restored. When slotted, it will additionally grant Major Vitality.

## Notes
- The passive effects of Hircine's Bounty apply even when the ability is slotted on an active, non-Werewolf skill bar.

Developer Comment
We're toning down how much raw healing this ability and its morphs can do while simultaneously adding more purpose to the button press for resource management for tanks or in PvP encounters. Now rather than being a binary heal vs restore, it will grant both, and will reward you in similar situations - granting more resources while at higher Health. Hircine's Fortitude sees a decrease in the initial healing, but gains the potential to be healed significantly more while in groups or using other effects and morphs, helping out the tankier paths, while Hircine's Rage continues to focus on the offensive nature but with a healthier balance as both the bonuses and the punishments degrade as you weaken while also healing more effectively for damage dealers.
