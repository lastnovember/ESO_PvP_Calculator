The Storm Voice restores your resources when you activate an Ultimate ability. Having more Dragonknight abilities slotted increases the amount of resources restored.

## Notes
- The Booming Voice Class Mastery ability also grants 5 Health, Magicka, and Stamina Recovery for every Ultimate spent for 10 seconds after a delay of 15 seconds.
- The Lead from the Front Class Mastery ability also grants you and group members within 28 meters of you Major Berserk and Protection for 1 second per 15 Ultimate spent.
