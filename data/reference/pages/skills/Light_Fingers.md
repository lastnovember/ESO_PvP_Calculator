Light Fingers increases your chances of successfully pickpocketing. Your chances at each level are listed below (if you are playing a Khajiit, add 5 to each of these values). Note that your chance may be higher if you can get into the "sweet spot" (numbers in green) by waiting a few seconds. Note that even a Khajiit with maxed skill under ideal circumstances still has a 5% chance of failure against hard targets.
