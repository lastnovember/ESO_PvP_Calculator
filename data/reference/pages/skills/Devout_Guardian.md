Devout Guardian improves Sacred Ground. When it is active, you gain a damage shield for 6 seconds in addition to the Minor Mending and improved blocking. While the shield is active you gain Health, Magicka, and Stamina Recovery. If the shield breaks, you will gain Ultimate.

## Patch Notes
[Patch 12.0.0] *This passive causes you to gain a Damage Shield for 6 seconds, up to once every 6 seconds, while Sacred Ground is active. The shield absorbs up to 20% of your Max Health and grants 300 Health, Magicka, and Stamina Recovery while active. If the shield breaks, you gain 10 Ultimate.
