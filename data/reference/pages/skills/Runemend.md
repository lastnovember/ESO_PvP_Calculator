Runemend either heals you or an ally in a cone in front of you three times and generates a crux. Each active crux reduces the cost of this skill. The Evolving Runemend morph also heals over time while the Audacious Runemend morph gives Minor Heroism to low health targets, including yourself.

## Notes
- You do not generate a Crux if you are out of combat.
