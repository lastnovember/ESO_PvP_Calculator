Mages' Fury calls down a bolt of lightning which deals Shock Damage to a single opponent. If the target is below 20% Health, it will deal a much greater amount of damage, as well as a small amount of splash damage to any enemies near them. Its quick casting speed and low cost make it an effective starter spell, and it is good for finishing off already-weakened opponents, but its initial damage may be too low except as a finisher. In this circumstance it is very useful for the last portion of boss fights. The Mages' Wrath morph increases its splash damage. Alternatively, Endless Fury returns Magicka provided it dealt damage to the target shortly before it died.

## Notes
- It is very useful to have this skill available to deal with larger boss fights, due to the extra damage to low-Health opponents. Having it on your back bar can mitigate the loss of that slot on your front bar, since it isn't particularly effective until the end of the battle.
- Mages' Wrath is a recommended morph for the Sorcerer Initiate and Elemental Arcanist builds.
