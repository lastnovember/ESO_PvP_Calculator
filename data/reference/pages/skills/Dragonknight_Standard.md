Dragonknight Standard deals Fire Damage over time and reduces effectiveness of healing and damage shields on enemies within its radius. An ally may activate the Shackle Synergy synergy, which deals more damage and also immobilizes enemies within its range. The Shifting Standard morph reduces the cost, increases the duration and allows you to move the Standard around by re-casting it. Alternatively, the Standard of Might morph will increase your damage dealt and decrease your damage taken while within range.

## Notes
- The banner placed by Dragonknight Standard depends on the character's Alliance. Playing in Battlegrounds may overwrite the displayed banner.
- Standard of Might is a recommended morph for the Dragonknight Initiate build.
