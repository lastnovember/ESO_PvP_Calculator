Rushed Ceremony heals a nearby ally, or possibly yourself. The ability will usually heal the ally with the lowest health within 28 meters. The Breath of Life morph will also heal a second ally for one third of the amount, while Honor the Dead restores some of its casting cost over time when it heals a target with lower than 75% Health.

## Notes
- Breath of Life is a recommended morph for the Templar Initiate and Beacon of Hope builds.

## Patch Notes
* Honor the Dead will now properly return magicka if your target is healed to over 50% health.
* Added new graphical effects to Rushed Ceremony and its morphs.
* Breath of Life: This morph now only fires one additional secondary heal, previously two heals.
* This ability and its morphs can no longer heal allies through walls and other line of sight blockers.
* Honor the Dead: Fixed an issue where the heal from this morph was ignoring line of sight.
* Honor the Dead: Fixed an issue where this morph's refund bonus was not occurring if you healed the target to above 75% Health.
ESO Patch Note|3.0.5|* Fixed an issue where this ability and its morphs would occasionally not display their full animation.
- This ability and its morphs now heal friendly targets in a 180 degree cone in front of you, instead of all targets in a radius around you.
- Developer Comments: Rushed Ceremony has been an extremely potent heal, and we wanted to adjust its effectiveness without making the ability heal for less. With these goals in mind, we made it so the ability only heals targets in front of the Templar. This makes the ability require more strategic positioning and gives the healer more control over who gets healed. You can now position yourself to ensure an ally being attacked will be healed, rather than another player standing in the back.
ESO Patch Note|4.0.5|* Breath of Life: Reduced the healing done from this morph's secondary heal by approximately 33%.
- Honor the Dead: This morph now refunds part of the ability's cost over 6 seconds, down from 8 seconds.
* Honor the Dead: Added a visual effect to the Magicka restore.
* Breath of Life: Fixed an issue where the secondary heal granted from this ability was able to ignore the caster's line of sight and target players behind them.
* Honor the Dead: Fixed an issue where this ability would cause animation stutters when attempting to cast the ability at a target who was using Mist Form or its morphs.
