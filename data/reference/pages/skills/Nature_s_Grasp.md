Nature's Grasp causes you to teleport to an ally, restoring their Health over time. This allows you to use your other short-range healing abilities more effectively. The spell is built for dedicated healers, and is less effective in solo play, as you need an ally to jump to in order to cast it. However, you can use it along with Feral Guardian. Bursting Vines significantly reduces the healing given, but causes it to occur instantly instead of over time and restores more ultimate if the ally is at low health., while Nature's Embrace will heal both you and your target.

## Notes
- Nature's Embrace is a recommended morph for the Warden Initiate build.
- While this skill is slotted, you will notice colored auras around other characters which are not normally there. So in addition to the usual red auras for hostile targets, yellow auras for neutral, and white auras for justice neutral, you will now see light blue auras for other friendly players, and green auras for companions and friendly pets, such as Feral Guardian or allied Sorcerer pets.
