Consuming Darkness casts an aura of protection over the area and slows down enemy movement in the area. Allies can activate the Hidden Refresh Synergy synergy, which grants them Invisibility, increases their movement speed, and restores their Health for 4 seconds. The Bolstering Darkness morph reduces your damage taken of you and your allies for a short duration after leaving the effect, while Veil of Blades adds damage over time to enemies in the area.

## Notes
- Bolstering Darkness is a recommended morph for the Living Shadow build.
