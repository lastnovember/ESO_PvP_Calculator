Undaunted Mettle increases your maximum Health, Stamina, and Magicka for each type of armor you have equipped, to a total of 3%, or 6% with a second point invested in this passive.

## Notes
- Each of the armor skill lines have active abilities which require five pieces, and there are seven armor slots. If you want to take advantage of one of these active abilities, you obviously need to wear five pieces of the respective armor type, and one of each of the other two types to take full advantage of Undaunted Mettle. Investing in all passives of all three armor skill lines will make this approach very skill-point expensive.
