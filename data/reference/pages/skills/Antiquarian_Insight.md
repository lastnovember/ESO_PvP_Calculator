Antiquarian Insight allows you to Scry for Antiquities up to a specific item quality. You may be able to find Leads prior to obtaining this skill, but you cannot actually scry them unless the appropriate level is met. Antiquities whose leads have been found, but cannot be scried, will have their difficulty class marked in red.

It is immediately unlocked upon completion of The Antiquarian Circle quest.

## Notes
- Despite not being listed, this passive increases the total number of facets (and the positions of foci) according to its rank, regardless of the tier of the lead being scried.
