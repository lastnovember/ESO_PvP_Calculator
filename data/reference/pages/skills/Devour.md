Devour allows you to stay in Werewolf form longer by devouring the corpses of your enemies. Devouring a corpse also restores a large amount of Health.

## Notes
- Devour actually works on beast corpses as well as humanoid corpses. Some daedra cannot be devoured, even when they have a humanoid appearance, as Dremora do. NPC vampires and their thralls are considered undead and cannot be devoured. Some dro-m'Athra can be devoured. NPCs that are always dead and your fellow dead group members can also be devoured, this doesn't impair their ability to ressurect.
- You can only devour a corpse every few seconds, and feeding is interrupted by heavy and interrupting attacks in combat. This prevents you from remaining in werewolf form indefinitely by constantly killing and consuming weak enemies.

- Improved the targeting and reliability for devouring corpses.

[Patch 12.0.5] *Devour -> Insatiable Hunger: This synergy now has a minor grace period for movement, allowing you to move slightly before and after activating it without causing the cast to be interrupted.
