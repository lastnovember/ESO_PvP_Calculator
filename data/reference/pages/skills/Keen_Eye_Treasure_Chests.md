Keen Eye: Treasure Chests causes Treasure Chests in the world to become covered in a green light as you approach them. They will also make a humming noise when close. When the skill is upgraded, this effect will activate from further away.

Treasure chests in the Imperial City and the Imperial Sewers are not affected by this skill.
