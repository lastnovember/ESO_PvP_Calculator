Flurry batters an enemy with a series of four attacks, each dealing physical damage. Because this counts as four separate attacks, each has a chance of causing a Critical attack, so combining this with Spell Critical or Weapon Critical makes it much more effective. The Rapid Strikes morph increases the damage for each hit, while the Bloodthirst morph restores your Health based on the damage done and now deals bleed damage.

## Notes
- The Cruel Flurry set from Maelstrom Arena will cause your next single-target damage over time ability to have increased Spell Damage and Weapon Damage after using this ability.
- Prior to Update 35 Flurry hit the target five times per cast and had a different animation where you attacked with four simple forward thrusts then a double forward thrust. Now it is two slashes from the right side then two slashes from the left side. Some enemies still use the old Flurry skill, such as in Dragonstar Arena.
- Bloodthirst was added in Update 10, replacing Blinding Flurry, which had a 7% chance with each hit to set the target off balance.
- Bloodthirst is a recommended morph for the Venomous Dragon build, while Rapid Strikes is recommended for Arcane Warrior.
