Future Focus grants you Magicka charges for use when scrying for an antiquity. At Future Focus II, you will have 3 Magicka Charges in total.

Magicka Charges are necessary to use the Coalescence, Dilation, or Farsight skills. Using any of these skills consumes a Magicka Charge but does not use up a turn of Scrying. Since Magicka Charges can only be recharged by claiming foci, it is better to have as many Magicka Charges as possible to begin with in order to use these skills which themselves will make it easier to claim additional foci and thus recharge the Magicka Charges.
