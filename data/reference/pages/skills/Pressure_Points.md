Pressure Points increases your Critical Strike rating for each active skill from the Assassination line you have slotted. This includes the Ultimate ability, Death Stroke.

## Patch Notes
[Patch 1.3.3] * The tooltip for this ability no longer reports an incorrect value when multiple assassination values are slotted.
[Patch 1.6.5] * The tooltip for this passive ability has been adjusted to display only your current critical strike rating bonus.
[Patch 2.3.5] * Fixed an issue where this passive ability was granting less Critical Strike Chance than intended.
[Patch 11.0.5] * Increased the Critical Chance rating from this passive to 1.25/2.5% per Assassination ability slotted, up from 1/2% per ability.
