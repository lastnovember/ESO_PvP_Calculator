Steadfast Candescence upgrades Sacred Ground. It will now activate and refresh itself while you are Bracing, and also increases the amount of damage you can block while stationary in addition to the Minor Mending and already improved blocking.

## Patch Notes
[Patch 12.0.0] *This passive increases the amount of damage you can block by 20% while stationary and causes you to gain Sacred Ground while Bracing.
