Apocryphal Gate creates a portal where you point and right in front of you. Touching a portal teleports you to the other and generates a Crux. The Fleet-Footed Gate morph gives you Major Expedition after teleporting. The Passage Between Worlds morph reduces its cost with each rank and allows your allies to use your portals by using a unique synergy.

## Notes
- As with all other Crux-generating skill, you do not generate a Crux if you teleport while out of combat.
- You cannot put your portal too close to you, there is a minimal distance of you required from where you can put your portal.
- Allied Arcanists taking your "Passage Between Worlds" portal do not generate a Crux, only you can generate a Crux by teleporting.
