Lacerate deals massive bleed damage to all enemies in front of you for a short time, restores your Health for half of the damage done and applies the Hemorrhaging status effect every tick. The Rend morph doubles the duration (and thus total damage dealt), while Thrive in Chaos converts the 8x8 meters area into a wide cone and increases all your damage done for 15 seconds by 3-6% for each enemy hit, up to 18-36% more damage.
