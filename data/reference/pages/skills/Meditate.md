Meditate causes you to enter a meditative state, restoring Health, Magicka and Stamina over time. You can remain in this state indefinitely until interrupted, or cast again to end the effect. Note that you cannot move, attack, block, or use any other skills while in this state. Introspection will restore more Health the longer you stay in meditative state, while Deep Thoughts will increase the rate of Magicka and Stamina Recovery.
