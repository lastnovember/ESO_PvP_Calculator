Magicka Detonation curses an enemy, which will cause an explosion around them after 4 seconds, dealing heavy Magic Damage to them and any enemies near them. The more enemies that are near them, the greater the damage dealt to all targets. If you find yourself cursed with this effect, you should get as far away from your allies as possible to avoid heavily damaging yourself and them. The effect can be removed by various purifying effects, unless you use the Inevitable Detonation morph, which causes it to detonate immediately if the target or their allies attempt to remove it. Alternatively, the Proximity Detonation changes it to center around yourself instead of an enemy. It will take twice as long to detonate, allowing you to charge into the enemy ranks to affect as many targets as possible.

## Notes
- This ability was added to the game in Update 6.
- This ability is the cornerstone of "bombing" in PvP, where it particularly excels against large densely-packed enemy groups (i.e. capturing a flag, standing near a battering ram, etc.).

## Patch Notes
ESO Patch Note|1.6.5|* Magicka Detonation (new ability)
- Using this new ability will place a bomb on your target. After 4 seconds, the bomb will explode dealing high area of effect damage. This ability has a 2 second cast time.
- Inevitable Detonation: This ability will deal area of effect damage if dispelled.
- Proximity Detonation: This ability, cast upon yourself, will blow up and damage all nearby enemies after 4 seconds.
ESO Patch Note|2.1.4|* This ability now adds bonus damage for the amount of targets hit by it with 5% per target, up to a maximum of 25%.
- Proximity Detonation: This ability is now an instant cast. We also increased the detonation time to 8 seconds, and reduced the damage by 28% to compensate.
ESO Patch Note|2.1.6|* Shadow Cloak: Fixed an issue where you were not being hit by some area of effect abilities while Shadow Cloak was active. The following abilities will now correctly damage a Shadow Cloaked target:
- Dark Talons
- Inhale
- Ash Cloud
- Volley
- Arrow Spray
- Magicka Detonation
- Caltrops
ESO Patch Note|2.3.5|* Reduced the damage from this ability and its morphs by 15%.
- Increased the amount of additional damage per hit to 10% additional damage per target hit from 5%
- Increased the cap for the bonus damage to a total of 100% more damage done, from 25% more damage done.
- Inevitable Detonation (Magicka Detonation morph): Fixed an issue where this morph's effect could not be purged or cleansed. It will continue to detonate immediately when it is removed early.
ESO Patch Note|2.4.5|* Reduced the damage of this ability and its morphs by 45%. The net result should be significantly less damage to 1 target, and slightly less damage to 10 targets.
- Proximity Detonation: Fixed an issue where the red telegraph circle from this ability was remaining at the last seen spot the enemy player was before they became invisible. It will now also be invisible when the enemy player is invisible.

- Increased the damage of all morphs by approximately 7% to ensure they deal our AoE damage standard.

- Removed the maximum limit to the target scaling of these abilities so they may actually combat large groups of enemies for their intended design.
