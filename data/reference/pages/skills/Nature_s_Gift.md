Nature's Gift restores your Magicka or Stamina, whichever is lower, whenever you use any Green Balance ability to heal an ally.

## Notes
- Nature's Gift has no effect if you're only healing yourself.

## Patch Notes
* Updated this passive ability's tooltip to indicate its cooldown.
