Tome-Bearer's Inspiration periodically adds direct magic damage to an attack made with a class ability and generates a Crux if you have none. It also passively increases your weapon and spell damage by being slotted in either bar. The Inspired Scholarship increase the rate you get the active bonuses while the Recuperative Treatise morphs adds a magicka and stamina restoration effects to the active bonuses.

## Notes
- As with all other "While slotted on either ability bar" skills, this doesn't work if you have Oakensoul Ring and have this skill in the disabled ability bar.
