Blade of Woe lets you instantly kill NPC targets, provided you approach them while in stealth from behind. You only receive one quarter of the experience from such kills, meaning that it is primarily useful for avoiding a bounty when attacking innocent NPCs. You cannot use it on other players or bosses.

You receive this skill for free during the quest Voices in the Dark as part of the Dark Brotherhood DLC. It functions as a synergy.

This skill can be buffed significantly by slotting the Shadowstrike Champion Point perk.

## Tutorials Description
Members of the Dark Brotherhood can call upon the Blade of Woe, an artifact with the power to instantly slay unaware targets.

To use the Blade of Woe, position yourself behind your target while hidden. If that target is vulnerable to the Blade of Woe, you will be prompted to press [MB4] to trigger the assassination. This is similar to the way you would activate a Synergy.

Killing a target with the Blade of Woe has a chance to grant you Dried Blood or Tainted Blood, which can be used to craft gear in the Assassins League style.

The Blade of Woe can only be used on humanoids, cannot be used in Group Dungeons or in Trials, and cannot be used against certain powerful enemies.

Using the Blade of Woe does not prevent you from being witnessed by bystanders, so keep an eye on your surroundings!

## Notes
- The Blade of Woe also appeared in both Oblivion and Skyrim
- The Blade of Woe deals 100% of the target's Max Health in oblivion damage.
- The Blade of Woe takes precedence over feeding as a vampire in close range. To feed after acquiring this skill, you must be slightly further away from your target.
- Blade of Woe does not work on all humanoid NPC targets. For example, it will work on Bretons or Dremora, but not on goblins. It also cannot be used against most of the stronger enemies (those with additional pips on their health status bars), such as guards and boss-level enemies; some exceptions include the Elite Enemies in the Whalefall delve. It cannot be used at all against non-humanoid targets, except the Caterwaul Watchlings. It cannot be used against named NPCs related to quests, vendors and bards.
- Although the Synergy prompt is shown, Blade of Woe cannot be used if the Prevent Attacking Innocents option enabled, and will show an appropriate message instead.
