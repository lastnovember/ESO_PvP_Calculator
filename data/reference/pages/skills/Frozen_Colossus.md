Frozen Colossus summons a Flesh Colossus to deal Frost Damage to all enemies within a given area. Affected enemies also receive increased damage from other sources for 12 seconds. The Pestilent Colossus morph changes to Disease Damage, and both increases the damage dealt and applies Diseased for each hit. Alternatively, Glacial Colossus applies a Stun to all affected enemies with the last hit and increases the duration of their vulnerability.

## Notes
- Pestilent Colossus is a recommended morph for the Deathbringer build, while Glacial Colossus is recommended for Necromancer Initiate and Baleful Summoner.
- Early plans for this skill apparently were to be a Bone Dragon instead. The effects (still found in the database) were:
- Bone Dragon: Unleash an undead frost wyrm to freeze an enemy to the bone, causing them to take 2429 Frost Damage over 6 seconds and applying Major Vulnerability to them, increasing their damage taken by 30%.
- Plague Dragon: Unleash an undead green wyrm to spread pestilence to all enemies in front of it, causing them to take 2513 Disease Damage over 6 seconds and applying Major Vulnerability to them, increasing their damage taken by 30%.
- Frost Dragon: Unleash an undead frost wyrm to freeze an enemy to the bone, causing them to take 3590 Frost Damage over 9 seconds and applying Major Vulnerability to them, increasing their damage taken by 30%.
:The numbers here may not represent the actual damage it would have done, but it is notable that except for the Plague Dragon morph, this was evidently a single target ability. The Bone Dragon summon animation was seemingly reused for the Bone Dragon Summons Focus memento instead.

## Patch Notes
[Patch 5.0.9] * Fixed an issue where this ability and its morphs had a 1-second delay before they displayed a hostile telegraph to enemy players. It will now display a hostile circle telegraph in the area the colossus is going to smash, the moment the ability is cast.
[Patch 5.1.5] * Pestilent Colossus: Reduced the escalating damage bonus each hit this morph deals. The total adjustment will result in an approximate 5% damage reduction.
[Patch 5.2.5] * Increased the total duration of Major Vulnerability to 8 seconds, up from 5. Enemies hit by Major Vulnerability cannot be affected again for 20 seconds after it applies, to prevent situations where this debuff could be kept up indefinitely by rotating an army of Necromancers.
[Patch 6.1.5] * Pestilent Colossus (morph): Fixed an issue where the damage from this Ultimate could be dodged, despite being a player-sourced Area of Effect attack.
ESO Patch Note|6.2.5|* Removed this Ultimate and the morphs' Major Vulnerability invulnerability.
- Increased the duration of Major Vulnerability applied from these to 12 seconds per hit, up from 8. This will increase the total duration to 15 seconds per cast.
[Patch 6.3.5] * Fixed an issue where this Ultimate and its morphs could return as Damage over Time in some cases.
[Patch 7.2.5] * Fixed an issue where this ability and its morphs could hit targets outside of its line of sight.
ESO Patch Note|9.1.5|* Reduced the cost of this Ultimate and its morphs to 175, down from 225.
- Increased the damage per hit of all versions by approximately 11%.
- Pestilent Colossus (morph): This morph now also always guarantees the Diseased status effect on each hit.
- Glacial Colossus: This morph now also extends the duration of Major Vulnerability to 17 seconds per hit, up from 12.
- This Ultimate has been significantly reigned in after Major Vulnerability became less outright potent and more accessible from other actions in the game, causing the Ultimate to no longer instill the same fear it once did. In order to help rectify this and give some much-needed love to Necromancers' ability to bring death to their foes, we've made it easier to cast with a significant cost reduction and added a small damage bonus to boot. We've also tried to add some more distinction between these morphs; Pestilent now reinforces additional damage and makes it harder for your foes to bounce back with healing, while Glacial brings a slow and bitter end to those who try to weather the cold by extending the Major Vulnerability duration.
