Berserker Strike deals Physical Damage to a target and other targets nearby, ignoring their Physical and Spell Resistance and increasing yours by the amount ignored from the initial target for a time. As such, it is most effective against heavily resistant opponents. The Onslaught morph will give you Penetration instead of Resistances for a short time, providing an opportunity for a strong burst of damage. Or you can give yourself immunity to most Crowd Control abilities by choosing the Berserker Rage morph.

## Notes
- Berserker Strike is very useful in PVP, as enemy players may have high Physical Resistance and Spell Resistance scores that are too tough to penetrate through normal means.
- Onslaught is best combined with hard-hitting direct damage attacks, such as Blighted Blastbones or Uppercut.
