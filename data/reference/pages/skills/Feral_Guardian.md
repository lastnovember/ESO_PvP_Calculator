Feral Guardian summons a bear to fight for you that deals Magic Damage over time and occasionally deals Area Effect damage in front of it, stunning enemies hit. Summoning the bear has no cost, but once summoned, you can activate Guardian's Wrath for 75 Ultimate which causes Magic Damage and deals 100% additional damage to targets under 25% health. The Eternal Guardian morph will respawn when killed (once per minute) so you don't need to worry about re-summoning it each time and increases the damage of Guardian's Wrath to low Health targets, while Wild Guardian deals Bleed Damage instead and quintuples the chance of applying the Hemorrhaging status effect.

## Notes
- Players who purchase the Morrowind Collectors Edition or Collectors Pack upgrade are granted access to the Slate-Gray Summoned Bear, a unique gray skin for the bear which can be equipped through the Collections menu.
- While summoning the bear can be done at any time (and should be done well in advance of battle given its long casting time), using its special ability requires a target, which the bear will rush and attack immediately. In solo PvE play against tougher opponents, this is most effective as an opening attack, as NPCs tend to concentrate their attacks on whoever attacks them first, so by opening with this ability and standing back for a few seconds, you can let the bear take the brunt of the attacks.
- Feral Guardian will be unsummoned if you use your weapon swap unless you have selected the skill as your ultimate on both bars. If you have the Bond With Nature passive, this can be used to quickly restore some Health at no cost, but given the casting time of re-summoning the bear when you switch back (even though this is also free), this is not a particularly viable strategy.
- Eternal Guardian is a recommended morph for the Warden Initiate build.
- This skill was called "War Bear" in pre-release material.
