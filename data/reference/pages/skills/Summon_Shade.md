Summon Shade summons a shade of yourself to fight at your side. The shade does Magic Damage, and reduces its targets' ability to cause damage. It can be controlled, but unlike most familiars, they are intangible. This means that they cannot be killed- and, likewise, can neither taunt nor tank damage. If you attempt to call a shade to attack, it will not persist unless you're in combat.

The Dark Shade morph looks identical to the unmorphed Shade, but persists for longer. It does more damage than its counter-morph, and will occasionally use a Whirlwind attack, hitting all enemies nearby. Aside from its extra skill, it behaves identical to its unmorphed version.

The Shadow Image morph wields a bow instead of daggers, and is stationary where you cast it. Casting the spell again while the Shadow Image is active will cause you to teleport to its location, which can be a good way to escape if you're overwhelmed. Although it has less overall damage than its counter-morph, its teleport feature gives it a bit more utility.

## Notes
- The Shadow Image morph is called "Gloom Wraith" in the logs and shoots "Corrosive Arrows" dealing magic damage.
