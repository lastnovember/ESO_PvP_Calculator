Living Vines surrounds you or the lowest Health ally nearby with vines which will restore their Health whenever they take damage. Leeching Vines also heals the affected target when they attack an enemy that attacked them, while Living Trellis gives guaranteed healing when the effect expires.

## Notes
- This skill and its morphs don't work on Companions.
