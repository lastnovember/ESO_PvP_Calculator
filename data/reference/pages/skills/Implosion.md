Implosion gives all physical attacks and Shock Damage spells a chance to deal additional damage to low Health targets. This includes not only the skills in the Storm Calling line, but also skills in Destruction Staff, when using a Lightning Staff.plain

## Notes
- Prior to Update 10 this passive was named Disintegrate and only affected shock spells.
- In Update 21, this skill was replaced with Amplitude, which increase damage against enemies based on their current health percentage.
