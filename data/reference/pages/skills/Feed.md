Feed causes targets you feed upon to be Off-Balanced and stunned for a short time. It can be triggered by sneaking behind a target and activating the "Feed" synergy that pops up (akin to Blade of Woe), displaying an animation where you feed upon the target's blood for a few seconds, killing them and increasing your Vampire Stage. As Feed and the Blade of Woe use the same toggle, Feed must be used by standing at least 3 metersplain behind the target, while Blade of Woe is used by standing much closer.

Various animations can play when Feed is used, some of which depend on the relative heights of the user and the target:
- Teleporting in front of the target, biting their neck and then pushing them to the ground, and licking your hand as they fall
- Teleporting behind the target, taking a bite out of their neck, and licking your arm once they fall
- Jumping onto the target's back, pinning them to the ground as you feed on their neck
- Levitating the target as they helplessly flailed around in the air, turning them around to face you as you drink their blood through a magical bloody stream leading directly
- Jumping on the target's back and feeding on their neck (short user, tall target)

- Grabbing the target with one hand and dangling them, biting their neck, and then dropping them to the ground (typically tall user, short target)

## Notes
- Feed was introduced in Update 26, replacing Savage Feeding.
- NPCs will have special dialogue if they catch a player feeding.
