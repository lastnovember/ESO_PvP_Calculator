Overload applies Shock Damage to all standard light and heavy attacks for the duration of the effect. The light attack affects a single target (at a range, regardless of your equipped weapon), while a heavy attack hits all enemies in front of you. The heavy attack can be held down for a continuous attack until you run out of Ultimate. Power Overload increases the attack range and damage done, while Energy Overload restores Magicka and Stamina with each attack.

## Notes
- This ability was reworked in Update 20. Prior to this update:
- When activating Overload, you would gain an additional skill bar that can only be modified while Overload was active. You could use any skills except for weapon skills on this bar. Note that just like your weapon-swap bar, any toggle-type skills (such as summons) had to be in the same slot on both your normal and Overload skill bars, or they would be canceled when you switched between normal and Overload mode.
- You could not use your weapon-swap while Overload was active. Any passive bonuses from your equipped weapon would also be disabled. Set bonuses from weapons and/or shield would still apply, however.
- Unlike other Ultimate abilities, Overload does not fully empty your Ultimate pool, it only subtracts a small amount of Ultimate for each light attack, or drains slowly over time while performing a heavy attack.
- The heavy attack does direct damage and hits every 1 second.
