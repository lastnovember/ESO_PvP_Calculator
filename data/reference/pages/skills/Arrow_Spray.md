Arrow Spray deals physical damage. The area of effect is cone-shaped; at close range, fewer targets will be hit. The Bombard morph will immobilize your targets for a few seconds in addition to reducing the cost, while the Acid Spray morph changes the damage type to poison damage and deals additional poison damage over time.

## Notes
- The Piercing Spray set from Asylum Sanctorium reduces an enemy's armor against all bow attacks after using this ability.
