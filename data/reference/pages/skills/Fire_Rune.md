Fire Rune creates a rune trap on the ground which deals Fire Damage to an enemy when they touch it. The Volcanic Rune morph will also knock up and stun most enemies, while the Scalding Rune morph adds a damage over time effect.

## Notes
- Only one Fire Rune may be active at a time. Casting a second one before the first has been triggered or expires will cause it to disappear.
- It is possible to affect multiple enemies at once with this spell if there are multiple enemies near each other when it arms. Casting the spell does not count as an attack, and will not cause enemies to attack you until after the trap has been triggered.
