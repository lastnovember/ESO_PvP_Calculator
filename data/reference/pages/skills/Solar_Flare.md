Solar Flare deals Magic Damage to an enemy, increases the damage of your other class abilities and increases your damage dealt with Heavy Attacks. The Dark Flare morph reduces both your target's as well as other nearby targets' ability to heal and shield strength (though only your primary target actually takes damage). Or you can choose the Solar Barrage morph which changes this to an instant-cast, pulsing Area Effect damage over time that's centered on you.
