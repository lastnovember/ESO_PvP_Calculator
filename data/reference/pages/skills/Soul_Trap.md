Soul Trap deals Magic Damage over time to a target, and if the target is killed while the spell is still in effect, it will fill an empty soul gem (provided you have one). While the damage is modest, filled soul gems can be used to recharge your magical weapons, or to revive yourself or your allies on the battlefield, thus avoid the long trek back from the nearest Wayshrine. The Soul-Splitting Trap morph also affect enemies in an area around the target, allowing you to full multiple soul gems at once but halves the duration, while the Consuming Trap morph will restore your Health, Magicka, and Stamina if the enemy is killed while the spell is in effect.

## Notes
- This skill is given to you for free as soon as you are given the Soul Magic skill tree. Morphing it still costs a skill point however.
- It is possible for multiple players to fill soul gems from the same enemy if both of them cast Soul Trap on it.
- The target need not be killed by you to receive the soul (and restoration from Consuming Trap). If it is killed by any means, you will receive the benefits.
- The Soul Lock passive skill grants you a chance of automatically collecting souls without the use of Soul Trap. When used in combination with Soul Trap, you have a chance at getting two souls from a kill (or even three with the Soul-Splitting Trap morph).
