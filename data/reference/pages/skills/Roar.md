Roar instills fear in up to 6plain nearby enemies, causing them to flee and be set off balance. This ability also grants a stack of Blood Hunger which further empowers Gnash Rending Claws and up to 12 nearby allies can activate the Feeding Frenzy Synergy synergy. When activated it will increase damage and apply Minor Force for 30 seconds. Having this ability slotted will grant you Major Prophecy and Savagery, increasing your Weapon Critical and Spell Critical.

Ferocious Roar grants you and your allies Major Courage and also allows you to use your own Feeding Frenzy synergy.

The Deafening Roar morph will apply Major Cowardice and Maim debuffs to enemies, and converts your slot bonuses to Major Evasion and Minor Protection. Using this morph will also turn the Gnash ability into a taunt while Bracing.

## Notes
- The passive effects of Roar apply even when the ability is slotted on an active, non-Werewolf skill bar.
- Deafening Roar replaced the ability Rousing Roar in Update 19 which previously increased yours and your allies Weapon Damage.

Developer Comment
We're making Roar and its morphs the home of the Feeding Frenzy synergy in efforts to make the effect more easily accessible, while also adding a little incentive to bringing the pack to groups with the unique damage bonus. Additionally, we're improving the Terrified mechanic into Blood Hunger, which can now stack for multiple uses, helping reward a more diverse rotation in PvE and PvP, while also being easier to use at a baseline. Blood Hunger isn't completely exclusive to Roar either, as the effect can now be referenced by other morphs, for those who are looking to sink their teeth into more complicated rotations. This should help keep the Werewolf experience simpler, but with some added forms of mastery for those who find themselves hungering for more.
