Bone Goliath Transformation causes you to transform into a fearsome skeletal beast, healing you and significantly increasing your Max Health. While transformed, your light and heavy attacks restore your Health. You can still use all of your normal active abilities while transformed. Using this ability in front of justice neutral witnesses will earn you a bounty. The Pummeling Goliath morph gives you a powerful Bash attack which can hit multiple opponents, while Ravenous Goliath deals damage to all nearby enemies and heals you by the same amount.

## Notes
- While transforming you are invincible and untargetable but you are locked in place, you cannot perform any action and you do not count for capturing/defending a capture point in PvP.
- Ravenous Goliath is a recommended morph for the Necromancer Initiate, Baleful Summoner, and Undying Warrior builds, while Pummeling Goliath is recommended for Deathbringer.

ESO Patch Note|5.0.10|* Pummeling Goliath:
- The bash attack from this ability will no longer target bridges or targets behind you in some circumstances.
- The bash attack will now always cost resources, instead of only costing resources if you hit a target.

- The special Bash augment will now use both your Weapon Damage and Stamina, rather than just Weapon Damage. This Ultimate will now also scale dynamically and use your Spell Damage and Max Magicka if they are higher, which is a standard for our Ultimates that scale off your offensive stats.

- Fixed an issue where activating the special Bash attack from this morph would not turn your character to face the direction you cast it in.
[Patch 7.0.5] * Pummeling Goliath's bash is now considered a Player Class Ability.
[Patch 7.3.5] * Pummeling Goliath (morph): Fixed an issue where this Ultimate's Bash override did not receive the cost and cooldown adjustments that the core mechanic Bash got. It can now also proc enchantments or poisons.
