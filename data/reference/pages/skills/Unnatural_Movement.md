Unnatural Movement is a passive ability that reduces sprint cost at Stage 4 Vampirism. It also turns you invisible if you sprint without pause for a few seconds. At higher ranks, the amount of time it takes you to turn invisible is reduced by half.

This skill can ease the burden of fleeing from town guards and combat with hostile NPCs by allowing you to vanish from their sight.
