The Unblinking Eye creates an area of effect dealing magic damage over six seconds at the location. You can move the area by activating "The Unblinking Eye" again. The Languid Eye morph adds a snare and deals more damage as time passes. The Tide King's Gaze morph can only be cast on an enemy and follows it. The duration is also increased to eight seconds.
