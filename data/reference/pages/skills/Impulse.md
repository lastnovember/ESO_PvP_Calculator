Impulse causes a ring of elemental damage that hits all targets around you. That damage will be either Flame Damage, Frost Damage, or Shock Damage depending on the type of staff you have equipped. The Pulsar morph triples your chances of applying your staff's respective status effect and reduces your targets' Maximum Health, which limits the amount of health they can restore, while Elemental Ring can be cast at a target location.

This spell changes its name and icon depending on the type of staff you have equipped. Specifically, Impulse becomes:
- 18 Fire Impulse
- 18 Frost Impulse
- 18 Shock Impulse
Pulsar becomes:
- 18 Flame Pulsar
- 18 Frost Pulsar
- 18 Storm Pulsar
and Elemental Ring becomes:
- 18 Fire Ring
- 18 Frost Ring
- 18 Shock Ring

## Notes
- Inferno types' Afterburn does up to 300% more damage to enemies under 50% Health
- Frost types' Minor Protection is applied for 6 seconds and also applies to nearby Group Members.
- Shock types' damage are increased by 5% per enemy hit, up to 30%. A yellow number is briefly shown on the skill icon to tell how many targets were hit by the spell.
- Hitting a full-health enemy with Pulsar will have the effect of doing 10% of their health as damage before the Pulsar damage is applied.
- The Wild Impulse set from Blackrose Prison will reduce the Magicka cost by 10% and add additional Flame, Frost and Shock damage over 8 seconds.
- Before Update 13, Elemental Ring dealt additional damage over time instead of being cast at a target area.
