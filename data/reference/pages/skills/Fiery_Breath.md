Fiery Breath deals Fire Damage to enemies within a 10 meter radius in front of you, and applies fire damage over time. Noxious Breath changes the cost to use Stamina and the damage from fire to poison, as well as reducing your targets' resistances. Or you can choose Engulfing Flames, which will increase the target's vulnerability to other flame attacks, either from you or other players.

## Notes
- The Damage Over Time effects applied by all three skills are considered as "Area DoT" for the purposes of status effects chance of apply.
- The Noxious Breath morph was added in Update 10, replacing 20 Burning Breath, which dealt Flame Damage instead of Poison Damage, and cost magicka.
- Engulfing Flames is a recommended morph for the Inferno Dragon build, while Noxious Breath is recommended for Venomous Dragon.
