Miner Hireling gives you a hireling, Valinka Stoneheaver (later Karamel Little-Leaf, Aksel Rockbiter and Ludovic Laumont), who will give you an assortment of Blacksmithing items every day. These will arrive in your mailbox as letters with attachments which must be claimed within 30 days or they will be lost. You do not receive shipments if you don't log on, so they won't pile up if you don't play for a few days (or use a different character).
