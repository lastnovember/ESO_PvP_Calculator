Nova deals magic damage over the target area and reduces enemies' attack damage. Allies can activate the Supernova Synergy synergy to deal additional damage and stun enemies within range. Solar Prison changes the Synergy to Gravity Crush Synergy Supernova, which increases the damage and stun duration. Or Solar Disturbance reduces the cost and reduces enemies' damage done for a longer duration.

## Notes
- Solar Disturbance is a recommended morph for the Templar Initiate build, while Solar Prison is recommended for Bastion of Light and Beacon of Hope.
