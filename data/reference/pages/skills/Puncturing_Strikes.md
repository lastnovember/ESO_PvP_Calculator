Puncturing Strikes deals Magic Damage reduces the movement speed of up to six enemies in front of you with each strike. The Biting Jabs morph changes it to a Stamina-based skill dealing Physical Damage, increases chance of applying Sundered to 10% and increases your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage. Puncturing Sweep heals you for a percentage of the damage done instead.

## Notes
- This skill uses a Nighthollow Style Staff for the appearance of the spear.
- A number is displayed on the top right of the skill icon on your bar and tells how many enemies got hit by the last strike.
- Up until Update 35 this skill dealt damage 5 times and had a different animation and model.

- Puncturing Sweep is a recommended morph for the Templar Initiate and Gleaming Champion builds, while Biting Jabs is recommended for Luminous Warrior.
