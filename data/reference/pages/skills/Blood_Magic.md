Blood Magic will restore a percentage of your Health each time you cast a Dark Magic ability with a cost if you are not at full health. If you are, the higher of your Max Magicka or Stamina is increased for a time instead.

## Notes
- The Conservation of Energy Class Mastery ability makes rank 2 work with any ability with a cost, and restores Magicka and Stamina when it activates.

## Patch Notes
[Patch 1.6.5] * Increased healing on both ranks of this passive to 4%/8% maximum health from 3%/5%.
ESO Patch Note|4.0.5|* Increased the healing done when this passive ability procs to 5/10% of your Max Health from 4/8%.
- Fixed an issue where this passive ability would not trigger if you hit an enemy immune to snares with your Daedric Mines abilities.
[Patch 10.0.5] * This passive now activates when you cast a Dark Magic ability with a cost, rather than when you hit an enemy with a directly applied Dark Magic ability. This was done to make the passive more reliable and easy to understand.
ESO Patch Note|11.1.5|* This passive now only heals you if you are not at full Health. If you are at full Health, it now increases the higher of your Max Magicka or Stamina by 5/10% for 10 seconds.
- Developer Comment: We’re taking the passive slot effect from Bound Armor and its morphs and placing it here to ensure base Sorcerer classes can retain the effects (and get a small boost in some cases), while also moving it to a less utilized skill line. We know this isn’t a silver bullet to making Dark Magic a desired skill line - but we’re using this as an experiment to see if this sort of shuffling of power is seen as a good thing or not in the brave new subclassing world. Definitely give us your feedback on this.
