Render Flesh heals you or an ally in front of you by a significant amount compared to other similar healing spells, but has a drawback in that it reduces your healing received and shield strength for a short time afterwards. The Resistant Flesh also increases the Physical Resistance and Spell Resistance of the healed target, while Blood Sacrifice can heal an additional target by consuming a nearby corpse.

## Notes
- Resistant Flesh is a recommended morph for the Necromancer Initiate and Baleful Summoner builds, while Blood Sacrifice is recommended for Flesh Sculptor.
