# Online:Torchbearer

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Torchbearer
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

:Conjure an imbued torch and sweep the area in front of you three times with its power.

Torchbearer is a Scribed skill Grimoire, on the Fighters Guild skill line. It can be purchased after earning the Fighters Guild Skill Master achievement from Chronicler Firandil in the Scholarium once per character.
It costs 50,000 the first time, and 10,000 for other characters on your account after that. The name of the scribed skill changes depending upon your choice of Focus Script.

- Channel Time: 2 seconds
- Target: Cone
- Radius: 8 meters
