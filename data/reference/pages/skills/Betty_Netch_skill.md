Betty Netch summons a small Netch to increase your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage and periodically remove negative effects. If no effects are removed it instead increase your damage done by 5%. It is free to cast and lasts for 19-25 seconds. The Blue Betty morph will also restore Magicka over time, while Bull Netch will restore Stamina over time.

## Notes
- The netch will remain for its full duration, even if the skill is removed from your skill bar (most likely via weapon swap). Thus, it can be useful to keep it on your secondary weapon swap and only switch over to summon when needed, freeing up the slots on your main skill bar for other things.
- Due to this skill's zero cost, casting this with the Bond With Nature passive unlocked can be a good way to regain health when out of resources.
- Blue Betty is a recommended morph for the Warden Initiate, Beast Caller, and Master Herbalist builds, while Bull Netch is recommended for Savage Stalker and Guardian of the Wild.

ESO Patch Note|10.0.5|* This ability and its morphs now also increase your damage done by 5% for 5 seconds if no negative effect was removed from the ability.
- Developer Comment: In order to help the Warden's damage production without rocking the boat too much in PvP (where they already are plenty strong), we're adding a conditional bonus that will mainly activate in PvE. We are also trying to add more sources of damage to their damage focused skill line, Animal Companions, so we can slowly look at shifting passive damage, such as Piercing Cold and the density of Glacial Presence, out of their tanking focused skill line. To clear up some of the confusion and concern we saw during PTS testing, we are NOT looking to remove morph diversity that helps frost mage DPS shine, like Arctic Blast or Winter’s Revenge. Our main concerns are on how much passive damage frost Wardens rely on to do reliable damage, and how it is allowing tankier Wardens to often out-damage others in PvP with little to no draw backs.
