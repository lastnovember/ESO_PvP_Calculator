Drain Power deals Magic Damage to all nearby enemies, and increases your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage if an enemy is hit. The Power Extraction morph converts this into a Stamina cost, and changes the damage to Disease Damage, increases your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage even more and reduces the Weapon Damage and Spell Damage of affected targets. Or you can use the Sap Essence morph, which adds healing for you and your allies that scales higher the more targets are hit.

## Notes
- Sap Essence is a recommended morph for the Nightblade Initiate and Blood Magus builds.
