Shadow Cloak causes you to become invisible for as long as the skill is toggled and you have the magicka to sustain it, enabling you to slip away from a thorny situation or prepare a stealth attack. It works well with other abilities that depend on stealth, most notably Concealed Weapon. You also get a unique damage bonus when the skill starts and ends that is equivalent to Major Berserk. It also passively reduces your damage taken You can guarantee a Critical Strike for the next attack with the Shadowy Disguise morph. While Dark Cloak removes the invisibility and the toggle, and instead restores Health over time, restoring more if you are bracing.

## Notes
- As with all other "While slotted on either ability bar" skills, this doesn't work if you have Oakensoul Ring and have this skill in the disabled ability bar.
- Shadowy Disguise is a recommended morph for the Nightblade Initiate build.
- Before Update 44, Shadow Cloak and Shadowy disguise weren't toggle-based but granted Invisibility for 3 seconds for a static cost.
- Born From Shadow buff uses the following icon: ON-icon-misc-Born From Shadow.png

ESO Patch Note|10.2.5|* This ability and the Shadowy Disguise morph are now toggled abilities, rather than duration-based ones. While active, the ability will drain 3240 Magicka (base cost) every 2 seconds, rather than costing 4590 base Magicka cost and persisting for 3 seconds. While active and moving the ability will also disable your Magicka Recovery, but while stationary the cost is cut in half, to allow for more strategic use and a closer feeling to the Crouch functionality all characters can use. In addition to this, these abilities will now grant Born from Shadow for 10 seconds when the ability is activated and again when the abilities end, which increases your damage done against monsters by 10%. This rework has not adjusted how the invisibility functionality removes itself, and casting certain abilities will continue to naturally disable the effect (and penalties) the same as before.
- Developer Comment: In this pass we're looking at the opportunity costs of Shadow Cloak and trying to create a more accessible skill for any Nightblade build, while simultaneously ensuring there are more stark costs associated with maintaining invisibility. The toggled nature should make the activation of Shadow Cloak feel significantly smoother to weave in and out of the shadows, while the disabling of Magicka Recovery while moving should stomp out the ability to perpetually remain undetected without serious investment and strategic use. In addition to this, we're adding a small benefit for activating the skill in PvE situations, where the invisibility often outright doesn't work or incurs a heavy DPS loss. The goal with Born from Shadow is not to outright carry damage in PvE, but rather give us a foundation to potentially build off of by allowing us to easier track when Nightblades utilize the sneaky parts of their toolkit in future iterative work.
