Caltrops deals Physical Damage over time in a large area, it also reduces the movement speed of all affected targets. Anti-Cavalry Caltrops increases the duration and also drains the Stamina of all enemy mounts, which may force them to dismount, while the Razor Caltrops morph also reduces the resistances of affected targets.

## Notes
- Previously, the Anti-Cavalry Caltrops morph would automatically dismount any mounted enemies, but now it just drains their mounts' Stamina instead. Thus, players who have invested in the Stamina Riding Skill may be able to withstand this attack for longer without being dismounted.
