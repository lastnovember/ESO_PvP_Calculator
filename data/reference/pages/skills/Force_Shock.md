Force Shock deals elemental damage from all three magical elements. The Crushing Shock morph reduces its cost and interrupts enemies who are casting a spell, putting them off-balance and stunning them, while Force Pulse also damages up to two nearby enemies if they are currently afflicted with a status effect.

## Notes
- This attack counts as three separate simultaneous direct attacks, each of which has an individual chance of causing a Critical strike and applying its status effect, so it pairs well with effects that trigger when dealing damage such as the Exsanguinate verse in Infinite Archive which triggers thrice when you cast Force Shock or its morphs, and with bonuses that increase your damage done by a flat amount such as the Draugrkin's Grip set which boosts each separate part of Force Shock and its morphs.
- The magic damage done by the Force Pulse morph is considered Area of Effect damage.
- Force Pulse is a recommended morph for the Sorcerer Elemental Arcanist build.
- The Concentrated Force set from Asylum Sanctorium will guarantee the Burning, Concussion, and Chilled status effects are applied on every second cast.
