Steadfast Ward grants a Damage Shield to you or an ally with the lowest Health, scaling based on the targets missing Health. Ward Ally always shields you when you cast it, while Healing Ward restores Health to the target equal to 33% of the shield's remaining strength every second.

## Notes
- The Mender's Ward set from Blackrose Prison will apply Major Vitality to your targets for 4 seconds when they are affected by this skill, increasing their healing received and damage shield strength by 12%.
- Ward Ally is a recommended morph for the Elder Dragon build.
