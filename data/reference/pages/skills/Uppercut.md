Uppercut is a simple skill that deals Physical Damage. The Dizzying Swing morph puts off-balance every hit and stuns if the enemy is already off-balance, it is a good way to interrupt enemies who are charging a powerful attack. The Wrecking Blow grants you Empower and Major Berserk for a short time, making it a good opener for a larger follow-up attack.

## Notes
- The Radial Uppercut and Perfected Radial Uppercut sets from Blackrose Prison will cause this ability to also damage nearby targets for 45% of the damage.

ESO Patch Note|5.2.5|* Reduced the damage dealt by this ability and its morphs by approximately 16% to ensure it does not eclipse other damage abilities such as DoTs.

ESO Patch Note|5.3.4|* Dizzying Swing (morph):
- This morph now stuns enemies who are already off balance.
- Enemies hit by this ability that are immune to Off Balance are now snared by 40% for 2 seconds.
- Developer Comment: Like the situation of Power Lash, the changes to Off Balance greatly affected the power of this morph in one-on-one scenarios. While we are okay with the loss of a frequent stun on a spammable, we wanted to still ensure this morph didn't feel like it wasn't adding anything to your gameplay throughout the entirety of the fight; we've opted to add in a function that helps you become more "sticky" to your target when trying to use it multiple times. Traditionally, we try and avoid CC's without intense requirements on high damage abilities, but similar to Puncturing Strikes, cast time abilities are far harder to land at a frequent cadence, so we are more comfortable adding the snare here in addition to enabling a contextual stun with Off Balance.
* Wrecking Blow (morph): Increased the duration of Empower granted to 5 seconds, up from 3.
ESO Patch Note|8.3.5|* Fixed an issue where this ability and the Wrecking Blow morph would lose targeting far easier than the Dizzying Swing morph.
