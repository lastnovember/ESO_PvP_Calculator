Bitter Harvest lets you extract Health and Ultimate from nearby corpses. It also reduces your damage taken while slotted. The Deaden Pain morph further reduces your damage taken while the healing is in effect, while Necrotic Potency increases the amount of Ultimate gained.

## Notes
- Necrotic Potency is a recommended morph for the Undying Warrior build.
- The Necrotic Potency morph's tooltip erroneously adds the word "additional" in reference to the number of corpses needing to be present. Despite this, the morph works the same way as its counterpart and the base skill-the Ultimate and Health gain both occur even if only one corpse is consumed.
- When out of combat, consuming corpses does not grant Ultimate points.
