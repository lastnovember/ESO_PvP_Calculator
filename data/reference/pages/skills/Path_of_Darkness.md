Path of Darkness increases yours and your allies' movement speed when on the path and for 4 seconds after leaving it. The Twisting Path morph deals Magic Damage to enemies in the target area, reduces the cost and increases the area of effect, while the Refreshing Path morph also heals you and your allies while on the path and provides a boost to Stamina and Magicka Recovery when on the path and for 4 seconds after leaving it.

## Notes
- Refreshing Path is a recommended morph for the Nightblade Initiate, Living Shadow, and Blood Magus builds.
- Path of Darkness and Refreshing Path create a rectangular area of effect. Twisting Path creates an approximately 60 degree cone.
- Enemies will see a highlighted border along the sides of the area of effect. This is not visible to allies.
