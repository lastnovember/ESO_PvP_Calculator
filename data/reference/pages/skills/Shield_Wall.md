Shield Wall causes you to automatically block all blockable attacks without costing Stamina and without needing to block yourself for a short time. The Spell Wall morph will also reflect projectiles back to their casters, while Shield Discipline will also negate the cost of all non-ultimate One Hand and Shield abilities.

## Notes
- Spell Wall is very useful in PVP, as it can reflect hard hitting projectile bursts back to the attacker.
