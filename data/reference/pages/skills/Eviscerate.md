Eviscerate deals Magic Damage to a single enemy with the damage increasing based on your missing Health. The Blood for Blood morph changes it to a Health cost and deals even greater damage based on your missing Health but prevents you from receiving healing from your allies for a short period. Alternatively, the Arterial Burst morph will always Critically Strike when you are under half Health.

## Notes
- This skill replaced Drain Essence in Update 26. Drain Essence leeched Health from the target over 3 seconds and stunned them.
- Prior to Update 27, you could still be healed by allies after using Blood for Blood. This made the skill particularly effective in groups.
