The Imperfect Ring applies a magic damage over time effect to a target and enemies nearby it. It also creates a synergy on the original target which if activated, deals area of effect frost damage to it and enemies nearby. The Fulminating Rune morph adds a frost damage area of effect after some on time on the original target and makes the synergy usable by 3 allies. The Rune of Displacement morph only activates after two seconds and pulls nearby enemies to the original target.
