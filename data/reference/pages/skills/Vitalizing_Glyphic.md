Vitalizing Glyphic summons a glyphic at the pointed location which gives effects to you and your allies in a wide area around it for a duration. You can heal it to increase the potency of the effects it gives. The Glyphic of the Tides morph spawns at a higher health and gives Major Protection when fully healed. The Resonating Glyphic spawns at even higher health but you have to deal damage to it to increase the potency of its effects, also you cannot cast it while out-of-combat.
