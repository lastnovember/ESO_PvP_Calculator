Mesmerize stuns enemies that are facing you. The Hypnosis morph changes the area of effect from a cone in front of you to a circle around you, while the Stupefy morph snares enemies for 5 seconds after the 5-second stun ends.

Notably, this skill is the only way to talk to some vendor NPCs as a stage 4 vampire or when you have a Notorious or higher infamy level.
