Cleansing Ritual removes up to two negative status effects from the caster and heals nearby allies. Allies can activate the Purify Synergy synergy to remove negative status effects from themselves and receive additional healing. Ritual of Retribution damages enemies in the area instead of healing allies, while Extended Ritual increases the duration and the amount of harmful effects cleansed from yourself.

## Notes
- Ritual of Retribution was added in Update 10, replacing Purifying Ritual, which had a reduced Magicka cost and cleansed one additional effect per Rank. The cleansing of additional effects was moved to Extended Ritual, which only increased the duration.
- Extended Ritual is a recommended morph for the Templar Initiate build, while Ritual of Retribution is recommended for Gleaming Champion.
