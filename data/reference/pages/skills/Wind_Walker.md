Wind Walker increases your Stamina Recovery and reduces the Stamina cost of abilities for each piece of Medium Armor you are wearing, up to a total of 14% or 28% recovery, and 7% or 14% cost reduction, depending on how many points you invest in this passive.

## Patch Notes
* Adjusted this ability so it now reduces the cost of stamina abilities by 1% per piece of medium armor equipped, and 2% at rank 2.
* This ability now reduces stamina costs by 14/21% at Rank I and Rank II.
* This passive ability now reduces the cost of your Stamina abilities by 1/2% per piece of Medium Armor, down from 2/3%.
