Swiftly Forgotten causes your bounty and heat to decrease more quickly. The first level is roughly double the normal rate of bounty degradation. The amount of bounty and heat removed is dependent on player level. This is not a handicap at lower levels, however, as the amount of bounty and heat you earn from witnessed crimes is also proportional to your level by the same factor. The numbers displayed here are based on a character of Level 50 CP160.
