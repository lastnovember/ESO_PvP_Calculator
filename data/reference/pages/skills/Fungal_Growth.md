Fungal Growth restores the Health of the caster and any allies in front of you in a conical area. The Enchanted Growth morph will increase the Magicka and Stamina Recovery of all affected targets for 20 seconds, while Soothing Spores has a Stamina cost and has a stronger healing effect on allies close to the caster.

## Notes
- Enchanted Growth is a recommended morph for the Warden Initiate, Beast Caller and Master Herbalist builds, while Soothing Spores is recommended for Savage Stalker.
