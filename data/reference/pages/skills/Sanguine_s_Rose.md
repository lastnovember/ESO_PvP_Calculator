# Online:Sanguine's Rose

Source: https://en.uesp.net/wiki/Online:Sanguine's_Rose
License: CC BY-SA, UESP

Sanguine's Rose is an artifact associated with the Daedric Prince Sanguine and is an artifact to potentially be released in the future. Sheogorath unleashed the various artifacts upon Cyrodiil for the purpose of putting an end to his boredom surrounding the lack of chaos in the Three Banners War, and for the fun of keeping his Daedric siblings on their guard.

Sanguine's Rose increases all of your stats, making you a truly formidable foe. While you carry this weapon, you may not use any other skills.
