Amphibian gives Argonians a natural affinity for Restoration Staves, allowing them to gain experience more quickly in that skill line, and also increases their speed while swimming. All Argonians have this ability for free from the start.

## Notes
- Prior to Update 11, this skill was called "Restoration Expertise", and only improved your Restoration experience gain.

## Patch Notes
* This passive ability has been renamed to "Amphibian." Also, in addition to the current benefit it now increases your swimming speed by 50%.
