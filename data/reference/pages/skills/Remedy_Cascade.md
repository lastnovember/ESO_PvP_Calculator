Remedy Cascade is a channeled skill which continuously heals yourself and allies in a rectangle. You can consumme your Crux to also restore Magicka and Stamina to your allies. The Cascading Fortune morphs heals more on low health targets while the Curative Surge morph heals more the longer you channel the skill.
