## Patch Notes
ESO Patch Note|10.2.5|* Impervious Runeward: Reduced the size of this morph’s shield after the 1 second window by ~56%.
- Developer Comment: We're introducing a new case on this morph that results in a portion of the total shield strength becoming front loaded, rather than giving a standard sized shield if the primary one doesn't break. Our goal is to retain the feeling of being able to shrug off a tremendous amount of damage within the first second, akin to something like a parry, while ensuring the ability has a tradeoff, so if you miss the activation window, it's weaker than the safer morph option.
