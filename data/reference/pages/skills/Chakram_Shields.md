Chakram Shields makes you launch to yourself or up to 4 allies in a cone in front of you green circles projectiles which grants a damage shield. The discs prioritizes the ally you are pointing and allies with low health and without damage shields. The Chakram of Destiny morph reduces the cost with the skill's rank, generates a crux and grants extra damage shield strength if you cast it on a target which already has your damage shield from this skill. The Tidal Chakram morph instead consumes crux to heal allies every second equal to the remaining strength of the damage shield they have, a second for every crux consumed.

## Notes
- The discs that go to preferred targets have extra visuals. They display them with players, friendly NPCs, even player's summons but not the mounts furnishings in houses which always get the base disc visual instead.
- The discs do not have to reach their targets for them to grant a damage shield, it is only visual.
- As with all Crux-generating skills, you do not create a Crux with the "Chakram of Destiny" morph if you cast it while out of combat.
