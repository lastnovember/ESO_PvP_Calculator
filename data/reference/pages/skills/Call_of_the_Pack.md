Call of the Pack lets you and your allies remain in Werewolf form for longer (obviously provided they are also werewolves).

## Notes
- Transforming into a werewolf using the Pack Leader skill will give you a 60% reduction in the cost of remaining in your werewolf form by yourself.

## Patch Notes
[Patch 2.0.1] * Fixed an issue with this passive not correctly giving additional transformation time to nearby Werewolf allies. Call of the Pack now increases the duration of nearby allies' Werewolf.
[Patch 2.4.5] * Redesigned this passive ability so it now reduces the cost of staying in Werewolf form by 10/20% for each transformed Werewolf in your group, including yourself, up to a maximum of 40/80% at Ranks I/II.
[Patch 6.0.5] * This passive now works with Direwolves summoned from the Pack Leader.
[Patch 6.1.5] * Fixed an issue where this passive was not counting your Dire Wolves in some cases.
[Patch 12.0.5] *Call of the Pack -> Call of the Hunt: This passive now works by reducing the Ultimate cost of maintaining Werewolf form, rather than reducing the timer drained every second. It reduces the cost by 8/16% plus 8/16% per transformed group member or direwolf, including yourself, up to 40/80%.
