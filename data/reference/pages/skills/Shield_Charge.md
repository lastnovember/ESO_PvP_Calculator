Shield Charge makes you charge at an enemy, stunning them and dealing Physical Damage. It is a good way to close the distance between you and the target, or get yourself out of a tight spot by using it on a distant target when you are surrounded. Shielded Assault also gives you a Damage Shield, protecting you after your charge, while Invasion increases the stun duration when used at greater distances.

## Notes
- Like most charging attacks, it cannot be used when you are already within melee range of your target. You must back up or use a knockback attack to get them far enough away to use again, or charge at another more distant target.
- The Gallant Charge set from Blackrose Prison will reduce the Stamina cost of this ability, and make your next One Hand and Shield ability free to cast after using this ability.
