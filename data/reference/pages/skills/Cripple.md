Cripple deals Magic Damage over time to the enemy and reduces their movement speed. The Debilitate morph is five times more likely to apply the Overcharged Status Effect, which restores yours and your allies' Magicka when attacking the target. Or you can use the Crippling Grasp morph, which converts some of the damage over time to up front damage and immobilizes your target.
