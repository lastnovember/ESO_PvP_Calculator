Runeblades are three runeblades launched by your right hand while holding a book open with your left hand. They deal direct magic damage thrice and generate a Crux per cast. The Damage is increased by how many Crux you have. The Writhing Runeblades morph also increases this skill's critical chance based on how many Crux you have. The Escalating Runeblades morph increases the damage of the second runeblade by a bit and the third runeblade by a lot. The third runeblade now deals damage in an area.

## Notes
- The Crux generation only works when you're in combat before you cast the skill, thus initiating a combat with this skill will not generate a Crux.
