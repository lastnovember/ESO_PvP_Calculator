Rapid Fire causes you to fire a barrage of arrows at a single enemy. You can move around while firing without having to continue aiming at them. The Toxic Barrage morph adds Poison Damage to the attack, which lasts for another 8 seconds after the barrage. Or you can use Ballista, which drops a turret to perform the attack for you, freeing you up to use other attacks, but doing decreased damage

## Notes
- The Piercing Spray set from Asylum Sanctorium can cause this ability to ignore some armor when combined with Arrow Spray.
