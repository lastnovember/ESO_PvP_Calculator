Magicka Controller increases your Maximum Magicka and Magicka Recovery rate for each Mages Guild ability you have slotted. This includes the Ultimate, Meteor, so with the other four active skills, this gives you a maximum of 5% increase, or 10% with a second point invested in this passive.

## Patch Notes
* Fixed an issue where this passive ability was not dynamically updating the stats on the Character UI when you added Mages Guild abilities to your skill bar.
