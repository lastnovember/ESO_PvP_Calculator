Heavy Weapons gives you a variety of bonuses depending on your weapon type:
- Greatswords increase your Weapon Damage and Spell Damage.
- Battle Axes increase your critical damage.
- Mauls increase your Offensive Penetration.

## Notes
Prior to Update 29, this passive gave Greatswords an increase to your damage by a percentage, it gave Battle Axes a chance to apply a Bleed Damage over time and it made Mauls ignore a percentage of your target's armor value.
