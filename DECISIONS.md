# DECISIONS

Short record of modelling choices. One line each, newest at the bottom.

## Scope

- Phase 1 computes the character sheet only. No combat, no targets. (Scope from the task statement, no game claim.)
- Fixed: level 50 and CP160 gear (task statement). Every item and glyph is gold unless `flags.itemQuality` is on; then each item's `quality` and `enchantQuality` are read: traits from the quality columns of tables/uesp_Online_Traits_t00..t03.csv, glyphs from the pages/glyphs rows, weapon damage from tables/uesp_Online_Nirnhoned_t01..t02.csv row '160', set bonuses from tables/uesp_Online_Craftable_Sets_t03..t08.csv row '160'; armor rating below gold is an UNKNOWN. Set ranges resolve to their maximum because "6-300" runs from level 1 white to CP160 gold (pages/Craftable_Sets.md line 511).
- Out of scope this phase because they need a target or combat state (listed so they are not forgotten): damage done vs a specific target, penetration vs target resistance, Bloodthirsty trait, execute bonuses, Off Balance, status effect chance, Enemy Keep and Scroll bonuses, Emperorship, Empower, all 5 piece and 1 piece proc effects, weapon glyph procs, Major or Minor buffs from casting abilities, potions, Vengeance campaign stat standardisation, scribing signature scripts, companion skills. (Task statement, no game claim.)

## Data

- Patch notes (`data/patch-notes/html`) were searched for every constant. Where they hold a value, the newest note wins and constants.json cites it by manifest number and date. Where UESP and a note disagree (armor Infused 20% vs 25%) the note wins. Base character numbers never appear in console notes; pages/Health.md, Magicka.md, Stamina.md, Attributes.md, depth/Weapon_Critical_effect.md, Critical_Damage.md and depth/Physical_Resistance.md carry them, and fixture 003 (naked) reads them back.
- UESP tables are treated as the truth for anything they hold. Community values fill the gaps and are flagged `verified: false`.
- Set piece count comes from the bonus column index in sets.csv: `bonus_N` is the N piece bonus. Monster sets are `bonus_1` and `bonus_2`, mythics `bonus_1`, arena weapons `bonus_2`, 12 piece sets up to `bonus_12`.
- Perfected sets are separate set names in sets.csv ("Perfected Saxhleel Champion"), so the build references the perfected name per piece. Perfected and normal pieces count together under the base name; the perfected extra applies only with the full perfected count (data/patch-notes/html 103, 2021-06-08, Update 30: mixing grants the non-perfected bonuses until all 5 pieces are perfected).

## Build inputs

- Attribute points must sum to 64. The engine reports an error otherwise but still computes. (pages/Health.md line 12: a level 50 character with all 64 stat points.)
- Champion Points on: every non slottable star at max rank, four slottable stars per constellation at max rank. Off: nothing at all. Slottable stars are the "Active Perks" and "Base Active Perks" rows of the UESP Champion tables.
- All passives the character has access to are assumed purchased at max rank (`passives.mode: "all"`), with an exclusion list. Access: the three class lines (subclassing supported via `classSkillLines`), racial line, all three armor lines, all six weapon lines, Fighters Guild, Mages Guild, Psijic Order, Undaunted, Assault, Support, Soul Magic, Vampire (stage above 0), Werewolf (flag). Legerdemain, Dark Brotherhood, Thieves Guild, Scrying, Excavation and crafting lines have no sheet effects and are ignored. (Task statement; passive texts and lines from data/reference/skills.csv.)
- Class Mastery (Update 50, note 194): each class has five passives, two Class Mastery Points exist, and the line is hidden while subclassing. The build lists up to two purchased passives in `classMastery`; the engine ignores them when `classSkillLines` are not the native lines.
- Effects that state a lower value "against targets with Battle Spirit" (Above and Beyond, Feral Cruelty) keep both values. The sheet shows the unreduced value (STRATEGIES.battleSpiritTargetValues = sheet); pvpTarget applies the reduced value when Battle Spirit is on.
- Weapon line passives that say "with X equipped" apply per bar based on that bar's weapons. Armor passives count pieces across the seven armor slots; the shield does not count as armor for piece counts. (skills.csv passive texts; fixtures 001 and 002: Ancient Knowledge applied on the ice staff bar only.)
- A two handed weapon counts as two pieces of its set and only on the bar it is equipped on. One handed weapons and shields count as one piece on their bar. (pages/Weapon_Sets.md line 7: a single two-handed weapon receives the full bonus; sets.csv settype Weapon rows hold bonus_2.)
- At most one mythic item. Monster sets only on head and shoulders. Arena weapon sets only on weapons. (pages/Sets.md: only one mythic item can be worn at a time; a Monster Set has only the head and the shoulders; Weapon Sets hold weapons and shields.)
- Vampire stage effects (Health Recovery, ability costs) come from the UESP Vampire table; Unnatural Resistance softens the recovery penalty.
- Werewolf passives marked "while in werewolf form" only apply when `werewolfForm` is true. The sheet in human form does not show them. (skills.csv Werewolf passive texts, ten rows say "werewolf form".)
- Battle Spirit: the confirmed UESP effects. The flat +5000 Max Health is behind a flag, off by default, until a PvP zone fixture settles it. Effects that state a different value against Battle Spirit targets are stored as conditional effects and only the sheet-facing value is applied. (constants.json battleSpirit: data/patch-notes/html 108, 103 and 007; the +5000 timeline is in UNKNOWNS.md.)
- Food: catalog ids in constants.json, or a custom object typed straight from the tooltip, because food magnitudes are the least verifiable constants.

## Engine

- Order of operations is documented in `engine/src/engine.js` (header comment) and every uncertain point is a named strategy in `STRATEGIES` so it can be switched during validation.

## App

- One page, vanilla JavaScript, no framework, no runtime requests. The build script converts the ES module engine into a plain script and inlines a slimmed effects.json (per effect raw text dropped, bonus and passive text kept) so the page stays near 1 MB. (App design, no game claim.)
- Skill pickers list final nodes only (unmorphed base, or a morph) grouped by line, own class first. Passives are never picked one by one: class, racial, armor and weapon passives follow the build automatically (weapon passives follow each bar's weapons through their conditions), Vampire and Werewolf follow their toggles, and guild plus Alliance War lines are on or off as whole lines (`skillLines`). Lines with no sheet effect (crafting, Legerdemain and the like) are not shown. (App design; morph structure from skills.csv.)
- A set is offered on a slot only where it can be worn: monster sets on head and shoulders, arena weapon sets on weapons, mythics on their slot, three piece jewelry sets on jewelry and weapons. Arena weapon sets are further limited to the weapon kind they drop as (see the weaponTypes entry below). (pages/Sets.md slot rules; sets.csv settype and tags.)
- Results show both bars side by side and a per source breakdown per stat, plus the list of conditional effects the sheet did not apply. (App design, no game claim.)
- The last edited build is restored on reload; named builds live in localStorage under one key. (App design, no game claim.)
- Scribing: the twelve grimoires are slottable abilities of their skill line (Torchbearer counts as a Fighters Guild ability for Slayer, and so on) with focus, signature and affix pickers. Scripts act on cast and change no sheet value this phase, so the pickers only record the choice. Focus names come from the esolog coefficient table, affix scripts from the UESP Buffs page; the catalog is partial (UNKNOWNS.md).
- Attribute points default to 0. Unspent points are not flagged at all (the page shows "N of 64" next to the heading); a total above 64 is an error. (App design; the 64 total from pages/Health.md.)
- A monster set picked on head or shoulders fills the other slot only when it is empty; afterwards either can be changed on its own. (App design; pages/Sets.md.)
- Poisons: one per bar, a combat proc with no sheet effect. A poison overrides the weapon enchant while it has charges, so the page dims the enchant fields on that bar but keeps their values. (pages/Poisons.md: using a poison on a weapon will override its Enchantment until the poison has been depleted.)
- Sets that only drop in one weight (settype Light, Medium or Heavy Armor in sets.csv) lock the weight of their armor pieces. Monster sets and "All Weights" sets stay free.
- Guild and Alliance War lines default on and are whole line toggles; Soul Magic is one of them so its abilities can be slotted. (App design; lines from skills.csv.)
- Champion Point pickers group slottable stars by theme (Warfare: Damage, Healing, Damage reduction, Resources. Fitness: Health and resources, Defense and shields, Utility and CC. Craft: Movement, Stealth and crime, Gathering and fishing). The grouping is a UI convenience agreed with the user and lives in the app template, not in the engine data.
- Two mythics have no slot word in their name and sets.csv gives none, so the parser carries the slot from the in game tooltip supplied by the user: Rakkhat's Voidmantle is medium shoulders, Stormweaver's Cavort is light legs. sets.csv also dropped the leading "Adds 300 Magicka Recovery" line of Stormweaver's Cavort, so the parser overrides that bonus text with the full tooltip.
- "For each X ability slotted" counts every ability on that bar whose skill line (or class, for "each Sorcerer ability") matches, ultimate slot included, morphs by their base line, scribed grimoires by their grimoire's line. Parser prose line names are mapped onto real line names ("Animal Companion" to "Animal Companions"). (skills.csv passive texts such as Slayer and Bracing Anchor; fixtures 001 and 002 for the Fighters Guild count.)
- Scribing scripts come from the UESP Scribing page tables `data/reference/tables/uesp_Online_Scribing_t01..t03.csv`, extracted from a PDF print by `tools/extract_scribing_pdf.py`. Each grimoire offers only the focus, signature and affix scripts that page lists for it; the pickers still accept a typed name for anything missing. Script names follow the page; older names (Class Mastery, Passive Master, Healing Absorption) are aliases applied when a build is loaded.
- Sheet semantics settled by fixture 001: Critical Damage is shown as the bonus above the 50% base on both panels (a naked character reads 0%); Healing Taken, Damage Taken and Damage Shield Strength on the sheet leave Battle Spirit out (Healing Taken read 4% in Cyrodiil), so the engine reports them that way and puts the Battle Spirit adjusted values in `advanced.battleSpirit`. Health Recovery on the sheet does include the Battle Spirit halving.
- Jewelry Glyph of Increase Physical Harm and Magical Harm both add Weapon and Spell Damage (data/patch-notes/html 135, 2023-03-28, Update 37), so the "Weapon Damage" and "Spell Damage" jewelry enchants are the same thing on the sheet.
- A fixture with `"status": "open"` is reported by the runner but does not fail the suite; it is a calibration in progress. Remove the flag once every reading matches.
- "When slotted on either bar" (Merciless Resolve, Inner Light) applies from either bar; plain "While slotted" needs the ability on the active bar. Fixtures 001 and 002: Major Savagery showed on the staff bar without the ability, Minor Berserk from Bird of Prey did not.
- Cost reductions: flat first, armor passives add up, a weapon passive multiplies on top (`STRATEGIES.costWeaponPercent`); Sprint Cost is the exception, see the 2026-09-19 sprint line below. Block cost base is 1750 and sprint 500 per second from the two fixtures.
- Battle Spirit adds 1600 Max Health before percent bonuses (two fixtures, 7 attribute points apart). On by default; `flags.battleSpiritFlatHealth: false` leaves it out. (fixtures 001 and 002; superseded by the fixture 007 line below.)
- Fixture 005 (Templar, geared, front bar) rules: Block Mitigation is 50 x (1 + Champion Point percent) plus one point per heavy piece (52 naked, 54 with two, 55 with three); a flat added to Spell Resistance alone (Spell Warding) is not multiplied by an armor percent (Balanced Warrior), so the sheet reads Spell Resistance = Physical Resistance + 726, and neither is the Defending weapon trait (fixture 006: the resistance gap between the bars was exactly 6% of 3276); Disease and Poison Resistance add a racial flat (Resist Affliction 2310) to the physical rating before the divide; The Shadow counts for Critical Healing (Dexterity 6 + Fighting Finesse 8 + The Shadow 11 = 25); Dark Stalker removes the whole sneak penalty (Sneak Speed 100); passive Champion Point stars are assumed bought unless listed in `championPoints.notTaken` (Sprint Cost 470 = 500 x 0.94 with no Sprinter); Expert Evasion leaves the regular Roll Dodge Cost on the sheet here with a note (the game shows 0 while the free roll is primed).
- Fixture 007 (Necromancer healer, Cyrodiil) rules: Battle Spirit adds no flat Max Health (33289 lands exactly without the 1600 that fixtures 001 and 002 had suggested; `flags.battleSpiritFlatHealth` now defaults to false and the Yeets fixtures keep it on so the earlier evidence stays visible, its difference sits on Yeets' back bar); Champion Point stars can carry the stages actually bought (`championPoints.points`), which is what separates Sneak Cost 34, 55 and 94 across the three characters (Sustaining Shadows at 50, 31 and 10 of 50 stages).
- Fixture 008 (Necromancer back bar, dagger and shield) rules: Curative Curse applies under Battle Spirit (Healing Done sits 12 above every other source on both bars in Cyrodiil, with and without the staff), so the engine treats Battle Spirit as the negative effect and leaves the passive a proc elsewhere; Deadly Bash's 50% changes the bash base before the Champion Point flat and the armor percent (257 = (765 x 0.5 - 90) x 0.88), while block keeps its flat first order (`STRATEGIES.bashWeaponPercent`); a gold shield is 1720 armor (1995 with Reinforced, the bar to bar difference); Battlefield Mobility reads Block Move Speed 54 with a shield (a fit from one reading, the passive text would give 64).
- Fixture 009 (Dragonknight, Cyrodiil gate, dual maces) rules: Combat Medic's 20% Healing Done is on the sheet near a Keep (`flags.nearKeep`, a toggle beside Battle Spirit; fixtures 001 and 009 both read it at a gate), food health values scale by 1.17442 (4316/3675) while Magicka and Stamina values scale by 1.1735 (Sugar Skulls 4624 Health and 4250 Magicka and Stamina both exact), and the dual mace Weapon Damage gap is the same 236 flat as Yeets' (Nirnhoned main, Sharpened off), so it is a weapon rule and not a set.
- Mythics with a rule beyond stats: Torc of the Last Ayleid King ("Disable all other item set bonuses", `disablesOtherSets`) keeps only its own bonus and lists the others as dropped; Velothi Ur-Mage's Amulet's permanent Minor Force is split out of its monster clause. The other 33 mythics are stat lines (parsed) or combat and stack effects (procs); Mora's Whispers (books collected) and Thrassian Stranglers (kill stacks) are character state the build does not carry yet. (sets.csv bonus_1 texts of Torc of the Last Ayleid King and Velothi Ur-Mage's Amulet; the user's tooltip for the Velothi text.)
- Passive Champion Point stars: grouped by tree in the app, ticked means every stage; unticking shows the attribute style stepper (0, minus, value, plus, max) and writes `championPoints.points`; `notTaken` is read as zero stages for old exports. (App design, no game claim; stars and stages come from the Champion tables in constants.json.)
- Oakensoul Ring (any set whose text says you cannot swap weapon sets, `locksBackBar` in effects.json): the front bar is the only bar. Its named buffs (Major Brutality, Sorcery, Prophecy, Savagery, Resolve, Minor Berserk, Courage, Force, Protection, Mending, Fortitude, Intellect, Endurance, Heroism, Slayer, Aegis) apply through the named buff table; Empower has no sheet stat. The back bar result is the front bar's, marked `locked`, only the front bar's skills count for "on either bar" effects, and the app greys the back bar and shows one column. (sets.csv bonus_1 text of Oakensoul Ring; buff magnitudes from constants.json namedBuffs.)
- Cyrodiil advanced sim (`flags.cyrodiil`, shown only while Battle Spirit is on): near a keep (Combat Medic 20% Healing Done; fixtures 001 and 009 read it at a gate, 007 at the same gate did not, so it is where the character stands), after a capture (Continuous Attack: 10% Weapon and Spell Damage, 20% recoveries, for 10 minutes), offensive and defensive scrolls held (bonus I and II: 2% and 5% damage or resistance, UESP campaign table), enemy keeps held (1% crit each, I to IX), alliance Emperorship by home keeps held (900 to 1750 Max Health, I to VI, scaling per Update 36), and "I am the Emperor" (the Emperor skill line is not archived, so it adds nothing yet). How the scroll and keep percents combine with the rest of the sheet is unverified until a reading carries one.
- Dual wield: the off hand reaches the sheet as 17.67% of its own rating (trait included) plus Dual Wield Expert's 6%, so 23.67% with the passive; the main hand's Nirnhoned adds its 15% as before. The share was fitted from fixtures 002, 005 and 009 (agreeing to 0.05%) and is confirmed by the UESP build editor's author on the forum (348673, 2017: "DW = 1335 + 1335*0.177 = 1571", two maces equal a two handed weapon). `STRATEGIES.offHandRating` keeps the passive only reading as an alternative.
- Weapon damage rating: 1335 for one handed weapons, bows and staves, 1571 for greatswords, battle axes and mauls (UESP Nirnhoned page, CP160 rows, and the same forum post). No reading on file carries a two handed melee weapon yet.
- Emperor: with "I am the Emperor" on, Domination (recoveries), Monarch (healing taken) and Emperor (Max Health, Magicka and Stamina) apply by the home keeps count from the Emperor page; Authority and Tactician have no sheet stat. (pages/Emperor.md passives table.)
- Vampire: the stage Health Recovery penalty applies in full (stage 3: 60%, UESP Vampire page table and note 087). Unnatural Resistance was removed in Greymoor (replaced by Undeath, the page's Update 26 note lists it among the removed skills) and is dropped by the parser; the archived skill text was stale.
- Armor ratings come from the DK's tooltips (2026-09-19): heavy chest 2772 and big pieces 2425 (Reinforced 3215 and 2813 at 16%), medium big 1823, light big 1221, light hands 698, light waist 523. Slot factors are 0.875 for head, shoulders, legs and feet, 0.5 for hands and 0.375 for the waist, truncated; the weights step by 688 (light 1396, medium 2084, heavy 2772 chests). The medium and light chests and the small heavy and medium pieces are derived from those factors and stay unverified. Tooltips truncate (2114 = 1823 x 1.16); the sheet total matches per piece rounding on the DK and the Necro exactly.
- Sheet Bash Damage = (flat bash bonuses: Bashing Brutality 120, Deadly Bash 500, Glyph of Bashing 500) plus 0.02252 x the average of Physical and Spell Resistance, times (1 + Physical damage done + direct damage done). The esolog Bash row gives the form (0.0224424 x MaxResist); the readings fit the average of the two resistances (the Breton healer separates them) and a coefficient of 0.02252. Fixtures 001 to 003 and 007 to 010 exact, 006 within 2. Light Armor Penalties' bash reduction does not show on the sheet.
- Every glyph magnitude comes from its UESP glyph page (pages/glyphs, Truly Superb row, CP160), with the Glyph of Bashing kept at the patch note 087 value (500 bash damage) because the page still lists the pre Greymoor 348 Weapon and Spell Damage. Small armor pieces sum at 40.4% truncated on the sheet (385, 192, 350) where the pages say 40%.
- Glyph values on the sheet: large pieces read as the tooltip (954 Health, 477 / 434 / 434 Prismatic Defense); small pieces are 0.4043 of the large piece truncated (385, 192 / 175 / 175, 350) where the tooltip rounds (386, 193, 351). Four characters' Max Health hold the truncated values.
- `build.activeBuffs`: named buffs the user marks as up at the reading (a potion, a skill cast beforehand), applied through the buff table without self stacking; a checklist under the food picker. Fixture 005 carries Major Brutality and Sorcery this way.
- The sheet's Damage Done line folds the single target star in, like the per type lines (fixtures 001 to 004).
- Readings are a database (`reading-log` skill): one fixture file per bar with the build JSON, photos, every number, and a `corrections` list that records what the user changed later; `npm run fixtures` keeps `engine/tests/fixtures/INDEX.md` current.
- Base values settled by fixture 003 (naked): Max Health 16000, Max Magicka and Stamina 12000, 122 and 111 per attribute point, Magicka and Stamina Recovery 514, Health Recovery 309 with Capacitor and Robustness at face value, block 1750, sprint 500, bash 765, break free 5400, roll dodge 4040, sprint speed 140% (movement speed bonuses do not show in Sprint Speed), and a 1320 baseline Critical Resistance (Update 26 note: 20% critical damage reduction for every player).
- Small armor pieces (shoulders, hands, waist, feet) carry 40.3% of a large piece's glyph value: seven Prismatic Defense pieces total 2002 Magicka in fixtures 002 and 003 with 434 on each large piece. Applied to every armor glyph.
- The app lists the main sheet and the advanced stats in the same order as the in game character sheet (fixtures 001 to 004), with Critical Damage and Critical Healing added at the end of the main sheet. Damage type rows show the sheet's FLAT 0 / PERCENT pair; the percent is general damage done plus the single target Champion star the sheet folds in plus the type's own bonus (Energized). Elemental resistance percents are the spell mitigation, Disease, Poison and Bleed the physical mitigation.
- Food and drink magnitudes come from the esolog coefficient table's buff rows times 1.1735 for CP160 gold (Bewitched Sugar Skulls 3937/3622/393 in the table against the known 4620/4250/462; the fitted Smoked Bear Haunch 4316/406/369 against the table's 3675/346/315). Magicka and Stamina values are truncated (Bear Haunch recovery 369, pinned by fixtures 001 and 002 reading 1433 and 1457 from the same 1156 pre-percent sum); Health values round. Recipes not in that table keep their UESP scaling table values (the Food and Drinks pages' per level tables give the standard green, blue and purple recipes at CP160; the special recipes live only in the esolog buff rows). Candied Jester's Coins (4928 Stamina, 492 Magicka Recovery), Ghastly Eye Bowl (4928 Magicka, 492 Magicka Recovery) and the generic purple tri stat food (the Sugar Skulls magnitudes without the recovery) come from those rows.
- Jewelry harm glyphs carry recovery: Spell Damage (Spell Harm) adds 10 Magicka Recovery and Weapon Damage (Physical Harm) 10 Stamina Recovery at every quality (note 135, Update 37), scaled by Infused like the damage part. A glyph value entry may carry its own `magnitude` for this. Fixtures 001, 002, 007 to 010 match on Magicka Recovery with it; the Yeets fixtures now say Spell Damage (user correction, recorded in their `corrections`).
- Prismatic Recovery is 84 per recovery and Reduce Skill Cost 133 (glyph tooltip image from the user, 2026-09-19). The Templar reading (005, 006) is exact with nothing from its ring1; the user's tooltip of that ring (photos/tooltip-templar-ring1-monomyth-reforged.jpg, 2026-09-19) reads Reduce Prismatic Cost 133, so the export's Prismatic Recovery was wrong and both fixtures carry the correction (46 and 48 of 51 now).
- ESO Plus (`flags.esoPlus`, default on) shows the 10% Experience, Gold, Crafting Inspiration, Tel Var and Alliance Points the sheet lists. (fixtures 001 to 004, active effects list.)
- Sneak cost reductions multiply per source (Sustaining Shadows, Medium Armor Bonuses, Improved Sneak), the one cost that does; Sneak Speed's 60% base already includes the Champion Point passives, so only armor passives move it. (fixtures 002, 005 and 007: Sneak Cost 34, 55 and 94; skills.csv passive texts.)
- Item quality: `flags.itemQuality` off means every item is gold whatever its `quality` field says; on, each armor, jewelry and weapon piece carries white to gold. Trait magnitudes come from the five quality columns of the UESP trait tables (verified); armor and weapon ratings scale by `items.qualityFactor` (unverified below gold until a purple piece is read). Glyph quality is a separate axis, see the `enchantQuality` entry below.
- Skill slots are positional: `bars[i].skills` has five entries, null for an empty slot, because the slots are the buttons. Nothing moves up when an earlier slot is empty. (App design, no game claim.)
- Weapon and armor skill line passives follow the equipped weapons and worn weights, never the skills on the bar (a Restoration Staff skill slotted without a staff gives no Restoration Staff passives). Every such passive in the data carries an equipment condition; a test holds it. (skills.csv passive texts carry the equipment condition; fixtures 001 and 002.)
- Attack classification: every ability's damage or heal placeholders in the esolog table are tagged single target or area and direct or over time, so `actives[name].attack` says which of Deadly Aim (single target), Master-at-Arms (direct), Biting Aura (area) and Thaumaturge (over time) apply to it. The character sheet folds only the single target star into its per type percents; the four category bonuses are listed separately in the advanced panel.
- Glyph quality (`enchantQuality` per item, read only while `flags.itemQuality` is on): each glyph's magnitude at white to gold is the Truly Superb (CP160) row of its UESP glyph page (`enchants.armor.*.byQuality`, `enchants.jewelry.*.byQuality`, read by `tools/build_constants.py`; the gold row must equal the value already in use, so the stale Glyph of Bashing page is excluded and that glyph falls back to `enchants.glyphQualityFactor`). Small armor pieces take `enchants.glyphSmallRatio` (0.4043) of the large value truncated, as every gold reading shows; below gold that derivation is unverified. The harm glyphs' 10 recovery holds at every quality (note 135). The Infused trait's percent follows the item's own quality (trait table columns). The app shows a glyph quality stepper under every armor, jewelry and shield glyph, and a bulk field; weapon glyphs are procs and get none.
- Set bonuses scale with item quality as well as level. Online:Craftable Sets (pages/Craftable_Sets.md, "Many sets have a bonus specified as a range which depends on item level and quality") carries a CP160 row by quality for six bonus types: tables/uesp_Online_Craftable_Sets_t03.csv row '160' (recovery 112 / 117 / 121 / 124 / 129 white to gold), t04 (Max Magicka or Stamina 956 to 1096), t05 (Max Health 1051 to 1206), t06 (Weapon or Spell Damage 112 to 129), t07 (Critical 726 to 833), t08 (Resistance 2595 to 2975). A range like "6-300" runs from level 1 white to CP160 gold, so taking its maximum is right for gold CP160 and for no other reason. constants.json `sets.bonusByQuality` holds the rows and the per type multipliers; the parser marks every effect parsed from a range (`ranged`), and with the item quality toggle on the engine multiplies such flat bonuses by the multiplier of the set's piece quality on that bar. Percent bonuses and fixed numbers never scale. Two ranged types have no table (Offensive Penetration, Critical Resistance) and stay at gold with a note; which piece decides a mixed quality set is `STRATEGIES.setBonusQuality`, default lowest piece; both are UNKNOWNS. This replaces the earlier line that held set bonuses constant under quality, which rested on no archive source.
- Arena weapon sets exist for one weapon kind, so the parser maps the UESP set tags onto the build's weapon types (`weaponTypes` in effects.json: Bow; Destruction Staff to the three elemental staves; Restoration Staff; Two Handed Weapon to greatsword, battle axe and maul; One Handed Weapon alone to axe, mace, sword and dagger for the dual wield sets; One Handed Weapon plus Shield to those and the shield for the one hand and shield sets). The engine reports a set on the wrong kind as a validation error, the app offers a set only on the matching kind and clears it when the weapon type changes, and the weapon pair fill covers the shield of a one hand and shield set but never a shield for a dual wield set. (sets.csv "ESO Sets With" tags; pages/Weapon_Sets.md.)
- The rebuilt sets.csv (2026-09-17) carries perfected extras in their own columns, `bonus_2_perfected` (30 arena weapon sets) and `bonus_5_perfected` (32 trial sets), on the Perfected row; the parser reads them onto that bonus index with `pieces` equal to the index (older exports folded "N perfected items: ..." into the text and are still understood). Before this the regenerated effects.json had silently lost every perfected extra, and the arena weapons' perfected lines had never been on file.
- Audit of 2026-09-19 (tools/audit_citations.py, the archive-lookup skill): the base constants that had been carried as community values are in the archive after all. pages/Health.md, Magicka.md, Stamina.md and Attributes.md give 16000 / 12000 / 12000, 122 and 111 per point, 64 points, 309 / 514 / 514 recovery; pages/depth/Weapon_Critical_effect.md gives the 10% base and the 21912 rating for 100% at CP160 (219 per percent, and data/patch-notes/html 100 says 657 = 3%); pages/Critical_Damage.md gives 50% base and the 125% cap; pages/depth/Physical_Resistance.md gives the 33,000 cap at 50% (660 per percent).
