# DECISIONS

Short record of modelling choices. One line each, newest at the bottom.

## Scope

- Phase 1 computes the character sheet only. No combat, no targets.
- Fixed: level 50, CP160 gear, gold quality, set ranges resolve to the maximum ("6-300" is 300). Quality per item is reserved in the schema (`quality: "gold"`) for a later revision, as is a proper set bonus scaling formula.
- Out of scope this phase because they need a target or combat state (listed so they are not forgotten): damage done vs a specific target, penetration vs target resistance, Bloodthirsty trait, execute bonuses, Off Balance, status effect chance, Enemy Keep and Scroll bonuses, Emperorship, Empower, all 5 piece and 1 piece proc effects, weapon glyph procs, Major or Minor buffs from casting abilities, potions, Vengeance campaign stat standardisation, scribing signature scripts, companion skills.

## Data

- Patch notes (`data/patch-notes/html`) were searched for every constant. Where they hold a value, the newest note wins and constants.json cites it by manifest number and date. Where UESP and a note disagree (armor Infused 20% vs 25%) the note wins. Base character numbers never appear in console notes and stay unverified.
- UESP tables are treated as the truth for anything they hold. Community values fill the gaps and are flagged `verified: false`.
- Set piece count comes from the bonus column index in sets.csv: `bonus_N` is the N piece bonus. Monster sets are `bonus_1` and `bonus_2`, mythics `bonus_1`, arena weapons `bonus_2`, 12 piece sets up to `bonus_12`.
- Perfected sets are separate set names in sets.csv ("Perfected Saxhleel Champion"), so the build references the perfected name per piece. Perfected and normal pieces count together under the base name; the perfected extra applies only with the full perfected count (data/patch-notes/html 103, 2021-06-08, Update 30: mixing grants the non-perfected bonuses until all 5 pieces are perfected).

## Build inputs

- Attribute points must sum to 64. The engine reports an error otherwise but still computes.
- Champion Points on: every non slottable star at max rank, four slottable stars per constellation at max rank. Off: nothing at all. Slottable stars are the "Active Perks" and "Base Active Perks" rows of the UESP Champion tables.
- All passives the character has access to are assumed purchased at max rank (`passives.mode: "all"`), with an exclusion list. Access: the three class lines (subclassing supported via `classSkillLines`), racial line, all three armor lines, all six weapon lines, Fighters Guild, Mages Guild, Psijic Order, Undaunted, Assault, Support, Soul Magic, Vampire (stage above 0), Werewolf (flag). Legerdemain, Dark Brotherhood, Thieves Guild, Scrying, Excavation and crafting lines have no sheet effects and are ignored.
- Class Mastery (Update 50, note 194): each class has five passives, two Class Mastery Points exist, and the line is hidden while subclassing. The build lists up to two purchased passives in `classMastery`; the engine ignores them when `classSkillLines` are not the native lines.
- Effects that state a lower value "against targets with Battle Spirit" (Above and Beyond, Feral Cruelty) keep both values. The sheet shows the unreduced value (STRATEGIES.battleSpiritTargetValues = sheet); pvpTarget applies the reduced value when Battle Spirit is on.
- Weapon line passives that say "with X equipped" apply per bar based on that bar's weapons. Armor passives count pieces across the seven armor slots; the shield does not count as armor for piece counts.
- A two handed weapon counts as two pieces of its set and only on the bar it is equipped on. One handed weapons and shields count as one piece on their bar.
- At most one mythic item. Monster sets only on head and shoulders. Arena weapon sets only on weapons.
- Vampire stage effects (Health Recovery, ability costs) come from the UESP Vampire table; Unnatural Resistance softens the recovery penalty.
- Werewolf passives marked "while in werewolf form" only apply when `werewolfForm` is true. The sheet in human form does not show them.
- Battle Spirit: the confirmed UESP effects. The flat +5000 Max Health is behind a flag, off by default, until a PvP zone fixture settles it. Effects that state a different value against Battle Spirit targets are stored as conditional effects and only the sheet-facing value is applied.
- Food: catalog ids in constants.json, or a custom object typed straight from the tooltip, because food magnitudes are the least verifiable constants.

## Engine

- Order of operations is documented in `engine/src/engine.js` (header comment) and every uncertain point is a named strategy in `STRATEGIES` so it can be switched during validation.

## App

- One page, vanilla JavaScript, no framework, no runtime requests. The build script converts the ES module engine into a plain script and inlines a slimmed effects.json (per effect raw text dropped, bonus and passive text kept) so the page stays near 1 MB.
- Skill pickers list final nodes only (unmorphed base, or a morph) grouped by line, own class first. Passives are never picked one by one: class, racial, armor and weapon passives follow the build automatically (weapon passives follow each bar's weapons through their conditions), Vampire and Werewolf follow their toggles, and guild plus Alliance War lines are on or off as whole lines (`skillLines`). Lines with no sheet effect (crafting, Legerdemain and the like) are not shown.
- A set is offered on a slot only where it can be worn: monster sets on head and shoulders, arena weapon sets on weapons, mythics on their slot, three piece jewelry sets on jewelry and weapons. Weapon type restrictions of arena weapon sets are not enforced yet.
- Results show both bars side by side and a per source breakdown per stat, plus the list of conditional effects the sheet did not apply.
- The last edited build is restored on reload; named builds live in localStorage under one key.
- Scribing: the twelve grimoires are slottable abilities of their skill line (Torchbearer counts as a Fighters Guild ability for Slayer, and so on) with focus, signature and affix pickers. Scripts act on cast and change no sheet value this phase, so the pickers only record the choice. Focus names come from the esolog coefficient table, affix scripts from the UESP Buffs page; the catalog is partial (UNKNOWNS.md).
- Attribute points default to 0. Unspent points are not flagged at all (the page shows "N of 64" next to the heading); a total above 64 is an error.
- A monster set picked on head or shoulders fills the other slot only when it is empty; afterwards either can be changed on its own.
- Poisons: one per bar, a combat proc with no sheet effect. A poison overrides the weapon enchant while it has charges, so the page dims the enchant fields on that bar but keeps their values.
- Sets that only drop in one weight (settype Light, Medium or Heavy Armor in sets.csv) lock the weight of their armor pieces. Monster sets and "All Weights" sets stay free.
- Guild and Alliance War lines default on and are whole line toggles; Soul Magic is one of them so its abilities can be slotted.
- Champion Point pickers group slottable stars by theme (Warfare: Damage, Healing, Damage reduction, Resources. Fitness: Health and resources, Defense and shields, Utility and CC. Craft: Movement, Stealth and crime, Gathering and fishing). The grouping is a UI convenience agreed with the user and lives in the app template, not in the engine data.
- Two mythics have no slot word in their name and sets.csv gives none, so the parser carries the slot from the in game tooltip supplied by the user: Rakkhat's Voidmantle is medium shoulders, Stormweaver's Cavort is light legs. sets.csv also dropped the leading "Adds 300 Magicka Recovery" line of Stormweaver's Cavort, so the parser overrides that bonus text with the full tooltip.
- "For each X ability slotted" counts every ability on that bar whose skill line (or class, for "each Sorcerer ability") matches, ultimate slot included, morphs by their base line, scribed grimoires by their grimoire's line. Parser prose line names are mapped onto real line names ("Animal Companion" to "Animal Companions").
- Scribing scripts come from the UESP Scribing page tables `data/reference/tables/uesp_Online_Scribing_t01..t03.csv`, extracted from a PDF print by `tools/extract_scribing_pdf.py`. Each grimoire offers only the focus, signature and affix scripts that page lists for it; the pickers still accept a typed name for anything missing. Script names follow the page; older names (Class Mastery, Passive Master, Healing Absorption) are aliases applied when a build is loaded.
- Sheet semantics settled by fixture 001: Critical Damage is shown as the bonus above the 50% base on both panels (a naked character reads 0%); Healing Taken, Damage Taken and Damage Shield Strength on the sheet leave Battle Spirit out (Healing Taken read 4% in Cyrodiil), so the engine reports them that way and puts the Battle Spirit adjusted values in `advanced.battleSpirit`. Health Recovery on the sheet does include the Battle Spirit halving.
- Jewelry Glyph of Increase Physical Harm and Magical Harm both add Weapon and Spell Damage (Update 37 note), so the "Weapon Damage" and "Spell Damage" jewelry enchants are the same thing on the sheet.
- A fixture with `"status": "open"` is reported by the runner but does not fail the suite; it is a calibration in progress. Remove the flag once every reading matches.
- "When slotted on either bar" (Merciless Resolve, Inner Light) applies from either bar; plain "While slotted" needs the ability on the active bar. Fixtures 001 and 002: Major Savagery showed on the staff bar without the ability, Minor Berserk from Bird of Prey did not.
- Cost reductions: flat first, armor passives add up, a weapon passive multiplies on top (`STRATEGIES.costWeaponPercent`). Block cost base is 1750 and sprint 500 per second from the two fixtures.
- Battle Spirit adds 1600 Max Health before percent bonuses (two fixtures, 7 attribute points apart). On by default; `flags.battleSpiritFlatHealth: false` leaves it out.
- Fixture 005 (Templar, geared, front bar) rules: Block Mitigation is 50 x (1 + Champion Point percent) plus one point per heavy piece (52 naked, 54 with two, 55 with three); a flat added to Spell Resistance alone (Spell Warding) is not multiplied by an armor percent (Balanced Warrior), so the sheet reads Spell Resistance = Physical Resistance + 726, and neither is the Defending weapon trait (fixture 006: the resistance gap between the bars was exactly 6% of 3276); Disease and Poison Resistance add a racial flat (Resist Affliction 2310) to the physical rating before the divide; The Shadow counts for Critical Healing (Dexterity 6 + Fighting Finesse 8 + The Shadow 11 = 25); Dark Stalker removes the whole sneak penalty (Sneak Speed 100); passive Champion Point stars are assumed bought unless listed in `championPoints.notTaken` (Sprint Cost 470 = 500 x 0.94 with no Sprinter); Expert Evasion leaves the regular Roll Dodge Cost on the sheet here with a note (the game shows 0 while the free roll is primed).
- Fixture 007 (Necromancer healer, Cyrodiil) rules: Battle Spirit adds no flat Max Health (33289 lands exactly without the 1600 that fixtures 001 and 002 had suggested; `flags.battleSpiritFlatHealth` now defaults to false and the Yeets fixtures keep it on so the earlier evidence stays visible, its difference sits on Yeets' back bar); Champion Point stars can carry the stages actually bought (`championPoints.points`), which is what separates Sneak Cost 34, 55 and 94 across the three characters (Sustaining Shadows at 50, 31 and 10 of 50 stages).
- Fixture 008 (Necromancer back bar, dagger and shield) rules: Curative Curse applies under Battle Spirit (Healing Done sits 12 above every other source on both bars in Cyrodiil, with and without the staff), so the engine treats Battle Spirit as the negative effect and leaves the passive a proc elsewhere; Deadly Bash's 50% changes the bash base before the Champion Point flat and the armor percent (257 = (765 x 0.5 - 90) x 0.88), while block keeps its flat first order (`STRATEGIES.bashWeaponPercent`); a gold shield is 1720 armor (1995 with Reinforced, the bar to bar difference); Battlefield Mobility reads Block Move Speed 54 with a shield (a fit from one reading, the passive text would give 64).
- Base values settled by fixture 003 (naked): Max Health 16000, Max Magicka and Stamina 12000, 122 and 111 per attribute point, Magicka and Stamina Recovery 514, Health Recovery 309 with Capacitor and Robustness at face value, block 1750, sprint 500, bash 765, break free 5400, roll dodge 4040, sprint speed 140% (movement speed bonuses do not show in Sprint Speed), and a 1320 baseline Critical Resistance (Update 26 note: 20% critical damage reduction for every player).
- Small armor pieces (shoulders, hands, waist, feet) carry 40.3% of a large piece's glyph value: seven Prismatic Defense pieces total 2002 Magicka in fixtures 002 and 003 with 434 on each large piece. Applied to every armor glyph.
- The app lists the main sheet and the advanced stats in the same order as the in game character sheet (fixtures 001 to 004), with Critical Damage and Critical Healing added at the end of the main sheet. Damage type rows show the sheet's FLAT 0 / PERCENT pair; the percent is general damage done plus the single target Champion star the sheet folds in plus the type's own bonus (Energized). Elemental resistance percents are the spell mitigation, Disease, Poison and Bleed the physical mitigation.
- Food and drink magnitudes come from the esolog coefficient table's buff rows times 1.1735 for CP160 gold (Bewitched Sugar Skulls 3937/3622/393 in the table against the known 4620/4250/462; the fitted Smoked Bear Haunch 4316/406/369 against the table's 3675/346/315). Recipes not in that table keep their UESP scaling table values.
- ESO Plus (`flags.esoPlus`, default on) shows the 10% Experience, Gold, Crafting Inspiration, Tel Var and Alliance Points the sheet lists.
- Sneak cost reductions multiply per source (Sustaining Shadows, Medium Armor Bonuses, Improved Sneak), the one cost that does; Sneak Speed's 60% base already includes the Champion Point passives, so only armor passives move it.
- Item quality: `flags.itemQuality` off means every item is gold whatever its `quality` field says; on, each armor, jewelry and weapon piece carries white to gold. Trait magnitudes come from the five quality columns of the UESP trait tables (verified); armor and weapon ratings scale by `items.qualityFactor` (unverified below gold until a purple piece is read). Glyph quality is a separate axis and is not modelled yet.
- Skill slots are positional: `bars[i].skills` has five entries, null for an empty slot, because the slots are the buttons. Nothing moves up when an earlier slot is empty.
- Weapon and armor skill line passives follow the equipped weapons and worn weights, never the skills on the bar (a Restoration Staff skill slotted without a staff gives no Restoration Staff passives). Every such passive in the data carries an equipment condition; a test holds it.
- Attack classification: every ability's damage or heal placeholders in the esolog table are tagged single target or area and direct or over time, so `actives[name].attack` says which of Deadly Aim (single target), Master-at-Arms (direct), Biting Aura (area) and Thaumaturge (over time) apply to it. The character sheet folds only the single target star into its per type percents; the four category bonuses are listed separately in the advanced panel.
